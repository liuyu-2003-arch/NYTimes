普丁的「棋局」：重塑美俄關係而無需作出任何讓步

Putin Nears a Prize: Restoring Ties With the U.S. While Giving Up Nothing

VALERIE HOPKINS • 2026-10-23
Last October, President Trump imposed significant sanctions on Russia’s two largest oil companies, complaining that negotiations with President Vladimir V. Putin over ending the war in Ukraine “just don’t go anywhere.”
去年10月，川普總統對俄羅斯兩家最大的石油公司實施了重大制裁，並抱怨與普丁總統就結束烏克蘭戰爭的談判「就是毫無進展」。
A red-carpet welcome for the Russian leader in Alaska had done nothing to bring peace. A planned summit in Budapest had fallen apart. Mr. Trump had started to acknowledge that Mr. Putin might just be playing him, with no intention of making peace. Faint Ukrainian hopes glimmered of a Trump-Putin break.
在阿拉斯加迎接這位俄羅斯領導人的紅毯儀式並未帶來和平。計劃中的布達佩斯峰會告吹。川普已開始承認，普丁可能只是在耍他，並無意實現和平。烏克蘭方面對川普與普丁決裂抱有微弱的希望。
But the Russian president was playing a longer game.
但這位俄羅斯總統在下更長遠的棋。
Now, exactly a year after the oil sanctions, Mr. Putin has never been so close to his goal of normalizing relations with Washington without having to make any compromises on Ukraine.
如今，恰好在石油制裁一年之後，普丁距離他的目標從未如此接近——在不就烏克蘭問題作出任何妥協的情況下，與華盛頓實現關係正常化。
廣告
Over the past week and a half, Mr. Trump has set aside years of U.S. foreign policy by openly embracing Mr. Putin and easing the economic pressure on the Kremlin. At the same time, Mr. Trump has vilified President Volodymyr Zelensky of Ukraine, the putative American ally trying to defend his nation against a Russian invasion.
過去一週半裡，川普拋開了美國多年的外交政策，公開擁抱普丁，放鬆了對克里姆林宮的經濟壓力。與此同時，川普詆毀烏克蘭總統澤連斯基，這位被視為美國盟友的領導人正竭力抵禦俄羅斯的入侵。
On Oct. 9, Mr. Trump announced that the United States would buy Russian diesel, asking for no concessions from Mr. Putin in return. The Trump administration went on to pressure Ukraine to stop striking oil refineries — Kyiv’s most powerful lever — and suspended Russia sanctions that Mr. Trump had just signed into law. Mr. Trump declared that “Russia, frankly, has been fine on Ukraine.”
10月9日，川普宣布美國將購買俄羅斯柴油，且不要求普丁作出任何讓步作為回報。川普政府隨後向烏克蘭施壓，要求其停止襲擊俄羅斯煉油廠——這是基輔最有力的籌碼——並暫停了川普剛剛簽署成法的對俄制裁。川普宣稱，「坦率地說，俄羅斯在烏克蘭問題上一直還好。」
The diesel deal was in part a political calculation by Mr. Trump before the midterm elections, as he tries to lower fuel prices. But that action and others that followed also reflect the president’s long-evident affinity for Mr. Putin, which the Russian leader has assiduously cultivated even while continuing the war and denying Mr. Trump a coveted prize.
柴油協議一定程度上是川普在期中選舉前為降低燃油價格而做出的政治考量。但這一行動以及隨後的其他行動也反映出總統長期以來對普丁顯而易見的親近，而這位俄羅斯領導人在繼續戰爭、並且讓川普得不到他所渴望的成果的同時，一直在精心培植這種親近。
Ever since Mr. Trump returned to office, Russia has sought to separate the conflict in Ukraine from potential business agreements and closer diplomatic ties with the United States. The Trump administration held back, even as it slashed aid to Ukraine, largely continuing a Biden administration policy of refraining from economic deals with Russia until Mr. Putin stopped the war.
自川普重返白宮以來，俄羅斯一直試圖將烏克蘭衝突與潛在的商業協議以及與美國的更緊密外交關係區分開來。儘管川普政府大幅削減了對烏克蘭的援助，但仍保持克制，基本上延續了拜登政府的政策：在普丁停止戰爭之前，不與俄羅斯做經濟交易。
The White House now says such deals will help Russia see the benefits of peace. The Kremlin said on Wednesday that Mr. Putin, meeting with Mr. Trump’s special envoy, Steve Witkoff, had agreed to talks with Ukraine and the United States next month. But they would involve relatively low-level representatives, and a Russian official played down the Kremlin’s openness to forging a comprehensive agreement now.
白宮現在表示，此類協議有助於讓俄羅斯看到和平的好處。克里姆林宮週三表示，普丁在會見川普特使史提夫·威特科夫時，同意下個月與烏克蘭和美國舉行會談。但會談將由相對低級別的代表參加，且一名俄羅斯官員淡化了克里姆林宮現在願意達成全面協議的可能性。
Newly emboldened, Moscow is moving to assert itself as a global power equal to the United States and China, promoting a trilateral summit among them in November.
莫斯科新近受到鼓舞，正採取行動，將自己定位為與美國和中國平起平坐的全球大國，推動在11月舉行三方峰會。
廣告
“In my personal view, victory has already been achieved,” the pro-Putin journalist Marina Akhmedova wrote on social media on Wednesday. “It is not just a matter of the 20 to 25 percent of territory we have liberated,” she added, referring to Russian-occupied land in Ukraine. “It is also about how the United States now engages with Russia.”
「在我看來，勝利已經實現，」親普丁的記者瑪麗娜·阿赫梅多娃週三在社群媒體上寫道。「這不僅僅關於我們解放的20%到25%的領土，」她還說，她指的是俄羅斯佔領的烏克蘭土地。「還關於美國現在如何與俄羅斯打交道。」
Many analysts call the economic incentives offered by Mr. Trump the wrong solution to a war being waged over political and security questions. Still, during his visit to St. Petersburg, Russia, on Wednesday, Mr. Witkoff said that the diesel deal was “another example of what we can achieve by working together.” Mr. Putin, in turn, suggested a deal on Russian fertilizer.
許多分析人士認為，對於這場因政治和安全問題而爆發的戰爭來說，川普提供的經濟激勵措施並非正確的解決方案。不過，威特科夫週三在俄羅斯聖彼得堡訪問期間表示，這一柴油協議是「我們通過合作能夠取得成果的又一個例子」。普丁則提議就俄羅斯化肥達成協議。
Other potential agreements are on the horizon. Russia and the United States have discussed the sale of the international assets of the Russian energy giant Lukoil to an investor group that includes the U.S. government, The New York Times reported. The Times also reported that American negotiators have pitched the idea of Europe resuming purchases of large quantities of Russian gas piped through Ukraine.
其他潛在協議也初現端倪。據《紐約時報》報導，俄羅斯和美國已討論將俄羅斯能源巨頭盧克石油的國際資產出售給一個包括美國政府的投資者集團。《紐約時報》還報導，美國談判代表已提出讓歐洲恢復通過烏克蘭管道大量購買俄羅斯天然氣的構想。
After the St. Petersburg meeting, Mr. Putin’s economic envoy, Kirill Dmitriev, said that there was “no question” that Russian-U.S. economic cooperation “will strengthen.” He said that Mr. Putin had talked not only about a potential fertilizer deal with Mr. Witkoff, but also about “different projects such as data centers, many projects in energy and energy cooperation.”
聖彼得堡會晤後，普丁的經濟特使基里爾·德米特里耶夫表示，俄美經濟合作「必將加強」，這「毫無疑問」。他說，普丁不僅與威特科夫談到了潛在的化肥協議，還談到了「數據中心等不同項目、能源領域的許多項目以及能源合作」。
Playing to Mr. Trump’s style of personal diplomacy, Mr. Putin has portrayed actions that benefit Russia — like selling diesel to the United States — as favors to the American leader. That includes the release on Wednesday of Stephen James Hubbard, 74, a Michigan teacher whom the State Department called “wrongfully detained” by Russia.
普丁迎合川普的個人外交風格，將有利於俄羅斯的行動——比如向美國出售柴油——描繪成給這位美國領導人的恩惠。這包括週三釋放74歲的斯蒂芬·詹姆斯·哈伯德，美國國務院此前稱其被俄羅斯「錯誤拘留」。
Mr. Trump described the release — which was not part of any prisoner swap — as a sign of Mr. Putin’s high regard for him. Referring to himself in the third person, Mr. Trump wrote on social media that “the United States, out of respect for President Trump, was not required to give anything in return.”
這次釋放並非任何囚犯交換協議的一部分，川普將其描述為普丁對他高度尊重的體現。川普在社群媒體上用第三人稱指代自己，寫道，「出於對川普總統的尊重，美國無需提供任何回報。」
But later on Wednesday, Mr. Trump told reporters that Russia would not have let Mr. Hubbard go if sanctions had remained in place. The stage was also set, analysts said, by the diesel agreement and the potential for other business deals, giving Mr. Putin the sort of diplomatic victory he is craving after Western nations piled sanctions on Russia over its invasion of Ukraine.
但週三晚些時候，川普告訴記者，如果制裁仍然有效，俄羅斯就不會釋放哈伯德。分析人士說，柴油協議和其他商業交易的可能性也為這一局面鋪平了道路，在西方國家因俄羅斯入侵烏克蘭而對其實施大量制裁後，給普丁帶來了渴望的那種外交勝利。
廣告
“Even this small amount of normalization helped Putin to really deactivate Trump and the U.S. as potential helpers or enablers of Ukraine,” said Alexander Gabuev, director of the Carnegie Russia Eurasia Center in Berlin.
「即便是這種小規模的關係正常化，也確實幫助普丁將川普和美國從烏克蘭潛在的援助者或支持者角色中剔除，」柏林卡內基俄羅斯歐亞中心主任亞歷山大·加布耶夫說。
The diesel deal bolstered an argument Moscow has made that the Western countries that imposed sanctions on Russia would suffer without cheap Russian energy and other business ties. Mr. Putin has sought to engage with Washington to prove that Russia has too much heft to be ignored, analysts said.
柴油協議強化了莫斯科一直以來的主張：對俄羅斯實施制裁的西方國家，如果沒有廉價的俄羅斯能源和其他商業聯繫，將會遭受損失。分析人士說，普丁尋求與華盛頓接觸，是為了證明俄羅斯的分量太重，不容忽視。
Russia “tried all of last year to establish the idea that you can do stuff bilaterally with the U.S. short of settling the war in Ukraine,” said Hanna Notte, the author of “We Shall Outlast Them,” a book about Russia’s wartime foreign policy.
「俄羅斯去年全年都在試圖確立一個想法：在不解決烏克蘭戰爭的情況下，仍可與美國開展雙邊合作，」《我們將比他們堅持得更久》(We Shall Outlast Them)一書的作者漢娜·諾特說，該書講述俄羅斯戰時外交政策。
“It wasn’t just the economic deals,” she added. “They also wanted to talk about the Middle East. They wanted to talk about the Arctic. They wanted to talk about nuclear arms control.”
「這不僅僅是經濟交易，」她補充說。「他們還想談中東。他們想談北極。他們想談核軍控。」
Russia’s goal, she said, was to advance “this whole idea that you can compartmentalize the relationship with the United States and do stuff together even while you’re still prosecuting the war.”
她說，俄羅斯的目標是推進「這樣一種整體想法：你可以把與美國的關係分門別類地處理，在繼續戰爭的同時，在某些領域開展合作」。
The Trump administration’s offering of carrots to end the war is likely to fail, Ms. Notte predicted.
諾特預測，川普政府以胡蘿蔔策略結束戰爭的做法很可能失敗。
Sam Charap, a Russia analyst at the RAND Corporation, and other analysts noted that an irony of Russia’s rapprochement with Washington was that it may no longer be a silver bullet to a settlement of the war on Moscow’s terms.
蘭德公司的俄羅斯問題分析師薩姆·查拉普和其他分析人士指出，俄羅斯與華盛頓改善關係的一個諷刺之處在於，這種緩和可能不再是讓戰爭按照莫斯科的條件得到解決的靈丹妙藥。
廣告
Europe has taken on a bigger role in providing Kyiv with economic and political support, and relations between Mr. Putin and many European leaders have completely broken down.
歐洲在向基輔提供經濟和政治支持方面發揮了更大作用，而普丁與許多歐洲領導人的關係已完全破裂。
“Winning the Americans isn’t the prize it once was,” said Jeremy Shapiro, research director at the European Council on Foreign Relations. “It was the whole game two years ago. Now it’s a much smaller piece.”
「贏得美國人的支持已不再像過去那樣重要，」歐洲對外關係委員會研究主任傑瑞米·夏皮羅說。「兩年前這曾是整個局勢的關鍵。現在它只是其中小得多的一部分。」
The Kremlin is also aware that Mr. Trump could change tack again after the midterms, Mr. Shapiro said. Russian decision makers, he said, see the U.S. president as “very inconsistent” and feel little security about his approach to Russia.
夏皮羅說，克里姆林宮也意識到，川普可能在期中選舉後再次改變路線。他說，俄羅斯決策者認為這位美國總統「非常反覆無常」，對他的對俄方針缺乏安全感。
