西藏那曲發生7.0級地震，十餘人死亡

Powerful Earthquake Kills at Least 16 People in Tibet

艾莎 • 2026-10-23
A 7.0-magnitude earthquake struck China’s southwestern region of Tibet on Thursday, killing at least 16 people and sending others fleeing outdoors, according to Chinese state media.
據中國官方媒體報導，週四，一場7.0級地震襲擊了中國西南部的西藏地區，造成至少16人死亡，許多人被迫逃到戶外。
The earthquake hit at 10:33 p.m. local time around 119 miles from the city of Nagqu in northern Tibet, according to the U.S. Geological Survey. Several subsequent tremors were reported in the region. The quake left at least 54 people injured and destroyed more than 300 homes, state media reported.
根據美國地質調查局的數據，地震發生在當地時間晚上10點33分，震中距離西藏北部城市那曲約190公里。據報導，該地區隨後發生了多次餘震。據官方媒體報導，地震造成至少54人受傷，300多棟房屋被毀。
Video footage released by China’s state broadcaster showed dozens of people running from stores, restaurants and apartment buildings as the quake shook the ground. Soldiers and rescue workers were also shown loading water and food supplies onto military trucks and driving along a narrow road to reach the affected area.
中國國家電視台發布的影片畫面顯示，地震引發地面晃動，數十人紛紛從商店、餐廳和公寓樓中奔跑出來。畫面還顯示，士兵和救援人員正將水和食物等物資裝上軍用卡車，沿著狹窄的公路驅車前往受災地區。
The quake disrupted water and electricity for residents living in villages around Maru township, the epicenter of the earthquake. There are around 7,605 residents in Maru, according to a 2020 census.
地震導致震中瑪如鄉周邊村莊居民的供水和供電中斷。根據2020年的人口普查數據，瑪如鄉約有7605名居民。
廣告
More than 1,600 search and rescue workers have been sent to the city of Nagqu, the official Chinese news agency, Xinhua, reported. Eleven of those injured were seriously hurt. At least one person trapped under rubble was successfully rescued.
據中國官方通訊社新華社報導，已有1600多名搜救人員被派往那曲市。傷者中有11人受重傷。至少有一名被困在廢墟下的被救者已被成功救出。
Xi Jinping, China’s leader, called for “intensified effort” by rescue workers and urged officials to continue to monitor the situation and weather in the area, which is at a high altitude where the air is cold and snow is expected in the coming days.
中國領導人習近平呼籲「全力開展」救援工作，並敦促官員們繼續監測該地區的局勢和天氣。該地區海拔較高，氣候寒冷，預計未來幾天會有降雪。
Nagqu is around 300 miles from Lhasa, the capital of Tibet. Annexed by Beijing in the 1950s, Tibet is tightly surveilled and has been a place of protest, including self-immolations, by Tibetans who have long fought to maintain their religious freedom and cultural identity, which is separate from the majority Han Chinese.
那曲距離西藏首府拉薩約480公里。西藏於20世紀50年代被北京吞併，受到嚴格監控，長期以來一直是藏人抗議活動的發生地，包括自焚事件。長期以來，藏人一直在爭取維護獨立於占人口多數的漢族的宗教自由和文化身份。
It is also one of the most underdeveloped parts of China, with many areas difficult to reach. Deadly floods that swept through another part of Tibet in September killed at least dozens in Tibet and 1,300 people in Nepal.
西藏也是中國最欠發達的地區之一，許多地方難以抵達。今年9月，席捲西藏另一地區的致命洪流在西藏造成至少數十人死亡，在尼泊爾造成1300人死亡。
The Himalayan region has recorded powerful earthquakes in the recent past. A magnitude-7.8 earthquake in Nepal in 2015 killed nearly 9,000 people.
喜馬拉雅地區在不久的過去曾錄得強震。2015年尼泊爾發生的7.8級地震造成近9000人死亡。
