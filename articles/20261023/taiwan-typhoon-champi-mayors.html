在台灣，颱風可能決定政治人物的命運

For Taiwan’s Mayors, a ‘Typhoon Day Off’ Is a Major Political Test

郭莉莉, PEI-LIN WU • 2026-10-23
In Taiwan, typhoons are not just tropical storms that cause flooding and damage. They can make or break a mayor’s chance at re-election for reasons one might not expect.
在台灣，颱風不僅是造成洪澇和破壞的熱帶風暴。出於一些意想不到的原因，颱風甚至能決定一位市長能否連任成功。
Several times a year between summer and fall, when the subtropical island is hit by cyclones, city and county mayors must decide whether to order a “typhoon day off,” when government offices, schools and most businesses will close.
每年夏秋之交，當這座亞熱帶島嶼遭受氣旋襲擊時，各縣、市長必須決定是否下令放「颱風假」，屆時政府機關、學校和大多數企業都將關門停業。
Taiwan has been bracing for Typhoon Champi, which was at one point a super typhoon and the strongest storm of 2026. On Thursday, the mayor of Taipei, Chiang Wan-an, called a typhoon day off for Friday, and urged residents to be careful. The storm is expected to pass the northeastern part of Taiwan, bringing heavy rain and winds of 141 miles per hour.
台灣一直在積極應對颱風「薔琵」，它曾一度發展為超強颱風，也是2026年最強勁的風暴。週四，台北市長蔣萬安宣布週五放颱風假，並提醒市民注意安全。預計該風暴將經過台灣東北部，帶來暴雨和每小時約226公里的強風。
The decision did not come as a surprise to many, not least because of the weather forecast. Mr. Chiang faces re-election in late November, and typhoon days are popular with residents, who often spend the day at home. If the storm turns out to be weaker than forecast, many go shopping, to the movies or to sing karaoke. (Mr. Chiang, a member of the Nationalist Party, also called the Kuomintang, is in a close race with Puma Shen, a popular legislator from the governing Democratic Progressive Party.)
對許多人來說，這一決定並不意外，這不僅是因為天氣預報的緣故。蔣萬安面臨著11月下旬的連任選舉，而颱風假很受市民歡迎，他們通常會在家中度過這一天。如果風暴最終比預報的要弱，許多人會去逛街、看電影或唱卡拉OK。（蔣萬安是國民黨成員，目前正與執政的民進黨中頗具人氣的立法委員沈伯洋進行激烈競爭。）
廣告
Almost all cities and counties across Taiwan’s main island had suspended work and class on Friday.
週五，台灣本島幾乎所有的縣市都已停班停課。
Because typhoons are such a common part of daily life in Taiwan, decisions like whether or not to call a typhoon day matter to voters. Between 2001 and 2023, Taipei had 40 typhoon days off, an average of almost two days a year, according to The News Lens, a local publication. Other cities like Yilan on the northeastern coast, which is often hit badly by storms, had 45 days off in that period.
正因為颱風在台灣日常生活中十分常見，是否宣布放颱風假這樣的決定對選民來說至關重要。據當地媒體關鍵評論網報導，在2001年至2023年期間，台北放了40天颱風假，平均每年近兩天。而經常遭受風暴嚴重襲擊的東北海岸城市宜蘭等其他城市在該時期內放了45天假。
According to Austin Wang, an associate professor at the University of Nevada who has studied the electoral politics of typhoon days, the likelihood that a mayor announces closure of work and school increases 31 percent during an election year.
曾深入研究颱風假選舉政治的內華達大學副教授王宏恩表示，在選舉年，市長宣布停班停課的可能性會增加31%。
Unlike other policies that can take months or years to evaluate, he said, it is clear within a day or two whether an official made the correct call. For instance, if a typhoon day is called and the storm turns out to be severe, the official made the right decision. The fact that the mayor’s office alone determines whether to call a day off also means that the decision is entirely the mayor’s responsibility.
他指出，與可能需要數月或數年才能評估的其他政策不同，官員的決策是否正確，在一兩天內就能見分曉。例如，如果宣布了颱風假且風暴確實很嚴重，那麼官員就做出了正確的決定。而僅由市長辦公室來決定是否停班停課這一事實，也意味著該決定完全由市長承擔責任。
“It is a very easy criteria for people to evaluate whether their incumbent was doing a good job or a bad job,” Mr. Wang said. When it comes to re-election, he said, “people will remember if the mayor made the right or wrong decision.”
「對於人們來說，這是一個非常簡單直觀的標準，可以評估現任官員做得好還是不好，」王宏恩說。談到連任時，他表示：「人們會記住市長做出的決定是正確還是錯誤。」
Tickets for many high-speed trains leaving Taipei during working hours on Friday were fully or almost fully booked on Thursday. Supermarkets stocked extra supplies of water, produce and instant noodles, a common staple during typhoons.
週四，許多週五工作時間離開台北的高鐵車票已被預訂一空或接近售罄。超市增補了飲用水、農產品和方便麵等額外物資，方便麵是颱風期間常見的日常主食。
廣告
On social media, residents discussed their plans to spend the four-day weekend binge watching television shows and sleeping. (Monday is a public holiday in Taiwan.) Earlier in the day, residents left comments on Mr. Chiang’s Facebook page, asking him to announce a day off.
在社群媒體上，居民們討論著利用這個四天長週末追劇和睡覺的計劃。（下週一也是台灣的公休假日。）當天早些時候，居民們在蔣萬安的Facebook頁面上留言，要求他宣布放假。
Despite the popularity of such days, unnecessarily calling one can come back to haunt a candidate. Business groups often oppose typhoon days off because of the cost to companies, most of which still pay their employees for the day. Taiwan’s stock market also closes for the day if work and school are suspended in Taipei.
儘管這類假期很受歡迎，但無必要地宣布放假可能會給候選人帶來負面影響。商業團體往往反對颱風假，因為這會增加企業的成本，其中大多數企業仍然需要支付員工當天的工資。如果台北停班停課，台灣股市當天也會休市。
In July, when Typhoon Bavi approached Taiwan, Mr. Chiang ordered a day off on a Friday for residents to prepare for the storm’s arrival on the weekend. Commentators and his political rivals accused him of inventing the concept of a “typhoon preparation day” just to pander to voters.
今年7月，當颱風「巴威」逼近台灣時，蔣萬安下令在週五放假一天，以便居民為週末風暴的到來做準備。評論員和他的政治對手指責他炮製「防颱準備日」的概念，純粹是為了討好選民。
But not calling one quickly enough may be even worse. When Typhoon Mikra passed over central Taiwan in June, the mayors of the city of Hsinchu and the county of Hsinchu, home to Taiwan’s largest semiconductor companies, were criticized for not issuing a typhoon day off. The next day, as the storm arrived, the county mayor, Yang Wenke, issued an apology and announced the suspension of work and school. That storm left two people dead.
但不夠迅速宣布放假可能會更糟。今年6月，當颱風「米克拉」橫掃台灣中部時，台灣最大半導體公司所在地新竹市和新竹縣的市長及縣長因未發布颱風假而受到批評。第二天，隨著風暴到來，縣長楊文科發表道歉聲明並宣布停班停課。那場風暴造成兩人死亡。
Lai Ching-te, the president of Taiwan, has been both praised and derided for his weather-related decisions. In 2012, when Typhoon Tembin approached the southern city of Tainan, Mr. Lai, then that city’s mayor, decided it would be business as usual.
台灣領導人賴清德曾因與天氣相關的決定而同時獲得讚揚和嘲諷。2012年，當颱風「天秤」逼近南部城市台南時，時任該市市長的賴清德決定照常上班上課。
When the storm proved to be light, he earned the nickname “God Lai.”
當風暴後來證明較輕時，他贏得了「賴神」的綽號。
廣告
But the next year, when once again he resisted pressure to declare a day off during another typhoon, the storm caused severe flooding in his city. He issued a public apology.
但到了第二年，當另一場颱風來襲時，他再次頂住壓力拒絕宣布停工，結果風暴在他的城市引發了嚴重的水災。他為此公開道歉。

郭莉莉(Lily Kuo)是《紐約時報》報導中國記者，常駐台北。

Pei-Lin Wu是《紐約時報》記者/研究員，報導台灣和中國新聞。
