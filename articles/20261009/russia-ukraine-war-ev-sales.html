戰爭造成燃油短缺，俄羅斯人開始擁抱中國電動汽車

Russians Snap Up Chinese E.V.s as Ukrainian Attacks Make Fuel Scarce

IVAN NECHEPURENKO • 2026-10-09
Sergei Tselikov, Russia’s leading auto industry expert, has owned more than 20 vehicles, including a Volvo, a Volkswagen and a Range Rover. All had gasoline engines.
謝爾蓋·采利科夫是俄羅斯首屈一指的汽車行業專家，擁有超過20輛車，包括富豪、大眾和路虎攬勝，全部為汽油發動機。
But in July, as Ukrainian drone strikes against oil refineries left drivers across the country waiting for hours at gas stations, he bought a fully electric Chinese hatchback. He had to act quickly — the price of the car, made by Great Wall Motor, went up by about $2,000 within days as demand surged.
但在7月，烏克蘭無人機襲擊煉油廠，導致全國司機在加油站排隊數小時，於是他買了一輛中國產純電掀背車。出手一定要快——由於需求激增，這款長城汽車製造的車幾天內售價漲了約2000美元（約合人民幣13400元）。
“I tried to fill up one of my cars and drove around to five nearby gas stations, but they had only diesel or lower-grade petrol,” he said, referring to the poorer-quality fuels the government has allowed to be sold to address the shortages. “I was just too lazy to stand in line.”
「我試著給其中一輛車加油，跑了附近五個加油站，但只有柴油或低標號汽油，」他說，其中的「低標號汽油」是政府為應對短缺而允許銷售的劣質燃料。「我只是懶得排隊。」
As Ukraine increasingly takes the war to Russia, the effects may be most visible on the roads. Since the end of June, Russia’s electric-vehicle market has more than doubled. Most of the E.V.s sold in the country are Chinese, a testament to Moscow’s economic pivot in response to Western wartime sanctions.
隨著烏克蘭將戰火越來越多地引向俄羅斯，其影響在道路上可能最為明顯。自6月底以來，俄羅斯電動汽車市場已增長了一倍多。該國銷售的電動汽車大多為中國車，也彰顯了莫斯科為應對西方戰時制裁而進行的經濟轉向。
Russia is playing catch-up. For years, while other nations built out E.V. supply chains and charging infrastructure, Russia looked the other way. Its position as one of the world’s top oil producers used to keep gasoline cheap. Vast distances between major cities meant charging networks were economically unviable without guaranteed demand. And harsh winters required automakers to heavily adapt models to the climate.
俄羅斯正在追趕。多年來，當其他國家建設電動汽車供應鏈和充電基礎設施時，俄羅斯視而不見。作為世界最大石油生產國之一，俄羅斯曾長期保持汽油低價。主要城市之間距離遙遠，意味著在沒有穩定需求的情況下，充電網路在經濟上難以維繫。而嚴酷的冬季也要求汽車製造商大幅調整車型以適應氣候。
Now, E.V.s are suddenly a more common sight on Russian roads, particularly in major cities. The number of electric vehicles, including hybrids, sold in the country went from 43,000 in the January-to-August period of 2025 to more than 83,000 over the same period this year, according to the Russian Ministry of Industry and Trade.
如今，電動汽車在俄羅斯道路上突然變得更加常見，尤其是在大城市。據俄羅斯工業和貿易部數據，該國電動汽車（含混合動力車）銷量從2025年1月至8月的43000輛增至今年同期的83000多輛。
廣告
By the end of September, the share of pure electric vehicles and plug-in hybrids that can run without gas had reached 11.5 percent of the Russian car market, up from about 5 percent at the beginning of the year, according to Avtostat, an auto research agency led by Mr. Tselikov. The E.V. market share in Russia is now slightly higher than in the United States, but it is only a small fraction of the share in China, which is 45 percent.
據采利科夫領導的汽車研究機構Avtostat的數據，截至9月底，純電車和可不用汽油行駛的插電式混合動力車在俄羅斯汽車市場中的份額已達11.5%，而年初約為5%。俄羅斯電動汽車市場份額目前略高於美國，但離中國的45%還很遙遠。
While the E.V. surge in Russia stems from a specific factor — Ukrainian attacks — the shift there is the latest example of how war and supply shocks can quickly transform car-buying decisions.
俄羅斯電動汽車激增源於一個特定因素——烏克蘭的襲擊——但那裡的轉變是戰爭和供應衝擊如何迅速改變購車決策的最新例證。
E.V. sales have boomed in other parts of the world because of the U.S.-Israeli war in Iran, which has caused oil and gasoline prices to soar. Globally, E.V.s are on track to account for a record 29 percent of all new car purchases this year, according to the International Energy Agency.
由於美國和以色列在伊朗的戰爭導致石油和汽油價格飆升，世界其他地區的電動汽車銷量激增。據國際能源署數據，全球範圍內，電動汽車今年在所有新車購買量中的佔比有望達到創紀錄的29%。
E.V. sales have risen in more than 90 countries this year, including Brazil, Australia, India and South Korea. The I.E.A. said the energy crisis had “clearly reinforced the case for E.V.s.”
今年，包括巴西、澳洲、印度和韓國在內的90多個國家的電動汽車銷量有所上升。國際能源署表示，能源危機「顯然鞏固了電動汽車的地位」。
In Russia, the fuel shortages are unnerving many Russians who had grown accustomed to the war in Ukraine being only a distant nuisance. They threaten to become a serious political issue for the government.
在俄羅斯，燃料短缺正讓許多人感到不安，他們已習慣於烏克蘭戰爭只是遙遠的麻煩。這可能成為政府面臨的嚴重政治問題。
On Friday, only 46 percent of gas stations across Russia were selling fuel, according to Gdebenz, a crowdsourced tool that tracks the fuel market. In Moscow, gasoline was available at 53 percent of stations, leading to some lines. In Nizhny Novgorod, a major city east of Moscow, the figure was only 27 percent.
據追蹤燃料市場的眾包工具Gdebenz的數據，週五，俄羅斯全國僅46%的加油站銷售燃料。在莫斯科，53%的加油站有汽油，導致一些地方出現排隊的情況。在莫斯科以東的主要城市下諾夫哥羅德，有油加油站的比例僅為27%。
廣告
The situation in Moscow was worse in early July and mid-August, when only a quarter of stations had fuel after strikes on refineries that supply the city.
莫斯科的情況在7月初和8月中旬更糟，在供應城市的煉油廠遭襲後，僅有四分之一的加油站有燃料。
At the time, gasoline lines clogged traffic across the capital. Drivers waited hours for gas at stations on the highways connecting Moscow with St. Petersburg, Kazan and the country’s south. President Vladimir V. Putin had opened these roads with great pride over the past decade.
當時，加油排隊的車輛堵塞了首都交通。司機們在連接莫斯科與聖彼得堡、喀山和該國南部的高速公路上的加油站等待數小時。這些過去十年開通的道路是弗拉基米爾·V·普丁總統引以為傲的工程。
To try to quell the crisis, the government permitted the sale of lower-grade gasoline and diesel. But some cars began to break down after using the lower-quality fuel.
為平息危機，政府允許銷售低標號汽油和柴油。但一些汽車在使用劣質燃料後開始出現故障。
As the fuel crisis has continued, E.V. dealerships have been cleared out, and online listings for used electric cars have dwindled.
隨著燃料危機持續，電動汽車經銷商的庫存已被清空，網上在售的二手電動汽車也越來越少。
At a sprawling dealership complex northwest of Moscow, a manager at Evolute, a brand of Russian-assembled electric vehicles based on Chinese designs, said there was no inventory left on the lot and no way to join a waiting list.
在莫斯科西北部一個龐大的經銷商綜合體，Evolute——一個基於中國設計的俄羅斯組裝電動汽車品牌——的一位經理表示，停車場已無庫存，而且也無法預訂。
The complex houses more than 40 dealerships, most of which used to sell primarily brands from the West, Japan and South Korea. It now has more than 25 Chinese showrooms.
該綜合體擁有40多家經銷商，其中大多數過去主要銷售西方、日本和韓國品牌。現在它有超過25個中國展銷廳。
廣告
Many Russian car factories used to produce Western brands but were abandoned after the invasion of Ukraine under pressure from Western governments. Most now assemble Chinese cars.
許多俄羅斯汽車工廠過去生產西方品牌，但在入侵烏克蘭後因西方政府壓力而被廢棄。其中大多數現在組裝中國汽車。
On Moscow’s streets and in its parking lots, lines have appeared at charging stations, albeit shorter ones.
在莫斯科的街道和停車場，充電站也出現了排隊，儘管較短。
Aleksandr, 40, was charging a red Moskvich, an iconic Soviet brand revived using a rebadged Chinese design, one recent day. He said he had to drive around central Moscow to find a working charger with no line.
最近一天，40歲的亞歷山大正在給一輛紅色的「莫斯科人」充電，這是一個標誌性的蘇聯品牌，通過重新貼牌的中國設計復活。他說他不得不開車繞莫斯科市中心尋找一個沒有排隊的可用充電樁。
“Overall, the infrastructure is lacking,” Aleksandr said. The Moskvich belonged to his employer, he said, adding that he would never buy an E.V. for himself because of the expense and the lack of charging stations.
「總的來說，基礎設施缺乏，」亞歷山大說。他說這輛「莫斯科人」屬於他的僱主，並補充說他自己永遠不會買電動汽車，因為費用高且充電站缺乏。
The shift toward making Russia more E.V.-friendly began in 2022, when the government started subsidizing fast-charging stations and grid connections. In 2023, updated codes required E.V. infrastructure for new buildings. The number of public charging stations grew to more than 8,000 this year from fewer than 1,500 in 2022.
俄羅斯轉向電動汽車的趨勢始於2022年，當時政府開始補貼快速充電站和電網連接。2023年，更新後的規範要求新建築配備電動汽車基礎設施。公共充電站數量從2022年的不到1500個增至今年的8000多個。
Fully electric vehicles have been exempted from the transport tax paid by owners of other vehicles in many Russian regions. In Moscow and other big cities, E.V. drivers do not have to pay parking fees, which can run as high as $9.50 an hour. Russian-made E.V.s are eligible for government subsidies and can use most toll highways free of charge nationwide.
在俄羅斯許多地區，純電車已免除其他車輛車主需繳納的運輸稅。在莫斯科和其他大城市，電動汽車司機無需支付每小時可能高達9.50美元的停車費。俄羅斯製造的電動汽車有資格獲得政府補貼，並可在全國範圍內免費使用大多數收費高速公路。
 Tselikov said that while the share of fully electric cars had doubled, they remained somewhat unusual in Russia, primarily because of their cost. Hybrid cars, he predicted, will continue to gain market share.
采利科夫說，雖然純電車份額翻了一番，但它們在俄羅斯仍然有些不同尋常，主要是因為成本。他預測，混合動力汽車將繼續獲得市場份額。
E.V.s may also face an image barrier in Russia. For some, they just are not manly enough.
電動汽車在俄羅斯可能還面臨形象障礙。對一些人來說，它們就是不夠有男子氣概。
廣告
Waiting in a long line for gas outside Moscow, Igor, 51, reflected that sentiment. “I would never buy an E.V.,” he said. “They’re bad for men’s health and way too expensive.”
在莫斯科郊外排隊等待加油時，51歲的伊高爾反映了這種情緒。「我永遠不會買電車，」他說。「它們對男性健康有害，而且太貴了。」
