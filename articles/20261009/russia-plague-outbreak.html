為何俄羅斯鼠疫疑雲引發全世界擔憂

Russia’s Vague Account of a Mysterious Death Is Stoking Cover-Up Fears

NEIL MACFARQUHAR • 2026-10-09
As rumors swirled this week that a woman working in a Siberian lab may have died of the plague, news programs on Russian state television paid scant attention. The authorities in Moscow released little information.
本週，有傳聞稱一名在西伯利亞實驗室工作的女性可能死於鼠疫，但俄羅斯國家電視台的新聞節目對此鮮有關注，莫斯科當局也幾乎沒有發布信息。
Faced with this potential new threat, the Kremlin seems to be resorting to a familiar playbook: avoiding bad news.
面對這一潛在的新威脅，克里姆林宮似乎正訴諸一種慣用策略：迴避壞消息。
The lack of information has caused unease around the world, particularly given the memory of China’s effort to withhold information about Covid-19.
信息的匱乏已在全球引發不安，尤其是考慮到人們尚未遺忘中國此前隱瞞新冠疫情相關信息的記憶。
The Kremlin has a history of hiding bad news
克里姆林宮有隱瞞壞消息的歷史
廣告
In Russia, federal officials are generally reluctant, even fearful, about publishing anything that might cast the Kremlin in a negative light. The pace of news in Russia tends to be determined by the government, not by when events actually occur. The independent press has been driven into exile.
在俄羅斯，聯邦官員通常不願、甚至害怕發布任何可能讓克里姆林宮顯得負面的內容。俄羅斯的新聞發布時間點往往由政府決定，而非事件實際發生的時間。獨立媒體已被迫流亡。
Perhaps the most notorious case was the hidden news that an explosion at the Chernobyl nuclear plant in April 1986 had contaminated a wide area with dangerous radiation. The Kremlin began to release information only after Sweden and other European countries, alarmed by high radiation readings, demanded an explanation.
最臭名昭著的案例或許是1986年4月車諾比核電廠爆炸的消息被隱瞞，該事故導致大片地區被危險輻射污染。直到瑞典和其他歐洲國家注意到高輻射讀數並要求解釋後，克里姆林宮才開始發布信息。
In 1979, there was an accidental leak of anthrax spores from a secret biological weapon facility run by the Soviet military near what is now Yekaterinburg. Someone turned on a machine that dried the spores without realizing that a maintenance technician had removed a clogged exhaust filter.
1979年，在現葉卡捷琳堡附近，蘇聯軍方控制的一個祕密生物武器設施意外洩漏了炭疽孢子。有人啟動了一台乾燥孢子的機器，卻未意識到一名維修技術員已拆除了堵塞的排氣過濾器。
More than 60 people died, as well as countless farm animals downwind of the facility. The Soviet authorities blamed the human toll on tainted meat, and the truth did not emerge until 1992, after the Soviet Union collapsed.
超過60人死亡，設施下風處還有無數農場動物死亡。蘇聯當局將死亡歸咎於受污染的肉類，真相直到1992年蘇聯解體後才浮出水面。
But the obfuscation did not stop, especially after Vladimir V. Putin became president in 2000.
但混淆視聽的做法並未停止，尤其是在弗拉基米爾·V·普丁於2000年就任總統之後。
In two of the most tragic episodes, the government either concealed the truth or promoted outright falsehoods. One was the sinking of the Kursk nuclear-powered submarine in the Barents Sea in 2000, with all 118 sailors aboard killed. In 2002, Chechen terrorists took over 900 people hostage during a performance of the “Nord-Ost” musical. Interior Ministry special forces knocked everybody out by pumping a chemical agent into the theater. But they failed to coordinate with the medical services, leading to the deaths of at least 116 hostages, as well as all 41 attackers.
在兩起最悲慘的事件中，政府要麼隱瞞真相，要麼散布徹頭徹尾的謊言。一是2000年巴倫支海庫爾斯克號核潛艇沉沒，船上118名水兵全部遇難。另一起發生在2002年，車臣恐怖分子在音樂劇《東北》演出期間劫持了900多人。內政部特種部隊向劇院泵入化學劑致所有人昏迷，卻未能與醫療服務部門協調，導致至少116名人質和全部41名襲擊者死亡。
廣告
Since Russian forces mounted a large-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, there have been examples of the government’s effort to camouflage the true scope of the war.
自2022年俄軍大規模入侵烏克蘭以來，已出現政府試圖掩蓋戰爭真實規模的例子。
Earlier this year, when a bomb exploded in a Moscow restaurant where a top Russian general was celebrating, Russian state news initially reported it as a gas canister explosion. The Kremlin has resorted to extensive censorship to hide Russian losses in its war in Ukraine — which the government made illegal to call a war. Independent researchers have estimated that 450,000 Russian soldiers have died.
今年早些時候，一枚炸彈在莫斯科一家餐廳爆炸，當時一名俄羅斯高級將領正在那裡慶祝，俄羅斯國家新聞最初將其報導為煤氣罐爆炸。克里姆林宮已訴諸廣泛審查來掩蓋俄羅斯在烏克蘭戰爭中的損失——將這場戰爭稱為戰爭是違法行為。獨立研究人員估計，已有45萬名俄羅斯士兵喪生。
Russia’s press is full of disinformation
俄羅斯媒體充斥虛假信息
Although several state-run newspapers initially ran articles about the death in Siberia, the news disappeared from their websites.
儘管幾家國有報紙最初刊登了關於西伯利亞死亡事件的文章，但相關新聞隨後從它們的網站上消失了。
Alexandra Arkhipova, a Russian anthropologist in exile, wrote on social media, “Instead of a straightforward description of what is happening in Irkutsk, news columns are filled with journalistic euphemisms — clearly by order from above.”
流亡的俄羅斯人類學家亞歷山德拉·阿爾希波娃在社群媒體上寫道：「新聞專欄沒有直接描述伊爾庫茨克正在發生的事情，而是充斥著新聞委婉語——顯然是奉上面之命。」
There is no mention of plague, she added, only headlines about the “Irkutsk infection,” referring to the region of Siberia where the woman, who was 28, died.
她補充說，報導沒有提到鼠疫，只有關於「伊爾庫茨克感染」的標題，指的是這名28歲死亡女性所在的西伯利亞地區。
廣告
Local social media accounts are full of questions and concerns about the case, although the occasional comment reflects the official line that people should rely on government statements.
當地社群媒體帳戶充滿了對此案的疑問和擔憂，儘管偶爾有評論反映官方立場，即人們應相信政府聲明。
Alyona, reached via social media and identified by only one name to avoid retribution from the authorities, said she was an old school friend of the victim but had not been in touch for a long time. “Everyone is worried, especially since there have been no clear statements,” she said.
為免遭當局報復，通過社群媒體聯繫的阿廖娜只使用一個名字。她說自己是受害者的老同學，但已很久沒有聯繫。「每個人都很擔心，尤其是因為一直沒有明確的聲明，」她說。
The mystery surrounding the death in Irkutsk deepened because the governor of a neighboring region had initially posted on social media that the woman had died of the plague, and then changed it later to say that she had “possibly” died of the plague.
圍繞伊爾庫茨剋死亡事件的神秘感加深，因為鄰近地區的州長最初在社群媒體上發帖稱該女性死於鼠疫，後來改口說她「可能」死於鼠疫。
Dmitri S. Peskov, the Kremlin spokesman, criticized the news media for reporting rumors rather than relying on official reports from the public health safety agency. Even though the agency’s head, a doctor, flew to Irkutsk to assess the situation, it avoids news conferences, communicating only by intermittent press releases.
克里姆林宮發言人德米特里·S·佩斯科夫批評新聞媒體報導謠言，而非依賴公共衛生安全機構的官方報告。該機構負責人是一名醫生，已飛往伊爾庫茨克評估情況，但該機構避免新聞發布會，僅通過間歇性新聞稿與公眾溝通。
Russia’s public health agency has said the pathogen that killed the woman was not the one she had been researching in the lab, for which she had been vaccinated.
俄羅斯公共衛生機構表示，導致該女性死亡的病原體並非她在實驗室研究的那個，她已接種後者的疫苗。
On Thursday, the governor of Irkutsk, Igor Kobzev, who has been posting regularly on his social media channels, wrote that all preventive measures had been completed and after about 5,000 laboratory tests, nobody else the victim might have been in contact with at the institute or the hospital was infected. “Amid intense media scrutiny, the specialists ​thoroughly investigated the situation,” he wrote. “It was a test, but we passed it in worthy fashion.”
週四，伊爾庫茨克州長伊高爾·科布澤夫一直在其社群媒體頻道上定期發帖。他寫道，所有預防措施已完成，經過約5000次實驗室檢測，受害者在研究所或醫院可能接觸過的其他人都未感染。「在媒體密集審視下，專家們徹底調查了情況，」他寫道。「這是一次考驗，但我們體面地通過了。」
廣告
His post attracted comments from area residents asking for a clearer explanation of what exactly had happened and what had killed the woman.
他的帖子引來當地居民評論，要求更清楚地解釋到底發生了什麼，以及是什麼導致該女性死亡。
Trust is low post-Covid
新冠疫情後信任度低
Russian officials have said the lab worker in Siberia died from pneumonia, of an unknown cause, on Friday night. Although the World Health Organization sent an initial query, the Russian authorities did not provide an answer in the required 24 hours, but three days later, the organization said.
俄羅斯官員表示，西伯利亞的實驗室工作人員於週五晚上死於不明原因的肺炎。世界衛生組織發出了初步的詢問，但該組織稱，俄羅斯當局未在規定24小時內答覆，而是三天後才答覆。
The W.H.O. said Russia had not yet provided the details needed to make a formal “risk assessment” about the disease’s possible spread. Senior officials in both the E.U. and the United States, including President Trump and Marco Rubio, the secretary of state, all encouraged Russia to provide more information.
世衛組織表示，俄羅斯尚未提供必要細節以進行正式「風險評估」，判斷該疾病可能傳播的風險。川普總統、國務卿魯比奧及歐盟和美國高級官員都敦促俄羅斯提供更多信息。
From 2019 to 2025, 10 countries reported almost 4,000 plague cases and 423 deaths, according to W.H.O. statistics. The United States reports about seven cases annually — the most recent was a woman in her 50s who died in June in New Mexico. Plague can be successfully treated with antibiotics if caught early.
據世衛組織統計，2019年至2025年，10個國家報告了近4000例鼠疫病例和423例死亡。美國每年報告約七例——最近一例是一名50多歲的女性，於6月在新墨西哥州死亡。如果及早發現，鼠疫可通過抗生素成功治療。
Wrestling with the Covid pandemic, which completely disrupted ordinary life, left a legacy of mistrust toward public health agencies and their ability to protect people.
新冠疫情完全擾亂了人們的正常生活，也給公共衛生機構及其保護人民的能力留下了不信任的陰影。
廣告
At a news conference on Wednesday, Maria Van Kerkhove, director of epidemic and pandemic management at the W.H.O., said, “Covid is certainly in the back of everybody’s mind.”
在週三的新聞發布會上，世衛組織流行病和大流行病管理主任瑪麗亞·范克爾克霍夫說：「新冠肯定在每個人的腦海裡。」
“If this individual did not die from the plague, then how did she die?” Dr. Van Kerkhove asked, adding, “We expect further answers from Russia.”
「如果這個人不是死於鼠疫，那她是怎麼死的？」范克爾克霍夫問道。「我們期待俄羅斯給出進一步答案，」她還說。
