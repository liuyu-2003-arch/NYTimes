美政府暫停微軟等科技公司綠卡項目資格

Microsoft Suspended From Green Card Program as Trump Officials Widen Fraud Drive

ERICA L. GREEN • 2026-10-09
Vice President JD Vance announced on Thursday that Microsoft would be suspended from using a visa program that allowed foreign workers to permanently reside in the United States after an investigation found that the tech giant had “abused” the system.
副總統萬斯週四宣布，在一項調查發現微軟公司「濫用」允許外國員工獲得美國永久居留權的簽證項目後，政府將暫停微軟使用該制度的資格。
Mr. Vance made the announcement in a news conference where he and other Trump administration officials made a slew of allegations that American companies and universities were committing visa fraud. Microsoft, they announced, is just the largest of several technology companies to face such a suspension, and schools including Harvard and Yale are now under investigation.
萬斯在一場新聞發布會上宣布了這一決定。他和川普政府其他官員在會上提出了一系列指控，稱美國企業和大學存在簽證欺詐行為。他們宣布，微軟只是面臨此類暫停措施的多家科技公司中規模最大的一家，哈佛和耶魯等高校目前也正在接受調查。
Mr. Vance took aim at the embattled H-1B visa program, which allows companies to hire skilled foreign workers in hard-to-fill jobs. The Trump administration has sought to overhaul the program, including imposing fees for the visas, claiming that it has unfairly allowed companies to sideline Americans and hire foreign workers. Mr. Vance has called the program “broken” and said he would support eliminating it.
萬斯把矛頭指向了飽受爭議的H-1B簽證項目。該項目允許公司為難以填補的崗位僱用外國高技能員工。川普政府一直試圖改革這一計劃，包括對簽證收費，聲稱它不公平地讓企業得以不公平地將美國人排除在外，轉而僱用外國員工。萬斯曾稱該項目「已經失靈」，並表示支持取消它。
Mr. Vance said on Thursday that companies like Microsoft had been using the H-1B visa program to exploit the Labor Department’s permanent labor certification program, known as PERM, which allows H-1B visa recipients to obtain a green card and potentially stay in the United States indefinitely.
萬斯週四說，微軟等公司一直在利用H-1B簽證項目，濫用勞工部的永久勞工認證項目(PERM)，該項目允許H-1B簽證持有者獲得綠卡，從而有可能無限期留在美國。
廣告
Officials said that other companies suspended from PERM would include Adobe and several large information technology outsourcing firms. Mr. Vance said the suspension would “last as long as it needs to.”
官員們表示，其他被暫停使用PERM項目的企業還包括Adobe和幾家大型信息技術外包公司。萬斯說，暫停措施將「持續到不需要為止」。
The announcement on Thursday, which included officials from several agencies, was the latest attempt by the administration to seize on purported fraud to argue that exploitation of the country’s immigration system — and not President Trump’s policies — has been depriving Americans of jobs, wages and paths to prosperity.
多個機構的官員出席了週四的發布會。這是本屆政府最新一次試圖利用所謂的欺詐行為來辯稱，是美國移民制度的漏洞——而不是川普總統的政策——剝奪了美國人的工作、工資和致富之路。
“The Department of Labor is taking historic action by shutting down the pipeline of systemic fraud, the fraud that has flooded our country with foreign labor at the expense of American workers,” said Keith Sonderling, the labor secretary. “It begins with universities and companies that have turned into visa mills, promoting foreign workers that have lied about their qualifications to take jobs from our beloved American workers.”
「勞工部正在採取歷史性行動，切斷系統性欺詐的管道。正是這種欺詐讓外國勞動力大量湧入我國，損害了美國就業者的利益，」勞工部長基思·桑德林說。「我們首先要針對那些淪為簽證工廠的大學和企業。它們幫助謊報資歷的外國員工獲得簽證，讓他們搶走我們親愛的美國就業者的工作。」
But the administration has not always provided evidence to match its fraud claims. And several of its targets on Thursday disputed its assertions.
但川普政府提出欺詐指控時，並非總能拿出相應證據。週四被針對的幾家機構也對政府的說法提出了異議。
Mr. Vance directed his harshest criticism at Microsoft, saying that there had been “no company in the United States, unfortunately, that has abused this system more.”
萬斯對微軟的批評最為嚴厲。他說，「遺憾的是，美國沒有哪家公司比它更嚴重地濫用了這一制度」。
Mr. Vance said that in the past year, the company had laid off 6,000 American workers, while it benefited from 6,300 H-1B visas and nearly 3,000 green cards. He referred to the visa holders as “indentured servants” who were paid a fraction of what their American counterparts would be.
萬斯說，過去一年，該公司裁掉了6000名美國就業者，同時卻受益於6300份H-1B簽證和近3000張綠卡。他把這些簽證持有者稱為「契約勞工」，說他們的薪酬遠低於同等崗位的美國人。
“In other words, if you do the math, for every worker that Microsoft laid off, they replaced that worker with one and a half foreign indentured servants,” Mr. Vance said. “Now that is a scandalous system that we’ve allowed in this country for far too long.”
「換句話說，你們可以算一算，微軟每裁掉一名員工，就用一個半外國契約勞工來取代他，」萬斯說。「這是一個可恥的制度，我們國家容忍它已經太久了。」
廣告
“And so our message to Microsoft is: You’re a great American company, but you’ve got to hire great American workers,” he added. “You cannot lay off American workers and then replace them with foreign indentured servants.”
「所以，我們要向微軟傳達的信息是：你們是一家偉大的美國公司，但你們必須僱用優秀的美國就業者，」他接著說。「你們不能先裁掉美國就業者，再用外國契約勞工取代他們。」
In a statement, Microsoft challenged some of Mr. Vance’s claims.
微軟在聲明中反駁了萬斯的部分說法。
The company said the “vast majority” of its employees in the United States are citizens. It said that of the roughly 6,000 H-1B applications it made last fiscal year, 80 percent were to extend or change the status of existing Microsoft employees. The remaining applications were for people already legally in the country who then joined Microsoft, the company said.
該公司表示，其在美國的員工「絕大多數」是美國公民。微軟稱，上一財年提交的約6000份H-1B簽證申請中，80%是為了延長現有微軟員工的簽證有效期或變更其身份。公司表示，其餘申請涉及的也是已經合法身在美國、隨後加入微軟的人員。
“They are not new arrivals to our country,” it added.
「他們並不是新近來到我國的人，」微軟補充道。
Microsoft said its pay is among the highest in tech and among H-1B filings.
微軟表示，其薪酬水平在科技行業以及提交H-1B簽證申請的企業中均位居前列。
“Microsoft succeeds as a global technology leader by developing the world’s best technology work force,” the company said. “We do this by strengthening this country’s talent pipeline, hiring American workers and attracting the best talent from around the world.”
「微軟之所以能夠成為全球科技行業的領導者，是因為我們致力於打造世界一流的科技人才隊伍，」該公司表示。「為此，我們不斷加強美國本土的人才培養體系，僱用美國工人，同時吸引來自世界各地的頂尖人才。」
廣告
On the academic front, Mr. Vance maintained that universities were employing foreign graduate students as research assistants at lower wages, and suggested that the visas were being overused and sucking up federal funding.
關於學界方面，萬斯堅持認為，大學以較低工資僱用外國研究生擔任研究助理，並暗示相關簽證被過度使用，消耗了聯邦資金。
Anthony D’Esposito, the inspector general of the Labor Department, announced that the agency was launching investigations into several universities for potential abuses of the J-1 visa, a cultural exchange program that allows foreign students to work and study in the United States.
勞工部監察長安東尼·德埃斯波西托宣布，該部門正對多所大學是否濫用J-1簽證展開調查。這是一項文化交流簽證計劃，允許外國學生在美國工作和學習。
“Subpoenas have been served, investigations are underway, and nobody will be getting a free pass because their name is carved into an expensive building,” he said.
「傳票已經送達，調查正在進行，沒有人會因為自己的名字刻在一棟昂貴的大樓上而獲得特殊待遇，」他說。
The schools under investigation are Harvard, Yale, Stanford, Arizona State, Brown, M.I.T., the University of Pittsburgh, the University of California, Davis, and the California Institute of Technology.
接受調查的學校包括哈佛、耶魯、史丹佛、亞利桑那州立、布朗、麻省理工、匹茲堡大學、加州大學戴維斯分校和加州理工。
Stanford said in a statement that it “complies with all applicable visa laws, including those relating to J-1 visas,” and “will cooperate with the investigation.”
史丹佛在聲明中說，該校「遵守所有適用的簽證法律，包括與J-1簽證相關的法律」，並將「配合調查」。
Brian Clark, a spokesman for Brown University, said in a statement that the university had received the subpoena “for various records pertaining to foreign labor programs administered by the U.S. government, and we will respond promptly and directly.”
布朗大學發言人布萊恩·克拉克在聲明中說，該校已收到傳票，「要求提供與美國政府管理的外國就業者項目有關的各種記錄，我們將迅速、直接地作出回應。」
廣告
“Brown’s international scholars, students and employees make essential contributions on our campus, including to teaching and research that advances American innovation, competitiveness and economic vitality,” Mr. Clark said.
「布朗大學的國際學者、學生和員工為我們的校園作出了不可或缺的貢獻，包括通過教學和研究推動美國的創新、競爭力和經濟活力，」克拉克說。
Arizona State University, noting that it educates more science, technology, engineering and math majors than any other university and employs one of the largest technical and scientific workforces in the country, said that it “has hired the talent needed to perform these functions and done so in complete compliance with existing United States policy and law.”
亞利桑那州立大學指出，該校培養的科學、技術、工程和數學專業學生人數超過美國其他大學，而且擁有全美規模最大的技術和科研人才隊伍之一。該校表示，它「聘用了履行這些職能所需的人才，而且完全遵守美國現行政策和法律」。
The H-1B program has long been the target of bipartisan calls for changes, but has become a centerpiece of Mr. Trump’s immigration crackdown agenda. Economists have maintained that the visa holders boost American productivity and raise wages even for American workers.
長期以來，美國兩黨都呼籲改革H-1B簽證計劃，但如今它已成為川普收緊移民政策議程的核心內容之一。經濟學家一直認為，H-1B簽證持有人提高了美國的生產率，甚至能夠提高美國就業者的工資。
Mr. Vance, pressed on how the use of the certification program amounted to fraud, said that companies were misrepresenting that they were bringing in workers with “hyper-specialized and hyper-capable” skill sets.
當被追問企業使用永久勞工認證制度為何構成欺詐時，萬斯表示，企業虛假宣稱自己引進的是擁有「高度專業化、能力超群」技能的人才。
Mr. Vance said that there was a difference between bringing in foreign workers like the tech tycoon Elon Musk, “who wants to come in and build something great in the United States of America,” versus an accountant at a lower wage.
萬斯說，引進像科技大亨伊隆·馬斯克這樣「希望來到美利堅合眾國成就一番偉業」的外國人才與僱用一名工資較低的會計師是兩回事。
“You’re not bringing in a genius to build a great American corporation,” Mr. Vance said. “You are committing fraud against the American people and against this visa program.”
「你引進的並不是一個能打造偉大美國企業的天才，」萬斯說。「你是在欺騙美國人民，也是在欺騙這一簽證項目。」
廣告
Hours after the announcement, President Trump awarded Satya Nadella, Microsoft’s chief executive, the National Medal of Technology and Innovation at a summit in Washington where Mr. Musk and other tech executives also were honored.
就在這一消息宣布數小時後，川普總統在華盛頓舉行的一場峰會上向微軟首席執行官薩蒂亞·納德拉頒發了國家技術與創新獎章。馬斯克和其他科技企業高管也在會上受到表彰。
Mr. Nadella came to the United States for graduate school and joined Microsoft in 1992. Two years later, he wrote in his memoir, he turned in his permanent residency for an H-1B visa so that his wife could immediately join him in the United States. (At the time, he said, the spouses of green card holders faced a long wait for visa approval.) Both eventually gained citizenship, and he rose through the ranks to revive Microsoft from a yearslong slump. It is now worth almost $4 trillion.
納德拉來美國讀研究生，並於1992年加入微軟。他在回憶錄中寫道，兩年後，為了讓妻子能夠立即赴美與他團聚，他放棄了自己的永久居民身份，轉而申請H-1B簽證。（他說，當時綠卡持有人的配偶需要等待很長時間才能獲得簽證批准。）兩人最終都獲得了公民身份，而他也一路晉陞，把微軟從多年低迷中拯救出來。微軟如今市值近4萬億美元。
In awarding Mr. Nadella the highest honor in technology on Thursday, Mr. Trump said it was “so well deserved,” and that Mr. Nadella had turned Microsoft into “an early leader in superintelligence.”
週四，川普向納德拉頒發科技領域最高榮譽時表示，他「完全當之無愧」，並稱納德拉已將微軟打造為「超級智能領域的早期領軍者」。
