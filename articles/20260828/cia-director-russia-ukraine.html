中情局局長為何祕密訪問莫斯科

C.I.A. Chief Delivered Bleak Assessment of Russia’s War in Secretive Moscow Visit

ADAM ENTOUS, JULIAN E. BARNES • 2026-08-28
John Ratcliffe, the C.I.A. director, made a secretive visit to Moscow this week to privately share his bleak assessment of the state of the Russian war against Ukraine, and to urge the Russians to cut a deal before their military and economic situation gets worse, current and former officials said.
現任和前任官員表示，中央情報局局長約翰·拉特克利夫本週祕密訪問了莫斯科，私下分享了他對俄羅斯在烏克蘭戰爭現狀的悲觀評估，並敦促俄方在其軍事和經濟狀況進一步惡化前達成協議。
Mr. Ratcliffe delivered the message to Aleksandr V. Bortnikov, the head of Russia’s F.S.B. intelligence service, and made no threats about potential consequences for the Russian side should President Vladimir V. Putin ignore the C.I.A. director’s assessment and choose to continue fighting.
拉特克利夫將這一信息傳達給了俄羅斯聯邦安全局局長亞歷山大·博爾特尼科夫，並且沒有發出任何威脅，未說明如果普丁總統無視中情局局長的評估並選擇繼續作戰，俄方可能會面臨什麼後果。
C.I.A. analysts have long questioned whether Mr. Putin’s advisers have given him honest assessments of the war’s trajectory and toll.
中情局的分析人士長期以來一直質疑，普丁的顧問們是否就戰爭的走向和代價向他提供了如實評估。
The C.I.A. has long maintained communications channels with the F.S.B. and Russia’s foreign intelligence service, which is known as the S.V.R.
中情局長期以來一直與俄羅斯聯邦安全局以及俄羅斯對外情報局保持著溝通管道。
廣告
Officials said Mr. Ratcliffe’s intelligence channel with Bortnikov runs parallel to separate channels that President Trump’s envoy, Steve Witkoff, and Jared Kushner, the president’s son-in-law, have established with other senior advisers to Mr. Putin.
官員們表示，拉特克利夫與博爾特尼科夫之間的情報管道與川普總統特使史提夫·維特科夫及總統女婿賈里德·庫什納同普丁其他高級顧問之間建立的獨立管道是平行運作的。
Some officials said they hoped that Mr. Putin, a former intelligence officer himself, would see a message from the C.I.A. director as more urgent and credible than communications through normal diplomatic channels.
一些官員表示，普丁本人也曾是情報官員，他們希望普丁會認為由中情局局長傳達的信息比常規外交管道的溝通更為緊迫和可信。
For much of last year, Mr. Trump and some of his top advisers appeared to hold the belief that a Russian battlefield victory over Ukraine was inevitable because of Russia’s larger population and its nuclear arsenal.
在去年的大部分時間裡，川普及其部分高級顧問似乎認為，鑒於俄羅斯龐大的人口基數和核武庫，俄羅斯在戰場上戰勝烏克蘭是不可避免的。
But the Russians have suffered massive losses, and their battlefield gains have stalled.
但俄方遭受了巨大損失，其戰場推進也已停滯。
It is not clear whether Mr. Ratcliffe’s message presaged any change in U.S. policy toward Russia or Ukraine.
目前尚不清楚拉特克利夫傳達的信息是否預示著美國對俄或對烏政策將發生變化。
Mr. Ratcliffe’s visit was the latest effort by the Trump administration to breathe new life into stalled negotiations. It has become increasingly clear that Ukraine is not going to give up its fight in the east, even with reduced U.S. military support.
拉特克利夫此訪是川普政府為重啟陷入僵局的談判所做的最新努力。越來越明顯的是，即使美國減少了軍事支持，烏克蘭也不會放棄在東部的戰鬥。
Given what he presented as the bleak military and economic reality for Russia, Mr. Ratcliffe told Mr. Bortnikov that he believed it was time for Moscow to engage in serious negotiations to end the war.
鑒於他所描述的俄羅斯慘淡的軍事和經濟現狀，拉特克利夫告訴博爾特尼科夫，他認為，莫斯科是時候參與旨在結束戰爭的嚴肅談判了。
Since Mr. Trump’s return to office, the United States has scaled back its military support to the Ukrainians, forcing them to rely more heavily on European partners.
自川普重返白宮以來，美國縮減了對烏克蘭人的軍事支持，迫使他們更加依賴歐洲夥伴。
The C.I.A. has maintained its close intelligence partnerships with the Ukrainians, which began more than a decade ago.
中情局則繼續維持著與烏克蘭人密切的情報合作夥伴關係，這種關係始於十多年前。
The private message that Mr. Ratcliffe delivered to Mr. Bortnikov about Ukraine echoed some of the director’s public statements.
拉特克利夫向博爾特尼科夫私下傳達的關於烏克蘭的信息與這位局長的部分公開言論相呼應。
Last month, Mr. Ratcliffe spoke out pointedly about Russian troop losses during the war, and Moscow’s inability to advance on the battlefield. He noted that the life expectancy of a Russian soldier on the front lines was less than 35 minutes.
上個月，拉特克利夫直言不諱地談及了俄軍在戰爭中的傷亡情況，以及莫斯科在戰場上無法推進的窘境。他指出，俄羅斯前線士兵的預期壽命不到35分鐘。
廣告
And he added that Russia had gained only about 1 percent of Ukraine’s total territory in the 18 months since he became C.I.A. director, a development he attributed to Ukraine’s “mastery” of drone technology.
他還稱，在他擔任中情局局長以來的18個月裡，俄羅斯僅佔領了烏克蘭約1%的領土，他將這一狀況歸因於烏克蘭對無人機技術的「精通」。
Before Mr. Ratcliffe met with Mr. Bortnikov in Moscow, he spoke with his Ukrainian counterparts, assuring them that he would be exploring Russian positions, not making concessions on behalf of the Ukrainians.
在前往莫斯科與博爾特尼科夫會面之前，拉特克利夫與烏克蘭情報部門對等官員通了話，向他們保證此行是為了探明俄方立場，而非代表烏克蘭人做出讓步。
European diplomats believe there may be an opening to secure a peace agreement between Russia and Ukraine in the coming winter months, given the largely stalled Russian offensive in the Donbas, and because of Ukraine’s ability to continue imposing deep costs on Russia with its drone warfare.
歐洲外交官們認為，鑒於俄羅斯在頓巴斯的攻勢已基本停滯，再加上烏克蘭有能力利用其無人機戰法，繼續讓俄羅斯付出沉重代價，今冬可能會出現促成俄烏和平協議的契機。
Some of those European officials have been pressing the Trump administration to add a national security official to the mix of officials pressing the Russians for a peace deal, someone who could deliver the kind of tough message Mr. Ratcliffe delivered this week.
一些歐洲官員一直敦促川普政府在向俄方施壓以求達成和平協議的團隊中增加一名國家安全官員，此人應能傳達拉特克利夫本週所傳達的那種強硬信息。
The Ukrainians have long viewed Mr. Witkoff and Mr. Kushner as too close to the Russians. They view Mr. Ratcliffe, a longtime Russia hawk, as a potentially more neutral mediator.
烏克蘭人一直認為維特科夫和庫什納與俄羅斯人走得太近。他們認為長期以來的對俄鷹派拉特克利夫可能是更為中立的調解人。
Mr. Trump has repeatedly used Mr. Ratcliffe as a discreet messenger.
川普曾多次將拉特克利夫用作謹慎的傳話人。
廣告
In January, he became the first cabinet-level official to visit Venezuela after U.S. special operation forces seized President Nicolás Maduro.
今年1月，在美國特種作戰部隊抓獲馬杜羅總統後，拉特克利夫成為首位訪問委內瑞拉的內閣級官員。
In May, he traveled to Cuba, giving its leaders a bleak assessment of the state of the Cuban economy in the face of a U.S. oil blockade. He urged the country to make a series of sweeping political and economic changes before the situation got worse.
今年5月，他前往古巴，向該國領導人詳細闡述了在美國石油封鎖下古巴經濟慘淡現狀的評估。他敦促該國在局勢進一步惡化前，進行一系列全面的政治和經濟改革。
Direct contacts between U.S. and Russian spy chiefs have been sporadic in recent years.
近年來，美俄情報首腦之間的直接接觸時斷時續。
In November 2021, William J. Burns, the C.I.A. director in the Biden administration, met face to face in Moscow with Mr. Bortnikov and other top Russian officials to warn Mr. Putin against launching a full-scale invasion of Ukraine.
2021年11月，拜登政府時期的中情局局長威廉·伯恩斯在莫斯科與博爾特尼科夫等俄羅斯高官進行了面對面會晤，警告普丁不要對烏克蘭發動全面入侵。
A year later, in November 2022, Turkey hosted a face-to-face meeting in Ankara between Mr. Burns and Sergei Naryshkin, the leader of Russia’s foreign intelligence service. U.S. intelligence agencies had intercepted Russian communications about the possible use of a nuclear device by the Russians to slow Ukrainian advances in that fall’s counteroffensive. Mr. Burns warned Mr. Naryshkin of serious consequences if the Russians acted on the alleged threat.
一年後的2022年11月，土耳其在安卡拉安排了伯恩斯與俄羅斯對外情報局局長謝爾蓋·納雷什金的面對面會晤。當時，美國情報機構截獲了俄方的通訊，內容涉及俄羅斯可能會使用核裝置來阻滯烏克蘭在當年秋季反攻中的推進。伯恩斯警告納雷什金，如果俄方將這一所謂威脅付諸行動，將面臨嚴重後果。
The Turkish hosts wanted the meeting to be the start of a lasting channel of direct communication between Washington and Moscow to reduce tensions. But the C.I.A. was angry at Moscow when, after the meeting, the Russian side made it public. Mr. Burns and Mr. Naryshkin did not have a second face-to-face meeting, though they continued to communicate occasionally by phone.
作為東道主的土耳其希望這次會晤能成為華盛頓與莫斯科之間建立持久直接溝通管道的開端，以緩和緊張局勢。但會後俄方將此事公之於眾，這激怒了中情局。伯恩斯與納雷什金此後沒有進行第二次面談，儘管兩人偶爾還會通過電話交流。
廣告
After Mr. Trump took office last year, Mr. Ratcliffe and Mr. Bortnikov worked out the details of a prisoner swap, but this week’s face-to-face meeting in Moscow was their first.
去年川普上任後，拉特克利夫與博爾特尼科夫曾敲定了一項換囚協議的細節，但本週在莫斯科是兩人首次面對面會晤。

Adam Entous是《紐約時報》駐華盛頓調查記者，主要關注國家安全和請報事務。

Julian E. Barnes為《紐約時報》報導美國情報機構和國際安全事務。他撰寫安全相關議題已有20餘年。
