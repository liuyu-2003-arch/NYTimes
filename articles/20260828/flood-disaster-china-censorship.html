中國信息管控或阻礙西藏洪水救援

China’s Information Control Could Hamper Flood Rescue Efforts in Tibet

郭莉莉 • 2026-08-28
As journalists in Nepal gather eyewitness accounts and up-to-date information about the deadly flood that swept entire villages away on Wednesday, the scene across the border in China could not be more different.
當尼泊爾的記者們正在收集關於週三這場奪命洪水的目擊者證詞和最新信息時，中國邊境一側的景象截然不同。
By Thursday, videos taken and posted online by locals, including a widely circulated clip of a mudslide engulfing a border crossing in Gyirong County, in China’s Tibet, were censored on Chinese social media platforms. More than 500 people were missing in Gyirong, with three confirmed deaths in the county of about 2,000 people. The authorities have provided no details about which villages and settlements in the mostly rural area have been affected.
到週四，當地居民拍攝並上傳到網上的影片——包括一段廣泛傳播的、顯示土石流吞沒中國西藏吉隆縣一個邊境口岸的片段——已在中國社群媒體平台上遭到審查。吉隆縣有500多人失蹤，這個約2000人口的縣已有三人確認遇難。對於這個以農村為主的地區哪些村莊和定居點受災，官方沒有給出任何細節。
Instead, the only information has come from China’s government-controlled state media outlets, where coverage has focused mostly on the rescue efforts. The government has sent members of the Chinese military and other emergency workers into the area, the outlets report, as well as drones and helicopters. The People’s Daily, the official newspaper of the Chinese Communist Party, led on Thursday with a story about China’s leader, Xi Jinping, ordering that all-out efforts be made to help the victims in Gyirong.
唯一的信息來自中國政府控制的官方媒體，其報導主要集中在救援工作上。這些媒體報導稱，政府已向該地區派遣了軍隊和其他應急人員，以及無人機和直升機。中共官方報紙《人民日報》週四在頭版刊登了一篇報導，稱中國領導人習近平下令全力幫助吉隆的受災群眾。
Censorship and tight control of information is common throughout China, but nowhere is that more apparent than in Tibet, which has been under Chinese control since the 1950s and where calls for greater autonomy have erupted into protests.
審查和嚴格的信息控制在中國很普遍，但在西藏尤為突出——這裡自20世紀50年代以來一直處於中國控制之下，曾爆發過要求更多自治權的抗議活動。
廣告
No foreign journalists are allowed in except on heavily orchestrated government tours. Officials in Tibet have tightened restrictions on Tibetan monks and nuns and expanded policing to prevent any renewed protests. Tibetans in the region can be detained or arrested for speaking to journalists or posting anything online that is seen as counter to the government narrative, according to rights activists.
除了經過嚴格安排的政府參觀團外，外國記者不允許進入西藏。西藏官員加強了對藏傳佛教僧尼的限制，並擴大了警力，以防止任何新的抗議活動。據人權活動人士稱，該地區的藏人可能因與記者交談或在網上發布任何被視為違背政府說法的內容而被拘留或逮捕。
The result, according to Ryan Fioresi, the executive director of the International Campaign for Tibet, is a situation where “only the Chinese government controls the information flow.” In a disaster situation, that means that local residents, afraid of punishment, are not posting about where people are trapped or where the damage is the worst.
國際聲援西藏運動總幹事萊恩·菲奧雷西表示，其結果是「只有中國政府控制著信息流動」。在災難情況下，這意味著當地居民因害怕受到懲罰而不會發布關於人員被困地點或受災最嚴重地區的信息。
“If people are afraid to share that information, or if their posts are removed,” he said, “potentially useful information may not reach families, relief workers or the wider public.”
「如果人們害怕分享這些信息，或者他們的帖子被刪除，」他說，「那麼可能有用的信息就無法到達家屬、救援人員或更廣泛的公眾手中。」

郭莉莉(Lily Kuo)是《紐約時報》報導中國記者，常駐台北。
