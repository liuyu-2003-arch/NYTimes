比爾·蓋茲：科技行業刻意淡化人工智慧風險

Bill Gates Warns A.I. Is More Dangerous Than Big Tech Will Admit

KAREN WEISE • 2026-08-28
Bill Gates, the billionaire co-founder of Microsoft, is loudly warning that artificial intelligence poses a grave threat to jobs and human life, and that urgently addressing the risks should be “the world’s top priority.”
微軟聯合創始人、億萬富翁比爾·蓋茲強烈警告稱，人工智慧對就業和人類生命構成了嚴重威脅，緊急應對這些風險應該成為「全球頭等大事」。
In an hourlong interview with The New York Times last week, Mr. Gates said the tech industry was knowingly downplaying those threats because there was too much money on the line.
在上週接受《紐約時報》長達一小時的採訪時，蓋茲表示，科技行業明知這些威脅卻在刻意淡化，因為其中牽涉的利益太過巨大。
“In private, people who understand how good this stuff is, and how much better it’s getting, they’re very worried,” he said. But few tech executives, Mr. Gates said, are willing to publicly admit that. “They’re now saying to each other: ‘Hey, man, don’t say that. It’s bad for us — the next trillion dollars we’re trying to raise.’”
他說：「私下裡，了解這項技術有多強大、發展速度有多快的人都非常擔憂。」但蓋茲表示，幾乎沒有科技高管願意公開承認這一點。「他們現在互相打招呼說：『嘿，老兄，別這麼說。這對我們沒好處——我們還打算籌集下一個萬億美元呢。』」
On Wednesday, Mr. Gates published a nearly 6,000-word essay on his personal website laying out his concerns about A.I. and offering solutions, such as new taxes and bans. He said he was motivated to speak now because recent improvements in A.I. had far surpassed his expectations and because the industry had ignored technology milestones — like A.I.’s escaping the control of its creators or making recipes for bioweapons — that it once said would warrant more caution.
週三，蓋茲在其個人網站上發表了一篇近6000字的文章，闡述了他對人工智慧的擔憂，並提出了解決方案，例如徵收新稅和實施禁令。他表示，他之所以決定現在發聲，是因為人工智慧近期的進步已經遠遠超出了他的預期，而且整個行業忽視了一些技術里程碑——比如人工智慧逃脫其創造者的控制，或制定生物武器配方，業界曾認為這些里程碑一旦出現就需要更加謹慎對待。
廣告
“They’re just full speed ahead and hoping that the good outweighs the bad,” he said.
「他們只是在全速前進，並且希望利能大於弊，」他說。
Mr. Gates, 70, is the latest in a string of tech luminaries to call out the risks of A.I. as the industry spends trillions of dollars on new data centers, and as young A.I. companies edge toward some of the largest initial public offerings in history.
現年70歲的蓋茲是最新一位警示人工智慧風險的科技界巨擘。與此同時，該行業正斥資數萬億美元建設新的數據中心，一些年輕的人工智慧公司也正朝著歷史上規模最大的首次公開募股邁進。
He is taking his stand as he emerges from an extended period of scandal. Early this year, the release of the sexual predator Jeffrey Epstein’s emails brought renewed attention to Mr. Gates’s interactions with him.
在經歷了一段漫長的醜聞風波後，蓋茲如今站出來發聲。今年早些時候，性犯罪者傑弗里·愛潑斯坦電子郵件的曝光讓人們重新關注起蓋茲與他的交往。
Mr. Gates had to pull out of an A.I. conference in February, and testified for six hours in June before a House committee. Disclosures of his infidelity, combined with his 2021 divorce, have also badly damaged his reputation. He acknowledged that they were all “mistakes” of his own doing.
蓋茲不得不退出2月的一場人工智慧大會，並於6月出席了眾議院委員會長達六小時的聽證會。婚外情的曝光以及2021年的離婚也嚴重損害了他的聲譽。他承認這些都是他自己犯下的「錯誤」。
He appears more willing to step back into the spotlight after answering every question from the House Oversight Committee. “I think people understand what I did do, what I didn’t do and that I learn when I make mistakes,” he said in the interview.
在回答了眾議院監督委員會的所有問題後，他似乎更願意重新回到聚光燈下。「我認為人們明白我做了什麼，沒做什麼，也明白我在犯錯後會吸取教訓，」他在採訪中說道。
Mr. Gates conceded that he also might be a flawed messenger simply because of his vast wealth and history as Microsoft’s aggressive chief executive. In a landmark federal case, Microsoft was found in 2000 to have repeatedly abused its monopoly during his watch. (Mr. Gates left the company’s board of directors in 2020.)
蓋茲承認，因為他擁有巨額財富，且曾擔任微軟強勢的首席執行官，他可能是一個有瑕疵的信息傳遞者。在2000年一項具有里程碑意義的聯邦訴訟中，微軟被裁定在他掌舵期間多次濫用其壟斷地位。（蓋茲於2020年離開公司董事會。）
廣告
While Mr. Gates avoided criticizing Microsoft or other companies by name, he argued that he was uniquely positioned to work on the A.I. issue. He is a technologist and industry insider who is no longer driven by the profit incentives of running a company, and he has spent decades addressing inequality through his philanthropic foundation.
儘管蓋茲避免點名批評微軟或其他公司，但他認為自己在應對人工智慧問題上處於得天獨厚的位置。他是一位技術專家和行業內部人士，不再受經營公司的利潤動機驅動，並且數十年來一直通過自己的慈善基金會致力於解決不平等問題。
“The irony of ‘innovation lover is scared and says we’re not ready and we’re crossing thresholds,’ maybe it’s even more poignant,” he said.
「一個熱愛創新的人感到害怕，說我們還沒做好準備，說我們正在跨過臨界點——這種諷刺意味也許更加發人深省，」他說。
Mr. Gates said technological advances had truly stunned him three times: In 1980 when he first saw a graphical user interface, which led to the modern personal computer. In 2022 when the leaders of OpenAI came to his house to demonstrate what would soon become ChatGPT. And this year when he looked closely at the advances being made by Claude Code, Anthropic’s A.I. tool.
蓋茲表示，曾有三次技術進步真正令他驚嘆：1980年，他第一次看到圖形用戶界面，這最終催生了現代個人電腦。2022年，OpenAI的領導者來到他家，展示了不久後成為ChatGPT的產品。還有今年，他仔細審視了Anthropic的人工智慧工具Claude Code所取得的進展。
Coding, Mr. Gates said, is the subject he knows best, and Claude’s abilities shocked him. “The idea that now these things are in a meaningful sense better than I am is like: What the hell?” he said.
蓋茲說，編程是他最了解的領域，而Claude的能力震驚了他。「現在這些東西在某種實質意義上比我還厲害了，我簡直想問：這到底是怎麼回事？」他說。
Technological breakthroughs in the past created more jobs than they eliminated, Mr. Gates said. But he is in disbelief that industry boosters say that will happen again this time.
蓋茲表示，過去的技術突破創造的工作崗位比淘汰的更多。行業擁躉宣稱這一次也會重演同樣的情況，他表示對此無法相信。
The A.I. era is “utterly, absolutely, completely, totally different,” he said. He acknowledged that this unpopular opinion (in tech circles) was “a hell of a claim, and I’m staking everything I know — everything — saying, ‘Are you kidding?’”
他說，人工智慧時代是「徹底、絕對、完全、截然不同的」。他承認，這個（在科技圈內）不受歡迎的觀點「是一個極端的斷言，我正押上我所知道的一切——一切——來發問：『你們是認真的嗎？』」
廣告
He said mass job losses, if not addressed, were inevitable because A.I. would spread across the economy and leave little room for one industry to absorb the refugees from another. The biggest tech companies in the world, including Microsoft, are racing to help corporate customers adopt A.I., which could replace many workers.
他表示，如果不加以解決，大規模失業將是不可避免的，因為人工智慧將蔓延至整個經濟領域，一個行業幾乎沒有餘地去吸納來自其他行業的失業人員。包括微軟在內的全球最大型科技公司正競相幫助企業客戶採用人工智慧，這可能會取代許多工人。
Mr. Gates said that companies were just responding to market incentives, but that those incentives might conflict with what was best for society.
蓋茲表示，企業只是在對市場激勵做出反應，但這些激勵可能與社會的最佳利益相衝突。
He proposed a new tax on A.I. use, known as a “token tax,” to make it more expensive to replace people and provide money to help those who do lose work. A token is a basic unit of A.I. computing. The idea of taxing it has gained some traction, including on Wall Street, as a way to increase the cost of using machines instead of human labor.
他提議對人工智慧的使用徵收一種名為「詞元稅」的新稅，以增加取代人類的成本，並為失業者提供經濟援助。詞元即token，是人工智慧計算的基本單位。對其徵稅的想法已經獲得了一些支持，包括在華爾街，它被視為提高使用機器取代勞動力的成本的一種方式。
Some jobs, Mr. Gates said, should be prohibited from being replaced by A.I. — an idea he called “Human Reserved” — because the role is deeply personal, like a caregiver, or the people in those jobs are unlikely to find new employment.
蓋茲表示，一些工作應被禁止由人工智慧取代——他將這一概念稱為「人類保留」(Human Reserved)——因為這些角色具有極強的個人化屬性（比如護理人員），或者從事這些工作的人不太可能找到新工作。
He argued for new international agreements to set clear criteria to monitor and restrict extreme risks like bioterrorism. He said it could take only a few people with access to advanced A.I. to develop new pathogens.
他主張達成新的國際協議，設定明確的標準，以監測和限制生物恐怖主義等極端風險。他說，只需少數幾個人掌握先進的人工智慧技術，就足以開發出新的病原體。
He said he would happily debate anyone who argued against mandatory reviews of any A.I. system that could be weaponized to make new molecules and unleash a pandemic. Mr. Gates added that he was dumbfounded by the kind of voluntary measures that the White House and industry leaders were developing.
他說，如果人工智慧系統可以被用作武器，製造新分子並引發大規模流行病，必須接受強制審查。如果有人反對這一點，他很樂意與其辯論。蓋茲補充說，白宮和行業領導者正在制定的那種自願性措施令他感到目瞪口呆。
廣告
“Self-regulation on the most dangerous tool ever invented?” he said incredulously. “No, thanks!”
「對有史以來發明過的最危險的工具進行自我監管？」他難以置信地說。「不，謝謝！」
Mr. Gates said he was in a “state of shock” as to why there was not more urgency on these issues. Many of his concerns echo those raised by the Anthropic co-founder Dario Amodei, but Dr. Amodei’s warnings have put him at odds with the Pentagon, the White House and much of the tech industry.
蓋茲說，對於為何這些問題沒有引起更多緊迫感，他感到「震驚」。他的許多擔憂與Anthropic聯合創始人達里奧·阿莫代伊提出的擔憂相呼應，但阿莫代伊的警告讓他與五角大廈、白宮以及大部分科技行業產生了分歧。
“You have people attacking the person who’s the most open about the downsides,” Mr. Gates said.
「人們反而去攻擊那個最坦率地指出弊端的人，」蓋茲說。
(The Times has sued OpenAI and Microsoft, claiming copyright infringement of news content related to A.I. systems. The two companies have denied those claims.)
（《紐約時報》起訴了OpenAI和微軟，指控其在與人工智慧系統相關的新聞內容上侵犯了版權。這兩家公司否認了這些指控。）
Along with global health issues, Mr. Gates vowed to make his A.I. concern part of every conversation he had with world leaders. “I only have two issues where I’m going to say, ‘Please think more about this, please care more about this,’” he said.
蓋茲還承諾，要將他對人工智慧的擔憂與全球健康問題一起納入他與世界各國領導人的每一次對話中。「我只有在這兩個事項上會說：『請多考慮一下這個問題，請多關注一下這個問題』，」他說。
Mr. Gates acknowledged that he was in an odd spot: He has arguably done as much as anyone in the world to evangelize for and shape the modern computer industry. But now he is warning that the technology it is creating presents global risks.
蓋茲承認他處於一個尷尬的境地：可以說，在倡導和塑造現代計算機行業方面，他做出的貢獻幾乎無人可及。但現在他卻警告稱，該行業正在創造的技術帶來了全球性風險。
廣告
“I don’t like bringing bad news to people, and I don’t like saying that innovation may be a net negative,” he said. “But that’s where we are.”
「我不喜歡給人們帶來壞消息，我也不喜歡說創新可能會帶來凈負面影響，」他說。「但這就是我們目前的處境。」

Karen Weise為時報報導科技新聞，常駐西雅圖，主要關注亞馬遜和微軟這兩家極具具影響力的公司。
