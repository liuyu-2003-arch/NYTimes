「女版賈伯斯」紀錄片：矽谷驚天騙局的「真相」與現實

‘You Can See Everything’ Review: The Slippery Mind of Elizabeth Holmes

MANOHLA DARGIS • 2026-10-19
The nominal subject of the trippy “You Can See Everything” is Elizabeth Holmes, the celebrated entrepreneur turned celebrity jailbird. As a portrait, the movie is intimate and analytic, informative and incomplete. It’s also off-the-charts bonkers, which isn’t surprising given the participants. Directed by the comic Nathan Fielder and the filmmaker Lance Oppenheim, the movie is, among other things, a wily, disturbing, self-aware exploration of the relationship between documentarians and their subjects. It’s often funny, even if the questions that it raises about truth, reality and knowledge can make it feel like a tragedy — few movies speak as uncannily to the pathos and destabilizing confusions of the present moment.
這部讓人心神恍惚的《你能看到一切》名義上的主角是伊麗莎白·霍姆斯——曾備受讚譽的企業家，後來成了著名的階下囚。作為一幅肖像，這部電影既私密又具分析性，既詳實又不完整。同時它還離譜得沒邊，考慮到都是些什麼人在參與其中，這並不意外。可以從多個角度去看待這部由喜劇演員內森·菲爾德和電影製作人蘭斯·奧本海姆執導的影片，例如將其看做一場狡猾、令人不安、有自我意識的探索，檢視紀錄片工作者與其拍攝對象之間的關係。它常常有趣，即使它提出的關於真相、現實和知識的問題可能給人一種悲劇的感覺——很少有電影能如此詭異地捕捉當下的悲情與令人失穩的困惑。
At this point, it’s a tossup whether Holmes is better known for her pop-cultural profile or her real-world exploits or if Fielder, the creator of absurdist comedy TV shows like “Nathan for You,” is now the bigger draw. Her fame was relatively brief but sensational. In 2014, Forbes deemed Holmes, the founder of a blood-testing start-up named Theranos, the youngest self-made female billionaire. She claimed, among other things, that her technology would revolutionize health care, which proved untrue. By 2022, Holmes had been found guilty of four charges of fraud for deceiving investors, a turn of events that seemed to baffle even the presiding judge. “What was it that caused Ms. Holmes, regrettably, to make those decisions that she did,” Judge Edward J. Davila asked, before sentencing her to 11 years in prison.
事到如今很難說，究竟是霍爾姆斯的流行文化名氣更響、還是以現實事蹟為人所知，抑或菲爾德——荒誕喜劇電視節目《救援高手》的創作者——才是影片更吸引人的因素。她的名氣相對短暫，卻足夠轟動。2014年，《富比士》將霍姆斯——血液檢測初創公司Theranos的創始人——認定為最年輕的白手起家女性億萬富翁。她聲稱自己的技術將徹底改變醫療保健，後來證明並非如此。到2022年，霍姆斯因欺騙投資者被判四項欺詐罪名成立，這一轉折似乎連主審法官都感到困惑。「究竟是什麼，讓霍姆斯女士遺憾地做出了她所做的那些決定？」法官愛德華·J·達維拉在判處她11年監禁前問道。
That question hovers over the movie, and is as much a head-scratcher as how Fielder and Oppenheim came to make it. At one point there’s an approving reference to Fielder being someone who looks “at things differently.” It’s a rich line for those familiar with his work; it’s obvious that Holmes and her life partner, Billy Evans, were not. It’s also obvious that they were out to shift public perceptions about her or, at least, the ostensible narrative, and that the filmmakers were aware of the role they were being solicited to play. It’s not for nothing that the documentary opens on a tight shot of Evans’s face. “Nobody knows who she is,” Evans says to someone on the phone, shortly before dropping the words “radical transparency.”
這個問題籠罩著整部影片，其令人費解的程度不亞於菲爾德和奧本海姆究竟如何拍成了它。片中有個地方用讚許的語調說，菲爾德是一個「以不同方式看事物」的人。對熟悉他作品的人來說，這句話意味深長；霍姆斯和她的生活伴侶比利·埃文斯顯然不熟悉。同樣明顯的是，他們意在扭轉公眾對她的看法，至少是表面敘事，而電影製作者也清楚自己受邀扮演的是什麼角色。紀錄片以埃文斯面部的特寫開場並非沒偶然。「沒人知道她是誰，」埃文斯在電話裡對某人說，沒過多久便拋出了「徹底的透明度」這個詞。
It’s instructive that Holmes isn’t the first person you see in the movie given how she appears in the movie. She was the most famous woman tech entrepreneur to emerge from Silicon Valley, which helped elevate her profile and added frisson of her fall. Her signature look was striking, including long hair that always seemed a smidge messy, as if she were just too busy innovating to comb it. She didn’t seem to blink, which emphasized the whites of her eyes and presumably held others rapt. It’s a neat movie actor trick for creating dominance, which might have been useful in tech-bro land. She blinks in “You Can See Everything” and her distinctive low-pitched voice also seems more natural; it’s about the only thing that does.
考慮到霍姆斯在片中的呈現方式，她並非觀眾在電影裡看到的第一個人，這一點本身就耐人尋味。她是矽谷走出的最著名的女性科技企業家，這既抬升了她的知名度，也為她的墜落平添了一層顫慄。她的標誌性形象極為醒目：長髮似乎總有些凌亂，彷彿她忙於創新而無暇梳頭；她似乎不眨眼，這讓她的眼白顯得格外突出，想必也讓旁人凝神屏息。這是一個製造支配感的巧妙電影演員技巧，在科技兄弟的地盤上也許很管用。在《你能看到一切》裡，她眨眼了，她標誌性的低沉嗓音也顯得更自然；這幾乎是片中唯一自然的東西。
廣告
Shot over several years, the documentary opens in 2023, 34 days before Holmes entered prison. Throughout it, the filmmakers mix original and extant material, and embrace different documentary approaches (participatory, ethnographic and so on). For the most part, though, “You Can See Everything” — the title refers to a text Holmes sent Fielder — unfolds as a series of intense conversations with Fielder serving as the on-camera interlocutor. Many of these take place in Holmes and Evans’s beachfront place in Del Mar, Calif., where Fielder prods and pushes them in the dining room, on the sofa, wherever, sometimes with the couple’s two young children and her parents in attendance. A number of encounters are shot in such extreme, dermatologic close-up that you become intimately familiar with Fielder’s zits.
影片拍攝歷時數年，以2023年開篇，當時距離霍姆斯入獄還有34天。整部紀錄片中，製作者混合使用原創與既有素材，並採納了不同的紀錄片路徑（參與式、民族志式等）。不過大體而言，《你能看到一切》——片名取自霍姆斯發給菲爾德的一條短訊——以一系列密集對話展開，菲爾德本人出鏡參與對話。許多對話發生在霍姆斯和埃文斯位於加州德爾馬的海濱住處，菲爾德在餐廳、沙發等等地方隨時發問，有時他們的兩個年幼孩子和她的父母也在場。若干場會面用了極端的、皮膚科檢查般的大特寫，以至於你對菲爾德臉上的痘痘瞭若指掌。
Every so often, other members of the small film crew show up on camera, too. These onscreen appearances are reminders of the inherently constructed aspect of the movie and, by extension, the documentary genre, and they dovetail with a movie’s preoccupation with questions of authenticity and artificiality. All the conversations in the movie take place, after all, in front of a small audience that is, in turn, recording these interactions for the far larger movie audience. Whether Holmes’s occasional blinks and tears in these chats are performative is open to question as are her grasp on language, truth, science and reality itself, all of which Fielder seizes on in a series of interactions that grow progressively weirder.
攝製組的其他成員時不時也會入畫。這提醒觀眾：這部電影——推而廣之，整個紀錄片類型——從根本上具有一種建構性，也與影片對真實性與人造性的執著相契合。畢竟，片中所有對話都發生在一小群觀眾面前，而這群人又在為數量龐大得多的電影觀眾記錄這些互動。霍姆斯在這些交談中偶爾的眨眼與淚水是否有表演成分，你可以有自己的答案；她對語言、真相、科學乃至現實本身的把握同樣也可以存疑，而菲爾德在一連串愈發怪異的互動中抓住的正是這些。
One of Fielder’s superpowers as a performer is his deadpan, which he generally manages to maintain no matter how outlandish the conceit. He’s put it to recurrently unsettling effect on shows in which he — or a version of himself — aggressively toys with the genre of reality television through his interactions with real people. In “Nathan for You,” he offers absurd advice to small-business owners, successfully persuading a frozen-yogurt store manager, for instance, to sell a lab-created fecal-flavored offering, a.k.a. “poo.” The more conceptually ambitious show, “The Rehearsal,” involves him helping people work through assorted personal issues with the use of hired actors and elaborate re-creations of real life.
作為表演者，菲爾德的超能力之一是他那張冷臉，無論設定多麼荒誕，他通常都能保持這種表情。他在自己的節目裡反覆用這張冷臉製造令人不安的效果：在那些節目中，他——或者說他的某個化身——通過與真實人物互動，對真人秀這一節目類型進行大膽的戲謔。在《救援高手》(Nathan for You)裡，他向小企業主提供荒誕建議，例如，他成功說服了一家冷凍酸奶店的經理，推出一款實驗室合成的糞便口味產品，也就是「便便」。在概念上更有雄心的節目《綵排》(The Rehearsal)中，他藉助雇來的演員和對現實生活的精心重現，幫助人們處理五花八門的個人問題。
Both shows are finally less interesting for what they say about their putative subjects than what they say about those of us who watch them and keep on watching in stitches, shock or horror. Fielder likes to test the limits of propriety and (canned) reality, and he’s having some laughs in the process. He’s also creating social psychological experiments that, in their effect, bring to mind Orson Welles’s 1938 broadcast of the H.G. Wells novel “The War of the Worlds,” about an alien invasion. Welles presented part of the drama as breaking news, which panicked listeners, made headlines and irked the Federal Communications Commission. Welles later said that he had underestimated “the extent of our American lunatic fringe.”
這兩檔節目之所以引人入勝，與其說在於它們揭示了所謂受訪者的什麼內容，不如說在於它們折射出我們這些觀眾的內容——我們一邊觀看，一邊忍俊不禁、震驚不已或毛骨悚然，卻依然欲罷不能。菲爾德喜歡試探得體與（預錄）現實的邊界，並在此過程中找些樂子。他同時也在製造社會心理學實驗，其效果讓人想起奧遜·威爾斯1938年對H·G·威爾斯的小說《世界大戰》(The War of the Worlds)的廣播改編——該作品講述了外星人入侵的故事。威爾斯把部分劇情包裝為突發新聞，令聽眾陷入恐慌，事件登上新聞，也惹惱了聯邦通信委員會。威爾斯後來說，他低估了「我們美國瘋子邊緣群體的規模」。
That fringe no longer seems marginal, if it ever was. That the world in “You Can See Everything” appears sane is partly because of the legitimacy conferred on Holmes by her fame and the hundreds of millions of dollars that went into Theranos. Serious people took her seriously, which can seem hard to believe given how slippery Holmes and Evans seem here. Indeed, the first image of Fielder in the movie suggests that he and Oppenheim have come to this project with a degree of incredulity. It’s a murky shot of Fielder peering through a window with what looks like comic skepticism. If it’s uncertain what Fielder is thinking at that moment, it is certain that he and Oppenheim have already begun to test your preconceptions.
那個邊緣群體如今似乎已經不再邊緣——如果它曾經是邊緣的話。《你能看到一切》中的世界看起來顯得理智，部分原因是霍姆斯從名氣以及投入Theranos的數億美元中所獲得的公信力。嚴肅的人士曾認真對待她，而看她與埃文斯在片中表現得如此狡猾，這似乎難以置信。的確，菲爾德在影片中的第一個畫面就暗示，他和奧本海姆是帶著幾分難以置信的感覺進入這個項目的。那是一個模糊的鏡頭：菲爾德透過窗戶窺視，神情中透著一種喜劇式的懷疑。儘管無法確定菲爾德當時在想什麼，但可以確定的是，他和奧本海姆已經已經開始測試觀眾的先入之見。
One of the most gob-smackingly wild things about Holmes is how she was able to scam so many. That’s part of the story here, though the more interesting one concerns what we mean by truth in a world of profound epistemological uncertainty. Again and again, Fielder presses Holmes about her past and her present. He stares into her eyes, and she stares right back, as he interrogates her linguistic elisions and wonders about her grasp on a famous old comedy routine. He also watches her as she watches the 2022 TV drama series “The Dropout,” in which Amanda Seyfried plays Holmes. It’s a dizzying moment, all the more so given that we’re watching, too, swept up in a ricochet of gazes in the mirrored fun house we now live in.
霍姆斯身上最令人瞠目結舌的一點是她居然能騙過那麼多人。這是片中故事的一部分，但更有趣的是，在一個充滿深刻認識論不確定性的世界裡，我們究竟該如何定義「真相」。菲爾德一次又一次追問霍姆斯的過去與現在。他凝視她的眼睛，她也直直回望，同時他盤問她語言中的省略，質疑她對一段著名老喜劇橋段的理解。他還在她觀看2022年電視劇《輟學生》(The Dropout)時從旁觀察——劇中阿曼達·塞弗里德飾演霍姆斯。那是一個令人目眩的時刻，尤其因為我們也在觀看，被捲入我們如今棲居的這座鏡像迷宮中的目光相互反彈。
