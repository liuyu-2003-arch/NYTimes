祕密線人和失蹤的兇器：辛普森案塵封30年的謎團

5 Takeaways From The Times’s Re-Examination of the O.J. Simpson Case

MIKE BAKER, TIM ARANGO • 2026-10-19
In the failed murder prosecution of O.J. Simpson in 1995, the government professed that he alone had killed his ex-wife, Nicole Brown Simpson, and her friend Ronald Goldman.
在1995年對O·J·辛普森謀殺案起訴失敗的那起案件中，政府聲稱是他獨自殺害了前妻妮可·布朗·辛普森和她的朋友羅納德·戈德曼。
Among the issues that hampered the case: the lack of a murder weapon, which has never been found. Its absence has long stoked speculation about whether the killer had assistance.
阻礙本案調查的問題之一是兇器的缺失，而且兇器至今從未被找到。兇器缺失長期以來引發了關於兇手是否曾獲他人協助的猜測。
Now, records unsealed after 30 years at the request of The New York Times show that a prized government informant had told law enforcement key information about the killings, including the supposed location of the murder weapon and details about Mr. Simpson’s efforts to evade prosecution. Together, the findings challenge the popular narrative around the case.
如今，應《紐約時報》請求解封的塵封30年記錄顯示，一名政府重點線人曾向執法部門提供關於這起殺人案的關鍵信息，包括兇器據稱所在的位置，以及辛普森為逃避起訴所採取的行動細節。這些發現共同挑戰了圍繞此案的普遍說法。
This year, the informant’s brother told The Times that his sibling’s role in the case went much deeper. And the brother said he actually helped discard a bag, containing a heavy object, in the hours after the murders.
今年，這名線人的兄弟告訴《紐約時報》，其兄弟在此案中的角色遠比外界所知更為深遠。這名兄弟還表示，他本人在謀殺案發生後的幾小時內曾協助丟棄了一個內裝重物的袋子。
廣告
An ‘extremely reliable’ informant offered information about O.J. Simpson.
一名「極其可靠」的線人提供了關於O.J.辛普森的信息。
Joseph Ippolito, known as Joey, a convicted drug trafficker and mafia associate, escaped from a Florida prison a month before the 1994 murders.
約瑟夫·伊波利托（人稱喬伊）是一名被定罪的毒販和黑手黨成員，在1994年謀殺案發生前一個月從佛羅里達一所監獄逃脫。
Mr. Ippolito’s brother said the siblings went west after the escape to Los Angeles. Before his incarceration, Mr. Ippolito had operated a popular restaurant there and was a close friend of Al Cowlings, the longtime confidant of Mr. Simpson.
伊波利托的兄弟說，逃脫後，兄弟倆向西前往洛杉磯。入獄前，伊波利托曾在那裡經營一家受歡迎的餐廳，並且是辛普森長期親信阿爾·考林斯的密友。
In the wake of the infamous Bronco chase with Mr. Simpson and Mr. Cowlings, investigators found photos in the vehicle of one of Mr. Ippolito’s girlfriends, along with her phone number.
在辛普森和考林斯那場臭名昭著的布朗科越野車追逐事件之後，調查人員在這輛車中發現了伊波利托其中一名女友的照片以及她的電話號碼。
Mr. Ippolito was eventually brought back into custody a year later. Right away, records show, he fed federal investigators in New York information about the mafia and set up meetings with undercover drug agents, with prosecutors saying that they “found Ippolito to be a remarkable source of information.” Mr. Ippolito risked his own safety to help them, prosecutors wrote in a memo lobbying a federal judge for leniency in his sentence.
伊波利托最終在一年後再次被拘留。記錄顯示，他隨即向紐約的聯邦調查人員提供了關於黑手黨的信息，並安排了與卧底緝毒探員的會面，檢察官稱，他們「發現伊波利托是一個極其寶貴的信息來源」。檢察官在一份遊說法官對其從輕判決的備忘錄中寫道，伊波利托冒著自身安全風險協助了調查。
He also divulged information about Mr. Simpson and the killings.
他還洩漏了關於辛普森和這起殺人案的信息。
The informant spoke about the missing murder weapon.
這名線人談到了失蹤的兇器。
Prosecutors said that Mr. Ippolito’s close relationship with Mr. Cowlings and Mr. Simpson made him “privy to many facts relevant to the case, including Mr. Simpson’s alleged domestic violence, the Simpsons’ narcotics usage, Mr. Simpson’s attempted flight from justice, and his disposal of the murder weapon.”
檢察官說，伊波利托與考林斯和辛普森的密切關係使他「知曉許多與本案相關的事實，包括辛普森涉嫌的家庭暴力、辛普森夫婦吸毒、辛普森試圖逃避司法，以及他處理兇器的情況」。
Details of Mr. Ippolito’s cooperation were outlined in a federal court memo, as well as a sentencing hearing transcript, obtained by The Times. A federal prosecutor, a former U.S. marshal, a former agent for the Drug Enforcement Administration and an official involved in prosecuting the murder case also independently confirmed Mr. Ippolito’s cooperation.
《紐約時報》獲得的一份聯邦法院備忘錄以及一份量刑聽證會記錄概述了伊波利托合作的細節。一名聯邦檢察官、一名前美國法警、一名前緝毒局探員以及一名參與起訴此謀殺案的官員也分別獨立證實了伊波利托的合作。
廣告
Federal officials said they shared the information with prosecutors in Los Angeles. But the information arrived near the end of the murder trial, which was attracting global attention. Mr. Ippolito was never called as a witness. He died in 2006.
聯邦官員說，他們已將信息分享給洛杉磯的檢察官。但信息送達時，這場吸引全球關注的謀殺案審判已接近尾聲。伊波利托從未被傳喚作證。他於2006年去世。
The informant’s brother said his sibling was at the murder scene.
這名線人的兄弟說，其兄弟曾在謀殺現場。
Earlier this year, one of Mr. Ippolito’s brothers, Sam, told The Times that they were staying at a West Hollywood apartment on the night of the murders in 1994. Joey told Sam he was going out for the night to help a friend. Sam said his brother came back hours later with what appeared to be blood on his hands and a bag that contained a heavy object. Joey asked his brother to get rid of the bag, Sam Ippolito recalled. Sam Ippolito said he tossed it into a construction site dumpster nearby.
今年早些時候，伊波利托的兄弟之一薩姆告訴《紐約時報》，1994年謀殺案發生當晚，他們住在西好萊塢的一套公寓裡。喬伊告訴薩姆，他當晚要出去幫一個朋友。薩姆說，幾個小時後，他的兄弟回來了，手上似乎有血，還帶著一個裝有重物的袋子。薩姆·伊波利托回憶說，喬伊讓自己把袋子處理掉。薩姆·伊波利托說，他把它扔進了附近一個建築工地的垃圾箱。
Sam Ippolito said he was confident his brother had been at the scene of the murders. Months after the Times interview, Sam Ippolito asked for compensation. When a reporter declined, he recanted his story and said he “was offered a ton of money” to tell his story elsewhere, apparently for an upcoming documentary.
薩姆·伊波利托說，他確信自己的兄弟曾出現在謀殺現場。在接受《紐約時報》採訪數月後，薩姆·伊波利托要求獲得報酬。當一名記者拒絕時，他推翻了之前的說法，並說自己「被提供了一大筆錢」去別處講述自己的故事，顯然是為了一部即將上映的紀錄片。
Simpson once described a ‘hypothetical’ accomplice.
辛普森曾描述過一名「假設」的同夥。
In 2007, Mr. Simpson went public with a “hypothetical” confession to the murders, published in the book “If I Did It.” In that account, he described a friend who was with him on the night of the murders, who first was a horrified bystander but then agreed to take the murder weapon and Mr. Simpson’s bloody clothes.
2007年，辛普森公開了對這起謀殺案的「假設性」認罪，發表在《如果是我幹的》(If I Did It)一書中。在書中，他描述了一位在謀殺當晚與他在一起的朋友，此人起初是一名驚恐的旁觀者，但後來同意幫他處理兇器和辛普森沾滿血跡的衣服。
廣告
Prosecutors and investigators gave little credence to the details of Mr. Simpson’s tale, and they did little to follow up on the idea that someone may have aided in helping Mr. Simpson get rid of the murder weapon.
檢察官和調查人員對辛普森敘述的細節幾乎不予採信，也幾乎沒有跟進有人可能幫助辛普森處理兇器的說法。
Investigators remain skeptical. The case remains open.
調查人員仍持懷疑態度。此案仍未結案。
The Times found that Mr. Ippolito’s name had surfaced early — and repeatedly — in the murder investigation. Police investigators asked about him. The defense team examined him, checking into his mafia ties and exploring whether he could be considered a suspect.
《紐約時報》發現，伊波利托的名字早在謀殺案調查初期就出現過——而且反覆出現。警方調查人員曾詢問過他。辯護團隊也對他進行了調查，核查了他與黑手黨的聯繫，並探究他是否可能被視為嫌疑人。
Tom Lange, who was the lead detective in the Simpson case, said he recalled Mr. Ippolito’s name coming up but didn’t consider him a serious suspect. He still does not believe Mr. Simpson had help.
曾擔任辛普森案首席偵探的湯姆·蘭格表示，他記得伊波利托的名字曾被提到，但並未將其視為重要嫌疑人。他至今仍不相信辛普森有幫手。
Mr. Simpson was eventually held civilly liable for the deaths. He died in 2024.
辛普森最終被判定對這兩起死亡事件承擔民事責任。他於2024年去世。
The Times learned that last year, investigators with the Los Angeles Police Department’s cold case unit were making calls to collect information about accomplice theories.
《紐約時報》獲悉，去年，洛杉磯警察局懸案組的調查人員曾致電多方，收集關於共犯理論的信息。
廣告
A police department spokesman said last week that the “O.J. Simpson case remains an open and ongoing investigation.” Because there was no conviction in the murders, “the department has an obligation to continue to pursue justice in this matter.” The spokesman added, “at a point that new evidence is found, detectives will follow those investigatory leads.”
警方發言人上週說，「O·J·辛普森案仍是未結且正在調查中的案件。」 由於該謀殺案尚未作出定罪判決，「警局有義務繼續在此事中追求正義」。發言人還說，「一旦發現新證據，警探們將循著這些調查線索跟進。」

Mike Baker是《紐約時報》國內新聞調查記者，常駐西雅圖。

Tim Arango是一名美國國內新聞記者，常駐洛杉磯。
