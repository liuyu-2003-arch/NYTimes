白宮鬧劇影響共和黨期中選舉前景

On the Campaign Trail, Trump Tries to Outrun a Bad Week in Washington

TYLER PAGER • 2026-10-19
President Trump continued to sell himself as the Republican Party’s savior as he crisscrossed the country in recent days, making it clear in words and sheer presence that this midterm election is about him.
近幾日，川普總統穿梭於全國各地，繼續將自己標榜為共和黨的救世主。他通過言辭和親身到場明確表明，這場期中選舉就是對他本人的公投。
But back in the nation’s capital, Mr. Trump’s White House was consumed by dramas that reinforced why polls show that his presidency itself has become a drag on Republican tickets.
但在華盛頓，川普的白宮卻深陷連串風波，進一步印證為何民調顯示他的總統任期本身已成為共和黨選情的拖累。
On Monday, Mr. Trump asked for the resignation of Kimberly Guilfoyle, his ambassador to Greece, after a string of embarrassing reports, including that she urged a donor to pay for her $100,000 credit card debt. The president on Thursday canceled the Pentagon’s planned livestream of an execution of the Army officer convicted in the 2009 Fort Hood mass shooting after bipartisan outrage. And on Friday, he ousted his press secretary of five days after her bumpy debut.
週一，在一系列令人尷尬的報導曝光後（包括她要求一名捐助者為其償還10萬美元信用卡債務），川普要求駐希臘大使金伯利·吉爾福伊爾辭職。週四，在引發兩黨憤怒後，總統取消了五角大廈直播2009年胡德堡大規模槍擊案獲罪軍官死刑的計劃。到了週五，他又解僱了上任僅五天、首秀表現不佳的新聞秘書。
Meantime, the White House Counsel’s Office was investigating a dispute between two prominent Trump allies and former administration officials over claims one of them misallocated funds for the John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts, a heated conflict stemming from a transaction from the Democratic Republic of Congo to a Texas defense company. And the administration deepened its standoff with the federal judge presiding over a lawsuit challenging the president’s plan for a golf course, pushing to disqualify her after she excoriated federal officials for ripping up hundreds of mature trees with little notice.
與此同時，白宮法律顧問辦公室正在調查一起糾紛，涉及兩名知名川普盟友及前政府官員，指控其中一人挪用約翰·F·甘迺迪表演藝術中心的資金。這場激烈衝突源於一筆交易，從剛果民主共和國一直牽扯到得克薩斯州的一家國防公司。此外，政府與一名聯邦法官的僵局進一步加深。該法官正在審理一項挑戰總統高爾夫球場計劃的訴訟，此前她嚴厲斥責聯邦官員在未提前通知的情況下砍伐了數百棵成年樹木，川普政府隨後推動取消其資格。
廣告
Together, the string of controversies invoked the chaos of Mr. Trump’s first term, when infighting among his aides regularly burst into public view. The week also encapsulated how Mr. Trump’s approval ratings have been dragged down by his administration’s policies and allegations of self-dealing that have shadowed much of his inner circle.
這一連串爭議加在一起，讓人回想起川普第一任期內的混亂局面——當時幕僚內鬥頻頻暴露在公眾視野中。這一週也濃縮體現了川普的支持率如何受到政府政策及中飽私囊指控的拖累，這些指控一直籠罩著他的大部分核心圈子。
“It feels like late 2020,” said Alyssa Farah Griffin, who served in Mr. Trump’s White House during his first term. “There’s almost too much chaos. It's drinking from the fire hose, and you can’t anticipate the next plot twist from this administration.”
「感覺就像回到了2020年末，」曾在川普第一任期白宮任職的阿麗莎·法拉·格裡芬說，「太過混亂。就像在用消防水管喝水，你根本預料不到這屆政府的下一個劇情轉折。」
She added: “You couldn’t dream up a worse week for the White House heading into the midterms.”
「對於即將迎來期中選舉的白宮來說，想像不出比這更糟的一週了，」她說。
Mr. Trump's approval rating sits around 37 percent, the lowest of his presidency, according to The New York Times average of polls. A Washington Post-ABC News-Ipsos poll conducted Oct. 9-13 and released on Sunday shows his approval among Republicans dropped from 88 percent near the start of his term to 79 percent. The survey also found, for the first time in a decade, that voters trust Democrats more on the economy, and 66 percent of voters said Mr. Trump is using the presidency to enrich himself.
根據《紐約時報》的民調平均值，川普的支持率目前徘徊在37%左右，為其總統生涯最低點。週日公布的《華盛頓郵報》-ABC新聞-益普索民調（於10月9日至13日進行）顯示，他在共和黨人中的支持率已從任期初的88%降至79%。調查還發現，十年來首次出現選民在經濟議題上更信任民主黨人的情況，且66%的選民認為川普正利用總統職位中飽私囊。
A Fox News poll from this month found that government corruption ranked first among voters’ concerns, followed by inflation, gas prices, government leadership and health care. Democrats are making the case that corruption is in fact a cause of higher prices.
本月的一項福克斯新聞民調顯示，政府腐敗在選民最關心的問題中排名第一，其次是通貨膨脹、油價、政府領導力和醫療保健。民主黨人正試圖論證，腐敗實際上正是物價上漲的根源之一。
“To the extent that Republicans are successful in keeping the Senate, it will be because of their success in distancing themselves from some of the president’s policies,” former Senator Jeff Flake, Republican of Arizona and longtime Trump critic who served as ambassador to Turkey in the Biden administration, said in an interview. “And that’s not a position you want to be in. And it’s not just the policies. It’s the weirdness of it. The crass and crude and vulgar language. It’s awful. It doesn’t help Republicans.”
「如果共和黨人成功保住了參議院，那將是因為他們成功地與總統的一些政策保持了距離，」亞利桑那州共和黨前參議員、長期批評川普並在拜登政府擔任過駐土耳其大使的傑夫·弗萊克在接受採訪時說，「這可不是什麼理想的處境。而且不只是政策問題，還有那種怪異感。粗俗、粗鄙和下流的語言。這太糟糕了。這對共和黨毫無幫助。」
On the road, Mr. Trump made no mention of the latest controversies. He held rallies in six different states, stretching from Alaska to Georgia, serving up a familiar message: His presidency has been an unbridled success, and Democratic victories next month threaten America’s future.
在各地奔走時，川普對最新的爭議隻字未提。他在從阿拉斯加到喬治亞的六個州舉行了集會，傳遞著熟悉的信息：他的總統任期取得了巨大成功，而下個月民主黨的勝利將威脅美國的未來。
Inside the cosseted bubble of a Trump rally, composed largely of MAGA superfans, that message was met with hearty concurrence from the president’s adoring fans.
川普集會是一個精心呵護的肥皂泡，主要由MAGA超級粉絲組成的群體對總統的這一信息報以熱烈贊同。
廣告
Tara Green, a front desk office administrator who attended the rally in Wasilla, Alaska, said seeing Mr. Trump was “an opportunity of a lifetime.”
參加了阿拉斯加瓦西拉集會的前台行政人員塔拉·格林表示，見到川普是「一生一次的機會」。
“Trump has a vision, and it might take time, but it is fixing things,” she said. “I have every belief and trust in him.”
「川普有一個願景，這可能需要時間，但他正在解決問題，」她說，「我對他充滿信念和信任。」
Still, there were signs of Mr. Trump’s eroding support, even inside his bubble. The president was interrupted by protesters at each of his rallies, once a rare occurrence. And swaths of the arenas, which Mr. Trump used to pack with ease, remained empty.
不過，即使在這個肥皂泡內部，也出現了川普支持率下滑的跡象。總統在每場集會上都遭到抗議者打斷，這曾經十分罕見。而過去能輕鬆擠滿的大片場地，如今卻空著。
At a check-in desk for special guests on Saturday outside Truist Arena in Highland Heights, Ky., one volunteer asked a staffer for Mr. Trump how many people they were expecting at the rally. The staffer, outfitted in a “Team Trump” jacket, said they had expected a larger audience than was on hand. (The entire upper level of the arena remained empty during the rally.) The White House did not respond to a request for comment.
週六，在肯塔基州高地海茨的儲億中心外，一名特別嘉賓簽到處志願者詢問川普的一名工作人員，預計會有多少人來參加集會。這位身穿「川普團隊」夾克的工作人員表示，他們原本預計的觀眾人數比實際到場的要多。（集會期間，場館的整個上層看台一直空著。）白宮未回應置評請求。
Mr. Trump’s closing message also was notably meandering, even for a man who boasts about “the weave,” his description of how he bounces from one topic to another without any discernible connection.
川普在最後階段發出的訊息也明顯變得漫無邊際，即使是對一個吹噓自己擅長「編織法」（即東拉西扯、跳躍話題）的人來說也是如此。
The president kicked off most rallies this week by praising the appearance of Republican male candidates. (He seemed particularly enthralled by the backside of Senator Jon Husted, Republican of Ohio, bringing it up at two different rallies this week.)
總統這一週在大多數集會上都讚揚了共和黨男性候選人的外表。（他似乎特別著迷於俄亥俄州共和黨參議員喬恩·赫斯特德的臀部，在兩場不同的集會上都提到了這一點。）
廣告
He recounted his own political successes and margins of victory in the states he was visiting, most of which are historically Republican strongholds and a sign of how much ground his party is defending this year.
他講述了自己的政治成就以及在到訪各州取得的勝利優勢。這些州大多是傳統的共和黨大本營，這也表明他的政黨今年需要防守的陣地有多大。
And he reminded voters of the basket of incentives he’s offering if they elect Republicans, expanding it from cash bonuses to pardons and investments from the Pentagon.
他還提醒選民，如果他們讓共和黨獲勝，他將提供一攬子激勵措施，並將這些措施從現金獎勵擴大到赦免和五角大廈的投資。
But even the president downplayed the impact of his signature midterm giveaway: a controversial promise to give $5,000 for every American adult — which he calls the “Trump dividend” — if Republicans retain control of Congress.
但就連總統本人也淡化了他標誌性期中選舉承諾的影響：他曾有爭議地承諾，如果共和黨保住國會控制權，將給每個美國成年人5000美元——他稱之為「川普紅利」。
Describing a hypothetical conversation about a couple looking to spend the money, “Let’s go to Palm Beach and join the president for lunch. Let’s have lunch at Mar-a-Lago, where for $5,000, you’ll get one hamburger and a small dessert,” he said, with a laugh. “I shouldn’t say that.”
他描述了一段假設的對話，關於一對夫婦想怎麼花這筆錢：「我們去棕櫚灘和總統共進午餐吧。去馬阿拉歌吃午餐，花5000美元，你能得到一個漢堡和一個小甜點，」他笑著說，「我不該這麼說。」
That line in particular underscored how Mr. Trump has repeatedly seemed to minimize voter anxieties about rising costs, one reason Republicans have tried distancing themselves from the president.
這句話尤其凸顯了川普如何一再淡化選民對生活成本上升的焦慮——這也是共和黨人試圖與總統保持距離的原因之一。
Even among rally-goers, there seemed more wariness than in earlier elections, and loyalty to Mr. Trump seemed more important than concerns about policy or the turbulence in his administration.
即使在集會參與者中，很多人似乎也比以往的選舉更加謹慎，而對川普的忠誠似乎比政策或政府動盪的擔憂更重要。
廣告
Ustun Yuksel, a Trump supporter who has a Ph.D in political science and lives in Dawsonville, Ga., acknowledged that he needs to work on addressing cost of living issues.
擁有政治學博士學位、居住在喬治亞州道森維爾的川普支持者烏史東·於克塞爾承認，他需要努力解決生活成本問題。
“They can definitely do better,” he said, while waiting for Mr. Trump in Dalton, Ga.
「他們絕對可以做得更好，」他在喬治亞州道爾頓等待川普時說。
But he said he likes Mr. Trump’s foreign policy. And he had no interest in discussing the latest headlines out of the White House.
但他表示自己喜歡川普的外交政策，並且對討論最新的白宮熱點新聞毫無興趣。
“I don’t want to talk about it,” Mr. Yuksel said just after the news broke that Katie Zacharia, the White House press secretary, was ousted. “I don’t want to say anything.”
「我不想談論它，」於克塞爾在白宮新聞秘書凱蒂·扎卡裡亞被解職的消息傳出後不久說道，「我什麼都不想說。」
A few hours later, as Amy Thomas, a bail bonds agent from Cleveland, Tenn., waited in traffic to exit the rally, she jumped out of her car to capture Mr. Trump depart on Marine One. She said Mr. Trump was “getting the job done” and demurred when asked about the turmoil in the administration.
幾個小時後，來自田納西州克利夫蘭的保釋擔保人艾米·托馬斯在等待離開集會時，跳下車拍攝川普乘坐「海軍陸戰隊一號」離開的畫面。她說川普「正在把事情搞定」，但在被問及政府的動盪時閃爍其詞。
“My friend follows it more than I do,” she said of the firings and latest drama.
「我的朋友比我更關注這些，」她在談到解僱和最新風波時說。

Tyler Pager是時報白宮記者，報導川普總統及其政府。
