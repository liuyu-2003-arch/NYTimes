中國第三季度經濟增速微升

China’s Export Boom Fuels Growth Despite Slump at Home

KEITH BRADSHER • 2026-10-19
Surging exports and the industrial production needed to sustain them kept China’s economy growing over the past three months, even as consumer spending stagnated and investment plunged.
過去三個月，激增的出口以及維持這些出口所需的工業生產支撐中國經濟保持增長，而消費支出停滯、投資大幅下滑。
China’s National Bureau of Statistics announced on Monday that gross domestic product grew 1.2 percent from the second quarter of this year. If that pace continues for a year, the Chinese economy will expand at an annual rate of about 4.9 percent.
中國國家統計局週一公布，國內生產總值較今年第二季度增長1.2%。若按此速度延續全年，中國經濟年化增長率約為4.9%。
The figures are an improvement from the 4.3 percent growth rate China reported from April through June. The economy was 4.5 percent larger in the third quarter than in the same quarter last year, suggesting that the country remains on track to meet its official target of 4.5 to 5 percent growth this year.
這一數據較中國公布的4月至6月4.3%的增速有所回升。第三季度經濟規模同比增長4.5%，顯示中國仍有望實現今年4.5%至5%的官方增長目標。
But many Western economists have become increasingly skeptical of China’s official statistics, particularly as Beijing has gradually stopped publishing thousands of narrowly focused data sets that had provided separate checks on broader economic figures. Independent estimates of recent growth have generally been only half to two-thirds as high as the official numbers.
但許多西方經濟學家對中國官方統計數據日益懷疑，尤其是因為北京已逐步停止發布數千個細分數據集，而這些數據此前為更廣泛的經濟指標提供獨立校驗。近期增長的獨立估算普遍僅為官方數字的一半至三分之二。

China’s export boom from July through September, which further widened its enormous trade surplus with the rest of the world, helped shield the economy from a much worse outcome. Exports in September rose at their fastest year-over-year pace since July 2010, excluding months distorted by the timing of Lunar New Year celebrations, which disrupt factory production for weeks.
中國7月至9月的出口繁榮進一步擴大了其與世界其他地區之間本就龐大的貿易順差，幫助經濟避免了更糟的結果。9月出口同比增長速度創2010年7月以來新高，不計因春節時間安排而數據偏差的月份——春節會使工廠生產中斷數週。
At home, the picture was far bleaker.
國內形勢則黯淡得多。
Retail sales were exceptionally weak throughout the third quarter, though September sales rebounded somewhat from a particularly bad August. A prolonged housing market crash, which has driven existing-home prices down roughly 40 percent since 2021, has wiped out much of the middle class’s savings and left millions of households reluctant to spend.
整個第三季度零售銷售異常疲弱，儘管9月銷售較形勢尤為嚴峻的8月有所反彈。房地產市場持續崩盤，自2021年以來成屋價格已下跌約40%，抹去了中產階級的大部分積蓄，也使數百萬家庭不願消費。
Sales of cars and other durable goods, including household appliances, have plummeted.
汽車及其他耐用品（包括家用電器）的銷售大幅下滑。
An extensive government program of trade-in subsidies, introduced in the summer of 2024 and continued through the end of 2025, encouraged households to make purchases they might otherwise have waited to buy. Many consumers have little reason to now replace their cars or appliances. But car sales have fallen below their pre-subsidy levels, as rising unemployment further undermined consumer confidence.
一項覆蓋面廣泛的政府以舊換新補貼計劃於2024年夏季推出，持續至2025年底，鼓勵家庭進行原本可能推遲的購買。如今許多消費者幾乎沒有理由更換汽車或家電。然而汽車銷量已跌破補貼前水平，失業率上升進一步削弱了消費者信心。
Investment has also slumped, particularly in real estate construction. Even infrastructure spending, long a mainstay of Chinese economic growth, has faltered. The collapse of many real estate developers has devastated local governments’ revenue from land leases, historically their largest source of funds for building roads, bridges, rail lines and other public works.
投資同樣下滑，尤其是在房地產建設領域。甚至長期作為中國經濟增長支柱的基礎設施支出也顯露疲態。大量房地產開發商的倒閉嚴重衝擊地方政府的土地出讓收入——這歷來是地方建設道路、橋樑、鐵路及其他公共工程的最大資金來源。
廣告
The result is an economy increasingly dependent on selling goods abroad, even as domestic demand deteriorates.
其結果是一種日益依賴向海外銷售商品的經濟，而國內需求卻在惡化。
“China basically must keep global exports markets open for this system to continue to operate,” said Logan Wright, a partner at Rhodium Group, a research firm.
「中國基本上必須保持全球出口市場開放，這一體系才能繼續運轉，」研究公司榮鼎集團合伙人洛根·賴特表示。
Mao Shengyong, the spokesman and deputy commissioner of the National Bureau of Statistics, said at a news conference in Beijing on Monday: “We should be aware that the international environment is complex and volatile, the issue of insufficient domestic demand remains prominent, and the foundation for steady economic growth still needs to be consolidated.”
國家統計局新聞發言人、副局長毛盛勇週一在北京的新聞發布會上表示：「也要看到，國際環境複雜多變，國內需求不足問題仍較突出，經濟穩中向好基礎尚需鞏固。」
The stark divide between China’s industrial strength and its struggling domestic economy is visible in cities like Nanjing, a former capital in central China, and Guangzhou, the commercial hub of southeastern China.
中國工業實力與國內經濟困境之間的鮮明反差在南京和廣州這樣的城市清晰可見。位於中國中部的南京是昔日的國都，而廣州則是東南部的商業中心。
Nanjing South Railway Station is a colossal monument to China’s world-leading high-speed rail system. Some of the country’s busiest north-south rail lines intersect there with major east-west lines that stretch inland from Shanghai along the Yangtze River.
南京南站是中國領先世界的高鐵系統的巨型標誌。該國一些最繁忙的南北鐵路線與從上海沿長江向內陸延伸的東西向幹線在此交匯。
But just a 10-minute walk away, a nearly deserted three-story shopping mall offers a strikingly different portrait of the economy. Its arches painted in golden and burnt sienna hues, the mall was almost devoid of shoppers last week. Many storefronts were shuttered. The few businesses still operating had only one employee, or sometimes no one at all, relying instead on China’s ubiquitous security cameras to deter theft.
但僅步行十分鐘，一座幾乎空無一人的三層購物中心便呈現出截然不同的經濟景象。有著金色和焦赭色拱門的商場上週幾乎看不到顧客。許多店面關門歇業。仍在營業的少數商鋪僅有一名員工，有時甚至無人值守，轉而依靠中國無處不在的監控攝像頭防範盜竊。
廣告
In one nearly empty store, cans of tile grout and other flooring supplies sat piled up, with no customers in sight. “Our sales have fallen by around 30 to 50 percent over the past two years,” said the store owner, who gave his family name, Zheng. “It’s the same across all industries, not just ours.”
在一家幾乎空蕩的店鋪裡，瓷磚填縫劑和其他地板材料成罐堆放著，看不到顧客。「這兩年我們的銷量下降了得有百分之三十到五十，」稱自己姓鄭的店主說。「所有行業都是這樣的，不只是我們。」
At a nearby wood paneling shop, sales have nearly halved over the past five years.
附近一家木板材店的銷售額過去五年幾乎減半。
“The real estate market hasn’t recovered, and that’s affecting all kinds of businesses; the Nanjing economy was built on real estate,” said the shop owner, who gave her family name, Liu.
「因為房地產起不來，各行各業都受影響，南京的經濟也是靠房地產起來的，」劉姓店主說。
While businesses tied to the housing market struggle, some of China’s strongest manufacturers are benefiting from the global rush to build data centers, including in the United States. Exports of everything from power management equipment to steel racks for computer servers have soared.
在房地產相關企業掙扎的同時，中國一些實力最強的製造商正受益於全球數據中心建設熱潮，包括在美國。從電源管理設備到計算機伺服器鋼架的各類產品出口大幅攀升。
Yet even as exports jumped 36 percent in September from a year earlier, Beijing remains wary of letting the country’s weak currency regain much of its previous value. The government has also been cautious about scaling back more than $300 billion a year in tax breaks for exporters. That support is essential for many lower-tech manufacturers, like the garment makers, which face tariffs in overseas markets and consumers unwilling to spend at home.
然而，儘管9月出口同比增長36%，北京仍不願讓疲軟的人民幣大幅收復此前失地。政府對於削減每年逾3000億美元的出口退稅同樣持謹慎態度。這一支持對許多低技術製造商至關重要，例如服裝製造商，它們既面臨海外市場關稅，又面對國內不願消費的消費者。
In Guangzhou, Tang Xiangfa, who has run a zipper shop for more than a decade, has watched his sales tumble.
在廣州，經營拉鏈店十多年的唐祥發目睹了銷售額的暴跌。

“If business gets so bad that we can’t keep going, then we’ll have no choice but to switch to another business,” he said. “If we can keep going, we’ll continue.”
「如果到時候生意實在太差乾不下去了，我們就只能換其他生意了，」他說。「撐得下去就還能繼續做。」
