不止打擊非法移民：今年年初美國合法移民數量銳減

Legal Immigration Fell Sharply Under Trump Policies Adopted Early This Year

ALBERT SUN • 2026-10-19
The Trump administration cut legal immigration in half early this year with policies blocking visas for immigrants from nearly half the globe.
今年年初，川普政府實施一系列政策，阻止了來自全球近半數國家的移民獲得簽證，從而將合法移民數量削減了一半。
The State Department issued just 26,000 immigrant visas, which grant a green card and legal permanent resident status, in February, compared with an average of 52,000 a month in the years before Trump took office, according to the most recent available government data.
根據最新可獲得的政府數據，國務院2月僅發放了2.6萬份移民簽證（持有人可獲得綠卡和合法永久居民身份）；在川普上任前的幾年裡，平均月簽發量為5.2萬份。
The policies, which took effect late last year and early this year, made it all but impossible to immigrate from 91 countries and reduced the number of immigrant visas available in another 61. Africa and the Middle East were hit particularly hard, and immigration from Muslim-majority countries plunged by more than 90 percent.
這些政策於去年底和今年初生效，令來自91個國家的人幾乎不可能移民，並減少了另外61個國家可獲得的移民簽證數量。非洲和中東受到的打擊尤其嚴重，來自穆斯林占多數國家的移民暴跌了90%以上。
President Trump has drawn attention for his crackdown on illegal immigration, but he has also been tough when it comes to limiting legal immigration. With the exception of the coronavirus pandemic, these cuts to legal immigration were the largest since the Hoover administration unilaterally denied visas to otherwise qualified legal immigrants during the Great Depression, according to David Bier, an immigration expert at the libertarian Cato Institute, which favors increased immigration.
川普總統因打擊非法移民而引人關注，但他在限制合法移民方面也同樣強硬。據主張擴大移民的自由主義智庫卡托研究所的移民專家戴維·比爾表示，除新冠疫情期間外，此次對合法移民的削減幅度是自大蕭條時期胡佛政府單方面拒絕向符合條件的合法移民發放簽證以來最大的。
In the first year after Mr. Trump returned to office, the number of visas declined only slightly, by about 10 percent. Then in January, an expanded travel ban blocked people from 39 countries from receiving visas or entering the country, and a pause on visa issuance barred immigrants from 52 more countries. The immigrant diversity visa, a special visa for people from countries with few immigrants, was suspended a month earlier.
川普重返白宮後的第一年，簽證數量僅小幅下降約10%。然後在1月，一項擴大後的旅行禁令阻止來自39個國家的人獲得簽證或進入美國，簽證發放暫停措施則使來自另外52個國家的移民無法獲得簽證。針對移民人數較少國家者的特殊簽證移民多樣性簽證在此前一個月被暫停。
廣告
The administration said the policies were put in place to improve the screening of visa applicants for security risks and for potential future use of public benefits.
政府表示，實施這些政策是為了改進對簽證申請人的安全風險篩查，並防範其未來可能濫用公共福利。
For thousands of hopeful immigrants, the policies threw up an often heartbreaking final roadblock at the end of what, in most cases, is a years-long process to follow every rule to immigrate legally.
對成千上萬滿懷希望的移民來說，在他們通常長達數年、期間始終恪守各項規定的合法移民歷程的終點，這些政策設置了一個往往令人心碎的最終障礙。
Daniel Brackins, a U.S. citizen living in Los Angeles, said his son was born in Kazakhstan because the new policies blocked his wife, Zhanna, from receiving a visa to enter the United States at the last step of the legal immigration process.
住在洛杉磯的美國公民丹尼爾·布拉金斯說，他的兒子在哈薩克出生，因為新政策阻止了他的妻子詹娜在合法移民程序的最後一步獲得進入美國的簽證。
“My wife and I have broken down many times, and it puts a lot of stress on the relationship,” he said, “especially watching my son grow up on video chat.”
「我和妻子多次崩潰，這給我們的關係帶來很大壓力，」他說，「尤其是只能通過影片通話看著兒子長大。」
For more than eight months, the State Department has issued almost no immigrant visas to people from the affected countries. Canada and Mexico, where visas were not restricted, were among the few countries that saw increases in new immigrant visas; together, they made up about 30 percent of all immigrant visas issued in February.
八個多月來，國務院幾乎未向受影響國家的人發放移民簽證。加拿大和墨西哥的簽證未受限制，是少數新移民簽證增加的國家；兩國合計佔2月所有移民簽證的約30%。
More recent data was not available, and the State Department did not answer questions about why it had published visa statistics less frequently under Mr. Trump.
目前無法獲得更近期的數據，國務院也未就川普執政期間為何減少簽證統計數據發布頻率的問題作出回應。
The new policies were imposed without congressional approval and have since been blocked in federal court. At the end of August, federal courts in two separate lawsuits ordered the government to begin issuing visas again. However, despite the court orders, a vast majority of the people whose visas had been blocked are still waiting.
新政策未經國會批准就實施，此後被聯邦法院叫停。8月底，兩起不同訴訟中的聯邦法院命令政府重新開始發放簽證。然而，儘管有法院命令，絕大多數簽證被擱置的人仍在等待。
廣告
The wait has stranded Wilian Nelson Hernandez Morales in Guatemala since January. He has been unable to return to Middlesex, N.J., where he had been raising four kids with his wife, Angela Duro.
等待使得威廉·納爾遜·埃爾南德斯·莫拉萊斯自一月起滯留在瓜地馬拉。他一直無法返回紐澤西州米德爾塞克斯，此前他一直與妻子安吉拉·杜羅在那裡撫養四個孩子。
Mr. Hernandez came to the United States illegally as a minor 26 years ago and decided to apply for a marriage-based green card after he wed Ms. Duro, a U.S. citizen, in 2021. After four years, they traveled to Guatemala for an interview and were told that their application would have been approved if not for the visa freeze. Mr. Hernandez is still waiting for a visa to return to the United States.
埃爾南德斯26年前作為未成年人非法來到美國，並在2021年與身為美國公民的杜羅結婚後決定申請婚姻綠卡。四年後，他們前往瓜地馬拉參加面談，被告知如果不是因為簽證凍結，他們的申請本應獲得批准。埃爾南德斯仍在等待返回美國的簽證。
Non-immigrant visas fell too
非移民簽證也下降了
Immigrant visas have been most affected, but visas for students and workers declined as well, falling by 30 percent compared with a year before. Visas for tourists and business visitors were down about 25 percent in February.
移民簽證受影響最大，但學生和工作簽證也下降了，較一年前下降30%。2月，旅遊和商務訪客簽證下降了約25%。
The travel ban fully prevented people from those 39 countries from obtaining student or work visas — and new rules and fees meant that fewer student and H-1B visas were issued to people from other countries, too. India and China, for example, were not subject to any bans, but still saw sharp declines in student and work visas.
旅行禁令完全阻止來自39個國家的人獲得學生或工作簽證——新規則和費用意味著向其他國家的人發放的學生和H-1B簽證也更少。例如，印度和中國未受任何禁令限制，但學生和工作簽證仍大幅下降。
Visas for seasonal agricultural workers and exchange workers remained at roughly the same levels as before, but visas for other seasonal workers in industries like hospitality and tourism declined.
季節性農業工人和交流項目工人的簽證大致保持在以前的水平，但酒店和旅遊等行業其他季節性工人的簽證數量有所下降。
Mr. Trump has also closed or narrowed other legal pathways to enter the country, sharply limiting the refugee program, blocking people at ports of entry from applying for asylum and pulling all manner of bureaucratic levers to push immigrants to leave.
川普還關閉或收窄了其他合法入境途徑，大幅限制難民計劃，阻止人們在入境口岸申請庇護，並動用各種行政手段推動移民離開。
廣告
Immigration experts say these changes, along with the administration’s mass deportation campaign, are part of an effort to limit immigration of all kinds. Together, they may accomplish the same goals as the sweeping anti-immigration laws passed by Congress in the early 20th century with executive action alone.
移民專家說，這些變化以及政府的大規模驅逐行動都是旨在限制各類移民的舉措的一部分。綜合來看，僅憑行政措施，這些舉措就可能實現與20世紀初國會通過的那些全面反移民法律相同的目標。

註：地圖顯示了美國國務院在2026年2月向來自各個地區和國家的人員簽發的移民簽證數量，並與2023年和2024年簽發的簽證月平均數量進行了比較。
