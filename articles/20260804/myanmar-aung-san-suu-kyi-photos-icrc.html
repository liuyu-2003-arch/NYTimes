翁山蘇姬在拘禁中罕見公開露面

Myanmar Junta Allows Aung San Suu Kyi Rare Contact With an Outsider

MAX KIM • 2026-08-04
Myanmar’s junta allowed Daw Aung San Suu Kyi, the detained, 81-year-old former leader of the country, to meet with a humanitarian organization on Monday, offering a rare public glimpse of her amid growing concerns about her health.
週一，緬甸軍政府允許現年81歲、目前被拘禁的前領導人翁山蘇姬與一個人道主義組織會面。在外界對她的健康狀況日益擔憂之際，此舉讓公眾得以難得一窺她的近況。
The government released photos of Ms. Aung San Suu Kyi, a Nobel laureate, meeting with Arnaud de Baecque, the local representative of the International Committee of the Red Cross, in the capital, Naypyidaw. In an email, Mr. de Baecque confirmed he had met her but declined to comment further.
政府發布了諾貝爾獎獲得者翁山蘇姬在首都內比都與紅十字國際委員會當地代表阿諾·德貝克會面的照片。德貝克通過電子郵件證實曾與她會面，但拒絕進一步置評。
Francisco Javier Pavón, a spokesman for the Red Cross in Myanmar, said in a statement that the visit had included the opportunity to speak with Ms. Aung San Suu Kyi in private as well as the opportunity for her to exchange news with her family.
紅十字會駐緬甸發言人弗朗西斯科·哈維爾·帕馮在聲明中表示，此次探視包括與翁山蘇姬私下交談，以及讓她與家人交流近況。
“As is customary in the I.C.R.C.’s detention work, observations and recommendations related to the visit are shared exclusively and confidentially with the detaining authorities,” he said.
他說：「按照紅十字國際委員會在拘禁探視工作中的慣例，與此次探視相關的觀察結果和建議僅以保密方式與拘禁當局分享。」
Since being arrested by the military in a coup that overthrew her democratically elected government in 2021, Ms. Aung San Suu Kyi has been in detention on a slew of charges widely seen by international observers as manufactured by her captors. While the junta has since reduced her original decades-long prison sentence, leaving her now with around 13 years left to serve, she has been seldom seen in public and has been denied contact even with her lawyers, leading to increasingly troubled speculation about her health.
2021年，緬甸軍方發動政變，推翻了翁山蘇姬的民選政府並將其逮捕。自那以後，她一直因一系列指控被拘禁，國際觀察人士普遍認為這些指控是由抓捕她的人捏造的。雖然軍政府後來將她原本長達數十年的刑期做了減刑——目前她尚需服刑約13年，但她極少在公開場合露面，甚至被禁止與律師接觸，這導致外界對其健康狀況的擔憂日益加劇。
廣告
In recent months, Kim Aris, Ms. Aung San Suu Kyi’s younger son, has been demanding proof of life for his mother, who he has said has heart problems that require medical attention.
近幾個月來，翁山蘇姬的小兒子金·阿里斯一直要求軍方提供母親尚在人世的證明。他表示母親患有心臟病，需要醫療護理。
Following the meeting with the Red Cross, several photos of which were published by the Myanmar government — including images of Ms. Aung San Suu Kyi smiling and shaking Mr. de Baecque’s hand — Mr. Aris said he remained cautious.
在與紅十字會的這次會面之後，緬甸政府公布了幾張現場照片——包括翁山蘇姬面帶微笑與德貝克握手的畫面——但阿里斯表示，他依然持謹慎態度。
“I am yet to receive independent confirmation from the I.C.R.C. of the meeting, or of my mother’s condition or well-being,” he told The Times in an email on Monday. “ I sincerely hope this marks the beginning of greater humanitarian access, including direct contact with my mother, access for our family, and regular, independently verified information about her well-being.”
「我尚未收到紅十字國際委員會關於此次會面的獨立確認，也沒有收到關於我母親身體狀況或安危的確認，」他週一在一封發給《紐約時報》的電子郵件中說。「我真誠地希望，這標誌著更多人道主義接觸的開始，包括與我母親的直接聯繫、我們家屬的探視權，以及關於她健康狀況的定期、經獨立核實的信息。」
Neither Mr. de Baecque nor Mr. Pavón of the Red Cross commented on Ms. Aung San Suu Kyi’s health, citing the terms of confidentiality underpinning such visits.
紅十字會的德貝克和帕馮都沒有對翁山蘇姬的健康狀況發表評論，理由是此類探視必須遵循保密原則。
“That confidentiality is what allows to obtain access in the first place,” Mr. Pavón said.
帕馮說：「正是這種保密性才使我們最初能夠獲得探視機會。」
The long-awaited proof of life for Ms. Aung San Suu Kyi comes months after a parliamentary election that was widely seen as a sham and elevated Min Aung Hlaing, the leader of the coup, to president in April. Since then, his government has portrayed itself as a peaceful civilian administration even as it has intensified bombings of unarmed populations.
翁山蘇姬這份期待已久的生存證明是在一場被廣泛視為騙局的議會選舉數月之後。那場選舉在4月將政變領導人敏昂萊推上了總統寶座。從那以後，儘管軍方對手無寸鐵的平民加劇轟炸，但敏昂萊政府仍將自己塑造成和平的文職政府。
廣告
Earlier this year, state media reported that Ms. Aung San Suu Kyi had been moved from prison to house arrest, a move that some analysts interpreted as an attempt by the military-backed government to sanitize its image by appearing benevolent to the leader it had deposed.
今年早些時候，官方媒體報導稱，翁山蘇姬已被從監獄囚禁轉移為軟禁。一些分析人士將此舉解讀為有軍方背景的政府試圖對被其推翻的領導人展現仁慈，從而美化自身的形象。
But Hunter Marston, the director of the Southeast Asia Program at the Lowy Institute, a research institute based in Sydney, said he believed that the military’s recent showing of Ms. Aung San Suu Kyi signaled something very different — growing boldness among its leaders.
但總部位於雪梨的研究機構洛伊研究所的東南亞項目主任杭特·馬斯頓表示，他認為軍方最近讓翁山蘇姬露面是在發出一個截然不同的信號——其領導人越來越有恃無恐。
“It’s a sign that Min Aung Hlaing and his government feel more confident now, and can dangle this proof of life as a way to show what they’re capable of,” he said.
他說：「這表明敏昂萊及其政府現在更加自信，他們拋出這份生存證明，以此來展示他們的掌控力。」
Even though it faced steady international pressure to release Ms. Aung San Suu Kyi, he said, the military-backed government was likely to be wary of doing so.
他表示，儘管國際社會不斷施壓要求釋放翁山蘇姬，但這個由軍方支持的政府很可能對此持謹慎態度。
Ms. Aung San Suu Kyi spent about 15 years under house arrest during a previous period of military rule, and she was awarded a Nobel Peace Prize in 1991. From 2016 to 2021, she led an elected government in a rare power-sharing agreement with the military — which has had a firm hand on Myanmar’s politics since the country’s independence from the British Empire in 1948. While Ms. Aung San Suu Kyi’s international reputation was tarnished during that period, amid criticism that she downplayed the military’s violent campaign against Myanmar’s Rohingya Muslim minority, she remains the country’s most beloved and influential political figure.
在此前的一段軍政府統治時期，翁山蘇姬曾被軟禁約15年，並於1991年獲諾貝爾和平獎。從2016年到2021年，她領導了一屆民選政府，與軍方達成了罕見的權力分享協議——自緬甸於1948年脫離大英帝國獨立以來，軍方一直牢牢把控著該國的政治。儘管在此期間，由於被批評對軍方針對緬甸羅辛亞穆斯林少數民族的暴力運動輕描淡寫，翁山蘇姬的國際聲譽有所受損，但她依然是該國最受愛戴、最具影響力的政治人物。
“They could conceivably release her now that they’ve had elections and there’s no meaningful opposition party in the country,” Mr. Marston said. “But I don’t think they’re going to do so because they are still afraid of Aung San Suu Kyi inspiring grass-roots opposition.”
馬斯頓說：「既然他們已經舉行了選舉，而且該國已經沒有有實質意義的反對黨，他們理論上是可以現在就釋放她的。但我認為他們不會這樣做，因為他們仍然害怕翁山蘇姬會激發基層民眾的反抗。」
廣告
Mr. Aris, her son, said that his ultimate goal remained unchanged — to see his mother free, along with the thousands of other political prisoners detained under the military-backed government.
翁山蘇姬的兒子阿里斯表示，他的最終目標始終未變——希望母親能夠獲釋，同時數以千計在軍方背景政府統治下被拘禁的其他政治犯能夠重獲自由。
The officials of that government, he added, had gotten his mother’s birthday wrong on the pink-frosted cake in one of the photos they had published. Underneath the words “Happy Birthday Aunty Suu,” rendered in a dark icing, appeared to be the date June 29.
他還說，在政府官員公布的一張照片中，他們甚至在粉色糖霜蛋糕上寫錯了他母親的生日。在用深色糖霜寫成的「素阿姨生日快樂」字樣下方，出現的日期似乎是6月29日。
His mother’s birthday, Mr. Aris said, was June 19.
阿里斯說，母親的生日是6月19日。
