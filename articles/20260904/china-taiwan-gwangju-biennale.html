「台灣館」引發政治風波：中國抵制韓國光州雙年展

China Boycotts a Top Art Festival Over Taiwan Exhibition

JIN YU YOUNG, MURPHY ZHAO • 2026-09-04
The Gwangju Biennale in South Korea, one of the most prominent art exhibitions in Asia, was engulfed by politics on Thursday as China announced a boycott of the event over Beijing’s claim that Taiwan is Chinese territory.
由於北京對台灣的領土主張，中國於週四宣布抵制韓國光州雙年展，從而使這個亞洲最負盛名的藝術展之一捲入政治風波。
The controversy erupted from the biennale’s Pavilion Project, which is separate from the main exhibition and is being curated by international artists and galleries. The entry from Taiwan, a self-governing island democracy that China considers its territory, was slated to be shown under the title “Taiwan Pavilion.”
此次爭議源於獨立於主展區且由國際藝術家及畫廊策劃的雙年展展館項目。來自台灣的參展作品原定以「台灣館」的名稱展出，台灣是一個實行自治的島嶼民主政體，中國視其為領土的一部分。
“Because the biennale organizers used an incorrect designation for the participation of China’s Taiwan region, allowing political factors to interfere with art, the Chinese curatorial team finds this unacceptable and has unanimously decided to withdraw from the exhibition in strong protest,” Ruan Yuelai, a curator for the “China Pavilion,” said at a news conference on Thursday.
「由於雙年展主辦方對中國台灣地區的參與使用了不正確的稱謂，允許政治因素干擾藝術，中國策展團隊認為這是不可接受的，並已一致決定退出展覽以示強烈抗議，」中國館策展人阮悅來在週四的新聞發布會上表示。
The episode threatened to grow into a diplomatic rift. The Chinese Embassy in Seoul criticized the move as “directly damaging the political foundation of China-South Korea relations.”
這一風波面臨升級為外交摩擦的風險。中國駐韓國大使館批評此舉「直接損害中韓關係的政治基礎」。
China is South Korea’s largest trading partner and has close ties with North Korea, which has been increasingly belligerent toward the South. Since taking office last year, the South Korean president, Lee Jae Myung, has been trying to improve ties with Beijing in an effort to reverse the hard-line stance of his predecessor.
中國是韓國最大的貿易夥伴，並與對韓日益強硬的朝鮮關係密切。自去年就職以來，韓國總統李在明一直試圖改善與北京的關係，以此扭轉其前任的強硬立場。
South Korea, like the United States, follows a “one China” policy. As part of this, it acknowledges China’s belief that Taiwan is part of China but maintains informal ties with Taiwan’s government. On Thursday, a spokesman for South Korea’s Foreign Ministry said that its policy toward Taiwan had not changed and noted that the biennale dispute had its roots in the private sector.
與美國一樣，韓國奉行「一個中國」政策。在此框架下，韓國承認中國關於台灣是中國一部分的立場，但仍與台灣政府維持非正式關係。週四，韓國外交部發言人表示，韓國的對台政策未發生改變，並指出雙年展爭議起於私營部門。
The Gwangju Biennale started in 1994 and attracts hundreds of thousands of visitors each year. This year’s edition, the 16th, runs from Saturday through mid-November and is titled “You Must Change Your Life.”
創辦於1994年的光州雙年展每年吸引數十萬名參觀者。今年的第16屆展覽於週六拉開帷幕並將持續至11月中旬，主題為「你必須改變你的生活」。
廣告
The city of Gwangju was the site of an uprising in 1980 and an ensuing massacre, ordered by the country’s military dictator. These events were pivotal to South Korea’s transformation into a democracy. The biennale was established with the goals of healing the wounds of the tragedy and portraying artwork with themes of democracy, human rights and peace.
光州市是韓國軍事獨裁者下令鎮壓的1980年起義及隨後屠殺事件的發生地。這些歷史事件對韓國向民主轉型起到了關鍵作用。光州雙年展創立的初衷便是撫平這場悲劇帶來的創傷，並展示以民主、人權與和平為主題的藝術作品。
One of the first public signs of discord came in June, when the biennale’s organizers, seemingly under pressure from Chinese participants, changed the name of the “Taiwan Pavilion” to “NTMoFA Pavilion,” a reference to the National Taiwan Museum of Fine Arts.
最早的不和信號在6月便已公開顯現，當時雙年展主辦方似乎在中國參展者的壓力下，將「台灣館」更名為台灣國立美術館的縮寫「NTMoFA館」。
But they reversed themselves last month after criticism from dozens of artists participating in the biennale and from the Taiwanese culture ministry.
但在遭到參與雙年展的數十名藝術家以及台灣文化部批評後，主辦方於上個月撤銷了這一決定。
On Thursday, the participants of the “China Pavilion” took down their works, according to a spokeswoman for the biennale. Several Chinese artists and curators did not respond immediately to a request for comment.
據雙年展發言人透露，週四，「中國館」的參展者撤下了他們的作品。幾位中國藝術家和策展人未立即回應置評請求。
A day earlier, the Gwangju Biennale Foundation issued a statement in which it apologized for causing concern and confusion and took “full responsibility for the escalation of controversy.”
就在前一天，光州雙年展基金會發表聲明，為引發外界擔憂與混亂致歉，並表示對「爭議升級承擔全部責任」。
It added that it hoped that the biennale would “fulfill the Pavilion Project’s original purpose: fostering meaningful artistic exchange and dialogue across borders.”
該基金會補充道，希望雙年展能夠「實現展館項目的初衷：促進跨越國界的有意義的藝術交流與對話」。

Jin Yu Young自首爾報導韓國、亞太和全球突發新聞。
