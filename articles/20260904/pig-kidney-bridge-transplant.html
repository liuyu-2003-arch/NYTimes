世界首例：患者靠移植豬腎生活九個月

In a First, a Pig Kidney Kept a Patient Alive Until He Got a Human One

RONI CARYN RABIN • 2026-09-04
Two years ago, Tim Andrews was so worn out that all he wanted to do was sleep. Diabetes and high blood pressure had destroyed his kidneys, and the frequent dialysis treatments that kept him alive by filtering his blood were grueling. Though only 66, he had no energy or appetite, was too weak to walk and had experienced two heart attacks.
兩年前，蒂姆·安德魯斯疲憊不堪，唯一想做的就是睡覺。糖尿病和高血壓毀掉了他的腎臟，而通過過濾血液維持他生命的頻繁透析治療令人精疲力竭。儘管只有66歲，他卻毫無精力與食慾，虛弱得無法行走，還曾兩次心臟病發作。
“I was dying,” Mr. Andrews said. A kidney transplant could have saved his life, but the organs are in short supply. Roughly 90,000 people are on the waiting list for a kidney transplant, and on average, 11 people die every day while waiting for one, according to United Network for Organ Sharing. And his rare blood type meant that Mr. Andrews’ odds of getting a matching human kidney were especially slim.
「我當時快死了，」安德魯斯說。腎移植本可以救他的命，但器官供應短缺。根據美國器官共享聯合網路的數據，約有9萬人在等待腎移植，平均每天有11人在等待中去世。而且，由於安德魯斯的血型較為罕見，他獲得匹配的人類腎臟的機率尤其渺茫。
So when he heard that doctors at Mass General Brigham were transplanting patients with kidneys from genetically modified pigs, he reached out.
因此，當他聽說麻省總醫院布裡格姆的醫生正在為患者移植轉基因豬的腎臟時，他主動聯繫了他們。
“I figured, if I’m going to die, I might as well do something for humanity and be part of this experiment,” he said.
「我想，如果我要死了，不如為人類做點事，參與這個實驗，」他說。
廣告
Mr. Andrews received a genetically modified pig kidney on Jan. 25, 2025, and lived with it for nine months. This year, he became the first known patient to receive a human donor kidney after a pig kidney transplant, a milestone described in a paper published Thursday in The Lancet.
2025年1月25日，安德魯斯接受了轉基因豬腎的移植，帶著它生活了九個月。今年，他成為已知首例在豬腎移植後接受人類供體腎臟的患者，週四發表於《柳葉刀》的一篇論文中描述了這一里程碑式的成就。
His case provides early proof of an intriguing concept in transplant medicine: that pig kidneys, while not yet a permanent solution, could offer “bridges” to transplants with human organs. The kidneys come from pigs that have undergone numerous genetic edits, including some designed to prevent a severe organ rejection response and others to inactivate viruses in the pig genome.
他的病例為移植醫學中一個引人關注的概念提供了初步證據：豬腎雖尚非永久性解決方案，但可以作為通往人類器官移植的「橋樑」。這些豬腎來自經過大量基因編輯的豬，其中一些編輯旨在防止嚴重的器官排斥反應，還有一些用於滅活豬基因組中的病毒。
By using the pig kidneys as a stopgap, doctors hope to keep patients alive while they make their way up the waiting list for a human kidney, and to provide a respite from dialysis, which can be debilitating.
醫生們希望患者在等待人類腎臟移植期間將豬腎作為過渡性方案維持生命，並讓患者免受可能使人衰弱的透析的折磨。
“Dialysis causes so much wear and tear on our patients that they become too sick to get a transplant, or die while waiting,” said Dr. Leonardo V. Riella, the paper’s lead author and associate director of the Legorreta Center for Clinical Transplant Tolerance at Mass General Brigham.
「透析對我們的患者造成極大損耗，以至於他們病重到無法接受移植，或在等待中死亡，」論文第一作者、麻省總醫院布裡格姆萊戈雷塔臨床移植耐受中心副主任李奧納多·列拉博士說。
The idea that pig kidneys could be used this way wasn’t a given. When researchers first considered the idea, there was a concern that patients who had received a pig kidney might become more prone to rejecting a human kidney later.
豬腎可以這樣使用的想法並非理所當然。當研究人員最初考慮這一想法時，有人擔心，接受過豬腎的患者日後可能更容易排斥人類腎臟。
But Mr. Andrews’s experience, along with that of one other patient, suggests that “it doesn’t seem to change how you react to human tissue at all,” said Mike Curtis, president and chief executive at eGenesis, the biotechnology company that produces the genetically modified pigs used at Mass General Brigham and helped fund the study.
但安德魯斯先生以及另一位患者的經歷表明，「它似乎根本不會改變你對人體組織的反應，」eGenesis總裁兼首席執行官邁克·柯蒂斯說。eGenesis是一家生物技術公司，生產麻省總醫院布裡格姆所使用的轉基因豬，並為該研究提供了部分資助。
廣告
“Right now, we don’t think there’s any downside of having a pig kidney followed by a human kidney, but when we started this, we had no idea that that would be true,” Dr. Curtis said.
「目前，我們不認為先移植豬腎再移植人腎有任何弊端，但在我們開始這項工作時，我們並不知道這一點會成立，」柯蒂斯說。
Altogether, five patients at Mass General Brigham have received pig kidneys produced by eGenesis under a Food and Drug Administration program allowing expanded access for patients who have no other treatment options. Three, including Mr. Andrews, lived with the organs for more than eight months, the company said Thursday. (One patient died of an unexpected cardiac event shortly after transplantation, and one patient was transplanted only in June.)
在麻省總醫院布裡格姆，共有五名患者根據美國食品藥品監督管理局一項允許無其他治療選擇患者擴大使用的計劃，接受了eGenesis生產的豬腎。該公司週四表示，包括安德魯斯在內的三名患者帶著這些器官存活了八個多月。（一名患者在移植後不久因意外心臟事件死亡，另一名患者6月才接受移植。）
The nine months Mr. Andrews spent with the animal organ were critical to getting his health back on track, his doctors said. The pig’s kidney made urine and cleared waste from his body 24 hours a day, seven days a week, doing what dialysis does by machine only every other day, during hourslong sessions. His diet improved, and he regained muscle mass.
安德魯斯的醫生們說，帶著動物器官度過的九個月對於他的健康重回正軌至關重要。這顆豬腎每天24小時、每週七天不間斷地生成尿液並清除體內廢物，而透析只能通過機器，每隔一天進行數小時的治療。他的飲食改善了，肌肉量也恢復了。
“It gave me life,” Mr. Andrews said.
「它給了我生命，」安德魯斯說。
Doctors who specialize in kidney disease are often struck by how miserable patients say they are on dialysis. “The message from patients — and it was very shocking for us — is that life on dialysis is no life,” Dr. Riella said. “So they’re all looking for that alternative, which is why so many patients have volunteered and stepped forward, even with all the unknowns of research.”
專門研究腎臟疾病的醫生常常對患者口中透析生活的痛苦感到震驚。「患者傳遞的信息對我們來說非常令人震驚，他們說透析生活根本算不上生活，」列拉說。「所以他們都在尋找替代方案，正因如此這麼多患者自願站出來參與，即使研究充滿未知。」
The new research is the latest advance in the field of cross-species transplantation, or xenotransplantation, which has progressed at a remarkable pace in recent years, with advances in genetic engineering and cloning and the development of new anti-rejection drugs. The F.D.A. has allowed the experimental procedures under strict oversight.
這項新研究是跨物種移植（即異種移植）領域的最新進展。近年來，隨著基因工程和複製技術的進步，以及新型抗排斥藥物的開發，該領域以驚人的速度發展。美國食品藥品監督管理局已允許在嚴格監管下開展這些實驗性手術。
廣告
Dr. Muhammad Mohiuddin, a professor of surgery at the University of Maryland School of Medicine and a pioneer in xenotransplantation, said even he was impressed by the speed of development and the support among patients and the public generally.
馬里蘭大學醫學院外科學教授、異種移植先驅穆罕默德·莫希烏丁博士說，就連他本人也對這一領域的發展速度以及患者和公眾的廣泛支持印象深刻。
Dr. Mohiuddin spent decades putting pig hearts into baboons before being involved in the first transplant of a genetically modified pig’s heart into a human patient in 2022. “I never imagined in my lifetime that I would see the human translation of this,” he said, adding, “The initial progress with nonhuman primates in the lab was slow.”
莫希烏丁花了數十年時間，將豬心移植到狒狒體內，之後於2022年參與了首例將轉基因豬心移植到人類患者體內的手術。「我從未想過，有生之年能看到這項技術應用於人類，」他說，他還說，「最初在實驗室中對非人靈長類動物的研究進展很慢。」
The first heart recipients had very advanced disease and died shortly after receiving the pig hearts. But just a few years later, in March 2024, surgeons at Mass General in Boston transplanted the first kidney from a gene-edited pig into a human patient, Rick Slayman. Mr. Slayman, 62, was discharged from the hospital two weeks later, but he had a chronic heart condition and died within two months of an apparently unrelated cardiac event.
第一批心臟接受者病情已非常晚期，在接受豬心後不久便去世了。但僅僅幾年後，2024年3月，波士頓麻省總醫院的外科醫生將首顆基因編輯豬的腎臟移植到了人類患者裡克·斯萊曼體內。62歲的斯萊曼兩周後出院，但他患有慢性心臟病，在兩個月內死於一次顯然無關的心臟事件。
More work is ongoing. EGenesis is starting a formal clinical trial next year with 33 patients. Another biotechnology company that produces genetically modified pigs, United Therapeutics Corporation, began a clinical trial of its kidneys in November 2025 and will soon start a second trial, it said in a statement this week. It is also starting a very small clinical trial of pig heart transplants to humans, the statement said.
更多工作正在推進中。eGenesis明年將啟動一項有33名患者參與的正式臨床試驗。另一家生產轉基因豬的生物技術公司聯合治療公司本週在聲明中表示，該公司已於2025年11月啟動豬腎臟的臨床試驗，並將很快啟動第二項試驗。聲明還稱，該公司正在啟動一項規模很小的豬心移植到人體的臨床試驗。
Dr. Curtis, the eGenesis executive, said that while “bridge” kidneys would prove valuable, the ultimate goal was to develop pig kidneys that could last as long as human transplants.
eGenesis高管柯蒂斯表示，雖然「過渡性」腎臟將被證明很有價值，但最終目標是開發出能夠像人類移植腎一樣持久的豬腎。
“Maybe the first more reachable goal is for this treatment to be a bridge, and maybe 10 or 15 years from now, we may be talking about an alternative as good as human kidneys,” Dr. Riella said. “We’re at the beginning of the journey.”
「也許第一個更可實現的目標是讓這種治療成為一種過渡，或許10年或15年後，我們可能會討論一種與人類腎臟一樣好的替代方案，」列拉說。「我們正處於這段旅程的起點。」
廣告
Mr. Andrews had to be hospitalized several times while he had the pig kidney, and ultimately it had to be removed after blood clots formed in the organ’s vessels and damaged it. He resumed dialysis briefly, and in January he received a human kidney from a matching donor.
安德魯斯在帶著豬腎期間曾多次住院，最終因器官血管中形成血栓並造成損傷而不得不將其摘除。他短暫恢復了透析，並於1月從一位匹配的供體那裡接受了人類腎臟。
Although he has had some health problems since, he said he was fitter than he had been in years and was back to living much of the year in a cabin by a pond. He swims and kayaks in the pond and takes short bike rides, and his appetite — dampened during dialysis — is back.
儘管此後他仍有一些健康問題，但他說自己比多年來都要健康，並已恢復到一年中大部分時間住在池塘邊小屋的生活。他在池塘裡游泳、劃皮划艇，還會騎單車短途兜風，在透析期間減退的食慾也恢復了。
“I went from thinking I had months, maybe a year, because I just felt so bad,” he said of his time on dialysis. But his transplants have changed the way he looks at life, he said. “When you say ‘stop and smell the roses,’ that’s my life now. I look at everything and go ‘wow.’”
「當時我感覺非常糟糕，以為自己只剩幾個月，或者一年時間，」他在談到透析時期時說。但他說，移植手術改變了他看待生活的方式。「當你說『停下腳步，聞聞玫瑰花香』，那就是我現在的生活。我看著周圍的一切，都會感嘆『哇』。」

Roni Caryn Rabin是《紐約時報》健康記者，主要關注婦幼健康、醫療保健中的種族和經濟差異以及金錢對醫療的影響。
