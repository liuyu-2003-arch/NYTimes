大眾汽車計劃額外裁員5萬人

Volkswagen Plans to Cut 50,000 Jobs Amid Dire Need to Reduce Costs

JAMES PATON • 2026-09-04
Volkswagen said on Thursday that it would go forward with the most sweeping restructuring in its 89-year history, cutting about 50,000 additional jobs in the face of surging Chinese competition, high energy prices and a costly transition to electric vehicles.
大眾汽車週四表示將推進89年歷史上最徹底的重組計劃。面對日益激烈的中國競爭、高昂的能源價格，以及向電動汽車轉型的高昂成本，該公司將額外裁減約5萬個崗位。
“This is a strong signal for the future of the Volkswagen Group,” Oliver Blume, the German automotive giant’s chief executive, said in a statement.
「這對大眾汽車集團的未來是一個強烈信號，」這家德國汽車巨頭的首席執行官奧博穆(Oliver Blume)在聲明中表示。
The decision, announced after a meeting of Volkswagen’s supervisory board, followed a tense standoff with unions representing its work force, which numbers more than 650,000 worldwide. But labor leaders backed the plan on Thursday.
這一決定是在大眾汽車監事會會議後宣布的，此前該公司與代表其全球超過65萬名員工的工會之間曾發生緊張對峙。但工會領導人週四對該計劃表示支持。
“In this crisis situation, we fought hard for good solutions,” Christiane Benner, an official with the IG Metall union, said in the statement. “The executive board now has the foundation to tackle the major tasks ahead.”
 「在這種危機形勢下，我們為爭取好的解決方案進行了艱苦鬥爭，」德國金屬工業工會官員克里斯蒂安·本納在聲明中說。「執行董事會現在有了應對未來重大任務的基礎。」
廣告
Since the end of 2024, Volkswagen had already agreed to reduce its work force by about 50,000 by the end of the decade. Agreements have been reached with 37,000 employees so far, the company said. The plan on Thursday would bring the total cuts to about 100,000.
自2024年底以來，大眾汽車已經同意到2030年結束前裁員約5萬人。該公司表示，目前已與3.7萬名員工達成協議。週四的計劃將使裁員總數達到約10萬人。
Volkswagen, one of the world’s largest carmakers, is an icon of European industry and German manufacturing. It is now also an emblem of their challenges as China has risen as the world’s leading maker of autos and electric vehicles. The company’s business in China powered it for decades, but its Chinese sales have plummeted. All the while, a flood of cheap Chinese cars has hollowed out German factories.
大眾汽車是全球最大的汽車製造商之一，是歐洲工業和德國製造業的一個標誌。如今，隨著中國崛起成為世界領先的汽車和電動汽車製造國，大眾汽車也成為歐洲工業和德國製造業所面臨挑戰的縮影。數十年來，該公司在中國的業務一直是其發展的重要動力，但如今在中國市場銷量已大幅下滑。與此同時，大量廉價中國汽車湧入，掏空了德國工廠。
The fate of four factories in Germany that have been targeted for possible closure remains unclear. But the company said it had far more manufacturing capacity than it needed. It added that “alternative uses for these plants are being assessed.”
德國境內四家可能面臨關閉的工廠命運仍不明朗。但該公司表示，其製造產能遠超需求。它還說，「正在評估這些工廠的替代用途。」
The “reality” is that those four plants — in Emden, Hanover, Zwickau and Neckarsulm — don’t have a clear future beyond 2030, Mr. Blume said last month. “We cannot carry that disadvantage indefinitely,” he said.
奧博穆上個月表示，「現實」是，位於埃姆登、漢諾威、茨維考和內卡蘇爾姆的這四家工廠在2030年之後的前景尚不明確。「我們無法無限期地承受這種劣勢，」他說。
There could be ways to avoid shutting factories, potentially by converting them for use by the defense industry as Germany ramps up spending to deter Russia’s military threat. At its Osnabrück site, for instance, Volkswagen is in talks with defense companies, Mr. Blume said last month.
或許存在避免關閉工廠的途徑，例如隨著德國加大軍費開支以遏制俄羅斯的軍事威脅，可以將這些工廠改造，供國防工業使用。奧博穆上個月表示，大眾汽車正在與國防企業就奧斯納布呂克工廠相關事宜進行洽談。
Mr. Blume has said Volkswagen needs to rein in costs that are still about 30 percent higher than those of comparable companies.
奧博穆曾表示，大眾汽車需要控制成本，其成本仍比同類公司高出約30%。
“It’s blindingly obvious that they desperately need to cut costs,” said Matthias Schmidt, a European auto industry analyst in Germany.
「顯而易見，他們迫切需要削減成本，」德國歐洲汽車行業分析師馬蒂亞斯·施密特說。
Cheap imports from China are fueling calls to take tougher measures against Beijing to counter what businesses see as unfair competition. Spurred by government subsidies, Chinese carmakers began focusing on electric vehicles years ago, investments that have allowed them to capitalize on rising demand for such models. About one in five new vehicles sold in Europe is electric.
來自中國的廉價進口車正引發呼聲，要求對北京採取更嚴厲的措施，以反制企業眼中這種不公平的競爭。在政府補貼的推動下，中國汽車製造商多年前就開始專注於電動汽車，這些投資使它們得以利用對此類車型不斷增長的需求。在歐洲銷售的新車中，約五分之一是電動汽車。
The plan is more like a cease-fire than a peace treaty between worker representatives and Volkswagen management, said Ferdinand Dudenhöffer, director of the Center Automotive Research in Bochum, Germany.
德國波鴻汽車研究中心主任費迪南德·杜登霍夫表示，該計劃更像是工人代表與大眾汽車管理層之間的停火協議，而不是和平條約。
廣告
“The plan formulates many goals, but fewer concrete steps,” Mr. Dudenhöffer said.
 「該計劃制定了許多目標，但具體措施較少，」杜登霍夫說。
Still, it is a break from Volkswagen’s recent feud with employees. Workers at Volkswagen, as is the case for the German carmakers Mercedes-Benz and BMW, hold half the 20 seats on the supervisory board. Shareholders also have 10 seats. At Volkswagen, however, there are additional complications. Two shareholder seats are held by the State of Lower Saxony, which owns 20 percent of the company’s voting stock. And the state, home to Volkswagen’s headquarters in Wolfsburg, almost always takes the side of workers.
儘管如此，這仍是大眾汽車近期與員工紛爭的緩和。與德國汽車製造商梅賽德斯-奔馳和寶馬一樣，大眾汽車的工人佔據了監事會20個席位中的一半。股東也擁有10個席位。然而，在大眾汽車，情況更為複雜。其中兩個股東席位由下薩克森州持有，該州擁有公司20%的有表決權股份。而作為大眾汽車總部沃爾夫斯堡的所在地，下薩克森州幾乎總是站在工人一邊。
On Thursday, Olaf Lies, minister-president of Lower Saxony, endorsed the turnaround plan.
週四，下薩克森州州長奧拉夫·利斯對該轉型計劃表示支持。
“The challenges facing Volkswagen and the German automotive industry are enormous,” he said. The deal, he said, signals that Volkswagen is willing to make investments and find “long-term prospects” for factories.
 「大眾汽車和德國汽車工業面臨的挑戰是巨大的，」他說。他表示，該協議表明大眾汽車願意進行投資，並為工廠尋找「長期前景」。
For the company and industrial manufacturing in Germany more widely, the crisis is “existential,” said Harald Hendrikse, a managing director at Citi Research. He estimates that Volkswagen’s cost to make a car across Europe far exceeds the costs at the best sites in China.
花旗研究董事總經理哈拉爾德·亨德里克塞表示，對該公司乃至更廣泛的德國工業製造業而言，這場危機是「生死攸關的」。他估計，大眾汽車在歐洲生產一輛汽車的成本遠超中國最佳工廠的成本。
The company aims to produce nine million cars a year, compared with a goal of 12 million before the Covid-19 pandemic and 10 million more recently. In July, Volkswagen said it would cut the number of models it offered by as much as half in a bid to reduce costs and take on Chinese competition.
大眾公司的目標是年產900萬輛汽車，而疫情前的目標是1200萬輛，最近的目標是1000萬輛。7月，大眾汽車表示將把提供的車型數量削減多達一半，以降低成本，並應對來自中國的競爭。
廣告
Volkswagen, Mr. Schmidt said, can’t afford to wait.
施密特說，大眾汽車等不起。
“It’s almost getting to the point of whether VW can survive in their current form,” he said. “It’s now or never.”
 「現在幾乎到了大眾汽車能否以目前的形式生存下去的時刻，」他說。「現在不行動就永遠沒有機會了。」
