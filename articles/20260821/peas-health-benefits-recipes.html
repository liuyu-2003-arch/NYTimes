豌豆有多健康？

How Healthy Are Peas?

AMANDA SCHUPAK • 2026-08-21
The humble pea has a lot going for it. Gregor Mendel, the “father of genetics,” used pea plants to study how heritable traits were passed from parents to offspring. The plants are also friendly to the environment, with a low carbon footprint and roots that naturally nourish the soil.
不起眼的豌豆其實好處多多。「遺傳學之父」格雷高爾·孟德爾曾用豌豆植株來研究可遺傳性狀如何從親代傳給子代。這種植物對環境也十分友好，碳足跡低，其根系還能天然滋養土壤。
And, love them or hate them, “peas are powerhouses of nutrition,” said Rebecca McGee, a plant geneticist at Washington State University.
而且，無論你喜不喜歡吃，「豌豆都是重量級的營養來源，」華盛頓州立大學植物遺傳學家麗貝卡·麥吉說。
Peas, which are members of the legume family, are high in protein and fiber, she said. And though they taste sweet, they are full of complex carbohydrates that won’t spike your blood sugar. Garden peas (also sometimes called sweet peas or English peas) are the most common type to be found in grocery stores, often sold frozen, fresh or canned. Other common varieties, usually eaten with their pods intact, are snow peas and snap peas.
她說，豌豆屬於豆科植物，富含蛋白質和膳食纖維。雖然嘗起來很甜，但它們含有複合碳水化合物，不會讓血糖急劇升高。菜園豌豆（有時也稱為甜豌豆或英國豌豆）是雜貨店中最常見的品種，通常以冷凍、新鮮或罐裝形式出售。其他常見品種還有荷蘭豆和甜脆豆，通常連豆莢一起食用。
Here’s a taste of what’s great about peas, no matter the variety, along with recipes from New York Times Cooking.
不論哪個品種，以下概括了豌豆的諸多好處，隨附來自紐約時報烹飪頻道的食譜。
廣告
Peas are a good source of protein.
豌豆是優質蛋白質來源。
While peas don’t pack the same protein punch as other legumes like lentils or beans, they still rank high among plant sources, said Gary Frost, a professor of nutrition and dietetics at Imperial College London.
倫敦帝國理工學院營養與膳食學教授加里·弗羅斯特表示，雖然豌豆的蛋白質含量不如其他一些豆類，比如小扁豆或各種乾豆，但在植物性來源中仍位居前列。
A standard half-cup portion of cooked garden peas, for instance, contains about four grams of protein. You’ll get even more if you center them in your meal. One cup has around as much protein as you’d get from a three-ounce serving of firm tofu. A cup of cooked snow peas has about five grams of protein.
例如，標準半杯煮熟的菜園豌豆約含四克蛋白質。如果以豌豆為主菜，攝入量會更多。一杯豌豆的蛋白質含量大約相當於一份三盎司（約85克）老豆腐。一杯煮熟的荷蘭豆約含五克蛋白質。
Tom Warkentin, a professor of plant sciences at the University of Saskatchewan, said the relatively high protein content of peas makes them an ideal source for protein powders, alternative meat products and plant milks.
薩斯喀徹溫大學植物科學教授湯姆·沃肯廷表示，豌豆相對較高的蛋白質含量使其成為蛋白粉、替代肉製品和植物奶的理想來源。
They’re packed with ‘good’ carbohydrates.
它們富含「好」碳水化合物。
One cup of cooked garden peas has about nine grams of fiber — supplying about a quarter of the 38 grams per day that is recommended for men and nearly a third of the recommended 25 grams per day for women.
一杯煮熟的菜園豌豆約含九克膳食纖維——約為男性每日推薦攝入量38克的四分之一，接近女性每日推薦攝入量25克的三分之一。
廣告
Most of that is insoluble fiber, which passes through the gut undigested and helps prevent constipation by bulking up stool. The rest is soluble fiber, which slows digestion by attracting water and forming a gel in the intestines. Eating 5 to 10 grams of soluble fiber per day, research suggests, can help lower LDL (or “bad”) cholesterol; one cup of frozen green peas has just over 2.5 grams.
其中大部分為不溶性纖維，這種纖維不經消化通過腸道，通過增加糞便體積來預防便秘。其餘的可溶性纖維在腸道中吸水形成凝膠，從而延緩消化。研究表明，每天攝入五至10克可溶性纖維有助於降低低密度脂蛋白（「壞」膽固醇）；一杯冷凍青豌豆的含量略高於2.5克。
Peas also contain what’s called resistant starch, which passes through the small intestine undigested and — along with the soluble fiber — feeds the good bacteria in the colon. The bacteria, in turn, produce molecules that suppress hunger, keeping you feeling full, Dr. Frost said.
豌豆還含有所謂的抗性澱粉，會不經消化通過小腸，並與可溶性纖維一起餵養結腸中的有益細菌。弗羅斯特說，這些細菌反過來會產生抑制飢餓感的分子，讓你保持飽腹感。
Peas are relatively high in amylose, a component of starch that is digested slowly, so it doesn’t cause a sharp rise in blood sugar, Dr. Warkentin added. Over time, glucose spikes increase the risk of insulin resistance, which can lead to Type 2 diabetes.
沃肯廷補充說，豌豆中的直鏈澱粉含量相對較高，這種澱粉成分消化緩慢，因此不會引起血糖急劇升高。長期血糖飆升會增加胰島素抵抗的風險，進而可能導致2型糖尿病。
They’re beneficial for your eyes.
它們對眼睛有益。
As with other legumes, peas contain vitamins and minerals like folate (important for fetal development), magnesium (beneficial for nerve and muscle function) and zinc (necessary for immune health and wound healing).
與其他豆類一樣，豌豆含有維生素和礦物質，如葉酸（對胎兒發育重要）、鎂（有益於神經和肌肉功能）和鋅（對免疫健康和傷口癒合必不可少）。
One cup of cooked garden peas has just over four milligrams of lutein and zeaxanthin, two antioxidants that are important for eye health. Research suggests that getting about six milligrams of both antioxidants from your diet each day may reduce the risk of age-related macular degeneration, a progressive eye disease that can cause blurry vision.
一杯煮熟的菜園豌豆含有略高於四毫克的葉黃素和玉米黃質，這兩種抗氧化劑對眼睛健康至關重要。研究表明，每天從飲食中攝入約六毫克這兩種抗氧化劑，可降低患老年性黃斑變性的風險，這是一種可能導致視力模糊的進行性眼病。

What’s the best way to enjoy peas?
享用豌豆的最佳方式是什麼？
You can get the nutritional benefits of peas by eating them fresh, frozen, canned or raw. For a complete mix of amino acids (the building blocks of protein), pair them with corn, rice or other whole grains, which are high in the amino acids that peas lack.
你可以通過新鮮、冷凍、罐裝或生食豌豆來獲取其營養益處。要獲得全面的氨基酸成分（蛋白質的構成單元），可將豌豆與玉米、大米或其他全穀物搭配，這些穀物富含豌豆所缺乏的氨基酸。
