美國或懲罰伊朗貿易夥伴

Trump Threat Puts Focus on Iran’s Trading Partners

LEO SANDS • 2026-08-21
President Trump has threatened “tremendous economic consequences” on any nation that does business with Iran, without offering details. The threat appeared to be his latest effort to isolate the country from the global economy and pressure its leadership into negotiating an end to the war.
川普總統威脅稱，任何與伊朗開展業務的國家都將面臨「巨大的經濟後果」，但沒有說明具體措施。這一威脅似乎是他試圖進一步將伊朗與全球經濟隔絕、向伊朗領導層施壓迫使其談判結束戰爭的最新舉措。
The United States has already imposed debilitating sanctions on Iran’s economy and its leadership, and the Trump administration has previously sanctioned certain foreign businesses and organizations that trade with Tehran. Mr. Trump’s vaguely worded social media post on Wednesday seemed to suggest that he could target nations that buy Iranian oil, without naming any.
美國已對伊朗經濟及其領導層實施了嚴厲制裁，川普政府此前也曾制裁過某些與德黑蘭有貿易往來的外國企業和組織。川普週三在社群媒體上措辭含糊的帖子似乎暗示，他可能會把購買伊朗石油的國家列為目標，但未點名具體國家。
The president has, on occasion, backed down from such threats. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi of Iran dismissed his latest warning, calling it “a diversion” from America’s own crises.
川普總統此前也有過收回此類威脅的時候。伊朗外交部長阿巴斯·阿拉格齊駁斥了他的最新警告，稱這是在「轉移視線」，以掩蓋美國自身面臨的危機。
If Mr. Trump follows through, these are some of the countries that could be most affected:
如果川普真的付諸行動，以下是一些可能受影響最大的國家：
廣告
China
中國
China is Iran’s largest trading partner and the primary consumer of its oil, according to recent analysis by the U.S.-China Economic and Security Review Commission, a group founded by Congress to examine America’s bilateral ties to China.
根據美中經濟與安全審查委員會（由國會設立的研究美國對華雙邊關係的機構）的最新分析，中國是伊朗最大的貿易夥伴，也是其石油的主要買家。
For years, China has been practically alone in its willingness to defy Western sanctions on Iranian oil, buying up to as much as 90 percent of Tehran’s oil exports. In recent months, however, Iran’s ability to ship oil by sea has been all but cut off by a U.S. naval blockade.
多年來，中國幾乎是唯一願意無視西方對伊朗石油制裁的國家，購買的伊朗石油一度佔德黑蘭石油出口的90%。不過近幾個月來，由於美國實施海上封鎖，伊朗通過海運出口石油的能力幾乎被完全切斷。
Last year, China estimated its bilateral trade with Iran to be worth nearly $10 billion, a figure that doesn’t include oil.
去年，中國估計與伊朗的雙邊貿易額接近100億美元，這一數字不包括石油。
But from a Chinese perspective, that is a drop in the ocean compared to the size of its overall economy, said William Figueroa, an expert in Chinese-Iranian relations at the University of Groningen in the Netherlands. “It wouldn’t be catastrophic for China if it was to have its trade or its ability to import Iranian oil impacted,” he said.
但荷蘭格羅寧根大學中伊關係專家威廉·菲格羅亞表示，從中國的角度來看，這與其整體經濟規模相比只是九牛一毛。「如果中國與伊朗的貿易，或者進口伊朗石油的能力受到影響，對中國來說也不會是災難性的，」他說。
The biggest advantage Beijing draws from that trade relationship is geopolitical, said Andrea Ghiselli, a political scientist who specializes in China at the University of Exeter in England. Beijing has some interest in preserving the Iranian regime as a thorn in the side of the United States, he said.
英國埃克塞特大學專攻中國研究的政治學家李安風(Andrea Ghiselli)表示，北京從這種貿易關係中獲取的最大好處在於地緣政治。他說，北京有動力保留伊朗政權這個牽制美國的「眼中釘」。
廣告
Dr. Ghiselli described the share of oil that China imports from Iran as marginal, and said that “it can easily be swapped out” for other sources on the global market.
李安風認為，中國從伊朗進口的石油佔比微不足道，「很容易就可以從全球市場上的其他來源取而代之」。
In response to a question on Thursday about the possible effects of coercive U.S. economic measures, the Chinese foreign ministry’s spokesman, Lin Jian, called for all parties to resolve their differences through diplomatic channels.
週四，在被問及美國強制性經濟措施可能產生的影響時，中國外交部發言人林劍呼籲各方通過外交管道解決分歧。
“Sanctions and pressure will not help solve the problem,” he said.
他說：「制裁施壓無助於問題的解決。」
India
印度
Iran was once India’s most important energy supplier. But U.S. sanctions have pushed New Delhi to reduce ties, and the value of trade between the two countries has shrunk dramatically in recent years, according to official data from the Indian embassy in Iran.
伊朗曾是印度最重要的能源供應國。但美國的制裁已促使新德里減少雙邊關係，根據印度駐伊朗大使館的官方數據，近年來兩國貿易額大幅縮水。
The data estimates that in the 2025-26 financial year, total bilateral trade was $1.63 billion, down sharply from over $17 billion in 2018-19.
數據顯示，在2025至2026財年，雙邊貿易總額為16.3億美元，遠低於2018至2019財年的170多億美元。
廣告
In 2019, India stopped buying Iranian oil altogether, under pressure from Mr. Trump. In the last financial year, Indian officials said the country mainly imported apples, pistachios, dates and kiwis from Iran.
2019年，在川普政府的壓力下，印度完全停止購買伊朗石油。印度官員說，在上一財年，印度從伊朗進口的主要商品是蘋果、開心果、椰棗和獼猴桃。
United Arab Emirates, Iraq, Turkey and Pakistan
阿聯酋、伊拉克、土耳其和巴基斯坦
These four Iranian trading partners also appear vulnerable to Mr. Trump’s threat, said Esfandyar Batmanghelidj, chief executive of the Bourse & Bazaar Foundation, a London-based think tank focused on Iran’s economy.
總部位於倫敦、專門研究伊朗經濟的智庫Bourse & Bazaar Foundation首席執行官埃斯凡迪亞爾·巴特曼格利吉說，這四個伊朗貿易夥伴似乎也容易受到川普威脅的影響。
On Wednesday, the United Arab Emirates appeared to pre-empt the president’s comments by announcing a halt to all trade and financial transactions with Iran. According to data from the World Trade Organization, Iranian-Emirati trade was worth roughly $28 billion in 2024. The Emirati decision could affect the ability of Iranian importers to pay for goods, as a lot of those financial services are provided through the U.A.E., Mr. Batmanghelidj said.
週三，阿聯酋似乎提前回應了川普的表態，宣布停止與伊朗之間的所有貿易和金融交易。根據世界貿易組織的數據，2024年伊朗與阿聯酋的貿易額約為280億美元。巴特曼格利吉說，阿聯酋的決定可能會影響伊朗進口商的支付能力，因為大量相關金融服務都是通過阿聯酋提供的。
Pakistan and Turkey have so far been relatively shielded from U.S. economic measures against Tehran, even as they have continued significant overland trade with Iran, Mr. Batmanghelidj said.
巴特曼格利吉說，儘管巴基斯坦和土耳其一直與伊朗保持規模可觀的陸路貿易，但到目前為止，這兩個國家受到美國針對德黑蘭經濟措施的影響相對有限。
In 2022, the latest year for which World Trade Organization data was available, Iran imported more than $11 billion worth of goods from Turkey, its third largest source of imports that year.
根據世界貿易組織可查的最新數據，伊朗2022年從土耳其進口了價值超過110億美元的商品，後者是其第三大進口來源國。
廣告
For both Pakistan and Turkey, “it is politically and geopolitically very important that both these countries maintain a good relationship with Iran,” said Mr. Batmanghelidj. “And I think this is where the Trump administration is really going to struggle.”
巴特曼格利吉說，對於巴基斯坦和土耳其而言，「從政治和地緣政治角度來看，維持與伊朗的良好關係都非常重要」。「我認為，這正是川普政府將真正感到棘手的地方。」
Burcu Ozcelik, a researcher at the Royal United Services Institute research group in London, said Iraq was also vulnerable to U.S. economic pressure because of its continued trade with Iran. American sanctions have already targeted Iranian-linked groups in Iraq.
倫敦皇家聯合軍種研究所研究員布爾庫·奧茲切利克表示，伊拉克由於繼續與伊朗進行貿易，也容易受到美國經濟壓力的影響。美國此前已經將一些與伊朗有關聯的團體列為制裁對象。
But Dr. Ozcelik said that broader sanctions on Iran’s trading partners would be far more complicated to impose, and that implementing them “will be slow, uneven and difficult to monitor.” It was far from clear, she added, that “greater pressure would produce the political behavior Trump is seeking.”
但奧茲切利克表示，對伊朗的貿易夥伴實施更廣泛的制裁要複雜得多，實施起來「將是緩慢、不均衡且難以監控的」。她還說，目前遠遠無法確定，「施加更大壓力是否真的會促使伊朗採取川普所尋求的政治行為。」

Keith Bradsher和Siyi Zhao對本文有研究貢獻。

Leo Sands是《紐約時報》突發新聞中心記者，常駐倫敦。
