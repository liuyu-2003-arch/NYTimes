美國認定美籍學者遭中國不當拘留

State Dept. Declares U.S. Citizen in China Wrongfully Detained

KAROUN DEMIRJIAN • 2026-08-21
The State Department on Thursday designated an American scholar arrested in China on spying charges in June as having been wrongfully detained.
週四，美國國務院將一名於6月在中國因間諜罪名被捕的美國學者認定遭到不當拘留。
The detained U.S. citizen, U Min Zin, disappeared in China’s Yunnan province after traveling there to attend an academic conference at the invitation of the Chinese government, according to the State Department.
美國國務院稱，這名被拘留的美國公民名為敏辛(U Min Zin)，他在應中國政府邀請前往雲南省參加一場學術會議後失蹤。
“We call on Beijing to release all U.S. citizens arbitrarily detained or exit banned in China,” Dylan Johnson, the assistant secretary of state for global affairs, wrote on social media. “The safety and security of U.S. citizens is the State Department’s top priority.”
「我們呼籲北京釋放所有在中國被任意拘留或被禁止出境的美國公民，」負責全球事務的助理國務卿迪倫·強生在社群媒體上寫道。「美國公民的安全與保障是國務院的首要任務。」
The official designation comes about a month before President Trump is expected to host President Xi Jinping of China for an official visit, following Mr. Trump’s trip to Beijing this spring.
這一官方認定發布於川普總統預計接待中國國家主席習近平正式訪美的約一個月前。此前川普已於今年春天訪問北京。
廣告
It also comes as U.S. allies in the Asia-Pacific region have grown increasingly concerned about the United States’ willingness to help defend them against China and its partners, after Mr. Trump and the Pentagon recently scaled back major military exercises with South Korea.
與此同時，在川普和五角大廈近期縮減了與韓國的重大軍事演習之後，亞太地區的美國盟友越來越擔心，美國是否願意幫助他們抵禦中國及其合作夥伴的威脅。
Mr. Min Zin is a political scientist and the executive director of a strategy and policy group founded in Myanmar, though it moved its headquarters after a 2021 military coup in that country, formerly known as Burma. The Chinese province where he was detained borders Myanmar.
敏辛是一位政治學家，也是一家戰略與政策組織的執行主任。該組織在緬甸創立，但在2021年緬甸發生軍事政變後總部搬遷。他被拘留的中國省份與緬甸接壤。
Mr. Min Zin, who was living in Thailand, has written on the politics of Myanmar for the opinion section of The New York Times, among other U.S. news organizations.
敏辛居住在泰國，曾為《紐約時報》及其他美國新聞機構的評論版塊撰寫過有關緬甸政治的文章。
His arrest took place less than two weeks before President Min Aung Hlaing of Myanmar made a five-day visit to China, and less than three weeks after Mr. Trump’s state visit to Beijing.
在他被捕前不到兩週，緬甸總統敏昂萊對中國進行了為期五天的訪問。在他被捕前不到三週，川普對北京進行了國事訪問。
Since returning to the Oval Office, Mr. Trump has adopted a warmer stance toward China, in pursuit of what both Trump and Xi administration officials have labeled “constructive strategic stability” between the two economic and military powerhouses. Mr. Min Zin’s arrest has complicated that trajectory.
自重返白宮橢圓形辦公室以來，川普對中國採取了更加溫和的立場，以期在兩個經濟和軍事強國之間實現川普與習近平政府官員所稱的「建設性戰略穩定」。敏辛的被捕讓這一進程變得複雜。
He is currently one of two U.S. citizens in China to have been officially declared by the State Department as wrongfully detained. The other, Youlin Chen, a seismologist, was detained at a Beijing airport in 2024 while trying to return to the United States.
目前，他是被美國國務院正式宣布在中國被不當拘留的兩名美國公民之一。另一位是地震學家陳友麟，他於2024年試圖返回美國時在北京某機場遭到拘留。
廣告
Under statute, the U.S. government is obligated to treat people it declares as wrongfully detained abroad as hostages of a foreign government and to work to secure their release. The cases are referred to the special presidential envoy for hostage affairs, and congressional committees are notified and periodically updated.
根據法規，美國政府有義務將被其宣布在海外遭不當拘留的人員視為外國政府的人質，並努力確保他們獲釋。這些案件會被移交給負責解救人質事務的總統特使，同時國會相關委員會也會接到通知，並定期獲得最新情況通報。
Dr. Chen’s family said this year that Mr. Trump pressed his case with Mr. Xi when the two leaders met in Beijing.
陳友麟的家人今年表示，當兩國領導人在北京會晤時，川普先生曾向習近平交涉過他的案件。
The group Mr. Min Zin led, the Institute for Strategy and Policy-Myanmar, praised the State Department’s move and urged the Trump administration to make his detention “a top priority, particularly ahead of President Xi’s visit to meet with President Trump next month.”
敏辛領導的組織「緬甸戰略與政策研究所」讚揚了美國國務院的這一舉措，並敦促川普政府將敏辛被拘留一事作為「首要任務，特別是在下個月習近平主席訪美與川普總統會面之前」。
“This designation also reaffirms that independent, open-source researchers and academics should be able to conduct their work without fear of intimidation or arbitrary detention,” the group said in a statement.
「這一認定也再次確認，獨立的、利用公開資料的研究人員和學者應當能夠在無需擔心受到恐嚇或任意拘留的情況下開展工作，」該組織在聲明中表示。
