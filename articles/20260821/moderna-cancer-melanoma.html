mRNA癌症疫苗取得突破進展，莫德納股價大漲

How Moderna Nearly Tripled Its Stock Price in a Single Day

REBECCA ROBBINS • 2026-08-21
Covid vaccines made Moderna a household name, but the company’s fortunes cratered as demand for the shots plummeted. Hostility from the Trump administration also spooked investors. As of Tuesday, the company’s stock had fallen nearly 90 percent from its peak, a loss of $170 billion in market value.
新冠疫苗曾讓莫德納家喻戶曉，但隨著需求驟降，這家公司的境況也急轉直下。川普政府的敵意態度也讓投資者感到不安。截至週二，莫德納股價已較峰值下跌近90%，市值蒸發1700億美元。
All the while, the company was quietly mounting a comeback in cancer.
與此同時，這家公司一直在癌症領域悄悄布局，謀求東山再起。
On Wednesday, those efforts began to pay off in a big way. Moderna and the big drugmaker Merck reported that their experimental cancer vaccine extended the time before melanoma recurred in a clinical trial — the first late-stage study of its kind to succeed. The two drugmakers announced their success in news releases, without providing numbers that would indicate how much patients had benefited. Moderna’s stock closed up 177 percent on Wednesday.
週三，這些努力開始獲得巨大回報。莫德納和製藥巨頭默克宣布，雙方合作的一款實驗性癌症疫苗在一項臨床試驗中延緩了黑色素瘤患者的復發——這是同類疫苗首次在後期臨床試驗中取得成功。兩家藥企通過新聞稿公布了這一成果，但未提供顯示患者獲益程度的具體數據。週三，莫德納股價收盤大漲177%。
The investors were betting that Moderna could help jump-start a new approach to treating certain cancers. The experimental vaccine uses the same messenger RNA, or mRNA, technology that powered Moderna’s Covid-19 shot, but it works differently to treat cancer. Tailored to each patient’s tumor genetics, the vaccine delivers instructions to produce a fragment of a tumor to teach the body’s immune system to attack the cancer.
投資者押注的是，莫德納或許有望幫助開啟一種治療某些癌症的新路徑。這款實驗性疫苗採用了與莫德納新冠疫苗相同的信使核糖核酸(mRNA)技術，但治療癌症的方式有所不同。疫苗根據每位患者腫瘤的基因特徵量身定製，通過傳遞指令讓身體產生腫瘤片段，從而訓練免疫系統攻擊癌細胞。
廣告
Cancer vaccines using mRNA could become a hugely lucrative business, if the approach works across cancer types. Cutting-edge cancer drugs typically cost several hundred thousand dollars a year.
如果這項技術對多種癌症類型都有效，那麼mRNA癌症疫苗有望成為一項利潤豐厚的業務。當前最前沿的抗癌藥物通常每年要花費數十萬美元。
For Moderna, cancer “is by far the most significant pipeline opportunity,” said Tyler Van Buren, an analyst at the Wall Street bank TD Cowen.
華爾街投行TD Cowen的分析師泰勒·范布倫說，對莫德納而言，癌症「無疑是其研發管線中最重要的機會」。
Moderna’s chief executive, Stéphane Bancel, said on CNBC on Wednesday that the company’s work on the cancer vaccine was its latest effort to “take very big, scientific clinical bets.”
莫德納首席執行官斯特凡納·邦塞爾週三接受CNBC採訪時說，公司在癌症疫苗方面的研發是「在科學臨床項目上進行大膽押注」的最新嘗試。
The prospect of using mRNA vaccines to treat cancer has tantalized researchers for decades. But momentum in the field has accelerated only recently, as manufacturing and other technology has improved, thanks in part to investments during the pandemic.
利用mRNA疫苗治療癌症的前景已讓研究人員憧憬了數十年。但該領域直到最近才加速發展，部分原因在於疫情期間的投資推動了製造工藝及其他技術的進步。
As of last year, more than 60 cancer vaccines using mRNA or similar technology were in development, being tested in more than 120 clinical trials, according to one count. Many of those studies are funded by philanthropies, the government and smaller biotechnology companies.
據一項統計，截至去年，已有60多種採用mRNA或類似技術的癌症疫苗正在研發中，並正在接受超過120項臨床試驗的測試。其中許多研究由慈善機構、政府和規模較小的生物科技公司提供資金。
Four major drugmakers are leading the development efforts. Two are Moderna and Merck, whose collaboration on the approach dates back a decade; they are also testing the same vaccine as a therapy for cancers of the lung, kidney and bladder. (In the study whose results were announced on Wednesday, the cancer vaccine was given in combination with Keytruda, Merck’s blockbuster cancer immunotherapy.)
目前有四家大型藥企領跑這一領域的研發。其中兩家就是莫德納和默克，它們在這一領域的合作可以追溯到十年前；它們還在測試同一款疫苗用於治療肺癌、腎癌和膀胱癌。（在週三公布結果的那項試驗中，測試對象在使用這款癌症疫苗的同時，還使用了默克公司的重磅抗癌免疫藥物Keytruda。）
廣告
BioNTech, another developer of an mRNA vaccine for Covid, is working with Roche’s Genentech unit to develop a vaccine to treat cancers of the pancreas and colon. Results from a small safety study of that therapy in pancreatic cancer generated excitement this spring.
另一家新冠mRNA疫苗開發商BioNTech正在與羅氏旗下的基因泰克合作，開發一款用於治療胰腺癌和結腸癌的疫苗。今年春季公布的一項針對胰腺癌患者的小規模安全性試驗結果令人振奮。
The approach has historically been seen as risky. The manufacturing is complex, the politics are fraught and there had been little evidence it would work. Other large major cancer drugmakers, like Pfizer and Bristol Myers Squibb, are not working on mRNA vaccines for cancer.
這種方法過去一直被視為風險很高。其生產流程複雜，相關政治環境複雜，而且此前幾乎沒有證據證明它能夠奏效。其他大型抗癌藥企，如輝瑞和百時美施貴寶，目前並沒有開展mRNA癌症疫苗的研發。
Under Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr., the Trump administration has made a series of funding and policy changes hostile to mRNA technology. A year ago, the federal government canceled nearly half a billion dollars’ worth of contracts and other funding for mRNA technology.
在衛生部長小羅伯特·甘迺迪領導下，川普政府接連出台針對mRNA技術不利的資金和政策調整。一年前，聯邦政府取消了價值近5億美元的mRNA技術相關合同及其他資金支持。
“Those political headwinds were serious, and it caused a lot of companies to take great pause,” said Jeff Coller, a scientist who works on mRNA at Johns Hopkins University. Dr. Coller advises several small mRNA companies and is on the executive committee of the Alliance for mRNA Medicines, a trade group.
約翰斯·霍普金斯大學從事mRNA研究的科學家傑夫·科勒表示：「這些政治阻力非常大，讓許多公司望而卻步。」科勒為幾家小型mRNA公司提供諮詢，同時也是行業組織「mRNA藥物聯盟」的執行委員會成員。
But the administration has signaled it may be more open when it comes to mRNA for treating cancer. This year, it quietly started a public-private partnership expected to fund clinical trials of different cancer vaccines, including those using mRNA. The Department of Health and Human Services did not return a request for comment on Wednesday about the status of that initiative and how much funding the department has granted.
不過，在將mRNA用於治療癌症的問題上，川普政府釋放出的信號可能更為開放。今年，政府悄然啟動了一項公私合作項目，預計將為不同癌症疫苗的臨床試驗提供資金，其中包括採用mRNA技術的疫苗。美國衛生與公眾服務部週三未就該項目進展及撥款金額回應置評請求。
Moderna was founded in 2010 in Cambridge, Mass., to develop medicines using mRNA. Its Covid vaccine, its first product, brought in tens of billions of dollars of revenue during the pandemic.
莫德納於2010年在麻薩諸塞州劍橋成立，致力於利用mRNA技術開發藥物。其新冠疫苗是公司的第一款產品，在疫情期間帶來了數百億美元的收入。
廣告
But as the public and the government soured on its Covid shot, Moderna had hardly anything to replace it with. Over the past few years, Moderna looked for different ways to reinvent itself. It won approvals for mRNA vaccines for respiratory syncytial virus and, most recently, flu. It also explored experimental therapies for rare diseases.
但隨著公眾和政府對新冠疫苗態度轉冷，莫德納幾乎沒有替代產品。過去幾年，它嘗試感覺哦多種轉型路徑。其針對呼吸道合胞病毒的mRNA疫苗已獲批，最近又獲批了流感疫苗。公司還探索了針對罕見疾病的實驗性療法。
Last year, the company laid off more than 800 workers, a tenth of its work force. It also lost more than $700 million in contracts to develop a shot to protect humans against bird flu after the Trump administration canceled the agreements. The company shelved vaccines to protect against herpes, chickenpox and shingles.
去年，公司裁員800餘人，占員工總數的十分之一。在川普政府取消相關協議後，公司損失了超過7億美元的禽流感疫苗開發合同。它還擱置了針對皰疹、水痘和帶狀皰疹的疫苗研發。
Even though it had started years before the pandemic, Moderna’s work on mRNA cancer vaccines flew relatively under the radar. In addition to the experimental cancer vaccine it is developing with Merck, Moderna solely owns several others that it is testing in different cancers.
儘管莫德納早在新冠疫情暴發前就已經開始研發mRNA癌症疫苗，但這項工作相對不受關注。除了與默克合作開發的實驗性癌症疫苗之外，莫德納還獨自擁有數款其他癌症疫苗，目前正在進行實驗。
“People started to pay attention” as Moderna and Merck began presenting promising results from midstage clinical research, said Andrew Tsai, an analyst at the Wall Street bank Jefferies.
華爾街投行Jefferies分析師安德魯·蔡（音）表示，隨著莫德納和默克開始公布中期臨床研究的積極結果，「人們開始注意到了」這一領域。
Now, Moderna is back in the spotlight. Crucial questions remain: Will the promising results in melanoma, which has repeatedly proved to be more responsive to treatment, hold up in cancers that are tougher to treat? And will the detailed results look as good as the companies made them sound on Wednesday? (The drugmakers said they would present the full data soon at a medical meeting.)
如今，莫德納重回聚光燈下。但關鍵問題依然存在：在黑色素瘤這種一再被證明對治療較為敏感的癌症中取得的積極結果，能否在那些更難治療的癌症中同樣成立？詳細的臨床數據是否如兩家公司週三所說那樣出色？（兩家藥企表示，他們很快將在一次醫學會議上公布完整數據。）
But now that an mRNA cancer vaccine appears to have succeeded, Mr. Van Buren of TD Cowen said he anticipated that drugmakers would flock into the technology.
不過，既然一款mRNA癌症疫苗似乎已經取得成功，TD Cowen的范布倫預計，製藥企業將紛紛湧入這一技術領域。
廣告
“Now that this trial was successful,” he said, “I definitely think we can expect more investment by other pharma companies.”
「既然這項試驗已經成功，」他說，「我認為我們完全可以預期，其他製藥公司會加大投資。」

Rebecca Robbins是時報記者，主要報導製藥行業。她自2015年以來一直從事健康和醫學方面的報導。
