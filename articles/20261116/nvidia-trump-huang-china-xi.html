黃仁勛事件為本週「習川會」蒙上陰影

Nvidia Fallout Looms Over Trump-Xi Meeting

KATIE ROGERS • 2026-11-16
At major moments in President Trump’s second term, he has looked up during an economic speech or diplomatic dinner and asked a version of the same question: “Where’s Jensen?”
在川普總統第二任期的幾個重要時刻，他曾在發表經濟演講或出席外交晚宴時抬起頭，問出一個大致相同的問題：「詹森在哪兒？」
Jensen Huang has usually been out there, somewhere in the room.
黃仁勛通常就在現場，在場內的某個地方。
Mr. Huang, who was until Thursday the leather-jacketed chief executive of Nvidia, one of the world’s largest companies, had impressed the president with his success, his money and his argument that keeping China dependent on American-made semiconductor chips and technology could help maintain U.S. dominance over Beijing.
直到週四前，黃仁勛還是全球最大企業之一英偉達的那個身穿皮夾克的首席執行官。他憑藉自身的成功、財富，以及這樣一種論點給總統留下了深刻印象：讓中國保持對美國製造的半導體晶片和技術的依賴，有助於維持美國對北京的壓制優勢。
Mr. Trump made sure to have the technology executive with him during pivotal U.S.-China meetings this year. The president invited him to come to China in May when he went to meet with Xi Jinping, China’s leader, stopping in Alaska, where Mr. Huang joined him on Air Force One at the last minute. And Mr. Huang was at the head table with the president when Mr. Trump hosted a state dinner for Mr. Xi at the White House in September.
今年在美中兩國舉行關鍵會晤期間，川普都特意讓這位科技高管隨行。5月前往中國會見中國領導人習近平時，總統邀請他同行，並在經停阿拉斯加期間，讓黃仁勛在最後一刻登上了「空軍一號」。9月川普在白宮為習近平舉行國宴時，黃仁勛也與總統同坐主桌。
廣告
But when Mr. Trump travels to China for another meeting with Mr. Xi at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation in Shenzhen this week, Mr. Huang, the man who has been influential in shaping the president’s views about technological competition with China, is not expected to be at his side.
但當川普本週前往中國、在深圳亞太經合組織會議上與習近平再次會晤時，這位影響了總統對華科技競爭看法的人物預計將不會出現在他身旁。
Last week, Mr. Huang stepped down from his role at Nvidia after revelations that he had engaged in affairs, including one with Alice Huang, a woman who U.S. intelligence officials believe is an asset of Beijing.
上週，在被曝出有多段婚外情後，黃仁勛辭去了在英偉達的職務，其中包括與黃樂(Alice Huang)的一段關係——美國情報官員認為該女子是北京的情報線人。
Mr. Huang’s sudden fall and the U.S. finding that he was targeted by Chinese intelligence do not appear to have altered Mr. Trump’s estimation of the former Nvidia leader — or his approach to dealing with his Chinese counterpart in this week’s meeting. (Beijing has called the U.S. intelligence conclusions “smears and slanders.”)
黃仁勛的突然下台，以及美國關於他成為中國情報部門目標的調查結論，似乎並未改變川普對這位前英偉達領導人的評價——也未改變他在本週會晤中與中方領導人打交道的方式。（北京方面稱美方情報結論是「污衊抹黑」。）
So far, the president has not pressed Mr. Xi about his country’s espionage efforts or stepped in to resolve a raging debate within his administration about tightening export controls of A.I. technology and certain chips to China. Howard Lutnick, the commerce secretary, who has argued for less strict limits on chip exports to Asia, is expected to travel with him. In Shenzhen, Mr. Trump will participate in a meeting with Mr. Xi and, later, with Mr. Xi and President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia, in the first such gathering of the three leaders.
迄今為止，總統尚未就中國的情報活動向習近平施壓，也未介入平息政府內部關於收緊對華人工智慧技術和特定晶片出口管制的激烈爭論。一直主張放寬對亞洲晶片出口限制的商務部長霍華德·盧特尼克預計將隨行。在深圳，川普將先與習近平舉行會晤，隨後還將與習近平及俄羅斯總統弗拉基米爾·V·普丁共同會晤，這將是三位領導人的首次此類會晤。
“Well, the big thing is, was the woman a spy?” Mr. Trump said in an interview with the podcaster and sports commentator Stephen A. Smith on Thursday, when asked if he thought Mr. Huang should have resigned. “And I don’t know if that’s been determined yet, but if the woman was a spy, yes. If the woman was not a spy, perhaps it would be different. But I can tell you this: Somehow, he’ll end up in his feet. I mean, I know him. I know him pretty well. I always thought of him as a fine man.”
「嗯，關鍵問題是，那個女人是不是間諜？」川普週四在接受播客主持人、體育評論員斯蒂芬·A·史密斯採訪時，被問及是否認為黃仁勛應該辭職時這樣說道。「我還不知道這件事是否已有定論，但如果那個女人是間諜，那他確實應該辭職。如果那個女人不是間諜，情況也許會有所不同。但我可以告訴你：無論如何，他最終都會重新站穩腳跟的。我是說，我了解他。我相當了解他。我一直覺得他是個好人。」
Mr. Trump said last week that he did not plan to bring up the Nvidia matter with Mr. Xi but suggested that the Chinese leader could bring it up with him.
川普上週表示，他不打算向習近平提及英偉達一事，但表示這位中國領導人可能會向他提起此事。
The president also said that he did not recall being briefed that Mr. Huang was in a close relationship with Alice Huang, a Chinese executive believed by the United States to be a Chinese intelligence asset.
總統還表示，他不記得曾聽取過有關黃仁勛與黃樂關係密切的簡報——後者是一名中國高管，被美方認為是中國情報線人。
But Secretary of State Marco Rubio told Mr. Trump aboard Air Force One in May en route to China about the sensitive intelligence showing that Nvidia’s chief executive was in a relationship with the woman and that she was believed to be a Chinese spy, The New York Times reported last week. It was at least the second time the president was briefed on the intelligence, The Times reported.
但據《紐約時報》上週報導，國務卿馬爾科·魯比奧5月在飛往中國的「空軍一號」上就曾向川普通報過這份敏感情報，該情報顯示，英偉達首席執行官與這位被認為是中國間諜的女子為情人關係。《紐約時報》報導稱，這至少是總統第二次聽取有關該情報的簡報。
Mr. Trump has continued to defend Mr. Huang, calling him an “honorable man” and questioning reports that he was briefed on the government’s intelligence findings.
川普繼續為黃仁勛辯護，稱他是一個「正派的人」，並質疑有關自己曾聽取政府情報調查結果簡報的報導。
廣告
“I’d have to check,” he told reporters last week in response to a question about when he first learned of the matter. “I’d certainly check with the intelligence people. They could have been mentioned. Certainly, it didn’t make much of an impact on me.”
「我得核實一下，」他上週在回答有關何時首次得知此事的提問時對記者說。「我肯定會向情報人員核實。相關情況可能被提起過。但顯然，這事沒給我留下什麼深刻印象。」
The president’s blasé response to espionage allegations that have upended one of the world’s largest companies belies the economic and political storm caused by the revelations of Mr. Huang’s behavior. It also raises questions about how Mr. Trump would conduct himself during his meeting with Mr. Xi, an authoritarian leader whose friendship and admiration the president has courted.
面對已使全球最大企業之一陷入動盪的間諜指控，總統漫不經心的回應與黃仁勛行為曝光所引發的經濟與政治風暴形成了鮮明對比。這也讓人質疑，川普在與習近平會晤時將如何表現——總統一直極力爭取這位威權領導人的友誼與讚賞。
“Trump has stood by Huang personally, and my read is that he sees espionage as something major powers just do to each other,” Craig Singleton, a China expert at the Foundation for Defense of Democracies, a nonpartisan think tank, said during a briefing last week. “He wants a very successful meeting and to show that he can manage the relationship with Xi Jinping.”
「川普在個人層面上一直支持黃仁勛，我的解讀是，他認為間諜活動不過是大國之間彼此都會做的事情，」無黨派智庫捍衛民主基金會的中國問題專家克雷格·辛格爾頓上週在一場簡報會上表示。「他希望這次會晤非常成功，並展示自己能夠把握好與習近平的關係。」
Indeed, Mr. Trump has said that espionage is the price of dealing with a powerful adversary. “You know what they say? That China spies on us. I say you’re right, and we spy on them, too,” he said in September.
事實上，川普曾表示，間諜活動是與強大對手打交道的代價。「你知道他們怎麼說嗎？他們說中國對我們搞間諜活動。我說你們說得對，但我們也對他們搞間諜活動，」他在9月說道。
White House officials said Friday that the Nvidia matter was a national security issue, and they did not expect the president to discuss it with Mr. Xi during their encounter.
白宮官員週五表示，英偉達一事屬於國家安全問題，他們預計總統不會在會面期間與習近平討論此事。
In his two previous meetings this year with the Chinese leader, Mr. Trump showed little interest in extracting concessions from China.
在今年此前與中國領導人的兩次會晤中，川普幾乎沒有表現出爭取中國讓步的興趣。
廣告
Mr. Trump struck a conciliatory tone with Mr. Xi during his visit to Beijing in May, emphasizing their personal relationship. And when Mr. Xi visited Washington in the fall, weeks after Mr. Trump decried what he claimed was meddling by Beijing in the 2020 election, the president leaned into the pageantry of the occasion. The state visit produced little in terms of policy announcements on matters of tension between the two countries — on A.I., trade or the war in Iran.
5月訪問北京期間，川普對習近平採取了和解語調，強調兩人的私人關係。秋季習近平訪問華盛頓時——就在幾週前川普還曾譴責他所稱的北京對2020年大選的干預——總統卻全心投入到盛大的禮賓儀式中。這次國事訪問在兩國存在分歧的議題上——包括人工智慧、貿易或伊朗戰爭——幾乎沒有產生任何政策聲明。
But as Mr. Trump prepares to head to China again, Mr. Singleton noted that “the politics back in Washington are moving in a very different direction.”
但就在川普準備再次前往中國之際，辛格爾頓指出，「華盛頓的政治風向正在朝截然不同的方向發展。」
The Trump administration is now facing questions and criticism over whether officials had properly policed the sale of chips to China. On Friday, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent publicly blamed the Commerce Department for failing to curb the sale of advanced Nvidia technology to the Singaporean firm Megaspeed, which employed Ms. Huang and built data centers that provide computing power to Chinese companies.
川普政府目前正因官員們是否妥善監管對華晶片銷售而面臨質疑和批評。週五，財政部長史考特·貝森特公開指責商務部未能遏制向新加坡公司Megaspeed出售先進英偉達技術的行為——該公司曾僱用黃樂，並建設了向中國企業提供算力的數據中心。
“You will have to ask them about their internal processes,” he said of the Commerce Department, adding that his own agency had supported a stricter approach. “Treasury and my deputy has been pushing for this for months at the deputies level.”
「你們得去問問他們的內部流程，」他在談及商務部時說，並補充稱他所在的部門一直支持採取更嚴格的做法。「數月來，財政部和我的副手一直在副部級層面上推動此事。」
Mr. Bessent has led the administration’s efforts to extend a trade truce with Beijing that expires in January. Despite continuing negotiations and the pause of a tit-for-tat trade war, administration officials remain frustrated with China’s restrictions on exports of crucial rare-earth metals and rare-earth magnets. Mr. Trump is expected to bring up that issue with Mr. Xi in Shenzhen.
貝森特一直主導著政府與北京延長貿易休戰期的努力，該休戰期將於1月到期。儘管談判仍在繼續，且針鋒相對的貿易戰暫時休止，但政府官員仍對中國的關鍵稀土金屬及磁體出口限制感到不滿。預計川普將在深圳與習近平會晤時提及這一問題。
While administration officials say the president will focus his meetings on rare-earth exports, nuclear proliferation and the war with Iran, Republicans and Democrats in Washington are demanding answers on Nvidia from Mr. Huang and Mr. Lutnick, who both have been asked to testify before the Senate Banking Committee in December.
儘管政府官員表示總統將在會晤中聚焦稀土出口、核擴散以及與伊朗的戰爭，但華盛頓的兩黨人士正要求黃仁勛和盧特尼克就英偉達一事作出交代，兩人都已被要求於12月在參議院銀行委員會作證。
廣告
“If Beijing was trying to keep American chips flowing, it may have helped build the bipartisan case for cutting that access off,” Mr. Singleton said.
「如果北京當時試圖讓美國晶片繼續流入，這一做法可能為兩黨切斷該獲取管道的主張提供依據，」辛格爾頓說。
It remains to be seen if that political pressure will force Mr. Trump to impose new restrictions on technology exports, or make another move that could constrain China.
這種政治壓力是否會迫使川普實施新的技術出口限制，或採取其他可能制約中國的舉措，仍有待觀察。
Kenton Thibaut, a senior resident China fellow at the Atlantic Council, said that the Nvidia scandal was a “miserable” scenario for the Chinese, whose diplomatic aim with Mr. Trump is to placate him and keep a competitive relationship steady while reducing dependence on the economic choke points that Americans control.
大西洋理事會駐會中國問題高級研究員肯頓·蒂博表示，英偉達醜聞對中方而言是一個「糟糕」的局面——中方對川普的外交目標是安撫他、保持競爭關係的穩定，同時減少對美國所控制的經濟咽喉要道的依賴。
“They wanted to keep this stasis in U.S.-China relations, prevent sort of any more export controls from coming into place, play nice with Trump and not rock the boat in any way,” Ms. Thibaut said. “And this is kind of like a disaster for them.”
「他們原本希望維持美中關係的這種靜止狀態，防止出台更多出口管制措施，對川普保持友好，不以任何方式製造事端，」蒂博說。「而這件事對他們來說簡直是一場災難。」

Katie Rogers是《紐約時報》白宮記者，報導川普總統相關新聞。
