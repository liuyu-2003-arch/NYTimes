Megaspeed Says Alice Huang Left Company in August 2025

ALI WATKINS • 2026-11-16
Megaspeed, the Singaporean data center company, said in a statement on Sunday that Alice Huang, a former executive at the center of what U.S. officials call a Chinese spy plot targeting the Nvidia co-founder Jensen Huang, had left the company in August 2025.
新加坡數據中心公司Megaspeed週日發表聲明稱，前高管黃樂已於2025年8月離職。黃樂是美國官員所稱的一起針對英偉達聯合創始人黃仁勛的中國間諜陰謀的核心人物。
The statement, which was reported by The Associated Press, was Megaspeed’s first to directly address the status of Ms. Huang since she became the focus of international scrutiny over her ties to Mr. Huang, who resigned from Nvidia on Thursday.
據美聯社報導，這是自黃樂因與黃仁勛的關係受到國際社會密切關注以來，Megaspeed首次直接回應她在公司的任職情況。黃仁勛已於週四從英偉達辭職。
News of their relationship rocked Nvidia, the dominant maker of artificial intelligence chips, as well as the global technology sector and the U.S. government.
兩人關係的曝光震動了英偉達這家人工智慧晶片領域的主導企業，也波及全球科技行業和美國政府。
Last month, The New York Times reported that American officials had concluded Ms. Huang was a Chinese intelligence asset and had a close relationship with Mr. Huang, one of the most powerful figures in the A.I. boom and an adviser to President Trump.
上個月，《紐約時報》報導稱，美國官員已認定黃樂是中國情報機構的一名情報人員，並與黃仁勛關係密切。黃仁勛是人工智慧熱潮中最有影響力的人物之一，也是川普總統的顧問。
廣告
Mr. Huang acknowledged last Sunday that he’d had an affair with Ms. Huang, and Nvidia’s board of directors said they had begun an investigation into his conduct. On Wednesday, The New York Times reported that he also had an extramarital relationship with an executive at Nvidia who reported to him. He abruptly stepped down as chief executive the next day from the company he had led for more than 30 years.
上週日，黃仁勛承認自己曾與黃樂有過婚外情，英偉達董事會則表示，已開始調查他的行為。週三，《紐約時報》報導稱，他還與一名直接向他彙報工作的英偉達高管有婚外情。第二天，他突然辭去了這家由自己領導了30多年的公司的首席執行官職務。 
The chip maker’s stock has fallen about 30 percent below its all-time high, which was set just over a month ago, erasing more than $1 trillion in market value.
這家晶片製造商的股價已較一個多月前創下的歷史高點下跌約30%，市值蒸發超過1萬億美元。
The scandal has trained attention on Megaspeed, a small company that purchased billions of dollars of Nvidia’s coveted chips from 2024 to 2026.
這場醜聞也使Megaspeed受到關注。這家規模不大的公司在2024年至2026年間購買了價值數十億美元的英偉達晶片，而這些晶片在市場上一直供不應求。
That set off an investigation by the U.S. Department of Commerce as to whether some of the chips were funneled to China, in possible violation of American export restrictions. The United States has not lodged any formal charges against the company.
這引發了美國商務部的一項調查，以確定部分晶片是否被轉運至中國，從而可能違反美國的出口限制。美國尚未對該公司提出任何正式指控。
On Thursday, Megaspeed’s chief executive, James Tan, said in a statement that the company had not broken U.S. laws, had cooperated with Commerce Department investigators and was “ready to cooperate fully with any other relevant U.S. government authority.”
週四，Megaspeed首席執行官詹姆斯·陳(James Tan)在一份聲明中表示，公司沒有違反美國法律，已配合商務部調查人員，並「準備與美國政府任何其他相關部門全面合作」。
Megaspeed’s statement on Sunday reiterated that its business was “legitimate and lawful” and for the first time mentioned Ms. Huang, who helped set up the company starting in 2023, saying she had no current role there.
Megaspeed週日的聲明重申，公司業務「正當合法」，並首次提及黃樂。黃樂從2023年起參與籌建該公司。聲明稱，她目前已不在公司擔任任何職務。
廣告
“Megaspeed does not comment on Ms Huang’s personal and private matters,” it told The A.P. in an emailed statement.
「Megaspeed不對黃女士的個人及私事發表評論，」該公司在發給美聯社的電子郵件聲明中表示。
The company’s timeline involving Ms. Huang could not be independently verified. Its statement on Sunday came after more than a year of scrutiny by the authorities in the United States and Singapore.
Megaspeed提供的有關黃樂任職時間的信息尚無法得到獨立核實。週日發表這份聲明之前，美國和新加坡當局已對該公司進行了長達一年多的審查。
“The company has retained external legal counsel to conduct a thorough internal investigation,” Megaspeed added.
「公司已聘請外部法律顧問進行徹底的內部調查，」Megaspeed還表示。
Ms. Huang has not responded to requests for comment. China has denied the espionage claims.
黃樂尚未回應置評請求。中國否認了有關間諜活動的指控。
The Times reported in October 2025 that Ms. Huang had stepped down from the company months earlier, citing a human resources manager, though it was unclear where she went.
《紐約時報》曾於2025年10月援引一名人力資源經理的說法報導，黃樂已在數月前離開該公司，但當時不清楚她去了哪裡。
Last week, the government of Singapore said it had confiscated Ms. Huang’s passport from June 2025 to February 2026 over “potential false declarations” about Megaspeed. She was ultimately released and barred from re-entering Singapore.
上週，新加坡政府表示，由於黃樂在Megaspeed的相關事務中「可能作出虛假申報」，當局曾於2025年6月至2026年2月期間扣押她的護照。她最終獲釋，但被禁止再次入境新加坡。
廣告
U.S. officials, including Secretary of State Marco Rubio, had informed Mr. Trump of concerns about Ms. Huang and her relationship with Mr. Huang as early as October 2025.
包括國務卿馬可·魯比奧在內的美國官員，早在2025年10月就已向川普通報了對黃樂及其與黃仁勛關係的擔憂。 
Mr. Trump declined to take action. This May, Mr. Trump brought Mr. Huang with him on a trip to China, another sign of Mr. Huang’s importance to the A.I. data center boom that is a key driver of the American economy.
川普沒有採取行動。今年5月，川普還帶著黃仁勛一同訪問中國。這再次表明，黃仁勛對於人工智慧數據中心建設熱潮的重要性，而這股熱潮正是美國經濟增長的關鍵驅動力之一。
The scandal has touched off a new round infighting in the Trump administration over its approach to A.I. and China.
這場醜聞引發了川普政府內部圍繞人工智慧及對華政策的新一輪內鬥。
Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick, a prominent voice in favor of allowing U.S. companies like Nvidia to sell products in Asia, has faced accusations from others in the administration and in Congress that his department has been too cozy with Nvidia and Mr. Huang.
商務部長霍華德·盧特尼克一直積極主張允許英偉達等美國企業向亞洲銷售產品。如今，政府內部其他官員和國會議員指責他領導的商務部與英偉達及黃仁勛的關係過於密切。 
Scott Bessent, the Treasury secretary, said on Friday that his department and others had supported putting Megaspeed on a trade blacklist, but that only the Commerce Department could take that step. The Commerce Department said that such a move would be an administration-wide decision.
財長史考特·貝森特週五表示，財政部和其他部門支持將Megaspeed列入貿易黑名單，但只有商務部才有權採取這一步驟。商務部表示，此類決定需要整個政府共同作出。
Mr. Bessent said on Sunday that Nvidia should look externally for a new chief executive. On “Sunday Morning Futures” on Fox News, he said that the company needs “a dramatic shake-up.”
貝森特週日表示，英偉達應該從公司外部尋找新任首席執行官。他在福克斯新聞的《週日早間期貨》節目中說，該公司需要「一場大刀闊斧的變革」。
廣告
He added that the company’s board and outside counsel within the next 10 days needed to answer questions ​about the company’s culture of compliance with export ⁠restrictions.
他還表示，英偉達董事會及外部法律顧問必須在未來10天內，就公司遵守出口管制規定的合規文化回應外界的質疑。
