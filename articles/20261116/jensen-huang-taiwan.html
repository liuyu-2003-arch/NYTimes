台灣民眾如何看待「國民爸爸」黃仁勛醜聞

Tears and Disbelief in Taiwan, Where Jensen Huang Is a Celebrity

袁莉 • 2026-11-16
When the news broke in Taiwan on Thursday night that Jensen Huang had resigned as Nvidia’s chief executive, Ray Wang was too upset to sleep. He drank a whole bottle of red wine and prayed for Mr. Huang.
週四晚間，黃仁勛辭去英偉達首席執行官職務的消息傳到台灣時，王永泰難過得睡不著。他喝了整整一瓶紅酒，還為黃仁勛祈禱。
A hairstylist, Mr. Wang, has been cutting Mr. Huang’s hair, watching it change from salt-and-pepper to silver, for nearly a decade.
王永泰是一名髮型師，近十年來一直為黃仁勛剪頭髮，看著他的頭髮從花白變成銀灰。
“Maybe they’ll bring him back,” Mr. Wang said, “the way Apple brought back Steve Jobs.”
「也許他們會請他回來，」王永泰說，「就像蘋果當年請回史提夫·賈伯斯一樣。」
Mr. Huang has many well-wishers like this in Taiwan. An American citizen, he moved to the United States when he was 9 years old, but he was born on the island and enjoys a godlike status there. Fans mobbed him at night markets and lined up for autographs. Restaurants where he has eaten hang his photo, Hollywood-celebrity style. He has been hailed as the “pride of Taiwan” and affectionately called “the people’s dad.” The frenzy around his visits became known as “Jensanity,” a play on the “Linsanity” that followed Jeremy Lin, the Taiwanese-American professional basketball player.
在台灣，像王永泰這樣關心黃仁勛的人還有很多。黃仁勛是美國公民，九歲時移居美國，但他出生在台灣，在當地享有近乎神一般的地位。粉絲在夜市圍住他，排隊索要簽名。他光顧過的餐廳像展示好萊塢明星一樣掛出他的照片。他被譽為「台灣之光」，還被親切地稱為「國民爸爸」。人們甚至仿照當年台裔美國職業籃球運動員林書豪引發的「林來瘋」(Linsanity)熱潮，創造出「仁來瘋」(Jensanity)一詞來形容這種現象。

Mr. Huang co-founded Nvidia in 1993, and it became one of the world’s most valuable companies in recent years by making advanced chips for artificial intelligence. Over the decades, Mr. Huang made Nvidia’s relationship with Taiwan personal.
黃仁勛於1993年與他人共同創辦英偉達，近年來，英偉達憑藉生產用於人工智能的先進晶片成為全球最有價值的公司之一。幾十年來，黃仁勛把英偉達與台灣的商業關係經營成了一種個人情誼。
Taiwan is home to TSMC, the premier semiconductor chip maker that produces roughly 90 percent of the world’s most advanced chips and is the main reason Taiwan’s economy is projected to grow around 12 percent this year. It is Taiwan’s most important company, and Nvidia is TSMC’s biggest customer.
台灣是台積電的所在地。台積電是首屈一指的半導體晶片製造商，生產全球約90%的最先進晶片，也是台灣今年經濟預計增長約12%的主要原因。台積電是台灣最重要的企業，英偉達則是它最大的客戶。
Mr. Huang’s resignation followed reports of affairs, one with an Nvidia executive and another with a businesswoman, Alice Huang, whom U.S. intelligence officials suspected of providing intelligence for China. Beijing has denied the allegation.
黃仁勛辭職之前，媒體接連曝光了他的婚外情，其中一段涉及英偉達的一名高管，另一段則涉及商界女性黃樂(Alice Huang)。美國情報官員懷疑黃樂為中國提供情報。北京方面否認了這一指控。
His downfall landed with dismay and disbelief in Taiwan, where he had effectively cultivated relationships. Mr. Huang knew the people who made his chips and servers, and he made a point of crediting them in public.
黃仁勛的失勢令台灣民眾感到震驚和難以置信。他一直在台灣用心經營人脈。他熟悉那些為英偉達生產晶片和伺服器的人，也總是特意在公開場合肯定他們的貢獻。
He went to great lengths to show his appreciation for business partners — and get what he wanted. Last November, when he needed TSMC to allocate more production capacity to Nvidia, he flew to Taiwan and sat down for hot pot with its senior executives, Liang-Rong Chen, a tech journalist who was there, told me. He patiently signed whiskey bottles for its employees.
他不遺餘力地表達對商業夥伴的賞識，並藉此爭取自己想要的東西。台灣的科技記者陳良榕告訴我，去年11月，當黃仁勛需要台積電為英偉達提供更多產能時，他飛往台灣，與台積電高管一起吃火鍋。他還耐心地為台積電員工在威士忌酒瓶上簽名。 
Mr. Chen explained why that mattered. Squeezing more chips out of a busy factory took many managers willing to rearrange production and staff willing to work overtime. Mr. Chen, who writes the newsletter Tech Taiwan, suspected they would not have been as eager to do it for other American tech giants because none of them had gone to the great lengths as Mr. Huang in cultivating relationships.
撰寫科技電子報《胡說科技》的陳良榕解釋了這些舉動為什麼重要。讓已經滿負荷運轉的工廠生產更多晶片需要許多管理人員願意重新安排生產計劃，也需要員工願意加班。他認為，如果換成其他美國科技巨頭，台積電的員工未必會如此積極地配合，因為沒有哪家公司像黃仁勛那樣，在培養關係上投入如此多的心力。
A longtime semiconductor analyst, who was not authorized to speak publicly, told me that without Nvidia there would be no A.I. as we see it today. Suppliers have grown accustomed to taking their cues from Mr. Huang, he said, who had a sense of where the technology was headed and was able to persuade the companies to go along with him despite the risks at times. With him gone, the analyst wondered whose judgment they would trust.
一名長期研究半導體行業的分析師告訴我，沒有英偉達，就不會有我們今天所看到的人工智慧。由於未經授權公開發言，這名分析師要求匿名。他說，供應商們已經習慣了聽從黃仁勛的指引。黃仁勛對技術的發展方向有著敏銳的判斷，即使有時需要承擔風險，也能說服這些公司跟隨他的步伐。如今黃仁勛離開了，這名分析師不禁要問，今後他們還會信任誰的判斷。
“We hope things won’t change too much because he understands Taiwan better than anyone.” Chin-Tsang Ho, Taiwan’s deputy minister of economic affairs, told me just hours before Mr. Huang stepped down.
「我們還是希望這個變化不要太大啦，因為他是最懂得台灣的人，」就在黃仁勛辭職前幾小時，台灣經濟部次長何晉滄告訴我。
廣告
Four days before Mr. Huang’s resignation, TSMC was arranging a call with Mr. Huang to discuss plans for the next few years, said Chun-Hsien Yeh, who heads Taiwan’s National Development Council and sits on TSMC’s board. Mr. Yeh told me he expected Mr. Huang’s resignation would have little impact on TSMC, however. Demand for A.I. chips was already exceeding supply, and other customers, including AMD, needed them, too. “If Nvidia takes less,” he said. “others will take more.”
台灣國家發展委員會主委、台積電董事葉俊顯說，黃仁勛辭職四天前，台積電還正安排與黃仁勛通話，討論未來幾年的計劃。不過，他告訴我，預計黃仁勛的辭職不會對台積電產生太大影響。人工智慧晶片的需求已經超過供應，包括AMD在內的其他客戶也需要這些晶片。「NVIDIA要的少的話，」他說，「其他人就多。」 
Quite a few people I talked to, including semiconductor insiders, said they believed that Mr. Huang didn’t deserve the treatment. “We felt the process didn’t seem quite fair,” Da-Xiang Hu, a tech professional in Taipei, told me. “We felt it all happened too fast.”
我採訪的許多人，包括半導體行業內部人士，都認為黃仁勛不應該受到這樣的對待。「這次的事情在程序上不太公正，」台北科技業從業者胡大橡告訴我，「太快了。」
A few even saw his downfall in conspiratorial terms, imagining that other American business and political leaders grew wary of the growing influence of tech executives of Taiwanese and Chinese descent and worked to push Mr. Huang out.
少數人甚至從陰謀論角度看待他的失勢，猜測其他美國商界和政界領袖對台灣和華裔科技高管日益增長的影響力感到警惕，因此設法將黃仁勛排擠出局。
Other people were annoyed that their Nvidia shares fell. In a place where many people own shares in TSMC, social media quickly filled with joking anxieties about the fate of that company. On Threads, one user posted a photo of TSMC’s chief executive, Che-Chia Wei, captioning it, “Please don’t have an affair. Please. Please.” A commenter responded, “If you do have an affair, please don’t choose a Chinese spy. Please ” Both the post and the comment were liked tens of thousands of times.
另一些人則因英偉達股價下跌而惱火。在台灣，許多人持有台積電股票，社群媒體上很快出現了很多對這家公司的命運帶有調侃意味的擔憂。在Threads上，一名用戶發布了台積電董事長魏哲家的照片，配文寫道：「拜託你不要外遇，拜託拜託。」一名網友回覆：「如果你要外遇不要選中國間諜就好，拜託了。」這條帖子和評論都獲得了數萬次點讚。
Many Taiwanese commentators and social media users are supportive of Mr. Huang. They thanked him for bringing business to the island and for shining a spotlight on its companies.
許多台灣評論人士和社群媒體用戶支持黃仁勛。他們感謝他為台灣帶來生意，並讓台灣公司受到關注。
Emmy Hu, a financial content creator, wrote on Facebook that other tech giants had squeezed Taiwanese suppliers on price. “His generosity toward Taiwan was unusual,” she wrote of Mr. Huang. Yu Ting-hao, a financial commentator, called Mr. Huang “Taiwan’s biggest advocate” on his YouTube program. Kang Wen-teng, who runs a hot pot restaurant in Taichung City, told me he wanted people to remember what Mr. Huang had done for Taiwan. “Thank you, Papa Huang,” he wrote on Threads.
財經內容創作者胡采蘋在Facebook上寫道，其他科技巨頭曾在價格上壓榨台灣供應商。「他對台灣的慷慨是少見的，」她這樣評價黃仁勛。財經評論員游庭皓在自己的YouTube節目中稱黃仁勛是「台灣科技產業最重要的代言人」。台中市一家火鍋店老闆康文騰（音）告訴我，他希望人們記住黃仁勛為台灣所做的一切。「謝謝，黃爸爸，」他在Threads上寫道。 
Still, the accusations that Mr. Huang had an affair with a woman suspected of being a Chinese intelligence asset troubled even his fans. Da-Xiang Hu, the tech professional in Taipei, told me how moved she was whenever Mr. Huang spoke about Taiwan abroad. After decades of Chinese pressure that has kept Taiwan isolated internationally, hearing him say “Taiwan” mattered to her. At the same time, she said, she feels uneasy about Mr. Huang’s public push to sell advanced chips to China. In a Threads post in which she thanked him, Ms. Hu wondered whether Nvidia’s chips might someday help a Chinese missile strike Taiwan more precisely. “I wish you well,” she wrote, “and I hope Taiwan stays safe.”
不過，即使是黃仁勛的支持者，也對他被指與一名涉嫌為中國情報機構提供情報的女性發生婚外情感到不安。台北科技業從業者胡大橡告訴我，每當黃仁勛在海外談起台灣時，她都很感動。幾十年來，中國的施壓使台灣在國際上遭到孤立，因此，聽到黃仁勛公開說出「台灣」二字，對她有著特殊的意義。但與此同時，黃仁勛公開推動向中國出售先進晶片也讓她感到不安。在Threads上一則感謝黃仁勛的帖子中，胡大橡擔心，英偉達的晶片有朝一日是否可能幫助中國的導彈更精確地打擊台灣。「祝福你，」她寫道，「也祝福台灣平安。」
In Taiwan, there is even chatter that maybe Mr. Huang could come back and become the chief executive or a board member of TSMC.
在台灣，甚至有人開始議論，也許黃仁勛可以回來，出任台積電首席執行官或董事會成員。
To Mr. Wang, the hairstylist, Mr. Huang was both a visionary entrepreneur and an old friend. He was planning to write to him. “If he comes back to Taiwan,” he said, “I’m sure he’d come in for a haircut.”
在髮型師王永泰看來，黃仁勛既是一位富有遠見的企業家，也是一位老朋友。他正打算給黃仁勛寫信。「如果他回到台灣，」他說，「我相信他一定還會來找我剪頭髮。」
