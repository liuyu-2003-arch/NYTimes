黃仁勛的婚外情祕密是如何洩漏的

Jensen Huang Carried a Secret for More Than a Year. It Led to His Downfall.

TRIPP MICKLE, ANA SWANSON, 孟建國, JONATHAN SWAN, JULIAN E. BARNES, MAGGIE HABERMAN • 2026-11-16
Jensen Huang was agitated.
黃仁勛焦躁不安。
It was September 2025, and The New York Times had just called his company about a Singaporean data center firm called Megaspeed that had been led by a Chinese woman named Alice Huang, or Huang Le. The Times was reporting that Mr. Huang, the chief executive of the chip maker Nvidia, had a close relationship with her.
那是2025年9月，《紐約時報》剛剛致電他的公司，詢問一家名為Megaspeed的新加坡數據中心公司。該公司曾由一名叫黃樂(Alice Huang)的中國女性領導。《紐約時報》當時報導稱，晶片製造商英偉達的首席執行官黃仁勛與她關係密切。
Mr. Huang, who Nvidia employees say could have a volcanic temper, wanted to know what The Times was insinuating. Though he didn’t deny that he knew the woman, he was bothered by the suggestion that they might be close, people familiar with the discussion said.
當時，在英偉達員工口中脾氣非常火爆的黃仁勛想知道，《紐約時報》究竟在暗示什麼。儘管他沒有否認認識這名女性，但他對兩人關係可能很密切的暗示感到不滿。
Nvidia mounted an aggressive defense. Madison Huang, Mr. Huang’s daughter who acted as her father’s chief of staff, pressed the company’s communications team to push back forcefully. They also had reinforcements, a crisis communications firm called Heller.
英偉達展開了強力辯護。黃仁勛的女兒黃敏珊是父親的幕僚長，她敦促公司公關部門予以強力反擊。此外，他們還得到了危機公關公司Heller的支援。
廣告
They insisted that Mr. Huang and the woman from Megaspeed just had a business relationship.
他們堅稱，黃仁勛和Megaspeed的那名女性只是業務關係。
Over the next 13 months, Mr. Huang stuck with his story, even after The Times reported last month that the U.S. had gathered intelligence showing that Ms. Huang was secretly working with China’s top spy agency.
接下來的13個月裡，黃仁勛堅持這一說法。即使《紐約時報》上個月報導稱，美國已收集到情報，顯示黃樂祕密為中國最高間諜機構工作，他也沒有改變說辭。
But last weekend, facing new revelations about the relationship, Mr. Huang finally came clean to Nvidia’s general counsel. He had been having an affair with the woman, after all, said the people familiar with the discussions.
但上週末，面對有關這段關係的新爆料，黃仁勛終於向英偉達總法律顧問坦白。據熟悉討論情況的人士透露，他確實曾與黃樂有過婚外情。
The relationship with the Megaspeed executive wasn’t Mr. Huang’s only secret.
與Megaspeed高管的關係並不是黃仁勛唯一的祕密。
On Wednesday, a Times reporter called Mr. Huang to ask him about a second extramarital relationship. This time, it was an affair with an executive who reported to him.
週三，《紐約時報》一名記者致電黃仁勛，詢問他關於第二段婚外情的情況。這一次是與一名向他彙報的女性高管。
Nvidia’s board of directors soon convened an emergency meeting to discuss Mr. Huang’s future with his company. Still, it was unimaginable to think of Nvidia without him.
英偉達董事會很快召開緊急會議，討論黃仁勛在公司的未來。儘管如此，人們仍難以想像沒有黃仁勛的英偉達會是什麼樣子。
廣告
Less than 24 hours later, Mr. Huang resigned.
不到24小時後，黃仁勛辭職了。
The revelations about Mr. Huang, 63, set off a cascade of events that have reverberated from Silicon Valley to Washington to Beijing. Administration officials are now reconsidering A.I. export regulations, which could hurt some Silicon Valley businesses but tilt the A.I. race with China decidedly in America’s favor.
關於現年63歲的黃仁勛的這些披露引發了一系列連鎖反應，從矽谷到華盛頓、再到北京，都產生了迴響。美國政府官員現在正在重新考慮人工智慧出口管制，此舉雖可能對部分矽谷企業造成衝擊，但將使美國在與中國的人工智慧競爭中佔據明顯優勢。
It has also brought into public view a feud among several senior administration officials and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick. On Friday, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent told CNBC that his department had been pushing Commerce for months to crack down on the flow of U.S. technology to China.
此事也讓幾名政府高級官員與商務部長霍華德·盧特尼克之間的不和公開化。週五，財政部長史考特·貝森特告訴CNBC，他的部門數月來一直敦促商務部打擊美國技術流向中國。
Now Nvidia, which was in many ways propping up the A.I. boom, which, in turn, was propping up the U.S. economy, is operating without a man many consider to be one of the most indispensable executives in the history of American business.
如今，英偉達正在缺少一位被許多人視為美國商界歷史上最不可或缺的高管的情況下運營。這家公司在許多方面支撐著人工智慧熱潮，而人工智慧熱潮又支撐著美國經濟。
This is the story — based on a year of reporting, documents and discussions with more than 100 people, including current and former Nvidia executives and government officials who spoke on the condition of anonymity — of how Mr. Huang’s secret slowly spilled into the open.
本文基於一年的報導、文件以及與100餘人的訪談——其中包括英偉達現任及前任高管，以及要求匿名接受採訪的政府官員——講述黃仁勛的祕密如何慢慢洩漏到公眾視野。
What is Megaspeed?
Megaspeed是什麼公司？
廣告
Last fall, business was booming for Nvidia. Its soaring stock price had it within a whisper of becoming the world’s first $5 trillion company.
去年秋天，英偉達生意興隆。飆升的股價讓它距離成為世界首家市值5萬億美元公司僅一步之遙。
Mr. Huang joined President Trump in September for a formal state dinner at Windsor Castle hosted by King Charles III. Mr. Trump lavished praise on Mr. Huang at a gathering of business and tech leaders hosted by Prime Minister Keir Starmer.
9月，黃仁勛與川普總統一同出席查爾斯三世國王在溫莎城堡舉辦的正式國宴。在英國首相斯塔默主持的一場商界和科技領袖聚會上，川普對黃仁勛大加讚賞。
“You’re taking over the world, Jensen,” Mr. Trump said.
「你正在接管世界，詹森，」川普說。
But at Nvidia’s headquarters in Santa Clara, Calif., some of Mr. Huang’s advisers were wrestling with The Times’s questions about Megaspeed. The company had purchased more than $2 billion of Nvidia technology in a year, making it one of the largest buyers of A.I. chips in Southeast Asia.
但在英偉達位於加州聖克拉拉的總部，黃仁勛的一些顧問正忙於應對《紐約時報》關於Megaspeed的提問。這家公司在一年內購買了超過20億美元的英偉達技術，成為東南亞最大的人工智慧晶片買家之一。
It was an example of the kind of middlemen popping up across the region. Companies like Megaspeed were legally buying American chips and building A.I. data centers that rented computing power to Chinese companies. But the U.S. government suspected that some, including Megaspeed, were breaking the law by funneling chips to China.
這正是該地區如雨後春筍般湧現的中間商的典型例子。像Megaspeed這樣的公司合法購買美國晶片，建設人工智慧數據中心，向中國公司出租算力。但美國政府懷疑，包括Megaspeed在內的一些公司正在違法將晶片轉運到中國。
Nvidia’s sales from Singapore were swelling. By last summer, the island city-state accounted for 22 percent of the company’s quarterly total sales, or $10 billion, making it the second-largest chip buying region after the United States.
英偉達來自新加坡的銷售額不斷攀升。到去年夏天，這個城市國家占該公司季度總銷售額的22%，即100億美元，成為僅次於美國的第二大晶片購買地區。
Over the previous two years, Mr. Huang had been seen twice with Ms. Huang in public at industry gatherings. She waited behind him patiently in a pink dress as he passed out noodles from a restaurant in Taipei to onlookers and spoke to the Taiwanese press. Then she climbed into the car with him to be shuttled away.
過去兩年裡，黃仁勛曾兩次與黃樂一同在行業聚會上公開露面。有一次是黃仁勛在台北一家餐廳向圍觀者分發麵條，並與台灣媒體交談。她穿著粉色連衣裙，耐心地跟在他身後。然後她和他一起上車離開。
A senior Nvidia official told The Times that the dinner attended by Mr. Huang, Ms. Huang and several industry executives was a “business dinner.”
一名英偉達高級官員告訴《紐約時報》，黃仁勛、黃女士和幾名行業高管參加的那次晚餐是「商務晚宴」。
On another occasion, Ms. Huang was chatting at an industry party at a Taipei bar when she offered to ask Mr. Huang to join them. “She said, ‘I bet you guys I can get Jensen here,’ and then Jensen immediately showed up,” said Parker Schmitt, who is chief executive of NetThunder, a data software provider. Shortly after, Mr. Huang arrived and drank a whiskey shot with the group. He left the party, which he’d been invited to, shortly afterward with two other Nvidia executives who had joined him.
還有一次，黃樂在台北一家酒吧參加行業聚會時正與人聊天，主動提出請黃仁勛加入他們。「她說，『我跟你們打賭，我能把詹森叫來』，然後黃仁勛立刻出現了，」數據軟體提供商NetThunder首席執行官帕克·施密特說。不久之後，黃仁勛到了，和大家一起喝了一杯威士忌。隨後，他與另外兩名一同前來的英偉達高管離開了這個他受邀參加的派對。
After the article about Megaspeed and Mr. Huang’s relationship with Alice Huang appeared that October, Nvidia changed how it reported geographic sales. Singapore vanished from its financial disclosures as it started reporting sales based on “customers’ headquarters” rather than “billing location.” The company had previously said that large customers billed through Singapore, where taxes can be lower, but that products “are almost always shipped elsewhere.”
那年10月，關於Megaspeed以及黃仁勛與黃樂關係的報導發表後，英偉達改變了報告地區銷售的方式。隨著公司開始根據「客戶總部」而非「帳單地點」來報告銷售額，新加坡從其財務披露中消失了。公司此前曾表示，大客戶通過新加坡開帳單，那裡稅率較低，但產品「幾乎總是運往其他地方」。
Late that month, Mr. Huang took a convention center stage in Washington in his signature leather jacket and black jeans. He trumpeted his company’s lead in A.I. chips and praised Mr. Trump for focusing on increasing the nation’s energy supplies. He thanked everyone for their work “making America great again.”
當月下旬，黃仁勛穿著標誌性的皮夾克和黑色牛仔褲，登上華盛頓一個會議中心的舞台。他宣揚公司在人工智慧晶片領域的領先地位，並讚揚川普致力於增加國家能源供應。他感謝所有人為了「讓美國再次偉大」所做的工作。
廣告
The next day, as Mr. Trump was flying to South Korea to meet with China’s leader Xi Jinping, the president told reporters he was planning to speak to Mr. Xi about Nvidia’s most advanced A.I. chip, known as Blackwell.
第二天，當川普飛往韓國與中國領導人習近平會晤時，總統告訴記者，他計劃與習近平談論英偉達最先進的人工智慧晶片Blackwell。
Some people in government interpreted Mr. Trump’s comments as a signal that he might lift prohibitions on China’s access to America’s best technology — an outcome that could be a boon to Nvidia’s business, even as it flew in the face of longstanding policy goals to limit China’s access to American technology and protect America’s lead in artificial intelligence. (A White House spokesman declined to comment.)
政府中一些人將川普的言論解讀為信號：他可能解除中國獲得美國頂尖技術的禁令。這一結果可能利好英偉達業務，儘管它與限制中國獲得美國技術、保護美國在人工智慧領域領先地位的長期政策目標相悖。（白宮發言人拒絕置評。）
The possibilities were huge for Mr. Huang. Though Mr. Trump had cleared the company to sell some of its lower-performing chips to China, Beijing had banned tech companies from buying them. The offer of Blackwell chips had the potential to break that impasse.
這對黃仁勛來說蘊含著巨大的機遇。儘管川普已批准公司向中國出售一些性能較低的晶片，但北京禁止科技公司購買。Blackwell晶片的提議有可能打破僵局。
But after Cabinet officials, including Secretary of State Marco Rubio and the U.S. trade representative, Jamieson Greer, objected on national security grounds, Mr. Trump abandoned the idea.
但在國務卿馬爾科·魯比奧和美國貿易代表賈米森·格里爾等內閣官員以國家安全為由反對後，川普放棄了這一想法。
Roughly a year ago, Mr. Trump was briefed about how American spy agencies had surveillance of Ms. Huang, the Megaspeed executive, whom they had determined was passing information to Chinese intelligence services. The surveillance revealed that she had developed a close relationship with Mr. Huang.
大約一年前，川普聽取了有關美國間諜機構監視Megaspeed高管黃樂的簡報。他們認定她正在向中國情報機構傳遞信息。監視顯示她與黃仁勛發展了親密關係。
It was an alarming development for national security officials who long worried that Mr. Huang was too nonchalant about concerns that his chips could fall into the hands of the Chinese military or intelligence.
對國家安全官員來說，這是一個令人擔憂的事態發展。他們長期擔心黃仁勛對其晶片可能落入中國軍方或情報部門之手的問題過於漫不經心。
廣告
Though Mr. Trump chose not to do anything with the information, it raised questions inside the U.S. government about Mr. Huang’s judgment. It also raised the possibility that Ms. Huang may have gathered insight through Mr. Huang into Mr. Trump’s thinking on artificial intelligence, a vital issue of importance to Washington and Beijing.
儘管川普選擇不對信息採取任何行動，但它在美國政府內部引發了對黃仁勛判斷力的質疑。這同時也引發了一種可能性：黃樂可能通過黃仁勛了解到川普在人工智慧問題上的想法——這是對華盛頓和北京都至關重要的議題。
For reasons that remain unknown, government officials didn’t tell Mr. Huang about the intelligence. Law enforcement officials sometimes warn Americans who are being targeted by a foreign government’s intelligence service — a practice known as a “defensive briefing.” In weighing whether to give such a warning, officials typically consider the risks of tipping off the target of their own surveillance, which in this case would have been Megaspeed’s Ms. Huang.
出於仍未知的原因，政府官員沒有告訴黃仁勛這些情報。執法官員有時會對成為外國政府情報機構目標的美國人做出警告——這種做法被稱為「防禦性簡報」。在權衡是否發出此類警告時，官員通常考慮打草驚蛇的風險，在本案中意味著驚動Megaspeed的黃樂。
Chips for China
對中國出售的晶片
Mr. Huang didn’t abandon his vision of selling chips to China. He continued to argue that that would make Chinese companies depend on American technology and create competition for China’s tech giant, Huawei. It would also be good for his business.
黃仁勛沒有放棄向中國出售晶片的願景。他繼續主張，這將使中國公司依賴美國技術，並對中國科技巨頭華為形成競爭。這也有利於他的生意。
In December, he returned to Washington to press his case. He met with Mr. Trump at the White House and proposed selling a less powerful chip to China. Known as the H200, it was a generation older than the Blackwell, which was limited to the United States.
12月，他回到華盛頓力推此事。他在白宮會見川普，提議向中國出售一款性能較低的晶片。這款名為H200的晶片比僅限美國境內使用的Blackwell晶片落後一代。
Mr. Trump was open to the idea, but he wanted something in return: a 25 percent cut for the U.S. government of any chips sold to China. Mr. Huang agreed.
川普對這個想法持開放態度，但他想要回報：美國政府從任何對華銷售的晶片中抽取25%。黃仁勛同意了。
By March, pre-purchase orders from Chinese customers were rolling in. At the company’s annual conference in San Jose, Calif., Mr. Huang told a hotel ballroom packed with more than 100 reporters and influencers, including a woman who refers to herself as the “A.I. princess,” that Nvidia was receiving purchase commitments from China and starting to manufacture chips to sell.
3月，中國客戶的預購訂單滾滾而來。在加州聖何塞的公司年會上，面對把酒店宴會廳擠得滿滿當當的100多名記者和網紅（其中包括一位自稱「AI公主」的女性），黃仁勛說英偉達正在收到中國的採購承諾，並開始製造晶片出售。
In Washington, Mr. Rubio started to put together an update about the surveillance of Ms. Huang. The classified memo, based on highly sensitive intelligence, outlined Ms. Huang’s close relationship with the Nvidia founder and her contacts with China’s civilian spy agency, the Ministry of State Security.
在華盛頓，魯比奧開始整理關於監視黃樂的最新情況。這份基於高度敏感情報的機密備忘錄概述了黃樂與英偉達創始人的密切關係，以及她與中國非軍事間諜機構國家安全部的接觸。
The memo was circulated to only a handful of Cabinet officials. It was sent as the administration made plans for the president to visit Beijing in May for a summit with Mr. Xi. When it came time to extend invitations to more than a dozen chief executives for the trip, Mr. Huang was left off the list.
這份備忘錄只分發給了少數內閣官員。發出時，政府正在籌備總統5月訪問北京時與習近平的首腦會晤。向十多位首席執行官發出的隨行邀請中沒有黃仁勛。
But with little warning, Mr. Trump, who had seen news coverage of Mr. Huang’s exclusion, asked the Nvidia executive to meet up with Air Force One at a refueling stop in Alaska.
但看到黃仁勛被排除在外的新聞報導後，川普突然要求這位英偉達高管在空軍一號經停阿拉斯加加油期間與他會合。
When Mr. Rubio learned of the invitation, while en route to Alaska, he briefed the president on the intelligence showing Mr. Huang had a relationship with a Chinese intelligence asset. Mr. Trump was not particularly moved by the information. Instead, he celebrated Mr. Huang with a social media post after the chief executive boarded the plane, saying it was an honor to have “the Great Jensen Huang” on the trip to China.
在前往阿拉斯加途中得知該邀請的魯比奧向總統彙報了中國情報線人與黃仁勛有關係的情報。川普對這些信息不以為意，反而在黃仁勛登機後上社群媒體發帖讚揚他，稱「偉大的黃仁勛」能參加中國之行是榮幸。
On the ground in Beijing, Mr. Trump said he discussed sales of Nvidia chips to China with Mr. Xi. The Chinese government had been reluctant to give its approval to purchases of the less advanced H200 chips. But the conversation didn’t appear to persuade China to allow its companies to buy the Nvidia chips.
在北京，川普說他與習近平討論了向中國銷售英偉達晶片的問題。中國政府一直不願批准購買性能較低的H200晶片。但這次談話似乎沒有說服中國允許其公司購買英偉達晶片。
Some officials in Washington worried that Mr. Huang, to break the impasse, would push for approval to sell the more advanced Blackwells.
華盛頓一些官員擔心，黃仁勛為了打破僵局，會推動批准銷售更先進的Blackwell晶片。
During Nvidia’s all-hands meeting in August, he was asked what he considered to be Nvidia’s greatest risk.
在英偉達8月的全員大會上，有人問他認為英偉達最大的風險是什麼。
“It’s Washington, D.C.,” he said. “Policies that are made that are uninformed affect us, and they could affect us in a business way, and that could be irritating and frustrating.”
「是華盛頓，」他說。「不了解情況的人制定的政策會影響我們，可能在商業層面上影響我們，這可能令人惱火和沮喪。」
Midway through the meeting, his phone buzzed.
會議進行到一半，他的電話響了。
“There are only two people in the world that can call me,” he said. “One of them is President Trump.”
「世界上只有兩個人能給我打電話，」他說。「其中一個是川普總統。」
He picked up Mr. Trump’s call and stepped away.
他接了川普的電話，走開了。
The secret slips
祕密洩漏
Mr. Huang didn’t know it, but his secret was starting to spill.
黃仁勛不知道，他的祕密正在洩漏。
Late last month, The Times reached out to Nvidia for comment on a report that would publicly reveal for the first time that Ms. Huang was being surveilled. U.S. intelligence had believed she was an asset for Chinese intelligence who was feeding information to Beijing and was in a close relationship with Mr. Huang. (Among U.S. intelligence and national security officials, the relationship was routinely described more pointedly as an affair. )
上個月底，《紐約時報》聯繫英偉達，就一篇將首次披露黃樂正被監視的報導尋求置評。美國情報機構認為她是向北京傳遞信息的中國情報線人，並與黃仁勛關係密切。（在美國情報和國家安全官員中，這段關係常被更直白地描述為婚外情。）
Even after The Times reported in October 2025 that Megaspeed was under investigation for smuggling chips into China, Nvidia had kept doing business with the company. And Mr. Huang had kept up his relationship with Ms. Huang, according to U.S. officials who reviewed the intelligence.
即使《紐約時報》2025年10月報導Megaspeed因涉嫌向中國走私晶片而受到調查後，英偉達仍繼續與該公司做生意。據曾審閱情報的美國官員說，黃仁勛也繼續保持與黃樂的關係。
Megaspeed’s sister company in Malaysia bought at least $4.8 billion worth of advanced A.I. equipment containing Nvidia chips and technology from June 2024 to February 2026, according to trade records obtained from ImportGenius, a trade platform.
根據從貿易平台ImportGenius獲得的貿易記錄，Megaspeed在馬來西亞的姊妹公司從2024年6月到2026年2月購買了至少價值48億美元的先進AI設備，其中包含英偉達晶片和技術。
Mr. Huang told people around him that it was the first time he was learning that the U.S. government had been tracking his relationship with Ms. Huang.
黃仁勛告訴身邊的人，這是他第一次得知美國政府一直在追蹤他與黃樂的關係。
Nvidia insisted that the company that employed Ms. Huang, Megaspeed, was like any other regional customer and that it had previously investigated and debunked claims that the Singaporean company was smuggling chips to China.
英偉達堅稱，僱用黃樂的Megaspeed就是該地區的一個普通客戶，公司此前已經調查並駁斥了這家新加坡公司向中國走私晶片的說法。
(China has rejected the espionage claims. Alice Huang, the Chinese executive accused of spying for Beijing, has not responded to requests for comment.)
（中國否認了間諜指控。被指控為北京從事間諜活動的中國高管黃樂未回應置評請求。）
Mr. Huang jumped to his own defense. In an interview with Bloomberg News, he said that his interactions with Ms. Huang had been “all completely normal and not unusual.”
黃仁勛立即做出了自辯。在接受彭博新聞採訪時，他說他與黃樂的互動「完全正常，並不罕見」。
Other outlets jumped on the story. Nvidia roped in Heller, the crisis communications firm that had been working for the Huang family since early 2025, to help once again. The company represents high-profile individuals, with past clients including Harvey Weinstein and Anthony Weiner, as well as the business side of The Times.
其他媒體紛紛跟進。英偉達再次拉來Heller——這家危機公關公司自2025年初以來一直為黃家工作。Heller代理知名人物，過去的客戶包括哈維·韋恩斯坦和安東尼·韋納，以及《紐約時報》的商業部門。
On Nov. 6, The Times reported on Mr. Rubio’s memo and said U.S. intelligence had identified her as working with the Chinese intelligence service.
11月6日，《紐約時報》報導了魯比奧的備忘錄，並稱美國情報機構已確認她為中國情報機構工作。
Later that night, The Financial Times reported the U.S. government believed Mr. Huang’s relationship with the woman was romantic. Nvidia declined to comment.
當晚晚些時候，《金融時報》報導稱美國政府認為黃仁勛與這名女性的關係是情人關係。英偉達拒絕置評。
Tim Teter, Nvidia’s general counsel, who had been defending Mr. Huang, went to Mr. Huang’s house in Woodside, Calif. Mr. Huang confessed, said people familiar with the events that night.
一直為黃仁勛辯護的英偉達總法律顧問蒂姆·泰特前往黃仁勛位於加州伍德賽德的家中。知情人士說，黃仁勛坦白了。
Madison, his daughter, was flying to South Korea at the time. She was scheduled to participate in an A.I. event and meet local dignitaries. But the moment she landed, she was told the news and encouraged to fly back home.
他的女兒黃敏珊當時正飛往韓國。她原定參加一場AI活動並會見當地政要。但她一落地就被告知消息，並被建議飛回家。
Mr. Huang’s betrayal caused deep pain inside Nvidia. His family — including Madison; his son, Spencer; and son-in-law Nico Caprez — worked in prominent roles at the company. He had been married to Lori Huang for more than 40 years.
黃仁勛的背叛在英偉達內部造成深切的痛苦。他的家人——包括黃敏珊、兒子黃勝斌和女婿尼科·卡普雷斯——都在公司擔任重要職務。他與洛麗·黃結婚40多年。
After Mr. Huang’s confession, Mr. Teter urged Nvidia’s board to act. The board and Mr. Huang separately drafted public statements. The board said it “had full confidence in Jensen,” even as it planned to hire an outside law firm to investigate the matter.
黃仁勛坦白後，泰特敦促英偉達董事會採取行動。董事會和黃仁勛分別起草公開聲明。董事會表示「對黃仁勛充滿信心」，同時計劃聘請外部律師事務所調查此事。
Mr. Huang apologized in a message to the company. “I was unfaithful to my wife,” he said. “My actions hurt her and our children. There is no excuse for what I did. I am deeply sorry.”
黃仁勛在公司內部消息中道歉。「我對妻子不忠，」他說。「我的行為傷害了她和我們的孩子。我所做的事沒有任何藉口。我深感抱歉。」
He promised that he would earn back the confidence of employees and the board. Privately, he assured people around him, including Mr. Teter, who spoke with the board, that there were no other infidelities.
他承諾將贏回員工和董事會的信任。私下裡，他向包括與董事會溝通的泰特在內的人保證，沒有其他不忠行為。
Bloomberg News earlier reported on Mr. Teter’s visit. The Information, a technology news site, earlier reported on the board’s deliberations.
彭博新聞此前報導了泰特的到訪。科技新聞網站The Information此前報導了董事會的審議。
Over the next few days, Mr. Huang received texts and calls of encouragement from friends, such as Elon Musk of Tesla and SpaceX, OpenAI’s Sam Altman and the financier Larry Fink. President Trump also called him on Tuesday with a word of advice: Fight.
接下來幾天，黃仁勛收到朋友的鼓勵短訊和電話，如特斯拉和SpaceX的伊隆·馬斯克、OpenAI的薩姆·奧特曼和金融家拉里·芬克。川普總統週二也打電話給他，給了一句建議：戰鬥。
Some allies began to make plans to turn the tide. Mr. Huang told many of them that the woman wasn’t a spy. She was just a business customer.
一些盟友開始計劃扭轉局面。黃仁勛告訴他們中的許多人，黃樂不是間諜。她只是商業客戶。
But four days later, The Times contacted the company and Mr. Huang about another affair. This one was with a woman who directly reported to him, Kimberly Powell, a 48-year-old who led Nvidia’s health division. The two had been caught on camera in an affectionate moment during Nvidia’s annual conference in San Jose.
但四天後，《紐約時報》聯繫公司和黃仁勛，詢問另一段婚外情。這次是與直接向他彙報的英偉達健康部門負責人、48歲的金伯利·鮑威爾。兩人在聖何塞英偉達年會上被拍到有親密舉動。
The company declined to comment. Mr. Huang, who was reached by phone, said, “I don’t think you should be talking to me directly.” Then, he hung up.
公司拒絕置評。通過電話聯繫到的黃仁勛說：「我認為你不應該直接跟我談。」然後掛了電話。
After the news broke, the 10-member board called an emergency meeting. Members called in from remote locations and deliberated over a decision none of them ever thought they would have to make.
消息傳出後，10人董事會召開緊急會議。董事們從各地遠程接入，研究一個他們從未想過必須做出的決定。
The revelation of another infidelity showed that Mr. Huang, who had extolled the company’s code of conduct rules over the years, had been violating them. First, there was the relationship with a customer. Then, there was the relationship with an employee.
另一段婚外情的曝光表明，多年來一直推崇公司行為準則的黃仁勛一直在違反這些準則。首先是與客戶的關係。然後是與員工的關係。
But in the world of corporate governance, the worst thing a chief executive can do is lie, especially in a crisis. It is a reason they get fired.
但在公司治理世界裡，首席執行官能做的最糟糕的事就是撒謊，尤其是在危機中。這是他們被解僱的原因之一。
On Thursday morning, the company said Mr. Huang had resigned. The board had named Colette Kress, the company’s chief financial officer, as interim chief executive officer.
週四上午，公司表示黃仁勛已辭職。董事會任命公司首席財務官科萊特·克雷斯為臨時首席執行官。
Mr. Huang would have to surrender his life’s work.
黃仁勛將不得不放棄他畢生經營的企業。
In a series of extensive interviews last month with Emily Chang of Bloomberg TV, Mr. Huang acknowledged it was a high pressure role that required sacrifice.
在上個月與彭博電視台艾米麗·張的一系列詳盡訪談中，黃仁勛承認這是一個需要犧牲的高壓角色。
“It should not be an easy job,” he said. “It shouldn’t be a job you take for granted.”
「這不應該是一份輕鬆的工作，」他說。「這不應該是一份你理所當然該擁有的工作。」
He added that he hoped to be with the company for another 20 years.
他還說，他希望在公司再待20年。
