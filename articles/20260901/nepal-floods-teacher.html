洪水摧毀校舍前，尼泊爾學校校長疏散900名學生

A Principal Saved 900 Students Before His School Was Washed Away in Nepal

BINOD GHIMIRE, CLAIRE MOSES • 2026-09-01
Wednesday seemed like any other morning for Rajendra Dawadi, the principal of a school in Nepal.
對尼泊爾一所學校的校長拉金德拉·達瓦迪來說，上週三早晨似乎與往日無異。
“Everything was going normally,” he said.
「一切都很正常，」他說。
That changed around 9:10 a.m., when Mr. Dawadi was teaching a class at the Tribhuvan Trishuli School in Nuwakot, a district just north of Kathmandu, the country’s capital. A staff member burst into the classroom, shouting that a major flood was headed their way.
上午9點10分左右，情況發生了變化。當時達瓦迪正在首都加德滿都以北努瓦科特地區的特里布萬·特里舒爾學校上課。一名職工衝進教室，大喊一場大洪水正朝他們襲來。
The news began to spread as relatives living upstream sent warnings to anyone in the path of the fast-approaching flood waters.
消息開始傳開，上游的親屬紛紛向這場急速逼近的洪水即將席捲之處的所有人發出警告。
廣告
Mr. Dawadi had to make some quick decisions.
達瓦迪必須當機立斷。
He told all the children who were present — about 900 of the school’s total 1,643 students — to run to higher ground. Only older pupils, ages 11 to 18, were at school when the floods hit, which aided the quick evacuation, Mr. Dawadi said. (The younger students did not start class until 10 a.m.)
他告訴所有在校的孩子——學校總共1643名學生中的約900人——向高處跑。達瓦迪說，洪水來襲時，只有年齡較大的學生（11至18歲）在學校，這有助於快速疏散。（年紀較小的學生上午10點才開始上課。）
“They were old enough to run immediately,” he said in a phone interview on Monday. “It would not have been possible to save everyone, had kindergarten and primary school students also been there.”
他在週一接受電話採訪時說：「他們年齡夠大，可以立即跑開。如果幼稚園和小學的學生當時也在，就不可能救下所有人。」
Pupils who had arrived at the school earlier by bus were told to get back on them, and Mr. Dawadi instructed the drivers to leave the area as quickly as possible.
早些時候乘公車到校的學生被要求回到車上，達瓦迪指示司機儘快離開這裡。
“A couple of students became traumatized and fainted,” Mr. Dawadi said, adding that those children were transported to safety on teachers’ motorbikes.
「有幾個學生受到驚嚇暈倒了，」達瓦迪說，並補充說這些孩子被老師用摩托車送到了安全地帶。
“I thought that even if the warning turned out to be wrong, the worst it would cost us was one day of study,” Mr. Dawadi said. “But taking it lightly could mean the loss of hundreds of lives.”
「我當時想，即使警報是錯的，最壞的結果也就是浪費一天的學習時間，」達瓦迪說。「但如果掉以輕心，就可能意味著失去數百條生命。」
His swift evacuation plan saved those lives.
他迅速的疏散計劃挽救了許多生命。
Around 9:23 a.m., about 13 minutes after Mr. Dawadi heard the first warning, a deluge of water, mud and debris swept through the area. The school was destroyed in seconds, and at least one teacher remained missing as of Monday, Mr. Dawadi said.
上午9點23分左右，即在達瓦迪聽到第一次警告後大約13分鐘，一股裹挾著水和泥漿的洪流席捲了該地區。學校在幾秒鐘內被摧毀，達瓦迪說，截至週一，至少有一名教師仍然失蹤。
At least 4,200 people were missing in Nepal as of Monday, less than a week after a torrent of water tore through the Himalayan river valleys, sweeping away buildings, homes, roads and other infrastructure.
截至週一，尼泊爾至少有4200人失蹤。上週的洪流衝過喜馬拉雅山河谷，沖毀了建築物、房屋、道路和其他基礎設施。
廣告
Only a fraction of the thousands of missing had been identified among the dead, and officials said they expected the death toll to increase. So far, the authorities said, the disaster had killed nearly 1,000 people in Nepal.
數千名失蹤者中只有一小部分被確認為死者，官員們表示，預計死亡人數還會增加。當局表示，到目前為止，這場災害已在尼泊爾造成近1000人死亡。
The students’ rescue was a rarity among the reports of missing and dead and amid the destruction from the floods. In Bharatpur, a city southwest of the school, hundreds of bodies had washed up downstream. The local morgues had received far more bodies than they could accommodate.
這場洪水造成了嚴重破壞和大量人員傷亡，這些學生的獲救實屬罕見。在學校西南方的城市巴拉特普爾，從上游衝下來數百具屍體。這遠遠超出了當地停屍房的容納能力。
Over the weekend, rescue workers struggled to extricate workers trapped in hydropower tunnels. The Nepal Army said its thermal drones were not detecting any signs of life.
上週末，救援人員奮力解救被困在水電站隧洞中的工人。尼泊爾軍方表示，他們的熱成像無人機沒有探測到任何生命跡象。
The floods also destroyed miles of roads and disrupted communications. Video footage and images showed a debris-choked river wiping out bridges and partly burying towns in mud.
洪水還摧毀了大量道路，導致通訊中斷。影片片段和圖像顯示，滿是碎石的河流衝垮了橋樑，並使部分城鎮被泥漿掩埋。
“I am extremely emotional to see the school completely swept away,” said Mr. Dawadi, who was a student there before becoming the principal years later. “The school holds so many memories for me.”
「看到學校被完全沖走，我無比感慨，」達瓦迪說。他在成為校長之前曾是該校的學生。「這所學校承載了我太多的回憶。」
The building had been built over many years, he said. Now, all that remained were two school buses.
他說，學校的房子是花了多年建成的。現在只剩下兩輛校車。
廣告
“We saved lives, but now there is no place to teach the students,” Mr. Dawadi said. “I am already worried about how we can start teaching and learning again.”
「人是救下來了，但現在沒有地方教課了，」達瓦迪說。「我已經開始擔心怎麼才能重新開始上課和學習。」

Claire Moses是時報駐倫敦記者，主要報導突發新聞和熱點新聞。
