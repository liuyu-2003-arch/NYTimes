俄羅斯重返G20財長會議，歐洲不滿

Europe Protests as U.S. Invites Russian Minister to G20 Finance Meeting

JIM TANKERSLEY, ALAN RAPPEPORT • 2026-09-01
Russia’s finance minister made a surprise appearance at a gathering of top global economic policymakers on Monday after a prolonged absence, an invitation that was extended by the Trump administration but drew rebukes and protests from European officials committed to isolating Moscow for its war against Ukraine.
長期缺席國際舞台後，俄羅斯財長週一意外現身一場全球主要經濟政策制定者會議。川普政府向他發出了邀請，但此舉遭到歐洲官員譴責，他們一直因俄羅斯入侵烏克蘭而推動孤立莫斯科。
The summit of finance ministers and central bankers from the Group of 20 nations, in the mountain town of Asheville, was billed by the Treasury Department as a chance to find global cooperation on new economic punishments for Iran and new strategies to boost growth at a time of stubbornly high inflation and rising borrowing costs that are straining government budgets.
二十國集團(G20)財長和央行行長會議在北卡羅萊納州山城阿什維爾舉行，原本被美國財政部定位為一次尋求全球合作的機會，議題包括對伊朗實施新的經濟懲罰，以及在通膨居高不下、借貸成本上升給政府預算帶來壓力的背景下尋找促進經濟增長的新策略。
Instead, the meeting opened on Monday morning with European outrage over the Russian finance minister, Anton Siluanov, who was physically in attendance for the first time since Russia invaded Ukraine in 2022.
但會議週一上午開場時，歐洲對俄羅斯財長安東·西盧安諾夫表示強烈不滿。這是自2022年俄羅斯入侵烏克蘭以來他首次出席該會議。
Lars Klingbeil, the German vice chancellor and finance minister, told reporters that he had scolded Mr. Siluanov during an early session of the meeting for the invasion, demanding that Russia end the war and pledging ongoing support for Ukraine, which was not represented in person at the gathering.
德國副總理兼財長拉爾斯·克林拜爾告訴記者，在會議早些時候的一場討論中，他曾當面斥責西盧安諾夫，譴責俄羅斯的入侵行徑，要求結束戰爭，並承諾德國將繼續支持烏克蘭。烏克蘭並未派代表出席此次會議。
廣告
Serhiy Marchenko, Ukraine’s finance minister, is expected to participate by video conference on Monday afternoon in a meeting of finance ministers from the Group of 7 nations that France is leading. France is host of the G7 this year and will be holding the meeting on the sidelines of the broader G20 gathering.
烏克蘭財長謝爾蓋·馬爾琴科預計將於週一下午通過影片參加七國集團(G7)財長會議。此次會議由法國主持，於G20會議期間舉行。法國今年是G7的輪值主席國。
European leaders have worked to isolate Russia in the global economy and on the diplomatic stage, and, at a moment in the war when Moscow has relentlessly bombarded Kyiv, they did not want anything at the meeting to suggest that has changed.
歐洲領導人一直致力於在全球經濟和外交舞台上孤立俄羅斯。在莫斯科持續猛烈轟炸基輔的戰爭背景下，他們不希望會議上的任何舉動讓人以為這一孤立立場已經鬆動。
“Receiving the Russian finance minister here sends a signal I find troubling,” Mr. Klingbeil told reporters on Monday afternoon. “I would have preferred clear positioning from the U.S. side that he not be received like a regular guest.”
克林拜爾週一下午對記者表示：「俄羅斯財政部長獲邀來到這裡，我認為這是一個令人不安的信號。我更希望看到美方明確表態，不應該像接待一名常規來賓那樣接待他。」
Mr. Siluanov’s presence was not announced publicly by American officials before the summit, and White House officials did not respond to questions on Sunday over whether he had been invited. His appearance came as President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia was set to join President Xi Jinping of China and President Masoud Pezeshkian of Iran at a summit in Kyrgyzstan. China’s finance minister and the head of its central bank were, as is typical, also in attendance at the G20 meeting.
在峰會召開前，美國官員並未公開宣布西盧安諾夫將出席。白宮官員週日也沒有回答有關他是否受邀的問題。西盧安諾夫現身之際，俄羅斯總統弗拉基米爾·普丁正準備在吉爾吉斯斯坦參加一場峰會，與中國國家主席習近平以及伊朗總統馬蘇德·佩澤希齊揚會面。中國財長和央行行長也依慣例出席了G20會議。
Mr. Klingbeil said European representatives had unanimously demanded that their American hosts not include Mr. Siluanov in a traditional group photo from the summit, threatening to boycott if he was in the picture.
克林拜爾表示，歐洲代表一致要求美國東道主不要讓西盧安諾夫參加峰會傳統的集體合影，並威脅如果有他出現，他們就將抵制合影。
It was eventually taken without him, and with the Europeans.
最終，西盧安諾夫沒有參加拍攝，歐洲代表悉數出席。
廣告
Many Europeans were also upset that representatives from South Africa, a longtime member of the G20 and Africa’s largest economy, were not invited by the Treasury Department to Asheville. President Trump announced last year, after skipping a G20 summit hosted by South Africa, that he would exclude the country from this year’s meetings, citing a false narrative that white South Africans were being indiscriminately killed and that their land was being seized.
許多歐洲官員還對南非未被美國財政部邀請參加阿什維爾峰會感到不滿。南非是G20的長期成員國，也是非洲最大的經濟體。川普總統去年在缺席了南非主辦的G20峰會後宣布，將把南非排除在今年會議之外，他聲稱，南非白人正遭到不分青紅皂白的殺害，土地也正被強行奪走；但這一說法並無事實依據。
Administration officials invited a group of American business executives, from industries like banking and cryptocurrency, to meet with the government leaders in an effort to create networking opportunities between government officials and the private sector. They rebuffed requests to also include executives from other G20 countries. The moves have unsettled some attendees at the gathering; Mr. Klingbeil appeared to have left the first C.E.O. session to speak to reporters.
川普政府官員還邀請了包括銀行業和加密貨幣行業人士在內的一批美國企業高管與各國領導人會面，希望藉此為政府官員和私營部門創造交流機會。但對於將其他G20國家的企業高管也納入其中的請求，他們予以拒絕。這些做法令一些與會者感到不安。克林拜爾似乎還提前離開了首場CEO會議，隨後接受了記者採訪。
As he welcomed his international counterparts to the summit, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said the U.S. wanted to refocus the G20 on its original mission of accelerating global growth. He said countries needed to work together to reverse global economic imbalances that were harming many nations.
美國財長史考特·貝森特在歡迎各國代表與會時表示，美國希望讓G20重新聚焦於其最初的使命，即促進全球經濟增長。他表示，各國需要共同努力，扭轉正在損害許多國家的全球經濟失衡。
“A durable global economy cannot rest on beggar-thy-neighbor acts that stifle fair, market-based competition,” Mr. Bessent said.
貝森特表示：「一個持久的全球經濟不能建立在以鄰為壑、扼殺公平的市場競爭的行為之上。」
The summit comes at a pivotal moment, with the International Monetary Fund projecting that global growth will slow this year and inflation will rise. The decision by the United States to attack Iran in February disrupted the flow of oil and pushed energy prices higher, darkening the outlook for a world economy that has been battered by wars and a pandemic in recent years.
此次峰會召開之際，全球經濟正處於一個關鍵節點。國際貨幣基金組織(IMF)預計，今年全球經濟增長將放緩，通膨將上升。美國今年2月決定對伊朗發動的打擊行動擾亂了石油供應，推高了能源價格，給近年來飽受戰爭和疫情衝擊的全球經濟前景進一步蒙上陰影。
Huddled in a North Carolina mountain resort, officials were expected to discuss ways to reinforce global supply chains for commodities such as critical minerals, opportunities to increase international investment and strategies for reducing or restructuring debt. The conversations are taking place as the U.S. gross national debt just topped $40 trillion, creating anxiety among bondholders and raising fears about higher borrowing costs.
官員們預計將在北卡羅萊納州的一處山區度假勝地舉行討論，議題包括如何加強關鍵礦產等大宗商品的全球供應鏈、增加國際投資的機會，以及減少或重組債務的策略。與此同時，美國國債總額剛剛突破40萬億美元，這讓債券持有人感到焦慮，並引發了對借貸成本上升的擔憂。
廣告
“Mounting fiscal pressures, as evidenced by rising bond yields, and a stalled disinflation process — they are sources of worries both for markets and for policymakers,” Kristalina Georgieva, managing director of the I.M.F., told reporters ahead of the meetings.
IMF總裁克里斯塔利娜·格奧爾基耶娃在會前對記者表示：「不斷加劇的財政壓力（債券收益率上升就是明證）及通膨回落進程的停滯都令市場和政策制定者感到擔憂。」
Those concerns have been especially evident in the United States. In recent weeks, Mr. Bessent initiated surprising interventions in global currency markets and in the U.S. bond market in an effort to contain rising borrowing costs. Those measures have so far been only modestly effective, and they have drawn pushback from the European Union, which has seen its own currency suffer as a result of some of Mr. Bessent’s machinations.
這些擔憂在美國尤其明顯。最近幾週，貝森特出人意料地對全球外匯市場和美國債券市場進行了干預，試圖遏制不斷上升的借貸成本。到目前為止，這些措施的效果都比較有限，而且已經受到歐盟的反對，因為貝森特的一些操作導致歐盟貨幣承壓。
Global leaders also remain wary of the U.S.-Israeli war with Iran, which has driven up energy costs worldwide, disrupted supply chains and dragged on growth.
全球領導人對美國和以色列對伊朗的戰爭也仍然感到擔憂。這場戰爭推高了全球能源成本，擾亂供應鏈，並拖累經濟增長。
Unifying the G20 has been a challenge for the administration, as countries continue to cope with the uncertainty surrounding Mr. Trump’s trade policies and the steep tariffs that he has imposed on imports, along with fallout from the war.
對於川普政府而言，要讓G20形成統一立場一直是一個挑戰。各國仍在應對川普貿易政策帶來的不確定性，以及他對進口商品徵收的高額關稅，同時還要承受戰爭造成的連鎖影響。
American officials have asked allies to join their “Operation Economic Outcast” initiative, which aims to strangle the Iranian economy and resolve the conflict through economic sanctions. Mr. Bessent warned last week that countries that did not sever economic ties with Iran would also face U.S. sanctions.
美國官員已要求盟友加入其「經濟孤立」行動，該行動旨在通過經濟制裁扼殺伊朗經濟、解決衝突。貝森特上週警告稱，不與伊朗切斷經濟聯繫的國家也將面臨美國的制裁。
A senior Treasury official said in a briefing ahead of the Asheville meetings that the Iran plans would feature prominently in the discussions. Mr. Bessent signaled last week that the United States would have limited patience for countries that maintained economic ties with Iran, and that he hoped other countries would comply with the Trump administration’s demands to cut off Iran in order to avoid a broader global economic war.
一名美國財政部高級官員在阿什維爾會議召開前的吹風會上表示，對伊朗的相關計劃將成為會議討論的重點。貝森特上週表示，對於那些繼續與伊朗保持經濟聯繫的國家，美國政府的耐心將非常有限。他希望其他國家能夠配合川普政府的要求，切斷與伊朗的經濟聯繫，以避免演變成一場更廣泛的全球經濟戰爭。
廣告
“Why would I want to blow up the global financial system?” Mr. Bessent said.
「我怎麼會想要把全球金融體系搞垮？」貝森特說。
In another controversy, the Treasury has denied press credentials for attending the meetings to certain U.S.-based reporters for The New York Times and The Wall Street Journal, along with correspondents for Bloomberg News from several countries.
另一場爭議則涉及媒體。美國財政部拒絕向《紐約時報》和《華爾街日報》的部分美國記者以及來自多個國家的彭博新聞社記者發放參加此次會議的採訪證。
Germany’s finance ministry intervened with the Treasury after it initially denied credentials to German-based reporters from The Times and Bloomberg, who planned to travel with Mr. Klingbeil to Asheville.
德國財政部隨後介入，與美國財政部交涉。此前，美國財政部拒絕向幾名常駐德國、原計劃隨克林拜爾一同前往阿什維爾的《紐約時報》和彭博新聞社記者發放採訪證。
The ministry succeeded in securing a credential for the Times reporter, the newspaper’s Berlin bureau chief, but not the Bloomberg reporter.
德國方面成功為《紐約時報》記者（柏林分社社長）爭取到了證件，但彭博新聞社記者仍未獲批准。
A diplomatic official, speaking on the condition of anonymity under a customary practice in the official’s home country, said German officials viewed the denial of credentials for Bloomberg, an international news agency, as unprecedented and unacceptable.
一位外交官員（根據其本國慣例要求匿名）表示，德國官員認為拒絕向彭博新聞社這一國際通訊社發放證件是「前所未有且不可接受的」。

Jim Tankersley是時報柏林分社社長，領導對德國、奧地利和瑞士的報導。

Alan Rappeport是時報經濟政策記者，常駐華盛頓，負責報導財政部，並撰寫有關稅收、貿易和財政問題的文章。
