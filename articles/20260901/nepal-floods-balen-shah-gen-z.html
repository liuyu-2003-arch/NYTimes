如何重建家園？尼泊爾年輕總理面臨考驗

For Nepal’s Young Leader, Flood Recovery Will Be a Herculean Task

BINOD GHIMIRE, MUJIB MASHAL • 2026-09-01
Nepal’s government is led by a 36-year-old former engineer turned rapper, Balendra Shah, who swept to power just months ago on the back of youth anger over an economy troubled by corruption and mismanagement.
尼泊爾政府如今由36歲的前工程師、後來轉行說唱歌手的巴倫德拉·沙阿領導。就在幾個月前，年輕人對飽受腐敗和管理不善困擾的經濟感到憤怒，他乘勢一舉上台。
Now, he and his fledgling government face a herculean test after devastating floods ravaged his impoverished country. A majority of Mr. Shah’s 17 ministers are under 40, and they were still finding their way around governance before the disaster struck.
如今，毀滅性的洪水在這個貧困國家肆虐之後，他和組建不久的政府正面臨著一項艱巨的考驗。沙阿先生的17名部長中，大多數人都在40歲以下，在災難發生前，他們仍在摸索施政之道。
Five days after a deluge caused by a glacier breaking in the Himalayas flooded northern Nepal and parts of Tibet, the number of missing in Nepal jumped Monday to more than 4,700 and the death toll rose past 900 as the scale of the disaster became more apparent.
五天前，喜馬拉雅山冰川崩塌引發的洪水淹沒了尼泊爾北部和西藏部分地區。尼泊爾的失蹤人數在週一激增至4700多人，死亡人數超過900人，災難規模也變得更加清晰。
Mr. Shah has not spoken publicly since the floods. Rather, he has visited places affected by the disaster, wearing his trademark double-breasted suit, black shades and white sneakers. He has led meetings with his ministers, who have also been in the field overseeing emergency efforts.
洪水發生以來，沙阿一直沒有公開發表講話，而是身穿標誌性的雙排扣西裝、戴著黑色墨鏡、腳蹬白球鞋，走訪了受災地區。他主持了與部長們的會議，這些部長也一直在現場監督應急工作。
“There can be no greater pain than losing loved ones, homes and livelihoods,” Mr. Shah, who prefers communicating on social media to taking questions from reporters, said in a statement on Facebook on Sunday. He added, “Without making excuses, we have mobilized the entire state machinery on a war footing and are working around the clock to save lives and provide relief to every citizen in the affected areas.”
「沒有什麼比失去親人、家園和生計更令人痛苦，」沙阿於週日在Facebook發帖寫道，相較於接受記者提問，他更喜歡在社群媒體上交流。他還寫道：「我們沒有任何藉口，已經以戰時狀態動員了整個國家機器，正夜以繼日地拯救生命，並為受災地區的每一位公民提供救援。」
The task is daunting. Entire towns have been wiped out, and tens of miles of infrastructure are devastated. Rescue operations at tunnels of hydropower projects have been slow, with the military struggling to drill openings through mounds of debris.
這項任務極其艱巨。整座整座城鎮被夷為平地，數十公里範圍內的基礎設施遭到破壞。水電項目隧道的救援行動進展緩慢，軍方正艱難地在成堆的廢墟中鑽孔開道。
廣告
When Mr. Shah looks up from the immediate work of emergency response, he is likely to find a country with the deeper economic woes that inevitably follow natural disasters.
當沙阿從眼下緊急的響應工作中抬起頭來時，他很可能會發現，這個國家正面臨著自然災害後不可避免的更深層經濟困境。
That is bad news for Nepal, which was already one of the world’s poorest nations and struggled to meet its basic needs. But analysts say it is even more demoralizing for a government that was grappling with how to deliver on the populist promises that helped it defeat the old guard.
對尼泊爾來說，這是個壞消息，該國本已是世界上最貧窮的國家之一，連滿足基本需求都十分困難。但分析人士表示，對現任政府而言，這更令人沮喪，因為該政府正竭力兌現那些當初助其擊敗舊勢力的民粹主義承諾。
Mr. Shah’s strength has been in a populist wave he rode by responding to demands from a large disillusioned youth population forced to seek jobs abroad. Even before the floods, analysts said, he began to realize the honeymoon period was over. His grip over the social media narrative was waning as he faced the realities of insufficient funds for his agenda and an entrenched bureaucracy.
沙阿的優勢在於他順應了民粹主義浪潮，回應了大量被迫出國謀生、對現狀感到失望的年輕人的訴求。分析人士說，甚至在洪水發生之前，他就開始意識到，蜜月期已經結束。面對其議程資金不足和根深蒂固的官僚主義等現實問題，他對社群媒體話語權的掌控力正在減弱。
“This government’s focus has been ‘Let’s build this economy, let’s create jobs, let us create small and medium enterprises, let’s create start-ups,’” said Lila Pieters Yahia, the top United Nations official in the country. “For this government, who is putting the pillars, the foundation of a new economic model, having this hitting you now? You can imagine.”
「本屆政府的重點一直是『我們要建設經濟，我們要創造就業，我們要建立中小企業，我們要創辦初創企業』，」聯合國駐該國最高官員莉拉·彼得斯·葉海亞說。「對於這個正在為新經濟模式奠定支柱和基礎的政府來說，現在遭受這樣的打擊？你可以想像。」
Mr. Shah’s rise has been swift since he entered politics five years ago to run for mayor of Kathmandu, the country’s capital. His victory was an initial indication of how fed up young people were with politics as usual, as the country’s transition from monarchy and civil war had given way to misrule by entrenched dynastic parties swapping power.
自從五年前步入政壇競選該國首都加德滿都市長以來，沙阿的崛起十分迅速。他的勝利初步表明了年輕人對墨守成規的政治有多麼厭倦，因為該國從君主制和內戰過渡後，便陷入了輪流執政、根深蒂固的政治世家政黨的糟糕治理中。
The anger spilled onto the streets last year in protests started by Nepal’s Gen Z that toppled the previous government. After national elections this year, a large number of young and first-term lawmakers entered Parliament and Mr. Shah became prime minister, bringing many of the Gen Z protest leaders into his fold.
去年，這種憤怒蔓延到了街頭，由尼泊爾Z世代發起的抗議活動推翻了上一屆政府。在今年的全國大選之後，大量年輕人和首次當選者進入了議會，沙阿也成為總理，並將許多Z世代抗議領袖納入麾下。
廣告
The work of changing governance has not been easy.
改變治理模式的工作並非易事。
Nepal’s disaster response agency, a key institution, has long been caught in a tussle for power between government departments and has lacked the funding and expertise to plan vigilantly, analysts and former officials say.
分析人士和前官員表示，作為關鍵機構的尼泊爾災難響應機構長期以來一直陷入政府部門之間的權力鬥爭中，缺乏足夠的資金和專業知識來進行未雨綢繆的規劃。
Ajaya Dixit, the executive director of the Institute for Social and Environmental Transition, said Mr. Shah’s young team appeared to be doing its best in the face of a “colossal” disaster. But he said the problem was much deeper, with countries in the Himalayas ill prepared despite suffering nearly a dozen catastrophes over the past four decades.
社會與環境轉型研究所執行主任阿賈亞·迪克西特表示，面對一場「巨大」的災難，沙阿年輕的團隊似乎正在盡其所能。但他表示，問題要深遠得多，儘管過去40年來遭受了近十幾次災難，但喜馬拉雅地區的國家依然準備不足。
“What have the states learned from these episodic events?” he said. “The learning seems very limited.”
「這些國家從這些偶發事件中學到了什麼？」他說。「學到的東西似乎非常有限。」
Critics have accused Mr. Shah of using people’s dissatisfaction with the old ways of governance to centralize power in a small team of largely inexperienced young operators. That has created bottlenecks that have been apparent in some areas of the disaster response, where local administrations feel helpless because the central authorities have taken over command, analysts and former officials say.
批評人士指責沙阿利用人們對舊有治理方式的不滿，將權力集中在一個主要由缺乏經驗的年輕人員組成的小團隊手中。分析人士和前官員表示，這造成了在災難響應的某些領域顯而易見的瓶頸，在這些領域，地方行政部門感到無助，因為中央當局接管了指揮權。
The dissatisfaction around centralized governance had begun manifesting in protests even before the floods. Mr. Shah’s team members also appeared at odds with each other over finding an economic policy balanced between fulfilling his populist welfare promises and taking the austerity measures needed to put the economy on a path to growth.
對中央集權治理的不滿甚至在洪水發生之前就已經在抗議活動中顯現出來。沙阿的團隊成員之間似乎也存在分歧，他們難以找到一項既能兌現其民粹主義福利承諾，又能採取使經濟走上增長軌道所需的緊縮措施的平衡經濟政策。
廣告
“Changing a few people at the top cannot instantly transform institutions and a system that has evolved over decades,” said Uddbab Pyakurel, a professor of political sociology at Kathmandu University.
加德滿都大學政治社會學教授烏德巴布·皮亞庫雷爾說：「改變高層的幾個人，並不能立刻改變經數十年演變而成的機構和系統。」

Mujib Mashal是時報南亞分社社長，領導對印度及其周邊地區（包括孟加拉國、斯里蘭卡、尼泊爾和不丹）的報導。
