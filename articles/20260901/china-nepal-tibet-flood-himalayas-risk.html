為什麼西藏邊境山洪令準備充分的中國措手不及？

Why the Floods Took a Well-Prepared China by Surprise

郭莉莉, PEI-LIN WU • 2026-09-01
Before last week’s deluge in a Himalayan valley killed more than 900 people along the border with Nepal, Chinese officials had spent months repairing the damage from an earlier landslide in the same area and trying to learn from it.
上週喜馬拉雅山谷的洪水導致中國與尼泊爾邊境900多人喪生之前，中國官員已經花了數月時間修復該地區早些時候因山體滑坡造成的破壞，並試圖從中吸取教訓。
The trouble was that the next disaster was unlike anything they had prepared for.
麻煩在於，下一場災難與他們所準備應對的任何情況都截然不同。
Fourteen months earlier, a lake on a nearby glacier had burst, causing a landslide that swept away container trucks, houses, and a bridge at the Gyirong Port border crossing used by pilgrims, tourists and traders.
14個月前，附近冰川上的一個湖泊潰決，引發了山體滑坡，沖走了朝聖者、遊客和商人通行的吉隆口岸過境點的集裝箱卡車、房屋和一座橋樑。
Afterward, the authorities closed the port for almost half a year, ordered emergency plans be updated to include the threat of glacial lake outbursts and set up a 24-hour sentry for sudden changes in water level. Construction began on a flood prevention system that included a new flood wall, reinforced dikes and channels to divert floodwaters, according to government tenders.
此後，當局將該口岸關閉了近半年，下令更新應急預案，以納入冰湖潰決的威脅，並設立了24小時哨所來監測水位的突變。根據政府招標信息，一個防洪系統的建設也隨之開始，其中包括新建防洪牆、加固堤壩以及用於分流洪水的管道。
Gyirong County added “glacial lake outburst defense” to its training of local officials and held a special session with experts on the topic in May. Villagers near the border simulated a landslide, alerting residents through loudspeakers, WeChat messages and by knocking on doors. In July, the county’s top official inspected towns for their flood preparedness and ordered his staff to “detect early, report early and resolve early” any signs of glacial floods.
吉隆縣在對地方官員的培訓中增加了「冰湖潰決防禦」的內容，並在5月與專家舉行了關於該主題的特別會議。邊境附近的村民模擬了山體滑坡，通過擴音器、微信消息和挨家挨戶敲門來警告居民。7月，吉隆縣最高官員視察了各鎮的防洪準備情況，並命令其工作人員對冰川洪水的任何跡象做到「早發現、早報告、早處置」。
None of that preparation was enough. On Wednesday, a different hazard sent a tsunami-like wave of ice, boulders and water through the Gyirong Port complex, demolishing 27 buildings, before it surged into Nepal. Officials estimated that there were only six or seven minutes between the avalanche and when the border was hit.
但這些準備工作都遠遠不夠。上週三，一種完全不同的災害帶來了由冰塊、巨石和水組成的巨浪，如同海嘯，席捲了吉隆口岸建築群，摧毀了27座建築物，然後湧入尼泊爾。官員估計，從雪崩發生到衝擊邊境僅間隔了六七分鐘。。
On Monday, Chinese rescuers were still searching the border area and clearing roads. The landslide killed at least 903 people in Nepal and 16 in Tibet. In Nepal, 4,247 people were missing as of Monday, including 592 foreigners, the country’s disaster agency said. In Gyirong County in Tibet, 546 people remained missing, Chinese state media reported.
週一，中國救援人員仍在邊境地區搜救並清理道路。山體滑坡導致尼泊爾至少903人死亡，西藏16人死亡。尼泊爾災害應對機構表示，截至週一，尼泊爾已有4247人失蹤，其中包括592名外國人。據中國官方媒體報導，西藏吉隆縣仍有546人失蹤。
廣告
“It’s spooky that a different, strange, climate-related event happened in the same river just a year later,” said Austin Lord, a senior fellow at the Stimson Center who studies disaster and climate risk in the Himalayan region. “I think this event really is yet another wake-up call.”
「僅僅一年後，在同一條河流中發生了一場不同的、奇怪的、與氣候相關的事件，這令人毛骨悚然，」史汀生中心研究喜馬拉雅地區災害和氣候風險的高級研究員奧斯汀·洛德說。「我認為這一事件確實是又一次警鐘。」
Both disasters sent torrents of water, rock and debris hurtling downstream. But Wednesday’s came from another phenomenon that the monitoring systems were not built to anticipate — ice collapsing from a glacier, according to geologists, that could have been caused by thawing permafrost and weakened bedrock.
這兩場災難都導致洶湧的水流、岩石和碎片向下游傾瀉而下。但地質學家表示，上週三的災難源於另一種監測系統無法預測的現象——冰川冰體崩塌，這可能是由永久凍土融化和基岩變弱引起的。
Chinese scientists are working on an early warning system for ice avalanches in a different part of Tibet, the southeastern Sedongpu Valley. In Gyirong, though, monitoring the area’s more than 200 glacial lakes for potential breaches has been the priority.
中國科學家正在西藏另一個地區——東南部的色東普溝——致力於建立冰崩預警系統。然而在吉隆，監測該地區200多個冰川湖是否可能發生潰壩一直是工作的重中之重。
Why Was the Crossing Built There?
為什麼過境點建在那裡？
The fact that the same area was hit twice by catastrophic glacier-linked flash floods in 14 months also raises questions about why settlements and major infrastructure were built downstream in a narrow valley gorge.
同一地區在14個月內兩次遭到與冰川有關的災難性山洪襲擊，這一事實也引發了質疑：為什麼定居點和主要基礎設施要建在狹窄峽谷的下游？
For the last decade, the Gyirong Port has been the main link between China and Nepal, accounting for almost a third of China’s trade with Nepal, much of it electric vehicles going to Nepal, as well as more than 180,000 people traveling between the two countries.
過去十年來，吉隆口岸一直是中國和尼泊爾之間的主要紐帶，佔中國對尼泊爾貿易的近三分之一（其中大部分是運往尼泊爾的電動汽車），並且兩國之間有超過18萬人次在此通行。
The five-story complex was in one of the most vulnerable places possible for a border crossing. It perched on a narrow strip of land in a steep gorge where two rivers meet. Both rivers, fed by glaciers, descend about 10,000 feet before they come together at Gyirong Port.
這座五層樓的建築群位於邊境口岸最易受衝擊的地點之一。它坐落在兩條河流交匯的陡峭峽谷中一條狹窄地帶。這兩條河流由冰川融水匯成，河水下落約3000米後在吉隆口岸匯合。
“That flat ground is exactly where the bridge, customs yard, dry port and truck park sit so they take the direct hit every time,” said Saswata Sanyal, a specialist in disaster risk reduction at the International Center for Integrated Mountain Development, or ICIMOD, an intergovernmental group that has studied the border region. “People were swept away from the customs yard in 2025 and the same sites were struck again this year.”
「那片平地正是橋樑、海關貨場、內陸港和卡車停車場的所在地，因此它們每次都受到直接衝擊，」研究該邊境地區的政府間組織國際綜合山區發展中心的減災專家薩斯瓦塔·桑亞爾說。「2025年，人們從海關貨場被沖走，今年同樣的地點再次遭到衝擊。」
But experts say there are few alternatives in this steep mountain region where disasters continue to strike. The Gyirong border crossing only became a major trade route over the last decade after another crossing further east, between Zhangmu in China and Kodari in Nepal, was damaged in the 2015 Nepal earthquake.
但專家表示，在這個災害持續發生的陡峭山區，可選的替代路線寥寥無幾。吉隆口岸之所以在過去10年間成為主要貿易通道，是因為位於更東側、連接中國樟木與尼泊爾科達裡的另一處口岸在2015年尼泊爾地震中受損。
廣告
According to a travel advisory on Sunday issued by the U.S. Embassy in Beijing, some travelers have been directed to use the Zhangmu crossing. That area was also hit by a glacier lake flood in 2016 and is downstream from another large glacial lake that needs to be monitored, according to Dr. Lord of the Stimson Center.
根據美國駐華大使館週日發布的旅行建議，一些旅行者已被引導使用樟木口岸。史汀生中心的洛德指出，樟木口岸在2016年也曾遭受冰川湖洪水襲擊，並且它位於另一個需要監測的大型冰川湖的下游。
“It’s like Whac-a-Mole. You have risks emerging in one place and you shift to another location,” he said.
「這就像打地鼠遊戲。一個地方出現風險，你就轉移到另一個地方，」他說。
A study in 2024 by Chinese researchers in Chengdu had concluded that of the transportation corridors between Nepal and China, the border crossing at Gyirong was at the highest risk of glacial lake outburst floods.
2024年，成都的中國研究人員進行的一項研究得出結論：在尼泊爾和中國之間的交通走廊中，吉隆口岸面臨的冰湖潰決洪水風險最高。
On Monday, the state-run China National Radio ran an article titled “Why is the Gyirong Port built in a deep river valley?” in an apparent effort to head off criticism of the border’s location.
週一，官方的中國中央人民廣播電台發表了一篇題為《吉隆口岸為何建在深山河谷中？》的文章，此舉顯然是為了平息對該口岸位置的批評。
“History showed it was relatively safe, especially for a border crossing like this, which is why such a route was built,” Tian Bingwei, associate professor at the Institute for Disaster Management and Reconstruction of Sichuan University, told the radio station.
四川大學災後重建與管理學院副教授田兵偉告訴該電台：「歷史就證明它是比較安全的，尤其是這種通關口岸，所以才有了這樣一條路。」
Disasters More Difficult to Predict
災害變得更難預測
廣告
The region has always experienced landslides, avalanches and floods but rising temperatures have made those events more destructive, complex, and difficult to predict.
這一地區一直存在山體滑坡、雪崩和洪水等災害，但氣溫升高使得這些事件變得更具破壞性、更加複雜且更難以預測。
The landslide last week as well as the one in 2025 exhibited what experts call a “cascade of hazards,” where several disasters happen one after another, compounding and interacting with each other.
上週的山體滑坡以及2025年的山體滑坡都表現出專家所謂的「級聯災害」，即多種災害接連發生，相互疊加、相互作用。
Liu Weiming, a researcher at the Chinese Academy of Sciences’ Institute of Mountain Hazards and Environment, told China National Radio that Wednesday’s disaster was caused by new factors such as the increase in global warming that causes glaciers to melt more rapidly.
中國科學院成都山地災害與環境研究所研究員劉維明告訴中央人民廣播電台，週三的災難是由新因素引起的，例如導致冰川加速融化的全球暖化加劇。
“In this case, the ice avalanche directly transformed into a debris flow without any pause, moving at an exceptionally high speed. This is extremely rare in our country,” he told the radio station.
「這次是冰崩後直接轉化成土石流，中間沒有停歇，速度非常快，在我們國內也是非常罕見的，」他告訴該電台。
The disaster shows that traditional monitoring that focuses on threats in isolation like a flood or an avalanche are not enough. “These mountains are very different from anywhere else. We need to understand the mountain risks and hazards much more closely going forward,” said Dr. Sanyal of ICIMOD.
這場災難表明，孤立地關注洪水或雪崩等單一威脅的傳統監測已經不夠了。「這些山脈與任何其他地方都截然不同。未來我們需要更密切地了解山區的風險和災害，」國際綜合山區發展中心的桑亞爾說。
“We cannot just think traditionally as we think of a single hazard,” said Dr. Sanyal. “There were sensors for flood, flash flood risk, right? But there were not sensors for this kind of ice avalanche.”
「我們不能只局限於傳統思維，把它當作單一的災害來考慮，」桑亞爾說。「有檢測洪水、山洪風險的傳感器，對吧？但沒有針對這種冰崩的傳感器。」
