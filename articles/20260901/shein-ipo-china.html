Shein尷尬IPO背後，快時尚的黃昏

Shein’s Lackluster Debut Shows a Fast-Fashion Model Left Behind

黃瑞黎 • 2026-09-01
When Shein unveiled itself to the world at the height of its popularity several years ago, it touted a modern era for fashion — one built on speed, technology and an on-demand system capable of creating 4,700 new styles a day.
幾年前，希音(Shein)在巔峰出道時，曾標榜自己開創了時尚產業的新時代——一個建立在速度、科技和按需生產系統之上的時代，號稱這套體系每天能夠推出4700種新款。
Today, that model is increasingly looking like a relic.
而如今，這種模式越來越像是上一個時代的產物。
In the small factories that form the backbone of Shein’s operations in China’s southeastern manufacturing hub of Guangdong, there are no humanoid robots, quantum computers or state-of-the-art technology. Instead, there are dozens of workers toiling away in 10- to 14-hour shifts — in a way that, some say, feels almost like China of yesteryear.
在位於中國東南部的製造業中心廣東那些支撐著希音運營的小型工廠裡，看不到人形機器人、量子計算機或最先進的技術設備，而是幾十名工人每天工作10到14個小時——有人說，這種場景讓人幾乎有種回到了舊社會的感覺。
“It represents the old tech, as opposed to the new tech,” said Nirgunan Tiruchelvam, who leads the consumer and internet division at Aletheia Capital, an investment advisory firm focused on Asia. “Shein would have had a lot more traction with investors in the 2021 vintage. But the world has moved on from blockbuster e-commerce listings.”
「它代表的是舊技術，而不是新技術，」專注亞洲市場的投資諮詢公司Aletheia Capital消費者與互聯網業務負責人尼爾古南·蒂魯切爾瓦姆說。「如果放在2021年那種市場環境下，希音本來會更受投資者青睞。但世界已經不再追捧當年那種轟動一時的電商上市了。」
廣告
On Tuesday, investors largely agreed. Shares in Shein fell 6 percent in early trading, a humbling debut for a company once valued at $100 billion but now worth about a quarter of that. Its offering came after a wave of Chinese A.I. companies went public in Hong Kong and Shanghai, and years after Shein tried, and failed, twice in its efforts to list in New York and London amid opposition from officials and activists over working conditions at its facilities.
週二，投資者基本印證了這一判斷。希音股價在早盤交易中下跌6%，對於一家曾估值高達1000億美元、如今僅剩約四分之一市值的企業來說，這是一次相當尷尬的亮相。希音此次上市前，一批中國人工智慧公司相繼在香港和上海上市；而此前數年，希音曾兩次嘗試在紐約和倫敦上市，均因監管官員和維權人士對其工廠勞動條件的質疑而未能成功。
The drop in valuation reflects questions many investors have about the sustainability of Shein’s business — and whether it can find new ways to grow after the United States and Europe, two of the company’s biggest markets, ended tariff exemptions on cheap goods that had helped propel its low-cost model.
估值縮水反映出許多投資者對希音業務可持續性的疑慮——在美國和歐洲這兩大核心市場取消針對低價商品的關稅豁免後，希音是否還能找到新的增長點。此前正是這些關稅優惠助推了它的低價模式。
Shein’s offering comes weeks after shares of Unitree Robotics and CXMT — two companies that are driving the A.I. investment boom in China — skyrocketed in their public listings in Shanghai. Both firms are textbook examples of what the Chinese government calls “new, quality productive forces” — the slogan for the country’s plan to drive economic growth and innovation through high-value-added manufacturing.
希音上市前數週，宇樹科技和長鑫儲存在上海上市後股價飆升，這兩家公司推動了中國的人工智慧投資熱潮。這兩家公司堪稱中國政府所謂「新質生產力」的典型代表。新質生產力是中國通過高附加值製造業推動經濟增長和創新的戰略口號。
Weiheng Chen, a senior partner at Wilson Sonsini, a law firm in Hong Kong, and head of its Greater China practice, noted that institutional buyers that were offered Shein shares before the stock went public took up just over 20 percent of the offering, compared with the roughly 50 percent typical of popular public listings.
香港威爾遜律師事務所高級合伙人、負責大中華區業務的陳煒恆指出，在希音上市前獲配售股份的機構投資者最終認購的比例僅略高於20%，而熱門新股上市時，這一比例通常約為50%。
“It’s a fashion company that is not that fashionable to today’s investors,” said Mr. Chen, who has been an adviser on many high-profile listings.
「這是一家時尚公司，但對今天的投資者來說，它已經沒那麼時尚了，」陳煒恆表示。他曾為多宗備受矚目的上市項目提供諮詢。
The challenge for Shein will be to regain interest among Generation Z, the group that powered the company’s rise during the pandemic. Michael Gunther, senior vice president of research and market intelligence at Consumer Edge, a firm that tracks spending data in the United States and Europe, said Shein had continued to cede market share in the United States, with the steepest losses among 18- to 34-year-olds.
希音面臨的挑戰在於如何重新贏回Z世代的青睞，正是這一群體在疫情期間推動了希音的崛起。追蹤歐美消費數據的公司Consumer Edge研究及市場情報高級副總裁麥可·岡瑟表示，希音在美國的市場份額仍在持續流失，其中18至34歲年齡段的流失最為嚴重。
廣告
“The fact that it’s continuing to lose share more with that group than other groups is notable,” Mr. Gunther said, adding that affordability and sustainability concerns could have prompted the shift. In May 2025, Shein raised prices in the United States to offset the surge in import costs.
「它在這個群體中的份額流失比其他群體更明顯，這一點值得注意，」岡瑟說。他補充道，對價格可負擔性和可持續性方面的擔憂可能是導致這一變化的原因。2025年5月，希音在美國上調了價格，以對衝進口成本的大幅上升。
In Britain, Shein’s market share gains have slowed despite no change in government policy, Mr. Gunther said. In Europe, where there is a fee of 3 euros on parcels entering the bloc, Shein’s market share has continued to slide, with declines most pronounced in France and Spain, he said.
岡瑟表示，在英國，儘管政府政策沒有變化，希音的市場份額增長也已經放緩。在歐洲，由於進入歐盟的包裹需繳納每件3歐元的費用，希音的市場份額持續下滑，其中法國和西班牙的跌幅最為明顯。
Its current fortunes are a stunning reversal for a company that came out of nowhere to become the world’s largest online fashion retailer.
希音如今的境況與這家公司當年的崛起形成了驚人的反差。它曾經橫空出世，一躍成為全球最大的在線時尚零售商。
Shein got its start in 2012 when Sky Xu Yangtian, a Chinese search engine expert, founded the company with three former colleagues. The company compressed the fashion cycle from months to days, using algorithms to track emerging trends and placing initial orders of a few hundred units to test demand.
希音成立於2012年，由中國搜索引擎專家許仰天與三位前同事共同創立。該公司將時尚週期從數月壓縮至數天，利用算法追蹤新興潮流，並以數百件的小批量訂單試水市場需求。
But behind its success are tens of thousands of garment workers subjected to a punishing pace. To complete orders on time, workers say, they put in 20 or more consecutive days without a single day off, according to a forthcoming report by China Labor Watch, a U.S.-based nonprofit that investigates labor conditions in China. The report, which has not yet been made public, was shared with The New York Times.
然而，希音成功的背後是數以萬計制衣工人承受著極其繁重的勞動強度。據美國非營利組織「中國勞工觀察」即將發布的一份報告，為了按時完成訂單，工人們表示他們曾連續工作20天甚至更久，沒有一天休息。《紐約時報》提前獲得了這份尚未發布的報告。
The organization interviewed 13 workers who made apparel at suppliers to the company as well as Shein employees who worked at its warehouses and corporate offices. The workers said they were given 10-minute breaks and reprimanded if they took too long to sew — for a pay of 7 to 28 cents per item.
該組織採訪了13名在希音供應商工廠生產服裝的工人，以及在希音倉庫和公司辦公室工作的員工。工人們說，他們每次只能休息10分鐘；如果縫製服裝的速度太慢，還會受到責罰。而每件衣服的計件工資只有七至28美分。
廣告
Workers earn, on average, $850 to $1,130 per month, but only by working weekends and taking no days off, according to China Labor Watch.
據中國勞工觀察的數據，工人平均每月收入850至1130美元，但前提是他們必須週末也工作，而且沒有休息日。
Li Qiang, the director of China Labor Watch, said garment factories in China generally limited work hours to around 60 per week. “None are quite like Shein,” he said. “With Shein, working 100 hours a week is entirely possible.”
中國勞工觀察負責人李強說，中國的服裝工廠通常把每週工作時間限制在大約60小時。「沒有哪家能跟希音相比。」他說，「在希音，每週工作100個小時完全有可能。」
The findings from China Labor Watch appear to contravene Shein’s official supplier policy, which states that workers should not work more than 60 hours a week, including overtime.
中國勞工觀察的調查結果似乎與希音官方的供應商政策相矛盾。該政策規定，包括加班在內，工人的每週工作時間不得超過60小時。
In a written statement, Shein said it categorically rejected these characterizations of working conditions within Shein’s supplier ecosystem. It also said that the payment figures presented in the report “are not factual and misrepresent the wage structures used by suppliers.”
在書面聲明中，希音堅決否認對其供應商生態系統中勞動條件的上述描述。聲明還稱，報告中引用的薪酬數據「不符合事實，且歪曲了供應商的工資結構」。
On Douyin, China’s version of TikTok, several Chinese workers say they are exhausted from working at Shein, with one calling it “Guangdong’s most tiring logistics factory.” Several factory owners say they no longer want to work with Shein because they face heavy fines for failing quality checks.
在抖音上，一些中國工人表示，在希音工作讓他們筋疲力盡，其中一人稱希音是「廣東最累的物流廠」。一些工廠老闆也表示，他們已經不願再與希音合作，因為質檢不達標會面臨高額罰款。
Shein has also come under fire for copying other people’s designs. Since 2021, it has been named as a defendant in 58 lawsuits in the United States alleging trademark infringement and racketeering, according to court records. The company said being named as a defendant in litigation “does not establish the validity of the allegations made” and that it continues to strengthen its intellectual property protection and enforcement.
希音還因抄襲他人設計而備受詬病。根據法庭記錄，自2021年以來，希音在美國已被列為58起商標侵權和涉嫌通過一系列違法活動牟利的訴訟被告。希音表示，被列為訴訟被告「並不代表相關指控成立」，並表示公司將繼續加強知識產權保護和執行力度。
廣告
Taken together, the challenges have complicated the story that Shein once sold investors — that it had reinvented fashion for the digital age. Now, the technology cycle has moved on.
綜合來看，這些問題使希音過去向投資者講述的那個故事變得越來越複雜——那個故事曾宣稱，希音重新發明了數位時代的時尚業。如今，科技發展的浪潮已經向前推進了。
Winston Ma, a former managing director at the China Investment Corporation, the country’s largest sovereign wealth fund, compared Shein to a stock like Zoom, which surged during the pandemic but has since fallen from its valuation highs.
曾任中國最大主權財富基金中投公司董事總經理的馬文彥把希音比作Zoom這樣的股票。Zoom在疫情期間股價暴漲，但隨後便從估值高點回落。
“It was a superpromising concept during Covid,” Mr. Ma said of Shein. “But now it’s overshadowed by the new A.I. era.”
「疫情期間，這是一個前景極其誘人的概念，」馬文彥談到希音時說，「但如今，它已經被新的人工智慧時代的浪潮蓋過了。」
