上合組織峰會：習近平展示影響力

Summit With Russia, India and Iran Shows Leverage Wars Have Given Xi of China

PAUL SONNE, DAVID PIERSON • 2026-09-01
As the leaders of China, Russia, India, Iran and other nations converged on Central Asia for a two-day summit beginning on Monday, one thing was clear from the outset. Even as President Trump tries to bend the world to revolve around Washington, Xi Jinping of China has his own powerful orbit.
中國、俄羅斯、印度、伊朗及其他國家領導人週一齊聚中亞，參加為期兩天的峰會。有一件事從一開始就顯而易見：儘管川普總統試圖讓世界圍繞華盛頓運轉，中國領導人習近平依然擁有自己強大的勢力軌道。
The Chinese leader’s arrival in Bishkek, the capital of Kyrgyzstan, for a meeting of the 10-nation political and security grouping known as the Shanghai Cooperation Organization underscored Mr. Xi’s broad ability to sustain Washington’s foes, both in peacetime and in war.
中國領導人抵達吉爾吉斯斯坦首都比什凱克，參加由10個國家組成的政治與安全集團上海合作組織的會議，這凸顯了習近平在和平時期與戰爭時期都能廣泛維繫華盛頓對手的能力。
The talks come against the backdrop of two wars that have pushed the globe into a period of intense geopolitical instability, suppressing economic growth and driving up inflation. Those challenges were at the heart of discussions taking place simultaneously on the other side of the globe in Asheville, N.C., at a gathering of finance ministers from the Group of 20 countries.
此次會談是在兩場戰爭的背景下舉行的，這兩場戰爭將全球推入了一個地緣政治劇烈動盪的時期，不僅抑制了經濟增長，還推高了通膨。在地球另一端的北卡羅萊納州阿什維爾市，這些挑戰是20國集團財長會議正在討論的核心議題。
The meeting in Bishkek, in contrast, was a showcase for the incredible leverage that the wars have given Mr. Xi.
相比之下，比什凱克的會議充分展示了戰爭賦予習近平的驚人籌碼。
廣告
No nation has been more critical to Russia and Iran in sustaining their economies and war efforts than China. Mr. Xi has undermined efforts by the United States and its allies to pressure Russia and Iran economically, employing Beijing’s vast geopolitical and economic clout as the world’s largest importer of crude oil and exporter of consumer goods.
在維持俄羅斯和伊朗的經濟運轉及戰爭消耗方面，沒有哪個國家比中國更為關鍵。利用北京作為全球最大原油進口國和消費品出口國所擁有的巨大地緣政治和經濟影響力，習近平削弱了美國及其盟友對俄羅斯和伊朗施加經濟壓力的努力。
Mr. Xi’s biggest rival, the United States, has been distracted by the war in Iran and has pulled some of its military forces from Asia and Europe. Last month, the Chinese leader was able to brush off threats by President Trump that the United States would inflict an “economic D-Day” on countries that provided Tehran with a lifeline. Mr. Trump once made similar threats about purchasers of Russian oil, but did not follow through with any actions against Beijing.
習近平最大的競爭對手美國已被伊朗的戰爭分散了精力，並從亞洲和歐洲撤走了一部分軍事力量。上個月，川普總統威脅稱，美國將對那些為德黑蘭提供生命線的國家發動「經濟諾曼第登陸」，但這並未對中國領導人產生影響。川普也曾對俄羅斯石油的買家發出過類似威脅，但並未對北京採取任何實質行動。
“After the past year, Trump has a fundamental credibility problem with Xi,” said Julian Gewirtz, who served as the senior director for China and Taiwan Affairs at the National Security Council during the Biden administration. “Both his threats and his assurances appear to Beijing, as another world leader put it, to be written in pencil.”
曾在拜登政府時期擔任國家安全委員會中國與台灣事務高級主任的朱利安·格維爾茨表示：「過去一年裡，川普在習近平面前面臨著根本性的信譽問題。正如另一位世界領導人所言，無論他的威脅還是承諾，在北京看來都像是用鉛筆寫的。」
In Bishkek, Mr. Xi’s priority is to burnish his image as the leader of non-Western nations and to push back against what he calls U.S. hegemony. Despite that, he will most likely be cautious about how much support he publicly offers Iran.
在比什凱克，習近平的首要任務是提升自己作為非西方國家領導人的形象，並反擊他口中的美國霸權。儘管如此，在公開向伊朗提供多少支持這一問題上，他很可能會保持謹慎。
The Chinese leader cannot afford to provoke Mr. Trump less than a month before he is scheduled to travel to Washington to attempt to extend a fragile economic truce between the United States and China. Before heading to the United States, Mr. Xi will travel to Egypt and India for state visits.
不到一個月之後，中國領導人將前往華盛頓，試圖延長中美之間脆弱的經濟休戰，因此他承受不起激怒川普的代價。在前往美國之前，習近平還將對埃及和印度進行國事訪問。
The series of trips leading up to his summit with Mr. Trump is aimed at casting Mr. Xi as a powerful statesman at a time when Mr. Trump has frayed U.S. relations globally, analysts said.
分析人士指出，與川普舉行峰會之前的一系列出訪旨在將習近平塑造為一位強有力的政治家，而川普此時正令美國與各國的關係變得緊張。
廣告
“Xi appears to see an opportunity to show that China has friends and options around the world at precisely the moment Washington is alienating many of its allies,” Mr. Gewirtz said.
格維爾茨表示：「在華盛頓疏遠其許多盟友的當下，習近平似乎看到了機會，藉此向外界展示中國在世界各地都擁有朋友和選擇。」
This week’s summit in Bishkek is a reminder of the limits of U.S.-led economic warfare. President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia and President Masoud Pezeshkian of Iran, both critically reliant on China, arrived in the Kyrgyz capital on Monday as they work to defy Western efforts to isolate them and persevere with their own wartime goals.
本週在比什凱克舉行的峰會提醒人們，美國主導的經濟戰存在局限性。高度依賴中國的俄羅斯總統普丁與伊朗總統馬蘇德·佩澤什基安於週一抵達吉爾吉斯斯坦首都，他們正努力對抗西方試圖孤立他們的舉措，並堅持推進各自的戰時目標。
Beijing has largely obliged, extending economic lifelines to both Moscow and Tehran and leveraging its economic clout to the frustration of Washington.
北京在很大程度上滿足了這些需求，向莫斯科和德黑蘭拋出了經濟救生索，利用自身的經濟影響力，令華盛頓感到受挫。
While Mr. Xi’s power is on the rise, there are limits to it. Though the Shanghai Cooperation Organization is partly a security grouping, Beijing has been unwilling to deploy its military to defend members such as Iran, which joined the organization in 2023, or Russia, instead formally calling for dialogue and an end to the conflicts.
儘管習近平的實力不斷增強，但也存在局限性。上海合作組織在某種程度上是一個安全集團，但北京一直不願部署軍隊來保衛成員國，比如伊朗（2023年加入該組織）或俄羅斯，而是從官方層面呼籲對話、結束衝突。
Mr. Xi does not want to be entangled in foreign wars, analysts have said, viewing them as one of the primary reasons the United States has seen its power wane over the decades. But Beijing is willing to increase trade and provide technologies that can be used on the battlefield.
分析人士表示，習近平不願捲入外國戰爭，他認為這是幾十年來美國實力衰退的主要原因之一。但北京願意增加貿易，並提供可用於戰場的各種技術。
The Chinese leader met with Mr. Putin for bilateral talks in Bishkek on Monday, praising what he called ever-brighter prospects for relations between China and Russia.
週一，這位中國領導人在比什凱克與普丁舉行了雙邊會談，並讚揚中俄關係前景「更加光明」。
廣告
Mr. Putin, in turn, highlighted the success of visa-free travel between the two nations and said he was ready to make the arrangement permanent. The Russian leader noted that the first scheduled voyage of a Chinese container ship passed through what is known as the Northern Sea Route in mid-August, presaging broader cooperation in the Arctic.
普丁則強調了兩國間免簽旅行的成功，並表示他已準備好將這一安排永久化。這位俄羅斯領導人指出，一艘中國集裝箱船的首次定期航行已於8月中旬通過了所謂的「北方海路」，這預示著雙方將在北極地區開展更廣泛的合作。
Whether Mr. Xi meets with the Iranian leader separately from the summit will indicate how much the Chinese leader is willing to push the line ahead of the scheduled visit to the United States.
習近平是否會在峰會之外單獨會晤伊朗領導人將表明，在定於前往美國訪問之前，這位中國領導人願意在多大程度上試探底線。
Though it once counted only Russia, China and four Central Asian nations as members, the Shanghai Cooperation Organization expanded in 2017 to include India and Pakistan and more recently added Belarus and Iran. In the process, it has become a growing counterpoint to Western-led political and security groupings.
儘管上海合作組織的成員國最初只有俄羅斯、中國和四個中亞國家，但該組織在2017年進行了擴員，納入了印度和巴基斯坦，最近又吸納了白俄羅斯和伊朗。在此過程中，該組織日益成為西方主導的政治和安全集團的對手。
Some of the leaders gathering for the summit in Bishkek have helped Mr. Putin continue his war against Ukraine, which is now in its fifth year, and aided Iran in its attempt to weather a monthslong onslaught by the United States.
參加比什凱克峰會的部分領導人曾協助普丁繼續對烏克蘭的戰爭——這場戰爭現已進入第五年，同時也協助伊朗抵禦美國長達數月的猛烈攻勢。
Not long after Mr. Putin launched the all-out invasion in 2022, Iran supplied Russia with Shahed drones and helped Moscow establish domestic production facilities for the weapons.
就在普丁於2022年發動全面入侵後不久，伊朗向俄羅斯提供了「沙赫德」無人機，並幫助莫斯科在國內建立了這些武器的生產設施。
Central Asian nations have become critical import and payment routes for Russia, as Moscow works its way around Western restrictions. Iran has turned to Central Asia as an alternative import route amid a U.S. blockade.
隨著莫斯科設法繞過西方的種種限制，中亞國家已成為俄羅斯關鍵的進口與支付通道。在美國的封鎖下，伊朗也轉向中亞，將其作為一條替代的進口路線。
廣告
India, Kazakhstan and Belarus are among the countries that have stepped in to ship gasoline to Russia in recent weeks as Ukrainian long-range strikes have decimated Russian oil refineries.
由於烏克蘭的遠程打擊摧毀了俄羅斯的多家煉油廠，印度、哈薩克和白俄羅斯等國在最近幾週介入，向俄羅斯輸送汽油。
India emerged as one of the biggest purchasers of Russian oil in the aftermath of Mr. Putin’s invasion. Though its imports dropped following pressure from Mr. Trump and new U.S. sanctions on Russian oil giants, the U.S. war against Iran roiled oil markets and pushed Indian suppliers to turn once again to imports from Russia. China is the biggest importer of Russian and Iranian crude.
在普丁發動入侵之後，印度成為了俄羅斯石油的最大買家之一。儘管在川普施壓以及美國對俄羅斯石油巨頭實施新制裁後，印度的進口量有所下降，但美國針對伊朗的戰爭擾亂了石油市場，迫使印度供應商再次轉向從俄羅斯進口。中國則是俄羅斯和伊朗原油的最大進口國。
Mr. Xi’s relationship with Mr. Putin is increasingly one-sided in Beijing’s favor. Western sanctions related to Russia’s war with Ukraine have enabled China to stockpile discounted Russian oil, cushioning the blow of a blockade in the Strait of Hormuz.
習近平與普丁的關係正日益向有利於北京的一方傾斜。與俄烏戰爭相關的西方制裁使得中國能夠囤積折扣的俄羅斯石油，從而緩衝了霍爾木茲海峽被封鎖帶來的衝擊。
Even the site of this week’s summit highlights China’s growing hand over Russia. Once a Soviet republic, Kyrgyzstan had been firmly within Moscow’s sphere of influence. But China’s influence in the Central Asian nation has grown, and the five nations of Central Asia have banded closer together in response to Russia’s war against Ukraine.
甚至本週峰會的舉辦地也凸顯了中國對俄羅斯日益增強的掌控力。吉爾吉斯斯坦曾是蘇聯的加盟共和國，一直穩穩地處於莫斯科的勢力範圍之內。但如今中國在這個中亞國家的影響力已然擴大，中亞五國為應對俄烏戰爭也更加緊密地團結在一起。
In opening remarks before a meeting with President Sadyr Japarov of Kyrgyzstan on Monday, Mr. Xi said relations between Beijing and Bishkek had “made a leap forward” and that cooperation was “flourishing across all areas.”
在週一與吉爾吉斯斯坦總統薩德爾·扎帕羅夫會晤前的開場致辭中，習近平表示，北京與比什凱克之間的關係已經「實現跨越式發展」，各領域合作正在「全面開花」。
On Monday evening, the leaders attended a lavish opening ceremony for the World Nomad Games, held in a newly built stadium in Bishkek and hosted by Mr. Japarov. Dancers and singers in traditional costume performed, as riders on horseback traversed a stage area ringed by bursts of fire.
週一晚間，各國領導人出席了在比什凱克一座新建體育場舉行的世界遊牧民族運動會盛大開幕式，開幕式由扎帕羅夫主持。身著傳統服飾的舞者和歌手登台獻藝，騎手們騎著馬穿過被陣陣火焰環繞的舞台區域。
廣告
When Mr. Xi arrives in the United States, Mr. Trump is likely to seek the Chinese leader’s cooperation on both Iran and North Korea, but it is not clear that China will oblige, Mr. Gewirtz said.
格維爾茨表示，當習近平抵達美國時，川普可能會在伊朗和朝鮮問題上尋求這位中國領導人的合作，但目前尚不清楚中國是否會予以配合。
“Trump has painted himself into a corner,” he said. “The administration is struggling to square two seemingly irreconcilable goals: tightening pressure on Iran and maintaining its truce with China.”
「川普把自己逼入了一個死角，」他說道。「美國政府正艱難地試圖平衡兩個看似不可調和的目標：加大對伊朗的施壓，同時維持與中國的休戰狀態。」

Paul Sonne是時報莫斯科分社社長，領導對俄羅斯、高加索和中亞地區的報導。他常駐柏林。

David Pierson報導中國外交政策和中國與世界的經濟與文化交互。他從事新聞工作已超過20年。
