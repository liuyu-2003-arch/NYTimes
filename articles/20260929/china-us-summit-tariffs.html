China and the U.S. Pledge to Cut Tariffs on $60 Billion in Goods

艾莎 • 2026-09-29
China and the United States have agreed to lower tariffs on $60 billion worth of goods, from fishing hooks to medical devices, following a visit to Washington last week by China’s top leader, Xi Jinping.
在中國最高領導人習近平上週訪問華盛頓之後，中美兩國已同意降低價值600億美元商品的關稅，涉及商品涵蓋從魚鉤到醫療設備等多個領域。
The world’s two biggest economies announced separate lists of nonsensitive goods worth $30 billion each that they planned to cut tariffs on, in a bid to ease economic tensions.
為了緩解經濟緊張局勢，這兩個全球最大的經濟體分別宣布了各自價值300億美元的非敏感商品清單，計劃對這些商品降低關稅。
Washington’s list of Chinese imports eligible for reduced tariffs contained 77 items, including Christmas toys and ornaments, hunting and recreational goods such as fishing hooks and skeet targets, tableware and home appliances, and car seats.
華盛頓公布的有資格獲得減免關稅的中國進口商品清單包含77項，包括聖誕玩具和裝飾品，魚鉤和雙向飛碟靶等狩獵和休閑用品，餐具和家用電器，以及汽車座椅。
Beijing’s list had 1,619 products, including frozen meat from primates and reptiles, live animals such as whales and dugongs, agricultural products including corn and vegetables, and medical devices like X-ray machines, pacemakers, ultrasound equipment and surgical robots.
北京的清單上包括1619種產品，包括靈長類動物和爬行動物的冷凍肉，鯨魚和儒艮等活體動物，玉米和蔬菜等農產品，以及X光機、心臟起搏器、超聲波設備和手術機器人等醫療設備。
廣告
Missing from both countries’ lists, unveiled on Sunday, were raw materials and products each side deems sensitive, including rare earth minerals and technologies critical to advancing artificial intelligence. Some of those products are now subject to export controls.
在週日公布的兩國清單中都沒有包含各自認為敏感的原材料和產品，包括稀土礦物和對推進人工智慧發展至關重要的技術。其中部分產品目前受到出口管制。
The promise to lower tariffs on a wide range of goods is the most meaningful gesture yet from both countries to show that they are making sincere efforts to improve relations, which have reached new lows over the past year. Following President Trump’s revival of the U.S. coal industry, China will import at least 10 million metric tons of coal from the United States in 2027 and again 2028.
降低各類商品關稅的承諾是兩國迄今為止最具有實質意義的舉措，表明雙方正在真誠努力改善過去一年中跌至新低的雙邊關係。隨著川普總統重振美國煤炭工業，中國將在2027年及2028年再次從美國進口至少1000萬公噸煤炭。
Jamieson Greer, the U.S. trade representative, described the lowered tariffs as part of a new Board of Trade between the two countries that would provide better market access for 30 percent of U.S. exports to China “while benefiting consumers with imports from China.” President Trump is eager to ease pressure on farmers ahead of the upcoming midterm elections and may also be concerned about the inflationary impact of tariffs during the Christmas shopping season.
美國貿易代表傑米森·格里爾表示，降低關稅是兩國新成立的貿易委員會框架下的一項舉措，該委員會將為30%的美國對華出口商品提供更好的市場准入，「同時讓消費者從中國進口商品中受益」。川普總統迫切希望在即將到來的期中選舉前減輕農民的壓力，並且可能還擔心關稅在聖誕購物季期間帶來的通膨影響。
In a statement, China’s Ministry of Commerce said the effort would “help to stabilize China-U.S. trade” and “strengthen trade cooperation” in areas such as agricultural and consumer goods.
中國商務部在聲明中表示，此舉將「有助於穩定中美貿易」，「加強」雙方在農產品和消費品等領域的「貿易合作」。
Ahead of talks between Mr. Xi and Mr. Trump last week, China agreed to extend a trade truce by two months. The pact was reached in South Korea last year after a brief but punishing tit-for-tat tariff war that led China to cut off the flow of rare earths that the United States needs for cars and weapons.
在上週習近平與川普舉行會談之前，中國同意將貿易休戰協議延長兩個月。該協議於去年在韓國達成，此前雙方曾爆發一場短暫但代價高昂的報復性關稅戰，導致中國切斷了美國製造汽車和武器所需的稀土供應。
In broader trade negotiations, the Trump administration has been focused on reducing the trade deficit with China. After Mr. Trump met with Mr. Xi in Beijing in May, the United States said China had agreed to sharply increase its purchases of American agricultural goods. So far, however, that pledge has not translated into meaningful buying.
在更廣泛的貿易談判中，川普政府一直專注於減少對華貿易逆差。今年5月川普在北京與習近平會晤後，美國表示中國已同意大幅增加購買美國農產品。然而到目前為止，這一承諾尚未轉化為實質性的採購。
