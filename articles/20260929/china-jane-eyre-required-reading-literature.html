中國當局為何要求中學生讀《簡·愛》

What China Sees in ‘Jane Eyre,’ Now Required Reading for Ninth Graders

YAN ZHUANG, LI YOU • 2026-09-29
Nearly two centuries after “Jane Eyre” first defied the conventions of Victorian England, China is requiring a new generation of teenagers to read her story.
在《簡·愛》首次挑戰英國維多利亞時代的傳統習俗近兩個世紀後，中國正要求新一代的青少年閱讀她的故事。
The British classic has long been popular in China and was already recommended reading for schoolchildren. But this fall, ninth-grade students across the country are being required to read the novel in full, according to Chinese state media. One report in an official newspaper held it up as an anti-materialistic love story that young people could learn from.
這部英國經典名著長期以來在中國一直很受歡迎，此前已經成為學童的推薦讀物。但據中國官方媒體報導，今年秋天，全國各地的九年級學生被要求完整閱讀這部小說。一家官方媒體的報導將其譽為反物質主義愛情故事，年輕人可以從中學習。
The book, in which the young orphan Jane Eyre works as a governess in the household of the wealthy but elusive Mr. Rochester, is widely celebrated in China for its themes of independence and equality between women and men.
這本書講述了年輕孤女簡·愛在富有但難以捉摸的羅切斯特家中擔任家庭教師的故事，因其倡導獨立精神和男女平等的主題而在中國廣受讚譽。
A Chinese translation appeared as early as 1925, and there have since been numerous translations and adaptations for stage and screen. A British film adaptation was shown in Chinese theaters in 1979, just a few years after the end of the Cultural Revolution, as China was reopening to foreign culture.
這本書的中文譯本最早出現於1925年，此後有了許多譯本，以及舞台和影視改編作品。1979年，文化大革命結束僅幾年後，正值中國重新向外國文化開放之際，一部英國改編電影在中國影院上映。
廣告
Ping Zhu, a literature professor at the University of California, San Diego, who researches Chinese feminism and literature, said the novel is compatible with the Chinese government’s vision of women’s equality — one that can coexist with traditional gender roles and the heterosexual nuclear family. Its inclusion in the ninth grade textbook comes as China struggles with population decline and the government encourages young people to marry and have children, she said.
加州大學聖地亞哥分校研究中國女權主義和文學的文學教授朱平（音）表示，這部小說符合中國政府關於女性平等的願景——一種可以與傳統性別角色和異性戀核心家庭共存的平等。她說，這部小說被納入九年級教科書，背景是中國努力應對人口下降，且政府鼓勵年輕人結婚生育。
“‘Jane Eyre’ is about an independent woman, but she’s not too outrageous in her challenge of patriarchal power,” Ms. Zhu said. By the end of the novel, Jane has married Mr. Rochester and had a child. “All these things are what the Chinese government wants the younger generation to do,” Ms. Zhu said.
「《簡·愛》講述的是一個獨立女性的故事，但她在挑戰父權制力量方面並沒有做得太過火，」朱平說。在小說結尾，簡嫁給了羅切斯特並生了一個孩子。「所有這些都是中國政府希望年輕一代去做的，」朱平說。
Published in Britain in 1847, “Jane Eyre” was striking for its depiction of a poor young woman insisting on her independence, dignity and equality in a society in which women — particularly married women — had severely limited legal and economic autonomy.
《簡·愛》於1847年在英國出版，其引人注目之處在於，它描繪了一位貧窮的年輕女性，在一個女人——尤其是已婚婦女——法律和經濟自主權受嚴重限制的社會中，堅持自己的獨立、尊嚴和平等。
The novel has long been celebrated in China for its feminist themes, with state media often quoting Jane’s declaration to Mr. Rochester: “Do you think, because I am poor, obscure, plain, and little, I am soulless and heartless? You think wrong! I have as much soul as you — and full as much heart!”
這部小說長期以來因其女權主義主題而在中國受到讚譽，官方媒體經常引用簡對羅切斯特的宣言：「你以為，就因為我貧窮、低微、不美、矮小，我就沒有靈魂，也沒有心嗎？你想錯了！我的靈魂跟你一樣——我的心也跟你完全一樣！」
“Jane Eyre” was progressive in its 19th-century context but some contemporary feminists would view its idea of liberation as limited, Ms. Zhu said.
朱平說，《簡·愛》在19世紀的背景下是進步的，但一些當代女權主義者會認為其解放理念存在局限。
The kind of feminism depicted in the novel has long been supported by the Chinese Communist Party, Ms. Zhu said. It is at odds with how the party sees more modern kinds of feminism, like the #MeToo movement, she added, “or the kind of feminism that teaches women to not marry and not have children — that is a feminism they cannot accept at all.”
朱平說，小說中描繪的那種女權主義長期以來一直受到中國共產黨的支持。她還說，這與共產黨對更為現代的女權主義（如#MeToo運動）的看法不一致，「或者那種教導女性不結婚、不生孩子的女權主義——那是他們完全無法接受的女權主義。」
廣告
In recent years, the Chinese government has restricted some other portrayals of romance and relationships.
近年來，中國政府對其他一些關於愛情和人際關係的描寫也進行了限制。
The authorities have detained dozens of writers of Boys’ Love, a genre of gay romance novels written largely by and for straight women, that state media said could shape young readers’ ideas about sexuality.
當局拘留了數十名「耽美」作家，這是一種主要由異性戀女性創作並面向異性戀女性讀者的男同性戀愛情小說，官方媒體稱這類小說可能會影響年輕讀者的性觀念。
Regulators have also sought to rein in a popular genre of micro-dramas about wealthy executives falling in love with less privileged people, urging creators to avoid glorifying marriages to powerful and wealthy people.
監管機構還試圖限制一種廣受歡迎、講述富有高管愛上地位較低者的流行微短劇類型，敦促創作者避免美化與權貴富豪的婚姻。
State media has suggested that novels like “Jane Eyre” offer young readers an alternative to what they call superficial love stories. An article in Beijing Daily, an official Chinese Communist Party publication, argued that teaching literary classics like “Jane Eyre” could help children “establish a more balanced value system.”
官方媒體表示，像《簡·愛》這樣的小說為年輕讀者提供了一種替代他們所謂「膚淺」的「愛情故事」的選擇。中國共產黨官方出版物《北京日報》的一篇文章認為，教授《簡·愛》等文學經典有助於孩子們「樹立積極人生觀」。
Chinese readers have long found their own meanings in Jane’s story.
長期以來，中國讀者一直在簡的故事中尋找屬於他們自己的意義。
Vicky Li, 46, read “Jane Eyre” when she was young and saw something of herself in its heroine. Years later, she said, she shared the novel with her daughters, now 13 and 15, because she wanted them to become independent and courageous.
46歲的薇姬·李（音）年輕時讀過《簡·愛》，並在女主人公身上看到了自己的一些影子。她說，多年後，將這部小說推薦給了現年13歲和15歲的兩個女兒，因為她希望她們變得獨立勇敢。
廣告
Ms. Li said she was happy to see the novel included in the textbook, even if some might think Jane did not embody the modern idea of a strong woman. “Given the historical context in which she lived, the choices she made were truly groundbreaking — a profound act of defiance,” she said.
薇姬·李表示，她很高興看到這部小說被納入教科書，即使有些人可能認為簡併不符合代女強人的理念。「考慮到她生活的歷史背景，她做出的選擇確實是開創性的——這是一種深刻的反抗行為，」她說。
The novel itself complicates the idea that Jane ultimately submits to convention. She leaves Mr. Rochester rather than become his mistress and returns only after inheriting a fortune. Mr. Rochester has lost his estate and his sight, dramatically altering the balance of power.
小說本身也讓「簡最終屈服於傳統」這一論斷變得複雜。她離開了羅切斯特，而不是成為他的情婦，並在繼承了一筆財產後才回來。而羅切斯特失去了莊園和視力，這極大地改變了他們之間的權力平衡。
Wang Sirui, 30, pointed to Jane’s refusal to become a mistress — even if it meant starving to death — as a key moment that showed the character’s commitment to independence.
30歲的王思睿（音）指出，簡拒絕成為情婦——即使這意味著餓死——是一個關鍵時刻，展現了該角色對獨立的執著。
“The core of the work lies in the message that a woman must make choices based on her own will and values, rather than succumbing to various temptations,” said Ms. Wang, who first read the book when she was 13.
「這部作品的核心在於傳達了這樣一個信息：女性必須基於自己的意願和價值觀做出選擇，而不是屈服於各種誘惑，」王思睿說，她在13歲時第一次讀了這本書。
Ms. Zhu said the Chinese authorities may ultimately have limited control over what messages young people take from “Jane Eyre.”
朱平說，中國當局對年輕人從《簡·愛》中獲得什麼信息，最終的控制力可能是有限的。
“Literary works, especially these great literary works, they’re always very rich, and they’re open to millions of ways of interpretation,” she said.
「文學作品，尤其是這些偉大的文學作品，內容總是非常豐富，可以有無數種解讀方式，」她說。

Yan Zhuang是時報駐首爾記者，報導突發新聞。

Li You是一名新聞研究員，為時報報導中國大陸的新聞。
