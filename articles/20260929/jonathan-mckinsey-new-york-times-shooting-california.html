一對灣區夫婦被控槍殺跨性別女婿

Couple Accused of Killing Their Son-in-Law in Bay Area Park

SOUMYA KARLAMANGLA, AMY QIN • 2026-09-29
A Bay Area couple have been accused of shooting and killing their son-in-law in a suburban sports park on Saturday afternoon, the local police said.
當地警方稱，一對灣區夫婦被指控於週六下午在郊區一個體育公園槍殺他們的女婿。
The man, Jonathan McKinsey, 40, was found dead with multiple gunshot wounds around 3 p.m. in a parking lot at Dublin Sports Grounds, an athletic complex and playground in Dublin, Calif., about 35 miles east of downtown San Francisco, according to Dublin Police Services.
據都柏林警察局稱，這名男子是40歲的喬納森·麥金西，下午3點左右被發現死於都柏林體育場的停車場，身中數槍。該體育場是加州都柏林的一個體育綜合設施和遊樂場，位於舊金山市中心以東約35英里（約合55公里）處。
Mr. McKinsey was director of engineering for the games department of The New York Times, according to a company spokesman. Before Mr. McKinsey began working at The Times in 2023, he worked for several tech companies including Splunk, Coursera and Oracle, according to his LinkedIn profile.
據紐約時報公司發言人稱，麥金西是公司遊戲部門工程總監。根據他的領英個人資料，2023年加入時報之前，他曾在Splunk、Coursera和Oracle等幾家科技公司工作。
The authorities arrested Shouyong Zhang and Shili Chen, both 76, in the shooting. They are a married couple who live in Dublin and are the parents of Mr. McKinsey’s wife, the police said.
警方稱，當局已就該槍擊案逮捕了年齡皆為76歲的張守勇（音）和陳詩麗（音）。他們是一對居住在都柏林的夫妻，是麥金西妻子的父母。
廣告
No motive was described by the police for the shooting, but Mr. McKinsey and his wife had a turbulent marriage and were living separately, records show. Each had filed requests for domestic violence restraining orders against the other last year in Alameda County. Mr. McKinsey had also pleaded not guilty last year to two charges of misdemeanor child abuse.
警方沒有說明槍擊動機，但記錄顯示麥金西與妻子婚姻破裂，兩人已分居。去年，兩人都在阿拉米達縣申請了對對方的家庭暴力限制令。麥金西去年還對兩項輕罪兒童虐待指控表示不認罪。
Mr. McKinsey was a transgender man and accused his wife, Candice Jang, and his in-laws of abusing him because of his identity, according to his filing for a restraining order in December 2025. He said his mother-in-law called him and one of his sons a “pervert” and a “monster” after the boy tried on dresses, and he accused his father-in-law of punching him in the chest while he was recovering from double mastectomy surgery in 2010.
根據麥金西2025年12月提交的限制令申請，他是一名跨性別男性，指控妻子坎迪斯·張和岳父母因他的這一身份而虐待他。他說，岳母在他的一個兒子試穿裙子後，稱他和兒子是「變態」和「怪物」；他還指控岳父在2010年他雙乳切除術恢復期間拳打他的胸部。
In her October 2025 filing, Ms. Jang said that Mr. McKinsey had grabbed her by the forearms during one argument and dropped her onto the corner of a table, fracturing vertebra in her upper back. Ms. Jang also accused Mr. McKinsey of emotionally abusing her mother.
坎迪斯·張在2025年10月的申請中說，麥金西在一次爭吵中抓住她的前臂，令她撞到桌角，導致背部椎骨骨折。她還指控麥金西對她的母親進行情感虐待。
Efforts to reach Ms. Jang and her lawyer on Monday were unsuccessful.
週一，本報試圖聯繫張女士及其律師未果。
Assad Razawi, a resident of Dublin, said he was outside a grocery store near his car on Saturday at around 3 p.m. on Saturday when he heard what sounded like five or six gunshots coming from the sports park across the street. When he looked over, he said he saw a man drop to the ground in the parking lot and another man and woman walking calmly away from the scene. The San Francisco Chronicle was first to report Mr. Razawi’s account.
都柏林居民阿薩德·拉扎維說，週六下午3點左右，他在雜貨店外自己的車旁，聽到街對面體育公園傳來像是五六聲槍響。他看到一名男子在停車場倒地，另一名男子和一名女子平靜地離開現場。《舊金山紀事報》最先報導了拉扎維的陳述。
Around the same time, a Dublin police sergeant was driving by the park when he saw a man lying on the ground and stopped to investigate, the local police said. Emergency personnel began arriving after receiving 911 calls from people in the park about the shooting.
當地警方稱，大約同一時間，一名都柏林警局警佐開車經過公園，看到一名男子躺在地上，便停車調查。在接到公園內人們關於槍擊的911報警後，急救人員開始抵達。
廣告
Witnesses directed officers to two individuals standing nearby, whom police identified as Mr. Zhang and Ms. Chen. They are being held without the possibility of bail on suspicion of murder, and detectives are continuing to investigate. They also are being held on suspicion of willful discharge of a firearm with gross negligence; conspiracy; and child endangerment likely to produce great bodily injury or death. It was not clear which children the last booking charge pertained to.
目擊者指引警員找到站在附近的兩人，警方確認他們是張先生和陳女士。他們因涉嫌謀殺被關押，不得保釋，警探正在繼續調查。他們還因涉嫌故意開槍並嚴重疏忽、共謀，以及可能造成嚴重身體傷害或死亡的兒童危害而被關押。目前不清楚最後一項收押指控涉及的兒童是誰。
They are scheduled to be arraigned on Wednesday. It was unclear whether Mr. Zhang and Ms. Chen had legal representation.
他們定於週三被提審。目前不清楚張先生和陳女士是否有法律代表。
A report from the Dublin Police Services noted that on Oct. 17, 2025, an officer met with Ms. Jang, who said that her husband had struck their 6-year-old “in the face with an open hand.” When officers went to the residence to contact Mr. McKinsey, they discovered a 2-year-old child in an upstairs bedroom who had been left without supervision. The officers say that Mr. McKinsey had left the child alone for over an hour while he went shopping.
都柏林警察局的一份報告指出，一名警員於2025年10月17日見了張女士，她說丈夫「用手掌打了他們六歲孩子的臉」。當警員前往住所聯繫麥金西時，發現一名兩歲兒童被留在樓上卧室，無人監管。警員稱，麥金西讓孩子獨自待了一個多小時，自己去購物。
Mr. McKinsey was later charged in Alameda County Superior Court with two misdemeanor counts of child abuse in connection with the separate incidents. He had pleaded not guilty to both charges and was released from jail on the condition that he complete 12 parenting classes. A pretrial hearing had been scheduled for next month.
鑒於這些本身無關聯的事件，麥金西後來在阿拉米達縣高等法院被指控兩項輕罪兒童虐待。他在對兩項指控均不認罪後獲釋，條件是完成12節育兒課程。審前聽證會原定於下個月舉行。
In December, Mr. McKinsey filed his own request for a temporary restraining order against his wife, citing years of domestic violence. According to his filing, his wife had recently been granted full custody of the children, and he feared for their safety. That same month, he filed for divorce.
12月，麥金西提交了自己對妻子的臨時限制令申請，理由是多年的家庭暴力。根據他的申請，妻子最近獲得了孩子的完全監護權，他擔心他們的安全。同月，他申請離婚。
Mr. McKinsey said that he had been framed for the October incident in which the couple’s 2-year-old son was found alone. He said that Ms. Jang was responsible for the children during the workweek and that she had not told him before leaving that the child was sleeping in his crib upstairs. “She did this on purpose, to set me up,” he wrote.
麥金西說，在10月這對夫婦的兩歲兒子被發現獨自一人的事件中，他是被陷害的。他說張女士在工作日負責孩子，她離開前沒有告訴他孩子正在樓上嬰兒床裡睡覺。「她故意這樣做，來陷害我，」他寫道。
廣告
In his filing, he accused his wife of regularly abusing their children and neglecting to properly bathe and feed them.
在申請中，他指控妻子經常虐待孩子，並且沒有給他們適當洗澡和餵食的照護。
“When I protested about any of this, she threatened me with harm or said she would call the police and tell them I was hurting our children,” he said in the filing.
「當我對此提出抗議時，她威脅要傷害我，或者說她會報警並告訴他們，我在傷害我們的孩子，」他在申請中說。
He said that Ms. Jang had once hit him on the head with a frying pan and forced him to ingest a cockroach. He accused her, on another occasion, of striking him on the head with a mirror, which shattered as he tried to wrest it from her control and severed a tendon and nerve in his hand, requiring surgery and leaving him with a permanent loss of sensation in his thumb.
他說張女士曾用煎鍋打他的頭，並強迫他吞下一隻蟑螂。他還指控她有一次用鏡子打他的頭，當他試圖從她手中奪走鏡子時，鏡子碎裂，切斷了他手部的一條肌腱和神經，需要手術，並導致他拇指感覺永久性喪失。
