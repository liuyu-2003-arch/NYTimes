產能過剩背景下，中國汽車業走向併購重組時代

China’s Crowded Car Industry Moves Toward Consolidation

KEITH BRADSHER • 2026-09-29
Two leading electric carmakers in China said on Monday they would combine their battery charging subsidiaries, in the latest sign of consolidation in a severely crowded Chinese car industry.
週一，兩家中國領先的電動汽車製造商表示，將合併旗下的電池充電子公司，這是中國汽車行業競爭異常激烈背景下行業整合的最新跡象。
The deal will combine divisions of Nio and Zhejiang Geely Holding Group, with Nio also acquiring 10 percent of a separate Geely business. The transaction comes just two weeks after another Chinese automaker, Guangzhou Automobile Group, announced plans for a complex tie-up with First Automotive Works, a large manufacturer.
此次交易將合併蔚來和浙江吉利控股集團的相關部門，蔚來還將收購吉利另一項獨立業務10%的股份。就在兩週前，另一家中國汽車製造商廣州汽車集團宣布了與大型製造商中國一汽進行複雜結盟的計劃。
Last year, two of the Chinese car industry’s giants, Dongfeng Motor and Changan Automobile, were in discussions to merge but did not reach a deal.
去年，中國汽車行業的兩大巨頭東風汽車和長安汽車曾就合併進行談判，但未能達成協議。
China’s car industry has immense overcapacity: enough car factories to build every car sold in China as well as all of the production in the United States and Europe. But its domestic car market has been shrinking since 2017, as Chinese consumer spending has been depressed by a plunge in housing prices.
中國汽車行業存在巨大的產能過剩：其擁有的汽車工廠不僅足以生產在中國銷售的所有汽車，甚至還能覆蓋美國和歐洲的全部產量。但自2017年以來，由於房價暴跌壓抑了中國消費者的支出，其國內汽車市場一直在萎縮。
廣告
“There are simply too many automakers in China — mergers, acquisitions and restructuring will be a trend going forward,” said David Zhang, a visiting professor at Huanghe University of Science and Technology in Zhengzhou, China.
「中國的汽車製造商實在太多了——併購重組將成為未來的趨勢，」中國鄭州黃河科技學院客座教授張翔說。
Car sales in China shrank 20.8 percent in the first eight months of this year from the same period in 2025. Carmakers have tried to compensate by increasingly shipping their cars overseas. China’s vehicle exports are expected to reach at least 10 million this year, up from one million cars in 2020.
今年前八個月，中國汽車銷量較2025年同期萎縮了20.8%。汽車製造商試圖通過加大汽車出口來彌補這一缺口。中國今年的汽車出口量預計將達到至少1000萬輛，遠高於2020年的100萬輛。
The European Union, concerned about the rapid decline of Europe’s auto sector, has been considering possible restrictions on further Chinese car exports. Volkswagen has struggled to compete with Chinese imports and recently announced plans to cut an additional 50,000 jobs. Senior European officials are scheduled to arrive in Beijing next week for trade talks.
歐盟對歐洲汽車行業的快速衰退感到擔憂，一直在考慮是否對中國的汽車出口實施進一步限制。大眾汽車在與中國進口汽車的競爭中舉步維艱，最近宣布計劃再裁員5萬名員工。歐洲高級官員定於下週抵達北京，進行貿易談判。
Even with surging exports, the average car factory in China is still operating at slightly over half of capacity. In turn, automakers have cut prices below the cost of building cars, resulting in widespread losses for car manufacturers and delays in their payments to auto parts suppliers.
即便出口激增，中國普通汽車工廠的平均產能利用率仍然僅略高於一半。汽車製造商進而將售價降至造車成本以下，導致汽車製造商普遍虧損，並延遲了向汽車零部件供應商付款。
In Monday’s transaction, Geely agreed to merge its battery-swapping business into Nio’s larger battery-swapping business and pay $95 million in exchange for a 30 percent stake in the combined business, which plans to have 10,000 battery-swapping stations by 2030. Nio also will take a 10 percent stake in Geely’s extensive electric car charging station division, which is expected to have 22,000 charging stations by the end of next year.
在週一的交易中，吉利同意將其換電業務併入蔚來規模更大的換電業務中，並支付6.4億元，以換取合併後業務30%的股份，合併後的業務計劃到2030年建成1萬座換電站。蔚來還將獲得吉利龐大的電動汽車充電站業務10%的股份，該部門預計到明年年底將擁有2.2萬個充電站。
In addition, the companies said they would coordinate the designs of their cars related to battery swapping and recharging.
此外，兩家公司表示將協調各自汽車在換電和充電方面的設計。
廣告
Many Chinese automakers have been talking for more than a decade about coordinating designs for battery swapping, according to Stephen Dyer, head of the Asia automotive practice at Alix Partners, a global consulting firm.
全球諮詢公司艾睿鉑亞洲汽車業務負責人戴加輝(Stephen Dyer)表示，十多年來，許多中國汽車製造商一直在探討協調換電設計的事宜。
The agreement between Geely and Nio, which together sold 1.3 million battery-electric or plug-in hybrid cars in the first half of this year, represents a step toward standardization in battery manufacturing.
吉利和蔚來在今年上半年共售出了130萬輛純電動或插電式混合動力汽車，兩家公司達成的這項協議代表著向電池製造標準化邁進了一步。
“It’s just really invasive to design your vehicle around a battery-swapping standard,” Mr. Dyer said.
「圍繞換電標準來設計車輛，對設計的干預程度確實很高，」戴加輝說。
Battery swapping, in which drivers pull up to a roadside facility and an automated shed replaces a depleted battery with a fully charged one, takes three or four minutes. It takes twice as long for an 80 percent charge with even a very fast charger. But battery swapping remains far less popular than plug-in chargers in China and elsewhere, and many auto analysts have questioned whether battery swapping will ever catch up.
換電是指司機把車停在路邊的設施旁，由自動更換艙將耗盡的電池更換為充滿電的電池，整個過程需要三四分鐘。而即使使用非常快的充電樁，將電池充到80%電量所需的時間也是換電的兩倍。但在中國和其他地方，換電的普及程度仍然遠不及插電式充電樁，許多汽車分析師也質疑，換電究竟能否迎頭趕上。
BYD, the industry leader in China, has suffered a 34 percent drop in domestic retail sales so far this year, while Geely’s sales have dropped half as much. As a result, Geely has nearly caught up this year to BYD in the Chinese market.
今年迄今為止，行業領軍企業比亞迪在中國的國內零售銷量下降了34%，而吉利的銷量降幅僅為其一半。因此，吉利今年在中國市場幾乎趕上了比亞迪。
Guangzhou Auto disclosed in a stock market filing on Sept. 15 that it planned to issue shares to First Auto Works, which China’s national government owns directly. In exchange, Guangzhou Auto will gain part ownership of a longtime joint venture between First Auto Works and Toyota. The transaction will turn First Auto Works into the second-largest shareholder in Guangzhou Auto after the Guangzhou municipal government.
廣汽集團在9月15日的一份股市文件中披露，計劃向中國中央政府直接持有的中國一汽配售股份。作為交換，廣汽集團將獲得一汽集團與豐田長期合資企業的一部分股權。此項交易將使一汽集團成為廣汽集團的第二大股東，僅次於廣州市人民政府。
廣告
Guangzhou Auto has its own joint venture with Toyota that competes with the First Auto Works joint venture with the Japanese giant.
廣汽集團自身也有一家與豐田的合資企業，與中國一汽同這家日本巨頭的合資企業存在競爭關係。
Since the 1980s, China has required foreign automakers to partner with Chinese companies to enter the Chinese market. Chinese automakers learned much from these partnerships.
自20世紀80年代以來，中國一直要求外國汽車製造商必須與中國企業結成合作夥伴才能進入中國市場。中國汽車製造商從這些合作關係中獲益良多。
As part of its response to the flood of Chinese exports, the European Union has been mulling whether to require joint ventures for Chinese electric car manufacturers. China’s Ministry of Commerce said in a statement in March that it strongly opposed any imposition by the E.U. of joint venture requirements, technology sharing mandates and similar rules, describing them as “serious investment barriers and institutional discrimination.”
作為應對中國出口產品湧入的部分舉措，歐盟一直在考慮是否要求中國電動汽車製造商成立合資企業。中國商務部在今年3月的一份聲明中表示，強烈反對歐盟強加合資要求、技術共享強制令及類似規定，稱其為「嚴重的投資壁壘和制度性歧視」。
