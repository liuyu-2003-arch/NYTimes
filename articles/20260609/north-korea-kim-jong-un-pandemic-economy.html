「奇蹟般的轉變」：金正恩如何重塑朝鮮

A ‘Miraculous Transformation’: How Kim Jong-un Fortified North Korea

CHOE SANG-HUN • 2026-06-09
During the pandemic, the North Korean leader Kim Jong-un, who had long billed himself as invincible, made a stunning, teary-eyed apology on national television.
在疫情期間，長期以來一直自詡戰無不勝的朝鮮領導人金正恩在國家電視台上發表了熱淚盈眶的驚人道歉。
“I am really sorry,” he said, as the coronavirus, coupled with food shortages and international sanctions, was ravaging his country. “My efforts and sincerity have not been sufficient enough to rid our people of the difficulties in their life.”
「我深感歉意，」他說。當時，新冠疫情加之糧食短缺和國際制裁正無情地摧殘著他的國家。「我的努力和誠心不足以使人民擺脫生活困難。」
But while ordinary North Koreans were suffering, Mr. Kim, 42, seized the crisis as a unique opportunity. Now, he is brimming with confidence. He is recognized at home and abroad as North Korea’s most powerful leader to date, surpassing even his grandfather, the country’s founder, because he has achieved the status of a de facto nuclear power.
然而，就在普通朝鮮民眾深陷苦難之時，42歲的金正恩卻將這場危機視為一個絕佳的機遇。如今，他信心滿滿。由於他使得朝鮮成為事實上的核大國，他被國內外公認為迄今為止朝鮮最有權勢的領導人，甚至超越了他的祖父——該國的開國元勛。
He started during the pandemic by shutting down the border with China, issuing shoot-to-kill orders to stop North Koreans fleeing across the boundary. He clamped down on trade and smuggling across the border, forcing his people to rely less on imports and produce more goods domestically. His campaign also targeted the informal markets where many had eked out a living — trading Chinese goods and foreign entertainment smuggled in on thumb drives — ever since the devastating famine of the 1990s.
在疫情期間，他首先關閉了與中國接壤的邊境，下達格殺勿論的命令，阻止朝鮮人越境逃亡。他嚴厲打擊邊境貿易和走私活動，迫使民眾減少對進口的依賴，轉向增加國內生產。他的整肅行動還把矛頭對準了非正規市場——自20世紀90年代毀滅性的饑荒以來，許多人一直依靠這些市場裡買賣中國商品以及USB隨身碟走私入境的外國娛樂節目勉強度日。
廣告
By dismantling these underground markets, Mr. Kim strengthened the regime’s monopoly over the economy and ideology. Enforcement was brutal. Those caught distributing K-pop or K-dramas were publicly executed.
通過摧毀這些地下市場，金正恩加強了該政權對經濟和意識型態的壟斷。執法手段異常殘暴。被發現傳播韓國流行音樂或韓劇的人遭到公開處決。
At the same time, he flouted international sanctions to steadily expand his nuclear arsenal. North Korea built a new generation of missiles capable of striking its nearest adversaries, South Korea and Japan, with nuclear warheads. It is also developing the means to target the American mainland, through nuclear-powered submarines and intercontinental ballistic missile technology.
與此同時，他無視國際制裁，穩步擴充其核武庫。朝鮮製造了新一代導彈，能夠用核彈頭打擊其最近的對手——韓國和日本。朝鮮還在研發能夠打擊美國本土的手段，包括核動力潛艇和洲際彈道導彈技術。
Mr. Kim also seized an unexpected opening abroad. As Russia struggled in its war against Ukraine​, he gave it weapons and troops.​ Moscow reciprocated with weapons technology to help modernize North Korea’s air defenses and other vulnerable parts of its military, along with badly needed food, oil and even tourists. The two nations signed a mutual defense and cooperation treaty, easing Pyongyang’s economic isolation and lifting its international status.
金正恩還在海外抓住了一個意想不到的突破口。當俄羅斯在對烏克蘭的戰爭中陷入困境時，他向俄羅斯提供了武器和軍隊。作為回報，莫斯科提供了武器技術，幫助朝鮮實現防空系統及軍隊中其他薄弱環節的現代化，並提供了朝鮮急需的糧食、石油甚至遊客。兩國簽署了共同防禦與合作條約，從而緩解了平壤的經濟孤立局面，提升了朝鮮的國際地位。
Since then, he has rebuffed any idea of renewing talks with President Trump or reconciling with South Korea. And his tilt toward Russia has prodded China to warm up to North Korea, a neighbor that Beijing views as hard to manage but whose hostility toward Washington gives it diplomatic leverage.
從那時起，金正恩拒絕了任何與川普總統重啟談判或與韓國和解的提議。而他向俄羅斯的傾斜也促使中國開始主動向朝鮮示好；在北京看來，這個鄰國雖然難以駕馭，但其對華盛頓的敵意卻能為中國提供外交籌碼。
China’s top leader, Xi Jinping, arrived in North Korea on Monday for a two-day state visit, his first in seven years. He is expected to underscore China’s indispensable position as North Korea’s dominant trading partner. The countries are already expanding trade again, resuming train service and adding more flights between Beijing and Pyongyang. They are close to completing a new modern road bridge across the Yalu River border, which is expected to facilitate bilateral exchanges, including an influx of Chinese tourists and aid.
中國最高領導人習近平於週一抵達朝鮮，開始為期兩天的國事訪問，這是他七年來首次訪朝。預計他將強調中國作為朝鮮主導貿易夥伴不可或缺的地位。兩國已經在重新擴大貿易，恢復了列車服務，並增加了北京與平壤之間的航班。一座橫跨鴨綠江邊境的全新現代化公路橋即將竣工，預計這將促進雙邊交流，令大批中國遊客和援助物資湧入朝鮮。
Poverty endures outside Pyongyang, yet analysts say the recent developments have given the government confidence and the public some hope. At his Workers’ Party congress earlier this year, Mr. Kim triumphantly declared that North Korea was in a glorious and more prosperous new era, a far cry from his weeping apology in 2020. People can now hope to have “both sweets and bullets,” the party said, referring to its policy of seeking both economic recovery and military prowess.
在平壤之外，貧困依然存在，但分析人士表示，近期的這些發展為政府賦予信心，也給了公眾一絲希望。在今年早些時候召開的勞動黨代表大會上，金正恩得意洋洋地宣布，朝鮮正邁入一個輝煌且更加繁榮的新時代，這與他2020年聲淚俱下的道歉形成了鮮明對比。勞動黨表示，人們現在可以期望同時擁有「糖果與子彈」，這是指其同時謀求經濟復甦和軍事強盛的政策。
廣告
To uncover how Mr. Kim pulled off this transformation, The New York Times spoke with more than a dozen post-pandemic defectors, interviewed experts with inside connections, and scrutinized state media and leaked regime documents.
為了揭示金正恩如何完成這一逆轉，《紐約時報》採訪了十多位在疫情後逃離的脫北者，走訪了擁有內部人脈的專家，並仔細研究了朝鮮官方媒體和遭洩漏的政權文件。
Jiro Ishimaru, the chief editor of the Japan-based Asia Press International, which has clandestine correspondents inside North Korea, said, “In the past few years, Kim Jong-un has traveled from hell to heaven.”
總部位於日本的亞洲新聞在朝鮮國內安插有祕密通訊員，該社主編石丸次郎說：「在過去幾年裡，金正恩可以說是從地獄升入了天堂。」
The ​Threat
威脅
North Korea claims that it defeated Covid without vaccines, losing only a few dozen people.
朝鮮聲稱，該國在沒有疫苗的情況下戰勝了新冠疫情，僅有幾十人喪生。
But as the virus spread, Kim Il-hyeok saw people ​die ​in unusual numbers in his neighborhood​ in Hwanghae Province, a breadbasket region. The authorities were siphoning rice from ​the region to feed the elites in Pyongyang and the military, he said. There were no Covid tests available so it was hard to tell whether ​they died of hunger or the virus. Those who fell sick ​did not report it to the authorities for fear of being quarantined without food. Lacking medication, they resorted to folk remedies, such as drinking tree-bark juice, that often worsened their condition.
但隨著病毒的蔓延，金日赫（音）目睹了他所在的黃海道（朝鮮產糧大省）的自家社區裡，出現了非同尋常的死亡人數。他說，當局不斷從該地區調取大米，供養平壤的精英階層和軍隊。由於無法進行新冠檢測，很難分辨這些人究竟是死於飢餓還是死於病毒。生病的人不敢向當局報告，生怕面臨被隔離後斷糧的絕境。由於缺乏藥物，他們只能求助於偏方，比如喝樹皮汁，但這往往只會使他們的病情加重。
“Families didn’t have money for coffins,” said Mr. Kim,​ 36, who fled to South Korea by boat with eight relatives in 2023. “Some were not even strong enough to dig a hole.”
「很多家庭沒錢買棺材，」36歲的金日赫說。2023年，他與八名親屬乘船逃到了韓國。「有些人甚至連挖坑掩埋屍體的力氣都沒有了。」
廣告
Kim Yu-mi, his sister-in-law, made the two-hour escape with him. She said her hometown on the southwestern coast of North Korea was so close to South Korea that in clear weather ​she could see shiny passenger jets flying in and out of ​the South’s Incheon International Airport​.
他的嫂子金有美（音）與他一同完成了這場耗時兩小時的逃亡。她說，她位於朝鮮西南海岸的家鄉離韓國非常近，在天氣晴朗的時候，她甚至能看到閃閃發光的客機在韓國仁川國際機場起降。
“​Those planes symbolized freedom for our children​,” Ms. Kim said.​ “When a North Korean patrol ship ​detected ​our boat​ fleeing to the South and chased, ​we threw everything ​we had overboard to help it gain speed.​”
「飛機象徵著我們孩子未來的自由，」金有美說。「一艘朝鮮巡邏艇發現了我們逃往韓國的船隻，展開了追擊，我們把船上所有的東西都扔進海裡，就是為了讓船跑得更快。」
​The family’s escape represented the threat ​the outside world, especially South Korea, posed to the regime​.
這個家庭的逃亡折射出外部世界——尤其是韓國——對該政權構成的威脅。
North Koreans’ trust in the government has been waning since​ the 1990s famine, according to defectors. ​As the government’s ration system collapsed, North Koreans learned to fend for themselves through informal markets where they traded goods, including those smuggled from China. The markets also became a breeding ground for corruption as officials collected kickbacks from tradespeople.
脫北者們表示，自上世紀90年代的饑荒以來，朝鮮民眾對政府的信任就一直在減退。隨著政府配給制度的崩潰，朝鮮人學會了通過非正規市場買賣包括中國走私品在內的商品，以此自謀生路。這些市場也成為了腐敗的溫床，因為官員們會向商販索取回扣。
With those goods came memory sticks containing South Korean shows, including “Crash Landing on You,” in which a paragliding mishap takes a South Korean heiress into North Korea and into the arms of a People’s Army officer. North Koreans consumed them with a vengeance — behind closed doors and under blankets for fear of the secret police, who routinely monitored neighborhoods, Ms. Kim said.
伴隨這些商品一同流入的還有裝滿韓國影視節目的USB隨身碟，其中就包括《愛的迫降》——該劇講述了一位韓國財閥女繼承人因滑翔傘事故意外墜落朝鮮，並與一名人民軍軍官相愛的故事。金有美說，朝鮮民眾對此類節目的渴望達到了痴迷的程度——由於害怕常常在社區巡查的祕密警察，他們只能關起門來，躲在被窩裡偷偷觀看。
In 2010, Ms. Kim’s coastal town was caught in an artillery duel between the two Koreas. Residents rushed to take down portraits of state leaders to protect the images, a sign of their devotion and loyalty. By 2023, ​Ms. Kim said, about eight out of 10 people in her neighborhood ​were watching South Korean shows — a sign of how much had changed.
2010年，金有美所在的沿海小鎮捲入了朝韓兩國間的一場炮戰。當時，居民們爭先恐後地取下國家領導人的畫像加以保護，以此展現他們的虔誠與忠心。然而，金有美說，到了2023年，她所在的社區裡大約有八成的人都在觀看韓國節目——這足以說明變化是多麼巨大。
“Loyalty to the party ​was no longer as important as making money,” she said.
「對黨的忠誠已經不再像賺錢那麼重要了，」她說。
This shift alarmed Pyongyang. Kim Jong-un was increasingly worried about “nonsocialist” ​content corrupting young North Koreans, likening it to a “vicious cancer.” His regime called those who exposed themselves to outside influence “treasonous” and called for “striking them mercilessly,” according to a classified document dated June ​2024 that was obtained by the Human Rights Foundation, a New York-based nonprofit. The Times has also viewed similar internal documents smuggled out of North Korea.
這種轉變引起了平壤的警覺。金正恩越來越擔心「非社會主義」內容會腐蝕朝鮮的年輕人，他將其比作「惡性腫瘤」。總部位於紐約的非營利組織人權基金會獲取了一份日期標註為2024年6月的機密文件。文件顯示，該政權將受外界影響的人稱作「叛國分子」，要求對他們進行「無情打擊」。《紐約時報》也查看過被偷偷帶出朝鮮的類似內部文件。
廣告
For Mr. Kim, the threat posed by freedom of information was deeply personal — his crackdown, analysts say, was driven in part by a desire to suppress knowledge of his lineage.
對金正恩而言，信息自由帶來的威脅不僅關乎政權，更牽涉其切身隱秘——分析人士指出，他發起這場嚴打行動的部分原因是為了掩蓋有關其身世血統的真相。
His mother, Ko Yong-hui, was born in Japan, according to outside historians. That made her of an inferior class in North Korea, a country built on deep hatred for Japan after decades of colonial rule. His father apparently never introduced Ms. Ko and their son to Mr. Kim’s grandfather, Kim Il-sung, defectors have said. The boy was sent to spend part of his adolescence in Switzerland.
外部歷史學家指出，他的母親高英姬出生在日本。在經曆數十年殖民統治、對日本懷有深仇大恨的朝鮮，這樣的身世令她處於較低階層。據脫北者透露，金正恩的父親顯然從未向金正恩的祖父金日成引見高英姬及其兒子。這個男孩也被送到瑞士，度過了部分青春期時光。
The background of Mr. Kim’s mother — long suppressed inside North Korea — became a centerpiece of propaganda for South Korean activists in recent years. They flew leaflets by balloons with this information into North Korea, which helped set off Mr. Kim’s sweeping crackdown on outside information.
金正恩母親的背景在朝鮮國內長期遭到封鎖，但近年來卻成了韓國活動人士對朝宣傳的核心內容。他們利用氣球將印有這些信息的傳單空投進朝鮮，這也成了觸發金正恩全面封殺外部信息的導火線之一。
“He was a child hidden from his grandfather, exiled overseas and brought back home after the grandfather’s death,” said Kim Kang, a North Korean diplomat in Russia who defected to South Korea.
「他是一個被藏起來不讓祖父看見的孩子，曾被流放海外，直到祖父死後才被接回家，」叛逃至韓國的前朝鮮駐俄羅斯外交官金江（音）說。
Before she died in 2004, Ms. Ko helped ensure that Kim Jong-il chose their son as heir. Still, Mr. Kim’s “complex was that of the son of a royal concubine,” said Kwak Gil-sup, a North Korea expert at the Seoul-based One Korea Center.
在2004年去世前，高英姬協助確保金正日選定他們的兒子作為接班人。即便如此，首爾的朝韓一家中心(One Korea Center)的朝鮮問題專家郭吉燮（音）表示，金正恩心裡仍有一種「王室庶子情結」。
“When his father was alive, he struggled to win his favor,” Mr. Kwak said. “After he took power, he was driven to prove different and better than his father.”
「父親還在世時，他曾竭力爭取父親歡心，」郭吉燮說。「掌權之後，他一心想要證明自己與父親不同，並且比父親更出色。」
廣告
The ​Crackdown
鎮壓
When he succeeded his father in 2011, Mr. Kim promised North Koreans that they would no longer have to “tighten their belt.” He cast himself as a “people-first” leader, surveying floodwaters by speedboat, visiting victims in temporary shelters and cradling babies on his lap.
2011年接掌父位之初，金正恩向朝鮮人民承諾，他們不必再「勒緊褲腰帶過日子了」。他將自己塑造成一位「以民為本」的領導人——乘快艇視察洪澇災區，到臨時安置點探望災民，把嬰兒抱在膝上。
​But his expensive pursuit of nuclear weapons and his own extravagan​t lifestyle jarred with the reality of regular citizens.
然而，他對核武器的巨額投入與自身的奢靡生活與普通百姓的現實處境形成了刺眼的反差。
While humanitarian agencies reported widespread malnourishment in the country, Mr. Kim’s waistline ballooned, his weight raising questions about his health. Inheriting his father’s love for wining and dining, he gorged on Wagyu steak, toro tuna, shark fin soup, Swiss cheese, lobster, caviar and foie gras, according to chefs familiar with his dining habits. He guzzled Bordeaux wine and Cristal Champagne.
當國際人道主義機構不斷報告朝鮮廣泛存在營養不良問題時，金正恩的腰圍卻不斷見漲，他的體重甚至引發外界對其健康狀況的猜測。據熟悉其飲食習慣的廚師透露，他繼承了父親對美酒佳肴的嗜好，大吃和牛牛排、金槍魚腩、魚翅羹、瑞士奶酪、龍蝦、魚子醬和鵝肝，還狂飲波爾多紅酒和水晶香檳。
Defectors said the capital, Pyongyang, often didn’t have enough electricity to run elevators. But Mr. Kim spent millions of dollars to smuggle a fleet of Mercedes and other luxury cars for personal use, Orlov Trotters from Russia for his private horse-riding tracks and dolphins for a new dolphinarium.
據脫北者稱，就連首都平壤有時都沒有足夠電力維持電梯運行。但金正恩卻花費數百萬美元走私多輛梅賽德斯奔馳和其他豪車供自己使用，從俄羅斯引進奧爾洛夫快步馬供其在私人馬場馳騁，還為新建海豚館購置海豚。
He exerted power by unleashing what South Korean officials called a reign of terror. He executed those who rubbed him the wrong way, including his own uncle, Jang Song-thaek. He sent agents to assassinate Kim Jong-nam, his half brother who, as his father’s first son, posed a threat to Mr. Kim’s dynastic throne.
他通過發動韓國官員所稱的「恐怖統治」來鞏固權力。凡是令他不滿的人都可能被處決，其中包括他的姑父張成澤。他還派特工暗殺同父異母的兄長金正男。由於金正男是其父的長子，因此被視為對金正恩世襲統治地位的潛在威脅。
Lee Ilkyu, a former North Korean diplomat in Cuba who fled to Seoul in 2023, recalled how Han Song-ryol, another North Korean diplomat, was executed on spying charges in 2019. Mr. Lee said witnesses told him that hundreds of bullets tore Mr. Han to smithereens.
2023年逃往首爾的朝鮮前駐古巴外交官李日圭回憶說，另一名朝鮮外交官韓成烈於2019年因間諜罪被處決。李日圭說，有目擊者告訴他，數百發子彈將韓成烈打得稀爛。
“They lost their appetite for days,” he said.
他說，「他們好幾天都吃不下飯。」
Still, the state’s control had loosened in the decades following the famine. Then the pandemic helped Mr. Kim reassert his grip.
儘管如此，在經歷了大饑荒之後的幾十年裡，國家的控制已經有所鬆動。隨後，新冠疫情幫助金正恩重新收緊了控制。
He enacted laws with draconian penalties, including executions by firing squad, for those consuming and distributing anti-socialist content, or even mimicking South Korean speech and fashion styles. Officials raided homes, pulling out drawers and turning over blankets to find memory sticks with foreign entertainment, according to defectors
他頒佈了一系列嚴苛法律，對觀看和傳播反社會主義內容、乃至模仿韓國口音和時尚風格的人處以槍決。據脫北者稱，官員會突擊搜查民宅，翻抽屜、掀被褥，搜繳存有境外娛樂內容的存儲設備。
Kim Il-hyeok, the defector who fled by boat, said he saw a score of killings in his town in the three years before his escape, compared with half a dozen in the previous two decades. Executions took place in ​schoolyards and other open spaces where the condemned were denounced before thousands of people. Mr. Kim recounted how a 22-year-old man he knew was executed in 2022 for distributing three South Korean dramas and 70 K-pop songs. He was clad in a long white sack-like robe and had a hood over his head. Three gunmen each fired five shots.
乘船逃離朝鮮的脫北者金日赫表示，在他逃離前的三年裡，僅在自己生活的城鎮就目睹了約20起處決，而此前20年總共也不過六起。處決在學校操場和其他空地上進行，被處決者會在數千名群眾面前遭公開批鬥。金日赫回憶，他認識的一名22歲男子在2022年因傳播三部韓劇和70首K-pop歌曲而被處決。當時那名年輕人身上套著一件白色麻袋狀的長袍，頭上罩著頭套，三名行刑者各開五槍。
“When you watch enough of them, you grow numb, unable to tell a human from an animal,” ​he said.​
「看多了，人就麻木了，分不清人跟動物有什麼區別，」他說。
​By the time they ​fled North Korea,​ ​the authorities had all but stamped out unofficial markets, his sister-in-law, Ms. Kim, said. ​They reinstalled socialist control on the production and distribution of goods​. Rice and other grains were available only through government stores.
到他們逃離朝鮮時，當局幾乎已經完全取締了地下市場，金日赫的嫂子說道。政府重新建立起社會主義體制下對商品生產和流通的控制，大米等糧食只能通過國營商店購買。
“The most difficult part of Kim Jong-un’s rule was that we were not allowed to make money,”​ Ms. Kim said. “​He tightened the noose on his people, as if he didn’t want them to have a better life.”
「金正恩統治時期最困難的一點，就是不讓我們賺錢，」金日赫的嫂子說。「他不斷收緊套在人民脖子上的絞索，彷彿不願讓他們過上好日子。」
The ​War Bonanza
發戰爭財
Around this time, Mr. Ishimaru of Asia Press received covert reports from North Korea of a nation sinking into despair.
大約在這一時期，《亞洲新聞》的石丸次郎不斷收到來自朝鮮的祕密報告，描繪的是一個正在陷入絕望的國度。
Citizens, he said, “saw no way forward, didn’t know how they were supposed to live on” — paralyzed by Mr. Kim’s relentless push for a “self‑reliant” economy, a tired slogan that took on a devastating new meaning during pandemic-driven shortages.
他說，民眾「看不到出路，不知道該如何活下去」，他們被金正恩對「自力更生」經濟的執念所困，這個老套的口號在疫情期間的物資短缺中產生了毀滅性的新含義。
But even as Mr. Kim strangled foreign influence at home, he was scheming abroad — maneuvering to arrest his country’s economic tailspin.
然而，就在金正恩在國內扼殺外來影響的同時，他也在國際上悄然布局，試圖挽救國家經濟的螺旋式下滑。
With all its major exports blocked​ under international sanctions, North Korea sought revenue by smuggling out coal and collecting wages earned by its workers in China, who ​continued to work for Mr. Kim’s government during the pandemic​. Mr. Kim unleashed an army of hackers and computer engineers online to steal billions of dollars worth of cryptocurrency ​and earn cash by doing freelance IT work with false identities.​
由於主要出口產品都受到國際制裁限制，朝鮮開始通過走私煤炭以及收取在中國工作的朝鮮勞工工資來獲取收入。這些工人在疫情期間也繼續為金正恩政府工作。金正恩還派出了一支龐大的駭客和計算機工程師隊伍，通過網路竊取了價值數十億美元的加密貨幣，並以虛假身份承接IT外包項目換取現金。
Gold nuggets became an important item to smuggle out of North Korea to sell and raise foreign currency for the state, favored because of their small size, said a senior North Korean trade official who operated in Asia for a decade before defecting to South Korea last year. An internal North Korean document seen by The Times noted that a North Korean consulate vehicle in Dandong, a Chinese border city, was used to smuggle gold into China.
據一名在亞洲為朝鮮從事了十年貿易工作、去年逃至韓國的朝鮮高級貿易官員透露，金塊因體積小而成為朝鮮走私出境、換取外匯的重要商品。《紐約時報》看到的一份朝鮮內部文件顯示，朝鮮駐中國邊境城市丹東的領事辦公室的車輛曾被用於向中國走私黃金。
North Korea​n government smugglers ​imported precision tools used to make weapons​, paying high margins for middlemen involved in evading sanctions, said the former ​trade official​, who spoke on condition of anonymity to protect his relatives in the North.
這名前貿易官員還說，朝鮮政府的走私人員還進口了用於武器製造的精密工具，並為規避制裁的中間商支付了高額傭金。出於保護仍留在朝鮮親屬的考慮，這名官員要求匿名
​But Mr. Kim needed more to revive an economy that had been hobbled by the double whammy of the world’s severest sanctions and the pandemic.
但金正恩需要更多手段來重振因全球最嚴厲制裁和疫情雙重打擊而陷入癱瘓的經濟。
He saw an opportunity after Russia’s full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, offering North Korean troops and munitions.
2022年俄羅斯全面入侵烏克蘭後，金正恩看到了機會。他向俄羅斯提供軍隊和彈藥。
​Munitions factories ​roared to meet demand for Russia’s war effort.​ About 16,000 North Korean troops have fought in the war, testing their weapons in combat and gaining valuable insights into modern warfare, including the use of drones, according to South Korean intelligence officials. North Korea also sent workers to Russia to earn cash for its regime, they said. Military experts fear that with Russian help, ​North Korea is building its first nuclear-powered submarine and other modern warships, as well as a fleet of military drones.
軍工廠為了滿足俄羅斯戰爭需求而開足馬力。韓國情報機構稱，約有1.6萬名朝鮮士兵參與了戰爭，他們在實戰中測試武器，並獲得了關於現代戰爭的重要經驗，包括無人機使用。韓國官員還表示，朝鮮向俄羅斯派遣勞工為政權賺取外匯。軍事專家擔心，在俄羅斯幫助下，朝鮮正在建造其首艘核動力潛艇以及其他現代化軍艦，並打造一支軍用無人機機群。
In 2024, North Korea’s economy expanded 3.7 percent, the highest growth rate in eight years​, according to most recent estimates by South Korea’s central bank. ​
據韓國央行最新估算，2024年朝鮮經濟增長了3.7%，為八年來最高增速。
Outside Threats
外部威脅
The Russia-North Korea military partnership has openly undermined U.N. sanctions — one of Washington’s most relied-upon diplomatic levers. The deepening relationship also serves Mr. Kim’s broader strategic aim: balancing ties between China and Russia.
俄朝軍事夥伴關係公然破壞了聯合國制裁——而這正是華盛頓最依賴的外交槓桿之一。這一日益深化的關係也服務於金正恩更大的戰略目標：在中俄之間尋求平衡。
“North Korea has the most leverage today that it has had in the last 30 years,” said Frank Aum, a former Korea expert at the Pentagon.
「朝鮮如今所擁有的籌碼是過去三十年來最多的，」前五角大廈朝鮮問題專家弗蘭克·奧姆表示。
But North Korea is not out of the woods yet.​
但朝鮮仍未完全擺脫困境。
Its official exports to China have been dominated by items it could export without violating U.N. sanctions — wigs and false eyelashes and mustaches and tungsten ores. ​Mr. Kim’s crackdown on informal markets impeded the flow of cash. As trade with China rebounded, North Korea imported far more than it exported, eroding its foreign currency reserves. In the past year,​ the North Korean won has depreciated more than two-thirds against the dollar while rice prices there have more than tripled.
其對華官方出口以不違反聯合國制裁的商品為主——假髮、假睫毛、假鬍鬚以及鎢礦石。金正恩對地下市場的打擊阻礙了現金流通。隨著對華貿易反彈，朝鮮的進口遠大於出口，持續消耗外匯儲備。過去一年，朝鮮元對美元貶值超過三分之二，而大米價格漲幅超過兩倍。
Transactions with Russia have not significantly helped ease North Korea’s shortage of foreign currency. Neither side can convert their cash piles into usable currencies without attracting regulatory scrutiny, said Olena Guseinova, a lecturer at the Hankuk University of Foreign Studies in Seoul​.
與俄羅斯的交易也未能顯著緩解朝鮮外匯短缺問題。首爾韓國外國語大學講師奧萊娜·古謝諾娃表示，俄朝雙方都難以在不引起監管機構關注的情況下將各自持有的大量現金兌換成可自由使用的貨幣。
​But in the past couple of years, there have been clear signs ​of economic improvement in North Korea. ​Mr. Kim finished some of his long-delayed pet projects, like large seaside and ski and spa resort towns​ and greenhouse complexes built on airfields, including one the size of 400 soccer fields. New apartment towers​ rose not only in Pyongyang but also in provincial cities. Mr. Kim mobilized soldiers for these construction projects, funding them with remittances from North Koreans working abroad and forced donations from individuals who grew wealthy through smuggling and market activities, according to defectors and analysts.
不過，過去幾年裡，朝鮮經濟確實出現了一些明顯的改善跡象。金正恩完成了一些長期拖延的重點項目，例如大型海濱度假村、滑雪溫泉度假村以及建在機場舊址上的大型溫室群，其中一處面積相當於400個足球場。新的住宅高樓不僅出現在平壤，也出現在地方城市。根據脫北者和分析人士說法，金正恩動員軍隊參與建設項目，並利用海外勞工匯款以及依靠走私和各種市場活動積累起財富的人被強制攤派的捐款來籌集資金。
North Korea has added more oil terminals at its ports and there are more cars, buses, trucks and construction equipment visible across the country, said Joung Eunlee, an analyst at the Seoul-based Korea Institute for Unification Studies, who studied satellite imagery and interviewed recent visitors.
位於首爾的韓國統一研究院研究員鄭恩利（音）通過研究衛星圖像和採訪近期訪朝人士分析稱，朝鮮已在多處港口新建石油碼頭，全國範圍內可見的汽車、公車、卡車和工程設備也增加了。
In Pyongyang, which was once dimly lit at night, neon signs blaze brighter than ever.
在曾經晚間燈光昏暗的平壤，霓虹燈比以往任何時候都明亮。
High-rise apartment towers operate their elevators at least for a few hours a day​, according to recent defectors​ and visitors.​ There are more gas stations and more privately owned cars, some of them electric vehicles from China​. People have lined up at beer halls​ in Pyongyang. Families use smartphone apps to shop and order home-delivery food. ​
據近期的脫北者和訪朝人士稱，高層公寓樓的電梯每天至少能運行幾個小時。加油站越來越多，私家車也更多了，其中一些是中國產的電動車。人們在平壤的啤酒屋前排起長隊。家庭可以通過智慧型手機應用購物和點外賣。
Senior officials and their families in ​Pyongyang still live a life the rest of the country can hardly imagine: spacious apartments with saunas, maids, Chinese and Japanese electronics, and elevators running 24/7, said the former trade official who defected ​last year.
去年脫北的那名前貿易官員表示，平壤的高級官員及其家屬依然過著朝鮮其他地區難以想像的生活：帶有桑拿浴室、有傭人服務、配備了中國和日本產電子產品的寬敞公寓，以及全天候運行的電梯。
Addressing North Korea’s rubber-stamp Parliament in March, Mr. Kim spoke in triumphant terms of a “miraculous transformation.” He pointed to manifold increases in investment and sweeping large-scale residential construction.
今年3月，金正恩在朝鮮橡皮圖章式的最高人民會議上發表講話，用勝利的口吻談到了「奇蹟般的轉變」。他列舉了投資大幅增長以及大規模住宅建設項目等成果。
“Ours is no longer a country that is susceptible to threats from others, ” he said.
「我們的國家不再是一個容易受到他人威脅的國家了，」他說。
