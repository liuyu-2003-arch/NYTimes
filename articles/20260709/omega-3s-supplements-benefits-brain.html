歐米伽-3補充劑真的對大腦有益嗎？

Are Omega-3 Supplements Actually Good for Your Brain?

DANA G. SMITH • 2026-07-09
In theory, taking an omega-3, or fish oil, supplement makes a lot of sense.
從理論上講，服用歐米伽-3（即魚油）補充劑是合理的。
Omega-3 fatty acids are vital for brain health: They are used to build brain cells, keeping the cell walls flexible and enabling the neurons to sprout new connections and communicate with other cells. Numerous studies have shown that people with higher levels of omega-3s in their blood have better cognition and healthier looking brains, as well as a lower risk of developing dementia. In contrast, people with Alzheimer’s disease have been shown to have lower omega-3 levels.
歐米伽-3脂肪酸對大腦健康至關重要：它們被用來構建腦細胞，保持細胞壁的彈性，使神經元能夠長出新的連接並與其他細胞交流。眾多研究表明，血液中歐米伽-3水平較高的人擁有更好的認知能力和看起來更健康的大腦結構，同時患上失智症的風險也更低。相比之下，阿茲海默病患者已被證實歐米伽-3水平較低。
But there’s a catch: The vast majority of clinical trials have found that taking omega-3 supplements offers virtually no benefit for cognition or dementia symptoms.
但這裡存在一個問題：絕大多數臨床試驗發現，服用歐米伽-3補充劑對改善認知或緩解失智症癥狀而言，幾乎沒有任何益處。
“It kind of intuitively makes sense” that neurons need fatty acids for their health, so you should take a fatty acid supplement, said Dr. Kristine Yaffe, a professor of psychiatry, neurology and epidemiology at the University of California, San Francisco. “The problem is that most of the evidence, particularly the trial evidence, just doesn’t support it at all,” she said.
加利福尼亞大學舊金山分校的精神病學、神經病學和流行病學教授克里斯汀·雅菲博士表示，神經元的健康需要脂肪酸，因此應該服用脂肪酸補充劑，「這在直覺上是說得通的」。她說：「問題在於，大多數證據，尤其是試驗得出的證據，根本不支持這一點。」
廣告
A study published last month offers a prime example. The scientists who ran the clinical trial tried to cover all their bases: The participants were older adults who didn’t eat a lot of fish (which is rich in omega-3s), suggesting they might benefit the most from a supplement. Roughly half of the participants had an increased genetic risk for Alzheimer’s, which is another group that experts think might need more omega-3s. The researchers even did lumbar punctures on some of the participants to confirm that the supplement caused omega-3 levels in the brain to go up.
上個月發表的一項研究就是一個典型的例子。進行該臨床試驗的科學家們試圖面面俱到：參與者是平時不怎麼吃魚（魚類富含歐米伽-3）的老年人，這表明他們可能最能從補充劑中受益。大約一半的參與者患阿茲海默病的遺傳風險在增加，這是專家認為可能需要更多歐米伽-3的另一類人群。研究人員甚至對部分參與者進行了腰椎穿刺，以確認補充劑確實使大腦中的歐米伽-3水平有所上升。
But compared with a placebo, the supplement didn’t result in any benefit when it came to people’s cognition or brain structure.
但與安慰劑相比，該補充劑在改善認知或大腦結構方面沒有帶來任何益處。
So what’s behind the disconnect? Scientists have a few hypotheses, and most are connected to diet and lifestyle.
那麼，這種脫節背後原因何在？科學家有幾種假設，且大多數都與飲食和生活方式有關。
Hypothesis 1: Most people already get enough omega-3s.
假設一：大多數人已經攝入了足夠的歐米伽-3。
There are three main types of omega-3s that are important for health: EPA and DHA, which are primarily found in fish, and ALA, which is in nuts and seeds. The liver can convert small amounts of ALA into EPA and DHA.
對健康至關重要的歐米伽-3主要有三種類型：主要存在於魚類中的EPA和DHA，以及存在於堅果和種子中的ALA。肝臟可以將少量的ALA轉化為EPA和DHA。
DHA is the most vital for cognition, and there’s a large reserve of it in our brains. According to Richard Bazinet, a professor of nutritional sciences at the University of Toronto, the amount of DHA our brains use every day is just a tiny fraction of that reserve.
DHA對認知最為關鍵，我們的大腦中有大量的DHA儲備。多倫多大學營養科學教授理查德·巴齊內表示，我們的大腦每天消耗的DHA僅占該儲備中極其微小的一部分。
廣告
Even if people don’t eat a lot of fish, Dr. Bazinet thinks they still probably get enough omega-3s through their diet, particularly from ALA, to replace the small amount of DHA the brain uses.
巴齊內認為，一個人即使不怎麼吃魚，很可能也會通過飲食（尤其是通過ALA）獲取足夠的歐米伽-3，來補充大腦消耗掉的少量DHA。
“We all eat a ton of” ALA, Dr. Bazinet said. And if people’s DHA intake is low, the liver can convert the ALA to DHA and send it to the brain.
「我們都會吃很多」ALA，巴齊內說。如果DHA攝入量較低，肝臟就可以將ALA轉化為DHA並輸送給大腦。
In Dr. Bazinet’s mind, since virtually everyone gets enough omega-3s from their diet, the brain benefits that scientists see in people who have high blood levels of DHA are probably caused by something besides omega-3s — most likely from other healthy habits that tend to come with eating a diet rich in fish.
在巴齊內看來，既然幾乎每個人都能從飲食中攝入足夠的歐米伽-3，那麼科學家在血液DHA水平高的人群中所看到的大腦益處，可能並不是歐米伽-3本身帶來的——而更可能是伴隨攝入富含魚類飲食的其他健康習慣所致。
For example, when someone has fish for dinner, they typically pair it with vegetables, not junk food. “If you’re going to have a nice tuna steak, you might have a little bit of salad,” Dr. Bazinet said. And “when you’re eating that meal, you’re not eating something else” that’s less healthy for your brain, he added.
例如，晚餐吃魚時，人們通常會搭配蔬菜，而不是垃圾食品。巴齊內說：「如果你準備吃一盤精美的金槍魚排，你可能會配上一點沙拉。」他補充道。而且「當你吃那一餐時，你就不會去吃其他對你的大腦健康不那麼有幫助的東西了」。
Hypothesis 2: It’s how you metabolize omega-3s that really matters.
假設二：如何代謝歐米伽-3才是真正關鍵所在。
Dr. Hussein Yassine, a professor of neurology at the University of Southern California’s Keck School of Medicine, thinks it is how the brain uses omega-3s that matters most.
南加州大學凱克醫學院的神經學教授侯賽因·雅辛(Hussein Yassine)博士認為，大腦如何利用歐米伽-3才是最核心的問題。
廣告
In the brain, there is a molecule that breaks down and gets rid of omega-3s. Everyone has this molecule, but Dr. Yassine has found that it is more active in people with a genetic risk for Alzheimer’s. It’s possible that their brains metabolize omega-3s more quickly, depleting the levels.
在大腦中，存在一種能夠分解並清除歐米伽-3的分子。每個人都有這種分子，但雅辛發現，在具有阿茲海默病遺傳風險的人群中，這種分子更加活躍。他們的大腦代謝歐米伽-3的速度可能更快，從而導致迅速耗盡。
To improve brain health, Dr. Yassine, who also led the recent omega-3 supplement trial, thinks some people need not only to increase their omega-3 intake, but also to decrease the activity of that other molecule. Research is preliminary, but there’s some evidence that this molecule is affected by the gut microbiome and is less active in people who have a diet rich in plants, fiber and fermented foods. In other words, to benefit the brain, you need the nutrients from the tuna, as well as those from the salad or other healthy side dishes.
同樣主持了近期這項歐米伽-3補充劑試驗的雅辛認為，為了改善大腦健康，一些人不僅需要增加歐米伽-3的攝入量，還需要降低那種分子的活性。雖然研究尚處於初步階段，但有證據表明，這種分子會受到腸道微生物組的影響，並且在飲食富含植物、纖維和發酵食品的人群中活性較低。換句話說，要讓大腦獲益，你既需要金槍魚中的營養，也需要沙拉或其他健康配菜中的營養。
If someone has a poor diet “and you just give them a supplement where the only thing you change is the level of omega-3s in their blood and in their brain, our study would suggest that it’s not going to work,” Dr. Yassine said.
雅辛說，如果一個人的飲食習慣很差，「而你只是給他們提供一種補充劑，其中你唯一改變的是他們血液和腦部的歐米伽-3水平，我們的研究表明這是不會起作用的。」
Hypothesis 3: To see benefits, you need to consume large amounts of omega-3s for decades.
假設三：要想看到益處，你需要連續幾十年攝入大量的歐米伽-3。
Not everyone is giving up on omega-3 supplements. Gene Bowman, an assistant professor of neurology at Harvard Medical School, thinks one reason that clinical trials tend to fail is because they only test supplements for a few years, and that may not be enough time to detect changes in the brain. When people have high blood levels of omega-3s, it’s typically because they’ve eaten foods rich in omega-3s — by some estimates three weekly servings of fish — for decades.
並非所有人都在放棄歐米伽-3補充劑。哈佛醫學院神經學助理教授吉恩·鮑曼認為，臨床試驗往往失敗的原因之一在於，它們只對補充劑進行了幾年的測試，而這段時間可能不足以檢測出大腦中的變化。當人們血液中含有高水平的歐米伽-3時，通常是因為他們幾十年來一直食用富含歐米伽-3的食物——據某些估計，每週要吃三次魚。
“I think the reason for the disconnect or inconsistency, one explanation could be strictly methodological,” Dr. Bowman said.
「我認為造成這種脫節或不一致的原因，有一種解釋可能純粹是方法學上的，」鮑曼說。
廣告
“So we either start much earlier and do much longer trials,” he added, “or we come up with a more powerful treatment that has a larger magnitude of effect.” For instance, omega-3s may work best when combined with other nutrients (the fish-plus-salad theory), particularly in people who have nutritional deficiencies.
他補充道：「因此，我們要麼更早開始並進行更長時間的試驗，要麼開發出一種效果更明顯的、更強效的治療方法。」例如，當歐米伽-3與其他營養素相結合時（即魚加薩拉理論）效果可能最好，尤其是在存在營養缺乏的人群中。
Regardless of the reason for why omega-3 supplements seem to fall short, the current research suggests it’s a balanced diet, rather than a pill, that can help keep your brain healthy. That is also likely true for heart health, another reason many people take an omega-3 supplement.
無論歐米伽-3補充劑似乎效果不佳的原因為何，目前的研究所表明的都是，通過均衡的飲食——而非吃藥——才能有助於保持你的大腦健康。對於心臟健康而言情況很可能也是如此，而這也是許多人服用歐米伽-3補充劑的另一個原因。
“The best evidence, as limited as it might be, is that eating foods with omega-3s seems to be a good thing and correlates with better brain outcomes,” Dr. Bazinet said. “But taking the shortcut with the supplements” doesn’t seem to show the same benefit.
「最好的證據（儘管它可能還很有限）表明，食用含有歐米伽-3的食物似乎是一件好事，並且與更好的大腦結果相關，」巴齊內說。「但在服用補充劑走捷徑這件事上」，似乎並沒有表現出同樣的益處。

Dana G. Smith是《紐約時報》記者，報導個人健康，特別是衰老和大腦健康問題。
