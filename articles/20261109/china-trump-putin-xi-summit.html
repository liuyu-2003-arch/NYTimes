美中俄三國首腦在深圳會晤？習近平的雄心與顧慮

A Trump-Putin-Xi Summit? That Might Be Awkward for One of Them.

DAVID PIERSON, BERRY WANG • 2026-11-09
In early 1945, the leaders of the United States, the Soviet Union and Britain met in the Crimean city of Yalta and struck a secret deal promising Moscow special rights in China in return for joining the war against Japan.
1945年初，美國、蘇聯和英國領導人在克里米亞城市雅爾達舉行會晤，達成了一項祕密協議：作為蘇聯加入對日作戰的交換條件，美英同意給予莫斯科在中國的特殊權益。
But China was not at the table.
但中國沒有參與談判。
“It is a historical lesson of great-power politics that China will never forget,” said Xin Qiang, a U.S.-China expert at Fudan University in Shanghai. “It was all done behind China’s back and at the expense of Chinese interests.”
「這是中國永遠不會忘記的大國政治歷史教訓，」上海復旦大學研究中美關係的專家信強說。「這一切都是背著中國進行的，犧牲的是中國的利益。」 
More than eight decades later, Beijing is weighing whether to host a three-way meeting of President Trump, President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia and Xi Jinping, China’s leader.
八十多年後的今天，北京正在考慮是否主辦一場由美國總統川普、俄羅斯總統普丁和中國領導人習近平參加的三方會晤。 
The meeting could affirm China’s ambition to be an indispensable global power on an equal footing with the United States, but it risks echoing the kind of great-power bargaining Beijing has claimed to resent. The prospect of such a gathering, floated by the Kremlin, has raised fears in Ukraine that Mr. Putin might push for a deal with Mr. Trump to end his war and decide the fate of Ukraine — without Kyiv’s involvement.
這樣的會晤可以彰顯中國想成為與美國平起平坐、不可或缺的全球大國的雄心，但也可能重演北京聲稱反感的那種大國交易。克里姆林宮放風提出舉行這樣的會晤，在烏克蘭引發了擔憂：普丁可能推動與川普達成結束戰爭的協議，在基輔缺席的情況下決定烏克蘭的命運。
China confirmed on Wednesday that it is in talks with Russia and the United States about a meeting between the countries’ leaders, presumably on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit in the southern Chinese city of Shenzhen, later this month. But it remained unclear if such a gathering would take place.
中國週三證實，正在與俄羅斯和美國就三國領導人會晤進行磋商，據推測會晤將在本月晚些時候於中國南方城市深圳舉行的亞太經合組織峰會期間舉行。但這樣的會晤是否會舉行，目前仍不清楚。
How Ukraine Complicates China’s Decision
烏克蘭問題如何讓中國的決定變得複雜
廣告
China is wary of providing a platform for Mr. Putin to cut a deal with Washington over Ukraine.
中國對為普丁提供與華盛頓就烏克蘭問題達成交易的平台持謹慎態度。
Doing so would contradict Beijing’s position that peace talks require participation from Ukraine and Europe. Mr. Xi appeared to make that point as recently as last week.
這樣做將與北京的立場相矛盾：北京認為，和平談判需要烏克蘭和歐洲參與。習近平上週似乎還強調了這一點。
President Frank-Walter Steinmeier of Germany, who visited Beijing, told a German broadcaster that when he met with Mr. Xi, the Chinese leader made it clear that he did not think the United States, Russia and China would be discussing the fate of Ukraine in Shenzhen.
訪問北京的德國總統施泰因邁爾對一家德國廣播公司說，他在與習近平會晤時，後者明確表示，他認為美國、俄羅斯和中國不會在深圳討論烏克蘭的命運。
“You could see the irritation on his face. He even asked where such rumors were coming from,” Mr. Steinmeier said.
「你可以看出他臉上的不悅。他甚至問，這些傳言是從哪裡來的，」施泰因邁爾說。
“He explicitly stated that this is a European conflict and that the Europeans must be at the table,” he added.
「他明確表示，這是歐洲的衝突，歐洲人必須參與談判，」他補充說。 
President Volodymyr Zelensky of Ukraine has also expressed reservations about the possible talks, saying last week, “I really don’t want this to be ‘Yalta 2, 3, 4’ when there is a redistribution of influence,” according to Ukraine’s state news agency, Ukrinform.
烏克蘭總統弗拉基米爾·澤連斯基也對可能舉行的會談表示保留。據烏克蘭國家通訊社報導，他上週說：「我真的希望這不會演變成某種『雅爾達2.0、3.0或4.0』式的局面，讓各方重新劃分勢力範圍。」
Some in China view the secret bargain agreed on at the Yalta conference near the end of World War II as a betrayal. China’s then Nationalist government was pressed to relinquish its claim to what is now Mongolia and grant the Soviets special naval, transportation and commercial rights in Manchuria, in northeastern China.
一些中國人認為，第二次世界大戰接近尾聲時雅爾達會議達成的祕密交易是一種背叛。當時的中國國民政府受到壓力，被迫放棄對今蒙古國領土的主權主張，並給予蘇聯在中國東北滿洲地區的特殊海軍、交通運輸和商業權益。
廣告
A modern-day huddle involving the world’s three biggest nuclear-armed states would sit awkwardly with the image that Beijing has sought to project of itself as a champion for the interests of weaker nations. (In reality, China is not shy about flexing its muscle against smaller countries like the Philippines.)
如今，如果全球三大核武國家的領導人聚首，將與北京一直試圖塑造的形象格格不入：中國一直將自己描繪為弱小國家利益的捍衛者。（事實上，中國在面對菲律賓等較小國家時，並不吝於展示自己的實力。）
“No matter what Putin, Xi, and Trump might discuss, the imagery of them meeting would raise questions about whether it implied some kind of condominium of great powers that would disregard the interests of smaller nations,” said Joseph Torigian, a historian of the Chinese Communist Party at American University in Washington.
「無論普丁、習近平和川普可能討論什麼，他們會面的畫面本身就會引發疑問：這是否意味著某種無視小國利益的大國共治，」華盛頓美利堅大學研究中國共產黨歷史的學者唐志學(Joseph Torigian)說。 
“It would indeed be ironic for the Chinese if they decide that a trilateral meeting is nonetheless worth it for some reason,” he added.
「如果中國人出於某種原因，仍然認為三方會晤值得舉行，那確實會很具諷刺意味，」他補充說。
Beijing Does Not Want to Discuss Nukes
北京不想討論核問題
Mr. Trump has said he would like to use a three-way meeting with Mr. Xi and Mr. Putin in Shenzhen to discuss denuclearization. The last nuclear arms control treaty between the United States and Russia expired in February, and China is rapidly building its nuclear arsenal.
川普表示，他希望利用在深圳與習近平和普丁舉行三方會晤的機會討論無核化問題。美俄之間最後一項核軍控條約已於今年2月到期，而中國正在迅速擴充核武庫。
For Beijing, however, the topic is a nonstarter. Under Mr. Xi, China has shifted away from its decades-old strategy of maintaining an arsenal that would provide only a “minimum deterrent.” Instead, it is amassing a stockpile that could one day rival those of Washington and Moscow and has resisted placing limits on its development of nuclear weapons. It also has far fewer warheads — an estimated 620 — compared with more than 5,000 each for Russia and the United States, according to the Stockholm International Peace Research Institute.
然而，對北京來說，這個話題沒有商量餘地。在習近平領導下，中國已經放棄奉行數十年的僅維持「最低限度威懾」的核武庫戰略，轉而大力擴充一個有朝一日可能與華盛頓和莫斯科比肩的核武庫，並一直抵制對其核武器發展設定限制。據斯德哥爾摩國際和平研究所估計，中國的核彈頭數量要少得多，約為620枚，而俄羅斯和美國各自都超過5000枚。
Asked last month if China would agree to discuss denuclearization in Shenzhen with the United States and Russia, a Foreign Ministry spokeswoman, Mao Ning, deflected. She said that it was Washington that had the primary responsibility to disarm, adding that it was “neither fair, reasonable nor realistic” to ask China to engage in nuclear disarmament negotiations.
上個月，當被問及中國是否願意在深圳與美國和俄羅斯討論無核化問題時，中國外交部發言人毛寧迴避了該問題。她表示，華盛頓對核裁軍負有首要責任，並補充說，要求中國參與核裁軍談判「不公平、不合理，也不可行」。
Still, Chinese analysts suggest Beijing could agree to something narrower.
不過，中國分析人士認為，北京可能願意就範圍更有限的議題展開討論。
廣告
Zhao Long, a security expert at the Shanghai Institutes for International Studies, said China might be open to talks on reducing the risks around a nuclear conflict.
上海國際問題研究院安全問題專家趙隆表示，中國可能願意討論如何降低核衝突風險。
That could include improving channels for communicating during crises and ensuring that even in the era of artificial intelligence, the countries maintain human control over nuclear decision-making — an issue on which China and the United States agreed in 2024.
這可能包括改善危機期間的溝通管道，以及確保即使在人工智慧時代，各國仍由人類掌控核決策。中美兩國已於2024年在後一問題上達成共識。
The Risks of Playing Host
主辦會晤的風險  
For all its reservations, Beijing is unlikely to turn Mr. Trump and Mr. Putin down, said Ryan Hass, a former career diplomat and White House national security official now at the Brookings Institution.
曾任職業外交官和白宮國家安全官員、現任職於布魯金斯學會的何雷恩(Ryan Hass)認為，儘管有諸多保留，北京仍不太可能拒絕川普和普丁。
Mr. Hass recently made a trip to Beijing and met with Chinese officials and scholars who were interested in discussing what Mr. Trump wanted to get out of a trilateral meeting.
何雷恩最近訪問了北京，會見了一些中國官員和學者，他們有興趣探討川普希望從三方會晤中得到什麼。 
Mr. Hass came away with the impression that Beijing wanted to reap the rewards of a historic meeting, but was wary of the burden of making it a success.
這次訪問給何雷恩留下的印象是，北京希望從這場歷史性會晤中獲得好處，卻又擔心要承擔確保會晤成功的責任。
“Xi typically avoids owning responsibility for high-profile diplomatic events with uncertain outcomes, so this event will stretch the Chinese side’s comfort zone,” Mr. Hass said.
「習近平通常會避免為結果不確定的重大外交活動承擔責任，因此，這場會晤會超出中方的舒適區，」哈斯說。
To many officials in the West, China plays a key role in influencing Moscow because Russia has become so diplomatically and economically dependent on Beijing. In 2024, NATO accused China of being a “decisive enabler of Russia’s war against Ukraine,” urging it to halt shipments of “weapons components” and other technology that Russia needs to rebuild its military.
在許多西方官員看來，中國在影響莫斯科方面發揮著關鍵作用，因為俄羅斯在外交和經濟上已如此依賴北京。2024年，北約指責中國是「俄羅斯對烏克蘭戰爭的決定性助推者」，敦促其停止向俄羅斯運送重建軍隊所需的「武器部件」和其他技術。 
廣告
But to Beijing, China has little to do with the conflict that it did not help start and has not militarily supported. Some Chinese analysts suggest the most that Beijing could do was accommodate the talks while trying to distance itself from the idea that it can exert pressure on the Kremlin.
但在北京看來，中國與這場衝突沒有多大關係：戰爭不是中國挑起的，中國也沒有向俄羅斯提供軍事支持。一些中國分析人士認為，北京最多只能為談判提供便利，同時盡量避免給外界留下中國能夠向克里姆林宮施壓的印象。
“If the United States and Russia want to talk, then China can’t tell them not to,” said Song Guoyou, an expert on U.S.-China relations at Fudan University in Shanghai.
「如果美國和俄羅斯想談，中國總不能不讓他們談，」上海復旦大學中美關係專家宋國友說。
If the three leaders met and the topic of Ukraine was on the agenda, he said, China would just be “a facilitator of dialogue, a provider of the platform, and a neutral witness.”
他說，如果三國領導人舉行會晤，烏克蘭問題又被列入議程，那麼中國只會是「對話的促進者、平台的提供者和中立的見證者」。
