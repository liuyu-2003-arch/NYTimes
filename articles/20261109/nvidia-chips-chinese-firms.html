英偉達先進晶片流向中國「空殼」公司

U.S. Cleared Nvidia Chips to Chinese Firms With Empty Offices and Troubling Ties

ANA SWANSON, KALLEY HUANG, KEITH BRADSHER • 2026-11-09
In a recently built industrial park on the northwest fringe of Beijing, the listed address of a company called New Matrix stands empty, a hollow gray shell with wires dangling from the ceiling where lights should be.
在北京西北郊一個最近建成的工業園區裡，一家名為新矩陣的公司登記地址空無一人，那是一個空蕩蕩的灰色房間，天花板上本該裝燈的地方垂著電線。
Earlier this year, the Trump administration approved this Chinese firm to assemble Nvidia’s advanced chips into the computers that create artificial intelligence. President Trump allowed the sale of the chips, called the H200, to China late last year. But before they could be sold, the Commerce Department was required to review the companies involved for national security risks.
今年早些時候，川普政府批准這家中國公司組裝英偉達的先進晶片，用於創建人工智慧的計算機。川普去年年底允許向中國出售這種名為H200的晶片。但在晶片出售之前，美國商務部必須對相關企業進行審查，以評估其是否存在國家安全風險。
New Matrix passed that review. Yet recent checks by The New York Times found that, within China, there is little evidence the firm actually exists, raising questions about who in China is behind the company.
新矩陣通過了這項審查。然而，《紐約時報》近期的核查發現，在中國國內，幾乎沒有證據表明這家公司真實存在，這引發了關於究竟是誰在幕後掌控這家公司的疑問。
Only scant information can be found online about New Matrix, which was created 17 months ago. At the building that New Matrix lists as its address, a company directory does not include the firm. At the nearby property management agency for the industrial park, a receptionist and a manager both said they had no record of the company.
關於這家17個月前成立的公司，網上只能找到極少信息。在新矩陣登記地址所在的大樓裡，公司名錄中沒有這家公司。在附近工業園區的物業管理處，一名接待員和一名經理都說沒有該公司的記錄。
廣告
According to Chinese corporate records, New Matrix’s owners have ties to a prominent Chinese private equity fund that the U.S. government placed on a trade blacklist in 2024. Two analysts who examined the corporate records and other Chinese-language information said they believed New Matrix may be a front for firms associated with that fund.
根據中國企業登記記錄，新矩陣的所有者與一家知名中國私募股權基金有關聯，該基金已於2024年被美國政府列入貿易黑名單。兩名審查了公司記錄和其他中文信息的分析師表示，他們認為新矩陣可能是與該基金有關公司的幌子。
The sale of chips to New Matrix appears to be part of a pattern that has concerned technology experts inside and outside the government: The Commerce Department is giving clearance to buy and handle Nvidia’s chips to Chinese companies with ties to firms that have been subject to national-security-related trade restrictions.
晶片流向新矩陣似乎是令政府內外技術專家擔憂的一種模式的一部分：美國商務部正在批准某些中國公司購買和處理英偉達晶片，而這些公司與此前被列入國家安全相關貿易限制的中國企業有關聯。
Nvidia’s chips are the crucial technology in A.I. data centers and now one of the world’s most coveted commodities. They are also at the heart of the economic and geopolitical competition between the United States and China.
英偉達的晶片是人工智慧數據中心的關鍵技術，如今也是世界上最搶手的商品之一。它們也是美中經濟與地緣政治競爭的核心。
Corporate records show that several other Chinese companies approved to purchase H200 chips or assemble them into servers have ties to firms that have been blacklisted by the U.S. government, or that work as military contractors in China.
公司記錄顯示，其他幾家獲准購買H200晶片或將其組裝成伺服器的中國公司與被美國政府列入黑名單的公司有關聯，或者在中國擔任軍事承包商運營。
Liza Tobin, the former China director for the White House National Security Council in the first Trump and Biden administrations, said the approvals raised questions about how rigorously the Trump administration is policing who can get their hands on Nvidia’s chips.
曾在川普和拜登政府時期擔任白宮國家安全委員會中國事務主任的莉莎·托賓說，這些批准引發了疑問：川普政府在監管誰能獲得英偉達晶片方面究竟有多嚴格。
“I have a ton of concerns that neither the Commerce Department or Nvidia is doing the due diligence needed to ensure that the licensing terms are being followed,” she said.
「我有很多擔憂，商務部和英偉達都沒有進行必要的盡職調查，以確保許可條款得到遵守，」她說。
During the Biden administration, the United States began more broadly restricting sales of A.I. chips to China out of concern the technology would help China advance militarily. But Mr. Trump eased some of those limits after lobbying by Nvidia’s chief executive, Jensen Huang, with whom he has grown close during his second term.
在拜登政府時期，美國開始更廣泛地限制向中國出售人工智慧晶片，擔心該技術會幫助中國推進軍事發展。但川普在英偉達首席執行官黃仁勛的遊說下放鬆了部分限制，他在第二任期內與黃仁勛的關係日益密切。
In December, Mr. Trump gave Nvidia permission to sell the H200, an older but still powerful A.I. chip, to China, as long as the U.S. government was given a cut of the sales.
去年12月，川普允許英偉達向中國出售H200——一款較舊但仍性能強大的人工智慧晶片，條件是美國政府從銷售中抽成。
廣告
In an unusual arrangement, Mr. Trump is requiring these chips, which are assembled in Taiwan, to be routed through the United States on their way to China, so that the government can collect a 25 percent tariff on their value.
在一項不同尋常的安排中，川普要求這些在台灣組裝的晶片在運往中國途中經過美國，以便政府對其價值徵收25%的關稅。
Nvidia is now selling only small amounts of H200 chips to China. The Chinese government has been slow to approve its companies for the purchases, partly because it is trying to promote domestically made chips.
英偉達目前只向中國出售少量H200晶片。中國政府一直緩慢批准本國公司購買，部分原因是它試圖推廣國產晶片。
Although China is a potentially huge market for A.I. technology, sales directly to the country currently account for only a tiny percentage of Nvidia’s revenue. In the quarter that ended in July, H200 shipments to customers based in China made up less than 1 percent of Nvidia’s revenue from data centers. The company also said in August that it was not assuming any sales to China in its estimate for data center revenue for the current quarter.
儘管中國是人工智慧技術的潛在巨大市場，但直接對華銷售目前僅佔英偉達收入的很小比例。在截至7月的財季中，向位於中國的客戶交付H200的出貨量不到英偉達數據中心收入的1%。公司還在8月表示，在對本財季度數據中心營收的預估中，並未將對華銷售額納入考量。
But many Chinese companies are gaining access to Nvidia chips through data centers in Southeast Asia, and the chips still appear to be a critical focus for Beijing. The Times reported last month that Mr. Huang of Nvidia had a close relationship with a woman who American spy agencies concluded was an intelligence asset for China. The company she worked at, Megaspeed, operated data centers in Southeast Asia that served Chinese clients and was being investigated for smuggling some chips into China. On Sunday, Nvidia said that it was opening an investigation into Mr. Huang’s relationship with the woman after he acknowledged to the company’s board that he had been intimate with her.
但許多中國企業正通過東南亞的數據中心獲得英偉達晶片，而這些晶片似乎仍是北京關注的焦點。《紐約時報》上個月報導，英偉達的黃仁勛與一名女性關係密切，美國間諜機構認定她是中國的情報人員。她所在的公司Megaspeed在東南亞運營數據中心，為中國客戶服務，並因涉嫌將一些晶片走私到中國而受到調查。週日，英偉達表示，在黃仁勛向公司董事會承認他與該女性有親密關係後，公司正在調查黃仁勛與她的關係。
Another Empty Office
另一個空辦公室
When the Commerce Department granted its approval to New Matrix this year, the company’s registered address was at a compound on the outskirts of Beijing used for corporate registrations, where thousands of other firms are also registered on paper, according to the Chinese corporate information site Qichacha. But this fall, New Matrix’s address changed to the empty office in the industrial park visited by The Times.
根據中國公司信息網站企查查，今年美國商務部對新矩陣給與批准之時，該公司的註冊地址在北京郊區一個用於公司註冊的園區，該園區還有數千家其他公司於名義上在此註冊。但今年秋天，新矩陣的地址變更為《紐約時報》探訪的工業園區那間空辦公室。
廣告
New Matrix’s website does not name any staff members, advertise any jobs or include information about its history, as of Nov. 6. But two of its primary owners appear in the records of companies connected to a Chinese private equity firm under sanctions and its founder, a man named Li Bin, according to WireScreen, a data platform.
截至11月6日，新矩陣的網站沒有列出任何員工姓名，沒有布任何招聘信息，也沒有包含關於其歷史的信息。但根據數據平台WireScreen，其兩名主要所有者出現在一家受制裁的中國私募股權公司及其創始人的關聯企業記錄中，該創始人名為李斌。
In 2024, the U.S. government added the private equity firm, Wise Road, to the Commerce Department’s “entity list,” which restricts companies from doing business with the United States. The department said Wise Road had helped the Chinese government acquire strategically sensitive companies with chip technology.
2024年，美國政府將這家名為智路資本的私募股權公司列入商務部的「實體清單」，限制其與美國做生意。商務部稱，智路資本曾協助中國政府收購擁有晶片技術的戰略敏感企業。
Mr. Li is also the chairman of Tsinghua Unigroup, a technology conglomerate with government ownership. In 2021, one of Tsinghua Unigroup’s subsidiaries, New H3C Semiconductor Technologies, was added to the entity list for working with the Chinese military.
李斌同時還擔任清華紫光集團董事長，這是一家國有的科技集團。2021年，清華紫光的一家子公司新華三半導體技術有限公司因與中國軍方合作而被列入實體清單。
New Matrix is the second Chinese company approved to handle Nvidia’s H200 chips that does not seem to have any physical presence at its listed address.
新矩陣是第二家獲准處理英偉達H200晶片、但其登記地址處似乎沒有任何實體辦公場所的中國公司。
In September, The Times reported that another recently created Chinese firm called Maginfra had also received a license for H200s. Like New Matrix, Maginfra appears to have been approved not as the ultimate buyer of the chips, but as a company that would assemble them into A.I. servers for another customer.
《紐約時報》於9月報導稱，另一家名為Maginfra的中國新成立公司也獲得了H200晶片的出口許可。與New Matrix類似，Maginfra 獲批的身份似乎並非這批晶片的最終買家，而是一家為其他客戶將晶片組裝進人工智慧伺服器的代工企業。
But when a Times reporter visited Maginfra’s listed address in China in September, it also appeared deserted. A few chairs and empty desks sat inside a small locked room with no signs. Workers on the floor said they had not seen anyone use the office, and building receptionists said they had not heard of Maginfra.
但時報記者9月前往Maginfra在中國的註冊地址時，發現那裡也空無一人。一間上了鎖的小房間裡擺放著幾把椅子和幾張空桌子，沒有任何標識。該樓層的員工表示從未見過有人使用過這間辦公室，大樓的前台接待人員則表示從未聽說過Maginfra這家公司。
廣告
Analysts said they believed Maginfra was an offshoot of Inspur, a company whose headquarters were near Maginfra’s listed address. The U.S. government has added Inspur to the entity list for working with the Chinese military. Maginfra and Inspur share personnel and patents, and both have government ownership.
分析人士認為，Maginfra是浪潮集團(Inspur)的子公司，而浪潮集團的總部就在Maginfra的註冊地址附近。美國政府已將浪潮集團列入實體清單，理由是其與中國軍方有業務往來。Maginfra和浪潮集團共享人員和專利，且兩家公司均有國資背景。
Military Ties
軍方關係
Other Chinese companies that have been approved for licenses to buy Nvidia’s H200 chips also have ties through their corporate owners to military suppliers, according to people familiar with the approvals and documents viewed by The Times.
據熟悉審判情況的人士和《紐約時報》看到的文件，其他獲准購買英偉達H200晶片許可證的中國公司也通過其公司所有者與軍事供應商存在關聯。
One, called Zhejiang Yindun Cloud Technology, has ties to a cloud computing giant that has sold services to military contractors, called Range Intelligent Computing Technology Group. Yindun is partly operated from a data center owned by Range, and several of its executives are family members of Range’s founder.
其中一家名為浙江銀盾雲科技的公司與雲計算巨頭潤澤智算科技集團有關聯，潤澤智算曾向軍事承包商出售服務。銀盾部分業務在潤澤擁有的數據中心運營，其幾名高管是潤澤創始人的家庭成員。
Range serves as the cloud computing provider for a satellite program run by the defense bureau of Hebei province, which it has described on its website as serving the strategic needs of “national defense development.” It also started a subsidiary in 2016 to focus on quantum communications for the Chinese government and military. Part of the subsidiary is owned by a firm that the United States added to the entity list in 2021 for acquiring American items for the Chinese military.
潤澤是河北省國防部門運營的一個衛星項目的雲計算提供商，該公司在其網站上稱該項目服務於「國防發展」的戰略需求。它還在2016年成立了一家子公司，專注於為中國政府和軍方提供量子通信。該子公司部分股權由一家公司擁有，美國因該公司為中國軍隊採購美國物品，已於2021年將其列入實體清單。
Another company licensed to purchase H200 chips, Shanghai Jiatang, grew out of a state-owned enterprise called Datang Telecom, a chipmaker that provides technology to the Chinese military. Datang is controlled by the China Academy of Telecommunications Technology, a state-owned enterprise that has developed cryptography for the Chinese military.
另一家獲准購買H200晶片的公司上海嘉唐源自一家名為大唐電信的國有企業。大唐電信是一家向中國軍方提供技術的晶片製造商。大唐由中國電信科學技術研究院控制，這是一家為中國軍方開發密碼技術的國有企業。
廣告
Shanghai Jiatang, Yindun Cloud and Maginfra did not respond to requests for comment.
上海嘉唐、銀盾雲科技和Maginfra沒有回應置評請求。
