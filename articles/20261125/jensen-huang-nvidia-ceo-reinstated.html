為什麼黃仁勛不可撼動

I Can See Why Jensen Huang Was Irreplaceable

STEPHEN WITT • 2026-11-25
When Jensen Huang returned to the floor of his Silicon Valley headquarters on Monday, his employees greeted him with a standing ovation. The rank and file of Nvidia love Mr. Huang, who has made many of them wealthy. In the midst of this celebration, I imagine his top executives must have exchanged a few glances. They know all about Mr. Huang’s abusive treatment of staff members, his extramarital relationships and Nvidia’s funny business in Southeast Asia. But, in the end, I’m sure they clapped, too.
週一，黃仁勛回到英偉達位於矽谷的總部時，員工們起立鼓掌歡迎。英偉達的普通員工都很愛戴黃仁勛，他讓許多人賺得盆滿缽滿。在這歡慶的場面中，我想公司的高管們一定交換了幾下眼神。他們對黃仁勛粗暴對待員工的行為、他的婚外情以及英偉達在東南亞那些見不得光的生意都心知肚明。不過，我相信最後他們也鼓掌了。
In the 11 days he was gone, Nvidia lost almost 30 percent of its value. Upon his return, Nvidia’s stock price recovered, adding almost a trillion dollars to the company’s market capitalization. They’re saying Jensen Huang is too big to fire. I say he’s the trillion-dollar man.
在他離開的11天裡，英偉達市值損失近30%。他回歸後，英偉達股價回升，公司市值增加近1萬億美元。人們說，黃仁勛重要到不能解僱。要我說，他是個價值萬億美元的男人。
Mr. Huang still has a number of looming scandals. Nvidia is being investigated by Congress, the Justice Department, the Securities and Exchange Commission and its own corporate board. The U.S. intelligence community seems to have compromising intelligence on Mr. Huang; he’s reportedly had numerous affairs, including one with a married corporate executive at his company and another with a woman suspected of being a Chinese spy.
黃仁勛仍有多樁醜聞懸而未決。英偉達正受到國會、司法部、證券交易委員會及其自身董事會的調查。美國情報界似乎掌握了足以讓黃仁勛陷入被動的情報；據報導，他有多次婚外情，包括與公司一名已婚女高管，以及與一名疑似中國間諜的女子。
Reporters have produced detailed accounts of his scathing encounters with staff members, which include bodily threats and ritual humiliation. He is scheduled to have a closed-door interview with House committees next Tuesday and to testify before the Senate two days later. For another corporate executive, any one of these scandals would be a career-killer. For Mr. Huang, it’s Monday morning.
記者們詳細披露了他如何刻薄地對待員工，其中包括人身威脅和儀式化的羞辱。他定於下週二接受眾議院多個委員會的閉門問詢，並於兩天後赴參議院作證。換作其他企業高管，這些醜聞中的任何一樁都足以斷送職業生涯。對黃仁勛而言，這不過是一個尋常的週一早晨。
廣告
Mr. Huang’s invincibility derives from his close relationship with President Trump. Mr. Huang started cultivating this connection in 2025, when he and his wife, Lori, visited Mar-a-Lago. There, over dinner, he told Mr. Trump the compelling story of his rise from Denny’s waitstaff to semiconductor chief executive. In the course of writing Mr. Huang’s biography, I heard him tell this story many times. I never got tired of it — although I did notice the details change a bit at different tellings. The dazzled Mr. Trump fell hard for Mr. Huang. He also fell for Lori, whom on Monday he called a “fantastic woman” and “very supportive.”
黃仁勛之所以不可撼動，源於他與川普總統的密切關係。2025年，黃仁勛和妻子洛麗到訪馬阿拉歌莊園，從那時起，他便開始經營這段關係。席間，他向川普講述了自己如何從連鎖餐廳Denny\'s的一名服務員成長為半導體企業首席執行官的動人故事。在為黃仁勛撰寫傳記的過程中，我多次聽他講過這個故事，始終百聽不厭——儘管我確實注意到，每次講述的細節略有不同。聽得入迷的川普徹底被黃仁勛征服了。他也很喜歡洛麗，週一還稱她是個「了不起的女人」，對丈夫「非常支持」。
By the beginning of this year, Mr. Trump and Mr. Huang were talking often on the phone. When Marco Rubio, the president’s secretary of state and national security adviser, shared intelligence details about Mr. Huang’s romantic relationship with Alice Huang and the belief that she was a Chinese intelligence asset, Mr. Trump pooh-poohed it. Instead, he offered Mr. Huang a ride with him on Air Force One to meet with China’s president, Xi Jinping. The intelligence findings were eventually published by The Times, causing a cascade of revelations about Mr. Huang’s misbehaviors. Twelve days ago, Nvidia’s board unanimously accepted Mr. Huang’s resignation. But in the interim, the A.I. economy cratered, and when Mr. Trump pushed for him to be reinstated, the board unanimously voted to take him back.
到今年年初，川普和黃仁勛經常通電話。當國務卿兼國家安全顧問魯比奧向他彙報了關於黃仁勛與黃樂的私情以及認為她是中國情報機構線人的細節時，川普不以為然。他反而邀請黃仁勛乘坐「空軍一號」，隨他一起會見中國國家主席習近平。《紐約時報》最終披露了這些情報調查結果，引發了一系列關於黃仁勛不當行為的爆料。12天前，英偉達董事會一致接受黃仁勛辭職。但在此期間，人工智慧經濟遭遇重挫，在川普力促黃仁勛復職後，董事會又一致投票同意讓他回來。
Mr. Trump had reason to act: Nvidia’s plummeting stock price posed a threat to the global economy. When Nvidia underperforms, it drags the entire market down with it. By contrast, when Nvidia succeeds, it feeds a sprawling portfolio of data centers and developers. Mr. Huang is Nvidia; and Nvidia is A.I.; and A.I. is the American economy. To an extraordinary extent, the success of our stock market relies on this single man.
川普採取行動並非沒有理由：英偉達股價暴跌對全球經濟構成了威脅。英偉達表現不佳時，整個股市都會受到拖累。相反，英偉達的成功會帶動龐大的數據中心和開發商群體。黃仁勛就是英偉達，英偉達就是人工智慧，而人工智慧就是美國經濟。我們的股市能否繁榮，在很大程度上竟取決於這一個人。
I understand the board’s reasoning. It’s not the morality police. Its job is to make Nvidia’s stock price go up, and no one does that better than Mr. Huang. Several board members are themselves Nvidia billionaires, and some have been with Mr. Huang since Day 1. Also, what were they going to do — tell the president no?
我理解董事會的考量。它不是道德警察，它的職責是推動英偉達股價上漲，而在這方面，沒有人比黃仁勛做得更好。幾名董事會成員本身就是憑藉英偉達股票躋身億萬富翁行列的，其中一些人從公司創立之初就追隨黃仁勛。再說，他們又能怎麼辦呢——對總統說不嗎？
And, it must be admitted, Mr. Huang truly is an irreplaceable businessman. He does the work of an entire C-suite. He is a manager, a thinker, a worker. He is a ruthless operations specialist, a customer relations whisperer, a talent scout and a strategic visionary. He can forecast the existence of new markets years in advance, and he can predict what the competitive dynamics of those markets will be once they come into being. In reporting on the growing humanoid robotics industry, I found that practically every such robot uses an Nvidia microchip for its brain — because Mr. Huang had directed Nvidia to start building these brains years before the robots existed.
而且，不得不承認，黃仁勛的確是一位無可替代的商界人士。他一個人就能承擔整個高管團隊的工作。他是管理者、思考者，也是實幹家。他是無情的運營專家、深諳客戶關係之道的高手、善於發掘人才的伯樂，以及富有遠見的戰略家。他能提前數年預見新市場的出現，還能預測這些市場形成後的競爭格局。在報導蓬勃發展的人形機器人產業時，我發現，幾乎每一台此類機器人的大腦都採用了英偉達的微晶片——因為早在這些機器人問世之前，黃仁勛就已指示英偉達著手打造這些大腦。
Mr. Huang has many other talents. He is an accomplished public speaker — even though he has stage fright. He’s funny and charming and loves to tell jokes. He is an excellent draftsman and can draw, from memory, an elegant exploded diagram of one of his G.P.U.s. He can also label every part and tell you what it does, how much it costs and which of his Taiwanese suppliers he would pick up the phone to yell at if it were late. At 63, he retains a ton of energy and is surprisingly athletic. If Nvidia’s board had not rehired him, he could certainly have built a business to compete against the company.
黃仁勛還有許多其他才能。他是一位出色的演說家——儘管他也會怯場。他風趣迷人，喜歡講笑話。他的製圖功底十分出色，能憑記憶畫出一張精美的英偉達圖形處理器分解圖。他還能標出每個零部件，告訴你它的用途、成本，如果該部件未能按時交貨，他會打電話痛罵哪一家台灣供應商。63歲的他依然精力充沛，運動能力出奇地好。如果英偉達董事會沒有重新聘用他，他完全有能力創辦一家與英偉達競爭的企業。
廣告
In intervening to get Mr. Huang reinstated, Mr. Trump also played to the Main Street shareholder. Nvidia is one of the most popular stocks among retail investors, and its fame extends to unlikely places: While shooting clay pigeons in rural North Carolina earlier this year, I mentioned to a fellow shooter that I’d written a book about Nvidia. He pulled up his Robinhood account on his phone and walked me through his Nvidia trading history; then he yelled “pull” and nailed two targets in half a second. Such shareholders, I find, often have only a vague idea of what Nvidia makes. What they do know is that the stock should go up.
川普出面促成黃仁勛復職，也是在迎合普通股民。英偉達是散戶投資者最青睞的股票之一，其知名度甚至超乎想像：今年早些時候，在北卡羅萊納州鄉間打飛碟靶時，我向一位射擊同伴提到自己寫過一本關於英偉達的書。他立即在手機上打開自己的Robinhood帳戶，給我逐一介紹他買賣英偉達股票的記錄；隨後，他喊了一聲「放靶」，半秒內就擊中了兩個目標。我發現，這類股東往往對英偉達究竟生產什麼只有一個模糊的概念。但有一點他們很清楚：這隻股票應該上漲。
But if Mr. Trump’s reinstatement is good for average investors, it is terrible for the rule of law. Nvidia may have broken the law and must be thoroughly investigated. In 2022, the Biden administration announced restrictions on sales to China of Nvidia’s most advanced chips. Nvidia responded by joining with new partners in Southeast Asia. These so-called cloud partners received preferential treatment when Nvidia was allocating its most advanced microchips, which, given the supply-demand imbalance for Nvidia equipment in late 2023, was sort of like the partners being handed a license to print money. The partners, mostly based in Singapore, were supposed to install the Nvidia-powered hardware they received in data centers in Malaysia. Instead, investigators say, some skirted the export ban by reselling the equipment to China.
然而，如果說川普促成黃仁勛復職有利於普通投資者，此舉對法治而言則糟糕透頂。英偉達可能觸犯了法律，必須受到徹底調查。2022年，拜登政府宣布限制英偉達最先進的晶片銷往中國。作為回應，英偉達開始與東南亞的新合作夥伴開展業務。這些所謂的雲服務合作夥伴在英偉達分配最先進的微晶片時享有優先待遇。鑒於2023年底英偉達的產品供不應求，這幾乎等於給這些合作夥伴發了一張印鈔許可證。這些合作夥伴大多位於新加坡，原本應將它們獲得的搭載英偉達晶片的硬件設備安裝到馬來西亞的數據中心。然而，調查人員稱，其中一些合作夥伴將設備轉售給中國，繞過了出口禁令。
One cloud partner, Alan Wei Zhaolun, had accumulated enough money to buy a $40 million house. In February 2025, Mr. Zhaolun was arrested by the Singapore Police Force and accused of diverting his Nvidia chip allotment for resale to China. Singapore charged him with money laundering and fraud, and he could face up to 20 years in prison if convicted. He has pleaded not guilty. (Nvidia says it stopped doing business with Mr. Zhaolun before his arrest.)
其中一家雲服務合作夥伴的經營者韋兆倫積累的財富足以讓他買下一棟價值4000萬美元的豪宅。2025年2月，他被新加坡警方逮捕，被指將獲配的英偉達晶片轉售給中國。新加坡方面以洗錢和欺詐罪名對他提起指控，如果罪名成立，他可能面臨最高20年的監禁。他表示不認罪。（英偉達表示在他被捕前已停止與他的業務往來。）
Alice Huang’s company was also a Nvidia cloud partner — and she was Jensen’s romantic partner, too. In June 2025, the Singapore Police questioned her and seized her passport. They returned it in February 2026 and later cited a lack of cooperation from the U.S. Department of Justice. Ms. Huang was banned from Singapore and now is reported to be in China. The U.S. intelligence community assessed her to be a Chinese intelligence asset whose company was suspected of diverting chips to China. (China denies this.)
黃樂的公司也是英偉達的雲服務合作夥伴，而她本人是黃仁勛的情人。2025年6月，新加坡警方對她進行了問詢，並扣押了她的護照。警方於2026年2月歸還了她的護照，後來表示美國司法部不予配合。她已被禁止入境新加坡，據報導目前身在中國。美國情報界認定她是中國情報機構的線人，她的公司則涉嫌將晶片轉運至中國。（中國否認這一點。）
I would like to know why Nvidia designated these two as preferred customers. I would like to know what suspicions Nvidia had about these and other Singapore-based businesses and what they did to respond. I would like to know why Nvidia changed its accounting procedures after sales in Singapore grew to 22 percent of its revenue. But I fear I may never get the complete answer.
我想知道為什麼英偉達將這兩個人指定為優先客戶。我想知道英偉達對這些及其他新加坡企業存有哪些疑慮，又採取了什麼應對措施。我還想知道，在新加坡的銷售額增至公司營收的22%之後，英偉達為何改變了會計核算方式。但我擔心，我可能永遠得不到完整答案。
The U.S. Commerce Department, which was originally investigating alongside the Singapore Police Force, was transferred off the case after Nvidia’s general counsel, Tim Teter, directly emailed investigators and name-checked their boss. The Justice Department took over — and this, Singapore authorities say, was when their investigation into Alice Huang stalled. President Trump, with his endorsement of Mr. Huang, has signaled to Nvidia’s board that the F.B.I. will not find anything too damaging. I wonder how hard it’s really looking.
美國商務部原本與新加坡警方共同展開調查，但在英偉達總法律顧問蒂姆·泰特直接給調查人員發送電子郵件，並特意提及他們的上司後，商務部被調離了此案的調查工作。司法部接手了調查——新加坡當局稱，正是在這時，他們對黃樂的調查陷入停滯。川普對黃仁勛的支持向英偉達董事會釋放出一個信號：聯邦調查局不會查出任何對他過於不利的東西。我不禁懷疑，調查到底有多認真。
廣告
Mr. Huang’s reinstatement also sends a terrible signal regarding the fair treatment of women in the workplace. Mr. Huang had affairs with at least two subordinates: Kimberly Powell, a married health care executive at his company, and another woman, identified by Reuters, who remains anonymous. On Monday, The Wall Street Journal reported that Mr. Huang admitted to more affairs.
黃仁勛的復職也在職場女性能否受到公平對待的問題上釋放了一個極其惡劣的信號。黃仁勛至少與兩名下屬有過婚外情：一名是已婚的醫療保健業務高管金伯莉·鮑威爾，另一名是路透社查明身份但未公開姓名的女性。週一，《華爾街日報》報導稱，黃仁勛承認還有其他婚外情。
He will very likely face few repercussions for actions that would get anyone else in America fired. In fact, his romantic misadventures are being celebrated — on Monday, Mr. Trump posted a meme that riffed on the famous “Fight!” moment from his own assassination attempt, showing Mr. Huang with a bloody face and waving a red bra. The joke signals a return to “Mad Men” standards of behavior. The broader cultural implications for women are depressing to contemplate.
這些行為若發生在美國其他任何人身上都足以導致其被解僱，而他很可能幾乎不用承擔任何後果。事實上，他那些荒唐的情事甚至成了人們津津樂道的事情——週一，川普發布了一張網路惡搞圖片，以自己遭遇暗殺未遂時那個著名的高喊「戰鬥！」的瞬間為藍本，畫面中黃仁勛滿臉是血，揮舞著一件紅色胸罩。這個玩笑表明，人們的行為準則正在倒退回《廣告狂人》(Mad Men)所描繪的時代。這對女性來說意味著怎樣一種更廣泛的社會文化影響，想想就令人沮喪。
In an early draft of “The Thinking Machine,” my biography of Mr. Huang, I opined that he was one of the great men of history. I took that phrase out but I wish I’d left it in. Mr. Huang is as great as he is terrible.
在黃仁勛傳記《思考機器》（The Thinking Machine，中譯《黃仁勛：英偉達之芯》）的早期草稿中，我曾寫道，他是歷史偉人之一。後來我刪掉了這句話，但現在我希望自己當初沒有這樣做。黃仁勛有多偉大，就有多可怕。
He’s a titan of industry who reinvented the computer from the transistor up. He also betrayed his wife, his employees, his company and possibly his country. He’s a warm and charismatic jokester with a humble demeanor. He’s also a narcissist, an egomaniac and a manipulator — and when he gets in one of his moods, he behaves like a petty, insecure tyrant.
他是一位產業巨擘，從晶體管這一最基礎的層面徹底重塑了計算機。但他也背叛了妻子、員工、公司，甚至可能背叛了自己的國家。他熱情而富有魅力，愛開玩笑，舉止謙和。但他同時也是一個自戀、自大、善於操縱他人的人——一旦脾氣發作，他的表現就像一個心胸狹窄、缺乏安全感的暴君。
He’s a complicated figure, no question, and once the Democrats take office in January, they will go after him hard. But if I were on the board of Nvidia, and I were asking myself what to do, there’s also no question whom I’d choose.
毫無疑問，他是一個複雜的人物。等到明年1月民主黨人掌權，他們必將對他窮追猛打。但如果我是英偉達董事會的一員，面對該如何抉擇的問題，我會選擇誰同樣毫無疑問。
Welcome back, Mr. Huang. We hardly missed you.
歡迎回來，黃先生。我們還沒來得及想念你呢。

Stephen Witt是黃仁勛傳記《黃仁勛：英偉達之芯》(The Thinking Machine)的作者。
