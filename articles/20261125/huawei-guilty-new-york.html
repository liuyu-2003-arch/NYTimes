華為在美被判竊取商業機密等罪名成立

Huawei Found Guilty of Stealing Trade Secrets in U.S. Trial

SANTUL NERKAR, KATE CHRISTOBEK • 2026-11-25
A federal jury on Tuesday found Huawei, the Chinese telecommunications giant that U.S. officials have long viewed with suspicion, guilty of stealing trade secrets and evading sanctions against Iran.
週二，一個聯邦陪審團裁定，長期受到美國官員懷疑的中國電信巨頭華為竊取商業機密和規避對伊朗制裁的罪名成立。
The verdict, which came after an 11-week trial and nearly a week of deliberations in Federal District Court in Brooklyn, was a victory for the Justice Department in a long-running case over what federal prosecutors called systematic and brazen trade theft.
這一裁決是在布魯克林聯邦地區法院經過11周審理和近一週閉門商議後作出的，也是司法部在這起曠日持久案件中的一場勝利，聯邦檢察官稱該案涉及系統性且公然的商業盜竊。
The allegations burst into international view in 2018, when a senior executive at Huawei, Meng Wanzhou, was detained by Canadian authorities at the direction of the U.S. government. Ms. Meng spent three years in detention at her luxurious Vancouver homes before reaching an agreement with federal prosecutors that allowed her to return to China.
相關指控於2018年進入國際視野，當時華為高管孟晚舟在美國政府指示下被加拿大當局拘留。孟晚舟在溫哥華的豪宅中被拘禁了三年，隨後與聯邦檢察官達成協議，得以返回中國。
But the U.S. government continued its prosecution, even without her as a defendant, accusing Huawei of acting like a criminal enterprise set on stealing secrets from American competitors.
但即使沒有她作為被告，美國政府仍繼續推進起訴，指控華為的運作如同一個致力於竊取美國競爭對手機密的犯罪集團。
廣告
Since Huawei has been effectively banned from selling its products in the United States since 2019, the long-awaited verdict may not significantly affect its fortunes. Still, a stiff financial penalty and the stain of a guilty judgment could hamper its business.
由於華為自2019年以來實際上已被禁止在美國銷售產品，這一醞釀已久的裁決或許不會對其命運產生重大影響。不過，高額罰款以及有罪判決的污點仍可能阻礙其業務發展。
Huawei has consistently denied any wrongdoing and has rejected the allegation that it has operated at the behest of the Chinese Communist Party.
華為始終否認有任何不當行為，並駁斥了其聽命於中國共產黨的指控。
The company was found guilty of 10 of the dozen criminal counts it faced, which included bank fraud, conspiracy to steal trade secrets and conspiracy to defraud the United States.
該公司面臨的12項刑事指控中有10項被裁定罪名成立，其中包括銀行欺詐、串謀竊取商業機密以及串謀欺詐美國。
But jurors acquitted Huawei of racketeering conspiracy, a result that undercut the Justice Department’s central claim that the company operated as a criminal enterprise. The company was also acquitted of conspiring to obstruct justice.
但陪審員裁定華為勒索敲詐共謀罪名不成立，這一結果削弱了司法部關於該公司作為犯罪集團運作的核心主張。該公司串謀妨礙司法公正的罪名也被裁定不成立。
“Few crimes are as serious as Huawei’s decades-long pattern of endangering U.S. national security, violating the sanctity of U.S. commerce and impeding the enforcement of U.S. laws,” Joseph Nocella Jr., the U.S. attorney in Brooklyn, said in a statement.
「以華為數十年來危害美國國家安全、侵犯美國商業神聖性以及阻礙美國法律執行的行為模式，罪行之深重實屬罕見，」布魯克林聯邦檢察官小約瑟夫·諾塞拉在一份聲明中表示。
Brian Heberlig, a lawyer for Huawei, told reporters outside the courthouse that the company’s acquittal on the racketeering charge showed a “profound rejection of the United States’ narratives about what Huawei is.” He said he expected the company would appeal the guilty verdicts.
華為律師布賴恩·赫伯利格在法院外對記者表示，該公司勒索敲詐罪名不成立表明，陪審團「徹底否定了美國關於華為性質的敘事」。他表示預計公司將對有罪裁決提出上訴。
“The allegations that the government made in this case overreached and simply weren’t consistent with the successful, innovative and hard-working company that we came to know and deeply respect in this trial,” Mr. Heberlig said.
「政府在本案中提出的指控過於牽強，根本不符合我們在這次審判中所認識並深感敬佩的這家成功、創新且勤奮的公司，」赫伯利格說。
Over the course of the trial, which began in September, federal prosecutors in Brooklyn painted a portrait of a company that endeavored to cheat American firms out of their intellectual property.
在9月開始的審判過程中，布魯克林的聯邦檢察官將華為描繪成一家竭力騙取美國企業知識產權的公司。
Leaders at Huawei, prosecutors said, encouraged employees to steal secrets. Prosecutors said that Huawei stole intellectual property from T-Mobile and Cisco, among other companies.
檢察官稱，華為領導層鼓勵員工竊取機密。檢察官表示，華為竊取了包括T-Mobile和思科在內的多家公司的知識產權。
廣告
Huawei’s backers have argued for years that the case was politically motivated and a retaliation against a Chinese initiative to build 5G networks around the world.
多年來，華為的支持者一直認為此案具有政治動機，是對中國在全球建設5G網路倡議的報復。
Mr. Heberlig said in his closing statement that Huawei was “a global company that built its success through innovation and hard work.” The company, he said, did not steal technology; instead, it asked for it and paid for it.
赫伯利格在結案陳詞中表示，華為是「一家通過創新和辛勤工作取得成功的全球性公司」。他說，該公司沒有竊取技術，而是提出申請並支付了費用。
“Huawei didn’t steal its way to success; it earned it,” Mr. Heberlig said. “Plain and simple.”
「華為不是靠偷竊走向成功的；它是靠實力贏得的，」赫伯利格說。「就這麼簡單。」
Meredith Arfa, a federal prosecutor, told the jury in her closing statement that employees had been ordered to collect competitor information and had even been paid bonuses for their crimes.
聯邦檢察官梅雷迪思·阿爾法在結案陳詞中告訴陪審團，華為員工曾奉命收集競爭對手的信息，甚至因犯罪行為獲得獎金。
“For 20 years, Huawei committed a pattern of crimes targeted at American companies, the American financial system and the American government,” Ms. Arfa said.
「20年來，華為實施了一系列針對美國企業、美國金融體系和美國政府的犯罪活動，」阿爾法說。
In addition, prosecutors said, the company evaded U.S. sanctions by conducting business in Iran, where it sold gear that allowed the authoritarian government to surveil its citizens. Huawei, prosecutors said, lied to banks and to the news media about its business in Iran.
此外，檢察官稱，該公司在伊朗開展業務規避了美國制裁，在當地銷售允許這個威權政府監控其公民的設備。檢察官表示，華為在伊朗業務問題上向銀行和新聞媒體撒謊。
廣告
At trade shows, Huawei engineers would sneak photographs of other companies’ hardware, including their routers and internal circuitry, according to witness testimony. A former employee at a Huawei subsidiary testified that the company’s employees would intentionally get “lost in competitors’ facilities and end up in places they weren’t supposed to be.”
根據證人證詞，在貿易展會上，華為工程師會偷拍其他公司的硬件，包括路由器和內部電路。華為一家子公司的一名前員工作證稱，該公司員工會故意在「競爭對手的設施中迷路，最終出現在他們本不該出現的地方」。
A testing robot that belonged to T-Mobile, known as “Tappy,” was another target of Huawei’s illicit schemes, prosecutors said at trial. In 2012, T-Mobile allowed Huawei to use the robot so that Huawei could test its own phones. But in reality, prosecutors said, Huawei used its access to steal technical information about the robot, which the company then passed along to its research and development team in China.
檢察官在庭審中表示，T-Mobile一款名為「Tappy」的測試機器人是華為非法計劃的另一個目標。2012年，T-Mobile允許華為使用該機器人，以便華為測試自家手機。但檢察官稱，實際上華為利用這一權限竊取了該機器人的技術信息，隨後將其傳送給在中國的研發團隊。
Mr. Heberlig dismissed the theft of trade secrets as “isolated misconduct by company employees,” which he said was impossible to prevent at a company that employed hundreds of thousands of people.
赫伯利格將竊取商業機密的行為淡化為「公司員工的孤立不當行為」，並稱在一家擁有數十萬名員工的公司裡，這種行為無法完全杜絕。
Huawei was founded in the 1980s by Ren Zhengfei, Ms. Meng’s father. Its own rise mirrored that of China, as it transitioned toward capitalism and a market economy.
華為由孟晚舟的父親任正非於20世紀80年代創立。隨著中國向資本主義和市場經濟轉型，華為自身的崛起也折射出中國的發展軌跡。
The company’s ascent worried some U.S. officials, who said that its technology could be used to spy on American citizens. Officials also accused the company of dodging U.S. laws against doing business with Iran.
該公司的崛起令一些美國官員感到擔憂，他們稱其技術可能被用來監視美國公民。官員們還指責該公司規避美國禁止與伊朗開展業務的法律。
In the years since the prosecution began, Huawei has become a crucial part of China’s development of artificial intelligence chips, creating an alternative to American technology in a race for global A.I. hegemony.
自起訴開始以來的幾年裡，華為已成為中國人工智慧晶片研發的關鍵組成部分，在爭奪全球人工智慧霸權的競賽中打造出美國技術的替代方案。
廣告
Since the Huawei case was first brought, the Justice Department’s focus on China has evolved alongside shifts in the global economy. The department announced last week that it was pursuing a criminal investigation into allegations that Megaspeed, a Singaporean company, was smuggling A.I. chips into China.
自華為案首次提起訴訟以來，司法部對中國的關注點也隨著全球經濟的變化而演變。該部門上週宣布，正對新加坡公司Megaspeed涉嫌向中國走私人工智慧晶片的指控展開刑事調查。
Megaspeed had been led by Alice Huang, who U.S. officials believed worked as an asset for Chinese intelligence and who had an affair with Jensen Huang, the chief executive of Nvidia.
Megaspeed此前由黃樂(Alice Huang)領導，美國官員認為她曾為中國情報部門線人，並與英偉達首席執行官黃仁勛有過婚外情。
When the criminal case against Huawei began in 2018, the United States and China were locked in a trade war started by President Trump. Mr. Trump, then in his first term, mused that he could get involved with Ms. Meng’s case to help secure a potential trade deal.
2018年針對華為的刑事訴訟啟動時，美中正陷入由川普總統挑起的貿易戰。當時處於第一任期的川普曾考慮介入孟晚舟的案件，以利潛在貿易協議的達成。
Under the agreement Ms. Meng reached, she admitted that Huawei had misled a bank about the company’s dealings with Iran. In 2021, Ms. Meng was welcomed back as a hero in China, where she remains the company’s chief financial officer.
根據孟晚舟達成的協議，她承認華為就公司在伊朗的業務誤導了一家銀行。2021年，回到中國的孟晚舟得到英雄般的歡迎，目前她仍擔任該公司首席財務官。
“Meng committed crimes on Huawei’s behalf, and then Huawei put her in charge,” Ms. Arfa said in her closing statement. “That’s not a company that prohibits criminal conduct. That is a company that encourages it, demands it, rewards it.”
「孟晚舟代表華為實施了犯罪，而華為隨後讓她掌管大權，」阿爾法在結案陳詞中說。「這不是一家禁止犯罪行為的公司，而是一家鼓勵、要求並獎勵犯罪的公司。」

Santul Nerkar是《紐約時報》記者，報導布魯克林聯邦法院新聞。
