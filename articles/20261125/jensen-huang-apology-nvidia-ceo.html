「我犯了可怕的錯誤」：黃仁勛向英偉達員工緻歉

Jensen Huang Apologizes to Nvidia Employees and Says He Is in Therapy

TRIPP MICKLE, KALLEY HUANG • 2026-11-25
A day after returning as chief executive at Nvidia, Jensen Huang apologized on Tuesday to his entire company for violating its code of conduct during an hourlong meeting that began on a somber note before transforming into a defiant airing of grievances.
重返英偉達首席執行官職位一天後，黃仁勛於週二向全體員工緻歉，承認自己違反了公司行為準則。這場長達一小時的會議以沉重氣氛開場，隨後轉變為一場充滿對抗情緒的訴苦會。
“I made some terrible mistakes,” he said in his first all-hands meeting with employees since his return. Referring to his wife, he added, “I was unfaithful to Lori, and I hurt my kids and, of course, hurt all of you.”
「我犯了一些可怕的錯誤，」他在回歸後的首次全員會議上說。提到妻子時，他還說：「我對洛麗不忠，傷害了我的孩子，當然也傷害了你們所有人。」
Mr. Huang said he is in therapy every day for 90 minutes, adding that it is “not acceptable” that he violated the company’s code of conduct, some of which he wrote. He said his actions had forced the company’s board of directors to evaluate his future with the company, which led him to resign this month.
黃仁勛說，他每天接受90分鐘心理治療，並稱自己違反公司行為準則——其中部分條款由他本人撰寫——是「不可接受的」。他說，自己的行為迫使公司董事會評估他在公司的未來，導致他本月辭職。
The meeting cemented Mr. Huang’s return to Nvidia after two head-spinning weeks that threw the chip-making giant into turmoil, sent tech stocks into a nosedive and threatened the tech industry’s artificial intelligence boom.
在經歷了令人目眩的兩周後，此次會議標誌著黃仁勛正式回歸英偉達，此前，這起風波令這家晶片製造巨頭陷入動盪，導致科技股暴跌，並威脅到科技行業的人工智慧熱潮。
廣告
Mr. Huang resigned after revelations that he violated the company’s code of conduct. He had an affair with a customer and Chinese citizen, Alice Huang, who U.S. government officials determined was feeding information to Chinese intelligence services. He also had a relationship with a direct report at the company. The company’s board unanimously accepted his resignation.
黃仁勛在被曝違反公司行為準則後辭職。他與客戶中國公民黃樂(Alice Huang)發生婚外情，美國政府官員認定她向中國情報機構提供信息。此外，他還與公司內的一名直屬下屬存在親密關係。公司董事會一致接受了他的辭職。
While Wall Street has cheered Mr. Huang’s reinstatement, there are still broad questions about his conduct, and he is expected to testify in a series of potentially contentious congressional hearings in Washington next week.
儘管華爾街對黃仁勛復職表示歡迎，但他的行為仍遭到廣泛質疑，預計他下週將在華盛頓一系列可能充滿爭議的國會聽證會上作證。
During his talk with employees, Mr. Huang sought to dismiss narratives about what had happened behind the scenes to ease his return before the board finished its own investigation into his behavior. He said he was back not because he was a “tech bro” who was “above the law” and “above the code of conduct.” Nor was he back, he said, because President Trump pressured Nvidia’s board to reinstate him.
在與員工的談話中，黃仁勛試圖駁斥有關董事會完成對他行為的調查前、幕後發生了一些事情才讓他順利回歸的說法。他說，自己之所以回歸，並非因為他是一個「凌駕於法律之上」和「凌駕於行為準則之上」的「科技兄弟」。他說，也不是因為川普總統向英偉達董事會施壓要求恢復其職務。
“That is also completely untrue,” Mr. Huang said in a livestream that was reviewed by The New York Times. He was back, he said, because Nvidia’s board “evaluated the entire situation” and “gathered facts.” It also received assurances from the Trump administration that Mr. Huang was not a national security threat.
「那也完全不屬實，」黃仁勛在《紐約時報》查閱的一場直播中說。他說，他之所以回歸，是因為英偉達董事會「評估了整個情況」並「收集了事實」。 此外，董事會還從川普政府那裡獲得了保證，確認黃仁勛不構成國家安全威脅。
Tuesday’s meeting was the second in the past six days at Nvidia. Last Wednesday, Colette Kress, who served as interim chief executive, sought to reassure a rattled company by telling employees that Nvidia’s “ambition will not change.” She was joined onstage by more than a dozen of the company’s leaders, several of whom spoke about how they planned to move forward without Mr. Huang.
週二的會議是英偉達過去六天內的第二次會議。上週三，擔任臨時首席執行官的科萊特·克雷斯試圖安撫動盪中的公司，告訴員工，英偉達的「雄心不會改變」。十多名公司領導人與她一同登台，其中幾人談到，在沒有黃仁勛的情況下，他們計劃如何推進。
Mr. Huang began Tuesday’s talk by thanking Ms. Kress and the company’s leadership team, which was seated on the front row, for doing “an incredible job.”
黃仁勛週二講話開始時首先感謝克雷斯和坐在前排的公司領導團隊「做得非常出色」。
廣告
He also praised Mr. Trump, who had encouraged the board to bring back Mr. Huang. He said Mr. Trump “really, really loves our company” and “gloats about Nvidia.”
他還讚揚川普，正是他鼓勵董事會讓黃仁勛回歸。他說，川普「真的、真的很愛我們公司」，並且「總是得意洋洋地談論英偉達」。
“Everything he’s done, it’s just his affection, his admiration, his recognition of the great company that you are,” Mr. Huang said.
「他所做的一切都源於他對你們所在的這個偉大公司的喜愛、欽佩和認可，」黃仁勛說。
Mr. Huang said he would be “in service of helping our president make our country successful,” but he said that did not make Nvidia a political company.
黃仁勛說，他將「致力於幫助我們的總統，讓我們的國家成功」，但他表示這不會使英偉達成為一家政治化的公司。
“Not even a little bit,” he said. “We are the most apolitical company.”
「一點都不，」他說。「我們是最不政治化的公司。」
Mr. Huang told employees that he had returned to the company because Nvidia’s board had decided it was “in the best interest of the company and shareholders.”
黃仁勛告訴員工，他回到公司是因為英偉達董事會認定這「符合公司和股東的最佳利益」。
He also said the board’s decision did not mean other employees could violate the code of conduct. That would not be “appropriate” or “tolerated,” he said.
他還說，董事會的決定並不意味著其他員工可以違反行為準則。他說，那樣既不「恰當」，也不會被「容忍」。
廣告
“Sometimes, it’s appropriate to forgive,” he said. “Sometimes it’s not.”
「有時候，寬恕是恰當的，」他說。「有時候不是。」
Mr. Huang addressed a report by The Times about his management style, which he had described in the past as “torture you into greatness.” For years, he berated employees with insults, personal attacks and violent comments.
黃仁勛回應了《紐約時報》關於他管理風格的報導。他過去曾將這種風格描述為「通過折磨讓你變得偉大」。多年來，他用侮辱、人身攻擊和暴力言論斥責員工。
“I can go too far,” Mr. Huang said. “When I go too far, it hurts people, it belittles them, it disempowers them. And that’s not right.”
「我可能做得過分，」黃仁勛說。「當我做得過分時，會傷害他人，貶低他們，剝奪他們的力量。那是不對的。」
Mr. Huang said Nvidia’s human resources department would collect feedback about him and share it monthly with the board of directors. He said he wanted to make sure he was “able to channel my intensity in the service of making our company better.”
黃仁勛說，英偉達人力資源部門將收集關於他的反饋，並每月與董事會分享。他說，他想確保自己「能夠將我的激情轉化為推動公司進步的力量」。
He also attacked criticism of the company’s organizational structure, in which he had more than 60 direct reports, while boasting that he doesn’t “believe in succession planning” because it would imply some executives were more important than others.
他還抨擊了對公司組織結構的批評，該架構下他擁有超過60名直接下屬，同時他還誇耀自己「不相信繼任計劃」，因為這會暗示某些高管比其他高管更重要。
In his defense of Nvidia’s unusual management structure, Mr. Huang said it was “his pride and joy.” He credited it with allowing the company to move fast, innovate often and continue operating in his absence.
在為英偉達不尋常的管理架構辯護時，黃仁勛說這是「他的驕傲和喜悅」。 他認為正是這種架構使公司能夠快速行動、頻繁創新，並在自己缺席時仍能正常運轉。
廣告
“This company is wired up to execute and execute and execute and deliver on financial results,” he said. He said any of the company’s top 60 executives could succeed him.
「這家公司被設定為執行、執行、再執行，並且最終實現財務業績，」他說。他說，公司前60名高管中的任何一位都可以接替他。
“That is the definition of an extraordinary organization, not an example of brittleness,” he said. “I will fight that all day long.”
「這正是卓越組織的定義，而不是脆弱的例證，」他說。「我會為此奮戰到底。」

Tripp Mickle報導英偉達、谷歌和蘋果等全球最大的科技公司。他也報導整個科技行業的趨勢，包括裁員、生成式AI和機器人計程車等。

Kalley Huang是時報駐舊金山記者，報導科技行業和業內大型公司，包括英偉達和蘋果。
