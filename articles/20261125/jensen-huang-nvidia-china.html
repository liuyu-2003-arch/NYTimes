黃仁勛重返英偉達，北京喜憂參半

For China, Jensen Huang’s Return to Nvidia Is a Mixed Blessing

DAVID PIERSON, 黃瑞黎, CECILIA KANG • 2026-11-25
The reappointment of Jensen Huang as Nvidia’s chief executive appears to be good news for Beijing: The most influential advocate in the United States for selling advanced chips to China is back in charge after an extraordinary intervention by President Trump.
黃仁勛被重新任命為英偉達首席執行官，對北京來說似乎是好消息：在川普總統非同尋常的干預下，美國最具影響力的對華出售先進晶片的倡導者重新掌舵英偉達。
But the view in Beijing is likely to be murkier. While several Chinese tech executives welcomed Mr. Huang’s return, they also recognized that his hands may now be tied. Lawmakers in Washington are lining up to question him and tighten the rules on chip sales to China.
但北京對此事的看法可能更為複雜。儘管一些中國科技高管對黃仁勛的回歸表示歡迎，他們也認識到，黃仁勛如今可能處處受到掣肘。華盛頓的議員們正紛紛準備對他展開質詢，並收緊對華晶片銷售規定。
Either way, the whiplash will give Beijing further impetus in its efforts to wean Chinese companies off U.S. technology and develop their own. And the latest twist in the saga of the world’s most consequential chip company and the man who built it from the ground up ensures that Nvidia will remain at the center of U.S.-China relations.
無論如何，這一反覆將給北京提供進一步動力，推動中國企業擺脫對美國技術的依賴、發展自己的技術。圍繞全球最具影響力的晶片企業及其一手締造者的這場風波，如今又出現了新的轉折，這意味著英偉達將繼續處於美中關係的焦點。
No company can compete with Nvidia when it comes to designing advanced chips for artificial intelligence, a huge advantage for Washington as it presses to keep ahead of Beijing in the transformative technology.
在先進人工智慧晶片的設計方面，沒有任何企業能與英偉達匹敵，而華盛頓正力求在這項具有變革意義的技術上保持對北京的領先優勢。
In the broader competition with the United States, China has exceptional engineering talent, world-class A.I. models, and access to cheap power and vast land for its data centers. It has also repeatedly demonstrated an ability to do more with less.
從中美兩國更廣泛的競爭格局來看，中國擁有出色的工程技術人才、世界一流的人工智慧模型，以及建設數據中心所需的廉價電力和廣闊土地。中國也一再展現出以更少資源創造更多價值的能力。
But China is demonstrably weaker in chips and is still struggling to make enough of them to satisfy domestic demand. Epoch AI, a nonprofit research organization, estimates that smuggled chips account for roughly a third of China’s A.I. computing power, though it says the true share could be considerably smaller or larger.
但中國在晶片領域明顯處於劣勢，仍難以生產足夠的晶片來滿足國內需求。非營利研究機構Epoch AI估計，走私晶片約佔中國人工智慧算力的三分之一，但該機構也表示，實際比例可能遠低於或遠高於這一估算值。
For China’s A.I. industry and A.I. models, “Huang’s return is good news given its dependence on Nvidia and the importance of American technology,” Lizzi C. Lee, a fellow focusing on the Chinese economy at the Asia Society Policy Institute, said in an email. Beijing, she added, may also see it as “reassurance that practical and commercial interests still carry the day in Washington.”
亞洲協會政策研究所中國經濟研究員李其在電子郵件中說，對中國人工智慧產業和人工智慧模型而言，「鑒於對英偉達的依賴和美國技術的重要性，黃仁勛的回歸是好消息。」她還說，北京或許也會因此感到安心，因為這表明「務實考量和商業利益在華盛頓依然能夠佔據上風」。
廣告
But Ms. Lee said Chinese policymakers were unlikely to stake everything on Mr. Trump’s good will, given the results of the midterm elections and his lame-duck status. “If anything,” she said, “expect the push for domestic substitution to accelerate after this episode, which already exposed the vulnerability of China’s A.I. ambition.”
但李其表示，鑒於期中選舉的結果以及川普已成為「跛腳鴨」總統，中國的政策制定者不太可能把所有希望都寄托在川普的態度上。她說：「如果說有什麼影響的話，這場風波已經暴露出中國人工智慧發展雄心所面臨的軟肋，預計此後國產替代的努力將進一步加快。」
Much will depend on Washington, where a debate has been raging about how to approach Chinese access to advanced A.I. chips. That debate intensified in recent weeks after The New York Times reported that U.S. intelligence officials believed that Alice Huang, a Chinese businesswoman who allegedly had an extramarital affair with Mr. Huang, was an asset working for China’s spy agency.
很大程度上，事態的發展將取決於華盛頓。圍繞如何處理中國獲取先進人工智慧晶片的問題，華盛頓一直存在激烈爭論。最近幾週，在《紐約時報》報導美國情報官員認為，據稱與黃仁勛有婚外情的中國女商人黃樂(Alice Huang)是為中國情報機構工作的線人後，這場爭論進一步升級。
China hawks and national security officials have argued that the best way to stay ahead on A.I. is to curtail exports of American technology. Mr. Huang and his supporters say it is better to lift restrictions and make China dependent on U.S. chips.
對華鷹派和國家安全官員認為，在人工智慧上保持領先的最佳方式是限制美國技術出口。黃仁勛及其支持者則認為，更好的做法是取消限制，讓中國依賴美國晶片。
Two key issues for Beijing are whether it will continue to receive Nvidia chips, and whether new rules blocking Chinese companies from remotely accessing chips will persist.
北京的兩個關鍵問題是，它是否會繼續獲得英偉達晶片，以及阻止中國公司遠程訪問晶片的新規則是否會持續。
The Trump administration reversed a ban on sales of Nvidia’s H20 chip to China in 2025 and then approved the more powerful H200. Lawmakers disclosed last week that the Commerce Department had approved the sale of more than 800,000 advanced A.I. chips to Chinese companies, including some with ties to China’s military.
2025年，川普政府撤銷了對華銷售英偉達H20晶片的禁令，隨後又批准了性能更強大的H200晶片的對華銷售。多名議員上週披露，商務部已批准向中國企業出售80多萬枚先進人工智慧晶片，其中一些企業與中國軍方存在關聯。
Chinese companies had also exploited a loophole until recently. Unable to import Nvidia’s most advanced chips, they rented access to them through data centers in Southeast Asia and elsewhere. That was legal until last week, when the Commerce Department issued a rule clamping down on the ability of Chinese companies to access advanced Nvidia chips through data centers in third countries, and requiring licenses for chip exports to companies in Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, Indonesia and Brazil.
直到不久前，中國企業還一直在利用一個監管漏洞。由於無法進口英偉達最先進的晶片，它們通過東南亞及其他地區的數據中心租用這些晶片的算力。這種做法此前一直是合法的，直到上週，美國商務部發布新規，嚴格限制中國企業通過第三國的數據中心使用英偉達先進晶片，並要求向馬來西亞、新加坡、泰國、印度尼西亞和巴西的企業出口晶片必須獲得許可證。
A week after his administration unveiled those rules, though, Mr. Trump has helped restore to power their most prominent opponent.
然而，就在川普政府公布這些規定一周後，川普卻幫助這些規定最具影響力的反對者重新掌權。
Mr. Trump has framed Mr. Huang’s return partly as a way of keeping him away from Beijing. “Jensen could take the next plane to China, and I guarantee you, they would give him whatever he wanted,” Mr. Trump told CNBC on Monday.
川普在解釋黃仁勛回歸的原因時表示，此舉部分是為了防止他倒向北京。「黃仁勛可以馬上搭乘下一班飛機去中國，我向你保證，他想要什麼，他們就會給他什麼，」川普週一對CNBC說。
廣告
In China, where Mr. Huang’s plight has generated widespread sympathy, some voices have greeted his return as vindication. Chinese officials have dismissed as “smears and slanders” the allegations of espionage.
在中國，黃仁勛的遭遇引發了廣泛同情，一些輿論認為，他的回歸證明了他是清白的。中國官員此前駁斥有關間諜活動的指控，稱其為「污衊抹黑」。
Ms. Huang, who has not responded to The Times’ requests for comment, was previously at the head of Megaspeed, a Singapore-based data center company that bought large quantities of Nvidia chips and is now under federal investigation over accusations that it smuggled those chips to China.
黃樂此前執掌總部位於新加坡的數據中心公司Megaspeed，該公司購買了大量英偉達晶片，目前正因將這些晶片走私到中國的指控而接受聯邦調查。她未回應《紐約時報》的置評請求。
On Tuesday, Asian shares, led by tech stocks, surged on news of Mr. Huang’s return.
週二，受黃仁勛回歸消息的提振，亞洲股市在科技股的帶動下大幅上漲。
China’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs declined to comment on Mr. Huang. However, the Global Times, a Communist Party tabloid, suggested that his reinstatement was proof that the spy affair was a “farce” instigated by U.S. media.
中國外交部拒絕就黃仁勛發表評論。不過，中共小報《環球時報》暗示，黃仁勛復職證明，這起間諜風波是美國媒體一手挑起的一場「鬧劇」。
On Monday, the same day Mr. Huang returned, the Commerce Department added Megaspeed to a trade blacklist. Mr. Huang is also scheduled to face questions from House investigators and to testify before the Senate next week.
週一，即黃仁勛回歸同一天，商務部將Megaspeed列入貿易黑名單。黃仁勛還定於下週在眾議院接受質詢，並在參議院作證。
“Jensen is returning to a new political reality for U.S. export controls, which is that there is significantly more skepticism now about having a laissez-faire approach to chip sales all over the world,” said Vivek Chilukuri, director of the Technology and National Security Program at the Center for a New American Security.
「黃仁勛回歸之際，美國出口管制面臨新的政治現實，即對全球晶片銷售採取放任自流做法受到了更多質疑，」新美國安全中心技術與國家安全項目主任維韋克·奇盧庫裡說。
Experts widely believe that China is six to nine months behind the United States in A.I. development. Mr. Chilukuri said that if Washington “actually closed the loopholes in export control policy and enforced the rules that are already on the books, that lead could double.”
專家普遍認為，中國的人工智慧發展落後美國六至九個月。奇盧庫裡表示，如果華盛頓「真正堵住出口管制政策中的漏洞，並嚴格執行現行規定，這一領先優勢可能翻倍」。
廣告
Tighter controls will only deepen mistrust of Washington in Beijing, where the controversy has been read as further proof that the United States “is not a reliable partner,” said Wu Xinbo, an American studies scholar at Fudan University in Shanghai who advises China’s Foreign Ministry. Washington, he said, could cut off China’s supply chains at any time and on any pretext.
上海復旦大學美國問題學者、中國外交部顧問吳心伯說，更嚴格的管制只會加深北京對華盛頓的不信任，在北京看來，這場風波進一步證明，美國「不是一個可靠的合作夥伴」。他說，華盛頓隨時都可以找個藉口切斷中國的供應鏈。
To Mr. Wu, China has been less a protagonist in the Nvidia case and more of a prop in U.S. political drama.
在吳心伯看來，在英偉達事件中，中國與其說是主角，不如說是美國政治大戲中的一個道具。
“The Jensen Huang episode reflects the intense political infighting within the United States and China is just the background music,” he said.
「黃仁勛事件反映了美國內部激烈的政治內鬥，中國只是背景音樂，」他說。
Other Chinese analysts warn Washington should not underestimate Beijing’s leverage. China dominates the supply of rare earths and magnets that American factories and weapons makers rely on. Under a trade truce reached last year, Beijing suspended sweeping controls on rare earth exports. But that truce expires on Jan. 10, and its extension could become entangled with the debate over U.S. chip exports.
其他中國分析人士警告華盛頓不應低估北京的影響力。中國主導著美國工廠和武器製造商依賴的稀土和磁鐵供應。根據去年達成的貿易休戰，北京暫停了對稀土出口的全面管制。但該休戰將於1月10日到期，是否延長協議可能會與美國晶片出口的爭論糾纏在一起。
Chinese analysts also say China is too big a market for American chipmakers to give up. And new restrictions, they add, would only speed up China’s efforts to power its A.I. boom with domestic chips and software. DeepSeek, one of China’s leading A.I. companies, is developing software for chips made by Huawei Technologies, partly as a way to circumvent Nvidia’s own software.
中國分析人士還說，中國市場太大，美國晶片製造商不可能放棄。他們補充說，新的限制措施只會加快中國利用國產晶片和軟體推動人工智慧產業蓬勃發展的步伐。中國領先的人工智慧企業之一DeepSeek正在為華為生產的晶片開發軟體，部分目的是為了繞過英偉達自己的軟體。
But China’s government and its A.I. companies do not always have the same incentives, said Olivia Shen, director of the strategic technologies program with the United States Studies Centre at the University of Sydney. While Beijing wants its industry to use Chinese-made chips, publicly listed companies like MiniMax and Z.ai want to make globally competitive models, which — for now — can only be trained with Nvidia chips, she said.
但雪梨大學美國研究中心戰略技術項目主任奧利維婭·沈表示，中國政府與本國人工智慧企業的利益訴求並不總是一致。她說，北京希望本國人工智慧產業使用國產晶片，稀宇科技和智譜等上市企業則希望開發具有全球競爭力的模型，而至少目前，這樣的模型只能依靠英偉達晶片進行訓練。
Still, Beijing’s ambitions will not change.
儘管如此，北京的雄心不會改變。
“Longer term, they don’t want their access to this important resource to be based on the whims of a particular character in the White House,” Ms. Shen said.
「從長遠來看，他們不希望能否獲得這種重要資源取決於白宮某個人的一時興起，」奧利維婭·沈說。
