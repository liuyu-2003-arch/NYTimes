上海市委書記陳吉寧落馬

Shanghai’s Party Leader Is Put Under Investigation as Xi’s Purges Expand

儲百亮 • 2026-11-25
The Communist Party leader of Shanghai, Chen Jining, has been placed under investigation on suspicion of “serious violations,” the Chinese government announced on Tuesday. It was a stunning downfall for an official who had widely been seen as destined for a higher role under the country’s top leader, Xi Jinping.
中國政府週二宣布，上海市委書記陳吉寧因涉嫌「嚴重違紀違法」正接受調查。作為一位普遍被視為將在中國最高領導人習近平治下晉陞更高職位的官員，他的落馬令人震驚。
The investigation into Mr. Chen was announced in a brief bulletin from Xinhua, China’s official news agency, which gave no details about reasons. The bulletin said that Mr. Chen was suspected of “serious violations of discipline and the law,” a phrase that usually refers to allegations of corruption.
對陳吉寧的調查是通過中國官方通訊社新華社的一則簡短通報宣布的，通報未給出具體原因。通報稱，陳吉寧涉嫌「嚴重違紀違法」，這一表述通常指涉腐敗指控。
Mr. Chen, 62, an environmental scientist trained in Britain, rose as a university president and minister for environmental protection. He went on to become mayor of Beijing and, from 2022, party secretary of Shanghai — a key post in charge of one of China’s most important commercial cities. Previous Shanghai party secretaries have often risen to a seat on the party’s Politburo Standing Committee, a core leadership body that currently has seven members, led by Mr. Xi.
現年62歲的陳吉寧是一名曾在英國深造的環境科學家，歷任大學校長和環保部部長。此後他出任北京市市長，並於2022年起擔任上海市委書記——這一關鍵職位掌管著中國最重要的商業城市之一。上海市委書記往往能晉陞為中共中央政治局常委，該核心領導機構目前共有七名成員，由習近平領導。
 Mr. Chen’s abrupt undoing suggests that Mr. Xi’s expanding drive against corruption and disloyalty means that even seemingly secure protégés cannot feel safe as he prepares to claim an all-but-certain fourth term ruling China. Other officials made subject to investigations have gone on to face prosecution, conviction and imprisonment.
陳吉寧的突然落馬錶明，隨著習近平準備開啟幾乎毫無懸念的第四個執政任期，在其不斷擴大的反腐與整肅不忠行動之下，即便是看似穩固的親信也無法高枕無憂。以往接受調查的官員隨後會面臨起訴、定罪和監禁。
廣告
“This is shocking,” Wang Hsin-hsien, a professor at National Chengchi University in Taiwan who studies the Chinese Communist Party, said of the announcement of an investigation into Mr. Chen.
「這是蠻震撼的，」台灣國立政治大學研究中國共產黨問題的教授王信賢在談到對陳吉寧展開調查的公告時說道。
“There might well be more major shake-ups to come,” he added. “If even Chen Jining has been taken down, the same could happen to others. There’s no safety for anyone.”
「所以說不定可能會有一些大的變動，」他補充說，「如果連陳吉寧都下，其他（人）都還是有可能。沒有誰是安全的。」
Later on Tuesday, the Shanghai Communist Party leadership announced that it had endorsed the removal of Mr. Chen and pledged loyalty to Mr. Xi at a meeting. There must “always be absolute loyalty to the party,” the official summary of the meeting said.
週二晚些時候，上海市委領導層宣布，已在一次會議上批准了免去陳吉寧職務的決定，並宣誓效忠習近平。會議的官方摘要指出，必須「始終保持對黨的絕對忠誠」。
One month ago, Mr. Xi presided over a meeting of the Communist Party Central Committee where he called for a renewed campaign for discipline and loyalty. The meeting also announced preparations for a party congress next year, at which Mr. Xi is almost certain to win another five years as general secretary.
一個月前，習近平主持召開了一次中共中央委員會會議，他在會上呼籲開展新一輪紀律和忠誠整頓行動。會議還宣布籌備明年的黨代會，習近平幾乎肯定將在會上再次獲得五年的總書記任期。
This month, Mr. Chen convened a meeting in Shanghai where he warned officials to abide by Mr. Xi’s warnings against corruption and other abuses of power. “Elevate your political stance, and remain clearheaded and vigilant,” Mr. Chen told them.
本月，陳吉寧在上海主持召開了一次會議，他在會上告誡官員要遵守習近平關於反腐敗及其他濫用權力行為的警示。「提高政治站位，保持清醒警醒，」陳吉寧對他們說。
Last week, Mr. Xi was in the international limelight as he hosted President Trump and President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia for a brief three-way summit in the southern city of Shenzhen, and Mr. Xi may have waited until after that meeting before moving against Mr. Chen, Professor Wang said.
王信賢表示，上週，習近平在南部城市深圳主持了川普總統和俄羅斯總統普丁參加的簡短三方峰會，成為國際矚目的焦點；習近平可能是在該會議結束後才對陳吉寧採取行動的。
廣告
Mr. Xi could become increasingly preoccupied with cleaning out the party and the military ahead of the party congress, at which he will choose a new generation of leading officials, said Christopher K. Johnson, a former senior China analyst at the C.I.A.
美國中央情報局前高級中國問題分析師克里斯托弗·強生表示，在黨代會召開之前，習近平可能會越來越專注於清理黨內和軍內問題，在黨代會上，他將選出新一代領導層。
“Chen’s downfall is a stark reminder that, despite all the fanfare over last week’s trilateral summit with Presidents Putin and Trump, and China’s ongoing competition with the United States, Xi remains laser-focused on the politics around his bid for a fourth term at next year’s party congress,” said Mr. Johnson, who is now head of the political risk firm China Strategies Group.
「陳的倒台是一個鮮明的警示：儘管上週與普丁和川普總統舉行的三方峰會聲勢浩大，且中國與美國之間的競爭仍在持續，但習近平依然將全部精力集中在爭取明年黨代會上連任第四屆領導職務的政治博弈，」現任政治風險諮詢公司中國戰略集團負責人的強生表示。
Since Mr. Xi began his third leadership term in 2022, investigations and removals have brought down around 100 senior military officers and a growing number of civilian party officials. In July, the party expelled Ma Xingrui, another senior official, after accusing him of corruption and of abusing his power for favors and sex.
自2022年習近平開啟第三個領導任期以來，調查和撤職行動已導致約100名高級軍官及越來越多的文職黨內官員落馬。7月，另一名高級官員馬興瑞因腐敗及濫用職權謀取私利和性交易被開除黨籍。
“As with Xi’s purge of the high command, he is telegraphing he intends to cut deep into the party’s senior ranks to find officials he thinks he can trust,” Mr. Johnson said.
「正如習近平對軍方高層進行的清洗一樣，他正在釋放信號，表明他打算深入黨內高層，物色他認為值得信賴的官員，」強生說。
Many analysts thought that Mr. Chen could rise to become premier, or at least a vice premier, in Mr. Xi’s next administration. Mr. Chen had won a reputation as a technocrat whose scientific education blended well with Mr. Xi’s technological priorities.
許多分析人士曾認為，陳吉寧有望在習近平的下一屆政府中晉陞為總理，或至少擔任副總理。陳吉寧一直被視為技術官僚，其科學教育背景與習近平的科技優先事項十分契合。
Mr. Chen was also already a member of the party’s Politburo, a council of leading central and local officials. He is now virtually certain to be expelled from the party and from the Politburo, which would mean that four of the 24 Politburo members elected in 2022 had been removed and investigated. One of those was Zhang Youxia, China’s topmost general until his removal this year.
陳吉寧同時也是中共中央政治局委員，該機構由中央和地方主要領導官員組成。如今他幾乎肯定將被開除黨籍並被免去政治局委員職務，這意味著2022年選出的24名政治局委員中，已有四人被免職和接受調查。其中包括今年被免職前一直是中國軍隊最高將領的張又俠。
廣告
“While the specifics about Chen Jining are not yet clear, the effect will be that the other members of the Politburo will be living in dread,” Professor Wang said.
「現在看起來陳吉寧（落馬的細節）其實還不是這麼清楚，但是他會造成的效應就是其他的政治局委員，應該是人心惶惶，」王信賢說。

儲百亮(Chris Buckley)是《紐約時報》首席中國記者，自台北報導中國和台灣問題，重點關注政治、社會變革以及安全和軍事問題。
