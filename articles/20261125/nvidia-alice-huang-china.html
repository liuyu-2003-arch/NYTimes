黃樂的人生軌跡：從兵工廠小鎮到英偉達核心圈

From Chinese Factory Town to the Nvidia Scandal: The Rise of Alice Huang

孟建國, KEITH BRADSHER, 馬語琴, 傅才德, ANA SWANSON • 2026-11-25
The rows of matching yellow apartment blocks are each four stories tall and bear a name from another era: Patriotic Village No. 5.
一排排樣式一致的黃色公寓樓，都是四層高，帶著一個屬於另一個時代的名字：愛國五村。
One of the weathered apartments belonged to the family of Alice Huang, whose father worked as a clerk at a sprawling complex of weapons factories in Nanzhushan, a village nestled in the hills of southern China’s Hunan Province.
其中一套略顯斑駁的公寓曾屬於黃樂(Alice Huang)的家庭，她的父親曾在中國南部湖南省群山環抱的楠竹山鎮一座龐大的兵工廠園區內當文員。
The complex had a number too: State-Owned Factory No. 282. It made everything from hand grenades to parts for guided missiles, and under China’s Soviet-style planned economy, life in the area revolved around it.
這座工廠綜合體也有一個編號：國營282廠。它生產從手榴彈到制導導彈零部件的各類產品，在中國的蘇式計劃經濟體制下，當地的生活都圍繞著這座工廠運轉。
Today, the town is well past its prime. The factory complex remains, but after state-owned enterprise layoffs, many young people started moving away. By the mid-2000s, one of them was Ms. Huang, who rose from this fading industrial town to the inner circle of the man behind America’s artificial intelligence boom — and into the middle of a geopolitical crisis.
如今，這座小鎮早已風光不再。廠區依然存在，但在國有企業下崗潮之後，許多年輕人開始外遷。到2000年代中期，黃樂也成為其中之一，她從這座日漸衰落的工業小鎮一路崛起，進入美國人工智慧熱潮推動者的核心圈子——並捲入了一場地緣政治危機的中心。
廣告
The New York Times traced Ms. Huang’s life through visits to Nanzhushan village and four countries, drawing on internal company documents, corporate filings and interviews with more than a dozen people familiar with her. Ms. Huang has not responded to requests for comment.
《紐約時報》通過走訪楠竹山鎮和四個國家，結合公司內部文件、企業備案資料以及對十餘名熟悉她的人士的採訪，追蹤了黃樂的人生軌跡。黃樂未回應置評請求。
The reporting reveals previously unreported details of her upbringing, education and business dealings that show how Ms. Huang’s life has been intertwined with China’s military-industrial complex since childhood.
此次報導披露了她此前未曾公開的成長背景、教育經歷和商業往來細節，揭示了她的人生自童年起便與中國軍工複合體緊密交織。
U.S. intelligence officials believe Ms. Huang was in contact with an officer of China’s Ministry of State Security from the spring of 2025 through this year. That period overlapped with at least part of the time she allegedly had an affair with Jensen Huang, the chief executive of American chip giant Nvidia. It also coincided with large purchases of Nvidia’s advanced chips by her company Megaspeed. U.S. intelligence agencies have also gathered evidence that her business partner, Kang Wei, who was often introduced as her husband, worked with the same Chinese spy agency.
美國情報官員認為，從2025年春季到今年，黃樂一直與中國國家安全部的一名官員保持聯繫。這一時期與據稱她和美國晶片巨頭英偉達首席執行官黃仁勛保持婚外情的時間至少有部分重疊，也與她的Megaspeed公司大量採購英偉達先進晶片的時間相吻合。美國情報機構還收集了證據，表明她經常對外介紹為丈夫的商業夥伴康偉也與同一家中國情報機構有合作。
Those contacts led some U.S. officials to suspect that Mr. Huang, who resigned as Nvidia’s chief executive on Nov. 12, had been targeted in a Chinese intelligence operation, which Beijing denies. The case heightened concerns in Washington about Beijing’s efforts to acquire advanced American chips and accelerate its A.I. ambitions.
這些接觸讓一些美國官員懷疑，已於11月12日辭去英偉達首席執行官職務的黃仁勛曾是中國情報行動的目標，北京方面對此予以否認。這一案件加劇了華盛頓對北京試圖獲取美國先進晶片並加速其人工智慧雄心的擔憂。
The claims about Ms. Huang continue to reverberate. On Monday, in a stunning turnabout, Nvidia’s board of directors reinstated Mr. Huang as C.E.O. after pressure from President Trump. Despite the president’s support, Mr. Huang and his ties to Ms. Huang are expected to be scrutinized in Congress next week, when he is set to appear before three committees.
圍繞黃樂的指控仍在持續發酵。週一發生了一場令人震驚的逆轉，英偉達董事會在川普總統施壓後恢復了黃仁勛的首席執行官職務。儘管有總統支持，黃仁勛及其與黃樂的關係預計仍將在下週受到國會審查，屆時他將出席三個委員會的聽證會。
Raised in a Weapons Town
兵工廠小鎮的童年
Since childhood, her life was surrounded by China’s defense establishment. She attended a school for children of workers at a weapons factory owned by a state-run company repeatedly sanctioned by the U.S. government.
自兒時起，她的生活便被中國的國防體系所包圍。她就讀的是一所子弟學校，其中的學生是一家國有企業旗下的兵工廠職工子女，該企業曾屢次遭到美國政府制裁。
廣告
Ms. Huang had established her own ties to the Chinese government by the time she was an adult. She worked briefly for China’s state broadcaster and later made an investment that would connect her to the son of a former Chinese vice premier. As an entrepreneur, she remained within the state’s orbit, securing backing and funding from local government officials for a business that served Beijing’s strategic imperatives.
到成年時，黃樂已建立了自己與中國政府的聯繫。她曾在中國官方廣播電視機構短暫工作，後來進行的一項投資使她與一位中國前副總理之子產生了交集。作為一名企業家，她始終處於體制的軌道之內，為一項服務於北京戰略要務的業務贏得了地方政府官員的支持和資金。
Through a web of family and business relationships that gave her access to Nvidia chips, she is linked to Chinese state-owned defense contractors that supply the People’s Liberation Army.
通過一張讓她得以獲取英偉達晶片的家庭和商業關係網，她與為中國人民解放軍供貨的中國國有國防承包商保持了關聯。
The story of a factory worker’s daughter from rural China who found herself at the center of a global struggle over technology and espionage is one of ambition, reinvention and China’s rise from a drab socialist state to a shiny world power.
一個出身於中國鄉間工廠的工人家庭之女發現自己進入了全球技術與間諜之爭的中心——這是一個關於野心、自我重塑，以及中國從了無生氣的社會主義國家崛起為光鮮世界大國的故事。
Alice Huang grew up in the shadow of Mao Zedong, whose birthplace is just a 20-minute drive away. Her father worked at the local plant, part of Jiangnan Industries Group, a subsidiary of the military supplier Norinco.
 黃樂從小在毛澤東的影響下長大，他的出生地離她家僅20分鐘車程。她的父親在當地工廠工作，該廠隸屬於軍工供應商中國兵器工業集團旗下的江南工業集團。
She attended local schools, where she was a good student but not at the top of her class, according to the neighborhood committee cadre. She later enrolled in a nursing school in the nearby city of Xiangtan, according to a current student named Joanna, who said a teacher had described Alice Huang as a graduate.
據居委會幹部介紹，她在當地學校就讀，成績不錯，但並非名列前茅。她後來就讀於鄰近湘潭市的一所護理學校，據一名名叫喬安娜的在校學生說，一位老師曾提到黃樂是該校畢業生。
After graduating, Ms. Huang, who turns 44 next month, did what tens of millions of Chinese in her generation did. She left small-town life for better opportunities in bigger cities like Beijing and Shanghai.
畢業後，下個月將滿44歲的黃樂做了她那一代數千萬中國人都在做的事：離開小鎮生活，前往北京、上海等大城市尋找更好的機會。
廣告
Over two decades, she became a globe-trotting executive overseeing Megaspeed, a Singapore-based company that bought billions of dollars’ worth of Nvidia’s advanced chips.
20多年來，她成長為一名奔走於世界各地的高管，執掌著總部位於新加坡的Megaspeed公司，該公司採購了價值數十億美元的英偉達先進晶片。
Yet traces of her early life proved difficult to pin down. Villagers gossiped about the family, whom some remembered, but none could identify the precise house where she grew up. Local officials disagreed about her mother’s background.
然而，她早年生活的痕跡卻難以捉摸。村民們對這家人議論紛紛，有些人還記得他們，但沒人能指出她家的屋宅在哪。地方官員對她母親的背景也說法不一。
Two Times journalists interviewed village leaders and several residents before the police halted their reporting and took them to the nearest police station for questioning.
時報的兩名記者採訪了村幹部和幾位村民，隨後警方叫停了他們的採訪，並將他們帶到最近的派出所問話。
By her 20s, Alice Huang had left Nanzhushan. She worked for state television at the flagship channel of China’s state broadcaster, according to an online résumé and a former classmate.
20多歲時，黃樂已離開楠竹山鎮。根據一份在線簡歷和一位前同學的說法，她曾在中國國家廣播電視機構的旗艦頻道工作。
She attended Hong Kong Baptist University in 2006 to pursue a master’s degree in media management. The program was new and drew many students from mainland China, who, one former professor recalled, were often there for reasons beyond education. Hong Kong was a gateway to the world, with open internet and easy travel.
2006年，她進入香港浸會大學攻讀傳媒管理碩士學位。該課程當時剛剛開設，吸引了許多來自中國內地的學生，一位前教授回憶說，他們來這裡往往有著教育以外的目的。香港當時是通往世界的門戶，擁有開放的互聯網和便利的出行條件。
A classmate said she often wore designer brands, a contrast with the jeans and T-shirts most other students wore. She often skipped classes, the person said, and many students suspected that connections in China kept her in the school.
一位同學表示，她經常穿名牌服裝，與大多數穿牛仔褲和T恤的學生形成鮮明對比。這名人士還說，她經常曠課，許多學生懷疑她在內地的關係網保住了她的學籍。
廣告
In 2010, she and her older sister, Huang Yu, registered a company at the address of a building inside a members-only golf course in Beijing. Their father, Huang Guobin, by then of retirement age, was listed as a company supervisor in Chinese corporate records.
2010年，她和姐姐黃瑜用北京一家會員制高爾夫球場內一座建築的地址註冊了一家公司。中國企業工商記錄顯示，當時已到退休年齡的父親黃國斌被列為公司監事。
The golf course was a magnet for government officials and the businessmen who courted them. Its restaurant served shark fin, and its chef had worked at the walled compound where Chinese leaders host heads of state, according to Chinese media reports. A senior civil-aviation official was once punished for accepting more than 50 free rounds of golf there.
這家高爾夫球場是政府官員和巴結他們的商人的聚集地。據中國媒體報導，其餐廳供應魚翅，廚師曾在中國領導人接待外國元首的國賓館工作。一名民航系統高級官員曾因在該球場接受超過50次免費高爾夫球服務而受到處分。
It is unclear what the Huang family business did. It reported taking in only about $4,500 in 2012. In 2014, Alice transferred her stake to her sister.
目前尚不清楚黃家這家公司的具體業務。該公司申報2012年的收入僅約4500美元。2014年，黃樂將自己的股份轉讓給了姐姐。
Soon, the Huang sisters moved on to far larger ventures.
不久，黃氏姐妹便轉向了規模大得多的商業項目。
The Data Center Highway
數據中心高速公路
Alice Huang’s hometown Nanzhushan sits next to the G60 expressway. Follow it some 700 miles to the northeast and it ends in Shanghai, passing in its final miles by a cluster of giant data centers in the city’s Songjiang District.
黃樂的家鄉楠竹山鎮緊鄰G60高速公路。沿這條公路向東北方向行駛約1200公里，終點便是上海，並且在最後的路段經過該市松江區的一處大型數據中心集群。
廣告
There, she made her mark with a sprawling data center featuring advanced American A.I. chips. The project also brought her into the orbit of Nvidia’s Mr. Huang.
在那裡，她憑藉一座配備美國先進人工智慧晶片的大型數據中心嶄露頭角。該項目也將她帶入了英偉達的黃仁勛的社交圈。
Alice Huang was in Shanghai by the mid-2010s. In meetings, she introduced a well-known financier, Kang Wei, as her husband. Together, they invested in a network of companies with overlapping ownership, shareholders and contact information, according to corporate filings and records from WireScreen, a data platform.
到2000年代中期，黃樂已身處上海。在商務會面中，她將知名金融家康偉介紹為自己的丈夫。企業備案資料和數據平台WireScreen的記錄顯示，兩人共同投資了一系列在所有權、股東和聯繫信息上存在重疊的公司。
She put her money alongside some of China’s most sophisticated and politically connected financiers. At one point, she used a Caribbean shell company to invest in a Chinese medical device maker before its public share sale, according to a filing with the Hong Kong Stock Exchange. Among the other investors was a fund founded by the son of Liu He, who was then a vice premier of China and a member of the Communist Party’s ruling Politburo.
她與一些中國最精明、政治人脈最廣的金融家共同出資。根據提交給香港交易所的一份文件，她曾通過一家加勒比空殼公司在一家中國醫療器械製造商公開募股前對其進行投資。其他投資者中包括時任中國副總理、中共中央政治局委員的劉鶴的兒子所創立的一隻基金。
Alice Huang initially owned a key holding company for the network. In 2017, as with the golf company, she transferred her stake to her older sister.
黃樂最初擁有該商業網路一家核心控股公司的股權。2017年，與高爾夫公司如出一轍，她將股份轉讓給了姐姐。
During Covid, Alice Huang and Mr. Kang helped develop a data center with local government support — a project that made her a potentially useful contact for State Security. When it was finished, the project, called the Yangtze River Delta A.I. Advanced Computing Center, featured Nvidia chips and leased computing power to companies.
新冠疫情期間，黃樂和康偉在地方政府支持下參與開發了一座數據中心——這一項目使她成為國家安全部門眼中有潛在利用價值的聯絡對象。該項目名為長三角人工智慧超算中心，建成後配備了英偉達晶片，並向企業出租算力。
The data center and a company set up to acquire Nvidia servers and rent out computing power, called Century Litong, soon became a key link in a chip supply pipeline. The flows traveled into a network of companies linked to Chinese military contractors like the Aviation Industry Corporation of China, China’s state aerospace and defense conglomerate, and an A.I. lab linked to the People’s Liberation Army.
這座數據中心以及一家為採購英偉達伺服器並出租算力而成立的名為世紀利通的公司很快成為晶片供應管道中的關鍵一環。這些晶片流向了一個企業網路，其中包括中國國有航空航天與國防企業集團中國航空工業集團等中國軍工承包商，以及一家與中國人民解放軍有關聯的人工智慧實驗室。
As borders reopened after the pandemic, the couple looked abroad. In 2022, Nvidia’s sales team had introduced Alice Huang to the Chinese technology giant Alibaba. She and Mr. Kang began pitching investors on a venture to buy Nvidia chips, install them in Southeast Asia and sell the A.I. computing power to Chinese companies.
隨著疫情後跨境往來恢復，這對伴侶將目光投向了海外。2022年，英偉達的銷售團隊將黃樂介紹給了中國科技巨頭阿里巴巴。她和康偉開始向投資者推介一個項目，計劃購買英偉達晶片並部署在東南亞，再向中國企業出售人工智慧算力。
In 2023, Alice Huang used a British Virgin Islands company to buy a Singaporean subsidiary of 7Road, a gaming company. It became Megaspeed.
2023年，黃樂通過一家英屬維爾京群島公司收購了遊戲公司第七大道旗下的一家新加坡子公司。該公司隨後更名為Megaspeed。
The U.S. government had begun tightening restrictions on sales of advanced A.I. chips to China, prompting companies to eliminate ties to the mainland. Alice Huang began cultivating an image as an international executive. She posted pictures of herself drinking wine at fine restaurants across Asia alongside Nvidia’s Mr. Huang and tycoons from the region’s chip and electronics supply chain.
美國政府當時已開始收緊對華銷售先進人工智慧晶片的限制，促使相關企業切斷與中國大陸的聯繫。黃樂開始塑造自己作為國際化高管的形象。她在社群媒體上發布自己在亞洲各地高檔餐廳飲酒的照片，身旁正是英偉達的黃仁勛以及該地區晶片和電子供應鏈的商界大亨。
To those around them, their China connections were obvious. In one pitch deck from 2025, their data center business heralded computing centers operating in Shanghai, Shenzhen, Anhui, Sichuan, Malaysia, Thailand and Indonesia — a mix of locations that tied a data center network in China to one in Southeast Asia, where the most advanced Nvidia chips could be bought.
對周圍的人而言，他們與中國的聯繫顯而易見。在2025年的一份融資推介演示文稿中，他們的數據中心業務宣傳了在上海、深圳、安徽、四川以及馬來西亞、泰國和印度尼西亞運營的計算中心——這種布局將中國境內的數據中心網路與東南亞網路聯繫在一起，而在東南亞可以買到最先進的英偉達晶片。
Although the relationship with Mr. Huang was not yet public, U.S. intelligence agencies had identified Alice Huang as an asset of China’s Ministry of State Security. By the fall of 2025, President Trump had been briefed on the intelligence.
儘管她與黃仁勛的關係當時尚未公開，但美國情報機構已認定黃樂為中國國家安全部的線人。到2025年秋季，川普總統已聽取了有關該情報的簡報。
‘We Are All Gossiping.’
「大家都在議論這件事。」
Alice Huang’s newfound prominence has reached Nanzhushan.
黃樂新近獲得的知名度已經傳到了楠竹山鎮。
One store manager said locals had been discussing the news quietly. Although coverage has not been widely censored, the subject remains sensitive.
一家商店經理表示，當地人一直在私下議論這一消息。儘管相關報導尚未遭到大範圍審查，但這一話題依然敏感。
In Patriotic Village No. 5, people were less cautious.
在愛國五村，人們的顧忌則少得多。
“We are all gossiping about it,” admitted a neighbor surnamed Zheng, who once made hand grenades at the factory. Another resident said many had been discussing her looks.
「大家都在議論這件事，」一位曾在廠裡製造手榴彈的鄭姓鄰居坦言。另一位居民說，很多人一直在討論她的長相。
In nearby Xiangtan, a man surnamed Han said he looked up to Alice Huang. Times have been tough, and it was becoming harder for local people to make money, he said. That a homegrown person could get rich was impressive.
在鄰近的湘潭，一位姓韓的男子表示他很欽佩黃樂。他說，如今世道艱難，當地人賺錢越來越不容易，一個本土出身的人能夠發家致富，令人印象深刻。
“I don’t care if she is the mistress of Jensen Huang,” he said. “She is great because she is a successful businesswoman.”
「我不在乎她是不是黃仁勛的情婦，」他說。「我覺得她挺厲害的，因為她是一個成功的生意人。」
