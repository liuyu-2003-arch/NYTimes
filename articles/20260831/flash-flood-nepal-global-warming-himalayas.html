全球暖化時代，「整個喜馬拉雅山脈都在融化」

In the Age of Global Warming, Some Fear ‘the Entire Himalayas Are Melting’

FINN-OLAF JONES • 2026-08-31
On Wednesday morning, part of a glacier and the mountainside beneath it collapsed on the north side of Langtang Lirung, a 23,734-foot peak near Nepal’s border with Tibet, setting off a 60-mile flood of water, mud and debris.
週三上午，朗塘裡隆峰北坡的一部分冰川及其下方的山體發生坍塌。這座海拔7234米的山峰靠近尼泊爾與西藏的邊境，坍塌引發了長度近100公里、混合著水、泥漿和碎石的洪水。
By Friday, Nepal’s National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority said that 579 people had been killed. Many, many more are still missing.
到週五，尼泊爾國家災害風險減災管理局表示已有579人遇難。仍有更多人下落不明。
More than 4,000 miles west, victims of another disaster in the region were being mourned. That morning, in Dorset, England, family and friends gathered to commemorate Nirmal Purja, the celebrated Nepali mountaineer known as Nimsdai. Among them was Mingma Gyalje Sherpa, one of Nepal’s leading climbers and the founder of the expedition company Imagine Nepal. After an avalanche on Broad Peak in Pakistan’s Karakoram range killed Mr. Purja and nine other climbers on July 30, Mr. Mingma Sherpa led the expedition that recovered Mr. Purja’s body.
在6500多公里以西的英國多塞特郡，人們正在哀悼該地區的另一場災難中的遇難者。那天上午，親友們聚集在一起，紀念尼馬爾·普爾賈——一位著名的尼泊爾登山家，人們常稱他為「尼姆哥」。在場的包括尼泊爾頂尖登山家之一、探險公司Imagine Nepal的創始人明馬·加爾傑·夏爾巴。7月30日，巴基斯坦喀喇崑崙山脈的布洛阿特峰發生雪崩，普爾賈和其他九名登山者遇難，隨後明馬·夏爾巴帶領探險隊找到了普爾賈的遺體。
The two disasters occurred within a month of each other on the mountainous region that extends from Pakistan through Nepal, where scientists, climbers and trekkers have witnessed accelerating losses of snow and ice.
這兩起災難發生在一個月之內，地點都位於從巴基斯坦延伸至尼泊爾的山區。在那裡，科學家、登山者和徒步旅行者都目睹了冰雪的加速消融。
For Nepal, the consequences extend far beyond the mountains. Tourism represents 6.1 percent of the country’s economy and supported about 1.2 million jobs in 2023, roughly 15 percent of all employment, according to the World Bank.
對尼泊爾而言，其影響遠遠超出了山區範圍。根據世界銀行的數據，旅遊業占尼泊爾經濟的6.1%，2023年支撐了約120萬個就業崗位，約佔全國就業總數的15%。
廣告
Of the more than 1.16 million international visitors who went to Nepal in 2025, 165,056 listed trekking and mountaineering as their primary reason for visiting. The industry that has grown around them includes more than 3,000 trekking agencies and tens of thousands of registered guides, as well as expedition companies, porters, cooks, teahouses, hotels, airlines, helicopter operators and drivers.
在2025年來到尼泊爾的超過116萬名國際遊客中，有165056人將徒步和登山列為到訪的首要目的。圍繞這一行業發展的相關機構包括3000多家徒步機構和數萬名註冊嚮導，此外還有探險公司、背夫、廚師、茶館、酒店、航空公司、直升飛機運營商和司機。
At the lucrative extreme is high-altitude climbing. This spring, 1,195 climbers obtained permits for 31 mountains, generating about $9 million in government royalties alone. Nearly 500 received permits for Mount Everest, where the spring permit for a foreign climber now costs $15,000.
高海拔登山是利潤豐厚的領域。今年春季，1195名登山者獲得了31座山峰的登山許可，僅政府特許權使用費就產生了約900萬美元收入。近500人獲得了攀登珠穆朗瑪峰的許可證，目前外國登山者春季攀登珠峰的許可證費用為15000美元。
Over seven years working on expeditions, Mr. Purja had become one of the world’s best-known climbers and the owner of an international expedition company, Elite Exped, with his own sports clothing line. Mr. Mingma Sherpa is part of the same generation. His company runs expeditions on Everest and other Himalayan peaks. “It’s one of the best jobs in Nepal,” he said, speaking after the Langtang Lirung flood. A successful high-altitude guide, he estimated, can make “10 times” an ordinary Nepali salary.
在七年探險工作生涯中，普爾賈已成為全球最知名的登山者之一，並擁有自己的國際探險公司Elite Exped，還推出了個人運動服裝系列。明馬·夏爾巴和他屬於同一代人。他的公司在珠穆朗瑪峰和其他喜馬拉雅山峰運營探險活動。「這是尼泊爾最好的工作之一，」他在朗塘裡隆峰洪水發生後接受採訪時說。據他估算，一名成功的高海拔嚮導可以賺到「普通尼泊爾人工資的10倍」。
But many believe that this livelihood is threatened by global warming. Arbindra Khadka, 35, a glaciologist and research scientist at Tribhuvan University in Kathmandu, noted that Himalayan glaciers are losing about a meter (nearly 3.3 feet) of ice a year. “It’s not just Everest” that is in trouble, he said. “The entire Himalayas are melting.”
但許多人認為，這種生計正受到全球暖化的威脅。35歲的加德滿都特里布萬大學冰川學家和研究科學家阿爾賓德拉·卡德卡指出，喜馬拉雅冰川每年融化約一米的冰。「不只是珠穆朗瑪峰」面臨危機，他說，「整個喜馬拉雅山脈都在融化。」
Disappearing Snow and Ice
消逝的冰雪
Increasingly, those in the trekking business depend on mountains that are changing beneath climbers’ feet. Tul Singh Gurung, president of the Nepal National Mountain Guide Association, believes the Broad Peak avalanche may have begun when a serac, a tower of glacial ice, collapsed and triggered the slab avalanche below. What troubled him was where it happened. Climbers generally regarded the slope between Camps II and III as one of Broad Peak’s more stable sections at that time of year.
登山者腳下那些登山產業賴以生存的山峰正在發生越來越多的變化。尼泊爾國家登山嚮導協會主席圖爾·辛格·古隆認為，布洛阿特峰雪崩可能起始於一座冰塔的坍塌，進而觸發了下方的板狀雪崩。讓他擔憂的是事發地點。登山者通常認為，布洛阿特峰二號營地與三號營地之間的坡面在每年該時節屬於較為穩定的路段。
“The mountains are getting black,” Mr. Gurung said, describing the disappearing snow and ice across the Himalayas and Karakoram. “Every mountain has a rockfall problem.”
「山峰正在變黑，」古隆在描述喜馬拉雅山脈和喀喇崑崙山脈冰雪消融時說，「每座山都有落石問題。」
Dave Watson, a 50-year-old American mountaineer who has summited Mount Everest seven times since 2004, has witnessed the change. “Some of this hazard has always been there, but in a lower volume,” Mr. Watson said.
50歲的美國登山者戴夫·沃森自2004年以來已七次登頂珠穆朗瑪峰，他親眼目睹了這一變化。「這些危險過去一直存在，但規模較小，」沃森說。
The changes are visible not just at the extreme elevations reached by Everest climbers but also on mountains visited by the trekkers and climbers vital to Nepal’s economy.
變化不僅出現在珠峰登山者所到達的極高海拔區域，也出現在那些對尼泊爾經濟至關重要的徒步者和登山者所造訪的山峰上。
Dr. Khadka cautioned that it was too early to say precisely what triggered the Langtang disaster. But he theorized that a relatively small cornice collapse high on the ridges of Langtang Lirung had started an avalanche of loose rock and moraine across slippery, thawing ground during the wet mid-monsoon season.
卡德卡提醒說，現在確定朗塘災難的具體觸發因素還為時過早。但他推測，在雨季中期的濕季，朗塘裡隆峰山脊高處一個相對較小的雪檐崩塌，導致在濕滑的解凍地面上發生由鬆動岩石和冰磧物組成的冰岩崩。
廣告
For Dr. Khadka, the latest disaster is personal. Speaking from Nepal, he was waiting for news of missing relatives and friends.
對卡德卡來說，最近的災難關係到他個人。他在尼泊爾接受採訪時正在等待失蹤親友的消息。
Mr. Gurung said guides were already encountering places where traditional climbing and trekking routes had become difficult to recognize as snow and ice disappear and bare rock emerges. One of the most closely watched is the Khumbu Icefall, which begins the climbing route up Everest from Nepal. Dr. Khadka expects it to keep changing. Some sections may become easier to cross, he said, while hanging ice and exposed slopes create new dangers.
古隆說，嚮導們已經發現，隨著冰雪消融和裸露岩石的出現，一些傳統的登山和徒步路線已變得難以辨認。最受關注的路線之一是昆布冰瀑，始於尼泊爾一側攀登珠穆朗瑪峰的起始路段。卡德卡預計那裡還將繼續變化。他說，有些路段可能變得更容易通過，但懸掛的冰體和裸露的坡面會帶來新的危險。
Mr. Mingma Sherpa has watched the transformation firsthand. When he first crossed the Icefall in 2004, he remembers negotiating numerous crevasses on aluminum ladders. This year, he said, climbers needed ladders in only two places.
明馬·夏爾巴親眼目睹了這一轉變。他記得2004年第一次穿越冰瀑時，需要在許多裂縫處藉助鋁合金梯子通過。他說，今年登山者僅在兩個地方需要使用梯子。
Mr. Watson knew the region devastated by the latest flood. He passed through the Nepal-Tibet border area twice in 2024 and remembered how dramatically it had developed since his early expeditions two decades ago. On journeys to the north side of Everest, crossing the border used to require an overnight stop while climbers waited for immigration formalities and transferred equipment between trucks. In recent years, roads, restaurants, hotels and residences have been built to cater to increasing traffic between the two countries.
沃森熟悉最近這場洪水所摧毀的地區。他在2024年兩次經過尼泊爾-西藏邊境地區，注意到與20年前早期探險時相比，那裡發生了巨大變化。在前往珠穆朗瑪峰北坡的旅程中，穿越邊境過去需要過夜停留，等待入境手續辦理並把裝備轉移到別的卡車上。近年來，為滿足兩國之間日益增長的往來需求，陸續建起了許多道路、餐館、酒店和住宅。
Mr. Watson saw the aftermath of another Himalayan catastrophe. An earthquake in 2015 shook loose rock and ice in Langtang, where an avalanche buried much of the village and killed hundreds. “The thousand-year flood is happening every few years now,” he said.
沃森曾目睹另一場喜馬拉雅災難的後果。2015年的一場地震震落了朗塘地區的岩石和冰，一場雪崩掩埋了村莊的大部分並造成數百人死亡。「千年一遇的洪水現在每隔幾年就會發生一次，」他說。
Yet Nepal’s younger climbers show little sign of abandoning the profession. Mr. Mingma Sherpa said young people continued to be drawn by the pay and the work, although their backgrounds are changing. Increasingly, Sherpa families live in Kathmandu and other lower-altitude cities. “We were used to playing at higher altitude,” he said. “Now Sherpas are born in the city.”
然而，尼泊爾的年輕登山者幾乎沒有表現出放棄這一職業的跡象。明馬·夏爾巴說，儘管他們的背景正在發生變化，但年輕人仍然被薪酬和工作本身所吸引。越來越多的夏爾巴家庭居住在加德滿都和其他低海拔城市。「我們過去習慣在高海拔地區玩耍，」他說。「現在雪巴人出生在城市裡。」
Drones, Transceivers and Lighter Equipment
無人機、收發器和更輕便的裝備
Dipan Gurung, 25, is part of the new generation of guides. He comes from the Makalu region and a family of farmers rather than the Khumbu communities traditionally associated with Himalayan guiding. He began as a trekking porter in 2019. Many young men from his region traditionally went into the army, he said; now growing numbers are seeking professional climbing and rescue training.
25歲的迪潘·古隆是新一代嚮導中的一員。他來自馬卡魯地區，出身於農民家庭，而非傳統上與喜馬拉雅嚮導相關聯的昆布社區。他說，他於2019年開始做徒步背夫。他所在地區的許多年輕人過去傳統上是去當兵；如今越來越多的人正在接受專業登山和救援訓練。
Mr. Gurung has watched glaciers melt into ponds. “We are facing a future of rockfalls and floods,” he said. Yet he does not believe that makes his career prohibitively dangerous. While the mountains are becoming less predictable, equipment and tools are more accurate and advanced. His generation has detailed weather forecasts, GPS devices, avalanche transceivers, lighter equipment and training in reading snowpacks. They also use drones, which provide information on conditions and carry supplies.
古隆目睹了冰川融化成池塘。「我們正面臨落石和洪水的未來，」他說。但他不認為這會使他的職業危險到無法再繼續。雖然山峰正變得愈發難以預測，但設備和工具更加精確和先進。他這一代人擁有詳細的天氣預報、GPS設備、雪崩收發器、更輕便的裝備以及解讀雪層的培訓。他們還使用無人機，既可提供路況信息，也可運送物資。
廣告
Some of those tools are already changing the way Everest is climbed, particularly for the Sherpas and other Nepali high-altitude workers who have long borne much of the danger of commercial expeditions. While foreign clients may cross the Khumbu Icefall a handful of times during an expedition, Nepali workers traditionally make repeated trips through it, carrying tents, oxygen, food and equipment to higher camps. On April 18, 2014, that disparity in risk became starkly apparent when a mass of ice collapsed above the route, killing 16 Nepali climbing workers in the icefall.
其中一些工具已經在改變攀登珠穆朗瑪峰的方式，尤其是對於雪巴人和其他尼泊爾高海拔工作者而言，他們長期以來承擔了商業探險活動中的大部分風險。外國客戶在整個探險過程中可能只穿越昆布冰瀑幾次，而尼泊爾工人傳統上需要多次往返，將帳篷、氧氣、食物和設備運往更高處的營地。2014年4月18日，這種風險差距變得極為明顯——冰瀑路線上方的一大塊冰體坍塌，造成16名尼泊爾登山工人在冰瀑中喪生。
Drones now offer a way to reduce some of that exposure. “We are trying to reduce people going through the icefall by moving loads up by drones,” Mr. Gurung said, describing machines capable of carrying roughly 88 pounds. Mr. Watson expects drones to increasingly scout for danger as well. “Drones will be a huge part of safety plans in the future,” he said.
無人機現在提供了一種降低這種風險的途徑。「我們正嘗試用無人機運送物資，以減少人員穿越冰瀑的次數，」古隆說道，他提到的無人機能夠承載約40公斤的重量。沃森預計無人機將越來越多地用於危險偵察。「無人機將成為未來安全計劃的重要組成部分，」他說。
Helicopters offer another way around dangerous terrain, although Mr. Gurung and Mr. Watson are wary of treating them as a solution because high-altitude flying depends on visibility, wind and landing conditions.
直升機提供了繞過危險地形的另一種方式，不過古隆和沃森不太願意將其視為一種解決方案，因為高海拔飛行取決於能見度、風向和降落條件。
On Wednesday, while Mr. Purja’s family and friends gathered in England, Mr. Gurung was back at work in Nepal, helping coordinate rescue teams in the disaster zone. With roads severed and some areas accessible only by helicopter, his association was sending trained guides into steep terrain and confined spaces difficult for conventional rescue teams to reach.
週三，當普爾賈的家人和朋友聚集在英國時，古隆已經回到尼泊爾工作，協助協調災區的救援隊伍。由於道路中斷，部分地區只能通過直升機進入，他所在的協會正派遣訓練有素的嚮導進入傳統救援隊難以抵達的陡峭地形和狹小空間。
Soon, if conditions allow, climbers and trekkers will return to the mountains. The autumn trekking season is approaching. Expeditions are booked, and guides, porters, teahouses and lodges are depending on them.
不久之後，如果條件允許，登山者和徒步者將重返山區。秋季徒步季即將來臨。人們在預訂探險活動，嚮導、背夫、茶館和旅店的生存有賴於此。
For Mr. Watson, perhaps the starkest evidence of how much those mountains have changed is at the very top of the world.
對沃森而言，這些山峰發生了多大變化，最明顯的證據或許就在世界之巔。
廣告
“If the summit of Everest has that much less ice,” Mr. Watson said, “then there is no place on Earth that is untouched.”
「如果珠穆朗瑪峰的頂峰都少了那麼多冰，」沃森說，「那麼地球上就沒有一處地方是能躲得過的了。」
