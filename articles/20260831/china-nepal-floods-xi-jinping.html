中國慰問鄰國但引導敘事，強調災害始於尼泊爾

China Offers Condolences to Nepal While Emphasizing Disaster Began There

郭莉莉 • 2026-08-31
China’s leader, Xi Jinping, extended his condolences to Nepal’s president on Friday over the hundreds of deaths caused by the landslide on the countries’ border and pledged China’s support in Nepal’s aid efforts.
週五，中國領導人習近平就中尼邊境土石流災害致數百人死亡一事，向尼泊爾總統致慰問電，並承諾中方將支持尼泊爾的救援工作。
“I am confident that the people of both countries will be able to join hands to overcome the disaster and rebuild their homes,” Mr. Xi said in a message to Nepal’s president, Ram Chandra Paudel, reported by Chinese state media. The message, his first addressed to Nepal’s government since the disaster on Wednesday, noted that the countries are geographically connected and “share a common destiny.”
據中國官方媒體報導，習近平在致尼泊爾總統拉姆·錢德拉·鮑德爾的慰問電中說：「相信兩國人民一定能攜手早日戰勝災害，重建家園。」這是自週三災害發生以來，他首次向尼泊爾政府致電。他在慰問電中指出，兩國山水相連，「命運與共」。
At the same time, Chinese officials have tried subtly to emphasize that the origins of the disaster were across the border.
與此同時，中國官員也在委婉地強調，這場災害的源頭位於邊境另一側。
On Thursday, China’s Ministry of Natural Resources released details of what it called the “basic disaster chain,” saying that the flood was caused by a high-altitude avalanche in Nepal that flowed into China along the Gyirong Tsangpo River and leveled a border crossing, before twisting back into Nepal, wiping out villages there. Almost all of China’s official statements and state media reports have used similar language.
週四，中國自然資源部發布了所謂「基本的災害鏈」過程，稱此次災害由尼泊爾境內高位冰川崩塌引發，雪崩沿吉隆藏布河湧入中國境內，沖毀了一處邊境口岸，之後又折回尼泊爾，摧毀了那裡的村莊。中國幾乎所有的官方聲明和官方媒體報導都採用了類似的措辭。
廣告
Satellite imagery analyzed by The New York Times suggests that the landslide was likely caused by a glacier collapsing on the Langtang Lirung, a mountain in Nepal.
《紐約時報》對衛星圖像進行分析後認為，這場土石流災害很可能是由尼泊爾境內朗唐裡隆峰的一處冰川崩塌引發的。
The official Chinese statements came as comments circulated on social media that Beijing bore responsibility for the disaster and the heavy numbers of dead and missing. Critics have accused China, without evidence, of not issuing an early warning after the avalanche occurred or of destabilizing the ground in the Himalayas by constructing hydropower dams in Tibet.
中國官方發表上述聲明之際，社群媒體上流傳著一些說法，認為北京應對此次災害及其造成的重大人員傷亡和失蹤負責。批評者在沒有證據的情況下指責中國在冰川崩塌發生後沒有及時發布預警，或者認為中國在西藏修建水電大壩，導致喜馬拉雅地區的地質環境變得不穩定，從而釀成災難。
In a Facebook post on Thursday, the Chinese Embassy in Nepal called those theories “fake news.” China’s ambassador to Kathmandu, Zhang Maoming, went on Nepali state television on Wednesday to emphasize that preliminary evidence showed the disaster had started on the Nepalese side of the border.
中國駐尼泊爾大使館週四在Facebook上稱這些說法為「假新聞」。中國駐尼泊爾大使張茂明週三還接受尼泊爾國家電視台採訪，強調初步證據顯示，災難是從邊境尼泊爾一側開始的。
“We stand strong with Nepal at this difficult time,” he said.
他說：「在這個艱難時刻，我們堅定地與尼泊爾站在一起。」
Beijing’s efforts to shape the narrative are in line with its focus on controlling information on the crisis.
北京方面引導災害敘事的努力與其一直以來對危機相關信息進行管控的做法相符。
China has confirmed that five people have died in Tibet and that 558 others are missing since the deluge, but has released scant detail. Independent journalists are barred from Tibet. Chinese social media platforms have censored content that is not state-sanctioned, including videos taken by bystanders and drone photos of the collapsed glacier taken by hikers.
中國已確認西藏有五人遇難，另有558人失聯，但公布的細節極少。獨立記者被禁止進入西藏。中國的社群媒體平台對未經官方認可的內容進行了審查，包括目擊者拍攝的影片和徒步者拍攝的冰川崩塌無人機照片。

郭莉莉(Lily Kuo)是《紐約時報》報導中國記者，常駐台北。
