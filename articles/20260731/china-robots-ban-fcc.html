美國擬禁止進口中國人形機器人

China Threatens Retaliation as U.S. Moves to Block Robot Imports

STEVE LOHR, XINYUN WU • 2026-07-31
Robot trade tensions escalated on Thursday as China angrily denounced a proposal by the United States to ban imports of Chinese-made humanoid robots, calling the measure discriminatory and threatening retaliation.
週四，機器人領域的貿易緊張局勢升級。中國憤怒譴責了美國一項旨在禁止進口中國製造的人形機器人的提議，稱該措施具有歧視性，並威脅將予以報復。
In a sharply worded statement, China’s commerce ministry accused the Trump administration of launching a new provocation that would “severely undermine the stability of China-U.S. economic and trade relations, and seriously disrupt the stability of global industrial and supply chains.”
在一份措辭嚴厲的聲明中，中國商務部指責川普政府發起了新的挑釁，稱這將「嚴重破壞中美經貿穩定局面，嚴重擾亂全球產業鏈供應鏈穩定」。
On Tuesday, the U.S. Federal Communications Commission said that it intends to restrict imports of humanoid and animallike robots on national security grounds, while promoting the development of advanced robots domestically.
週二，美國聯邦通信委員會表示，打算以國家安全為由限制人形及動物形態機器人的進口，同時推動美國國內先進機器人的發展。
Although the F.C.C. did not explicitly identify China, Chinese producers dominate the global humanoid robot industry. China’s commerce ministry said the proposal was unmistakably aimed at the country’s manufacturers, describing it as “discriminatory and suppressive against Chinese companies and products.”
儘管聯邦通信委員會沒有明確點名中國，但中國生產商主導著全球人形機器人產業。中國商務部表示，該提議無疑是針對中國製造商的，並將其描述為「歧視性對待和打壓中國企業和產品」。
廣告
The proposal thrusts the emerging robotics industry into the latest front in the technology rivalry between the United States and China, which have already imposed tit-for-tat restrictions on access to artificial intelligence systems, advanced semiconductors and other critical technologies.
這一提議將新興的機器人產業推向了中美技術競爭的最新前線。此前，兩國已在人工智慧系統、先進半導體及其他關鍵技術領域實施了針鋒相對的限制。
China’s statement warned that if Washington proceeds with the ban, “China will resolutely retaliate to safeguard its legitimate rights and interests.” It did not specify what measures it might take.
中國的聲明警告稱，如果華盛頓推進這項禁令，「中方將堅決予以反制，維護自身正當權益。」但聲明並未具體說明可能採取的措施。
The Trump administration argues that increasingly capable humanoid robots pose national security risks on two fronts.
川普政府認為，能力日益強大的人形機器人在兩個方面構成了國家安全風險。
First, officials contend the machines could function as Trojan horses for data collection, harvesting personal information and even sensitive government data as they become more integrated into homes, workplaces and public spaces. Second, the administration argues that advanced robotics will become a strategically important technology in an economy increasingly dependent on A.I..
首先，官員們認為，隨著這些機器越來越深地融入家庭、工作場所以及公共空間，它們可能會充當數據收集的「特洛伊木馬」，搜集個人信息甚至敏感的政府數據。其次，政府認為，在日益依賴人工智慧的經濟體中，先進機器人將成為一項具有戰略重要性的技術。
China dismissed those concerns, accusing the United States of “overstretching the concept of national security.”
中國對這些擔憂予以駁斥，指責美國「泛化國家安全概念」。
The F.C.C. has previously invoked national security concerns to prohibit imports of telecommunications equipment from Chinese companies such as Huawei and ZTE.
聯邦通信委員會此前曾多次以國家安全擔憂為由禁止進口華為和中興等中國企業的電信設備。
Whether restrictions on Chinese robots would be effective is an open question.
對中國機器人的限制是否會有效仍是一個懸而未決的問題。
Chinese start-ups are producing thousands of humanoid robots priced below $5,000, far cheaper and at a greater scale than competitors elsewhere. While Chinese manufacturers once depended on Japanese and other foreign suppliers for critical components such as sensors and joints, those parts are now overwhelmingly produced in China.
中國的初創企業正在生產成千上萬台售價低於5000美元的人形機器人，其價格遠低於其他地區的競爭對手，生產規模也大得多。儘管中國製造商曾經在傳感器和關節等關鍵零部件上依賴日本及其他外國供應商，但如今這些部件已絕大部分在中國國內生產。
廣告
As a result, it has become nearly impossible to build a competitive humanoid robot without Chinese-made components.
因此，如果沒有中國製造的零部件，幾乎不可能製造出具有競爭力的人形機器人。
China has also established a significant lead in industrial robotics. It manufactures and installs factory robots at a pace unmatched by any other country. More than two million robots were operating in Chinese factories in 2024, with another 300,000 installed during the year — more than the rest of the world combined.
中國在工業機器人領域也確立了顯著的領先優勢。其製造和安裝工廠機器人的速度是其他任何國家都無法比擬的。2024年，中國工廠裡有超過200萬台機器人在運轉，當年新增安裝量達30萬台——這一數字超過了全球其他地區的總和。
