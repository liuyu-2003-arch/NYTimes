中國人工智慧模型日漸強大，可能對中共構成威脅

As China’s A.I. Gets Stronger, It Poses New Risks to Beijing

DAVID PIERSON, BERRY WANG • 2026-07-31
China has cast itself as a champion of low-cost, open-source artificial intelligence technology that it says should be made widely available to the world. It has accused the United States of “A.I. hegemonism” as the Trump administration has debated regulating Chinese open-source A.I. models.
中國將自己塑造成低成本、開源人工智慧技術的倡導者，主張應讓這項技術廣泛惠及世界。面對川普政府討論是否對中國開源人工智慧模型實施監管，中國指責美國推行「人工智慧霸權主義」。
Unlike closed systems such as ChatGPT and Claude, open models can be downloaded, modified and run by anyone, including with safeguards removed. The open approach, which is lower in cost, has won Chinese A.I. systems, made by companies like Alibaba and Moonshot, a start-up, millions of global converts, including Silicon Valley companies like Airbnb and DoorDash.
與ChatGPT、Claude等封閉系統不同，開源模型可以被任何人下載、修改和運行，甚至可以移除其中的安全防護措施。這種成本更低的開放模式使阿里巴巴、月之暗面等中國企業開發的人工智慧系統在全球贏得了數以百萬計的用戶，其中包括Airbnb和DoorDash等矽谷公司。
But the same openness that has helped Chinese models win influence abroad now raises concerns for Beijing, particularly about security and the potential threats the technology might pose to the Communist Party’s rule. In a speech earlier this month, China’s top leader, Xi Jinping, said that even as China supported openness in A.I., the government needed to “constantly refine measures to forestall loss of control.”
然而，正是這種幫助中國模型在海外擴大影響力的開放性如今也讓北京感到擔憂，尤其是在安全層面，以及這項技術對共產黨執政可能構成的潛在威脅。本月早些時候，中國最高領導人習近平在一次講話中表示，儘管中國支持人工智慧開放合作，但防範失控的措施要及時完善。
The party is concerned that the technology could be used by hackers, scammers, terrorists or other bad actors seeking to cause harm in China; that models could circumvent its tight censorship filters; and that the government could be blamed if Chinese systems spin out of control elsewhere.
中共擔心該技術可能被駭客、詐騙分子、恐怖分子或其他試圖在中國製造危害的惡意行為者利用，擔心模型可能繞過其嚴密的審查過濾機制，還擔心如果中國開發的人工智慧系統在海外失控，中國政府可能因此受到指責。
The question of how much, if any, control Beijing or Chinese companies should exert over who gets to use and modify Chinese models has become more acute with the emergence of powerful homegrown A.I. systems. One is Kimi K3, released by Moonshot to the public earlier this week, which performs some tasks as well as the best models from American rivals, including OpenAI and Anthropic. Models like these could help companies write code and improve cybersecurity, but could also help hackers find vulnerabilities and exploit them more quickly.
隨著強大的國產人工智慧系統的出現，北京或中國企業應在多大程度上控制誰可以使用和修改中國模型這一問題已變得更加緊迫。其中一個例子是月之暗面本週早些時候發布的Kimi K3，它在某些任務上的表現可與OpenAI和Anthropic等美國競爭對手的頂尖模型相媲美。這類模型可以幫助企業編寫代碼、提升網路安全，但也可能幫助駭客更快地發現和利用漏洞。
“The Chinese Communist Party is a security-first institution, especially under Xi,” said Matt Sheehan, a senior fellow at the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace.
「中共是一個把安全放在首位的體制，尤其是在習近平領導下，」卡內基國際和平基金會高級研究員馬特·希恩表示。
He said Beijing was concerned that advanced models could carry out cyberattacks or evade safeguards, or make it easier to design or create harmful new viruses or other biological agents.
他說，北京擔憂先進模型可能實施網路攻擊、繞過安全防護，或者使設計和製造危險新病毒及其他生物製劑變得更加容易。

“If these models do reach those dangerous capabilities, they are not going to let it be a free-for-all in terms of releasing them,” Mr. Sheehan said.
「如果這些模型真的具備了那種危險能力，他們絕不會任由其毫無限制地向公眾開放，」希恩說。
Anthropic and OpenAI say that some A.I. models are too dangerous to be developed in the open and must be tightly controlled, and have raised concerns in Washington about Chinese models. Chinese and other commentators, however, have noted that some of the most high-profile A.I. safety breaches have involved closed-source American models.
Anthropic和OpenAI表示，某些人工智慧模型過於危險，不宜公開開發，必須嚴加管控，它們也曾向華盛頓方面表達過對中國模型的擔憂。然而，中國及其他國家的評論人士指出，一些最引人注目的人工智慧安全漏洞恰恰涉及美國閉源模型。
Fears About A.I. Challenging Beijing’s Grip
對人工智慧挑戰北京掌控力的擔憂
China’s anxiety over A.I. was on display at a recent cybersecurity conference in Beijing, where speakers warned about risks that included data breaches and deepfake images. Zhou Hongyi, an influential Chinese tech executive, cautioned the audience that China was more vulnerable than ever to cyberattacks because of the advent of breakthrough models like Mythos, an advanced Anthropic system designed to find software flaws.
在北京近期舉行的一次網路安全會議上，可以清楚地感受到中國對人工智慧的焦慮，與會者就數據洩漏、深度偽造圖像等風險發出警告。中國知名的科技高管周鴻禕提醒聽眾，由於突破性模型的出現，例如Anthropic為尋找軟體缺陷而設計的高級系統Mythos，中國比以往任何時候都更容易受到網路攻擊。
The conference also discussed A.I. data poisoning, in which an attacker feeds A.I. chatbots with manipulated data that skews results. In Beijing’s telling, such tactics could be used to make A.I. systems generate politically subversive responses.
會議還討論了人工智慧數據投毒問題，即攻擊者向人工智慧聊天機器人投餵被操縱的數據以扭曲結果。按照北京方面的說法，此類手段可能被用來讓人工智慧系統生成具有政治顛覆性的回應。
China’s Ministry of State Security in April warned about data poisoning as a threat to “political and ideological security.” It said “hostile anti-China forces,” the party’s code for the West or for human rights activists who criticize Beijing, could exploit A.I. models by training them to smear the party and government.
今年4月，中國國家安全部警告稱，數據投毒「危害政治安全與意識型態安全」。該部表示，「反華敵對勢力」——這是中共對西方國家或批評北京的人權活動人士的慣用稱謂——可能訓練人工智慧模型來抹黑共產黨和政府。

That could include providing information about domestic protests or facts that undermine state narratives about the far western region of Xinjiang, where China has imposed a crackdown on Muslim ethnic groups, and Taiwan, the democratically-governed island that Beijing claims is its territory.
這可能包括提供關於國內抗議活動的信息，或傳播削弱官方關於新疆和台灣敘事的事實。新疆位於中國遙遠的西部地區，北京對這裡的穆斯林少數民族群體實施了嚴厲打壓；而台灣則是實行民主自治的島嶼，北京宣稱對其擁有主權。
The worst-case scenario for Beijing, when it comes to public opinion, is that “A.I. chatbots start saying things the party doesn’t want people to hear,” said Alex Colville, a cyber expert at the Australian Strategic Policy Institute.
澳洲戰略政策研究所網路專家亞歷克斯·科爾維爾表示，就輿論而言，北京最擔心的情況是「人工智慧聊天機器人開始說黨不想讓人們聽到的話」。
The concern extends to “any information that loosens their monopoly on dictating what is true and what is false,” he added.
他補充說，北京擔心的是「任何可能削弱其對是非真偽定義壟斷權的信息」。
What Controls Could Look Like
管控措施可能是什麼樣
Reuters and The Financial Times reported this month that China’s Ministry of Commerce had met with leading Chinese A.I. firms like Alibaba and ByteDance to discuss restricting overseas access to their top models. The ministry and companies did not respond to requests for comment.
據路透社和《金融時報》本月報導，中國商務部曾與阿里巴巴、字節跳動等國內主要人工智慧企業舉行會議，討論限制海外獲取其最先進模型的問題。商務部和相關企業均未回應置評請求。
Even the handling of Kimi K3 suggested that Chinese companies are taking things more slowly. The release on Monday of its weights — the numerical values that show how the software works — came a week after the model was launched. That is unlike other Chinese open-source models such as those developed by DeepSeek or Alibaba, which released their weights at the same time their models were unveiled.
就連Kimi K3的發布方式也表明中國企業正在放慢節奏。其模型權重——顯示軟體如何運行的數值——是在本週一，也就是模型發布一周後才公開的。這與DeepSeek、阿里巴巴等其他中國開源模型不同，後者通常會在發布模型的同時公布權重。
The Power of Giving it Away
舍即為得
China’s long-term goal, analysts say, is to set global A.I. standards to get ahead of the United States and make the world dependent on Chinese software, hardware and data systems, which are known collectively as a stack.
分析人士表示，中國的長期目標是通過制定全球人工智慧標準在競爭中搶在美國前面，並讓世界依賴中國的軟體、硬件以及數據系統——這些共同構成所謂的技術棧。
廣告
“This is a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity for China,” said Kendra Schaefer, a partner at Trivium China, a research and advisory firm. “China has spent the last 20 years trying to develop a tech stack that third-party countries would find as attractive or more attractive than U.S. origin technology and that has proven very, very difficult.”
「這對中國來說是一個千載難逢的機會,」策緯諮詢公司的合伙人凱娜(Kendra Schaefer)說。「過去20年來，中國一直在努力構建一套技術棧，希望能讓它國覺得比美國原創技術更具吸引力——至少不遜色。但事實證明，這非常、非常困難。」
Given those stakes, China will try to thread a needle if it decides to impose restrictions on foreign access to some A.I. systems, analysts said.
分析人士稱，鑒於這些利害關係，如果中國決定對某些人工智慧系統的海外訪問施加限制，它會努力在開放與管控之間尋求平衡。
That could mean limiting access to the most advanced A.I. systems for a time while leaving access to weaker models unchanged, not unlike what the Trump administration has done with Anthropic. That idea was put forward on Monday by Yuyuan Tantian, a blog linked to China’s state broadcaster.
這可能意味著在一段時間內限制對最先進人工智慧系統的訪問，同時保持對較弱模型的訪問不變——這與川普政府對Anthropic的做法並無不同。中央廣播電視總台的關聯部落格玉淵譚天在週一提出了這個設想。
“China supports openness, but this does not mean it advocates for the unconditional proliferation of all capabilities,” the blog said.
該帳號寫道：「中國支持開放，但不意味著主張所有能力無條件擴散。」
Beijing could also require exporters of Chinese A.I. to apply for licenses, much like how it controls exports of critical minerals and rare earth magnets, Ms. Schaefer said.
凱娜表示，北京也可能要求出口中國人工智慧技術的企業申請許可證，就像目前管控關鍵礦產和稀土磁體出口一樣。
That would be in line with signals from Beijing that A.I. capabilities are strategic national assets that it is unwilling to let flow to rivals in a superpower competition. In April, Chinese regulators blocked a $2 billion acquisition by Meta of Manus, a Singapore-based A.I. company that was founded in China, after a security review concluded that foreign investment in the company should be prohibited.
這也符合北京近年來釋放出的信號：人工智慧能力屬於戰略性國家資產，在大國競爭背景下，不會允許其毫無限制地流向競爭對手。今年4月，中國監管機構在安全審查後叫停了Meta對新加坡人工智慧公司Manus的20億美元收購案。該公司創立於中國，監管部門認定，不應允許外國資本投資這家公司。

How Risky is Openness?
開放性究竟有多大風險？
The prospect that China might impose controls on Chinese A.I. models comes as a debate has raged in Silicon Valley about whether open-source A.I. software is inherently risky.
中國可能對本國人工智慧模型實施管控之際，矽谷也一直在激烈爭論：開源人工智慧軟體是否存在固有風險。
Earlier this month, OpenAI said two of its A.I. models went rogue and successfully hacked into Hugging Face, a digital library of A.I. software that is popular among developers.
本月早些時候，OpenAI表示其兩個人工智慧模型出現失控行為，成功入侵了深受開發者歡迎的人工智慧開源平台Hugging Face。
Hugging Face said it had to deploy an open source model called GLM-5.2 made by a Chinese start-up, Z.ai, to help thwart the breach because American A.I. models carried restrictions that prevented them from taking action. GLM-5.2, which was released in June, is on the cutting edge of Chinese A.I. — it is nearly as powerful as Anthropic’s models Mythos and Fable.
Hugging Face表示，為了阻止這次攻擊，它不得不部署中國初創公司智譜AI開發的開源模型GLM-5.2，因為美國人工智慧模型自帶使用限制，無法執行相應操作。GLM-5.2於今年6月發布，代表了中國人工智慧的最前沿水平，其能力已接近Anthropic的Mythos和Fable模型。
“We’re all learning that secrecy is not the answer & that all defenders (not just a few selected ones) everywhere need more powerful models without restrictions, especially open ones!” wrote Clement Delangue, the chief executive of Hugging Face, on X shortly after the hack.
"我們都認識到，保密並非答案，所有地方的防禦者（而不只是少數幾個被選中的人）都需要更強大、不受限制的模型，尤其是開源模型！"Hugging Face首席執行官克萊門特·德朗格在駭客入侵事件後不久在X平台發帖寫道。
