中國經濟持續疲軟

China, Its Economy Stumbling, Signals Only Cautious Support

KEITH BRADSHER • 2026-07-31
Faced with weak consumer spending, persistently high youth unemployment and a tumbling stock market, China’s ruling Politburo signaled the need for stronger fiscal support and directed authorities to do more to tackle joblessness.
面對消費疲軟、青年失業率居高不下以及股市下跌的多重壓力，中共中央政治局釋放出將加大財政支持力度的信號，並要求有關部門採取更多措施應對就業問題。
In a broadly worded statement issued on Thursday after its annual midyear review of the economy, China’s Communist Party leadership urged the government to pursue “more proactive” tax and spending policies, and increase employment support.
週四，中共領導層在完成每年的年中經濟審議後發表措辭寬泛的會議介紹，呼籲政府推行「更加積極」的財政政策和貨幣政策，並加大就業扶持力度。
Politburo statements rarely detail specific policy measures, instead setting the broad direction for government action. Even by those standards, however, the statement offered few signs of urgency or a major policy shift, reinforcing expectations that Beijing will continue to rely on gradual stimulus rather than sweeping reforms.
政治局會議介紹通常不會詳述具體政策舉措，而是為政府下一階段的工作確定總體方向。然而，即便以這一標準衡量，這份會議介紹也幾乎未顯露出緊迫感或重大政策轉向的跡象，這進一步強化了市場預期——北京方面將繼續依賴漸進式刺激，而非大刀闊斧的改革。
The Politburo said it would “fully leverage the effectiveness of existing policies, promptly plan and introduce practical and effective incremental policies.”
政治局表示，將「在充分發揮各項存量政策效能的基礎上，及時謀劃出台務實管用的增量政策」。
廣告
China’s domestic economy has faltered and is being sustained largely by a record trade surplus and strong exports from the technology and clean energy sectors.
中國國內經濟持續低迷，目前主要依靠創紀錄的貿易順差以及科技和清潔能源行業強勁的出口支撐增長。
“The Politburo continues to rely on incremental steps to pull the economy along,” said Eswar Prasad, a Cornell University economics professor and former head of the China division at the International Monetary Fund. “But revving household consumption will require substantial direct financial assistance as well as confidence-building measures, perhaps coupled with some tax relief.”
「政治局繼續依賴漸進步驟來拉動經濟，」康乃爾大學經濟學教授、國際貨幣基金組織前中國部主任埃斯瓦爾·普拉薩德說。「但要真正激活居民消費，需要切實的直接財政支持和提振信心的舉措，也許還需輔以一定程度的稅收減免。」
Even many of China’s electric vehicle and solar panel manufacturers are losing money, while some tech companies are also beginning to encounter difficulties. The CSI 300 index of major Chinese companies has fallen 8.6 percent in July after months of strong gains, putting it on track for its worst monthly performance since January 2016. It remains up nearly 10 percent over the past year.
就連許多中國電動汽車和太陽能電池板製造商也處於虧損狀態，同時一些科技企業也開始遇到困難。涵蓋中國大型企業的滬深300指數在連續數月強勁上漲後，7月份已累計下跌8.6%，可能會創下自2016年1月以來最差的單月表現。不過，該指數在過去一年仍累計上漲近10%。
Unemployment among urban residents ages 16 to 25 has hovered around 15 percent. Youth joblessness has created particular social stress for families because almost everyone in this age bracket is an only child, born when China’s “one child” policy was most strictly enforced.
16至25歲城鎮居民的失業率一直徘徊在15%左右。青年失業問題給家庭帶來了尤為突出的社會壓力，因為這一年齡段的青年幾乎都是獨生子女——他們出生在中國「獨生子女」政策執行最為嚴格的時期。
Youth unemployment has remained stubbornly high even after Beijing changed its methodology in January 2024 to exclude many young people previously counted as unemployed.
即便北京在2024年1月調整了統計口徑，將此前納入統計的大量年輕人剔除在外後，青年失業率依然居高不下。
China’s National Bureau of Statistics has yet to release July data for investment, retail sales and industrial production, but June data were very weak, and private surveys point to further deterioration since then.
中國國家統計局尚未發布7月固定資產投資、社會消費品零售總額和工業生產數據，而6月數據已相當疲弱，民間調查顯示此後形勢進一步惡化。
With the exception of semiconductor and data-center equipment manufacturers, businesses across a wide range of industries are suffering from weak sales this summer, prompting cuts to overtime, hiring and borrowing.
除了半導體和數據中心設備製造商外，今年夏天，各行各業的企業普遍受到銷售疲軟影響，因此紛紛削減加班、招聘和借貸。
“Every key indicator from revenue and profits to sales prices and hiring weakened from a year ago,” said the China Beige Book, a New York-based data service, in a report released on Thursday based on a survey of 1,436 businesses conducted in the last two weeks.
「從營收、利潤到銷售價格和招聘，所有關鍵指標均較上年同期走弱，」總部位於紐約的數據服務機構「中國褐皮書」在週四發布的一份報告中表示，該報告基於過去兩週對1436家企業的調查。
Consumer spending remains weak, while much of the housing market is still in crisis. A five-year nationwide decline in apartment prices has wiped out much of the middle class’s savings in a country where households typically hold about three-quarters of their assets in real estate.
消費者支出依然低迷，樓市基本上仍深陷危機。持續五年的全國性房價下跌令中產階級的大量財富蒸發——在中國，家庭資產通常約有四分之三配置在房地產領域。
廣告
Economists inside and outside China have urged the government and the state-controlled banking system to devote fewer resources to industrial policies supporting exporters and high-tech industries, and instead provide more financial support to households, particularly lower-income rural families.
國內外經濟學家紛紛呼籲，政府和國有銀行系統應減少將資源向扶持出口企業和高科技產業的產業政策傾斜，轉而加大對居民家庭的財政支持力度，尤其是對農村低收入家庭。
In a speech last month, Liu Shijin, a former vice minister at the Development Research Center, an influential government think tank, said that China’s manufacturing sector is globally competitive, but that many industries face overcapacity while the needs of some people “remain unmet.” The East is Read, a Beijing newsletter, distributed a text of the speech this week.
上個月，有著相當影響力的政府智庫中國國務院發展研究中心的原副主任劉世錦在一次演講中表示，中國製造業具有全球競爭力，但許多行業存在產能過剩，而部分民眾的需求「仍未得到滿足」。北京的時事通訊The East is Read本週刊發了演講全文。
Chinese leaders have been reluctant to take more aggressive action as China’s debt, relative to the size of its economy, already exceeds that of the United States and most other countries. The Politburo statement offered no sign of a fundamental shift in policy toward supporting Chinese households.
中國的債務規模相對於經濟總量已超過美國及大多數國家，中國領導人對採取更大力度的行動一直保持審慎。政治局會議說明中也沒有任何跡象表明政策重點將發生根本性轉變，轉向支持居民家庭。
There have been tentative signs recently that housing prices are stabilizing in technology hubs such as Shanghai, Hangzhou and Shenzhen. But the housing market remains deeply weak across hundreds of other cities, particularly in the country’s interior, far from coastal export centers.
近期有初步跡象表明，上海、杭州和深圳等科技中心的房價正在企穩。但在全國數百座其他城市，房地產市場依然十分低迷，尤其是在遠離沿海出口中心的內陸地區。
According to official data, China’s economy grew 4.3 percent in the second quarter from a year earlier, the slowest pace of expansion Beijing has reported in three years. Many Western economists believe actual growth may have been considerably weaker.
官方數據顯示，中國經濟二季度同比增長4.3%，是北京三年來報告的最慢增速。許多西方經濟學家認為，實際增速可能遠低於這一數字。
Retail sales fell in May for the first time since China emerged from Covid-19 lockdowns in late 2022, before recovering modestly in June, according to the National Bureau of Statistics. But the government has stopped publishing many detailed data series that once helped analysts assess the reliability of its official statistics.
國家統計局數據顯示，自中國於2022年底解除新冠封控以來，社會消費品零售總額在今年5月首次出現下滑，6月雖略有回升，但幅度有限。不過，政府已經停止發布許多過去幫助分析人士評估官方統計數據可靠性的詳細數據。
廣告
The China Beige Book said its survey pointed to such broad weakness that official retail figures appeared to paint a stronger picture than conditions on the ground.
中國褐皮書表示，其調查顯示經濟疲軟範圍廣泛，官方零售數據所描繪的景象似乎比實際情況更為樂觀。
