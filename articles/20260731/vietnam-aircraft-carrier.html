美國航母停靠峴港，向中國展示實力

U.S. Aircraft Carrier Makes a Rare Port Call in Vietnam

DAMIEN CAVE • 2026-07-31
For the fourth time since the end of the Vietnam War, a United States aircraft carrier made a port call in Vietnam on Thursday, promoting joint security ties at a time when tariffs and the war in Iran have hurt American influence across Asia.
週四，一艘美國航空母艦在越南停靠，這是自越南戰爭結束以來的第四次。在關稅問題和伊朗戰爭損害了美國在亞洲影響力之際，此舉旨在促進兩國的聯合安全關係。
The carrier George Washington pulled into the quiet waters near Da Nang on day 70 of its deployment — not far from the beaches where U.S. Marines first landed in 1965, accelerating a war that killed around 58,000 Americans and 2 million Vietnamese.
「喬治·華盛頓」號航母在其部署的第70天駛入峴港附近平靜的水域——這裡距離1965年美國海軍陸戰隊首次登陸的海灘不遠，那次登陸加速了一場導致約5.8萬美國人和200萬越南人喪生的戰爭。
Both countries celebrated the 7-day port call, the longest stay in Vietnam for an American warship since the two countries restored relations in 1995, as a sign of how far the former enemies have come.
兩國都對這次為期七天的港口停靠表示歡迎。這是自1995年兩國恢復外交關係以來，美國軍艦在越南停留時間最長的一次，標誌著這兩個昔日宿敵的關係取得了很大進展。
“We consider this to be an extremely important visit for a variety of reasons,” said Capt. Nicholas DeLeo, the ship’s commander. He added that the 5,000 sailors making a stop in Da Nang, including a few Vietnamese Americans, were there “to build on this great relationship with the Vietnamese people.”
「出於多方面的原因，我們認為這是一次極其重要的訪問，」該艦艦長尼古拉斯·德萊奧說。他還說，在峴港停留的5000名水兵（包括一些越裔美國人）來到這裡是「為了進一步鞏固與越南人民的良好關係」。

Why Now?
為什麼是現在？
Vietnam has made itself an especially popular naval stopover for many countries as its foreign policy has shifted toward multi-alignment — seeking bonds beyond the superpower binary of the United States and China.
隨著越南外交政策轉向多邊結盟，尋求超越美國和中國兩大超級大國二元對立的紐帶，越南已成為許多國家特別青睞的海軍停靠站。
So far this year, Vietnam has also hosted naval vessels from Russia, China, India, Indonesia, Australia and Japan.
今年迄今為止，越南還接待了來自俄羅斯、中國、印度、印度尼西亞、澳洲和日本的海軍艦艇。
For the United States, Vietnam occupies a strategic position, geographically close to China and geopolitically important for any effort to keep Beijing from dramatically expanding its control over Asia’s disputed islands and vital commercial waterways.
對美國而言，越南佔據著戰略要地，它在地理上靠近中國，且在地緣政治上至關重要——若要阻止北京大幅擴張對亞洲爭議島嶼及重要商業水道的控制，越南是不可或缺的一環。
American officials view the port visit as a way to signal reassurance to Vietnam, and strength to China.
美國官員將此次港口訪問視為向越南發出安撫信號、並向中國展示實力的一種方式。
The George Washington, a nuclear-powered aircraft carrier, is a hard steel embodiment of an idea Adm. Samuel J. Paparo, the U.S. Indo-Pacific Commander, often emphasizes: Presence produces deterrence.
「喬治·華盛頓」號是一艘核動力航空母艦，它是美國印太司令部司令塞繆爾·帕帕羅海軍上將經常強調的「存在即威懾」理念的鋼鐵化身。
“Credible, prompt, and sustained combat power, visible across the Indo-Pacific region, will deter acts of military aggression that destabilize the region,” he told Congress in April.
「在整個印太地區展現可信、迅速且持久的戰鬥力，將遏制破壞該地區穩定的軍事侵略行為，」他於4月在國會發表講話時表示。
How Well do Vietnam and the U.S. Get Along?
越南與美國的關係究竟如何？
The United States and Vietnam are not formal allies and they differ in how strong they want their message of partnership to be.
美國和越南並非正式盟友，且雙方對於應傳達出多強的夥伴關係信號存在分歧。
廣告
American officials are often effusive about the potential for security collaboration with Vietnam. In contrast, Vietnam’s propaganda commission recently issued guidance to local media outlets covering the port visit, warning them not to make too much of the carrier’s arrival.
美國官員經常對與越南開展安全合作的前景表現出很大熱情。相比之下，越南的宣傳部門最近向報導此次港口訪問的地方媒體發布了指導意見，警告他們不要過度炒作這艘航母的到來。
“Emphasize that this is a routine friendship and exchange activity; do not glorify U.S. power,” it said.
指導意見稱：「要強調這是一次例行的友好交流活動；不要美化美國的實力。」
Nguyen Khac Giang, a visiting fellow at the ISEAS-Yusof Ishak Institute in Singapore, said Vietnam is seeking a careful balance.
新加坡尤索夫·伊薩東南亞研究院客座研究員阮克江（音）表示，越南正在尋求一種謹慎的平衡。
The country’s leaders want to show China that they have options if stronger partners are needed in the waters of the South China Sea that both countries claim. “But Hanoi doesn’t want to send the wrong signal to Beijing that it is becoming a part of U.S. containment or denial strategy against China,” Mr. Giang said.
該國領導人希望向中國表明，如果在兩國都聲稱擁有主權的南海水域需要更強大的合作夥伴，他們還有其他選擇。「但河內不想向北京發出錯誤的信號，讓北京以為它正在成為美國對華遏制或阻斷戰略的一部分，」阮克江說。
The bonds between Hanoi and Washington have also been undermined by conflicts over trade.
河內與華盛頓之間的關係也因貿易衝突而受損。
Vietnam exports far more to the United States than it imports, and the gap keeps widening. The latest round of tariff negotiations has stalled without producing an agreement, according to officials close to the discussions, over the role of Chinese products made in or moving through Vietnam.
越南對美國的出口遠超其進口，而且這一差距還在不斷擴大。據接近談判的官員透露，由於在越南製造或過境越南的中國產品問題上存在分歧，最新一輪關稅談判已經陷入停滯，未能達成協議。
廣告
“The U.S. might think these are two separate issues, but I doubt the Vietnamese will agree,” Mr. Giang said. “Trade problems will not prevent defense cooperation, but will definitely set the limit of how far both can go.”
「美國可能認為這是兩個獨立的問題，但我懷疑越南人是否會同意，」阮克江說。「貿易問題不會阻止防務合作，但肯定會限制雙方合作能走多遠。」

Damien Cave領導時報在越南胡志明市的新分社，報導亞洲及全球世界範圍內的權力轉移。
