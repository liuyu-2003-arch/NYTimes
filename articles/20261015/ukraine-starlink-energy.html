星盾使用受限、俄方襲擊升級：烏克蘭「腹背受敵」

Ukraine’s Exposure Grows as Satellite Use Is Restricted and Russia Escalates

CONSTANT MÉHEUT • 2026-10-15
Nearly five years into its fight for national survival, Ukraine has entered a moment of intense vulnerability, under growing pressure not only from Russia but also now from the United States.
在為國家生存而戰近五年之後，烏克蘭陷入了一個異常脆弱的時刻，不僅來自俄羅斯的壓力越來越大，如今美國也在施壓。
A double blow came overnight on Wednesday.
週三夜間，烏克蘭接連遭受兩次打擊。
Ukraine said that its military, with no warning, had lost some critical access to a military version of Starlink, a pillar of its battlefield communications. Kyiv relies on the satellite internet network, known as Starshield, which is paid for by the Pentagon. That led Ukrainian officials and analysts to surmise that the restrictions had come from Washington, as President Trump is locked in his latest feud with Ukraine’s leadership.
烏克蘭表示，在沒有收到任何預警的情況下，烏軍突然失去了對軍事版星鏈系統部分關鍵功能的使用權限，而這是烏克蘭戰場通信的重要支柱。基輔依賴這個名為星盾的衛星互聯網網路，費用由五角大廈支付。烏克蘭官員和分析人士因此推測，這些限制來自華盛頓，因為川普總統正與烏克蘭領導層陷入新一輪爭執。
At the same time, Russia unleashed dozens of ballistic missiles in a broad attack on Ukraine’s energy infrastructure early Wednesday, cutting power to 600,000 people in the capital, Kyiv. It was a dark omen for a rapidly approaching winter in which Russia is gearing up to try to push Ukraine to the breaking point as Kyiv runs low on lifesaving air defenses.
與此同時，俄羅斯週三凌晨發動大規模襲擊，向烏克蘭能源基礎設施發射了數十枚彈道導彈，導致首都基輔60萬人斷電。隨著冬季迅速臨近，這無疑是一個不祥的預兆。俄羅斯正準備在冬季進一步施壓，試圖將烏克蘭逼至崩潰邊緣，而基輔用於救命的防空力量日益薄弱。
廣告
It was unclear how long the Starshield restrictions would remain in place. Still, the Trump administration’s treatment of Ukraine has caused outrage in a country already reeling from a string of challenges.
目前尚不清楚星盾的使用限制會持續多久。不過，川普政府對待烏克蘭的方式已經在這個接連遭受重重打擊的國家激起憤怒。
Its energy system is at risk of collapse in the coming months as Russia pounds power plants and substations, trying to deprive Ukrainians of heat and water. Its economy is being pushed to the brink by Russian strikes on its ports, warehouses and steel factories. And its budget faces a multibillion-dollar gap this year and next that could leave its military severely underfunded.
隨著俄羅斯不斷轟炸烏克蘭的發電廠和變電站，企圖切斷烏克蘭的供暖和供水，該國能源系統在未來幾個月有崩潰的風險。俄羅斯對烏克蘭港口、倉庫和鋼鐵廠的襲擊正將烏克蘭經濟推向崩潰邊緣。與此同時，烏克蘭今明兩年的預算面臨數十億美元的缺口，可能導致軍費嚴重不足。
The Starshield development was interpreted in Ukraine as part of a Trump administration pressure campaign to limit Ukraine’s ability to fight. Mr. Trump is pushing Kyiv to stop attacking oil refineries so that Russia can pump more diesel onto global markets as high prices threaten the prospects of Mr. Trump’s Republican Party in the midterm elections.
在烏克蘭，星盾系統遭到限制一事被解讀為川普政府施壓行動的一部分，目的是削弱烏克蘭的作戰能力。川普正敦促基輔停止襲擊俄羅斯煉油廠，以便俄羅斯向全球市場供應更多柴油。目前，高油價正威脅川普所屬共和黨在期中選舉中的前景。
“Clearly, this action cannot be a coincidence and is linked to the current political situation,” Serhiy Beskrestnov, a Ukrainian military expert who advises President Volodymyr Zelensky on weapons technologies, wrote on social media about the Starlink restrictions.
「顯然，這一行動不可能是巧合，而是與當前政治局勢有關，」為澤連斯基總統提供武器技術諮詢的烏克蘭軍事專家謝爾希·別斯克列斯特諾夫在社群媒體上評論星鏈限制措施時寫道。
SpaceX, which operates Starlink and Starshield, and its chief executive, Elon Musk, did not respond to requests for comment. The Pentagon did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
運營星鏈和星盾的SpaceX及其首席執行官伊隆·馬斯克均未回應置評請求。五角大廈也沒有立即回應置評請求。
Ukraine was stunned to discover the Starshield restrictions, which it said caused disruptions to several combat operations. The Ukrainian military uses Starshield in part to guide its ever-growing fleets of drones, including interceptors used to down Russian drones. The restrictions, Ukrainian officials said, tied Ukraine’s hands while leaving Russia free to wage war.
烏克蘭軍方震驚地發現，星盾系統突然受到限制，並表示這干擾了多項作戰行動。烏軍使用星盾系統的一個重要用途是為規模不斷擴大的無人機機隊提供導航，包括用於擊落俄羅斯無人機的攔截機。烏克蘭官員表示，這些限制束縛了烏克蘭的手腳，卻讓俄羅斯可以放手作戰。
廣告
Perhaps nowhere was that imbalance clearer than in the Russian air assault overnight. The barrage included 39 ballistic missiles, Mr. Zelensky said. That would be the largest such salvo during the entire war, according to a New York Times data set based on Ukrainian Air Force figures.
這種不對等局面在俄羅斯夜間發動的空襲中表現得尤為明顯。澤連斯基表示，這輪襲擊包括39枚彈道導彈。根據《紐約時報》依據烏克蘭空軍數據建立的數據集，這是整場戰爭期間規模最大的一次彈道導彈齊射。
Even as Russia pounds Ukraine day after day, leading to some of the highest civilian death tolls seen in years of war, Kyiv says it is abiding by Mr. Trump’s demand not to strike Russian oil refineries. Its campaign against refineries, which had led to fuel shortages across Russia, made Russians feel the war as never before.
儘管俄羅斯日復一日地猛烈轟炸烏克蘭，造成多年來最嚴重的平民傷亡，基輔表示，自己仍在遵守川普停止襲擊俄羅斯煉油廠的要求。此前，烏克蘭針對煉油廠的襲擊行動導致俄羅斯各地出現燃料短缺，讓俄羅斯民眾以前所未有的程度感受到戰爭的影響。
Kyiv has said it is unfair that Russia is still attacking Ukraine’s power plants and substations. Mr. Zelensky has asserted that the strikes violate Mr. Trump’s self-declared energy cease-fire in Ukraine. The Trump administration views the cease-fire as covering only strikes on oil refineries, even though Ukraine has none that are operational.
基輔表示，俄羅斯仍在襲擊烏克蘭的發電廠和變電站，這有失公平。澤連斯基堅稱，這些襲擊違反了川普自行宣布的烏克蘭能源停火安排。川普政府則認為，這項停火安排僅涵蓋針對煉油廠的襲擊，儘管烏克蘭目前並沒有仍在運營的煉油廠。
Ukraine has set aside its most potent tool against Russia in part because it is worried that Mr. Trump could follow through on an apparent threat to stop providing intelligence that Kyiv needs to warn its people of incoming Russian strikes.
烏克蘭擱置了它對付俄羅斯最有力的手段，部分原因是擔心川普可能兌現其停止提供情報的威脅。基輔需要這些情報，才能向民眾預警俄羅斯即將發動的襲擊。
While Ukraine’s European partners have so far stepped in to patch Ukraine’s ballooning budget gaps as Russia batters the Ukrainian economy, Europe is not believed to be able to fully fill intelligence deficits if the United States were to pull back.
在俄羅斯持續打擊烏克蘭經濟之際，烏克蘭的歐洲夥伴一直在幫助填補該國不斷擴大的預算缺口。但外界普遍認為，如果美國撤出，歐洲將無法完全彌補由此產生的缺口。
Ukrainian fears have been elevated over the past two days as Mr. Zelensky has reported that Ukraine did not receive early warnings about Russian missile strikes, though Mr. Trump said on Tuesday that intelligence was still being shared.
過去兩天，烏克蘭的擔憂進一步加劇。澤連斯基報告稱，烏方沒有收到俄羅斯導彈襲擊的預警，儘管川普週二表示，美國仍在與烏克蘭分享情報。
廣告
The Ukrainian Air Force clarified on Wednesday that while it had received no advance warning before a Russian missile struck at about 10:40 p.m. on Tuesday, it did receive warnings related to three subsequent waves of attacks that night. Such intelligence sharing is separate from Ukraine’s use of the Starshield system.
烏克蘭空軍週三澄清說，雖然週二晚間10時40分左右，一枚俄羅斯導彈來襲前，烏方沒有收到提前預警，但對於當晚隨後發生的三波襲擊，烏方確實收到了預警。這類情報共享與烏克蘭使用星盾系統是兩回事。
The Starshield limitations, if they continue, could hobble Ukraine’s military operations. Starshield terminals are used for nearly everything the Ukrainian Army deploys on the battlefield: the cars that carry troops and matériel, the drones that ferry food and water to soldiers at the front, and the attack drones that fly long distances to hit Russian positions.
如果星盾的使用限制持續下去，烏克蘭的軍事行動可能受到嚴重阻礙。從運送部隊和物資的車輛，到向前線士兵運送食物和飲水的無人機，再到遠程飛行打擊俄軍陣地的攻擊無人機，烏軍投入戰場的幾乎所有裝備都要用到星盾終端。
The Ukrainian military uses thousands of terminals connected to Starshield, a version of the Starlink network that is harder to hack or jam. Mr. Musk said earlier this year that Starshield was “operated by the U.S. government.”
烏克蘭軍方使用數以千計與星盾相連的終端。星盾是星鏈網路的一個版本，更難被駭客攻擊或干擾。馬斯克今年早些時候說過，星盾由「美國政府運營」。
Officials from Ukraine’s defense ministry said on Wednesday that, without warning, SpaceX had restricted the use of Starshield for devices traveling faster than 100 miles per hour. When the device exceeded this speed, the terminals lost connection. The officials said the ministry was in touch with the U.S. government, SpaceX and NATO partners to address the issue.
烏克蘭國防部官員週三表示，SpaceX在沒有事先發出任何通知的情況下，限制了星盾在移動速度超過每小時100英里（約160公里）設備上的使用。一旦設備超過這一速度，終端就會斷開連接。官員們說，國防部正在與美國政府、SpaceX和北約夥伴聯繫，以解決這個問題。
Ukrainian soldiers said missions had been disrupted as a result. Oleksandr, an officer in the 8th Air Assault Corps, said the restrictions had mainly affected heavy bomber and fixed-wing drones targeting Russian logistical routes and positions in occupied territory. He spoke on the condition that he be identified by only his first name, in keeping with Ukrainian military rules.
烏克蘭士兵表示，相關限制已經干擾了作戰任務。第八空中突擊軍軍官亞歷山大說，受影響的主要是重型轟炸無人機和固定翼無人機的作戰行動。這些無人機用於打擊俄羅斯在烏克蘭佔領區的後勤補給線和軍事陣地。出於烏克蘭軍方規定，他只允許透露名字。
Oleksandr said that if the restrictions remained in effect, Russia would be able to reopen crucial supply routes in a matter of days, and could better protect expensive equipment, including ships, from Ukrainian attacks.
亞歷山大表示，如果這些限制繼續實施，俄羅斯將在幾天內重新打通關鍵補給路線，並能更有效地保護包括艦船在內的昂貴裝備，免遭烏克蘭的襲擊。
廣告
Starshield also provides connections to the interceptor drones that Ukraine uses to shoot down the Russian attack drones known as Shaheds, especially the fast-flying jet-powered models that Russia is now launching toward Ukraine en masse, said Mr. Beskrestnov, the military-technology adviser to Mr. Zelensky.
澤連斯基的軍事技術顧問別斯克列斯特諾夫表示，星盾還為烏克蘭用於擊落俄羅斯「見證者」攻擊無人機的攔截無人機提供通信連接，特別是在應對俄羅斯如今大規模發射、飛行速度極快的噴氣動力型號時。
“In effect, our partners’ actions are depriving us of one of the few ways we have to defend the country against jet-powered Shaheds,” Mr. Beskrestnov said.
「實際上，我們的夥伴正在通過這些行動剝奪我們保衛國家、抵禦噴氣動力『見證者』無人機的為數不多的手段之一，」別斯克列斯特諾夫說。
