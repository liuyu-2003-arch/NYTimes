那些從川普內閣中「消失」的女性

The Disappearing Women of the Trump Administration

ERICA L. GREEN • 2026-10-15
The ouster of Kimberly Guilfoyle, who until this week was the U.S. ambassador to Greece, was surprising in its particulars. It was announced on live TV, during the first-ever briefing by the new White House press secretary.
金伯利·吉爾福伊爾本週之前還是美國駐希臘大使，她的解職在具體情節上令人意外：消息是在新任白宮新聞秘書的首次簡報會上通過電視直播宣布的。
But Ms. Guilfoyle’s forced resignation also was part of a larger pattern in President Trump’s second term: The prominent figures who have departed the administration, either voluntarily or involuntarily, overwhelmingly are women.
但吉爾福伊爾的被迫辭職也折射出川普第二個任期內一個總體規律：離開政府的知名人物，無論自願還是非自願，絕大多數是女性。
Ms. Guilfoyle was the latest high-ranking woman to leave this year. In the spring, four of Mr. Trump’s eight female cabinet-level officials resigned or were fired in just three months: Attorney General Pam Bondi; Lori Chavez-DeRemer, the labor secretary; Homeland Security Secretary Kristi L. Noem; and Tulsi Gabbard, the director of national intelligence.
吉爾福伊爾是今年離任的最新一位高級別女性。今年春天，川普八位內閣級女性官員中有四位在短短三個月內辭職或被解僱：司法部長帕姆·邦迪；勞工部長洛麗·查韋斯-德雷默；國土安全部長克里斯蒂·諾姆；以及國家情報總監圖爾西·加巴德。
All were replaced by men.
繼任者均為男性。
Ms. Guilfoyle was asked to resign after The Wall Street Journal reported that she had asked a donor to pay off her $100,000 American Express bill before her confirmation vote. Ms. Guilfoyle has denied the allegation. But on Tuesday, Mr. Trump said that while Ms. Guilfoyle “was doing a good job,” he “didn’t like what I read.”
《華爾街日報》報導，吉爾福伊爾曾在確認表決前要求一名捐贈者為她支付10萬美元的美國運通卡帳單，報導刊出後她被要求辭職。吉爾福伊爾否認了這一指控。週二川普表示，儘管她「工作幹得不錯」，但他「不喜歡我讀到的東西」。
He also downplayed the idea that he had any substantial connection with Ms. Guilfoyle, who is one of the president’s staunchest defenders and was at one point engaged to Donald Trump Jr., the president’s eldest son. “She wasn’t a member of my family,” he told reporters. “She, I guess, dated my son a long time ago.”
吉爾福伊爾是總統最堅定的捍衛者之一，曾與他的長子小唐納德·川普訂婚，但川普淡化了與她之間的任何實質性聯繫。「她不是我的家人，」他告訴記者，「我想，她很久以前和我兒子約會過。」
廣告
The women in Mr. Trump’s orbit do not seem any more inclined to controversy than the men; his male appointees have repeatedly drawn scrutiny and calls for their ouster.
川普身邊的女性似乎並不比男性更容易惹上爭議；他任命的男性同樣屢屢引起熱議，並被要求解職。
Kash Patel, the F.B.I. director, has been forced to confront questions about his conduct, including allegations of excessive drinking, assigning a SWAT team to protect his girlfriend and using a government plane for his personal use. (He has denied the allegations.)
聯邦調查局局長卡什·帕特爾不得不面對關於其行為的質疑，包括酗酒過度的指控、派遣一支特警隊保護其女友，以及將政府飛機用於私人用途。（他否認了這些指控。）
Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick surfaced in files released by the Justice Department as one of the visitors to the private island owned by Jeffrey Epstein, the sexual predator. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth disclosed secret war plans in a conversation on a private messaging app that inadvertently included Jeffrey Goldberg, the editor of The Atlantic.
司法部公布的文件中，商務部長霍華德·盧特尼克名列性犯罪者傑弗里·愛潑斯坦私人島嶼的到訪者之中；國防部長彼特·海格塞斯則在一個私人聊天群中洩漏了祕密作戰計劃，該群無意中拉入了《大西洋月刊》主編傑弗里·戈德堡。
Mr. Trump did punish one male appointee related to that text chain: Mike Waltz, the national security adviser, who had added the journalist to the chat. But Mr. Waltz had a soft landing. He was appointed to serve as the ambassador to the United Nations, a position that Mr. Trump had promised to his longtime female ally in Congress, Elise Stefanik.
川普確實處理過一名與聊天群相關的男性——把那名記者拉入聊天的國家安全顧問邁克·沃爾茨。但沃爾茨得以平穩著陸：他獲任常駐聯合國代表，而這一職務原本被川普許諾給了他在國會的長期女性盟友愛麗絲·斯特凡尼克。
Ms. Stefanik, a self-proclaimed “ultra MAGA” warrior, ended up politically adrift and ultimately quit Congress.
自稱「超MAGA」戰士的斯特凡尼克的政治生涯由此淪落，最終退出了國會。
Experts on presidential appointments said the single-gender exodus was highly unusual.
研究總統人事任命的專家表示，這種僅限一個性別的離職潮極不尋常。
廣告
“I’ve never seen a situation where all of the first set of cabinet departures have been of one gender — all women,” said Kathryn Dunn Tenpas, a nonresident fellow at the Brookings Institution who has tracked diversity in presidential administrations since the Reagan era.
「我從未見過首批內閣離職者全部是同一種性別——全是女性的情形，」布魯金斯學會非駐所研究員凱瑟琳·鄧恩·滕帕斯說；她從雷根時代起一直在追蹤總統行政部門中的多元化狀況。
Women in the Top Ranks
高層的女性
As Mr. Trump assembled his top brass shortly after clinching the 2024 election, several women were among the ranks. He appointed Susie Wiles as chief of staff, the first woman to hold the powerful, cabinet-level position. Karoline Leavitt was the youngest person to serve as White House press secretary before she left her job in August to raise her two young children. She was replaced by another woman, Katie Zacharia.
在川普贏得2024年大選後不久組建核心團隊時，多位女性位列其中。他任命蘇西·威爾斯為白宮幕僚長，使其成為首位執掌這一重要內閣級職位的女性。一度是史上最年輕白宮新聞秘書的卡羅琳·萊維特於8月離任撫養兩個年幼的孩子。接替她的是另一位女性凱蒂·扎卡裡亞。
In a statement for this story, a White House spokeswoman said that “President Trump has assembled the greatest administration in history, selecting each member of his incredible team based on one criterion: their ability to deliver for the American people.”
一位白宮女發言人在就本報導發表的聲明中說：「川普總統組建了歷史上最偉大的政府，傑出團隊的每位成員只依據一個標準進行選拔：他們能否為美國人民兌現成果。」
“Unlike the identity politics-obsessed Democrats, the president judges individuals on merit and qualifications, not immutable characteristics,” added the spokeswoman, Taylor Rogers.
「與痴迷於身份政治的民主黨人不同，總統評判個人依據的是功績與資質，而非不可改變的特徵，」發言人泰勒·羅傑斯補充道。
At a Women’s History Month celebration last year, Mr. Trump declared that he was “thrilled to say that we have more women in our cabinet than any Republican president in the history of our country.”
在去年的一場婦女歷史月慶祝活動上，川普宣稱：「我非常高興地宣布，我們內閣中的女性人數超過我國歷史上任何一位共和黨總統。」
廣告
That is no longer the case. There are now four women remaining in cabinet-level positions.
這一點已經改變。目前內閣級職位上僅剩四位女性。
In many instances, the female officials who departed appeared to have failed some kind of loyalty test to Mr. Trump.
在許多情況下，離任的女性官員似乎都未能通過川普的某種忠誠度測試。
Ms. Bondi came under fire for her handling of the release of the Epstein files, and for falling short of Mr. Trump’s demands for retribution against his enemies. Mr. Trump was also dissatisfied with her performance in congressional hearings and on television.
邦迪處理愛潑斯坦文件公布事宜的方式受到猛烈批評，並因未能滿足川普報復政敵的要求而遭受非議。川普對她在國會聽證會及電視上的表現同樣感到不滿。
Testimony before Congress also appeared to be a catalyst for Ms. Noem’s firing, particularly her statement that Mr. Trump had approved of tens of millions of dollars of government ads in which she was prominently featured. Mr. Trump denied that account.
在國會作證似乎也是諾姆被免職的導火線，特別是她聲稱川普曾批准數千萬美元政府廣告——她本人在廣告中大量出鏡。川普否認了這一說法。
Ms. Gabbard resigned to care for her ill husband. But her announcement came amid a strained relationship with the White House, and after she refused to testify to Congress that Iran had posed an imminent threat, as Mr. Trump had asserted to justify his war against the country. Mr. Trump said she was “softer on that” issue than he was.
加巴德因照顧患病的丈夫而辭職。但她宣布離職之時與白宮關係緊張；此前，川普曾聲稱伊朗構成迫在眉睫的威脅，以此證明其對伊開戰的合理性，而加巴德拒絕就此向國會作證。川普表示，她在這個問題上「比我溫和」。
Ms. Chavez-DeRemer resigned after becoming embroiled in scandal amid allegations that her agency was rife with toxicity and dysfunction, and that she had grossly abused her power by having an inappropriate relationship with a staffer and by using public funding for private purposes.
查韋斯-德雷默在捲入醜聞後辭職。此前有指控稱，她領導的機構充斥著惡劣氛圍與運轉失調，她還與一名工作人員保持不當關係並將公共資金挪作私用，嚴重濫用職權。
廣告
But even some of Mr. Trump’s fiercest critics, who were glad to see the female cabinet members go, called out what they saw as a glaring double standard.
但即便是川普的一些最激烈批評者——儘管他們樂見這些女性內閣成員離開——也指出了在他們看來十分刺眼的雙重標準。
“Is he out of women?” Representative Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, Democrat of New York, said after the departures of Ms. Bondi and Ms. Noem, when asked by a reporter whom the president should fire next. “Trump only seems to have the capability to fire female secretaries.”
「他手上沒有女性可用了嗎？」在邦迪和諾姆離任後，被記者問及總統接下來該解僱誰時，紐約州民主黨眾議員亞歷山德里婭·奧卡西奧-科爾特斯回答說。「川普似乎只具備解僱女性部長的能力。」
Representative Jasmine Crockett, a Texas Democrat who is a frequent target of the president’s mockery, wrote in a social media post that Mr. Hegseth should be the next to go. “I see a theme,” she wrote of Mr. Trump. “He will throw the incompetent women under the bus a lot faster than the incompetent men.”
經常成為總統嘲諷目標的得克薩斯州民主黨眾議員賈絲明·克羅克特在一篇社群媒體帖文中寫道，下一個走人的應該是海格塞斯。「我看出一個規律，」她這樣評價川普。「他拋棄不稱職女性的速度比拋棄不稱職男性快得多。」
In April, Representative Madeleine Dean, Democrat of Pennsylvania, grilled Mr. Lutnick about the double standard during a congressional hearing, pointing to revelations about his connection to the sexual predator Jeffrey Epstein.
今年4月，賓夕法尼亞州民主黨眾議員瑪德琳·迪安在一場國會聽證會上就這一雙重標準嚴厲質問盧特尼克，直指外界關於他與性犯罪者傑弗里·愛潑斯坦存在牽連的爆料。
“Is the president concerned about your continued relationship that you had with Mr. Epstein?” she asked.
「總統是否擔心你過去與愛潑斯坦先生持續保持的關係？」她問道。
“I am not going to discuss conversations I have with the president,” Mr. Lutnick replied.
「我不會討論我與總統之間的談話，」盧特尼克回答道。
廣告
“President Trump has just fired three cabinet secretaries — three women,” Ms. Dean responded. “If President Trump has even a shred of concern about accountability for Jeffrey Epstein’s enablers, he would fire you too.”
「川普總統剛解僱了三位內閣部長——三位女性，」迪安回應道。「如果川普總統對追究傑弗里·愛潑斯坦幫凶的責任哪怕存有一絲一毫的關切，他也應該把你解僱。」
May Mailman, director of the Independent Women’s Law Center and a former senior White House official in both of Mr. Trump’s administrations, said that there were plenty of powerful women serving in key roles.
獨立女性法律中心主任、曾在川普兩屆政府中任職的前白宮高級官員梅·梅爾曼表示，仍有許多有權勢的女性在擔任關鍵職務。
She pointed to Ms. Wiles as well as to appointees like Erica Schwartz, who was recently confirmed to lead the Centers for Disease Control, and Harmeet Dhillon, who leads the civil rights division at the Department of Justice.
她列舉了威爾斯，以及最近獲得參議院確認領導疾病控制與預防中心的埃麗卡·施瓦茨，和領導司法部民權司的哈米特·迪隆等被任命者。
“There’s no loneliness of the female conservative in the Trump administration,” Ms. Mailman said. “There’s only empowerment. You’re a necessary member of the team for who you are, not your sex.”
「女性保守派在川普政府中不會孤單，」梅爾曼說。「只有賦權。你之所以成為團隊中不可或缺的一員，是因為你是你，而不是因為你的性別。」

Erica L. Green是《紐約時報》白宮記者，報導川普總統及其政府新聞。
