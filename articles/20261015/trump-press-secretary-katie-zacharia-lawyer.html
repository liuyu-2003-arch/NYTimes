新任白宮新聞秘書在中國做過律師嗎？

Trump’s New Press Secretary: Not-Quite Attorney at Law

SHAWN McCREESH • 2026-10-15
Is President Trump’s new press secretary a lawyer — or did she just play one on TV?
川普總統的新任新聞秘書是一名真正的律師，還是只是在電視上演過律師？
Three days into her job, Katie Zacharia is facing questions about whether she has embroidered her credentials. She describes herself as a lawyer, and the president called her “an attorney.” When she was asked on Tuesday in which state she passed the bar exam, she twice replied, “When I moved to China.”
上任僅三天，凱蒂·扎卡裡亞便面臨了履歷造假的質疑。她自稱是lawyer（律師），而總統則稱其為「an attorney」（一位執業律師）。週二，當被問及她在哪個州通過了律師資格考試時，她兩次回答稱：「我搬去中國的時候。」
Huh?
啊？
The moment capped off a shaky debut for the woman who has accepted what is perhaps the most challenging public relations post in the United States. Now she was struggling to tell her own story.
對這位接下了或許是全美最具挑戰性的公關職位的女性而言，這一幕為她波折不斷的首次亮相畫上了句點。如今，她正竭力把自己的經歷講圓。
廣告
On Wednesday evening, she told The New York Times that this whole mishegas over “lawyer,” “attorney,” and “China,” has been one big misunderstanding.
週三晚間，她對《紐約時報》表示，圍繞「lawyer」、「attorney」與「中國」的這場紛擾完全是一場大誤會。
“I assumed the question was, ‘Where were you after law school?’” she said, taking a quick phone call on the tarmac in Arizona, where the president was holding a midterm rally.
「我當時以為問題是：『你從法學院畢業後去了哪裡？』」她說。當時總統正在亞利桑那州舉行期中選舉集會，她是在停機坪上匆匆接通電話時作出這番回應的。
“That’s all I could hear over the sound of Marine One,” she said. “And, wanting to respect protocol, the distance behind the president, kept me from moving closer to hear the question.”
「在『海軍陸戰隊一號』的噪音下，我只能聽清這些，」她說.「而且出於遵守禮賓規程的考慮，我與總統保持著一定距離，這讓我無法靠前聽清問題。」
It was only her second day on the job, she pointed out.
她強調，那天只是她履新的第二天。
So here’s the truth about Katie Zacharia, not-quite attorney-at-law: She graduated with a law degree from Pepperdine University in 2011. That technically makes her a lawyer. But is she an attorney, someone who is licensed to practice law and give legal advice? For that, she would have needed to pass the bar. The Wall Street Journal reported Wednesday that she sat for the California bar exam after graduating law school, but didn’t pass. The Times was unable to find a law license for her in California, or anywhere else she has lived in the United States.
那麼，關於這位算不上真正的「attorney」的凱蒂·扎卡裡亞，事實究竟是怎樣的：她於2011年畢業於佩珀代因大學並獲法學學位。嚴格意義上講，這確實使她成了一名「lawyer」。但她是否是一名擁有執業許可、能夠提供法律諮詢的「attorney」？若想具備這一資格，她必須通過律師資格考試。《華爾街日報》週三報導稱，她在法學院畢業後曾參加加州律師資格考試，但並未通過。《紐約時報》也未能在加州或她在美居住過的任何其他地方查到其律師執業執照。
During the brief phone call Wednesday, The Times did not get a chance to press her again about whether she had ever passed a bar exam. But before she had to go, she did explain that when she first began appearing on cable news, she consulted with Pepperdine’s president as to how she should identify herself. She said she was warned not to use the words “attorney” or “esquire,” but that “lawyer” was OK.
在週三那通簡短的通話中，《紐約時報》未能再次追問她是否曾通過任何律師資格考試。但在掛斷前，她確實解釋稱，當初剛開始在有線電視新聞亮相時，她曾就該如何標明自己的身份諮詢過佩珀代因大學校長。她表示，當時對方告誡她不要使用「attorney」或「esquire」這類稱謂，但表示使用「lawyer」沒有問題。
廣告
But what about “general counsel,” would that be OK? Because, on LinkedIn, Ms. Zacharia identified herself as such for Fix California, a group led by Trump loyalist Richard Grenell.
但使用「總法律顧問」(general counsel)又是否合規呢？因為在領英上，扎卡裡亞在川普親信理查德·格雷內爾領導的團體「修復加州」名下，將自己的職務標註為總法律顧問。
“You can’t be a general counsel to a company in the United States unless you’re admitted to the bar to practice law,” said Erwin Chemerinsky, the dean of the law school at the University of California, Berkeley. “A general counsel implies that you’re practicing law, giving legal advice to the company. You can’t practice law without a license.”
「在美國，你要獲得律師資格並獲准執業，否則不得擔任企業的總法律顧問，」加州大學柏克萊分校法學院院長埃爾溫·切梅林斯基指出，「『總法律顧問』意味著你正在從事法律執業工作，正在為企業提供法律建議。沒有執照是不得從事法律執業的。」
On Wednesday, a watchdog group filed a complaint with the California State Bar Association, calling for an investigation into whether Ms. Zacharia “repeatedly held herself out to the public as entitled to practice law without a license to do so, conduct California law treats as a crime.”
週三，一家監督機構向加州律師公會遞交申訴，要求調查扎卡裡亞是否存在「在未獲執照的情況下，屢次向公眾聲稱自己有法律執業資質，而加州法律將這種行為視為犯罪」。
And that brings us to China.
這就牽涉到了中國之行。
The White House explained this week that Ms. Zacharia moved to China with her husband, Tyler, who had a job at a U.S. private equity firm. While there, she worked on “international transaction law,” Ms. Zacharia has claimed.
白宮本週解釋稱，扎卡裡亞是隨丈夫泰勒前往中國的，後者當時在一家美國私募股權機構任職。扎卡裡亞聲稱，在中國期間，她從事的是「國際交易法」業務。
On her LinkedIn profile, she listed nearly two years of employment at Zhong Lun Law Firm, which is one of China’s biggest law firms, with headquarters in Beijing and offices in Tokyo, New York, Los Angeles, San Francisco and Almaty, Kazakhstan.
在其領英個人主頁上，她列出了在中倫律師事務所近兩年的工作經歷。中倫是中國最大的律師事務所之一，總部設在北京，並在東京、紐約、洛杉磯、舊金山以及哈薩克阿拉木圖設有分所。
廣告
The law firm did not respond to a request for comment on Wednesday.
該律所週三未回應置評請求。
Opportunities for foreign lawyers in China are extremely narrow. Foreign nationals cannot sit for China’s bar exam, hold a Chinese lawyer’s license or advise on Chinese law. They can, however, be hired by Chinese law firms to work on cross-border deals and to advise on foreign or international law.
外國律師在中國的執業空間極為狹窄。外國公民不得參加中國的司法考試、不得持有中國律師執業執照，也不得就中國法律提供諮詢意見。不過，他們可受聘於中國律所參與跨境交易，並就外國法或國際法提供法律意見。
Gary Gao, a partner at Zhong Lun who is based in Shanghai, said the firm often hired foreigners for language assistance and to help Chinese staff understand foreign business culture.
中倫律師事務所常駐上海的合伙人高俊表示，律所經常聘用外籍人員協助語言溝通，並幫助中國員工理解外國商業文化。
“They are not practicing Chinese law at any time,” he said, adding that Ms. Zacharia’s role at the firm would have likely been similar to a paid internship.
「他們在任何時候都不具備中國法律執業資格，」他表示，並補充稱扎卡裡亞在律所的崗位很可能類似於帶薪實習。
Sida Liu, a law professor at the University of Hong Kong who specializes in mainland law, said that it is misleading for Ms. Zacharia to describe herself as a former associate at Zhong Lun, because it implies that she was a licensed lawyer in China.
專門研究內地法律的香港大學法學教授劉思達指出，扎卡裡亞將自己稱為中倫律所前「associate」（律師）具有誤導性，因為這暗示了她曾是在中國的持證律師。
“Chinese law firms can hire foreign nationals to work in their mainland offices, but such individuals are not classified as P.R.C. lawyers because P.R.C. lawyer qualification requires P.R.C. citizenship,” Mr. Liu said, referring to the People’s Republic of China. “The title ‘associate’ may therefore be somewhat controversial.”
「中國律所可以聘用外籍人士在其內地辦公室工作，但這類人員不會被歸類為中國律師，因為取得中國律師執業資格必須具備中國國籍，」劉思達說，「因此，使用『associate』這一頭銜可能存在爭議。」
廣告
So what are we to make of all this?
那麼，這一切到底說明了什麼？
It was Mr. Trump who called Ms. Zacharia an “attorney,” and she certainly never corrected the record. She called herself “general counsel,” which was misleading at best.
川普稱扎卡裡亞為「attorney」，而她顯然從未對此進行澄清。她還自稱為「總法律顧問」，這至少是一種誤導。
Confucius wrote in The Analects that without the trust of the people, the state cannot stand. The same goes for a White House press secretary. It is a job that’s all about credibility. It’s pretty tough to succeed if the public (let alone the fourth estate) feels that it cannot trust what it is being told.
孔子在《論語》中寫道：「民無信不立。」對於白宮新聞秘書而言亦是如此。這是一份完全建立在公信力之上的工作。如果公眾（更不用說媒體界了）覺得所聽到的內容不可信，那就很難做好這項工作。
