Hyrox北京賽選手失禁完賽引發爭議，官方道歉退款

Hyrox Athletes Are Refunded After Competitor Soiled Herself

MADISON MALONE KIRCHER • 2026-09-17
Joanna Wietrzyk was about halfway to winning a Hyrox, the international fitness competition, in China over the weekend when she experienced something unpleasant but not that uncommon for high-endurance athletes: She soiled herself.
上週末在中國舉行的一場Hyrox國際健身比賽賽程過半，勝利在望的喬安娜·維特日克經歷了一件對高耐力運動員來說不愉快但並非罕見的事：她失禁了。
But this was an indoor event with shared equipment stations, so there were concerns competitors might get sick. Wietrzyk, an elite Hyrox athlete from Australia, had visible feces running down her legs as she did burpee broad jumps and weighted lunges at the National Speed Skating Oval in Beijing, alongside more than 100 fellow competitors — many of whom would need to pass through the same stations and use the same weights.
但這是一場在共享設備站進行的室內賽事，因此有人擔心參賽者可能染病。在這位來自澳洲的精英Hyrox運動員在北京國家速滑館做波比跳和負重弓步時，有可見的排泄物順著她的腿流下，身邊還有100多名參賽者，其中許多人隨後都需要經過相同的比賽區並使用同一批重物器材。
Videos of the incident at Hyrox, the hugely popular events that combine running with exercise stations, ricocheted online and caused an uproar. In reaction posts across social media, Hyrox athletes and people in the broader fitness community and beyond criticized the event organizers and the athlete, asking why she was not pulled out of the competition.
Hyrox是將跑步與體能訓練站結合的極受歡迎的活動，事件影片在網上迅速傳播並引發軒然大波。在社群媒體上，Hyrox運動員以及更廣泛的健身圈內外人士批評賽事組織者和該運動員，問她為何沒有退出比賽。
Now, Hyrox is refunding some competitors of the event after apologizing and updating its rules and procedures, , and the athlete has also apologized and retroactively withdrawn from the race to forfeit her points.
在道歉並更新規則和程序後，Hyrox現在決定向部分參賽者退款。該運動員也已致歉，並追溯性退賽，放棄其積分。
廣告
In an email sent to Beijing competitors on Wednesday and reviewed by The New York Times, Hyrox said it would provide refunds for the value of the event ticket for athletes who competed at certain times on the same day as Wietrzyk. The email also said that after the event the “on-site team completed the sanitization procedures and full turf replacement” and that Hyrox had updated its “emergency‑response protocol for biological‑contamination incidents.”
在《紐約時報》審閱的一封週三發給北京參賽者的郵件中，Hyrox表示將向在維特日克同一天某些時段參賽的運動員退還參賽費用。郵件還說，賽後「現場團隊第一時間完成消殺處理工作，並於當日完成賽道草皮更換工作」，Hyrox還更新了「生物污染應急處置預案」。
Wietrzyk, who did not respond to requests for comment, issued an apology on Instagram on Thursday.
維特日克雖未回應置評請求，但週四在Instagram上發表了道歉聲明。
“Looking back, I regret the decision to not have stepped off the track,” she wrote, adding: “I take responsibility for my decision to continue and am deeply sorry for the discomfort and disruption it caused.”
「回頭看來，我後悔當時沒有選擇退賽，」她在帖文中寫道，並稱：「我為自己堅持參賽的決定承擔責任，並對由此造成的困擾和干擾深表歉意。」
She added that she had “decided to retroactively withdraw from the race and forfeit the points” she had earned toward future competitions.
她還說，她已「決定追溯性退賽，並放棄積分」，這些積分是她此前為參加未來賽事而積累的。
Hyrox did not respond to requests for comment, and it was not clear how many people were refunded or for how much. Entrance fees were just over $100 for most athletes.
Hyrox未回應置評請求，也不清楚有多少人獲得退款、金額多少。多數運動員繳納了100美元出頭的入場費。
“It’s the least they could do,” Joe Ho, an athlete who competed in the Beijing Hyrox, said on Wednesday of the refunds.
「這是最起碼的了，」參加北京Hyrox的運動員喬·何（音）週三談及退款時說。
In an earlier phone conversation, he described the high costs of a Hyrox, including flights, hotels, training and time away from work.
在早前的電話交談中，他提到參加Hyrox比賽成本很高，包括機票、酒店、訓練和請假時間。
“We’re paying the money to be in a safe environment,” Ho said during that conversation. “We’re already paying money to suffer. We’re not going to Hyrox and then going, ‘That was great.’ To add additional contamination to that suffering is not something that we are paying for.”
「我們花錢是為了身處安全環境，」他說。「我們已經花錢去受罪。我們不會去Hyrox然後說『太棒了』。給那種痛苦再添污染，不是我們花錢要買的東西。」
He also described watching Wietrzyk compete from the stands.
他還描述了從看台上觀看維特日克比賽的情景。
“Everyone could physically see that there was feces on her leg, but no one said anything,” he said in a phone interview from Taipei, describing watching the lunges part of the event. “It was just, like, business as normal.”
「每個人都能親眼看到她的腿上有糞便，但沒人說什麼，」他在台北接受電話採訪時說，描述觀看弓步環節的情景。「感覺就像，什麼都沒發生一樣。」
廣告
He added, “People were behind her and next to her, and no one warned people to step aside.”
他補充說，「人們在她後面和旁邊，卻沒人提醒大家讓開。」
He added that he did not want the athlete to be “bullied” or “humiliated,” but criticized the Hyrox organizers.
他補充說，他不希望該運動員被「霸凌」或「羞辱」，但批評了Hyrox組織者。
Before the refund announcement, Hyrox had issued an apology on social media on Monday. Hyrox “made a mistake by not reacting immediately during the race,” wrote Moritz Fürste, one of sport’s founders. Fürste added that Hyrox would be reviewing competition processes as a result of the outcry.
在退款公告前，Hyrox已於週一在社群媒體上道歉。該運動創始人之一莫裡茨·菲爾斯特寫道，Hyrox「犯了錯誤，沒有在比賽中立即反應」。菲爾斯特補充說，由於輿論嘩然，Hyrox將審查比賽流程。
The popularity of Hyrox, which was founded in 2017 in Hamburg, Germany, has skyrocketed in recent years; more than 1.5 million people in 30 countries have competed in at least one Hyrox race and versions of the part-running, part-strength exercise workouts are available in affiliated gyms for those looking to try their hand in a lower-pressure environment. Competitions involve shared equipment, like rowing machines and weighted sleds, and contestants are in close quarters. There are often strict rules: no spitting, no pouring water over one’s head.
Hyrox於2017年在德國漢堡創立，近年來人氣飆升；30個國家的150多萬人至少參加過一場Hyrox比賽，其旗下健身房還提供部分跑步、部分力量訓練，供人在壓力較小的環境下嘗試。比賽使用共享設備，如划船機和負重雪橇，參賽者距離很近。通常有嚴格規則：不準吐痰，不準往頭上倒水。
That rise has also coincided with the fact that social media has become omnipresent, and Ryan Gibney, a coach in Liverpool, England, who works with elite Hyrox athletes, said that might have played a role in the global reaction to Saturday’s incident.
Hyrox的這種增長恰逢社群媒體正變得無處不在，與精英Hyrox運動員合作的英格蘭利物浦教練萊恩·吉布尼說，這可能對週六事件的全球反應起了作用。
“Something that happens at a race can be filmed, clipped and distributed around the world within seconds,” Gibney said in an email. “Social media also strips away a lot of context. People are watching a short piece of footage and judging an athlete’s decision without necessarily understanding what that athlete is experiencing physically and psychologically in that moment.”
「比賽中發生的事幾秒內就能被拍攝、剪輯並傳遍世界，」吉布尼在郵件中說。「社群媒體還剝離了許多背景。人們看一小段影片就評判運動員的決定，卻未必理解該運動員當時身心經歷著什麼。」
廣告
Taiyyibah Moughal, a sports dietitian who lives in Bay Shore, N.Y., said it was not all that rare for people to suddenly empty their bowels during intense sports, like distance running.. The timed nature of Hyrox, compared with other events like CrossFit, means participants might be wary of wasting precious seconds.
住在紐約州貝肖爾的運動營養師泰伊巴·穆加爾說，人在長跑等劇烈運動中突然排空腸道並非罕見。相比CrossFit等賽事，Hyrox的計時競技性質意味著參賽者可能不願浪費寶貴的秒數。
“What bothered me was that they continued with the Hyrox, and it was an indoor event,” Moughal, 45, said. “If it was an outdoor event, that’s completely different, but when you’re sharing machines, that is where I felt like they should have taken the situation in their hands and stopped it.”
「讓我不安的是他們繼續進行Hyrox項目，而這是室內賽事，」45歲的穆加爾說。「如果是戶外，那完全不同；但當你們共享器械時，我覺得他們應該主動出手叫停。」
Rich Ryan, an elite Hyrox athlete who lives in Denver, said he had empathy for the “athlete tunnel vision” Wietrzyk might have been experiencing. “She’s an elite athlete in this race — there are different stakes than somebody racing for fun,” he said, describing how dropping out of a race could affect an athlete’s livelihood and standing for future competitions.
住在丹佛的精英Hyrox運動員里奇·萊恩說，他能理解維特日克可能經歷的「運動員隧道視野」。「她在這場比賽中是精英運動員——比賽的重要性跟為樂趣而賽的人不一樣，」他說，他還提到退賽會如何影響運動員的生計和未來參賽資格。
Brian Spin, who runs the endurance sport blog Barbell Spin, also said he felt the athlete should have been removed immediately by organizers.
經營耐力運動部落格Barbell Spin的布萊恩·斯平也說，他認為組織者應立即讓該運動員離場。
“In that moment, they didn’t take action, and may not have understood the gravity of what was to come,” Spin, who lives in Nashville, said.
「那一刻，他們沒採取行動，可能沒理解即將發生之事的嚴重性，」住在納什維爾的斯平說。
“Once an athlete is competing, the event has medical personnel, judges and race officials whose responsibility is to safeguard the competition and its athletes,” Gibney, the Hyrox coach, said. “I don’t think it should be left to the athlete to decide whether they continue,” he added.
「運動員一旦參賽，賽事就有醫療人員、裁判和比賽官員，他們的職責是保護比賽及其運動員，」Hyrox教練吉布尼說。「我不認為該由運動員自己決定是否繼續，」他補充說。
As Hyrox tries to pacify the online masses, Rob Perez, a fitness-content creator in Washington, D.C., who has completed nine Hyrox competitions, wondered if the recent events might deter people from future participation.
當Hyrox試圖安撫網路大眾時，已完成九場Hyrox比賽的華盛頓健身內容創作者羅布·佩雷斯在想，最近的事件是否會嚇退未來參與者。
“Whether they’re a new Hyrox athlete or looking to try their first Hyrox, a lot them are turned off, I think,” Perez said, adding that the videos shared online had “been a huge turnoff to a lot of people.”
「無論他們是新Hyrox運動員還是想嘗試第一場Hyrox，我認為很多人都被勸退了，」佩雷斯說，並補充說網上影片「對很多人來說極其掃興。」

Madison Malone Kircher是時報報導互聯網文化的記者。
