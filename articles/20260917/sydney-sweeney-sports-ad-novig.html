「雪梨妹」大尺度廣告引發爭議，被批物化女性

Sydney Sweeney Stars in a Controversial Ad. Again.

ALISHA HARIDASANI GUPTA • 2026-09-17
In a new one-minute ad for the sports betting site Novig, the actress Sydney Sweeney is dressed for about eight seconds. Well, if wearing just underpants or hockey shin guards counts as being dressed.
在體育博彩網站Novig一則新推出的一分鐘廣告中，演員席德妮·史威尼穿著衣服的時間大約只有八秒鐘。好吧，如果只穿內褲或者冰球護腿板也算穿衣服的話。
The ad begins with the camera trailing Sweeney in a dimly lit space before panning up to her face and zooming out. As the lights come up, viewers see Sweeney wearing nothing except two footballs held against her chest. In the next shot, she is straddling a basketball net, with only her long blonde hair to hide behind.
廣告開始時，鏡頭在昏暗的空間裡跟隨史威尼移動，然後向上平移到她的臉龐，隨後拉遠。隨著燈光亮起，觀眾看到史威尼身上除了緊貼胸前的兩隻橄欖球外，別無他物。在下一個鏡頭中，她跨坐在籃球網上，僅靠一頭金髮遮擋身體。
“No betting on wars or deaths and no politics,” she says in one shot. “Just you, your takes and sports.”
「不要對戰爭或死亡下注，也不要對政治下注，」她在一個鏡頭中說道。「只有你、你的觀點和體育。」
The ad, which was released online last Wednesday, led to a backlash that grew louder over the weekend, particularly from a range of female athletes, once again landing Sweeney at the center of a heated controversy. On one side, some, including athletes, said the actress was encouraging the objectification of female bodies and minimized women’s contributions to sports.
這則廣告於上週三在網上發布後，引發的輿論反響在週末愈演愈烈，尤其是來自眾多女性運動員的批評，這再次將史威尼推到了激烈爭議的中心。一方面，包括運動員在內的一些人表示，這位女演員在助長對女性身體的物化，並貶低了女性對體育的貢獻。
廣告
On Friday, Gracie Kramer, a former gymnast, posted a video montage on social media with the caption: “idk what Sydney Sweeney was doing, but this is what a woman in sports looks like.” It inspired waves of others in a range of sports to do the same.
週五，前體操運動員格雷西·克雷默在社群媒體上發布了一段影片剪輯，並配文：「我不知道席德妮·史威尼在幹什麼，但這才是體育界女性的樣子。」這激發了各個體育項目的眾多人士紛紛效仿。
Still, others online cast Sweeney as an empowered woman doing her job, and one who is unashamed of her body.
儘管如此，網上也有其他人將史威尼描繪成一位做好自己工作的獨立賦權女性，且對自己的身體非常坦然。
“The left has always been the party of sexual liberation and take your clothes off and all that,” said Raquel Debono, founder of the right-leaning Make America Hot Again organization. “And now, all of a sudden, they’re really upset because a pretty girl got naked doing a sports ad.”
右傾組織「讓美國再次性感」的創始人拉奎爾·德博諾說：「左翼一直代表著性解放，提倡脫掉衣服之類的。現在，突然之間，他們卻因為一個漂亮女孩在拍攝體育廣告時裸露身體而氣憤。」
The controversy, though, might be the point, said Joshua Lewis, a marketing professor at New York University’s Stern School of Business.
然而，紐約大學斯特恩商學院營銷學教授喬舒亞·劉易斯表示，引發爭議可能正是該廣告的目的所在。
Novig “is a company with not much market share, and so polarizing people is a reasonable strategy,” Lewis said. “They are betting, excuse the pun, that their own audience will not be the same as the people who are most offended by the ad.”
Novig「是一家市場份額不大的公司，因此引發人們產生兩極分化的看法，是一個合理的策略，」劉易斯說。「他們正在下注——原諒我的雙關語——押注他們自己的受眾與那些對這則廣告最反感的人群並不重合。」
So far, the ad has been viewed over 40 million times, and Novig, a company in which Sweeney has equity, is at least as recognizable as its competitors, Kalshi and Polymarket, for now. Ultimately, the controversy has functioned as publicity for a brand hoping to cater to men, who make up a majority of prediction market users. The company did not respond to requests for comment.
迄今為止，這則廣告的觀看次數已超過4000萬次，而目前來看，史威尼持有股份的Novig公司的知名度至少已與其競爭對手Kalshi和Polymarket旗鼓相當。歸根結底，這場爭議為一個希望吸引男性的品牌起到了宣傳作用，而男性構成了其預測市場用戶的大多數。該公司沒有回應置評請求。
廣告
“Now I know what this random sports betting company is that I had never heard of four days ago,” Debono said.
「現在我知道這個四天前我還從沒聽說過的體育博彩公司是什麼了，」德博諾說。
If this dynamic rings a bell, the ad comes a little over a year since Sweeney starred in an ad for American Eagle that played with “genes” as a homophone of “jeans,” which some critics said was a nod to white supremacy.
如果這種套路讓你覺得似曾相識，那是因為這則廣告距離史威尼主演「美國鷹」的一則廣告僅僅過去了一年多一點。那則廣告用「基因」(genes)和「牛仔褲」（jeans）的同音玩文字遊戲，一些批評人士稱這是對白人至上主義的迎合。
Despite the criticism, or perhaps because of it, the ad was credited with bumping up American Eagle stock and bolstering its sales. It also became a cause célèbre on the right, including with President Trump.
儘管遭到批評——或許正因如此——該廣告被認為推高了美國鷹的股價並促進了銷售。它也成為右翼陣營的熱門話題，甚至引起了川普總統的關注。
Sweeney has said, in an interview with GQ magazine, that the reaction surprised her because, in her eyes, it had been nothing more than an ad for jeans. “I love jeans!” she added, chirpily. She has continued working with American Eagle this year.
史威尼在接受《GQ》雜誌採訪時曾表示，這種反應讓她感到驚訝，因為在她看來，這只不過是一則牛仔褲廣告。「我愛牛仔褲！」她輕快地補充道。今年她繼續與美國鷹合作。
The fiercest criticism of Novig’s ad came from the world of sports, specifically from female athletes.
對Novig廣告最猛烈的批評來自體育界，特別是來自女性運動員。
“I’ve been over-sexualized my whole career, I’ve dealt with men only focusing on how I look and not what I’m capable of,” Kramer, the former gymnast, said in an interview, referring to comments some had made about the size of her legs or arms. “Women put in hours and years to build something that they’re proud of, and when you’re reduced to the way that you look, it’s dehumanizing.”
「在我的整個職業生涯中，我一直被過度性化，我一直在應付那些男人，他們只關注我的外表而不是我的能力，」前體操運動員克雷默在採訪中說，她指的是一些人對她腿部或手臂粗細的評論。「女性投入無數小時，投入多年時間，來塑造她們引以為傲的成就，如果你被簡化到只剩下外表，這是一種對人性的貶損。」
廣告
Her response video, which was viewed millions of times, became a kind of rallying cry for other female athletes to post videos of themselves too, skiing, running, playing basketball, water polo and more. On Monday, the British sprinter Amy Hunt, after finishing a 200-meter final, addressed Sweeney on the BBC, adding that “this is what women in sport look like.”
她的回應影片被觀看了數百萬次，成為一種號召，激勵其他女運動員也發布她們自己滑雪、跑步、打籃球、打水球等運動時的影片。週一，英國短跑運動員艾米·杭特在完成200米決賽後，在BBC的節目中向史威尼喊話，並稱「這才是女性在體育運動中的樣子。」
Sweeney, who did not respond to a request for comment, posted material on Sunday that obliquely responded to the controversy, highlighting past photo shoots in which female athletes, including Serena Williams and Sue Bird had posed without clothing for ESPN.
沒有回應置評請求的史威尼在週日發布了一些資料，隱晦地回應了這場爭議，這些資料重點展示了包括塞雷娜·威廉斯和蘇·伯德在內的女運動員過去都曾為ESPN拍攝過裸體照片。
In a way, Sweeney has become associated with causing buzz for a brand trying to raise its profile, said Katie Gatti Tassin, a co-host of the podcast Diabolical Lies and author of “Rich Girl Nation.”
播客《彌天大謊》的聯合主持人、《富家女國度》(Rich Girl Nation)一書的作者凱蒂·加蒂·塔辛表示，在某種程度上，史威尼已成為那些試圖提升品牌知名度的企業製造話題的代名詞。
In addition to Novig and American Eagle, there was an ad for the bath product brand Dr. Squatch, which produced a limited edition soap it said had been infused with Sweeney’s bath water, which raised some eyebrows.
除了Novig和美國鷹之外，還有沐浴產品品牌Dr. Squatch的一則廣告，該品牌生產了一款限量版肥皂，並稱其中融入了史威尼的洗澡水，這也引發了一些質疑。
These brands, Tassin said, book sexy shoots with Sweeney that seemed designed to set off controversy, which then “like a Bat Signal, promptly summons the conservatives” to defend the brand.
塔辛表示，這些品牌會與史威尼合作拍攝性感寫真，這些照片似乎專為引發爭議而設計，隨後「就像蝙蝠俠信號燈一樣，迅速召喚保守派」，出來捍衛該品牌。
Some of Sweeney’s supporters online say there may be extra scrutiny on Sweeney because of her association with the political right, according to Debono, who is also the founder of the communications company Public Affair Media. Sweeney is a registered Republican and, earlier this year, her lingerie brand Syrn, which she models herself, was a key partner of Stagecoach, a conservative country music festival.
同時也是公關公司Public Affair Media創始人的德博諾說，網上一些史威尼的支持者表示，由於她與政治右翼的聯繫，她可能會因此受到額外的審視。史威尼是一名註冊共和黨人，今年早些時候，由她親自擔任模特的個人內衣品牌Syrn是保守派鄉村音樂節驛馬節的重要合作夥伴。
The current ad lands in a political climate that has, in the past year, inspired a “Freaky Friday”-like cultural role reversal, Debono said. The liberal left, once known for sexual liberation, is painted as too “woke,” and prudish, while the right-leaning are rebranding around sexy femininity.
德博諾說，目前的這則廣告正處於一種政治氣候中，這種氣候在過去一年裡引發了類似電影《辣媽辣妹》(Freaky Friday)般的文化角色大反轉——曾經以性解放聞名的自由左翼如今被描繪成過於「覺醒」且假正經，而右傾人士則正在圍繞性感的女性氣質重塑形象。

“The whole thing is so ridiculous,” she added. “Why do people care so much?”
「整件事簡直荒謬，」她還說。「為什麼人們這麼在意呢？」
