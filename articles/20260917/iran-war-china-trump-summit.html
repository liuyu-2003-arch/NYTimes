“習川會”前伊朗外長訪華

In a Signal to Trump, Beijing Hosts Iranian Diplomat Ahead of U.S.-China Summit

DAVID PIERSON • 2026-09-17
Beijing showed off its close ties to Tehran on Wednesday by hosting Iran’s top diplomat days before an expected summit between President Trump and China’s leader, Xi Jinping.
週三，伊朗最高外交官訪問北京，在川普總統與中國領導人習近平即將會面的前幾天，北京展示了與德黑蘭的密切關係。
China’s top diplomat, Wang Yi, repeated Beijing’s calls for an end to the war in Iran and for the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz in a meeting with Abbas Araghchi, the Iranian foreign minister, according to an official Chinese summary of the meeting.
根據中國官方的會議紀要，中國最高外交官王毅在與伊朗外交部長阿巴斯·阿拉格齊的會面中重申了北京關於結束伊朗戰爭和重新開放霍爾木茲海峽的呼籲。
Mr. Araghchi told Mr. Wang that while Iran is prepared to defend itself against “aggressors,” it also “welcomes diplomatic solutions that secure the rights of the Iranian nation,” according to a statement about the meeting on Mr. Araghchi’s official social media account. He added that Tehran seeks the restoration of peaceful relations with its neighbors, and has begun dialogue with countries in the region.
根據阿拉格齊官方社群媒體帳號上關於此次會晤的聲明，阿拉格齊向王毅表示，雖然伊朗已準備好抵禦「侵略者」，但同時也「歡迎能夠保障伊朗民族權利的外交解決方案」。他還說，德黑蘭尋求與鄰國恢復和平關係，並已開始與該地區國家進行對話。
The meeting between Mr. Wang and Mr. Araghchi, who also met in Beijing days before Mr. Trump’s summit with Mr. Xi in China in May, sends a signal to the White House that Beijing has leverage over Tehran at a time when Mr. Trump appears to be running out of options to bring the war to an end on favorable terms for the United States.
王毅與阿拉格齊的此次會面（兩人此前曾在5月川普與中國國家主席習近平在北京會晤之前幾天會面）向白宮釋放了一個信號：在川普似乎已無計可施、無法以對美國有利的條件結束這場戰爭之際，北京對德黑蘭擁有影響力。

That influence stems from critical support that China provides to Iran, which China considers its closest strategic partner in the Middle East. Beijing supplies Tehran with technology and raw materials to build missiles and drones, and defied Western sanctions by buying over 90 percent of Tehran’s oil exports before the Strait of Hormuz closed.
這種影響力源於中國向伊朗提供的關鍵支持，中國將伊朗視為其在中東地區最親密的戰略夥伴。北京向德黑蘭提供用於製造導彈和無人機的技術和原材料，並在霍爾木茲海峽關閉前無視西方制裁購買了德黑蘭超過90%的石油出口份額。
“China and Iran are comprehensive strategic partners,” Mr. Wang said, invoking a phrase Beijing has used for years to describe the countries’ relationship. He added that “China is also willing to strengthen dialogue and cooperation with Iran to safeguard Iran’s legitimate rights and interests,” according to the meeting summary.
「中伊兩國是全面戰略夥伴，」王毅說，他使用了北京多年來描述兩國關係的表述。根據會議紀要，他還說：「中國也願同伊朗加強對話合作，維護好伊朗的正當權益。」
Mr. Wang did not assign blame to any country for the war and urged Iran and the United States to “remain rational and exercise restraint.” He encouraged Tehran to continue dialogue with countries it is fighting directly and indirectly in the Persian Gulf, a region where China has developed close diplomatic ties. And he expressed concern about the escalating conflict in Yemen between the internationally recognized government that is supported by Saudi Arabia and the Iran-backed Houthi militia.
王毅沒有將戰爭的責任歸咎於任何國家，並敦促伊朗和美國「保持理性和剋制」。他鼓勵德黑蘭繼續與在波斯灣地區直接和間接交戰的國家進行對話，中國在該地區已發展了密切的外交關係。他還對葉門局勢的升級表示關切，該國由沙烏地阿拉伯支持的國際公認政府與伊朗支持的胡塞武裝之間衝突不斷升級。
Beijing’s balancing act has recently faced pressure from the United States. Mr. Trump last month threatened, but has so far refrained from imposing, “tremendous economic consequences” on countries that do business with Iran. China would have seemingly been the main target of that effort.
北京的平衡策略最近面臨來自美國的壓力。川普上個月曾威脅要對與伊朗做生意的國家施加「巨大的經濟後果」，但迄今為止尚未付諸實施。中國似乎本應是這一舉措的主要目標。
Mr. Trump and Mr. Xi are scheduled to meet in Washington next week. Iran is expected to feature prominently in discussions between the two sides, along with the potential extension of a U.S.-China trade truce and ways to cooperate on artificial intelligence safety.
川普和習近平定於下週在華盛頓會晤。預計伊朗將成為雙方討論的重要議題，此外還包括中美貿易休戰的潛在延期，以及在人工智慧安全方面的合作途徑。
