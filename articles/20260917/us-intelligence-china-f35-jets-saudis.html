美國情報官員：中國試圖獲取F-35戰鬥機技術

U.S. Intelligence Warns of China Theft Over Sale of F-35 Jets to Saudis

黃安偉, DUSTIN VOLZ • 2026-09-17
U.S. intelligence officers have expressed concerns in internal reports about possible Chinese espionage related to a pending sale of dozens of advanced F-35 fighter jets to Saudi Arabia valued at $24 billion, according to American officials.
美國官員表示，情報官員在內部報告中就中國可能針對向沙烏地阿拉伯出售數十架先進F-35戰鬥機一事進行間諜活動表達了擔憂。這筆待完成的軍售價值240億美元。
Intelligence analysts have raised the possibility that China could acquire F-35 jet technology through spying in Saudi Arabia or through its military and security partnership with the kingdom, the U.S. officials said.
這些美國官員說，情報分析人士指出，中國可能通過在沙烏地阿拉伯的間諜活動，或通過其與沙烏地阿拉伯的軍事和安全夥伴關係獲取F-35戰機技術。
One detailed report issued by the Pentagon’s Defense Intelligence Agency months ago discusses Chinese military access to Saudi bases and Saudi Arabia’s use of Chinese technology in its telecommunications infrastructure, an official said. It also questions whether the Americans and Saudis will be able to maintain secure sites for the F-35 technology, the official said.
一名官員說，五角大廈國防情報局數月前發布了一份詳細報告，討論了中國軍方進入沙烏地阿拉伯基地以及沙烏地阿拉伯在電信基礎設施中使用中國技術的問題。這名官員說，報告還質疑美沙兩國能否維持F-35技術所在設施的安全。
That report has circulated among several administration agencies and congressional offices, as have assessments from other intelligence offices. The D.I.A. issued a more general report in the fall of 2025 on similar risks when the administration was discussing the possibility of the sales, The New York Times reported at the time.
這份報告以及來自其他情報機構的評估已在幾個政府機構和國會辦公室之間傳閱。2025年秋，在政府討論這筆軍售可能性時，國防情報局曾就類似風險發布過一份更籠統的報告，《紐約時報》當時對此進行了報導。
廣告
President Trump then announced the sale the day before he hosted Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, the de facto leader of Saudi Arabia, at the White House in November.
隨後，川普總統在於11月在白宮接待沙烏地阿拉伯王儲穆罕默德·本·薩勒曼的前一天宣布了這筆軍售。穆罕默德·本·薩勒曼是沙烏地阿拉伯事實上的領導人。
The sale is at a more advanced stage now, and the newer D.I.A. report raises sharper concerns, including technical ones. The State Department sent the proposed sales package of 48 jets and a spare engine to two congressional committees in June to get informal approval. Some lawmakers and aides have been pressing the administration on the concerns raised by the intelligence agencies.
這筆軍售現在已進入更深入階段，而國防情報局較新的報告提出了更為強烈的擔憂，包括技術方面的擔憂。國務院已於6月將包括48架戰機和一台備用發動機的擬議銷售方案送交兩個國會委員會，以尋求非正式批准。一些議員和國會工作人員一直就情報機構提出的擔憂向政府施壓。
The U.S. officials spoke about the concerns among intelligence officials on the condition of anonymity because of sensitivities around espionage and military matters.
由於間諜和軍事事務的敏感性，這些美國官員在談及情報官員的擔憂時要求匿名。
The State Department, the Pentagon and the Embassy of Saudi Arabia in Washington declined to comment. The White House and the embassy of China did not reply to requests for comment.
美國國務院、五角大廈和沙烏地阿拉伯駐華盛頓大使館拒絕置評。白宮和中國大使館沒有回應置評請求。
U.S. intelligence agencies raised similar concerns internally during Mr. Trump’s first term after he agreed in 2020 to sell F-35 jets to the United Arab Emirates as part of a deal in which the Emirates would normalize relations with Israel.
在川普第一個任期內，美國情報機構也曾在內部提出類似擔憂。當時，川普於2020年同意向阿拉伯聯合酋長國出售F-35戰機，作為阿聯酋與以色列關係正常化協議的一部分。
It takes years to produce the jets, which are made by Lockheed Martin, and the Biden administration decided to review the sale well before production and delivery. Intelligence agencies raised the same issues at that time because the Emirates was strengthening its military, intelligence and technology ties with China. U.S. officials then ended the sales process.
F-35戰機由洛克希德·馬丁公司生產，從生產到交付需要數年時間。因此，拜登政府在戰機生產和交付之前很久就決定對這筆軍售進行審查。當時，由於阿聯酋正在加強與中國在軍事、情報和技術領域的聯繫，美國情報機構也提出了同樣的問題。隨後，美國官員終止了這一軍售進程。
廣告
Prince Mohammed has been trying to build up his military. He began a disastrous seven-year war against the Houthis in Yemen, one in which Saudi-led forces killed civilians with American bombs. The Saudi military has failed to ward off attacks from Iran in the current U.S.-Israeli war against Iran, and the Saudis and the Houthis, which are supported by Iran, have begun fighting again.
穆罕默德王儲一直在努力提升沙烏地阿拉伯的軍事實力。他曾在葉門對胡塞武裝發動一場災難性的七年戰爭，沙烏地阿拉伯領導的部隊在那場戰爭中使用美國炸彈殺害平民。在當前美國和以色列對伊朗發動的戰爭中，沙烏地阿拉伯軍隊也未能阻止來自伊朗的襲擊，而沙烏地阿拉伯與伊朗支持的胡塞武裝已重新開始交戰。
Prince Mohammed has long sought to buy F-35 jets from the United States, and Mr. Trump finally agreed last November. Saudi Arabia and the United States have had decades of military cooperation, and the kingdom is by far the largest buyer of American weapons and defense equipment. But Saudi Arabia buys ballistic missiles from China, and the Chinese military is helping the Saudis learn to build ballistic missiles, set up production facilities and improve missile operations.
穆罕默德王儲長期以來一直尋求從美國購買F-35戰鬥機，川普最終於去年11月同意了這一要求。沙烏地阿拉伯和美國已經進行了數十年的軍事合作，而沙烏地阿拉伯也是美國武器和防務裝備的最大買家。但沙烏地阿拉伯同時也從中國購買彈道導彈，中國軍方還在幫助沙烏地阿拉伯學習製造彈道導彈、建立生產設施以及改進導彈作戰能力。
Democratic lawmakers sent President Joseph R. Biden Jr. a letter in June 2022 laying out their concerns over that program.
民主黨議員曾於2022年6月致信拜登總統，闡述他們對這一項目的擔憂。
The recent Pentagon intelligence report said that only Saudi and American officers and contractors should have access to sites where F-35 technology would reside, a U.S. official said. Pentagon officials are especially concerned about protecting the advanced radar and surveillance technology of the F-35.
一名美國官員說，五角大廈近期的情報報告指出，能進入存放F-35技術的設施的只有隻有沙烏地阿拉伯和美國的軍官及承包商。五角大廈官員尤其擔心如何保護F-35先進的雷達和監視技術。
The intelligence analysts ask in the report whether Saudi Arabia would more broadly agree to limit contact with Chinese military and intelligence personnel and associated entities.
情報分析人士在報告中質疑沙烏地阿拉伯是否會進一步同意限制與中國軍事和情報人員及相關實體的接觸。
The report also questions whether Saudi officials would agree to remove equipment from Huawei and ZTE, two prominent Chinese technology companies, from telecommunications and router infrastructure and defense installations.
報告還質疑沙烏地阿拉伯官員是否會同意將華為和中興這兩家知名中國科技公司的設備從電信和路由器基礎設施以及國防設施中移除。
廣告
Some Israeli officials also have concerns about the deal, as they did when Mr. Trump proposed such sales to the Emirates. Israel is the only country in the Middle East that has F-35s, which it has deployed in airstrikes against Iran in recent years. Since the 1973 Arab-Israeli war, U.S. policymakers have tried to ensure that Israel maintains its “qualitative military edge” in the region, as mandated by Congress.
一些以色列官員也對這筆軍售表示擔憂，就像川普當初提議向阿聯酋出售F-35時一樣。以色列是中東唯一擁有F-35戰鬥機的國家，近年來曾使用這些戰機對伊朗發動空襲。自1973年阿以戰爭以來，美國政策制定者一直試圖確保以色列在該地區保持國會所要求的「定性軍事優勢」。
Previous administrations ran a highly classified, monthslong process to review whether proposed weapons sales in the region would meet that requirement. The second Trump administration has continued to push through large arms sales to Israel, however, despite calls across the American political spectrum to end or severely decrease military support.
歷屆美國政府都會通過一個高度機密、持續數月的審查程序，評估擬議中的中東地區武器銷售是否符合這一要求。然而，川普第二屆政府仍在推動向以色列大規模軍售，儘管美國政治光譜的各派都有人呼籲停止或大幅減少軍事支持。
In several instances, Secretary of State Marco Rubio has bypassed the congressional approval process to expedite shipments of weapons to Israel, citing an emergency in the region. If congressional leaders do not give approval for the F-35 jet sales, he could decide to do the same with that.
在過去的一些情況下，國務卿魯比奧以地區緊急狀態為由，繞過國會批准程序，加快向以色列運送武器。如果國會領導人不同意F-35戰機銷售，他可能會採取同樣做法。
Mr. Trump has also agreed to help Saudi Arabia build a civilian nuclear program, a project that has raised its own security concerns because Prince Mohammed has vowed to build nuclear weapons if Iran does so.
川普還同意幫助沙烏地阿拉伯建立民用核項目。該項目本身也引發了安全擔憂，因為穆罕默德王儲曾誓言，如果伊朗建造核武器，沙烏地阿拉伯也會這樣做。

黃安偉(Edward Wong)為《紐約時報》報導全球事務、美國外交政策和國務院新聞。

Dustin Volz為時報撰寫網路安全和情報方面的文章，他常駐華盛頓。
