報告披露美國伊朗戰爭的代價

Pentagon Inspector General Details U.S. Losses and Challenges in Iran War

JOHN ISMAY • 2026-09-17
Some of the most detailed information about the Iran war has come not from the office of the defense secretary or the White House, but from a report released by the Defense Department’s inspector general.
關於伊朗戰爭的一些最詳盡的信息並非來自國防部長辦公室或白宮，而是來自國防部監察長髮布的一份報告。
The independent oversight agency offered glimpses into the U.S. military’s inventory shortfalls, and the damage from Iranian counterattacks to consulates and other diplomatic facilities in the region.
這個獨立監督機構讓人得以一窺美軍庫存短缺，以及伊朗反擊對該地區領事館和其他外交設施造成的損壞。
The document, which was issued on Monday, covered only the first four months of the war, from Feb. 28 to June 30. On Tuesday, the nonpartisan Congressional Budget Office said in a separate report that war had cost the United States roughly $38 billion through Aug. 1.
這份週一發布的文件僅涵蓋戰爭的前四個月，從2月28日到6月30日。週二，無黨派的國會預算辦公室在另一份報告中說，截至8月1日，這場戰爭已使美國花費約380億美元。
Here are some key takeaways from the inspector general’s report.
以下是監察長報告的一些關鍵要點。
A secret mission statement
祕密任務聲明
The Pentagon and White House have said that their goal in Iran is to destroy its nuclear programs, attack drones, ballistic and cruise missiles, and its means of producing those weapons.
五角大廈和白宮曾表示，他們在伊朗的目標是摧毀其核項目、攻擊無人機、彈道和巡航導彈，以及生產這些武器的手段。
But the inspector general’s report noted that the Pentagon’s full mission statement for the Iran war “is classified.” That suggests that the military may have other goals that have not been made public.
但監察長報告指出，五角大廈對伊朗戰爭的完整任務聲明「屬於機密」。這表明軍方可能有其他未公開的目標。
Costs
成本
The inspector general said that “it can be difficult to calculate the total ‘cost’” of the Iran war, given that the Pentagon has never had a separate appropriation from Congress to fund it.
監察長說，「計算伊朗戰爭的總『成本』可能很困難」，因為五角大廈從未從國會獲得專門的撥款作為這場戰爭的經費。
Noting that Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth did not request additional funds from Congress before launching combat operations, the report stated that funds for the war are coming from the department’s existing budget for training and maintenance of the force.
報告指出，國防部長彼特·海格塞斯在發動作戰行動前沒有向國會申請額外資金，並稱戰爭經費來自該部門用於部隊訓練和維護的現有預算。
The costs to repair or replace U.S. military facilities damaged or destroyed by Iranian attacks — such as the Navy’s base in Manama, Bahrain — have not been calculated, as the Pentagon has indicated it may choose to close or not rebuild them.
修復或更換被伊朗襲擊損壞或摧毀的美軍設施——例如位於巴林麥納麥的海軍基地——的成本尚未計算，因為五角大廈表示可能選擇關閉或不重建它們。
On June 24, the report said, the White House submitted a $67 billion supplemental funding request for the war, of which $21 billion was slated for replacing munitions expended up to that point.
報告說，6月24日，白宮提交了一份670億美元的戰爭補充資金請求，其中210億美元用於替換截至當時消耗的彈藥。
The inspector general also said that the State Department estimated that its embassies, consulates and other diplomatic facilities in the Middle East had suffered about $184 million in damage from Iranian strikes.
監察長還說，國務院估計其在中東的大使館、領事館和其他外交設施因伊朗襲擊而遭受約1.84億美元的損壞。
The U.S. diplomatic mission in Iraq was hit by “more than 600” Iranian attacks just in the first four months of the war, sustaining more than $157 million in damages.
美國在伊拉克的外交使團僅在戰爭前四個月就遭到「超過600次」伊朗襲擊，承受超過1.57億美元的損壞。
Shortfalls of munitions
彈藥短缺
The Pentagon’s top acquisition office told the inspector general’s office that the expenditure of munitions in the Iran war “has resulted in strategic inventory shortfalls.”
五角大廈的最高採購辦公室告訴監察長辦公室，伊朗戰爭中的彈藥消耗「已導致戰略性庫存短缺」。
廣告
Military leaders have tried to speed up the production of munitions, the report noted, but added that “the defense industrial base requires ‘significant lead time’ to expand production capacities” for weapons.
報告指出，軍方領導人試圖加快彈藥生產，但補充說，「國防工業基礎需要『較長的準備時間』來擴大武器生產能力」。
Bottlenecks persist in the production of solid rocket motors that missiles rely on, as well as the supply of “high-grade explosives and propellants.”
導彈所依賴的固體火箭發動機的生產以及「高能炸藥和推進劑」的供應仍然存在瓶頸。
The Congressional Budget Office report also found that the war had significantly depleted U.S. munitions. In response, Pentagon spokesman Sean Parnell said that “claims of U.S. munition shortages are false.”
國會預算辦公室的報告還發現，這場戰爭已大幅消耗美國彈藥。作為回應，五角大廈發言人肖恩·帕內爾說，「美國彈藥短缺的說法與事實不符。」
Troops, ships, aircraft and buildings
部隊、艦艇、飛機和建築
The U.S. military force in the Middle East supporting the war has numbered roughly 50,000, even as units rotate on deployment.
儘管一些單位會進行輪換部署，美國在中東地區支持這場戰爭的兵力仍保持在5萬人左右。
According to the inspector general’s report, the war has seen “a dozen each” of fighter squadrons, destroyers and air defense batteries deployed as well as mobile launchers for artillery rockets and guided missiles like ATACMS and PrSM.
根據監察長報告，這場戰爭已部署「十餘個」戰鬥機中隊，投入的驅逐艦和防空連數量大致也有這麼多，另外還有火箭和ATACMS、PrSM等制導導彈的發射車。
The war has also seen the deployment of four aircraft carrier strike groups thus far, and a “partial deployment” of the 82nd Airborne Division.
迄今為止，這場戰爭還動用了四個航母打擊群，「局部部署」了第82空降師。
Iranian counterattacks damaged and destroyed “hundreds of buildings and structures” at U.S. military bases in Bahrain, Iraq, Jordan, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates, the report said.
報告說，伊朗反擊損壞並摧毀了美國在巴林、伊拉克、約旦、科威特、阿曼、卡達、沙烏地阿拉伯和阿拉伯聯合酋長國軍事基地的「數百座建築和構築物」。
Additionally, dozens of U.S. military aircraft have been damaged or destroyed, including 30 Reaper drones, 12 KC-135 aerial refueling planes, four F-15 fighters and an A-10 attack jet.
此外，數十架美軍飛機被損壞或摧毀，包括30架「死神」無人機、12架KC-135空中加油機、4架F-15戰鬥機和1架A-10攻擊機。
Casualties and medical support
傷亡與醫療支持
The Defense Department has limited medical treatment facilities at every location where U.S. forces are deployed in the region, the report said, and has built more advanced facilities to provide surgical support in several countries.
報告說，國防部在該地區的每個美軍部署點都只有有限的醫療設施，並已在幾個國家建造更先進的設施以提供外科支持。
For care beyond what those facilities can provide, the Pentagon has relied on hospitals in those host nations.
至於這些設施無法提供的護理，五角大廈要依賴東道國的醫院。
廣告
Medical evacuation helicopters in Iraq and Kuwait had to be relocated after Iranian strikes, the report said, adding that “the nature of operations” during the war has meant most U.S. casualties have been transported by vehicles and ambulances to medical treatment facilities.
報告說，伊拉克和科威特的醫療後送直升機在伊朗襲擊後不得不轉移，並補充說，戰爭期間的「行動性質」意味著大多數美軍傷亡人員由一般車輛和救護車運送到醫療設施。
The U.S. has suffered 18 killed and 824 wounded in the war, according to a Pentagon casualty database.
根據五角大廈傷亡數據庫，美國已有18人在這場戰爭中死亡、824人受傷。
Logistics issues
後勤問題
The destruction of the Navy’s main logistics hub in Bahrain forced the Pentagon to look for additional supply ships that were outfitted to transfer fuel and supplies to warships at sea, the report said.
報告說，海軍在巴林的主要後勤樞紐被摧毀，迫使五角大廈尋找更多補給艦，這些艦船配備有向海上軍艦轉移燃料和物資的設備。
The Navy’s move to use the British-owned island Diego Garcia — roughly 2,200 miles from the Arabian Sea — as its new logistics center means that supply ships have to spend two to two and a half weeks to travel to the island, take on goods and fuel, and return to the Navy’s main operating areas outside the Persian Gulf, according to the report.
根據報告，海軍轉向使用英屬迪戈加西亞島——距阿拉伯海約2200英里——作為其新後勤中心，這意味著補給艦必須花費兩到兩週半時間前往該島，裝載貨物和燃料，然後返回海軍在波斯灣外的主要作戰區域。
Strain on other combatant commanders
對其他作戰指揮官的壓力
Of all the Pentagon’s “combatant commands,” which are responsible for military operations in different parts of the globe, U.S. European Command, based in Germany, has provided the most support to the Iran war, according to the report.
根據報告，在五角大廈所有負責全球不同地區軍事行動的「作戰司令部」中，駐德國的美國歐洲司令部為伊朗戰爭提供了最多支持。
It transferred “munitions, equipment, aircraft and personnel, and requisitioned and distributed fuel, food, and medical supplies” to Central Command, which oversees operations in the Middle East.
它向負責中東行動的中央司令部調撥了「彈藥、裝備、飛機和人員，並徵用和分發了燃料、食品和醫療用品」。
Mr. Hegseth ordered Pacific Command, which is based in Oahu, Hawaii, to pursue and board suspected Iranian merchant ships in the Indian Ocean — expanding the mission far outside the Middle East.
海格塞斯命令駐夏威夷瓦胡島的太平洋司令部在印度洋追擊並登臨疑似伊朗商船——意味著任務已經遠及中東之外的區域。
Pacific Command was also ordered to give up six warships for the Iran war. They included the aircraft carrier strike group centered around the U.S.S. Abraham Lincoln, which left San Diego on Nov. 21 and spent roughly nine months at sea without a port call for its crew to rest.
太平洋司令部還奉命抽調所部六艘軍艦用於伊朗戰爭。其中包括以林肯號航母為中心的航母打擊群，該艦於11月21日離開聖地牙哥，在海上度過約九個月，期間沒有靠港供船員休整。
The ship is currently returning to its home port in Southern California.
該艦目前正在返回其南加州母港。

John Ismay為《紐約時報》報導五角大廈新聞。他曾在美國海軍擔任爆炸物處理官。
