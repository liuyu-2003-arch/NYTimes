歐盟邀請加拿大成為「准成員國」

E.U.-Canada Ambitions Face Reality Check

MATINA STEVIS-GRIDNEFF, JEANNA SMIALEK • 2026-09-17
The European Union’s invitation to Canada on Wednesday to become an “associate member” of the bloc shows how intent traditional United States allies are on pursuing a more stable future by binding closer together, a move President Trump said could be perceived as “a hostile act.”
歐盟週三邀請加拿大成為該集團的「准成員國」，這表明美國的傳統盟友多麼急於通過更緊密地結合在一起來追求一個更穩定的未來。川普總統對此表示，這一舉措可能被解讀為「一種敵對行為」。
As if to confirm the deep concerns that pushed them closer, Mr. Trump threatened retribution.
彷彿是為了印證那種促使他們走近的深切擔憂，川普威脅要進行報復。
“If Europe does that, with a bad intention — if it’s a good intention, that’s fine — if it’s bad intention we’ll put very heavy tariffs on Europe, which is a possibility,” he told reporters in North Carolina before a rally.
「如果歐洲這樣做是出於惡意——如果是出於好意，那沒問題，如果是出於惡意，我們將對歐洲徵收非常高的關稅，這是有可能的，」他在北卡羅萊納州的一場集會前告訴記者。
Hours earlier, Ursula von der Leyen, the president of the European Commission, told the European Parliament in Strasbourg, France, “We must urgently reimagine our partnerships.” A beaming Prime Minister Mark Carney of Canada sat beside her.
幾個小時前，歐盟委員會主席烏爾蘇拉·馮德萊恩在法國斯特拉斯堡的歐洲議會上表示：「我們必須緊急重新構想我們的夥伴關係。」笑容滿面的加拿大總理馬克·卡尼就坐在她身旁。
While details of what this relationship might look like remain scant, Ms. von der Leyen noted some potential areas of cooperation, including partnerships in manufacturing, technology, defense, energy policy and economic security.
儘管目前這種關係具體會是什麼樣子仍缺乏細節，但馮德萊恩指出了一些潛在合作領域，包括製造業、技術、防務、能源政策和經濟安全方面的夥伴關係。
This would not be “a partnership against anyone else,” she insisted, “but for our common strength.”
她堅持說，這不會是「針對任何其他國家的夥伴關係」，而是「為了增強我們的共同實力」。
Mr. Carney’s office also said on Wednesday that Canada had formally applied to join the Joint Expeditionary Force, a British-led military coalition of 10 North Atlantic Treaty Organization members, seven of which are also part of the European Union.
卡尼的辦公室週三還表示，加拿大已正式申請加入遠征軍聯合部隊，這是一個由英國領導的軍事聯盟，由10個北大西洋公約組織成員國組成，其中七個也是歐盟成員國。
廣告
European officials said that, in the lead-up to the speech on Wednesday, the bloc wanted to ensure that Mr. Trump did not see this new partnership with Canada as an anti-American move.
歐洲官員說，在週三演講之前，歐盟希望確保川普總統不會把與加拿大的這一新夥伴關係視為反美舉動。
Still, if it isn’t precisely against the United States, then it’s unmistakably about it.
不過，即便它不完全是針對美國，也顯然與美國脫不了干係。
“Behind this is a desire to hedge against what for both partners is an increasingly fraught relationship with the United States,” said Ian Lesser, a distinguished fellow of the German Marshall Fund, based in Brussels.
「這背後反映出一種願望，即針對兩國與美國日益棘手的關係，採取措施降低風險，」總部位於布魯塞爾的德國馬歇爾基金會傑出研究員伊恩·萊瑟說。
“But it’s not just that it’s fraught,” he added. “It’s the most important geoeconomic and geopolitical relationship for both Europe and for Canada.”
「但問題不只是關係緊張，」他補充說。「無論對歐洲還是加拿大而言，這都是最重要的地緣經濟和地緣政治關係。」
Symbolism matters
象徵意義很重要
For Mr. Carney, the invitation is both timely and symbolically important. He has decided to stand up to Mr. Trump, who has imposed tariffs on Canada and regularly threatens its sovereignty.
對卡尼來說，這份邀請來得正是時候，也具有重要的象徵意義。他已經決定對川普採取強硬立場，而川普不僅對加拿大加徵關稅，還經常威脅加拿大的主權。
Mr. Carney has been pushing for Canada to get greater access to the European Union — its territory, its market, its funds and programs — without becoming a full member of the bloc.
卡尼一直致力於推動加拿大在不成為歐盟正式成員的情況下獲得更大的歐盟准入，包括其領土、市場、資金和項目。
He has found an enthusiastic partner in Ms. von der Leyen, who has painted Canada as a dependable source for Europe’s needs for energy and critical raw materials, as well as a steadfast ally who shares NATO membership, support for Ukraine and a commitment to the rule of law. Ms. von der Leyen does not represent the European Union’s 27 countries, but she sets the agenda for the bloc and commands its mighty bureaucracy.
他找到了馮德萊恩這個熱情的夥伴。後者一直將加拿大描繪成歐洲能源和關鍵原材料需求的可靠來源，也是一個堅定的盟友：雙方同為北約成員，都支持烏克蘭，並致力於維護法治。馮德萊恩並不代表歐盟27個成員國，但她為這個集團確定議程，並掌管著其龐大的官僚體系。
The tie-up seems to make sense at a moment of global turbulence and a shifting global role for the United States. Between them, the European Union’s 27 members and Canada have more than 490 million citizens and include four of the world’s 10 largest economies.
在全球局勢動盪、美國在世界上的角色不斷變化之際，雙方加強聯繫似乎順理成章。歐盟27個成員國與加拿大合計擁有超過4.9億人口，其中包括全球十大經濟體中的四個。
廣告
What’s in a name?
這個名稱意味著什麼？
The European Union is underpinned by dense, deep legal and procedural rules, built over decades by a group of nations that ceded sovereignty to one another, and to an unelected executive branch in Brussels.
歐盟建立在密集而深厚的法律與程序規則之上，這些規則是數十年來由成員國所建立：它們彼此讓渡主權，也把主權讓渡給布魯塞爾一個未經選舉產生的行政機構。
Inserting a country an ocean away deeper into this web is not a straightforward business.
把一個遠隔大洋的國家更深地納入這張網並不是一件簡單的事。
The label the European Union wants to confer on Canada, “associate member,” is not an existing type of partnership — meaning that it is not clear what actions Canada would have to take to earn it, or for it to be given.
歐盟希望賦予加拿大的「准成員國」這一稱謂並非現有的一種夥伴關係類型，這意味著，目前仍不清楚加拿大要採取什麼行動才能獲得這一身份，以及這一身份將如何被授予。
“It has no legal basis,” said Mujtaba Rahman, managing director for Europe at the Eurasia Group. “It’s more symbolism and signal,” he added.
「它沒有法律依據，」歐亞集團歐洲業務董事總經理穆傑塔巴·拉赫曼說。「它更多是一種象徵和信號，」他補充道。
Ms. von der Leyen was taking a risk using the term, which had been floating for weeks ahead of her speech, and drew consternation from several member states, according to European officials.
歐洲官員說，馮德萊恩使用這個稱謂是在冒險。在她演講前數週，該說法已在流傳，並引起幾個成員國的不安。
It is seen as vague, untethered from legal frameworks and potentially antagonistic toward countries that already have detailed legal agreements with the European Union. These range from candidate nations such as Albania to close partners including Britain, Switzerland and Norway. Those countries have deep trade ties to the bloc and, in the case of Switzerland and Norway, have free movement into and out of other European nations.
它被認為含糊不清、脫離法律框架，而且可能與那些已經與歐盟簽署了詳盡法律協議的國家產生摩擦。這些國家包括阿爾巴尼亞這樣的候選國，也包括英國、瑞士和挪威這樣的密切夥伴。這些國家與歐盟有著緊密的貿易聯繫，瑞士和挪威還享有與其他歐洲國家之間的人員自由流動。
Some Canadian officials do not appear to love the term, either, as membership in the European Union implies forfeiting some sovereignty, which is the opposite of what Mr. Carney has pledged to do. The Conservative opposition leader seized on the term, arguing that if Canada was never to be the 51st state of the United States, it should also not become the 28th member state of the European Union.
一些加拿大官員似乎也不太喜歡這個稱謂，因為成為歐盟成員意味著放棄部分主權，而這恰恰與卡尼所承諾的相反。加拿大保守黨反對黨領袖抓住這一說法大做文章，認為如果加拿大永遠不會成為美國的第51個州，那麼它也不應該成為歐盟的第28個成員國。
“There’s a lot of good will, but once one starts to look just a little bit closer, one also finds that many Europeans really don’t know Canada all that well, and they maybe aren’t fully taking on board Canada’s own sensitivities to sovereignty,” said Mr. Lesser.
「雙方有很多善意，但一旦仔細觀察就會發現，許多歐洲人其實並不十分了解加拿大，他們可能也沒有充分意識到加拿大自身對於主權問題的敏感，」萊瑟說。
Hours after Ms. von der Leyen spoke on Wednesday, the incoming Canadian ambassador to the European Union said that labels did not matter — but suggested that Canada preferred “alliance for the future” to describe the emerging new bond with the European Union. That phrase also appeared in Ms. von der Leyen’s speech.
馮德萊恩週三發表講話數小時後，即將上任的加拿大駐歐盟大使喬納森·威爾金森說，稱謂並不重要——但他暗示，加拿大更傾向於用「面向未來的聯盟」來描述正在形成的這種新關係。這一說法也出現在馮德萊恩的講話中。
“I think we’re still a ways away from actually landing on what we think the best way to describe it is,” said Jonathan Wilkinson.
「我認為，我們距離真正確定我們認為描述它的最佳方式還有一段路要走，」他說。
Three European diplomats said that the title “associate member” may not stick.
三名歐洲外交官說，「准成員國」這一稱謂未必會最終保留下來。
Old World problems?
舊世界的問題？
Officials from both sides are working to find tangible progress that they hope to announce at a European Union-Canada summit scheduled to take place in Montreal at the end of October.
雙方官員正在努力尋找切實進展，並希望在定於10月底在蒙特婁舉行的歐盟-加拿大峰會上宣布這些成果。
Two officials, one from the European Union and one from Canada, with knowledge of the talks, said that officials were trying hard to reach that goal, but that work had been slow and difficult, in part because it is highly technical.
兩名了解談判情況的官員（一名來自歐盟，一名來自加拿大）說，雙方正在努力實現這一目標，但進展緩慢且困難，部分原因是談判涉及大量技術性問題。
Just how hard it can be to get the European Union to agree on anything has been displayed frequently over the years. For example, talks over a trade agreement with Mercosur, the South American bloc, lasted a quarter century.
讓歐盟達成一致有多難多年來已顯而易見。例如，與南方共同市場這個南美集團的貿易協議談判持續了四分之一個世紀。
Canada has its own experience with these E.U. rigidities — and has some of its own. Its free-trade agreement with the bloc, in effect in practice for a decade, has still not been ratified by 10 of the 27 member states, for various reasons.
加拿大自己也經歷過歐盟這種僵化的體制，而且它自己也存在類似問題。加拿大與歐盟的自由貿易協定實際上已經實施了10年，但出於各種原因，歐盟27個成員國中仍有10個尚未批准該協定。
Canada already participates in a handful of E.U. ventures, including Horizon, a technology and innovation research effort, and SAFE, a defense industrial program. It had to pay $100 million to participate in Horizon, and participation in other E.U. programs is also likely to require Canada to pay into a budget.
加拿大已經參與了幾個歐盟項目，包括「地平線歐洲」計劃這一技術與創新研究項目，以及「歐洲安全行動」這一國防工業項目。加拿大為參加「地平線歐洲」支付了1億美元，而要參與歐盟其他項目，加拿大很可能也需要向相關預算繳納費用。
廣告
Big ideas
宏大構想
One more immediate benefit for Canada from closer ties to Europe may be in securing funding from the European Investment Bank. The major development bank, which is owned by the E.U. member states and has more than $630 billion in assets, is eager and ready to invest in Canadian projects, particularly in critical raw materials, and its leader is heading to Ottawa on Sunday to advance talks with the Canadian government.
加拿大加強與歐洲的聯繫，眼下可能獲得的另一項直接好處是從歐洲投資銀行獲得資金。這家大型開發銀行由歐盟成員國共同擁有，資產超過6300億美元，目前迫切希望投資加拿大的項目，尤其是關鍵原材料領域。該行行長將於週日前往渥太華，推動與加拿大政府的談判。
The bank is ready to start work in Canada, a European official with knowledge of the preparations said, but needed Ottawa to approve certain administrative formalities such as allowing E.U. diplomats to work there.
一名了解相關籌備工作的歐洲官員說，這家銀行已經準備好在加拿大開展工作，但需要加拿大政府先批准一些行政手續，例如允許歐盟外交官在加拿大開展工作。
Some of the ideas being discussed ahead of the October summit show the ambitious scope of the Canada-E.U. future relationship.
在10月峰會之前討論的一些設想顯示出加拿大與歐盟對於雙方未來關係的雄心有多大。
For example, officials said, Mr. Carney wants the bloc to work with Canada to build systems for payments and cloud computing that will offer an alternative to those of the United States in a bid to secure sovereignty.
例如，官員們說，卡尼希望歐盟與加拿大合作建立支付和雲計算系統，提供美國系統的替代方案，以維護加拿大的主權。
Discussions have also been underway on a digital trade agreement, which could deepen cooperation on cybersecurity and regulatory cooperation.
雙方還一直在討論一項數字貿易協定，該協定可能進一步深化雙方在網路安全和監管合作方面的協作。
Talks are more advanced to bring Canadian regulations in line with European ones in energy, including the monitoring, reporting and evaluation system used by the bloc to track methane emissions from vessels fueled by liquefied natural gas, as well as gas imports entering the European Union market. This is a necessity if Canada is going to export gas to the European Union.
在能源領域，使加拿大法規與歐洲法規保持一致的談判進展更快，其中包括歐盟用來追蹤以液化天然氣為燃料的船舶以及進入歐盟市場的天然氣進口所產生甲烷排放的監測、報告和評估系統。如果加拿大要向歐盟出口天然氣，這是必要的。
Another area where Canada is particularly eager to see movement is the ability of its citizens to spend longer stretches of time in the European Union visa-free, have their professional qualifications recognized there and be able to study in European universities.
加拿大特別希望看到進展的另一個領域，是其公民能夠免簽在歐盟停留更長時間、其專業資格在那裡得到承認，並能夠在歐洲大學學習。
Because these policy areas hinge on complex and politically fraught immigration rules, major breakthroughs could be difficult. Additionally, any agreement improving Canadians’ access to Europe would require reciprocity, meaning that it would grant the same access to Canada for 450 million Europeans.
由於這些政策領域取決於複雜且政治上敏感的移民規則，要取得重大突破可能會很困難。此外，任何改善加拿大人進入歐洲機會的協議都需要遵循對等原則，也就是說，也要給予4.5億歐洲人同樣的進入加拿大機會。
Some European diplomats have suggested that Erasmus — Europe’s popular student exchange program — could be an easier starting place for collaboration.
一些歐洲外交官提出，歐洲廣受歡迎的學生交流項目「伊拉斯謨」計劃可能是開展合作比較容易的切入點。

Matina Stevis-Gridneff是時報加拿大分社社長，領導對加拿大的報導。
