中國不在乎人工智慧安全，它只想贏

China Cares More About A.I. Victory Than Safety

SETH CENTER • 2026-09-17
The United States is preparing for its artificial intelligence dialogue with China, and our officials are under tremendous pressure from many sincere people to cooperate on A.I. risk with our foremost strategic competitor. Because American and Chinese companies are building the most powerful models, these people argue that an agreement, even a treaty, between the superpowers is humanity’s last backstop against potential catastrophe.
美國正籌備與中國的人工智慧對話，而許多懷著良好初衷的人正在向我國官員施加巨大的壓力，要求在人工智慧風險領域與我們首要的戰略競爭對手展開合作。由於美中兩國企業正在構建最強大的模型，這些人認為，超級大國之間達成協議甚至條約是人類防範潛在災難的最後防線。
I get the logic. But as the former State Department envoy who co-led the first U.S.-China A.I. dialogue, I fear the coming talks will not save us. If the past is prologue, China will have little interest in anything beyond gaining a competitive advantage.
我理解這種邏輯。但是，作為曾共同主導首輪美中人工智慧對話的前國務院特使，我擔心即將舉行的會談無法拯救我們。如果歷史可以作為參考，那麼除了獲取競爭優勢，中國對其他任何事情都不會太感興趣。
The agenda of the May 2024 meeting was crystal clear to both sides because our presidents had agreed to it: Focus on the risks of advanced A.I. systems and the potential for safety cooperation. Yet Chinese officials steered talks away from the potential risks of A.I. systems for hours. They even demurred from explaining their domestic A.I. regulations — despite having regulators in the room.
2024年5月那次會議的議程對雙方而言都極為明確，因為兩國元首已對此達成一致：重點關注高級人工智慧系統的風險及安全合作的潛力。然而，在長達數小時的時間裡，中方官員一直迴避人工智慧系統的潛在風險問題。他們甚至不願解釋其國內的人工智慧法規——儘管現場就有監管官員。
Instead, China chided us for our concerns about A.I. risks. They portrayed our safety approach as a barrier to unlocking the technology’s opportunity and a way to stop poorer countries from accessing A.I. tools. They unfurled proposals for subdialogues that would have most likely resulted in endless procedural diplomacy.
中方反而因我們對人工智慧風險的擔憂而指責我們。他們將我們的安全策略描繪成阻礙釋放技術機遇的屏障，以及阻止較貧窮國家獲取人工智慧工具的手段。他們還拋出了關於舉行子對話的提議，這極有可能引發無休止的程序性外交。

A moment outside a hotel conference room sticks in my mind. A Chinese counterpart turned to me with a puzzled expression and asked why we actually wanted to talk about A.I. risk. We took the mandate from our presidents seriously. They saw only the opportunity to jockey for strategic advantage.
酒店會議室外的一個瞬間令我記憶猶新。一位中方對等官員面帶困惑地轉向我，問我們究竟為何想談論人工智慧風險。我們認真履行了兩國元首的授權。他們只看到了爭奪戰略優勢的機會。
In this month's meetings, I expect China to again downplay safety risks. It could dangle the possibility of safety cooperation in exchange for concessions such as the softening of chip controls. I think it will continue to portray itself as an altruistic savior dedicated to ensuring that the developing world gains A.I. access. Meanwhile, it will be likely to keep doing everything it can to get ahead — like generating propaganda in the hopes of inflaming opposition to data centers and encouraging Chinese companies to continue exploiting U.S. models to improve their systems.
在本月的會議上，我預計中國將再次淡化安全風險。它可能會以安全合作的可能性為籌碼，換取諸如放寬晶片管制等讓步。我認為，中國將繼續把自己塑造成一個無私的救世主，致力於確保發展中國家獲得人工智慧的使用權。與此同時，它可能會繼續想方設法爭取領先——例如製造宣傳，試圖煽動對數據中心的反對情緒，並鼓勵中國企業繼續利用美國模型來改進自身系統。
Maybe China will toss its old playbook. Even President Xi is talking about risk and loss of control in A.I. Vivid new incidents show that A.I. systems can slip the leash of human control. Fear may converge with interest to produce coordinated action. Or at least a new understanding.
也許中國會摒棄舊的套路。甚至中國國家主席習近平也在談論人工智慧的風險和失控問題。一些新近發生的生動事件表明，人工智慧系統可能會掙脫人類的控制。恐懼可能會與利益交匯，從而促成協調一致的行動，或者至少促成一種新的共識。
But if Beijing now takes the risks of A.I. seriously, the message has not reached its domestic companies. The Chinese government touts the global adoption of its open models, but Chinese models do not currently match U.S. models in safety performance that matters — thwarting access to the most dangerous capabilities. Its companies rarely disclose these risks and say almost nothing about signs that their models, too, might act recklessly beyond human control.
但是，如果說北京現在正認真對待人工智慧的風險，那麼這一信息並未傳達到其國內企業。中國政府宣揚其開放性模型在全球被採用，但中國模型目前在關鍵的安全表現（即阻止獲取最危險的能力）上無法與美國模型相提並論。中國企業很少披露這些風險，對其模型也可能超出人類控制而魯莽行事的跡象幾乎閉口不談。
Complicating this already muddy situation for U.S. officials are the A.I. scientists, politicians, Wall Street and technology heavy hitters demanding an immediate A.I. alliance to thwart rogue A.I. I worry China will take advantage of these individuals’ sincerity, especially if diplomacy fails to produce a comprehensive safety agreement. Beijing could feign seriousness, float a hollow offer, then pin blame for its failure on the United States. The result could spark internal debates that weaken the core bipartisan consensus that the United States must lead in all facets of A.I., making it easier for China to forge ahead in the global technology competition.
對於美國官員來說，人工智慧科學家、政界人士、華爾街和科技界重量級人物紛紛要求立即建立人工智慧聯盟，以防範失控的人工智慧，這使原本就錯綜複雜的局面變得更加棘手。我擔心中國會利用這些人的真誠，特別是如果外交努力未能促成一項全面的安全協議。北京可能會假裝態度嚴肅，拋出一個空洞的提議，然後將其失敗的責任推給美國。其結果可能會引發內部辯論，削弱「美國必須在人工智慧各個領域保持領先」這一核心的兩黨共識，從而使中國更容易在全球技術競爭中佔據上風。
What’s more, no durable diplomatic agreement can be reached while domestic policy is under construction. Companies must first start with technical and oversight solutions, which can be turned into policies by the government and only then translated into international agreement. Rather than press American officials for a diplomatic solution that cannot be realized, safety advocates with influential voices should push developers in both countries to raise standards, invest real money in research to build safer A.I. and increase transparency and reporting. And they should continue unofficial conversations with Chinese business leaders and former officials — a step that could encourage the government to act.
更重要的是，在國內政策仍在制定中的情況下，無法達成持久的外交協議。企業必須首先從技術和監管解決方案著手，這些方案可由政府轉化為政策，隨後才能轉化為國際協議。具有影響力的安全倡導者與其向美國官員施壓以尋求無法實現的外交解決方案，不如推動兩國開發者提高標準，投入真金白銀研發更安全的人工智慧，並提升透明度與報告水平。此外，他們還應繼續與中國商業領袖及前官員進行非正式對話——這一舉措可能會促使政府採取行動。

That’s not to say these summits can’t accomplish anything. The United States can and should test China’s commitment to addressing A.I. risk and hold its developers to the same transparency and safety performance benchmarks that American developers follow. Future talks could verify that progress has been made.
這並不是說這些峰會毫無建樹。美國能夠且應當檢驗中國應對人工智慧風險的承諾，並要求其開發者遵守與美國開發者相同的透明度及安全表現基準。未來的會談可以驗證是否已取得進展。
If both sides meet in earnest, the very best I could envision is a nonbinding commitment in which each side would agree to put pressure on its companies to publish and improve safety benchmarking for cybersecurity and biological risks, and to disclose (rather than bury) reports of concerning incidents. Both sides could also affirm their existing shared commitments on A.I. safety, including the 2024 agreement to maintain human control over nuclear weapons decisions. This will be a multiyear endeavor; countries should begin by affirming they are players in a longer arc of progress.
如果雙方誠意會晤，我能設想的最佳情況是達成一項不具約束力的承諾，即雙方同意向各自企業施壓，要求其發布並完善針對網路安全和生物風險的安全基準，並披露（而非掩蓋）引發擔憂的事件報告。雙方還可以重申現有的人工智慧安全共同承諾，包括2024年達成的關於維持人類對核武器決策控制的協議。這將是一項歷時多年的努力；各國應首先確認自身是這一長遠進程的參與者。
I realize this sounds underwhelming for those who see catastrophe ahead. But considering the rigidity of great power diplomacy and the immaturity of domestic policy, this would be an achievement against which to measure whatever comes next.
我意識到，對於那些預見未來會發生災難的人而言，這聽起來或許有些平淡無奇。但是，考慮到大國外交的刻板性以及國內政策的不成熟，這將是一項成就，足以為衡量後續任何進展提供標尺。
I am confident U.S. officials understand the stakes, as well as the tactics of their counterparts. And I hope that their efforts to find a balance will be supported by our A.I. safety community. At home, the core work must continue to build a path that all countries can sign on to.
我深信美國官員了解其中的利害關係，也清楚對手的策略。我希望他們為尋求平衡所做的努力能得到我們的人工智慧安全社區的支持。在國內，核心工作必須繼續推進，以開闢一條所有國家都能認同的道路。
