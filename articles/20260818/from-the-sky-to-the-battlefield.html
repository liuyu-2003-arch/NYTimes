烏克蘭無人機何以精準打擊俄羅斯本土？

How Satellite Advances Are Changing the Game for Ukraine

MICHAEL SCHWIRTZ, TYLER HICKS • 2026-08-18
The Russian drone pilots had bedded down for the night. Their white vehicle was parked in the driveway of a small house in occupied southern Ukraine.
俄方無人機操作員已經躺下睡覺了。他們那輛白色汽車停在烏克蘭南部俄佔區一棟小房子的車道上。
About 30 miles away, two Ukrainian drone pilots had parked their truck in a tree line at the edge of a field and assembled a medium-size drone loaded with two bombs.
約50公里外，兩名烏克蘭無人機操作員將他們的卡車停在一片田地邊緣的林帶裡，組裝了一架中型無人機，上面掛載了兩枚炸彈。
Their target? The sleeping Russian drone pilots.
他們的目標？正在睡覺的俄方無人機操作員。
The Ukrainian team had a powerful new battlefield tool that night: high-tech satellite imagery providing near real-time information on troop movements and locations.
那天晚上，烏方的行動小組擁有一項強大的戰場新工具：高科技衛星影像，可以近乎實時地提供俄軍的移動和位置情報。
廣告
A year ago, it could take days for satellite imagery to get to frontline troops, slowed by less sophisticated technology and layers of command structure. That made it difficult to hit targets that can move locations, like troops in a house, with long-range drone strikes. Now, a support team monitoring the house provided the drone operators a fresh satellite image showing the car in the driveway, an indication the Russians were there.
一年前，衛星圖像可能需要數天才能傳到前線部隊手中，因為當時的技術不夠先進，而且指揮層級過多。這使得遠程無人機打擊難以命中那些可能會移動位置的目標——比如藏身於房屋中的部隊。而現在，一個負責監視該房屋的後援團隊向無人機操作員提供了一張最新的衛星圖像，顯示車道上停著那輛車，這意味著俄方人員就在裡面。
“The target is confirmed,” whispered one of the pilots, who identified himself with the call sign Tikhi, in accordance with Ukrainian military protocol.
「目標已確認。」其中一名操作員低聲說道。按照烏克蘭軍方規定，他只透露自己的呼號為「季基」。
The rapid delivery of high-quality satellite images to frontline troops is one of the key battlefield advancements of the last year, enabling Ukraine to strike quickly against weapons depots, logistical hubs and troop encampments deep within territory held by Russia, and in Russia itself.
將高質量衛星圖像快速送達前線部隊是過去一年戰場上的關鍵技術進步之一。這使得烏克蘭能夠迅速打擊俄佔區的縱深腹地乃至俄羅斯本土的武器庫、後勤樞紐和部隊營地。
The truck used by Tikhi and his partner, known by the call sign Teplo, was in fact a mobile command center, loaded with computers that connect to commercial satellites.
季基和他代號「捷普洛」的搭檔使用的卡車實際上是一個移動指揮中心，配備多台連接商用衛星的電腦設備。
In one new tool, developed by the Dutch company Bravo1Alpha, images are beamed to a device that looks like a hand-held video game console, from which pilots can search for targets, plot flight paths and even fire weapons from drones.
荷蘭公司Bravo1Alpha開發的一項新工具將圖像傳送到一個看起來像掌上遊戲機的設備上，操作人員可以通過它搜索目標、規劃飛行路線，甚至控制無人機發射武器。
Frontline troops in some cases can control satellites themselves, quickly analyzing the images for targets, and then firing their weapons. Rapid satellite imagery also helps them verify which targets have been hit and fire again if need be.
在某些情況下，前線部隊甚至可以自行操控衛星，迅速分析衛星圖像尋找目標，然後發動攻擊。快速獲取衛星影像還可以幫助他們確認哪些目標已被擊中，並在需要時再次發動攻擊。
Troops still need approval from more senior commanders to hit targets, reducing the risk of error by less experienced pilots. But the process is now much more streamlined.
不過，部隊仍然需要得到更高級別指揮官的批准才能打擊目標，從而降低經驗不足的操作員出錯的風險。但整個流程如今已經大為簡化。
These advances have underpinned Ukraine’s efforts to isolate the Russian-occupied Crimean Peninsula. Recent satellite-aided strikes have destroyed ferry terminals that Russia needs to supply the peninsula, and the logistical routes needed to keep troops fighting in southern Ukraine armed and fed.
這些進步為烏克蘭孤立俄羅斯佔領的克里米亞半島的努力提供了支撐。近期，烏軍藉助衛星的打擊摧毀了俄羅斯為該半島提供補給所需的渡輪碼頭，以及維持俄軍在烏克蘭南部作戰部隊的武器和給養後勤路線。
In June, soldiers from Ukraine’s 422nd Separate Unmanned Systems Regiment used satellite imagery to target and take out several installations in the occupied Zaporizhzhia region that Russia uses to jam Starlink signals, which many Ukrainian drones use for navigation. The regiment also recently took part in an operation to destroy several expensive Russian air defense systems in Crimea, among other targets.
6月，烏克蘭第422獨立無人系統團的士兵利用衛星影像，鎖定並摧毀了俄羅斯在被佔領的扎波羅熱地區部署的數個用於干擾星鏈信號的設施。許多烏克蘭無人機依靠星鏈進行導航。該部隊最近還參與了一項行動，在克里米亞摧毀了多套造價高昂的俄羅斯防空系統，以及其他一些目標。
“This is incredibly helpful support,” said Maj. Mykola Kolesnyk, the commander of the regiment.
「這是極為寶貴的支援，」該團指揮官尼古拉·科列斯尼克少校說。
廣告
With the war in Ukraine largely mired in a bloody stalemate, progress on the battlefield increasingly comes down to a race for new technologies that can give one side an edge. Drones, whether in the air, on land or at sea, have emerged as the most effective way to kill or maim enemies.
由於烏克蘭戰爭基本上陷入血腥的僵持狀態，戰場上的進展越來越取決於誰能率先掌握新技術以獲得優勢。無人機——無論是空中、陸地還是海上——已成為殺死或重創敵人的最有效手段。
But the technology is less efficient when it comes to finding them. Surveillance drones, known as I.S.R.s, can be shot down or their signals jammed. Their ranges are limited, as is the physical area they can cover.
但在發現敵人方面，這項技術的效率卻沒那麼高。偵察無人機可能被擊落或信號遭到干擾。它們的航程和覆蓋範圍也有限。
Space offers the next frontier.
太空是下一個前沿陣地。
Ukrainians used satellite imagery, provided by allied governments and commercial enterprises, to aid their fight against Russia even before the invasion of 2022. But until recently it has been a scarce commodity. Only senior commanders were given access to use it in high-level strategic planning.
早在2022年俄烏戰爭全面爆發之前，烏克蘭就已經利用盟國政府和商業企業提供的衛星影像來對抗俄羅斯。但直到最近，衛星影像仍然是一種稀缺資源。只有高級指揮官才能獲得這類信息，用於高層戰略規劃。
Even that access was limited and vulnerable to disruption. In March 2025, for example, President Trump ordered the Pentagon to stop sharing satellite imagery with Ukraine, though this was eventually restored.
即使是這種訪問權限也受到限制，而且容易受到干擾。。例如，2025年3月，川普總統下令五角大廈停止與烏克蘭共享衛星圖像，後來才恢復。
In the last year or so, access to satellite imagery has expanded greatly, provided by the U.S. government and private companies. Vantor, a commercial company that has worked with Ukraine for years, recently launched six new satellites, bringing the total to 10. Its satellites can now cover up to seven million square kilometers of the planet daily, enough to scan the Ukrainian battlefield many times over, said Will Cocos, the company’s chief transformation officer.
過去一年左右，烏克蘭獲得衛星影像的管道大為擴展，提供方包括美國政府和私人企業。商業公司Vantor多年來一直與烏克蘭合作，最近又發射了六顆新衛星，使其衛星總數達到10顆。公司首席轉型官威爾·科科斯表示，這些衛星如今每天最多可以覆蓋全球700萬平方公里的區域，足以對烏克蘭戰場進行多次掃描。
The satellites can provide images of the same area up to 15 times per day, helping soldiers look for subtle changes in the terrain that might indicate possible Russian targets.
這些衛星每天最多可以對同一地區拍攝15次，幫助士兵發現地形中細微的變化，這些變化可能意味著那裡可能出現了俄軍目標。
More important for Ukraine’s progress on the front, Mr. Cocos said, was the expanded access to battlefield troops.
科科斯說，對烏克蘭前線戰事幫助更大的是，前線部隊獲取圖像的門檻大大降低了。
Images can be obtained in as little as 15 minutes, though more often it can take up to a few hours. This is still an enormous improvement over the recent past, when it would sometimes take days.
最快只需15分鐘就能獲得影像，不過更常見的情況是需要幾個小時。即便如此，與不久前有時需要數天相比，這仍然是巨大的進步。
廣告
“We essentially hand the satellite over to them,” Mr. Cocos said. “They tell it what to shoot.”
「基本上，我們是把衛星直接交給他們使用，」科科斯說。「他們告訴衛星要拍什麼。」
The advent of drone warfare has changed the look of the battlefield since the beginning of this war. Gone are the large concentrations of armor, logistical hubs and groupings of soldiers that could easily be seen and targeted.
自戰爭爆發以來，無人機作戰的出現已改變了戰場的面貌。過去那種容易被發現和攻擊的大規模裝甲部隊、後勤樞紐和士兵集群已經不復存在。
“In our regiment, we haven’t seen a tank in six months,” Major Kolesnyk said. “The ones they have are tucked away in shelters.”
「我們團已經六個月沒見過坦克了，」科列斯尼克少校說。「他們的坦克都藏在掩體裡。」
Major Kolesnyk insisted on meeting in a tree line outside Zaporizhzhia, in southeastern Ukraine, rather than in a command post or the city itself, fearful of the drones circling overhead.
科列斯尼克少校堅持在烏克蘭南部扎波羅熱市外的一片林帶見面，而不是在指揮所或市區，因為他擔心頭頂上盤旋的無人機。
Commercial satellite companies maintain huge archives of images over time, allowing field commanders to study subtle changes in the environment — tracks in a field, fallen trees, newly cleared land — that might indicate a target being hidden.
商業衛星公司長期保存著大量衛星影像檔案，因此前線指揮官可以研究環境中細微的變化——比如田地裡的車轍、倒下的樹木、新近清理過的土地，這些都可能意味著有目標隱藏其中。
“Overall, this feels like a revolution in military technology,” said Robert Tollast, a military expert at the Royal United Services Institute, a think tank in London.
「總體而言，這感覺就像是一場軍事技術革命，」倫敦智庫皇家聯合軍種國防研究所軍事專家羅伯特·托拉斯特說。
In the tree line, the two Ukrainian drone operators monitoring the house with the Russians awaited confirmation of their target. The soldier known as Teplo pulled up video on his phone of a recent strike using satellite assistance. It showed a thicket of trees in the Kherson region with no discernible military targets. But after studying the satellite images over a period of weeks, soldiers discovered tracks leading into the trees that weren’t visible in earlier images. In another video, the same thicket had become a hellscape of burning vehicles.
在那片林帶裡，兩名烏克蘭無人機操作員一邊監視著那棟藏有俄方人員的房子，一邊等待目標得到確認。代號捷普洛的士兵在手機上播放了一段近期藉助衛星支援實施打擊的影片。畫面中是赫爾松地區一片茂密的樹林，看不出有什麼明顯的軍事目標。但經過數週對衛星圖像的研究，士兵們發現了一些通往樹林深處的車轍，而它們在更早的影像中並不存在。在另一段影片中，同一片樹林已化作車輛燃燒的地獄景象。
“It’s an ideal operation,” Teplo said. “Actually, it’s frightening. Nothing can help you.”
「這是一次理想的行動，」捷普洛說。「其實，這很可怕。沒有什麼能幫得了你。」
廣告
At the edge of the field, it was eerily tranquil, with the sound of crickets only occasionally interrupted by explosive booms in the distance. But the pilots were tense and on alert for enemy drones. Odd sounds made them flinch. The pilot known as Tikhi had a bandaged hand after a recent run-in with a Russian FPV drone.
田地邊緣一場安靜，只有蟋蟀的叫聲偶爾被遠處傳來的爆炸聲打斷。但兩名操作員始終高度緊張，警惕著敵方無人機。任何異常的聲響都會讓他們神經一緊。季基手上還纏著繃帶，那是他最近一次遭遇俄羅斯的一架第一人稱視角無人機時留下的傷。
The first attempt to take out the house with the Russian pilots was interrupted by a Russian bombardment. Shahed drones and powerful glide bombs streaked through the sky. The electronic warfare equipment Ukraine was using to scramble the Russian weapons’ GPS signals also prevented the Ukrainian team from launching their weapon.
第一次試圖摧毀那棟藏有俄方無人機操作員的房子的行動被俄軍的炮擊打斷了。沙赫德無人機和威力強大的滑翔炸彈劃過天空。烏克蘭方面使用電子戰設備干擾俄軍武器的GPS信號，但這些設備同時也干擾了烏方自己的武器系統，使他們無法發射無人機。
Several nights later, it was quieter, and the team could finally strike.
幾天後的一個夜晚，情況終於平靜了一些，行動小組終於能夠發動攻擊了。
A satellite image generated after the attack showed the damage: two large holes through the roof of the house.
襲擊後生成的一張衛星圖像顯示了破壞情況：那棟房子的屋頂被炸出了兩個大洞。
