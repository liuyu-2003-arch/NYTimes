AI垃圾滿天飛，科技公司開展人工智慧廢物大掃除

Sick of A.I. Slop? So Are Tech Giants.

TIFFANY HSU • 2026-08-18
The worst of artificial intelligence slop has cluttered Google searches with recipes for glue-topped pizza, Amazon listings with sham biographies and Facebook feeds with images of Shrimp Jesus.
人工智慧最糟糕的垃圾內容已經用膠水披薩食譜淹沒了谷歌搜索，用虛假傳記塞滿了亞馬遜商品列表，用大蝦耶穌圖片充斥於Facebook信息流。
The digital sewage heap has sprawled across tech platforms, cheapening meaningful content and polluting the information ecosystem, as chatbots and video generators continue to advance.
這片數字污水池已經在科技平台上蔓延開來，令有意義的內容變得廉價，污染了信息生態系統，而聊天機器人和影片生成器仍在步步推進。
Now, Silicon Valley wants to take out the trash.
現在，矽谷想要清除這些垃圾。
Spotify, a music streamer, said it removed 75 million bulk uploads, duplicate songs and other “spammy” tracks last year — a sizable chunk of its total portfolio. LinkedIn said in July that “A.I. slop is a top priority for all of us” and developed a button for users to report it. Researchers from YouTube’s parent company, Google, described how a video service wiped 130,000 channels of low-quality content off its platform over six months.
音樂流媒體平台Spotify表示去年下架了7500萬條批量上傳、重複歌曲和其他「垃圾」音樂——這占其總曲庫的相當大一部分。領英在7月表示，「應對AI垃圾內容是我們所有人的首要任務，」並開發了一個供用戶舉報的按鈕。YouTube母公司谷歌的研究人員描述了該影片平台如何在六個月內清除了13萬個內容質量低劣的頻道。
Across the internet, tech companies are undertaking a generative waste detox, overwhelmed in many cases by the same slop first made possible by their own A.I. tools. Mobile messaging apps, restaurant review sites, scholarly archives and dating services are all rooting out or downplaying junk generated by A.I. — many relying on the same technology to pull off the purge.
在整個互聯網，科技公司正在進行一場生成式廢物大掃除，在許多情況下，它們已被自家AI工具最初所催生的同樣垃圾內容所淹沒。移動消息應用、餐廳點評網站、學術檔案庫和婚戀服務都在根除或降級AI生成的垃圾內容——其中許多依賴同樣的技術來完成這次清理。
People are tiring of A.I.’s effect on the environment and its pressure on jobs and mental health. A.I. slop is just another “festering wound,” said Aidan Walker, a content creator and researcher studying online trends for the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace, a global affairs think tank.
人們已經開始厭倦AI對環境的影響、對就業和心理健康造成的壓力。全球事務智庫卡內基國際和平基金會研究員、內容創作者艾丹·沃克表示，AI垃圾內容只是又一個「潰爛的傷口」。
廣告
“Users have gradually gotten more and more upset about it,” he said. “The climate of opinion has now recognizably shifted, so these companies are finally confronting these problems.”
「用戶漸漸對此越來越不滿，」他說，「輿論氛圍已經明顯轉變，所以這些公司終於開始正視這些問題。」
Generative A.I. can produce increasingly realistic images, audio and videos, but only some of it qualifies as slop. Tech companies are largely targeting the kind of low-grade clickbait that is easy to make, spread, consume and forget — the fast fashion edging out couture, the pink slime subsuming the steak.
生成式AI能夠製作出越來越逼真的圖像、音頻和影片，但其中只有一部分算得上垃圾內容。科技公司主要針對的是那種低質量的點擊誘餌——容易製作、傳播、消費和遺忘——就像快時尚擠掉了高級定製，粉紅肉渣取代了牛排。
Unchecked, the synthetic swamp online could “crowd out human creativity, corrode cultural value and destabilize public discourse,” European researchers wrote last year.
如果不加遏制，網上的合成沼澤可能會「排擠人類創造力，腐蝕文化價值，並破壞公共話語」，歐洲研究人員去年寫道。
Slop has become so prevalent that Merriam-Webster, the dictionary publisher, chose it as its word of the year in 2025. Deezer, a French streaming service, said this spring that nearly half of the tracks updated to its system each day were artificially generated, many of them never heard by users. A.I. generates more than a third of what is uploaded to Apple Music.
AI垃圾內容已經變得如此普遍，以至於詞典出版商韋氏公司在2025年將其選為年度詞彙。法國流媒體服務Deezer今年春天表示，每天更新到其系統的曲目中近一半是生成的，其中許多從未被用戶聽過。在上傳到Apple Music的內容中，AI生成的音樂佔比在三分之一以上。
Adam Mosseri, the head of Instagram, said late last year that it would soon “be more practical” to trace “real media than fake media.” His observation came not long after Meta, Instagram’s parent company, rolled out an all-A.I. video stream derided by many as a showcase for slop.
Instagram負責人亞當·莫塞裡在去年年底表示，追蹤「真實媒體將比追蹤虛假媒體更實際」。他的觀察是在Meta（Instagram母公司）推出一個全AI影片流不久後發表的，該影片流被許多人嘲笑為垃圾內容的展示窗口。
Many tech companies now trying to manage A.I. slop are also the ones enabling it. In July, Meta promised to support a European pact to make A.I.-generated content more identifiable via detection, labeling and tagging. That same month, the company also released an image generator that automatically opted in public Instagram accounts as a reference for the tool. (Meta removed the feature after three days of backlash.)
許多現在試圖管理AI垃圾內容的科技公司也正是助長它的那批公司。7月，Meta承諾支持一項歐洲協議，通過檢測、標記和標註使AI生成的內容更容易識別。同月，該公司還發布了一款圖像生成器，自動將公開的Instagram帳戶作為該工具的參照。（經過連續三天的輿論抵制，Meta移除了該功能。）
廣告
“A.I. slop is a double-edged sword in that a lot of these platforms experienced upticks in engagement as a result of it,” said Paul Shovlin, an expert in A.I. at Ohio University. “But then, it got to a point where it became a liability because there’s so much flotsam and jetsam that it’s now hard for users to find the kind of quality that they’re looking for.”
「AI垃圾內容是一把雙刃劍，因為許多平台因此獲得了參與度的提升，」俄亥俄大學AI專家保羅·肖夫林表示，「但後來它變成了一種負擔，因為垃圾太多了，用戶現在很難找到他們想要的那種質量的內容。」
Google, for example, has gone all in on A.I., linking its Gemini model to its email, maps and travel services and search. More than 20 percent of the initial short videos on YouTube served to new users are A.I.-generated slop, according to an experiment conducted last year by Kapwing, an online video editor that is powered by A.I.
例如，谷歌已全力投入AI，將其Gemini模型與電子郵件、地圖和旅行服務以及搜索相連接。根據去年由在線影片編輯器Kapwing進行的一項實驗，YouTube上為新用戶推送的初始短片中有超過20%是AI生成的垃圾內容。
One of the most popular YouTube slop channels, Kapwing estimated, earned $4.25 million annually. (It has since been suspended from a YouTube monetization program.) Neal Mohan, YouTube’s chief executive, wrote in a blog post that “managing A.I. slop” was a top priority this year.
Kapwing估計，最受歡迎的YouTube垃圾內容頻道之一每年賺取425萬美元。（該頻道此後已被暫停參與YouTube的創收計劃。）YouTube首席執行官尼爾·莫漢在一篇部落格文章中寫道，「管理AI垃圾內容」是今年的首要任務。
Recently, researchers at Google considered the challenge. Rather than evaluate videos individually, they looked at upload pace, repetitive templates and other patterns that could signal coordinated abusive behavior. They found 50,000 clusters of accounts, which were removed. Some digital media experts, however, worried that the approach could overreach and also penalize legitimate content creators who publish large volumes of content.
最近，谷歌的研究人員考慮了這一挑戰。他們沒有逐個評估影片，而是著眼於上傳速度、重複模板和其他可能表明協同濫用行為的模式。他們發現了5萬個帳號群組，並將其移除。然而，一些數位媒體專家擔心這種方法可能矯枉過正，也會懲罰那些發布大量內容的合法創作者。
“While academic research helps advance industry understanding, it doesn’t reflect the many layers of defense we use to enforce our policies,” YouTube said in a statement. “This includes robust safeguards to ensure well-intentioned creators using A.I. tools aren’t impacted.”
「雖然學術研究有助於推動行業理解，但它並不能反映我們用於執行政策的許多層防禦措施，」YouTube在一份聲明中表示，「這包括強有力的保障措施，以確保使用AI工具的善意創作者不會受到影響。」
Other companies are turning to a grab bag of strategies to address A.I. slop, including rallying their own patrons to help identify it. In October, Pinterest updated its system to allow users to limit how many A.I.-generated posts they see. TikTok said it was testing a feature in the United States that lets people dial up or down the amount of visible A.I. content. (The company also said it had automatically labeled more than three billion A.I.-generated videos.) TikTok removed more than 377,000 videos in the first three months of the year for violating its A.I. policies.
其他公司正在轉向各種策略來應對AI垃圾內容，包括動員自己的用戶幫助識別。10月，Pinterest更新了系統，允許用戶限制他們看到的AI生成帖子數量。TikTok表示正在美國測試一項功能，讓用戶可以自行調節看到的AI內容量。（該公司還表示已自動標記了超過30億個AI生成的影片。）TikTok在今年前三個月刪除了超過37.7萬個違反其AI政策的影片。
A.I. was used to create nearly half of the posts with more than 50 words on X, Elon Musk’s social media platform, according to Pangram, an A.I. detection start-up. To deal with “a tremendous amount of A.I. slop & reply spam,” the social media platform this year banned apps that effectively encourage bots to post by paying for content, wrote Nikita Bier, who was X’s head of product until this month.
根據AI檢測初創公司Pangram的數據，在伊隆·馬斯克的社群媒體平台X上，字數超過50的帖子中有近一半是使用AI創建的。本月剛卸任X產品主管的尼基塔·比爾寫道，為了應對「大量的AI垃圾內容和回覆垃圾」，該社群媒體平台今年禁止了那些通過付費鼓勵機器人發帖的應用。
Chris Best, the chief executive of Substack, wrote last month that “it’s getting harder to tell what’s real on the internet,” and that “platforms that reward fakeness will create a race to the bottom.” The newsletter platform announced its own A.I. detection tool for users, powered by Pangram, which is designed to scan replies, comments and posts longer than 100 words and decide whether they were likely written by hand or with A.I. help.
Substack首席執行官克里斯·貝斯特上個月說，「在互聯網上越來越難分辨什麼是真實的，」並且「獎勵虛假內容的平台將導致逐底競爭」。這個通訊平台宣布為用戶推出自己的AI檢測工具，由Pangram提供支持，旨在掃描回覆、評論和超過100字的帖子，並判斷它們可能是人工撰寫還是藉助AI完成的。
廣告
Some newsletter writers complained that the tool mistook posts written by humans as A.I.-generated, did not account for A.I.’s potential role in the creative process and put undue pressure on authors to defend their reputations against an opaque metric.
一些通訊作者抱怨說，該工具將人類撰寫的帖子誤判為AI生成，沒有考慮到AI在創作過程中的潛在作用，並給作者帶來了不必要的壓力，要求他們根據一個不透明的標準來證明自己的清白。
This spring, Camille François and nearly two dozen other A.I. experts convened at a Slop Salon event in Menlo Park, Calif., where they discussed the difficulty of classifying, and thus managing, A.I. slop.
今年春天，卡米爾·弗朗索瓦和二十多位AI專家在加利福尼亞州門洛帕克的一次「垃圾沙龍」活動中會面，討論了分類並藉此管理AI垃圾內容的困難。
“The hodgepodge of tactics is a symptom that we’re looking at a systemic problem with our information environment and not just a bad class of content called slop that we can just define and remove,” Dr. François, who studies harmful digital technologies at Columbia University, said in an interview. “A.I. is a creativity tool at the end of the day, and multifaceted, and platforms that handle this in a poor way will see people walk and go to other places.”
「這種策略大雜燴表明，我們面臨的是信息環境中的一個系統性問題，而不僅僅是一類可以簡單定義和移除的名為垃圾內容的壞內容，」在哥倫比亞大學研究有害數位技術的弗朗索瓦接受採訪時說，「AI歸根結底是一種創造工具，而且具有多面性，那些處理不當的平台將會看到用戶流失並轉向其他地方。」
