世界如何看待美國期中選舉？

Countries Weigh, and Worry About, What Trump Will Do After Midterm Defeat

JIM TANKERSLEY, DAMIEN CAVE, ANNIE CORREAL • 2026-11-05
For two years, the world has weathered President Trump’s domineering unilateralism, enduring up-and-down tariffs, a war with Iran, aggression in Latin America, abrupt reversals on Ukraine, and a fusillade of demands for allies including Canada, Germany and Japan.
兩年來，世界經受了川普總統專橫的單邊主義，承受了起伏不定的關稅、對伊朗的戰爭、在拉丁美洲的侵入行動、在烏克蘭問題上的突然反轉，以及向包括加拿大、德國和日本在內的盟友提出的一連串要求。
Now, with Democrats having taken control of Congress as nearly all of Mr. Trump’s preferred Republicans fall to defeat, many countries are debating what to expect from yet another case of American political whiplash.
如今，隨著民主黨人取得國會控制權，且幾乎所有川普青睞的共和黨人均遭敗績，許多國家正探討美國的又一次政治劇變會帶來什麼。
Some analysts suggest a new Congress might slow President Trump’s most impulsive urges. Online, there was some obvious celebration. On Weibo, China’s dominant social media platform, the hashtag “Trump suffered a complete and utter defeat” was trending as results were announced.
一些分析人士指出，新一屆國會可能會抑制川普總統最衝動的念頭。網上出現了明顯的慶祝情緒。隨著結果公布，「川普全面慘敗」的標籤登上了中國主流社群媒體平台微博的熱搜。
But because presidents have enormous leeway with foreign policy, one persistent question emerged from Asia to Europe: Will the electoral losses at home spur Mr. Trump toward even more aggressive actions around the globe?
但由於總統在外交政策上擁有巨大的迴旋餘地，從亞洲到歐洲都浮現出一個持續存在的問題：國內選舉失利是否會刺激川普在全球採取更具侵略性的行動？
“In response to a Democrat-controlled Congress,” said Ja-Ian Chong, an associate professor of political science at the National University of Singapore, “Trump could try to find distractions elsewhere, including on tariffs and the use of force.”
新加坡國立大學政治學副教授庄嘉穎表示：「為了應對由民主黨控制的國會，川普可能會試圖把注意力吸引到別的地方，包括在關稅和使用武力方面。」
廣告
Countries around the world are assessing how the American elections results might affect the most pressing issues in international affairs.
世界各國正在評估美國選舉結果可能如何影響國際事務中最緊迫的議題。
War With Iran
與伊朗的戰爭
In Asia, which is heavily reliant on energy from the Middle East, the Democratic victory initially produced a wisp of optimism.
在嚴重依賴中東能源的亞洲，民主黨的勝利最初帶來了一絲樂觀情緒。
“In Southeast Asia, the main challenge has been inflationary pressure caused by the war in Iran. That’s made everything a lot more expensive,” Mr. Chong said. “Now there’s hope for some sort of reprieve.”
「在東南亞，主要的挑戰一直是伊朗戰爭引起的通膨壓力。這使得一切都變得昂貴得多，」庄嘉穎說，「現在有了獲得某種喘息的希望。」
And yet, he and many others doubt that notion. Many countries have expressed concern that the war with Iran would continue escalating in the short-term — perhaps with Mr. Trump striking Iran again after the elections — worsening the squeeze on oil and gas supplies.
然而，他和其他許多人對這種樂觀看法表示懷疑。許多國家已經表達擔憂，認為對伊戰爭在短期內會繼續升級——川普或許會在選舉後再次打擊伊朗——從而加劇油氣供應緊張。
For now, the mood remains more grim than gleeful: Oil prices are expected to stay high for the foreseeable future, challenging businesses and leaving many to wonder what Mr. Trump might do next.
目前，人們的情緒與其說是歡欣，不如說是凝重：預計油價在可預見的未來仍將保持高位，這給企業帶來挑戰，並讓許多人猜測川普下一步可能會做什麼。
Tariffs
關稅
U.S. trade investigators have spent months probing more than a dozen countries accused of labor violations and overproducing everything from chemicals to cars. Now that the White House is no longer concerned about drawing attention to inflation before the elections, countries might expect a reinvigorated push to impose tariffs.
美國貿易調查人員已花費數月時間，對十幾個國家展開調查，這些國家被指違反勞工規定，並在從化學品到汽車等各類產品上存在產能過剩。既然白宮不再擔心在選舉前引發外界對通膨的關注，各國可能預計美國將重新大力推進徵收關稅。
Japan, China and Vietnam are all likely targets for additional tariffs. In many countries, new levies would come on top of existing ones, lifting rates back toward where they were before the Supreme Court struck down earlier tariffs in February.
日本、中國和越南都是加徵關稅的潛在目標。在許多國家，新關稅將疊加在現有關稅之上，使稅率回升至2月份最高法院推翻先前關稅措施之前的水平。
Mr. Trump still has sweeping powers over trade. Congress, however, could use its newfound leverage over the federal budget and its oversight authority to shape some of the details.
川普在貿易問題上依然擁有廣泛權力。然而，國會可以利用其新獲得的聯邦預算籌碼及監督權，對部分細節施加影響。
“You might see Congress pushing for more tariff carve outs and exceptions in categories that affect their constituent’s pocket books,” said Stephen Olson, a former U.S. trade negotiator and visiting fellow at ISEAS – Yusof Ishak Institute in Singapore.
「你可能會看到國會推動在影響其選民錢包的類別中，爭取更多的關稅豁免和例外，」前美國貿易談判代表、新加坡尤索夫伊薩東南亞研究院客座高級研究員斯蒂芬·奧爾森說。
廣告
Generally, in Asia and elsewhere, many expect American protectionism to keep marching on, with a president seeking to leave a legacy and still determined to bend the global economy toward his views.
總體而言，在亞洲及其他地區，許多人預計美國的保護主義將繼續推進，因為這位總統正尋求留下政治遺產，且依然決心讓全球經濟屈從於他的主張。
“People are deeply uneasy about the Trump administration’s past actions and remain anxious about the future,” said Kazuhiro Maeshima, a professor of American politics and diplomacy at Sophia University in Tokyo, discussing the mood in Japan. “While there is a hope for change, whether that change will actually materialize is unknown.”
在談及日本的情緒時，東京上智大學美國政治與外交教授前島和弘說：「人們對川普政府過去的行動深感不安，並對未來保持焦慮。雖然對改變抱有希望，但這種改變是否能真正實現仍屬未知。」
Rivalry With China
與中國的競爭
The American elections were not expected to alter the direction of U.S.-China relations, especially regarding their on-again, off-again trade truce. Both political parties have increasingly agreed to take a tougher posture on China when it comes to economic competition and disputes over contested territory.
預計美國選舉不會改變美中關係的方向，尤其是在雙方斷斷續續的貿易休戰方面。在經濟競爭和爭議領土問題上，兩黨越來越認同對華採取更強硬的姿態。
But on Weibo, a leading Chinese social media platform, the early results led to some initial gloating at President Trump’s expense.Xu Shaolin, a prominent commentator with nearly 4 million followers, wrote that if the losses held in both houses of Congress “this unreliable president who talks big, but is prone to making empty promises, will likely become a lame duck.”
但在中國主流社群媒體平台微博上，初步結果一開始引起了一些對川普總統的幸災樂禍。擁有近400萬粉絲的知名評論員徐紹林寫道，如果兩院的失利成定局，「這個滿嘴跑火車不靠譜的總統，恐怕就會成為跛腳鴨。」
Shi Yinhong, a professor of international relations at Renmin University in Beijing, predicted nonetheless that the Democrats’ success would mean trouble for relations between the United States and China.
儘管如此，中國人民大學國際關係學院教授時殷弘預測，民主黨人的成功將意味著美中關係面臨麻煩。
廣告
Xi Jinping, China’s leader, and President Trump reached an understanding at a summit in May summit in favor of what China defines as “constructive, strategic stability.” But Mr. Shi predicted that this understanding would break down “because Trump suddenly becomes far from all-powerful.”
中國領導人習近平與川普總統在5月的一次峰會上達成諒解，支持中國所定義的「建設性戰略穩定」。但時殷弘預測這種諒解將會破裂，「因為川普突然變得遠非無所不能。」
A Democratic Congress might also strengthen America’s relations with traditional United States allies, including Japan, which he said would also not be good for China.
他表示，民主黨控制的國會也可能加強美國與包括日本在內傳統盟友的關係，這對中國也不利。
For Taiwan, the election results could be a “potentially positive sign,” according to William Yang, a senior analyst at the International Crisis Group in Taipei.
台北的國際危機組織高級分析師楊皓暐認為，對於台灣而言，選舉結果可能是一個「潛在的積極信號」。
He said he thought a Democrat-led Congress might intensify its pressure on the White House to approve a long-delayed $14 billion arms package that would be a boost to Taiwan’s defenses against Chinese military threats.
他表示，他認為由民主黨領導的國會可能會加大對白宮的壓力，促其批准一項被長期拖延的140億美元武器方案，這將提升台灣防禦中國軍事威脅的能力。
U.S. Alliances and Leadership
美國的聯盟與領導力
Mr. Trump has threatened his country’s own partners and allies with everything from tariffs to bombs. And in some of the once-friendly nations that Mr. Trump has alienated, the public reaction veered toward relief or celebration of the Democratic wins on Tuesday.
川普用從關稅到炸彈的各種手段威脅了本國的合作夥伴和盟友。週二，在一些被川普疏遠的前友好國家，公眾反應轉向了對民主黨獲勝的如釋重負或慶祝。
廣告
Ryan Cox, a philosophy lecturer at the University of Sydney, said Wednesday that he was hoping to see the Democrats “slow down the power of Trump,” to accelerate “the end of the Trump era.”
雪梨大學哲學講師萊恩·考克斯週三表示，他希望看到民主黨人「限制川普的權力」，以加速「川普時代的終結」。
Luka Leveque, a Japanese university student in Tokyo, said he was hoping “for greater consistency — both in the approach taken between our two nations and in the continuity of U.S. policy over time.”
東京的大學生盧卡·勒維克說，他希望「看到更好的一貫性——無論是在我們兩國採取的方法上，還是在隨著時間推移美國政策的連續性上」。
And yet, with two years of the Trump administration to go, skepticism about his transactional leadership style and reliability remains deeply entrenched. In a recent poll of 34 countries published by the Rockefeller Foundation, just 39 percent of respondents said they were comfortable with the United States leading on the world stage.
然而，由於川普政府還有兩年任期，外界對其交易型領導風格和可靠性的懷疑依然根深蒂固。在洛克菲勒基金會發布的一項涵蓋34個國家的近期民意調查中，只有39%的受訪者表示對美國在世界舞台上發揮領導作用感到安心。
As Max Yoeli, a senior research fellow at Chatham House in London, noted just before the election: “A president who has made it his business to be a shock to the system retains the capacity to deliver one.”
正如倫敦的查塔姆研究所高級研究員麥克斯·約埃利在選舉前指出的那樣：「一位將衝擊體製作為己任的總統依然保留著實施這種衝擊的能力。」
Even in Australia, which has fought alongside America in every major war since World War I, doubts remain. Dan Ramsay, a University of Sydney politics student, said Wednesday that it would be risky for any country to jump back into relying too heavily on the United States.
即便是在自一戰以來歷次重大戰爭中都與美國並肩作戰的澳洲，懷疑依然存在。雪梨大學政治學學生丹·拉姆齊週三表示，對任何國家來說，重新建立對美國的重度依賴都將是冒險的。
“We need to take more seriously our dependencies in a whole bunch of areas and think about how we can maintain an alliance and partnership with the U.S., while also looking elsewhere and strengthening those ties with middle powers around the world,” he said.
「我們需要更認真地對待我們在諸多領域的依賴，並思考我們如何在維持與美國的聯盟和夥伴關係的同時，將目光投向別處，並加強與世界各地中等強國的那些聯繫，」他說。
