「一夜爆紅」能否拯救她媽媽的中餐館？

Could a Viral Video Save Her Mom’s Chinese Restaurant?

ISABELLA KWAI • 2026-11-05
Sophie Li wiped the tables and swept the floors every day at China Star, even though few customers came through the door.
即使鮮有顧客上門，索菲·李每天仍會在麗都擦桌子、掃地，那是她開的中餐館。
For nearly 20 years, Mrs. Li’s Chinese restaurant in Edinburgh has been both her life’s work and her cross to bear. In better times, business was steady enough to support her and her two daughters and help send them to universities.
近20年來，位於蘇格蘭愛丁堡的這家中餐館既是索菲畢生的心血，也是她難以卸下的重擔。生意好的時候，餐館收入穩定，足以養活她和兩個女兒，還能供她們上大學。
But early this year, the restaurant was almost always empty. Still, Mrs. Li, approaching 70, scrimped and toiled seven days a week to keep China Star open.
但今年年初，餐館幾乎總是空空蕩蕩。儘管如此，年近70的索菲仍省吃儉用，每週七天辛勤勞作，維持麗都的經營。
“I am a person who can endure hardship better than humiliation,” she said.
「我這個人，寧可吃苦，也不願受辱。」她說。
Down in London, her younger daughter, Lisa He, worried about the toll on her mother’s health. She traveled up to Scotland and posted a plea on social media.
在倫敦，她的小女兒麗莎·賀擔心母親身體會拖垮。她專程北上蘇格蘭，並在社群媒體上發布影片求助。
“My mum’s Chinese restaurant is dying,” Ms. He, 29, said in a video on Instagram in April. She had decided to put aside several months to try to save it.
「我媽媽的中餐館快要倒閉了，」今年4月，29歲的麗莎在Instagram的一段影片中說。她決定抽出幾個月時間，設法挽救這家餐館。

“Follow the journey and let’s see if I can turn this around,” she said.
「關注接下來的故事，看看我能不能扭轉局面，」她說。
Within days, the post had been viewed millions of times. Influencers and sympathetic locals suddenly flocked to China Star, overwhelming the modest 13-table restaurant.
短短几天，這段影片的播放量就達到數百萬次。網紅部落客和富有同情心的當地居民突然蜂擁而至，讓這家只有13張餐桌的小餐館應接不暇。
Neither Ms. He nor her mother had been ready for internet stardom. She wondered whether she had made a mistake.
麗莎和母親都沒有做好在網上走紅的準備。她懷疑自己是不是做錯了。
“It was absolutely awful,” Ms. He said. Going viral “doesn’t help when your restaurant’s already broken.”
「真是糟透了，」麗莎說。「要是店本身已經撐不下去了，」在網上火了「也幫不上什麼忙」。
An Immigrant’s Ambition
一個移民的抱負
Sitting in China Star’s dining room can feel like taking a trip back in time.
坐在麗都裡面，時光彷彿倒流了。
An aquarium filled with shy fish welcomes diners, and lucky cat figurines sit in the window. Decorations from a long-ago wedding still hang on the back wall. Dishes are washed by hand.
門口的魚缸裡游著幾條膽小怕人的魚，窗台上擺著招財貓。一場多年前婚禮留下的裝飾品至今仍掛在後牆上。餐館裡的碗碟依然靠人工清洗。
Mrs. Li was born Wei Li but took the name Sophie after emigrating to Edinburgh in 1993 from mainland China with her husband, a doctoral candidate at the University of Edinburgh. She liked the regal sound of her new name, and hoped to live up to it. To support the family, she worked as a dishwasher and waited tables.
索菲原名李薇（音）。1993年，她隨丈夫從中國大陸移居愛丁堡。當時，丈夫正在愛丁堡大學攻讀博士學位。移民後，她給自己取了英文名Sophie，因為喜歡這個名字聽起來帶有的高貴氣質，也希望自己能活出與之相稱的人生。為了養家，她做過洗碗工，也當過服務員。
But she didn’t feel at ease in her new home. She worried that Lisa and her older sister, Emma, would not assimilate. In 1997, with loans from relatives and friends, she opened a restaurant, then a decade later moved to her current location, on busy West Maitland Street, beneath a large red sign with “China Star” in gold letters.
但在這個新的家園，她始終感到不自在。她擔心麗莎和姐姐艾瑪無法融入當地社會。1997年，她向親友借錢開了一家餐館。十年後，餐館搬到了如今的地址，位於繁忙的西梅特蘭街，門口高掛著一塊紅色大招牌，上面用金色寫著「麗都」。
“I felt like my standing wasn’t as high when I first came,” she said. She wanted her children “to hold their head high.”
「我剛來的時候覺得自己低人一等，」她說。她希望孩子們「能昂首挺胸」。
Her chefs were from Hong Kong and southern China, and they cooked a version of Chinese food that had been shaped in postwar Britain into a cuisine of its own: salt-and-pepper stir-fries, sweet and sour chicken, fried noodles and curries. Mrs. Li eventually kept two menus: one for Chinese visitors and one for locals.
她的廚師來自香港和中國南方，做的是一種在戰後英國逐漸發展出獨特風格的中餐：椒鹽菜、糖醋雞、炒麵和咖哩菜。後來，索菲乾脆準備了兩份菜單：一份供中國顧客點菜，另一份面向當地人。
She worked 15-hour days, sleeping only a few hours per night. Mornings were for cleaning and ingredient prep. Then came lunch service. She waited tables and delivered takeout orders herself, keeping the restaurant open until midnight, even on Christmas.
她每天工作15個小時，每晚只睡幾個小時。早晨打掃衛生、準備食材，接著忙午餐。她自己端盤子、招呼客人，還親自送外賣。餐館一直營業到午夜，即使聖誕節也不休息。
Time passed. Her husband returned to China. Her daughters moved to London. Mrs. Li paid off the mortgage on China Star.
歲月流逝。丈夫回了中國，兩個女兒搬去了倫敦。索菲也終於還清了麗都的房貸。
But as newer Chinese restaurants opened in Edinburgh and competition grew, the restaurant began to feel more like a burden. Foot traffic ebbed, and more of her business shifted to internet orders, whose commissions ate into their margins. Online ratings for the restaurant dropped.
然而，隨著愛丁堡不斷有新的中餐館開張，競爭日趨激烈，這家餐館漸漸成了她的負擔。上門顧客越來越少，生意日益依賴網路訂單，而平台抽取的傭金又不斷蠶食利潤。餐館的網上評分也開始下降。

Mrs. Li does not consider herself tech-savvy, so she didn’t see the customer reviews that complained about small portion sizes or bland fried rice. Arthritis was slowing her down. And she felt lonely.
索菲說自己不懂科技，因此沒有看到顧客在網上抱怨菜量太少、炒飯淡而無味的評論。關節炎讓她做起事來越來越吃力，她也感到孤獨。
“I didn’t think there was much hope,” she said. “But I had to keep going. There’s no other road to walk.”
「我覺得已經沒什麼希望了，」她說。「但我必須堅持下去。我沒有別的路可走。」
Going Viral
網路爆紅
In London, Ms. He, who was making a living as an actress and paralegal, kept tabs on China Star by logging into its CCTV camera. She saw how tired her mother looked. Early this year, she booked a trip home, hoping to give her a break.
在倫敦，靠當演員和律師助理謀生的麗莎·賀經常登錄餐館的監控系統，查看麗都的情況。她看到母親疲憊不堪的樣子。今年年初，她訂了回家的行程，希望能讓母親歇一歇。
“I came back out of a desperation to save my mom, rather than save the restaurant,” she said.
「我回來是因為太想救我媽媽了，而不是為了救這家餐館，」她說。
Ms. He has complicated feelings about restaurant life. When she was a baby, her mother brought her to work, putting her in a makeshift crib in a side room. After school, she felt guilty watching her work so hard.
麗莎對餐館生活有著複雜的感情。她還是嬰兒時，母親就帶著她去上班，把她放在餐館側間的一個臨時嬰兒床裡。上學後，每當看到母親如此辛苦地工作，她都會感到內疚。
“I often felt like I had to give up my social life to help out at the restaurant,” she said.
「我經常覺得，為了去餐館幫忙，我不得不放棄自己的社交生活，」她說。
Before April, Ms. He’s social media account consisted mostly of images of her dancing or in film rehearsals.
今年4月之前，麗莎的社群媒體帳號上主要是她跳舞或參加電影排練的照片。
But in this video, she turned the camera on her mother and China Star and gave her own, raw assessment of something many businesses may prefer to hide: what wasn’t working, from the uninviting facade to the outdated menu. And she invited people to follow her own journey as she tried to turn their fortunes around.
但在這段影片中，她把鏡頭對準了母親和麗都，坦率地指出了許多商家寧願遮掩的問題：從毫無吸引力的門面到過時的菜單，各種問題。她還邀請網友關注自己如何努力扭轉母女倆的處境。

Perhaps it was Ms. He’s candor, or the video’s documentary style. Or perhaps it was the feeling, for watchers online, that they could still stave off the loss of yet another family-run business in a world of corporations and chains.
也許是因為麗莎的坦率，也許是因為影片的紀錄片風格，這段影片吸引了很多人。又或許，在一個大公司和連鎖企業遍布的世界裡，網友們覺得自己還有機會阻止又一家家庭小店消失。
Ms. He’s comments lit up with offers of help, from improving the design to revamping the menu. Diners promised to visit. Restaurant owners encouraged them to rethink the business. “Don’t give up,” one said. “It isn’t over until it’s over.”
麗莎的評論區充滿了提供幫助的留言，從改進設計到更新菜單。食客們承諾會來。還有餐館老闆鼓勵他們重新思考這個生意。「別放棄，」有網友說。「沒到最後一刻就不算完。」
Ms. He posted the video on a Sunday. On Tuesday, she found herself back in the kitchen, trying to manage a situation for which they were entirely unprepared.
麗莎在一個週日發布了影片。週二，她發現自己回到餐館廚房，竭力應付一場母女倆完全沒有準備好的局面。
Food bloggers and new diners filled the restaurant in the days after that viral post. Mrs. Li, who learned of her daughter’s post only after it blew up, was shocked.
影片爆紅後的幾天裡，美食部落客和新顧客擠滿了餐館。索菲直到影片火了才知道女兒發了這段內容，她驚呆了。
Suddenly, daughter and mother were racing up and down stairs to take orders as their two cooks, used to a scattering of orders each day, suddenly had to pump out dishes.
突然之間，母女倆不得不在樓上樓下奔忙，接待顧客點餐。兩名廚師原本每天只需要應付零星幾單生意，如今卻要馬不停蹄地出菜。
Tensions boiled over when one frustrated chef walked out in protest during lunch as a full house waited for meals. Overwhelmed, the mother and daughter decided to shutter the restaurant temporarily.
緊張情緒最終爆發。一天午餐時段，餐廳座無虛席，顧客都在等著上菜，一名忍無可忍的廚師憤而離開，以示抗議。母女倆實在招架不住，只好決定暫時關門。
Food and Filial Devotion
食物與孝心
廣告
People following China Star’s struggles online replied to Ms. He with thoughts about what the restaurant needed to survive, and she decided to act on some of the advice.
關注麗都困境的網友紛紛給麗莎留言，提出餐館要想生存下去應該做出哪些改變。她決定採納其中一些建議。
She convinced her mother to slim down the menu and add Chinese wines, hired more servers and installed an electronic system for orders. They reopened after about a week with earlier hours and they closed on Mondays.
她說服母親精簡菜單，增加中國酒，僱用更多服務員，並安裝電子點單系統。大約一周後，餐館重新開門營業，但縮短了營業時間，並開始每週一休息。
She shared updates on Instagram, where more than 200,000 accounts are now following her.
她繼續在Instagram上發布餐館的最新動態，如今已有超過20萬個帳號關注她。
Five months after going viral, Ms. He was still working in the dining room, taking orders and mixing drinks. On a Thursday this September, the tables were filled with people who had seen her videos, drawn as much by the story of filial devotion and small-business survival as by the food.
影片爆紅五個月後，麗莎仍在餐廳裡忙碌，招待客人、調製飲品。今年9月的一個星期四，餐館裡坐滿了看過她影片的顧客。吸引他們前來的不僅是食物，還有這個關於女兒孝心和家庭小店艱難求生的故事。
“Usually the second generation — they don’t want to do the restaurant work,” said Wing Cheong, a visitor from Canada. “I think she is a good daughter.”
「通常第二代移民都不願意再做餐館生意，」來自加拿大的顧客張詠（音）說。「我覺得她是個孝順的女兒。」
Mrs. Li, energized, could not imagine giving up China Star, and was hopeful that her daughter would take it over one day. Revenues this summer more than tripled from the year before, and the online attention has led to sponsorships.
索菲重新煥發了幹勁，已經無法想像放棄麗都的生活。她希望有一天女兒能夠接手餐館。今年夏天，餐館的營業收入比去年同期增長兩倍多，網上走紅還為她們帶來了商業贊助。

But Ms. He wasn’t sure.
但麗莎卻拿不定主意了。
She was struggling to balance it all — the social media posts, the long days, the physical labor. There was a leak in the storeroom; a bathroom toilet seat needed fixing.
她發現，要兼顧所有事情實在太難了：發布社群媒體內容、每天長時間工作，還有繁重的體力勞動。儲藏室漏水，洗手間的馬桶座圈也需要修理。
Beneath it lay a familiar generational dilemma: She had put her own career, and her ambitions to act and dance, on hold to keep her mother’s business afloat.
這些瑣事背後，是一個人們熟悉的代際難題：為了維持母親的生意，她不得不暫時擱置自己的事業以及從事表演和舞蹈的夢想。
In one post this summer, she asked whether going viral had made their lives better, or just busier. In her mind, maybe it was time to let China Star go, for her mother to retire and for her to go back to London.
今年夏天，她在一篇帖子中發問：網路爆紅究竟讓她們的生活變得更好了，還是僅僅讓她們更加忙碌？在她看來，也許是時候放手，讓麗都關門，讓母親退休，自己則回到倫敦。
Her video had succeeded. But it had also revealed how differently mother and daughter imagined what should come next.
她的影片取得了成功，卻也揭示出母女倆對未來的不同設想。
“If I’m tiring myself and my mom to death every day,” she said at the restaurant, in a quiet moment before lunch, “I don’t really see that as a success.”
「如果我和媽媽每天都累得要死，」一天午餐前的片刻清靜中，她在餐館裡說，「那我真的不覺得這算什麼成功。」
For now, decisions on the future would have to wait. For the first time in a long time, the restaurant had large bookings for the holidays in December, and there was plenty of work to do: folding napkins, sorting prawn crackers and washing glasses. They would be back the next day to do it all again.
眼下，關於未來的決定只能暫時擱置。多年來，餐館第一次接到了大量12月節日期間的預訂，還有許多活等著她們去乾：折餐巾、分蝦片、洗杯子。第二天，她們還會回來，日復一日地重複這一切。
