黃之鋒因「勾結外國勢力」獲刑10年

Joshua Wong, Hong Kong Democracy Activist, Is Sentenced to 10 Years in Prison

TIFFANY MAY • 2026-11-05
Joshua Wong, a key figure in pro-democracy protests in Hong Kong, was sentenced on Thursday by a Hong Kong court to 10 years in prison on charges of conspiracy to collude with foreign forces, a national security offense.
週四，香港民主抗議活動的關鍵人物黃之鋒被香港一家法院以串謀勾結外國勢力的罪名判處10年監禁，這是一項國家安全罪。
Speaking in court on Thursday, Judge William Tam ruled that Mr. Wong’s offense was of a “grave nature,” which the national security law stipulates must result in a sentence of no less than 10 years.
法官譚耀豪週四在法庭上裁定，黃之鋒「罪行重大」，根據國家安全法的規定，須判處不少於10年的監禁。
Judge Tam said that Mr. Wong and Nathan Law, a fellow activist who had fled to Britain, were the “ringleaders” of a conspiracy to lobby foreign governments with “blatant calls for sanctions and hostile measures” and it was necessary for the severity of the punishment to act as a deterrent.
譚耀豪表示，黃之鋒與已逃往英國的另一名活動人士羅冠聰是串謀遊說外國政府並「公然呼籲制裁和敵對措施」的「主謀」，有必要通過嚴厲的懲罰來起到威懾作用。
Standing behind the dock, Mr. Wong, in a navy blue shirt, took notes as the judge spoke and appeared crestfallen as his sentence was announced. Before he was led away by police officers, Mr. Wong looked toward the public and press gallery. He smiled and said “thank you to you all” in Cantonese.
站在被告席後的黃之鋒身穿海軍藍襯衫，在法官講話時做著筆記，並在宣布刑期時顯得十分沮喪。在被警察帶走前，黃之鋒看向公眾席和記者席。他微笑著用粵語說道：「多謝大家」。
廣告
Mr. Wong, 30, was accused in 2025 of conspiring to lobby lawmakers, particularly those in the United States, to impose sanctions on Hong Kong and China. The maximum penalty for colluding with foreign forces is life in prison. Mr. Wong had pleaded guilty.
30歲的黃之鋒於2025年被控串謀遊說立法者（尤其是美國立法者）對香港和中國實施制裁。勾結外國勢力罪的最高刑罰為終身監禁。黃之鋒此前已認罪。
With virtually all national security cases ending in convictions, an increasing number of activists have chosen to plead guilty for a one-third reduction in sentence as part of a “harm reduction strategy,” said Thomas Kellogg, the executive director of the Georgetown Center for Asian Law.
喬治城大學亞洲法學中心執行主任托馬斯·凱洛格表示，由於幾乎所有的國家安全案件都以定罪告終，越來越多的活動人士選擇認罪以換取三分之一的減刑，將其作為一種「減害策略」。
Mr. Wong began his activism at age 14. He has already spent nearly six years behind bars as part of a conviction in a separate national security case and, according to friends, had been counting the months to his release in January before he was hit by the new charges.
黃之鋒從14歲起便開始參與社會活動。他因在另一起國家安全案件中被定罪，已在獄中度過了近六年。據朋友們說，在受到新指控之前，他一直在期盼著再過幾個月等到明年1月獲釋。
Friends of Mr. Wong said he struggled with the prospect of another long sentence. Chin Sze-man, who visited him in prison after the new charges were filed, said in an interview that it looked like “hope had disappeared from his eyes.”
黃之鋒的朋友們表示，他對可能面臨的又一個漫長刑期感到痛苦。在被指控新的罪名後曾去獄中探望他的錢詩文在接受採訪時說，他看起來就像「希望從他眼中消失了」。
Legal experts said ahead of Thursday’s sentencing that it was aimed at dampening Mr. Wong’s influence.
法律專家在週四的判決前表示，此舉旨在削弱黃之鋒的影響力。
“To many observers, he represented the Hong Kong pro-democracy movement’s future: not only young, but also charismatic, a gifted communicator, and an activist willing to take enormous personal risks,” said Eva Pils, a professor in international human rights law at FAU Nuremberg, Germany.
德國紐倫堡大學國際人權法教授伊娃·皮爾斯說：「對許多觀察人士而言，他代表了香港民主運動的未來：他不僅年輕、富有魅力、溝通天賦出眾，還是一名願意承擔巨大個人風險的活動人士。」
She added, “Putting Wong behind bars for so many years is probably intended to diminish his presence in the public mind and make his image fade away.”
「將黃之鋒關押在獄中這麼多年，目的可能是為了削弱他在公眾心目中的存在感，讓他的形象漸漸淡出，」她還說。
Beijing imposed a national security law on Hong Kong in 2020 that explicitly criminalized calls for sanctions and other forms of dissent.
2020年，北京在香港實施了國家安全法，明確將呼籲制裁及其他形式的異議行為定為犯罪。
廣告
“The ‘conspiracy to collude with foreign forces’ charge effectively paints him as a traitor,” said Steve Tsang, director of the SOAS China Institute in London. He added that China’s ruling Communist Party had decided that Hong Kong’s pro-democracy movement in 2019 was “an open rebellion” against the party. “Joshua is seen as a leading light guiding Hong Kong’s ‘unpatriotic’ acts,” he said.
倫敦亞非學院中國研究院院長曾銳生表示：「『串謀勾結外國勢力』的指控實際上將他描繪成了一個叛國者。」他補充道，中國共產黨已將香港2019年的民主運動定性為反黨的「公開叛亂」。「黃之鋒被視為引導香港『不愛國』行為的人物，」他說。
In 2012, when Mr. Wong was 14, he co-founded a student group called Scholarism opposing government plans to introduce a mandatory “moral and national education” course, which he said amounted to political indoctrination and “brainwashing.” Tens of thousands of protesters turned out outside government headquarters in support of Mr. Wong’s cause, and the government was forced to backpedal on its plans.
2012年，14歲的黃之鋒參與創立了學生團體「學民思潮」，反對政府推行強制性的「德育及國民教育」課程計劃，他稱該課程等同於政治灌輸和「洗腦」。數以萬計的抗議者聚集在政府總部外支持黃之鋒的訴求，政府最終被迫擱置了該計劃。
Two years later, in 2014, he became the distinctive face of young activists calling for freer elections in Hong Kong. The group held student strikes outside the government headquarters and pitched tents that blocked traffic in the city’s central business district for weeks. The protests became known as the Umbrella Movement.
兩年後的2014年，他成為呼籲在香港舉行更自由選舉的年輕活動人士中的標誌性面孔。該團體在政府總部外舉行了學生罷課，並搭起帳篷，導致該市中央商務區的交通癱瘓了數週。這場抗議活動後來被稱為雨傘運動。
By the time an unpopular extradition bill touched off months of antigovernment protests in 2019, “a lot of Joshuas emerged,” said Chan Kin-man, a prominent pro-democracy activist and former sociology professor at the Chinese University of Hong Kong, now living in Taiwan.
現居台灣的著名民主活動人士、香港中文大學前社會學教授陳健民說，當2019年一項不受歡迎的引渡法案引發數月的反政府抗議時，「湧現出了許多個黃之鋒。」
But as a pioneering young activist, Mr. Wong had something others lacked: an international profile.
但作為一名具有開拓性的青年活動人士，黃之鋒具備其他人所缺乏的優勢：國際知名度。
“He was already an icon. Many people in the West knew him. So he was able to meet political heavyweights in the United States,” he said. “It’s also this special role that has led him to such trying circumstances.”
「他當時已經是一個偶像。西方很多人都認識他。因此他能夠會見美國的政治重量級人物，」陳健民說，「也正是這種特殊的角色，導致他陷入了如此艱難的境地。」
廣告
In his five years behind bars, Mr. Wong has maintained a routine in which he has read widely, taken business courses, exercised and written letters, his friends said in interviews.
黃之鋒的朋友在接受採訪時表示，在獄中的五年裡，他保持著規律的作息，廣泛閱讀、學習商業課程、鍛煉身體並寫信。
“Typical people develop their careers for eight to ten years before they have energy left for the public good,” he wrote in a letter to a friend in January, reviewed by The New York Times. “But for most of us born after the 90s it is the opposite.”
「一般人通常會花八到十年時間發展事業，然後才有精力投入公益，」他在1月寫給朋友的一封信中寫道。「但對於我們大多數90後來說，情況恰恰相反。」時報查閱了該信件。
In the letter he had said that he was looking forward to his release in 2027, and that he hoped to gather with friends in 2032 to mark 20 years since they created Scholarism.
他在信中表示，自己期待著2027年獲釋，並希望能在2032年與朋友們重聚，以紀念他們創立「學民思潮」20週年。
Louis Lee, who frequently visits and writes to Mr. Wong, said that he has never given up, despite uncertainty over his future.
經常探視並給黃之鋒寫信的路易·李表示，儘管未來充滿不確定性，但他從未放棄過。
In a letter in 2025, also seen by The Times, Mr. Wong wrote that “the world has not been gentle” to him at all, but he would persevere.
在同樣由《紐約時報》查閱的一封2025年的信中，黃之鋒寫道，「這個世界對我一點也不溫柔，」但他會堅持下去。
“In this world where action seems fruitless, I will strive with all my might,” he wrote.
「在這個行動似乎徒勞無功的世界裡，我依然會傾盡全力去奮鬥，」他寫道。

Tiffany May是時報駐香港記者，報導香港和更廣泛地區的政治、商業和文化議題。
