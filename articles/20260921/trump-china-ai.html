習近平強勢訪美，川普該與他討論什麼？

Here’s How Trump and Xi Can Make Progress on A.I.

紀思道 • 2026-09-21
President Xi Jinping of China is throwing his weight around as he prepares to arrive in Washington.
中國國家主席習近平在準備抵達華盛頓之際，正在展示其影響力。
When Chinese and American leaders gathered for summits in the past, it was the American president who held the cards. Not so this time. What’s sobering about Xi’s visit to the United States in the coming days — and a reflection of the way President Trump has diminished America — is that this may be the first China-U.S. summit in which China appears in the lead role.
過去中美領導人舉行峰會時，掌握主動權的往往是美國總統。但這次情況不同。引人注目的是，習近平在未來幾天對美國的訪問可能成為首次由中國主導的中美峰會，這也反映了美國的地位如何在川普總統治下被削弱。
“In the past, American leaders generally drove the relationship, but now Xi is in the driver’s seat,” Julian Gewirtz, a China scholar at Columbia University who served in the Biden White House, told me. “Trump sees Xi as a peer and China as a superpower and is generally quite deferential to him.”
「過去通常是美國領導人主導這種關係，但現在習近平掌握了主動權，」曾在拜登政府白宮任職的哥倫比亞大學中國問題學者朱利安·格維爾茨告訴我。「川普將習近平視為同等的人物，將中國視為超級大國，且通常對他相當順從。」
For his part, Xi has demonstrated his strength by almost thumbing his nose at the United States.
就習近平而言，他通過對美國近乎輕蔑的態度展示了自己的實力。
廣告
Trump said he asked Xi in May, at their last meeting, to free Jimmy Lai, a media mogul who is among the most courageous advocates for democracy. Xi spurned the request. Instead, a Hong Kong court this month sentenced three other dissidents to harsh prison sentences, and one of the most famous of Hong Kong’s democracy campaigners, Joshua Wong, 29, is facing up to a life sentence for supposed crimes.
川普表示，在今年5月兩人最後一次會面時，他曾要求習近平釋放傳媒大亨、最勇敢的民主倡導者之一黎智英。習近平拒絕了。相反，一家香港法院本月將另外三名持不同政見者判處重刑，而香港最著名的民主活動人士之一、29歲的黃之鋒正因一些所謂的罪名面臨最高終身監禁的刑罰。
China, whose per capita income of more than $14,000 came within a whisker in July of achieving “high-income” status at the World Bank, also appears to be brazenly violating sanctions on North Korea this year. It buys Iranian oil and reportedly supports Iran’s war effort. And despite having officially suspended strict export controls on rare-earth elements, it has continued to exert control over them in ways that create problems for Western companies.
中國今年7月人均收入超過1.4萬美元，距離世界銀行的「高收入」標準僅一步之遙，然而同樣是這個國家，今年似乎也在公然違反針對朝鮮的制裁。中國購買伊朗石油，並據報導支持伊朗的戰爭行動。此外，儘管已正式暫停對稀土元素的嚴格出口管制，中國仍繼續以給西方企業製造麻煩的方式對其施加控制。
Asked about reports that Chinese organizations supplied intelligence that Iran used in an attack in July that killed three American soldiers, Trump was strikingly reticent. He seemed to downplay any suggestion that China had done something wrong.
有報導稱，中國機構提供的某些情報被伊朗用於7月份的一場襲擊，該襲擊導致三名美國士兵喪生。當被問及此事時，川普顯得異常沉默。他似乎在淡化任何關於中國存在過錯的暗示。
Xi has leverage in the relationship partly because the suspension of those export controls expires in November. If no deal is reached with Trump, then Beijing could further tighten access to rare earths that are vital for manufacturing everything from cars to submarines.
習近平在雙邊關係中擁有籌碼，部分原因是這些出口管制的暫停將於11月到期。如果未能與川普達成協議，北京可能會進一步收緊稀土供應，而稀土對於製造從汽車到潛艇的所有產品都至關重要。
The United States is weakened in part because Trump started a trade war with China last year, which he then effectively lost when China weaponized its control over rare earths. In addition, America’s war with Iran has depleted weapons stockpiles, reducing our ability to defend Taiwan or project power in the Pacific or the Middle East. We have antagonized allies and backtracked on democracy and freedom, and according to U.N. investigators, we may have committed war crimes in Iran — and all of this has tarnished our global image and reduced our influence and soft power worldwide.
美國的實力有所削弱，部分原因是川普去年挑起了對華貿易戰，但隨後在中國將稀土控制武器化時，他實際上輸掉了這場戰爭。此外，與伊朗的戰爭耗盡了美國的武器庫存，降低了我們保衛台灣或在太平洋及中東地區投射力量的能力。我們惹惱了盟友，在民主和自由問題上出現倒退，而且根據聯合國調查人員的說法，我們可能在伊朗犯下了戰爭罪——這一切都玷污了我們的全球形象，削弱了我們在全球的影響力和軟實力。
Trump is seen in much of the world as reckless and unpredictable, while Xi has tried to project an image of stability and maturity, soothing global oil markets that had been inflamed by Trump’s war on Iran. A new poll from the Rockefeller Foundation finds that Canadians, Indonesians, Brazilians, Turks, Mexicans and others all see the United States as more of a “major threat” than either Russia or China.
在世界大部分地區，川普被視為行事魯莽且不可預測，而習近平則試圖展現出穩定成熟的形象，安撫了因川普對伊戰爭而動盪不安的全球石油市場。洛克菲勒基金會的一項最新民意調查發現，加拿大人、印度尼西亞人、巴西人、土耳其人、墨西哥人等等均認為美國構成的「主要威脅」超過了俄羅斯或中國。
廣告
“The U.S. is in retreat,” Phillips P. O’Brien, an American historian, wrote this month. “It is watching bases of great strategic importance get destroyed and cannot protect them, it is abandoning allies that heretofore it considered of the highest priority, and it is making it clear that it cannot look after its own interests in some of the most important places of the world.”
「美國正在後退，」美國歷史學家菲利普斯·P·奧布萊恩本月寫道。「它眼睜睜看著具有重大戰略意義的基地被摧毀卻無力保護，它正在拋棄一直以來被其視為最優先的盟友，而且它正在表明，自己無法在世界上一些最重要的地方維護自身利益。」
O’Brien adds that “this is clearly the end of the American age” — but I’m not so sure about that. The United States still has strengths, not least its tech sector, while China, Europe and Japan all have significant weaknesses of their own, including demographic and economic challenges. Despite its propaganda, China remains an oppressive regime with no clear successor after Xi, while there is some prospect that American democracy will show its resilience in November’s elections.
奧布萊恩還說「這顯然是美國時代的終結」——但我對此並不完全確信。美國依然擁有優勢，尤其是其科技領域，而中國、歐洲和日本也都面臨著自身顯著的弱點，包括人口和經濟挑戰。儘管有其宣傳，中國仍是一個在習近平之後缺乏明確繼任者的專制政權，而美國的民主則有望在11月的選舉中展現其韌性。
If it is humbling to have Xi arrive in a position of strength, at least there may be an opportunity ahead. Both Xi and Trump seem to want to lower the temperature and stabilize the relationship, which would be a good thing.
如果習近平以強勢姿態到訪令人感到難堪，那麼至少前方可能還存在著機遇。習近平和川普似乎都有意為局勢降溫並穩定雙邊關係，這將是一件好事。
I hope that might also leave an opening for a sliver of progress on artificial intelligence, one of the most important topics the two leaders will discuss. It’s difficult to see how the world can make much progress on improving A.I. governance if America and China resist cooperation on safety.
我希望這也能為人工智慧領域的一絲進展創造契機，這是兩位領導人將要討論的最重要議題之一。如果美中兩國拒絕在安全問題上開展合作，很難想像世界能在改善人工智慧治理方面取得多大進展。
A major deal on safety is very unlikely, for Trump has called A.I. concerns “a hoax,” while Xi apparently thinks the problem is potential American abuse of the technology to target the Chinese regime, rather than A.I. itself. In China, A.I. is portrayed as a threat not to human existence but to Communist Party rule.
雙方達成一項關於安全的重大協議可能性微乎其微，因為川普已將對人工智慧的擔憂稱為「一場騙局」，而習近平顯然認為問題在於美國可能濫用該技術來針對中國政權，而非人工智慧本身。在中國，人工智慧被描繪為對共產黨的統治構成威脅，而非對人類生存構成威脅。
Still, perhaps there is space for a modest cooperative step forward. Rajiv Shah, the president of the Rockefeller Foundation, which is working on A.I. issues, told me that there may be an opportunity for progress on A.I. safety as it relates to specific, narrow domains such as reducing the risk of terrorist attacks using bioweapons.
儘管如此，或許雙方仍有空間邁出適度的合作步伐。致力於研究人工智慧相關議題的洛克菲勒基金會主席拉吉夫·沙阿告訴我，在涉及特定狹窄領域的人工智慧安全問題上，可能存在取得進展的機會，例如降低利用生物武器發動恐怖襲擊的風險。
廣告
That’s a real concern as synthetic biology and A.I. together create new perils: Imagine terrorists assembling a deadly virus, or engineering an animal ailment like cowpox or horse pox to be deadly and virulent like smallpox. It should be possible for China and America, working through both governments and unofficial channels, to collaborate on protocols to reduce risks like that.
這是一個切實的擔憂，因為合成生物學與人工智慧的結合帶來了新的危險：想像一下恐怖分子合成一種致命病毒，或者將牛痘或馬痘等動物疾病改造成像天花一樣致命且極具傳染性。中美兩國應當可以通過政府和非官方管道在降低此類風險的協議上展開合作。
That would at least be a major sign that A.I. safety cooperation is possible. “Doing one thing, even if it feels like it’s not solving the whole problem, at least creates a repeatable playbook,” Shah said.
這至少將成為人工智慧安全合作具備可行性的一個重大標誌。「做成一件事，即使感覺它無法解決所有問題，至少也創造了一個可複製的模式，」沙阿說。
China and the United States should also create some kind of hotline to discuss and defuse A.I.-related incidents that threaten to create military or diplomatic crises. After all, what if Open A.I. agents had hacked not Hugging Face but rather units of the Chinese People’s Liberation Army? Or generated deepfake video of Xi renouncing claims over Taiwan?
中美兩國還應建立某種熱線，以討論並化解可能引發軍事或外交危機的人工智慧相關事件。畢竟，如果OpenAI智能體黑入的不是Hugging Face，而是中國人民解放軍的部隊呢？又或者生成了習近平宣布放棄台灣主權主張的深度偽造影片呢？
Right now, at a time when America has blown up the geopolitical order that we ourselves created after World War II, modest steps may be the best we can hope for.
眼下，在美國破壞了自己在二戰後建立的地緣政治秩序之際，採取適度步驟或許是我們所能期盼的最好結果。
