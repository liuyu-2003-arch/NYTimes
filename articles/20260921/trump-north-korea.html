川普向金正恩示好，習近平或藉機「賣人情」？

Trump Courts North Korea’s Leader, but Might Need China to Reignite the Flame

黃安偉 • 2026-09-21
The overtures from President Trump to one of his favorite dictators came hot and heavy over the summer.
整個夏天，川普總統都在接二連三地向他最喜歡的其中一位獨裁者示好。
In June, Mr. Trump posted a photo of himself strolling through the tropical grounds of a luxury hotel with Kim Jong-un, the leader of North Korea, during their historic summit in Singapore eight years ago.
6月，川普發布了一張照片，是他與朝鮮領導人金正恩八年前在新加坡歷史性峰會期間在一家豪華酒店的熱帶庭院裡漫步的場景。
Weeks later, he wrote online that the two had a “very good relationship.”
幾周後，他在網上寫道，兩人的「關係非常好」。
In that post, Mr. Trump announced that the American military would dial back exercises with South Korea, shocking the close U.S. ally and handing Mr. Kim a gift. Three days later, when a reporter asked whether he would meet with Mr. Kim this year, the American president said, “Yeah, I will be.”
川普在那篇帖子中宣布，美軍將縮減與韓國的聯合軍演規模，這一決定令美國的親密盟友韓國大為震驚，卻讓金正恩白白得到了一份大禮。三天後，有記者問他今年是否會與金正恩會面，這位美國總統回答：「會，我會的。」
廣告
The courtship is on. The question in the coming weeks is whether it will be requited.
示好正在上演。未來幾週的問題是，對方是否會予以回應。
Mr. Trump is expected to discuss North Korea with Xi Jinping, the leader of China, when Mr. Xi makes a state visit to Washington next week. China is North Korea’s oldest and most important ally.
預計川普將在中國領導人習近平下週對華盛頓進行國事訪問時與其討論朝鮮問題。中國是朝鮮歷史最悠久、最重要的盟友。
Mr. Trump said he had spoken with Mr. Xi about North Korea during his visit to Beijing in May, weeks before he posted the photo taken in Singapore.
川普說，他5月訪問北京期間曾與習近平談到朝鮮問題。幾周後，他發布了那張在新加坡拍攝的照片。
Mr. Xi is reluctant to have the United States and North Korea grow closer. But he could see helping broker a Trump-Kim summit as a favor to Mr. Trump and a way to gain leverage on other U.S.-China issues, a former Trump administration official said. It would also give Mr. Xi some control over the diplomacy.
習近平並不願看到美朝走得過近。但一名前川普政府官員說，他也可能把幫助促成川普與金正恩的峰會視為賣給川普一個人情，並藉此在美中其他議題上獲得籌碼。這也會讓習近平在相關外交活動中擁有一定的掌控權。
For Mr. Trump, a summit with Mr. Kim would help him turn the spotlight away from the unpopular and costly war that he and Israel started against Iran and toward a leader-to-leader relationship that Mr. Trump has treasured since his first term, according to current and former officials and analysts.
現任和前任官員及分析人士表示，對川普來說，與金正恩舉行峰會將有助於把外界的注意力從他與以色列對伊朗發動的那場不得人心、代價高昂的戰爭上移開，轉向他從第一任期起就十分看重的這種領導人之間的關係。
Although Mr. Trump and Mr. Kim failed to reach a nuclear agreement during their second summit, in Hanoi, Vietnam, in 2019, the American president still feels a personal bond with the North Korean dictator, they say. He also values spectacle for the sake of spectacle and revels in main character energy.
他們說，儘管川普和金正恩在2019年越南河內第二次峰會上未能達成核協議，但這位美國總統仍然覺得自己與這位朝鮮獨裁者有著私人情誼。他也看重為場面而場面的戲劇效果，並陶醉於主角光環。
From Mr. Trump’s perspective, resetting the relationship with North Korea “would be a big win,” said Nicholas J.C. Snyder, a career diplomat who worked in embassies in Asia and in the White House during Mr. Trump’s first administration.
曾於川普第一任期內在亞洲多國的美國大使館和白宮任職的職業外交官尼古拉斯·斯奈德說，在川普看來，重啟與朝鮮的關係「將是一場重大勝利」。
“It’s been on his mind since Day 1,” said Mr. Snyder, now a business consultant and a senior fellow at the Hudson Institute, a conservative foreign policy research group. “And it’s also on the mind of those from Trump 1 who came back for Trump 2. It’s part of his administration’s desire to take care of unfinished business in foreign policy.”
「從第一天起，他就一直惦記著這件事，」斯奈德說。他現在是一名商業顧問，也是保守派外交政策研究機構哈德遜研究所的高級研究員。「那些從川普第一屆政府回歸第二屆政府的人也一直惦記著這件事。這也是本屆政府希望了結外交政策上一系列未竟之事的一部分」
A Trump-Kim meeting could be timed to a potential trip by Mr. Trump to an annual Asian economic summit that Mr. Xi will host in southern China in November.
川普與金正恩的會面可能會安排在川普有望前往參加11月於中國南方舉行的一年一度亞洲經濟峰會期間，屆時習近平將擔任東道主。
廣告
Unlike the Trump-Kim exchanges of effusive letters during the first Trump administration, Mr. Kim has yet to answer the American president’s overtures this time. Mr. Kim’s sister and close aide, Kim Yo-jong, said last month that she knew of no communications between her brother and Mr. Trump.
與川普第一屆政府時期川普和金正恩互通信件、熱情洋溢的情形不同，這一次金正恩迄今尚未回應這位美國總統的示好。金正恩的心腹、胞妹金與正上個月說，據她所知，她的哥哥與川普之間沒有任何聯繫。
She also said that any reduction in U.S.-South Korea military exercises would not change “the provocative and offensive nature of them.”
她還說，美韓軍事演習的規模無論如何縮減，都不會改變「它們的挑釁和進攻性質」。
Perhaps Mr. Kim is playing hardball because he thinks he is in a position of strength. He has been testing ballistic missiles and building up a nuclear weapons program while Mr. Trump has been mired in the war with Iran.
金正恩之所以態度強硬或許是因為他認為自己目前處於強勢地位。在川普深陷對伊朗戰爭之際，金正恩一直在試射彈道導彈，繼續推進核武器計劃。
Since 2018 during his first term, Mr. Trump has delighted in his diplomacy with Mr. Kim. After an initial summit in Singapore, the two negotiated over North Korea’s nuclear program in Hanoi in February 2019, though they walked away without a deal. The flame seemed to fade. But they stoked the embers four months later, when Mr. Trump became the first American president to step across the border from South Korea into the North, where he shook Mr. Kim’s hand.
自2018年第一任期以來，川普一直對自己與金正恩展開的外交活動津津樂道。在新加坡舉行首次峰會後，兩人於2019年2月在河內就朝鮮核計劃展開談判，但最終沒有達成協議便各自離去。兩人的熱絡關係似乎逐漸冷卻。但四個月後，他們又讓餘燼復燃：川普成為首位跨過韓朝邊界、踏上朝鮮土地的美國總統，並在那裡與金正恩握手。
Mr. Trump has raved about Mr. Kim — the two “fell in love,” he once said, and he has shown visitors to the White House the “beautiful letters” the two men exchanged. Violating federal records rules, he took the correspondence to his Florida resort, Mar-a-Lago, after he lost the 2020 election.
川普對金正恩讚不絕口——他曾說兩人「墜入愛河」，還曾向白宮訪客展示兩人互通的那些「美麗的信件」。2020年大選落敗後，他違反聯邦檔案管理規定，把這些信件帶到了自己位於佛羅里達州的馬阿拉歌莊園。
“I’m not sure there is much more to Trump’s strategy than summitry itself. This is purely about the theatrics of a meeting,” said John Delury, a professor of international politics at the Hopkins-Nanjing Center in China. “I think he’s waiting for Kim to send a positive signal back. Then he’ll tell his team to make it happen.”
「我不確定川普的策略除了峰會本身之外還有多少其他內容。這純粹是為了會面的戲劇效果，」南京大學-約翰斯·霍普金斯大學中美文化研究中心的國際政治學教授魯樂漢(John Delury)說。「我認為他在等金正恩發回積極信號。然後他就會告訴自己的團隊去促成這件事。」
“He’s always liked North Korea, even if it didn’t work out well the first time around,” Mr. Delury added.
「他一直很喜歡朝鮮，即便第一次並沒有取得什麼好結果，」魯樂漢補充說。
In past administrations, informal representatives from each country would meet in the hopes of opening the door to official dialogue. But those conversations stopped years ago. During the pandemic, North Korea sealed its borders and halted contact with the outside world.
在以往歷屆政府，兩國的非正式代表會先舉行會面，希望藉此為正式對話打開大門。但這類接觸多年前就已經停止。新冠疫情期間，朝鮮關閉邊境，中斷了與外部世界的接觸。
North Korea has less need to build ties with the United States now. Its economy is stronger than it has been in many years. And the country has bolstered its diplomatic and military positions through its alliances with China and Russia.
如今，朝鮮與美國建立關係的需要已經沒有以前那麼迫切。它的經濟處於多年來的最好時期。與此同時，朝鮮還通過與中國和俄羅斯結盟，鞏固了自己的外交和軍事地位。
廣告
Mr. Kim signed a mutual defense agreement with Russia two years after the country’s president, Vladimir V. Putin, ordered a full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022. North Korean forces have been fighting alongside the Russians against the Ukrainian military. Mr. Kim and Mr. Putin have met in each other’s countries. (A top aide to Mr. Putin said this month that the Russian leader might take part in a group meeting with Mr. Trump and Mr. Xi in China in November, which raises the possibility of a historic four-way summit if Mr. Kim joins.)
在俄羅斯總統普丁2022年下令全面入侵烏克蘭兩年後，金正恩與俄羅斯簽署了共同防禦條約。朝鮮軍隊一直與俄軍並肩作戰，對抗烏克蘭軍隊。金正恩和普丁也曾分別訪問對方國家並舉行了會晤。（普丁的一名高級助手本月表示，這位俄羅斯領導人可能會於11月在中國參加一場有川普和習近平出席的多方會晤。如果金正恩也參加，就有可能出現一次歷史性的四方峰會。）
North Korea has shown no signs of a softening toward the United States, despite Mr. Trump’s decision to curtail U.S. military exercises with South Korea.
儘管川普決定縮減美韓軍事演習規模，朝鮮卻沒有表現出任何緩和對美態度的跡象。
A North Korean Foreign Ministry spokesperson said recent U.S. statements and military actions showed that the United States had “fully reconfirmed its invariable hostile policy to wantonly violate the sovereignty, political system and security interests of the D.P.R.K.,” according to a report in late August by the Korean Central News Agency, an official outlet. (The formal name of North Korea is the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea.)
據朝鮮官方媒體朝中社8月底的一篇報導，朝鮮外務省一名發言人表示，美國最近的言論和軍事行動表明，它「再次充分暴露了其肆意侵犯朝鮮民主主義人民共和國主權、政治制度和安全利益的一貫反朝政策」。
The report also made clear that talks about the denuclearization of North Korea would lead nowhere.
該報導還明確表示，任何有關朝鮮無核化的談判都不會有結果。
North Korea, the spokesperson said, “can neither be weakened nor be diluted by the U.S. repeated expression of its stand on ‘denuclearization.’” The United States must not “absurdly stick to the past.”
這名發言人說，朝鮮（擁核地位）「既不會因美國反覆表明『無核化』立場而被削弱，也不會被淡化」。美國不應「荒謬地固守過去」。
Robert Carlin, a former U.S. intelligence analyst on North Korea, said Mr. Kim had maintained a policy change he enacted in 2021, two years after the Hanoi summit ended in disappointment for the two leaders.
美國前朝鮮問題情報分析員羅伯特·卡林說，金正恩一直堅持他在2021年作出的政策轉變。那是在河內峰會令兩位領導人失望而歸兩年之後。
廣告
“I think what people have to understand is there has been a tectonic shift in North Korean strategic perceptions and policy,” Mr. Carlin said. “They wouldn’t try to normalize relations with the U.S. And on top of that, there’s an even broader reality. Kim sees the world completely differently now. He sees himself as a leader of the anti-imperialist forces globally.”
「我認為，人們必須明白，朝鮮的戰略認知和政策已經發生了結構性轉變，」卡林說。「他們不會試圖實現與美國關係正常化。除此之外，還有一個更廣泛的現實。金正恩現在看待世界的方式已經完全不同。他把自己視為全球反帝力量的領導人。」
Mr. Carlin added that Mr. Kim believes “that a confrontation with the U.S. is inevitable.” Mr. Trump’s war against Iran bolsters that notion, he said.
卡林補充道，金正恩認為「與美國發生對抗不可避免」。他說，川普對伊朗的戰爭強化了這一看法。
Decades of American policy on North Korea, including harsh economic sanctions, have failed to deter the advancement of the country’s nuclear weapons program. So leader-to-leader diplomacy is still worth a shot, some analysts say.
美國幾十年來針對朝鮮採取的政策，包括嚴厲的經濟制裁，都未能阻止朝鮮推進核武器計劃。因此，一些分析人士認為，領導人之間的外交仍然值得一試。
“Any morsel of a response at all is good, especially if it leads to a conversation with the United States,” said Jung Pak, a former State Department official and C.I.A. analyst on North Korea.
「只要能得到哪怕一點點回應都是好事，尤其是如果這種回應能夠促成與美國的對話，」美國國務院前官員、中央情報局前朝鮮問題分析員朴鄭（音）說。
However, Ms. Pak faulted Mr. Trump for surprising South Korea with his announcement about rolling back military exercises.
不過，朴鄭批評川普在宣布縮減軍事演習規模一事上沒有事先告知韓國，令韓國措手不及。
“The way it was executed by the Trump administration is not great,” she said. “It made it look as if the alliance is not on an equal footing. The first rule is you make sure your best partner knows about what you’re doing.”
「川普政府處理這件事的方式不太好，」她說。「這讓人覺得這個同盟並不是建立在平等地位上的。最起碼，你得確保自己最重要的夥伴知道你在做什麼。」

黃安偉(Edward Wong)為《紐約時報》報導全球事務、美國外交政策和國務院新聞。
