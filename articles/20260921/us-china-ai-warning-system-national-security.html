美中討論建立AI安全問題通報機制

U.S. and China Discuss System to Warn of A.I. National Security Issues

ALAN RAPPEPORT, LAUREN HIRSCH, ANA SWANSON • 2026-09-21
The United States and China discussed on Sunday the creation of a notification system that would allow the countries to alert each other about national security issues related to artificial intelligence.
美國和中國於週日討論建立一種通報制度，使兩國能夠就與人工智慧相關的國家安全問題相互提醒。
The countries spoke about the need for such a mechanism during a day of economic talks involving Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent; Jamieson Greer, the U.S. trade representative; and China’s vice premier, He Lifeng. The meetings took place in New York ahead of President Xi Jinping’s state visit to Washington later this week, and they were intended to lay the groundwork for potential agreements between the world’s two largest economies on artificial intelligence, trade and the war in Iran.
兩國在為期一天的經濟會談中討論了建立這一機制的必要性。會談參與方包括財政部長史考特·貝森特、美國貿易代表賈米森·格里爾和中國副總理何立峰。會議在紐約舉行，雙方意在習近平主席本週晚些時候對華盛頓進行國事訪問之前為世界兩個最大經濟體之間在人工智慧、貿易和伊朗戰爭問題上可能達成的協議奠定基礎。
The idea a new mechanism for addressing A.I. underscores the growing concern around the world that the rapidly evolving technology could pose national security risks.
建立一種人工智慧應對新機制的想法凸顯了全世界日益增長的擔憂：快速發展的技術可能構成國家安全風險。
Speaking after the meetings, which were held at the New York offices of JPMorgan Chase, Mr. Bessent said that the mechanism would be called “the U.S.-China A.I. dialogue” and that it would aim to ensure that the two countries had a shared vision on the goals of A.I. and threats that it poses.
會議在摩根大通的紐約辦公室舉行。貝森特在會後表示，該機制將被稱為「美中人工智慧對話」，目標是確保兩國在人工智慧的目標及其構成的威脅方面擁有共同願景。
廣告
Explaining that the notification system would be activated when a matter related to A.I. reaches the national security level, Mr. Bessent added: “We think that just like with any cross-border activity, that moving from opaque to more transparency between the No. 1 and the No. 2 A.I. powers in the world is very important.”
貝森特解釋說，當與人工智慧相關的事項達到國家安全級別時，通報制度將被啟動。他還說：「我們認為，就像任何跨境活動一樣，世界第一和第二人工智慧大國之間從模糊走向更加透明，這是非常重要的。」
The United States and China have been racing to become the dominant powers in artificial intelligence technology at a time when there is increasing demand to regulate the industry because of concerns that it could spiral out of human control and be used for hacking and the development of lethal weapons.
美國和中國一直在競相成為人工智慧技術的主導力量。與此同時，由於擔心人工智慧可能失控並被用於駭客攻擊和開發致命武器，對該行業進行監管的需求日益增加。
America’s leading A.I. companies have recently published reports disclosing that their models have breached or bypassed contained testing environments. U.S. officials are also concerned about the potential for A.I. to enhance Chinese hacking operations, military capabilities and surveillance systems.
美國領先的人工智慧公司最近發布了報告，披露其模型已經突破或繞過了封閉測試環境。美國官員還擔心人工智慧可能增強中國的駭客行動、軍事能力和監視系統。
In a report earlier this month, the Trump administration accused Chinese companies of taking proprietary functions and capabilities from American A.I. models in an “aggressive, malicious and targeted” way. The issue is expected to be a topic of discussion between President Trump and Mr. Xi when they meet.
在本月早些時候的一份報告中，川普政府指責中國公司以「激進、惡意和有針對性的」方式從美國人工智慧模型中獲取專有功能和能力。預計該問題將成為川普總統與習近平會晤時討論的話題。
Mr. Bessent and Mr. Greer also said that they discussed trade with their Chinese counterparts. U.S. officials said they were moving forward with setting up a new so-called Board of Trade, a concept they introduced earlier this year, which would promote trade in nonsensitive goods between the United States and China.
貝森特和格里爾還表示，他們與中方對等官員討論了貿易問題。美國官員表示，他們正在推進設立一個新的所謂的貿易委員會，這是他們今年早些時候提出的概念，該委員會將促進美國和中國之間非敏感商品的貿易。
The goods would include Chinese-made consumer goods and low-tech items, as well as American energy products, agricultural goods and potentially medical devices, Mr. Greer said.
格里爾說，這些商品將包括中國製造的消費品和低技術產品，以及美國的能源產品、農產品，可能還有醫療設備。
廣告
Government officials on both sides were continuing to work, Mr. Greer said, to find a critical mass of goods that could be “traded in a balanced way, and create a kind of ballast for the relationship.”
格里爾說，雙方政府官員正在繼續努力尋找足夠數量的商品，以便能夠「以平衡的方式進行貿易，為兩國關係充當一種壓艙石」。
The United States and China agreed to a one-year trade truce last October after engaging in a tit-for-tat war of tariffs and export controls that destabilized the global economy for months.
美國和中國去年10月達成了一項為期一年的貿易休戰，此前雙方進行了針鋒相對的關稅和出口管制戰，對全球經濟的穩定構成了持續數月的破壞。
