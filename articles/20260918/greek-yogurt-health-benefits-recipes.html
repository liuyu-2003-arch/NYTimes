How Healthy Is Greek Yogurt?

CAROLINE HOPKINS LEGASPI • 2026-09-18
Greek yogurt doesn’t necessarily come from Greece. (In fact, New York State’s Greek yogurt production surpassed Greece’s in 2017.) This thick, creamy style of yogurt is made by straining out the liquid whey. Greek yogurt has been a staple of the Mediterranean diet for centuries.
希臘酸奶不一定來自希臘。（事實上，紐約州的希臘酸奶產量在2017年超過了希臘。）這種濃稠順滑的酸奶通過濾出液體乳清製成。幾個世紀以來，希臘酸奶一直是地中海飲食的重要組成部分。
Here’s what nutrition experts have to say about why it's so good for you, along with recipes from New York Times Cooking.
以下是營養專家對希臘酸奶為何有益健康的解釋，以及來自《紐約時報》烹飪頻道的食譜。
It’s loaded with protein.
它富含蛋白質。
Straining out the whey results in a more concentrated product, which makes Greek yogurt higher in protein than regular yogurt. One 5.3-ounce container of plain, nonfat Greek yogurt contains 15.4 grams of protein, which is more than double the amount in regular yogurt and more than a quarter of the protein that a 160-pound adult needs daily. (Straining out the whey doesn’t make Greek yogurt more nutritious overall, however; for example, regular yogurt typically has more calcium.)
濾出乳清會得到更濃縮的產品，因此希臘酸奶的蛋白質含量高於普通酸奶。一盒5.3盎司的原味脫脂希臘酸奶含15.4克蛋白質，是普通酸奶的兩倍多，也超過一名體重160磅（約合73公斤）的成年人每日所需蛋白質的四分之一。（不過，濾出乳清並不會讓希臘酸奶的整體營養價值更高；例如，普通酸奶通常含更多鈣。）

Greek yogurt is also a “complete” protein source, said Ethan Balk, an associate professor of clinical nutrition at New York University. This means that its protein contains all of the nine essential amino acids that your body can’t make on its own.
紐約大學臨床營養學副教授伊桑·巴爾克說，希臘酸奶也是一種「完全」蛋白質來源。這意味著其蛋白質含有人體無法自行合成的全部九種必需氨基酸。
Most people already consume enough protein and don’t need to up their intake. But it’s best to get your protein from a nutrient-dense food like Greek yogurt, rather than from powder or supplements, said Angie Hasemann Bayliss, the director of clinical nutrition at UVA Health.
大多數人已經攝入了足夠的蛋白質，無需增加攝入量。但UVA Health臨床營養主任安吉·哈澤曼·貝利斯說，最好從希臘酸奶這類營養密集的食物中獲取蛋白質，而不是從蛋白粉或補充劑中。
It’s good for your gut.
它對你的腸道有益。
Most yogurt, including Greek, contains probiotics, “good bacteria” that help keep your gut microbiome healthy, said Elaine Siu, a dietitian at the City of Hope Cancer Center in Duarte, Calif.
加州杜阿爾特市希望之城癌症中心的營養師伊萊恩·蕭說，包括希臘酸奶，大多數酸奶都含有益生菌，即有助於保持腸道微生物組健康的「好細菌」。
Probiotics may also help prevent some types of cancer, said Dr. Andrew T. Chan, a gastroenterologist at Massachusetts General Hospital and professor at Harvard Medical School.
麻省總醫院胃腸病學家、哈佛醫學院教授安德魯·T·陳博士說，益生菌也可能有助於預防某些類型的癌症。
In one of Dr. Chan's studies, which followed more than 130,000 adults over several decades, those who ate yogurt at least twice a week were 20 percent less likely to develop one type of colon cancer than those who ate yogurt less than once a month. The study looked at all yogurt consumption, not just Greek yogurt, and it couldn’t directly prove that the yogurt caused lower cancer rates. Even so, Dr. Chan said that the findings add to a growing body of research suggesting that the bacteria found in fermented foods like yogurt may reduce your cancer risk.
陳博士的一項研究在數十年間追蹤了超過13萬名成年人，結果顯示，每週至少吃兩次酸奶的人患一種結腸癌的可能性比每月吃酸奶少於一次的人低20%。該研究考察了所有酸奶攝入情況，而不僅是希臘酸奶，也無法直接證明酸奶能降低癌症發病率。即使如此，陳博士說，這些發現為一個不斷擴充的研究體系增添了證據，表明酸奶等發酵食品中的細菌可能降低癌症風險。

It’s rich in vitamin B12.
它富含維生素B12。
One 5.3-ounce serving of nonfat Greek yogurt contains just over one microgram of vitamin B12. While that might not sound like much, it’s nearly half of the 2.4 micrograms that adults should consume daily.
一份5.3盎司的脫脂希臘酸奶含略多於一微克的維生素B12。這聽起來可能不多，但已接近成年人每日應攝入2.4微克的一半。
Vitamin B12 helps to form red blood cells, maintain energy and keep your nervous system functioning at its best, Ms. Bayliss said.
貝利斯女士說，維生素B12有助於形成紅細胞、維持能量，並讓神經系統保持最佳運作。
Ready to dig in?
準備好開吃了嗎？
To fully reap the health benefits of Greek yogurt, there are a few things to keep in mind.
要充分獲得希臘酸奶的健康益處，有幾件事需要記住。
For one, check the ingredients on flavored varieties. These yogurts can be loaded with added sugars, and some contain additives (such as artificial dyes and preservatives), making them ultraprocessed foods.
其一，檢查風味酸奶的配料。這些酸奶可能添加大量糖，有些還含添加劑（如人工色素和防腐劑），使其成為超加工食品。
廣告
Look for yogurt with under 12 grams of added sugar per serving and try to choose one with the fewest ingredients, recommended Candace Pumper, a dietitian at the Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center. Or buy the plain variety and then add honey or maple syrup to sweeten it.
俄亥俄州立大學韋克斯納醫學中心營養師坎迪斯·龐珀建議，選擇每份添加糖低於12克的酸奶，並盡量挑選配料最少的一種。或者買原味酸奶，再加蜂蜜或楓糖漿增甜。
While you’re at it, throw in some fiber. The good bacteria in Greek yogurt need to feed on healthy prebiotic fiber to grow and multiply. Yogurt doesn’t contain any, so it’s smart to pair it with fiber-rich fruits like berries, mangos or peaches, or mix in nuts or seeds, Ms. Siu said.
順便加一些纖維。希臘酸奶中的好細菌需要以健康的益生元纖維為食才能生長繁殖。酸奶本身不含纖維，因此蕭女士說，明智的做法是搭配富含纖維的水果，如漿果、芒果或桃子，或拌入堅果、種子。
And get creative: Along with using it to make parfaits and smoothies, it’s also delicious in savory dips, salad dressings and marinades. Here are some ideas to try from NYT Cooking.
還可以發揮創意：除了做芭菲和冰沙，希臘酸奶用在鹹味蘸醬、沙拉醬和腌料中也很美味。以下是《紐約時報》烹飪頻道提供的一些做法。
