全球經濟迴旋餘地正在減少，前景惡化

Global Economy Is Running Out of Wiggle Room

PATRICIA COHEN • 2026-09-18
For a while now, the global economy has been beating the odds.
全球經濟已經持續了一段時間的逆勢而行。
The energy shock prompted by the American and Israeli attacks on Iran in February set off the largest disruption of worldwide energy flows in history. Yet despite jumps in oil prices, widening conflict in the Middle East, fuel shortages and inflation pressures, the feared crash never came and most economies managed to muddle through.
美國和以色列2月對伊朗的襲擊引發能源衝擊，導致史上最大規模的全球能源流動中斷。然而，儘管油價飆升、中東衝突擴大、燃料短缺且通膨壓力加劇，人們擔心的崩潰並未出現，大多數經濟體勉力支撐。
But wiggle room is narrowing at the same time the outlook is darkening. Look at what has happened in just the last few days.
但迴旋餘地正在變小，前景在惡化。看看過去幾天發生的事。
Attacks forced Saudi Arabia to shut a vital pipeline. Houthi militia seized a strategic Red Sea island and a port city, which could further restrict shipping in the region. And proposed negotiations among Gulf nations fell apart. Oil prices have shot toward $110 a barrel.
襲擊迫使沙烏地阿拉伯關閉一條關鍵管道。胡塞武裝奪取一個戰略性的紅海島嶼和一座港口城市，可能進一步限制該地區航運。海灣國家擬議的談判破裂。油價已飆升至每桶110美元。
廣告
The price spike has caused rolling blackouts, rationing and angry protests from Asia to Latin America. In the Philippines, fisherman docked their boats because they can’t afford fuel. In Bangladesh, power outages caused hourslong work stoppages at factories. In Guatemala, protesters burned tires and cars.
價格飆升導致從亞洲到拉美的限電、物資配給和憤怒抗議。菲律賓漁民因加不起油而停止出海。孟加拉國停電導致持續數小時的工廠停工。瓜地馬拉抗議者燒毀輪胎和汽車。
Until now, some key moves to ease demand and bolster oil supplies helped blunt extreme price increases and acute shortages.
迄今為止，一些緩解需求、增加石油供應的關鍵舉措幫助減輕了價格極端上漲和嚴重短缺。
“The No. 1 reason why crude oil prices and product prices have not been higher than they are is that China has cut its crude oil imports,” said David L. Goldwyn, a former U.S. diplomat and Energy Department official.
「原油和成品油價格沒有漲得更高，首要原因是中國削減了原油進口，」前美國外交官、能源部官員戴維·L·戈德溫說。
The world’s largest importer of oil, China stopped stockpiling oil and relied on its own inventories, easing pressure on the global oil market at a critical moment. It also reduced or withheld exports of products made from oil, like jet fuel, meaning it needed less crude oil.
作為全球最大石油進口國，中國停止囤油，轉而動用自身庫存，在關鍵時刻緩解了全球石油市場壓力。中國還減少或暫停出口航空煤油等石油製品，從而減少了原油需求。
At the same time, the United States, Japan and countries across Europe dipped into their own reserves to keep prices from rising further.
與此同時，美國、日本和歐洲各國動用自身儲備，遏制價格進一步上漲。
The measures helped. Even Asia — the destination for about 80 percent of Middle Eastern crude and liquefied natural gas exports — was largely able to procure supplies.
這些措施奏效了。就連亞洲——約80%的中東原油和液化天然氣出口的目的地——也基本能夠獲得供應。
廣告
Such strategies can work for only so long, though.
不過，此類策略只能奏效一時。
Storage levels among the 38 members that make up the Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development have dropped to their lowest levels in decades, according to the U.S. Energy Information Administration.
美國能源信息署的數據顯示，經濟合作與發展組織38個成員國的石油庫存已降至數十年來最低。
And in the Gulf, alternate delivery routes, like Saudi’s East-West pipeline, are currently not operating.
而在海灣地區，沙烏地阿拉伯東西管道等替代運輸路線目前並未運行。
“We’re going to be dealing with prices in the $80 to $100 a barrel level at least through 2027,” said Mr. Goldwyn. Higher prices for fuel and related products like fertilizer will further push up food and transportation costs.
「至少到2027年，我們都要面對每桶80至100美元的油價，」戈德溫說。燃料及化肥等相關產品漲價將進一步推高食品和運輸成本。
The disruption in energy shipping routes in the Middle East will mean higher inflation. “What’s happening is that we’ve got very low levels of inventories now, and there’s still no sign that the straits can be reopened,” said Neil Shearing, group chief economist at Capital Economics.
中東能源航運路線中斷將意味著更高通膨。「眼下庫存水平極低，而且仍無跡象顯示海峽能重新開放，」凱投宏觀集團首席經濟學家尼爾·希林說。
The Asian Development Bank projects that inflation in the region will accelerate to 5.2 percent this year from 3.0 percent last year. In Europe, Britain and the United States, inflation is likely to hover in the 3.5 to 4 percent range until at least the middle of next year, Mr. Shearing said.
亞洲開發銀行預計，該地區今年通膨率將從去年的3.0%升至5.2%。希林說，在歐洲、英國和美國，通膨率至少在明年年中前可能維持在3.5%至4%之間。
Worries over prices are putting pressure on central bankers to raise rates, which will make borrowing more expensive and slow economies. That would be a blow for a country like Germany, which has already been teetering on the edge of recession.
對價格的擔憂正迫使央行官員加息，這將推高借貸成本並拖累經濟。對於已徘徊在衰退邊緣的德國等國來說，這將是一記打擊。
廣告
Limited supplies of refined as well as crude petroleum from the Middle East are pushing up prices. Buffers are shrinking, the International Energy Agency warned last week, and the global refining system is “stretched to the limit.”
中東成品油和原油供應有限，推高價格。國際能源署上週警告，緩衝庫存正在萎縮，全球煉油系統「已綳到極限」。
Ukrainian attacks on Russian refineries have contributed to the squeeze, forcing a cut in its refining capacity by roughly 30 percent over the next 18 months, according to the agency. Russia has extended its ban on diesel exports to the end of this month.
該機構稱，烏克蘭襲擊俄羅斯煉油廠加劇了供應緊張，迫使俄煉油能力在未來18個月削減約30%。俄羅斯已將柴油出口禁令延長至本月底。
The surge in diesel prices prompted President Trump this week to blame Ukraine and urge it to not target Russian refining facilities.
柴油價格飆升促使川普總統本週指責烏克蘭，並敦促其不要襲擊俄羅斯煉油設施。
Mr. Goldwyn worries that rising prices may push Mr. Trump to ban diesel exports before the midterm elections in November. That would temporarily tamp down the price of diesel at home. But without foreign customers, he said, oil refiners are likely to reduce production, tightening global supplies.
戈德溫擔心，價格上漲可能促使川普在11月期中選舉前禁止柴油出口。這會暫時壓低國內柴油價格。但他說，失去外國客戶後，煉油商可能減產，從而收緊全球供應。
An export ban would be particularly painful for Latin America, which is a big importer of American diesel.
出口禁令對拉美尤其痛苦，因為該地區大量進口美國柴油。
No region is immune.
沒有哪裡能倖免。
Persian Gulf economies are already reeling from the fallout of the Iran war. Qatar, a leading exporter of liquefied natural gas, is expected to see its economy shrink by 8.6 percent this year, according to the International Monetary Fund. Saudi Arabia’s economy shrank by 4.8 percent in the second quarter compared with the previous year.
波斯灣經濟體已因伊朗戰爭衝擊而搖搖欲墜。國際貨幣基金組織預計，主要液化天然氣出口國卡達今年經濟將萎縮8.6%。沙烏地阿拉伯經濟第二季度同比萎縮4.8%。
Global shocks often cause the biggest tremors in the poorest countries. Countries across Africa rely heavily on energy and fertilizer imports. Those high costs may end up driving down harvest yields and further pushing up food prices.
全球衝擊往往在最貧窮國家引發最大震盪。非洲各國嚴重依賴能源和化肥進口。高昂成本可能壓低收成，進一步推高食品價格。
Although most Asian countries have been able to avoid the kind of energy shortages and halts to production that were initially feared, they have had to pay much more for supplies.
儘管大多數亞洲國家得以避免最初擔心的能源短缺和生產停頓，但它們不得不為供應支付高得多的價格。
Energy prices, of course, are not the only concern. Tariffs are raising costs for consumers and manufacturers. The World Food Program warned last month that weather conditions related to El Niño could push about 50 million people into acute hunger. And government deficits have reached record highs.
當然，能源價格並非唯一擔憂。關稅正推高消費者和製造商成本。世界糧食計劃署上月警告，厄爾尼諾相關天氣可能使約5000萬人陷入嚴重飢餓。政府赤字也達到歷史新高。
Japan and Indonesia have spent billions of dollars on fuel subsidies to help consumers weather the price hikes. Concerns about countries’ ability to pay back growing debt levels have seeped into the bond market. In Japan this month, yields on 10-year government bonds rose to a three-decade high.
日本和印尼已投入數十億美元補貼燃料，幫助消費者應對漲價。對各國償債能力的擔憂已滲入債市。本月日本10年期國債收益率升至30年高點。
Anxiety about rising risks have pushed the 10-year U.S. Treasury bond — a benchmark that affects interest rates and investments around the world — to ulcer-inducing levels.
對風險上升的焦慮已將10年期美國國債——影響全球利率和投資的基準——推至令人揪心的水平。
廣告
Although the United States has been relatively insulated from the worst economic slaps, that could change.
儘管美國相對不容易受到最嚴重的經濟衝擊，但這種情況可能改變。
As Mr. Goldwyn, the former energy official, said, “at some point when all your trading partners are suffering from high prices and food is more expensive and transportation is more expensive, you can’t really escape the laws of economic gravity.”
正如前能源官員戈德溫所說，「當所有貿易夥伴都受困於高企的價格，食品和運輸變得更貴時，你無法真正逃脫經濟引力法則。」
