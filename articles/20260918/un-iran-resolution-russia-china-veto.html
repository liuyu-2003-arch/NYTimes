中俄否決延長監督伊朗核計劃決議

Russia and China Veto U.S. Resolution to Renew U.N. Monitoring of Iran Nuclear Program

ZANE IRWIN • 2026-09-18
Russia and China vetoed a U.S. bid to renew the mandate of U.N. experts monitoring nuclear sanctions against Iran at the U.N. Security Council on Thursday. The move deepened tensions between the geopolitical rivals over the Trump administration’s efforts to isolate the Islamic republic.
俄羅斯和中國週四在聯合國安理會否決了美國提出的伊朗制裁委員會專家小組授權延期決議草案。此舉加深了美國與其地緣政治對手中俄之間因川普政府試圖孤立伊朗伊斯蘭共和國而產生的緊張關係。
The nations’ U.N. representatives traded pointed remarks at the Security Council meeting in New York, less than a week before a much-anticipated meeting in Washington between President Xi Jinping of China and President Trump.
在紐約舉行的安理會會議上，這些國家的駐聯合國代表發表了針鋒相對的言論，而就在不到一周後，中國國家主席習近平和美國總統川普將在華盛頓舉行一場備受期待的會晤。
Fu Cong, China’s U.N. ambassador, urged Council members to avoid “double standards, political manipulation, pressure and confrontation” at the meeting.
中國常駐聯合國代表傅聰敦促安理會「避免雙重標準、政治操弄、施壓對抗」。
Vasily Nebenzya, Russia’s U.N. ambassador, condemned what he called “the destructive path of our Western colleagues, determined purely by their economic interests in exerting pressure on the Islamic Republic of Iran.”
俄羅斯常駐聯合國代表瓦西裡·涅邊賈譴責了他所稱的「我們西方同事的破壞性道路，這完全由他們在向伊朗伊斯蘭共和國施壓時所追求的經濟利益所決定」。
廣告
Iranian officials did not immediately address the vote or the veto.
伊朗官員沒有立即就這次表決或否決作出回應。
The clash revolved around a U.S. resolution to extend the work of a panel of experts created to monitor Iran’s nuclear program and its efforts to evade international sanctions. A separate U.N. nuclear watchdog, the International Atomic Energy Agency, reported this month that it had been denied access to Iran’s nuclear facilities.
這場衝突圍繞美國提出的一項決議展開。該決議旨在延長一個專家小組的工作期限。這個小組負責監督伊朗的核計劃及其規避國際制裁的行為。另一個聯合國核監督機構——國際原子能機構——本月報告稱，伊朗方面已拒絕該機構進入其核設施。
Britain and France, along with eight nonpermanent members of the Council, voted in favor of the U.S. resolution, with two countries abstaining. But China and Russia, two permanent members of the Council with veto powers, voted down the measure, saying that the sanctions had expired and that the issue should no longer be up for debate. Their representatives also urged a diplomatic end to the U.S. war with Iran and said they supported Tehran’s right to a civilian nuclear program.
英國和法國以及八個非常任理事國投票支持美國提出的決議，兩個國家棄權。但中俄這兩個擁有否決權的常任理事國投票否決了這項措施，稱相關制裁已經到期，因此這一問題不應再成為討論議題。兩國代表還敦促通過外交途徑結束美國與伊朗之間的戰爭，並表示支持德黑蘭發展民用核計劃的權利。
Jennifer Locetta, a U.S. ambassador to the United Nations, called the Russian and Chinese vetoes an “act of obstructionism” and said they were aimed at stifling reporting “that implicates their own activities in support of rogue regimes.”
美國常駐聯合國副代表珍妮佛·洛切塔稱俄羅斯和中國的否決是「阻撓行為」，並表示其目的是壓制「揭露它們自己支持流氓政權活動」的報告。
She added that the vetoes would create “a monitoring gap that only benefits the Iranian regime and those who profit from evading those measures.”
她還說，否決將造成「監督真空，只會讓伊朗政權以及那些從規避這些措施中獲利的人受益」。
In May, U.S. officials said Russia was shipping drone components to Iran via the Caspian Sea, helping Tehran rebuild its offensive abilities amid its war with the United States. Previously, officials also said Russia was providing intelligence to Iran during the war, including satellite imagery showing the locations of warships and military personnel.
美國官員5月表示，俄羅斯正通過裏海向伊朗運送無人機部件，幫助德黑蘭在與美國的戰爭中重建進攻能力。此前，美國官員還表示，俄羅斯在戰爭中向伊朗提供情報，包括顯示軍艦和軍事人員位置的衛星圖像。
廣告
The war, which began with U.S.-Israeli strikes in Iran in late February, has opened a new front in the rivalry between China and the United States. Chinese purchases of Iranian oil have helped keep Iran’s economy afloat.
這場戰爭始於2月底美國和以色列對伊朗發動的襲擊，它還為中美之間的競爭開闢了新戰線。中國購買伊朗石油幫助維持了伊朗經濟的運轉。
In August, China reacted forcefully after Mr. Trump announced a new and aggressive sanctions campaign against Iran, with Beijing ordering Chinese companies not to comply with the measures.
8月，川普宣布針對伊朗發起新的、力度更大的制裁行動後，中國作出強烈反應，下令中國企業不得遵守這些制裁措施。
Mr. Fu said on Thursday that U.S. decisions to pull out of a 2015 nuclear agreement with Iran and to wage war against the nation this year were “at the root of the current crisis.”
傅聰週四表示，美國決定退出2015年與伊朗達成的核協議並於今年對伊朗發動戰爭是「當前危機的根源」。
Complicating matters for the United States on Thursday, a U.N. fact-finding mission issued a report stating that it had “found reasonable grounds to believe the United States committed a war crime by launching indiscriminate attacks resulting in the loss of life or injury to civilians or damage to civilian objects.” The report cited two U.S. attacks, including a missile strike in February on an Iranian school that killed more than 150 people, mostly children.
週四，讓美國處境更加複雜的是，一個聯合國實況調查團發布報告稱，它「有合理理由認為，美國發動無差別攻擊，造成平民傷亡或民用物體受損，因而犯下戰爭罪」。報告列舉了兩起美國發動的襲擊，其中包括2月對伊朗一所學校的導彈襲擊，造成150多人死亡，其中大多數是兒童。
U.S. officials acknowledged in March that a preliminary Pentagon investigation had found the United States was at fault for the strikes. The Trump administration has denied that the U.S. military targets civilians, but has not released the findings of the investigation.
美國官員3月承認，五角大廈的一項初步調查認定，美國應對這些襲擊負責。川普政府否認美軍以平民為攻擊目標，但尚未公布調查結果。
The latest confrontation at the U.N. came the day after a high-level meeting between the foreign ministers of China and Iran in Beijing, at a time when Mr. Trump appears to be running out of options on Iran.
聯合國的最新一輪交鋒發生在中伊兩國外長在北京舉行高級別會晤的第二天，而與此同時，川普似乎在伊朗問題上可用的選項已經越來越少。
廣告
Mr. Trump and Mr. Xi are scheduled to meet in Washington next week. Iran is expected to feature prominently in their discussions, along with the potential extension of a U.S.-China trade truce and ways to cooperate on artificial intelligence safety.
川普和習近平定於下週在華盛頓會晤。預計伊朗問題將成為兩人討論的重要議題之一，此外還包括延長中美貿易休戰的可能性，以及雙方如何在人工智慧安全方面開展合作。

Zane Irwin是一名國際新聞記者，也是2026-27年時報獎學金項目成員，這是一個為職業生涯初期的記者設置的項目。
