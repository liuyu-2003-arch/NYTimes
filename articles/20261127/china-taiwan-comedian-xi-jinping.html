台灣喜劇演員黃逸豪：「習近平是我的繆斯」

Attacked After Mocking China’s Leader, His Shows Are Now Selling Out

郭莉莉, PEI-LIN WU • 2026-11-27
A Taiwanese comedian was attacked possibly because he made fun of Xi Jinping. It may be the best thing that’s ever happened to him.
一位台灣喜劇演員可能因為調侃習近平而遭到襲擊。這或許是他人生中發生過的最好的事。
At a neighborhood with noodle shops and foot massage parlors in southern Taipei, we met the entertainer, Huang Yi-hao, and walked with him to a quiet alley outside his apartment. He pointed to the spot where, one early evening in September, a car swerved into him as he was parking his scooter after picking up fried chicken for dinner.
在台北南部一個遍布麵館和足底按摩店的社區，我們見到了這位名叫黃逸豪的藝人，並與他一起走到公寓外一條安靜的小巷裡。他指著那個地點——9月的一個傍晚，他剛買好炸雞準備回家吃晚飯，正停放自己的踏板摩托車時，一輛汽車突然急轉彎撞上了他。
The collision knocked him backward and his scooter into him. The driver and another man came out of the car, apologizing profusely before they called an ambulance. Mr. Huang, who was slightly injured, thought it was just an accident. “They were like, ‘Bro, bro, I’m so sorry, my hand slipped on the wheel,’” Mr. Huang said. The comedian hobbled to the side of the road and ate his dinner while waiting for the ambulance.
他被向後撞倒，踏板摩托車也砸在了他身上。司機和另一名男子從車裡出來，連連道歉，叫了救護車。受了輕傷的黃逸豪當時以為這只是一場意外。「他們當時說，『大哥，不好意思，我剛剛手滑掉啦，』」黃逸豪說。這位喜劇演員一瘸一拐地走到路邊，一邊等救護車一邊吃完了晚餐。
He would soon learn that the authorities in Taiwan suspected the car crash was deliberate, an attack directed by the Communist Party in China to punish the comedian for mocking China’s leader and to stop him from taking the stage.
他很快得知，台灣當局懷疑這起車禍是蓄意為之，是中國共產黨指使的一次襲擊，目的是懲罰這位喜劇演員嘲諷中國領導人，並阻止他登台演出。
China has denied being connected to any attack on Mr. Huang. It called the accusations a “malicious smear” by Taiwan’s government and an effort to incite anti-China sentiment before local elections in Taiwan this week.
中國否認與任何針對黃逸豪的襲擊有關。中方稱這些指控是台灣政府的「惡意抹黑」，是本週台灣地方選舉前煽動反華情緒的企圖。
Mr. Huang, 44, said that the attack would not stop him from performing. “We can’t let them think that violence is an effective tool. That’s not OK,” he said.
44歲的黃逸豪表示，這次襲擊不會阻止他繼續演出。「我們不可以讓對方覺得暴力是一種有用的手段，這是不可以的，」他說。
He would resist any effort, even by one of the most powerful men in the world, to strip a comic of the right to ridicule political figures. “I have no choice. I was forced onto the battlefield,” he said.
他將抵制任何剝奪喜劇演員嘲諷政治人物權利的企圖，哪怕這種企圖來自世界上最有權勢的人之一。「我別辦法。我也是被逼上戰場，」他說。

The comedian roasted Mr. Xi during a comedy festival last year, comparing Mr. Xi’s physique with that of Winnie the Pooh. (The beloved cartoon bear is a meme commonly used by critics of Mr. Xi.) He panned many of the leader’s signature policies and led the audience in a raucous call-and-response in which he threw out a vulgar term and the audience yelled Mr. Xi’s name in return.
去年，這位喜劇演員在一場喜劇節上調侃了習近平，將習近平的體型比作小熊維尼。（這隻深受喜愛的卡通熊是批評習近平的人士常用的網路迷因。）他抨擊了這位領導人的許多標誌性政策，並帶領觀眾進行了一場喧鬧的呼應互動——他拋出一個粗俗的詞彙，觀眾則大喊習近平的名字作為回應。
Clips of that routine were reposted widely on X and amplified by accounts that routinely criticize the Communist Party.
那段演出的影片片段在X上被廣泛轉發，並由經常批評共產黨的帳號進一步擴散。
Taiwanese prosecutors said late last month that the police had arrested the driver and four other men for attempted aggravated assault on Mr. Huang. The five men are expected to go on trial in December. The prosecutors said that one of the men, with the surname Cheng, had been recruited by an individual from China, known as “K,” to stop Mr. Huang from performing in September by breaking his arms and legs. In return, Mr. Cheng would have been paid nearly $14,000.
台灣檢方上月底表示，警方已逮捕了肇事司機和另外四名男子，罪名是涉嫌對黃逸豪實施重傷害未遂。這五名男子預計將於12月受審。檢方稱，其中一名姓鄭的男子受一名來自中國、代號為「K」的人士招募，要求令黃逸豪斷手斷腳，來阻止他在9月演出。作為回報，鄭姓男子將獲得台幣45萬元的報酬。
Prosecutors said that a GPS tracker was installed on Mr. Huang’s motorbike so that the attackers could learn his daily routine. He was also tailed for several days before the staged crash.
檢方表示，襲擊者在黃逸豪的摩托車上安裝了GPS追蹤器，以便摸清他的日常行蹤。在這起精心策劃的車禍發生前，他還被跟蹤了好幾天。
Before this happened, Mr. Huang had had a moderate following as a performer of xiangsheng, a traditional form of Chinese comedy. Today, he is more popular than ever.
在此之前，黃逸豪作為一名中國傳統喜劇形式「相聲」的表演者，擁有中等規模的粉絲群。如今，他比以往任何時候都更受歡迎。
Tickets to his comedy troupe’s two upcoming shows are sold out. He has been invited on talk shows almost every day since the arrests. Chen Shui-Bian, a former president of Taiwan, posted a meme in support of him.
他所屬喜劇團體即將舉行的兩場演出門票已全部售罄。自嫌犯被捕以來，他幾乎每天都受邀參加脫口秀節目。台灣前總統陳水扁也發布了一張網路迷因圖以示對他的支持。
As Mr. Huang announced on Facebook that he would be adding extra shows, he posted a cartoon image of the Chinese leader with the words: “Deepest thanks to the best ticket promoter, Chairman Xi Jinping.”
當黃逸豪在Facebook上宣布將加開演出場次時，他發布了一張這位中國領導人的卡通畫像，並配文：「最感謝的售票員，習近平主席。」

“You could say Xi Jinping is my comedy muse,” Mr. Huang told us.
「習近平也算是我一個喜劇的繆斯，」黃逸豪告訴我們。
The attack has given Mr. Huang great material. When he tested his jokes recently at Comedy Plus, a popular venue in Taipei, the audience was quickly in stitches.
這次襲擊為黃逸豪提供了絕佳的素材。最近他在台北一家熱門俱樂部卡米地喜劇俱樂部試演新段子時，觀眾很快就笑得前仰後合。
In his account of “being transformed from a comedian to a pro-democracy freedom fighter,” he said the plot made little financial sense: Someone was willing to pay $14,000 to stop Mr. Huang from performing a show in September when the ticket sales for that gig amounted to only about $4,000.
在講述自己「從一個喜劇演員變成一個民主鬥士」的經歷時，他表示這場陰謀在經濟上毫無道理：竟然有人願意花45萬台幣阻止黃逸豪9月的一場演出，而那場演出的門票總收入才大約13萬台幣。
“Are you brain dead? Just give the cash directly to me,” he shouted from the stage. (According to the prosecutors, the men never received their full payment because Mr. Huang’s injuries were deemed too light.)
「腦袋有問題嗎？錢直接給我就好了啊，」他在台上大喊。（據檢方稱，這夥人從未拿到全額報酬，因為黃逸豪的傷勢被認為太輕了。）
He also pointed out how little his attackers understood about the world of stand-up.
他還指出，襲擊者對喜劇演員的世界是多麼一無所知。
Even with broken arms and legs, “who among us wouldn’t still get up onstage?” he asked the room. The comedians in the audience cheered.
哪怕斷手斷腳，「誰會不上台？」他向全場發問。台下坐著的喜劇演員們紛紛歡呼。

“We would be up there in a full cast,” he said.
「我們等等包著石膏也會上台，」他說。
Even though Mr. Huang made light of the attack, some in the audience said they had been shocked by the news.
儘管黃逸豪對這次襲擊輕描淡寫，但一些觀眾表示他們聽到這個消息時感到非常震驚。
“I never thought that the other side would take this so seriously,” said Han Chang, 24, a resident of the nearby city of Taoyuan. “It’s definitely scary.”
「沒想道在對方那邊會把這種事看得這麼嚴重，」來自鄰近城市桃園的24歲居民張涵（音）說。「確實會害怕。」
Chinese intimidation of Taiwanese public figures, especially those opposed to Beijing’s claim over the self-governed island, is not unusual. But an attack on a Taiwanese comedian is new, and it is not clear why Mr. Huang was singled out.
中國大陸對台灣公眾人物的恐嚇並不罕見，尤其是針對那些反對北京對這座自治島嶼主權主張的人。但針對一名台灣喜劇演員的襲擊卻是前所未有的，目前尚不清楚為何唯獨黃逸豪會成為目標。
Mr. Huang thinks that it may be because he used a classic storytelling format known as “bashanping,” where performers describe the great deeds of Chinese historical figures.
黃逸豪認為，這可能是因為他使用了一種名為「八扇屏」的傳統說書形式，表演者通常用這種形式來講述中國歷史人物的豐功偉績。
It is performed in a style called guankou, a dense, rapid-fire recitation that requires creativity as well as perfect articulation, timing and rhythm — like spoken word on fast forward, or a rap battle in highbrow Chinese.
這種表演採用一種名為「貫口」的技巧，這是一種密集、連珠炮式的背誦，需要極高的創造力以及完美的吐字、時機把握和節奏感，就像快進播放的朗誦詩，或者用文雅的中文進行的說唱對決。

Using traditional Chinese culture and symbolism to ridicule Mr. Xi’s authoritarianism could be “very destructive,” Mr. Huang said, which is one reason he chose to do it. Mr. Xi has promoted traditional Chinese culture at home and abroad, in an attempt to connect Communist Party rule with China’s long history.
黃逸豪說，利用中國傳統文化和象徵符號來嘲諷習近平的威權主義可能具有「極強的殺傷力」，這也是他選擇這麼做的原因之一。習近平在國內外大力弘揚中國傳統文化，試圖將共產黨的統治與中國悠久的歷史聯繫起來。
“I felt this was something that could only be done in Taiwan, and perhaps only something that I would do,” Mr. Huang said.
「我就覺得它是一個只有台灣能做，然後甚至只有我會做的東西，」黃逸豪說。
Albert Liu, a comedian in Taipei whose set at Comedy Plus preceded Mr. Huang’s, said the attackers’ plan had clearly backfired.
台北喜劇演員阿爾伯特·劉（音）在卡米地喜劇俱樂部的演出排在黃逸豪之前，他表示襲擊者的計劃顯然適得其反。
“If they felt the original content was something no one should ever see, they actually ended up causing even more people to see it,” Mr. Liu said.
「他們如果覺得原本哪個東西不可以讓任何人看到的話，他們反而讓這個內容被更多人看到，」阿爾伯特·劉說。
Jokes aside, Mr. Huang also sees tragedy in what happened — that a person could be so desperate that they would violently hurt another for so little money. He feels mainly pity toward the driver who rammed into him, Huang Chia-ching.
拋開玩笑不談，黃逸豪也從整件事中看到了悲劇的一面——一個人竟然能如此走投無路，為了區區一點錢就去暴力傷害他人。對於開車撞他的司機黃家慶，他主要感到的是同情。
Huang Chia-ching, 32, who is not related to Mr. Huang, could not be reached for comment.
32歲的黃家慶與黃逸豪並無親屬關係，記者未能聯繫到他置評。
For days after the crash, the driver would message him to ask how he was doing. One day, he brought Mr. Huang a box of apples as an apology. On a messaging app, the two talked about how difficult it was to make a living. The driver said he worked in construction, which he believed was the only kind of work that he, with little education, could do.
車禍後的幾天裡，這名司機一直發信息詢問黃逸豪的恢復情況。有一天，他還給黃逸豪拎來一箱蘋果賠禮道歉。在即時通訊軟體上，兩人聊起謀生有多麼艱難。司機說自己在建築工地幹活，他覺得對於沒什麼學歷的自己來說，這是唯一能做的工作。
In one chat, the driver told Mr. Huang not to underplay his success as a comedian, while Mr. Huang encouraged the driver to try new things like filming videos about his work and sharing them on social media. Even after the police told Mr. Huang about the driver’s involvement in the hit job, he found it hard to blame, much less hate the man.
在一次聊天中，那位司機告訴黃逸豪不要低估自己作為喜劇演員的成功，黃逸豪則鼓勵司機嘗試新事物，比如拍攝自己日常工作的影片並分享到社群媒體上。即使在警方告知黃逸豪這名司機參與了這起買兇傷人案後，他依然感覺自己很難去責怪對方、更不用說去恨這個人了。
Mr. Huang thinks that the driver stopped the car after hearing him shout in pain. “He snapped back to his senses. So, ultimately, I don’t hold a grudge against him,” the comedian said from a cafe just blocks away from where he was hit.
黃逸豪認為，那位司機當時是在聽到他痛苦的叫喊聲後才剎住了車。「他是有稍微清醒過來的。所以我後來真的對他沒有很怨恨，」這位喜劇演員在距離被撞地點僅幾個街區的一家咖啡館裡說。
