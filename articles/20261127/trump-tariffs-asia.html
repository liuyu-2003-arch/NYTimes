新關稅再將矛頭指向北京

Trump’s Latest Tariffs Revive Focus on Pressuring China

艾莎 • 2026-11-27
Fresh tariffs proposed by President Trump this week were a reminder to the world that he still intends to settle scores with trading partners he believes are hurting the U.S. economy, despite persistent political and legal pushback.
川普總統本週提出的新關稅措施提醒世界：儘管持續面臨政治和法律阻力，他仍打算與那些在他看來正在損害美國經濟的貿易夥伴算賬。
The administration proposed new import taxes on goods from dozens of other countries it accused of unfair trade practices related to “excess capacity.” For most countries, the tariffs would simply use a different legal authority to put back in place levies previously negotiated but struck down by the U.S. Supreme Court in February. The new tariffs are not set to take effect until early next year, following a public comment period and hearings that begin in January.
川普政府提議對來自其他數十個國家的商品徵收新的進口關稅，指責這些國家存在與「產能過剩」有關的不公平貿易行為。對大多數國家而言，川普政府不過是換了一個法律依據，以恢復此前通過談判確定、卻在今年2月被美國最高法院裁定無效的關稅。新關稅預計要到明年初、在公眾意見徵詢期和1月開始的聽證會之後才會生效。
The latest battery of proposed tariffs revived President Trump’s focus on reducing China’s dominant role in supply chains. China was mentioned hundreds of times in the reports released to justify the levies.
最新提出的一系列關稅措施再次凸顯了川普總統對削弱中國在供應鏈中主導地位的重視。在川普政府解釋加徵關稅理由的報告中，中國被提及數百次。
Countries were blamed for failing to restrict Chinese investment and imports, acts that the Trump administration argued led to a “dependence on and vulnerabilities in supply chains for firms and in sectors critical to the functioning of the U.S. economy.”
川普政府指責一些國家未能限制來自中國的投資和進口，這些做法造成了「對美國經濟運轉至關重要的企業和行業在供應鏈上的依賴和脆弱性」。
廣告
China, in response to the tariffs, called the concept of excess capacity “a false premise” and a “pretext for political maneuvering.” Japan and India bluntly said their economies had no excess capacity.
針對這些關稅，中國稱產能過剩是「一個偽命題」，是「政治操弄的藉口」。日本和印度則直截了當地表示，本國經濟不存在產能過剩問題。
In Singapore, a former official pointed out that the United States exported more to his country than it imported. In India, a former trade official called the threat of new tariffs “bait” to force countries into “one-sided” deals.
在新加坡，一名前官員指出，美國對新加坡的出口多於進口。在印度，一名前貿易官員稱，新關稅威脅是「誘餌」，意在迫使各國接受「不對等的」協議。
And while China looked relatively unscathed under a pause in trade hostilities that capped U.S. tariffs at 20 percent, the new proposal signaled that tariffs on Chinese goods could double if the cease-fire between the two countries expires in January. For the Trump administration, the aim is to preserve leverage if the two sides were to return to a full-blown trade war.
雖然在美國關稅上限為20%的貿易休戰安排下，中國似乎並未受到太大衝擊，但新提案釋放出一個信號：如果兩國之間的休戰協議於明年1月到期，中國商品面臨的關稅可能翻倍。川普政府的目的是，一旦雙方重新陷入全面貿易戰，美國仍能握有談判籌碼。
The latest tariff proposal will loom over an important meeting between Xi Jinping, China’s leader, and Mr. Trump next month, experts said.
專家表示，最新的關稅提案將給中國領導人習近平與川普下個月舉行的一場重要會晤蒙上陰影。
For nations in Asia that depend on trade and investment from both the United States and China, the new plan evoked Mr. Trump’s earlier calls to decouple their economies from Beijing.
對於在貿易和投資方面既依賴美國又依賴中國的亞洲國家而言，這項新計劃讓人想起川普此前要求它們在經濟上與中國脫鉤的呼籲。
The spate of tariffs also highlighted the differences between countries that have already negotiated with the Trump administration on tariff rates and those that have not yet done deals.
這一連串關稅措施也凸顯了已經與川普政府談妥關稅稅率的國家和尚未達成協議的國家之間的處境差異。
廣告
Relief for Those With a Deal
已達成協議的國家鬆了一口氣
In Japan, South Korea and Taiwan, governments expressed confidence that their negotiated 15 percent tariffs would remain in place.
日本、韓國和台灣政府均表示有信心維持此前通過談判確定的15%關稅稅率。
Japan on Thursday said that this was the rate it had negotiated following its pledge to invest in and help finance $550 billion in projects in the United States. South Korea also expressed relief that the 15 percent tariff cap on its exports, a provision secured under a $350 billion investment agreement with the United States, remained in place.
日本週四表示，這是日本承諾向美國價值5500億美元的項目提供投資和融資支持後，通過談判確定的稅率。韓國也對其出口商品15%的關稅上限得以維持感到寬慰，這一條款是韓國在與美國達成的3500億美元投資協議中爭取到的。
For others that had signed trade deals but not negotiated a cap on tariffs, like Malaysia, Indonesia, Cambodia and Bangladesh, there was more apprehension about what comes next. “The terms and conditions within the deal have changed for these countries,” said Deborah Elms, head of trade policy at the Singapore-based Hinrich Foundation.
對於馬來西亞、印度尼西亞、柬埔寨和孟加拉國等雖已簽署貿易協議但未談成關稅上限的國家而言，它們對接下來的事態發展更為憂慮。「對這些國家來說，協議的條款和條件已經發生了變化，」總部位於新加坡的韓禮士基金會貿易政策主管黛博拉·埃爾姆斯說。
While each country had signed deals with the Trump administration for an 18 percent tariff, now they faced the possibility that those tariffs could increase to 25 percent. Thailand was also in limbo, having agreed to sign a deal with an 18 percent tariff earlier this month, without finalizing it.
儘管這些國家分別與川普政府簽署了將關稅稅率定為18%的協議，但現在它們面臨稅率可能升至25%的局面。泰國的前景也尚不明朗，該國本月早些時候已同意簽署一項將關稅稅率定為18%的協議，但協議尚未最終敲定。
Risks for Those Without a Deal
尚未達成協議的國家面臨風險
廣告
Countries that have not signed trade deals with the United States appeared on shakier ground.
尚未與美國簽署貿易協議的國家似乎處境更不穩固。
India and Vietnam stood out. Both countries, while still engaged in trade negotiations, face tariffs of 25 percent and 35 percent if they could not agree to the terms of a deal with the United States.
印度和越南的情況尤為突出。兩國仍在進行貿易談判，但如果無法就協議條款與美國達成一致，它們將分別面臨25%和35%的關稅。
In Vietnam, where the pressure is most acute, officials have made clear to their American counterparts that they are cracking down on exports made in China and shipped through Vietnam. They have also said they aim to reduce dependence on Chinese suppliers and to build up a homegrown industrial base, while arguing that no country can be expected to completely decouple from China given its dominance in the supply chain of nearly every manufactured product.
越南面臨的壓力最為嚴峻。越南官員已向美方明確表示，他們正在嚴厲打擊在中國生產、經越南轉運出口的商品。他們還表示，越南力求減少對中國供應商的依賴，建立本土工業基礎，但同時指出，鑒於中國在幾乎所有製成品的供應鏈中都佔據主導地位，不能指望任何國家與中國徹底脫鉤。
The proposed 35 percent rate for Vietnam was among the highest in the world. Vietnamese officials expressed surprise but responded cautiously, mostly trying to downplay the latest tariff announcement.
擬對越南徵收的35%關稅是全球最高的關稅稅率之一。越南官員對此表示意外，但反應謹慎，主要試圖淡化最新關稅公告的影響。
“I must stress that this is a proposed tariff rate,” Chu Thang Trung, a senior official at Vietnam’s Ministry of Industry and Trade, said in an interview on Thursday. “It has not taken effect.”
「我必須強調，這只是擬議中的關稅稅率，」越南工貿部高級官員朱勝忠週四在接受採訪時說。「它尚未生效。」
In India, the tone from the government was more defiant. Its ministry of commerce said it had “engaged constructively throughout this investigation” and added that “India does not have structural excess capacity in any sector.”
在印度，政府語氣更為強硬。印度商務部表示，它「在整個調查過程中始終以建設性的態度參與其中」，並稱「印度任何行業都不存在結構性產能過剩」。
廣告
India produces more than what its domestic economy can consume in some traditional industries, like carpet-weaving, as well as in basic foodstuffs, going from an importer of rice to a major exporter.
在地毯編織等一些傳統行業以及基本食品生產領域，印度的產量已經超過國內經濟所能消化的水平，該國也已從大米進口國轉變為主要出口國。
Increasingly, as India pushes its factories to expand in areas like consumer electronics and renewable energy, it is making more iPhones than its citizens can buy and more solar modules than its power distributors can connect to the grid.
隨著印度推動本國工廠在消費電子和可再生能源等領域擴張，其生產的iPhone越來越多地超出本國民眾的購買能力，生產的太陽能組件也超過配電公司的併網能力。
India has been stuck in trade negotiations with the United States since April 2025. In its new report, the Trump administration said Indian goods could get a 17 percent tariff. Without a deal, their exports would be hit with a 25 percent tariff.
自2025年4月以來，印度與美國的貿易談判一直陷於僵局。在最新報告中，川普政府表示，印度商品可能適用17%的關稅稅率。如果無法達成協議，印度出口商品將面臨25%的關稅。
This offer was “bait,” said Ajay Srivastava, a retired Indian trade official, that was being dangled by the U.S. government in order to “force trading partners into signing one-sided trade deals.”
印度退休貿易官員阿賈伊·斯裡瓦斯塔瓦表示，這是美國政府拋出的「誘餌」，目的是「迫使貿易夥伴簽署單方面有利於美國的貿易協議」。

Damien Cave和Tung Ngo自越南、River Akira Davis自日本、Choe Sang-Hun自韓國、Alex Travelli自印度對本文有報導貢獻。

艾莎(Alexandra Stevenson)是《紐約時報》記者，主要報導中國經濟及其在全球的影響。
