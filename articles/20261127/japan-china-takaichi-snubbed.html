高市早苗訪問中國時是否遭到冷遇？

Was Japan’s Leader Snubbed at China’s Big Summit? A Visual Evaluation.

赫海威, PABLO ROBLES • 2026-11-27
Was Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi of Japan snubbed during China’s big summit?
日本首相高市早苗在中國舉辦的重要峰會期間是否遭到了冷遇？
That question has dominated the headlines in Japan in recent days, with some officials saying that Beijing intentionally undermined Ms. Takaichi during her visit last week to Shenzhen, China, for the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation forum.
這一問題近幾天成為日本的新聞熱點，一些官員表示，北京上週在高市早苗訪問中國深圳出席亞太經合組織(APEC)論壇期間蓄意怠慢了她。
Japanese commentators drew attention to a sizable drop at the top of the stairs that greeted Ms. Takaichi’s plane, which forced her to descend awkwardly. They noted the lack of cheering crowds at the airport, a gesture that was afforded to other leaders. They pointed out that she was met on the tarmac by a low-ranking official.
日本評論人士指出，迎接高市早苗專機的舷梯頂部存在明顯落差，迫使她走下飛機時顯得十分狼狽。他們注意到機場沒有歡呼的人群，而其他領導人則享有這一禮遇。他們還指出，在停機坪迎接她的是一名級別較低的官員。
The scrutiny comes as relations between the two countries are at a low point. China has been at odds with Japan since last year, when Ms. Takaichi suggested that Japan could be forced to get involved militarily in case of a Chinese attack on Taiwan. Beijing considers Taiwan, a self-governing democracy, part of its territory.
引發這一審視之際，兩國關係正處於低谷。自去年高市早苗暗示若中國進攻台灣日本可能被迫進行軍事介入以來，中國一直與日本存在摩擦。北京將實行自治民主制度的台灣視為其領土的一部分。
廣告
Asked about the pared-down welcome, a spokesman for China’s Foreign Ministry, Guo Jiakun, brushed aside the criticism at a recent news conference in Beijing.
當被問及簡化歡迎儀式一事時，中國外交部發言人郭嘉昆近日在北京舉行的記者會上對批評不以為然。
“As the host,” he said, “China makes appropriate protocol arrangements for all participants in accordance with international practice.”
「作為東道主，」他說，「中方根據國際慣例，對所有參會方作出適當的禮賓安排。」
The Chinese Foreign Ministry did not respond to a request for comment.
中國外交部未回應置評請求。
Here is a look at how Ms. Takaichi was received in China, compared with other world leaders.
以下是高市早苗在中國所受接待與其他世界領導人的對比情況。
The Stairs
舷梯
When Ms. Takaichi arrived in Shenzhen, she faced an immediate problem. There was a drop of roughly 16 inches between her aircraft’s doorway and the boarding stairs. Ms. Takaichi seemed surprised; she gripped the door and proceeded slowly down the first step.
高市早苗抵達深圳時，立刻遇到了一個問題。她的飛機艙門與登機舷梯之間存在約40厘米的落差。高市早苗似乎感到驚訝；她緊緊抓住艙門，緩慢邁下了第一級台階。
Ms. Takaichi has said in the past that she has rheumatoid arthritis and an artificial joint in her leg. Japanese commentators said the incident posed a safety risk.
高市早苗過去曾表示自己患有類風濕性關節炎，腿部裝有人工關節。日本評論人士稱，這一事件構成了安全隱患。
“What if the prime minister, who is disembarking in a skirt and high heels, were to lose her footing and get injured?” Yasuhide Nakayama, a lawmaker with the governing Liberal Democratic Party, wrote on social media. “We should demand a proper explanation from the Chinese side regarding the installation of the gangway and the safety checks.”
「穿著裙子和高跟鞋下飛機的首相如果踩空受傷怎麼辦？」執政黨自民黨議員中山泰秀在社群媒體上寫道。「我們應該要求中方就舷梯的安裝及安全檢查作出合理解釋。」
Still, some officials said it was unclear whether the discrepancy was purposeful, and that it could have been a simple malfunction.
不過，一些官員表示，尚不清楚這一落差是否為有意為之，並表示可能僅僅是一次普通的設備故障。
Koichi Hagiuda, an L.D.P. official, said at a news conference this week: “I believe that the host nation, China, did not intend to be disrespectful. It was likely an unforeseen, spontaneous occurrence.”
自民黨官員萩生田光一本週在記者會上表示：「我認為東道主中國並非有意失禮。這很可能是一起無法預見的突發事件。」
The Crowds
人群
China put on the pomp and circumstance for visiting leaders. President Trump, President Lee Jae​ Myung of South Korea and Vietnam’s leader, To Lam, were greeted on the tarmac by boisterous crowds, dancers, bouquets and a flowing red carpet.
中國為來訪領導人安排了隆重的歡迎儀式。美國總統川普、韓國總統李在明以及越南領導人蘇林在停機坪上受到了歡呼的人群、舞者、花束和紅地毯的迎接。
But for Ms. Takaichi the crowds were gone, and there was no cheering, dancing or red carpet.
但到了高市早苗這裡，人群不見了，也沒有歡呼、舞蹈或紅地毯。
廣告
Akio Takahara, a visiting professor at Tokyo Woman’s Christian University who specializes in Chinese politics, said the reception showed that “some in China wanted to tell Takaichi that they didn’t like her and that she’s not welcomed.”
專攻中國政治的東京女子大學訪問教授高原明生表示，這一接待表明「中國國內有些人想告訴高市，他們不喜歡她，也不歡迎她」。
“Japan-China relations are a bit like U.S.-Europe relations: Both sides harbor a psychological complex toward the other side,” he said. “The Chinese — although they are objectively bigger and stronger than Japan — they still aren’t confident enough to get over this complex.”
「日中關係有點像美歐關係：雙方都對彼此抱有一種心理情結，」他說。「中國人——儘管客觀上比日本更龐大、更強大——仍然沒有足夠的自信來克服這種情結。」
China’s Foreign Ministry did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
中國外交部未立即回應置評請求。
The Greeter
接機官員
China is known to choreograph every detail of major summits. So it was striking, analysts said, that the government dispatched a relatively low-level official, Zhang Qiyue, a retired Chinese ambassador to Greece, to welcome Ms. Takaichi on the tarmac.
中國向來以精心編排重大峰會的每一個細節而聞名。因此，分析人士指出，中國政府派出級別相對較低的官員——退休的中國前駐希臘大使章啟月——在停機坪迎接高市早苗，顯得格外引人注目。
By contrast Xi Jinping was there in person to accept a hug from Mr. Trump, the first time Mr. Xi had personally received a foreign dignitary at the airport as China’s leader. Vladimir V. Putin was met by Cai Qi, a trusted deputy of Mr. Xi’s and one of the most influential members of the Chinese leader’s circle. And Mr. Lee was greeted by a senior official from the Chinese province of Guangdong.
相比之下，習近平親自到場接受了川普的擁抱，這是習近平作為中國領導人首次到機場迎接外國政要。弗拉基米爾·V·普丁則由習近平信任的副手、中國領導人核心圈最具影響力的成員之一蔡奇迎接。李在明由中國廣東省的一名高級官員迎接。
廣告
Still, as the APEC meeting got underway, Ms. Takaichi seemed keen to move on. She enthusiastically greeted Mr. Xi at a banquet a few hours later. The two shook hands briefly and posed for photos, their first encounter since the start of the diplomatic feud over Taiwan. They repeated the routine the next morning. After the forum concluded, Ms. Takaichi returned to the airport in Shenzhen, boarded her plane and headed back to Tokyo.
不過，隨著APEC會議拉開帷幕，高市早苗似乎急於翻過這一頁。幾小時後，她在晚宴上熱情地向習近平致意。兩人簡短握手併合影留念，這是自圍繞台灣問題爆發外交爭端以來兩人的首次會面。次日上午，兩人重複了這一禮節。論壇結束後，高市早苗返回深圳機場，登上專機飛回東京。
