英偉達暫停向東南亞銷售部分先進晶片

Nvidia Halts Sales to Southeast Asia as U.S. Tightens China Restrictions

ADAM SATARIANO, 孟建國 • 2026-11-27
Nvidia has halted some sales of its most advanced A.I. chips to four countries in Southeast Asia as the Trump administration intensifies efforts to cut Chinese companies off from American computing power.
隨著川普政府進一步加緊切斷中國企業獲取美國算力的管道，英偉達已暫停向東南亞四國銷售部分最先進人工智慧晶片。
Data center operators in Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia have had orders put on hold, according to two people with knowledge of the sales suspension, who spoke on the condition of anonymity to discuss private business dealings.
據兩名此次銷售暫停情況的知情人士透露，馬來西亞、新加坡、泰國和印度尼西亞的數據中心運營商已有訂單被擱置。因涉及非公開的商業往來，這兩名人士要求匿名。
The freeze threatens one of the world’s fastest-growing data center markets, where billions of dollars of projects are under construction. Much of the region’s growth has been fueled by Chinese companies renting access to powerful Nvidia chips that American export controls prohibit them from purchasing directly.
這一凍結措施威脅到全球增長最快的數據中心市場之一，該地區目前有價值數十億美元的項目正在建設中。這種增長很大程度上由中國企業推動，它們通過租賃方式使用高性能的英偉達晶片，而美國出口管制禁止它們直接購買這些晶片。
Nvidia did not provide immediate comment.
英偉達未立即置評。
廣告
The U.S. Commerce Department last week proposed new measures aimed at closing that loophole. Under the new policy, which is still under review, data center operators in several Southeast Asian countries would be required to obtain licenses to acquire Nvidia’s most advanced chips if they supply “significant volumes of computing power” to companies headquartered in a handful of countries deemed high-risk, including China, Russia and Iran.
美國商務部上週提出了旨在堵住這一漏洞的新措施。根據這項仍在審查中的新政策，如果東南亞數個國家的數據中心運營商向總部位於中國、俄羅斯和伊朗等少數被視為高風險國家的企業提供「大量算力」，就必須獲得許可證才能採購英偉達最先進的晶片。
The measures extend Washington’s efforts to restrict China’s technological development, targeting both its ability to acquire advanced American chips and its ability to use them overseas.
這些措施延伸了華盛頓限制中國技術發展的舉措，既針對中國獲取美國先進晶片的能力，也針對其在海外使用這些晶片的能力。
It is the latest turmoil facing Nvidia’s business amid widening concerns in Washington over Chinese access to its powerful chips after Jensen Huang, the company’s chief executive, resigned two weeks ago following allegations of an affair with an executive at the Singaporean data center company Megaspeed.
這是英偉達業務面臨的最新動盪。在公司首席執行官黃仁勛兩週前因被指與新加坡數據中心公司Megaspeed的一名高管有染而辭職後，華盛頓方面對中國獲取該公司高性能晶片的擔憂隨之不斷擴大。
U.S. intelligence officials believe the woman, Alice Huang, was in contact with a Chinese Ministry of State Security officer during their relationship. Beijing has denied spying. This week, the company’s board of directors reinstated Mr. Huang as chief executive following pressure from President Trump.
美國情報官員認為，這位名為黃樂(Alice Huang)的女性在與黃仁勛交往期間曾與中國國家安全部的一名官員有聯繫。北京方面否認從事間諜活動。本週，在川普總統的施壓下，公司董事會恢復了黃仁勛的首席執行官職務。
It is unclear how long the sales suspension will last. One person with knowledge of the halt said it was a stopgap until the Trump administration clarified how companies could obtain licenses to continue buying chips. American-owned data center operators appeared to be exempt from the freeze, which also extended to suppliers like Dell and Supermicro that package Nvidia chips onto A.I. servers, the person said.
目前尚不清楚此次暫停將持續多久。一名了解暫停情況的人士表示，這是一項權宜之計，直至川普政府明確企業如何獲得許可證以繼續購買晶片。該人士稱，美資數據中心運營商似乎不受此次凍結措施的影響，該措施也已擴大至戴爾和超微電腦等將英偉達晶片組裝到人工智慧伺服器上的供應商。
The Financial Times first reported the sales suspension.
《金融時報》率先報導了此次銷售暫停的消息。
廣告
Southeast Asia has become a lucrative market for Nvidia, which has sold billions of dollars worth of chips to data center operators in the region. Many of those companies, known as “neoclouds,” specialize in renting out computing power to other customers. While some cater to local businesses, others count on demand from some of China’s biggest technology companies, including Alibaba, Tencent and ByteDance.
東南亞已成為英偉達利潤豐厚的市場，公司已向該地區的數據中心運營商售出價值數十億美元的晶片。其中許多被稱為「新雲」(neoclouds)的公司專門向其他客戶出租算力。儘管部分公司服務於本地企業，但另一些公司則依賴阿里巴巴、騰訊和字節跳動等中國最大的一些科技公司的需求。
For years, that arrangement has allowed Chinese companies to access some of the world’s most advanced computing infrastructure without owning the chips themselves.
多年來，這種安排使中國企業無需自己擁有晶片，即可使用全球最先進的計算基礎設施。
The sales freeze could have particularly wide-ranging implications in Malaysia, where investors have poured billions of dollars into one of the world’s ambitious data center build-outs. A prolonged interruption in chip supplies could stall construction and expansion plans, jeopardizing investments and putting pressure on the lenders that have financed the projects.
此次銷售凍結可能在馬來西亞產生尤為廣泛的影響，投資者已向該國投入數十億美元，用於開展全球規模最宏大的數據中心建設計劃之一。晶片供應的長期中斷可能會使建設和擴張計劃陷入停滯，危及投資，並給為這些項目提供融資的放貸機構帶來壓力。
