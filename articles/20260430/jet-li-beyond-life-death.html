李連杰談人生中意義重大的10件事

Jet Li Believes in Showing Up on Time, or Earlier

ELISABETH VINCENTELLI • 2026-04-30
In his new memoir, “Beyond Life and Death,” Jet Li describes being sent to the Beijing Sports and Exercise School when he was 8, in 1971. He could have ended up in the swimming section, say, or the soccer one. Instead it was martial arts, and film history was changed.
在新近出版的回憶錄《超越生死》(Beyond Life and Death)中，李連杰回憶了1971年自己八歲時被送入北京體校的經歷。他本可以被分到游泳班或者足球班，但最終進入了武術班，電影史就此改變。
After years of high-level competitions, Li put his training to good use in “Shaolin Temple” in 1982. He became an action star with a string of hits that include four installments of the “Once Upon a Time in China” franchise (1992-1997), “Hero” (2004), “Fearless” (2006), and several Hollywood outings such as “The Expendables” (2010).
多年高水平賽事的磨礪之後，李連杰在1982年的《少林寺》中把一身功夫用到了極致。此後他憑藉一系列賣座影片躋身動作巨星之列，包括《黃飛鴻》系列四部（1992—1997年）、《英雄》（2004年）、《霍元甲》（2006年），以及《敢死隊》（The Expendables, 2010年）等數部好萊塢作品。
Li talks about his career in the book, but he also dives into his journey of self-discovery, most specifically his practice of Tibetan Buddhism, which he started investigating while seeking relief from injury-related pain in 1997. “I learned martial arts and made movies, but I couldn’t find the meaning of life,” the actor said. “For almost 30 years, I have gone to teachers in India, Nepal, Tibet — many, many places.”
在這本書中，李連杰不僅回顧了自己的演藝生涯，也深入探討了他的自我發現之旅，尤其是他修習藏傳佛教的經歷——1997年，他尋求緩解傷病帶來的疼痛，由此開始接觸藏傳佛教。「我學武術、拍電影，卻始終找不到生命的意義，」他說，「將近30年來，我走訪了印度、尼泊爾、西藏的許多上師，去過很多很多地方。」
In a video interview from his home in Singapore, Li, 62, discussed 10 things that mean a lot to him; his daughter Jada (with whom he hosts the YouTube videos series “So Be It”) occasionally helped with translation. These are edited excerpts from that conversation and an email follow-up.
62歲的李連杰在新加坡家中接受影片採訪，聊起了對他意義重大的10件事；他的女兒Jada（父女共同主持YouTube系列節目「So Be It」）時不時幫忙翻譯。以下內容為採訪節選及後續郵件補充，已做編輯整理。
廣告
Mala Beads
念珠
I will ask each of my teachers for one prayer bead [which are strung on a string to form necklaces or garlands]. They remind me of them and their teachings. That’s become a habit. They teach me the most important thing is compassion and wisdom. I love beads. I collect a lot of them.
我會向每位上師求一顆念珠（這些竹子串在一起做成項鏈或念珠串）。它們讓我想起上師們和他們的教誨。這已經成了一種習慣。他們教導我，最重要的是慈悲與智慧。我喜歡念珠，收集了很多。
Stir-Fried Shredded Potatoes
炒馬鈴薯絲
When I grew up, my family was very poor and my mom always cooked potatoes. Since then, wherever I go, I just ask, “Do you have a fresh potato?” I love the taste, and it reminds me of my mom.
我小時候家裡很窮，媽媽總是做馬鈴薯。從那以後，無論我走到哪裡，我都會問：「有沒有新鮮的馬鈴薯？」我喜歡那個味道，它讓我想起媽媽。
Massages
按摩
I was 8 and learning martial arts, and would get injuries. Then I had more when making movies. I would always get injured. You try to fix it, but you never 100 percent fix it. So I get sports massages wherever I go, in the style of the country.
我八歲開始學武，受傷是家常便飯。後來拍電影，受傷更多。總是受傷。你想把它治好，但永遠不可能百分百痊癒。所以我走到哪裡都要做運動推拿，按當地的方式來。 
Instant Coffee
速溶咖啡
I was 11 the first time I went to America, and that was the first time I drank coffee. It was like Chinese traditional medicine — bitter. So I put more sugar [laughs]. It was strange, but good. I went back to China, but we didn’t have coffee in the 1970s. When I made my first movie, the whole crew in Hong Kong would bring in instant coffee. If I did well, they would give me one [laughs]. Now you can have a very special coffee machine, but my memories still go back to that coffee. If I travel, I will buy instant coffee, just for the memory.
我11歲第一次去美國，那也是我第一次喝咖啡。味道像中藥——苦的。所以我就多放糖（笑）。味道很奇怪，但還不錯。回到中國後，70年代國內還沒有咖啡。我拍第一部電影時，劇組從香港帶速溶咖啡過來。如果我表現好，他們就給我一杯（笑）。現在你可以用很高級的咖啡機，但我的記憶還是停留在那種咖啡上。我旅行的時候，會買速溶咖啡，只是為了那份回憶。
Li Shizhen
李時珍
He was a herbalist and physician in the Ming dynasty. He passed away hundreds of years ago, but his book “Compendium of Materia Medica” is still helping people. When you become a little famous, people will ask, “Who’s your idol?” I say I don’t have an idol, but I like Li Shizhen, because he’s not beating up somebody [laughs]. I fight people in movies, and some think it’s cool. But deep inside, I want to help people.
他是明代的草藥師和醫生。死於好幾百年前，但他的《本草綱目》至今仍在造福世人。當你小有名氣之後，別人會問：「你的偶像是誰？」我說我沒有偶像，但我喜歡李時珍，因為他不是打打殺殺的人（笑）。我在電影裡打打殺殺，有些人覺得這很酷。但內心深處，我想幫助別人。 
廣告
Punctuality
守時
When we were late for training as kids, the coach would punish us, make us run 400 meters in a circle, around 20 times. Every time you were late, you ran. So that became stuck in my mind when I was a little boy: You cannot be late. Show your pride and respect people. If you tell me eight o’clock, I will be there 10 or 15 minutes before and wait.
小時候訓練如果遲到，教練會懲罰我們，讓我們繞操場跑20圈左右，一圈400米。遲到一次就跑一次。所以從小這件事就刻在我腦子裡：不能遲到。要有自尊，也要尊重別人。如果你跟我說8點，我會提前10到15分鐘到，在那裡等。
George Lam’s ‘A Man Should Strengthen Himself’
林子祥的《男兒當自強》
This is from “Once Upon a Time in China.” It’s about the need to be strong, and it’s very emotional. Whether I’m learning about myself or making a movie, I hear the song and I think, “Wow, I need to do it again, try again.”
這是《黃飛鴻》的主題歌。唱的是要自強不息，非常有感染力。不管是自我修鍊，還是拍電影，只要聽到這首歌，我就會想：「哇，我要再來一次，再試一次。」
Sunglasses
墨鏡
I became famous when I was young and everybody knew me, everybody tried to take pictures. I was not very comfortable, so I wore a cap. I was also bald for a lot of roles, so it kept me warm. Then I got sunglasses. I have a lot of them. People know I like them and give them to me for my birthday.
我年少成名，人人都認識我，大家都想拍我。我覺得不太自在，所以戴上了帽子。很多角色也要剃光頭，帽子還能保暖。後來又戴上了墨鏡。我有很多副，大家知道我喜歡墨鏡，就會在我生日時送我。
廣告
Awareness
覺知 
A lot of people realize they need to meditate, they need to concentrate, and meditation has become popular. But the goal of meditation is awareness, knowing without judgment. I do an hour daily, and try to maintain awareness the rest of the day.
很多人意識到需要冥想、需要專注，冥想也因此變得流行。但冥想的目標其實是覺知——不加評判地了知。我每天做一小時冥想，其餘時間也盡量保持覺知的狀態。
Appreciation
感恩 
People know how to train their body — be healthy, exercise — but not so many understand how to train their mind. For the past 30 years I’ve been training my mind to figure out the meaning of life and be happier. I say: Relax. Appreciate life, appreciate your teacher, parents, partner, whatever you need to appreciate.
人們知道如何鍛煉身體——保持健康、勤於運動——但真正懂得如何修鍊心靈的人並不多。過去30年裡，我一直在修鍊心靈，探尋生命的意義，讓自己更快樂。我常說：放鬆下來，感恩生活，感恩你的老師、父母、伴侶，感恩一切值得感恩的人和事。
