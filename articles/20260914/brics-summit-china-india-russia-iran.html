金磚峰會：各國營造團結景象，但難掩內部分歧

BRICS Leaders Demand a Global Voice, but Settle for a Vague Call for Peace

DAVID PIERSON, ALEX TRAVELLI • 2026-09-14
The summit of the BRICS group of emerging economies was designed to show what world leadership looks like when the West is not in the room.
金磚國家這個新興經濟體集團的峰會本意是要展示沒有西方參與時的世界領導格局會是什麼樣子。
And as widening clashes in the Middle East send global energy prices soaring, the gathering in New Delhi this weekend gave the leaders of China, India, Russia and several other countries something Washington lacks: a seat at the table with Iran.
而隨著中東衝突不斷升級導致全球能源價格飆升，本週末在新德里舉行的這次峰會讓中國、印度、俄羅斯及其他多個國家的領導人獲得了華盛頓不具備的一樣東西：與伊朗同桌對話的機會。
The summit’s grand setup at a large convention hall in the center of New Delhi, surrounded by a security cordon, relied heavily on visual staging to project unity. The host, Prime Minister Narendra Modi of India, held hands with President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia and President Xi Jinping of China at the center of a group photo in front of a massive water feature. They planted trees and sat around in a venue festooned with slogans like: “Together we build, together we rise.”
峰會在新德里市中心一座戒備森嚴的大型會議中心舉行，通過精心安排的場面竭力營造出團結的形象。在一處巨大的水景前拍攝金磚國家領導人合影時，東道主印度總理莫迪與俄羅斯總統普丁和中國國家主席習近平手牽手站在中心位置。他們一起植樹，並在一個裝飾著「我們共同建設，我們共同崛起」之類口號的會場中圍坐交流。
But the event could not mask the BRICS group’s unwieldy internal divisions, a dynamic that raises questions about the loose bloc’s ability to solve geopolitical crises like the war in the Middle East.
但峰會終究無法掩蓋金磚國家內部難以調和的分歧，而這種態勢也引發了外界對這一鬆散集團能否解決諸如中東戰爭這類地緣政治危機的質疑。
廣告
The group has been split by tensions between Iran and the United Arab Emirates, a U.S. security partner that has been targeted by thousands of Iranian ballistic missiles, cruise missiles and drones since the war started in February. India, which as host influences the agenda, also does not share the same opposition to the West as Russia and Iran, preferring to try to juggle strategic friendships with powers from across the ideological spectrum.
該集團受到伊朗與阿拉伯聯合酋長國之間緊張關係的影響而出現分歧。自2月戰爭爆發以來，伊朗已經向阿聯酋發射了數以千計的彈道導彈、巡航導彈和無人機，阿聯酋是美國的安全夥伴。作為東道國，對峰會議程擁有影響力的印度並不像俄羅斯和伊朗那樣對西方抱持對立立場，而是更傾向於在意識型態光譜兩端的大國之間努力周旋，維持與它們的戰略友好關係。
During the photo op, the president of Iran stood near one end of the line, while the crown prince of Abu Dhabi — representing the United Arab Emirates — stood at the other. Later that day, the group released a joint statement calling for “maximum restraint” in the Middle East while avoiding casting blame on any country.
在合影時，伊朗總統站在一側，代表阿拉伯聯合酋長國的阿布達比王儲站在另一側。當天晚些時候，該集團發表了一份聯合聲明，呼籲中東地區「保持最大限度的剋制」，卻避免指責任何國家。
“They were able to get everyone to agree to a watered down statement,” said Sarang Shidore, the director of the global south program at the Quincy Institute, a research group in Washington. “It is more of a relief for BRICS than it is a great victory.”
「他們能讓所有人同意一份措辭軟化的聲明，」華盛頓智庫昆西研究所全球南方項目主任薩朗·希多爾說，「這對金磚國家來說更像是如釋重負，而不是重大勝利。」
The wording in the lengthy joint statement about the war in the Middle East that apportioned no blame was a retreat from a BRICS statement in 2025, which condemned a 12-day war against Iran from June of that year, noted Manoj Kewalramani, the head of Indo-Pacific studies at the Takshashila Institution in Bengaluru, India.
印度邦加羅爾的智庫塔克沙希拉研究所印太研究負責人馬諾傑·凱瓦爾拉馬尼指出，這份冗長的聯合聲明在中東戰爭問題上未就責任歸咎於任何一方，它是對金磚國家2025年聲明的一次退步——當時的聲明譴責了那年6月對伊朗為期12天的戰爭。
It also acknowledged the “respective national positions” of each member. Such language “preserves room for disagreement and presents a diluted expression of concern in place of a unified BRICS view on the issue,” he wrote.
聲明還承認各成員國「各自的立場」。他寫道，這種措辭「為分歧保留了空間，以一種淡化的關切表達取代了金磚國家在這一問題上本應展現的統一立場」。
If the bloc struggled to address regional wars, it was far more unified in pushing back against American economic pressure. The statement took indirect aim at the United States by raising concerns about tariffs and condemning the use of economic sanctions.
如果說該集團在應對地區戰爭問題上顯得步調不一，那麼在反制美國經濟施壓方面，它們的立場則要團結得多。聲明雖未點名美國，卻對關稅問題表達了關切，並譴責了動用經濟制裁的做法。
廣告
“It’s very difficult to have such a big and expanded club of countries that are united by their animosity to Western hegemony but have very different interests, sometimes opposing each other, sometimes at war with each other,” said Alexander Gabuev, director of the Carnegie Russia Eurasia Center in Berlin.
「要讓這樣一個龐大且不斷擴大的國傢俱樂部保持團結非常困難——它們因對西方霸權的共同反感而走到一起，但它們彼此之間的利益卻截然不同，有時相互對立，有時甚至兵戎相見，」柏林的卡內基俄羅斯歐亞中心主任亞歷山大·加布耶夫說。
A Stage for Iran
伊朗的舞台
Both Mr. Putin and President Masoud Pezeshkian of Iran, whose countries are mired in wars and under fierce pressure from the West in the form of sanctions, moved quickly on Friday to press their agendas even before the summit started.
普丁和伊朗總統佩澤希齊揚——這兩個國家都深陷戰爭，並承受著西方制裁帶來的巨大壓力——在峰會尚未正式開始前，便於週五迅速推進各自的議程。
Mr. Pezeshkian criticized U.S. power and urged fellow members to build alternative financial tools to protect themselves from Western pressure. “Who said it should decide for the entire world, that others have no rights, and that whatever they want everyone else has to obey?” he said in a speech. “This is something we will not accept.”
佩澤希齊揚批評美國的強權，並敦促其他成員國建立替代性金融工具，以保護自身免受西方壓力。「誰規定它可以替整個世界做決定，規定其他國家沒有權利，規定他們想讓別人做什麼，別人就必須服從？」他在演講中說。「這是我們不會接受的。」
Officials have not said if Mr. Pezeshkian will have a formal sit-down with Mr. Xi, the Chinese leader. At a recent regional security summit in Kyrgyzstan, Mr. Xi and Mr. Pezeshkian met only briefly on the sidelines rather than in a separate bilateral meeting.
官方尚未透露佩澤希齊揚是否會與中國領導人習近平舉行正式會晤。不久前在吉爾吉斯斯坦舉行的地區安全峰會上，習近平與佩澤希齊揚僅在會議間隙短暫會面，並未安排單獨的雙邊會晤。
That fueled speculation that Beijing was unhappy over the war and the closure of the Strait of Hormuz. Mr. Xi could have also been distancing himself from Tehran to avoid provoking President Trump, whom he is scheduled to meet in Washington later this month.
這引發了外界對北京是否對這場戰爭以及霍爾木茲海峽關閉感到不滿的猜測。習近平也可能是在刻意與德黑蘭保持距離，以免激怒川普——按計劃，他本月晚些時候將在華盛頓與川普會晤。
Any substantive meeting between the leaders “could help clarify whether China still sees Iran as a strategic partner worth investing political capital in at a moment of acute pressure, or whether Beijing is increasingly keeping Tehran at arm’s length,” said Hamidreza Azizi, senior Iran analyst at the International Crisis Group.
國際危機組織的伊朗問題高級分析師哈米德雷扎·阿齊茲表示，兩位領導人之間若能舉行實質性會晤，「可能有助於釐清：在壓力巨大的時刻，中國是仍將伊朗視為值得投入政治資本的戰略夥伴，還是日益與德黑蘭保持距離」。
Perhaps more important to China was Mr. Xi’s meeting with Mr. Modi later in the day.
對中國來說，也許更重要的是習近平當天晚些時候與莫迪的會晤。
Mr. Xi’s visit is the first he has made to India in seven years, and is a sign of the continued stabilization of relations between Beijing and New Delhi. Ties hit a low in 2020 following a clash of Indian and Chinese forces in a disputed border region.
這是習近平七年來首次訪問印度，標誌著中印關係持續走向穩定。兩國關係曾在2020年因雙方軍隊在一個存在爭議的邊境地區發生衝突而跌入低谷。
廣告
During talks on Saturday, Mr. Modi and Mr. Xi discussed reaching a “mutually acceptable resolution” to the border issues, according to India’s foreign ministry.
據印度外交部透露，在週六的會談中，莫迪與習近平就邊境問題探討了達成"雙方均可接受的解決方案"的可能性。
Mr. Xi called for “peace and stability” in the border region and said China and India were “partners, not adversaries,” according to Chinese state media.
據中國官方媒體報導，習近平呼籲維護邊境地區「和平安寧」，並表示中國和印度是「合作夥伴而不是對手」。
For South Africa, It’s Complicated.
對南非來說，情況很複雜
Other leaders brought their own awkward entanglements with the group’s Western-skeptic tilt.
其他領導人也帶著各自與這個對西方持懷疑態度的集團之間複雜而尷尬的關係而來。
President Cyril Ramaphosa of South Africa shares Mr. Modi’s preference for using BRICS to build relationships across the globe. But the coalition has also created diplomatic headaches for Pretoria.
南非總統拉馬福薩與莫迪一樣，都傾向於利用金磚國家來與世界各地建立關係。但與此同時，這一聯盟也給南非帶來了不少外交上的頭疼問題。
In January, South Africa was criticized by the Trump administration for allowing Iran to participate in BRICS naval drills off the South African coast. Mr. Ramaphosa said that China was running the exercises and that South African waters were chosen because of the convenient location.
今年1月，川普政府批評南非允許伊朗參加在南非海岸附近舉行的金磚國家海軍演習。拉馬福薩說，演習由中國負責組織，而之所以選擇南非水域，是因為地理位置便利。
廣告
South African officials have been disappointed that the bloc has not been able to reach consensus opinions on divisive conflicts like the wars in Iran and Gaza, said David Africa, an international relations analyst based in Cape Town.
據開普敦國際關係分析師戴維·阿非利加表示，南非官員一直對金磚國家未能就伊朗戰爭、加薩戰爭等引發嚴重分歧的衝突達成共識感到失望。
“South Africa has knocked its head and realizes it won’t extract much diplomatically on the issues it’s concerned about,” Mr. Africa said. Instead, he said, South Africa is likely to focus on economic issues like trade, business and development at the summit.
「南非碰了壁，也意識到，在它所關切的這些問題上，靠外交手段很難撈到多少好處，」阿非利加說道。他表示,南非此次峰會很可能會轉而聚焦貿易、商業和發展等經濟議題。
Demoting the Dollar?
削弱美元地位？
One of the BRICS group’s most ambitious economic goals is to reduce global reliance on the dollar.
金磚國家最具雄心的經濟目標之一就是降低全球對美元的依賴。
“De-dollarization,” as the effort is known, would throw a lifeline to countries like Russia and Iran, which have struggled to pay for imported goods since the United States and other Western countries restricted their access to the dollar.
這項被稱為「去美元化」的努力將為俄羅斯和伊朗等國提供一條生命線——自美國和其他西方國家限制它們獲取美元以來，這些國家一直難以支付進口商品的貨款。
The dollar’s status as the world’s reserve currency is an “exorbitant privilege” for the United States, economists say — and it’s a privilege that Mr. Trump intends to keep. At the start of his second term, he wrote that any country trying to “create a new BRICS Currency” to circumvent the dollar could “say goodbye to selling into the beautiful U.S. economy.”
經濟學家表示，美元作為世界儲備貨幣的地位是美國享有的一種「過度特權」——而這正是川普打算繼續保住的特權。在他第二任期開始時，他曾寫道，任何試圖「創建新的金磚貨幣」來繞開美元的國家都「別想再向美麗的美國經濟賣東西了」。
廣告
An alternative reserve currency is not realistic at this point. But many countries that benefit from having the dollar as the world’s standard legal tender — India, for instance, which fears a financial future dominated by China — still want to see its dominance decrease. India has proposed that BRICS countries should link their banking systems more directly, allowing them to pay debts to one another without converting sums into dollars.
就目前而言，打造一種替代性儲備貨幣並不現實。但許多從美元作為世界標準法定貨幣中受益的國家——例如擔心未來金融格局被中國主導的印度——仍然希望看到美元主導地位的削弱。印度提議，金磚國家應當讓各國銀行體系更加直接地連接起來，使彼此間的債務清償無需再經過美元兌換。
It is already buying oil from Russia using rupees and rubles.
印度已經在用盧比和盧布購買俄羅斯石油。
