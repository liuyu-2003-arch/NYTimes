Anthropic CEO呼籲放緩AI發展速度

Top A.I. Leaders Call for Slowing Down A.I. Development

MIKE ISAAC • 2026-09-14
Dario Amodei, the chief executive of Anthropic, called for a global slowdown of artificial intelligence development in a 3,800-word essay on Saturday, just days after one of the company’s employees quit over concerns about the safety of the technology.
Anthropic首席執行官達里奧·阿莫迪週六發表了一篇3800詞的文章，呼籲全球放緩人工智慧發展。就在幾天前，該公司的一名員工因擔憂人工智慧技術的安全性辭職。
Top executives of other major A.I. companies quickly signed on to the effort. Sam Altman, the chief executive of OpenAI; Elon Musk, whose SpaceX rocket company has been ramping up spending on A.I.; and Demis Hassabis, the chair of Google DeepMind, wrote in social media posts that they agreed on the need for a slower pace.
其他主要人工智慧公司的高管迅速響應這一呼籲。OpenAI首席執行官薩姆·奧爾特曼、SpaceX（這家火箭公司一直在加大對人工智慧的投入）的伊隆·馬斯克，以及谷歌DeepMind董事長戴密斯·哈薩比斯在社群媒體上發帖，表示他們同意需要放慢步伐。
Mr. Amodei, who co-founded Anthropic to focus on securely and carefully building A.I., wrote in his essay that while he believed the technology could bring many benefits, it was advancing at too rapid a pace for researchers to continue safely.
阿莫迪是Anthropic的共同創始人，致力於安全、謹慎地構建人工智慧。他在文章中寫道，儘管他相信這項技術可以帶來許多好處，但其推進速度過快，研究人員無法繼續安全地發展。
“Over the last few months, I have become convinced that fully addressing the risks requires even more prudence — not just investing in risk prevention, but pacing the rate of capabilities advancement so that risk prevention has time to keep up,” Mr. Amodei wrote. “We must slow the pace at which we improve the capabilities of A.I. models. Progress will still seem fast, and we must make wise use of the time we gain.”
「過去幾個月裡，我逐漸確信，要完全應對風險需要更加審慎——不僅是投資於風險預防，還要調節能力迭代的速度，以便風險預防有時間跟上，」阿莫迪寫道。「我們必須放慢改進人工智慧模型能力的速度。進展仍將看起來很快，我們必須明智地利用爭取到的時間。」
廣告
Mr. Amodei has warned about the safety risks of A.I. for years. But his essay amounted to one of the most full-throated calls for urgent action from a leading A.I. company executive to date.
阿莫迪多年來一直警告人工智慧的安全風險。但在所有領先人工智慧公司的高管中，他的文章構成了迄今為止最全力呼籲採取緊急行動的聲音之一。
He called for independent auditors to check the companies for safety standards and urged global cooperation in creating rules around A.I. development.
他呼籲獨立審計師檢查公司的安全標準，並敦促全球合作制定圍繞人工智慧發展的規則。
The appeal for an industrywide pause follows months of escalating concerns across the technology industry as A.I. model development has made significant strides at companies like Anthropic, the developer of the Claude chatbot, as well as OpenAI, the creator of ChatGPT, and Google’s DeepMind.
他發出這一全行業暫停的呼籲之際，Anthropic（Claude聊天機器人的開發者）、OpenAI（ChatGPT的創造者）和谷歌DeepMind等公司在人工智慧模型開發方面取得了重大進展，數月來科技行業的擔憂也不斷升級。
Tensions rose further after the leading A.I. labs discovered security breaches carried out by their own programs without their knowledge. In one incident this year, OpenAI’s systems breached an A.I. start-up called HuggingFace, which was not detected until HuggingFace informed OpenAI weeks after it had happened. Since then, A.I. researchers have conferred to discuss what to do.
在領先的人工智慧實驗室發現其自身程序在它們不知情的情況下利用安全漏洞後，緊張局勢進一步加劇。在今年的一起事件中，OpenAI的系統入侵了一家名為HuggingFace的人工智慧初創公司，直到HuggingFace在事件發生數週後通知OpenAI才被發現。此後，人工智慧研究人員召開會議討論應對之策。
On Tuesday, Jacob Coxon, an Anthropic researcher, resigned and posted a series of social media posts about how the leading A.I. labs were not responsibly developing the technology and were gambling with people’s lives, setting off a furor. Other A.I. researchers have called on their leaders to pause A.I. development, while some are forming splinter groups to raise more awareness about the risks of A.I.
週二，Anthropic研究員雅各布·科克森辭職並發布了一系列社群媒體帖子，講述領先的人工智慧實驗室如何沒有負責任地開發這項技術，並在拿人們的生命賭博，引發軒然大波。其他人工智慧研究人員呼籲他們的領導人暫停人工智慧發展，而一些人正在組建分裂團體以提高對人工智慧風險的認識。
A.I. leaders are girding themselves for a day when an A.I. system can rely on itself to grow more advanced rather than requiring the aid of human researchers. This dynamic, which computer scientists call “recursive self improvement,” is what some top A.I. developers believe may lead to the software spinning out of their control.
人工智慧領導人正在為某一天做好準備，屆時人工智慧系統可以自主進化，不需要人類研究人員的幫助。計算機科學家將這種動態稱為「遞歸自我改進」，一些頂級人工智慧開發者認為這可能導致軟體失控。
廣告
“Left unchecked, it could outrun our ability to understand and control these systems, and so must be pursued very carefully, if at all,” Mr. Amodei said.
「如果任其發展，它可能超出我們理解和控制這些系統的能力，因此必須非常謹慎地推進，如果真要推進的話，」阿莫迪說。
Hours after Mr. Amodei published his essay, Mr. Altman posted a statement to social media agreeing that a slowdown of A.I. development was necessary.
在阿莫迪發表文章數小時後，奧爾特曼在社群媒體上發布聲明，同意放緩人工智慧發展是必要的。
“This has been a primary topic of discussions we’ve had at OpenAI in recent weeks,” Mr. Altman wrote. “Committing to having independent evaluators with employee-like access is a great idea, and we will do the same. We’ll have more to share soon.”
「這是OpenAI最近幾週討論的主要話題，」奧爾特曼寫道。「承諾讓獨立評估者擁有類似員工的訪問權限是一個好主意，我們也會這樣做。我們很快會有更多分享。」
Mr. Musk, in a post on his social media platform X, said simply, “Dario is right.”
馬斯克在其社群媒體平台X上簡明地發帖表示，「達里奧是對的。」
On Saturday evening, Mr. Hassabis said Mr. Amodei’s essay was a step in the right direction. “The details need working through, but the direction is correct for meeting this critical moment,” Mr. Hassabis posted on social media.
週六晚間，哈薩比斯表示阿莫迪的文章是朝著正確方向邁出的一步。「細節需要梳理，但方向對於迎接這一關鍵時刻是正確的，」哈薩比斯在社群媒體上發帖寫道。
For years, many of the top A.I. labs have been locked in bitter competition with one another. But as these systems have advanced, there has been some broader agreement among executives at rival companies that something needs be done.
多年來，許多頂級人工智慧實驗室一直陷於彼此之間的激烈競爭。但隨著這些系統的進步，競爭對手公司的高管之間已達成一些更廣泛的共識，即需要採取一些行動。
廣告
Last month, as OpenAI prepared to release an advanced A.I. model called Astra, the company said it had intentionally slowed down its pace of development, citing advancements in cybersecurity capabilities. In July, Google’s Mr. Hassabis published an open framework for what he believed would be a safe way to cooperatively develop next-generation A.I.
上個月，準備發布一款名為Astra的先進人工智慧模型的OpenAI表示已有意放慢發展步伐，理由是網路安全能力的進步。7月，谷歌的哈薩比斯發布了一個開放框架，闡述他認為合作開發下一代人工智慧的安全方式。
(The New York Times has sued OpenAI and Microsoft, claiming copyright infringement of news content related to A.I. systems. The two companies have denied those claims.)
（《紐約時報》已起訴OpenAI和微軟，主張其與人工智慧系統相關的新聞內容存在版權侵權。兩家公司已否認這些指控。）
There are few rules on A.I. development, though President Trump signed an executive order in June asking for A.I. labs to voluntarily submit their models for review before public release.
人工智慧發展幾乎沒有規則約束，儘管川普總統在6月簽署了一項行政命令，要求人工智慧實驗室在公開發布前自願提交其模型接受審查。
In his essay on Saturday, Mr. Amodei suggested actions that the industry might take to slow the pace of development.
在週六的文章中，阿莫迪提出了行業可能採取的行動，以放慢發展步伐。
Mr. Amodei said that all A.I. labs could agree to third-party technology assessments from “embedded evaluators,” or outside specialists who could verify best safety practices across companies. He also suggested that countries with democratic governance systems coordinate to create safety standards, which could take the form of regulatory action.
阿莫迪說，所有人工智慧實驗室都可以同意接受來自「嵌入式評估者」的第三方技術評估，即可以驗證各公司最佳安全實踐的外部專家。他還建議擁有民主治理體系的國家協調制定安全標準，這可以採取監管行動的形式。
He added that it would probably require a global effort working with other nations, including authoritarian ones, to properly coordinate a slowdown.
他補充說，這可能需要與包括威權國家在內的其他國家合作，進行全球努力，才能適當協調放緩。
Not everyone in Silicon Valley believes that Mr. Amodei’s intentions are purely altruistic. Some critics have said that Anthropic and OpenAI are looking to cement their position as A.I. market leaders by stoking fear and asking the federal government to create regulatory frameworks that would make it more difficult for smaller companies to compete with them.
並非矽谷所有人都認為阿莫迪的意圖是純粹利他的。一些批評者說，Anthropic和OpenAI正尋求通過煽動恐懼，並要求聯邦政府建立監管框架，以鞏固其人工智慧市場領導者的地位，這些框架將使較小的公司更難與它們競爭。
At a tech conference in San Francisco this past week, Jensen Huang, the chief executive of the A.I. chip maker Nvidia, said that A.I. labs were ratcheting up security concerns because they planned to introduce new cybersecurity products.
在上週於舊金山舉行的一次科技會議上，人工智慧晶片製造商英偉達的首席執行官黃仁勛說，人工智慧實驗室正在加劇安全擔憂，因為它們計劃推出新的網路安全產品。
“What better way to create demand than to create a problem?” Mr. Huang said at the event. “Who doesn’t want their market to be hysterical about their product and line up around the corner for it?”
「還有什麼比製造問題更好的創造需求的方式？」黃仁勛在活動中說。「誰不希望自己的產品讓自己的市場陷入狂熱，並排隊購買？」
On Saturday, shortly after Mr. Amodei published his essay, Clément DeLangue, the chief executive of HuggingFace, said in a social media post that it was imperative that A.I. safety issues not “be solved behind the closed doors of a handful of frontier labs.” Nvidia agreed to buy HuggingFace this month for $12.9 billion.
週六，在阿莫迪發表文章後不久，HuggingFace首席執行官克萊門特·德朗格在社群媒體帖子中說，人工智慧安全問題絕不能「由幾家前沿實驗室關起門來自行解決」。英偉達本月同意以129億美元收購HuggingFace。
In his essay, Mr. Amodei stressed that he still finds A.I. capable of bringing “incredible benefits” to humanity, including potentially curing diseases and accelerating economic growth. But even so, he said the risks of A.I. were too great to not proceed with extreme caution.
在他的文章中，阿莫迪強調，他仍然認為人工智慧能夠為人類帶來「不可思議的好處」，包括可能治癒疾病和加速經濟增長。但即便如此，他說人工智慧的風險太大，務必要極其謹慎地推進。
“The measures I propose to advance the frontier at a safe pace will not be easy,” Mr. Amodei wrote. “But I believe we owe it to humanity to try.”
「我提出的以安全步調推進前沿的措施執行起來不會容易，」阿莫迪寫道。「但我相信，為了人類，我們應該嘗試。」

Mike Isaac是《紐約時報》矽谷記者，常駐舊金山。他報導全球最具影響力的科技公司，以及它們如何塑造互聯網和現實世界的文化。
