印度試圖減少進口中國商品，卻為何越買越多？

India Wants to Buy Fewer Chinese Imports, but Keeps Needing More

ALEX TRAVELLI • 2026-09-14
To understand India’s trade imbalance with China, look inside Indian factories.
要理解印度與中國之間的貿易失衡，只需去印度的工廠看看就能找到答案。
Many of the big machines are Chinese, as are the electronic components and materials feeding them. An electric vehicle plant needs rare-earth magnets from China, while Indian drugmakers rely on Chinese pharmaceutical ingredients.
許多大型機器來自中國，為其提供支持的電子元件和原材料也同樣來自中國。一家電動汽車廠需要從中國進口稀土磁體，而印度的製藥企業則依賴中國的原料藥。
India has spent years trying to become less dependent on its giant neighbor. Instead, it has been buying more.
印度多年來一直試圖減少對這個龐大鄰國的依賴。但結果恰恰相反：它從中國購買的東西更多了。
The total volume of goods exchanged between the two countries has nearly doubled over the past five years, to $151 billion a year. But the trade has become more unbalanced than ever. India now buys roughly seven times as much from China as it sells there.
兩國之間的商品貿易總額在過去五年裡幾乎翻了一番，達到每年1510億美元。但貿易失衡也比以往任何時候都更加嚴重。印度現在從中國進口的商品金額大約是其對華出口額的七倍。
廣告
That surge came even as relations between India and China went into a deep freeze. A bloody hand-to-hand skirmish at the Himalayan border in 2020 killed at least 24 soldiers and brought a run of high-level diplomacy to an abrupt halt. On Saturday, Xi Jinping, China’s top leader, will set foot in India for the first time in more than six years.
這一激增發生在印中關係陷入嚴重僵局之際。2020年，兩國軍隊在喜馬拉雅邊境地區發生血腥的肉搏戰，至少造成24名士兵死亡，也使兩國一系列高層外交活動戛然而止。週六，中國最高領導人習近平時隔六年多首次踏上印度的土地。
For India, the imbalance creates a difficult problem. Its industrial ambitions depend heavily on Chinese goods, even as that dependence exposes India to strategic risks and makes it harder for its companies to gain the scale needed to compete with Chinese rivals. Now, with the U.S.-Israeli war with Iran driving up energy costs, Chinese imports are widening India’s global trade deficit and weakening its currency.
對印度而言，這種貿易失衡造成了一個棘手的問題。印度的工業發展雄心高度依賴中國商品，但這種依賴又使印度面臨戰略風險，並讓印度企業更難獲得與中國競爭對手抗衡所需的規模。如今，隨著美以對伊朗的戰爭推高能源成本，中國進口進一步擴大了印度的全球貿易逆差，並令印度貨幣承壓。
India is not alone in struggling with a flood of low-cost Chinese goods. China’s record trade surplus reached nearly $1.2 trillion last year, deepening concerns among trading partners over the growing dominance of Chinese companies across industries.
印度並不是唯一一個難以應對廉價中國商品大量湧入的國家。去年，中國創下近1.2萬億美元的貿易順差，加劇了貿易夥伴對中國企業在各個行業日益佔據主導地位的擔憂。
Those tensions spilled into the open this month at a gathering of economic officials from the Group of 20 nations. The U.S. Treasury secretary, Scott Bessent, accused China of blocking a joint statement criticizing countries “with excessive and persistent external surpluses.”
本月，這些矛盾在二十國集團各國經濟官員的一次會議上公開爆發。美國財政部長史考特·貝森特指責中國阻撓一份批評那些「長期存在過度對外順差」國家的聯合聲明。
As India hosts China at the BRICS summit this weekend, analysts expect trade to loom large as a source of tension, alongside the two countries’ disputed border.
隨著印度於週末在金磚國家峰會上接待中國，分析人士預計，貿易問題將成為一個重要矛盾來源，與兩國存在爭議的邊境問題並列。
Both sides seem to prefer quiet along the border for now. The harder problem may be their economic relationship.
目前，雙方似乎都希望邊境地區保持平靜。更棘手的問題或許是兩國的經濟關係。
For India, keeping Chinese goods out is not an option.
對印度來說，將中國商品拒之門外並不是一個選項。
廣告
“We will need to engage with them,” said Shekhar Aiyar, director of the Indian Council for Research on International Economic Relations. India should “aggressively invite Chinese firms in — especially in critical sectors like green technology, semiconductors, manufacturing, where China is the world leader,” Mr. Aiyar said. In batteries and solar power alone, he estimated, China has a 30 percent cost advantage over any other exporter.
「我們需要與他們接觸，」印度國際經濟關係研究委員會主任謝卡爾·艾亞爾說。印度應該「積極邀請中國企業進來——尤其是在綠色技術、半導體、製造業等中國作為世界領先者的關鍵領域」，艾亞爾說。他估計，僅就電池和太陽能領域，中國的成本優勢就比其他任何出口國高出30%。
After the 2020 border clash, India took an aggressive posture toward China. In retaliation, it banned TikTok and dozens of other consumer-facing Chinese apps and restricted Chinese companies’ ability to invest in Indian start-ups.
2020年邊境衝突後，印度對中國採取了強硬姿態。作為報復，它封禁了TikTok和數十款面向消費者的中國應用，並限制中國企業對印度初創公司的投資。
At the time, multinational companies were looking to move some supply chains out of China, and India appeared well positioned to benefit. For a brief moment, India looked capable of challenging China’s status as the “factory to the world.”
當時，跨國公司正尋求將部分供應鏈遷出中國，而印度看起來正處於一個有利位置，有望從中受益。短暫的一段時間裡，印度似乎有能力挑戰中國作為「世界工廠」的地位。
That did not happen. Vietnam and other Southeast Asian countries captured much of the investment, while China remained at the center of most supply chains.
但這並沒有發生。越南和其他東南亞國家吸引了大部分投資，而中國仍處於大多數供應鏈的中心位置。
Santosh Pai, a lawyer in New Delhi who advises Chinese and Indian companies, said Vietnam had secured large-scale investments as a manufacturing alternative to China — even though it, like India, imports heavily from China for essential equipment and materials. Vietnam’s supply chain, he said, is more integrated with China and the country courts Chinese investment more aggressively.
新德里律師桑托什·帕伊為中國和印度企業提供諮詢。他說，越南作為中國的製造業替代地獲得了大規模投資——儘管越南和印度一樣，嚴重依賴從中國進口關鍵設備和原材料。他說，越南的供應鏈與中國的融合程度更高，而且它也更積極地招攬中國投資。
Mr. Pai said India’s efforts to reduce its economic dependence on China had produced mixed results. Even imports ostensibly coming from elsewhere are often Chinese goods processed or repackaged in Southeast Asia.
帕伊說，印度減少對華經濟依賴的努力產生了喜憂參半的結果。即便是名義上來自其他國家的進口商品，很多實際上也是中國商品，只是在東南亞經過加工或重新包裝。
廣告
“The trade measures are well intentioned, but they don’t all work the way we want,” he said. “There is a lot of excess supply in China and a lot of excess demand in India.”
「這些貿易措施的出發點是好的，但它們並不都能按照我們希望的方式發揮作用，」他說。「中國有大量過剩產能，而印度有大量過剩需求。」
That leaves room for dealmaking. India wants to have access to Chinese money and technology. China wants India’s potentially enormous market.
這就給雙方留下了談判和交易的空間。印度希望獲得中國的資金和技術，中國則希望進入印度這個潛力巨大的市場。
But neither is willing to end up on the losing side of that exchange. Chinese companies do not want to miss the chance to build their brands in fast-growing India, even if its average consumer still has relatively little to spend. India, meanwhile, wants to protect its companies from far more efficient competitors.
但雙方都不願意在這筆交易中成為輸家。中國公司不想錯過在快速增長的印度建立品牌的機會，即使印度普通消費者的消費能力仍然相對有限。與此同時，印度希望保護本國企業免受效率高得多的競爭對手的衝擊。
The tension is sharpest in high-value, high-tech manufacturing. India wants Chinese investments, but investment that helps build an Indian industrial base. Apple’s iPhone manufacturing, about a quarter of which has shifted to India, is a prime example.
這種矛盾在高價值、高科技製造業領域最為突出。印度希望獲得中國投資，但想要的是有助於建立印度工業基礎的投資。蘋果公司的iPhone生產就是一個典型例子，目前約有四分之一的iPhone產能已經轉移到印度。
China has an obvious interest in keeping its position as the world’s indispensable manufacturer, even if individual Chinese companies want to take advantage of India’s young work force and government subsidies. Some of its restrictions on trade with India appear intended to preserve that advantage.
中國顯然希望保持自己作為世界不可或缺的製造中心的地位，即便個別中國企業希望利用印度年輕的勞動力和政府補貼。中國對印貿易的一些限制措施似乎意在維護這一優勢。
The struggle extends even to the movement of people.
這種角力甚至延伸到人員流動。
廣告
For much of the five years after the 2020 clash, travel was constrained by tit-for-tat restrictions, beginning with India’s blocking Chinese business and tourist visas and China’s responding in kind, with Covid-19 restrictions further complicating travel.
2020年衝突後的五年大部分時間裡，雙方採取了針鋒相對的限制措施，導致人員往來受限。印度先是禁止發放中國商務和旅遊簽證，中國隨後採取對等措施，而新冠疫情的限制又進一步給人員往來增加了複雜性。
India began issuing visas more freely last year. But China has tightened its exit rules, preventing some Chinese citizens from traveling to places like India if it deems them a threat to national industrial or technological security.
印度去年開始放鬆簽證的發放。但中國收緊了出境規定，如果認為某些中國公民前往印度等地可能對國家工業或技術安全構成威脅，就會阻止他們出境。
Visas are among the practical disputes that Mr. Xi and India’s prime minister, Narendra Modi, could address at the summit, Mr. Pai said. “India will want some reassurances, both for business visas to China and engineers from China,” he said.
帕伊說，簽證問題是習近平和印度總理莫迪在峰會上可能討論的實際分歧之一。「印度希望得到一些保證，包括有關赴華商務簽證以及中國工程師來印的問題，」他說。
India has leverage, too. By working with companies like Taiwan’s Foxconn, one of Apple’s main contract manufacturers, it can gradually erode pieces of China’s industrial dominance. It can also restrict access to its domestic market of 1.4 billion people, a prize that will become more valuable as the average incomes in India rise.
印度也有籌碼。通過與台灣富士康等企業合作——富士康是蘋果的主要代工廠商之一——印度可以逐步削弱中國在工業領域的部分主導地位。印度還可以限制中國企業進入其14億人口的國內市場；隨著印度人均收入不斷提高，這一市場將變得越來越有價值。
But China has shown a willingness to use its dominance in strategic sectors as leverage. Rare-earth magnets, needed for electric cars and many other high-growth industries, are just one example. China has also gained enormous power over the production of many pharmaceutical ingredients, making India’s world-leading drug companies dependent on Chinese supplies.
但中國已經表明，它願意利用自己在戰略性行業中的主導地位作為籌碼。電動汽車以及許多其他高速增長行業所需的稀土磁體就是一個例子。中國還在許多原料藥的生產上擁有巨大的影響力，使印度這個世界領先的製藥業大國依賴中國供應。
Somnath Mukherjee, chief investment officer at ASK Wealth Advisors in Mumbai, worries about India’s global trade deficit.
孟買ASK財富顧問公司首席投資官索姆納特·慕克吉對印度的全球貿易逆差感到擔憂。
廣告
It is “simple arithmetic,” he said, that the Indian rupee will remain under pressure as long as India buys more than it sells. The central bank can manage that, he said, even though higher oil prices and a flight to artificial intelligence stocks in the United States have made the task trickier.
他說，只要印度買得多、賣得少，印度盧比就會持續承壓，這是「簡單的算術」。他說，印度央行可以對此進行管理，但油價上漲以及資金湧向美國人工智慧股票使這項工作變得更加棘手。
“It is a fact that many countries, China and now the U.S. as well, have started weaponizing trade,” Mr. Mukherjee said. “China’s weaponized product access has become a source of massive concern.”
「一個事實是，許多國家——中國，現在美國也是如此——已經開始把貿易武器化，」慕克吉說。「中國將商品供應變成武器已經成為一個令人極其擔憂的問題。」

Keith Bradsher and Hari Kumar contributed reporting.

Alex Travelli是時報駐新德里記者，報導印度和南亞其他地區的商業和經濟新聞。
