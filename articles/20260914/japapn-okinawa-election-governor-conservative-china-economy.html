面臨中國威脅和經濟困境，沖繩選出保守派知事

Okinawa Elects a Conservative Amid Fears About China and the Economy

赫海威, HISAKO UENO • 2026-09-14
A 42-year-old conservative politician prevailed on Sunday in the race to be governor of Okinawa, a win for Japan’s hawkish prime minister, Sanae Takaichi, as she seeks to accelerate a military buildup to counter China.
週日，一名42歲的保守派政治人物在沖繩知事選舉中勝出。這對日本鷹派首相高市早苗來說是一場勝利，她正尋求加快軍事建設以對抗中國。
Genta Koja, a deputy mayor of Naha, the Okinawan capital, unseated a two-term governor, Denny Tamaki, who had criticized Ms. Takaichi’s defense policies and opposed the construction of new American armed forces facilities on Okinawa island. When he takes office later this month, Mr. Koja will be the youngest governor in the history of the Okinawa prefecture, a group of subtropical islands in southern Japan.
那霸副市長古謝玄太擊敗了已連任兩屆的現知事玉城丹尼。玉城曾批評高市早苗的防務政策，並反對在沖繩島建設新的美軍設施。古謝將於本月晚些時候就職，屆時他將成為沖繩縣歷史上最年輕的知事。沖繩縣位於日本南部，由多個亞熱帶島嶼組成。
Mr. Koja won about 59 percent of roughly 718,000 votes, according to results released early on Monday, compared with about 38 percent for Mr. Tamaki, the son of a Japanese mother and a U.S. Marine, who had the backing of left-leaning parties in Japan. He had made reviving the economy a centerpiece of his campaign.
根據週一早些時候公布的計票結果，古謝在約71.8萬張選票中贏得了約59%的選票，對手玉城的得票率約為38%。玉城的母親是日本人，父親是一名美國海軍陸戰隊員，得到日本左傾政黨的支持。他將振興經濟作為競選的核心綱領。
“I interpret the results as a choice by many to move Okinawa forward,” Mr. Koja said in a statement on Sunday.
「我認為選舉結果說明，許多人選擇推動沖繩向前發展，」古謝週日在一份聲明中表示。
Ms. Takaichi said on Monday that Mr. Koja’s win “reflected the will of the people of Okinawa, who seek economic growth.” She did not mention security issues.
高市早苗週一表示，古謝的勝選「反映了沖繩人民尋求經濟增長的意願」。她沒有提及安全問題。
“I intend to do my utmost to strengthen Okinawa’s economy while working in close cooperation with the new governor,” she told reporters gathered at the prime minister’s office in Tokyo.
「我將與新任沖繩知事密切合作，盡我所能加強沖繩的經濟，」她在東京首相官邸對聚集在那裡的記者們說。
Mr. Tamaki conceded the election in a statement, saying: “I want to heed the will of the people. There was still much I wanted to accomplish in a third term, but I intend to use this result as a source of strength.”
玉城在一份聲明中承認敗選，他說：「我想尊重民眾的意願。我還有很多想在第三個任期中實現的事，但我打算將這一結果作為力量的來源。」
廣告
Mr. Koja’s victory is a blow to Okinawa’s pacifist movement, which for years has opposed the American military presence on the island. Okinawa, with a population of 1.5 million, hosts 70 percent of Japan’s American bases despite accounting for only 0.6 percent of the country’s landmass. (The United States seized Okinawa from Japan after World War II; it returned the island in 1972 but has continued to operate bases there.)
古謝的勝利將打擊沖繩的和平運動，該運動多年來一直反對美國在島上的軍事存在。沖繩人口150萬，面積僅占日本國土面積的0.6%，卻容納了日本70%的美國基地。（美國在二戰後從日本手中奪取沖繩，1972年歸還該島，但繼續在那裡設有基地。）
Many members of older generations, who still have memories of the war, have rejected the central government’s plans to make Okinawa a bulwark in its efforts to counter Chinese military aggression. They say the presence of more troops makes the island even more of a target.
許多仍記得那場戰爭的年長一代拒絕中央政府將沖繩打造成對抗中國軍事侵略的堡壘的計劃。他們說，更多軍隊的存在使該島愈發成為目標。
Ms. Takaichi, who took office last year, has pushed forward with plans to further militarize Okinawa by sending more troops to the area and expanding missile and electronic warfare capabilities.
去年上任的高市已推進沖繩進一步軍事化的計劃，包括向該地區派遣更多部隊，並擴大導彈和電子戰能力。
Okinawa, the southernmost prefecture of Japan, is strategically significant because it sits close to China and Taiwan. It has long had a large American military footprint, with roughly 30,000 active-duty personnel, which has been a source of tension on the island for decades, amid concerns about pollution, noise and crime.
沖繩是日本最南端的縣，因靠近中國和台灣而具有戰略意義。長期以來，美軍在沖繩駐紮了大量兵力，約有3萬名現役軍人。由於當地居民對污染、噪音及犯罪問題的擔憂，美軍的存在幾十年來一直是引發該島局勢緊張的根源之一。
Mr. Koja, who was backed by Ms. Takaichi’s Liberal Democratic Party and other conservative parties, had expressed a willingness during the campaign to work with the Americans. He said he would support a plan to transfer a busy air base used by the Marines from southern Okinawa to a less populated coastal area in the north. Mr. Tamaki had fiercely opposed the plan for years and made it a signature campaign issue.
古謝得到高市的自民黨和其他保守政黨支持，在競選期間表示願意與美國人合作。他表示將支持一項計劃，把海軍陸戰隊使用的一個繁忙的空軍基地從沖繩南部轉移到北部人口較少的沿海地區。玉城多年來一直強烈反對該計劃，並將其作為競選的標誌性議題。
Mr. Koja also spoke forcefully about rising security threats in the region, including from China, and the need to modernize Japan’s military, known as the Self-Defense Forces.
古謝還用堅決的語氣談論該地區不斷上升的安全威脅，包括來自中國的威脅以及日本軍自衛隊現代化的必要性。
廣告
“It is an undeniable fact that the security environment surrounding Okinawa has become increasingly severe, making the Japan-U.S. security alliance and the deterrent capabilities of the Self-Defense Forces essential,” he said at a debate last month.
他在上個月的一場辯論中說：「沖繩周圍的安全環境日益嚴峻，這是不可否認的事實，使得日美安全同盟和自衛隊的威懾能力必不可少。」
There has been rising concern in Okinawa about China’s growing assertiveness in recent years, especially among younger islanders, who were core to Mr. Koja’s victory, according to exit poll data reported by NHK.
近年來，中國日益強硬的姿態給沖繩帶來越來越多的擔憂，尤其是年輕島民。根據NHK報導的出口民調數據，他們是古謝獲勝的核心。
Yet despite geopolitical questions, voters appeared most concerned about the economy. Wages in Okinawa are below the national average; prices have risen; and unemployment and child poverty are concerns in many areas.
儘管存在地緣政治問題，但選民似乎最關心經濟。沖繩工資低於全國平均水平，物價上漲，許多地區存在令人擔憂的失業和兒童貧困問題。
Mr. Koja promised to put in place policies that would help lift wages, and he said he would provide more support to parents for child care. He also vowed to do more to promote tourism.
古謝承諾實施有助於提高工資的政策，並表示將為父母提供更多兒童保育支持。他還誓言為促進旅遊業做更多事情。
Masaaki Gabe, a professor emeritus at the University of the Ryukyus in Okinawa, said Mr. Koja would face pressure to improve the economy.
沖繩琉球大學榮休教授我部政明說，古謝將面臨改善經濟的壓力。
“The people of Okinawa want prosperity,” he said.
「沖繩人民想要繁榮，」他說。
廣告
Yet for Okinawan citizens, including the crowds of pacifist activists who still hold regular protests outside American bases, it is becoming increasingly difficult to oppose the defense buildup, Mr. Gabe said, as the central government has grown more assertive in its push to militarize the region.
我部表示，對於沖繩公民，包括仍定期在美國基地外抗議的大批和平活動人士來說，反對軍備擴張變得越來越困難，因為中央政府在推動該地區軍事化方面顯得更有底氣了。
“The situation has reached a point,” he said, “where the prefecture has almost no authority to resist.”
「情況已到了縣方几乎無權抵抗的地步，」他說。

赫海威(Javier C. Hernández)是《紐約時報》東京分社社長，領導時報對日本及周邊地區的報導。在過去十年的大部分時間裡，他一直在亞洲進行報導，此前曾任駐華記者。

Hisako Ueno是常駐東京的記者/研究員，撰寫日本政治、商業、勞工、性別及文化相關議題。
