美國在阿富汗的「永久戰爭」：25年影像全記錄

The ‘Forever War’ in Afghanistan: 25 Years in Pictures

《紐約時報》 • 2026-09-11
America’s “forever war” in Afghanistan began only weeks after the Sept. 11 attacks and lasted just short of 20 years, ending in the chaos of Aug. 15, 2021, when the capital, Kabul, fell once again to the Taliban.
美國在阿富汗的「永久戰爭」始於9·11襲擊僅數週後，持續近20年，最終在2021年8月15日的混亂中結束——首都喀布爾再次落入塔利班之手。
Journalists for The New York Times covered the war from beginning to end and have continued capturing the lives of Afghanistan’s people as they navigate a scarred landscape and an uncertain future.
《紐約時報》的記者全程報導了這場戰爭，並繼續記錄阿富汗人民的生活——他們穿行於傷痕累累的土地，面對不確定的未來。
2001-2002
2001-2002
The War Begins
戰爭開始
廣告
Operation Enduring Freedom began on Oct. 7, 2001, with an American bombing campaign against Al Qaeda and the Taliban. American Special Operations forces teams linked up with Afghan militias opposed to the Taliban, mainly the Northern Alliance, to drive the Taliban from power. Kabul fell in mid-November, along with the Taliban stronghold of Kandahar.
「持久自由行動」於2001年10月7日開始，美國對基地組織和塔利班發動轟炸。美國特種作戰部隊與反對塔利班的阿富汗民兵——主要是北方聯盟——聯手，意圖將塔利班趕下台。喀布爾於11月中旬陷落，塔利班據點坎達哈也一併陷落。
In December, Osama bin Laden, Al Qaeda’s leader, escaped to Pakistan through the mountains around Tora Bora. That same month, an interim Afghan government led by Hamid Karzai was installed.
12月，基地組織領導人奧薩馬·賓·拉登穿過托拉博拉周邊山區逃往巴基斯坦。同月，由哈米德·卡爾扎伊領導的阿富汗臨時政府成立。
Northern Alliance troops firing on Taliban positions in rugged territory outside the northeastern city of Taloqan in October 2001.
2001年10月，北方聯盟部隊在東北部城市塔洛坎外圍的崎嶇地帶向塔利班陣地開火。
Fighters for the Northern Alliance heading to the front lines near the besieged Taliban stronghold of Kunduz in November 2001.
2001年11月，北方聯盟的戰士前往被圍困的塔利班據點昆都士附近的前線。
On their way into Kabul in November 2001, Northern Alliance members found a Taliban fighter in a ditch and killed him, despite his pleas.
2001年11月，北方聯盟成員在進軍喀布爾途中發現一名塔利班武裝分子藏身溝渠，儘管對方苦苦哀求，北方聯盟成員仍將其殺害。
An American B-52 bomber circling above Tora Bora in eastern Afghanistan in December 2001.
2001年12月，一架美國B-52轟炸機在阿富汗東部托拉博拉上空盤旋。
Fighters for the Eastern Alliance, another anti-Taliban militia, running for cover from Al Qaeda sniper fire in the Tora Bora mountains in December 2001.
2001年12月，另一支反塔利班民兵組織東部聯盟的戰士在托拉博拉山區躲避基地組織狙擊手的火力。
2003-2007
2003-2007
Drift to Iraq
轉向伊拉克
Defense Secretary Donald Rumsfeld announced an end to major combat operations in Afghanistan in May 2003. Even with a major reconstruction effort underway there, and about 8,000 American troops in place, President George W. Bush’s administration began shifting combat resources to the war in Iraq.
國防部長唐納德·拉姆斯菲爾德於2003年5月宣布阿富汗主要作戰行動結束。儘管那裡正在進行大規模重建，並且約有8000名美軍駐紮，喬治·W·布希政府已開始將作戰資源轉向伊拉克戰爭。
廣告

[閱讀《紐約時報》關於過去阿富汗戰爭的歷史照片文章《帝國終結者》.]
The Taliban-led insurgency grew stronger in 2006, carrying out more ambushes and suicide bombings. Despite training and equipment supplied by the United States and allied forces, Afghan security forces could not contain the Taliban resurgence, which was aided by militants across the border in Pakistan.
塔利班領導的叛亂在2006年變得更強大，實施更多伏擊和自殺式爆炸襲擊。儘管美國和盟軍提供了訓練和裝備，阿富汗安全部隊仍無法遏制塔利班的捲土重來，後者得到巴基斯坦邊境對面武裝分子的幫助。
By 2007, the number of American troops in Afghanistan had climbed to 25,000.
到2007年，駐阿富汗美軍兵力已升至2.5萬。
American soldiers from the 10th Mountain Division yelling to others to get out of the line of fire after being ambushed by Taliban fighters in southern Afghanistan in June 2006.
2006年6月，在阿富汗南部遭到塔利班戰士伏擊後，美國第10山地師的士兵大聲呼喊其他人離開火力線。
Women in Kabul waiting to vote in October 2004. Afghanistan’s landmark presidential election quickly fell into dispute.
2004年10月，喀布爾的婦女排隊等待投票。阿富汗具有里程碑意義的總統選舉很快陷入爭議。
Mohammad Akbar holding the hand of his nephew, Mohammad, age 3, at a hospital in Kandahar in May 2006. The boy was injured in an attack by U.S. forces and suffered burn injuries.
2006年5月，穆罕默德·阿克巴在坎達哈一家醫院握著三歲侄子穆罕默德的手。這名男孩在美國部隊的一次襲擊中受傷，遭受燒傷。
British soldiers of the Royal Anglians B Company, part of NATO forces, firing toward Taliban positions after coming under attack during a patrol in Kajaki in July 2007.
2007年7月，作為北約部隊一部分的英國皇家安格利亞團B連士兵在卡賈基巡邏時遭到襲擊，向塔利班陣地開火。
Wounded soldiers in the Korengal Valley in eastern Afghanistan in October 2007.
2007年10月，阿富汗東部科倫加爾山谷中受傷的士兵。
Afghan National Army soldiers detained a man whom they believed to be a Taliban fighter during the search of a village in southern Afghanistan in May 2006.
2006年5月，阿富汗國民軍士兵在阿富汗南部搜查一個村莊時，拘留了一名他們認為是塔利班戰士的男子。
2008-2010
2008-2010
廣告
Recommitment and Surge
重新承諾與增兵
In February 2009, the new American president, Barack Obama, declared a recommitment to the war and deployed 17,000 more troops to Afghanistan, adding to the 36,000 already there.
2009年2月，新任美國總統貝拉克·歐巴馬宣布重新投入於這場戰爭，並在已有的3.6萬兵力基礎上，向阿富汗再增兵1.7萬。
In December, Mr. Obama announced a “surge” meant to build and train an Afghan security force that would be strong enough to assume responsibility for fighting the Taliban insurgency. His plan included sending 30,000 more American troops, bringing the total number to nearly 100,000 by mid-2010.
12月，歐巴馬宣布「增兵」，旨在建立和訓練一支阿富汗安全部隊，使其強大到足以承擔打擊塔利班叛亂的責任。他的計劃包括再派遣3萬名美軍人員，到2010年中期使總兵力達到近10萬。
An Army patrol worked its way through the Korengal Valley in April 2009 to meet with a group of village elders. An I.E.D. exploded. Then the Taliban attacked. Soldiers scrambled. One died.
2009年4月，一支陸軍巡邏隊穿過科倫加爾山谷，準備與一群村中長老會面。一枚簡易爆炸裝置爆炸。隨後塔利班發動襲擊。士兵們倉皇應對。一人死亡。
A German soldier burning a flare at a campsite in the desert of Kunduz Province, in October 2009.
2009年10月，一名德國士兵在昆都士省沙漠中的一個營地點燃信號彈。
A suicide bomber struck near a hotel in Kabul in December 2009, killing at least eight people and wounding dozens.
2009年12月，一名自殺式炸彈襲擊者在喀布爾一家酒店附近發動襲擊，造成至少八人死亡、數十人受傷。
A bullet-pierced window at a Kabul guesthouse attacked by the Taliban in November 2009. Five United Nations workers were killed.
2009年11月，喀布爾一家賓館遭到塔利班襲擊，窗戶被子彈擊穿。五名聯合國工作人員遇害。
Marines came under attack in Marja, in Helmand Province, in February 2010 while on their way to check a building that had been bombed earlier in the day. Most of the Marines were experiencing combat for the first time.
2010年2月，海軍陸戰隊員在赫爾曼德省馬爾賈遭到襲擊，當時他們正前往檢查一座當天早些時候被轟炸的建築。大多數海軍陸戰隊員是首次經歷實戰。
Village elders meeting in Marja in March 2010.
2010年3月，村中長老在馬爾賈開會。
Afghan soldiers rushing a wounded police officer to an American helicopter in Kunar Province in March 2010.
2010年3月，庫納爾省，阿富汗士兵將一名受傷的警察緊急送往一架美國直升機。
American soldiers on a transport plane about to land in Mazar-i-Sharif in April 2010.
2010年4月，一架即將降落在馬扎裡沙裡夫的運輸機上的美國士兵。
An Afghan man who was detained on suspicion of being involved in an attack by militants on the first day of a national peace council meeting in Kabul in June 2010.
2010年6月，一名涉嫌參與武裝分子在喀布爾全國和平委員會會議首日的襲擊而被拘留的阿富汗男子。
Members of the First Battalion, 87th Infantry, tending to a wounded comrade in Kunduz in September 2010.
2010年9月，第87步兵團第一營的成員在昆都士照料一名受傷的戰友。
2011-2013
2011-2013
廣告
A Troop Drawdown
部隊撤減
In May 2011, a U.S. Navy SEAL team killed Osama bin Laden in a compound in Abbottabad, Pakistan, where he had been living for years. In June, Mr. Obama announced that he would pull 33,000 troops from Afghanistan by mid-2012.
2011年5月，一支美國海軍海豹突擊隊擊斃奧薩馬·賓·拉登，地點在巴基斯坦阿伯塔巴德的一處院落，他已在那裡居住多年。6月，歐巴馬宣布，他將在2012年中期前從阿富汗撤出3.3萬名軍人。
In 2012, Afghanistan’s president, Hamid Karzai, began blaming United States and coalition troops for rising civilian casualties. His relationship with American leaders deteriorated.
2012年，阿富汗總統哈米德·卡爾扎伊開始將不斷上升的平民傷亡歸咎於美國和聯軍部隊。他與美國領導人的關係惡化。
Afghans took over most security responsibilities in 2013, with the U.S.-led coalition’s forces shifting to training and counterterrorism operations.
2013年，阿富汗人接管了大部分安全職責，美國領導的聯軍部隊轉向訓練和反恐行動。
Soldiers boarding a transport helicopter in Kunduz in March 2011.
2011年3月，士兵們在昆都士登上一架運輸直升機。
The mother of Khan Mohammad, a 3-month-old baby who died from exposure to the cold, being comforted before her child was washed and buried in the Nasaji Bagrami Camp for displaced persons in Kabul in February 2012.
2012年2月，在喀布爾的納薩吉·巴格拉米流離失所者營地，三個月大的嬰兒汗·穆罕默德因受寒而亡。他的母親在為孩子清洗遺體並下葬前接受安慰。
Afghan soldiers, left, and American soldiers blew up a Taliban firing position in the village of Layadira, in Kandahar Province, in February 2013.
2013年2月，阿富汗士兵（左）和美國士兵在坎達哈省拉亞迪拉村炸毀一個塔利班射擊陣地。
Members of the 101st Airborne Division in Paktia Province in April 2013.
2013年4月，第101空降師的成員在帕克蒂亞省。
Samiullah, 8 months old and malnourished, being held by his mother, Islam Bibi, 15, at a hospital in Lashkar Gah, in Helmand Province, in September 2013.
2013年9月，在赫爾曼德省拉什卡爾加一家醫院，15歲的母親伊斯蘭·比比懷抱著八個月大、營養不良的薩米烏拉。
Sayed Wazir, 40, a former mujahedeen, firing a rocket toward Taliban positions in Wardak Province in November 2013.
2013年11月，40歲的前聖戰者賽義德·瓦齊爾在瓦爾達克省向塔利班陣地發射火箭彈。
The view from a Humvee of Highway 1, which links Kabul with Afghanistan’s other major cities, in November 2013.
2013年11月，從悍馬車上眺望連接喀布爾與阿富汗其他主要城市的一號高速公路。
2014-2018
2014-2018
廣告
A Taliban Resurgence
塔利班捲土重來
On Dec. 31, 2014, the combat mission in Afghanistan formally ended, but the American military presence in the country did not. Mr. Obama announced a timetable for the withdrawal of most troops by the end of 2016.
2014年12月31日，阿富汗的戰鬥任務正式結束，但美國在該國的軍事存在並未結束。歐巴馬宣布了在2016年底前撤出大部分軍隊的時間表。
After a 2014 election marred by fraud, Ashraf Ghani became president, but signed a power-sharing agreement with his top opponent, Abdullah Abdullah.
在2014年一場因舞弊而備受詬病的選舉後，阿什拉夫·加尼成為總統，但與他的主要對手阿卜杜拉·阿卜杜拉簽署了權力分享協議。
On the battlefield, the Afghan security forces increasingly struggled against the Taliban, taking heavy casualties and losing territory.
在戰場上，阿富汗安全部隊日益難以對抗塔利班，承受重大傷亡並丟失領土。
In August 2017, President Trump, early in his first term in office, said he would continue to prosecute the war.
2017年8月，川普總統在其第一個任期初期表示，他將繼續推進這場戰爭。
An Afghan police officer, Mohammad Qassim, 28, recovering at a hospital run by Emergency, an Italian nonprofit group, after losing his right leg to a Taliban explosive in Marja, Helmand Province.
在赫爾曼德省馬爾賈，28歲的阿富汗警察穆罕默德·卡西姆在義大利非營利組織「急診」運營的醫院中康復，他因塔利班的爆炸物失去了右腿。
The aftermath of a U.S. airstrike that hit a Doctors Without Borders hospital in Kunduz in October 2015. Forty-two people were killed in the attack.
2015年10月，美國空襲擊中昆都士一家無國界醫生醫院後的景象。42人在襲擊中喪生。
The bodies of 20 commandos lined up in November 2018 outside the governor’s office in Sang-e-Masha, in Ghazni Province. Their company was almost destroyed fighting the Taliban.
2018年11月，加茲尼省桑格-馬沙省長辦公室外，20名突擊隊員的遺體排成一列。他們的連隊在對抗塔利班時幾乎被摧毀。
A view of the outskirts of Lashkar Gah in Helmand Province in March 2016.
2016年3月，赫爾曼德省拉什卡爾加郊區的景象。
The Kart-e-Sakhi cemetery in Kabul in April 2016.
2016年4月，喀布爾的卡爾特薩希公墓。
2018-2020
2018-2020
A Deal With the Taliban
與塔利班達成協議
In late 2018, American and Taliban negotiators began holding peace talks in Doha, Qatar. The Afghan government was excluded from the talks; the Taliban refused to meet with its officials.
2018年底，美國和塔利班談判代表開始在卡達杜哈舉行和平談判。阿富汗政府被排除在談判之外；塔利班拒絕與其官員會面。
On Feb. 29, 2020, the United States signed a peace deal with the Taliban, opening the door to a gradual, final troop withdrawal from Afghanistan and the beginning of direct talks between the Afghan government and the Taliban to determine the country’s future.
2020年2月29日，美國與塔利班簽署和平協議，為從阿富汗逐步撤軍打開大門，並開啟阿富汗政府與塔利班之間的直接談判，以決定該國的未來。
About 12,000 American troops were still in the country.
約1.2萬名美軍仍留在該國。
A funeral for one of 63 people killed at a wedding in Kabul by an Islamic State suicide bomber in August 2019.
2019年8月，一名伊斯蘭國自殺式炸彈襲擊者在喀布爾一場婚禮上製造爆炸，造成63人遇難，圖為其中一名遇難者的葬禮。
A crater left by a car-bomb attack in Kabul in September 2019. The Taliban claimed responsibility.
2019年9月，喀布爾汽車炸彈襲擊留下的彈坑。塔利班聲稱對此負責。
Afghan police officers inspecting a replacement trench after their base was overrun by the Taliban in February 2020.
2020年2月，阿富汗警察在其基地被塔利班攻佔後檢查一條新建的戰壕。
Two children passing members of a Taliban Red Unit, an elite force, in the Alingar district of Laghman in March 2020.
2020年3月，兩名兒童在拉格曼省阿林加區經過塔利班精銳部隊「紅色部隊」成員身旁。
2021-2026
2021-2026
The Taliban’s Return
塔利班回歸
On April 14, 2021, the recently sworn-in U.S. president, Joseph R. Biden Jr., called for a complete withdrawal of all American forces from Afghanistan by Sept. 11, 2021.
2021年4月14日，剛剛宣誓就職的美國總統小約瑟夫·R·拜登呼籲在2021年9月11日前從阿富汗完全撤出所有美軍。
The United States had spent about $2 trillion on the war effort. About 2,400 American troops and nearly 700 troops from other nations in the coalition died. More than 40,000 civilians were killed, as well as an estimated 60,000 Afghan security forces.
美國在這場戰爭中花費了約2萬億美元。約2400名美軍和近700名來自聯軍其他國家的軍人死亡。超過4萬名平民被殺，另有估計達6萬名阿富汗安全部隊人員死亡。
After the rushed withdrawal of U.S. forces, the Afghan government did not mount a significant resistance, and Taliban forces quickly regained control. Many Afghans feared a return to civil war between the Taliban and the remaining anti-Taliban fighters, but the violence affecting civilians dropped sharply with the end of the U.S. war.
在美軍匆忙撤離後，阿富汗政府沒有組織有效的抵抗，塔利班部隊迅速重新控制局勢。許多阿富汗人擔心塔利班與剩餘反塔利班戰士之間再次爆發內戰，但隨著美國戰爭的結束，影響平民的暴力事件急劇下降。
In 2022, the Taliban government imposed a series of edicts governing every aspect of daily life, which remain in place. The rules are particularly strict for women and girls, who are no longer allowed to work at most jobs or seek formal education beyond sixth grade.
2022年，塔利班政府頒佈了一系列管理日常生活各個方面的法令，這些法令仍然有效。這些規定對婦女和女孩尤其嚴格，她們不再被允許從事大多數工作或尋求六年級以上的正規教育。
Military conflict has not disappeared. In late 2025, Pakistan began a campaign against the armed groups operating on its border with Afghanistan, while the Taliban tighten their grip on Afghan society and defend their rule against the few remaining forces of resistance.
軍事衝突並未消失。2025年底，巴基斯坦開始對在其與阿富汗邊境活動的武裝團體發起行動，同時塔利班收緊對阿富汗社會的控制，並抵禦少數殘餘抵抗力量以捍衛其統治。
An American soldier aboard a Chinook helicopter as U.S. troops began their withdrawal from the country, loading up ammunition and supplies from Kandahar Airfield in May 2021.
2021年5月，美國軍隊開始從阿富汗撤軍，一名美國士兵在坎達哈機場的一架支奴乾直升機上裝載彈藥和補給。
A passenger in a car awaiting clearance at a security checkpoint in Herat in July 2021. The Taliban would soon seize Herat, the country’s third-largest city.
2021年7月，赫拉特一個安全檢查站，一名車內乘客等待放行。塔利班很快將奪取該國第三大城市赫拉特。
On the day that Afghanistan’s capital, Kabul, fell to the Taliban, the group’s fighters rode a Humvee near a roundabout in the city on Aug. 15, 2021. The date is now celebrated in Afghanistan as the moment of the country’s liberation from U.S. occupation.
2021年8月15日，阿富汗首都喀布爾落入塔利班之手當天，該組織戰士乘坐一輛悍馬車經過城中一個環島。這一天現在在阿富汗被視為慶祝國家從美國佔領下解放的時刻。
With the U.S. withdrawal deadline looming, mayhem persisted outside the Kabul airport as American Marines stood guard in August 2021. Even some Afghans holding special immigrant visas were turned away to give priority to U.S. citizens.
2021年8月，隨著美國撤軍期限臨近，喀布爾機場外混亂持續，美國海軍陸戰隊員站崗守衛。甚至一些持有特殊移民簽證的阿富汗人也因美國優先處理本國公民而被拒之門外。
In August 2021, during the last days of the U.S. evacuation, there was another spasm of violence, when a suicide bomb exploded outside the gates of the crowded airport in Kabul. An Islamic State affiliate claimed responsibility for the attack, which killed 13 U.S. service members and scores of civilians.
2021年8月，在美國撤離的最後幾天，又發生了暴力事件，一枚自殺式炸彈在擁擠的喀布爾機場門外爆炸。一個與伊斯蘭國有關聯的組織聲稱負責，襲擊造成13名美國軍人和數十名平民死亡。
