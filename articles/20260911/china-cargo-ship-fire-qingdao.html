青島造船廠內外籍貨船起火25人遇難

Cargo Ship Fire in Eastern China Kills 25

BERRY WANG, ALI WATKINS • 2026-09-11
Twenty-five people were killed on Thursday after a fire broke out on a cargo ship that was docked in Qingdao, China, state media outlets reported.
據官方媒體報導，週四，一艘停靠在中國青島的貨船發生火災，造成25人遇難。
The fire broke out at around 11:15 a.m. local time on a foreign-flagged ship that was undergoing maintenance, according to CCTV, China’s state broadcaster. The vessel was docked in a shipyard owned by Qingdao Beihai Shipbuilding, a subsidiary of a state-owned conglomerate.
據中國國家廣播電視台中央電視台報導，火災發生在當地時間上午11點15分左右，起火的是一艘正在接受維修的外籍貨船。該船停靠在一家國有企業集團的子公司青島北海造船公司所屬的造船廠內。
Qingdao, in Shandong Province in eastern China, is a high-volume trade hub that has one of the busiest ports in the world and is also home to one of the country’s most strategically important naval bases.
青島位於中國東部的山東省，是一個吞吐量巨大的貿易樞紐，擁有世界上最繁忙的港口之一，也是中國最具戰略意義的海軍基地之一的所在地。
The ship had 42 people onboard at the time of the blaze, which was extinguished several hours later and whose cause remained unclear. Twelve people were safely evacuated and five injured people were taken to hospital where they were in a stable condition, according to state media.
火災發生時船上共有42人。大火在幾小時後被撲滅，起因尚不清楚。據官方媒體報導，有12人安全撤出，五人受傷送醫，目前情況穩定。
廣告
The fire prompted a large-scale emergency response at the shipyard. China’s leader, Xi Jinping, has called for a prompt investigation into its cause.
這場大火在造船廠引發了大規模的緊急響應。中國領導人習近平已要求對起火原因展開迅速調查。
Chinese state media identified the vessel as the Ocean Melody. According to Marine Traffic, a ship tracking platform, a Liberian-flagged vessel with the same name had been docked in Qingdao for at least 10 days.
中國官方媒體確認這艘船名為「海洋旋律」號。根據船舶跟蹤平台Marine Traffic的數據，一艘同名的利比裡亞籍船隻已在青島停靠了至少10天。
