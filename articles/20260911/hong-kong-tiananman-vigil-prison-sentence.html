香港六四守夜活動組織者被判入獄

Organizers of Hong Kong Vigils for Tiananmen Massacre Sentenced to Prison

TIFFANY MAY • 2026-09-11
A court in Hong Kong, once the only place in China where the victims of the 1989 military massacre in Tiananmen Square could be publicly commemorated, sentenced three organizers of those vigils to years in prison on Friday.
週五，香港一家法院判處三名天安門事件守夜活動組織者數年監禁。香港曾是中國唯一可以公開悼念1989年天安門廣場屠殺的地方。
Over three decades, the annual commemoration events were seen as a symbol of Hong Kong’s freedoms relative to the mainland. The sentences on Friday were another clear sign of how Beijing has eroded those freedoms in the name of national security.
三十多年來，一年一度的紀念活動一直被視為香港相對於內地自由的象徵。週五的判決再次明確表明了北京正如何以國家安全為名侵蝕這些自由。
The three organizers, Chow Hang-tung, Lee Cheuk-yan and Albert Ho, were sentenced under a national security law imposed on Hong Kong by Beijing in 2020.
三名組織者鄒幸彤、李卓人和何俊仁是根據北京2020年在香港實施的國家安全法被判刑的。
Ms. Chow, a lawyer, received the longest prison term of seven years and three months. Mr. Lee, a labor organizer, was sentenced to seven years. Mr. Ho, a lawyer and a founder of what was the city’s largest opposition party before it disbanded last year, was sentenced to five years and two months in prison.
身為律師的鄒幸彤獲刑最重，刑期為七年三個月。工運組織者李卓人被判處七年監禁。去年解散的香港最大反對黨創始人之一、同為律師的何俊仁則被判處五年兩個月監禁。
廣告
Veterans of Hong Kong’s pro-democracy movement, the three were leaders of the Hong Kong Alliance, a group that organized the Tiananmen vigils and called for the “end of one-party dictatorship.” A panel of three judges handpicked by the city’s leader to handle national security cases ruled that the group’s core mission posed a challenge to the ruling Communist Party and found them guilty of inciting subversion.
作為香港民主派的資深人士，這三人是香港支聯會的領導人，該團體曾組織天安門燭光守夜並呼籲「結束一黨專政」。由香港行政長官指定負責處理國家安全案件的三法官審判庭裁定，該組織的核心使命對執政的共產黨構成挑戰，判定三人煽動顛覆國家政權罪名成立。
They have already spent several years behind bars since their arrests in September 2021, some of which will count toward their sentences. Their sentencing concluded one of the many lengthy national security trials involving Hong Kong’s best known pro-democracy figures.
自2021年9月被捕以來，他們已被關押數年，其中部分關押時間將折抵刑期。對他們的宣判結束了涉及香港最知名民主派人士的眾多漫長國家安全審判之一。
After mass antigovernment protests in Hong Kong in 2019, the authorities have sought to prevent any public commemorations of the 1989 Tiananmen Square crackdown in Beijing.
2019年香港爆發大規模反政府抗議後，當局一直試圖禁止任何公開紀念1989年北京天安門廣場鎮壓事件的活動。
The soccer pitches where tens of thousands once gathered to remember those killed when the Chinese military cleared student protesters from Tiananmen Square, killing hundreds and possibly thousands, are now sealed off days ahead of the sensitive anniversary, and crowds of police officers question lone commemorators.
數萬人曾聚集在一個足球場，悼念天安門廣場學生抗議遭軍隊清場時的遇難者——清場行動造成數百甚至數千人死亡，如今，在敏感的週年紀念日前數天，球場便會被封鎖，成群的警員還會盤查獨自前往的悼念者。
Freedom of speech in Hong Kong has also been considerably stifled. The trial was made notable by the uncompromising testimony of Ms. Chow, who defended herself. (Before her arrest, she had been preparing to defend Gwyneth Ho, a former journalist who was sentenced in 2024 to seven years in prison for running in an unofficial primary election.)
香港的言論自由也遭到了嚴厲打壓。選擇為自己辯護的鄒幸彤發表了毫不妥協的證詞，使這場審判備受矚目。（在被捕前，她一直在準備為前記者何桂藍辯護，後者因參加非官方初選於2024年被判處七年監禁。）
In the courtroom, Ms. Chow used every opportunity she could to keep alive the memory of the Tiananmen victims. She read aloud the testimony of mothers whose children had been gunned down by tanks in 1989. She argued with a judge when he requested that she replace her repeated references to the “Tiananmen massacre” with the “1989 incident.” Behind bars, she commemorated every anniversary with hunger strikes.
在法庭上，鄒幸彤抓住一切機會表達對天安門受害者的追思。她當庭大聲朗讀了一些母親的證詞，她們的孩子在1989年死於坦克之下。當法官要求她將其反覆提及的「天安門大屠殺」替換為「1989年事件」時，她與法官展開辯論。身陷囹圄後，每逢週年紀念，她都會發起絕食抗議。
廣告
Mark Clifford, the president of the Committee for Freedom in Hong Kong Foundation based in New York, said that Ms. Chow was noteworthy, even among other outspoken Hong Kong activists who have become more circumspect in court as they battle serious charges and the prospect of years behind bars.
總部位於紐約的香港自由委員會基金會主席馬克·克利福德表示，在所有這些因面臨重罪指控和多年刑期而在法庭上顯得謹小慎微的活動人士中，鄒幸彤的表現格外引人注目。
“Other defendants have either pled guilty or said very little or fought on pretty narrow technical grounds,” he said in a phone interview. “She’s made this full-throated case for freedom and for free speech and for historical memory in a way that is unprecedented in a Hong Kong courtroom.”
「其他被告要麼認罪，要麼噤聲少言，要麼僅在非常狹隘的技術層面進行抗辯，」他在電話採訪中表示。「而她以香港法庭上前所未有的方式全力為自由、言論自由和歷史記憶展開辯護。」
Whenever the judges overseeing the trial asked whether she would ask for leniency, she expressed no regrets for her actions. “I would rather be a criminal than a person who betrays their conscience,” she wrote in her mitigation statement.
每當主審法官詢問她是否請求從寬處理時，她都對自己的所作所為毫無悔意。「我寧願做一個罪犯，都不要做一個背叛良知的人，」她在自己的陳情文中寫道。
Zhou Fengsuo, a student leader of the Tiananmen protests now living in the United States said by telephone that essays Ms. Chow has written from prison made him feel again the fearless spirit of pro-democracy demonstrators at Tiananmen Square. He compared her courage to that of Tank Man, an unidentified person who was photographed boldly confronting a convoy of tanks barreling down a Beijing avenue after the bloody military crackdown.
現居美國、天安門抗議活動學生領袖周鋒鎖在電話中表示，鄒幸彤在獄中撰寫的文章讓他再次感受到天安門廣場民主示威者無畏的精神。他將鄒幸彤的勇氣與「坦克人」相提並論。「坦克人」是一位身份不明的男子，在血腥的軍事鎮壓後，他被拍到英勇地擋在一隊在北京大道上轟鳴前行的坦克面前。
Mr. Zhou thanked the three leaders for bearing the burden of memory. “They suffered on behalf of our generation from 1989,” he said. “I salute them.”
周鋒鎖感謝三位支聯會領導人承擔了歷史記憶的重擔。「他們代我們八九一代受難，」他說。「我向他們致敬。」
The court also ordered the now-defunct Hong Kong Alliance to pay a $190,000 fine. In 2021, the government froze the group’s assets and ordered it to scrub its online presence.
法院還下令對現已解散的香港支聯會處以150萬港元（約合人民幣130萬元）罰款。2021年，政府凍結了該團體的資產，並命令其清除所有網路痕跡。
廣告
Human Rights Watch, an advocacy group, wrote in a statement that “the harsh sentences against peaceful Tiananmen vigil organizers expose the cruelty and insecurity of a government afraid of memory and the people.”
人權倡導組織人權觀察在聲明中表示：「對和平悼念六四的組織者判以重刑，揭露出一個害怕記憶、害怕人民的政府的殘酷與不安。」
For Ms. Chow, memory has been a guiding force.
對鄒幸彤而言，記憶一直是一股指引她的力量。
As the case came to a close, Ms. Chow repeated a promise to strive for democracy and to demand accountability for Tiananmen victims “until the day democracy arrives.”
隨著案件審理接近尾聲，她再次承諾將爭取民主並要求為天安門受害者追責，「直到民主到來的那一天」。
“I will work hard, study, improve myself and try all sorts of things,” she wrote. “This is a solemn vow I made to the departed in a sea of candles, and I have no intention on breaking it.”
「我會去努力、去學習、去充實自己、去做各種的嘗試，」她寫道。「這是我在燭海之中對死難者許下的莊嚴誓言。」

Tiffany May是時報駐香港記者，報導香港和更廣泛地區的政治、商業和文化議題。
