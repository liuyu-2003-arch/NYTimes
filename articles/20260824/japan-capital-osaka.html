日本多座城市加入「副首都」爭奪戰

Cities Compete in Dizzying ‘Gold Rush’ to Be Japan’s Backup Capital

赫海威, HISAKO UENO • 2026-08-24
For 158 years, Tokyo has stood as Japan’s glittering capital, a world-famous nexus of food, culture, business and government.
158年來，東京一直是流光溢彩的日本國都，是世界聞名的美食、文化、商業與政府中心。
But Tokyo might soon get an understudy. Japan’s legislature recently passed a bill to create a secondary capital that would serve as an alternate center for the government’s operations, ensuring continuity in the case of a natural disaster or other emergency.
但東京可能很快就會有一個「替補」。日本立法機構最近通過了一項法案，擬設立一個副首都，作為政府運作的備用中心，以確保在自然災害或其他緊急情況下的連續性。
With hundreds of millions of dollars potentially in the offing, and the chance for tax breaks, tourism and urban revival, the plan has set off a frenzy in Japan. Thirteen local governments have already entered the race, including the business hub of Osaka, the southern prefecture of Fukuoka and the northern island of Hokkaido. Even the laid-back, mountainous region of Gunma, home to about 1.9 million people, has thrown its hat into the ring.
 這項計劃有望帶來數億美元的資金，並帶來稅收減免、旅遊業發展和城市復興等機會，在日本引發了一陣熱潮。已有13個地方政府加入角逐，包括商業中心大阪、南部福岡縣和北部的北海道。甚至連人口約190萬、環境悠閑的山區群馬縣也參與其中。
Mayors and governors have held flashy news conferences to declare their interest. Osaka has even created a mascot for its bid, a talking “welcoming cat” named Nyaniwa Fukumaru.
各地市長和知事紛紛召開華麗的新聞發布會，宣布其參選意向。大阪甚至為其申辦創造了一個吉祥物——一個會說話的「招財貓」，名叫浪花福丸。
廣告
Masashi Kawai, a senior policy officer at the Tokyo Foundation, a think tank, said the competition had the feel of a grand festival: “A ‘don’t-miss-the-Gold-Rush’ mentality has taken hold.”
智庫東京基金會的高級政策官員河合雅司表示，這場競爭感覺像一場盛大的節日：「一種『不容錯過的淘金熱』心態已經形成。」
The idea has divided Japanese citizens. Supporters of the plan say that Japan, long prone to earthquakes, typhoons and floods, must be prepared for the possibility that its government offices in Tokyo could become debilitated. Opponents say the idea is a misguided proposal meant to reward allies of Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi: One of her closest political partners is the Japan Innovation Party, which has worked for years to establish its home base of Osaka as an alternate capital.
這一想法在日本民眾中引發了分歧。支持者表示，日本長期以來易受地震、颱風和洪水侵襲，必須為東京的政府機關可能癱瘓做好準備。反對者則認為，這是一個不明智的提議，旨在給首相高市早苗的政治盟友帶去好處：她最密切的政治合作夥伴之一是日本維新會，後者多年來一直致力於將其大本營大阪建設為副首都。
It will be up to Ms. Takaichi to crown the winner. No official timeline has been set, but her cabinet is expected to put together a list of requirements for the secondary capital in the next few months.
最終獲勝者將由高市早苗來決定。目前尚未設定官方時間表，但預計她的內閣將在未來幾個月內製定一份副首都的要求清單。
Guarding Against Disaster
防範災害
The idea of a second capital has some precedent in Japanese history. During the Edo period, from 1603 to 1868, Japan had two capitals. Edo, which later became Tokyo, was a political and administrative capital where the shogun ruled. Kyoto was the cultural and spiritual capital, home to the emperor.
設立第二首都的想法在日本歷史上有先例。在江戶時代（1603年至1868年），日本有兩個首都。江戶（後稱東京）是政治和行政首都，幕府將軍在此統治。京都則是文化和精神首都，是天皇的居所。
In recent decades, the threat posed by natural disasters has led some experts and politicians to revive the idea. Japan is situated along the Pacific “Ring of Fire,” a chain of seismologically active faults encircling the Pacific Ocean. Experts have warned about the possibility of a so-called megaquake in Tokyo and other large cities.
近幾十年來，自然災害帶來的威脅讓一些專家和政治人士重新提起了這一想法。日本位於太平洋「火環」地帶，這是一條環繞太平洋的地震活躍斷層鏈。專家們已就東京和其他大城市發生「特大地震」的可能性發出警告。
In 2011, a 9.1-magnitude megaquake and tsunami in northeastern Japan killed more than 19,000 people and triggered a nuclear disaster. The quake was a reminder of the vulnerability of Tokyo, which lies about 230 miles southwest from the epicenter.
2011年，日本東北部發生9.1級巨大地震和海嘯，造成超過19000人死亡，並引發了一場核災難。那次地震提醒人們注意東京的脆弱性——東京位於震中西南約230英里處。
Designating another capital could also help decentralize Japan’s population and drive economic growth outside the capital. Many people have left rural areas in recent years for jobs and schooling in Tokyo. While the countryside is hollowing out, the population of the Tokyo metropolitan area has grown, rising slightly to 37 million last year.
指定另一個首都也有助於分散日本的人口，並在首都以外地區推動經濟增長。近年來，許多人為了工作和求學而離開農村地區前往東京。在鄉村日漸空心化的同時，東京都市圈的人口卻在增長，去年小幅增至3700萬。

Concerns About Costs
對成本的擔憂
Experts agree about the threat that natural disasters could pose to Tokyo’s government. But some critics say the plan would do little to address those risks.
專家們一致認為自然災害可能對東京政府構成威脅。但一些批評者表示，該計劃對應對這些風險作用不大。
Nobuo Sasaki, a professor emeritus at Tokyo’s Chuo University and a former Tokyo official, said the legislation failed to account for important factors like the stability of the ground in the prospective cities.
中央大學榮休教授、前東京都官員佐佐木信夫表示，該法案未能考慮到一些重要因素，如候選城市的地基穩定性。
“It remains entirely unclear what exactly constitutes a ‘secondary capital’ or how it should be developed,” he said.
「究竟什麼是『副首都』，或者應該如何發展它，至今完全不清楚，」他說。
Opposition lawmakers argue that Osaka, a top contender, is not suitable to serve as an alternate, given its own vulnerability to earthquakes. They have also expressed concern about the costs of building backup structures to house government offices.
反對黨議員認為，主要候選城市大阪因其自身易受地震影響，不適合作為替補。他們也表示擔心建設政府辦公樓備用設施的成本。
Makiko Kishi, a lawmaker for the Constitutional Democratic Party of Japan, said the proposal was “half-baked” and “tainted by partisan maneuvering and the pursuit of vested interests.”
日本立憲民主黨議員岸真紀子表示，該提議「不成熟」，且「受到黨派操作和追求既得利益的影響」。
Some experts argue that Japan should focus instead on fortifying Tokyo’s own defenses against earthquakes and other threats. They say that designating a backup capital could also exacerbate problems in rural Japan, causing millions to flee the countryside for another megacity.
一些專家認為，日本應集中精力加強東京自身抵禦地震和其他威脅的能力。他們表示，指定備用首都也可能加劇日本農村的問題，導致數百萬人逃離鄉村前往另一個超大城市。

The Race to Be Second
第二首都之爭
Despite the conflicting views, local leaders across Japan have rushed in recent weeks to explain why their regions should be chosen.
儘管觀點不一，日本各地的地方領導人在最近幾週紛紛競相解釋為何自己的地區應被選中。
In the city of Nagoya, Mayor Ichiro Hirosawa skipped a planned dance festival last month to hold a news conference promoting his city. He has said the city plans to play up its manufacturing prowess and to “do everything in our power to capture the country.”
在名古屋市，市長廣澤一郎上月取消了參加舞蹈節的計劃，轉而召開新聞發布會宣傳他的城市。他表示，名古屋計劃發揮其製造業實力，並「盡一切努力贏得全國青睞」。
Officials in the northern prefecture of Hokkaido and its capital, Sapporo City, have set up teams to focus on securing second-capital status. They have emphasized their region’s distance from Nankai Trough, a seismically active zone off southern Japan.
北海道及其首府札幌市的官員已組建團隊，專注於爭取副首都地位。他們強調本地區距離南海海槽（日本南部近海的地震活躍帶）較遠。
Mr. Kawai, the policy expert, said it would be hard to replicate Tokyo’s success. Regions should instead focus on coming up with creative ways to promote economic growth, he said, to offset the declining population.
政策專家河合表示，複製東京的成功是很困難的。他說，各地區應轉而專注於提出促進經濟增長的創新方法，以抵消人口下降的影響。
“Without that approach,” he said, “the secondary-capital initiative will bring 100 harms and not one good.”
「不從這個角度去思考，」他說，「副首都計劃將百害而無一利。」
