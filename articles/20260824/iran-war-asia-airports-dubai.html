洲際旅行杜拜轉機？不，也許是首爾

Why Your Next Long-Haul Layover May Be in Seoul, Not Dubai

JOHN YOON • 2026-08-24
Flying across the world? You’re now more likely to stop in Asia than you would have been before war broke out in the Middle East.
計劃飛越全球？與中東爆發戰爭前相比，你現在更有可能在亞洲中轉。
Major Persian Gulf airlines like Emirates, Qatar Airways and Etihad dominated intercontinental travel before the war, accounting for roughly a third of the traffic between Europe and Asia and about one in two travelers between Europe and Australia.
戰爭之前，阿聯酋航空、卡達航空和阿提哈德航空等波斯灣主要航空公司主導著洲際旅行，約佔歐洲和亞洲之間客流量的三分之一，以及歐洲和澳洲之間旅客量的大約一半。
But transfer traffic in the Middle East on routes connecting Asia to Europe has nearly halved since the war started in late February, according to one estimate.
但據一項估計，自2月底戰爭爆發以來，在連接亞洲和歐洲的航線上，中東地區的中轉客流量已減少了近一半。
And for now at least, travelers between Asia, Australia and Europe who once flew through the Middle East are taking nonstop flights or transiting through airports like Hong Kong, Istanbul, Seoul and Singapore. Airlines have added flights to handle this new demand.
至少在目前，曾經途經中東往返於亞洲、澳洲和歐洲之間的旅客正在選擇直飛航班，或通過香港、伊斯坦堡、首爾和新加坡等地的機場進行中轉。航空公司已增加航班以應對這一新需求。
廣告
Here’s how the war is reshuffling international flights, and why this is an opportunity for Asian airports and airlines.
以下是關於戰爭如何重組國際航班，以及為何這對亞洲的機場和航空公司來說是一種機遇。
How we got here
事態發展歷程
The major Middle Eastern hubs — Dubai and Abu Dhabi in the United Arab Emirates, and Doha in Qatar — sit at the intersection of Africa, Asia and Europe, a few hours by air from most of the world’s population. They grew rapidly over the past two decades with hefty government backing.
中東的主要樞紐——阿聯酋的杜拜、阿布達比和卡達的杜哈——坐落於非洲、亞洲和歐洲的交匯處，乘飛機距離世界上大多數人口都只有幾個小時的航程。在政府的大力支持下，它們在過去的20年裡迅速發展。
Last year, 16 percent of the world’s 417 million connecting passengers transited through the region, according to the International Air Transport Association, the airline trade group.
航空貿易組織國際航空運輸協會的數據顯示，去年全球4.17億中轉旅客中，有16%通過該地區中轉。
But after the war started, the drop was stark. Middle Eastern transfer traffic on routes connecting Asia and Western Europe fell by 47 percent in April year-over-year, according to Alton Aviation Consultancy, an advisory firm headquartered in New York.
但在戰爭爆發後，下降幅度非常明顯。總部位於紐約的諮詢公司奧爾頓航空諮詢表示，4月份連接亞洲和西歐航線上的中東中轉客流量同比下降了47%。
As the conflict dragged on and airports in Doha, Dubai and Abu Dhabi were targeted by airstrikes, many flights were suspended or rerouted. Some governments urged passengers not to travel through the region.
隨著衝突的持續，加上杜哈、杜拜和阿布達比的機場遭到空襲，許多航班被暫停或改變航線。一些國家政府也敦促乘客不要途經該地區。
“People have been looking for alternative routes,” said John Strickland, an aviation consultant in London. “Asian hubs have been natural alternatives.”
「人們一直在尋找替代航線，」倫敦航空顧問約翰·斯特里克蘭說。「亞洲的航空樞紐成了天然的替代選擇。」
Major Asian airports handled massive traffic before the war and had the infrastructure to process more flights and travelers.
亞洲的主要機場在戰爭爆發前就處理著龐大的客流量，並具備處理更多航班和旅客的基礎設施。
IATA reported in May that bookings for June to September had fallen across all regions except the Asia-Pacific, where they grew 33 percent year-over-year. It also said in July that bookings to the region had expanded the most at the height of the war disruptions.
國際航空運輸協會在5月報告稱，除亞太地區外，所有地區6月至9月的預訂量均有所下降，而亞太地區的預訂量同比增長了33%。該協會在7月還表示，在戰爭干擾最嚴重的時期，飛往該地區的預訂量增幅最大。
廣告
Airports
機場
This has been good for Asian hubs.
這對亞洲的航空樞紐來說是個利好。
Incheon International Airport, which serves greater Seoul, said this month that its transfer traffic in the first half of 2026 had increased by 18 percent year-over-year, and by 63 percent on European routes. That helped make it the busiest international airport in the world in that period, according to preliminary internal data from the trade group Airports Council International that Incheon provided to The New York Times.
服務於大首爾地區的仁川國際機場本月表示，2026年上半年其中轉客流量同比增長了18%，其中歐洲航線客流量增長了63%。根據仁川機場向《紐約時報》提供的行業組織國際機場理事會的初步內部數據，這助推其成為這一時期內世界上最繁忙的國際機場。
Incheon said that it had risen from third place — surpassing Dubai International Airport and London’s Heathrow Airport — because the geopolitical crisis had pushed travelers away from the Gulf hubs.
仁川機場表示，其排名是從第三位躍升至首位——超越了杜拜國際機場和倫敦希思羅機場，因為地緣政治危機迫使旅客遠離了海灣地區的樞紐。
The data also shows that international passenger traffic at Hong Kong International Airport grew by more than 11 percent in the same period, and nearly 10 percent at Taiwan Taoyuan International Airport.
數據還顯示，同期香港國際機場的國際客流量增長了超過11%，台灣桃園國際機場增長了近10%。
Singapore’s Changi Airport, the connecting point for about one in four passengers traveling between Europe and Australia, said that airlines had added more than 800 flights between Singapore and cities like London, Munich, Paris and Sydney between March and July.
新加坡樟宜機場是往返於歐洲和澳洲之間約四分之一乘客的中轉站。該機場表示，在3月至7月期間，各航空公司在新加坡與倫敦、慕尼黑、巴黎和雪梨等城市之間增加了800多個航班。
These airports stand to benefit because travelers, whether landing or transiting, pay passenger fees. Each traveler also spends roughly $7.57 on average on airport shopping, dining and other nonaviation expenses that are critical to airports’ financial viability, according to Airports Council International.
這些機場將從中受益，因為無論是降落還是中轉的旅客都會支付乘客費。國際機場理事會的數據顯示，每位旅客平均還會在機場的購物、餐飲和其他非航空類消費上花費約7.57美元，而這些對於機場的財務生存能力至關重要。
Airlines
航空公司
廣告
The disruption has also helped airlines outside the Middle East capture more customers.
這種干擾也幫助了中東以外的航空公司吸引了更多的客戶。
A snapshot:
概況如下：
• In April, Cathay Pacific, a Hong Kong airline, said that a 24 percent rise in passengers in March compared with the same month last year was a result of shifting demand created by the “ongoing situation in the Middle East.”
• 4月，香港的航空公司國泰航空表示，3月乘客數量同比增加24%，這是由「中東持續局勢」引發的需求轉移所致。
• Singapore Airlines attributed its 7-percent rise in passenger traffic to the rerouting of travel to Europe and the Americas.
• 新加坡航空將其客流量7%的增長歸因於飛往歐洲和美洲的旅行路線改變。
• Turkish Airlines reported earlier this month that it had added capacity to China, Hong Kong and Thailand as passengers avoided flying to, or through, the Middle East.
• 土耳其航空本月早些時候報告稱，由於乘客避免飛往或途經中東，該公司增加了飛往中國、香港和泰國的運力。
• Lufthansa Group said in May that its airlines had added additional flights, particularly to Asia and Africa, while Air France said in June that it had redeployed aircraft to meet surging demand on Asian routes.
• 漢莎航空集團在5月表示，其旗下航空公司增加了航班，特別是飛往亞洲和非洲的航班；而法國航空在6月表示，已重新部署飛機以滿足亞洲航線激增的需求。
Where we go from here
未來走向
It remains unclear how long this trend will last. As Iran and the United States settle into an uneasy standoff, the threat of a flare-up remains. But the major Middle East airlines have started to rebuild passenger confidence and aggressively reduced prices, and aviation experts said the windfall in Asia is already eroding as a result.
目前尚不清楚這種趨勢將持續多久。隨著伊朗和美國陷入令人不安的僵局，衝突加劇的威脅依然存在。但中東主要航空公司已開始重建乘客信心並大幅降價，航空專家表示，亞洲收穫的意外收益已經因此開始逐漸減少。
廣告
IATA said that bookings to the Middle East had steadily recovered since April.
國際航空運輸協會表示，自4月以來，飛往中東的預訂量已穩步回升。
“You might be tempted to take a risk of flying through the Middle East because the price is so good,” said Jeffrey Goh, a managing director at Alton.
「由於票價非常優惠，你可能會忍不住冒風險選擇經中東轉機，」奧爾頓公司的董事總經理傑弗里·高表示。

John Yoon是時報駐首爾記者，報導突發新聞和熱點新聞。
