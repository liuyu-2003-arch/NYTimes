中國宣布規模最大汽車召回行動，涉及特斯拉等車企

In China’s Biggest Car Recall, Tesla and 8 Others Will Address Door Safety

KEITH BRADSHER, JACK EWING • 2026-08-24
The Chinese government announced its biggest car recall ever on Friday, forcing nine automakers to address the dangers of retractable door handles popularized by Tesla particularly for electric cars.
中國政府週五宣布了有史以來最大規模的汽車召回行動，要求九家汽車製造商解決隱藏式門把手帶來的安全隱患，這種門把手由特斯拉率先推廣，在電動汽車中廣泛流行。
The recall involves 4.3 million vehicles. Most of the automakers, including Tesla, will attach stickers to their cars to show where the handles are. Tesla will also push online software updates to the cars so their windows will open automatically after a crash.
此次召回涉及430萬輛汽車。包括特斯拉在內的大多數汽車製造商將在車上貼上貼紙，標明門把手的位置。特斯拉還將向這些車輛推送在線軟體更新，以便在發生碰撞後車窗能自動降下。
Concerns about the doors have also flared in the United States, where families have sued Tesla, claiming its design is defective and led to the deaths of people who could not exit the company’s cars after accidents.
在美國，對這類車門的擔憂也日益加劇。一些家庭起訴了特斯拉，稱其設計存在缺陷，導致人們在事故發生後無法逃離汽車導致喪生。
China’s State Administration of Market Regulation said Friday that Tesla and eight Chinese automakers — Geely, Dongfeng, Xiaomi, Chery, Beijing Automotive, First Auto Works, Leapmotor and Xpeng — would each be conducting recalls. The largest of them by far will be by Tesla, covering almost three million cars, with the other manufacturers recalling a total of 1.3 million cars.
中國國家市場監督管理總局週五表示，特斯拉和八家中國汽車製造商——吉利、東風、小米、奇瑞、北汽、一汽、零跑和小鵬——將分別進行召回。其中規模最大的當屬特斯拉，涉及近300萬輛汽車，其他製造商共計召回130萬輛汽車。
廣告
Tesla has long been one of the biggest sellers of electric vehicles in China.
特斯拉長期以來一直是中國電動汽車銷量最大的品牌之一。
The recalls follow growing public anger in China about cases when drivers and passengers, as well as rescuers, had trouble finding and operating interior and exterior door handles during fires. Fires have been a problem in some electric cars, particularly older models that used less stable battery chemistries that are prone to extreme overheating after collisions.
此次召回源於中國公眾日益高漲的憤怒情緒。此前曾發生多起案例，在火災事故中，司機、乘客以及救援人員往往難以找到並操作車內和車外的門把手。火災問題在某些電動汽車中較為突出，尤其是那些採用穩定性較低的電池化學成分的舊款車型，在發生碰撞後極易嚴重過熱。
In early February, Chinese regulators banned fully retractable door handles from new cars starting next year. Many of these handles are powered, prompting concern about whether they will work when the car suffers an electrical failure, as can occur after a crash.
今年2月初，中國監管機構宣布，從明年起將禁止新車使用全隱藏式門把手。這些門把手大多是電動的，這引發了人們的擔憂：當汽車在碰撞後可能發生電氣故障時，這種門把手是否還能正常工作。
Chinese regulators have allowed the continued installation of semi-retractable door handles that do not become flush with the vehicle’s surfaces. In the United States, the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration has begun drafting new regulations as well.
中國監管機構允許廠商繼續安裝不與車身表面完全平齊的半隱藏式門把手。在美國，國家公路交通安全管理局也已開始起草新規。
A video was widely circulated in China this year of a Dongfeng electric car that slid into a fence in southwestern China and caught fire after a collision with a truck. The driver exited, allowing his door to close behind him, but then had great difficulty opening any doors to extricate his passengers. He was eventually able to do so with the help of another motorist.
今年，一段影片在中國廣泛流傳：在中國西南部，一輛東風電動汽車撞上護欄，隨後與一輛卡車相撞並起火。司機下了車，車門在他身後自動關上，隨後他發現難以打開任何一扇車門來解救乘客。最終，在另一位司機的幫助下，他才將乘客救出。
The crash, which left his passengers with burn injuries, took place in March 2025 but did not become widely known until this year.
這起導致乘客燒傷的碰撞事故發生在2025年3月，但直到今年才廣為人知。
廣告
Tesla popularized retractable door handles as providing a more streamlined look, although automotive engineers have said the gains in aerodynamic efficiency are modest. Chinese makers of electric cars quickly followed suit as they rapidly expanded from 2020 onward.
特斯拉普及了隱藏式門把手，稱其能帶來更具流線型的外觀，儘管汽車工程師表示，這種設計在空氣動力學效率方面的提升微乎其微。隨著中國電動汽車製造商從2020年起迅速擴張，它們也迅速效仿。
Many electronic door latches rely on a 12-volt battery that is separate from the high-voltage battery that drives the motors of electric cars. If a crash cuts off the power from that smaller battery, the electronic door mechanism may not work. And people in the car may not be able to quickly find manual door latches, which are often not installed in the same area as the electronic mechanisms.
許多電子車門鎖依賴於一個12伏的電池，該電池與驅動電動汽車電機的高壓電池是分開的。如果碰撞切斷了這塊小電池的電源，電子車門的機械裝置可能就會失效。此外，車內的人員可能無法迅速找到手動車門開關，因為它們通常不與電子裝置安裝在同一個位置。
Tesla did not respond to a request for comment.
特斯拉沒有回應置評請求。
Fully electric cars and plug-in gasoline-electric cars accounted for three-fifths of the cars sold in China last month, as China leads the world in adoption of electric automotive technology. David Zhang, the dean of vehicle technology research at the Jiangxi New Energy Technology Institute, said the enormous recall showed China’s readiness to set safety standards for electric cars.
隨著中國在電動汽車技術應用方面引領全球，上月中國售出的汽車中，純電動汽車和插電式混合動力汽車佔了五分之三。江西新能源科技職業學院新能源汽車技術研究院院長張翔表示，此次大規模召回表明中國已準備好為電動汽車制定安全標準。
“Automotive quality and safety issues have been improved through repeated updates,” he said.
「通過多次更新，汽車的質量和安全問題已經得到改善，」他說。
In the United States, lawsuits and news coverage about electronic doors have prompted federal regulators to begin drafting new rules, a process that could take many months and would apply to new cars several years hence.
在美國，關於電子車門的訴訟和新聞報導已促使聯邦監管機構開始起草新規，這一過程可能需要數月時間，將適用於幾年後的新車。
廣告
In California, Tesla faces lawsuits by the parents of two college students who died inside the company’s Cybertruck after the pickup crashed into a tree in Piedmont, an affluent suburb in the San Francisco Bay Area, in 2024.
在加利福尼亞州，特斯拉正面臨著兩名大學生父母的起訴。2024年，這兩名學生乘坐該公司的Cybertruck皮卡在舊金山灣區富裕的皮埃蒙特郊區撞樹後，在車內死亡。
The young people were unable to escape the burning vehicle, the suit claims, because the electronic doors were disabled in the crash, and it was too difficult to find manual release mechanisms.
訴訟稱，這些年輕人未能逃離燃燒的車輛是因為碰撞導致電子車門失靈，且極難找到手動解鎖裝置。
Tesla is contesting the suit, arguing in court filings that the victims’ own negligence caused their deaths. A jury trial has been scheduled for next year, according to court records.
特斯拉正在對該訴訟進行抗辯，在法庭文件中辯稱是受害者自身的疏忽導致了他們的死亡。根據法庭記錄，陪審團審判已定於明年進行。
The automaker on Friday also announced a separate recall of 2.74 million cars in China to enhance the capacity of its cars to monitor whether drivers are paying attention to the road in front of them while automated driving systems are engaged. Tesla will transmit software to all of the recalled cars so that the cameras can more reliably monitor drivers’ eye movements and overall alertness.
該汽車製造商週五還宣布在中國單獨召回274萬輛汽車，以提升其車輛在啟動自動駕駛系統時監控駕駛員是否關注前方道路的能力。特斯拉將向所有召回的車輛推送軟體更新，以便攝像頭能夠更可靠地監控駕駛員的眼部活動和整體警覺性。
Many established Western and Asian automakers, including BMW, Ford Motor and Hyundai, have also started installing electronic doors, including in some cases on fossil fuel and hybrid models.
寶馬、福特汽車和現代汽車等許多老牌西方和亞洲汽車製造商也已開始安裝電子車門，某些情況下甚至在燃油和混合動力車型上安裝。
