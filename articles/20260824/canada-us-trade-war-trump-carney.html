詳解美加貿易戰：盟友關係因何破裂

Canada-U.S. Trade War: What to Know

《紐約時報》 • 2026-08-24
Talks between the United States and Canada failed late Friday to head off steep new tariffs on Canadian goods, prompting Canada to promise “dollar for dollar” retaliation.
美國與加拿大之間的談判於週五晚間破裂，美國將對加拿大商品加徵高額新關稅，加拿大承諾將採取「等額」報復措施。
Prime Minister Mark Carney of Canada said he had suspended the talks because the American side had introduced last-minute terms that were “unfair, uneconomic and called into question the reliability of any deal.” Then on Saturday, in a powerful speech to Canadians, Mr. Carney said Canada was under attack by President Trump’s tariffs and was “at war” with the United States.
加拿大總理馬克·卡尼表示，他暫停了談判，因為美方在最後時刻提出了「不公平、有違經濟常理且令人對任何協議的可靠性產生懷疑」的條件。隨後在週六向加拿大人發表的強硬講話中，卡尼表示加拿大正受到川普總統關稅的「攻擊」，並稱加拿大與美國處於「戰爭」狀態。
Didn’t President Trump say there was a deal?
川普總統不是說過已達成協議了嗎？
He did, twice. On Tuesday, he said the two countries had reached a deal aside from some details and had extended an earlier deadline that would have introduced the new tariffs.
是的，他說過兩次。週二，他表示兩國已達成協議，只有一些細節尚待商榷，另外還延長了之前本將開始實施新關稅的截止日期。
廣告
On Friday, only hours before the two sides announced the collapse of the negotiations, Mr. Trump said he thought a deal could be reached.
週五，就在雙方宣布談判破裂的幾個小時前，川普還表示他認為可以達成協議。
Why did negotiations fall apart?
談判為何破裂？
Mr. Carney blamed last-minute demands for the collapse of negotiations. He said, among other things, that American negotiators pushed to roll back Canada’s efforts to promote Canadian, including French-language, content in online streaming services; subsidies for industries like publishing and film production; and even mandatory labeling in French as well as English on packaging. French is an official language in Canada.
卡尼將談判破裂歸咎於美方最後一刻的要求。他表示，美方談判代表要求加拿大停止在在線流媒體服務中推廣加拿大內容，包括法語內容；取消對出版、電影製作等行業的補貼；甚至取消包裝上必須同時使用法語和英語標註的規定。法語是加拿大的官方語言之一。
Speaking to reporters on Friday, a U.S. official said Canada wanted concessions that the United States wasn’t prepared to give, particularly when it came to the automotive sector, as well as trade in steel, aluminum and lumber.
一位美國官員週五對記者表示，加拿大希望獲得美國不願做出的讓步，尤其是在汽車行業以及鋼鐵、鋁和木材貿易方面。
Carney’s fighting words.
卡尼的戰鬥檄文。
Slamming the U.S. proposal as “a bad deal,” Mr. Carney’s forceful speech on Saturday made it clear that he considered Mr. Trump’s tariffs an attack on Canada. “We’ve recognized from the start that America has changed,” he said. “We recognize that sometimes, its signature is written in pencil.”
卡尼在週六的強硬講話中抨擊美國的提議是「一份糟糕的協議」，並明確表示他認為川普的關稅是對加拿大的攻擊。「我們從一開始就認識到美國已經變了，」他說。「我們也認識到，有時它的簽名是用鉛筆寫的。」
廣告
“We cannot accept what they offered and we will not give what they asked,” he added. “We were not prepared to compromise Canada’s sovereignty or undermine our key industries.”
「我們不能接受他們提出的條件，也不會滿足他們的要求，」他補充道。「我們不準備在加拿大主權上做妥協，也不準備削弱我們的關鍵產業。」
Mr. Carney, who is burnishing his reputation as a leader who will stand up to Mr. Trump, said Canada would retaliate against the new tariffs “to protect our workers and businesses.”
卡尼正在鞏固自己作為敢於對抗川普的領導人的聲譽，他表示加拿大將對新關稅進行報復，「以保護我們的工人和企業」。
What is the U.S. saying?
美方有何說法？
The United States had offered to reduce its tariffs on steel, aluminum and autos, and to eliminate a recently imposed tariff on Canadian lumber, before the negotiations suddenly collapsed, the U.S. trade representative, Jamieson Greer, told The New York Times in an interview on Saturday.
美國貿易代表賈米森·格里爾週六在接受《紐約時報》採訪時表示，在談判突然破裂之前，美國曾提議降低對鋼鐵、鋁和汽車的關稅，並取消最近對加拿大木材加徵的關稅。
Early Sunday morning, Mr. Trump wrote on his social media platform, Truth Social, that Canada wanted the “benefits of being a State, without being one!!! They have also charged our great farmers, for many years, massive amounts of Tariffs. No more!!!”
週日清晨，川普在其社群媒體平台Truth Social上寫道，加拿大想要「作為美國一個州的好處，卻不想成為一州！！！他們還多年來向我們偉大的農民徵收巨額關稅。不能再這樣了！！！」
But he did not seem to respond directly to Mr. Carney’s muscular speech against the United States.
但他似乎並未直接回應卡尼針對美國的強硬講話。
廣告
What will the U.S. impose tariffs on?
美國將對哪些商品加徵關稅？
The new tariffs account for $20 billion of the $382 billion worth of products Americans bought from Canada last year and cover a wide array of goods, from fake mustaches to dog leashes.
新關稅涉及去年美國從加拿大購買的3820億美元產品中的200億美元，涵蓋從假鬍鬚到狗繩等廣泛商品。
The full list of more than 500 products can be found here, here and here.
包含500多種產品的完整清單可參見此處、此處和此處。
The lists single out several industries, particularly forestry, a sector long targeted by the United States. Building products, especially plywood, are affected, as well as other products like tissue used to make toilet paper. Dairy products, clothing and alcohol are also on the lists.
清單特別針對多個行業，尤其是林業——這一長期被美國瞄準的領域。建築材料（特別是膠合板）以及用於製造衛生紙的原紙等產品受到影響。乳製品、服裝和酒類也在清單上。
Many of the tariffs are on products like honey that often come from small businesses, likely without the financial means to withstand a substantial loss of business for any sustained period.
許多關稅針對的是蜂蜜等通常來自小企業的產品，這些小企業很可能沒有財力承受任何持續性的重大業務損失。
The collapse of the talks also means that Canada did not roll back the tariffs of up to 50 percent that Mr. Trump put on the auto, aluminum and steel industries last year. The president also added 10 percent tariffs on softwood lumber tariffs of 35 percent.
談判破裂也意味著加拿大未能撤銷川普去年對汽車、鋁和鋼鐵行業加徵的高達50%的關稅。總統還對軟木材增加了一項10%的關稅，使總稅率達到35%。
The tariffs came into effect on Saturday. Canada’s retaliatory tariffs come into effect on Sept. 8, Mr. Carney said.
新關稅於週六生效。卡尼表示，加拿大的報復性關稅將於9月8日生效。
What goods face Canada’s retaliatory tariffs?
哪些商品將面臨加拿大的報復性關稅？
The government has yet to unveil its list. But Mr. Carney said it would largely target “steel, dairy, appliances, agricultural equipment, pulp and paper, electronics.”
政府尚未公布清單。但卡尼表示，將主要針對「鋼鐵、乳製品、家電、農業設備、紙漿和紙張、電子產品」。
廣告
Doug Ford, the premier of Ontario, Canada’s most populous province, is urging Mr. Carney to place export taxes on oil, gas and electricity, as well as critical minerals. Energy is, by far, the largest Canadian export to the United States.
加拿大人口最多的省份安大略省省長道格·福特敦促卡尼對石油、天然氣和電力以及關鍵礦產徵收出口稅。能源是迄今為止加拿大對美最大的出口產品。
Weren’t things already tense?
此前局勢不就已經緊張了嗎？
Yes. Since Mr. Trump began his second term, relations between the United States and Canada have been strained.
是的。自川普開始第二任期以來，美國與加拿大之間的關係一直緊張。
Not only has Mr. Trump targeted Canada with tariffs, he has insulted its leaders, suggested the border is an arbitrary line, and repeatedly proposed the nation be annexed as the 51st state. He said he was willing to inflict “economic pain” on Canada to get what he wanted, and recently said the country only “lives because of the United States.”
川普不僅用關稅打擊加拿大，還侮辱其領導人，暗示邊境線是一條主觀劃定的線，並多次提議將加拿大吞併為美國第51個州。他曾表示願意對加拿大施加「經濟痛苦」以得到他想要的東西，最近還稱加拿大「之所以存在，全靠美國」。
In a speech in Davos, Switzerland, in January, Mr. Carney said that the United States was no longer a guarantor of stability, and that middle powers like Canada must unite to survive. The speech, and its enthusiastic global reception, angered Mr. Trump.
今年1月，卡尼在瑞士達佛斯的一次演講中表示美國不再是穩定的保障者，並稱像加拿大這樣的中等強國必須團結起來才能生存。這場演講及其在全球的熱烈反響激怒了川普。
Also, on July 1, Canada’s national holiday, the Trump administration declined to renew the United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement on trade for another 16 years. That means the agreement will now undergo annual reviews.
此外，在7月1日加拿大國慶日，川普政府拒絕將美加墨貿易協定再續期16年。這意味著該協定現在將接受年度審查。
廣告
Asked how the new U.S. tariffs will affect the agreement’s continued renewal, Mr. Carney said, in French, that “it certainly isn’t good news.”
當被問及美國的新關稅將如何影響該協定的持續續簽時，卡尼用法語回答說：「這肯定不是好消息。」
