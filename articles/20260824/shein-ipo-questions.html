Shein能「開啟第二春」嗎？

Shein Was Fast Fashion’s Next Big Thing. Now It’s Fighting for a Second Act.

黃瑞黎 • 2026-08-24
Shein was once one of the most talked-about companies in retail.
希音(Shein)曾是零售業最具話題性的公司之一。
It could spot what Generation Z wanted and, within days, turn those trends into $10 mesh dresses and $5 crop tops. Its clothes were so cheap and its churn of new styles was so relentless that #Sheinhaul became a phenomenon on TikTok. Shein overtook bigger rivals like Zara and H&M, started planning an initial public offering and, at one point, was valued at nearly $100 billion.
它能夠敏銳地捕捉到Z世代的需求，並在幾天內將這些趨勢轉化為10美元的網眼連衣裙和五美元的露臍上衣。它的衣服極其便宜，新潮款式的上新速度之快讓人目不暇接，以至於#Sheinhaul（Shein購物分享）成了TikTok上的一種現象。Shein超越了Zara和H&M等規模更大的競爭對手，開始籌備首次公開募股，估值一度接近1000億美元。
Now the buzz has faded.
如今，這種熱度已經褪去。
After years of delay, Shein is close to going public — this time at a fraction of its peak valuation. It is searching for new ways to grow after the United States and Europe dismantled tariff exemptions on cheap goods that had helped underpin its low-cost model. Sales have fallen sharply in the United States, its largest market. And the company has offered investors few specifics about where meaningful new growth will come from.
經過多年的推遲，希音即將上市——但這一次的估值僅為其巔峰時期的一小部分。在美國和歐洲取消了曾經支撐其低成本模式的廉價商品關稅豁免後，該公司正在尋找新的增長途徑。在其最大市場美國，銷售額已大幅下降。而且，對於未來實質性的新增長點將從何而來，該公司幾乎沒有向投資者提供任何具體細節。 「對於一家被炒作了這麼久的公司來說，熱潮似乎已經消退，」獨立電商分析師尤奧扎斯·卡齊烏克納斯表示。
廣告
“For a company as hyped as it was for such a long time, the music has seemingly run out,” said Juozas Kaziukenas, an independent e-commerce analyst.
在走向全球這方面，沒有任何一個中國時尚品牌能像希音這樣成功。然而，這家公司卻異常低調隱秘。
No Chinese fashion brand has been as successful as Shein at going global. And yet the company remains unusually reclusive.
其創始人兼首席執行官許仰天極為低調，直到今年2月他公開露面之前，網上流傳的絕大多數據稱是他本人的照片都是假的。甚至一位因未獲授權接受媒體採訪而要求匿名的高管表示，他從未見過許仰天，也沒有與他有過任何接觸。
Its founder and chief executive, Sky Xu Yangtian, is so private that until he appeared publicly in February, most of the photographs circulating online purporting to show him were false. Even a senior overseas Shein executive, who spoke on the condition of anonymity because he was not authorized to speak with the media, said he had never met or interacted with Mr. Xu.
希音拒絕了採訪請求。其上市的時間和定價細節尚未公布，但該公司上個月已向香港證券交易所提交了招股說明書。
Shein declined requests for an interview. The timing and pricing details of its market debut have not been announced, but the company filed a prospectus with the Hong Kong Stock Exchange last month.
希音煞費苦心地與自己的中國背景保持距離。2019年，它將總部設立在新加坡，並在幾年後註銷了其最初的中國企業實體。這一舉措的部分目的在於規避中國對尋求海外上市公司的規定，並為在紐約首次公開募股掃清障礙。
Shein has taken pains to distance itself from its Chinese origins. In 2019, it established its headquarters in Singapore and de-registered its original Chinese corporate entity a few years later. The aim was, in part, to navigate Chinese rules governing companies seeking offshore listings and clear a path toward an I.P.O. in New York.
這一策略失敗了。在美國，希音因其供應鏈和勞工做法遭遇了兩黨立法者的密切審查。在2024年放棄紐約上市的努力後，它轉向了倫敦，在那裡同樣遭到了非政府組織和其他抗議者的抵制。
The strategy failed. In the United States, Shein encountered intense, bipartisan scrutiny from lawmakers over its supply chain and labor practices. After abandoning its New York listing effort in 2024, it turned to London, where it faced similar resistance from nongovernmental organizations and other protesters.
這使得香港成為最後一個主要的可行上市地點。但分析人士表示，經過多年的耽擱，希音可能已經錯失了最佳時機。
That left Hong Kong as the last major viable venue. But after years of delay, analysts say Shein may have missed its moment.
轉向香港也讓希音陷入了尷尬的境地，不得不重新擁抱它曾經試圖淡化的中國身份。在中國，該公司面臨著「中國開花，國外結果」的批評——意思是希音在中國打下了成功的基礎，卻將利潤轉移到了海外。
The turn toward Hong Kong has also put Shein in the awkward position of having to re-embrace the Chinese identity it once tried to play down. In China, the company has faced criticism for “blooming in China but bearing fruit abroad” — a phrase suggesting that Shein built its success in the country while redirecting profits overseas.
隨後在2月，素以低調著稱的許仰天突然現身廣東省政府官員面前。
廣告
Then, in February, the famously elusive Mr. Xu suddenly appeared before officials in Guangdong Province.
「廣東是希音的根，」他宣稱，並宣布希音將在未來三年內投資100億元，在廣東建立一個供應鏈樞紐。
“Guangdong is Shein’s root,” he declared, announcing that Shein would invest about $1.4 billion over the next three years to build a supply chain hub there.
中國媒體最初報導了這次講話。隨後，一些文章開始從互聯網上消失。資深記者呂明合發布了一份通知，顯示希音以「侵權」為由，要求他的微信公眾號刪除一篇關於許仰天露面的文章。
Chinese media initially covered the speech. Then some articles began disappearing from the internet. Lu Minghe, a veteran journalist, posted a notice showing that Shein had demanded that his public WeChat account remove an article about Mr. Xu’s appearance, citing “infringement.”
儘管希音正在調整與中國的關係，一個更根本的問題仍然籠罩著它的任何潛在上市計劃。
Even as Shein recalibrates its relationship with China, a more fundamental problem hangs over any potential listing.
密切關注希音的諮詢公司墨騰創投創始人李江玕表示，潛在投資者告訴他，他們幾乎感覺不到希音創始人的熱情。
Jianggan Li, founder of Momentum Works, a consultancy that has followed Shein closely, said prospective investors had told him that they sensed little enthusiasm from its founders.
「他們沒有做出任何努力來激發人們的興趣，」李江玕說。「除了投資者想要退出這一事實之外，我並沒有感覺到管理層有強烈的讓公司上市的意願。」
“There hasn’t been any effort to drum up interest,” Mr. Li said. “I don’t feel there’s a strong desire for the management to make the company public, aside from the fact that the investors want to exit.”
投資者們都在問，希音如何才能保持增長。
廣告
Investors are asking how Shein can keep growing.
川普總統去年決定終止一項允許數十億美元低成本進口商品免稅進入美國的政策，這給希音最大的單一市場帶來了沉重打擊。2025年5月，希音開始在美國提高價格。該公司表示，在2026年第一季度，其美國凈營收下降了14.3%，至20億美元。
President Trump’s decision last year to end a policy that had allowed billions of dollars of low-cost imports to enter the United States duty-free dealt a major blow in its single biggest market. In May 2025, Shein began raising prices in the United States. In the first quarter of 2026, U.S. net revenue fell 14.3 percent to $2 billion, the company said.
歐洲可能帶來更大的挑戰。上個月，歐洲當局宣布對以前免稅入境的電商包裹徵收三歐元的統一費用。
Europe could pose an even greater challenge. Last month, European authorities announced a 3-euro flat fee on e-commerce parcels that had previously entered duty-free.
歐洲占希音營收的三分之一以上。希音在其招股說明書中警告稱，儘管現在全面評估歐洲局勢的影響還為時過早，但它「可能與在美國觀察到的影響基本一致，甚至超過美國」。
Europe accounts for more than a third of Shein’s revenue. In its prospectus, Shein warned that although it was too early to assess the impact of the situation in Europe fully, it “could be generally in line with or exceed the impact observed in the U.S.”
希音現在正試圖向投資者證明，其未來的發展不僅限於超低價服裝，還將出售其構建的系統，來實現產品的快速設計、生產、營銷和交付。
Shein is now trying to convince investors that its future goes beyond ultracheap clothes by selling access to the system it built to design, produce, market and deliver products quickly.
到目前為止，幾乎沒有證據表明這一供應鏈策略正在取得成效。自2023年推出該服務試點以來，希音僅吸引了約20個品牌加入。據該公司稱，該項目在2025年產生的凈營收不到其總凈營收的1%。
So far, there is little evidence that the supply chain strategy is paying off. Since offering the service as a pilot in 2023, Shein has signed up only about 20 brands. The program generated less than 1 percent of its total net revenue in 2025, according to the company.
北京投資銀行香頌資本董事沈萌表示，希音之所以成功，部分原因是它願意壓縮自己的成本和利潤空間——而使用其平台的其他品牌可能不願接受這種妥協。
Shen Meng, a director at Chanson & Co., an investment banking firm in Beijing, said Shein succeeded in part because it was willing to squeeze its own costs and margins — trade-offs that other brands using its platform may be unwilling to accept.
「我覺得它向供應鏈管理去轉型的話，很難能夠看出來他的那種突出的優勢，」沈萌說。
“It’s hard to see any outstanding advantages in Shein’s transition,” Mr. Shen said.
希音還將其業務拓展至傢具、化妝品、寵物用品和電子產品。在第一季度，非服飾類商品占其營收的三分之一以上。
Shein is also branching out into furniture, cosmetics, pet products and electronics. Nonapparel goods accounted for more than a third of its revenue in the first quarter.
其他分析師同樣對希音能否「開啟第二春」持懷疑態度。
廣告
Other analysts are similarly skeptical that Shein has found a second act.
研究機構Forrester的電商專家蘇查裡塔·科達利表示：「除了非常便宜的超快時尚之外，目前尚不清楚他們還能創造什麼。」
“It’s not clear, other than really cheap, ultrafast fashion, what they’re going to be able to generate,” said Sucharita Kodali, an e-commerce expert at Forrester, a research agency.
希音的第一階段發展令人矚目。
Shein’s first act was remarkable.
該公司由許仰天及其三位同事於2012年創立，當時他們供職於一家幫助中國出口商在線銷售產品的營銷公司。2015年，公司名稱從Sheinside簡化為Shein。
It was founded in 2012 by Mr. Xu and three colleagues at a marketing firm that helped Chinese exporters sell products online. In 2015, it shortened its name from Sheinside to Shein.
在新冠大流行期間，隨著網購的爆髮式增長，希音的人氣也急劇攀升。該公司在互聯網上投放了大量針對年輕消費者的廣告，以極低的價格和不斷變化的款式吸引他們。
During the Covid-19 pandemic, as online shopping exploded, Shein’s popularity surged. The company saturated the internet with advertisements aimed at young consumers, enticing them with extremely low prices and constantly changing designs.
到了2022年，希音成為全球最大的在線時尚零售商。那一年，其估值達到982億美元，並得到了一批知名風險投資機構的支持。
By 2022, Shein became the world’s biggest online fashion retailer. That year, it was valued at $98.2 billion and backed by a roster of big-name venture capital investors.
其秘訣在於建立了一個壓縮傳統時尚週期的系統。希音利用算法追蹤新興趨勢，然後下幾百件的初始訂單來測試需求。這種模式使其能夠每天推出約4700款新服裝款式。相比之下，據分析師稱，Zara一年生產約兩萬款服裝。
廣告
The secret was a system built to compress the traditional fashion cycle. Shein used algorithms to track emerging trends, then placed initial orders of a few hundred units to test demand. The model allowed it to introduce approximately 4,700 new apparel styles a day. Zara, by comparison, produces 20,000 a year, according to analysts.
希音將這項技術與集中在廣東的產業生態系統結合起來，那裡的紡紗工、織布工、裁縫和小工廠可以迅速生產小到100件的訂單。
Shein paired that technology with an industrial ecosystem concentrated in Guangdong, where spinners, weavers, seamstresses and small factories could rapidly manufacture orders as small as 100 pieces.
但據墨騰創投的李江玕稱，希音一直難以在土耳其和越南等國家複製其中國供應鏈模式。與此同時，Temu這樣的競爭對手帶著更豐富的商品品類和更低的價格入場了。
But Shein has struggled to duplicate its Chinese supply chain model in countries such as Turkey and Vietnam, according to Mr. Li of Momentum Works. At the same time, competitors like Temu arrived with even broader product assortments and even lower prices.
卡齊烏克納斯表示，他仔細研讀了希音的招股說明書，試圖找到自己這些年來一直想知道的答案：「希音的未來是什麼？」他還說：「我認為我們沒有得到那個答案。」
