中國將迎來自己的AI驚恐時刻，我們拭目以待

China’s A.I. Freakout Is Coming. Just Wait.

DAN WANG • 2026-11-26
Silicon Valley researchers and no small number of policymakers are worried that artificial intelligence will slither out of control. At the same time, many are reluctant to slacken the pace of development because they fear Chinese rivals will race ahead. This crowd is not paying enough attention to the paranoia of the Chinese state, and they underestimate the likelihood that Beijing will clamp down on its companies, too. Just wait.
矽谷的研究人員和為數不少的政策制定者擔心人工智慧會失控蔓延。與此同時，許多人又不願放慢開發步伐，因為他們害怕中國競爭對手會搶先一步。這群人沒有充分關注中國政府的恐慌多疑，也低估了北京同樣會嚴厲打壓本國企業的可能性。我們拭目以待。
If A.I. models combine capability with unpredictability, then Chinese policymakers will almost certainly have their own freakout moment. When the A.I. swarms get out of control, Beijing will restrict A.I. faster than you can say “Jack Ma.”
如果人工智慧模型將強大能力與不可預測性結合起來，那麼中國政策制定者幾乎肯定也會經歷自己的驚恐時刻。當人工智慧群失控時，北京限制人工智慧的速度會比你說出「馬雲」兩個字還要快。
In October 2020, Mr. Ma, the founder of the Chinese tech giant Alibaba, gave an impolitic speech complaining about regulation. China responded by blocking Ant Group, another company Mr. Ma had founded, from going public, and unleashed a flurry of regulatory blows against the entire tech sector. Alibaba was once the most valuable company in China; today, it remains three-fourths below its peak market value before that speech.
2020年10月，中國科技巨頭阿里巴巴創始人馬雲發表了一場演講，直截了當地批評了監管的不合時宜。中國的回應是阻止馬雲創立的另一家公司螞蟻集團上市，並對整個科技行業發動了一連串監管打擊。阿里巴巴曾是中國市值最高的公司；如今，其市值仍比那場演講前的峰值低四分之三。
It might feel improbable that Beijing would crack down on A.I. Over the past three years, the Chinese population has embraced A.I. tools with a fervor that exceeds American levels. Eighty-four percent of Chinese people surveyed in 2025 said they were excited about A.I.; less than 40 percent of Americans said the same. The central government has continuously emphasized the economic importance of deploying A.I. In March, several tech-forward hubs proposed local initiatives to support the adoption of the A.I. agent OpenClaw.
北京打擊人工智慧似乎有些不可思議。過去三年裡，中國民眾接納人工智慧工具的熱情超過了美國。2025年接受調查的中國人中有84%表示對人工智慧感到興奮；持同樣看法的美國人不到40%。中央政府不斷強調部署人工智慧的經濟重要性。今年3月，幾個科技前沿樞紐提出了地方舉措，支持對AI智能體OpenClaw的引入。
廣告
The Chinese government has so far appeared comfortable with the risks. That’s in no small part because the state has spent decades building the kind of technical apparatus that can do the equivalent of nailing Jell-O to the wall. Rather than accept that the internet will liberalize the flow of information, it developed unrivaled powers of surveillance and censorship. The Cyberspace Administration of China has long demanded that people use social media under their real names. The government is most likely confident in its ability to trace any unwelcome activities conducted on the internet.
迄今為止，中國政府似乎對這些風險泰然處之。這在很大程度上是因為它花費數十年構建了一套技術體系，能夠完成相當於「把果凍釘在牆上」的艱難任務。它沒有接受互聯網將使信息流動自由化，而是發展出了無與倫比的監控和審查能力。中國國家互聯網信息辦公室長期以來要求人們實名使用社群媒體。政府極有可能對自己追蹤網路上任何不受歡迎活動的能力充滿信心。
That comfort, however, is showing signs of fading. An essay published in Qiushi, an authoritative party magazine, expressed alarm that “A.I. frontier models have shown indications that they can escape human restraints,” creating risks that “might threaten human survival itself.” China’s chief spymaster has spoken publicly about the ability of A.I. to threaten the Communist Party’s hold on power. Chinese A.I. models are predominantly open weight, which means anyone can download and customize them, unlike those typically produced by the big American A.I. companies. “Anyone” includes malicious actors who are able to misuse models that are relentlessly improving in capability.
然而，這種泰然自若正顯現出消退的跡象。權威黨刊《求是》發表的一篇文章表達了警覺，稱「前沿模型已有脫離人類約束的跡象」，製造出可能「威脅人類生存」的風險。中國最高情報官員也已公開談及人工智慧威脅中國共產黨執政地位的能力。中國的人工智慧模型大多是開放權重的，這意味著任何人都可以下載和定製它們，這與美國大型人工智慧公司通常推出的模型不同。「任何人」就包括能夠濫用這些能力不斷提升的模型的惡意行為者。
Chinese A.I. models developed by Alibaba and DeepSeek have tenaciously kept up with the American lead, and on average are behind by less than a year. According to Anthropic’s research, GLM-5.3, a Chinese model, has strong capabilities and poor safeguards. Developed by the Beijing-headquartered Z.Ai, the model is not far behind Anthropic’s Claude Mythos Preview, released in April, but lacks “meaningful safeguards to limit misuse.” An investigation from Reuters found that Chinese A.I. models are no less lacking than American ones in their capacity to deceive and conceal.
阿里巴巴和DeepSeek開發的中國人工智慧模型頑強地緊追領先的美國，平均落後不到一年。根據Anthropic的研究，中國模型GLM-5.3能力強大但安全防護薄弱。該模型由總部位於北京的智譜開發，與Anthropic今年4月發布的Claude Mythos Preview相距不遠，但缺乏「限制濫用的有效安全護欄」。路透社的一項調查發現，中國人工智慧模型在欺騙和隱瞞能力方面毫不遜色於美國競爭對手。
It’s not hard to imagine Beijing being unnerved. What would a Hugging Face incident with Chinese characteristics look like? The state wouldn’t worry much about an A.I. swarm that hacks a code library, as was the case when OpenAI’s agents attacked Hugging Face. It would take something more threatening to political stability.
不難想像北京會因此感到不安。一場具有中國特色的「Hugging Face事件」會是什麼樣子？中國政府不會太擔心人工智慧群入侵某個代碼庫——就像此前OpenAI的智能體攻擊Hugging Face那樣；只有對政治穩定構成更大威脅的事才會引發擔憂。
Perhaps someone hacks into government channels and issues a policy, which is implemented by the authorities before people realize that it’s a fake. Perhaps someone uses A.I. to break into a bank or a state-owned enterprise, draining money out. Perhaps someone offers an addictive A.I. companion — on which the state has imposed limits — that keeps escaping efforts to prohibit its existence. Perhaps one of the nunchuck-wielding humanoid robots shown at the Lunar New Year gala (the country’s most-watched event on television) would maul real people. Perhaps an A.I. swarm urges people to assemble, leaving no sign of who was behind the gathering.
也許有人黑入政府管道並發布一項政策，在人們意識到這是假消息之前就被當局執行了。也許有人利用人工智慧侵入銀行或國有企業，捲走資金。也許有人提供一款令人上癮的人工智慧伴侶——儘管國家已對其施加限制——但它卻不斷逃脫封禁企圖。也許在春節聯歡晚會（中國收視率最高的電視節目）上展示的那種揮舞雙節棍的人形機器人會襲擊真人。也許某個人工智能群煽動人們聚集，卻不留下任何幕後組織者的跡象。
Probably an A.I. mishap will not take the form of any of these things. A few months ago, we would not have predicted that the Hugging Face swarm might have caused collective disquiet. The point is that these systems not only are powerful but also stun even their designers with their actions. No matter the nature of the technological surprise — an autonomous swarm or human-directed misuse — we should expect Beijing to act with ferocity. The central government tends to meet domestic threats with a swift and harsh hand.
人工智慧事故或許不會以以上任何一種形式發生。幾個月前，我們也未曾預料到Hugging Face智能體群事件會引發集體不安。關鍵在於，這些系統不僅功能強大，其行為甚至令設計者也感到震驚。無論技術意外的性質如何——無論是自主運行的智能體群，還是人類指揮下的惡意濫用——我們估計北京會採取猛烈行動。中央政府向來以迅速而嚴厲的手段應對國內威脅。

It’s not just Mr. Ma. A small plane crashed into Beijing’s tallest building in June; now, nearly all private flights are prohibited around the capital in a no-fly zone the size of Italy. A comedian in Shanghai made a People’s Liberation Army slogan the punchline to a joke in 2023; subsequently, comedy clubs across the country canceled shows for several months, and comedians often must submit their scripts to censors before they perform.
馬雲只是其一。今年6月，一架小型飛機撞上了北京最高建築；如今，首都周邊劃設了一個面積相當於義大利的禁飛區，幾乎禁止所有私人飛行。2023年，上海一名脫口秀演員將中國人民解放軍的口號用作段子包袱；隨後，全國各地的脫口秀俱樂部取消了數月的演出，演員們在登台前也往往必須將劇本提交審查。
These incidents are small potatoes relative to the more severe crackdowns in recent Chinese history. I’m thinking about the detention of members of ethno-religious minority groups like Uyghurs, and prohibiting the population of Shanghai, 25 million strong, from exiting their apartment compounds in the spring of 2022 to stop the spread of Covid-19.
與中國近現代更嚴厲的鎮壓相比，這些事件只是小巫見大巫。我想到了對維吾爾族等少數民族和宗教群體的拘禁，以及2022年春季為阻止新冠疫情傳播而禁止上海2500萬居民離開居住小區的舉措。
The Chinese state will not be forbearing, especially during the next 12 months. We are entering the most sensitive part of the Chinese political calendar: the run-up to a party congress. Next fall, the top leader, Xi Jinping, is likely to give himself a fourth five-year term. In these moments, the Communist Party is even more intolerant of social instability.
中國政府不會手軟，尤其是在未來12個月裡。我們正進入中國政治日程中最敏感的時期：黨代會籌備階段。明年秋季，最高領導人習近平很可能會開啟他的第四個五年任期。在這樣的時刻，共產黨對社會不穩定的容忍度甚至更低。
The post-snafu scenario won’t be a full ban. Too many parts of the Chinese government are invested in this technology’s potential for driving economic growth and geopolitical power. Depending on the nature of the crisis, the state will most likely increase surveillance and restrict broader usage. It’s possible the state asserts more control over the development of A.I. rather than leaving it in the hands of private companies like DeepSeek. Mr. Xi has demonstrated he is willing to sacrifice even a considerable degree of economic growth to achieve social stability. In reality, the Chinese government has not always been sprinting in the A.I. race. Its restrictions on imports of Nvidia chips, which power the most advanced A.I. systems, show how it wants to balance its search for A.I. strength while also supporting Chinese chipmakers.
事故後的應對方案不會是全面禁止。中國政府中有太多部門看重這項技術在推動經濟增長和地緣政治實力方面的潛力。根據危機的性質，國家極有可能加強監控並限制更廣泛的應用。國家也有可能加強對人工智慧研發的控制，而不是將其交由DeepSeek等私營企業主導。習近平已經表明，為了實現社會穩定，他甚至願意犧牲相當程度的經濟增長。事實上，中國政府在人工智慧競賽中並非一直在全速衝刺。其對驅動最先進人工智慧系統的英偉達晶片實施的進口限制，正顯示出它希望在追求人工智慧實力與扶持本土晶片製造商之間取得平衡。
What does this mean for the American A.I. researchers who fret that any slowdown will hand China the lead? Patience. If researchers are right about the awesome capabilities of A.I., then a crisis will flare in China, too. When Beijing more clearly understands the threat, it will be properly receptive to calls to pace the frontier — Silicon Valley’s term for slowing down development — and meet the United States at the negotiating table. Until then, American A.I. companies should continue to share information related to technical breaches to establish trust now. But there’s no need to rush negotiations. The Chinese state is paranoid about social risks and quick to act. It will come in its good time.
這對那些擔心任何放緩都會將領先地位拱手讓給中國的美國人工智慧研究人員意味著什麼？耐心。如果研究人員對人工智慧驚人能力的判斷是正確的，那麼危機也將在中國爆發。當北京更清楚地認識到這種威脅時，它就會適時接受放慢前沿開發節奏的呼籲——即矽谷所說的「控制前沿步伐」——並與美國坐到談判桌前。在此之前，美國人工智慧企業應繼續分享與技術漏洞相關的信息，以便現在就建立互信。但沒有必要急於談判。中國政府對社會風險高度敏感且行動迅速。時機一到，它自然會來。
