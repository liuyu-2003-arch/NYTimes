77歲華人夫婦被控謀殺女婿：一場失控的家庭悲劇

The 77-Year-Old Grandparents Accused of Killing Their Son-in-Law

SOUMYA KARLAMANGLA • 2026-11-26
On a warm Saturday afternoon this fall, Jonathan McKinsey had just finished playing with his three children at a Bay Area park. He dragged a wagon past a baseball diamond and crowded playground and began to load items into the trunk of his SUV.
今年秋天一個溫暖的週六下午，喬納森·麥金西剛在灣區一個公園裡和三個孩子結束玩耍。他拉著一輛小拖車經過棒球場和擁擠的遊樂場，開始把東西裝進SUV後備箱。
Then a bullet struck the back of his head, prosecutors say. Within 40 seconds, at least 17 more rounds were fired at Mr. McKinsey.
檢察官說，隨後一顆子彈擊中了他的後腦勺。在40秒內，至少又有17發子彈射向麥金西。
It was shocking enough that Mr. McKinsey was shot repeatedly in a park filled with children in Dublin, Calif., a Bay Area suburb popular among tech workers.
麥金西在加利福尼亞州都柏林市一座滿是兒童的公園裡遭到多次槍擊，本身就已經令人震驚。都柏林是舊金山灣區一座深受科技行業從業者青睞的郊區城市。
But the killing, on Sept. 26, reverberated far beyond Northern California after the authorities said the assailants were Mr. McKinsey’s 77-year-old in-laws. And headlines all over the world noted Mr. McKinsey’s role as director of engineering for the department at The New York Times that produces Wordle and other games.
而在當局稱行兇者是麥金西77歲的岳父母后，這起發生在9月26日的凶殺案引起的震動遠遠超出了北加州。世界各地的新聞報導紛紛提到，麥金西是《紐約時報》負責開發Wordle等遊戲的部門的工程總監。
廣告
Prosecutors say that the in-laws, Shouyong Zhang and Shili Chen, jointly carried out a murder plot amid contentious divorce proceedings and a custody battle between Mr. McKinsey and their daughter, Candice Jang. Mr. McKinsey had been married to Ms. Jang for 15 years and had three children with her before they were recently separated.
檢方稱，在麥金西與妻子坎迪斯·張陷入激烈的離婚訴訟和子女監護權爭奪戰之際，女方父母張守勇（音）和陳詩麗（音）共同實施了這起謀殺。麥金西與坎迪斯·張結婚15年，育有三個孩子，兩人不久前分居。
Defense lawyers have not disputed that the couple shot Mr. McKinsey, but say that they did so out of fear for their safety as well as the safety of their daughter and grandchildren. They cite child abuse allegations against Mr. McKinsey as well as the ongoing family court proceedings.
辯護律師沒有否認這對夫婦槍殺了麥金西，但表示他們這樣做是出於對自己以及女兒和孫輩安全的擔憂。律師援引了有關麥金西虐待兒童的指控，以及仍在進行中的家事法庭訴訟。
“The larger court record is replete with facts supporting both self-defense and defense of others,” Amy Cheng, a deputy public defender who represents Mr. Zhang, said in a recent filing.
「從更廣泛的法庭記錄來看，有大量事實可以為自衛和保護他人免受侵害的辯護主張提供依據，」代理張守勇的公設辯護人辦公室的副辯護律師艾米·程在最近提交的一份法庭文件中說。
The details of the allegations are laid out in court documents that have been unsealed or filed in recent weeks. Mr. Zhang, Ms. Chen and their lawyers could not be reached for comment. The Alameda County District Attorney’s office, which is prosecuting the case, said it would not comment on an ongoing case.
有關指控的細節見於近幾週解封或提交的法庭文件。記者未能聯繫到張守勇、陳詩麗及其律師置評。負責此案起訴工作的阿拉米達縣地方檢察官辦公室表示，不會對仍在審理中的案件發表評論。
Both Mr. Zhang and Ms. Chen have pleaded not guilty and are being held without bail. They are expected to return to court on Monday for a pretrial hearing.
張守勇和陳詩麗均不認罪，目前處於羈押中，不得保釋。他們預計週一返回法庭參加審前聽證。
Prosecutors say that they believe Mr. Zhang and Ms. Chen planned the shooting, and the murder charge the couple faces includes a special circumstance of “lying in wait.”
檢方表示，他們認為張守勇和陳詩麗策划了這起槍擊案，兩人面臨的謀殺指控還包含「埋伏行兇」這一特殊情節。
Mr. Zhang typically got around by bicycle, prosecutors say, but he rented a car the day before the shooting. His wife of nearly half a century, Ms. Chen, was behind the wheel and wearing a wig as the couple drove to the park. They brought with them two loaded pistols, according to the documents.
檢察官說，張守勇通常騎單車出行，但他在槍擊前一天租了一輛車。與他結婚近半個世紀的妻子陳詩麗戴著假髮開車，夫婦倆前往公園。根據文件，他們帶了兩把裝滿子彈的手槍。
廣告
The couple, prosecutors say, waited at the park for several minutes until Mr. McKinsey handed off the three children to their daughter, who drove off.
檢察官說，這對夫婦在公園等了數分鐘，直到麥金西把三個孩子交給他們的女兒，女兒開車離開。
Then, just after 3 p.m. in the Dublin Sports Grounds parking lot, Mr. Zhang shot Mr. McKinsey once in the back of the head, causing him to fall to the ground, police say. Mr. Zhang continued to fire at him while he lay motionless, according to footage captured by a school camera across the street that was cited by prosecutors and defense lawyers.
警方說，下午3點剛過，在都柏林體育場停車場，張守勇朝麥金西後腦勺開了一槍，導致其倒地。檢察官和辯護律師援引了街對面一所學校攝像頭拍下的錄像，畫面顯示張守勇繼續朝一動不動躺在地上的麥金西開槍。
Seconds later, Ms. Chen left the vehicle and fired a second gun several times at Mr. McKinsey, who remained on the ground, prosecutors say. Mr. Zhang fired at him again, and the couple stared down at Mr. McKinsey, prosecutors said.
檢察官說，幾秒鐘後，陳詩麗下車，用第二把槍朝仍躺在地上的麥金西開了數槍。檢察官說，張守勇再次向他開槍，隨後這對夫婦低頭盯著麥金西。
After the shooting, detectives found four photo albums in the Dublin apartment where Mr. Zhang and Ms. Chen live. Mr. McKinsey’s face was cut out of some of the pictures. On one photo, there were black ink marks on his chest and over his right eye, the very places he was later shot, prosecutors say.
槍擊發生後，警方在張守勇和陳詩麗居住的都柏林公寓裡發現了四本相冊。其中一些照片上，麥金西的臉被剪掉了。檢察官說，有一張照片上，他的胸部和右眼上方有黑色墨水痕跡，正是他後來中槍的部位。
After his arrest, Mr. Zhang was interviewed by the police, through a Mandarin interpreter. According to court documents, Mr. Zhang told the police that he had been thinking about killing Mr. McKinsey all day and that he had rented the car so he could drive to a gun range and practice shooting.
被捕後，張守勇通過普通話翻譯接受了警方訊問。根據法庭文件，張守勇告訴警方，案發當天，他一直有殺死麥金西的念頭，租車是為了能開車去射擊場練習射擊。
Mr. Zhang, a retired metallurgist, immigrated to Albuquerque from China in 1985. He was joined three years later by his wife and daughter. In 2016, he and his wife relocated to Northern California to be closer to their daughter and her family.
張守勇是一名退休冶金學家，1985年從中國移民到阿爾伯克基。三年後，妻子和女兒與他團聚。2016年，他和妻子搬到北加州，以便離女兒一家更近一些。
廣告
Years later, at the time of the shooting, Mr. Zhang believed that Mr. McKinsey had a “dark soul” and had treated his grandchildren poorly, including physical abuse, he told the police, according to court documents. He also believed his son-in-law had wanted to harm him.
根據法庭文件，張守勇告訴警方，多年後發生這起槍擊案時，他認為麥金西「內心陰暗」，一直對自己的外孫不好，甚至對他們實施身體虐待。他還認為女婿曾有意傷害自己。
Mr. McKinsey and his wife, Ms. Jang, 46, had each accused each other of verbal, physical and psychological abuse, according to previous court records. Mr. McKinsey was a transgender man and had accused Ms. Jang and his in-laws of abusing him because of his identity.
此前的法庭記錄顯示，麥金西與46歲的妻子坎迪斯·張曾相互指控對方實施言語、身體和心理虐待。麥金西是一名跨性別男性，他曾指控坎迪斯·張及其父母因他的性別身份而虐待他。
Katharine Tinto, a clinical law professor at the University of California, Irvine, said the outcome of the case could hinge on the defendants’ state of mind during the killing.
加州大學歐文分校臨床法學教授凱瑟琳·廷托表示，案件結果可能取決於被告在殺人時的心理狀態。
Mr. Zhang was diagnosed with memory loss last year, his lawyers said. Ms. Chen’s lawyer said that Mr. Zhang was “in the grips of serious mental deterioration” and becoming increasingly paranoid about Mr. McKinsey.
律師說，張守勇去年被診斷出存在記憶力減退的問題。陳詩麗的律師說，張守勇「嚴重的精神機能衰退」，對麥金西的猜疑也日益嚴重。
Those factors or a self-defense argument could throw into question the proof of advanced planning required for a murder conviction, Ms. Tinto said.
廷托說，這些因素或被告提出的正當防衛主張，都可能使檢方用於證明被告事先預謀的證據受到質疑，而證明存在預謀是判定謀殺罪成立的必要條件。
“That’s what the heart of this case seems to be: how culpable are these parents, and what was their intention, what was their mental state when they did these acts?” she said.
「這似乎正是本案的核心問題：這對父母應承擔多大的罪責？他們的意圖是什麼？實施這些行為時，他們的心理狀態又是怎樣的？」她說。 
廣告
Mr. Zhang told detectives that he told his wife to drive them to the park and to bring a gun, but that she did not know anything more. They did not discuss killing Mr. McKinsey, Mr. Zhang told detectives.
張守勇告訴警方，他讓妻子開車帶他們去公園並帶上一把槍，但她不知道更多情況。他告訴警方，他們沒有討論殺死麥金西。
Ms. Chen’s attorney, Curtis Briggs, wrote in a recent court filing that Ms. Chen could not see clearly what was happening from the driver’s seat of the car when Mr. Zhang was shooting at Mr. McKinsey. She saw what appeared to be her husband doubled over in pain clutching his abdomen, and rushed out to protect him from what she feared was an assault from a “violent and dangerous Mr. McKinsey,” Mr. Briggs said.
陳詩麗的律師柯蒂斯·布裡格斯在最近提交的一份法庭文件中寫道，張守勇向麥金西開槍時，陳詩麗從駕駛座上看不清發生了什麼。布裡格斯說，她看到丈夫似乎正捂著腹部，痛苦地彎著腰，擔心他遭到了「暴力、危險的麥金西」的襲擊，於是衝出去保護他。
But prosecutors argue that Ms. Chen knew of her husband’s plan because she brought a firearm and wore a black wig to cover her normally silver hair.
但檢察官認為，陳詩麗知道丈夫的計劃，因為她帶了槍，還戴了黑色假髮遮住白髮。
It remains unclear what, if anything, Ms. Jang knew about their plans. Investigators have searched her home, but she has not been charged with a crime. She declined to be interviewed when reached by phone.
目前尚不清楚坎迪斯·張是否知道父母的計劃，以及如果知情其了解程度如何。調查人員已經搜查了她的住所，但她尚未受到任何刑事指控。記者通過電話聯繫到她時，她拒絕接受採訪。
On a recorded jail-line call between Ms. Jang and Ms. Chen, Ms. Chen asked her daughter to return the rental car so that it didn’t accumulate late fees, according to Mr. Briggs’ court filings.
根據布裡格斯提交的法庭文件，在母女通過羈押地點的電話系統進行的一次有錄音記錄的通話中，陳詩麗讓女兒歸還租來的車，以免產生滯納金。
She told her repeatedly to take good care of the children. And, according to the filings, she apologized for “all the stress I’m causing you.”
她一再叮囑女兒要照顧好孩子。根據法庭文件，她還因為「給你造成了這麼多壓力」向女兒道歉。
