《毛澤東私人醫生回憶錄》合著者石文安逝世

Anne Thurston Has Died. She Revealed Salacious Details of Mao’s Life.

MAX BEARAK • 2026-11-26
Anne F. Thurston, an American political scientist and expert on China who co-wrote a best-selling book with Mao Zedong’s longtime personal physician that revealed intimate — and often bawdy — details about the dictator’s personal life, died on Oct. 2 in Alexandria, Va. She was 83.
美國政治學家、中國問題專家石文安(Anne F. Thurston)於10月2日在維吉尼亞州亞歷山大市逝世，享年83歲。她曾與毛澤東的長期私人醫生合著了一本暢銷書，披露了這位獨裁者私人生活中私密且往往頗為露骨的細節。
The death, at a senior-care facility, was from complications of dementia, said Adam Nelson, a longtime friend and colleague.
她的長期好友兼同事亞當·納爾遜表示，她在一家老年護理機構因失智症併發症去世。
“The Private Life of Chairman Mao,” published in 1994 and co-written with Dr. Li Zhisui, was Dr. Thurston’s sole best seller, but she wrote, co-wrote or edited numerous books that, despite being academic in tone, were acclaimed studies of the individual experiences of Chinese people.
1994年出版、與李志綏醫生合著的《毛澤東私人醫生回憶錄》(The Private Life of Chairman Mao)是石文安博士唯一的暢銷書，但她還撰寫、合著並編輯了許多書籍，儘管這些書籍的風格偏向學術，卻是對中國人個體經歷的廣受讚譽的研究。
She often focused on dissidents of one kind or another and how their lives were upended during the transformations of Chinese society in the latter half of the 20th century, beginning with Mao’s decade-long campaign of persecution known as the Cultural Revolution.
她經常關注形形色色的異見人士，以及他們在20世紀下半葉中國社會變革期間的生活是如何被顛覆的，而這一切都始於毛澤東發起的、持續十年的迫害運動，即文化大革命。
David Lampton, the former director of the China program at the Johns Hopkins School of Advanced International Studies in Washington, remembered choosing Dr. Thurston in 1999 as one of his first hires.
華盛頓約翰斯·霍普金斯大學高級國際研究學院中國研究項目前主任戴維·蘭普頓回憶，1999年，他將石文安選為自己最早聘用的人員之一。
“Anne was one of the greatest and most credible contributors to our understanding of China,” he said. “Hers was a unique mixture of empathy and understanding about why China behaved the way it did, but it never slid into being an apologist. Her sympathy was with people on China’s periphery.”
「在理解中國方面，安妮是我們最傑出、最可信的貢獻者之一，」他說。「她身上具備一種獨特的共情與理解，明白中國為何會那樣行事，但她從未滑向辯護者。她的同情心始終向著中國社會邊緣的人群。」
廣告
Dr. Thurston was one of the first American political scientists to be granted research access to China during the thaw in U.S.-China relations after Mao’s death in 1976. After an initial visit three years later, Dr. Thurston spent much of her career going back and forth to China.
1976年毛澤東逝世後，美中關係解凍，石文安是首批獲准前往中國開展研究訪問的美國政治學者之一。三年後，石文安首次訪問中國，此後她職業生涯的大部分時間都在中美兩國之間往返。
In a personal statement she wrote while working as a researcher at the Wilson Center in Washington, one of the think tanks, universities and other institutions with which she was affiliated over the decades, she said her mission was “to listen to Chinese people tell their stories — and then to relate those stories here in the West in a way that makes sense to both us and the storytellers themselves.”
數十年來，她曾任職於多家智庫、大學及其他機構，華盛頓的威爾遜中心便是其中之一。在該中心擔任研究員期間撰寫的一份個人聲明中，她說自己的使命是「傾聽中國人講述他們的故事，然後以一種既能讓我們理解、又能讓講述者自己認同的方式，將這些故事傳達給西方世界」。
Her trip to China in 1979 was a “turning point,” she wrote. The accounts of the Cultural Revolution relayed to her by Chinese counterparts in political science programs were “at such odds with the official, printed accounts,” she recalled, that she resolved to center her first published book on extensive field work with Chinese intellectuals.
她寫道，1979年的中國之行是一個「轉折點」。她回憶說，政治學項目中的中國同行向她轉述的文革經歷「與官方出版的說法大相逕庭」，這讓她下定決心，將自己的第一本出版著作建立在對中國知識分子的廣泛實地調查之上。
Dozens of interviews, with private citizens and Chinese officials, resulted in what she considered her favorite of all her books, “Enemies of the People: The Ordeal of the Intellectuals in China’s Great Cultural Revolution” (1987).
對普通公民和中國官員進行的數十次採訪，最終匯聚成了她在自己所有著作中最喜愛的一本書——《文革中的知識分子》（Enemies of the People: The Ordeal of the Intellectuals in China’s Great Cultural Revolution，1987年）。
Writing in The New York Times, the Sinologist Merle Goldman called the work “an important book because the victims believe that the more the outside world (as well as the Chinese) knows about what happened in the Cultural Revolution, the less likely it is that such a phenomenon may reoccur.” The Times included the volume on its list of the year’s notable books.
漢學家谷梅(Merle Goldman)在《紐約時報》撰文稱，該作品是「一本重要的書，因為受害者們相信，外界（以及中國人自己）對文化大革命中所發生的事情了解得越多，這種現象重演的可能性就越小」。《紐約時報》將該書列入了當年的年度最值得關注好書榜單。
But it was Dr. Thurston’s third book, “The Private Life of Chairman Mao,” that received worldwide attention. It was a collaboration with Mao’s personal physician, Dr. Li, who kept journals while he cared for Mao for more than two decades, burned them because he feared retribution during the Cultural Revolution, and then recreated them from memory for the book.
但引起全球關注的是石文安的第三本書《毛澤東私人醫生回憶錄》。這本書是她與毛澤東的私人醫生李志綏的合作成果；李志綏在為毛澤東服務的二十多年間一直記日記，在文革期間因害怕遭到報復而將其燒毀，後來又憑記憶重構了這些內容供該書使用。
廣告
She conducted extensive interviews with Dr. Li, who began living in the United States in 1988, followed by separate research to confirm details, and she basically wrote the book.
她對自1988年起定居美國的李志綏進行了廣泛採訪，隨後又開展了獨立研究以核實細節，整本書基本上是由她執筆寫成的。
“The Private Life of Chairman Mao” was deemed so newsworthy that, soon before its official publication day, it merited a front-page article in The Times, although the reporter credited Dr. Thurston only once, in passing, as Dr. Li’s “editorial assistant.”
《毛澤東私人醫生回憶錄》被認為極具新聞價值，以至於在正式出版日前不久，《紐約時報》用頭版文章對其進行報導，儘管記者在文中僅順帶提及了石文安一次，稱她為李志綏的「編輯助理」。
The Times review that followed, by the historian Marilyn B. Young of New York University, credited her correctly as the co-author and recounted the book’s descriptions of “Mao’s appetite for young women and fatty pork, his addiction to sleeping pills, his adherence to esoteric Taoist sexual practices, his reluctance to bathe or brush his teeth.”
隨後，紐約大學歷史學家瑪麗蓮·B·楊在時報發表書評，正確地將石文安列為合著者，並概述了書中「毛澤東對年輕女性和紅燒肉的嗜好、對安眠藥的依賴、對秘傳道教房中術的信奉，以及他不願洗澡或刷牙」的描寫。
The most intimate portrait of Mao ever released, it became an international best seller and was denounced by the Chinese government as slander. The authorities took issue with Dr. Li’s characterizations of how Mao’s tyrannical attitude led to disastrous policy choices, but the salacious details garnered much of the public’s attention.
作為迄今最為私密的毛澤東傳記，該書成為了國際暢銷書，受到中國政府的譴責，稱其為誹謗。中國當局對李志綏關於毛澤東專橫暴虐的態度如何導致災難性政策抉擇的論述提出異議，但吸引公眾大部分注意力的卻是那些桃色細節。
Mao's sprawling sex life had been alluded to in other works, but the stories Dr. Li recounted in his book with Dr. Thurston spoke to a closeness to the man that others simply didn’t have.
雖然其他作品也曾隱晦提及毛澤東無度的性生活，但李志綏與石文安合著的書所中講述的故事體現出了與毛澤東本人的近距離接觸，是其他人根本不具備的。
Dr. Li recalled, for instance, how Mao had repeatedly sent him young women with symptoms of the protozoan parasite Trichomonas vaginalis, which is sexually transmitted. “The young women were proud to be infected,” he and Dr. Thurston wrote. “The illness, transmitted by Mao, was a badge of honor, testimony to their close relations with the Chairman.”
例如，李志綏回憶，毛澤東曾多次將出現陰道滴蟲病（一種通過性傳播的原蟲寄生蟲）感染癥狀的年輕女性送到他那裡看病。「這些年輕女性以被傳染為榮，」他與石文安寫道。「這種由毛澤東傳染的疾病是一種榮譽勳章，證明了她們與主席的親密關係。」
Anne Elizabeth Fritter, the older of two sisters, was born on July 16, 1943, in Manhattan. Her father was Lee W. Fritter, an Army colonel, and her mother was Aileene (O’Dea) Fritter.
石文安於1943年7月16日出生在曼哈頓，是家中兩姐妹中的長女。她的父親是陸軍上校李·W·弗裡特，母親是艾琳·（奧迪）·弗裡特。
廣告
She graduated in 1965 from Tufts University with a bachelor’s degree in government and received a master’s degree (1966) and a doctorate (1974), both in political science from the University of California, Berkeley.
她於1965年畢業於塔夫茨大學，獲政府學學士學位，隨後於1966年和1974年分別在加州大學柏克萊分校獲得政治學碩士學位和博士學位。
Her marriages to Thom B. Thurston and Patrick G. Maddox ended in divorce. She is survived by a sister, Mary Fritter of Germantown, Md.
她與湯姆·B·瑟斯頓和派翠克·G·馬多克斯的兩段婚姻均以離婚告終。她的遺屬是居住在馬里蘭州日耳曼敦的妹妹瑪麗·弗裡特。
Later in her career, Dr. Thurston’s attention shifted to Tibet. For years, she directed the Grassroots China Initiative at the School of Advanced International Studies, often taking groups of researchers and students to the Tibetan plateau. Beginning in 2012, Beijing refused her a visa, and she never returned to China.
在職業生涯後期，石文安的注意力轉向了西藏。多年來，她在高級國際研究學院主持「草根中國計劃」，經常帶領研究人員和學生團隊前往青藏高原。從2012年起，北京拒絕向她發放簽證，她再也沒有回到過中國。
In a tribute to her, Yawei Liu, the senior adviser on China to the Carter Center, another Washington think tank Dr. Thurston was affiliated with, said she was a “bridge between scholars and the public long before the concept of the public intellectual was coined.”
華盛頓另一家與石文安博士有關聯的智庫卡特中心的中國項目高級顧問劉亞偉在悼詞中表示，「她是學者與公眾之間的橋樑——在『公共知識分子’這個概念被發明出來之前，她早就已經是了」。
Moreover, he wrote, she exuded a sense of style and “no matter where she was or where she was going, she always dressed elegantly,” even in rural areas of China.
此外，他寫道，她風度優雅，「無論身在何處、去往何方，她總是衣著考究」，即便是在中國的農村地區也是如此。
Her final book, “The Noodle Maker of Kalimpong: the Dalai Lama’s Brother and His Struggle For Tibet” (2015), was co-written with Gyalo Thondup, the 14th Dalai Lama’s elder brother. It documented the story of Mr. Thondup’s decades as his family’s secret diplomatic middleman between officials in China, India and the United States.
她的最後一部著作是與第十四世達賴喇嘛的二哥嘉樂頓珠合著的《噶倫堡的制面師：達賴喇嘛二哥回憶錄·不為人知的圖博奮鬥故事》（The Noodle Maker of Kalimpong: the Dalai Lama’s Brother and His Struggle For Tibet，2015年）。該書記錄了嘉樂頓珠數十年來作為家族祕密外交中間人周旋於中國、印度和美國官員之間的故事。
