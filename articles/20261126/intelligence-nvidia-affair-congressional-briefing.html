美國官員向國會通報英偉達相關情報

Officials Brief Congressional Staff on Intelligence Surrounding Nvidia Affair

JULIAN E. BARNES, MAGGIE HABERMAN, JONATHAN SWAN • 2026-11-26
A day after President Trump publicly proclaimed that Jensen Huang was not a national security threat or facing criminal prosecution, U.S. officials provided a tempered account of the intelligence at the heart of the Nvidia scandal in a briefing for Congress on Tuesday, people told about the briefing said.
據知情人士透露，在川普總統公開宣稱黃仁勛不構成國家安全威脅，也不會面臨刑事起訴的次日，美國官員在週二為國會舉行的簡報會上對英偉達醜聞核心的情報做出了較為克制的說明。
The briefing, for staff members of the House and Senate intelligence committees and aides to the leaders of both chambers, outlined in broad terms some of what intelligence agencies have collected on Alice Huang, the Chinese woman whose affair with the Nvidia chief is at the center of the episode.
此次簡報會面向眾議院和參議院情報委員會的工作人員以及兩院領導人的助手，大體概述了情報機構收集到的有關黃樂(Alice Huang)的部分信息。這名中國女子與這位英偉達首席執行官的婚外情是此次事件的核心。
The U.S. intelligence officials told the aides to the lawmakers that they still do not fully understand the role played by Ms. Huang, who helped to run a Singapore-based data center firm, Megaspeed, that is suspected of diverting advanced Nvidia artificial intelligence chips to China.
美國情報官員告訴議員助手們，他們仍未完全弄清黃樂所扮演的角色。黃樂曾協助運營一家總部位於新加坡的數據中心公司Megaspeed，該公司涉嫌將先進的英偉達人工智慧晶片轉移到中國。
The briefers, who included officials from the F.B.I., C.I.A. and the Office of the Director of National Intelligence, explained the contacts between Ms. Huang and China’s Ministry of State Security, according to people briefed on the meeting. The intelligence officials told the group there was no evidence she was directed by that agency to initiate an affair with Mr. Huang, though they did not dispute that she passed intelligence back to the agency after the affair began.
據聽取了會議簡報的人士稱，包括來自聯邦調查局、中央情報局和國家情報總監辦公室官員在內的簡報人員詳細說明了黃樂與中國國家安全部之間的聯繫。情報官員告訴與會人員，沒有證據表明她是受該機構指使才與黃仁勛發生婚外情的，但他們並未否認她在婚外情開始後向該機構傳遞了情報。
廣告
The briefers told the congressional staffers that the man who was often introduced as Ms. Huang’s husband, Kang Wei, was a more significant figure. He had been, at least at times, an M.S.S. asset and had more significant contacts with the Chinese spy agency, the briefers said.
簡報人員告訴國會工作人員，經常被介紹為黃樂丈夫的男子康偉是一個更重要的人物。簡報人員稱，他至少在某些時候曾是中國國安部的線人，並且與這家中國間諜機構有著更密切的聯繫。
The briefing did not include the most sensitive intelligence that the U.S. has collected about Ms. Huang, nor did it include a highly classified March 30 memo overseen by Marco Rubio, the secretary of state and national security adviser. That memo described Mr. Huang’s relationship with Ms. Huang, and hers with Chinese intelligence services, according to people briefed on it.
此次簡報並未包含美國收集到的有關黃樂的最敏感信息，也沒有包括一份由國務卿兼國家安全顧問魯比奧監督起草、於3月30日發布的高度機密備忘錄。據知情人士透露，該備忘錄描述了黃仁勛與黃樂的關係，以及黃樂與中國情報部門的關係。
The briefers, in a two-hour session, provided the senior congressional staff members with sensitive material that had been recently collected and with the information provided to senior intelligence officials, according to multiple people briefed of the session.
據多名聽取了會議情況介紹的人士稱，在長達兩小時的會議中，簡報人員向國會高級幕僚提供了近期收集的敏感材料，以及提供給高級情報官員的信息。
The congressional intelligence committees have requested a copy of the Rubio memo.
國會情報委員會已要求獲取魯比奧備忘錄的副本。
The New York Times reported last month that the U.S. government had collected intelligence showing that Mr. Huang, an ally of Mr. Trump, was having a close relationship with Ms. Huang, who U.S. officials believed was an asset of Chinese intelligence services.
《紐約時報》上個月報導稱，美國政府收集到的情報顯示，川普盟友黃仁勛與黃樂關係密切，而美國官員認為黃樂是中國情報部門的線人。
The Times reported on Sunday that people briefed on the U.S. intelligence said Ms. Huang was in contact with the Chinese spy agency from the spring of 2025 until the fall of this year, covering at least part of the period when she was having the affair with Mr. Huang. The Times also reported that Mr. Kang was in contact with the spy agency, as well.
《紐約時報》週日報導稱，知情人士透露，黃樂從2025年春季到今年秋季一直與中國間諜機構保持聯繫，這至少涵蓋了她與黃仁勛保持婚外情的部分時期。《紐約時報》還報導稱，康偉也與該間諜機構有聯繫。
廣告
Soon after The Times first reported last month on Ms. Huang’s links to Chinese intelligence, Mr. Trump publicly suggested that he was unsure whether Ms. Huang was a spy, and declared Mr. Huang a good man and a genius deserving of support from the government. Over this past weekend, the president set in motion a drive by the White House to reinstate Mr. Huang as Nvidia’s chief executive, reversing the Nvidia board’s decision to force his resignation.
上月《紐約時報》首次報導黃樂與中國情報部門的聯繫後不久，川普公開表示，他不確定黃樂是否是間諜，並宣稱黃仁勛是個好人、是個天才，值得政府支持。在剛剛過去的這個週末，總統啟動了一項由白宮主導的行動，旨在恢復黃仁勛作為英偉達首席執行官的職務，從而推翻了英偉達董事會迫使其辭職的決定。
The company’s announcement Monday that Mr. Huang would return was quickly followed by a social media statement from Mr. Trump in which the president asserted that the Nvidia chief was not a security threat and that he was not a target of any criminal prosecution. Mr. Trump made those statements even though he was briefed at least twice about Ms. Huang’s ties to Chinese intelligence, including once by Mr. Rubio, The Times has reported, and even though the Justice Department investigation into the diversion of chips to China is still in an early stage.
該公司週一宣布黃仁勛將回歸後，川普緊接著在社群媒體上發表聲明，堅稱這位英偉達首席執行官不構成安全威脅，也不是任何刑事起訴的目標。據《紐約時報》報導，儘管川普至少聽取過兩次有關黃樂與中國情報部門聯繫的簡報（其中一次由魯比奧進行），且司法部對晶片轉移至中國的調查仍處於早期階段，但他仍發表了上述言論。
The briefing on Tuesday for congressional aides continued, behind closed doors, the public pattern by the administration of seeking to minimize any damage to Mr. Huang and Nvidia from the episode.
週二為國會助手舉行的閉門簡報會延續了政府此前公開表現的模式，即力圖將此次事件對黃仁勛和英偉達造成的損害降至最低。
On Tuesday, Attorney General Todd Blanche followed up on Mr. Trump’s statements, saying there was no evidence that Nvidia was anything other than “a good corporate citizen” and repeating that there was no evidence of wrongdoing by Mr. Huang.
週二，司法部長托德·布蘭奇呼應了川普的聲明，稱沒有證據表明英偉達不是「一家優秀的企業公民」，並重申沒有證據表明黃仁勛有任何不當行為。
In an interview with the New York Post on Tuesday, Mr. Trump said Mr. Huang had nothing to hide and that he should not invoke his Fifth Amendment right against self-incrimination during a scheduled hearing before Congress next week. Asked about issuing a pardon for Mr. Huang, Mr. Trump replied, “It’s always an option, I guess, but I don’t think it’ll be necessary.”
在週二接受《紐約郵報》採訪時，川普表示，黃仁勛沒有什麼可隱瞞的，在下週國會舉行的聽證會上，他不應援引憲法第五修正案賦予的不自證其罪的權利。當被問及是否會赦免黃仁勛時，川普回答說：「我想這始終是一個選項，但我認為沒有必要。」
Megaspeed, the data center company that employed Ms. Huang, is under investigation by the Justice Department for diverting advanced Nvidia chips to China to evade U.S. export controls. The Trump administration on Monday placed Megaspeed on a trading blacklist, meaning that U.S. companies cannot ship technology to Megaspeed without obtaining a license.
僱傭黃樂的數據中心公司Megaspeed目前正因涉嫌將先進的英偉達晶片轉移至中國以規避美國出口管制而接受司法部的調查。川普政府週一將Megaspeed列入貿易黑名單，這意味著美國公司在未獲得許可證的情況下不能向Megaspeed出口技術產品。
廣告
In restoring Mr. Huang to the chief executive role on Monday, Nvidia agreed to establish a chief compliance officer, in effect acknowledging that it needs to do more to police where its chips end up.
在週一恢復黃仁勛首席執行官職務的同時，英偉達同意設立首席合規官一職，這實際上承認了該公司需要採取更多措施來監管其晶片的最終流向。
In recent years the M.S.S., China’s top spy agency, has prioritized trying to break what Beijing calls an American “blockade” that is restricting their access to the most advanced computer chips.
近年來，中國最高間諜機構國家安全部將突破北京所稱的美國「封鎖」作為優先任務，該「封鎖」限制了中國獲取最先進計算機晶片的管道。
The spy agency has worked to help Chinese companies acquire and smuggle advanced chips into China. The M.S.S., according to U.S., European and Asian national security officials, also collects intelligence about the semiconductor sector and its supply chains.
該間諜機構一直致力於協助中國企業獲取先進晶片並將其走私到中國。據美國、歐洲和亞洲的國家安全官員稱，中國國安部還收集有關半導體行業及其供應鏈的情報。
