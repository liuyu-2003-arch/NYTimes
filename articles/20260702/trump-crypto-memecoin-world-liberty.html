川普靠加密貨幣狂賺14億美元，投資者損失慘重

Crypto Brought Trump a Huge Windfall, Even as Many Investors Lost Big

ERIC LIPTON, ANDREA FULLER, DAVID YAFFE-BELLANY • 2026-07-02
A large chunk of the $2 billion haul President Donald Trump took in last year came as hundreds of thousands of his fans and other investors bet on a speculative cryptocurrency called $TRUMP, hoping its value would soar with his return to the White House.
去年，川普總統獲得了20億美元的巨額收益，其中很大一部分來自數十萬支持者和其他投資者對名為$TRUMP的投機性加密貨幣的押注，他們希望這種貨幣的價值能隨著川普重返白宮而飆升。
But while Trump amassed an eye-popping $636 million from the cryptocurrency, known as a memecoin, many of his followers who heeded his call to purchase the coin came out losers.
然而，當川普從這種被稱為「迷因幣」(memecoin)的加密貨幣中獲得了高達6.36億美元的驚人收益，許多聽從他的號召購買該貨幣的追隨者卻成了輸家。
That outcome, documented by an independent analysis of trades and fees paid out from $TRUMP token sales, is drawing renewed attention this week, as Trump for the first time has detailed the extraordinary $1.4 billion in revenue he secured just from the cryptocurrency industry since he returned to the White House.
這一結果由對$TRUMP代幣的銷售交易和支付費用的獨立分析所證實。本週，隨著川普首次詳細披露自重返白宮以來從加密貨幣行業獲得的14億美元驚人收入，這一結果再次引起了人們的關注。
The president’s 927-page financial disclosure showed how Trump and his family reaped huge financial rewards in 2025 through his money-losing Trump Media venture and a separate cryptocurrency firm called World Liberty Financial, even as routine investors suffered vast losses.
總統長達927頁的財務披露顯示，2025年，儘管普通投資者遭受了巨大損失，川普及其家人仍通過其虧損的川普傳媒企業和一家名為世界自由金融的獨立加密貨幣公司獲得了巨額財務回報。
廣告
He also amassed hundreds of millions through deals that involved foreign governments or corporations with agenda items pending before the Trump administration.
他還通過涉及外國政府或企業的交易積累了數億美元，而這些交易涉及的議題當時正由川普政府審議。
On Wednesday, Trump dismissed questions about how much money he had made after returning to the White House, suggesting that he left personal financial decisions related to his investments to others.
週三，川普對關於他重返白宮後賺了多少錢的質疑不予理會，表示他把與投資相關的個人財務決策交由他人處理。
“I don’t know if I had a better career in politics or business,” Trump said as he was about to board his new Air Force One jet donated by the government of Qatar with his two oldest sons, Eric Trump and Donald Trump Jr., looking on. “But I had a great career in business. And you saw the cash.”
「我不知道自己政界還是商界的職業生涯更好，」川普在準備登上卡達政府捐贈的新「空軍一號」專機時說，他的兩個年長的兒子埃里克·川普和小唐納德·川普在一旁看著。「但在商界，我確實有過輝煌的職業生涯。你們也看到了那些錢。」
The memecoin, which features an image of Trump pumping his fist the way he did after a 2024 assassination attempt, has no intrinsic value itself. Instead, it was a bet on the aura around Trump and the idea that the coin’s fortunes would rise with his presidency.
這種迷因幣上有川普在2024年遭遇暗殺未遂後揮舞拳頭的形象，其本身並沒有內在價值。相反，它更像是對川普個人光環的押注，賭的是這種代幣價值會隨著他的總統任期而水漲船高。
In a way, Trump’s cryptocurrency windfall is a reflection of the speculative nature of the nascent industry, in which executives behind these often highly volatile ventures are at times able to generate huge profits at the expense of smaller investors, who often lose vast sums on experimental coins.
在某種程度上，川普從加密貨幣中獲得的意外之財反映了這個新興行業的投機性質。在這些通常會劇烈波動的項目中，幕後高管有時能夠以犧牲小投資者為代價賺取巨額利潤，小投資者則常因投資實驗性代幣而損失慘重。
Former federal financial regulators said Trump has taken that to a new extreme, structuring his crypto ventures so he always made money on the front end, according to disclosures from the companies, no matter what happened to the business in the long run.
前聯邦金融監管機構人員表示，根據這些公司的披露，川普將這種做法推向了一個新的極端：他構建自己的加密貨幣項目架構，確保無論項目長期發展如何，自己總能在先期獲利。
“It is hard to wrap your head around that the president of the United States would engage in this level of self-enrichment at the expense of so many of his supporters,” said Lee Reiners, a former Federal Reserve Bank examiner who now studies cryptocurrency issues at Duke University. “This is a president of the United States who has made more money off crypto since he took office than he made in any prior year in his entire business career.”
「美國總統竟然會以犧牲這麼多支持者為代價，中飽私囊到這個程度，實在令人難以理解，」曾在聯邦儲備銀行擔任審查員、現於杜克大學研究加密貨幣問題的李·雷納斯說。「這位美國總統上任以來靠加密貨幣賺到的錢比他在整個商業生涯中任何一年賺的錢都多。」
廣告
The White House, in a statement Wednesday, rejected the suggestion that Trump is exploiting his followers.
白宮在週三的一份聲明中駁斥了川普正在利用其追隨者的說法。
“All actions by President Trump and his administration are taken in the best interest of the American people,” Anna Kelly, a White House spokesperson, said in the statement. “Neither the president nor his family has ever engaged — or will ever engage — in conflicts of interest.”
「川普總統及其政府的所有行動都是為了美國人民的最大利益，」白宮發言人安娜·凱利在聲明中說。「無論是總統還是他的家人，過去從未——將來也絕不會——捲入利益衝突。」
As of the middle of last year, according to Chainalysis, a crypto analytics firm, 58 investors in the $TRUMP coin had made profits in excess of $10 million, totaling an estimated $1.1 billion — most of them early traders who got out before the coin crashed in value. At the same time, some 764,000 crypto wallets, most with small holdings, lost money on $TRUMP, the Chainalysis data shows.
加密貨幣分析公司Chainalysis的數據顯示，截至去年年中，$TRUMP幣的58名投資者已經獲得了超過1000萬美元的利潤，總計約11億美元，他們當中大多數是早期交易者，在該代幣暴跌前就已經套現離場。與此同時，Chainalysis的數據顯示，大約76.4萬個加密貨幣錢包（大部分持有量較小）在$TRUMP上虧損。
But every time $TRUMP was traded, the president and his partners collected transaction fees, which along with other revenues from the coin totaled hundreds of millions of dollars, his financial disclosure shows.
但總統的財務披露顯示，每當$TRUMP被交易一次，總統及其合作夥伴都會收取交易手續費，這與該代幣的其他收入加起來，總計達數億美元。
Trump’s annual report shows that he earned an additional $799 million from World Liberty, including a large share when the government of the United Arab Emirates secretly purchased a stake in the company, just as Trump was returning to the White House.
川普的年度報告顯示，他從世界自由金融額外賺取了7.99億美元，其中包括阿聯酋政府在川普重返白宮之際祕密購買該公司股份時他所分得的一大筆收益。
Meanwhile, the price of $WLFI, a cryptocurrency coin created by World Liberty Financial, has sunk to less than 6 cents, a more than 80% drop from its peak, generating enormous losses for investors who bought in at a higher price.
與此同時，由世界自由金融創建的加密貨幣$WLFI的價格已跌至不到6美分，較峰值下跌了80%以上，給高價買入的投資者造成了巨大損失。
廣告
That decline did not matter much for Trump: A Trump entity received a 75% cut of all sales of $WLFI, according to a disclosure by the company, allowing him to profit from the coin long before its price cratered.
這種下跌對川普來說影響不大：根據該公司的披露，一家川普旗下的實體從$WLFI的所有銷售中抽取了75%的份額，這使他能夠在該代幣價格暴跌之前很久就從中獲利。
While he was accumulating this new wealth, Trump’s administration was taking steps that allowed his crypto ventures to raise their profits. Early last year, the Securities and Exchange Commission announced that it would not regulate memecoins as a security. And Trump promoted legislation he signed into law expanding the use of stablecoins in the United States, a few months after World Liberty issued its own such coin.
在他積累這些新財富的同時，川普政府也在採取措施，為其加密貨幣業務創造更多利潤空間。去年年初，美國證券交易委員會宣布，將不會把迷因幣作為證券進行監管。此外，在世界自由金融發行了自己的穩定幣數月後，川普推動並簽署了一項立法，擴大了穩定幣在美國的適用範圍。
(Stablecoins are a form of cryptocurrency that are supposed to maintain a lock on a $1 value and then are used as a way to store crypto investment funds or buy goods or services. Memecoins are considered collectibles that buyers speculate might increase in value.)
（穩定幣是一種加密貨幣，設計初衷是將其價值鎖定在一美元，通常被用作存儲加密投資資金或購買商品及服務的方式。迷因幣則被認為是收藏品，買家投機猜測其價值可能會上升。）
“I find it sad that these investors have no protection from the SEC — the agency has gleefully abandoned them,” said John Reed Stark, the former chief of the SEC office that investigated online investment fraud. “It is all about a scam that is as old as securities markets themselves — and it is called a pump-and-dump scheme that enriches a few at the expense of the masses.”
「我痛心的是，這些投資者得不到證券交易委員會的保護——這個機構已經興高采烈地拋棄了他們，」曾任證券交易委員會網路投資欺詐調查辦公室主任約翰·里德·斯塔克說。「這完全是一個與證券市場本身一樣古老的騙局——也就是所謂的『拉高出貨』騙局，以犧牲大眾為代價讓少數人致富。」
The agency did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
證券交易委員會沒有立即回應置評請求。
Trump has not shown signs that he is discouraged by the financial losses of his investors. Instead, he has looked for ways to temporarily increase the price of his cryptocurrency ventures through special promotions.
川普並沒有表現出因其投資者遭受財務損失而沮喪的跡象，而是一直想方設法通過特別促銷活動來暫時提高其加密貨幣項目的價格。
廣告
Last year, for example, he held a dinner at his Virginia golf club for the top buyers of his memecoin, sparking a bidding war that attracted investors from around the world, temporarily pushing up the price.
例如，去年，他在自己位於維吉尼亞州的高爾夫俱樂部為他的迷因幣主要買家舉辦了一場晚宴，引發了一場吸引全球投資者的競價戰，從而暫時推高了該幣價格。
A similar event at Mar-a-Lago this year generated much less investment, with the price of $TRUMP hovering around $2.83, about an 80% decline from a year earlier.
今年在馬阿拉歌莊園舉辦的一場類似活動吸引到的投資少得多，$TRUMP的價格徘徊在2.83美元左右，較一年前下跌了約80%。
The coin is “completely dead,” said Morten Christensen, an investor who attended the $TRUMP event at Mar-a-Lago. “Nobody cares about a meme that’s down 97%.”
參加過馬阿拉歌莊園$TRUMP活動的投資者莫滕·克里斯滕森說，該代幣已經「徹底死透了」。「沒有人會去在乎一個暴跌了97%的迷因幣。」
Even some of the president’s staunchest supporters in the crypto world have grown disillusioned.
就連加密貨幣界中一些總統最堅定的支持者們也逐漸感到幻。。
In April, crypto mogul Justin Sun, who spent tens of millions of dollars on $WLFI and $TRUMP, sued World Liberty, claiming that the company had illegally prevented him from selling his stash in order to prop up the price. (World Liberty denied the claims.)
4月，曾在$WLFI和$TRUMP上投入數千萬美元的加密貨幣大亨孫宇晨起訴了世界自由金融，聲稱該公司為了支撐價格而非法阻止他出售自己持有的代幣。（世界自由金融否認了這些指控。）
