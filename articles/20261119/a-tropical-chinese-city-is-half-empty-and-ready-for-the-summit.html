APE峰會期間，深圳變「空城」

A tropical Chinese city is half-empty and ready for the summit.

KEITH BRADSHER • 2026-11-19
World leaders coming to Shenzhen this week are arriving in an almost spotless, ultramodern metropolis, its streets bedecked with pastel banners but mostly emptied of the crowds typical of many Chinese cities.
本週來到深圳的世界領導人們看到的是一座幾乎一塵不染的超現代化大都市，街道上點綴著色彩柔和的橫幅，但基本上看不到許多中國城市特有的人潮。
Shenzhen lies slightly closer to the Equator than Havana, providing a balmy venue for the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation gathering. Under sunny skies with mild temperatures, a festive mood prevails in the city, which bears a strong resemblance to Miami — only much larger and with taller, newer high-rises.
比哈瓦那更稍靠近赤道的深圳為亞太經濟合作組織會議提供了一個宜人的舉辦地。在晴朗的天空與溫和的氣溫下，節慶氛圍在城市中瀰漫，這座城市與邁阿密極其相似——只不過規模大得多，且擁有更高、更新的高層建築。
Schools in Shenzhen have closed this week, many residents have left town and local authorities have instructed companies to allow those employees who are still here to work from home. The result has been a holiday atmosphere of sorts in a city with a tech sector known for its punishing “996” work schedules: 9 a.m. to 9 p.m., six days a week.
深圳的學校本週已經停課，許多居民離開了這座城市，地方當局已指示企業允許留在這裡的員工居家辦公。這在一座以科技行業嚴酷的「996」工作制——早9點至晚9點，每週六天——聞名的城市中營造出了一種近似假日的氛圍。
Youthful volunteers — mainly students at local universities — stand on street corners wearing bright red vests, having been offered academic credit in exchange for being trained as guides. Thousands of gawkers have been promenading past the large conference center that will host heads of state from across the Asia Pacific region, taking photos of themselves in front of the flower gardens inside the fence.
年輕的志願者（主要是當地大學的學生）身穿鮮紅色的馬甲站在街角，他們通過接受嚮導培訓來換取學分。成千上萬的旁觀者在將接待來自亞太地區各國元首的大型會議中心外漫步，並在圍欄內的花園前拍照留念。
Among the crowds walking outside the high steel fences around the conference center was a local resident who had brought her parents and her dog. The resident said that workers had been planting flowers for a year ahead of the summit, and added that a community worker had given her a big package of napkins as a gift after she agreed not to drive her car in the coming week. Shenzhen has halved fares this week on its extensive subway system to help clear the roads for summit buses.
在會議中心周圍高聳的鋼製圍欄外漫步的人群中，有一位與父母和寵物狗一同前來的當地居民。她表示，在峰會舉行前，工人們已經在此種植了一年的花卉，她還說，在同意接下來的一週不開車後，一名社區工作者贈送了她一大包紙巾作為禮物。深圳本週將其四通八達的地鐵系統的票價減半，以協助為峰會巴士保持道路暢通。
At the same time, however, residents are aware of occasional hassles. “Many friends who live around here plan to travel outside the city because of the inconvenience of transportation — many roads will be closed to traffic,” said the resident, who gave only her family name, Zhang. As is common at high-profile and high-security events in China, she was wary of giving her full name, which can be risky for Chinese citizens.
然而與此同時，居民們也意識到了偶爾的不便。「許多住在這附近的朋友計劃去城外旅遊，因為交通不便——許多道路將封閉，」這位僅透露自己姓張的居民說。正如在中國高規格且安保嚴密的活動中常見的那樣，她對提供全名保持警惕，因為這對中國公民來說可能存在風險。
Few dare to express any misgivings, however, accepting the disruption with resignation. “Even if we’re unhappy about it, we can’t really say so,” said a resident surnamed Wang. “The government wouldn’t welcome our dissatisfaction.”
然而，幾乎沒有人敢表達任何疑慮，只能無奈地接受這種干擾。「即使我們對此感到不悅，也不能直說，」一位姓王的居民表示。「政府不喜歡聽到我們的不滿。」
While gatherings of world leaders always prompt extensive security, China’s efforts in Shenzhen are particularly intensive. Surveillance cameras are ubiquitous along streets in China and not noticeably more common this week in Shenzhen, but there are other, unobtrusive signs of the elaborate security effort underway.
儘管世界領導人的聚會總會引發大規模的安保行動，但中國在深圳的舉措尤為嚴密。監控攝像頭在中國的街道上無處不在，本週在深圳並未明顯增多，但仍有其他不易察覺的跡象表明，精心布置的安保工作正在進行中。
Xiangmei Road, located near the main summit venue and lined with some of China’s costliest condominium towers, was much quieter than usual on Monday. A cleaner outside one of the towers who gave only her family name, Li, said that the family employing her has simply left town this week.
位於峰會主會場附近的香梅路兩旁林立著中國一些最昂貴的公寓大樓，週一，這裡比平時安靜了許多。其中一棟大樓外一名僅透露自己姓李的清潔工表示，僱傭她的家庭本週索性離開了這座城市。
Police have been stationed in apartments on upper floors facing the conference center, drones have been prohibited and the remaining residents have received social media messages urging them to support the gathering, Ms. Li said.
李女士說，警方已經進駐了面向會議中心的高層公寓內，無人機已被禁止使用，而留下的居民則收到了敦促他們支持此次聚會的社群媒體信息。

“This place is usually very crowded,” she said, “but look how quiet it is now.”
「這個地方平時很多人的，」她說，「但看看現在有多安靜。」
