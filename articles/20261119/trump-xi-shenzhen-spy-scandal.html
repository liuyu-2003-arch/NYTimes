川普抵達深圳參加峰會

Trump Arrives in China Focused on Friendship, Not Spy Scandal

PETER BAKER • 2026-11-19
President Trump was welcomed to China on Wednesday with a warm embrace both figuratively and literally, as he arrived for a fraught visit shadowed by a major spy scandal that he seemed determined to ignore.
川普總統週三抵達中國時受到了熱情歡迎，不僅有象徵性的友好姿態，還有實實在在的擁抱。然而，一樁重大間諜醜聞給此次訪問蒙上陰影，令此行平添波折，而川普似乎決意對此視而不見。 
Upon landing in Shenzhen, a bustling technology center, for a series of meetings, Mr. Trump was greeted at the airport by President Xi Jinping of China, who hugged him on a red carpet as a colorfully dressed crowd waved flowers and flags and chanted “warm welcome” in Mandarin.
川普抵達繁華的科技中心深圳，準備展開一系列會談。中國國家主席習近平親自到機場迎接，兩人在紅地毯上擁抱。身著鮮艷服裝的歡迎人群揮舞鮮花和旗幟，用普通話高喊「熱烈歡迎」。
The visit, however, may have a see-no-evil, hear-no-evil quality because Mr. Trump has said he does not intend to raise the biggest China spy scandal of recent years, one that has upended one of America’s technology giants and pitted members of his own administration against each other. Instead, Mr. Trump prioritized a show of friendship amid the allegations of espionage.
然而，這次訪問或許帶有「視而不見、聽而不聞」的意味，因為川普已經表示，他無意提起近年來最大的中國間諜醜聞。這樁醜聞已使一家美國科技巨頭陷入動盪，並在川普政府內部引發爭執。面對間諜活動指控，川普選擇優先展示友好關係。
Mr. Trump reveled in the pageantry of the moment as he joined Mr. Xi for a banquet dinner that opened the annual meeting of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation forum, a group of 21 major economies in the region. The two leaders toasted future prosperity and looked forward to meeting separately on Thursday as well as together with President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia in a much-anticipated trilateral summit.
川普沉醉於盛大的歡迎儀式中，隨後與習近平共同出席亞太經合組織年度峰會的開幕晚宴。該組織由亞太地區21個主要經濟體組成。兩國領導人舉杯祝願未來繁榮，並期待週四舉行雙邊會晤，以及與俄羅斯總統普丁舉行備受矚目的三方峰會。
Mr. Xi’s decision to personally welcome Mr. Trump at the airport was a rare act by the Chinese leader. He did not do so when Mr. Trump visited Beijing in May. But Mr. Trump made a point of traveling to Joint Base Andrews outside Washington to greet Mr. Xi in September, an uncommon move for an American president and the first time Mr. Trump had done so for a foreign visitor.
習近平決定親自到機場迎接川普，這對這位中國領導人來說實屬罕見。今年5月川普訪問北京時，習近平並未到機場迎接。不過，9月習近平訪問美國時，川普特意前往華盛頓郊外的安德魯斯聯合基地迎接。這對於美國總統而言是不同尋常的舉動，也是川普首次親自到機場迎接外國來訪領導人。 
So, Mr. Xi returned the gesture on Wednesday. “China is a nation of etiquette with a tradition of reciprocity,” Mr. Xi said, according to Chinese state media.
因此，習近平週三也以同樣的禮遇回報川普。據中國官方媒體報導，習近平說：「中國是禮儀之邦，有禮尚往來的傳統。」
By contrast, Mr. Xi sent lower-level officials to greet Mr. Putin and other regional leaders arriving for the summit meeting, as is customary.
相較之下，習近平按照慣例，派級別較低的官員到機場迎接前來參加峰會的普丁和其他區域領導人。
廣告
Still, Mr. Xi met separately with Mr. Putin for more than two hours in advance of the three-way meeting planned for Thursday, and he hailed the Chinese-Russian relationship as “steadfast as a rock through wind and rain.”
不過，在定於週四舉行的三方會晤之前，習近平仍與普丁單獨舉行了兩個多小時的會談，並稱中俄關係「風雨不動安如山」。
The two encounters underscored Mr. Xi’s efforts to balance relations with Russia and the United States. While Russia has become a close partner in recent years and Mr. Xi and Mr. Putin have developed a bond over dozens of meetings, China has a far more extensive and important economic relationship with the United States.
習近平親自迎接川普，又與普丁舉行長時間會談，凸顯了習近平在對俄、對美關係之間尋求平衡的努力。近年來，俄羅斯已成為中國的親密夥伴，習近平與普丁也通過數十次會晤建立了密切關係，但中國與美國之間的經濟關係規模更大，重要性也遠超中俄經濟關係。
The atmosphere at the summit with other leaders was sometimes tense. Mr. Xi barely offered a smile to Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi of Japan, with whom he is in a bitter, yearlong diplomatic spat. Mr. Trump and Prime Minister Mark Carney of Canada, who have an uneasy relationship as they tussle over trade, had their first in-person meeting of the year. Days earlier, Mr. Carney angered the Trump administration by repeating that he would look elsewhere, including to China, if Mr. Trump did not lift tariffs on Canada’s auto exports.
峰會期間，各國領導人之間的氣氛有時頗為緊張。面對與自己陷入長達一年激烈外交爭端的日本首相高市早苗，習近平幾乎沒有露出笑容。川普與加拿大總理馬克·卡尼也舉行了今年首次面對面會晤。兩人因貿易爭端關係緊張。就在幾天前，卡尼再次表示，如果川普不取消對加拿大汽車出口產品徵收的關稅，加拿大就會尋求其他合作夥伴，包括中國。這番話激怒了川普政府。
Left unaddressed was the recent revelation that Jensen Huang, chief executive of Nvidia, had an extramarital affair with a business executive who U.S. intelligence agencies believe was spying for China. The executive, Alice Huang, who is not related, worked for Megaspeed, a Singapore-based data center firm and was suspected of gathering intelligence for Beijing.
然而，有一個議題始終沒有觸及：最近曝光的英偉達首席執行官黃仁勛與一名商界高管發生婚外情，而美國情報機構認為，這名高管一直在為中國從事間諜活動。這名高管名叫黃樂(Alice Huang)，曾在總部位於新加坡的數據中心公司Megaspeed任職，她被懷疑為北京收集情報。
Mr. Huang resigned under pressure and Mr. Trump’s senior advisers have been squabbling over administration policy on chip exports that could benefit China. The administration announced earlier this week new rules meant to restrict remote access to advanced chips and servers that could help develop Chinese artificial intelligence.
黃仁勛迫於壓力辭職，而川普的高級顧問們一直就政府的晶片出口政策爭吵不休，該政策可能使中國受益。本週早些時候，川普政府宣布了新規定，旨在限制通過遠程方式使用先進晶片和伺服器，以防這些技術被用於幫助中國發展人工智慧。
Scott Bessent, the treasury secretary, has blamed Howard Lutnick, the commerce secretary, for resisting restrictions on chip sales. Mr. Bessent and Mr. Lutnick are both accompanying Mr. Trump on his Asia trip, though they were seen standing apart.
財政部長史考特·貝森特指責商務部長霍華德·盧特尼克拒絕執行限制晶片銷售的措施。兩人此次都陪同川普出訪亞洲，不過有人看到兩人站得相距甚遠。
The Nvidia scandal has put Mr. Trump on the defensive because of his close relationship with Mr. Huang, who joined the president’s trip to China in May. Mr. Trump has denied being told about the matter, despite reporting by The New York Times that he was informed at least twice, including en route to China in May. He has said he does not know if Ms. Huang was a spy and urged Mr. Huang to fight for his job. Even after Mr. Huang’s resignation, Mr. Trump voiced support for his reinstatement.
英偉達醜聞使川普陷入被動，因為他與黃仁勛關係密切。今年5月，黃仁勛曾陪同川普訪問中國。川普否認有人向他通報過此事，儘管《紐約時報》報導稱，他至少兩次被告知相關情況，其中一次是在今年5月前往中國的途中。川普表示，他不確定黃樂是否為間諜，並敦促黃仁勛努力保住自己的職位。即使在黃仁勛辭職後，川普仍公開支持他復職。
This is not the first time Mr. Trump has opted not to confront Mr. Xi with allegations of Chinese interference in the sensitive U.S. matters. In a prime-time address to the nation in July, Mr. Trump accused China of hacking or purchasing U.S. voter rolls, but then said nothing about it publicly when he hosted Mr. Xi for a lavish state visit at the White House in September.
川普並非第一次選擇不就中國涉嫌干預美國敏感事務的問題與習近平對質。今年7月，川普在一次黃金時段的全國電視講話中指責中國黑入或購買了美國選民登記名冊，但到了9月，他在白宮以盛大的儀式接待前來進行國事訪問的習近平時，卻沒有公開提及此事。
廣告
Although Mr. Trump once regularly lashed out at China over its trade practices, he has become more intent over the past year on forging closer ties between the world’s two largest economies. “We have a very good relationship with China, and you don’t want to insult them,” he said before leaving the United States.
川普過去經常猛烈抨擊中國的貿易行為，但過去一年，他越來越傾向於讓世界兩大經濟體建立更緊密的關係。離開美國前，他說：「我們與中國的關係非常好，你不應該去得罪他們。」
Mr. Trump arrived in China after a stop in Manila for the annual meeting of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations, or ASEAN. He met with President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr. of the Philippines, who engaged in some fast-food diplomacy with the American president, who is known for his taste for greasy meals.
抵達中國之前，川普先在馬尼拉停留，參加東南亞國家聯盟（簡稱東盟）年度會議。他會見了菲律賓總統小費迪南德·馬科斯。馬科斯知道這位美國總統喜歡油膩的食物，於是開展了一場快餐外交。
Mr. Marcos said that he sent some fried chicken to Mr. Trump at his hotel the night before from Jollibee, a chain that is considered a national treasure in the Philippines. “Since you’re strapped for time, I thought a taste of Manila might be good,” he said. Mr. Trump did not say whether he ate the food.
馬科斯說，前一天晚上，他讓人從快樂蜂(Jollibee)給川普下榻的酒店送去了一些炸雞。這家連鎖快餐店被菲律賓人視為國民品牌。「考慮到您的時間很緊，我想讓您嘗嘗馬尼拉的味道，應該不錯，」他說。川普沒有透露自己是否吃了這些食物。
He did, however, make a point of paying a visit to Mr. Marcos’s mother, Imelda Marcos, 97, the former first lady of the Philippines, who got to know Mr. Trump at New York parties in the 1990s.
不過，川普特意去探望了馬科斯的母親、97歲的前菲律賓第一夫人伊梅爾達·馬科斯。兩人早在20世紀90年代就曾在紐約的社交聚會上結識。
“Welcome to the Philippines,” she told Mr. Trump. She handed him a gift and blew him a kiss as he walked away with her son.
「歡迎來到菲律賓，」她對川普說。她送給川普一份禮物，又在川普與她的兒子一同離開時向他送上飛吻。
“She’s always the most beautiful,” Mr. Trump said.
「她永遠是最美麗的，」川普說。
廣告
While Mr. Trump failed to sign a trade deal with Thailand that had been expected to be finalized during his stop in Manila, he announced that he did unexpectedly reach a trade agreement with the Philippines. “We didn’t know that was going to take place, but it took place very quickly and it’s a magnificent deal for both,” he said.
川普原本預計在馬尼拉停留期間與泰國敲定一項貿易協議，但最終未能簽署。不過，他宣布意外地與菲律賓達成了一項貿易協議。「我們事先並不知道會有這項協議，但很快就談成了，這對雙方來說都是一項非常棒的協議，」他說。
Mr. Marcos’s office later said the deal would cover energy and food security, without providing further details. Mr. Trump said on social media that the deal would benefit American farmers.
馬科斯的辦公室隨後表示，該協議將涉及能源和糧食安全，但沒有提供更多細節。川普則在社群媒體上表示，這項協議將使美國農民受益。
There was no such accord between Mr. Trump and Mr. Carney after their tense encounter in Shenzhen later in the day. In a statement, Mr. Carney’s office said that the two leaders “discussed the work underway to reach a mutually beneficial trade agreement” but offered no further details.
當天晚些時候，川普與卡尼在深圳舉行了氣氛緊張的會晤，但雙方並未達成類似協議。卡尼的辦公室在一份聲明中表示，兩國領導人「討論了目前為達成互利貿易協議而開展的工作」，但沒有透露更多細節。
Much attention was focused on Mr. Trump’s meeting with Mr. Xi and Mr. Putin on Thursday. The White House said Mr. Trump will also meet separately with Mr. Putin, their first get-together since a much-ballyhooed Alaska summit failed to produce an agreement to end the war in Ukraine.
外界的注意力很大程度上集中在川普週四與習近平和普丁的會晤上。白宮表示，川普還將與普丁單獨會面。自備受關注的阿拉斯加峰會未能就結束烏克蘭戰爭達成協議以來，這將是兩人的首次會晤。
Mr. Putin arrived in Asia with a show of force. Even as he landed in Shenzhen, Russian strategic nuclear bombers landed in Vietnam. It was the first time that Russian strategic nuclear bombers had deployed to Vietnam since the Soviet era and served as a reminder before the trilateral talks that Moscow remains the world’s largest nuclear power.
普丁此次亞洲之行伴隨著一番軍事力量展示。就在他抵達深圳之際，俄羅斯戰略核轟炸機也降落在越南。這是自蘇聯時代以來，俄羅斯戰略核轟炸機首次部署到越南。在三方會談舉行之前，此舉提醒各方，莫斯科仍是世界上擁有最多核武器的國家。
