習近平罕見前往機場迎接川普

In a Rare Move, Xi Greets Trump as He Lands in China

郭莉莉 • 2026-11-19
When President Trump arrived in Shenzhen on Wednesday, Xi Jinping was waiting for him at the bottom of the steps descending from Air Force One.
週三，當川普總統抵達深圳時，習近平在「空軍一號」的舷梯下等候他。
It was the first time that Mr. Xi had personally received a foreign dignitary at the airport in more than a decade as China’s leader. Visiting leaders are usually welcomed by senior Chinese officials and greeted by Mr. Xi at the Great Hall of the People in central Beijing.
這是習近平擔任中國領導人十多年來首次親自在機場迎接外國政要。來訪領導人通常由中國高級官員迎接，習近平則在北京市中心的人民大會堂接待他們。
Mr. Xi’s appearance, while unusual, was likely about reciprocating Mr. Trump’s hospitality. When Mr. Xi traveled to Washington for a state visit in September, Mr. Trump greeted the Chinese leader at Joint Base Andrews. That was the first time the U.S. president had personally greeted a foreign visitor upon their arrival.
習近平此舉雖然不同尋常，但很可能是為了回報川普的熱情款待。今年9月習近平前往華盛頓進行國事訪問時，川普在安德魯斯聯合基地迎接了這位中國領導人。那是川普總統首次在外國訪客抵達時親自前往迎接。
After Air Force One landed in Shenzhen for the last stop on his whirlwind trip to Asia, Mr. Xi rolled up to the tarmac in a black luxury sedan made by the Chinese carmaker Hongqi, or Red Flag. He walked toward the president’s plane along a red carpet flanked by young men and women cheering and waving American and Chinese flags. After Mr. Trump walked down the stairs from his plane toward Mr. Xi, the two shook hands. Mr. Trump patted Mr. Xi on the back before the two embraced.
在「空軍一號」降落在其亞洲旋風式訪問的最後一站——深圳——之後，習近平乘坐由中國汽車製造商紅旗製造的黑色豪華轎車駛入停機坪。他沿著紅地毯走向總統專機，兩側是歡呼並揮舞中美兩國國旗的年輕男女。在川普走下飛機舷梯走向習近平後，兩人握了手。川普在兩人擁抱前拍了拍習近平的背。
廣告
Wu Xinbo, the dean of the Institute of International Studies at Fudan University in Shanghai, said the leaders’ personal receptions reflected a shift in the U.S.-China relationship to focus on “respect, fairness, and reciprocity.”
上海復旦大學國際問題研究院院長吳心伯表示，領導人的親自接待反映了美中關係轉向側重於「尊重、公平與對等」。
Mr. Wu said that Mr. Xi’s presence was also likely a message of support for the American president, to show that China will “still treat him seriously and would like to continue to work with him,” after the Republican Party suffered a bruising defeat in the recent midterm elections.
此外，他認為在共和黨期中選舉慘敗後，習近平的到場迎接很可能是向美國總統傳遞支持的信息，以表明中國將「依然認真對待他，並願意繼續與他合作」。
The high-level reception accorded to Mr. Trump was a step up from the welcome given to President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia, who arrived earlier on Wednesday. Mr. Putin was greeted by Cai Qi, a longtime deputy of Mr. Xi and one of the most influential officials in the Chinese leader’s inner circle. Mr. Cai is a member of the powerful Politburo Standing Committee, the Chinese Communist Party’s top echelon.
川普得到的高級別接待比週三早些時候抵達的俄羅斯總統弗拉基米爾·V·普丁要高出一個層級。普丁由習近平的長期副手、中國領導人核心圈中最具影響力的官員之一蔡奇迎接。他是掌握實權的中共中央政治局常務委員會的成員，也就是中國共產黨的最高領導層。
When Mr. Trump visited China in May, he was greeted by China’s vice president, Han Zheng, a ceremonial figurehead who often attends formal diplomatic events. When he visited in 2017, the president was greeted by Yang Jiechi, China’s then-top diplomat and a member of the Politburo, the party’s second-most powerful body.
今年5月川普訪問中國時是由中國國家副主席韓正迎接，韓正是經常出席正式外交活動的禮節性代表人物。2017年訪華時，迎接他的是當時中國的最高外交官、中國共產黨第二大權力機構政治局的委員楊潔篪。
In 2009, when Mr. Xi was vice president and already seen as the likely successor to China’s leader at the time, Hu Jintao, Mr. Xi waited at the airport to greet President Barack Obama on his arrival in Beijing.
2009年，習近平在北京機場迎接了來訪的貝拉克·歐巴馬總統，他當時任國家副主席，且被視為時任領導人胡錦濤的可能繼任者。
