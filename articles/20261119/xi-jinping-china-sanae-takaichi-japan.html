習近平與高市早苗簡短會面，中日關係無解凍跡象

Leaders of China and Japan Meet Amid Political Feud

赫海威, KIUKO NOTOYA • 2026-11-19
The atmosphere was tense. The handshake was quick.
氣氛緊張。握手匆匆。
President Xi Jinping of China and Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi of Japan met briefly on Wednesday at the opening of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation forum in Shenzhen, China, their first encounter since the start of a bitter diplomatic feud a year ago.
週三，中國國家主席習近平與日本首相高市早苗在中國深圳舉行的亞太經濟合作組織論壇開幕式上進行了簡短會面，這是自一年前兩國爆發激烈外交爭端以來，兩人首次接觸。
Mr. Xi offered Ms. Takaichi a slight smile as she made her way to a banquet with other world leaders. He did not appear to respond when she said a few words to him, before they posed for a photo with Mr. Xi’s wife, Peng Liyuan.
當高市與其他世界領導人一同赴宴時，習近平向她微微一笑。當她向他說了幾句話時，他似乎沒有回應，隨後他們與習近平的夫人彭麗媛一起合影。
Experts said the interaction was unlikely to signify any serious thaw in relations, which have been at a low point since China reacted furiously last year to a comment Ms. Takaichi made about Taiwan.
專家表示，此次互動不太可能標誌著兩國關係出現任何實質性解凍。自去年中國對高市有關台灣的言論作出激烈反應以來，兩國關係一直處於低谷。
廣告
Tetsuo Kotani, a professor at Meikai University in Chiba Prefecture in Japan, said that Ms. Takaichi seemed “eager to make the most of this opportunity” in Shenzhen. Mr. Xi, he said, “maintained a businesslike demeanor.”
日本千葉縣明海大學教授小谷哲男表示，高市似乎「渴望充分利用在深圳的這次機會」。他說，習近平則「保持了不動聲色的態度」。
“I don’t think there’s any hope this time around for improved relations,” Mr. Kotani said.
「我認為這次沒有任何改善關係的希望，」小谷哲男說。
As the summit got underway, there were reminders of the frosty state of relations. While other world leaders received warm welcomes at the Shenzhen airport — a crowd of dancing young people with flowers greeted the South Korean leader, for example — there were no such gestures for Ms. Takaichi, according to Japanese news reports.
隨著峰會的舉行，一些跡象提醒著人們兩國關係的冷淡狀態。據日本媒體報導，雖然其他世界領導人在深圳機場受到了熱烈歡迎——例如，一群跳舞的年輕人手捧鮮花迎接韓國領導人——但高市卻沒有這樣的待遇。
The dispute began last year, when Ms. Takaichi, a longtime critic of China, told Japan’s Parliament that any attempt by Beijing to blockade or seize Taiwan could constitute a “survival-threatening situation” for Japan, implying a potential military response. Beijing considers Taiwan, a self-governed democracy, part of its territory.
爭端始於去年，當時長期批評中國的高市在日本國會表示，北京封鎖或奪取台灣的任何企圖都可能對日本構成「威脅生存的事態」，這暗示了潛在的軍事回應。北京認為實行自治民主的台灣是其領土的一部分。
China unleashed a wave of reprisals against Japan, urging tourists to avoid the country, limiting the export of critical minerals and restricting Japanese seafood imports. At the same time, Chinese officials and commentators have hardened the language they use to describe Japan, with some seeking to belittle Ms. Takaichi by omitting her title in official settings.
中國對日本發起了一波報復措施，敦促遊客避開該國，限制關鍵礦產出口和日本海鮮進口。與此同時，中國官員和評論員在描述日本時使用了更為強硬的措辭，一些人試圖通過在官方場合省略高市的頭銜來貶低她。
China’s punishments have started to squeeze Japan. The decline in tourism has hurt sales at some duty-free stores, hotels and restaurants. Japan could soon run low on supplies of some rare earths — minerals used to make a range of essential technologies, from M.R.I. machines to drones — according to analysts.
中國的懲罰措施已經開始擠壓日本。旅遊業的衰退損害了一些免稅店、酒店和餐廳的銷售。分析人士表示，日本可能很快就會面臨部分稀土供應短缺的問題——稀土是從核磁共振儀到無人機等一系列重要技術的製造所需的礦物。
廣告
Chinese officials and scholars have suggested that anything short of Ms. Takaichi retracting her comment on Taiwan would not satisfy Mr. Xi.
中國官員和學者表示，除非高市收回她關於台灣的言論，否則任何舉措都無法讓習近平滿意。
Shao Yuqun, director of the Institute for Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macau Studies at the Shanghai Institutes for International Studies, said that Mr. Xi was responding in part to intense anger among the Chinese public over Ms. Takaichi’s remark.
上海國際問題研究院台港澳研究所所長邵育群表示，習近平在一定程度上是在回應中國公眾對高市言論的強烈憤怒。
Ms. Shao added that finding a way to improve relations with Japan was “certainly not the priority” in Shenzhen.
設法改善與日本關係「肯定不是在深圳的首要任務」，她說。
Ms. Takaichi has been working to accelerate Japan’s defense buildup to counter a volatile security environment, including threats from China, North Korea and Russia.
高市一直致力於加速日本的國防建設，以應對動盪的安全環境，包括來自中國、朝鮮和俄羅斯的威脅。
There is little appetite among the Japanese public to curry favor with Beijing and Ms. Takaichi is unlikely to give in to Chinese demands to retract her remark on Taiwan. As she enters her second year in office, she enjoys relatively robust public support, and won a historic supermajority in the lower house of Japan’s bicameral Parliament in February.
日本公眾幾乎沒有討好北京的意願，高市也不太可能屈服於中國提出的收回有關台灣言論的要求。在上任第二年之際，她享有相對有力的公眾支持，並於2月在日本兩院制國會的下議院贏得了歷史性的絕對多數席位。
The meeting had echoes of an encounter in 2014, when Mr. Xi offered an icy handshake to Shinzo Abe — then Japan’s prime minister and a mentor to Ms. Takaichi — at the Great Hall of the People in Beijing during another moment of tension between the two countries. Mr. Abe was eventually able to get more time with Mr. Xi, and relations between the two countries improved somewhat.
此次會面讓人回想起2014年的一次接觸，當時正值另一個兩國關係緊張的時刻，習近平在北京人民大會堂與時任日本首相、也是高市導師的安倍晉三進行了一次冰冷的握手。安倍最終得以與習近平進行了更多時間的交流，兩國關係有所改善。
廣告
After their encounter on Wednesday, Mr. Xi and Ms. Takaichi are unlikely to have an extended meeting in Shenzhen, officials from both countries said.
兩國官員表示，在週三的接觸之後，習近平和高市不太可能在深圳舉行長時間的會談。
Ms. Takaichi is expected to face another challenge on Thursday: a potential sit-down on the sidelines of the summit with Russia’s president, Vladimir V. Putin. The meeting would take place as Mr. Putin is escalating pressure on Japan. He recently seized Russian assets of Japan Tobacco, detained a Japanese journalist and visited islands claimed by both Moscow and Tokyo.
高市預計將在週四面臨另一項挑戰：可能在峰會期間與俄羅斯總統弗拉基米爾·V·普丁舉行會談。此次會晤正值普丁加大對日本施壓力度之際。他最近沒收了日本煙草公司在俄羅斯的資產，拘留了一名日本記者，並視察了莫斯科和東京均聲稱擁有主權的島嶼。
Japan will also be watching the outcome of a first-ever trilateral gathering of the leaders of the United States, China and Russia.
日本也將密切關注美國、中國和俄羅斯領導人首次三方會議的結果。
Tobias Harris, the founder of the advisory firm Japan Foresight, said Tokyo hopes the meeting is “light on substance.”
諮詢公司日本前瞻創始人托比亞斯·哈利斯表示，東京希望這次會議「缺乏實質內容」。
“You can’t rule out the eventual possibility of some attempts at a big grand settlement that is prejudicial to the global order that Japan prefers,” he said.
「你不能排除最終可能會有人試圖達成某種宏大協議的可能性，而這種協議會對日本所偏好的全球秩序造成損害，」他說。

赫海威(Javier C. Hernández)是《紐約時報》東京分社社長，領導時報對日本及周邊地區的報導。在過去十年的大部分時間裡，他一直在亞洲進行報導，此前曾任駐華記者。
