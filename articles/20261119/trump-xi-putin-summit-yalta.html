中美俄元首會晤不會成為另一個雅爾達會議

Trump, Xi and Putin Will Sit Down for the First Time. It Won’t Be Like Yalta.

DAVID E. SANGER, PETER BAKER • 2026-11-19
The meeting Thursday between President Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir V. Putin marks the first time in history that the leaders of the United States, China and Russia — possessors of more than 90 percent of the world’s nuclear weapons — have sat down together.
川普總統、習近平和普丁週四的會晤標誌著美國、中國和俄羅斯這三個擁有全球90%以上核武器的國家的領導人歷史上首次齊聚一堂。
But not every first in history results in a significant accomplishment — or any accomplishment. And as Mr. Trump landed on late Wednesday in Shenzhen, the chip-making, telecommunications, software and robotics heart of China, it was already clear that this meeting was unlikely to live up to the many comparisons being made to Yalta, the end-of-World-War-II conference where Roosevelt, Churchill and Stalin divided up the globe.
但並非歷史上的每一個「首次」都會帶來重大成就——甚至可能一無所獲。隨著川普於週三晚些時候降落在深圳——這個中國晶片製造、電信、軟體和機器人技術的中心，情況已經很明朗，此次會晤不太可能達到人們將其與雅爾達會議相提並論的期望，後者是第二次世界大戰結束時的一次會議，羅斯福、邱吉爾和史達林曾在那裡瓜分世界。
Together, talking largely in secret, the Yalta participants laid the foundation for a postwar order that lasted nearly half a century, while leaving millions of people trapped behind the Iron Curtain.
雅爾達會議的參與者們主要進行祕密會談，共同為持續近半個世紀的戰後秩序奠定了基礎，同時也使成百上千萬民眾被困在鐵幕之後。
There are many reasons that Yalta is a poor analog when thinking about the Shenzhen summit. Yalta stretched over eight days, with a bevy of diplomats and generals working the back rooms to turn the visions discussed by the three leaders into action plans. The meeting involving Mr. Trump, Mr. Putin and Mr. Xi is set to last around an hour or so — essentially a glorified photo op, by comparison, and not one in which major issues are likely to be settled.
在思考深圳峰會時，雅爾達會議作為參照並不恰當，原因有很多。雅爾達會議長達八天，大量外交官和將軍在幕後工作，將三位領導人討論的願景轉化為行動計劃。而川普、普丁和習近平的會晤預計只持續一個小時左右——相比之下，這基本上只是一次被美化的合影活動，不太可能解決任何重大問題。
廣告
And instead of a conclave of three allies on the verge of a joint victory over a mutual foe, this meeting features three leaders who have not been partners in a great venture, and are unlikely to be any time soon.
這次會晤並不是三個聯手共同戰勝共同敵人的盟友之間的祕密會議，參與者是三位從未在重大事業上合作過、且短期內也不太可能合作的領導人。
The three men have major differences on issues as far-ranging as China’s fast-growing nuclear arsenal, the regulation of artificial intelligence, the future of space weapons, the war in Ukraine and the war in Iran — a conflict that has haunted Mr. Trump, enriched Mr. Putin and angered Mr. Xi.
這三人在廣泛的問題上存在重大分歧，包括中國快速增長的核武庫、人工智慧的監管、太空武器的未來、烏克蘭戰爭以及伊朗戰爭——這場衝突一直困擾著川普，讓普丁獲利，並激怒了習近平。
So they will not enter the room expecting that they will have time to raise, much less resolve, many critical issues.
因此，他們走進會議室時，不會指望有時間提出許多關鍵問題，更不用說解決這些問題了。
But even photo ops can linger as important symbols. And the one that may appeal to all three men is the message that they are the true global deciders, engaged in a project to shape world order.
但即使是合影機會，也可以作為重要的象徵而久久留存。而對這三位領導人而言，最能引起共鳴的或許是這樣一個信息：他們才是真正的全球決策者，正致力於塑造世界秩序。
After enduring a resounding electoral defeat this month, that image may be particularly appealing to Mr. Trump, who makes no secret of his admiration for strong leaders who do not have to put up with pesky legislatures or an intrusive press.
在本月遭遇選舉慘敗之後，這種形象可能對川普特別有吸引力，他從不掩飾自己對強權領導人的欽佩，這些領導人無需忍受煩人的立法機構或干涉過多的媒體。
For Mr. Putin, the meeting serves to help readmit him to the world community, and to recognize that he remains too powerful to ignore, even after losing half a million soldiers in a nearly five-year-long battle for Ukraine.
對普丁來說，這次會晤有助於讓他重新被國際社會接納，並讓外界承認，即便在為爭奪烏克蘭而進行的近五年戰爭中損失了50萬士兵，他依然擁有不容忽視的強大實力。
And Mr. Xi gets to play the role of global convener, albeit of a gathering with an exclusive list of participants.
而習近平則扮演了全球召集人的角色，儘管這是一場參會名單極為有限的聚會。
Among those not at the table: Japan, India, South Korea, Australia, Britain, France, Germany, Italy, Brazil, Canada, Mexico, South Africa and, perhaps most notably, Ukraine and Iran.
未出席的國家包括：日本、印度、韓國、澳洲、英國、法國、德國、義大利、巴西、加拿大、墨西哥、南非，以及也許最引人注目的烏克蘭和伊朗。

Still, Mr. Trump, Mr. Putin and Mr. Xi clearly have conflicting agendas. Mr. Trump has repeatedly said in recent days that his No. 1 priority is to take on “denuclearization,” his somewhat sloppy way of referring to arms control. Mr. Putin likely wants to talk about Ukraine, potentially figuring that Mr. Trump wants a peace deal and will not be especially picky about the details.
儘管如此，川普、普丁和習近平顯然有著相互衝突的議程。川普最近幾天一再表示，他的首要任務是推進「無核化」，這是他對軍備控制略顯草率的稱呼方式。普丁很可能想談論烏克蘭，他可能認為川普想要達成和平協議，而且不會對細節特別挑剔。
And Mr. Xi, who was wary about holding any meeting without a predetermined outcome, will be looking for ways to leverage his role in the service of becoming the world’s largest military, economic and technological power by 2049 — the 100th year of Mao’s revolution.
而習近平向來對沒有預定結果的會晤持謹慎態度，他將尋求利用自己的角色，以實現到2049年（即毛澤東革命100週年）成為世界最大的軍事、經濟和科技強國的目標。
For his part, Mr. Putin appears happy to possibly be perceived as out of the penalty box for his brutal invasion of Ukraine.
就普丁而言，他似乎很樂意讓人認為，自己因對烏克蘭的殘暴入侵而受到的制裁已告解除。
“He’s at the big boys table with the other great superpowers,” said Alina Polyakova, the president of the Center for European Policy Analysis. “For Putin, it’s credibility, it’s legitimacy. He sees himself as the tsar of an empire rather than the president of a frankly declining” country.
「他正與其他超級大國一起坐在大佬們的桌邊，」歐洲政策分析中心主席阿麗娜·波利亞科娃說。「對普丁來說，這就是信譽，這就是合法性。他將自己視為一個帝國的沙皇，而不是一個坦率地說正在衰落的國家的總統。」
Spheres of Influence
勢力範圍
Mr. Trump’s national security policy refers to an updated “Monroe Doctrine” aimed at keeping foreign powers out of the Western Hemisphere. Mr. Putin talks about restoring much of the empire of Peter the Great, starting with Ukraine — but perhaps not ending there. Mr. Xi has expanded his military operations in the Pacific, building bases out of artificial islands in the South China Sea, confronting the Philippines over ownership of some of its islands, and running near-constant military exercises that encircle Taiwan.
川普的國家安全政策提到了更新版的「門羅主義」，旨在將外國勢力排除在西半球之外。普丁談到要恢復彼得大帝帝國的大部分版圖，從烏克蘭開始——但或許不會止步於此。習近平擴大了在太平洋的軍事行動，在南海用人工島建設基地，就部分島嶼的主權問題與菲律賓發生衝突，並幾乎不間斷地舉行環繞台灣的軍事演習。
廣告
It all feels very much like the late 1800s, the peak of the “spheres of influence” approach to world governance by a few dominant nations. In January, asked whether he believed in a spheres-of-influence approach to the world, Mr. Trump said, “It’s not necessarily where I want it to go.” But he added that as he looked around the world, “it’s where it’s going.”
這一切讓人感覺非常像19世紀末，那是少數主導國家通過「勢力範圍」方式進行全球治理的巔峰時期。今年1月，當被問及是否相信基於勢力範圍的世界治理模式時，川普說：「這不一定是我希望它發展的方向。」但他還說，當他環顧世界時，「它正在朝著那個方向發展。」
It is clear that none of the three superpowers want to be limited to their own neighborhoods, even if their maps are expansively drawn. China may be focused on the Pacific first, but it wants access to copper mines in Peru and Ecuador. The United States may talk the talk of withdrawing from the world, but it wants to retain influence across the Middle East, which is why it has kept the bases that Iran attacked in the Gulf in recent months, forcing some to all but shutter. And it is not about to give up its presence in Japan or South Korea.
顯而易見，這三個超級大國都不希望被局限在自己的周邊地區，即便它們劃定的勢力範圍已經相當廣闊。中國雖然目前可能將重點放在太平洋地區，但它也希望獲得秘魯和厄瓜多的銅礦。美國雖口頭上宣稱在世界舞台上撤退，但它希望保持在整個中東地區的影響力，這正是它為何在過去幾個月裡繼續保留伊朗在海灣地區襲擊過的軍事基地，儘管這些基地中有些已幾乎被迫關閉。而且，它也不打算放棄在日本或韓國的存在。
Russia has ambitions that also reach into space and cyberspace, as well as the Arctic and Africa.
俄羅斯的野心也延伸到了太空和網路空間，以及北極和非洲。
Geopolitical Swagger
地緣政治上的虛張聲勢
Still, there is a serious imbalance among the three nations that will be represented at the meeting. While the United States and China are the two leading global powers, Russia pales by comparison in most respects, other than its residual nuclear arsenal and its energy resources.
儘管如此，將出席此次會議的三個國家之間仍存在嚴重的不平衡。美國和中國是兩個領先的全球大國，而俄羅斯除了殘存的核武庫和能源資源外，在大多數方面都相形見絀。
While the United States and China are racing to develop artificial intelligence, Russia is at best a bit player in that field. Other than its nuclear capacity, Russia’s military has been shown over the past four years to be far weaker than Mr. Putin projected.
美國和中國正競相開發人工智慧，而俄羅斯在這一領域充其量只是一個小角色。除了核能力之外，俄羅斯的軍事力量在過去四年中已被證明遠比普丁預期的要弱。
廣告
And economically, Russia cannot compete with the other two countries at the table. While the United States and China are the world’s largest and second-largest economies, respectively, Russia places only eighth. Its economy is barely one-eighth of the size of China’s, and one-eleventh the size of the United States’. It manufactures almost nothing of value for much of the rest of the world, other than military hardware — and vodka and caviar.
在經濟上，俄羅斯無法與桌面上的另外兩個國家競爭。美國和中國分別是世界第一和第二大經濟體，而俄羅斯僅位列第八。其經濟規模僅為中國的八分之一，美國的11分之一左右。除了軍事裝備——以及伏特加和魚子醬——俄羅斯幾乎不生產任何對世界其他地區有價值的商品。
So for Mr. Putin, the meeting allows him to project the illusion that he and his country are more important than they actually are, to claim a certain geopolitical swagger unjustified by reality.
因此，對於普丁來說，這次會晤讓他能夠營造出他和俄羅斯比實際情況更重要的錯覺，以此展現出一種不符合現實的地緣政治氣勢。
The meeting is also a way for Mr. Putin to snub Europe and essentially say that he does not need to deal with those countries, because he can sit down with the leaders of the two most important powers in the world.
這次會晤也是普丁冷落歐洲的一種方式，實質上是在表明他不需要與那些國家打交道，因為他可以與世界上最重要兩個大國的領導人坐下來談判。
“Demonstrating Europe’s irrelevance is very important to Putin at this point,” said Michael Kimmage, the director of the Kennan Institute, named for George Kennan, the famed American diplomat who designed much of the strategy around the Cold War.
「在現階段，證明歐洲無關緊要對普丁來說非常重要，」凱南研究所所長麥可·基馬奇說，該研究所以喬治·凱南的名字命名，這位著名的美國外交官曾制定了冷戰時期的大部分戰略。
“I think nothing is going to come of it, and it will be fairly quickly forgotten,” he added. “But still, to go from the Treaty of Versailles to Yalta to this shows that Europe is being erased.”
「我認為這一次不會有什麼結果，它很快就會被遺忘，」他補充道。「但是，從凡爾賽條約到雅爾達會議再到如今，這一切仍然表明歐洲正在被抹去。」
Ukraine may be discussed, as Mr. Trump indicated shortly before leaving the United States for Asia. But many Americans and Europeans close to the peace talks have concluded that Mr. Putin has no interest in making a deal to end the war. His government’s current proposal largely repeats the same maximalist demands it made before the full-scale invasion of 2022, conditions that were unacceptable to Europe and Ukraine then and remain so today.
正如川普在啟程前往亞洲前夕所暗示的那樣，烏克蘭問題可能會被討論。但是，許多接近和平談判的美國人和歐洲人已經得出結論，普丁對達成結束戰爭的協議沒有興趣。他的政府目前的提議在很大程度上重複了其在2022年全面入侵之前提出的相同的最高限度要求，這些條件當時是歐洲和烏克蘭不可接受的，今天依然如此。
Michael McFaul, a former ambassador to Russia, said Mr. Putin had made plain he was not seeking peace. “If he really thought this was an event for peace, the last thing he would be doing would be bombing the bridges in Kyiv that is now crippling the capital of Ukraine,” Mr. McFaul said. “I don’t think he thinks of this as a genuine effort to advance peace. He gets the photo op and he gets to call it Yalta 2.0.”
前駐俄羅斯大使麥可·麥克福爾表示，普丁已經清楚地表明他並不尋求和平。「如果他真的認為這是一次為了和平的會議，他絕不會去轟炸基輔的橋樑，這種行為現在正使烏克蘭首都癱瘓，」麥克福爾說。「我不認為他把這當成是推進和平的真正努力。他只是得到了拍照的機會，並將其稱之為雅爾達2.0。」
