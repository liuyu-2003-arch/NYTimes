如何解讀川普與習近平的擁抱

How to Decode the Xi-Trump Hug

MAX BEARAK • 2026-11-19
Around three thousand years ago, the kings of Babylon and Assyria shook hands to cement an alliance. We know that because a sculptor carved the moment into limestone, and the work is now on display in a Iraqi museum.
三千年前，巴比倫和亞述國王通過握手來鞏固聯盟。我們之所以知道這一點，是因為一位雕塑家將這一刻刻在了石灰岩上，而這件作品現在正在一家伊拉克博物館展出。
The artifact helps us measure how long world leaders have been making the same gestures of respect, and how long the rest of us have been scrutinizing the slightest differences in their body language. We read volumes into the placement of a hand, let alone subtle deviations from tradition, which is what the world saw on Wednesday when President Trump and China’s leader Xi Jinping embraced each other briefly, ahead of a series of meetings.
這件文物讓我們得以丈量兩件事：世界領導人的此種致敬手勢已經延續了多久；而我們這些旁觀者又已經花了多久去細究他們肢體語言中的微妙之處。我們從手的姿態都能解讀出大量信息，更不用說對傳統的微妙偏離了，這正是週三川普總統和中國領導人習近平在一系列會議前短暫擁抱時世界所看到的。
Mr. Xi’s presence on the tarmac as Mr. Trump descended from Air Force One in China alone was already a break with decades of diplomatic custom, under which lower-ranking officials are generally sent to welcome visiting heads of state.
僅僅是當川普從「空軍一號」走下時習近平出現在停機坪上，就已經打破了數十年的外交慣例——接待國通常會派級別相對低的官員迎接來訪的國家元首。
China’s state-run news agency reported that Mr. Xi was reciprocating Mr. Trump’s mold-breaking decision to greet Mr. Xi in person when he arrived at Andrews Air Force Base for a state visit to the United States in May.
今年9月習近平抵達安德魯斯空軍基地對美國進行國事訪問時，川普曾親自迎接，中國官方通訊社報導稱，習近平此舉是對川普這一打破常規決定的回應。
廣告
The leaders of the world’s two superpowers are clearly trying to signal closeness at a time when the entire world seems increasingly fractious, and when relations between their two nations are strained over several tough issues, including trade barriers, nuclear arms control and the artificial intelligence race.
在世界似乎日益容易發生摩擦，且美中關係因貿易壁壘、核軍備控制和人工智慧競賽等幾個棘手問題而緊張之際，兩個超級大國的領導人顯然正試圖發出關係親密的信號。
“This gesture likely reflects Xi and Beijing’s desire to show respect to Trump, which they know the U.S. President enjoys,” William Yang, a senior analyst for Northeast Asia at the International Crisis Group, wrote in a social media post. “And it is likely also an attempt to uphold the positive rapport between Xi and Trump ahead of a potentially turbulent period in U.S.-China relations in the coming months.”
「這一姿態可能反映了習近平和北京希望向川普展示尊重，他們知道美國總統喜歡這樣，」國際危機組織東北亞高級分析師楊皓暐(William Yang)在社群媒體上寫道。「這也可能是為了在未來幾個月美中關係可能動盪的時期到來之前，試圖維持習近平與川普之間的積極關係。」
Mr. Xi and Mr. Trump will be joined by the Russian president, Vladimir V. Putin, for three-way talks on Thursday, the first trilateral meeting of its kind. Mr. Putin, who has met Mr. Xi dozens of times, was greeted not by Mr. Xi but by Cai Qi, a member of China’s Politburo Standing Committee.
習近平和川普將於週四與俄羅斯總統弗拉基米爾·V·普丁一起舉行三方會談，這是第一次舉行這樣的會議。與習近平見過數十次面的普丁並未得到習近平的迎接，而是由中共中央政治局常委蔡奇迎接。
All of this is being dissected by the media and by historians, just as these leaders’ past gestures have been. In 2017, The Times brought on two experts in body language to analyze Mr. Trump’s handshakes with various world leaders after a string of awkward interactions provided ample fodder for speculation about the geopolitical meanings behind his etiquette.
所有這些都正在被媒體和歷史學家剖析，就像這些領導人過去的姿態一樣。2017年，在一系列尷尬的互動為關於其禮儀背後的地緣政治含義的猜測提供了充足素材之後，《紐約時報》請來兩位肢體語言專家，分析川普與多位世界領導人的握手。
A mangled handshake with French president Emmanuel Macron was “very patronizing,” remarked Jacqueline Whitmore, an author and etiquette instructor. Mr. Trump’s missed cue to shake hands with the German chancellor at the time, Angela Merkel, signaled he was “disinterested,” said Chris Ulrich, who teaches body language and presentation skills.
作家、禮儀教練賈桂琳·惠特莫爾評論說，（川普）與法國總統埃馬紐埃爾·馬克宏笨拙的握手「非常居高臨下」。教授肢體語言和演講技巧的克里斯·烏爾裡希表示，川普錯過了與時任德國總理安哥拉·梅克爾握手的時機，表明他「不感興趣」。
But Mr. Trump hit the mark with a strong face-to-face handshake with Canada’s prime minister, Justin Trudeau, in 2017, Mr. Ulrich said. It wasn’t just that they made sustained eye contact or touched each other’s left arms. “You face your belly button toward people you like, admire and trust,” he said. “This is a great way to build rapport fast.”
但烏爾裡希表示，川普在2017年與加拿大總理賈斯汀·杜魯道的有力握手表現得恰到好處。這不僅僅在於他們進行了持續的眼神交流或觸摸了對方的左臂。「你會把肚臍朝向你喜歡、欽佩和信任的人，」他說，「這是快速建立良好關係的絕佳方式。」
廣告
In Shenzhen, where Mr. Trump, Mr. Xi and Mr. Putin have planned their trilateral meeting on Thursday on the sidelines of a larger gathering of leaders for the Asia Pacific Economic Cooperation forum, the differences in greetings were clearer.
在深圳，川普、習近平和普丁計劃於週四在亞太經合組織論壇領導人的大型會議間隙舉行三方會晤，而這裡的問候方式差異更加明顯。
Mr. Xi’s personal welcome of Mr. Trump appeared to reflect a desire for a measure of stability despite a growing disagreements. And the more conventional reception for Mr. Putin by one of Mr. Xi’s underlings signaled a kind of continuity in relations between China and Russia.
習近平對川普的親自歡迎似乎反映了儘管中美分歧不斷擴大，他仍渴望獲得一定程度的穩定。而由習近平的下屬迎接普丁這一常規待遇則標誌著中俄關係的一種連續性。
In contrast, Japan’s prime minister, Sanae Takaichi, was practically shunned by Mr. Xi when she arrived in Shenzhen.
相比之下，日本首相高市早苗抵達深圳時，堪稱遭到了習近平的冷落。
Relations between Japan and China are at their lowest point in decades, and Ms. Takaichi was greeted when she arrived not by a top minister, but by military guards and a woman whom Japanese media said was the Chinese foreign ministry’s ambassador of protocol.
日中關係正處於幾十年來的最低點，高市抵達時，迎接她的不是一位高級別部長，而是衛兵和一名被日本媒體稱為中國外交部禮賓大使的女性。
Later, at the hall where the forum was taking place, Mr. Xi barely took note of Ms. Takaichi. He offered her only a slight smile as she made her way to a banquet with other world leaders, and he did not appear to respond when she said a few words to him, just before they posed for a photo.
後來，在舉行論壇的大廳裡，習近平幾乎沒有注意到高市。當她與其他世界領導人一起赴宴時，他只對她微微一笑，當她在他們合影前對他說了幾句話時，他似乎沒有回應。

Max Bearak是《紐約時報》記者，主要報導突發新聞和國際新聞。
