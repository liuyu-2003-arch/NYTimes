美國將華大列入貿易黑名單

U.S. Restricts Trade With Chinese Genomics Company

REBECCA ROBBINS, ANA SWANSON • 2026-10-29
The Trump administration on Wednesday added BGI, a major Chinese genomics company that U.S. officials have long eyed with suspicion, to a trade blacklist aimed at barring products made with forced labor in China.
川普政府週三將華大列入了一份旨在禁止中國強迫勞動產品進入美國的貿易黑名單。華大是一家大型中國基因組學公司，美國官員長期以來一直對其心存疑慮。
BGI will be prohibited from shipping goods to the United States under criteria a 2021 law established.
根據2021年一項法律確立的標準，華大將被禁止向美國出口產品。
In recent years, both Democrats and Republicans have called out and imposed sanctions on BGI, saying its close ties to Beijing and its access to sprawling genetic data pose a threat to U.S. national security. U.S. officials have also linked BGI subsidiaries to abuses in the Xinjiang region of China, including conducting genetic analyses of Uyghurs and other predominantly Muslim minorities.
近年來，民主黨和共和黨人均對華大公開點名批評並實施制裁，稱其與北京關係密切，並且能夠獲取大量基因數據，對美國國家安全構成威脅。美國官員還將華大的子公司與中國新疆地區的侵權行為聯繫起來，包括對維吾爾人和其他以穆斯林為主的少數民族進行基因分析。
In a statement on Wednesday, BGI said it “has not engaged in any form of forced labor.” The company has previously said it performs work “for civilian and scientific purposes only,” denying that it poses a risk to U.S. national security or collects Americans’ genetic information.
華大週三在聲明中稱，該公司「不涉及任何強迫勞動行為」。它此前曾表示，其工作「僅用於民用和科學目的」，否認對美國國家安全構成風險，也否認收集美國人的基因信息。 
廣告
BGI sells DNA sequencing machines and operates laboratories that crunch genetic data on behalf of academic researchers and drug companies around the world. The company, which has a laboratory in San Jose, Calif., and offices in Massachusetts, does not directly market its sequencers and the chemicals involved in that process in the United States, though experts say the company has found backdoor avenues to ship them. Over the years, it has announced partnerships with several American companies and universities.
華大銷售DNA測序儀，並運營多家實驗室，為世界各地的學術研究人員和製藥企業處理和分析基因數據。該公司在加利福尼亞州聖何塞設有實驗室，在麻薩諸塞州設有辦事處。雖然它並未在美國直接銷售測序儀及測序過程中使用的化學試劑，但專家表示，該公司已找到其他管道向美國輸送這些產品。多年來，華大曾宣布與多家美國企業和大學建立合作關係。
The move is likely to be considered an affront by the Chinese government, which has traded tit-for-tat actions with the United States in recent months despite a broader trade truce. President Trump and the Chinese leader, Xi Jinping, are expected to meet in China next month and possibly the month after in Miami.
此舉很可能被中國政府視作冒犯。近幾個月來，儘管中美達成了範圍更廣的貿易休戰協議，雙方仍採取了針鋒相對的行動。川普總統和中國領導人習近平預計將於下個月在中國會面，並可能於之後的一個月在邁阿密會面。 
Beyond BGI, the Trump administration has been escalating its scrutiny of China’s surging biotechnology sector, with proposed rules issued last week that would more strictly regulate certain investments.
除華大外，川普政府還加強了對中國迅速發展的生物技術行業的審查，上週公布的擬議新規將對某些投資實施更嚴格的監管。
The Department of Defense deems BGI and several of its subsidiaries as “Chinese military companies operating in the United States.” In response, BGI said in a statement that it was not controlled by the Chinese government or military.
美國國防部將華大及其多家子公司認定為「在美國運營的中國軍事企業」。對此，華大發表聲明稱，公司不受中國政府或軍方控制。
In a notice, the Trump administration said the new restriction would also apply to BGI Genomics, a BGI subsidiary, and MGI, a Chinese company spun off from BGI. MGI said in a statement on Wednesday that it had “zero tolerance” for forced labor and would seek to be removed from the list.
川普政府在一份公告中表示，新的限制措施也將適用於華大旗下子公司華大基因，以及從華大分拆出來的華大智造。華大智造週三發表聲明稱，對強迫勞動採取「零容忍」態度，並將尋求從名單中移除。
Dozens of Chinese companies have been added to the list related to forced labor. This past summer, the Trump administration added 43 to the list in industries like aluminum, apparel, copper and cotton.
已有數十家中國企業被列入這份與強迫勞動相關的黑名單。今年夏天，川普政府又將43家企業列入名單，涉及鋁業、服裝、銅業和棉花等行業。 
廣告
China denies the presence of forced labor in Xinjiang. But the U.S. government has said the Chinese government uses forced labor, mass detentions and other coercive practices to subdue the region’s predominantly Muslim ethnic groups, particularly the Uyghurs.
中國否認新疆存在強迫勞動。但美國政府表示，中國政府利用強迫勞動、大規模拘禁和其他脅迫性做法來壓制該地區以穆斯林為主的少數民族，尤其是維吾爾族。
For years, several of BGI’s subsidiaries have also been on a separate Commerce Department list restricting their access to U.S. technologies.
多年來，華大旗下多家子公司還被列入美國商務部另一份限制其獲取美國技術的實體清單。
Under a law Mr. Trump signed late last year, U.S. government agencies are barred from contracting with certain foreign biotech service providers in adversarial countries like China. The law did not name BGI, but it is affected because of its inclusion on the Pentagon’s list of firms deemed Chinese military companies.
根據川普去年年底簽署的一項法律，美國政府機構不得與中國等對手國家的某些外國生物技術服務提供商簽訂合同。該法律並未直接點名華大，但由於該公司被列入五角大廈認定的中國軍事企業名單，因此也受到這項法律的影響。
Last week, the Treasury Department proposed regulations prohibiting U.S. companies and investors from taking investment stakes in Chinese biotechnology. Those rules could chill certain types of agreements between U.S. and Chinese firms, though they crucially exempt the booming area of dealmaking that the world’s biggest pharmaceutical companies prize most, in which they have been filling their pipelines with experimental drugs from China.
上週，美國財政部提出擬議法規，禁止美國企業和投資者入股中國生物技術企業。這些規定可能抑制美中企業之間某些類型的合作協議，但值得注意的是，它們豁免了全球大型製藥公司最為看重、目前蓬勃發展的交易領域，這些公司一直在通過從中國引進實驗性藥物來充實自己的研發管線。

Rebecca Robbins是《紐約時報》記者，報導製藥業。她自2015年以來一直從事健康和醫學方面的報導。

Ana Swanson報導貿易和國際經濟新聞，常駐華盛頓。她從事新聞工作已超過十年。
