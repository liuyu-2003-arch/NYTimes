歐巴馬川普「口水戰」中的芝樂坊

Don’t Cry for the Cheesecake Factory. It’s Weathered Worse.

KIM SEVERSON • 2026-10-29
First, the man responsible for the way the Cheesecake Factory looks wants you to know he isn’t mad. And his feelings aren’t hurt.
首先，負責芝樂坊(Cheesecake Factory)裝潢設計的那個人想讓你知道：他沒生氣。他不覺得受傷。
Rick McCormack is the designer behind the eclectic décor of murals, towering columns and oversize amber light fixtures that gives each Cheesecake Factory the vibe of a postmodern suburban Versailles.
裡克·麥考馬克就是這種壁畫、高聳立柱和超大琥珀色燈具混搭造型的設計者，讓每家芝樂坊都有了一種後現代郊區凡爾賽宮的氛圍。
When he first learned that former President Barack Obama had told a political rally on Saturday that President Trump’s golden glow-up of the Oval Office looked “like Saddam Hussein opened a Cheesecake Factory,” he was surprised. And when the White House responded by denouncing Mr. Obama as an elitist — setting off an internet dumpster fire about class optics in America — Mr. McCormack just shook his head.
週六，前總統貝拉克·歐巴馬在一場政治集會上說，川普總統對橢圓形辦公室的金色改造看起來「像薩達姆·侯賽因開了一家芝樂坊」。麥考馬克第一次聽說時很驚訝。白宮隨後譴責歐巴馬是精英主義者，在互聯網上引發了一場關於美國階級形象觀的激烈爭論，而麥考馬克只是搖了搖頭。
“I’m not emotionally attached to the Cheesecake Factory design, but don’t we have bigger issues in the country?” he said in an interview on Tuesday.
「我對芝樂坊的設計沒有特別的感情，可我們的國家有比這更重要的問題吧？」他在週二的採訪中說。
Then he thought about the bigger picture. Yes, like Cracker Barrel and Starbucks before it, the Cheesecake Factory has found itself an unwitting player in the culture wars. And yes, he said, the whole thing seems silly.
然後他想了想更大的圖景。是的，像之前的Cracker Barrel和星巴克一樣，芝樂坊不知不覺成了文化戰爭中的一個角色。是的，他說，整件事看起來很傻。

But on the positive side, the midterm dust-up has focused the attention of an entire nation — at least those following politics — on his true love, restaurant design.
但好的一面是，這場期中選舉的爭吵讓整個國家——至少是關注政治的那部分人——把注意力集中到了他真正的愛好上：餐廳設計。
“No design is universally loved,” said Mr. McCormack, 69. “I realize that. That is one thing I like about design. That makes the world of design go around.”
「沒有哪種設計是所有人都喜歡的，」69歲的麥考馬克說。「我明白這一點。這恰恰是我喜歡設計的一個原因。設計的世界就是這樣運轉的。」
The Cheesecake Factory — which began in 1978 in an epicenter of elitism, Beverly Hills, Calif. — is a fortress, accustomed to absorbing attacks perhaps not as clever but equally forceful.
芝樂坊1978年起步於精英主義的中心地帶加州比弗利山。它是一座堡壘，習慣了承受攻擊，那些攻擊也許不那麼巧妙，但力量絲毫不小。
A writer for Vice once called it “the culinary embodiment of the Kardashians.” The Centers for Science in the Public Interest has assailed its portion sizes, calling its food “heart attacks disguised as dinner.”
Vice的一位作者曾稱它是「卡戴珊家族在餐飲界的化身」。公共利益科學中心抨擊其分量，稱其食物是「偽裝成晚餐的心臟病發作」。
The chain’s look, dreamed up by its founder and chief executive, David Overton, and refined over 16 years by Mr. McCormack, has long had its own band of critics.
由創始人兼首席執行官大衛·奧弗頓構想、再由麥考馬克用16年時間提煉而成的餐館裝潢長期以來一直遭到一些人的詬病。
“If you want a fully immersive ‘postmodern design hellscape’- themed dining experience, I highly recommend dinner at The Cheesecake Factory,” the game developer and commentator Max Krieger wrote in a 2017 rant on Twitter. “The interior is a world of aesthetic chaos that feels like a mix between a Fry’s Electronics, an overgrown Panera, and a laser tag arena.”
「如果你想要一個完全沉浸式的『後現代設計地獄景觀』主題用餐體驗，我強烈推薦晚飯去芝樂坊，」遊戲開發者兼評論員馬克斯·凱瑞格2017年在Twitter上的一通咆哮中寫道。「內部是一個美學的混沌世界，感覺像是Fry\'s電器城、過度生長的Panera麵包店和鐳戰館的混合體。」

Whatever. It worked. The Cheesecake Factory has defined the term “upscale casual dining,” and has done such a good job reading the psyche and the appetite of the American diner that it reported on Tuesday that its revenue had risen to $1.03 billion, from $907.2 million a year earlier.
隨便吧。反正效果不錯。芝樂坊定義了「高檔休閑餐飲」這個詞，而且在解讀美國食客的心理和胃口方面做得如此之好，以至於週二財報的收入已從去年同期的9.072億美元增至10.3億美元。
Sales from locations that had been open at least a year were up more than 15 percent over last year. (A competitor, Texas Roadhouse, reported 6 percent growth.) The Cheesecake Factory expects to add 26 new restaurants to its roster of 218 this year.
開業至少一年的門店銷售額同比增長超過15%。（競爭對手 Texas Roadhouse報告增長6%。）芝樂坊今年計劃在218家餐廳的基礎上新增26家。
Mr. Overton grew up in Detroit, where his mother, Evelyn, sold cheesecakes to restaurants that she baked in the family’s basement, using a recipe she had clipped from a newspaper.
奧弗頓在底特律長大，母親伊夫林向餐廳供應芝士蛋糕，並在自家地下室烘焙，用的是從報紙上剪下的配方。
He moved to San Francisco, dropped out of law school and started playing drums for a rock band whose leader wrote the Jimi Hendrix hit “Hey, Joe.” In the early 1970s, he persuaded his parents and sister to move west and open a cheesecake bakery in North Hollywood called the Cheesecake Factory.
他搬到舊金山，從法學院退學，開始給一支搖滾樂隊打鼓，樂隊主唱是吉米·亨德里克斯的名曲《嘿，喬》(Hey, Joe)的作者。1970年代初，他說服父母和姐姐搬到西海岸，在北好萊塢開了一家名為芝樂坊的芝士蛋糕烘焙店。
It wasn’t making much money, so Mr. Overton decided to turn it into a restaurant. In 1978, he opened a small spot a block from Rodeo Drive in Beverly Hills’ Golden Triangle.
烘焙店賺不了多少錢，奧弗頓便決定把它改成餐廳。1978年，他在比弗利山黃金三角區距羅迪歐大道一個街區的地方開了一家小店。
He was such a novice that he served hash browns because he didn’t know how to make French fries. As business grew, he studied the menus at upscale restaurants like Wolfgang Puck’s Spago, trying veal dishes and figuring out how to make them with less-expensive chicken. He doubled down on portion sizes and sauces.
剛入行的他連炸薯條都不會做，只好供應薯餅。隨著生意日漸紅火，他開始研究沃爾夫岡·帕克 Spago之類高檔餐廳的菜單，嘗試小牛肉菜肴，琢磨怎麼用更便宜的雞肉做出來。他在份量和醬汁上不斷加碼。
The menu kept growing into the 250-item behemoth it is today. (Ellen DeGeneres once called it “the only menu visible from space.”) Mr. Overton said it appealed to “palate of the common man.” “I’m not a gourmet,” he told the “Today” show. “I don’t like a lot of things, but what I like millions of people like, thank goodness.”
菜單不斷擴張，成了今天250項的巨獸。（艾倫·德傑尼勒斯曾稱它是「唯一從太空可見的菜單」。）奧弗頓說它迎合的是「普通人的味蕾」。「我不是美食家，」他告訴《今日秀》。「很多東西我都不喜歡，但是，謝天謝地，我喜歡的東西正是數百萬人都喜歡的。」
Mr. McCormack came along in 1992, and stayed until he opened his own design firm in 2008. He embraced Mr. Overton’s design sensibility, which drew on Victorian-era San Francisco and quirky ideas like the tall columns topped with palm fronds that he had seen at bathhouses. And, of course, each restaurant featured a glass case near the front filled with cheesecake.
麥考馬克1992年加入，一直待到2008年開設自己的設計公司。他接受了奧弗頓的設計審美，這種審美借鑒了維多利亞時代的舊金山，還有他在澡堂見過的頂部飾有棕櫚葉的高柱之類古怪念頭。當然，每家餐廳前台附近都擺著一個裝滿芝士蛋糕的玻璃櫃。
“We were going for a little bit of an exotic or European feel,” Mr. McCormack recalled. Hand-painted plaster walls, limestone floors and booths in dark wood made it feel fancy.
「我們想要一點異國情調，或者歐洲感覺，」麥考馬克回憶道。手繪灰泥牆、石灰岩地板和深色木製卡座讓它顯得高檔。

“You’d see families coming in celebrating life’s milestones in a nice environment without having to spend every penny,” he said.
「你會看到一家人來慶祝人生里程碑，環境不錯，又不必花光每一分錢，」他說。
In the song “Childs Play,” the singer Drake calls it “a place for families that drive Camrys and go to Disney.”
歌手德雷克在《兒戲》(Childs Play)中稱它是「開凱美瑞、去迪士尼的家庭專屬」。
This week, Mr. Trump and his allies hailed the Cheesecake Factory as America’s great middle-class restaurant and proof of how out-of-touch Mr. Obama is with the mainstream.
本週，川普和他的盟友稱讚芝樂坊是美國偉大的中產階級餐廳，證明歐巴馬與主流多麼脫節。
Democrats responded that Mr. Trump was the elitist, pointing out that the Cheesecake Factory isn’t exactly a bargain. Spaghetti and meatballs go for $25.95, and a burger close to $20. A slice of cheesecake is $10.95.
民主黨人回擊說川普才是精英主義者，指出芝樂坊並不便宜。意大利麵和肉丸25.95美元，漢堡接近20美元。一塊芝士蛋糕10.95美元。
At a Cheesecake Factory in Atlanta on Tuesday night, many of the staff hadn’t heard about Mr. Obama’s remarks. But they laughed when they did.
週二晚上，亞特蘭大一家芝樂坊的許多員工還沒聽說歐巴馬的言論。但聽說了之後，他們笑了。
One waiter, who asked not to be identified, pointed out that Cheesecake Factory stock was performing better than some of the market giants. (Indeed, the restaurant’s share price has risen 100 percent this year, compared with 15 percent for Amazon’s, and 18 percent for Apple’s.)
一位要求匿名的服務員指出，芝樂坊的股票表現比一些市場巨頭還好。（確實，該餐廳股價今年上漲了100%，亞馬遜為15%，蘋果為18%。）

Over the years, the maximalist design that marked the early restaurants has been toned down, a casualty of economics and scale. But the Atlanta restaurant still has the high ceilings, giant murals and that amber glow.
多年來，早期餐廳那種極繁主義設計已被淡化，成了經濟和規模的犧牲品。但亞特蘭大那家店仍然有高天花板、巨大的壁畫和琥珀色的光暈。
“It does kind of look like Trump Tower in here,” the waiter said.
「這裡確實有點像川普大廈，」那位服務員說。

Kim Severson常駐亞特蘭大，報導美國飲食文化，並為時報烹飪版撰稿。
