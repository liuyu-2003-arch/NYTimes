人員動盪、派系對立：期中選舉前的白宮陰霾密布

An Unpredictable President, a Grim White House: Trump Faces Down the Midterms

KATIE ROGERS • 2026-10-29
President Trump’s private dining room, located just off the Oval Office, is where he goes to watch television, make phone calls and sift through stacks of printed-out news articles and documents that eat up most of the space. A spot has been cleared for Natalie Harp, a devoted aide who now works out of that room when she is not seated behind the president at his desk.
川普總統的私人餐廳就在橢圓形辦公室旁邊，他常去這個餐廳裡看電視、打電話，翻閱堆積如山、佔據了大部分空間的打印版新聞報導和文件。如今，那裡為娜塔莉·哈普騰出了一塊地方。這位忠誠的助手在不坐在總統辦公桌後方時，就在這個房間裡工作。
Elsewhere, Bill Pulte, a top federal housing official and purveyor of A.I.-generated images and memes that make their way to the president’s social media feed, spends some of his time making posters. He recently made one of Senator Jon Husted of Ohio, a Republican whose backside Mr. Trump has admired, for the president to display while riding aboard Air Force One.
在另一處，聯邦住房高級官員比爾·普爾特會花些時間製作海報。他擅長製作人工智慧生成的圖像和表情包，這些內容會進入總統的社群媒體信息流。他最近為俄亥俄州共和黨參議員喬恩·赫斯特德製作了一張海報，供總統在乘坐空軍一號時展示。川普曾稱讚赫斯特德的臀部。
Amid the loyalists, news clippings, poster boards and pieces of art made by his fans is an increasingly unpredictable president staring down the possibility that the Republican Party could lose both chambers of Congress in next week’s elections.
在這些忠誠擁護者、新聞剪報、海報板和粉絲創作的藝術品之間，是一位越來越難以預測的總統，他正面臨共和黨可能在下週的選舉中失去國會兩院的局面。
Many G.O.P. candidates in tight races have tried to distance themselves from Mr. Trump’s policies, his behavior and the widespread allegations of self-dealing in his administration. Behind the scenes, some political advisers and outside Republican groups have warned the president’s aides that Mr. Trump could do more harm than good by holding rallies in battleground states with uncomfortably close races, according to people with knowledge of the conversations.
許多選情膠著的共和黨候選人試圖與川普的政策、行為以及其政府中廣泛存在的以權謀私指控保持距離。據了解相關談話的人士說，在幕後，一些政治顧問和外部共和黨團體已警告總統的助手，在選情異常膠著的搖擺州舉行集會對川普來說可能弊大於利。
廣告
But the president, under pressure and angry with naysayers, has dismissed polls that reflect his unpopularity, and has raged against unflattering stories that plunge him into a dark mood. Determined to win by turning the midterm season into a referendum on himself, he is set to headline rallies around the country all week, including in Ohio on Wednesday.
但總統在壓力之下又對反對者心懷憤怒，他無視那些反映其支持率低迷的民調，並對那些令他心情低落的負面報導大發雷霆。他決心將期中選舉季轉變為針對他本人的公投，並以此贏得勝利，因此本週他將親自主持全國各地的競選集會，包括週三在俄亥俄州的集會。
Mr. Trump remains convinced that his dominant place in the Republican Party is enough to mitigate a tough political climate. But a grim mood has set in around the White House, which has been rocked by turnover, tension among internal factions and anxiety over what happens should Republicans lose legislative control in Washington, according to 16 current and former administration officials and allies of Mr. Trump’s, who spoke on the condition of anonymity to describe private conversations.
川普仍然確信，他在共和黨內的主導地位足以緩解艱難的政治氣候。但據16名現任和前任政府官員及川普的盟友們說（他們要求在匿名情況下描述私下談話），白宮內部已瀰漫著一種陰鬱的氣氛，人員頻繁更迭、派系之間緊張對立，人們擔心共和黨若在華盛頓失去立法控制權後局勢將如何發展。
Danielle Alvarez, a White House spokeswoman, described the president and his aides as unified in their focus.
白宮發言人丹妮爾·阿爾瓦雷斯稱總統及其助手在重點上團結一致。
“There is only one team that matters in this White House, and that is the team focused on serving President Donald J. Trump and the American people,” Ms. Alvarez said in a statement, adding: “The president’s team remains strong, united and focused on delivering his America First agenda.”
「在這個白宮，只有一支重要的團隊，那就是專注於服務唐納德·J·川普總統和美國人民的團隊，」阿爾瓦雷斯在聲明中說，並稱：「總統的團隊仍然強大、團結，專注於推進他的美國優先議程。」
To several current and former White House officials who were around between early 2017 and early 2021, the past few weeks have felt particularly reminiscent of Mr. Trump’s first term. That period was marked by short, explosive staff tenures and infighting. Toward the end, Mr. Trump was struggling to govern during a pandemic. He lashed out as his poll numbers sank, then stoked fear and outrage among his supporters with false claims that the 2020 election was rigged, to the point that a large group attacked the Capitol.
對於幾位在2017年初至2021年初期間在白宮任職的現任和前任官員來說，過去幾週感覺特別讓人想起川普的第一個任期。那段時期以幕僚任期短暫且動盪不安、內部鬥爭頻發為特徵。臨近任期結束時，川普在疫情肆虐期間苦於無法有效施政。隨著民調數字下滑，他大肆發洩不滿，然後用2020年選舉被操縱的虛假說法煽動支持者的恐懼和憤怒，最終導致一大群人襲擊了國會大廈。
The difference between that term and this one, of course, is that Mr. Trump returned to power with a vengeance, and with a crew of loyalists who stood by him through his years as a political pariah.
當然，那個任期和本次任期的區別在於，川普捲土重來時氣勢如虹，身邊還聚集了一群忠實追隨者，這些人在他淪為政治棄兒的那些年裡始終不離不棄。
Since returning, he has ordered his Justice Department to carry out a retribution campaign against his perceived enemies. He has unleased a federal crackdown on undocumented immigrants that has swept up U.S. citizens and, at times, led to deadly confrontations. He has continued to trample on the ethical norms that have governed the presidency. And he has two more years to go.
自重返白宮以來，他已命令司法部對他眼中的敵人展開報復行動。他發動了聯邦對無證移民的打擊，這場打擊波及美國公民，有時還導致致命對抗。他繼續踐踏著規範總統職權的道德準則。而他的任期還有兩年才結束。
廣告
Ahead of Tuesday, the stakes are high: Mr. Trump is trying to fend off the oversight and constraints that a Democratic-controlled Congress would bring, and to show that he still commands a strong political base.
週二之前局勢十分嚴峻：川普正試圖抵擋民主黨控制的國會將帶來的監督和約束，並表明他仍然掌握強大的政治基礎。
Mr. Trump’s recent appearances have included some of the economic policy-related messaging that Republicans have hoped to see. But that message has often been overshadowed by protesters, the president’s comments about the male physique and his venting sessions about Democrats, including Barack Obama, who recently said that Mr. Trump’s gilded White House renovations looked like “Saddam Hussein opened a Cheesecake Factory.” (The Cheesecake Factory “is a very successful company,” Mr. Trump said at a Mississippi rally on Monday.)
川普最近的公開露面中包含了一些共和黨人希望看到的與經濟政策相關的表態。但這些信息常常被抗議者、總統關於男性體型的言論，以及他對民主黨人（包括歐巴馬）的發洩所掩蓋。歐巴馬最近說，川普鍍金的白宮翻修工程看起來像「薩達姆·侯賽因開了一家芝樂坊(Cheesecake Factory)」。（川普週一在密西西比州集會上說，芝樂坊「是一家非常成功的公司」。）
The president also frequently complains about how hard he is working, privately and publicly.
總統還經常私下和公開抱怨自己工作多麼辛苦。
“I’m working my ass off,” Mr. Trump said in Tennessee earlier this month. “You better get out there and vote.”
「我在拚命工作，」川普本月早些時候在田納西州說。「你們最好出去投票。」
A person close to the White House political operation who was granted anonymity to describe the strategy said that Mr. Trump’s advisers were aware of the concerns that his presence in critical states could hurt G.O.P. candidates. But they have largely discounted them, believing, as Mr. Trump does, that he should be the centerpiece of this election cycle, the person added. Multiple advisers say the strategy is simple: Focus on red states and juice turnout.
一位獲准匿名描述策略、且接近白宮政治運作的人士說，川普的顧問知道一種擔憂：他在關鍵州的露面可能會傷害共和黨候選人。但這名人士補充說，他們在很大程度上不以為意，因為他們和川普一樣認為，他應該成為這個選舉週期的核心。多名顧問說，策略很簡單：專注於紅州，提高投票率。
“The president does have a way of defying history,” Karoline Leavitt, his former press secretary, said during an appearance on the Bill Maher Show last weekend. “He’s working really hard out on the road to turn Republicans out to vote.”
「總統確實有辦法挑戰歷史，」他的前新聞秘書卡羅琳·萊維特上週末在《比爾·馬赫秀》節目中說。「他正在外面非常努力地工作，讓共和黨人出來投票。」
廣告
While speaking to allies in recent days, Mr. Trump has talked up the sizes of the crowds at his rallies, and has noted that Vice President JD Vance, the presumptive Republican front-runner in 2028, has not drawn large numbers to events that he is headlining.
最近幾天與盟友交談時，川普大肆誇耀自己競選集會的參與人數，並指出2028年共和黨推定領跑者、副總統萬斯領銜的活動中沒有吸引到大量人群。
Mr. Vance’s office did not respond to a request for comment, but the White House came to the vice president’s defense.
萬斯的辦公室沒有回應置評請求，但白宮為副總統辯護。
“Donald Trump is the hardest working person in the country, followed by JD Vance,” Ms. Alvarez said. “Both men are raising money, crisscrossing the country and making sure that candidates are successful these midterms.” Advisers close to the White House, who are bracing for Republicans to at least lose the House, expect Mr. Trump to make changes to his administration, depending on how next Tuesday goes. Presidents have typically reset their staffing after rough election cycles.
「唐納德·川普是這個國家最努力工作的人，其次就是JD·萬斯，」阿爾瓦雷斯說。「兩人都在籌款、奔波全國，確保候選人在這次期中選舉中獲勝。」接近白宮的顧問們正為共和黨至少失去眾議院控制權做準備，他們預計，川普將根據下週二選舉結果對內閣進行調整。總統們通常會在艱難的選舉週期後重置其團隊。
Mr. Trump made one jarring hiring decision on his own when he led the charge this fall to hire Katie Zacharia, a cable news pundit, as his new press secretary. The president, who had grown frustrated with the slow process to find a replacement for Ms. Leavitt, had watched clips of Ms. Zacharia and decided to quickly name her to the role, before a formal vetting process could be completed, according to people with knowledge of the situation. She found few internal allies.
川普在今年秋天親自做出了一個令人震驚的任命決定：他推動聘請有線電視新聞評論員凱蒂·扎卡裡亞擔任新任新聞秘書。據了解情況的人士說，總統對尋找萊維特替代者的緩慢過程感到沮喪，在觀看了扎卡裡亞女士的片段後，他決定在正式審查程序完成之前迅速任命她擔任新聞秘書。她在白宮內部幾乎沒有盟友。
While Ms. Zacharia traveled with Mr. Trump during her short tenure, she was not invited to attend daily senior staff meetings convened by Mr. Trump’s chief of staff, Susie Wiles, according to people familiar with the dynamic. After a rocky debut in the press briefing room, Ms. Zacharia was ousted after five days.
據熟悉內情的人士說，扎卡裡亞在其短暫任期內曾隨川普出行，但沒有被邀請參加由川普幕僚長蘇西·威爾斯召集的每日高級職員會議。在新聞簡報室經歷了一場艱難的首秀後，扎卡裡亞在五天後被撤換。
Some White House observers viewed Ms. Zacharia’s hiring as a sign of Ms. Wiles’s declining influence with Mr. Trump, so much that they began circulating ideas about who could succeed her. Mr. Trump has publicly pushed back on reports that he is unhappy with or seeking to replace her.
一些白宮觀察人士認為，扎卡裡亞被聘任表明威爾斯在川普心中的影響力正在減弱，甚至有人開始討論誰能接替威爾斯的職位。川普公開反駁了有關他對威爾斯不滿或尋求替換她的報導。
廣告
“Susie is here,” Mr. Trump told reporters earlier this month, “and she’s going to be here for a long time.”
「蘇西在這裡，」川普本月早些時候告訴記者，「而且她會在這裡待很長時間。」
