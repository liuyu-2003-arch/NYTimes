經濟機遇還是長期風險？

A Chinese Port Could Be a Lifeline for Spain, but Also a Threat

JASON HOROWITZ • 2026-10-29
The port town of Ferrol has long been synonymous with Spanish national defense.
費羅爾這座港口城市長期以來一直是西班牙國防的代名詞。
After the Spanish Navy built its arsenal and shipyards in Ferrol’s almost impregnable harbor in the 18th century, the city boomed. Naval engineers built elegant streets lined with glass balconies that became the envy of northwestern Spain, and more than a century later, Spain’s future dictator, Francisco Franco, the son of a naval officer, was born there.
18世紀，西班牙海軍在費羅爾幾乎固若金湯的港灣中興建軍械基地和造船廠，帶動了這座城市的繁榮發展。海軍工程師修建了雅緻的街道，兩旁的建築裝飾著玻璃陽台，令西班牙西北部其他城市艷羨不已。一個多世紀後，西班牙後來的獨裁者弗朗西斯科·佛朗哥——一位海軍軍官的兒子——就出生在這裡。
Now the city, long since marred by industrial decay, unemployment and declining population, is becoming known for its warm welcome of Chinese industry into Spain and Western Europe.
如今，這座長期飽受工業衰敗、失業和人口減少困擾的城市正因熱情歡迎中國企業進入西班牙和西歐而受到關注。
In June SAIC Motor, one of China’s largest electronic vehicle manufacturers, chose a sleepy port just outside Ferrol as the site for a major plant. The project, which is expected to provide more than 1,000 jobs, has prompted widespread celebration across Ferrol.
今年6月，中國最大的電動汽車製造商之一上汽集團選擇費羅爾郊外一個沉寂的港口作為一座大型工廠的廠址。該項目預計將提供1000多個就業崗位，在費羅爾受到廣泛歡迎。
廣告
That reflects a broader national consensus — even amid a rancorous election campaign in which politicians are sparring over other issues — over the need for Chinese investment.
這反映出西班牙國內對於引進中國投資必要性的廣泛共識。即便目前正值一場競爭激烈的選舉，各派政客在其他議題上爭論不休，但在這一點上卻少有爭議。
The left-wing government of Pedro Sánchez is expected to sign off on the deal before the election on Nov. 29. If it doesn’t, a new conservative government is expected to. Either way, Ferrol will become the latest Spanish city to bet its economic future on hosting Chinese companies compatible with the country’s emphasis on green technology. Chinese battery makers, solar panel producers and other renewable energy companies are sprouting across Spain, where Chinese electric vehicle investments have grown faster over the past year than anywhere else in Europe.
預計佩德羅·桑切斯領導的左翼政府將在11月29日大選前批准這筆投資。如果未能獲批，預計新上台的保守派政府也會予以放行。無論如何，費羅爾都將成為又一座把經濟未來押在中國企業身上的西班牙城市。這些中國企業的業務與西班牙重視綠色技術的發展方向相契合。中國電池製造商、太陽能電池板生產商和其他可再生能源企業正在西班牙各地湧現。過去一年，中國在西班牙的電動汽車投資增速快於其在歐洲其他國家的投資增速。
The proposed plant in Ferrol embodies the trade-off between short-term economic opportunity and long-term risk that these Chinese investments present to Europe.
費羅爾擬建的這座工廠體現了中國投資給歐洲帶來的短期經濟機遇與長期風險之間的權衡。
This summer, the Spanish news media reported that Spain’s security services were concerned that China might use the plant for espionage. The defense minister declined to comment on the reports, and the regional government quickly dismissed them as inaccurate.
今年夏天，西班牙媒體報導稱，西班牙安全部門擔心中國可能利用這座工廠從事間諜活動。西班牙國防大臣拒絕就報導置評，而地方政府則迅速否認了報導的準確性。
What has lingered are deep questions about whether the increased Chinese presence, especially in one of Spain’s military heartlands, will eventually make Spain too reliant on Chinese supply chains, materials and technology, and make local Spanish firms more vulnerable to Chinese competition.
然而，一些深層次的問題始終揮之不去：隨著中國在西班牙的存在不斷擴大，尤其是深入到西班牙的軍事重鎮，西班牙最終是否會過度依賴中國的供應鏈、原材料和技術？西班牙本土企業是否會因此在與中國企業的競爭中變得更加脆弱？
Those concerns have led leaders in other major European powers, like Germany, to make it harder for Chinese firms to set up shop on their soil.
這些擔憂已促使德國等歐洲主要大國的領導人提高中國企業在其境內設廠的門檻。
“The question is,” said Rafael Dezcallar, until recently Spain’s ambassador to China, “what price are we willing to pay to have these advantageous relations with China?”
西班牙前駐華大使拉斐爾·德斯卡利亞爾說，「問題在於，為了與中國維持這種互利關係，我們願意付出多大的代價？」
廣告
In Ferrol, a town of about 65,000, residents struggle to see a downside. They see the plant as a golden opportunity for rebirth after decades in which the Spanish Navy deployed some of its fleet further south, Asian competition eroded the shipbuilding industry, causing jobs to disappear and the population to crater.
在擁有約6.5萬人口的費羅爾，居民們卻很難看到這筆投資有什麼壞處。在他們看來，這座工廠是城市重獲新生的黃金機遇。過去幾十年，西班牙海軍將部分艦隊部署到更靠南的地區，亞洲企業的競爭又不斷侵蝕當地造船業，導致就業崗位流失、人口銳減。
“So, the plant is from China. What’s the difference?” said Rosi Vásquez, 68, as she carried groceries past one of the dozens of shuttered shops in town. “It would mean work for everyone.”
「就算是中國企業來建廠，那又怎麼樣？」68歲的羅西·巴斯克斯提著剛買的食品雜貨，走過城裡一家關門歇業的商店時說。當地像這樣的商店有數十家之多。「這能給大家帶來工作機會。」
The investment promised “a radical shift regarding the nature of the city,” according to Alfredo Martín García, a historian at the University of León who has studied Ferrol, his hometown. “It changes the historical dynamic Ferrol has had over the last 300 years.”
萊昂大學研究費羅爾的歷史學家、費羅爾本地人阿爾弗雷多·馬丁·加西亞說，這項投資意味著「這座城市的性質將發生根本性轉變」。「它將改變費羅爾過去300年來的歷史發展軌跡，」他說。
Supporters also say the plant would give a lifeline to the Spanish car industry.
支持者還表示，這座工廠將為西班牙汽車工業帶來一線生機。
Spain doesn’t have its own competitive flagship brand, but it does produce gas-powered cars for other country’s automakers, traditionally those from Europe and America. That business accounts for 10 percent of the country’s G.D.P. — almost as much as tourism does — and Spain exports the most cars in Europe after Germany.
西班牙沒有具有競爭力的本土旗艦汽車品牌，但長期以來一直為其他國家的汽車製造商生產燃油汽車，傳統上是來自歐洲和美國的品牌。汽車產業佔西班牙國內生產總值的10%，幾乎與旅遊業相當。西班牙也是僅次於德國的歐洲第二大汽車出口國。
Facing the potential loss of that industry to electronic vehicles, Spanish officials are hoping the Chinese factories will provide Spanish workers with the technological know-how to become essential to the Continent’s new green production chains.
面對該行業可能被電動汽車取代的風險，西班牙官員希望中國企業在當地設廠能夠讓西班牙工人掌握相關技術知識，使他們成為歐洲新興綠色製造產業鏈不可或缺的一環。
廣告
But some worry that China will keep key parts of the tech secret from the workers. That would extend a power disparity between China and Spain, which is already experiencing a historically high trade imbalance with China that has doubled over the last six years. Many fear Ferrol will be used merely as an assembly plant, or warehouse for cars, to get around high tariffs on Chinese imports to Europe.
但也有人擔心，中國企業會對工人保密關鍵技術。這將進一步擴大中國與西班牙之間的實力差距。目前，西班牙對華貿易逆差已達到歷史高位，而且過去六年翻了一番。許多人擔心，費羅爾最終只會淪為中國汽車的組裝基地或汽車倉庫，幫助中國企業繞過歐洲對中國進口產品徵收的高額關稅。
Mr. Dezcallar said that when government officials have asked him about the SAIC plant in Ferrol, “I insist this has to involve a transfer of technology,” because Spain could not allow itself to just house Chinese assembly plants.
德斯卡利亞爾說，當政府官員向他詢問上汽在費羅爾的工廠時，「我堅持這必須涉及技術轉讓」，因為西班牙不能允許自己僅僅成為中國企業的組裝基地。
He argued that unlike Hungary, which has long been one of China’s preferred ports of call in Europe, Spain was in line with the goals and interests of the European Union, willing to call out its unfair trade imbalances and human rights violations. He also said that Spain was hardly an outlier given that muchof Europe was “fighting for the same thing,” an energy shot of Chinese investment.
他認為，與長期是中國在歐洲首選落腳點之一的匈牙利不同，西班牙與歐盟的目標和利益保持一致，願意公開批評中國不公平的貿易失衡和侵犯人權的行為。他還指出，西班牙並非特例，因為歐洲許多國家都在「爭取同樣的東西」，即通過中國投資為經濟注入活力。
While the Ferrol project enjoys support across the political spectrum, some have questioned if Mr. Sánchez has gotten too cozy with China.
儘管費羅爾項目獲得了西班牙各政治派別的支持，但一些人質疑桑切斯是否與中國走得過近。
The prime minister has gone to Beijing four times in four years and boasted in April that “we have raised political dialogue with China to its highest level in fifty-three years.”
這位首相四年內四次前往北京，並在今年4月自豪地宣稱：「我們已將與中國的政治對話提升到53年來的最高水平。」
Mr. Dezcallar, who has briefed Mr. Sánchez about China, said the prime minister — who has been a leading global critic of Mr. Trump’s strikes on Iran and his immigration policy — had not turned to Beijing as a replacement for Washington.
曾向桑切斯介紹中國情況的德斯卡利亞爾表示，這位首相——一直是國際上批評川普對伊朗發動打擊行動及其移民政策的主要聲音之一——並沒有把北京當作華盛頓的替代選項。
There was “for now,” Mr. Dezcallar said, no alternative to the United States, though “our fundamental values are being put into question by some American policies.”
德斯卡利亞爾說，「目前」還沒有任何國家能夠替代美國，儘管「美國的一些政策正在對我們的基本價值觀提出挑戰」。
廣告
But some deals have caused concern in Washington.
不過，一些交易已經引起華盛頓的擔憂。
In 2025, the U.S. House and Senate intelligence committees called for a review of classified information-sharing agreements with Spain after Madrid contracted China’s telecom giant, Huawei, considered a security risk by Washington, to digitally classify and store communications intercepted by the police. The left-wing Spanish government has insisted that the system posed no risk of China scraping the information, which pertained to judicial, rather than counterintelligence investigations.
2025年，美國眾議院和參議院的情報委員會呼籲審查與西班牙的機密情報共享協議，此前馬德里與中國電信巨頭華為簽約，由華為對警方截獲的通信進行數字分類和存儲。華盛頓認為華為構成安全風險。西班牙左翼政府堅稱，該系統不存在中國搜集這些信息的風險，這些信息涉及司法調查而非反間諜調查。
Not everyone is assured.
但並非所有人都感到放心。
“Where on earth do you have China getting access to the tapes of important police records?” asked Fidel Sendagorta, a former senior foreign ministry official under Mr. Sánchez. He said the contract was “very symbolic of how much the Spanish government was willing to give to China to get something back. And what is this something back?” he asked.
「世界上還有哪個地方會讓中國接觸到重要的警方錄音記錄？」曾在桑切斯政府擔任外交部高級官員的菲德爾·森達戈塔問道。他說，這份合同「極具象徵意義，表明西班牙政府為了從中國獲得某些回報，願意向中國作出多大讓步。而這是什麼回報？」他問道。
In August, a leading left-of-center daily, El País, reported that the defense ministry had raised concerns that Chinese intelligence services might use the Ferrol plant to spy on Spanish naval bases that house ships containing American-made missile interception systems.
今年8月，西班牙主要中左翼日報《國家報》報導稱，國防部曾提出擔憂，認為中國情報部門可能利用費羅爾工廠監視西班牙海軍基地，這些基地駐紮著配備了美國製造導彈攔截系統的軍艦。
Alfonso Rueda, the conservative leader of the surrounding Galicia region, complained that the defense ministry surprised him with “these pronouncements” and his office later said in a statement that the defense ministry informed them that its report on the car factory “will be favorable.” The defense ministry and a representative for SAIC declined to comment.
費羅爾所在的加利西亞自治區保守派領導人阿方索·魯埃達抱怨，國防部的「這些表態」讓他感到意外。隨後，他的辦公室發表聲明稱，國防部已通知他們，對這座汽車工廠的評估報告「將是正面的」。西班牙國防部以及上汽集團的一名代表均拒絕置評。
Locals, including Ferrol’s mayor, suggested jealous neighboring port towns planted the story, and few expressed concern.
包括費羅爾市長在內的當地人士暗示，這篇報導可能是眼紅費羅爾的周邊港口城市向媒體放出的風聲，當地幾乎沒人對此感到擔憂。
廣告
As Father Pedro Rodríguez Paz, 77, left the town’s main church near a docked battleship, he welcomed the plant as a chance to return to a “golden era” full of workers and “new buildings and more baptisms.”
77歲的佩德羅·羅德里格斯·帕斯神父走出市內主要的教堂時，對這座工廠表示歡迎。教堂附近停泊著一艘軍艦。他希望工廠能幫助費羅爾重返昔日的「黃金時代」，再現工人云集、「新建築增多、洗禮增多」的景象。
Similarly optimistic messages reverberated around the city’s naval museums, its guarded arsenal, octopus restaurants and on the blighted streets around Franco’s birthplace.
類似的樂觀言論迴盪在整座城市，從海軍博物館、戒備森嚴的軍械基地、供應章魚料理的餐館，到佛朗哥出生地點周圍破敗的街道，隨處可聞。
“The important thing is the work and money. It’s a question of a rescue,” María José Valle Leivas, 43, said in her bookstore. “Everyone here wants it.”
「重要的是工作和錢。這關係到拯救我們的城市，」43歲的瑪麗亞·何塞·巴列·萊瓦斯在她的書店裡說。「這裡的每個人都希望這座工廠落戶。」

Jason Horowitz是《紐約時報》馬德里分社社長，報導西班牙、葡萄牙以及歐洲各地人們的生活方式。
