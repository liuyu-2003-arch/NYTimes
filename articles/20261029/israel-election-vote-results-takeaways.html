以色列大選的四個初步結論

4 Takeaways From the Incomplete Israeli Election

DAVID M. HALBFINGER • 2026-10-29
The results of Israel’s pivotal parliamentary election will not be finalized at least until Thursday and it could be weeks until it is clear what kind of government will be formed and who will lead it. But some conclusions are already inescapable.
以色列這場關鍵議會選舉的最終結果至少要到週四才能揭曉，新政府將由哪些政黨組成、由誰領導可能還需要數週時間才能明朗。但有些結論已經顯而易見。
Arab citizens of Israel turned out to vote in record numbers. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu’s Likud party gobbled up many votes that his right-wing and Orthodox Jewish coalition partners had expected would be theirs. And the pre-election polls failed to anticipate both phenomena.
以色列阿拉伯裔公民的投票人數創下歷史新高。總理本雅明·納坦尼雅胡領導的利庫德集團奪走了大量原本被其右翼和猶太教正統派聯盟夥伴視為囊中之物的選票。而選前民調未能預見這兩種現象。
Much could still change. But with most of the votes counted by Wednesday afternoon, here are some initial takeaways.
局勢仍可能發生很大變化。不過，截至週三下午，大部分選票已經清點完畢，以下是對選舉結果的初步分析。
Netanyahu didn’t win, but Eisenkot lost.
納坦尼雅胡沒贏，但埃森科特輸了。
廣告
Mr. Netanyahu’s coalition appears to have shrunk considerably from the 64 parliamentary seats that it won in 2022: The latest results show it with around 54, well short of a majority in the 120-member Knesset, or Parliament. But he could wind up in better position to form a government than his chief rival, Gadi Eisenkot.
納坦尼雅胡的執政聯盟在議會中的席位似乎較2022年贏得的64席大幅減少。最新計票結果顯示，該聯盟拿下約54席，遠未達到120個席位組成的以色列議會的多數席位。不過，與主要競爭對手加迪·埃森科特相比，納坦尼雅胡最終組建政府的機會更大。
Mr. Eisenkot, a former Israeli military chief of staff, had two goals in the election: to lead the Jewish opposition parties to an outright majority or at least get very close to one, and to make sure his own party, Yashar, got more votes than any other. He failed on both counts, the initial results indicate.
曾任以色列國防軍總參謀長的埃森科特在此次選舉中有兩個目標：一是帶領猶太反對黨贏得議會絕對多數席位，或至少非常接近這一目標；二是確保自己領導的亞沙爾黨獲得比其他任何政黨都多的選票。初步結果顯示，這兩個目標均未實現。
Reuven Hazan, a political science professor at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, said that it had been hard to imagine better conditions for Mr. Eisenkot’s alliance ahead of the election. Mr. Netanyahu and his coalition were weighed down by anger over the Hamas-led Oct. 7, 2023 attacks and their failure to do more to free Israeli hostages; by anger over ultra-Orthodox Jewish draft-dodging in wartime; and by outrage over the government’s efforts to neuter the judiciary and allow growing settler violence against Palestinians in the Israeli-occupied West Bank, he said.
耶路撒冷希伯來大學政治學教授魯文·哈贊說，選前很難想像對埃森科特聯盟更有利的條件。他說，納坦尼雅胡及其聯盟背負著多重壓力：對2023年10月7日哈馬斯發動的襲擊以及政府未能採取更多行動解救以色列人質的憤怒；對戰時極端正統派猶太人逃避兵役的憤怒；以及對政府試圖架空司法、放任定居者在以占約旦河西岸不斷升級針對巴勒斯坦人的暴力行為感到憤慨。
“The anti-Netanyahu camp had an election handed to them on a silver platter, and they still lost,” Mr. Hazan said. “Israelis have sent an ambiguous message: ‘We don’t really like the current government, but we don’t see a viable alternative.’”
「反納坦尼雅胡陣營原本勝券在握，卻還是輸了，」哈贊說。「以色列選民傳遞了一個模稜兩可的信息：『我們並不真正喜歡現任政府，但也看不到可行的替代選擇。』」
Netanyahu’s coalition shrank, but he has options.
納坦尼雅胡的執政聯盟席位減少，但他仍有選擇。
As votes were being counted on Wednesday afternoon, Likud was poised to be the largest party in the larger of the two blocs of Jewish-led parties, meaning Mr. Netanyahu could well be given the chance to form a government, Mr. Hazan said.
哈贊說，週三下午計票期間，利庫德集團有望成為兩個由猶太人主導的政黨陣營中較大陣營的第一大黨，這意味著納坦尼雅胡很可能獲得組建政府的機會。
廣告
The long-serving Israeli premier may try to peel away some of Mr. Eisenkot’s putative partners in order to achieve a majority of 61 seats in Parliament. A number of them are as right-wing as he is, though all have more or less ruled out entering a Netanyahu-led government.
這位長期執政的以色列總理可能試圖爭取埃森科特陣營中的部分潛在盟友，以湊足議會多數所需的61個席位。其中一些政黨的右翼立場與納坦尼雅胡不相上下，但它們都已或多或少排除了加入納坦尼雅胡領導的政府的可能性。 
He could also try to form a national unity government with Mr. Eisenkot — perhaps with the assistance of President Trump, who has praised Mr. Netanyahu as a “warrior prime minister” and has made clear he wants Israel to join the United States in another round of war against Iran.
他還可能嘗試與埃森科特組建民族團結政府——或許在川普總統的協助下。川普曾稱讚納坦尼雅胡是「戰士總理」，並明確表示希望以色列與美國一道對伊朗再打一輪戰爭。
Perhaps the most mind-bending option available to Mr. Netanyahu, given that he ran a scaremongering campaign that demonized Arab lawmakers and stoked fears of Arab voter turnout, would be to recruit an Arab party to join him in a government. He nearly did so once before, in 2022.
考慮到納坦尼雅胡在競選期間不斷妖魔化阿拉伯裔議員，煽動選民對阿拉伯裔投票率上升的恐懼，他還有一個或許最令人難以置信的選擇：拉攏一個阿拉伯政黨加入自己的政府。他曾在2022年差點這樣做。
“It could very well be that they become indispensable,” said Dahlia Scheindlin, a pollster and political analyst, said of Arab lawmakers. “And Netanyahu has certainly demonstrated his willingness to do anything to stay in power.”
 「他們很可能變得不可或缺，」民調專家、政治分析師達莉婭·沙因德林談到阿拉伯裔議員時說。「而且納坦尼雅胡已經充分表明，為了繼續掌權，他什麼都願意做。」
The liberal camp still has a shot.
自由派陣營仍有機會。
Speaking of Jewish opposition parties in isolation ignores the fact that, when Arab parties are included, Mr. Netanyahu’s rivals appeared headed toward a significant majority in Parliament, with about 66 seats to roughly 54 for the prime minister’s bloc.
如果只看猶太人主導的反對黨，就忽略了一個事實：把阿拉伯政黨計算在內，納坦尼雅胡的對手似乎有望在議會中獲得顯著多數，約66席，而總理陣營約為54席。
廣告
“There is no need to go with the racist assumption that you need not count the Arabs,” said Gayil Talshir, a political scientist at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem who is advising the opposition.
「沒有必要抱著一種帶有種族歧視色彩的假設，認為阿拉伯人的席位不需要計算在內，」正在為反對派提供諮詢的耶路撒冷希伯來大學政治學家蓋伊爾·塔爾希爾說。 
That means the Knesset in effect will be controlled by the anti-Netanyahu opposition, regardless of who ends up being prime minister. If the vote breakdown roughly holds, Ms. Talshir said she expected Mr. Eisenkot and his Jewish and Arab allies to elect a new parliamentary speaker, begin repealing unpopular laws and at least try to form a government — perhaps including some combination of Arab and ultra-Orthodox lawmakers.
這意味著無論最終誰當總理，議會實際上將由反納坦尼雅胡的反對派控制。塔爾希爾說，如果各黨得票情況大體保持不變，她預計埃森科特及其猶太和阿拉伯盟友將選出新議長，開始廢除不受歡迎的法律，並至少嘗試組建政府——或許包括阿拉伯和正統派議員的某種組合。
“I’m not saying it’s a good scenario to take the Knesset and not be able to form the government,” Ms. Talshir said. “It’s a disaster. But this is the hand they’re dealt, and they have to play it.”
「我不是說拿下議會卻組不成政府是好局面，」塔爾希爾說。「這是一場災難。但他們手裡就是這麼一副牌，他們必須打下去。」
Arab voters broke records but could face rejection.
阿拉伯裔選民創下紀錄，卻可能面臨排斥。
Tuesday’s election appears to have had many losers. Mr. Netanyahu’s support shrank and Mr. Eisenkot came up short of his goal. The ultra-Orthodox parties were projected to lose seats, according to the latest returns, and two right-wing nationalist parties failed to make gains, despite four years in which they drove the Netanyahu government’s extreme policies in the West Bank.
週二的選舉似乎有許多輸家。納坦尼雅胡的支持縮水，埃森科特未達目標。最新計票顯示正統派政黨預計將失去席位，兩個右翼民族主義政黨也未取得進展，儘管它們四年來推動了納坦尼雅胡政府在約旦河西岸的極端政策。
The biggest ostensible winners were the Arab voters who voted in record numbers. But they could well wind up the most disappointed citizens of all, if the Jewish opposition leaders prove unwilling or unable to form a majority in an alliance with the Arab parties.
表面上的最大贏家是投票人數創下紀錄的阿拉伯選民。但如果猶太反對派領導人證明不願或無法與阿拉伯政黨結盟形成議會多數派，他們很可能最終成為最失望的群體。
廣告
“They’re saying, ‘We believe in Israeli democracy, we believe in the system, we want to be part of the system, and we want to be treated better,’” Mr. Hazan said. “And if Netanyahu forms a government, they’ll get none of that.”
「他們在說，『我們相信以色列民主，我們相信這個制度，我們想成為制度的一部分，我們希望得到更好的對待，』」哈贊說。「而如果納坦尼雅胡組建政府，他們的這些願望將一個也無法實現。」

David M. Halbfinger是時報耶路撒冷分社社長，負責以色列、加薩和西岸地區的報導。2017年至2021年他也擔任該職務，2021年至2025年任政治新聞編輯。
