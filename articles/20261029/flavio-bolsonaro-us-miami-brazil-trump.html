博索納羅敗選後逃離巴西抵達美國

Flávio Bolsonaro Flees to Miami, Declaring Himself Brazil’s Legitimate Leader

ANA LONOVA, 黃安偉, ALICE MACIEL, LUCÍA CHOLAKIAN HERRERA • 2026-10-29
Flávio Bolsonaro, the candidate defeated in Brazil’s elections, landed in Miami on Wednesday, declaring himself the legitimate leader of the nation he fled after Brazilian authorities asked him to surrender his passports as part of a money-laundering investigation.
在巴西選舉中落敗的候選人弗拉維奧·博索納羅週三抵達邁阿密，宣稱自己是巴西的合法領導人。巴西當局目前在一起洗錢調查中要求他交出護照，他隨後離開了巴西。
Mr. Bolsonaro arrived in the United States after apparently vanishing for a whirlwind 24 hours during which it was largely unclear where he was and how, or if, he would reach the United States.
博索納羅抵達美國之前，曾旋風般消失了24小時。這段時間裡，外界基本不清楚他在何處，也不清楚他如何、或者是否能夠抵達美國。
The State Department plans to issue visas allowing him and his family to enter the country, a senior department official said on Tuesday. The official spoke on the condition of anonymity to discuss sensitive diplomatic matters.
一名國務院高級官員週二表示，國務院計劃向他及其家人發放簽證，允許他們入境美國。該官員以匿名為條件討論這一敏感外交事務。
Just after 9:30 a.m., Mr. Bolsonaro could be seen leaving a plane at a private airport outside Miami along with his family, according to news media video footage. The group was ushered into an S.U.V. shortly after exiting the airport, hidden under umbrellas.
新聞媒體影片畫面顯示，上午9點30分剛過，博索納羅與家人一起從邁阿密郊外一個私人機場的飛機上走下。一行人離開機場後很快在雨傘遮擋下被送上一輛SUV。
廣告
Mr. Bolsonaro had embarked on the journey by crossing into Argentina, before traversing South America on a private plane to reach the United States.
博索納羅先進入阿根廷，隨後乘坐私人飛機穿越南美洲，抵達美國。
Mr. Bolsonaro, in a lengthy statement on social media on Wednesday afternoon, said he had left Brazil because he faced political persecution and thanked President Trump and President Javier Milei of Argentina. After initially declaring himself the “legitimate president-elect” of Brazil, Mr. Bolsonaro deleted his statement and reposted it using the word “leader” instead.
週三下午在社群媒體上發表的長篇聲明中，博索納羅稱自己離開巴西是因為面臨政治迫害，並感謝了川普總統和阿根廷總統哈維爾·米萊。他一開始曾宣布自己是巴西的「合法當選總統」，隨後刪除了聲明並重新發布，將「當選總統」改成了「領導人」。
“I address the Brazilian people and the international community as the true president-elect of Brazil,” Mr. Bolsonaro said. He claimed the election that he lost was fraudulent and that he was the subject of a “witch hunt” by Brazil’s judiciary.
「我以真正的巴西當選總統的身份向巴西人民和國際社會講話，」博索納羅說。他聲稱自己輸掉的選舉存在舞弊，並稱自己遭到巴西司法機構的「獵巫」。
The U.S. government’s assistance to Mr. Bolsonaro could escalate tensions between the Trump administration and President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva of Brazil, who has accused President Trump of interfering in his country’s internal affairs.
美國政府對博索納羅的協助可能加劇川普政府與巴西總統路易斯·伊納西奧·盧拉·達席爾瓦之間的緊張關係。盧拉指責川普干涉其國家內政。
Mr. Bolsonaro, a right-wing senator and the son of a former president serving a lengthy sentence for attempting a coup, lost Sunday’s presidential vote to Mr. Lula. After initially contesting the result, he conceded but claimed Mr. Lula had won through unfair tactics.
博索納羅是右翼參議員，也是一位因試圖發動政變而正在服長刑的前總統的兒子。他在週日的總統選舉中輸給了盧拉。他最初質疑結果，隨後承認敗選，但聲稱盧拉是通過不公平手段獲勝的。
Mr. Trump has also criticized the election, saying it appeared to have been “rigged.” When questioned about Mr. Bolsonaro on Wednesday, Mr. Trump struck a milder tone. “We gave him a pass to come into the country, so we’ll see,” he told reporters. “But I won’t get involved. It was a very strange election, that’s for sure.”
川普也批評了這次選舉，稱其似乎遭到了「操縱」。週三被問及博索納羅時，川普語氣較為溫和。「我們給了他入境許可，看看吧，」他告訴記者。「但我不會介入。這確實是一場非常奇怪的選舉。」
廣告
Following his loss, Mr. Bolsonaro seized on a change in the voting hours on Sunday to demand the election be annulled because of foul play. But the change affected only 0.03 percent of the vote and Brazil’s electoral authorities said it did not make any difference to the result, which saw Mr. Lula win by a margin of 4.4 million votes over Mr. Bolsonaro.
敗選後，博索納羅抓住週日投票時間的變化，以存在舞弊為由要求取消選舉。但這一變化隻影響了0.03%的選票，巴西選舉當局表示這對結果沒有影響——盧拉以440萬票的優勢擊敗了博索納羅。
In his statement on Wednesday, Mr. Bolsonaro doubled down on those complaints. He also insisted that Mr. Lula had unfairly used government funds to win, citing the leftist president’s move to raise a welfare stipend in the last stretch of the campaign. (Mr. Lula’s government claimed the increase was an overdue inflation adjustment.)
在週三的聲明中，博索納羅重申了這些不滿。他還堅稱盧拉不公平地利用政府資金獲勝，並提到這位左翼總統在競選最後階段提高了福利津貼。（盧拉政府稱，這一提高是遲來的通膨調整。）
Mr. Bolsonaro crossed from Brazil to Argentina by land on Tuesday night with his wife and two daughters, Argentina’s National Directorate of Migration said Wednesday. It said that he then boarded a private fight to Guayaquil, Ecuador. He flew from Ecuador to the United States.
阿根廷國家移民局週三表示，博索納羅週二晚間與妻子和兩個女兒從巴西經陸路進入阿根廷。該局稱，他隨後登上一架飛往厄瓜多瓜亞基爾的私人飛機，再從厄瓜多飛往美國。
On Monday, Brazilian authorities had asked Mr. Bolsonaro to hand over his passports within 24 hours because he is being investigated over his links to a Brazilian lawyer accused of laundering more than $1 billion through a scheme involving online gambling.
週一，巴西當局要求博索納羅在24小時內交出護照，原因是他與一名巴西律師的關聯正受到調查。該律師被指控通過一個涉及在線賭博的計劃洗錢超過10億美元。
Separately, Mr. Bolsonaro is also the subject of a police inquiry over millions of dollars he raised from a disgraced banker at the center of a huge corruption scheme to finance a biopic of his father, Jair Bolsonaro. The elder Mr. Bolsonaro was convicted of attempting a coup after losing the presidential election in 2022 to Mr. Lula and is serving his prison sentence under house detention.
另外，博索納羅還因從一名牽涉大規模腐敗窩案的失勢銀行家處籌集數百萬美元而受到警方調查，這筆錢用於資助他父親雅伊爾·博索納羅的傳記片。老博索納羅在2022年總統大選中輸給盧拉後被判試圖發動政變，目前正在軟禁中服刑。
When Mr. Bolsonaro didn’t comply by Tuesday morning, Brazilian authorities canceled his travel documents and issued an alert to immigration authorities barring Mr. Bolsonaro’s exit from the country. It was not immediately clear how he was able to cross the border into Argentina.
博索納羅週二上午之前沒有照做，巴西當局隨即取消了他的旅行證件，並向移民當局發出警報，禁止其離境。他如何得以越過邊境進入阿根廷，目前尚不清楚。
廣告
The Brazilian federal police said it had no official record of Mr. Bolsonaro exiting the country. The police also said, in a statement, that the restrictions on Mr. Bolsonaro’s travel and documents did not necessarily make him a fugitive or allow for his arrest. The police are now “investigating the circumstances surrounding the senator’s failure to comply with court orders.”
巴西聯邦警察表示，沒有博索納羅離境的官方記錄。警方還在聲明中稱，對博索納羅旅行和證件的限制並不一定使他成為逃犯，也不構成逮捕理由。警方目前正在「調查這名參議員未能遵守法院命令的相關情況」。
Argentina’s federal police also said they had not registered his entry. Still, Argentina’s immigration authorities said in a statement overnight that Mr. Bolsonaro, his wife and two daughters entered the country at 10:50 p.m. on Tuesday. They made the journey in a van, crossing what is known as the “Fraternity Bridge,” connecting Foz do Iguaçu in southern Brazil to Puerto Iguazú in Argentina.
阿根廷聯邦警察也表示沒有登記他的入境。但阿根廷移民當局在連夜發出的聲明中稱，博索納羅與妻子和兩個女兒於週二晚10點50分入境。他們乘坐一輛廂型車，通過連接巴西南部伊瓜蘇市與阿根廷伊瓜蘇港的「兄弟橋」過境。
The group crossed the border “with valid travel documentation and without any legal impediment to entering the country,” the Argentine immigration agency said in a statement. In addition to his Brazilian documents, Mr. Bolsonaro, who also holds Italian citizenship, had also been asked to turn over his Italian passport.
阿根廷移民局在聲明中稱，一行人過境時「持有有效旅行證件，入境不存在任何法律障礙」。博索納羅除持有巴西證件外，還擁有義大利公民身份，他也被要求交出義大利護照。
Some 40 minutes later, Mr. Bolsonaro and his entourage embarked on a plane owned by Baires Fly, a private company that has transported Mr. Milei in the past. Once in Ecuador, they took another flight early on Wednesday morning that was headed to Miami.
大約40分鐘後，博索納羅一行登上一架屬於Baires Fly的飛機。這是一家私人公司，過去曾運送過米萊。抵達厄瓜多後，他們於週三凌晨乘坐另一架飛機前往邁阿密。
Public U.S. government records suggest that Mr. Bolsonaro entered the United States with a canceled Brazilian diplomatic passport.
美國政府公開記錄顯示，博索納羅持一本已註銷的巴西外交護照進入美國。
At home, Mr. Bolsonaro’s abrupt and mysterious departure left many of his political allies perplexed. Ahead of his escape to the United States, his party had urged him to concede and harness the vast support he had drawn in Sunday’s election to lead the right-wing opposition in Brazil.
在國內，博索納羅突然神秘出走讓許多政治盟友感到困惑。出逃之前，他的政黨曾敦促他承認敗選，並利用他在週日選舉中獲得的廣泛支持領導巴西的右翼反對派。
廣告
On Monday morning, Mr. Bolsonaro signaled his intention to do just that. In a video posted to social media, he said he intended to comply with a court order to hand over his passports and claimed he had never considered leaving Brazil.
週一上午，博索納羅似乎正打算這樣做。在社群媒體發布的一段影片中，他表示打算遵守法院命令交出護照，並聲稱從未考慮離開巴西。
“We secured a majority in the Senate and the Chamber of Deputies — a victory you delivered for Brazil,” Mr. Bolsonaro told his followers.
「我們確保了參議院和眾議院的多數——這是你們為巴西帶來的勝利，」博索納羅對支持者說。
Mr. Bolsonaro vowed to turn Brazil’s legislature into a “fortress” guarding against Mr. Lula’s agenda.
博索納羅誓言要把巴西立法機構變成一座「堡壘」，以阻擋盧拉的議程。
Mr. Bolsonaro’s political party made major electoral gains and now holds the largest slice of seats in both chambers. Among those elected were Mr. Bolsonaro’s two brothers, his stepmother and an uncle.
博索納羅所屬政黨在選舉中斬獲頗豐，目前在參眾兩院都擁有最多席位。當選者中包括博索納羅的兩個兄弟、繼母和一位叔叔。
In the United States, Mr. Bolsonaro’s allies cheered his arrival online, claiming he will now lead an opposition movement while in exile.
在美國，博索納羅的盟友在網上為他的到來歡呼，聲稱他將在流亡中領導一場反對運動。
