伊朗衝突如何影響石油和天然氣供應

How War in the Middle East Is Choking Off the World’s Oil and Gas

BLACKI MIGLIOZZI, CHRISTIAAN TRIEBERT, PETER EAVIS, KEITH COLLINS, JACQUELINE GU, REBECCA F. ELLIOTT • 2026-03-05
Every day, around 80 oil and gas tankers typically pass through the Strait of Hormuz, the narrow waterway off Iran’s southern coast that carries a fifth of the world’s oil and a significant amount of natural gas.
通常，每天有約80艘油氣運輸船隻通過霍爾木茲海峽。這條位於伊朗南海岸的狹窄水道承載了全球五分之一的石油和相當一部分天然氣的運輸。
On Monday, just two oil and gas tankers appear to have crossed the strait, according to a New York Times analysis of shipping activity from Kpler, an industry data firm. On Tuesday, one tanker passed through.
通過分析行業數據公司Kpler的航運活動數據，《紐約時報》發現，週一似乎僅有兩艘油氣運輸船隻穿過該海峽，週二僅有一艘油輪通過。
“It’s a de facto closure,” said Dan Pickering, chief investment officer of Pickering Energy Partners, a Houston financial services firm. “You’ve got a significant number of vessels on either side of the strait but no one is willing to go through.”
休士頓金融服務公司Pickering Energy Partners的首席投資官丹·皮克林表示：「這實際上等同於封鎖。海峽兩側都有大量船隻滯留，但沒人願意冒險通過。」
Tankers have been staying away from Hormuz since the U.S.-Israeli attacks on Iran that began on Saturday. A prolonged conflict could ripple broadly across the global economy, threatening the energy supplies of countries halfway around the world and stoking inflation.
自上週六美以對伊朗發動襲擊以來，油輪一直遠離霍爾木茲海峽。長期衝突可能引發全球經濟廣泛的連鎖反應，威脅到半個地球之外國家的能源供應，並推高通膨。
廣告
International oil prices have climbed 12 percent since the fighting began, trading Tuesday around $81 a barrel, and natural gas prices have surged in Europe and in Asia.
自戰鬥開始以來，國際油價已上漲12%，週二交易價格在每桶81美元左右；同時，歐洲和亞洲的天然氣價格也大幅飆升。
A senior Iranian military official threatened this week to “set on fire” any ships traveling through the Strait of Hormuz. Vessels in the region have already come under attack. Several oil and gas facilities have also been struck or affected by nearby shelling, though the damage did not initially appear to be catastrophic.
一名伊朗將領本週威脅要將所有經過霍爾木茲海峽的船隻「付之一炬」。該地區已經有船隻遭到襲擊。數個石油和天然氣設施也遭到炮擊或受到影響，儘管損失初步看來不算太嚴重。
A fire broke out Tuesday at a major energy hub in Fujairah, United Arab Emirates, from the falling debris of a downed drone, the authorities said. On Monday, Qatar halted production of liquefied natural gas, or fuel that has been cooled so that it can be transported on ships, after attacks on its facilities.
阿聯酋當局表示，由於一架被擊落無人機的碎片墜落，阿聯酋富查伊拉的一個主要能源樞紐週二發生火災。週一，卡達在設施遭到襲擊後停止了液化天然氣的生產。液化天然氣是一種經過冷卻處理、便於船舶運輸的燃料。
The sharp reduction in tanker traffic is reducing the supply of oil and gas to world markets, pushing up prices for both commodities. And the longer that ships stay away from the Strait of Hormuz, the less oil and gas get out to the world, which could raise prices even more.
運輸流量銳減正在減少世界市場的油氣供應，推高了這兩種大宗商品的價格。船隻遠離霍爾木茲海峽的時間越長，運往全球的油氣就越少，這可能會進一步推高價格。
Shipping companies have paused their tankers to protect their crew and cargo, and because insurance companies are charging significantly more to cover vessels in the conflict area.
航運公司已暫停運輸船隻的航行，以保護船員和貨物，部分原因也是保險公司對衝突地區的船舶承保費用顯著增加。
On Tuesday, President Trump said that “if necessary,” the U.S. Navy would begin escorting tankers through the strait. He also said a U.S. government agency would begin offering “political risk insurance” to shipping lines in the area.
週二，川普總統表示，「如果有必要，」美國海軍將開始為通過海峽的運輸船隻護航。他還表示，一家美國政府機構將開始向該地區的航運公司提供「政治風險保險」。
廣告
In addition to tankers, other large vessels regularly go through the strait, including car carriers and container ships. In normal conditions, nearly 160 make the trip each day.
除油氣外，汽車運輸船和集裝箱船等其他大型船隻也會頻繁通過該海峽。在正常情況下，每天約有160艘此類船隻往返。
Some ships in the region turn off the devices that broadcast their positions, while others transmit false locations — making it hard to give a full picture of the traffic in the strait.
該地區的一些船隻會關閉廣播其位置的設備，有一些會發送虛假位置，這使得人們很難全面掌握海峽內的交通狀況。
The Shiva is a small oil tanker that has repeatedly faked its location, according to TankerTrackers.com, which tracks global oil shipments. It is suspected of carrying sanctioned Iranian oil, according to Kpler. The Shiva was one of the two tankers that crossed the strait on Monday.
根據追蹤全球石油運輸的TankerTrackers.com，濕婆號是一艘反覆偽造位置的小型油輪。Kpler公司懷疑該船運載的是受制裁的伊朗石油。它是週一穿過海峽的兩艘油輪之一。
The oil and gas that typically move through the strait come from big producing countries like Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Iran and United Arab Emirates, and are exported around the world.
通常流經該海峽的石油和天然氣來自沙烏地阿拉伯、伊拉克、伊朗和阿聯酋等主要產油國，並出口到世界各地。
In 2024, more than 80 percent of the oil and gas transported through the Strait of Hormuz went to Asia. China, India, Japan and South Korea were the top importers, according to the U.S. Energy Information Administration.
根據美國能源信息署的數據，2024年通過霍爾木茲海峽運輸的石油和天然氣中，有80%以上運往了亞洲，其中中國、印度、日本和韓國是最大的進口國。
Countries have energy stockpiles that could last them into the coming months, but a continued shutdown of the strait could damage their economies.
各國雖擁有可維持數月的能源儲備，但海峽持續關閉可能會損害其經濟。
廣告
Several big disruptions have roiled supply chains in recent years, but the tanker standstill in the Strait of Hormuz could have an outsize impact.
近年來，數次重大動盪擾亂了供應鏈，但霍爾木茲海峽的油氣運輸停擺可能會產生格外巨大的影響。
