戰火摧毀了緬甸，富人卻仍在享樂和傳播「正能量」

This Country Is Ravaged by War, but Its Rich Feel ‘Positive Vibes Only’

HANNAH BEECH • 2026-07-30
After midnight, amid a war, the D.J. hyped the crowd.
這是一個戰火瀰漫的國度。午夜已過，夜店裡的DJ正在賣力地調動人群的情緒。
“Are you ready to party?” he yelled into the mic, adding an expletive for emphasis.
「你們準備好開派對了嗎？」他對著麥克風大喊，還加了一句粗口來加強語氣。
The partygoers of Pioneer Plus, the most exclusive nightclub in the war-torn nation of Myanmar, were ready. The D.J. had come all the way from Croatia, after all. Young women gyrated onstage. Johnnie Walker flowed, but only Blue Label because that is the way these high-rollers roll. A private table for the evening — with hookah, imported fruit and pyramids of frosted shot glasses — costs hundreds of dollars to book.
緬甸這個飽受戰爭蹂躪國家裡最頂級的夜店Pioneer Plus的派對客們當然準備好了。畢竟，這位DJ可是從克羅埃西亞遠道而來的。年輕女孩們在舞台上扭動身體。尊尼獲加威士忌不斷供應，但只有藍牌，因為這些豪客就是這麼講究。一張當晚的私人包桌——包括水煙、進口水果以及磨砂酒杯金字塔——預訂費用高達數百美元。
In the bathrooms, and on the dance floor in stolen moments between flashes of the strobe, some denizens of Pioneer Plus swallowed pastel tablets that tinged their breath with vanilla or grape candy. The kick from these methamphetamine and caffeine concoctions, branded with the names of local drug syndicates, lasts for hours.
在洗手間裡，以及舞池中頻閃燈光掠過的間隙，一些Pioneer Plus的常客吞下彩色小藥丸，這些藥丸讓他們的呼吸帶有香草或葡萄糖果的味道。這種由冰毒和咖啡因調製而成的混合物以當地販毒集團的名字作為品牌，它們帶來的「勁頭」可以持續數小時。
Myanmar, which five years ago descended into full-scale civil war after a military junta overthrew the elected government, led by Daw Aung San Suu Kyi, is in desperate need of a diversion, a respite, a prolonged high.
五年前，軍政府推翻了由翁山蘇姬領導的民選政府，緬甸隨即陷入全面內戰。如今，這個國家迫切渴望某種消遣、一絲喘息，或者一種持續的飄然狀態。
No one in Myanmar is unaffected by the conflict, which has turned more than half the country into a battlefield but has largely spared the cities. Half the population is now impoverished. Drones, fighter jets and land mines bring horror to civilians. A military draft applies to both men and women. The Myanmar government is again an international pariah, just like it was during earlier decades of military rule.
緬甸沒有人能夠置身於這場衝突之外。戰事已將全國一多半土地變成戰場，但大城市基本倖免。如今，全國半數人口已陷入貧困；無人機、戰鬥機和地雷給平民帶來恐懼；徵兵令同時適用於男女。緬甸政府再次淪為國際棄兒，一如此前數十年軍事統治時期。
But even in war, life goes on. The partying class must party, and their consumption is conspicuous.
但即使在戰爭中，生活仍然繼續。愛派對的階層依然要尋歡作樂，他們的消費依然張揚。
廣告
International sanctions imposed by Western governments on military leaders and their business cronies — weapons dealers, drug lords, natural resource magnates, middlemen for Chinese enterprises — may have halted shopping jaunts to New York, Paris and London. Still, there’s always Dubai, Shanghai and Bangkok.
西方政府對軍方領導人及其商界盟友——軍火商、毒梟、自然資源大亨、中國企業的中間人——實施了國際制裁，這也許阻斷了他們去紐約、巴黎和倫敦的購物之旅。但還有杜拜、上海和曼谷可以去。
Yone Lay — the stage name means Little Bunny — is a regular at Pioneer Plus, which is in a tony district of Yangon, Myanmar’s largest city. He raps (“Stupid,” “Someone Who Was Unlucky in Love”), he acts (“Original Gangster,” “Underground”) and he lends his star power to military galas. He once made a music video praising U Min Aung Hlaing, the former military chief who orchestrated the coup five years ago and became Myanmar’s president in April.
永萊（這個藝名意為「小兔子」）是Pioneer Plus的常客，這家夜店位於緬甸最大城市仰光的一處高檔區域。他是說唱歌手（代表作有《愚蠢》、《情路不順的人》等），也演戲（《原始黑幫》、《地下》），他還利用自己的明星影響力為軍方舉辦的盛會助陣。他曾拍過一支音樂影片，讚美五年前策劃政變並於今年4月成為緬甸總統的前軍方首領敏昂萊。
When Mr. Yone Lay saunters into the nightclub, entourage in tow, security guards envelop him like a sheath of fine Myanmar silk. The cordon isn’t for show. Some celebrities who publicly cast their lot with the junta, rather than protesting the military’s killing of unarmed protesters, have found themselves targets of urban guerrillas. In 2023, one of Mr. Yone Lay’s acquaintances, Daw Lily Naing Kyaw, a singer who socialized with the generals, was killed by a bullet to the head in Yangon. Months before, Mr. Yone Lay was accosted by a man with a knife but escaped injury. A year later, a bomb exploded in his vehicle. No one was in the car at the time.
每當永萊帶著隨行人員來到這個夜店，保安便像裹上一層精美的緬甸絲綢般將他圍住。這道防線並非作秀。一些名人公開支持軍政府而非抗議軍方殺害手無寸鐵的示威者，他們已成為城市游擊隊的目標。2023年，永萊的一位熟人、與將軍們交往密切的歌手莉莉·奈因覺在仰光被子彈擊中頭部身亡。幾個月前，永萊曾遭一名持刀男子攔截襲擊，所幸未受傷。一年後，他的座駕被炸彈炸毀，事發時車內無人。
“Those people, they want to show that if you’re famous and connected to the military, we can kill you,” Mr. Yone Lay said one recent evening. “From the outside, people don’t realize that people like me are also oppressed.”
「那些人想要表明，如果你是名人，又和軍隊關係密切，我們就可以殺了你，」永萊在不久前的一個晚上說，「外人不明白，像我這樣的人其實也受到壓迫。」
Mr. Yone Lay, 40, said that it is easy to forget how much Myanmar has changed during his lifetime. Less than two decades ago, there was almost no internet. In secret, Mr. Yone Lay listened to Eminem, Dr. Dre and Snoop Dogg on bootlegged cassette tapes. But the advent of economic and political reforms in the 2010s, including elections in 2015 that ushered in a semi-civilian government, seemed to modernize the nation at warp speed. Mr. Yone Lay rapped about his concerns of that era: “Girls and parties and bling bling.”
40歲的永萊說，人們很容易忘記緬甸在他有生之年發生了多大變化。不到20年前，這裡幾乎沒有互聯網。他偷偷用盜版磁帶聽Eminem、Dr. Dre和Snoop Dogg的音樂。但2010年代開始的經濟和政治改革——包括2015年選舉後成立的半文官政府——似乎以驚人的速度使國家現代化。那時，永萊的說唱歌曲表達的是那個時代關注的話題：「女孩、派對和珠光寶氣。」
Now, he said, his worries are existential. He travels with bodyguards. The stress, he said, makes decompressing at the blingiest nightclubs mandatory.
如今，他說，他的憂慮關乎生死。他帶著保鏢出行。他說，壓力使得他必須去最奢華的夜店放鬆。
“We have to find ways to be happy,” Mr. Yone Lay said. “Why can’t we just love all our leaders and live peacefully?”
「我們必須找到讓自己快樂的方法，」永萊說。「為什麼我們不能愛我們所有的領袖，然後平靜地生活呢？」
廣告
Yangon, buzzing during the reform years with new construction and advertisements for investment opportunities and business English classes, has largely reverted to a state of decay. Western businesses have mostly left; billboards that once showed off American soft drinks and German sedans now display local instant coffee mixes and bathroom piping.
仰光在改革年代曾生機勃勃，到處都是新建築、投資機會和商務英語課程的廣告，如今已基本回到衰敗狀態。西方企業大多撤離，曾經展示美國軟飲和德國轎車的廣告牌如今換成了本土速溶咖啡和浴室管道產品的廣告。
Still, with enough squinting or blinkering, it is possible to pretend that life in Yangon is not so different from before the coup, at least for a privileged set. At The Yangon Restaurant, partly owned by the son of Mr. Min Aung Hlaing, the menu is showy European and studded with foie gras and caviar. With enough advance notice, a maître d’ said, a sommelier can be flown in from Singapore.
然而，只要肯眯起眼睛或選擇性無視，還是有可能假裝仰光的生活與政變前並無太大不同，至少對一群特權階層來說是這樣。在敏昂萊之子參股的仰光餐廳，菜單是華麗的歐式風格，上面列滿了各式鵝肝和魚子醬。一位餐廳經理說，只要提前足夠長時間預約，他們甚至可以安排侍酒師從新加坡飛過來服務。
On their social media accounts, Ma Christina Lim, 36, and her husband, Ko Thu Rayn Kyaw Moe, 38, parade their fabulous lifestyles in Yangon and beyond, at Club Med in the Maldives, at the Great Wall in China. There are color-coordinated outfits in leather and velvet, plus more than 200 brand collaborations with the likes of Samsung, Carlsberg and a local yacht club. On YouTube, Facebook and Instagram — the latter two are officially banned in Myanmar, requiring VPNs to access — the couple has garnered millions of views for baby gender reveals (a popped balloon cascading pink confetti for their firstborn, a girl) and photos with celebrities who are tight with Mr. Min Aung Hlaing’s family.
36歲的克里斯蒂娜·林和她38歲的丈夫圖瑞覺莫經常在社群媒體上展示他們在仰光以及國外的奢華生活，足跡包括馬爾地夫的Club Med度假村和中國長城。他們身著皮革與絲絨的同色系穿搭，與三星、嘉士伯及一家當地遊艇俱樂部等進行了200多次品牌合作。在YouTube、Facebook以及Instagram（後兩個平台在緬甸是官方明令禁止的，需要使用VPN才能訪問）上，這對夫婦獲得了數百萬瀏覽量，他們發布了揭曉嬰兒性別的影片（第一個孩子是女孩，氣球爆開後灑下粉色彩紙）以及與敏昂萊家族關係密切的名人合影。
“Our content, we like to stick to positivity, like positive vibes only,” Ms. Lim said. “You want viewers to feel good after watching our videos.”
「關於內容，我們喜歡保持正能量，只發正能量，」林女士說，「我們希望觀眾看完我們的影片後感覺心情愉快。」
In the days after the coup, Ms. Lim, like many members of Myanmar’s English-speaking jet set, posted photos on social media of the couple joining the protesters who peacefully marched to oppose the military intervention. Millions of people had taken to the streets nationwide to support the ousted civilian leader, Ms. Aung San Suu Kyi.
政變後的幾天裡，克里斯蒂娜·林和許多緬甸說英語的精英階層一樣，在社群媒體上曬出了夫婦兩人加入和平抗議隊伍的照片，當時全國數百萬人走上街頭，支持被推翻的文職領導人翁山蘇姬。
Within weeks, the military began killing protesters by gunshot — as well as children who happened to be nearby — and jailing dissenters. An armed resistance coalesced in the rainforests of Myanmar’s borderlands. Celebrities and influencers decried the military’s deadly crackdown. Ms. Lim posted on Facebook, the couple’s hands forming the three-finger gesture of resistance and memorializing “the martyrs who will never be able to return home.”
然而幾週之內，軍方開始開槍殺害抗議者——甚至包括碰巧在附近的兒童——並逮捕異議人士。隨後，一場武裝抵抗運動在緬甸邊境地區的熱帶雨林中悄然集結。明星和網路紅人紛紛譴責軍方的血腥鎮壓。克里斯蒂娜·林也曾在Facebook上發布照片，夫妻二人用雙手比出象徵抵抗的三指手勢，並紀念「永遠無法回家的烈士」。
Then, the couple went silent. In early 2022, in their first post since Myanmar’s civil war flared, Ms. Lim and Mr. Thu Rayn Kyaw Moe celebrated their wedding anniversary, outfitted in matching crimson. In the years since, the pair, whose families made their fortunes in real estate, paper, imported vehicles and cold storage, has eschewed politics, instead gushing over luxury cars, breast pumps and American vitamin gummies. They extol the best dishes at the best French restaurant in town. They lavish attention on a French bulldog named Blue, as well as their two children.
然後，這對夫婦沉默了。2022年初，他們在緬甸內戰爆發後的第一條社群媒體動態是慶祝結婚紀念日，兩人身穿同款深紅色服裝。此後，這對夫婦——他們的家族從事房地產、造紙、進口汽車和冷庫生意——避開政治話題，轉而熱衷於展示豪車、吸奶器和美國維生素軟糖。他們熱情推薦城裡最好的法國餐廳裡的招牌菜。他們把大量心思和精力都放在一隻名叫Blue的法國鬥牛犬以及兩個孩子身上。
“I feel like what happened happened,” said Ms. Lim, referring to the coup and its violent aftermath. She acknowledged that “it’s dark” — but because of chronic electricity shortages.
「我覺得，發生過的事情就讓它過去吧，」林女士談及政變及其之後的暴力時說。她承認「確實很黑暗」——不過她指的是總停電。
“At the same time,” she added, “people are just going to work regularly, going to school regularly.”
「與此同時，」她補充說，「人們還是照常工作，照常上學。」
廣告
Even their mundane posts attract big audiences, but perhaps that’s the point. A Facebook video in which Mr. Thu Rayn Kyaw Moe inexpertly fried eggs and boiled instant noodles received 1.1 million views.
甚至他們那些看似平常的帖子也能吸引大量觀眾，或許這正是賣點所在。圖瑞覺莫笨手笨腳煎雞蛋和煮方便麵的Facebook影片獲得了110萬次播放量。
The coup and civil war have thrown into relief the choices of Myanmar’s rich. In Mandalay, the nation’s second-largest city, the Chinese traders who once came for jade and other jewels are mostly gone. Occasionally, handmade bombs target military officers or people considered accomplices to the regime.
政變和內戰讓緬甸富人的選擇暴露在聚光燈下。在緬甸第二大城市曼德勒，曾經來採購翡翠和其他寶石的中國商人大多已離開。偶爾，自製炸彈會瞄準軍官或被視為軍政府幫凶的人。
Still, Ma May Nwe Htun, an actor, singer and social influencer better known as Sweet, said she had no interest in escaping Myanmar, where her family owns a gold business. Many friends have left for safer lives overseas, she said, but this is home, with its bustling teahouses and golden pagodas.
不過，演員、歌手以及網紅梅紐通（大家更熟悉她的藝名Sweet）表示，她沒有興趣離開緬甸。她的家族在緬甸經營黃金生意。她說，許多朋友已經離開，去海外尋找更安全的生活，但這裡是她的家，有熙攘的茶館，有金光閃閃的佛塔。。
“We gotta have fun, baby,” Ms. May Nwe Htun said. “We have to do our best if we want to stay in the country we love.”
「我們得開心起來，親愛的。」梅紐通說。「如果我們想留在自己熱愛的國家，就必須全力以赴。」
One recent evening, she threw a birthday party for her teenage son, who goes to an international school that has now lost most of its international students. There was a basketball-themed cake and a band of transgender entertainers. Overhead, in the hot, dark sky, fighter jets roared past on sorties to rebel-held territory, where civilians are terrorized by airstrikes. By the river in Mandalay, the thud of the bombing campaigns echoes in the night.
前不久的一個晚上，她為十幾歲的兒子舉辦生日派對，兒子就讀於一所國際學校，如今大部分國際學生都走了。現場有籃球主題蛋糕，還有一支由跨性別表演者組成的樂隊。頭頂，戰鬥機在炎熱漆黑的夜空中呼嘯而過，飛往反政府武裝控制的地區執行任務——那裡平民正遭受空襲恐懼。在曼德勒河畔，轟炸行動的沉悶聲響在夜晚迴盪。
Back in Yangon, at Pioneer Plus, the singer-girlfriend of Mr. Yone Lay, the rapper, downed shot after shot. A member of an ethnic minority group that has a large militia at war with the Myanmar military, she is afraid, she said, of being targeted as a turncoat for dating a musician close to the army regime. She spoke to The New York Times on the condition that her name not be used.
回到仰光的Pioneer Plus，說唱歌手永萊同樣為歌手的女友一杯接一杯地喝著酒。她來自一個少數民族，該族有一支正與緬甸軍隊交戰的武裝力量。她說，她害怕因為與一位與軍方關係密切的音樂人交往而被當作叛徒，並成為攻擊目標。她接受《紐約時報》採訪時要求不公開自己的姓名。

“The people outside, they only know that the military kills, they only know one side,” she said. “I just want to be an artist, not a businessman or politician.”
「外面的人，他們只知道軍方殺人，他們只看到事情的一面，」她說。「我只想做一個藝術家，不想做商人，也不想做政客。」
She stood up unsteadily, the alcohol muddling her movements. Her friends linked their arms with hers, supporting her.
她踉蹌著站起來，酒精使她的動作變得遲緩。朋友們挽住她的手臂，扶著她。
“Let’s dance,” she said, reaching out a manicured hand, her mascara smudged from earlier tears. “Let’s forget everything.”
「我們去跳舞吧，」她伸出一隻做過精緻美甲的手說，睫毛膏因早些時候的淚水已暈染開來。「讓我們忘掉一切。」
