紐西蘭外長攻擊華裔議員，中國抗議

Beijing Protests After New Zealand Minister Insults Chinese-Born Lawmaker

VICTORIA KIM • 2026-08-03
China has lodged a formal protest after New Zealand’s top diplomat told a Chinese-born lawmaker to “go back to your own country,” a comment that has also prompted calls at home for the minister’s dismissal.
紐西蘭外長在國會對一名出生於中國的議員說「回你自己的國家去」，這番言論在國內引發了要求其下台的呼聲，中國也提出了正式抗議。
The foreign minister Winston Peters, leader of the populist New Zealand First party and a longtime political kingmaker in New Zealand, made the remarks during a heated debate in the Parliament in Wellington on Wednesday in a retort to Lawrence Xu-Nan, a Green Party lawmaker whose family had immigrated when he was a child.
外長溫斯頓·彼得斯是民粹主義政黨紐西蘭優先黨的領袖，長期以來在紐西蘭政壇是能夠影響權力格局的關鍵人物。上週三，他在威靈頓國會的一場激烈辯論中對綠黨議員南西(Lawrence Xu-Nan)說出了這番話。南西幼年時隨家人移民紐西蘭。
Responding to a shouted question from Mr. Xu-Nan about whether he was vaccinated for Covid-19, Mr. Peters said: “I know you just came here five minutes ago. Go back to your own country, that’s where they lie like a flatfish. But they don’t lie like that here.”
南西大聲質問彼得斯是否接種過新冠疫苗，彼得斯回應道：「我知道你才來這裡五分鐘。回你自己的國家去，他們在那裡像比目魚一樣趴著，但這裡可不會。」
The Chinese Embassy in New Zealand said in a statement that it had “lodged solemn representations,” a phrase Beijing uses to signal it has made a formal complaint or protest, with the country’s foreign ministry, saying members of the Chinese diaspora were well-integrated and “deserve to be recognized and respected.” New Zealanders of Chinese heritage make up about 5.6 percent of the population, according to the latest census.
中國駐紐西蘭大使館發表聲明，表示已就此事向紐西蘭外交部「提出嚴正交涉」——這是北京方面慣用的措辭，用來表示已經正式提出投訴或抗議。聲明指出，紐西蘭華人華僑群體積極融入主流社會，「應當得到承認和尊重」。根據最近一次人口普查數據，華裔約佔紐西蘭總人口的5.6%。
廣告
Asked about Mr. Peters’s comments, Mao Ning, a spokeswoman for China’s foreign ministry, said, “It is hoped that relevant individual in New Zealand will do more things that contribute to the friendship between our two countries, and refrain from making China an issue in domestic politics.”
中國外交部發言人毛寧在回答相關提問時表示：「希望新方有關人士多做有利於促進兩國友好的事情，不要在國內政治中拿中國說事。」
On Chinese social media, some people were angered by Mr. Peters’s remarks, which they called racist. But others dismissed Mr. Xu-Nan’s connection to China, as he is not a citizen.
在中國社群媒體上，部分網友對彼得斯的言論表示憤慨，稱其具有種族主義色彩。但也有人不以為然，認為南西既然已經不是中國公民，就談不上和中國有什麼關係。
Prime Minister Christopher Luxon of New Zealand called Mr. Peters’s comments “very offensive and completely inappropriate,” and when asked by a reporter whether he thought the comment was racist, said he did.
紐西蘭總理拉克森稱彼得斯的言論「極具冒犯性且完全不恰當」。當記者問他是否認為這番話帶有種族主義色彩時，他給出了肯定的回答。
He nonetheless demurred on calls to fire him from an array of politicians, including a former prime minister, saying Mr. Peters, who is 81, was a successful foreign minister and that the decision was in the public’s hands with the general election three months away.
不過，面對包括一位前總理在內的多方人士要求解職彼得斯的呼聲，盧克森並未鬆口。他表示，現年81歲的彼得斯是一位成功的外交部長，且距離大選僅剩三個月，此事應交由公眾評判。
Mr. Luxon added that his party was focused on other priorities rather than “distractions and sideshows by populist politicians trying to bring attention to themselves.” Mr. Luxon’s National Party leads a coalition government with two smaller parties, including Mr. Peters’s New Zealand First.
拉克森還說，他的政黨目前專注於其他優先事項，而非「民粹主義政客試圖博取關注的干擾和噱頭」。拉克森領導的國家黨目前與兩個較小的政黨組建聯合政府，其中就包括彼得斯領導的紐西蘭優先黨。
Mr. Peters has refused to back down from or apologize for his comments, saying he was making the point that New Zealand was a democracy with free speech and rights.
彼得斯拒絕收回言論，也拒絕道歉。他說，自己想表達的是，紐西蘭是一個擁有言論自由和公民權利的民主國家。
廣告
“If that offends the pearl-clutchers and the communist shills labeling it as ‘dangerous’ — welcome to New Zealand,” he wrote in a social media post.
「如果這冒犯了那些大驚小怪的人，還有那些把這叫做『危險』的共產黨說客——那麼歡迎來到紐西蘭，」他在一條社群媒體帖子中寫道。
Mr. Peters is known for his brash rhetoric. He has previously made comments deriding or vilifying Asian immigrants and campaigned on lowering migration from Asia in the 1990s.
彼得斯以口無遮攔著稱，此前曾多次發表貶低或醜化亞裔移民的言論，並曾在1990年代以減少來自亞洲的移民為競選議題。
Mr. Xu-Nan, who the Green Party says is its first Chinese-born member of Parliament, told reporters after the parliamentary session that there needed to be accountability for “repeated behavior and racism from one particular member and a senior member” of Mr. Luxon’s cabinet.
綠黨稱南西是該黨首位出生於中國的國會議員。議會會議結束後，南西對記者表示，必須有人為拉克森內閣中「某位成員，而且還是一位資深議員一再出現的這種行為和種族歧視」承擔責任。
Wang Xiaolong, China’s ambassador to New Zealand, said in a social media post: “Sometimes, a statement says more about the person that makes it than anything or anybody else.”
中國駐紐西蘭大使王小龍在社群媒體上發文稱：「有時候，一個人說的話反映的往往是他自己，而不是他所評價的人或事。」

Berry Wang自香港對本文有報導貢獻。 

Victoria Kim是時報駐澳洲記者，常駐雪梨，報導澳洲、紐西蘭和泛太平洋地區。
