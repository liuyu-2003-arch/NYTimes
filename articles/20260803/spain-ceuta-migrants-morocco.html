休達移民危機：5萬人如何湧入西班牙非洲飛地？

How 50,000 People Surged Into a Spanish Exclave in Africa

JASON HOROWITZ • 2026-08-03
Like so many others in Morocco, Mohammed Ahmidho saw on his social media feeds early Thursday that the heavily guarded border his country shared with the Spanish territory of Ceuta was open.
像摩洛哥的許多其他人一樣，上週四早上，穆罕默德·阿米多在社群媒體動態中看到，他的國家與西班牙領土休達間戒備森嚴的邊境開放了。
Soon enough, he joined a crowd in the tens of thousands, many of whom were astonished to see Moroccan police urge them, as he recalled, to “come to Spain.” Some climbed over the fence, as he did, or dove through holes in the chain-link, or swam across the maritime border. Some wore wet-suits, sometimes kicking with flippers, or floated to the Spanish coast in orange life preservers, pink inflatable doughnuts around their waists. At least 60 people died in the mayhem.
很快，他加入了數以萬計的人潮。據他回憶，許多人驚訝地發現，摩洛哥警察竟催促他們「快去西班牙」。有些人像他一樣翻過柵欄，有人鑽過鐵絲網的破洞，還有人游過海上邊界。有些人穿著潛水服，有時還拍打腳蹼，有人穿著橙色的救生衣、腰間套著粉紅色的充氣救生圈，漂浮到西班牙海岸。至少有60人在混亂中喪生。
“A lot of people came,” said Mr. Ahmidho, 37. “A lot.”
「來了很多人，」37歲的阿米多說。「非常多。」
He was among roughly 50,000 people who crossed the border into a Spanish town of only 84,000 residents on Thursday and early Friday, according to the Spanish interior ministry. That was a more intense surge than in any equivalent period during the peak of the European migration crisis in 2015.
根據西班牙內政部的數據，週四和週五早些時候，大約有5萬人越境進入這個只有8.4萬居民的西班牙小鎮，他就是其中之一。這比2015年歐洲移民危機高峰期任何同時段的激增都要猛烈得多。
廣告
By Friday morning, the city looked like the emergency that Europe has been trying to avoid since that era of mass migration 11 years ago, the last time so many non-Europeans surged into European-held territory, upended the continent’s politics and spurred the rise of its anti-immigrant right.
到了週五早上，這座城市呈現出的景象正是歐洲自11年前那場大規模移民潮以來一直試圖避免的緊急狀況。那一次，眾多非歐洲人湧入歐洲管轄的領土，顛覆了歐洲的政治格局，並且刺激了歐洲反移民右翼勢力的崛起。
As the city’s residents hunkered down and shuttered their stores, groups of mostly young men with nowhere to go filled squares and avenues, condominium courtyards, hilltop overlooks and bridge underpasses. Some took dips in waters patrolled by a Spanish naval ship or waded through a band of floating detritus — plastic bags and water bottles — caught in the current. Others marched across a beach littered with the things their fellow arrivals had carried: single shoes, a shed wet-suit, mobile phone cases, an I.D. card from Casablanca.
城市居民躲在家裡，關掉店鋪，成群結隊無處可去的人湧入了廣場、街道、公寓庭院、山頂觀景台和橋下通道，其中大部分是年輕男子。有人跳進西班牙軍艦巡邏的水域裡游泳，有人淌過被水流裹挾的漂浮垃圾帶，裡面滿是塑料袋和水瓶。還有人走過海灘，那裡散落著同行者們遺落的物品：單只的鞋子、脫下的潛水服、手機殼、一張來自卡薩布蘭卡的身份證。
Spanish soldiers, many of whom had been mobilized on Thursday to deal with the crisis, stood on nearby tanks. They watched the migrants drink water from beach showers, each shaped like vertical surfboards and branded with the slogan, “Ceuta, where you feel the emotions.”
許多在週四被動員來應對危機的西班牙士兵站在附近的坦克上。他們看著移民從海灘淋浴器中喝水，這些淋浴器形似垂直的衝浪板，上面印著標語：「休達，觸動心靈。」
Nearby, young teens laughed under the whirl of patrol helicopters as they tried out the exercise equipment at a beach playground. Mr. Ahmidho wore a pink bathing suit and stood bare-chested, his black T-shirt slung over his shoulder. The soles of his feet were cut, he said, from clambering over rocks near the border.
在附近，幾名十幾歲的少年在巡邏直升機盤旋的轟鳴聲中開懷大笑，試玩海灘遊樂場的健身器材。阿米多穿著粉紅色的泳褲，赤裸著上身，黑色T恤搭在肩膀上。他說，他的腳底在攀爬邊境附近的岩石時被割破了。
His voyage had been a disappointment, he said. Like so many, he had come to find work, only to find soldiers and policemen harrying him to leave. “There’s no water, there’s no food, there’s no place to sleep,” he said. “There’s nothing.”
他說，他的這趟旅程令人失望。像許多人一樣，他來是為了找工作，結果卻發現士兵和警察不斷驅趕他離開。「沒有水，沒有食物，沒有睡覺的地方，」他說。「什麼都沒有。」
As the migrants took over the streets, residents mostly stayed hidden in their homes, keeping their shops and businesses closed.
隨著移民佔據了街道，居民大多躲在家裡，店鋪關門歇業。
廣告
Emerging briefly, Francisco Pérez, 68, rushed his grandchildren into car seats as the migrants walked around him. He said his granddaughter had asked why they couldn’t go to the beach, and why there were “so many Moroccans.” The locals, he said, were “scared” and furious with the Spanish government, which, he said, had abandoned them.
68歲的弗朗西斯科·佩雷斯短暫地走出家門，移民從他身邊走過時，他急忙把孫子孫女塞進汽車裡。他說，孫女問他為什麼不能去海灘，以及為什麼有「這麼多摩洛哥人」。他說，當地人感到「害怕」，並對西班牙政府感到憤怒，他認為政府拋棄了他們。
Pedro Sánchez, the Spanish prime minister, tried to avert such reactions by rushing to Ceuta on Friday morning to show his support for its citizens and also to condemn the influx as a “violation of Spain’s territorial integrity.”
西班牙首相佩德羅·桑切斯試圖避免這種反應，他於週五上午趕赴休達，表達對當地公民的支持，並譴責這種湧入是「對西班牙領土完整的侵犯」。
For Mr. Sánchez’s right-wing critics — both at home, around Europe and throughout the world, including at the White House — the surge of migrants was an indictment of his pro-immigration policies. His government allowed more than one million migrants illegally living in Spain to apply for legal residency this year — a move that his opponents said had helped to entice the migrants this week.
對於桑切斯先生的右翼批評者來說——無論是在國內、歐洲各地還是在包括白宮的全世界，移民激增是對其親移民政策的控訴。他的政府今年允許在西班牙非法居住的超過100萬移民申請合法居留權，反對者說，此舉助長了本週的移民潮。
To opponents of immigration, the footage of migrants sweeping into the tiny Spanish territory amounted to Exhibit A for their case. Ceuta, all of a sudden, became an international emblem for the perils of unchecked illegal immigration.
對移民反對者來說，移民湧入這塊小小西班牙領土的畫面無異於他們論點的首要證據。休達突然成了無節制非法移民危險性的國際象徵。
In response, Mr. Sánchez blamed human traffickers for misleading the migrants about how easy it would be to claim asylum in Spain — and noted that, despite his migrant-friendly policies, Spain has attracted fewer illegal migrants in recent years than in some European neighbors.
作為回應，桑切斯先生指責人口販子誤導移民，讓他們以為在西班牙申請庇護很容易，同時指出，儘管他採取了對移民友好的政策，但近年來西班牙吸引的非法移民少於一些歐洲鄰國。
It is still unclear why so many people came to Ceuta. Many of the Moroccans who wandered around Ceuta on Friday wondered if the doors hadn’t opened because of some unknown plan by Morocco, which has been accused of using immigrant flows in the past for political pressure.
目前尚不清楚為什麼會有這麼多人來到休達。週五在休達遊盪的許多摩洛哥人想知道，邊境大門之所以敞開，是否是因為摩洛哥的某個未知計劃——摩洛哥過去曾被指控利用移民潮施加政治壓力。
廣告
“I don’t know why they let us through,” said Ajoub el-Arrot, 24, who said he heard about the open border on social media. “Maybe they had some political reason. They opened the border and let everyone go by.”
「我不知道他們為什麼放我們過去，」24歲的阿喬布·埃爾-阿羅特說，他表示自己是在社群媒體上聽說了邊境開放的消息。「也許他們有一些政治原因。他們開放了邊境，讓所有人都過去了。」
He added, “When there are political problems, Morocco opens the doors and tells us to go.”
他還說：「當出現政治問題的時候，摩洛哥就會打開大門，讓我們離開。」
Douae Tabit, 19, who also said she heard about the unchecked crossing on social media, said it went “viral” and passed by word of mouth, attracting people from all around Morocco. Fluent in English, French and Spanish, she said she worked as a nurse and photographer and spontaneously decided to take advantage of the opportunity.
19歲的杜阿·塔比也說她是在社群媒體上看到了這一無限制越境的消息。她說，消息像「迅速瘋轉」，並且口口相傳，吸引了摩洛哥各地的人。她能說流利的英語、法語和西班牙語，並表示自己是護士兼攝影師，臨時決定抓住這個機會。
She climbed over a fence and swam to the Spanish shore, she said, but she had expected something more welcoming: No one had food, water or a place to sleep. Still, she said, “I’m not going back.“
她說，她翻過柵欄游到了西班牙海岸，但她原本期待會受到更熱情的歡迎：這裡沒有人提供食物、水或睡覺的地方。儘管如此，她還是說：「我不會回去了。」
Many seemed not to know exactly what they were doing there.
許多人似乎並不清楚自己到底為何來到這裡。
Young men, shirtless or in soccer jerseys, marched barefoot or sometimes in a single sandal, as if in an aimless parade. One teenager gently knocked on the grate of a closed food market while others hovered longingly around the city’s locked supermarkets.
赤膊或穿著足球服的年輕人們光著腳，有的人只穿著一隻涼鞋，彷彿在進行一場毫無目的的遊行。一名少年輕輕敲打著已關閉的食品市場的鐵柵欄，還有人在城中大門緊鎖的超市周圍滿懷渴望地徘徊。
廣告
As Friday morning turned to afternoon and the harsh sun burned away the clouds, men sought shade wherever they could. One group gathered under a hulking statue of Hercules near the port terminal. Others leaned against gas station pumps or reclined under the tents of abandoned stalls that had been raised for a local food fair. Still more looked in trash bins for food, or begged passers-by for water. One woman fainted by a Zara store.
到了週五午後，刺眼的陽光驅散了雲層，人們四處尋找能遮陽的地方。一群人聚集在港口碼頭附近一尊巨大的赫拉克勒斯雕像下。還有人靠在加油站的油泵邊，或者躺在為當地美食節搭建的、已經廢棄的攤位帳篷下。更多人在垃圾桶裡翻找食物，或者向路人討水喝。一名女子在一家Zara門店旁暈倒。
Sulayman Ahmed, 15, a resident of Ceuta who is of Moroccan descent, said he couldn’t believe that Morocco was letting so many people through.
15歲的蘇萊曼·艾哈邁德是一名有摩洛哥血統的休達居民，他說自己不敢相信摩洛哥竟然讓這麼多人過來。
“They have to put more resources to stop people from coming,” he said, complaining that the appearances of politicians at the border, including that of Mr. Sánchez, amounted to “theater.”
「他們必須投入更多資源，阻止人們過來，」他說，並抱怨包括桑切斯在內的政界人士在邊境的露面無異於「做戲」。
As the day progressed, it became clear many of the migrants were leaving. By the boardwalk, they hovered around Spanish soldiers who had hooked a mobile phone to stereos that played messages in Arabic telling them to go home. Other Spanish soldiers and police officers threatened the migrants, waving their sticks to push them back to the border. In town, a roving band of young Spanish men chased down and intimidated Moroccan stragglers.
隨著時間推移，顯然許多移民正在離開。在木板人行道旁，他們徘徊在西班牙士兵周圍，士兵將手機連接到立體聲音響上，播放阿拉伯語廣播，告訴他們回家。還有些西班牙士兵和警察揮舞警棍威脅移民，將他們趕回邊境。在鎮上，一群遊盪的西班牙年輕男子追逐並恐嚇滯留的摩洛哥人。
Even as a sprinkling of late arrivals waded through the water, a mass return was underway.
即使仍有零星的晚到者涉水而過，一場大規模的遣返行動已經在展開。
By 6 p.m., 48,300 people had gone back across the border, according to Spain’s interior ministry. They crossed into a small beach between the countries’ border fences, its sands strewed with clothes and plastic bags, as Spanish soldiers yelled for migrants to keep moving out of Ceuta. Then they walked past iron gates topped with barbed wire and floodlights, back into Morocco.
根據西班牙內政部的數據，截至週五下午6點，已有48300人越境返回。他們越過兩國邊境圍欄之間的一片小海灘，沙灘上散落著衣服和塑料袋，西班牙士兵大聲喊叫著讓移民繼續離開休達。隨後，人們走過頂部裝有鐵絲網和探照燈的鐵門，回到摩洛哥。
廣告
Mr. Ahmidho moved in the long line, deeply disappointed with his adventure across the border.
阿米多走在長長的隊伍中，對這次跨越邊境的冒險深感失望。
“They had nothing for us,” he said. “They were screaming, ‘Get out of Spain.’”
「他們什麼也沒給我們，」他說。「他們叫喊著，『滾出西班牙。』」

Jason Horowitz是時報馬德里分社社長，報導西班牙、葡萄牙新聞以及歐洲各地人們的生活。
