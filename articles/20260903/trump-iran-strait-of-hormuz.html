川普對伊朗已套路用盡、無牌可打？

Trump Returns to a Familiar Playbook as the War With Iran Flares Up Again

ERICA L. GREEN • 2026-09-03
As the on-again, off-again war in Iran flares up again, President Trump seems bogged down in a conflict that he cannot control or move past, despite his declarations of total victory.
隨著打打停停的伊朗戰爭再度升級，川普總統似乎陷入了一場他既無法控制、也無法脫身的衝突之中，儘管他一再宣稱已經取得全面勝利。
On Tuesday night, after the latest exchange of fire over control of the Strait of Hormuz, Mr. Trump called on the Iranian people to rise up — something he did at the outset of the war six months ago, before he acknowledged that his command was easier said than done.
週二晚間，在雙方圍繞霍爾木茲海峽控制權再次交火後，川普呼籲伊朗人民起來反抗——六個月前戰爭剛爆發時，他也曾發出過同樣的呼籲，後來他承認，下令讓伊朗人起義說起來容易做起來卻很難。
In a social media post, Mr. Trump also brushed off the stalled negotiations with Iran.
在一條社群媒體帖子中，川普還對陷入僵局的美伊談判輕描淡寫。
“I couldn’t care less if they sign a worthless, to them, agreement,” he wrote on Tuesday night, hours after the United States carried out a fresh wave of attacks and Iran hit back.
「我完全不在乎他們是否簽署一份對他們來說毫無價值的協議，」他在週二晚寫道。數小時前，美國剛發動了新一波攻擊，伊朗也予以了回擊。
廣告
“I like our position now much better, with almost total control of the Hormuz Strait, and their economy totally collapsing,” Mr. Trump continued. “They are just playing out the inevitable. When are the Iranian people going to rise up and fight?”
「我更喜歡我們現在的處境，幾乎完全控制了霍爾木茲海峽，而他們的經濟正在全面崩潰，」川普接著寫道。「他們只是在拖延一個不可避免的結局。伊朗人民什麼時候才能奮起反抗？」
The post followed a 24-hour stretch during which Mr. Trump’s by-now familiar playbook for the conflict was on full display. The president went from boasting that Iran was defeated militarily, a “failed nation” in economic disarray, to threatening an apocalyptic attack that would leave “very little left” of the country after it had defied his declarations and demands.
在這篇帖文發布前的24小時裡，川普在這場衝突中迄今已為人所熟知的套路展現得淋漓盡致。他先是吹噓伊朗在軍事上已被擊敗，是一個經濟混亂的「失敗國家」；在伊朗無視他的聲明和要求之後，他又威脅要對伊朗發動末日式攻擊，讓這個國家「幾乎什麼都剩不下」。
By Wednesday morning, as Iran and the United States assessed the damage of the most recent attacks, Mr. Trump was back to trolling the country on social media, asking his followers whether the United States should name the Strait of Hormuz the “Trump Strait.”
到了週三上午，當伊朗和美國正在評估最新一輪襲擊造成的損失時，川普又回到社群媒體上嘲諷伊朗，問他的粉絲們美國是否應該把霍爾木茲海峽改名為「川普海峽」。
Foreign policy experts say the latest round of hostilities point to a reality that has become increasingly clear of Mr. Trump’s handling of the war: He has run out of options.
外交政策專家表示，最新一輪敵對行動暴露出川普處理這場戰爭時一個日益清晰的事實：他已經沒有多少牌可打了。
“The strategy has been rinse and repeat, whether it’s rhetorical or kinetic action,” said Matthew Bartlett, a Republican strategist and former State Department appointee in the first Trump administration. “It’s been absolutely shocking how the United States has been exposed for a lack of a plan of engagement, and a lack of a plan to either win or exit this war.”
「不管是在口頭上還是實際行動上，他的策略一直都是翻來覆去地重複同一套，」共和黨策略師、川普第一任總統任期內曾任國務院官員的馬修·巴特利特說。「在應對這場戰爭方面，美國既缺乏作戰計劃，也沒有贏得或退出這場戰爭的計劃，這實在令人震驚。」
Mr. Bartlett said that Mr. Trump’s return to a call to action urging Iranians to take over the government — an unfulfilled objective of the war that Mr. Trump has admitted was not achievable — suggests he has no real strategy.
巴特利特說，川普再次呼籲伊朗人採取行動、推翻本國政府說明他實際上已經沒有真正的戰略了。這是戰爭中一個始終未能實現的目標，川普自己也承認這一目標無法實現。
廣告
“When you get to the bottom,” he added, “you go back to the top of the ladder of engagement.”
「當所有手段都用完了，」他說，「就只能回到原點，把同樣的套路再走一遍。」
But regardless of Mr. Trump’s combative words, Iran is making clear that it has leverage, as well. Even though Mr. Trump had predicted that the war would be over in a matter of weeks, there is no real end in sight. Iran has fired on ships passing through the Strait of Hormuz, severely curbing the ability of U.S. allies to export oil and gas.
但無論川普的言辭多麼好鬥，伊朗也在表明它同樣掌握著籌碼。儘管川普曾預測戰爭將在幾週內結束，眼下卻根本看不到真正結束的跡象。伊朗已向通過霍爾木茲海峽的船隻開火，嚴重限制了美國盟友出口石油和天然氣的能力。
That is not to say that the U.S. operations in Iran have not hurt the country. Michael Froman, the president of the Council on Foreign Relations, said that it was undeniable that Iran was in a fundamentally weaker position than it was a few years ago, and that the U.S. military had achieved many of the war’s objectives.
當然，這並不是說美國在伊朗的軍事行動沒有對伊朗造成打擊。美國外交關係委員會主席麥可·弗羅曼表示，不可否認，伊朗目前的處境從根本上說已經比幾年前糟糕得多，美軍也已經實現了戰爭中的許多目標。
In the first weeks of the operation, the United States took out senior leaders, including Ayatollah Ali Khamenei, and decimated Iran’s navy. Mr. Trump has also claimed that the country has “no Air Force, they have no currency, they are not paying their soldiers or police, Inflation is at 300%, and their leadership is in total disarray and incapable of properly representing the country.”
在行動最初幾週，美國消滅了包括最高領袖阿亞圖拉阿里·哈梅內伊在內的多名高級領導人，重創了伊朗海軍。川普還宣稱，伊朗已經「沒有空軍，沒有貨幣，發不出士兵和警察的工資，通膨率達到300%，而且領導層徹底陷入混亂，無法有效代表這個國家」。
But Iran, now led by a more hard-line regime, has proved that it can disrupt the global economy even with a weakened or limited military, by wielding its leverage over the Strait of Hormuz.
但如今由一個更加強硬的政權領導的伊朗已經證明，即使軍事力量遭到削弱或受到限制，它依然可以憑藉自己對霍爾木茲海峽的控制力擾亂全球經濟。
“The U.S. has demonstrated its capacity, and that it continues to have one of the most effective militaries in the world,” Mr. Froman said. “I think the challenge is this asymmetric warfare — where we do have the most effective military in the world, but smaller powers with far less capacity, strategically deployed, could still wreak havoc and make it difficult for us to achieve 100 percent of our objectives.”
「美國已經展示了自己的實力，也證明了它仍然擁有世界上最有效的軍事力量之一，」弗羅曼說。「但我認為，真正的挑戰在於這種非對稱戰爭——我們的確擁有世界上最強大的軍事力量，但那些能力遠遜於我們的較小國家，只要有策略地加以運用，仍然能夠造成嚴重破壞，讓我們很難實現百分之百的戰爭目標。」
廣告
Mr. Trump has recently sought to move on from a military campaign in Iran by waging economic warfare.
川普最近試圖通過打一場經濟戰來擺脫對伊朗的軍事行動。
The United States has sought to squeeze Iran economically by enforcing a naval blockade on Iranian ports and increasing sanctions on Tehran. The administration is also banking on a so-called “economic D-Day” announced last month by Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, which the White House described “an unprecedented campaign to sever every remaining economic lifeline sustaining the Islamic Republic of Iran.”
美國試圖通過對伊朗港口實施海上封鎖以及加強對德黑蘭的制裁，從經濟上進一步擠壓伊朗。川普政府還寄希望於美國財政部長史考特·貝森特上個月宣布的所謂「經濟D日」。白宮將其描述為「一場前所未有的行動，旨在切斷維持伊朗伊斯蘭共和國生存的所有剩餘經濟命脈。」
The campaign is intended to exert pressure on other countries, including potentially China and Russia, that continue to trade with Iran or help it evade U.S. sanctions.
這場行動的目的是向那些仍在與伊朗進行貿易、或幫助伊朗規避美國制裁的其他國家施壓，其中可能包括中國和俄羅斯。
But few countries have shown any indication that it plans to heed the calls, including the two U.S. adversaries Mr. Trump has been reluctant to confront.
但到目前為止，幾乎沒有哪個國家表現出願意聽從美國要求的跡象，包括川普一直不願正面應對的這兩個美國對手。
China remains a lifeline for Iran, buying its oil and supplying it with raw materials and technology that support its economy and military. And just this week, President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia pledged that his country would continue to support Iran and help them defend their national interests.
中國仍然是伊朗的生命線，購買石油並向其供應支撐經濟和軍事的原材料和技術。就在本週，俄羅斯總統普丁承諾，俄羅斯將繼續支持伊朗，並幫助其捍衛國家利益。
Mr. Trump is also feeling the political price at home, in terms of the toll on the American economy ahead of the midterm elections. He is reluctant to resume an all-out bombardment, even though he issues regular threats, including one on Tuesday night announcing that the United States was hitting Iranian targets near the Strait of Hormuz.
與此同時，川普在國內也開始感受到這場戰爭帶來的政治代價，尤其是在期中選舉臨近之際，美國經濟所承受的壓力。他不願重新發動全面轟炸，儘管他仍不斷發出威脅，包括週二晚間，他宣布美國將打擊霍爾木茲海峽附近的伊朗目標。
廣告
“If the failed Nation of Iran retaliates for this very justified attack,” he wrote, “they will be hit again at a much harder and higher level, but it will not be the biggest attack of them all, that is waiting in the wings and, when it is over, there will be very little left of the Islamic Republic of Iran!”
「如果這個失敗的伊朗國家因為這次完全正當的攻擊而進行報復，」他寫道，「他們將再次遭到打擊，力度更大、級別更高，但這不會是最大的一次打擊——最猛烈的打擊已經伺機待發；等它結束之後，伊朗伊斯蘭共和國將幾乎什麼都不剩！」
Khaled Elgindy, a senior research fellow in the Middle East program at the Quincy Institute for Responsible Statecraft and adjunct instructor at Georgetown University’s Center for Contemporary Arab Studies, said that Mr. Trump’s bluster on social media is attempting to save face for the quagmire he has put himself, and Republicans heading into the midterms, in.
昆西治國方略研究所中東項目高級研究員、喬治城大學當代阿拉伯研究中心兼職講師哈立德·埃爾金迪表示，川普在社群媒體上虛張聲勢，是在試圖為自己——以及即將迎來期中選舉的共和黨——陷入困局挽回顏面。
“Part of the play is that he’s just playing to his base, whether it’s true or not,” Mr. Elgindy said. “I don’t think there’s much of a strategy beyond trying to wish things into existence by sheer force of words.”
「他這套做法的部分原因就是為了迎合他的基本盤——不管他說的是真是假，」埃爾金迪說。「我不認為這裡面有什麼真正的戰略，除了試圖靠言語本身的力量，把自己希望發生的事情硬生生說成現實。」

Erica L. Green是時報白宮記者，報導川普總統及其政府。
