深入剖析川普第二任期：誰應對他最成功？

Inside Trump’s Second Term

《紐約時報》 • 2026-09-03
If you want to understand the moment we’re in, you have to understand Donald Trump. That’s easier said than done; Trump thrives on drama — ideally in ALL CAPS on Truth Social — and prides himself on being unpredictable (although some would argue it’s all part of a strategy).
要理解當下，必須理解唐納德·川普。這說起來容易做起來難。川普熱衷於製造戲劇性事件，最好是在Truth Social上用全大寫字母發文。他對自己的行事不可預測感到自豪，儘管有人認為這都是其策略的一部分。
The flood of missives and missiles, of hyperbolic threats (“a whole civilization will die tonight”) and morphing deadlines coming out of the White House can seem surreal. But two of my colleagues have written a book full of riveting, granular detail that makes it feel, well, very real.
白宮發出的信息與行動似乎極不真實。這其中包括如潮水般的信件與導彈指令、誇張的威脅（例如「今晚一整個文明將走向消亡」），以及不斷變動的最後期限。但我的兩位同事撰寫的一本新書用引人入勝、事無鉅細的詳盡敘述讓這一切感覺非常真實。
In “Regime Change,” Maggie Haberman and Jonathan Swan take you into the room for the key moments of the president’s second term. I spoke to Jonathan about some of them.
在《政權更迭》(Regime Change)一書中，瑪吉·哈伯曼與喬納森·斯旺帶領讀者深入現場，回顧總統第二任期內的關鍵時刻。我就其中部分時刻與喬納森進行了對話。
Trump’s world
川普的世界
廣告
Jonathan, let’s start with Iran. A lot of people were baffled by Trump’s decision to go to war. What swayed him? Was it Iran’s plots to kill him? His success in Venezuela? Israel’s war pitch?
喬納森，我們先談談伊朗。許多人對川普決定開戰感到困惑。是什麼影響了他？是伊朗刺殺他的陰謀嗎？是他在委內瑞拉取得的成功？還是以色列對戰爭的遊說？
It was all those things. But also, Trump has always been hawkish about Iran.
以上因素兼而有之。但同時，川普對伊朗一直持鷹派立場。
In Term 1, he pulls out of the Iran nuclear deal. He orders the assassination of this very senior Iranian general, Qassim Suleimani. Then, when he’s out of office, he learns that the Iranian regime is trying to get hit men to kill him. Iran hacks the emails of his campaign manager. So, for Trump, this is also personal.
在第一任期內，他退出了伊朗核協議。他下令暗殺了地位極高的伊朗將領卡西姆·蘇萊曼尼。隨後在卸任期間，他得知伊朗政權正企圖僱傭殺手刺殺他。伊朗還黑入其競選經理的電子郵箱。因此，對川普而言，這也是私人恩怨。
How important was that meeting in February you guys describe in such incredible detail, when Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu persuaded Trump to actually go to war?
今年2月，本雅明·納坦尼雅胡總理說服川普對伊發動真正意義上的戰爭。你們在書中極為詳盡地描述了那次會議。那次會議有多重要？
It was very important. I’m not aware of any historical parallel for a foreign leader going down to the White House Situation Room and pitching the American president on going to war.
極其重要。據我所知，歷史上還沒有哪位外國領導人走進白宮戰情室向美國總統推銷戰爭的先例。
That war hasn’t gone to plan. How has that affected the Trump-Netanyahu relationship?
目前的戰局並未按計劃發展。這對川普與納坦尼雅胡的關係有何影響？
It was always a very up-and-down, fraught relationship. Trump views Netanyahu as a strong leader but also as someone who needs to be restrained. A couple of months ago Trump started to view Netanyahu as a problem — an obstacle to getting out of this war.
雙方關係一直起伏不定，充滿摩擦。川普認為納坦尼雅胡是一位強勢領導人，但也是一個需要加以約束的人。幾個月前，川普開始將納坦尼雅胡視為一個麻煩。他認為他是美國從這場戰爭脫身的主要阻礙。
廣告
He became very agitated about what Netanyahu was doing in Lebanon. And so the U.S. cut the Israelis out of their negotiations with Iran. Right now, it’s not a good relationship at all.
川普對納坦尼雅胡在黎巴嫩的行動感到非常不安。因此，美國將以色列排除在美伊談判之外。目前，兩人的關係非常糟糕。
You write that Netanyahu, President Xi Jinping and President Vladimir Putin were all better prepared to deal with Trump during his second term than his first. How so?
你在書中寫道，在應對川普第二任期時，納坦尼雅胡、習近平以及弗拉基米爾·普丁都比他第一任期時準備得更為充分。為什麼這麼說？
Yes, and on that list, Xi Jinping might have been the best prepared.
是的，而且在這份名單中，習近平可能準備得最充分。
In April last year, when Trump essentially declares a trade war with the whole world, Xi Jinping doesn’t negotiate. He just raises tariffs himself and implements a whole bunch of restrictions on rare earths and magnets and things that the Americans need. He had this all lined up — he was fully prepared to wage economic supply chain war against the United States. And he won that skirmish.
去年4月，川普實質上向全球宣告發起貿易戰。當時習近平沒有選擇談判，而是直接自行提高關稅。他還對美國急需的稀土和磁鐵等物資實施了一系列限制。他早已將這一切部署妥當。他完全做好了對美髮動經濟供應鏈戰爭的準備，並在這場交鋒中取得了勝利。
Trump sends his Treasury secretary to Geneva to negotiate an off-ramp with China. Which he does; the tariff rates go down. And ever since then, Trump has taken a very dovish posture with Beijing.
川普隨後派遣財政部長前往日內瓦，與中方談判尋找退讓方案。他的確退讓了，關稅稅率隨之下降。自那以後，川普對北京一直採取非常鴿派的姿態。
Are there lessons for the rest of the world in this approach to dealing with Trump?
這種應對川普的策略對世界其他地區有借鑒意義嗎？
廣告
There’s this whole conversation in the international diplomatic community: How do you deal with Trump? I’ve always thought that conversation is sort of absurd. The Europeans talk about this playbook — you flatter him, bring him gifts and what have you. That might get you something, for a day or two. But what Xi Jinping showed is the way to deal with Trump is through strength.
國際外交界一直有這樣一個討論：應該如何與川普打交道？我一直覺得這種討論有些荒唐。歐洲人談論的套路是奉承他、給他送禮諸如此類。這或許能讓你換來一兩天的甜頭。但習近平展現出的應對方式是通過實力。
Trump really understands leverage. He likes picking off countries one at a time so he can use the leverage of the U.S. military and economy and technology to squeeze that other party. And China — unlike some of the struggling small and middle powers — had leverage to force Trump down, in a very effective way.
川普非常懂得籌碼的作用。他喜歡逐一針對各個國家，以便利用美國在軍事、經濟和技術上的籌碼來擠壓對方。但與一些苦苦掙扎的中小強國不同，中國擁有籌碼，能以極為有效的方式迫使川普退讓。
What does that mean for Trump’s commitment, say, to defend Taiwan?
這對他諸如保衛台灣等承諾意味著什麼？
We have a scene in our book where he’s talking about Taiwan and he holds up a little teaspoon, basically, as a means of saying, Why would we fight over this little speck of land?
書中記錄了這樣一個場景：他在談論台灣時舉起一把小茶匙。其用意基本是說，我們為什麼要為了這麼一點微不足道的土地而開戰？
He hasn’t formally changed U.S. policy on Taiwan, but I’m not aware of anyone around him who thinks that Donald Trump would use the American military to fight for Taiwan’s independence.
他還沒有正式改變美國對台政策，但他身邊所有我認識的人中，沒有人認為唐納德·川普會動用美國軍事力量為台灣獨立而戰。
Does that suggest NATO is dead, too? If the Russians march into Estonia, would the U.S. intervene?
這是否意味著北約也名存實亡？如果俄羅斯人攻入愛沙尼亞，美國會幹預嗎？
廣告
At this point, you would have to be smoking something to think that Article 5, the mutual defense clause that is the bedrock of the alliance, is ironclad when it comes to Donald Trump. I wouldn’t want to predict one way or the other, but the notion that you could rely on him to defend a small nation against Russia is obviously questionable at best.
事到如今，如果還有人認為，作為北約同盟基石的第五條款——也就是共同防禦條款——在唐納德·川普眼裡不可動搖，那肯定是腦子有問題了。我不想預測結果如何，但指望他來保衛小國抵禦俄羅斯，至少是一個令人起疑的想法。
All this considered, how ideological is Trump, really?
綜上所述，川普究竟有多在乎意識型態？
Trump has never viewed the world the way American presidents of the last 50 years have viewed it, in terms of a post-World War II order and an alliance of liberal democracies. He basically sees the world as a bunch of countries that owe America money.
過去50年來的歷任美國總統都是從二戰後秩序以及自由民主同盟的角度看待世界，川普卻從未如此看待世界。他基本上將世界視為一群欠美國錢的國家。
At a sort of cellular level, he’s attracted to strongmen and has contempt for Europeans and multilateral institutions defending human rights and international law. But that doesn’t come from a deeply held consistent worldview. If there’s an ideology, it’s “Might makes right.”
從某種本質層面上看，他被政治強人吸引。他對歐洲人以及捍衛人權和國際法的多邊機構嗤之以鼻。但這並非源於某種根深蒂固且一以貫之的世界觀。如果非要說他有什麼意識型態，那就是「強權即公理」。
