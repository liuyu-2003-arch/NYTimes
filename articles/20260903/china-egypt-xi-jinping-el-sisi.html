習近平訪問埃及

In Egypt, Xi Challenges America’s Influence in the Middle East

郭莉莉, ERIKA SOLOMON • 2026-09-03
When the Chinese leader Xi Jinping arrived in Cairo, he was greeted by children wearing T-shirts emblazoned with his face. Performers staged a traditional dance. The president of Egypt himself came to the airport to receive him.
當中國領導人習近平抵達開羅時，孩童穿著印有他頭像的T恤夾道歡迎。表演者上演了傳統舞蹈。埃及總統親自來到機場迎接。
For China, the welcome on Tuesday evening was a way to show that its influence has grown in Egypt, a traditional U.S. security partner. Mr. Xi hailed China and Egypt as “close brothers” bonded by a shared history of fighting imperialism — as he sought to present Beijing as a different kind of global power from the West.
對中國而言，週二晚間的這場歡迎儀式是一次展示，表明中國對埃及這個傳統上的美國安全夥伴有著越來越大的影響力。習近平稱讚中埃兩國是「親密兄弟」，有共同抗擊帝國主義的歷史。他試圖以此將北京塑造成一種有別於西方的全球大國。
He arrived as fighting between Iran and the United States flared once again — the latest conflagration of a war most countries in the region, from Egypt to Gulf nations, had sought to push President Trump to avoid starting earlier this year.
他到訪之際，伊朗與美國再次爆發交火。今年早些時候，從埃及到海灣國家的該地區多數國家都曾試圖勸阻川普總統，以避免這場戰爭。
Egypt receives over $1.3 billion in U.S. military aid each year, but has also been drawing closer to China as the two countries expand their relationship beyond trade to include technology and military cooperation.
埃及每年接受逾13億美元的美國軍事援助，但同時也在不斷向中國靠攏。兩國正將雙邊關係從貿易領域拓展至技術與軍事合作。
Mr. Xi, who is making a three-day visit, sat down for talks with President Abdel Fattah el-Sisi of Egypt on Wednesday, as the war with Iran has unsettled the region, disrupted energy markets and raised questions about Washington’s role in the Middle East. 
週三，在埃及進行為期三天的訪問的習近平與埃及總統阿卜杜勒-法塔赫·塞西舉行了會談。對伊戰爭正令該地區陷入動盪。這場衝突擾亂了能源市場，也引發了外界對華盛頓在中東角色的質疑。
廣告
According to a readout of the meeting published by China’s official news agency, Xinhua, the two sides signed agreements to cooperate in artificial intelligence, trade and supply chains, and other economic areas.
據中國官方通訊社新華社發布的會談紀要，雙方簽署了在人工智慧、貿易和供應鏈等經濟領域開展合作的協議。
China said it was willing to work with “all countries” to safeguard international shipping lanes and “eliminate the breeding ground for conflict.”
中國表示願與「所有國家」合作，維護國際航道安全，「消除衝突滋生土壤」。
In a veiled reference to the United States, Mr. Xi called on “major powers outside the region” to “abandon double standards” and respect the sovereignty and territorial integrity of regional countries, according to the readout.
根據紀要，習近平在講話中含蓄地提到美國，呼籲「域外大國要摒棄雙重標準」，尊重地區國家的主權和領土完整。
Egypt said that the two countries “firmly rejected double standards in antiterrorism efforts and opposed linking terrorism to any specific religion, ethnicity, or nationality.”
埃及表示，兩國「堅決反對在反恐努力中採取雙重標準，反對將恐怖主義與任何特定宗教、種族或國籍聯繫起來」。
At a time when U.S. allies are questioning Washington’s reliability as a partner, it makes sense that countries like Egypt would want to strengthen ties with China, argued Ding Long, a professor at the Middle East Studies Institute of Shanghai International Studies University.
目前，美國盟友正在質疑華盛頓作為合作夥伴的可靠性。上海外國語大學中東研究所教授丁隆認為，在這一背景下，埃及等國希望加強對華關係在情理之中。
“You can’t put all your eggs in one basket and rely solely on the U.S. You need to diversify. Within this diversification, China is a very important partner,” he said.
「你不能把所有雞蛋都放在一個籃子裡，完全依賴美國。你需要實現多元化。在這種多元化進程中，中國是一個非常重要的夥伴，」他表示。
Mr. Xi’s visit to Egypt, which comes before an expected meeting with Mr. Trump later this month, is also about showing that his country has friends and influence even within the United States’ own sphere of influence.
習近平訪問埃及之後，預計將於本月晚些時候與川普舉行會晤。埃及之行同樣意在表明，即使在美國自身的勢力範圍內，中國也擁有朋友與影響力。
Earlier this week, he attended a security summit in Kyrgyzstan along with the leaders of Russia and Iran.
本週早些時候，他與俄羅斯及伊朗領導人共同出席了在吉爾吉斯斯坦舉行的安全峰會。
Mr. Xi is in Egypt to mark 70 years since the two countries established diplomatic relations. China, Egypt’s largest trading partner, has been expanding its investments in the country. Chinese automakers have signed agreements to assemble cars locally and Chinese firms have helped build some of Cairo’s key infrastructure, like a new administrative capital and a light rail line.
習近平此次訪問埃及是為了紀念兩國建交70週年。作為埃及最大的貿易夥伴，中國不斷擴大對該國的投資。中國汽車製造商已簽署協議，將在當地組裝汽車。中國企業還參與開羅部分關鍵基礎設施的建設，比如新行政首都及一條輕軌。
Trade between the countries has grown by 60 percent over the past decade to over $20 billion last year, compared to about $13 billion in 2015. Chinese companies have invested heavily in Egypt’s Suez Canal Economic Zone. Last year, China pledged $1.15 billion in new industrial projects.
過去十年間，兩國貿易額增長了60%。去年這一數字已突破200億美元，而2015年約為130億美元。中國企業在埃及蘇伊士運河經濟區進行了大量投資。去年，中國承諾為新工業項目提供11.5億美元資金。
That cooperation has expanded into more sensitive areas. Last month, the two countries held joint military exercises in which China sent its J-16 fighter jets for a combat exercise against Egypt’s French-made Rafales, giving Beijing an opportunity to more closely examine Western equipment that is used by other militaries.
這種合作已拓展至更為敏感的領域。上個月，兩國舉行了聯合軍事演習。中國派出殲-16戰機，與埃及的法制「陣風」戰機進行對抗演練。這為北京提供了一個機會，使其能近距離觀察其他國家軍隊所用的西方裝備。
廣告
“Cooperation between the two sides is deepening and expanding and including new areas, like military and security issues,” said Mr. Ding, who noted that the two countries are also collaborating on space-related projects, including satellites.
「雙方的合作正在深化與拓展，並涵蓋了軍事和安全問題等新領域，」丁隆指出。他還提到，兩國在包括衛星在內的航天項目上也開展了合作。
There is still little sign, though, that either country is looking to Beijing to play more than a supplementary role in Egypt’s military architecture.
不過，目前幾乎沒有跡象表明，中埃任何一方有意提升北京在埃及軍事架構中的份量，不再僅限於一種輔助性角色。
For Mr. Xi, visiting Egypt, the most populous Arab nation, is also a way to try to shape opinions about China throughout the Arab world. Because Egypt is not involved in the Iran war or too closely aligned with the United States, it “affords him the opportunity to be in the region, yet still control the optics and the message,” according to Eric Olander, editor in chief of The China-Global South Project, a research agency.
對習近平而言，訪問人口最多的阿拉伯國家埃及也是試圖在整個阿拉伯世界塑造中國形象。研究機構「中國-全球南方項目」總編輯埃里克·奧蘭德認為，埃及既未捲入對伊戰爭，也未與美國過度結盟。這「使他既能置身該地區，又能掌控外界觀感與傳遞出的信息」。
Egypt has been fostering closer ties with China for over a decade, since Mr. el-Sisi traveled to Beijing in 2014. Egypt is one of many countries, particularly in the Gulf, that have sought over the past decade to diversify its relationships, said Hisham A. Hellyer, a senior associate fellow at the Royal United Services Institute in London.
自塞西2014年訪問北京以來，埃及在十多年間一直致力於培育更緊密的對華關係。倫敦皇家聯合軍種國防研究所高級副研究員希沙姆·A·赫利爾表示，過去十年間，眾多國家都在尋求實現關係多元化。埃及正是其中之一，而這類國家在海灣地區尤為多見。
“It’s not about replacing the U.S. role,” he said. “I don’t think any country in the region that have established strong ties with the United States want to give them up.” But those countries are keenly aware that, despite the war with Iran, Washington has sought for years sought to disentangle itself from the region.
「這並非要取代美國的作用，」他表示。「我不認為該地區任何與美國建立了牢固關係的國家想要放棄這種關係。」但這些國家也敏銳地意識到，華盛頓多年來一直試圖從中東地區抽身，儘管目前爆發了對伊戰爭。
