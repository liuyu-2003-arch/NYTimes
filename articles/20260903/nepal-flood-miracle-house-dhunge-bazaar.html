尼泊爾洪災中的「奇蹟」綠房子

The ‘Miracle House’ That Survived Nepal’s Floods

MUJIB MASHAL, BINOD GHIMIRE • 2026-09-03
For some, it’s become known as the “miracle” green house. For others arriving to stare in wonderment and take selfies, it is simply “the viral house.”
一些人將它稱為「奇蹟」綠房子。而對那些前來圍觀驚嘆並且自拍的遊客來說，它就是那座「網紅房子」。
When a ferocious torrent of water, mud and debris swept away everything in its path in Nepal last week, it created a whirlpool of destruction around the Pyakurel family home in Dhunge Bazaar. A video spread far and wide on social media, showing the family helplessly looking down from their third-floor balcony at the angry deluge slamming against their home, the waves trying to lap them up.
上週，兇猛的水流、泥漿和碎片橫掃尼泊爾，將沿途一切沖得無影無蹤，丹格市集的皮亞庫雷爾一家的房屋周圍形成了一個毀滅性的漩渦。一段影片在社群媒體上被廣泛傳播，畫面顯示，這家人站在三樓陽台上，無奈地看著憤怒的洪水猛烈衝擊他們的家，洶湧的波濤似乎就要將他們吞沒。
But the house stood firm.
但這座房子卻巋然不動。
Chhatra Bahadur Pyakurel, 67, said he had just dropped his grandson off at the school bus and returned home when the floods — caused by a massive glacier collapse in the Himalayan region between Nepal and Tibet — reached their home.
67歲的查特拉·巴哈杜爾·皮亞庫雷爾說，他剛把孫子送上校車回到家，洪水就衝到了他們家。這場洪水是由尼泊爾和西藏之間喜馬拉雅地區的大規模冰川崩塌引發的。

For over an hour, all the family could do was watch the carnage around them — first from the balcony, and then after climbing to the roof, as the wall of water and debris kept growing taller and wilder.
在一個多小時裡，全家人能做的就是看著周圍慘烈的景象，他們起初是在陽台上，隨著由水和碎片的牆越積越高、越來越兇猛，他們爬上了屋頂。
“At first we did not know what would happen or whether we would survive,” Mr. Pyakurel said. “But after some time, we realized there was nothing more we could do. Our lives were in the hands of the almighty God, whether he would save us or let us drown.”
「一開始我們不知道會發生什麼，也不知道我們能不能活下來，」皮亞庫雷爾說。「但過了一段時間，我們意識到我們無能為力了。我們的生命掌握在全能的上帝手中，無論他是要拯救我們還是讓我們淹死。」
“The only thought that kept troubling me was about my grandson, who had already left for school,” he added. “I kept thinking: ‘If we all die, who will look after him?’”
「唯一讓我一直揪心的就是去上學的孫子，」他還說。「我一直在想：『如果我們都死了，誰來照顧他？』」
The house went viral as photographs of it still standing, alone amid a wasteland of mud and debris, circulated online. Almost a week after the devastation, as the road in front of the house was partially reopened and traffic returned slowly to Dhunge Bazaar, visitors climbed up the ridge to get a view.
隨著這座房子在泥濘和瓦礫的廢墟中孤獨屹立的照片在網上流傳，它迅速走紅。在災難發生近一周後，隨著房子前面的道路部分重新開放，交通慢慢恢復到丹格市集，遊客們爬上山脊來一睹它的真容。
The house has gotten its own pinpoint on Google Maps as a “flood-proof” attraction.
這座房子甚至在谷歌地圖上獲得了自己的專屬定位，成了一個「防洪」景點。
The river had calmed on Tuesday, but its water remained muddy. Little beyond the pillars of the first two stories of the house were left. On the third story balcony, the washing machine remained, along with a pile of laundry. The roof — with its water tanks, a solar water heater, a small family temple and many tall plants — looked neatly intact.
到了週二，河流已經平靜下來，但河水依然渾濁。這座房子的一樓和二樓除了柱子幾乎什麼都沒剩下。在三樓的陽台上，洗衣機還在，旁邊還有一堆待洗換的衣服。屋頂看起來完好無損，整整齊齊，上面有水箱、太陽能熱水器、一個小小的家庭神龕和許多高大的植物。
The house has become one of the rare stories of survival amid a grim picture of devastation, its full extent still growing by the day. The death toll in Nepal rose above 1,100 on Wednesday, with almost 4,000 more people still missing, and hundreds more remain unaccounted for in Tibet.
在滿目瘡痍的嚴峻景象中，這座房子成為了罕見的倖存故事之一，而災難的全面破壞程度仍日益加劇。週三，尼泊爾的死亡人數超過了1100人，仍有近4000人失蹤，西藏也有數百人下落不明。
廣告
Rescue missions — complicated by continuous rains and entire towns covered in the kind of soft mud that makes helicopter landings difficult — have managed to rescue over 11,000 people. Thousands of survivors are in temporary shelters.
目前降雨仍在持續，整個城鎮被覆蓋在軟泥中，導致直升機難以著陸，這一切使得救援任務變得極其複雜。儘管如此，救援隊仍成功救出了11000多人。成千上萬的倖存者目前被安置在臨時避難所中。
In the surrounding Nuwakot district alone, over 1,200 homes have been fully damaged and more than 1,000 others partially damaged.
僅在周邊的努瓦科特地區，就有1200多處房屋完全損毀，另有1000多處房屋部分受損。
Connectivity remains a major issue in a country with difficult terrain, dozens of bridges wiped out and entire stretches of road, including the one just beyond Dhunge Bazaar, completely destroyed.
在這個地形崎嶇的國家，交通聯絡仍然是一個重大問題，數十座橋樑被沖毀，大片路段被完全摧毀，包括緊挨著丹格市集外的那段道路。
Bishu Mani Rimal, 45, who was among the crowd staring at the surviving house, lives about three miles downstream, where the torrent of water had slowed slightly.
45歲的比舒·馬尼·裡馬爾站在人群中凝視著那棟倖存的房屋，他住在下游約五公里處，那裡的水流已稍微放緩。
“Who would believe people in that situation would survive?” he said. “I thought they didn’t even have a 1 percent chance.”
「誰會相信這種情況下， 人還能活下來？」他說。「我覺得他們連1%的生存機會都沒有。」
Mr. Rimal surveyed the destruction all around. He said that power turbines on the other side of the river were gone. So, too, was an entire bazaar.
裡馬爾環顧四周的破壞情況。他說，河對岸的發電機組不見了。整個市集也不復存在了。
廣告
Four homes owned by the same extended family had also vanished.
同一個大家族擁有的四座房屋也消失了。
“One house was so new, they hadn’t even moved in,” he said.
「有一座房子太新了，他們甚至還沒搬進去，」他說。
Mr. Pyakurel, the family patriarch, said the ground floor of the green house was built in 1995, when he had a grocery store. As his business expanded, including to selling liquor wholesale, the family kept adding floors.
家族長輩皮亞庫雷爾說，這座綠房子的底層建於1995年，當時他開了一家雜貨店。隨著生意的擴張（包括酒類批發業務），這家人不斷加蓋樓層。
He said that, after an hour of being trapped on the roof, neighbors came to help them out when water levels receded. The rescuers used metal pipes to create a makeshift ladder to lead them to safety, he said.
他說，在被困在屋頂一個小時後，水位開始消退，鄰居們前來營救他們。他說，救援人員用金屬管做了一個臨時梯子，將他們引導至安全地帶。
He said the house may have remained standing because its pillars were anchored by drilling deep into the rock, but he also said that it was spared by God. Theories about the house have cropped up, with some saying that a ridge jutting out into the path of the torrent had helped shield it.
他說，這座房子之所以能屹立不倒，可能是因為它的柱子深深鑽入岩石中進行了固定，但他也表示，這是上帝的庇佑。關於這座房子為何倖存的理論層出不窮，有人說是一條伸入洪流路徑的山脊起到了屏障作用。。
Mr. Pyakurel said they were still assessing the full damage, as he had temporarily shifted to Kathmandu, the capital. But he feared that little remained on the first two floors.
皮亞庫雷爾說，他們仍在評估全面的損失情況，而他已暫時搬到了首都加德滿都。但他擔心一樓和二樓已經所剩無幾了。
廣告
“Now, everything is gone,” he said. “Even our family’s most important documents, including citizenship certificates, passports and land registration certificates, were destroyed.”
「現在，一切都沒了，」他說。「甚至我們家最重要的文件，包括公民證書、護照和土地登記證，也都被毀了。」
But he was grateful that his grandson was safe, as the school had been on higher ground. They were all reunited the day after the flood.
但他很慶幸自己的孫子平安無事，因為學校地勢較高。洪水過後的第二天，他們全家終於團聚。

Mujib Mashal是時報南亞分社社長，領導對印度及其周邊地區（包括孟加拉國、斯里蘭卡、尼泊爾和不丹）的報導。
