關於人工智慧安全，中國真正在乎的是什麼

What China Really Wants on A.I. Safety

高安西 • 2026-09-24
If there is one person who represents the frontier of Chinese artificial intelligence, it is Tang Jie.
如果要說誰代表了中國人工智慧的前沿，那就是唐傑。
The soft-spoken data engineer is the co-founder, chief scientist and largest individual shareholder of China’s leading artificial intelligence lab, Z.ai, whose models have risen to the top tier of China’s performance tables and made Mr. Tang the flag-bearer for the Chinese A.I. ecosystem.
這位文質彬彬的數據工程師是中國頂尖人工智慧實驗室智譜AI(Z.ai)的聯合創始人、首席科學家兼最大個人股東。智譜AI旗下模型的性能已躍升至中國第一梯隊，也讓唐傑成為了中國人工智慧生態系統的領軍人物。
In some ways, Mr. Tang’s messaging about A.I. seems to mirror that of Dario Amodei, the co-founder and chief executive of Anthropic — the American company behind Claude — and one of the most prominent voices extolling A.I.’s promise while warning of its risks. Mr. Tang pairs an almost utopian excitement with what he has called the “heaviest responsibility” of “extreme safety governance.”
在某種程度上，唐傑關於人工智慧的表態似乎與達里奧·阿莫迪遙相呼應。阿莫迪是Claude背後的美國公司Anthropic的聯合創始人兼首席執行官，也是行業內既讚美人工智慧前景又警告其風險的代表性人物。唐傑將一種近乎烏托邦式的熱情與他所稱的「極致安全治理」這一「最重的責任」結合在了一起。
“Once a technology reaches a magnitude of power sufficient to alter the course of civilization, safety is no longer an accessory, but the fundamental precondition for that technology to survive and to be permitted for use,” Mr. Tang said in a letter to staff that was leaked to the Chinese media in July.
「當一項技術抵達足以改變文明進程的力量量級時，安全就不再是附屬品，而是技術得以存續與被允許應用的根本前提，」唐傑在7月曝光的一封致員工的信中這樣寫道。
廣告
Those words may sound strikingly similar to warnings now coming from leading American A.I. labs. But they are better understood through the language of President Xi Jinping of China, for whom A.I. “safety” is ultimately bound to one overriding concern: the security of the Chinese Communist Party regime.
這些話聽起來或許與美國頂尖人工智慧實驗室發出的預警驚人地相似。但若結合中國國家主席習近平的語境來理解，其含義會更加清晰——對習近平而言，人工智慧「安全」歸根結底指向一個核心關切：中國共產黨政權的安全。
While the debate in Western capitals has centered on the dangers A.I. poses to society, the animating concern in China is the threat it poses to the state. Chinese models are already well known for filtering or avoiding politically sensitive information in ways that reflect state censorship. But developers like Mr. Tang are going further, working to embed Communist Party orthodoxy deeply into the models, with potentially far-reaching implications for the values and vulnerabilities that Chinese A.I. systems bring with them.
西方多國政府將辯論焦點放在人工智慧對社會構成的危險上，中國最關切的則是其對國家政權構成的威脅。中國模型因過濾或規避政治敏感信息以契合國家審查制度而廣為人知。但像唐傑這樣的開發者正走得更遠，他們致力於將執政黨意識型態深刻植入模型底層，這對中國人工智慧系統所攜帶的價值觀及其潛在脆弱性具有深遠影響。
Recognizing these diverging views on the technology that will shape the next century is crucial in setting expectations for the talks on Thursday between President Trump and Mr. Xi at the White House. The leaders are set to discuss how to mitigate A.I. risks. Arriving at meaningful cooperation will be difficult — if not impossible — because there are two almost entirely unrelated conversations about what A.I. safety means happening on opposite sides of the Pacific.
認清對這項將塑造下個世紀的關鍵技術的理念分歧對於設定川普與習近平週四在白宮會談的預期至關重要。兩國領導人準備討論如何規避人工智慧風險。然而，要達成實質性合作將極其困難，甚至難以實現，因為太平洋兩岸對於「人工智慧安全」的理解完全是兩套互不相干的語境。
The wider stakes are potentially enormous. Mr. Tang and his colleagues are instilling values into their models that shape how they interpret the world and respond. As Chinese A.I. is increasingly being adopted globally, those values could travel with it, quietly carrying the Communist Party’s worldview far beyond Chinese shores.
這背後的影響可能是巨大的。唐傑和他的同事正向模型注入特定價值觀，從而塑造模型理解世界與做出回應的方式。隨著中國人工智慧系統在全球範圍內得到越來越廣泛的應用，這些價值觀也可能隨之傳播，悄然將執政黨的世界觀帶到遠超中國本土的地方。
Six days after Mr. Tang’s letter to Z.ai’s staff, Mr. Xi provided a glimpse into his thinking when he convened the annual World Artificial Intelligence Conference in Shanghai on July 17. In comments translated into English by state-run media, Mr. Xi was quoted as saying that “China lays great emphasis on safety and security in A.I.” and is committed to keeping it “safe, secure and controllable.” It seemed a reassuring signal ahead of the scheduled talks with Mr. Trump.
在唐傑發表致智譜員工信六天後，習近平於7月17日在上海出席年度世界人工智慧大會時，展現了他的思考方向。在由官方媒體翻譯成英文的表述中，習近平表示「China lays great emphasis on safety and security in A.I.」（中國高度重視人工智慧的安全和保障），並致力於確保其「safe, secure and controllable」（安全、穩固和可控）。在與川普預定的會談前夕，這似乎釋放出了一個令人放心的信號。
But Chinese state media often translates comments by the country’s leaders in ways that soften their political meaning for foreign audiences. What Mr. Xi actually said in Chinese was that China’s emphasis was on “development and security,” and on making sure “A.I. is secure, reliable and controllable.”
然而，中國官方媒體在翻譯領導人講話時，往往會面向外國受眾軟化其政治含義。習近平中文講話的原話是：中國堅持「發展和安全」並重，確保「人工智慧安全、可靠、可控」。
廣告
That may seem like a fine distinction in translation. But in Mr. Xi’s vocabulary, words like “security” and “control” ultimately refer to the political security of the Communist Party. Time and again, Mr. Xi has made clear that ensuring China’s Communist system endures in a rapidly changing world is at the heart of his agenda.
這在翻譯上或許看似只是微小的差別。但在習近平的語彙體系中，「安全」與「控制」等詞彙歸根結底指的是執政黨的政治安全。他多次明確強調，確保中國的社會主義制度在快速變化的世界中穩固長久是他施政的核心議題。
Today’s Silicon Valley pioneers grew up in a liberal democracy, consuming science fiction in which man builds machines that promise utopia but might ultimately destroy him. Mr. Amodei’s essay in January on A.I. risks, “The Adolescence of Technology,” opens with a scene from one of his favorite films, “Contact.” The protagonist, played by Jodie Foster, is asked what she will say to the advanced life form that has established radio contact from outer space.
如今的矽谷先行者成長於自由民主社會，從小接觸的科幻作品多描繪人類懷著建設烏托邦的本意，卻創造了可能最終毀滅人類的機器。在今年1月發表的關於人工智慧風險的文章《技術的青春期》中，阿莫迪開篇引用了他最喜歡的電影之一《超時空接觸》(Contact)的場景：由朱迪·福士特飾演的女主角被問及，她想對來自外太空並建立起無線電聯繫的高級智慧生命說些什麼。
She replies: “How did you do it? How did you evolve? How did you survive this technological adolescence without destroying yourself?”
她回答道：「你們是怎麼做到的？你們是如何演化的？你們是如何度過這個技術青春期而沒有毀滅自己的？」
The essay details how to manage the potentially catastrophic risks inherent in the technology that Mr. Amodei is helping build, including by baking safety principles into A.I. so they are inseparable from the model itself. Known as constitutional A.I., this method attempts to teach the technology what it means to be “a good A.I.” at the level of character, values and personality — “like a child forming their identity,” Mr. Amodei wrote.
該文詳細闡述了如何管理阿莫迪參與構建的技術所固有的災難性風險，其中包括將安全原則融入人工智慧，使其與模型本身密不可分。這種被稱為「憲制人工智慧」(constitutional A.I.)的方法，試圖在性格、價值觀和品格層面教授這項技術何為「合格的人工智慧」——「就像一個正在形成自身認同的孩子，」阿莫迪寫道。
The values that Anthropic instills in its models borrow from the Universal Declaration of Human Rights and are weighted heavily toward access to information, rule of law, political freedom, self-determination and individual liberty.
Anthropic注入其模型中的價值觀借鑒了《世界人權宣言》，高度側重於信息獲取、法治、政治自由、自決權和個人自由。
Similarly, Demis Hassabis, the co-founder and chairman of Google DeepMind, has developed a set of A.I. principles explicitly anchored in liberal democratic values and argues that democracies should lead in A.I. development, “guided by core values like freedom, equality, and respect for human rights.”
無獨有偶，Google DeepMind聯合創始人兼主席德米斯·哈薩比斯也制定了一套明確錨定於自由民主價值觀的人工智慧原則，並主張民主國家應當在「自由、平等和尊重人權等核心價值觀的指引下」引領人工智慧的發展。
廣告
China’s new generation of tech leaders has been brought up in an altogether different environment, an authoritarian world where liberal values are labeled hostile to China and technology is developed and controlled for the benefit of the Communist Party.
中國的新一代科技領袖則成長於截然不同的環境——在一個威權體系中，自由主義價值觀被標記為對華敵對，技術的開發與管控完全服務於執政黨的利益。
Mr. Xi’s minister for state security, Chen Yixin, made that clear in an essay published this month on the dangers of A.I. Mr. Chen made no mention of the existential threat of artificial intelligence turning against its masters, the fear that preoccupies leading American A.I. figures. Instead, he said China needed to raise its defenses against hostile human forces that would use this powerful new tool for a “direct assault on our political security, institutional security, and ideological security.”
習近平任命的國家安全部部長陳一新在本月發表的一篇關於人工智慧風險的文章中清晰闡明了這一點。陳一新完全沒有提及困擾美國人工智慧領軍人物的生存威脅，即人工智慧失控反噬人類的恐懼。相反，他強調中國必須加強防禦敵對勢力，防止其利用這一強大工具「直接威脅我政治安全、制度安全、意識型態安全」。
A.I. safety, in Mr. Chen’s telling, lies in aggressively pushing forward the development of Chinese artificial intelligence so that it does not fall behind the United States, a prospect that he implied posed the technology’s biggest danger for China. The country’s evolving A.I. regulatory system also places threats to socialist values, state power and political stability among the government’s core safety concerns.
在陳一新的論述中，人工智慧的安全在於大力推進中國人工智慧的發展，以防落後於美國，他暗示這種落後才是該技術對中國構成的最大危險。中國不斷完善的人工智慧監管體系也將對社會主義價值觀、國家權力和政治穩定的威脅列為政府的核心安全關切。
Very little personal information is known about Mr. Tang, who rarely speaks publicly. He formed Z.ai by joining together with other members of the Knowledge Engineering Group, a research unit at Beijing’s prestigious Tsinghua University. The group developed sophisticated tools for analyzing social networks and information flows, including systems for profiling and locating researchers in China and other countries and mapping their relationships, which were presented as recruiting tools.
外界對極少公開發言的唐傑個人信息知之甚少。他聯合北京名校清華大學的知識工程實驗室其他成員共同創立了智譜AI。該團隊曾開發出用於分析社交網路和信息流的先進工具，包括用於對國內外研究人員進行畫像、定位及繪製關係圖譜的系統，它們被定位為人才招聘工具。
In Mr. Tang’s July letter, he rejected the idea of “bolt-on” safety patches for A.I., saying instead that behavioral principles must be embedded in the foundations of the company’s models, including writing Chinese national laws, strategy and security concerns — in other words, the Communist Party’s political interests — into the A.I.’s basic DNA. In August, Mr. Tang went a step further, publishing a commentary that argued in favor of laws to impose ideological conformity on the Chinese artificial intelligence industry.
唐傑在7月的一封信中，拒絕了對人工智慧採取「外掛式」安全補丁的做法，而是主張必須將行為準則植入公司模型的底層架構中，包括將國家法律、戰略與安全關切——換言之，即執政黨的政治利益——寫入人工智慧的基礎基因。8月，唐傑更進一步，發表了一篇評論文章，主張通過立法對中國人工智慧產業強制實施意識型態的一致性。
Last month, his company released one of China’s most powerful A.I. models, GLM-5.3 — which Z.ai markets as a cheap, accessible and capable open-weight cyberdefense tool approaching the abilities of Mythos 5, Anthropic’s powerful cybersecurity model. Users can download and modify open-weight models, fine-tuning them for specific tasks, though deeply embedded behaviors may be difficult to strip out. Z.ai argues that advanced tools like these should be made widely available to developers and cybersecurity teams around the world, an approach that contrasts with the more closely guarded proprietary models of Anthropic and OpenAI.
上個月，他的公司發布了中國最強大的AI模型之一GLM-5.3。智譜AI將其定位為一款廉價、易獲取且功能強大的開源權重網路防禦工具，其性能已接近Anthropic強大的網路安全模型Mythos 5。用戶可以下載並修改開源權重模型，針對特定任務進行微調，儘管其中深度嵌入的行為可能難以徹底清除。智譜AI主張將這類先進工具向全球開發者和網路安全團隊開放，這一路線與Anthropic和OpenAI嚴格保密的閉源專有模型形成了鮮明對比。
廣告
Despite Mr. Tang’s rhetoric of openness, Z.ai has otherwise offered little transparency into how its models are trained and evaluated. And Chinese citizens, business and other entities are subject to a National Intelligence Law that obliges them to support state intelligence work when called upon. Z.ai’s financial reports state that vulnerabilities uncovered by its models are submitted to Chinese authorities, including the National Vulnerability Database, which collects and analyzes information on cybersecurity flaws. The database is managed by Mr. Chen’s Ministry of State Security.
儘管唐傑高調宣傳開放，智譜AI在模型訓練和評估方式方面卻幾乎沒有透明度。此外，中國公民、企業及其他實體均須遵守《國家情報法》，該法規定他們有義務在接到要求時配合國家情報工作。智譜AI的財務報告顯示，其模型發現的安全漏洞會提交給中國當局，包括收集和分析網路安全缺陷信息的國家安全漏洞數據庫。而該數據庫正是由陳一新主管的國家安全部負責管理。
This raises a troubling question, especially as use of Chinese open-weight models spreads overseas: When Z.ai finds a dangerous flaw, who learns about it first — the company that needs to fix it, or the Chinese state?
這引發了一個令人不安的疑問，尤其是在中國開放式模型在海外廣泛應用的背景下：如果智譜AI發現一個危險的安全漏洞，究竟誰會首先獲知——是需要修復該漏洞的公司，還是中國政府？
Researchers at leading American A.I. labs and government agencies in Washington and allied capitals are already voicing growing concern over the potential threat from “sleeper agents” — malicious hidden instructions that can be planted in A.I. models and remain dormant until triggered, making them difficult to detect or remove. Those dangers are amplified by newer “agentic” A.I., which is often given greater and more autonomous access to computer systems.
美國頂尖人工智慧實驗室的研究人員，以及美國與盟友國家的政府機構，已經對「潛伏特工」(sleeper agents)帶來的潛在威脅表達日益強烈的擔憂。這類惡意隱藏指令可以預先植入人工智慧模型中，保持潛伏狀態，直至被特定條件觸發，極難被檢測或清除。而隨著具有更高自主權限、可直接訪問計算機系統的「智能體式」人工智慧的發展，這種危險被進一步放大。
All of these things underscore the pressing need for the United States and China to find common ground on A.I. — and, at the same time, why that will prove exceedingly difficult.
所有這一切都凸顯出美中兩國在人工智慧領域尋求共識的緊迫性——但同時也說明了為什麼達成共識將極其困難。
The Communist Party’s obsession with gaining an advantage over the United States and prioritizing regime security leaves little room for agreement on the catastrophic risks of A.I. Mr. Trump, too, seems to feel strategic competition outweighs the danger of unchecked A.I. development, recently dismissing the need for regulation in public comments and on social media.
中共執著於在與美國的競爭中取得優勢，並將政權安全置於首位，這令雙方几乎沒有就人工智慧的災難性風險達成共識的空間。而川普似乎也認為，戰略競爭的重要性超過了不受約束的人工智慧發展所帶來的危險，他最近在公開講話中和社群媒體上均對監管的必要性持否定態度。
Trump’s free-market attitude misses the larger contest taking shape. The race over A.I. is no longer only about technological superiority. It is also a contest over whose concepts of freedom, security and the role of the state become embedded in the powerful systems that are increasingly shaping how societies think, make decisions and wield power.
川普這種自由市場的態度忽視了正在形成的更大博弈。圍繞人工智慧的競賽已不僅僅關乎技術領先地位，更是雙方關於自由、安全以及國家角色等理念的角逐。勝出的理念將被融入日益深刻地塑造社會思維方式、決策過程和權力運作方式的強大系統之中。
