美中同意將貿易休戰期延長兩個月

U.S. and China Agree to Extend Trade Truce by 2 Months, Bessent Says

ANA SWANSON, KEITH BRADSHER • 2026-09-24
Scott Bessent, the Treasury secretary who is leading trade talks with China this week, said Wednesday in an interview on Fox News that the United States and China had agreed to extend a trade truce for two more months past a November deadline.
週三，本週主持對華貿易談判的美國財政部長史考特·貝森特在接受福克斯新聞採訪時表示，美中兩國已同意將貿易休戰協議在11月到期後再延長兩個月。
The truce was struck in South Korea last year after President Trump imposed steep tariffs on Chinese exports and the United States and China became embroiled in a tit-for-tat trade war. The truce has involved the United States’ suspending certain tariffs and other restrictions on the Chinese economy, and China’s agreeing to provide a steady flow of rare earth minerals needed by U.S. factories making cars, semiconductors, planes, power tools and other products.
該休戰協議於去年在韓國達成。在那之前，川普總統對中國出口商品徵收了高額關稅，美中兩國陷入了針鋒相對的貿易戰。休戰協議的內容包括：美國暫停對中國經濟實施的部分關稅及其他限制，中國則同意穩定供應美國汽車、半導體、飛機、電動工具及其他產品所需的稀土礦物。
The agreement will be extended until Jan. 10, Mr. Bessent said, “to give us more time to see what we can do on the economic front.”
貝森特表示，協議將延長至明年1月10日，「給我們更多時間來探討經濟領域能採取哪些措施」。
Mr. Bessent gave the interview around the same time that Mr. Trump was greeting the Chinese leader Xi Jinping with a lavish ceremony at an air base outside Washington for the start of a multiday state visit.
貝森特接受採訪時，川普總統正在華盛頓郊外的一個空軍基地舉行盛大儀式，迎接中國國家主席習近平，他將進行為期數天的國事訪問。
廣告
The visit will include another welcome ceremony with an honor guard at the White House, a meeting between the two presidents, a state dinner and a tour of the National Archives. It may be the second of as many as four in-person meetings that the two leaders will host this year, Mr. Bessent said.
此次訪問行程還包括在白宮舉行的另一場由儀仗隊護衛的歡迎儀式、兩國元首會晤、國宴以及參觀國家檔案館。貝森特表示，兩位領導人今年可能進行多達四次的面對面會晤，這是其中的第二次。
Chinese officials have not yet confirmed the extension of the trade truce. In a statement issued by Chinese state media after he arrived in the United States, Mr. Xi called for Beijing and Washington to focus on cooperation, and said he was looking forward to in-depth exchanges with Mr. Trump.
中國官方尚未證實延長貿易休戰協議。習近平抵達美國後通過中國官方媒體發表聲明，呼籲北京和華盛頓聚焦合作，並表示期待與川普總統進行深入交流。
The Treasury secretary said he met again today with He Lifeng, the Chinese vice premier, whom he had also met with on Sunday, “to see if we could do a bigger deal.” Mr. Bessent said that the two sides were working to reduce tariffs on $30 billion of imports from each country, and that the countries would probably also make announcements relating to financial services and agricultural purchases.
財政部長表示，他週三再次與中國副總理何立峰舉行了會晤（兩人週日也曾會面），「看看我們能否達成一項更大的協議」。貝森特表示，雙方正在努力削減各自國家價值300億美元進口商品的關稅，且兩國可能還會就金融服務和農產品採購發布相關聲明。
Mr. Bessent admitted that China was “behind schedule” on some agricultural purchases, and he said the United States was encouraging the country to increase those.
貝森特承認，中國在某些農產品採購方面「落後於進度」，他表示美國正在鼓勵中國加大採購力度。
The agreement will allow the United States and China to put aside their differences through four major events in the coming weeks. For the United States, that includes the midterm elections on Nov. 3 and the Group of 20 summit of world leaders in Miami on Dec. 14 and 15.
協議將使美國和中國能夠擱置分歧，迎接未來幾週的四項重要事宜。對美國而言，包括11月3日的期中選舉和12月14日至15日在邁阿密舉行的二十國集團領導人峰會。
For China, that includes the annual gathering of the Communist Party’s Central Committee in Beijing from Oct. 26 to 29 and the summit of Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation presidents and prime ministers in Shenzhen, China, on Nov. 18 and 19.
對中國而言，這包括10月26日至29日在北京舉行的中共二十屆五中全會，以及11月18日至19日在深圳舉行的亞太經合組織領導人非正式會議。
廣告
If the current summit in Washington goes well, Mr. Trump and Mr. Xi are tentatively expected to attend each other’s events in Shenzhen and Miami. Those gatherings might provide more chances for the two leaders and their top trade negotiators to pursue more deals before Jan. 10.
如果華盛頓峰會進展順利，預計川普和習近平將分別出席對方在深圳和邁阿密舉辦的會議。這些會晤或許能為兩位領導人及其首席貿易談判代表提供更多機會，在1月10日之前達成更多協議。
Asked if China would agree to a new notification system for national security issues related to artificial intelligence that Mr. Bessent announced last weekend, he said he believed that they would and that China was “very eager” to engage with the United States on A.I.
當被問及中國是否會同意貝森特上週末宣布的關於人工智慧相關國家安全問題的新通報機制時，他表示相信中方會同意，並稱中國「非常渴望」在人工智慧領域與美國展開接觸。
The United States is working with China to define the most important A.I. risks, including uncontrolled A.I. agents and nonstate actors using A.I. to carry out cyberattacks or create biological weapons, Mr. Bessent added. He said he hoped that the United States and China “would be able to talk about any incidents that have occurred, especially any that have crossed national borders.”
貝森特還說，美國正與中國合作明確最重要的人工智慧風險，包括不受控制的人工智慧智能體，以及非國家行為體利用人工智慧發動網路攻擊或製造生物武器等。他表示，希望美中兩國「能夠就已發生的任何事件進行溝通，特別是那些涉及跨國的事件」。
The extension of the trade truce has broad implications for the global economy because it appears to include a postponement of China’s plans for even more stringent limits on rare earths, which are used extensively in automotive components, as well as in semiconductors, wind turbines, smartphones, computer displays, lasers, drones and hundreds of other technologies, including military hardware.
貿易休戰期的延長對全球經濟具有深遠影響，因為這似乎意味著中國將推遲實施對稀土實施更嚴格限制的計劃。稀土廣泛應用於汽車零部件、半導體、風力渦輪機、智慧型手機、電腦顯示器、雷射器、無人機以及包括軍事裝備在內的數百種其他技術領域。
China had previously issued export control regulations that would give it the right, starting on Nov. 10, to approve any movement across any country’s borders of any product for which at least 0.1 percent of the value consisted of Chinese rare earths.
中國此前曾頒佈出口管制條例，規定從11月10日起，凡產品價值中至少0.1%來自中國稀土的，其跨國境流動均須經中國批准。
That pending rule caused particular alarm in Europe, with automakers there warning that even moving a car seat from a factory in Poland to a car assembly plant in Germany would require permission from China. Mr. Bessent had warned strongly about the Chinese regulations shortly after they were first issued a year ago, saying that China had “pointed a bazooka at the supply chains and the industrial base of the entire free world.”
這項待實施的規定在歐洲引發了特別的擔憂，當地汽車製造商警告稱，即使是將一個汽車座椅從波蘭的工廠運往德國的汽車裝配廠也需要獲得中國的許可。貝森特在一年前中國首次發布這些規定後不久就曾對此發出強烈警告，稱中國「將火箭筒對準了整個自由世界的供應鏈和工業基礎」。
廣告
The postponed Chinese rules would also have expanded export controls to five more kinds of rare earths that China has not previously regulated.
被推遲實施的中國新規原本還將把出口管制範圍擴大到另外五種此前未受中國監管的稀土品種。
Jens Eskelund, the president of the European Union Chamber of Commerce in China, said that a two-month postponement of China’s plans for more stringent export controls on rare earths would not be long enough. “This short extension will not address the concerns of the European business community in regard to finding solutions that deliver the transparency and predictability so critical to business planning.”
中國歐盟商會主席彥辭(Jens Eskelund)表示，中國將實施更嚴格的稀土出口管制計劃推遲兩個月，這是不夠的。「如此短暫的延期無法消除歐洲商界對尋找解決方案的擔憂，而這些解決方案必須能夠提供對商業規劃至關重要的透明度和可預測性。」
