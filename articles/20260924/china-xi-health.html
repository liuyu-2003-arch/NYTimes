習近平訪美，其健康狀況引發廣泛關注

Xi’s Visit Will Fuel More Scrutiny Over His Health

儲百亮 • 2026-09-24
When China’s leader, Xi Jinping, arrives at Joint Base Andrews, every step he takes down the stairs from his plane will be scrutinized for clues about his health.
中國領導人習近平抵達安德魯斯聯合基地時，他從專機舷梯上走下來的每一步都會受到仔細審視，以尋找有關他健康狀況的蛛絲馬跡。
Mr. Xi is China’s most dominant leader in decades: He is preparing to claim an all-but-certain fourth term in power from late next year, and there is no sign of a likely successor waiting in the wings. And at 73, Mr. Xi is old enough that any stumble or missed meeting ignites speculation abroad about his health. (The topic is heavily censored in China.)
習近平是中國數十年來最強勢的領導人：他正準備從明年年底開始，幾乎毫無懸念地開啟第四個任期，而且沒有任何跡象顯示有潛在的繼任者在等待接棒。與此同時，現年73歲的習近平年事已高，以至於任何一次踉蹌或缺席會議都會在海外引發有關其健康狀況的猜測。(這一話題在中國國內受到嚴格審查。)
No matter what happens on the stairs or during Mr. Xi’s three-day visit to Washington, guesswork about his health and probable longevity is sure to persist.
無論習近平在舷梯上的表現如何，也無論他為期三天的華盛頓之行中發生什麼，外界圍繞其健康狀況與可能壽命的種種猜測，肯定還會持續下去。
China’s Communist Party does not have clear rules of succession if a leader becomes incapacitated or dies, and Mr. Xi magnified the uncertainties by overturning retirement norms. Mr. Xi’s predecessor, Hu Jintao, retired in 2012 at 69, and his cognitive decline appeared visible in 2022, when he was abruptly escorted from his chair at a party congress, looking confused and upset.
中國共產黨目前並沒有一套明確的繼承規則來應對領導人喪失行為能力或去世的情況，而習近平打破退休慣例更是進一步增加了這種不確定性。習近平的前任胡錦濤於2012年退休，當時69歲；到2022年，他的認知能力衰退跡象已較為明顯——當時他在一場黨代會上被突然攙扶離席，神情顯得困惑又不安。
廣告
In August, Mr. Xi stepped slowly down the stairway of his plane, holding his wife’s hand, when they visited Kyrgyzstan, igniting rumors online about his health.
今年8月習近平夫婦訪問吉爾吉斯斯坦時，他牽著妻子的手緩慢走下飛機舷梯，由此引發了網上有關他健康狀況的傳言。
The discussion intensified this month after he skipped a dinner at a regional leaders’ meeting in New Delhi. Mr. Xi was absent from a dinner for the BRICS group of developing countries — which includes Brazil, Russia, India and China — according to Indian news reports and a diplomat from one of the participating countries.
本月，他缺席在新德里舉行的一次地區領導人會議的晚宴後，相關討論進一步升溫。據印度媒體報導及一名與會國外交官透露，習近平缺席了金磚國家的一場晚宴。金磚國家是一個由發展中國家組成的集團，其中包括巴西、俄羅斯、印度和中國。
There was no suggestion that Mr. Xi was seriously ill. When he left New Delhi, he climbed the stairs to his plane unaided. Still, the absence fed questions in some foreign news reports about Mr. Xi’s health and stamina.
目前並無跡象表明習近平患有嚴重疾病。他離開新德里時是在無人攙扶的情況下獨自登上飛機舷梯的。儘管如此，這次缺席仍在一些海外媒體報導中引發了對習近平健康狀況與體力的種種疑問。
Mr. Xi is a bulky man who has said he became a smoker from his youth in the Chinese countryside, but details of his lifestyle are unclear. A friend of Mr. Xi told U.S. diplomats in 2009 that Mr. Xi, then preparing to succeed Mr. Hu, “did not drink or do drugs,” according to a diplomatic cable disclosed by WikiLeaks.
習近平身材魁梧。他曾說，自己年輕時在中國農村生活期間開始吸煙，但外界並不清楚他的生活習慣細節。維基解密披露的一份外交電報顯示，習近平的一位友人2009年曾告訴美國外交官，當時正準備接替胡錦濤的習近平「不喝酒不吸毒」。
But Dennis Wilder, a former C.I.A. senior analyst who tracked Chinese leaders, told The Wall Street Journal that Mr. Xi had a reputation as a frequent drinker and longtime smoker who did not like to exercise.
但據《華爾街日報》報導，曾長期跟蹤研究中國領導人的中情局前高級分析師韋德寧(Dennis Wilder)表示，習近平素有嗜酒的名聲，長期吸煙，而且不喜歡運動。
Mr. Xi has not publicly commented on his health or plans for retiring or staying in office. Last year, he was inadvertently caught on a microphone chatting with Russia’s president, Vladimir V. Putin, about living forever. “People rarely lived to be over 70, but these days, at 70, you are still a child,” a man who appeared to be translating Mr. Xi’s remarks to Mr. Putin was recorded saying in Russian.
習近平從未公開談論自己的健康狀況，也沒有公開談論退休或繼續執政的打算。去年，他與俄羅斯總統弗拉基米爾·普丁閑聊長生不老的話題時，談話意外被麥克風錄了下來。錄音中，一名似乎是在向普丁翻譯習近平講話的人用俄語說道：「過去人們活不過70歲，現在70歲還算孩。」
廣告
Whatever his health, Mr. Xi gets some of the most attentive medical care in the world. Chinese leaders have their own health care system that provides round-the-clock care and checkups at elite hospitals, including the military-run 301 Hospital near the leadership’s headquarters in Beijing.
無論習近平的健康狀況如何，他都能獲得世界上最周到的醫療照護。中國領導人擁有專門的醫療保健體系，可獲得全天候醫療服務和檢查，地點包括北京中央領導機關附近、由軍方管理的301醫院等頂級醫院。

儲百亮(Chris Buckley)是《紐約時報》首席中國記者，自台北報導中國和台灣問題，重點關注政治、社會變革以及安全和軍事問題。
