更多美國人以積極眼光看待中國

As Trump Welcomes Xi, More Americans Have a Positive View of China

ANTON TROIANOVSKI • 2026-09-24
President Trump’s shift to a friendlier stance on China mirrors a startling change in public opinion. Across the political spectrum, more Americans are seeing China in a positive light.
川普總統轉向更友好的對華立場，折射出公眾輿論中令人驚訝的變化。無論政治立場如何，越來越多的美國人正以積極眼光看待中國。
Surveys from leading pollsters, including two released this week, show that China’s image in the United States has improved markedly in recent years. People are still skeptical about China, but as Mr. Trump prepares to give the country’s leader, Xi Jinping, a warm welcome in Washington this week, his accommodation echoes what some experts see as a desire among Americans for a more stable relationship with Beijing.
包括本週發布的兩份報告在內，來自主流民調機構的調查顯示，近年來中國在美國的形象顯著改善。人們對中國仍存疑慮，但隨著川普準備本週在華盛頓熱烈歡迎中國領導人習近平，他的順應態度印證了部分專家的觀點，即美國人渴望與北京建立更穩定的關係。
“There really is this warming toward China, even if on balance views still remain fairly negative,” said Laura Silver, an associate director at the Pew Research Center, which published survey data on Wednesday showing that 54 percent of Americans now have a “cold” view of China, compared with 68 percent in 2021.
「對華態度確實在回暖，即使總體看法依然相當負面，」皮尤研究中心副主任勞拉·西爾弗(Laura Silver)說。該中心於週三發布的調查數據顯示，目前有54%的美國人對中國持「冷淡」看法，而2021年該比例為68%。
The surveys do not pinpoint the reasons for the shift, which appears to be more pronounced among younger Americans. But experts point to fading memories of the coronavirus pandemic, awareness of China’s tech innovations and disaffection with Mr. Trump’s trade wars as contributing to a changing mood that could affect how U.S. politicians approach China.
這些調查並未闡明情緒轉變的具體原因，且這種轉變在年輕美國人中似乎相對顯著。但專家指出，對新冠大流行的記憶淡化，對中國科技創新的認知，以及對川普貿易戰的不滿共同促成了一種可能影響美國政客對華態度的情緒轉變。
廣告
The Chicago Council on Global Affairs, a think tank that has long studied the country’s foreign policy views, has picked up a reversal over just the last two years. By 2024, the share of Americans who favored “friendly cooperation and engagement with China” had declined to just 40 percent. But when the organization asked the question again this July, that number had risen to 57 percent, including 68 percent of Democrats and 43 percent of Republicans, according to data published this week.
長期研究美國外交政策觀點的智庫芝加哥全球事務委員會發現，僅在過去兩年裡就發生了一次態度逆轉。2024年，支持「與中國進行友好合作與接觸」的美國人比例一度降至僅40%。然而，根據本週公布的數據，當該組織今年7月再次調查此問題時，這一比例已升至57%，其中包括68%的民主黨人和43%的共和黨人。
“The attitudes toward China have been really surprising,” said Dina Smeltz, a former State Department official who oversees the Chicago Council’s polling. “They’ve shifted from a majority wanting to limit the rise of China and limit its influence to now a majority of Americans wanting to engage cooperatively with China.”
「民眾對中國的態度著實令人驚訝，」負責監督芝加哥委員會民調的前國務院官員迪娜·斯梅爾茨說。「他們已從多數人希望限制中國的崛起與影響力，轉變為如今多數美國人希望與中國合作接觸。」
The research organization Gallup found earlier this year that the share of Americans with a favorable view of China had risen to 34 percent, more than double the low point in 2023.
研究機構蓋洛普今年早些時候發現，對中國持好感的美國人比例已升至34%，是2023年低谷時期的兩倍多。
The surveys all point to a changed national mood compared with a decade ago, when Mr. Trump’s harsh anti-China rhetoric helped define his winning presidential campaign. President Joseph R. Biden Jr. largely maintained and expanded Mr. Trump’s tough policies, including tariffs and export controls, cementing what appeared to be a bipartisan consensus that the United States needed to push back hard against China’s rise.
這些調查均反映出與十年前截然不同的國民情緒，當時川普嚴厲的反華言論助推了他的勝選。小約瑟夫·R·拜登總統在很大程度上維持並擴大了川普的強硬政策，包括關稅和出口管制，鞏固了一個似乎是兩黨共識的觀念：美國需要強硬反擊中國的崛起。
Now that consensus has weakened, as made plain by Mr. Trump himself. He stepped back last year from tariffs as high as 145 percent to pursue what has looked like an effort of reconciliation with China. It was on display in his deferential visit to Beijing in April and what is expected to be a lavish welcome for Mr. Xi this week — all without Beijing making major concessions on longstanding U.S. demands on trade or security issues.
正如川普本人所表明的那樣，現在這種共識已經弱化。去年，他從高達145%的關稅政策上退讓，似乎在尋求對華和解。這體現在他4月訪問北京時的恭順，以及預計本週對習近平的隆重歡迎中——而這一切都是在北京沒有就貿易或安全等美國長期訴求做出重大讓步的情況下發生的。
The polls show that Republicans remain more hawkish on China than Democrats, though Republicans’ views of China have grown more positive as well — perhaps a reflection of Mr. Trump’s own shift. Despite the country’s polarization, Democrats have also moved in the same direction.
民調錶明，共和黨人在對華問題上仍比民主黨人更為鷹派，不過共和黨人對中國的看法也變得更加正面——這或許反映了川普自身的立場轉變。儘管美國存在政治極化，但民主黨人也正朝著相同方向發展。
Just a year or two ago, politicians could “propose almost any kind of tough policy against China and the public would be behind it,” said Bruce Stokes, an expert on foreign policy polling at the German Marshall Fund, a think tank. “This public opinion data seems to suggest that support for a tough-on-China policy has weakened a little bit.”
僅僅一兩年前，政客們「提出幾乎任何針對中國的強硬政策，公眾都會予以支持」，智庫德國馬歇爾基金會外交政策民調專家布魯斯·斯托克斯說。「這些民調數據似乎表明，對強硬對華政策的支持已略有減弱。」
廣告
Americans’ views of China, of course, could quickly shift again, especially amid fears of its artificial intelligence prowess and the risk of armed conflict over Taiwan. Pollsters note that Americans generally spend little time thinking about foreign policy and rarely let international issues determine how they vote.
當然，美國人對中國的看法可能會再次迅速轉變，特別是在對其人工智慧實力的擔憂以及因台灣問題引發武裝衝突風險的背景下。民調專家指出，美國人通常很少花時間思考外交政策，也極少讓國際問題決定其投票意向。
But people who study the U.S. relationship with China see the polling figures as reflective of a changing geopolitical dynamic and, perhaps, angst over the United States’ place in the world. In the Pew and Chicago Council polls, younger adults are significantly more positively disposed toward China, evidence of a generational shift.
但研究美中關係的人士認為，民調數據反映出不斷變化的地緣政治動態，或許也折射出對美國國際地位的焦慮。在皮尤和芝加哥委員會的民調中，較年輕成年人對中國的態度明顯更加積極，這印證了代際轉變。
“Younger people have known China as a superpower,” Ms. Silver, the Pew researcher, said. Older generations, by contrast, are more likely to associate China with the developing world, Communism and the Tiananmen Square massacre of 1989, she said.
「年輕人所認識的中國是一個超級大國，」皮尤研究員西爾弗說。她表示，相比之下，老一代人更有可能將中國與發展中國家、共產主義以及1989年的天安門廣場大屠殺聯繫起來。
Younger people may also be more aware of China’s technological innovations and cultural exports, as evidenced by the “Chinamaxxing” trends ricocheting around the internet. That awareness comes in contrast to a sense of a decline in the United States’ global standing: 62 percent of Americans say the country is respected less in the world today than it was 10 years ago, according to the Chicago Council.
年輕群體可能也對中國的技術創新和文化輸出有更多認知，在互聯網上風靡的「中國最大化(Chinamaxxing)」趨勢便是明證。這種認知與對美國全球地位衰退的感受形成反差：根據芝加哥委員會的數據，62%的美國人認為，當今美國在世界上受到的尊重不如10年前。
“You see fears of what’s happening to America be refracted through assessments of China,” said Nick Zeller, a scholar at the Carter Center in Atlanta who tracks how China and the United States perceive each other. “This positive assessment toward China has more to do with American political tumult domestically than it does China itself.”
「你會發現，對美國現狀的恐懼通過對中國的評估折射出來，」位於亞特蘭大的卡特中心追蹤中美互視視角的學者尼克·澤勒表示。「這種對中國的正面評估更多地與美國國內的政治動盪有關，而非中國本身。」

Anton Troianovski自華盛頓為《紐約時報》報導有關美國外交政策和國家安全的文章。他此前曾在莫斯科和柏林任駐外記者。
