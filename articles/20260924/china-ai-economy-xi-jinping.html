傾國之力：習近平希望用人工智慧助中國贏得未來

How China’s Leader Plans to Win the Future With A.I.

王月眉 • 2026-09-24
In a stretch of former farmland now transformed into a gleaming technology park, China’s leader, Xi Jinping, watched two robotic hands slowly thread a needle.
在一座由農田改建的閃亮科技園區，中國國家主席習近平觀看了兩隻機械手緩慢穿針引線的過程。
What he was seeing, he declared, would help China seize its future.
他宣布，他所看到的這一切將幫助中國抓住未來。
It is a future in which artificial intelligence permeates China’s factories, hospitals and classrooms, reinvigorating the economy through advanced robotics and high-end manufacturing. It is a future in which China, not the United States, dominates A.I. development, turning the technology into what Mr. Xi has called a “lead goose” to fly the country into its next economic revolution.
在這個未來中，人工智慧將滲透至中國的工廠、醫院和教室，通過先進機器人和高端製造重振經濟。在這個未來中，將由中國而非美國主導人工智慧的發展，把這項技術轉化為習近平所稱的「頭雁」，引領國家飛向下一場經濟革命。
To realize that vision, the Chinese government is mobilizing the full weight of the state.
為了實現這一願景，中國政府正在動用傾國之力。
廣告
At the park that Mr. Xi visited, on the outskirts of Beijing in February, hundreds of companies have set up offices to develop cutting-edge chips and A.I. models. The city government is giving them tens of millions of dollars in free computing power. An exhibition hall shows off their creations, including chips designed to power nuclear missiles and brain implants that could help paralyzed patients speak.
在今年2月習近平訪問的這個位於北京郊區的園區內，已有數百家企業設立辦公點，研發尖端晶片和人工智慧模型。市政府正向他們提供價值數千萬美元的免費算力。一座展廳展示著這些企業的研發成果，其中包括用於驅動核導彈的晶片，以及能幫助癱瘓患者恢復語言能力的腦機接口。
Mr. Xi, during his visit, said that China’s strength lies in its ability to zero in on a direction and follow through.
習近平在考察期間表示，中國的優勢在於能夠錨定一個方向並貫徹到底。
“We must give full play to our nation’s advantage of concentrating resources to accomplish major tasks,” he told an assembled group of entrepreneurs, researchers and officials.
我們要「充分發揮我們國家集中力量辦大事的優勢」，他向在場的一群企業家、科研人員和官員表示。
China’s A.I. campaign is backed by enormous sums of government money. Economists estimate that state-led funds put more than $184 billion into A.I. firms between 2000 and 2023, and last year, the government and state-owned banks pledged hundreds of billions more. Private A.I. investment in China is far lower than in the United States, where private sources poured $285 billion into the technology in 2025 alone.
中國的人工智慧攻勢得到了巨額政府資金的支持。據經濟學家估計，在2000年至2023年間，國家主導的基金向人工智慧企業投入了超過1840億美元，去年政府和國有銀行又承諾提供數千億美元。中國在人工智慧領域的私人投資遠低於美國，僅在2025年，美國的民間資本便向該技術傾注了2850億美元。
Beijing’s approach to A.I. is distinct from the prevailing one in the United States in other ways, too.
北京對待人工智慧的策略在其他方面也與美國的普遍做法截然不同。
Many in Washington and Silicon Valley see A.I. development as a race toward artificial general intelligence, a still-theoretical form of A.I. that could think and learn as well as — or better than — humans.
華盛頓和矽谷的許多人將人工智慧的發展視為一場邁向通用人工智慧(AGI)的競賽，這是一種仍處於理論階段的人工智慧形式，旨在讓人工智慧做到像人類一樣——甚至超越人類。
廣告
Chinese officials have hardly mentioned A.G.I., focusing on more immediate goals.
而中國官員幾乎未曾提及通用人工智慧，他們更關注立竿見影的目標。
China’s population is rapidly shrinking and getting older, creating an urgent need for intelligent robots to replace retiring workers. Poor rural residents could seek medical care from digital avatars of top urban doctors. The authorities could more easily know where Chinese citizens are, what they say and perhaps even how they feel.
中國人口正在迅速萎縮並走向老齡化，這催生了用智能機器人替代退休勞動力的迫切需求。貧困地區的農村居民可以向頂尖城市醫生的數字替身尋求醫療服務。相關部門也能藉此更輕易地掌握中國公民的行蹤、言論，甚至可能感知他們的情緒。
The government has said that A.I.-powered tools and agents should permeate 90 percent of Chinese society by 2030, though it has not explained how that would be measured.
政府曾表示，到2030年，人工智慧驅動的工具和智能體應覆蓋90%的中國社會，不過並未解釋將如何衡量這一目標。
Mr. Xi has also endorsed Chinese companies’ use of open-source models, which have helped them win users globally when leading American companies have released closed systems.
習近平還對中國企業使用開源模型表示認可。當領先的美國企業相繼發布封閉系統時，開源策略已幫助中國企業贏得了全球用戶。
China has embraced A.I. despite the potential risks it poses to the Communist Party’s legitimacy and tight grip on society.
儘管人工智慧對共產黨的合法性及其對社會的嚴密掌控構成了潛在風險，但中國依然擁抱了這項技術。
The technology is already displacing taxi drivers, actors and office workers, heightening worries about joblessness and potential unrest.
該技術已經在取代計程車司機、演員和辦公室白領，加劇了人們對失業和潛在社會動盪的擔憂。
廣告
Chatbots could threaten the party’s controls on information, while advanced foreign models like Claude Mythos could make it easier for China’s enemies to launch complex hacking attacks on its critical infrastructure — a threat the country’s top spy chief recently acknowledged.
聊天機器人可能會威脅到中共對信息的管控，而像Claude Mythos這樣先進的外國模型可能會使中國的敵對勢力更容易對其關鍵基礎設施發動複雜的駭客攻擊——中國國家安全部高級官員近期也承認了這一威脅。
Mr. Xi is betting that China can control those dangers.
習近平押注中國能夠控制住這些危險。
Regulators have imposed strict rules on what chatbots can say and banned A.I. companion bots for children. Chinese courts have warned companies not to blindly replace workers with technology. The authorities have also barred some leading A.I. entrepreneurs from leaving China.
監管機構已對聊天機器人的發言內容施加了嚴格限制，並禁止了面向兒童的人工智慧陪伴機器人。中國法院已警告企業不要盲目用技術取代工人。相關部門還限制了一些頂尖的人工智慧企業家出境。
But Beijing’s confidence in its controls, and its determination to catch up with the United States, also mean it is skeptical of global calls to slow the pace of A.I. development.
但北京對其掌控力的信心以及趕超美國的決心也意味著其對全球要求放緩人工智慧發展步伐的呼籲持懷疑態度。
In Beijing’s view, China is already fighting from behind. Washington has limited China’s access to the most advanced chips, so China lacks the computing power that is needed to train cutting-edge A.I. Agreeing to slow down now, in this view, would only cement America’s lead.
在北京看來，中國本就處在落後追趕的位置。華盛頓限制了中國獲取最先進晶片的管道，導致中國缺乏訓練尖端人工智慧所需的算力。基於這種觀點，如果現在同意放慢腳步，只會鞏固美國的領先地位。
The 2014 Speech That Started It All
開啟一切的2014年講話
廣告
In Beijing’s Great Hall of the People, China’s top scientists gathered for a speech by Mr. Xi. It was June 2014, the first time that Mr. Xi addressed China’s Academies of Engineering and Sciences as the country’s leader.
在北京人民大會堂，中國頂尖科學家齊聚一堂，聆聽習近平的講話。那是2014年6月，習近平首次以國家領導人身份在兩院院士大會上講話。
The attendees had submitted reports beforehand, and Mr. Xi said he had read them closely. He said he was particularly intrigued by one report predicting that A.I.-powered robots would soon transform the global economy. Such machines, unlike traditional robots that repeat specific preprogrammed tasks, can use A.I. to learn from and adapt to their surroundings. China would be the biggest market, the report said.
與會者事先提交了報告，習近平表示他已仔細閱讀。他說自己對一份預測人工智慧驅動的機器人將很快改變全球經濟的報告特別感興趣。與重複預設任務的傳統機器人不同，這類機器能利用人工智慧從周圍環境中學習並適應。報告指出，中國將成為其最大的市場。
That wasn’t enough, Mr. Xi said. China should be not just the biggest buyer, but also the top maker.
習近平表示，這還不夠。中國不僅應成為最大的買家，還應成為頂尖的製造者。
“Reading this, I wondered,” he added, “can our technology and manufacturing capabilities keep up with the competition?”
「看到這裡，我就在想，」他說，「我們的技術和製造能力能不能應對這場競爭？」
That speech set off a frenzy that still shapes China’s A.I. policy today.
那次講話掀起的一股熱潮至今仍在塑造著中國的人工智慧政策。
Immediately afterward, Pan Yunhe, the Chinese Academy of Engineering’s vice president and a respected A.I. researcher, organized a team to design a long-term strategy for the country, according to Gong Ke, a professor in Tianjin who leads an A.I. research institute under the academy.
據在天津負責管理該院下屬一所人工智慧研究院的龔克教授透露，會後不久，時任中國工程院副院長、備受尊敬的人工智慧專家潘雲鶴便組織團隊，開始為國家設計一項長期戰略。
廣告
China was well positioned to take the lead in A.I., Professor Pan wrote in a research paper shortly afterward, because its huge population ensured that there was both a great supply of data to train models, and great demand for intelligent products. He also wrote that China urgently needed A.I. to address problems that the country’s rapid modernization had created; smart manufacturing and A.I.-assisted education, for example, could help address issues such as pollution and inequality. (Professor Pan declined an interview request.)
潘雲鶴在不久後發表的一篇研究論文中寫道，中國非常有望在人工智慧領域取得領先地位，因為其龐大的人口不僅能為訓練模型提供海量數據，也為智能產品創造了巨大需求。他還寫道，中國迫切需要人工智慧來解決快速現代化帶來的問題；例如，智能製造和人工智慧輔助教育能夠幫助應對污染和不平等等挑戰。（潘雲鶴拒絕了採訪請求。）
In 2017, China issued its first national A.I. plan, which, in parts, used language virtually identical to Professor Pan’s paper. It said that China should become a world leader in the technology by 2030.
2017年，中國發布了首個國家級人工智慧發展規劃，其中部分表述與潘雲鶴的論文幾乎如出一轍。規劃提出，中國應在2030年成為該技術的世界領導者。
In the years after the plan was released, China’s A.I. sector expanded rapidly. State and private investors pumped in money. The number of A.I. patents by China soon blew past that of the United States.
在該規劃發布後的幾年裡，中國的人工智慧產業迅速擴張。國家和私人投資者注入了大量資金。中國的人工智慧專利數量很快便超越了美國。
From the start, Beijing saw A.I. as a force, like the internet, that could shore up the party’s power. It could help maintain public satisfaction by boosting economic growth. It could also head off potential unrest by helping the party monitor China’s people.
從一開始，北京就將人工智慧視作一種類似於互聯網、能夠鞏固黨執政地位的力量。它可以通過推動經濟增長來幫助維持民眾的滿意度。它還能通過協助官方實施監控，防範潛在的社會動盪。
Manufacturers pushed A.I. into factories and warehouses. Surveillance companies became global leaders in facial and voice recognition. At one point in 2018, SenseTime, a Beijing-based company that has helped the government police China’s Uyghur minority, was the world’s most valuable A.I. start-up.
製造商將人工智慧引入工廠和倉庫。監控企業成為了面部和語音識別領域的全球領軍者。2018年，曾協助政府對少數民族維吾爾人進行警務監控的北京企業商湯科技一度成為全球估值最高的人工智慧初創公司。
But Chinese researchers were generally not focused on large language models, as American researchers were. When ChatGPT appeared in 2022, China reeled. Commentators who had boasted of China’s lead in A.I. asked why the breakthrough had not come from their country.
但當時中國研究人員普遍沒有像美國同行那樣專注於大型語言模型。當ChatGPT於2022年問世時，中國深受震動。曾經誇耀中國在人工智慧領域處於領先地位的評論員不禁發問：為什麼這一突破沒有誕生在自己的國家？
Then, last year, came DeepSeek. The previously unknown Chinese start-up stunned the world by showing that Chinese firms could compete with Silicon Valley on large language models, too. In February 2025, a few weeks after DeepSeek released its model, Mr. Xi summoned a group of Chinese tech executives — including DeepSeek’s founder — to a meeting.
隨後，深度求索(DeepSeek)在去年橫空出世。這家此前沒沒無聞的中國初創公司向世界證明了中國企業在大型語言模型上同樣能與矽谷一較高下，此舉震驚了全球。2025年2月，即DeepSeek發布其模型幾周後，習近平召集了一群中國科技企業高管舉行座談會，其中就包括DeepSeek的創始人。
“It is the right time for private enterprises and entrepreneurs to fully demonstrate their abilities,” he said. Those gathered must “unify our thinking” and “serve the country.”
「廣大民營企業和民營企業家大顯身手正當其時，」他表示。與會者必須「統一思想」，並「胸懷報國志」。
A Recipe for Dominance
佔據主導地位的秘訣
Beijing is not just tapping its biggest tech tycoons for that mission. State support is supercharging individual ambitions across the country, as Hobo Liang, a 42-year-old entrepreneur in the tech hub of Shenzhen, has seen firsthand.
為了實現這一使命，北京不僅在吸納最頂尖的科技巨頭。國家的支持也正在為全國各地個人的雄心壯志提供強大動力，在科技重鎮深圳，42歲的創業者梁哲對此深有體會。
The son of mathematics professors in northern China, Mr. Liang was drawn to machines from an early age. In elementary school, he built homemade radios from spare parts. At university in the mid-2000s, he studied electrical engineering and decided to start a robotics company.
作為兩位在北方任教的數學教授的兒子，梁哲從小就對機械著迷。小學時，他用廢舊零件自製收音機。在2000年代中期的大學時代，他主修電氣工程，並下定決心創辦一家機器人公司。
People around him were doubtful. One potential investor asked Mr. Liang why China needed robots when it had so many people. His parents urged him to apply for stable jobs at state-owned companies. China’s high-tech sector was in its infancy, and many of Mr. Liang’s heroes were in the United States: Steve Jobs, Elon Musk. He even idolized a fictional entrepreneur — Tony Stark, of Marvel’s Iron Man movies.
身邊的人對此表示懷疑。一位潛在投資者問他，既然中國人口眾多，為什麼還需要機器人。父母敦促他找一份國企的穩定工作。當時中國的高科技產業尚處在起步階段，梁哲心目中的英雄大多在美國：史提夫·賈伯斯、伊隆·馬斯克。他甚至崇拜一位虛構的企業家——漫威電影《鋼鐵人》中的托尼·斯塔克。
Today, Mr. Liang still keeps an Iron Man figurine on his desk. But that early skepticism feels far away. His start-up, Xeno Dynamics, which makes robotic exoskeletons for people with mobility issues, recently moved into a new government-built industrial park, next door to China’s leading drone maker.
如今，他的辦公桌上依然擺放著一個鋼鐵人手辦。但當年哪些懷疑的聲音已經感覺十分遙遠。他創辦的奇諾動力(Xeno Dynamics)專門為行動不便者製造外骨骼機器人，最近已搬進一個由政府新建的產業園，與中國首屈一指的無人機製造商比鄰而居。
Xeno has been recognized as a national high-tech enterprise, meaning its tax rate is reduced from 25 percent to 15 percent. It has received over $1.4 million in government research funds, as well as multiple rounds of venture capital funding.
奇諾動力已被認定為國家高新技術企業，這意味著其企業所得稅率由25%降至15%。該公司已獲得逾1000萬元的政府研發資金，以及多輪風險投資。
“We’re beneficiaries of the entire direction — whether it’s about robots, or A.I.,” Mr. Liang said in an interview in his office. “You have to follow the direction of major policies.”
「這個方向正好我們是個受益者，不管從機器人來講，AI也好，還是從現在講的銀髮經濟，我們都是受益者，」梁哲在其辦公室接受採訪時說。「所以這個就是跟著這個大的政策方向走，一定是這樣。」
In many ways, China’s playbook for A.I. mirrors the one it used to dominate electric vehicles and solar panels: subsidies, tax breaks and research money.
在許多方面，中國發展人工智慧的策略與其在電動汽車和太陽能電池板領域佔據主導地位的策略如出一轍：補貼、稅收減免和研發資金。
A district in Shanghai offered up to $29 million to any A.I. company that achieved a major “breakthrough.” A university hub in Beijing said it had spent about $580 million on A.I. last year, funding data annotation centers, labs and housing for researchers.
上海的一個區政府提出，將向任何取得重大「突破」的人工智慧企業提供最高達2億元的獎勵。北京一處大學城表示去年在人工智慧領域投入了約40億元，用於資助數據標註中心、實驗室以及為研究人員提供住房。
In Shenzhen, officials spent more than $6 billion on the industrial park where Xeno’s offices are located. The district also promised to help robotics companies find potential customers.
在深圳，官方耗資逾60億美元建設了奇諾動力所在的產業園。區政府還承諾協助機器人企業尋找潛在客戶。
The central government, for its part, has been spending heavily on physical infrastructure. Beijing’s newest five-year plan calls for building ultra-large computing clusters and ensuring electricity to power the A.I. boom. Officials are also setting up a national exchange to trade data to train A.I. models.
而中央政府一直在實體基礎設施上投入巨資。北京最新的五年規劃要求建設超大型計算集群，並確保電力供應以支撐人工智慧熱潮。官員們還正在籌建一個全國交易所，用於買賣訓練人工智慧模型所需的數據。
The Limits of State Planning
國家規劃的局限性
But it may be harder for Beijing to engineer mastery in A.I. than in electric vehicles or solar panels.
然而，北京若想在人工智慧領域取得像在電動汽車或太陽能電池板那樣的掌控力，可能難度更大。
In those sectors, the technology was fairly developed, and the state’s role was giving companies the financial support to edge out foreign competitors, said Cole McFaul, a researcher at the Center for Security and Emerging Technology at Georgetown University.
喬治城大學安全與新興技術中心研究員科爾·麥克福爾表示，在這些行業，技術已經相對成熟，國家的角色在於給予企業資金支持以擠走外國競爭對手。
With A.I., leadership will hinge on technological breakthroughs. Subsidies can do only so much.
但對於人工智慧而言，能否佔據領導地位將取決於技術突破。補貼的作用終究有限。
One obstacle is a shortage of specialized talent. Another is the lack of access to the most powerful chips. Indeed, DeepSeek’s founder, Liang Wenfeng, has said that the company needs high-end chips, not more money.
障礙之一是專業人才匱乏。其二則是無法獲取最強大的晶片。事實上，DeepSeek創始人梁文鋒曾表示，公司需要的是高端晶片，而不是更多的資金。
The government is trying to close those gaps. It has poured more than $150 billion into a decadelong effort to develop domestic cutting-edge chips, though that has not yet yielded major successes. It is also working to train more scientific talent.
中國政府正努力彌補這些差距。政府已投入逾1500億美元，開展了一項為期十年的自主尖端晶片研發計劃，儘管目前尚未取得重大成功。政府也正努力培養更多科研人才。
Still, “those are long-term strategies, when the challenges right now are much more pressing,” Mr. McFaul said.
「這些終歸是長期戰略，而眼下的挑戰要緊迫得多，」麥克福爾說。
Officials have acknowledged the need to give the private sector more room. Private companies like DeepSeek and Alibaba, not state-owned giants, have driven China’s breakthroughs.
官員們已經意識到有必要給予私營部門更多空間。推動中國取得突破的是像DeepSeek和阿里巴巴這樣的民營企業，而非國有巨頭。
Instead of micromanaging research, officials have focused on stimulating demand for A.I. Local governments have rushed to embed A.I. in hospitals, schools and traffic systems. Last year, Zhejiang Province organized mass study sessions about A.I., with more than 220,000 party officials watching a live-streamed lecture by a Tsinghua University scholar.
官員們沒有對研發進行微觀干預，而是將重點放在刺激人工智慧的需求上。地方政府紛紛將人工智慧融入醫院、學校和交通系統中。去年，浙江省組織了關於人工智慧的群眾性學習活動，逾22萬名黨員幹部觀看了由一位清華大學學者主講的直播課程。
The authorities have even rolled back some of their most burdensome rules.
當局甚至撤銷了一些最為繁瑣的規定。
Soon after ChatGPT’s release, the party had proposed strict regulations on chatbots and other generative A.I., requiring platforms to guarantee that A.I.-generated output was “true and accurate.” After companies argued that would be impossible, that requirement disappeared from the final rules.
就在ChatGPT發布後不久，中共曾提出對聊天機器人和其他生成式人工智慧實施嚴格監管，要求平台保證人工智慧生成的內容「真實準確」。在企業提出這根本無法實現之後，最終出台的管理辦法中已經看不到該要求。
Li Zhiqi, a policy adviser to the Beijing city government, said he helped channel feedback from companies to officials, and had proposed suggestions such as faster deployment of self-driving cars. “The government isn’t just making rules behind closed doors,” he said.
北京市政府政策顧問李志起表示，他協助將企業的反饋意見傳達給官員，並提出了加快自動駕駛汽車部署等建議。「政府也不會是這種一味的閉門造車，」他說。
The Race to the Top
巔峰之戰
For now, the approach seems to be working. At the end of 2025, China had more than 6,000 A.I. companies, and its core A.I. industry was worth $171 billion, according to official statistics. Chinese models now consistently outnumber American ones on a list of the most popular A.I. systems worldwide, according to OpenRouter, a closely watched leaderboard of A.I. models. Even Western tech companies like Airbnb have embraced them.
就目前而言，這種方法似乎奏效了。據官方統計，截至2025年底，中國擁有超過6000家人工智慧企業，核心人工智慧產業規模達1710億美元。備受關注的人工智慧模型排行榜OpenRouter顯示，在全球最受歡迎的人工智慧系統榜單上，中國模型的數量目前始終力壓美國模型。甚至連Airbnb等西方科技公司也擁抱了這些中國模型。
Two Chinese companies recently introduced models that are nearly as powerful as the leading American model, Anthropic’s Claude Fable 5, and cost much less to use.
兩家中國企業近期發布了與領先的美國模型——Anthropic的Claude Fable 5性能幾乎相當、但使用成本要低得多的模型。
Robots — the sector Mr. Xi first championed in his 2014 speech — have been a particular success. China has more than 2 million robots in its factories, more than in the rest of the world combined. Some Chinese researchers have argued that robots may be more promising for achieving general-purpose intelligence than large language models — Silicon Valley’s chosen method — because they can learn from interactions with the physical world.
習近平在2014年講話中首先提倡的機器人領域取得了尤為突出的成功。中國工廠裡擁有超過200萬台機器人，數量比世界其他地區的總和還要多。一些中國研究人員已經提出，在實現通用人工智慧方面，機器人或許比大型語言模型（矽谷所選擇的路徑）更具潛力，因為它們能夠從與物理世界的交互中學習。
Those rapid gains have fueled anxiety in Washington, where fears of being overtaken by China have become the main argument against reining in A.I. A congressional advisory group warned that China’s quick adoption of A.I. in robots and other practical applications would generate huge amounts of new data that could then be used to further improve its models. In July, the U.S. government proposed restricting imports of humanoid robots, a move Beijing denounced as discriminatory.
這些飛速進步加劇了華盛頓的焦慮，在那裡，被中國超越的恐懼已成為反對約束人工智慧發展的主要論點。一家國會諮詢機構警告稱，中國在機器人和其他實際應用中迅速推廣人工智慧將產生海量新數據，這些數據隨後可用於進一步提升其模型性能。今年7月，美國政府提議限制人形機器人的進口，北京方面譴責此舉具有歧視性。
For people like Mr. Liang, the robotics company founder in Shenzhen, the signs of China’s progress are already all around.
對於像深圳機器人公司創始人梁哲這樣的人而言，中國取得進步的跡象已隨處可見。
He still admires American entrepreneurs, but he no longer looks only to them for inspiration. He wears smart glasses from Huawei. He has hired interns from American universities who are eager to learn from China’s robotics expertise.
他依然欽佩美國企業家，但他已不再只從他們身上獲得啟迪。他戴著華為智能眼鏡。他聘用了來自美國大學的實習生，這些年輕人渴望學習中國機器人技術領域的專業知識。
“Before, we all used American technology as the benchmark, and we’d joke about ‘C-to-C’: ‘copy to China,’” Mr. Liang said. “I think it’s the mission of this generation of Chinese entrepreneurs to let the world know that some Chinese companies are different.”
「以前我們開玩笑就是摸著美國的科技圈過河，你有什麼我有這個對標就完了。很多年前中國講的模式就是C to C(Copy to China)，」梁哲說。「我覺得這其實是這一代中國企業家一個使命，就是讓世界要知道中國其實有一些企業是不一樣的。」
