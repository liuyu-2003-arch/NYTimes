習近平抵美展開訪問

Trump Greets Xi for State Visit Amid Tensions

ZOLAN KANNO-YOUNGS • 2026-09-24
President Trump took the rare step of meeting President Xi Jinping of China at Joint Base Andrews to kick off a visit between two superpower rivals confronting mounting concerns over artificial intelligence and a shaky trade truce. Presidents typically meet foreign leaders at the White House, but Mr. Trump instead welcomed Mr. Xi with a lavish ceremony when his plane landed on Wednesday evening.
川普總統採取罕見的舉措，在安德魯斯聯合基地會見中國國家主席習近平，以此拉開此次訪問的序幕，目前這兩個超級大國對手正面臨著日益加劇的人工智慧擔憂，以及搖搖欲墜的貿易休戰。總統通常在白宮會見外國領導人，但川普卻在週三晚上習近平的專機降落時，以一場奢華的儀式歡迎他。
Hints of common ground soon emerged. Shortly after the two leaders shook hands on the tarmac, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said the countries had agreed to extend a one-year trade agreement that had been set to expire in November. The two-month extension, which continues a suspension of certain U.S. tariffs in return for a steady flow of rare earth minerals from China, will “give us more time to see what we can do on the economic front,” Mr. Bessent told Fox News.
雙方很快顯露出達成共識的跡象。在兩國領導人於停機坪握手後不久，財政部長貝森特表示，兩國已同意將原定於11月到期的為期一年的貿易協議延長。貝森特告訴福克斯新聞，這項為期兩個月的延期協議，將繼續暫停徵收美國的某些關稅，以換取中國穩定供應稀土礦產，這將「給我們更多時間來探討經濟領域能採取哪些措施」。
The elaborate welcome ceremony, which included a red carpet lined by U.S. service members and a military flyover, is the start of a nearly two-day visit of pomp and circumstance, including another welcome ceremony with an honor guard at the White House, a meeting between the two presidents, a state dinner and a tour of the National Archives.
這場精心準備的歡迎儀式包括由美軍官兵列隊護衛的紅毯，以及軍機編隊飛越，標誌著這場為期近兩天的隆重訪問拉開帷幕，訪問行程還包括白宮的另一場由儀仗隊護衛的歡迎儀式、兩國總統會晤、一場國宴以及參觀國家檔案館。
Mr. Xi’s visit comes at a tumultuous time for the two leaders, even if foreign policy analysts expect few major breakthroughs by the summit’s end. Mr. Xi is overseeing an economy in its worst shape in decades. And just months before midterm elections, Mr. Trump is confronting low approval ratings rooted in Americans’ frustration over high energy prices caused in part by the U.S.-Israeli war in Iran.
習近平此次訪問正值兩位領導人面臨動盪時期，儘管外交政策分析人士預計峰會結束時幾乎不會取得什麼重大突破。習近平正面臨著中國數十年以來最嚴峻的經濟形勢。而在期中選舉前僅數月之際，川普正面臨著低迷的支持率，源於美國人對高能源價格的沮喪，而高能源價格部分是由美以在伊朗的戰爭造成的。
廣告
It remains to be seen whether Mr. Trump will confront Mr. Xi on several points of tension. Just last month, Mr. Trump gave a prime-time address accusing China of interfering in the 2020 election. And China has taken an active role assisting Iran, according to multiple U.S. officials, including by providing Tehran with satellite imagery and access to a spy satellite that Iran’s military forces have used to mount attacks in the region.
川普先生是否會在若干爭議問題上與習近平針鋒相對還有待觀察。就在上個月，川普發表黃金時段講話，指責中國干預2020年大選。此外，據多位美國官員稱，中國在援助伊朗方面發揮了積極作用，包括向德黑蘭提供衛星圖像以及間諜衛星的訪問權限，伊朗軍隊曾利用該衛星在該地區發動攻擊。
But recently, Mr. Trump has appeared more focused on accommodating the leader that he has described as “a friend.”
但最近，川普似乎更專注於安撫這位被他稱為「朋友」的領導人。
