北約華裔實習生涉嫌從事間諜活動被捕

Canadian Intern at NATO Military HQ Is Arrested on Suspicion of Spying

FRANCESCA REGALADO • 2026-07-27
The authorities in Belgium said on Saturday that a Canadian intern at the North Atlantic Treaty Organization’s military headquarters had been arrested on suspicion of spying for another country.
比利時當局週六表示，一名在北大西洋公約組織軍事總部實習的加拿大籍實習生因涉嫌為他國從事間諜活動而被捕。
The Belgian Federal Prosecutor’s Office said the woman was “of Chinese origin” and was arrested on Friday. “She is suspected of spying on behalf of a third country and of being a member of a criminal organization,” it said in a statement, which did not identify the country or the criminal organization.
比利時聯邦檢察官辦公室表示，該名女子為「華裔」，於週五被捕。該辦公室在聲明中稱：「她涉嫌代表第三國從事間諜活動，並涉嫌參與犯罪組織。」聲明中未透露該國或該犯罪組織的具體名稱。
The intern was first flagged by the security services at the Supreme Headquarters Allied Powers Europe, or SHAPE, which is near Mons, Belgium. The case was then referred to Belgian intelligence and a police investigation was launched, the federal prosecutor’s office said.
這名實習生最初被位於比利時蒙斯附近的歐洲盟軍最高司令部(SHAPE)的安全部門發現可疑。聯邦檢察官辦公室表示，此案隨後被移交給比利時情報部門，警方隨即展開調查。
That investigation led to searches of the woman’s home and place of work on Thursday, and a judge decided to place her under arrest on Friday, the prosecutor’s office said, adding in its statement that it would not comment further on the case.
檢察官辦公室稱，調查過程中，警方於週四搜查了這名女子的住所和工作場所，法官決定於週五對其下達逮捕令，並在聲明中補充說，不會對此案作進一步評論。
廣告
Canada’s Foreign Ministry and the Canadian Embassy in Brussels did not immediately respond to requests for comment on Saturday.
加拿大外交部及加拿大駐布魯塞爾大使館在週六沒有立即回應置評請求。
SHAPE, which plans and executes NATO’s military operations, was cooperating with the Belgian authorities, said Col. Martin O’Donnell, a SHAPE spokesman.
SHAPE負責規劃和執行北約軍事行動，其發言人馬丁·奧唐奈上校表示，該機構正在與比利時當局合作。
“At this time, there is no indication that NATO or SHAPE operational readiness, command and control arrangements, or ongoing tasks have been adversely affected,” he said in a statement.
他在聲明中表示：「目前，沒有任何跡象表明北約或SHAPE的戰備狀態、指揮和控制安排，以及正在進行的任務受到了不利影響。」
