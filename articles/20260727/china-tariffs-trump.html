川普全球新關稅對中國影響有限

China Has Come Through Trump’s Trade War in a Good Position

艾莎 • 2026-07-27
China’s response to President Trump’s new round of tariffs was muted. Beijing issued a statement in protest, but unlike in past rounds of tit-for-tat, it did not lob a counter.
中國對川普總統新一輪關稅的反應較為低調。北京發表了一份抗議聲明，但與以往針鋒相對的回合不同，此次沒有採取反制措施。
China has cause to be low key. It has so far emerged from the trade war with the United States in a better position. One year ago President Trump threatened tariffs on Chinese goods as high as 145 percent. The levies that Mr. Trump imposed on Chinese goods on Friday were just 12.5 percent.
中國有理由保持低調。到目前為止，中國在與美國的貿易戰中處於更有利的地位。一年前，川普總統曾威脅要對中國商品徵收高達145%的關稅。而上週五川普對中國商品徵收的關稅僅為12.5%。
Factoring in earlier tariffs, including those from Mr. Trump’s first presidency, the overall average weighted tariff on Chinese goods is now 23.1 percent.
考慮到早先的關稅，包括川普首個任期內徵收的關稅，目前對中國商品的總體平均加權關稅為23.1%。
Chinese officials are also confident that tariffs will not rise much further, after securing an assurance of a truce from U.S. trade negotiators. That agreement, which trade experts have placed at about a 20 percent cap, was later sealed with a handshake between China’s top leader, Xi Jinping, and Mr. Trump last October.
在獲得了美國貿易談判代表關於休戰的保證後，中國官員也確信關稅不會再大幅上漲。後來在去年10月，中國最高領導人習近平與川普握手正式敲定了一份協議。據貿易專家估算，該協議的關稅上限約為20%。
廣告
The new tariffs will “have a limited impact on China at present,” according to analysts at Guojin Securities, a financial firm in Chengdu, China, who calculated the new overall tariff rate. The rate was previously 21.9 percent.
位於成都的金融機構國金證券的分析師計算了新的總體關稅稅率，他們表示，新關稅「目前對中國的影響有限」。此前的稅率為21.9%。
The tariffs imposed on China are part of Mr. Trump’s broader effort to transform global trade. On Friday, the administration issued tariffs on more than 80 trading partners over claims of failing to pass or enforce laws banning forced labor. The new tariffs, which were between 10 percent and 12.5 percent, replaced a global 10 percent tariff as it expired.
對中國徵收的關稅是川普重塑全球貿易這一更廣泛舉措的一部分。週五，美國政府聲稱超過80個貿易夥伴未能通過或執行禁止強迫勞動的法律，對這些夥伴徵收了關稅。這些新關稅介於10%到12.5%之間，取代了隨著到期而失效的10%的全球關稅。
The White House issued the duties under Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974, which allows the president to impose tariffs on foreign countries that engage in unreasonable or discriminatory trade practices. More tariffs are expected in the coming weeks, after an administration proposal to offset what the White House calls unfair practices in several countries’ manufacturing sectors — including China. This month, Mr. Trump also imposed a 25 percent tariff on certain products from Brazil that just took effect, and has threatened a 50 percent tariff on many Canadian goods starting in August. For both countries, the new tariffs could push their effective rates above China’s.
白宮根據《1974年貿易法》第301條款發布了這些關稅，該條款允許總統對從事不合理或歧視性貿易做法的外國徵收關稅。美國政府提出了一項提案，旨在抵消白宮所稱的包括中國在內的多個國家製造業領域的不公平做法，預計未來幾週還會出台更多關稅。本月，川普還對巴西的某些產品徵收了剛剛生效的25%關稅，並威脅從8月開始對許多加拿大商品徵收50%的關稅。對於這兩個國家來說，新關稅可能會使其有效稅率超過中國的稅率。
None of those tariffs are expected to top those announced by Mr. Trump under an international emergency law on April 2, 2025, which he termed “Liberation Day.” The Supreme Court struck down Mr. Trump’s use of the law to impose tariffs in February.
預計這些關稅都不會超過川普於2025年4月2日根據一項國際緊急狀態法宣布的關稅規模，他將那一天稱為「解放日」。 最高法院已於2月裁定川普援引該法徵收關稅的行為無效。
On Friday, Chinese state media called the Trump administration’s policy “America’s never-ending tariffs,” and added that Washington “has found a new pretext for abusing tariffs.”
上週五，中國官方媒體將川普政府的政策稱為「美國永無止境的關稅」，並補充說華盛頓「找到了濫用關稅的新藉口」。
The foreign ministry showed more restraint. At a regular news conference on Friday, a spokesman said China opposed “all forms of unilateral tariffs,” but he did not respond when asked whether the country would retaliate.
中國外交部則表現得更為克制。在週五的例行記者會上，一位發言人表示，中國反對「各種形式的單邊關稅措施」，但當被問及中國是否會進行報復時，他沒有做出回應。
廣告
China may take comfort in a perceived promise by Mr. Trump to cap tariffs, trade experts said.
貿易專家表示，中國認為川普給出了限制關稅的承諾，這讓其感到寬慰。
“My understanding is that there is a ‘wink, wink, nod, nod’ on 20 percent additional tariffs and through these 301 investigations, any findings on China would conveniently not exceed that,” said Wendy Cutler, a senior vice president at the Asia Society Policy Institute.
亞洲協會政策研究所高級副總裁溫迪·卡特勒說：「我的理解是，雙方對20%的額外關稅有一種『心照不宣』的默契，並且通過這些301調查，任何針對中國的裁定都會恰好不超過這個數字。」
Anything more than a 7.5 percent tariff on China in the next round would risk the fragile truce and invite retaliation, she added. Mr. Trump said Mr. Xi will visit Washington in September.
她還說，在下一輪關稅中對中國徵收任何超過7.5%的關稅都會使脆弱的貿易休戰面臨風險，並招致報復。川普表示，習近平將於9月訪問華盛頓。
Trade negotiators set that ceiling last year during U.S.-Chinese trade talks in Kuala Lumpur, the Malaysian capital. Mr. Trump and Mr. Xi shook on the terms of a trade deal in South Korea several days later, agreeing to a tariff truce for one year.
去年在馬來西亞首都吉隆坡舉行的美中貿易談判中，貿易談判代表設定了這一上限。幾天後，川普和習近平在韓國就貿易協議條款握手達成共識，同意實施為期一年的關稅休戰。
During meetings with Mr. Xi in Beijing in May, Mr. Trump said the two did not discuss tariffs.
在5月於北京與習近平會面期間，川普表示，兩人沒有討論關稅問題。
But China’s commerce ministry pushed back on his comments, saying in a statement that the United States and China had “engaged in in-depth discussions on tariffs.” The ministry added that it hoped Washington would “honor its commitments” and ensure that the U.S. tariff levels on Chinese goods would “not exceed the level stipulated” during talks in October.
但中國商務部反駁了他的說法，在聲明中表示中美已「圍繞關稅問題深入溝通」。商務部還稱，希望華盛頓「信守承諾」，確保美國對中國商品的關稅水平「不能超過」10月份會談期間「約定的水準」。
廣告
Trade experts said Beijing was drawing a line on tariff rates after a year of standing up to Washington, matching each of Mr. Trump’s ever-higher tariffs with its own and tightening control over rare-earths exports. China dominates the global supply chains for rare earths, materials that are critical to everything from fighter jets to motors in car seats.
貿易專家表示，在對抗華盛頓一年之後，北京正在對關稅稅率劃出底線。在過去的一年裡，北京對川普不斷提高的各項關稅採取了相應的同等反制措施，並加強了對稀土出口的控制。中國主導著全球稀土供應鏈，這種材料對於從戰鬥機到汽車座椅電機等一切產品都至關重要。
“My personal understanding is that during the remainder of Trump’s term, there probably won’t be sharp, comprehensive tariff increases again,” said Tu Xinquan, dean of the China WTO Institute at the University of International Business and Economics in Beijing.
北京的對外經濟貿易大學中國WTO研究院院長屠新泉表示：「我個人的理解是，在川普剩餘的任期內，可能不會再出現大幅、全面的關稅增長。」

Ruoxin Zhang自北京、Murphy Zhao自香港對本文有研究貢獻。

艾莎(Alexandra Stevenson)是《紐約時報》上海分社社長，報導中國經濟和社會新聞。
