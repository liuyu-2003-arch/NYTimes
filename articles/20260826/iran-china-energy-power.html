伊朗戰爭凸顯中國能源戰略奏效

The Iran War’s Surprise Winner Is China

MICHAL MEIDAN • 2026-08-26
When the Iran war began in late February, China looked vulnerable.
當伊朗戰爭於2月下旬爆發時，中國看上去處境不妙。
The world’s largest crude oil importer, China has in recent years depended on imports for more than 70 percent of its consumption, with around half coming from the Middle East. The throttling of the Strait of Hormuz, months ­­after the United States had disrupted global oil flows from Venezuela, seemed to present Chinese leaders with one of their greatest strategic fears: a crippling oil shock they could not control.
作為全球最大進口國，中國近年來超過70%的原油消耗依賴進口，其中約一半來自中東。在美國此前已擾亂來自委內瑞拉的全球石油流通之後，霍爾木茲海峽的阻塞似乎給中國領導人帶來了其最大的戰略恐懼之一：一場無法控制的嚴重石油休克。
Instead, President Trump’s war on Iran has been a welcome revelation for China, which absorbed one of the most severe oil-supply disruptions of the modern era, demonstrating that years of preparation had mitigated a huge vulnerability and potential source of U.S. leverage over Beijing in a geopolitical confrontation.
然而，川普總統對伊朗的戰爭對中國而言卻成了一次可喜的揭示。中國經受住了現代史上最嚴重的石油供應中斷之一，證明多年的準備已改善了一個重大薄弱環節——以及在地緣政治對抗中美國可能對北京施壓的潛在籌碼。
China was self-sufficient in oil until 1993, when the escalating needs of its booming economy turned it into a net importer. Oil imports soared in the years that followed, prompting President Hu Jintao to warn in 2003 that hostile powers could seek to control the Malacca Strait, the sea lane in Southeast Asia through which much of the country’s imported oil travels.
中國在1993年之前石油自給自足，此後蓬勃發展的經濟需求使其轉為凈進口國。隨後幾年石油進口飆升，促使胡錦濤主席在2003年警告敵對勢力可能尋求控制馬六甲海峽——這條東南亞海上通道是中國相當一部分進口石油的必經之地。

Beijing responded with a wide-ranging strategy to reduce that exposure. It diversified supply sources to limit the share of oil coming from the Middle East and began to build up what are now estimated to be the world’s largest crude oil stockpiles, roughly equal to those of the United States and Japan combined. China has pushed aggressively to shift passenger cars from fossil fuels to electric power, making it by far the world’s largest market for electric vehicles today, and it is extending that effort to trucks. Electricity generation relies almost entirely on domestic energy sources such as coal, nuclear, hydro and, increasingly, wind and solar.
北京以一項廣泛戰略應對，以降低該風險。對供應來源進行多元化，限制中東石油所佔份額，並開始建立目前估計為全球最大的原油儲備，大約相當於美國和日本儲備的總和。中國大力推動乘用車從化石燃料轉向電力，使其成為迄今為止全球最大的電動汽車市場，並將這一努力延伸至卡車。發電幾乎完全依賴國內能源，如煤炭、核能、水能，以及日益增長的風能和太陽能。
That long-term strategy has been put through a real-world stress test by the Iran war — and, so far, it has passed.
這項長期戰略接受了伊朗戰爭的實際壓力測試——且到目前為止是合格的。
The Hormuz disruptions drove oil prices up. But unlike during a 2021 Chinese coal shortage, when the government instructed importers to urgently secure energy supplies, it didn’t hit the panic button this time. Inflows of oil to China plunged as importers slashed purchases. The country fell back on its stockpiles while restricting exports of transport fuels to keep more at home. Higher fuel prices also appear to have pushed many Chinese drivers to use public transportation, taxis or ride-hailing services, much of which run on electricity.
霍爾木茲航運中斷推高了油價。但與2021年中國煤炭短缺時政府指示進口商緊急確保能源供應不同，這次中國並未按下恐慌按鈕。中國的石油進口量驟降，進口商大幅削減採購。中國動用儲備，同時限制運輸燃料出口以將更多能源留在國內。更高的燃料價格似乎也促使許多中國駕駛者轉向公共交通、計程車或網約車服務，其中很大部分使用電力。
As a result, while some governments in Asia were forced to take painful emergency measures such as rationing fuel, raising prices and reducing public services, China’s economy was not materially disrupted. Its purchasing restraint in global oil markets also eased pressure on prices, sparing the world an even bigger oil shock and, ironically, softening the domestic political fallout for Mr. Trump by limiting the pain at the U.S. pump.
結果，當亞洲一些政府被迫採取痛苦的緊急措施（如燃料配給、漲價和減少公共服務）時，中國經濟並未受到實質性干擾。其在全球石油市場的採購克制也緩解了價格壓力，使世界免於一場更嚴重的石油衝擊，且諷刺的是，通過減輕美國國內加油站的痛苦，也緩解了川普在國內政治上的負面後果。
China’s ability to weather the Iran war’s oil disruptions came as a surprise to many. It shouldn’t have — this is exactly what the government has spent years preparing for.
中國承受伊朗戰爭石油中斷的能力令許多人感到意外。但他們不應該這麼覺得——這正是中國政府多年來一直在準備的事情。
Chinese leaders see energy as a source of national and geopolitical power on par with industrial, technological and military strength. In 2021 they called for China to become an “energy powerhouse” — less reliant on fossil fuels yet still able to provide abundant and reliable power to the economy. Even before the war began in February, Communist Party-controlled media said achieving that goal was critical for China in “gaining the initiative in great-power competition” — an unmistakable reference to the contest with the United States in power-hungry industries like advanced manufacturing, data centers and artificial intelligence.
中國領導人將能源視為與工業、技術和軍事實力同等的國家和地緣政治力量來源。2021年，他們呼籲中國成為「能源強國」——在減少化石燃料依賴的同時，仍能為經濟提供充足可靠的電力。甚至在2月戰爭爆發之前，共產黨控制的媒體就表示，實現這一目標對於中國「在大國競爭中贏得主動」至關重要——這明確指向了與美國在先進制造、數據中心和人工智慧等高耗能產業中的競爭。

China’s latest energy plan, adopted this year, reflects that ambition. It calls for developing more renewable energy, as well as nuclear and hydropower, while retaining coal — long the backbone of China’s power system — as a critical backstop. The country is also investing heavily in new transmission lines to move power from resource-rich regions to the cities and factories that need it, in batteries and other storage capabilities, as well as in grid upgrades to keep electricity supplies steady.
中國今年通過的最新能源規劃反映了這一雄心。它要求發展更多可再生能源以及核能和水電，同時保留煤炭——長期作為中國電力系統的支柱——作為關鍵後備。中國還大力投資新的輸電線路，將電力從資源豐富地區輸送到需要的城市和工廠，投資電池和其他儲能設施，以及升級電網以保持供電穩定。
The United States has great energy advantages of its own. It remains the world’s unrivaled oil and gas power, with abundant domestic supplies, the capacity to process and export them at enormous scale and large strategic reserves. But its electricity system is struggling to keep pace with rapidly rising demand from a new wave of power-gobbling industries.
美國自身擁有巨大的能源優勢。它仍是全球無可匹敵的石油和天然氣大國，擁有豐富的國內供應、大規模加工和出口的能力以及大型戰略儲備。但其電力系統正艱難應對新一波高耗能行業帶來的迅速增長的需求。
For decades, oil leverage seemed to rest with countries that controlled supply — OPEC, Russia and other major exporters, as well as U.S. shale producers — and could move prices by tightening or loosening the taps. The Hormuz crisis has shown that China is becoming a new type of energy power: an oil importer so large that it is able to influence global oil prices and withstand external disruptions.
幾十年來，石油槓桿似乎掌握在控制供應的國家手中——歐佩克、俄羅斯和其他主要出口國，以及美國頁岩油生產商——它們可以通過收緊或放鬆供應來影響價格。霍爾木茲危機表明，中國正在成為一種新型能源大國：一個體量如此龐大的石油進口國，以至於能夠影響全球油價並抵禦外部衝擊。
China’s dependence on imported oil remains substantial, but the geopolitics of energy are shifting from control of oil flows to control of electrified industrial systems. China has a clear head start in that transition, one that strengthens its hand in the industrial and strategic competition with the United States.
中國對進口石油的依賴仍然很大，但能源地緣政治正從控制石油流通轉向控制電氣化工業體系。中國在這場轉型中擁有明顯的先發優勢，這增強了其在與美國的工業和戰略競爭中的地位。

Michal Meidan博士是牛津能源研究所中國能源研究負責人。
