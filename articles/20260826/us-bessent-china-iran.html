中國抨擊美國對伊朗制裁行動

China Pushes Back After Trump Tightens the Screws on Iran

艾莎, AARON KROLIK, KEITH BRADSHER • 2026-08-26
China lashed out at the Trump administration’s new sanctions campaign against Iran on Tuesday, warning that it would defend its interests and accusing the United States of disrupting the global financial order.
週二，中國對川普政府針對伊朗的新一輪制裁行動予以猛烈抨擊，警告稱將捍衛自身利益，並指責美國破壞了全球金融秩序。
The reaction set the stage for renewed friction between the world’s superpowers.
這一反應為這兩個世界超級大國之間重新爆發摩擦埋下了伏筆。
Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent on Monday outlined plans to “sever every economic lifeline” to Iran, targeting a constellation of players that continue to provide the country with a financial lifeline. But the administration appeared to tread carefully around China, by far the largest customer of Iran’s most valuable export: oil.
美國財政部長貝森特週一概述了「切斷伊朗所有經濟命脈」的計劃，將目標對準了繼續為伊朗提供金融生命線的各類實體。但美國政府在對待中國時似乎小心翼翼，中國是伊朗最有價值出口產品——石油——迄今為止最大的買家。
The U.S. list of more than 60 brokers, companies and ships subject to sanctions included more than a dozen small companies from Hong Kong and mainland China. Conspicuously absent were heavyweights in China’s financial system — a sign, some experts said, that Washington was reluctant to jeopardize a fragile truce with China.
在美國列出的60多個面臨制裁的經紀商、公司和船隻名單中，包含十幾家來自香港和中國大陸的小公司。中國金融系統中的重量級機構卻明顯缺席——一些專家表示，這表明華盛頓不願破壞與中國之間脆弱的休戰狀態。
廣告
Beijing nevertheless responded forcefully. China will “take all necessary measures to firmly safeguard its own rights and interests,” Lin Jian, a spokesman for the foreign ministry, said at a news briefing on Tuesday.
儘管如此，北京還是做出了強硬回應。中國外交部發言人林劍在週二的新聞發布會上表示，中國將「採取一切必要措施堅定維護自身權益」。
Chinese state media went further. An editorial cartoon in China News portrayed the Statue of Liberty as a drug user with needles protruding from one arm, describing Washington’s use of sanctions as “an addiction.”
中國官方媒體的言辭則更為激烈。中國新聞網的一幅社論漫畫將自由女神像描繪成一個吸毒者，手臂上插著針頭，將華盛頓動輒使用制裁描述為「一種癮」。
The confrontational reaction underscored a dilemma for the Trump administration. Beijing has long defied American sanctions and continued to buy Iranian oil. At the same time, China supplied Tehran with raw materials and technology that support its economy and military. Yet nearly six months into a war with Iran, the United States has shown little appetite for opening another economic front with an increasingly retaliatory China.
這種對抗性的反應凸顯了川普政府面臨的困境。長期以來，北京一直無視美國的制裁，繼續購買伊朗石油。與此同時，中國還向德黑蘭提供支持其經濟和軍事的原材料及技術。然而，在與伊朗開戰近六個月後，美國幾乎沒有表現出與報復力度日益增強的中國開闢另一條經濟戰線的意願。
The timing is particularly delicate. China’s top leader, Xi Jinping, is expected to travel to the United States next month to meet with President Trump to continue talks aimed at stabilizing the countries’ relationship.
當前的時機尤為微妙。預計中國最高領導人習近平將於下個月前往美國，與川普總統會面，繼續就穩定兩國關係進行磋商。
On Monday, when he was asked why the administration had not targeted Chinese banks, Mr. Bessent said only that “quiet diplomacy” was the best form of engagement. He did vow at one point to still penalize a large international financial institution, though he did not offer specifics. To some analysts, Mr. Bessent’s failure to call out China amounted to a tacit acknowledgment that preserving the truce with China may, for now, outweigh the benefits of taking more aggressive action against Iran.
週一，當被問及為何美國政府沒有把中國各銀行列為目標時，貝森特僅表示，「低調外交」是最好的接觸方式。他一度承諾，仍會懲罰一家大型國際金融機構，但並未提供具體細節。在一些分析人士看來，貝森特沒有公開點名中國相當於默認：目前維持與中國的「休戰」狀態可能比對伊朗採取更強硬行動帶來的好處更為重要。
Washington “has been extremely reluctant to go after China,” said Daniel Tannebaum, a Treasury Department official during the George W. Bush administration and now a partner at Oliver Wyman, a consulting firm. “I don’t think that changes today, especially at a time when China has more leverage than the United States does.”
「華盛頓極不願意針對中國，」小布希政府時期的財政部官員、現任奧緯諮詢合伙人丹尼爾·坦南鮑姆表示。「我認為這種情況至今未變，尤其是在中國比美國擁有更多籌碼的時期。」
廣告
For decades, the United States has exploited its central position in the global financial system to isolate adversaries like Iran and North Korea, threatening to exclude companies and banks from conducting transactions in dollars if they do business with sanctioned entities. Successive rounds of sanctions have largely left Iran cut off from the global financial system, but they have failed to incapacitate its economy.
幾十年來，美國一直利用其在全球金融體系中的核心地位來孤立伊朗和朝鮮等對手，威脅若企業和銀行與受制裁實體做生意，將被禁止使用美元進行交易。連續多輪的制裁在很大程度上切斷了伊朗與全球金融體系的聯繫，但未能使其經濟陷入癱瘓。
But more recently, China has shown it is willing to use its own economic choke points. During the bruising trade war of the past year, Beijing restricted access to critical minerals and used its control over supply chains against American companies as leverage.
但最近，中國已表明願意利用自身掌握的經濟命脈。在過去一年殘酷的貿易戰中，北京限制了關鍵礦產的獲取，並利用其對供應鏈的控制來對付美國企業，以此作為施壓籌碼。
China agreed last year to temporarily postpone controls on exports of rare-earth minerals as part of a thaw in relations with the United States. That one-year reprieve is set to lapse weeks after Mr. Xi and Mr. Trump are scheduled to meet.
去年，作為中美關係緩和的一部分，中國同意暫時推遲對稀土礦物出口的管制。這一為期一年的暫緩期將在習近平和川普計劃會面幾周後到期。
That leverage has raised the potential cost of confronting Beijing over Iran, even as China has become critical to Tehran’s ability to withstand American pressure.
這種籌碼提高了在伊朗問題上與北京對抗的潛在成本，儘管此時中國已成為德黑蘭抵禦美國壓力能力的關鍵。
Since the United States and Israel launched their attack on Iran on Feb. 28, Washington has imposed sanctions on more than 400 companies, people and ships tied to Iran from countries including Turkey, the United Arab Emirates and China.
自2月28日美國和以色列對伊朗發動襲擊以來，華盛頓已對來自土耳其、阿拉伯聯合酋長國和中國等國與伊朗有關聯的400多家公司、個人和船隻實施了制裁。
It has added more than 1,000 entities to its sanctions list since the start of Mr. Trump’s second term, effectively cutting them off from the dollar, according to an analysis by The New York Times. The targets have included Chinese shipping companies and oil refiners.
據《紐約時報》分析，自川普開啟第二任期以來，美國已將1000多個實體列入制裁名單，實質上切斷了它們與美元的聯繫。這些制裁對象包括了中國的航運公司和煉油廠。
廣告
Yet Iranian oil has continued to flow to China.
然而，伊朗石油仍在源源不斷地流向中國。
Large quantities are now sitting aboard ships and in storage tanks around the Malacca Strait and the South China Sea and near Chinese ports, according to Muyu Xu, a senior oil analyst at Kpler, an industry data provider.
據行業數據提供商克卜勒的高級石油分析師許木魚（音）稱，目前有大量石油儲存在馬六甲海峽、南海周邊以及中國港口附近的船隻和儲油罐中。
Data from Kpler shows that China has received around 1.2 million barrels of oil a day from Iran so far in 2026, only slightly less than the same period last year. Up-to-date volumes are difficult to measure because ships have become increasingly skilled at evading detection, Ms. Xu said.
克卜勒的數據顯示，2026年迄今為止，中國平均每天從伊朗接收約120萬桶石油，僅略低於去年同期。許木魚表示，由於船隻規避監測的能力日益增強，最新進口量難以準確統計。
China’s three national state-controlled oil giants have avoided buying Iranian crude because of their extensive ties to Western banks. But privately owned Chinese refiners known as “teapots” have bought Iranian oil at steep discounts and turned it into gasoline and diesel for the domestic market.
由於與西方銀行有著廣泛的聯繫，中國三大國有控股石油巨頭一直避免購買伊朗原油。但被稱為「茶壺」的中國民營地方煉油廠卻以極大的折扣購買伊朗石油，並將其加工成汽油和柴油供國內市場使用。
Those companies can be especially difficult for Washington to pressure. Many have few international transactions that the Treasury Department can target by restricting access to the dollar through global banks. Instead, they are believed to conduct much of their trade in Chinese currency or cryptocurrencies.
對於華盛頓而言，要對這些企業施加壓力尤為困難。其中許多企業幾乎沒有國際交易，這就使得美國財政部無法通過全球銀行限制其獲取美元來進行精準打擊。相反，外界認為它們的大部分貿易是用人民幣或加密貨幣進行的。
The trade has also developed elaborate methods for shielding ships and cargoes from Western sanctions. Iranian oil is frequently transported to waters off Malaysia, where cargoes are transferred between vessels before continuing to Chinese ports.
這種貿易還發展出了保護船隻和貨物免受西方制裁的複雜方法。伊朗石油經常被運往馬來西亞附近海域，在那裡，貨物於船隻之間進行轉運，然後再繼續運往中國港口。
廣告
In April, the United States targeted a handful of Chinese teapot refineries, including Hengli Petrochemical Refinery. One of Iran’s biggest customers, the refinery was found to have purchased billions of dollars of Iranian crude from the Islamic Revolutionary Guards Corps, which wields considerable military, political and economic clout in Iran.
今年4月，美國將目標對準了包括恆力石化在內的幾家中國茶壺煉油廠。作為伊朗最大的客戶之一，該煉油廠被發現從伊斯蘭革命衛隊購買了價值數十億美元的伊朗原油，伊斯蘭革命衛隊在伊朗擁有巨大的軍事、政治和經濟影響力。
Still, even if the U.S. Treasury Department does not take overt measures against big Chinese banks, international banks might start curtailing business with them, said Han Lin, the China country director of the Asia Group, an American consulting firm. Big banks are wary about incurring the wrath of American regulators.
儘管如此，美國諮詢公司亞洲集團中國區總監林涵（音）表示，即使美國財政部不對中國的大型銀行採取公開措施，國際銀行也可能會開始減少與它們的業務往來。大型銀行擔心招來美國監管機構的怒火。
The big international banks “will become much more conservative about engaging in any transactions” with any Chinese financial institutions, he said.
他表示，大型國際銀行在與任何中國金融機構「進行任何交易時，都將變得更加保守」。
Washington has confronted China over Iranian oil before. Around 2012, during the administration of President Barack Obama, American diplomats pressed China and other countries to wind down purchases of Iranian crude, which were then conducted more openly.
華盛頓以前也曾因伊朗石油問題與中國發生過對抗。2012年前後，歐巴馬總統執政期間，美國外交官曾敦促中國和其他國家減少對伊朗原油的購買，當時的購買行為要公開得多。
Around that time, the United States also put sanctions on Bank of Kunlun, a Chinese financial institution owned by China National Petroleum Corporation, over its ties to Iran and for doing business with Iranian banks already under sanctions.
大約在同一時間，美國還對崑崙銀行實施了制裁，理由是這家由中國石油天然氣集團公司控股的中國金融機構與伊朗存在聯繫，且與已受制裁的伊朗銀行開展業務。
But the Trump administration has stopped short of imposing the kind of sanctions on major financial institutions that could fundamentally disrupt Beijing’s economic relationship with Tehran.
但川普政府並沒有對大型金融機構實施那種可能從根本上破壞北京與德黑蘭經濟聯繫的嚴厲制裁。
廣告
“We have not in recent years demonstrated a willingness to run the risk of undermining our economic standing” in exchange for pushing Chinese banks away from Iran, said Richard Nephew, an official in the Obama administration who helped negotiate the Iran nuclear agreement.
曾參與談判伊朗核協議的歐巴馬政府官員理查德·尼菲說：「近年來，我們並未表現出願意冒著削弱自身經濟地位的風險」來迫使中國銀行遠離伊朗。
There are powerful reasons for that caution. Mr. Nephew pointed to Washington’s 2018 sanctions against Oleg Deripaska and Rusal, the Russian giant that was then the world’s second-largest aluminum producer. Aluminum markets went haywire at the prospect that a major source of global supply could be cut off.
這種謹慎是有充分理由的。尼菲指出，華盛頓曾在2018年對奧列格·德里帕斯卡及俄羅斯鋁業聯合公司實施制裁，後者當時是全球第二大鋁生產商。在全球主要供應源可能被切斷的預期下，鋁市場一度陷入混亂。
Targeting systemically important Chinese banks or companies could produce a shock on an entirely different scale.
將具有系統重要性的中國銀行或公司作為打擊目標可能會產生規模截然不同的驚人衝擊。
“You could find markets going nuts,” Mr. Nephew said. “You could find companies going nuts. You can find insurance companies going nuts, banks going nuts.”
「你會看到市場陷入瘋狂，」尼菲說。「你會看到公司陷入瘋狂。你會看到保險公司陷入瘋狂，銀行也會陷入瘋狂。」
