圖集：歐洲遭遇極端高溫，酷熱顛覆日常生活

Europe Is Sweltering. Here’s What That Looks Like

MEGAN SPECIA • 2026-06-25
The commuters who clutched hand-held fans as they trudged through stiflingly hot London underground stations found little relief on Wednesday. Parisians dangled their legs in public fountains, desperate for a way to cool down. In Vatican City, sweat beaded on the foreheads of people gathered for a papal audience in St. Peter’s Square.
週三，在悶熱不堪的倫敦地鐵站裡，手裡抓著便攜風扇艱難前行的通勤者們幾乎沒有感到任何涼意。巴黎人把雙腿泡在公共噴泉裡，不顧一切地尋找降溫的方法。在梵蒂岡城，聚集在聖伯多祿廣場參加教宗接見活動的人們額頭上滲出了密集的汗珠。
Extreme heat is becoming Europe’s new summer normal, even in parts of the region, like Britain, where it was once a rarity. Countries ill-equipped to deal with the soaring temperatures are struggling to cope, with aging infrastructure and a scarcity of air-conditioning. Across the continent, the heat is upending daily life.
極端高溫正成為歐洲夏季的新常態，甚至在英國等曾經罕見高溫的地區也是如此。由於基礎設施老化和空調匱乏，那些對氣溫飆升準備不足的國家難以應對。在整個歐洲大陸，高溫正在徹底顛覆人們的日常生活。
Record temperatures were documented in a number of places this week. As the mercury climbed steadily higher on Wednesday, train cancellations caused travel chaos for some, shuttered schools early and caused some hospitals in England to cancel elective procedures.
本週多地氣溫打破紀錄。隨著週三水銀柱持續攀升，列車取消給一些人的出行帶來了混亂，學校提前放學，英格蘭的一些醫院也取消了非緊急手術。
Paris, 40° C or 104° F forecast high
巴黎，預報最高氣溫40攝氏度（104華氏度）
Construction workers in Paris braved the heat as temperatures were expected to surpass 100 degrees Fahrenheit in the city on Wednesday.
週三，巴黎的建築工人冒著酷暑工作，預計該市的氣溫將超過37攝氏度。
Dogs in Paris tried to cool off in a mist fountain.
巴黎的狗試圖在噴霧噴泉中降溫。
For people with no homes, the extreme heat can prove particularly dangerous without anywhere to seek shelter from the sun.
對於無家可歸的人來說，在沒有地方躲避太陽的情況下，極端高溫可能會顯得尤為危險。
Fans were in high demand in European capitals as residents scrambled to find ways to cool down.
隨著居民紛紛尋找降溫方法，歐洲各首都的風扇需求量極大。
London, 36° C or 96° F forecast high
倫敦，預報最高氣溫36攝氏度（96華氏度）
Children at a school in London being cooled with a hose. A number of schools closed or curtailed their hours this week. On Wednesday, Britain’s weather service said in a provisional assessment that temperatures in a county outside London reached 35.7 degrees Celsius, or 96.3 degrees Fahrenheit, the highest temperature ever recorded in the country in June.
倫敦一所學校的孩子們在淋水降溫。本週有許多學校停課或縮短了上課時間。週三，英國氣象局在一份臨時評估中表示，倫敦外圍某郡的氣溫達到了35.7攝氏度，這是該國有記錄以來的6月最高氣溫。
Some in central London sought shelter under umbrellas. A meeting planned to discuss adaptations to extreme heat, as part of London Climate Action Week, was canceled on Wednesday.
倫敦市中心的一些人打傘遮陽。作為倫敦氣候行動周的一部分，原定於週三舉行的一場討論如何適應極端高溫的會議被取消。
For commuters on London’s underground, most of which has no air-conditioning, hand-held fans offered little relief.
對於倫敦地鐵的通勤者來說，由於大部分地鐵沒有空調，手持風扇幾乎無法緩解炎熱。
Garbage collectors continued to work on Wednesday despite the extreme heat.
垃圾清運工人在週三冒著酷暑繼續工作。
Shade was at a premium at Finsbury Circus Gardens in London on Tuesday.
週二，在倫敦的芬斯伯裡馬戲團花園，遮陰處非常珍貴。
On Oxford Street, shoppers took shelter from the blazing midday sun, with some repurposing the umbrellas more commonly used to keep London’s rain at bay.
在牛津街，購物者躲避著正午烈日的暴晒，一些人重新利用起了那些在倫敦更常用來遮雨的雨傘。
Vatican City, 33° C or 91.4° F forecast high
梵蒂岡城，預報最高氣溫33攝氏度（91.4華氏度）
Newlyweds sweltered as they gathered during the weekly general audience of Pope Leo XIV in St. Peter’s Square.
在聖伯多祿廣場聚集參加教宗良十四世每週公開接見活動的新婚夫婦熱得大汗淋漓。
A priest in St. Peter’s Square sought shelter from the sun under a scarf on Wednesday.
週三，聖伯多祿廣場上的一位神父用圍巾遮陽。
Madrid, 37° C or 98° F forecast high
馬德里，預報最高氣溫37攝氏度（98華氏度）
Even in countries like Spain, which are more accustomed to the heat, such high temperatures so early in the summer could be a shock. Children in Madrid played football as they cooled off in a water fountain.
即使在西班牙這樣相對習慣炎熱的國家，在夏天這麼早出現這樣高的氣溫也讓人感到震驚。馬德里的孩子們在噴泉裡降溫的同時踢著足球。
Bordeaux, 43° C or 109° F forecast high
波爾多，預報最高氣溫43攝氏度（109華氏度）
In many cities, cooling centers were set up to accommodate people who needed somewhere to go. An emergency heat wave relief center was opened in Bordeaux, France.
許多城市設立了降溫中心，以容納那些需要尋找避暑場所的人。法國波爾多開設了一家高溫應急救濟中心。
Cooling off in the basin of the Girondins Fountain at Place des Quinconces, Bordeaux.
在波爾多坎孔斯廣場的吉倫特派紀念碑噴泉池中降溫。
Bathers flocked to lakes in Bordeaux. At least 40 people have drowned in France in the last five days as people tried to get cool in open water.
游泳者湧向波爾多的湖泊。在過去五天裡，法國至少有40人在試圖進入公開水域降溫時溺水身亡。
Some apartment dwellers got creative, using garden umbrellas for some improvised shade.
一些公寓居民發揮了創意，把花園遮陽傘暫作遮陽棚用。
Toulouse, 40° C or 104° F forecast high
圖盧茲，預報最高氣溫40攝氏度（104華氏度）
The extreme heat can be especially dangerous for older people and the very young. A patient suffering from heat exposure was treated at Purpan Hospital in Toulouse.
極端高溫對老年人和幼兒可能特別危險。一名因高溫引發不適的患者在圖盧茲的皮爾龐醫院接受了治療。
Nantes, 42° C or 107.6° F forecast high
南特，預報最高氣溫42攝氏度（107.6華氏度）
In Nantes, France, some residents used sheets to cover their windows in an attempt to protect their homes from the heat.
在法國南特，一些居民用床單遮擋窗戶，試圖以此抵禦熱浪侵襲。
La Rippe, Switzerland, 27°C or 80.6 forecast high
瑞士拉里普，預報最高氣溫27攝氏度（80.6華氏度）
Misting systems provided some relief for cows in a barn in Switzerland.
噴霧系統為瑞士一座牛棚裡的牛提供了一些涼意。

Megan Specia常駐倫敦，為時報報導英國、愛爾蘭和烏克蘭戰爭新聞。
