期中選舉兩週前川普支持率跌至新低

Trump Approval Hits New Low Two Weeks Before Midterms, Poll Finds

LISA LERER, RUTH LGIELNIK • 2026-10-22
President Trump’s approval rating hit a new low just a few weeks before the midterm elections, driven down by broad disapproval of his handling of the economy, the war in Iran and high gas prices, according to a new national poll from The New York Times and Siena University.
據《紐約時報》與錫耶納學院聯合發布的最新全國民調，川普總統的施政滿意度在距離期中選舉僅數週之際跌至新低，主要拖累因素為選民普遍不認可其對經濟、伊朗戰爭和高油價問題的處理。
Just 36 percent of likely voters said that they approved of Mr. Trump’s performance, while 59 percent said that they disapproved. That is his lowest approval rating recorded in any Times/Siena poll. More than two-thirds of independent voters said that they disapproved of the way he has handled his job as president.
僅36%的潛在選民認可川普的執政表現，59%表示不認可。這是他在《紐約時報》/錫耶納民調中的最低紀錄。超過三分之二的無黨派選民不認可他的履職表現。
Likely voters said that they preferred Democratic candidates over Republicans by 11 percentage points in their congressional districts in the final Times/Siena national poll before the midterm elections, a slight improvement for Democrats since last month. The metric is widely seen as a strong indicator of political momentum.
在期中選舉前《紐約時報》/錫耶納的最後一項全國民調中，可能投票的選民表示在其國會選區更支持民主黨候選人，領先共和黨11個百分點，這一差距比上個月略有擴大。該指標被廣泛視為衡量政治勢頭的重要風向標。
The national political environment is bleak for Republicans. Americans broadly disapprove of economic conditions under the Trump administration, with three-quarters rating the economy as fair or poor. A majority of voters blame Mr. Trump for the decline: 69 percent disapprove of his handling of the cost of living, 65 percent disapprove of his handling of the war in Iran and nearly three-quarters disapprove of his handling of gas prices, which are above $4 a gallon.
全國政治環境對共和黨而言頗為黯淡。美國民眾普遍不認可川普政府治下的經濟狀況，四分之三的人將經濟評為「一般」或「差」。多數選民將經濟下滑歸咎於川普：69%不認可其對生活成本問題的處理，65%不認可其伊朗戰爭應對，近四分之三不認可其油價應對——目前油價已超過每加侖4美元。
廣告
But Democrats still face serious challenges, particularly as they seek to win control of the Senate. To do so they are trying to flip seats in Republican-leaning states including Texas, Iowa, Alaska and Ohio, which Mr. Trump won by double digits in 2024. Other polls show that Democrats and Republicans are locked in tight races in several key Senate contests.
但民主黨仍面臨嚴峻挑戰，尤其是在爭取參議院控制權方面。為此，民主黨正試圖在傾向共和黨的州翻轉席位，包括得克薩斯、愛荷華、阿拉斯加和俄亥俄——川普2024年在這些州均以兩位數優勢獲勝。其他民調顯示，兩黨在幾場關鍵參議院選戰中勢均力敵。

Still, the deep economic frustration has even broken into the coalition that elected Mr. Trump to his second term. Over the past year, Democrats have improved their standing among men, young voters and Hispanic voters — winning back some voters in those groups who helped propel Mr. Trump to victory two years ago.
然而，深重的經濟挫敗感甚至已波及將川普送入第二任期的選民聯盟。過去一年，民主黨在男性、年輕選民和西班牙裔選民中的支持率有所回升——贏回了部分兩年前助推川普獲勝的選民。
Paul Gettings, a Republican who lost his job running a logistics company in June, said that he supported Mr. Trump in 2024 but plans to back Democrats this year. He cited frustration with high gas prices, the war in Iran and what he sees as harsh immigration enforcement in Simi Valley, Calif., where he lives.
共和黨人保羅·蓋廷斯6月因所在物流公司裁員而失業。他表示自己在2024年支持了川普，但今年計劃轉投民主黨。他列舉的原因包括對高油價、伊朗戰爭以及加州西米谷嚴厲移民執法的不滿。
“I’m very disillusioned with the Republican Party and how they can continue to support Trump,” said Mr. Gettings, 62, who is unemployed. “He’s turned into a politician just like the rest of them and it’s a lot of empty promises.”
「我對共和黨很幻滅，他們怎麼能繼續支持川普，」62歲、目前失業的蓋廷斯說。「他已經變成了一個和其他政客沒有兩樣的人，儘是空頭承諾。」
Among all registered voters, including those who are less likely to vote, Mr. Trump’s approval rating was even worse, with 34 percent approving of his job performance. Mr. Trump built his 2016 and 2024 wins partly on the strength of his support among white voters without a college degree, but the poll found that group is about evenly split in its approval of his job, with 48 percent approving and 46 percent disapproving.
在包括投票意願較低的選民在內的所有登記選民中，川普的滿意度更低，僅34%認可其執政表現。川普2016年和2024年的勝利部分得益於沒有大學學歷的白人選民的支持，但本次民調發現，該群體對其執政表現的態度大致持平：48%認可，46%不認可。

As views of Republicans have declined, public perceptions of Democrats have grown increasingly positive over the last two years on an array of measures.
隨著選民對共和黨的看法轉差，過去兩年公眾對民主黨的評價在一系列指標上持續改善。
A majority of likely voters now see the Democratic Party as the party of the working class — a shift from 2022, when voters gave the Republican Party the edge. Voters also see Democrats as more likely to keep their promises than Republicans, a striking reversal from two years ago, when Republicans held an advantage on that question.
多數可能投票的選民如今將民主黨視為工薪階層的政黨——這與2022年選民偏向共和黨的情況形成反轉。選民還認為民主黨比共和黨更可能信守承諾，這與兩年前共和黨在該問題上佔優的局面相比是一次顯著逆轉。
And majorities across nearly every demographic group now view Democrats as more understanding and able to fix the problems of average Americans.
幾乎所有人口群體中的多數人現在都認為，民主黨更理解普通美國人，並能解決他們的問題。
“The Republican Party is, if anything, causing more problems for people like me: being Latino, feeling the weight of that kind of prejudice that I think the Republican Party tends to kind of portray," said Vince Cordero, 25, an independent in Oak Lawn, Ill., who said he plans on voting for Democrats this year. “I also think the Democratic Party deserves an opportunity to see if they’re really up to snuff.”
「要說的話，共和黨反而在給像我這樣的人製造更多問題：作為拉丁裔，感受到那種偏見的壓力，我覺得共和黨往往在助長這種偏見，」25歲的文斯·科爾德羅說，他是伊利諾州奧克朗的無黨派人士，表示今年計劃投票給民主黨。「我也認為應該給民主黨一個機會，看看他們是否真的夠格。」
By a 15-point margin, more voters trust the Democratic Party over the Republican Party to tackle the cost of living, the single most important issue to voters. The party holds an 8-point advantage on the economy, an area that has historically been a strength for Mr. Trump. Democrats have also grown their lead since September on who voters trust to handle foreign wars and artificial intelligence.
在生活成本這一選民最關心的議題上，更多選民信任民主黨而非共和黨，前者領先15個百分點。在經濟議題上民主黨也領先八個百分點，而這一領域歷來是川普的強項。自9月以來，民主黨在選民信任誰處理對外戰爭和人工智慧方面的優勢也有所擴大。
But even amid his declining numbers, Mr. Trump has held on to strong support from his base. The vast majority of Republicans, 82 percent, still approve of his job performance and his signature policies, including the Iran war and tariffs.
但即便民調數字下滑，川普仍牢牢掌握基本盤的支持。高達82%的絕大多數共和黨人仍然認可他的執政表現及其標誌性政策，包括伊朗戰爭和關稅。
“People are upset, ‘Oh, Trump put these tariffs on people and now our food costs go up,’” said Sheila Murdock, a retired nurse from Shelley, Idaho, who has already voted absentee for Republicans this year. “Well, yes, but if you look at the bigger picture and what it eventually does, it makes things better.”
「人們很不滿，『哦，川普加徵了這些關稅，現在食品價格漲了，』」愛達荷州謝利的退休護士希拉·梅鐸說，她今年已經為共和黨人投了缺席票。「是的，但如果你看大局以及最終的效果，它會讓事情變得更好。」
廣告
The poll also found immigration re-emerging as a defining concern for voters. Ten percent of voters now say it is their top issue this fall, up from 6 percent in September. Among Republicans, almost 20 percent now say it is their top priority — nearly double the percentage who said the same last month.
民調還發現，移民問題重新成為選民的關鍵關切。10%的選民現在將其列為今年秋天的首要問題，高於9月的6%。共和黨人中近20%持此看法——幾乎是上個月的兩倍。
While Democrats still hold an edge over Republicans in who voters trust to handle immigration — which had long been a Republican strength — that advantage has shrunk since September. Now, 46 percent of voters say they trust Republicans on immigration, 50 percent say the same of Democrats.
儘管民主黨在移民問題上仍比共和黨更受選民信任——這長期以來是共和黨的強項——但這一優勢自9月以來已經收窄。目前46%的選民在移民問題上信任共和黨，50%信任民主黨。
Still, the survey indicates that even as Democratic candidates gain support, there is lingering discontent with their party. Just 28 percent of voters said they are satisfied with the Democratic Party, eight points lower than the share who express satisfaction with the Republican Party.
然而，調查表明，即便民主黨候選人獲得更多支持，選民對該黨仍存在不滿。僅28%的選民對民主黨表示滿意，比對共和黨的滿意度低八個百分點。
Much of the unhappiness stems from Democratic voters themselves. Forty-three percent of Democrats said they are unsatisfied with their party, compared to just 16 percent of Republicans who said the same about the G.O.P.
不滿主要來自民主黨選民自身。43%的民主黨人對本黨不滿意，而共和黨人中僅16%對本黨不滿。

Lisa Lerer是時報美國政治記者，常駐紐約。她報導美國政治已有20多年。

Ruth Igielnik是時報投票新聞編輯，對民意調查進行報導和分析。
