民主倒退、中國崛起，人類命運將走向何方

The Old World Order Is Over. This Is What Could Come Next.

SERGE SCHMEMANN • 2026-10-22
This essay concludes The New World Disorder, a series exploring the disintegration of the postwar international system and how countries are adjusting as America steps aside. Read more in the series below.
本文是《新世界失序》系列的收尾篇。該系列探討戰後國際體系如何瓦解，以及在美國抽身而退之際各國如何調整。
By now it is clear to most of America and much of the world that America and what it stands for have changed. The dominant face of that change is President Trump and his determined and deliberate efforts to accumulate greater powers than the Constitution apportioned to the president, to create a cult of personality based on grievance and vengeance and to challenge every aspect of the postwar global order, demeaning allies and courting autocrats.
如今，美國乃至世界的大部分人都已清楚：美國及其所代表的一切已經改變。這一變化最突出的面孔是川普總統。他堅決而精心地擴充權力，使之超出憲法賦予總統的範圍；他營造了基於怨恨與復仇的個人崇拜；他挑戰戰後全球秩序的每一個方面，貶低盟友、討好威權者。
Is that the future of America in the world? An increasingly autocratic, rude and unpredictable superpower guided by populism, rancor and whim? Or will Americans, once Mr. Trump is gone, try to restore the United States as the world’s repository of democratic values and human rights?
這就是美國在世界的未來嗎？一個由民粹主義、怨恨和任性驅動的日益威權、粗魯、不可預測的超級大國？還是說，川普離去之後，美國人會努力把美國重新變成為世界維護民主價值觀與人權的地方？
It is hard to imagine that even a MAGA-minded successor to Mr. Trump (say, Marco Rubio or JD Vance) would be as petulant, inconsistent and vindictive as this president has been. Yet too much has already shifted in the world — beyond anything Mr. Trump has done — to believe that the liberal, rules-based and values-driven order shaped by America and its allies after World War II can ever fully return to what it was.
很難想像，即便是川普的MAGA繼任者（比如馬爾科·魯比奧或JD·萬斯），會像這位總統一樣任性、反覆無常和睚眥必報。然而，世界已經發生太多變化——超出了川普所做的一切——使人無法相信由美國及其盟友在二戰後塑造的自由主義、基於規則、以價值觀驅動的秩序能夠完全恢復原狀。
廣告
New realities have come to the fore since the Berlin Wall came down almost four decades ago: the emergence of China as a greater economic and military rival to the United States than the Soviet Union ever was; the re-emergence of Russia as a menacing police state and its starting of a European war; the global retreat of liberal democracy, led by the premier democracy, the United States; fast-evolving technology undertaking tasks once reserved for the human brain. Globalization, once promising a borderless world and worldwide prosperity, has revealed its dark side: mass migration, inequality, cybercrime, polarization.
柏林牆倒塌近四十年來，新的現實已經浮現：中國崛起為美國的經濟和軍事對手，其程度超過當年的蘇聯；俄羅斯重新成為一個具有威脅性的警察國家，並發動了一場歐洲戰爭；自由民主在全球退卻，而帶頭退卻的正是頭號民主國家美國；快速演進的技術開始承擔曾經專屬於人類大腦的任務。全球化曾許諾一個無邊界的世界和全球繁榮，如今卻暴露出陰暗面：大規模移民、不平等、網路犯罪、兩極分化。
There is no reboot mechanism in history; there is no way back to the conveniently binary world of the last century any more than Mr. Trump can realize the illusions of Make America Great Again. The world is moving on.
歷史沒有重啟機制；無法回到上個世紀那種省心的二元世界，正如川普無法實現「讓美國再次偉大」的種種幻想。世界在繼續前行。
What lies ahead? There is no way to know. But we must consider possible scenarios, if only to prepare as best we can. The exercise is especially critical for Americans, because any coherent action against global disarray will have to begin with the return of American democracy, as Charles A. Kupchan argues in his book “Bringing Order to Anarchy: Governing the World to Come.” Though Mark Carney, Canada’s prime minister, might have articulated a widespread longing at Davos in January when he urged middle powers to join forces and “create a third path,” the power, wealth and traditions of a renewed United States would be critical for any meaningful reversal of illiberal and autocratic trends.
前方是什麼？無人知曉。但我們必須考慮可能的情景，至少是為了盡可能做好準備。這一推演對美國人尤其關鍵，因為正如查爾斯·A·庫普錢在《在無政府狀態中建立秩序：治理未來世界》(Bringing Order to Anarchy: Governing the World to Come)一書中所主張的那樣，任何應對全球失序的一致行動都必須從美國民主的回歸開始。加拿大總理馬克·卡尼今年1月在達佛斯或許已經表達了一種廣泛的渴望：他敦促中等強國聯合起來，「開闢第三條道路。」但一個復興的美國的實力、財富與傳統對於任何有意義地扭轉反自由和威權趨勢的努力來說，仍然至關重要。
The scenarios below, looking a dozen years or so into the future, are drawn from conversations with and the writings of people who professionally gaze into the crystal balls of politics and world affairs, as well as with people who are concerned with what we leave behind. They are meant not to be scientific or comprehensive but to reflect on this critical moment, because, as the great American sage Yogi Berra once said, “If you don’t know where you are going, you might wind up someplace else.”
以下是展望未來十幾年的可能情形，依據的是與那些以研判政治和世界事務為職業的人的對話和他們的著述，以及與那些關心我們留下什麼遺產的人的交流。這樣的展望無意保持科學或全面，而是意在反思這一關鍵時刻，因為正如美國的偉大智者尤吉·貝拉曾說過的：「如果你不知道要去哪裡，你可能會最終到別的地方。」
America Makes a Comeback
美國回歸
This is our most hopeful scenario. A leader committed to the primacy of law, honest governance and liberal democracy across the globe is back in the White House, and the United States has begun to clamber back to its place as the “shining city on a hill.”
這是我們最有希望的情景。一位致力於法律至上、誠實治理和全球自由民主的領導人回到白宮，美國開始努力恢復「光輝的山巔之城」的地位。
廣告
Europeans have taken on greater responsibility for their defense and are warily coming to accept the United States as a friend and ally again. Vladimir Putin has been ousted and the conflict with Ukraine frozen, allowing Ukraine to make a rapid economic recovery and eventually have its territory returned. China has surpassed the United States economically but not militarily, and the two superpowers work to avoid conflict and find a modus vivendi.
歐洲人對自己的防務承擔了更大責任，並正警惕地逐漸重新接受美國作為朋友和盟友。弗拉基米爾·普丁已被趕下台，與烏克蘭的衝突被凍結，使烏克蘭得以迅速實現經濟復甦並最終收回領土。中國在經濟上超越美國，但在軍事上沒有，兩個超級大國努力避免衝突並找到一種共處方式。
A reinvigorated and re-empowered United Nations, no longer undermined by Washington, is mobilizing once again to combat climate change and public health challenges and find ways to regulate artificial intelligence. The consensus formed among rich nations after World War II — that aid to poor countries builds stability, fosters growth and reduces suffering — is restored as America retreats from Mr. Trump’s “America first” use of foreign aid purely as a transactional lever.
一個重新煥發活力、重新獲得授權的聯合國不再被華盛頓削弱，再次動員起來應對氣候變化和公共衛生挑戰，並設法監管人工智慧。二戰後富裕國家之間形成的共識——援助窮國能夠建設穩定、促進增長和減少苦難——得以恢復，而美國則摒棄了川普將對外援助純粹用作交易槓桿的「美國優先」做法。
Before exiting, Mr. Trump called off the war in Iran, declaring victory even though Tehran retains its mullahs and its nuclear fuel. But at least the Middle East is calm, and with Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu of Israel voted out of power, the new administration in Washington gets to work on beginning another peace process between Israelis and Palestinians.
在離任前，川普叫停了伊朗戰爭，宣布勝利，儘管德黑蘭的毛拉們和核燃料安然無恙。但至少中東平靜了，隨著以色列總理本雅明·納坦尼雅胡被選下台，華盛頓的新政府開始著手啟動以色列人與巴勒斯坦人之間的又一輪和平進程。
Its own house in order and its democratic allies behind it, the United States is able to begin guiding the world to a new order. The verities long taken for granted about the benefits of globalization, free trade, automation, digital technology and liberal democracy have given way to a far more fragmented world, with multiple centers of power competing over influence and status, but at the same time, as Mr. Kupchan writes, “more tethered together and interdependent than it was during the 20th century.” A new system of global governance begins to take shape, perhaps along the lines of what Mr. Kupchan envisions: a global concert of major nations to preserve peace and cooperation while acknowledging the ideological and cultural diversity of a complex world.
自己的家事理順了，民主盟友在身後，美國得以開始引導世界走向一個新秩序。長期以來被視為理所當然的關於全球化、自由貿易、自動化、數位技術和自由民主之益處的真理已經讓位於一個遠為碎片化的世界，多個權力中心爭奪影響力和地位，但同時又——如庫普錢所寫——「比20世紀時更加相互拴系和相互依存。」一個新的全球治理體系開始成形，或許沿著庫普錢設想的路線：一個由主要國家組成的全球協調機制，以維護和平與合作，同時承認一個複雜世界的意識型態和文化多樣性。
The recovery of America is central to this future. However great the degradation of American ideals in the MAGA era, the United States remains unique in its power, resources and embedded values and in its ability to overcome serious failings. It took fourscore and seven years and a cruel civil war before the proposition that all men are created equal was expanded to include Black people and an additional century to translate that into law. Women have had the vote for only a bit more than 100 years, and the struggle for equality is far from finished. Yet to borrow further from Abraham Lincoln, America has succeeded in “a new birth of freedom” again and again, and it can do it once more.
美國的復甦對於這一未來至關重要。無論美國理想在MAGA時代經歷了多大退化，美國在其力量、資源和內嵌價值觀方面，以及在其克服嚴重缺陷的能力方面，仍然是獨特的。「人人生而平等」的設定用了87年的時間和一場殘酷的內戰才將黑人包括在內，又花了一個世紀才將其轉化為法律。女性獲得投票權僅有一百多年，爭取平等的鬥爭遠未結束。然而，進一步借用亞伯拉罕·林肯的話，美國一次又一次地成功實現了「自由的新生」，它能再一次做到。
We Muddle Along
我們得過且過
廣告
In this version of our collective future, the world carries on much as it is. A new U.S. administration ends Mr. Trump’s tariffs and tries to restore relations with allies and avoid conflicts, but a deep polarization continues to grip American politics, feeding constant tensions among the branches of government and destabilizing the executive.
在我們的共同未來的這個版本中，世界大體照舊延續。美國新一屆政府結束了川普的關稅，試圖恢復與盟友的關係並避免衝突，但深度的極化繼續攫住美國政治，助長政府各部門之間的持續緊張，並使行政分支不穩定。
America’s wealth and military power command fear and respect, but after all the back and forth of the Trump and Biden administrations, most of the world — including Europe and Canada — can no longer view the United States as either the democratic standard or a moral leader. American-led alliances and economic institutions weaken. Governments around the globe form shifting economic groupings and increasingly turn to China for trade and security.
美國的財富和軍事力量贏得恐懼和尊重，但在川普和拜登兩屆政府的反覆之後，世界大部分地區——包括歐洲和加拿大——不能再將美國視為民主標準或道德領袖。美國領導的聯盟和經濟體制被削弱。世界各國政府形成不斷變化的經濟組合，並越來越多地轉向中國尋求貿易和安全。
As China surpasses the United States economically and draws closer militarily, Beijing forges ever closer economic ties to Europe and emerging economies and escalates pressure on Taiwan, though it avoids confrontations that could provoke American action. Several of the countries once relegated to the developing world — like India, Mexico, Brazil and Indonesia — approach first rank but without a shared vision for how the world should work together.
隨著中國在經濟上超越美國並在軍事上拉近距離，北京與歐洲和新興經濟體建立越來越緊密的經濟聯繫，並升級對台灣的壓力，儘管它避免了可能招致美國行動的對抗。幾個曾被歸入發展中國家的國家——如印度、墨西哥、巴西和印度尼西亞——接近第一梯隊，但沒有關於世界應如何合作的共同願景。
In the Middle East, Mr. Trump finally found an exit ramp in Iran before leaving office, restoring passage through the Strait of Hormuz, but the mullahs, with much of their enriched uranium, remain in power in Iran. Mr. Netanyahu is gone, but Gaza remains devastated, and new Israeli settlements in the West Bank continue to fan Palestinian resentment. Mr. Putin has been overthrown by a junta of siloviki — the military and security barons through whom he ruled — but the war with Ukraine remains a smoldering stalemate. Europe accepts greater responsibility for its own defense but struggles to find a unified approach to China, Russia or the problems of the world.
在中東，川普在離任前終於在伊朗問題上找到了出口，恢復了霍爾木茲海峽的通行，但毛拉們帶著大部分濃縮鈾繼續在伊朗掌權。納坦尼雅胡走了，但加薩仍然滿目瘡痍，約旦河西岸的新以色列定居點繼續煽動巴勒斯坦人的怨恨。普丁被一個由西羅維基——他藉以統治的軍事和安全寡頭——組成的軍政府推翻，但與烏克蘭的戰爭仍是一場悶燒的僵局。歐洲對自己的防務承擔更大責任，但難以就中國、俄羅斯或世界問題找到統一方針。
Global tasks like battling climate change continue in fits and starts, without major commitments or progress. The United Nations and its major agencies, like the World Health Organization and UNICEF, remain suspect to much of America and thereby limited in reach and financing. The world is largely at peace, but this can change at any moment as China rapidly gathers force.
諸如抗擊氣候變化之類的全球任務斷斷續續地進行，沒有重大承諾或進展。聯合國及其主要機構，如世界衛生組織和聯合國兒童基金會，仍被美國大部分地區所懷疑，因而在覆蓋範圍和資金上受到限制。世界大體和平，但隨著中國迅速積聚力量，這隨時可能改變。
Unchecked Decline
失控的衰落
廣告
Here, polarization and recriminations paralyze the United States. NATO and other American-led alliances lose meaning in a maelstrom of tariff wars and mutual rancor. Major international organizations — the United Nations, the World Trade Organization, the World Bank, the International Monetary Fund — founder as countries north and south seek security and trade in shifting alliances and trading blocs. Beijing succeeds in annexing Taiwan without international military resistance, but China’s growth stalls, leaving the world without a dominant center. Unregulated A.I. leads to a host of new crises — rogue incursions and raids, proliferation of misinformation, loss of countless jobs, dangerous advances in cyberwarfare.
極化和相互指責使美國癱瘓。北約和其他美國領導的聯盟在關稅戰和相互怨恨的漩渦中失去意義。主要國際組織——聯合國、世界貿易組織、世界銀行、國際貨幣基金組織——在南北國家於不斷變化的聯盟和貿易集團中尋求安全和貿易之際紛紛動搖。北京成功吞併台灣而沒有遭遇國際軍事抵抗，但中國的增長停滯，使世界沒有一個主導中心。不受監管的人工智慧導致一系列新危機——擅自入侵和突襲、虛假信息擴散、無數工作崗位流失、網路戰在危險地推進。
An America distrusted by other nations and trapped in debilitating internal struggles leads to a deeply fragmented and unstable world, increasingly vulnerable to Chinese demands. Xenophobia and ancient grievances become the stuff of local politics and international relations, leading to flare-ups in the Caucasus, the Balkans, the Horn of Africa and other places where demagogues fan animosities.
一個不被其他國家信任、陷於衰弱內鬥的美國導致一個深度碎片化和不穩定的世界，越來越屈從於中國的要求。排外情緒和宿仇成為地方政治和國際關係的素材，導致高加索、巴爾幹、非洲之角以及其他煽動者煽動敵意的地方爆發衝突。
Unchecked global warming creates chaos the world over, from seawater sloshing through the Bowery to flooding and drought in Africa and Asia, setting off vast new migrations and conflicts. A total breakdown of alliances and international organizations leaves the world vulnerable to the full range of historical scourges, from famine to war.
不受制約的全球暖化在全世界製造混亂，從海水漫過鮑厄裡到非洲和亞洲的洪水和乾旱，引發大規模的新移民和衝突。聯盟和國際組織的全面崩潰使世界容易遭受從饑荒到戰爭的全方位歷史性災禍。
Russia, its oil and gas fields battered by Ukrainian drones, threatens a tactical nuclear strike against Ukraine. The United States and Iran remain at a yearslong stalemate, rendering passage through the Strait of Hormuz sporadic and dangerous, pushing the price of oil ever higher and adding, most grievously, to the woes of developing countries and the poor with rising energy, transportation and food costs. Saudi Arabia and other Middle Eastern powers lose faith in America. Terrorist attacks multiply in Israel and the occupied territories, the threat of war hanging over the region.
油氣田遭到烏克蘭無人機打擊的俄羅斯威脅對烏克蘭進行戰術核打擊。美國和伊朗仍處於長達數年的僵局，使霍爾木茲海峽的通航時斷時續且危險，推高石油價格，並且最令人痛心地，以不斷上漲的能源、運輸和食品成本加劇發展中國家和窮人的苦難。沙烏地阿拉伯和其他中東大國對美國失去信心。恐怖襲擊在以色列和被佔領土上成倍增加，戰爭的威脅籠罩著該地區。
Even in this scenario, a major clash of great powers — the United States, China, Russia or even Europe — remains unlikely, given that such conflicts tend to be over historical wrongs, artificial crises or regional borders, but the threat of regional conflicts going out of control is ever present. The strongmen in power are not above using conflict to extend control over their subjects.
即使在這一情景中，大國——美國、中國、俄羅斯甚至歐洲——之間的重大衝突仍然不太可能，因為此類衝突往往源於歷史仇怨、人為危機或地區邊界，但地區衝突失控的威脅始終存在。當權的強人們樂於利用衝突來擴大對其臣民的控制。
The world is a dark place, and America is no longer there to offer light.
世界是一個黑暗的地方，那個能帶來光明的美國已經不再。
廣告
There are, of course, innumerable other ways things could play out in coming years and decades. As our great American sage, again, reputedly said, “It’s tough to make predictions, especially about the future.”
當然，在未來幾年和幾十年裡，事情還有無數其他的演變方式。再次引用我們偉大的美國智者據稱說過的：「做預測很難，尤其是關於未來的預測。」
The dangers are huge, but the future need not be dystopian. However the old liberal order is viewed, its achievements cannot be denied — or quickly undone. Even as autocracies spread, the yearning for freedom remains strong. Ukrainians have demonstrated an extraordinary determination to stay free; American judges still often stand up to Mr. Trump’s efforts to subvert the law; journalists the world over risk freedom and even death to report the truth; countries still rush to help others when catastrophe strikes, as they did when floods ravaged Nepal and Tibet in August.
危險是巨大的，但未來不必是反烏托邦的。無論人們如何看待舊的自由秩序，它的成就無法被否認——也無法被迅速抹去。即使專制政體蔓延，對自由的渴望仍然強烈。烏克蘭人展現了堅守自由的非凡決心；美國的法官們仍然經常挺身對抗川普顛覆法律的企圖；全世界的記者冒著失去自由甚至死亡的風險報導真相；當災難來襲時，各國仍然會緊急行動起來幫助他人，正如他們在8月份尼泊爾和西藏遭受洪水肆虐時所做的那樣。
Elections can reverse an extremist drift, as they did in Poland and Hungary, but they can also raise warning signals, as with the gains of the Alternative for Germany in Germany’s recent regional elections.
