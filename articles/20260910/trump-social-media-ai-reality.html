10小時60條社群媒體動態：川普的「現實」世界

60 Posts in 10 Hours: How Trump’s Social Media Reflects His Version of Reality

PETER BAKER • 2026-09-10
Just before noon on a holiday weekend, while many Americans were at the beach, President Trump’s social media account roared to life. In what has increasingly become his weekend habit, he unleashed a daylong barrage of posts that put the bully in the modern bully pulpit.
在一個假期週末臨近中午時分，當許多美國人還在海灘上時，川普總統的社群媒體帳號突然活躍起來。這已越來越成為他週末的習慣——持續一整天的密集發帖，把總統的輿論平台變成了霸凌現場。
There were images of Mr. Trump intimidating Canada’s prime minister with a hockey stick, personally bombing enemy targets and patrolling dark streets with killer robots. There were maps showing the United States taking over other countries, and maps renaming a state. There were attacks on the “Dumocratic Party” and tributes to himself as “the Greatest President of All Time!”
川普發布了一些圖片：裡面有他手持冰球杆威嚇加拿大總理，或者親自轟炸敵方目標，或者與殺人機器人一起在漆黑的街道上巡邏的場景。還有顯示美國吞併其他國家以及給某個州改名的地圖。他還攻擊「Dumocratic Party」（故意將民主黨拼成蠢貨黨——譯註），並發帖自誇「有史以來最偉大的總統」。
The 60 messages posted online over 10 hours and 41 minutes offered a window into Mr. Trump’s own unique reality, one anchored not in fact but in an expression of ego. For years, his social media feed was said to be a mirror of his id. But with A.I., it has become the manifestation of how he wants to be seen in a more visual, visceral way than ever before — as a warrior, as a conqueror, as a historical figure, as a younger and thinner and more muscular version of himself.
這60條信息在10小時41分鐘內陸續發布，向人們展示了川普獨特的現實世界——一個並非建立在事實而是建立在自我意識表達之上的世界。多年來，人們一直說他的社群媒體動態就像他本我的一面鏡子。但藉助人工智慧，它比以往任何時候都更加直觀、更加震撼地展現了他想要呈現的形象——一名戰士、一名征服者、一位歷史人物，以及一個更加年輕、更加瘦削、肌肉更加發達的自己。
In Mr. Trump’s digitally enhanced version of the world, he is a giant in every way, a man who defeats every adversary, overcomes every obstacle and sinks every putt. He not only redraws the map of the world but vanquishes the heavens themselves. “The Moon Is Ours,” declared one post last weekend with an image of the earth’s only satellite. Another posted a few weeks ago showed him leading an extraterrestrial alien in shackles.
在川普經過數位技術強化的世界裡，他在方方面面都是一個巨人，是一個能夠擊敗所有對手、克服所有障礙、每一桿推桿都能進洞的人。他不僅重新繪製世界地圖，甚至還征服了整個蒼穹。他上週末在一條帖子中配上一張地球唯一衛星的圖片，宣稱：「月球是我們的。」幾週前的另一條帖子則顯示，他牽著一個戴著鐐銬的外星人。
廣告
The rat-a-tat of ever wilder, ever more outrageous posts each weekend has become a fixture of the president’s second term, seen more and more as a barometer of his mental state. Mr. Trump’s critics, who never considered him a model of stability in the first place, now point to these frenetic bursts of fantasy and vanity as proof that the 80-year-old president is deteriorating further with age.
每個週末越來越瘋狂、越來越離譜的連番發帖已成為總統第二任期的固定節目，也越來越多地被視為衡量其精神狀態的晴雨表。川普的批評者原本就不認為他是精神狀態穩定的典範，如今更是把這些狂亂迸發的幻想和虛榮視為這位80歲總統隨著年齡增長而進一步衰退的證據。
It is a measure of how much Mr. Trump has changed the nature of the presidency that any of this has become routine. Past presidents believed they had a duty to carry themselves with a certain dignity befitting the office. Mr. Trump sees politics like the cage match he sponsored this summer at the White House.
這些事情如今已經成為日常，恰恰說明川普在多大程度上改變了總統職位的性質。過去的總統認為，自己有責任以符合總統這一職位的某種莊重姿態行事。而川普看待政治的方式就像他今年夏天在白宮主辦的那場籠斗比賽。
Indeed, the images he posted over the Labor Day weekend included one with his face on Hulk Hogan’s rippling body. Another posted a day later imagined a grinning Mr. Trump easily winning an arm wrestling match against Mr. Hogan, the flamboyant wrestling star who died last year.
事實上，他在勞動節週末發布的圖片中，有一張把他的頭像合成到胡克·霍根肌肉虯結的身體上。一天後的另一條帖子則想像了一個笑容滿面的川普輕鬆贏得與霍根掰手腕——這位張揚的摔跤明星已於去年去世。
“He floods the zone with so much of it that we’ve become numb to just how bizarre it is,” said Sarah Matthews, Mr. Trump’s former deputy White House press secretary. “An 80-year-old president is spending a ridiculous amount of time consuming and reposting endless A.I. fan fiction portraying him as the biggest, strongest and most important person alive. It feels like we’re genuinely watching his brain rot in real time.”
「他用這麼多東西淹沒信息場，以至於我們已經對這一切有多離奇變得麻木了，」川普的前副白宮新聞秘書薩拉·馬修斯說。「一位80歲的總統正花費荒謬的時間去消費和轉發無窮無盡的人工智慧同人小說，把他描繪成活著的最高大、最強壯、最重要的人。感覺我們真的是正在看著他的腦子在我們眼前一點點腐爛。」
To his defenders, Mr. Trump is a master of baiting his adversaries, getting them to react. “It’s called: We do a little trolling,” as Mr. Trump once put it. The more out there the message is, the more his supporters cheer. As abnormal as the images that he posts may be for a president, Mr. Trump’s allies argue that they convey a certain raw, earthy quality that suits an age of no-holds-barred politics.
對於那些為他辯護的人來說，川普是引誘對手上鉤、讓他們做出反應的大師。就像他自己曾說過的：「這叫：我們稍微逗逗他們。」內容越出格，他的支持者就越歡呼。儘管他發布的圖片對一位總統來說可能很不尋常，但川普的盟友認為，它們傳遞出一種直白的、接地氣的特質，恰好適合這個不受任何規則約束的政治時代。
“With all due respect, the media and President Trump’s other critics have spent more than a decade complaining about his use of social media,” said Alex Bruesewitz, a former campaign aide. “They keep calling it ‘unpresidential,’ but if the president is doing it, it is presidential by definition. I think most Americans appreciate the authentic and transparent way he uses these platforms.​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​”
「恕我直言，媒體以及其他批評川普的人，已經抱怨他使用社群媒體超過十年了，」川普前競選團隊助理亞歷克斯·布魯塞維茨說。「他們一直稱這種做法『不符合總統身份』，但如果總統在這麼做，那麼按照定義，這就是總統的行為。我認為，大多數美國人都欣賞他使用這些平台時所展現出的真實和透明。」
Violence is a recurring theme in Mr. Trump’s feed. He relishes the idea of himself as a tough man staring down anyone who gets in his way. He smashes Iran with a giant hammer. He pilots a spacecraft blasting the planet. Most arresting was his post last weekend showing him in a hockey uniform with a stick towering over a fallen Prime Minister Mark Carney of Canada and taunting, “Get up, Governor,” as if Canada really were the 51st state that he says he wants to make it. That one, unsurprisingly, further inflamed international tensions.
暴力是川普社群媒體動態中反覆出現的主題。他熱衷於把自己想像成一個硬漢，正面硬剛擋他路的人。他用一把巨錘砸向伊朗。他駕駛宇宙飛船轟炸地球。最令人注目的是他上週末發布的一張圖片：他身穿冰球服，手持球杆，高高在上地俯視倒在地上的加拿大總理馬克·卡尼，並嘲弄道：「起來，州長。」彷彿加拿大真的是他心心念念的第51個州。毫不意外，這條帖子進一步加劇了國家間的緊張關係。
As Mr. Trump surely knew it would. It is hard to imagine any other president picturing himself physically assaulting the leader of an ally. But for Mr. Trump, who has been unable to force Mr. Carney to back down in a tariff fight, the cartoonish image allows him to present himself as winning even when he is not.
川普當然知道它會產生這樣的效果。很難想像還有哪位總統會想像自己對一個盟友領導人動粗。但對於在關稅爭端中始終無法迫使卡尼讓步的川普來說，這種漫畫式的形象讓他即使處於劣勢也能把自己塑造成勝利者。
Never mind that he may cry foul if anyone else were to picture him as the target of violence. Goaded by Mr. Trump, the Justice Department indicted James B. Comey, the former F.B.I. director, for a social media post showing seashells spelling out the numbers “86 47,” interpreting the image as a threat to the president.
但如果別人把他描繪成暴力目標，他可能會大喊不公。在川普的鼓動下，司法部起訴了前聯邦調查局局長詹姆斯·科米，原因是後者在社群媒體上發了一條帖子，上面用貝殼拼出數字「86 47」，司法部將其解讀為對總統的威脅。
廣告
“This is truly unhinged behavior from the President of the United States,” Representative Chellie Pingree, a Maine Democrat, wrote online after the Carney post. “And I don’t want to hear ‘oh, it’s just Trump being Trump’ or that he’s ‘trolling.’ Enough. None of this is normal or okay. Republicans cannot keep shrugging this off. Congress, the Cabinet, and everyone around him has a responsibility to stop enabling this dangerous behavior and start asking whether he is fit to hold this office.”
「美國總統做出這樣的行為，實在是極度失控，」緬因州民主黨籍眾議員謝莉·平格裡在川普發布的有關卡尼的帖子後寫道。「我不想再聽到什麼『哦，這就是川普，他一向如此』，或者說他只是在『引對手上鉤』。夠了。這一切都不正常，也沒有一件是可以接受的。共和黨人不能再繼續對此聳聳肩、置之不理。國會、內閣以及他身邊的所有人都有責任停止縱容這種危險的行為，並開始認真思考：他是否還適合擔任這一職務。」
The White House shrugged off the criticism. “Truth Social is the most powerful and popular social media platform in the world because it serves as President Trump’s authentic voice, where he communicates directly with the American people and the world without the biased media taking him out of context,” Davis Ingle, a White House spokesman, said in a statement, using the name of the president’s social media platform.
白宮對批評不以為意。「Truth Social是世界上最強大、最受歡迎的社群媒體平台，因為它是川普總統真實的聲音，他在這裡直接與美國人民和世界溝通，而不受帶有偏見的媒體斷章取義的影響，」白宮發言人戴維斯·英格爾在一份聲明中說。
In fact, by traditional measures, Truth Social is nowhere near as popular as major social media platforms like Facebook or X and its traffic has slumped since last year. But it has effectively become the town hall for Mr. Trump’s MAGA followers and therefore plays an outsize role in American politics. Either posting himself or approving posts crafted by aides, Mr. Trump uses it as his main outlet for presidential messages to the world.
事實上，按傳統標準衡量，Truth Social遠不及Facebook或X等主要社群媒體平台受歡迎，其流量自去年以來也大幅下滑。但它實際上已成為川普MAGA追隨者的市政廳，因此在美國政治中扮演著超乎尋常的角色。無論是自己發帖還是批准助手起草的帖子，川普都將其作為向世界傳遞總統信息的主要管道。
Last Sunday was a striking day on Truth Social but increasingly typical of the president’s weekend blitzes, when he has fewer staff members around him and more unscheduled time. While at his golf club in Bedminster, N.J., Mr. Trump started posting at 11:54 a.m. and kept returning to his account in staccato bursts throughout the day. At 1:47 p.m., he posted six messages within two minutes. From 2:40 p.m. to 3:43 p.m., he circulated another 27 posts. He continued through the evening, only letting up after his final one of the night at 10:35 p.m.
上週日，Truth Social上的活動異常頻繁，但這越來越符合總統的週末突擊作風——週末他身邊的工作人員較少，空閑時間也更多。在紐澤西州貝德明斯特的高爾夫俱樂部，川普從上午11點54分開始發帖，一整天都斷斷續續地頻繁更新。下午1點47分，他在兩分鐘內發布了六條消息。從下午2點40分到3點43分，他又轉發了27條。他持續到晚上，直到晚上10點35分發出最後一條後才停歇。
They were the kinds of posts that are common on the internet, just not normally from a head of state — impertinent, insulting, scornful, belligerent and sometimes out-and-out wacky. They offered a Rorschach test for followers, funny to those who admire what they see as Mr. Trump’s wry sense of humor, delusional to those who think he has lost his mind.
這些帖子是互聯網上司空見慣的那種，只不過通常不會來自一位國家元首——無禮、侮辱、輕蔑、好鬥，有時完全荒誕。它們為追隨者提供了一場羅夏測試：對欣賞川普那種挖苦的幽默的人來說很好笑，對認為他已失去理智的人來說則是妄想。
While Mr. Trump’s posts regularly influence domestic politics and foreign policy, he also uses A.I. to promote grandiose visions of himself as a superhero, a pope and at one point last spring a Jesus-like figure. Last weekend’s posts included one that he has circulated before offering his own chart ranking American presidents, with him, naturally, at the top as “The Greatest.” Another post asserted: “He Will Go Down In History As the Greatest President of All Time!”
雖然川普的帖子經常影響國內政治和外交政策，但他也用人工智慧來宣揚自己是超級英雄、教宗，以及去年春天某個時刻甚至是耶穌式人物的宏大幻象。上週末的帖子中有一條他之前也發過，提供了他自己的美國總統排名圖表，自然把自己列為榜首「最偉大」。另一條斷言：「他將作為有史以來最偉大的總統載入史冊！」
廣告
Mr. Trump, who avoided the draft during the Vietnam War with a diagnosis of bone spurs provided by a doctor who was one of his father’s tenants, particularly enjoys presenting himself as a martial hero.
川普在越戰期間憑藉醫生（他父親的租戶之一）出具的骨刺診斷躲過了徵兵，他特別喜歡把自己塑造成戰鬥英雄。
In an image posted last month, Mr. Trump wore the military uniform that he never wore in real life standing between Generals George S. Patton and Douglas MacArthur. George Washington has become a regular guest in Mr. Trump’s world lately. Sunday’s burst included images of him with the father of the country on horseback and in the Oval Office.
在上個月發布的一張圖片中，川普穿著他在現實生活中從未穿過的軍裝，站在喬治·巴頓將軍和道格拉斯·麥克阿瑟將軍之間。喬治·華盛頓近來已成為川普世界的常客。週日的連番發帖中包括他與這位國父一起騎馬以及在橢圓形辦公室的圖片。
The advent of A.I. has allowed Mr. Trump to go further than the mainly written social media posts of his first term. “Trump has always treated social media as a window into whatever he is thinking at that moment, but A.I. makes that window far more vivid and frankly more disturbing,” said Ms. Matthews, his former press aide. “And it feels like the more difficult reality gets for him, the more A.I. slop he posts.”
人工智慧的出現讓川普比第一任期時主要以文字為主的社群媒體帖子走得更遠。「川普一直把社群媒體當作了解他當時想法的窗口，但人工智慧讓這個窗口更加生動，坦率地說也更令人不安，」他的前新聞助理馬修斯說。「而且感覺對他來說現實越艱難，他發的人工智慧垃圾就越多。」
Mr. Bruesewitz, the former campaign aide, called Mr. Trump an innovator who is paving the way to the future of politics. “President Trump is the first true social media president,” he said. “He has always adapted to the times, using new technology and platforms to take his message straight to the public. His A.I. posts generate enormous attention whenever he hits send.”
前競選助手布魯塞維茨稱川普是一位創新者，正在為政治的未來鋪路。「川普總統是第一位真正的社群媒體總統，」他說。「他一直適應時代，利用新技術和平台把信息直接傳達給公眾。他的AI帖子每次一發出就引發巨大關注。」
No one would debate that they are attention generators. One way or another, that seems to be the point.
沒有人會否認它們很能引來關注。說到底，這似乎正是重點所在。

Peter Baker是《紐約時報》首席白宮記者。川普是他報導的第六任美國總統，有時他也撰寫將總統和美國政府置於更宏觀的背景和歷史框架下的分析性文章。
