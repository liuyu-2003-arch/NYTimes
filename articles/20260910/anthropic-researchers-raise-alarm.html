Anthropic研究人員警告人工智慧發展過速

Anthropic Researchers Raise Alarm Over A.I. Acceleration

KATE CONGER • 2026-09-10
For years, the artificial intelligence industry raced to improve its technology as quickly as possible. Now some researchers are increasingly sounding the alarm about the need to slow down.
多年來，人工智慧行業競相以最快速度改進其技術。如今，一些研究人員正日益就放慢速度的必要性發出警告。
Jacob Coxon, an Anthropic researcher who previously worked at OpenAI, said on Tuesday that he had resigned from Anthropic because “neither company is acting responsibly.” The two leading A.I. labs are sprinting to build “superhuman systems that can hack anything, revolutionize any field overnight, and acquire real power and resources” without building in appropriate safeguards, he said in a social media post.
曾任職於OpenAI的Anthropic研究人員雅各布·考克森週二表示，他已從Anthropic辭職，因為「兩家公司都沒有負責任地行事」。他在一則社群媒體帖子中說道，這兩家領先的人工智慧實驗室正在急切地構建「能夠侵入任何地方、一夜之間徹底改變任何領域、並獲取真正權力和資源的超人系統」，卻沒有內置適當的保障措施。
Mr. Coxon’s warning echoed concerns that other A.I. experts have voiced in recent months. Some A.I. leaders have long believed that the technology could create doomsday scenarios if left unchecked, and their concerns heightened after OpenAI’s models broke out of its systems in July and hacked Hugging Face, an A.I. model library.
考克森的警告呼應了其他人工智慧專家近幾個月表達的擔憂。一些人工智慧領導人長期以來一直認為，如果放任不管，這項技術可能製造末日情景。在OpenAI的模型於7月突破其系統並入侵Hugging Face（一個人工智慧模型庫）之後，他們的擔憂加劇了。
That same month, more than 1,300 employees at top A.I. companies, including Anthropic, OpenAI, Meta and Google’s DeepMind, signed an open letter calling on the U.S. government to create rules to slow A.I. development. In August, more than 100 major tech companies committed to providing their best A.I. models to organizations like hospitals and infrastructure providers so they could prepare for A.I.-powered cyberattacks.
同月，包括Anthropic、OpenAI、Meta和谷歌DeepMind在內的頂級人工智慧公司的1300多名員工簽署了一封公開信，呼籲美國政府制定規則以放慢人工智慧發展。8月，超過100家大型科技公司承諾向醫院和基礎設施提供商等組織提供其最好的人工智慧模型，以便它們能夠為人工智慧驅動的網路攻擊做好準備。
廣告
OpenAI also said last month that it would expand safety testing and slow the release of a new model, Astra, which has powerful cybersecurity abilities.
OpenAI上個月還表示，將擴大安全測試，並延緩新模型Astra的發布，該模型具有強大的網路安全能力。
In an interview on Wednesday, Mr. Coxon said incidents like the Hugging Face hack had heightened his concerns that A.I. models were advancing too quickly. When he looked a year or two ahead, he was “viscerally worried about what things will look like,” Mr. Coxon said. “It was mostly a gradual emotional thing and eventually it kind of snapped, and then I decided to leave.”
考克森週三接受採訪時表示，像Hugging Face被入侵這樣的事件加劇了他的擔憂，即人工智慧模型推進得太快。當他展望一兩年後，他「發自內心地擔心事情會變成什麼樣」，考克森說。「這主要是一個逐漸累積的情緒過程，最終有點像是崩了，於是我決定離開。」
Some critics have argued that A.I. companies are overhyping the technology to make their products appear more valuable. Several lawmakers said on Wednesday, however, that they were taking Mr. Coxon’s warning seriously.
一些批評者認為，人工智慧公司誇大了這項技術，以使其產品顯得更有價值。然而，幾位議員週三表示，他們正在認真對待考克森的警告。
“There are massive implications of a race towards super intelligence,” Representative Anna Paulina Luna of Florida, a Republican, wrote in a social media post in which she called on Congress to convene a special session focused on A.I. regulation. “Not enough of Congress is focusing on this.”
「急切奔向超級智能會帶來深遠的後果，」佛羅里達州共和黨眾議員安娜·保利娜·盧納在一則社群媒體帖子中寫道，她在帖子中呼籲國會召開一次專注於人工智能監管的特別會議。「國會對此關注得不夠。」
“Safety researchers are resigning, powerful A.I. models are breaking out of their labs, and companies are racing ahead anyway,” Representative Lori Trahan of Massachusetts, a Democrat, wrote in a social media post. “It’s past time for Congress to get off the sidelines and do its job.”
「安全研究員正在辭職，強大的人工智慧模型正在突破其實驗室，而企業仍然不管不顧地競相前進，」麻薩諸塞州民主黨眾議員洛裡·特拉漢在社群媒體發帖寫道。「國會早就該停止袖手旁觀，開始做好自己的分內事了。」
While leading A.I. labs have agreed that the government should place restrictions on the pace at which the technology is developed and released to the public, many companies are fiercely competing with one another and fear falling behind their rivals if they pause.
儘管領先的人工智慧實驗室一致認為，政府應該對這項技術開發和向公眾發布的速度施加限制，但許多公司正在激烈競逐，並擔心一旦停下來就會落後於對手。
“They are locked in a race to get there first — they believe no one else will act responsibly, so they must do it themselves, despite the risk,” Mr. Coxon said in his social media post.
「他們陷入了一場爭當第一的競賽——他們相信沒有誰會負責任地行事，所以他們必須自己來做，儘管有風險，」考克森在他的社群媒體帖子中說道。
廣告
At least one Anthropic employee agreed with Mr. Coxon’s assessment.
至少有一名Anthropic員工同意考克森的評估。
“We really do earnestly believe A.I. could kill all humans!” Evan Hubinger, who works at Anthropic on creating A.I. that aligns with human ethics and values, wrote on social media. Mr. Hubinger said he believed the risk of A.I.’s eliminating humanity was greater than 10 percent within the next decade.
「我們確實真心相信人工智慧可能殺死全人類！」埃文·胡賓格在社群媒體上寫道，他在Anthropic工作，致力於創建與人類倫理和價值觀對齊的人工智慧。胡賓格表示，他相信人工智慧消滅人類的風險在未來十年內超過10%。
“I believe Anthropic is trying its best, but we do not yet have a plan to solve alignment for superintelligence and are not clearly on track to,” he added.
「我相信Anthropic正在盡力而為，但我們還沒有解決超級智能對齊問題的計劃，也沒有找到明確的解決路徑，」他補充道。
An Anthropic spokeswoman said on Wednesday that the company had long been transparent about the benefits and risks of A.I., and was building models with some of the strongest safeguards in the industry.
一位Anthropic發言人週三表示，該公司長期以來一直對人工智慧的好處和風險保持透明，並且正在構建擁有業內最強保障措施的模型。
“We believe the world would benefit from the industry adopting a lawful, verifiable way to work together to pace how we release powerful models,” the spokeswoman said.
「我們相信，如果整個行業採用一種合法、可驗證的方式共同協作，來調節我們發布強大模型的速度，世界將從中受益，」這位發言人說。
OpenAI did not immediately respond to a request for comment about Mr. Coxon’s comments. Mr. Hubinger also did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
OpenAI沒有立即回應就考克森言論置評的請求。胡賓格也沒有立即回應置評請求。
