高薪養廉？新加坡總理漲薪64%

Why This Politician Is Getting a 64% Raise

MAX KIM • 2026-09-10
For a city-state with neither abundant natural resources nor a large population, the key to prosperity was ensuring things just worked, said Lee Kuan Yew, the founding father of modern Singapore.
現代新加坡的國父李光耀曾說，對於一個既沒有豐富自然資源也沒有龐大人口的城市國家而言，繁榮的關鍵在於確保一切正常運轉。
And keeping the country running smoothly, from its seaports and airports to financial services, were strict and unbendable rules, enforced by a phalanx of well-paid public servants.
而讓這個國家平穩運行——從海港和機場到金融服務——依靠的是嚴格且不可變通的規則，由一群薪酬優厚的公務員執行。
The idea was reaffirmed on Tuesday, as Prime Minister Lawrence Wong announced ministerial wage increases that will bump his annual salary by 64 percent to $3.6 million Singapore dollars ($2.85 million).
這一理念在週二得到重申。總理黃循財宣布部長級官員加薪，其本人的年薪將提高64%，達到360萬新加坡元（約合人民幣1910萬元）。
The move was part of a broader hike to cabinet ministers’ pay in Singapore, which has long had the highest political salaries in the world.
此舉是新加坡內閣部長薪酬整體上調的一部分。新加坡長期以來一直是全球政治人物薪酬最高的國家。
廣告
Junior ministers’ salaries will jump from the equivalent of $870,000 to as much as $1.4 million.
初級部長的薪酬將從相當於87萬美元（約合人民幣580萬元）躍升至最高140萬美元（約合人民幣934萬元）。
In a statement delivered to the country’s Parliament, Mr. Wong said that political salaries had been stagnant for 15 years, and that it was becoming harder to recruit top talent from the private sector at a time when the country needed to furnish the next generation of leaders.
黃循財在向國會發表的聲明中表示，政治人物薪酬已停滯15年，在國家需要培養下一代領導人之際，從私營部門招聘頂尖人才已變得越來越困難。
“We recognize that highly able people have alternatives outside politics,” he said. “So we pay political officeholders salaries that are competitive, but with a substantial discount to reflect political service.”
「我們認識到，能力出眾的人在政壇之外另有選擇，」他說。「因此，我們向擔任政治職務者支付具有競爭力的薪酬，但也會以大幅折扣來體現政治服務的性質。」
Singapore has been governed by Mr. Lee’s People’s Action Party since 1959. Its leaders have long argued that high wages for officials were a deterrent against corruption, as well as a way to attract competitive candidates who might otherwise take high-paying jobs at private companies.
新加坡自1959年以來一直由李光耀創建的人民行動黨執政。該黨領導人長期以來主張，官員的高薪既是對腐敗的抑制，也是吸引有競爭力候選人的一種方式——否則這些人可能會在私營企業從事高薪工作。
In 1985, Mr. Lee famously said: “I’m one of the best paid and probably one of the poorest of the Third World prime ministers. You abandon this for hypocrisy, you’ll end up with duplicity and corruption. Take your choice.”
1985年，李光耀曾有過一段著名的表述：「我是第三世界國家總理中薪酬最高但可能也是最窮的之一。如果你出於虛偽的目的放棄這一點，最終會得到欺騙和腐敗。你自己選。」
Today, Singapore is one of the world’s richest countries per capita, a thriving financial hub and, thanks to its ports, a maritime giant.
如今，新加坡是世界上人均最富有的國家之一，是一個繁榮的金融中心，並且憑藉其港口成為海運重鎮。
廣告
Even before the latest raise, the prime minister of Singapore was already the highest-paid public servant in the world, far out-earning the president of the United States, whose annual salary is $400,000 and was last increased a quarter-century ago.
即便在此次加薪之前，新加坡總理就已經是世界上薪酬最高的公職人員，遠遠超過美國總統——後者的年薪為40萬美元，上一次加薪還是在25年前。
Though Singapore has consistently been ranked as one of the least corrupt countries in the world — with the 2024 trial of a former transport minister marking the first major corruption case in 50 years — the ministerial wage policy has also faced criticism.
儘管新加坡一直被列為世界上最清廉的國家之一——2024年對一名前交通部長的審判標誌著50年來首起重大腐敗案件——但部長薪酬政策也一直面臨批評。
In 2012, cabinet salaries were slashed by around 36 percent amid accusations that high ministerial wages, which are pegged to the median annual income of Singapore’s top 1,000 earners, made the country’s leaders out of touch with the realities of ordinary citizens.
2012年，內閣薪酬被削減了約36%，原因是有人指責，與新加坡收入最高的1000人中位年收入掛鈎的部長高薪使國家領導人與普通民眾的現實脫節。
In his comments on Tuesday, Mr. Wong appeared to brace for such criticism. “Political salaries are a difficult and emotive issue,” he said. “The sums involved are more than what most citizens earn. So I understand why Singaporeans scrutinize them closely, and why many feel strongly about the matter.”
黃循財在週二的講話中似乎做好了面對此類批評的準備。「政治薪酬是一個困難且容易引發情緒的問題，」他說。「涉及的金額高於大多數公民的收入。所以我理解為什麼新加坡人對此密切關注，也理解為什麼許多人對這件事反應強烈。」
While the economy is performing well, many Singaporeans still say they feel anxious about the high cost of living.
儘管經濟表現良好，許多新加坡人仍然表示對高昂的生活成本感到焦慮。
On Tuesday, Mr. Wong said that, in light of his role in the salary adjustments, he would donate the full increase in his salary to charitable causes.
黃循財週二表示，鑒於他在薪酬調整中的角色，他將把自己薪酬的全部增幅捐給慈善事業。
廣告
“This is my own decision,” he said. “I do not expect other officeholders to do the same.”
「這是我個人的決定，」他說。「我不期望其他擔任公職者也這樣做。」
