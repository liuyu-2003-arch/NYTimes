川普引發「新仇」，但美國加拿大早有「舊怨」

A Hostile Trump Reopens Old Wounds in the U.S.-Canada Relationship

MICHAEL CROWLEY • 2026-09-10
The invasion of Canada was to be swift and overwhelming.
對加拿大的入侵本應迅速而且勢如破竹。
U.S. forces would take the port city of Halifax before seizing power plants near Niagara Falls. More American troops would storm across the border to capture Montreal, Quebec and other key cities. The secret U.S. military plan, known as War Plan Red, even authorized the use of poison gas.
美軍先奪取港口城市哈利法克斯，然後佔領尼亞加拉瀑布附近的發電廠。更多美軍越過邊境，攻佔蒙特婁、魁北克和其他關鍵城市。這項名為「紅色戰爭計劃」的祕密美軍計劃甚至授權使用毒氣。
The assault was never seriously considered. Drafted in 1927 and declassified decades later, it was a contingency plan for a highly unlikely war.
這次進攻從未被認真考慮過。它起草於1927年，數十年後被解密，是針對一場極不可能發生的戰爭的應急預案。
History took the opposite path: In the century since, the United States and Canada have been two of the friendliest and most integrated nations ever to share a border.
歷史走上了相反的道路：在此後一個世紀裡，美國和加拿大成為了有史以來關係最為友好、融合程度最高的兩個接壤國家。
廣告
Until recently, that is.
直到最近為止。
President Trump has thrown the U.S.-Canada relationship into its greatest crisis since his country’s northern neighbor became self-governing in 1867. Accusing Canada of unfair trade practices, Mr. Trump has instigated a trade war that on Tuesday led Prime Minister Mark Carney of Canada to retaliate with tariffs targeting roughly $20 billion in U.S. imports. Mr. Trump in turn escalated with bans on imports of some Canadian motorcycles, alcoholic beverages, dairy products and molasses.
川普總統將美加關係推入自這個北方鄰國1867年實現自治以來最嚴重的危機。川普指責加拿大採取不公平貿易做法，挑起了一場貿易戰，這導致加拿大總理馬克·卡尼於本週二採取報復措施，對約200億美元的美國進口商品加徵關稅。川普隨後升級行動，禁止進口部分加拿大摩托車、酒精飲料、乳製品和糖蜜。
Mr. Trump is hardly the first U.S. president to feud with Canada, especially on economic matters. Trade disputes shook relations for much of the 20th century, though they became far less common once the 1994 North American Free Trade Agreement came into force.
川普遠不是第一個與加拿大發生爭執的美國總統，尤其是在經濟問題上。貿易爭端在20世紀大部分時間裡動搖了兩國關係，但在1994年《北美自由貿易協定》生效後，這類爭端變得少得多。
Other heated cross-border disputes have flared on matters ranging from Alaska’s Yukon border to whether Canada would allow American nuclear weapons on its soil.
還有其他激烈的跨境爭端，包括從阿拉斯加育空邊界到加拿大是否允許美國核武器進入其領土等各種問題。
But Mr. Trump’s hostility toward Canada features a nationalist streak that goes well beyond economics. He has taunted its leaders as weaklings, denigrated its military as ineffective and even challenged its sovereignty. In particular, his talk of claiming its 3.9 million square miles of territory as a “51st state” has inflamed longtime Canadian anxieties about American domination, and even invasion.
但川普對加拿大的敵意帶有一種遠超出經濟範疇的民族主義色彩。他嘲諷加拿大領導人軟弱，貶低其軍隊無能，甚至質疑其主權。尤其是他聲稱要將加拿大998萬平方公里的領土變為「第51個州」，這點燃了加拿大人長期以來對美國支配甚至入侵的擔憂。
At times he even intimates violence, as in a recent Truth Social post depicting himself and Mr. Carney in hockey gear, with Mr. Trump standing over a fallen Mr. Carney as though he has just body-checked the Canadian leader.
有時他甚至暗示會訴諸暴力，比如他最近在Truth Social上發布的一條帖子，描繪自己和卡尼穿著冰球裝備，川普站在倒地的卡尼上方，彷彿剛剛對這位加拿大領導人進行了身體衝撞。
廣告
“I think it’s actually impossible to find a period where things were as bad,” said Robert Bothwell, a professor emeritus at the University of Toronto’s Munk School of Global Affairs who specializes in U.S.-Canada relations. “There’s never been an occasion, ever, where an American president has said the annexation of Canada was his objective.”
「我認為實際上不太可能找到比現在更糟的時期，」多倫多大學蒙克全球事務學院專門研究美加關係的榮休教授羅伯特·博思韋爾說。「從來沒有過，從來沒有過美國總統說吞併加拿大是他的目標。」
Before Mr. Trump came along, the popular American image of Canada was one of a friendly, harmless and perhaps somewhat dull junior partner to the United States. The notion of cross-border conflict was the stuff of satire. The 1995 Hollywood comedy “Canadian Bacon” imagined an unpopular U.S. president who whips up popular resentment toward Canada to distract from his domestic struggles. Now Canadians ask whether the movie was prescient.
在川普出現之前，美國人對加拿大的普遍印象是一個友好、無害、或許有些乏味的美國小夥伴。跨境衝突的想法是諷刺作品的素材。1995年的好萊塢喜劇《加拿大培根》(Canadian Bacon)設想了一位不受歡迎的美國總統，他煽動民眾對加拿大的不滿，以轉移對國內困境的注意力。如今，加拿大人不禁要問，這部電影是不是一種先見之明？
Canadians have always been warier of the United States, according to Christopher Sands, the director of the Center for U.S.-Canada Relations at Johns Hopkins University’s School of Advanced International Studies.
約翰斯·霍普金斯大學高級國際研究學院美加關係中心主任克里斯托弗·桑茲說，加拿大人一直對美國持更謹慎的態度。
Canada was formed by several British colonies that had never broken with London, unlike their American counterparts. Seeing the growth of U.S. economic and military power as a potential threat, they joined together in the name of collective strength while remaining part of the British Empire well into the 20th century. (War Plan Red did not reflect such a threat: It imagined a U.S. attack on Canada as a defensive response to war with Britain.)
加拿大由幾個英國殖民地組成，與美國的殖民地不同，這些殖民地從未與倫敦決裂。它們將美國經濟和軍事實力的增長視為潛在威脅，於是以集體力量的名義聯合起來，同時直到20世紀仍留在英帝國之內。（「紅色戰爭計劃」並未反映這種威脅：它設想美國對加拿大的攻擊是對與英國開戰的防禦性回應。）
Quarrels over a common 5,525-mile border were largely settled in a 1908 agreement, one that Mr. Trump has called invalid.
圍繞兩國8891公里邊界的爭端主要在1908年的一項協議中得到解決，川普曾稱該協議無效。
But economics still roiled the relationship. After Congress approved a 1911 agreement to drop tariffs on major Canadian imports, conservatives in Canada attacked it as a threat to the country’s independence — not entirely without justification.
但經濟仍然攪動著兩國關係。1911年，美國國會批准了一項取消加拿大主要進口商品關稅的協議後，加拿大的保守派抨擊該協議威脅加拿大國家獨立——這並非完全沒有道理。
廣告
“I look forward to the time when the American flag will fly over every square foot of British North America up to the North Pole,” Representative Champ Clark, a Missouri Democrat who would soon become speaker, declared during a House debate on the measure.
 「我期待美國國旗飄揚在英屬北美直到北極每一平方英尺的土地上，」密蘇里州民主黨眾議員錢普·克拉克在眾議院就該措施辯論時宣稱。他不久後就成了眾議院議長。
A backlash resulted in Canada. According to Mr. Bothwell, some Americans were stopped on the street and forced to sing “God Save the King.”
這在加拿大產生了強烈反彈。據博思韋爾說，一些美國人在街上被攔下，被逼著唱《天佑國王》。
Cold War politics produced more clashes. Canada fought alongside the United States in both world wars and joined the United States as a founding member of NATO. And President John F. Kennedy celebrated the relationship in a grandiloquent 1961 address to Canada’s parliament: “Those whom nature hath so joined together, let no man put asunder,” he declared.
冷戰政治產生了更多衝突。加拿大在兩次世界大戰中與美國並肩作戰，並作為創始成員與美國一起加入北約。1961年，約翰·甘迺迪總統在加拿大議會發表了一篇氣勢恢宏的演講，頌揚兩國關係：「自然使其結合的，人力不可使其分離，」他宣稱。
But Mr. Kennedy lashed out at Canada’s prime minister when he resisted the placement of certain U.S. nuclear weapons on Canadian soil that were intended to deter the Soviet Union, and when he hesitated to place Canada’s military on heightened alert during the Cuban Missile Crisis in 1962.
但加拿大總理抵制在加拿大領土上部署旨在威懾蘇聯的某些美國核武器，1962年古巴導彈危機期間，他又猶豫是否應讓加拿大軍隊進入高度戒備狀態，這兩次，甘迺迪都猛烈抨擊了這位總理。
An even greater shock came in the form of Richard Nixon’s 1971 move from the gold standard and imposition of 10 percent tariffs on all imports, including Canadian ones. While many countries were affected, none were quite so dependent on the U.S. economy as Canada.
更大的衝擊來自理查德·尼克森1971年放棄金本位，並對包括加拿大在內的所有進口商品徵收10%的關稅。雖然許多國家受到影響，但沒有哪個國家像加拿大那樣依賴美國經濟。
After a heated December 1971 meeting at the White House between Mr. Nixon and Prime Minister Pierre Trudeau, Mr. Nixon declared the Canadian a “pompous egghead” and a “son of a bitch.” Mr. Trudeau later responded that he had “been called worse things by better people.”
在1971年12月尼克森與皮埃爾·杜魯道總理在白宮的一次激烈會晤後，尼克森稱這位加拿大人是「自命不凡的書呆子」和「混蛋」。杜魯道後來回應說，他「曾被更優秀的人罵過更難聽的話」。
廣告
Mr. Nixon abandoned the plan months later. Mr. Sands said the economic disruption it created wound up driving support for greater free trade across the border, culminating in the 1994 NAFTA agreement. (Mr. Trump has derided that Clinton-era pact as a “nightmare,” and renegotiated it during his first term under the rubric of the United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement, or U.S.M.C.A.)
尼克森幾個月後放棄了該計劃。桑茲說，該計劃造成的經濟動盪最終推動了跨境自由貿易的支持，最終促成了1994年的北美自由貿易協定。（川普嘲笑這個柯林頓時代的協定是「噩夢」，並在第一個任期內以美墨加協定的名義對其進行重新談判。）
The shock of Mr. Nixon’s economic gambit also boosted Canadian mistrust of U.S. economic intentions. In 1973, the Canadian political thriller “Ultimatum” depicted an American president’s demand for free access to Canada’s vast oil and gas reserves.
尼克森經濟賭注帶來的衝擊也加劇了加拿大對美國經濟意圖的不信任。1973年，加拿大政治驚悚小說《最後通牒》(Ultimatum)描繪了一位美國總統要求自由獲取加拿大龐大石油和天然氣儲備的情節。
Canada’s prime minister resists, but the American president refuses to take no for an answer.
加拿大總理拒絕，但美國總統根本不肯接受拒絕。
“As of this moment, Canada will become part of the United States of America. The government of Canada is hereby dissolved,” he declares at its conclusion.
 「從此刻起，加拿大將成為美利堅合眾國的一部分。加拿大政府就此解散，」他在小說結尾宣稱。
“Ultimatum” became the year’s best-selling book in Canada.
《最後通牒》成為當年加拿大最暢銷的書。
Few expect Mr. Trump to go that far. Mr. Sands said Mr. Carney could still strike a deal to defuse the crisis. But he also warned that the dispute had unleashed forces that may take on a life of their own.
很少有人預計川普會走到那一步。桑茲說，卡尼仍可能達成協議以化解危機。但他也警告說，這場爭端已經釋放出一些力量，這些力量可能會發展出自己的生命力。
廣告
“Trump has pushed the nationalist buttons in Canada,” Mr. Sands said. “As long as Carney resists, it’s good for his politics.”
「川普按下了加拿大的民族主義按鈕，」桑茲說。「只要卡尼抵制，對他的政治前途就有好處。」
“The Canadians have really been shocked by this,” he added. To restore relations, he said, “we have to win the Canadian public back over — and that’s going to take a long time.”
「加拿大人確實對此感到震驚，」他還說。他說，要恢復關係，「我們必須重新贏得加拿大公眾的好感——而這需要很長時間。」

Michael Crowley是時報報導美國國務院和外交政策的記者。他曾在近三十個國家進行報導，並經常隨國務卿出訪。
