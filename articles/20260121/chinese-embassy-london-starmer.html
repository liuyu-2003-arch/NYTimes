英國批准中國在倫敦修建超大型使館

Britain Approves Contentious Chinese Mega-Embassy in London

MICHAEL D. SHEAR, STEPHEN CASTLE • 2026-01-21
The British government on Tuesday approved construction of a Chinese mega-embassy in the heart of London, rejecting the objections of critics who said the new building will make it easier for China to conduct spying operations.
英國政府於週二批准中國在倫敦市中心修建一座巨型大使館，駁回了批評人士的意見，後者認為這座新使館會為中國開展間諜活動提供便利。
The approval of the embassy, which comes after years of debate and delays, means China can move closer toward construction on a 5.5-acre site near the Tower of London. If it goes ahead, the new embassy will be China’s largest in Europe, although the plans are subject to numerous conditions and could yet be appealed in court.
歷經多年的爭論與擱置，使館修建計劃終獲批准，這意味著中國可著手推進倫敦塔附近這片約2.23公頃地塊的建設工作。若項目順利實施，這座新使館將成為中國在歐洲最大的使館，但該計劃仍需滿足諸多條件，且仍有可能遭到上訴。
The site was the home of Britain’s Royal Mint for more than 150 years. It was purchased by Beijing in 2018, when Boris Johnson was prime minister.
該地塊曾是英國皇家鑄幣廠所在地，歷時逾150年。2018年鮑里斯·強生擔任英國首相期間，中國收購了這一地塊。
Opponents of the new embassy have warned that the location is close to the City of London, the capital’s financial district, and next to fiber-optic cables that transport vast amounts of sensitive data. They argue that Chinese officials could use the cables to spy on companies, on dissidents living in exile or on ordinary Britons.
新使館的反對者警告稱，該地塊緊鄰倫敦金融城，且與傳輸海量敏感數據的光纖電纜相鄰，中國官員可能會利用這些電纜對企業、流亡異見人士及英國普通民眾實施監控。
廣告
A joint letter released on Tuesday by Ken McCallum, the director of MI5, the country’s domestic security service, and Anne Keast-Butler, the director of GCHQ, its intelligence and cybersecurity agency, said it that “as with any foreign embassy on U.K. soil, it is not realistic to expect to be able wholly to eliminate each and every potential risk.”
英國國內安全部門軍情五處局長肯·麥卡勒姆與情報及網路安全機構政府通信總部局長安妮·基斯特-巴特勒於週二聯合發布信函稱：「對於英國境內的任何外國使館，想要徹底消除其每一項潛在安全風險是不切實際的。」
But they said that work by the intelligence agencies and the government to create a “package of national security mitigations for the site” had been “expert, professional and proportionate.”
但兩人表示，情報機構與政府為該地塊制定了「國家安全風險緩解方案」，相關工作「專業、規範且恰當得體」。
They also noted that the new site would replace seven different Chinese diplomatic locations currently in use in London. “This consolidation should bring clear security advantages,” they wrote.
他們還指出，新使館建成後，將整合中國目前分散在倫敦各地的七處外交辦公場所，「這種整合理應帶來明確的安全利好。」
The decision angered China hawks, dissidents, local residents and some of Mr. Starmer’s own Labour Party lawmakers, all of whom campaigned for more than a year to stop its construction. Sarah Champion, a Labour lawmaker who has publicly opposed the embassy, wrote on social media that the decision was “deeply disappointing.”
這一決定激怒了對華強硬派、異見人士、當地居民，以及英國首相斯塔默所屬工黨內部的部分議員——這些群體已為阻止使館修建開展了逾一年的活動。公開反對該使館計劃的工黨議員薩拉·錢皮恩在社群媒體發文稱，這一決定「令人深感失望」。
The approval was formally announced by Steve Reed, the country’s housing minister. In a statement, he said that “all material considerations were taken into account when making this decision. The decision is now final unless it is successfully challenged in court.”
英國住房大臣史提夫·里德正式宣布了這一批准決定，他在聲明中表示，「做出該決定時，所有重要考量因素均已納入評估範圍。除非這一決定在法庭上被成功推翻，否則即為最終裁定。」
A legal fight appears likely, since opposition to the embassy has been building for several years. Protesters have declared their anger at the prospect, including by carrying signs and banners that read “CCP is Watching You. Stop the Mega Embassy!”
鑒於反對修建使館的呼聲已持續數年，相關司法訴訟似乎在所難免。抗議者們已對該計劃表達強烈不滿，有人舉著標語和橫幅走上街頭，上面寫著「中國共產黨正在監視你，制止巨型使館！」
廣告
Approval of the project was initially denied in 2022 by the borough council of Tower Hamlets, where the site is located. China later resubmitted the project for approval.
2022年，使館地塊所在的陶爾哈姆萊茨區地方議會曾首次駁回該項目的批准申請，中方此後重新提交了審批申請。
The announcement could be a diplomatic boost for the prime minister’s efforts to improve relations with China ahead of his first trip to the country while in office, which is expected to take place in the coming weeks. Mr. Starmer has said he wants to increase investment opportunities with China after years of icy relations between the two countries.
斯塔默首相預計將在未來數週內首次以首相身份訪華，此次使館獲批的消息或為其改善中英關係的外交努力帶來助力。斯塔默曾表示，在中英兩國關係歷經多年冷淡後，他希望擴大英方與中國的投資合作機會。
It also comes at a time when Britain, along with other European nations, is under extraordinary pressure from President Trump to submit to his desire to acquire Greenland, putting the trans-Atlantic alliance under strain.
與此同時，英國與其他歐洲國家都在承受著川普總統施加的巨大壓力——川普執意要求各國同意其收購格陵蘭島的訴求，讓跨大西洋同盟關係陷入緊張。
