脆弱的美中關係中最具破壞性的問題：對台軍售

One Issue That Could Upend Fragile U.S.-China Ties: Taiwan Arms

儲百亮, AMY CHANG CHIEN • 2026-09-22
For China’s leader, Xi Jinping, one important goal of his meeting with President Trump this week may be persuading him to keep delaying $14 billion in proposed U.S. arms sales to Taiwan.
對於中國領導人習近平來說，本週與川普總統會晤的一個重要目標可能是說服他繼續推遲擬議中對台灣的140億美元軍售。
China has long claimed the self-governed island of Taiwan as its territory, and Taipei has relied on American military power to counter military threats from Beijing. When they last met, Mr. Xi warned Mr. Trump that mishandling tensions over Taiwan could be “extremely dangerous” for the United States and China, a reminder of the risk of crisis between two nuclear-armed powers.
中國長期以來一直聲稱擁有台灣這個自治島嶼的主權，而台北則依靠美國的軍事力量來應對來自北京的軍事威脅。在兩人上次會晤時，習近平警告川普，若對台灣問題上的緊張局勢處理不當，可能對中美兩國來說「十分危險」，這提醒人們注意這兩個核武大國之間發生危機的風險。
“There’s only one issue that could cause relations between both sides to break down completely or erupt in conflict, and that is Taiwan,” said Xin Qiang, the director of the Center for Taiwan Studies at Fudan University in Shanghai.
「只有一個問題可能導致雙方關係徹底破裂或爆發衝突，那就是台灣問題，」上海復旦大學台灣研究中心主任信強說。
“Demanding a continued delay in any arms sales may be the most critical issue,” he said.
「要求繼續推遲軍售可能是最關鍵的問題，」他說。
廣告
Mr. Trump has described the arms sale plan as a source of leverage with China. After his May summit with Mr. Xi in Beijing, Mr. Trump said he had discussed the deal with the Chinese leader. “It’s a very good negotiating chip for us, frankly,” he said then.
川普曾將該軍售計劃描述為對華施壓的籌碼。在5月於北京與習近平舉行峰會後，川普表示，他已經與中國領導人討論了這筆交易。「坦率地說，這對我們來說是一個非常好的談判籌碼，」他當時說。
Mr. Trump has since held off approving the package of missiles, anti-drone weaponry and air-defense systems. His administration has also muted public references to Taiwan: Officials, including Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, have conspicuously avoided mentioning Taiwan in speeches.
此後，川普一直擱置批准包括導彈、反無人機武器和防空系統在內的一攬子計劃。他的政府也低調處理了公開提及台灣的言論：包括國防部長彼特·海格塞斯在內的官員在講話中都明顯避免提及台灣。
For Beijing, Mr. Trump’s willingness to delay arms sales is already a gain. In Washington, Mr. Xi could hint at potential costs if Mr. Trump approves the sale, including more restrictions on Chinese rare earth exports. Mr. Xi could also signal that he might skip the Group of 20 summit in Miami in December, which Mr. Trump will preside over.
對於北京來說，川普願意推遲軍售本身就已經是一個收穫。在華盛頓，習近平可能會暗示如果川普批准軍售將面臨的潛在代價，包括中國對稀土出口施加更多限制。習近平還可能發出信號，表示他可能不會出席將於12月在邁阿密舉行、由川普主持的二十國集團峰會。
Mr. Trump, for his part, may want China to increase pressure on Iran to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, the critical waterway for global oil and gas supplies that Iran has effectively blocked since the war began in February.
就川普而言，他可能希望中國加大對伊朗的壓力，以促使伊朗重新開放霍爾木茲海峽。這條對於全球油氣供應至關重要的水道自2月份戰爭爆發以來，實際上已被伊朗封鎖。
“I think in Beijing we saw the opening move in what will be a sophisticated play to get the Trump administration to reduce support for Taiwan,” said David Sacks, a fellow at the Council on Foreign Relations who studies U.S. relations with Taiwan and China. “Xi will use his meetings with Trump in Washington to continue to implement this strategy.”
「我認為，在北京，我們目睹了一場精心策劃的博弈的開局，其目的是促使川普政府減少對台灣的支持，」研究美國與台灣及中國大陸關係的外交關係委員會研究員戴維·薩克斯說。「習近平將利用在華盛頓與川普的會晤繼續實施這一戰略。」
Taiwan Wonders Where It Stands With Trump
台灣想知道自己在川普眼中的地位
廣告
Delayed arms sales are a political headache for Taiwan’s president, Lai Ching-te. He has promoted increased military spending and purchases of American weapons, in part to meet the Trump administration’s own demands that partners invest more in their defense. Mr. Trump’s stalling could make it harder for Mr. Lai to defend his agenda.
軍售的推遲對台灣總統賴清德來說是一個政治上的頭疼問題。他一直推動增加軍費開支和購買美國武器，部分是為了滿足川普政府提出的合作夥伴需增加自身國防投資的要求。川普的拖延可能會使賴清德更難捍衛其施政議程。
Longer delays could drag on the island’s military goals, partly by pushing Taiwan further back in the line for orders of scarce military hardware, some experts said.
一些專家表示，更長時間的拖延可能會拖累該島的軍事目標，部分原因是這會使台灣在稀缺軍事裝備的訂購排隊中進一步靠後。
“Ultimately, it’s a game of the Chinese trying to prolong this process as long as possible, with the idea being that if China can make gains faster than Taiwan can acquire capabilities, then the balance continues to tilt in Beijing’s favor,” said Ryan Hass, a former director for China at the National Security Council and a senior fellow at the Brookings Institution.
「歸根結底，這是中國人試圖盡可能延長這一進程的博弈，其思路是，如果中國取得進展的速度比台灣獲得武器能力的速度快，那麼天平就會繼續向有利於北京的方向傾斜，」前國家安全委員會中國事務主任、布魯金斯學會高級研究員何雷恩(Ryan Hass)說。
Some experts said they expected Mr. Trump to keep holding off approval of any arms sales for Taiwan until at least late this year. Others said the green light for some weapons could come sooner. Both Republicans and Democrats in Congress are likely to raise pressure on the administration to approve the proposed arms sales after the summit, Mr. Sacks said.
一些專家表示，他們預計川普至少要到今年晚些時候才會批准對台軍售。也有人表示，部分武器的批准可能會來得更早。薩克斯表示，在峰會之後，國會的共和黨人和民主黨人都可能加大對政府的壓力，要求其批準擬議的軍售。
The arms issue has highlighted Taiwan’s struggles to keep Mr. Trump onside. Taiwan does not have formal diplomatic relations with the United States, and Mr. Trump’s highly personalized, freewheeling style makes it more difficult for Taiwan to understand and shape U.S. decision-making, experts say.
軍售問題凸顯了台灣在爭取川普支持時所面臨的困境。專家表示，台灣與美國沒有正式外交關係，而川普高度個人化、隨心所欲的風格使得台灣更難理解和影響美國的決策。
“With an administration that is so top-down, I think that is hindering the Taiwanese ability to get a really good sense of what’s going on and the ability to help shape and influence that,” said Laura Rosenberger, a former Biden administration official who oversaw U.S. policies on China and Taiwan.
「面對這樣一個自上而下的政府，我認為這阻礙了台灣真正了解情況的能力，也阻礙了他們幫助塑造和影響決策的能力，」曾負責監督美國對華和對台政策的拜登政府官員勞拉·羅森伯格說。
In his first term, Mr. Trump signed off on about $18 billion of arms sales to Taiwan, and hawkish members of his government focused on Beijing as a long-term threat. But in his second term, Mr. Trump has emphasized pragmatic ties with China, largely retreating from a trade war after Beijing strangled exports of rare earths that are crucial for smartphones and advanced technology.
在第一任期內，川普批准了約180億美元的對台軍售，其政府中的鷹派成員將北京視為長期威脅。但在他的第二任期內，川普一直強調與中國建立務實的關係，在北京限制了對智慧型手機和先進技術至關重要的稀土出口之後，他在很大程度上從貿易戰中退卻了。
Taiwan has sought to maintain influence by cultivating Mr. Trump’s inner circle, hiring lobbyists and arranging visits to the island for prominent supporters of the president.
台灣一直試圖通過拉攏川普的親信、聘請遊說者以及安排總統的知名支持者訪問該島等方式力圖維持自身影響力。
But some Taiwanese officials who have traveled to the United States have also been told by Trump administration officials to curtail their visits to research groups in Washington, said Mr. Hass at the Brookings Institution.
但布魯金斯學會的何雷恩表示，一些前往美國的台灣官員也被川普政府的官員告知，要減少他們對華盛頓研究機構的訪問。
廣告
“All of this just betrays a clear preference by President Trump to ensure Taiwan does not become an obstacle to his ambitions as it relates to China,” he said.
「所有這一切都暴露出川普總統的明顯偏好，他希望確保台灣不會成為他實現與中國相關抱負的障礙，」他說。
Military and Trade Ties Are Steady
軍事和貿易關係保持穩定
Still, Taiwan’s security relationship with the United States remains robust. Officials and experts say working ties between government departments have remained steady, even deepened.
儘管如此，台灣與美國的安全關係依然穩固。官員和專家表示，政府部門之間的工作聯繫保持著穩定，甚至有所深化。
During Taiwan’s annual Han Kuang military exercises last month, American advisers were more visible than in past years. At a recent briefing with reporters, Taiwan’s defense minister, Wellington Koo, spoke coyly of growing numbers of “American English teachers,” a euphemism for the U.S. military advisers whose presence rankles Beijing.
在上個月台灣舉行的年度「漢光」軍事演習期間，美國顧問比往年更加引人注目。在最近一次記者會上，台灣國防部部長顧立雄含蓄地談到增多的「美語老師」——這是令北京感到惱火的美國軍事顧問的委婉說法。
“Our friends in the U.S. government understand how important continued arms sales are for Taiwan’s defense,” Chen Ming-chi, a deputy minister in Taiwan’s foreign ministry who handles relations with the United States, said in an interview.
「在美國政府裡的朋友明白繼續對台軍售對台灣國防有多重要，」負責處理對美關係的台灣外交部副部長陳明祺在接受採訪時表示。
Taiwan’s importance as an American trading partner has also grown sharply; this year it surpassed China as the third biggest source of U.S. imports, behind Mexico and Canada, according to U.S. figures. The bulk of those imports are semiconductors and other high-tech equipment sustaining the artificial intelligence boom in the United States. Taiwanese officials hope this growth will ultimately help entrench American support.
台灣作為美國貿易夥伴的重要性也急劇上升；根據美國的數據，今年台灣已經超過中國成為僅次於墨西哥和加拿大的美國第三大進口來源地。這些進口的大部分是維持美國人工智慧繁榮的半導體和其他高科技設備。台灣官員希望這種增長最終有助於鞏固美國的支持。
“We are probably the most cooperative partner in the whole world when it comes to tariffs and investment in the United States,” Mr. Chen said.
「我們大概是全世界最願意配合美國關稅和投資要求的夥伴，」陳明祺說。
Mr. Trump often complains about Taiwan’s dominance in making advanced semiconductors, raising his long-held, and widely debunked, accusation that the island pilfered chip-making know-how from the United States. “They got stolen from us and they were brought to Taiwan,” Mr. Trump said last month in a radio interview.
川普經常抱怨台灣在製造先進半導體方面的主導地位，重提了他長期以來所持，且已被廣泛駁斥的指控，即台灣竊取了美國的晶片製造技術。「他們從我們這裡偷走了技術，並把它們帶到了台灣，」川普上個月在一次廣播採訪中說。
But displacing Taiwan as a chief source of advanced chips is unlikely, even as Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company, or TSMC, the main global chip maker, expands production in the United States, experts say. TSMC has said it will spend $265 billion on its plants in Arizona. But Taiwan’s dense networks of suppliers and engineering talent remains difficult to replicate.
但專家表示，儘管全球主要晶片製造商台積電正在美國擴大生產，但取代台灣作為先進晶片主要來源地的可能性不大。台積電曾表示將斥資2650億美元建設其在亞利桑那州的工廠。但台灣密集的供應商網路和工程人才仍然難以複製。
廣告
“Building structural interdependence with our most critical ally provides the ultimate assurance for our national security,” said Jeremy Chih-Cheng Chang, the director of economic security studies at the Research Institute for Democracy, Society, and Emerging Technology in Taipei, which receives funding from the Taiwanese government.
「與我們最關鍵的盟友建立結構性的相互依存關係為我們的國家安全提供了最終保障，」由台灣政府資助的台北民主、社會與科技潛力研究中心經濟安保組組長張智程說。
“Presidents come and go," said Mr. Chang, “but Silicon Valley and Wall Street remain constant.”
「總統來來去去，」張智程說，「但矽谷與華爾街永遠都在。」

儲百亮(Chris Buckley)是《紐約時報》首席中國記者，自台北報導中國和台灣問題，重點關注政治、社會變革以及安全和軍事問題。

Amy Chang Chien是《紐約時報》記者/研究員，常駐台北，報導台灣和中國新聞。
