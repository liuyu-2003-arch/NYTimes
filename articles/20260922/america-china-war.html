美國再一次誤判中國的毀滅性代價

America Is Blundering Toward War With China

ROBERT D. KAPLAN • 2026-09-22
Before Iran, before Iraq, before Afghanistan there is no country that America has gotten as wrong as China. If this is mystifying, it is only because historical memories are short. America struggled through the previous fiascos. Getting it wrong again would be catastrophic. This is why it is crucial that President Trump strike the right balance in his summit this week with China’s leader, Xi Jinping.
在誤判伊朗、伊拉克和阿富汗之前，沒有哪個國家像中國這樣，讓美國產生如此嚴重的誤判。如果這令人費解，那僅僅是因為人們的歷史記憶太短暫。美國跌跌撞撞地熬過了一次次重大失策。若再次犯錯，將是災難性的。正因為如此，本週在與中國領導人習近平的峰會上，川普總統對分寸的拿捏至關重要。
During World War II, the United States backed China’s Chiang Kai-shek against the Japanese fascists who had invaded in the 1930s. The Roosevelt administration assumed that China under Chiang’s Nationalists would constitute one of the Big Four powers after World War II, along with the United States, Britain and the Soviet Union. When the Chinese Communists under Mao Zedong swept to victory in the 1946-1949 civil war, Washington was shocked that a movement it had barely acknowledged had toppled the beloved Chiang. It was also enraged that the Soviet Union and China were now both in Communist hands. Someone had to be held to account.
第二次世界大戰期間，美國支持中國的蔣介石對抗20世紀30年代入侵的日本法西斯。羅斯福政府設想，由蔣介石的國民黨領導的中國將在二戰後與美國、英國和蘇聯並列成為「四大國」之一。當毛澤東領導下的中國共產黨在1946至1949年的內戰中取得全面勝利時，華盛頓大驚失色——一個它幾乎未曾正眼相看的運動竟然推翻了備受推崇的蔣介石。同時，華盛頓也對中蘇兩國雙雙落入共產黨之手感到憤怒。必須有人為此承擔責任。
Thus was born the “who lost China?” debate, a critical element of the Red Scare of the 1950s. The State Department officials held to account were precisely the ones who during World War II had been warning, to no avail, that the Communists were likely to militarily triumph. Rather than being celebrated for their clairvoyance, these officials were denounced as traitors whose weak support for the Nationalists had led to Chiang’s fall. The idea that the State Department could have prevented a Communist takeover across a landscape of 550 million people — 37 times the population of South Vietnam during our war there — gravely misjudged the limits of American power.
「誰弄丟了中國？」的爭論由此誕生，並成為20世紀50年代「紅色恐慌」的關鍵組成部分。被追究責任的國務院官員恰恰是那些在二戰期間一直警告（儘管徒勞無功）共產黨可能在軍事上獲勝的人。這些官員未能因其先見之明而受到讚賞，反而被斥為叛徒，指責他們對國民黨支持不力導致了蔣介石的倒台。這種認為國務院本可以在一片5.5億人口的廣袤土地上阻止共產黨奪取政權的想法是對美國實力的嚴重誤判。5.5億人是越南戰爭期間南越人口的37倍。
The Foreign Service officers themselves weren’t innocent. Though accurate about Mao’s chances, they misunderstood the nature of his rule. They conceived of Mao’s Communists as benign compared with Chiang’s Nationalists. Based in the Nationalist wartime capital of Chongqing, they knew intimately Chiang’s authoritarian tendencies. They made only short visits to the Communist base camp in Yan’an, visits that were stage-managed by Mao and his propagandist Zhou Enlai. Impressed by the order and discipline of Yan’an, they were blind to Mao’s totalitarianism, which would eventually result in the murder and starvation of as many as 70 million Chinese.
外交人員本身也並非毫無過錯。雖然準確判斷了毛澤東獲勝的機率，但他們誤解了其統治的本質。他們認為，相比蔣介石的國民黨，毛澤東的共產黨並無威脅。由於駐紮在國民黨的戰時首都重慶，他們對蔣介石的獨裁傾向了如指掌。而他們對共產黨的延安大本營僅僅進行了短暫訪問，這些訪問還是由毛澤東及其宣傳家周恩來精心安排的。延安的秩序與紀律給他們留下了深刻印象，卻讓他們對毛澤東的極權主義視而不見，而這種極權主義最終導致了多達7000萬中國人遭受殺戮與饑荒。
廣告
The 1950s purge of State Department specialists actually lasted into the 1960s. The Vietnam War was encouraged by Europeanists and generalists because the China hands who would have warned against the dangers of guerrilla war in Asia, and who would have noted that Vietnam had never been a puppet of Communist China, were no longer in key positions in government. There is a direct historical link, through China, from the Red Scare of the early 1950s to the fiasco in Vietnam in the middle and late 1960s.
20世紀50年代對國務院專家的清洗實際上一直延續到了60年代。越南戰爭受到了歐洲事務專家和通才們的鼓動，因為「中國通」們已不再擔任政府要職，他們本會警告在亞洲陷入游擊戰的危險，並會指出越南從來不是共產主義中國傀儡。從20世紀50年代初的紅色恐慌到60年代中後期的越南慘敗，其間存在著一條因中國而相連的直接歷史脈絡。
The diplomatic opening to China accomplished by President Richard Nixon and his national security adviser, Henry Kissinger, in 1972 finally ended the Who Lost China debate. China was no longer lost and had instead become a geopolitical chess piece to be played against the Soviet Union. Nixon’s and Kissinger’s conception held until 1989, when the combination of China’s brutal crackdown at Tiananmen Square and the end of the Cold War meant that China was now both reviled, and, with the demise of Communism in Europe, less of an asset.
理查德·尼克森總統及其國家安全顧問亨利·季辛吉在1972年實現的對華外交破冰終結了「誰弄丟了中國」的爭論。中國不再是被「弄丟的」，而是變成了地緣政治棋盤上用來制衡蘇聯的一枚棋子。尼克森和季辛吉的這一構想一直維持到1989年，當時中國在天安門廣場的殘酷鎮壓與冷戰結束的雙重因素意味著，中國不僅遭到唾棄，而且隨著歐洲共產主義的終結，其作為一項資產的價值也大打折扣。
It was China’s economic and technological flourishing, particularly in the 1990s, that required the United States to treat the country as a great power. So dynamic were Jiang Zemin and his deputy Zhu Rongji that both Democrats and Republicans assumed that if China were admitted to the World Trade Organization, the country would be on a path toward a capitalistic and liberal society. President Bill Clinton held this view — and he was far from alone.
而使美國必須將中國作為大國來對待的是中國的經濟與技術繁榮，尤其是在20世紀90年代。江澤民和他的副手朱鎔基展現出了極大魄力，以至於民主黨和共和黨都認為，如果允許中國加入世界貿易組織(WTO)，它將走上一條通往資本主義和自由社會的道路。比爾·柯林頓總統持有這種觀點——但絕非只有他一人。
That was Washington’s third misjudgment. The Washington establishment — the wind at its back following the triumphalist end to the Cold War — assumed China would follow Europe’s path toward freedom. Alas, China was not Europe. Admitted to the W.T.O., China would eventually turn back toward full-bore authoritarianism under Xi Jinping.
這是華盛頓的第三次誤判。乘著冷戰勝利之勢，華盛頓建制派理所當然地認為中國會效仿歐洲走向自由之路。遺憾的是，中國不是歐洲。被接納加入世貿組織後，中國最終在習近平治下重新轉向了全面的威權主義。
Each of these mistakes has particular causes. They are also connected by China’s size, complexity and outright cultural difference. Only recently, thanks to pathbreaking scholarship by the Hoover Institution academic Frank Dikötter, have we learned the full story of just how much Stalin was aiding Mao during the civil war, making a Nationalist victory even more remote. Analysis of China, unlike that of the Middle East, is not distorted by intense partisanship, but even for an emotionally detached expert class, China is genuinely hard for the United States to fathom.
這些錯誤中的每一個都有其具體原因。它們也因中國的規模、複雜性以及徹底的文化差異而交織在一起。直到最近，依靠胡佛研究所學者馮客(Frank Dikötter)開創性的學術研究，我們才充分了解到史達林在內戰期間為毛澤東提供了多少援助，這使得國民黨獲勝的希望更加渺茫。與對中東的分析不同，對中國的分析並未被強烈的黨派偏見所扭曲，但即使對於一個情感抽離的專家階層來說，中國對美國而言也確實難以看透。
What stabilizes the bilateral relationship has been the deliberately vague understanding concerning Taiwan that emerged from Nixon’s visit, known as the Shanghai Communiqué. That formula signaled that there was only one China and its capital is Beijing, but that neither party would attempt violently to change the status quo on Taiwan.
雙方關係的穩定一直靠的是尼克森訪華後達成的一項關於台灣的刻意模糊的諒解，即《上海公報》。該方案明確世界上只有一個中國，其首都在北京，但任何一方都不會試圖通過暴力改變台灣的現狀。
廣告
For the first time since 1972, the Shanghai Communiqué may be in particularly serious danger. There have been slip-ups, as when President Joe Biden declared he would defend Taiwan, thus abandoning the Nixon formula of ambiguity. But generally presidents exercised rhetorical restraint.
自1972年以來，《上海公報》可能首次處於極其嚴重的危險之中。儘管曾經出現過失誤，比如喬·拜登總統曾宣布他將保衛台灣，從而放棄了尼克森的模糊方案。但總體而言，歷任總統在言辭上都保持了剋制。
That is no longer the case in the Trump administration, with the president’s stated position on Taiwan and the Asia-Pacific forever volatile. As for China, it is no longer governed by a mandarinate of enlightened Communist leaders, but by Mr. Xi, a Leninist totalitarian with openly aggressive designs on Taiwan. The guideposts are wobbling. Despite the happy talk at summits, we are back to the Who Lost China era, when fundamental mistakes are possible, this time with incalculable consequences. In addition, because neither China nor the United States seems interested in cooperating to regulate A.I., competition over the technology is poised to further destabilize the relationship.
川普政府時期情況已不再如此，總統在台灣和亞太問題上的公開立場總是變幻莫測。至於中國，它不再由開明的共產主義領導人組成的官僚階層統治，而是由習近平這個對台灣抱有公開侵略意圖的列寧主義極權統治者來統治。規則的路標正在動搖。儘管峰會上充滿了樂觀言辭，我們卻又回到了「誰弄丟了中國」的時代，在這個時代可能犯下根本性錯誤，而這一次將帶來無法估量的後果。此外，由於中美兩國似乎都無意合作監管人工智慧，圍繞該技術的競爭勢必將進一步破壞兩國關係的穩定。
The ultimate geopolitical catastrophe would be a military conflict over Taiwan and the South China Sea. Avoiding such a conflict is not merely a matter of reducing military tensions. As the Yale Sinologist Odd Arne Westad points out, it was Germany’s assumption that Britain might not come to the aid of its allies in 1914 that helped set off World War I. If China assumes the United States might not come to the aid of Taiwan, that might likewise increase the chances of war.
終極的地緣政治災難將是圍繞台灣和南海爆發軍事衝突。避免這種衝突不僅僅是緩解軍事緊張局勢的問題。正如耶魯大學漢學家文安立(Odd Arne Westad)所指出的，正是德國在1914年認為英國可能不會援助其盟友的假設助推了第一次世界大戰的爆發。如果中國假設美國可能不會援助台灣，那同樣可能增加戰爭的機率。
Wars in Ukraine, Gaza and Iran have led to severe differences of opinion among both the public and the elites, but have not fundamentally changed our lives. A high-end military conflict in the Western Pacific fought between the world’s largest economies would disrupt the most vital supply chains and change our world profoundly. The comparison with World War I is not an exaggeration.
烏克蘭、加薩和伊朗的戰爭導致公眾與精英之間產生了嚴重分歧，但並未從根本上改變我們的生活。如果世界兩大經濟體在西太平洋爆發高烈度軍事衝突，將破壞最至關重要的供應鏈，並深刻地改變我們的世界。將其與第一次世界大戰相提並論毫不誇張。
Who Lost China was born of an obsession and a misunderstanding. In the late 1940s, the obsession was with Communism, which replaced German Nazism and Japanese fascism as America’s principal enemy the moment World War II ended. Then, the United States thought it had the power to save Chiang’s weakened regime in a war fought over thousands of miles of territory.
「誰弄丟了中國」的爭論源於一種痴迷和誤解。在20世紀40年代末，這種痴迷是針對共產主義的，從二戰結束那一刻起，共產主義便取代了德國納粹主義和日本法西斯主義成為美國的主要敵人。當時，美國認為自己有能力在一場跨越數千公里領土的戰爭中拯救蔣介石被削弱的政權。
The obsession today is again with Communism, in the shape of China overtaking the United States as the world’s greatest military, economic and artificial intelligence power. The misunderstanding is that a softer, less resolute approach to defending Taiwan and our other Asian allies can somehow prevent a war. In reality, such an approach might embolden China to attack the island, leaving the United States in a hair-trigger crisis over the fate of the entire Pacific.
今天的痴迷再次針對共產主義，表現為擔憂中國超越美國，成為世界上最強大的軍事、經濟和人工智慧強國。一種誤解認為，在保衛台灣和其他亞洲盟友方面採取較為溫和、不那麼強硬的策略就能避免戰爭。在現實中，這種做法可能會助長中國攻擊台灣的信心，使美國陷入一場一觸即發的、關乎整個太平洋命運的危機。
廣告
The most important element in preventing a great war is policy discipline. Boring but true, this leads to steady, predictable signaling. America should not want to confuse China, just as China should not want to confuse us. World War I was in part caused by a misreading of intentions. Mr. Trump, by his vacillation — including using military aid to Taiwan as a bargaining chip with China and toying with the abandonment of Asia to a Chinese sphere of influence — irresponsibly encourages the kind of confusion that can lead to war.
預防大國戰爭最重要的因素是政策紀律性。這聽起來枯燥卻是事實，它能帶來穩定且可預測的信號傳遞。美國不應試圖去迷惑中國，正如中國也不應試圖去迷惑我們一樣。第一次世界大戰的部分原因就是對意圖的誤判。川普通過他的搖擺不定——包括將對台灣的軍事援助作為與中國討價還價的籌碼，以及戲謔般地考慮將亞洲讓給中國的勢力範圍——不負責任地助長了那種可能引發戰爭的困惑。
The historian Barbara Tuchman followed her classic book about the outbreak of World War I in 1966 with “The Proud Tower,” about the glittering, cosmopolitan world of 19th-century Europe, and how its texture and brilliance and naïveté were lost because of the Great War. The theme Tuchman chose for her book was taken from an 1845 Edgar Allan Poe poem: “While from a proud tower in the town/Death looks gigantically down.”
歷史學家芭芭拉·塔奇曼在1966年出版了關於一戰爆發的經典著作後，又創作了《高塔》(The Proud Tower)。這本書講述了19世紀那個光輝燦爛、心繫天下的歐洲，以及它的肌理、輝煌和天真是如何因那場「大戰」而喪失的。塔奇曼為她的書選擇的主題取自埃德加·愛倫·坡1845年的一首詩：「而在城中的一座高塔之上，死神正巍然地朝下眺望。」
No geopolitical conflict in the world today has the capacity, if miscalculated, of bringing ruin upon us all like the one between the United States and China. China tore through our domestic politics three-quarters of a century ago. It could be worse this time.
當今世界上，沒有任何地緣政治衝突像美中之間的衝突這樣，一旦出現誤判，將給我們所有人帶來毀滅。四分之三個世紀前，中國撕裂了我們的國內政治。這一次情況可能會更糟。

Robert D. Kaplan是《中國耳語者：塑造美國對其主要地緣政治對手看法的聲音》(「China Whisperers: The Voices That Have Shaped America’s Views of Its Chief Geopolitical Rival」)一書的作者，也是德克薩斯大學奧斯汀分校的傑出高級講師。
