張又俠被開除黨籍，將面臨審判

Ex-Commander of China’s Military Accused of Corruption and Expelled From Party

儲百亮 • 2026-09-22
China’s Communist Party leaders expelled the country’s former top general, Zhang Youxia, from the party on Monday, accusing him of crimes and misdeeds ranging from taking bribes to disloyalty to the party.
週一，中國共產黨領導人將該國前最高將領張又俠開除黨籍，指控他犯下從受賄到對黨不忠等一系列罪行和不當行為。
The announcement by the Politburo, a council of 21 top officials, said that the accusations against Mr. Zhang and his former subordinate, Liu Zhenli — who was also expelled from the party — would be handed to military prosecutors. That step makes the prosecution of the two fallen generals almost inevitable.
由21名高級官員組成的政治局在公告中表示，對張又俠及其同樣被開除黨籍的前下屬劉振立的指控將移交軍事檢察機關。此舉意味著這兩名落馬將領幾乎必然面臨起訴。
Their expulsion “opens the way to a military trial which will undoubtedly impose a harsh sentence,” said Drew Thompson, a former Pentagon official who in 2012 helped host Gen. Zhang on a visit to the United States.
將他們開除黨籍「為軍事審判鋪平了道路，毫無疑問他們將被判處重刑」，曾在2012年協助接待張又俠訪美的前五角大廈官員德魯·湯普森說。
The abrupt removal of the generals from their commands in January was one of the biggest political shocks in China in years. It touched off speculation about why Mr. Zhang had fallen out with the country’s top leader, Xi Jinping, who had promoted him to become the top uniformed commander of the People’s Liberation Army and had kept him in office beyond the customary retirement age.
今年1月，這些將領被突然被免去指揮職務，這是近年來中國最大的政治衝擊之一。這令外界猜測張又俠與中國最高領導人習近平之間關係為何破裂。習近平曾提拔張又俠成為中國人民解放軍最高級別的現役指揮官，並讓他打破慣例，在超過退休年齡後繼續留任。
廣告
Investigators found that Mr. Zhang and Mr. Liu had abused their positions to help others gain promotions, taken “exceptionally large” bribes, and failed to rein in the misconduct of relatives and subordinates, said the statement from the Politburo meeting, which was issued through the Xinhua news agency.
通過新華社發布的政治局會議聲明稱，調查人員發現，張又俠和劉振立濫用職權，幫助他人謀取職務晉陞，收受「金額特別巨大」的賄賂，且未能約束親屬和下屬的不當行為。
But the statement also pointed to the political reasons for their downfall. It accused them of betraying the party and, by implication, Mr. Xi, and forming their own cabal. They had “fallen away from the party in heart and mind” and had “seriously violated political discipline and political norms, engaged in cliques, and were disloyal and dishonest toward the party,” it said.
但該聲明也指出了他們倒台的政治原因。聲明指責他們背叛了黨——言下之意也背叛了習近平，並拉幫結派。聲明稱他們「與黨離心離德」，並且「嚴重違反政治紀律和政治規矩，搞團團伙伙，對黨不忠誠不老實」。
The wording of the official accusations suggested that both men were accused of trying to amass influence and followers in the military, partly by promoting their own minions, said James Char, an assistant professor at Nanyang Technological University in Singapore who studies the Chinese military. Mr. Zhang had been all but certain to retire at the next Communist Party congress late next year.
新加坡南洋理工大學研究中國軍隊的助理教授詹姆斯·夏爾表示，官方指控的措辭表明，兩人都被指控試圖在軍隊中培植影響力和追隨者，部分手段是提拔自己的親信。張又俠原本幾乎肯定會在明年年底的下屆中共黨代會上退休。
“It’s clearly a suggestion that there was sort of faction forming going on behind the scenes,” he said in a telephone interview, “that they were trying to make sure that they could retain some level of influence even after retirement by having their protégés put in charge of important positions.”
「這清楚地表明幕後存在某種結黨營私的行為，」他在接受電話採訪時說，「他們試圖讓自己的門生擔任重要職務，確保即使在退休後也能保持一定程度的影響力。」
The Politburo’s public statement did not provide details of the accusations leveled against the men. The wide-ranging allegations, however, indicated how deeply Mr. Xi had lost confidence in many members of the military command, right up to the top.
政治局的公開聲明並未提供針對兩人指控的具體細節。然而，這些廣泛的指控表明，習近平對軍隊指揮層的許多成員已極度失去信任，甚至直到最高層。
Mr. Xi and Mr. Zhang, 76, had once been allies, bonded by their shared upbringing as the sons of senior officials. Mr. Xi turned to the commander to help push through a difficult, and contentious, overhaul of the military.
習近平與76歲的張又俠曾是盟友，兩人因同為中共高幹子弟的成長背景而結下交情。習近平曾倚重這位將領來協助推行一項艱難且充滿爭議的軍隊改革。
廣告
The simultaneous removal of the two generals early this year was the culmination of a series of purges across the People’s Liberation Army that has left dozens of command posts still officially empty.
今年早些時候，這兩名將軍被同時免職，將涉及整個中國人民解放軍的一系列清洗推向了高潮。該行動導致在官方記錄中目前仍有數十個指揮崗位處於空缺狀態。
Beginning with a spreading scandal in the P.L.A. Rocket Force in 2023, Mr. Xi appeared to conclude that his previous efforts to clean up the military had failed. By earlier this year, more than 100 senior officers, including some retired ones, had been put under investigation or had simply disappeared — presumably detained — according to an analysis published by the Center for Strategic and International Studies.
從2023年中國人民解放軍火箭軍爆發不斷蔓延的醜聞開始，習近平似乎得出結論，他之前清理軍隊的努力失敗了。根據戰略與國際問題研究中心發表的分析，到今年早些時候，已有100多名高級軍官（包括一些退役軍官）被調查或直接失蹤——據推測已被拘留。
The Central Military Commission, through which the Communist Party controls the military, had seven members in 2022, including Mr. Xi as chairman and General Zhang as his most senior deputy. Today, there are only two members: Mr. Xi and Gen. Zhang Shengmin, the commander who has overseen the sweeping investigations and purges.
共產黨用來控制軍隊的中央軍事委員會在2022年有七名委員，包括擔任主席的習近平和擔任其最高級別副手的張又俠。如今，該委員會只剩下兩名成員：習近平和負責監督此次全面調查和清洗行動的張升民將軍。
So far, Mr. Xi has moved slowly to replace some purged commanders. Throughout this year, military officers have been undergoing indoctrination sessions intended to reinforce their discipline and loyalty to the party and Mr. Xi.
到目前為止，習近平在物色被清洗將領的接替人選方面動作緩慢。貫穿今年全年，軍官們一直在接受思想教育，旨在強化紀律性以及對黨和習近平的忠誠。
Many foreign experts believe that the removals of the commanders, plus the accompanying disruption, have damaged the Chinese military’s readiness for major operations, including against Taiwan, the island-democracy that rejects Beijing’s claim of sovereignty over it.
許多外國專家認為，指揮官的免職以及隨之而來的動盪損害了中國軍隊執行重大行動的戰備狀態，包括針對台灣的行動——這個實行民主制度的島嶼拒絕北京對其主權的主張。
But a page-length commentary in the Chinese military’s main newspaper this month argued that the drive for political loyalty and reliability would ultimately make the People’s Liberation Army better on the battlefield.
但本月中國軍隊主要報紙上發表的一篇整版評論文章認為，追求政治上的忠誠和可靠最終將使中國人民解放軍在戰場上更加出色。
廣告
“Looking back at the stormy years of war, how was our military able to vanquish powerful enemies despite its poor equipment and harsh, perilous conditions?” said the commentary in the Liberation Army Daily. “That was due not just to the courage to fight, but also thanks to unwavering loyalty to the party.”
「回望烽火歲月，為什麼我軍能夠在裝備落後、條件艱苦的險境之中屢克強敵？不是單憑鬥爭的勇氣，而是源於鐵心向黨的政治忠誠，」《解放軍報》的這篇評論文章寫道。

儲百亮(Chris Buckley)是《紐約時報》首席中國記者，自台北報導中國和台灣問題，重點關注政治、社會變革以及安全和軍事問題。
