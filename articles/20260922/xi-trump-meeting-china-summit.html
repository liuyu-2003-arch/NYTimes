習近平如何看待「習川會」：佔據政治優勢、底氣更足

With Time on His Side, Xi Seeks to Extend Truce With Trump

DAVID PIERSON • 2026-09-22
When China’s top leader, Xi Jinping, and President Trump meet in Washington this week, the two men will be operating on vastly different clocks.
本週，中國最高領導人習近平和川普總統在華盛頓會面時，兩人面對的政治時鐘將大不相同。
The American president is constrained by the immediate demands of a midterm election in November and a grinding war. The Chinese leader is gearing up for a norm-shattering fourth term as head of the ruling Communist Party, that will keep him in power for another five years.
美國總統受到11月期中選舉迫在眉睫的政治壓力以及一場曠日持久的戰爭的牽制。中國領導人則正準備開啟他打破常規的第四個中共中央總書記任期，這將使他繼續執政五年。
For Mr. Xi, the disparity in political realities gives him an edge in maintaining the truce that the two countries reached last year. A renewed trade fight would hit American consumers right before the election — a risk Mr. Trump can ill afford. China’s economy is struggling too, but Mr. Xi answers to no electorate, giving him the patience to prolong the détente on his terms.
對習近平來說，兩人在政治現實中的差異讓他在維持去年達成的休戰局面時佔據優勢。如果貿易戰再起，就會在選舉前打擊美國消費者，這是川普難以承受的風險。中國經濟同樣陷入困境，但習近平無需對選民負責，這讓他有足夠的耐心按照自己的節奏，將這段緩和局面盡可能延長。
“Each of Xi’s face-to-face encounters with Trump in his second term would only have reinforced Xi’s prior conviction that Trump is leading an America that is dangerous but declining,” said Jonathan Czin, a researcher at the Brookings Institution who previously worked at the C.I.A. analyzing Chinese politics.
「在川普第二個任期內，習近平每一次與他碰面可能都只會進一步強化習近平此前的判斷，即川普領導下的美國雖然危險，卻正在衰落，」曾在中央情報局從事中國政治分析工作、現為布魯金斯學會研究員的秦江南(Jonathan Czin)說。
廣告
Mr. Xi, who will sit down for talks with Mr. Trump on Thursday, may be wagering that leaning into Mr. Trump’s preference for leader-to-leader diplomacy can insulate the relationship from flash points like the status of Taiwan, the self-governed island claimed by Beijing, and the competition to dominate artificial intelligence.
習近平將在週四與川普舉行會談，他的盤算或許是：順應川普偏好的「領導人對領導人」外交模式或許能讓兩國關係免受一些敏感議題的衝擊，比如台灣的地位問題（北京宣稱對這個自治島嶼擁有主權），以及雙方在人工智慧領域爭奪主導地位的競爭。
Keeping Mr. Trump at bay would give Mr. Xi breathing room to work on rejuvenating his listless economy, which has been weakened by a property crisis, high youth unemployment and excess production. It would give him time to rebuild the upper echelons of his military leadership, which he has hollowed out in a drive against corruption and disloyalty. And it would allow him to continue expanding his country’s industrial, technological and military strength for a future showdown with the United States.
讓川普暫時不來找麻煩將給中國領導人喘息空間，著手提提振因房地產危機、青年失業率高企和產能過剩而陷入疲軟的經濟。這也將給他時間重建軍隊高層，此前他在一場反腐敗、反不忠行動中對軍隊高層進行了大清洗。同時，這還能讓他繼續增強中國在工業、技術和軍事上的實力，為未來與美國的對抗做準備。
Analysts say China benefits every time Mr. Trump’s attention drifts elsewhere. By quarreling with traditional American allies or sinking deeper into the war in Iran, Mr. Trump is weakening his country's global standing and pulling troops away from the Pacific.
分析人士說，每當川普的注意力轉向其他地方，中國都會從中受益。無論是與美國的傳統盟友發生爭執，還是在伊朗戰爭中越陷越深，川普都在削弱美國的全球地位，並將軍力從太平洋地區撤離。
“Xi would love to tell Trump, ‘Go on, stay the course,’” said Suisheng Zhao, executive director of the China Center at the University of Denver. “For Xi, Trump represents a window of opportunity for China, so his priority is to buy more time.”
「習近平巴不得對川普說，『繼續吧，照這個路子走下去，』」丹佛大學中國中心主任趙穗生說。「對習近平來說，川普給中國帶來了一個機會窗口，所以他的首要任務就是爭取更多時間。」
An Emboldened Xi Heads to Washington
習近平更有底氣地前往華盛頓
Since first meeting Mr. Trump at Mar-a-Lago more than nine years ago — a gathering best remembered for being when Mr. Xi learned about U.S. missile strikes on Syria while dining on what Mr. Trump described as the “most beautiful chocolate cake” — Mr. Xi has had an extraordinary amount of time for a Chinese leader to size up his chief geopolitical rival.
自九年多前在馬阿拉歌莊園首次與川普會面以來——那次會晤最令人記憶猶新的一幕是，習近平在品嘗川普口中「最美味的巧克力蛋糕」時得知了美國對敘利亞實施導彈打擊的消息，習近平擁有了一段對中國領導人而言異乎尋常的漫長時間，可以觀察和判斷自己的主要地緣政治對手。
Early in Mr. Trump’s first term, Mr. Xi was somewhat accommodating toward the American president. In 2018, his government agreed to purchase $200 billion in additional U.S. goods and services to offset a trade imbalance (though Beijing never fully honored that deal.)
在川普第一個任期之初，習近平對這位美國總統在一定程度上採取了遷就姿態。2018年，中國政府同意額外購買2000億美元的美國商品與服務，以緩解貿易失衡（儘管北京從未完全兌現這一承諾）。
Last year, Mr. Xi flipped the dynamic between the two superpowers.
去年，習近平扭轉了兩個超級大國之間的態勢。
Faced with Mr. Trump’s Liberation Day tariffs, Mr. Xi was the only world leader who refused to back down, choosing instead to match his opponent with one sky-high tariff after another. Mr. Xi then reached for his ace card by throttling exports of Chinese critical minerals, or rare earths, which companies globally need for modern technologies. Backed into a corner, the Trump administration called for a truce.
面對川普的「解放日」關稅，習近平是唯一拒絕退讓的世界領導人，轉而選擇一輪又一輪高額關稅針鋒相對。隨後，習近平祭出了殺手鐧，限制中國關鍵礦產（即稀土）的出口——全球企業製造現代科技產品都離不開這些資源。被逼到困境的川普政府於是呼籲休戰。
廣告
It was a payoff years in the making: a rare instance in which Beijing not only withstood U.S. pressure but turned the tables and forced it to relent. Beijing, as a result, is emboldened today and sees itself more as an equal to Washington, analysts say.
這是多年布局終於得到的回報：北京不僅頂住了美國的壓力，而且扭轉了局面，迫使其讓步，這是罕見的。分析人士指出，這讓今天的北京底氣更足，也愈發將自己視為與華盛頓平起平坐的一方。
The Trump administration “thought they could use massive unilateral pressure to force China to make concessions. And that did not occur. In fact, the opposite did,” said Scott Kennedy, a researcher at the Center for Strategic and International Studies in Washington.
川普政府「原以為可以通過巨大的單邊施壓迫使中國讓步。但事情並沒有這樣發展。事實上，結果恰恰相反」，華盛頓戰略與國際問題研究中心研究員甘思德(Scott Kennedy)說。
“I think now this is Xi Jinping’s world and we’re all living in it,” he added.
「我認為，如今這個世界已經是習近平的世界了，我們不過都生活在其中，」他補充道。
An Unstable Truce
並不穩固的休戰
The superpowers should put limits on competition, Mr. Xi’s argument goes, to avoid what he has warned could be a catastrophic clash. To this end, he has pushed a formula he calls “constructive strategic stability.”
按照習近平的主張，兩個超級大國應當為競爭設定邊界，以避免他警告過可能發生的那種災難性衝突。為此，他提出了一套稱為「建設性戰略穩定」的方案。
In practice, what this means is that Beijing wants Washington to accept boundaries on where it can challenge China.
落實到實際層面，這意味著北京希望華盛頓接受一定的邊界，明確它在哪些地方不可以挑戰中國。
In July, for example, when the U.S. Federal Communications Commission proposed a ban on Chinese-made humanoid robots, China’s Ministry of Commerce invoked the phrase, demanding that Washington roll back the restrictions to preserve “strategic stability.”
例如，今年7月，當美國聯邦通信委員會提議禁止中國製造的人形機器人時，中國商務部就搬出了這一說法，要求華盛頓撤銷限制，以維護「戰略穩定」。
American analysts view the slogan not as a serious effort at ironing out differences but as an attempt to get Washington to recognize China’s clout and back off. China could apply it to any new U.S. restrictions on chip exports or arms sales to Taiwan.
美國分析人士認為，這一口號並非真正試圖彌合分歧，而是要讓華盛頓承認中國的實力並作出退讓。對於美國今後出台的任何晶片出口限制或對台軍售，中國都可能套用這一說法。
“The truce is not constructive because little diplomatic infrastructure is being built,” Mr. Czin of the Brookings Institution said. “It is tactical rather than strategic. And therefore it is inherently unstable.”
「這種休戰並不具有建設性，因為雙方几乎沒有建立起什麼外交機制，」布魯金斯學會的秦江南說。「它是戰術性的，而非戰略性的。因此，它在本質上並不穩定。」
廣告
Summitry is the Strategy
峰會外交是一種策略
Mr. Xi appears to have calculated that the best way to manage the United States is to engage Mr. Trump in high-profile summits rich in optics but thin in substance.
習近平似乎已經認定，應對美國的最佳方式就是與川普舉行一場場備受矚目的峰會——場面十足，實質內容卻不多。
The planned meeting on Thursday will be their third face-to-face in less than a year, a pace that could continue with talks in November at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit in Shenzhen, China, and at the Group of 20 summit in Miami in December.
計劃於週四舉行的會晤將是他們在不到一年內第三次面對面會晤。這一節奏可能還會延續：11月在深圳舉行的亞太經合組織峰會，以及12月在邁阿密舉行的二十國集團峰會，他們都可能再度會晤。
“Head-of-state diplomacy is the cornerstone of China-U.S. relations,” China’s top diplomat, Wang Yi, told Secretary of State Marco Rubio in a call on Thursday.
「元首外交是中美關係的定盤星，」中國最高外交官王毅週四與美國國務卿魯比奧通話時表示。
But beyond extending the trade truce, the meetings have produced few breakthroughs. China may agree to more agricultural purchases this week, but expectations are low on issues like A.I. safety or China’s trade imbalances.
但除了延長貿易休戰之外，這些會晤幾乎沒有產生什麼突破。中國本週可能同意增加農產品採購，但在人工智慧安全或中美貿易失衡等問題上，外界的預期普遍不高。
That is by design, analysts say. Enticing Mr. Trump with the pageantry of summits he seems to relish lowers the risk that he may do something to derail relations — like greenlight a package of arms to Taiwan.
分析人士指出，這正是精心設計的結果。用川普似乎樂此不疲的峰會排場來吸引他，可以降低他做出某些可能破壞兩國關係舉動的風險，比如批准一攬子對台軍售方案。
廣告
Tellingly, Mr. Trump has avoided criticizing China in the lead-up to the talks, even downplaying a Wall Street Journal report that China had supplied Iran with satellite images of a U.S. base in Jordan where three American troops were killed in an Iranian strike.
頗能說明問題的是，在會談舉行前夕，川普一直避免批評中國，甚至淡化處理了《華爾街日報》的一篇報導，該報導稱，中國曾向伊朗提供美軍在約旦一處基地的衛星圖像，而三名美軍士兵在伊朗對該基地的襲擊中喪生。
The summitry will also serve a domestic purpose for Mr. Xi by burnishing his image at home as a global statesman, during a moment of economic anxiety for many Chinese. It is important for Mr. Xi to demonstrate that he can manage Beijing’s most critical bilateral relationship, analysts say.
這場峰會外交對習近平而言同樣具有對內意義：在許多中國民眾正為經濟前景感到焦慮不安之際，這有助於他在國內塑造自己作為全球政治家的形象。分析人士指出，對習近平來說，展現自己有能力處理北京最重要的雙邊關係至關重要。
Mr. Trump is expected to greet Mr. Xi personally on the tarmac at Joint Base Andrews in Maryland when his plane lands on Wednesday. The White House will host Mr. Xi and Peng Liyuan, the first lady, for a state dinner, the first such banquet since King Charles III and Queen Camilla visited in April.
預計川普將於週三在馬里蘭州安德魯斯聯合基地停機坪親自迎接習近平。白宮將為習近平和第一夫人彭麗媛舉行國宴，這是自今年4月國王查爾斯三世和卡蜜拉王后到訪以來舉辦的首場國宴。
“Xi wants to be seen by the Chinese public as a highly respected, influential and consequential world leader — and nothing can top being wined and dined at the White House,” said Minxin Pei, a professor at Claremont McKenna College who studies Chinese leadership politics.
「習近平希望，在中國民眾眼中，自己是一位備受尊敬、極具影響力、舉足輕重的世界領導人，而沒有什麼比在白宮受到盛情款待更能體現這一點了，」研究中國領導層政治的克萊蒙特·麥肯納學院教授裴敏欣說。
“Xi cannot afford to be distracted by a huge confrontation with the United States,” Mr. Pei said.
「習近平承受不起與美國爆發大規模對抗從而分散精力的代價，」裴敏欣說道。
