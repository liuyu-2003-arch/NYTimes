中國未兌現購買更多美國農產品承諾

China Promised to Buy More U.S. Farm Goods. It Hasn’t Happened.

黃瑞黎 • 2026-09-22
When President Trump met with the Chinese leader Xi Jinping in Beijing in May, the United States announced that China had agreed to sharply increase its purchases of American agricultural products.
川普總統今年5月在北京與中國領導人習近平會晤時，美國宣布，中國已同意大幅增加對美國農產品的採購。
The pledge, one of China’s major commitments after the high-profile meeting, appeared to offer relief to U.S. farmers battered by the trade war between the two countries.
這一承諾是那次備受矚目的會晤後中國作出的主要承諾之一，看起來有望讓飽受兩國貿易戰衝擊的美國農民鬆一口氣。
Four months later, there is little sign of a broad Chinese buying spree.
四個月過去了，卻幾乎看不到中國大舉採購的跡象。
The White House announced in May that China would buy at least $17 billion a year of U.S. farm goods through 2028, with the target prorated for 2026. Soybeans were not included: China had already vowed separately to step up purchases from American farmers.
白宮5月宣布，到2028年，中國每年將購買至少170億美元的美國農產品，2026年的目標則按全年比例折算。大豆不包括在內：中國此前已經另行承諾增加從美國農民手中採購大豆。
廣告
Yet in the first seven months of 2026, China bought only $3.9 billion of U.S. agricultural products, excluding soybeans, according to the most recent Agriculture Department data. The amount was only slightly more than during the same period last year, when sales were at a six-year low as the two countries imposed tit-for-tat tariffs on each other.
然而，根據美國農業部最新數據，2026年前七個月，中國僅購買了39億美元的美國農產品（不包括大豆）。這一數字僅略高於去年同期，而當時兩國相互加徵報復性關稅，相關銷售額跌至六年來最低水平。
“They’re not keeping that promise and it hasn’t been talked about,” said Ishan Bhanu, the lead agricultural commodities analyst at Kpler, which tracks trade flows.
「他們沒有兌現那個承諾，而且這件事也一直沒人提，」貿易流向追蹤機構Kpler的首席農產品分析師伊尚·巴努說。
The sluggish pace of China’s purchases could become a point of contention when Mr. Trump and Mr. Xi meet again at a summit this week. During his first term, Mr. Trump accused Beijing of “letting us down” by reneging on pledges to buy American agricultural products and called its failure to buy soybeans an “economically hostile” act.
中國採購進展緩慢可能成為川普與習近平本週再次舉行峰會時的一個爭議點。在第一個總統任期內，川普曾指責北京背棄了購買美國農產品的承諾，「讓我們失望」，並稱中國不購買大豆是「經濟上的敵對」行為。
The figures reveal a sharp divide in the composition of Beijing’s trade with American farmers. It is on track to fulfill its soybean pledge, while demand for other U.S. farm products remains soft.
數據顯示，北京與美國農民之間的貿易構成存在明顯分化。中國有望兌現大豆採購承諾，但對美國其他農產品的需求依然疲軟。
That uneven approach reflects China’s dual strategy in dealing with Mr. Trump, analysts say. Beijing wants to show that it is making a good-faith effort to improve relations with the president, who is eager to support farmers ahead of the upcoming midterm elections. But it also does not want to surrender its leverage or appear to be making unilateral concessions to the United States.
分析人士說，這種不均衡的做法反映了中國應對川普的雙重策略。北京想表明，它正在真誠努力改善與這位總統的關係，隨著期中選舉臨近，川普急於為農民提供支持。但中國也不想放棄自己的籌碼，或顯得在單方面向美國讓步。
“The U.S. may have a legitimate view that China is slow-walking the implementation of their May 14 commitments,” said Han Lin, the China country director for The Asia Group, a strategic advisory firm based in Washington.
「美國或許有合理理由認為，中國正在拖延履行5月14日作出的承諾，」總部位於華盛頓的戰略諮詢公司亞洲集團中國區負責人林漢說。
Mr. Lin said the approach stems from Beijing’s underlying distrust of Mr. Trump, even as Chinese officials view him as “certainly more manageable” than more hawkish officials in his administration.
林漢說，這種做法源於北京對川普的深層不信任，儘管中國官員認為，與他政府中更為鷹派的官員相比，川普「無疑更容易打交道」。
廣告
“They understand a large aspect of getting to substantive discussions with the U.S. is paying attention to the performative aspects and making sure Trump looks good,” he said.
「他們明白，要與美國展開實質性討論，很大程度上也得顧及表面上的功夫，確保川普臉上有光，」他說。
China has never confirmed the $17 billion figure, saying only that it had “set indicative targets for expanding two-way agricultural trade.”
中國從未確認170億美元這個數字，只稱將「設定擴大農產品雙向貿易目標」。
Kate Lair, assistant U.S. trade representative for public and media affairs, referred to a May fact sheet from the White House that said the commitment would be prorated for this year. China’s commerce ministry and foreign ministry did not respond to requests for comment.
美國貿易代表辦公室負責公共和媒體事務的助理貿易代表凱特·萊爾提到了白宮5月發布的一份情況說明，其中稱今年的承諾額度將按全年比例折算。中國商務部和外交部沒有回應置評請求。
But even with the prorated target, Beijing’s current pace of buying means it will have to accelerate spending significantly over the rest of the year.
但即便按比例折算，北京目前的採購速度也意味著它必須在今年剩餘時間裡大幅加快支出。
For now, soybeans appear to be the only sector seeing a significant boost.
目前看來，大豆是唯一採購量顯著增加的領域。
After Mr. Trump and Mr. Xi met last October, China bought 12.4 million tons of soybeans from the United States in 2025, according to Jim Sutter, the chief executive officer of the U.S. Soybean Export Council. So far this year, China has made additional purchases amounting to about half of the 25-million-ton, full-year commitment that Washington said was promised.
美國大豆出口協會首席執行官吉姆·薩特說，川普和中國領導人去年10月會晤後，中國在2025年從美國購買了1240萬噸大豆。今年迄今為止，中國又進行了更多採購，總量約為華盛頓所稱中國承諾的全年2500萬噸採購量的一半。
The purchases put the industry “sort of back on track,” Mr. Sutter said.
薩特說，這些採購使該行業「某種程度上重回正軌」。
Soybean prices are now trading near a three-year high, hovering around $13 a bushel, driven in part by Chinese demand.
目前大豆價格徘徊在每蒲式耳13美元左右，接近三年來的高點，其中一個推動因素就是中國的需求。
That is a far cry from Mr. Trump’s first term, when China imposed a 25 percent tariff on U.S. agricultural exports in retaliation for American tariffs on Chinese imports. China is the largest foreign buyer of U.S. soybeans and its purchases plunged.
這與川普第一個任期時的情況大不相同。當時，中國為報復美國對中國進口商品加徵關稅，對美國農產品加徵25%的關稅。中國是美國大豆最大的海外買家，此後採購量驟降。
廣告
The consequences for American farmers were severe. Storage bins filled up, crops risked rotting and the Trump administration ultimately stepped in with a $23 billion bailout program for farmers. The dispute also accelerated Beijing’s shift toward purchasing soybeans from Brazil, another major producer.
這給美國農民造成了嚴重後果。儲糧倉爆滿，農作物面臨腐爛風險，川普政府最終出手，推出了一項230億美元的農民救助計劃。這場爭端也加速了北京轉向從另一個主要生產國巴西採購大豆。
In recent weeks, as the meeting between the two leaders drew closer, China has sped up its purchases.
最近幾週，隨著兩位領導人會晤臨近，中國加快了採購速度。
In August, Sinograin, the state-owned enterprise that manages China’s grain reserves, began stepping up its orders of U.S. soybeans, according to Mr. Bhanu, the Kpler analyst. Usually, purchases happen earlier in the year, he said, suggesting that Beijing had initially been uncertain about whether to fulfill its commitments.
Kpler分析師巴努說，8月，管理中國糧食儲備的國有企業中儲糧開始增加美國大豆訂單。他說，通常採購會在一年中更早的時候進行，這表明北京最初對於是否兌現承諾可能並未拿定主意。
Mr. Bhanu said that Sinograin’s role was also telling.
巴努說，中儲糧在其中發揮的作用也很能說明問題。
“It shows that it’s a geopolitical deal,” he said. Sinograin did not respond to a request for comment.
「這表明這是一筆地緣政治交易，」他說。中儲糧沒有回應置評請求。
China has other reasons to buy American soybeans. Brazil’s soybean inventories have tightened significantly, potentially giving Beijing an incentive to seek an alternative supply source if the Brazilian crop disappoints.
中國購買美國大豆還有其他原因。巴西的大豆庫存已經明顯趨緊，如果巴西大豆收成不及預期，可能促使北京尋找替代供應來源。
But that commercial logic does not apply as clearly to other American agricultural products.
但這種商業邏輯在其他美國農產品上就沒有那麼明顯了。
Sorghum, for example, was among the products Mr. Trump said last October that China would buy in “massive amounts.” Yet sales have been relatively tepid.
例如，川普去年10月曾表示，中國將「大量」採購高粱。然而實際銷售相對溫吞。
Tim Lust, chief executive officer of the National Sorghum Producers, a group representing the American sorghum industry, said China had bought about 20 percent less sorghum from the United States than historical averages.
代表美國高粱行業的全國高粱生產者協會首席執行官蒂姆·拉斯特說，中國從美國購買的高粱比歷史平均水平少了約20%。
廣告
“All agriculture is struggling right now. From a profitability standpoint, it’s a tough situation,” Mr. Lust said. “It is important for us to get back to a managed trade situation and be able to know that we have consistency.”
「現在整個農業都在掙扎。從盈利角度看，形勢很艱難，」勒斯特說。「對我們來說，重要的是要恢復到一種受管控的貿易狀態，能夠確保我們擁有一種穩定性。」
For corn, the situation is bleak. Corn sales to China amounted to about $6.9 million from January to July this year, according to data from the U.S. Department of Agriculture. That is a fraction of the sales in 2022, when China bought $5.2 billion of the crop.
玉米的情況則十分慘淡。美國農業部數據顯示，今年1月至7月，美國對華玉米銷售額約為690萬美元。這與2022年相比只是一個零頭，當年中國購買了價值52億美元的美國玉米。
Analysts say the sales of wheat, cotton, beef and pork also remain well below recent levels.
分析人士說，小麥、棉花、牛肉和豬肉的對華銷售也仍遠低於近年的水平。
China also faces structural obstacles to buying more American agricultural products. An additional 10 percent Chinese tariff on U.S. goods remains in place, while weakness in the Chinese economy has weighed on demand.
中國要增加購買美國農產品還面臨結構性障礙。中國對美國商品額外加徵的10%關稅仍然有效，而中國經濟疲軟也抑制了需求。
From a purely commercial standpoint, buying significantly more U.S. agricultural goods “doesn’t really make sense for China,” said Fred Gale, an expert on China’s agricultural markets and a former economist at the U.S. Agriculture Department.
中國農業市場專家、美國農業部前經濟學家弗雷德·蓋爾說，單純從商業角度來看，大幅增加購買美國農產品「對中國來說其實並不划算」。
“For most Chinese private-sector buyers, it’s not really feasible to buy American products,” Mr. Gale said. “State-owned companies aren’t coming in to buy large amounts of grain or pork. It’s hard to see exactly what China has in mind, whether they actually intend to meet those objectives.”
「對大多數中國民營部門的買家來說，購買美國產品實際上並不可行，」蓋爾說。「國有企業也不會進場大量購買糧食或豬肉。很難看出中國到底怎麼想，他們是否真的打算實現那些目標。」
