在國宴上穿褲子的美國第一夫人

At State Dinner for China’s Leader, Melania Trump Wears the Pants

VANESSA FRIEDMAN • 2026-09-26
Well, that was a surprise.
這可真是出人意料。
In what may have been a first for an American first lady, Melania Trump did something at the state dinner for China’s leader on Thursday night that none of her predecessors had likely done before: She wore pants.
在週四晚為中國領導人舉行的國宴上，梅拉尼婭·川普做了一件可能是美國第一夫人史上前所未有、她的各位前任大概都沒做過的事：她穿了褲子。
Pants! To a black-tie banquet. Especially a black-tie banquet taking place at a highly sensitive time in global geopolitics and economic relations.
褲子！還是穿去參加正裝晚宴。更何況，這場正裝晚宴正值全球地緣政治和經濟關係極為敏感的時期。
Specifically, Mrs. Trump wore a pair of peony-print Dolce & Gabbana black tuxedo pants with a white Saint Laurent blouse displaying an enormous bow to join her husband in welcoming the Chinese president, Xi Jinping; his wife, Peng Liyuan; and assorted dignitaries (including many tech executives) to the formal occasion.
具體來說，川普夫人穿了一條印有牡丹圖案的杜嘉班納黑色禮服長褲，搭配一件帶有巨大蝴蝶結的聖羅蘭白色襯衫。她與丈夫一起出席這個正式場合，迎接中國國家主席習近平、其夫人彭麗媛以及各界要人（包括許多科技高管）。

The peony print on the pants may have been a nod to that flower’s importance in Chinese culture, where it is a symbol of beauty, wealth and happiness. In the end though, that subtle gesture was less significant than the norm-busting choice of the pants themselves on an occasion that has traditionally been synonymous with elaborate gowns. Or at least evening skirts.
褲裝上的牡丹圖案或許是在致意這種花在中國文化中的重要地位，在中國，它是美麗、財富和幸福的象徵。不過，在這樣一個傳統上與華麗禮服（或至少是晚禮服裙）畫等號的場合，這種微妙的致敬遠不如選擇長褲這一打破常規的舉動本身更有意義。
Pants for evening is no longer a radical idea in most situations, but in a city that has become the last bastion of the suit and tie, where staid rules about fashion and the clothing that is fit for office still apply, they were a statement.
在大多數情況下，晚宴穿褲裝已不再是一個激進的想法，但在這個已成為西裝領結最後堡壘的城市，在有關時尚和職場著裝的古板規則依然適用的地方，這個選擇就成為一種宣言。
Especially in contrast with the more classic dress of Ms. Peng — a long gown with a Mandarin collar in red, China’s color of prosperity — as well as Mr. Trump’s tuxedo and Mr. Xi’s Zhongshan suit, standard choices both. (Not to mention the apparently matching navy suits and magenta ties they wore during their meeting earlier in the day.)
特別是，這與彭麗媛更為經典的著裝形成鮮明對比——彭麗媛身穿中式立領紅色長禮服，紅色在中國象徵著繁榮——還有川普的無尾禮服和習近平的中山裝，兩者都是標準的選擇）。（更不用說他們兩人在當天早些時候會面時所穿、看似很搭配的海軍藍西裝和洋紅色領帶。）
When it comes to Melania Trump, however, the pants were also entirely on brand. They reflected the first lady’s continued reluctance to conform to archetypal expectations for first spouses.
然而，就梅拉尼婭·川普而言，褲裝也完全符合她的個人風格。這反映出這位第一夫人一如既往地不願順從人們對第一配偶這一角色的刻板期待。
And they underscored once again the individual brand Mrs. Trump has been building since the release of her documentary earlier this year, with its focus on slick black-and-white graphics and luxury labels, on-screen as in her wardrobe. (That they also matched the pandas Mr. Xi offered to the Atlanta zoo as “envoys of friendship” was probably a coincidence.)
這也再次凸顯了川普夫人自今年早些時候她的紀錄片發布以來一直在打造的個人品牌形象，無論是銀幕上還是她的衣櫥裡，都以精緻的黑白圖形和奢侈品牌為特色。（至於這樣的打扮剛好與習近平作為「友誼的使者」贈送給亞特蘭大動物園的大熊貓相呼應，大概只是個巧合。）
Indeed, a Dolce & Gabbana pantsuit has become a uniform of sorts for the first lady since her husband returned to office. She wore Dolce for her official portrait in 2025 (the one that made her look like chairman of the board), to the White House dinner with governors the same year and to the State of the Union address earlier this year.
事實上，自從她丈夫重返白宮以來，杜嘉班納的褲裝已經成為這位第一夫人的某種制服。她在2025年拍攝官方肖像時（那張讓她看起來像董事會主席的照片）、在同年各州州長出席的白宮晚宴上，以及在今年早些時候的國情咨文演講中，穿的都是杜嘉班納。
The look suggests that she views her role less like that of the hostess of the nation and more like that of an executive in a giant corporation. The buttoned-up styles also guard against the intrusive eyes and demands of the watching world and the country the first family represents.
這種造型表明，她將自己的角色更多地視為一家巨型企業的高管，而非國家的女主人。這種嚴謹的風格也幫她抵禦外界窺探的目光與種種要求——這一切既來自注視著她的世界，也來自第一家庭所代表的國家。
You could see it with the giant bow on the blouse Mrs. Trump wore to the state dinner, which jutted out like a shield from the shirt’s equally protective high neck. You could see it in the military-style charcoal Alexander McQueen skirt suit she wore earlier in the day to tour the Smithsonian alongside Ms. Peng, with the jacket’s officer collar and side closure. And you could see it in the Givenchy pinstriped suit she wore to accompany her husband to Joint Base Andrews for the arrival of the Chinese leader.
從川普夫人國宴所穿襯衫上的巨大蝴蝶結可以看出這一點，它像盾牌一樣，從同樣具有保護性的襯衫高領處突出。你可以從她當天早些時候陪同彭麗媛參觀史密森尼學會時穿的軍裝風炭灰色亞歷山大·麥昆裙裝中看出這一點，那件外套帶有軍官領和側邊紐扣。你還可以從她陪同丈夫前往安德魯斯聯合基地迎接中國領導人到來時穿的紀梵希細條紋套裝中看出這一點。
That’s why, though it would be nice to interpret Mrs. Trump’s choice of pants as a win for women’s liberation everywhere (really, it is ridiculous that it took until 2026 for a first lady to wear pants to a state dinner), it seems unlikely that the motivating factor was gender advancement. Rather than, say, personal priorities.
正因如此，儘管將川普夫人選擇褲裝解讀為全球女性解放的勝利，這樣的說法聽起來很美好（說真的，直到2026年才有一位第一夫人穿褲裝出席國宴，這太荒唐了），但其背後的動機似乎不太可能是為了推動性別進步，倒不如說是出於個人事項的優先考量。
