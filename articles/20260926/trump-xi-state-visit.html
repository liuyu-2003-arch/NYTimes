「習川會」落幕，中美分歧仍暗流涌動

As Xi Leaves Washington, Divisions Still Simmer Beneath the Surface

ZOLAN KANNO-YOUNGS, DAVID E. SANGER • 2026-09-26
The state visit by President Xi Jinping of China was full of lavish gestures and polite words, but there were few tangible policy announcements on some of the most pressing issues of modern times, such as artificial intelligence, the future of Taiwan and the war in Iran.
中國國家主席習近平的此次國事訪問不乏隆重的禮遇與客套的言辭，但在當今時代一些最為緊迫的議題上——如人工智慧、台灣前景以及伊朗戰爭——卻鮮有實質性的政策宣布。
At least in public, President Trump seemed more focused on playing host than challenging his geopolitical rival.
至少在公開場合，川普總統似乎更專注於履行東道主之誼，而非向其地緣政治對手發起挑戰。
Over tea at the White House with Mr. Xi, Mr. Trump described the summit as “tremendous” and said the two superpowers had made “great strides.” Mr. Xi said he and Mr. Trump had added “new substance” to the relationship.
在白宮與習近平茶敘時，川普稱此次峰會「非常成功」，並表示這兩個超級大國已取得「巨大跨越」。習近平則表示，他與川普「豐富了」兩國關係的「內涵」。
But after nearly two days of meetings, the leaders of the world’s two largest economies and artificial intelligence superpowers had little to show beyond the pageantry.
然而，除了盛大的排場之外，在歷時近兩天的會談之後，這兩位全球最大經濟體兼人工智慧超級大國的領導人幾乎未能拿出任何實質成果。
廣告
Gone were the days when Mr. Trump used meetings with the Chinese leader to celebrate big business deals, like purchases of soybeans and Boeings. Instead, he stressed how well he and Mr. Xi get along, as if that were a shield to questions about Chinese companies aiding Iran with satellite imagery that it has used to mount attacks in the region, or the rapid growth of its nuclear weapons program or its territorial declarations in the South China Sea.
川普過去利用與中國領導人的會晤來慶祝大額商業訂單（如採購大豆和波音飛機）的日子已一去不復返。取而代之的是，他極力強調自己與習近平相處融洽，彷彿這能成為擋箭牌，規避外界對中國企業向伊朗提供衛星圖像協助其在該地區發動襲擊、中國核武器計劃迅猛擴張以及中國在南海的領土主張等問題的質疑。
“Trump has increasingly collapsed the vast challenge of U.S.-China relations into the narrow question of his personal relationship with Xi,” said Julian Gewirtz, a former China director for the National Security Council in the Biden administration and now a research scholar at Columbia University. “He seems either unaware of or unconcerned by Beijing’s effort to turn today’s stabilization into tomorrow’s constraints on U.S. policy.”
「川普日益將美中關係的宏大挑戰簡化為其與習近平個人關係這一狹義問題，」拜登政府時期國家安全委員會前中國事務主任、現哥倫比亞大學研究學者朱利安·格維茨表示。「對於北京試圖將眼下的局勢穩定轉化為未來對美國政策的鉗制，他似乎要麼渾然不知，要麼漠不關心。」
Mr. Trump showed the Chinese leader the construction of the White House helipad and ballroom. He presented Mr. Xi a statue of a bald eagle as American tech titans dined on sea bass. And even as the White House runs a taxpayer-funded advertisement condemning communism, Mr. Trump took the leader of China on a tour of the National Archives to see the founding documents of American democracy.
川普陪同中國領導人參觀了白宮直升機停機坪和宴會廳的施工現場。在美國科技巨頭品嘗海鱸魚之際，他向習近平贈送了一尊白頭鷹雕像。甚至在白宮投放由納稅人資助的廣告譴責共產主義的同時，川普還陪同中國領導人參觀了國家檔案館，觀看了美國民主的立國文獻。
In many ways, the summit between Mr. Trump and Mr. Xi was more notable for what they appeared to avoid detailing rather than what they did announce.
在很大程度上，川普與習近平之間的這場峰會值得關注之處更多在於雙方似乎刻意迴避詳述的內容，而非他們實際公布的成果。
Of course, the administration could announce some progress in the days ahead. When asked if the visit had produced additional policy plans, the White House referred to one of Mr. Trump’s social media posts describing the visit as “one of Friendship, Strength, and Success.” “Much has been, and will be, accomplished,” Mr. Trump said without providing details.
當然，美國政府可能會在未來幾天公布一些進展。當被問及此次訪問是否產生了更多政策成果時，白宮建議媒體參考川普發表的一條社群媒體動態。在該貼文中，川普將這次訪問描述為「展現友誼、實力與成功之旅」。「許多工作已經完成，更多成果在後頭，」川普在未提供具體細節的情況下如是說道。
Mr. Trump’s aides and supporters say his personal relationship is key to his diplomatic approach with China.
川普的幕僚與支持者指出，私交是他開展對華外交策略的核心關鍵。
廣告
The two nations did agree to extend, briefly, a trade truce reached in October 2025 during a meeting in South Korea. That deal had eased an intensifying economic confrontation, during which Mr. Trump imposed tariffs that briefly reached 145 percent, and Mr. Xi suspended most exports of critical minerals and magnets.
雙方確實同意短暫延長2025年10月在韓國會晤期間達成的貿易休戰協議。該協議曾在一定程度上緩解了不斷升溫的經濟對抗；在對抗期間，川普一度加徵高達145%的關稅，而習近平則暫停了絕大多數關鍵礦物與磁體的出口。
The accord, set to expire in November, was extended by two months. Mr. Trump said his efforts with Mr. Xi would also be “great for the farmers” but neither side gave any details. China had agreed to sharply increase its purchases of American agricultural products when the two leaders last met, but there is little sign Beijing has made good on the promise.
這項原定於11月到期的協議獲展期兩個月。川普聲稱他與習近平的溝通也將「對廣大農民大有裨益」，但雙方均未透露任何細節。兩國領導人上次會晤時，中國曾同意大幅增加對美農產品的採購，但幾乎沒有跡象表明北京兌現了承諾。
There were no new announcements on how to rein in artificial intelligence, even after a summer in which A.I. agents broke out of the “sandboxes” where they were being tested, conducted cyberattacks on their own and covered their tracks. Mr. Trump had short-circuited the discussion using social media to declare that A.I. “will be a big topic of discussion, but I want to leave it exactly where it is,” posting hours before Mr. Xi arrived. “That is China’s position also.”
儘管在此前的夏季中，AI智能體曾突破測試「沙盒」並自主發起網路攻擊且掩蓋蹤跡，但本次會晤並未就如何約束人工智慧出台新舉措。早在習近平抵達前數小時，川普便通過社交平台發文打斷了該議題的討論，聲稱人工智慧「固然是重要議題，但我希望維持現狀」，並補充稱「這也是中國的立場」。
But the Chinese leader, in his opening statement, emphasized the need for “human control” over A.I. agents, a topic Mr. Trump has avoided. It is still unclear whether they discussed the subject at any length in private.
然而中國領導人在開場致辭中，強調了對AI智能體保持「人類控制」的必要性，而這一話題正是川普所刻意迴避的。雙方是否在閉門會談中深入探討了該議題，目前仍不得而知。
Much the same mystery surrounds their talks on Taiwan. Mr. Trump told reporters on the way back from his trip to China in May that he would soon decide on a new arms package for Taiwan, after consulting Taiwan’s “leader,” presumably a reference to President Lai Ching-te. Such a consultation would have been a breach of the rules in how to deal with the island territory, which China claims as its own, and there is no evidence that it happened. Mr. Trump never made public a decision.
雙方關於台灣問題的會談同樣籠罩在迷霧之中。川普在5月訪華歸程中曾向媒體表示，他在諮詢台灣「領導人」（推測指賴清德）後，將很快就對台新軍售案作出決定。此種協商本會違背處理該島嶼領土（中國聲明擁有其主權）的交往慣例，且並無證據表明此類協商確實發生過。川普也始終未公開任何決定。
Mr. Xi pressed Mr. Trump to oppose Taiwan independence, according to a Chinese readout of their meetings. (To shape the narrative, China turned out its version of the discussions almost immediately.)
根據中方發布的會談通稿，習近平敦促川普明確反對「台獨」。（為了引導輿論敘事，中方几乎在會後第一時間發布了其版本的會談情況。）
Mr. Trump appears to have not given him any commitments. David Purdue, the U.S. ambassador to China, told CNBC there had been no change in the administration’s approach to Taiwan.
川普似乎並未做出任何承諾。美國駐華大使龐德偉(David Purdue)向CNBC表示，本屆政府在台灣問題上的既定政策並未發生改變。
Allen R. Carlson, a professor at Cornell University who focuses on U.S.-China relations and Asia security, said Mr. Trump’s apparent refusal to acquiesce to Mr. Xi’s demands on Taiwan could be viewed as “good news for America’s standing and for the island.”
康乃爾大學關注美中關係與亞洲安全的教授艾倫·R·卡爾森指出，川普顯然拒絕默許習近平在台灣問題上的訴求，這可被視為「對美國的國際威望以及台灣方面的利好消息」。
廣告
But he said it is also a sign of the low expectations for the summit.
但他也表示，這同樣折射出外界對此次峰會的期望值普遍偏低。
“We are now relieved when an American president simply declines to narrow a commitment that has stood for forty years,” Mr. Carlson said. “His silence will be counted in some corners as an American win. In reality the game and set go to Beijing, if not yet the match.”
「如今只要美國總統沒有削弱一項延續了四十年的承諾，我們竟就會感到如釋重負，」卡爾森表示。「他的沉默在某些陣營看來將被奉為美國的勝利。但現實是，即便尚未輸掉整場比賽，這一局和這一盤的贏家也是北京。」
Mr. Xi came armed with the ultimate soft-power diplomatic gesture. He announced a decision to send two pandas to the Atlanta zoo and committed to accepting tens of thousands of additional American exchange students in China in the coming years. But that was reaching into an old playbook for Chinese leaders.
習近平此次展現了極具代表性的軟實力外交姿態。他宣布將向亞特蘭大動物園運送兩隻大熊貓，並承諾在未來幾年內額外接收上萬名美國交換生赴華學習。然而，這不過是沿用了中國領導人的傳統策略。
The two are expected to meet again this year. Mr. Trump told Mr. Xi that he would be attending the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit in Shenzhen, China, in November. He later added that Mr. Xi agreed to attend the Group of 20 summit in Florida in December. “I look forward to our next meeting!” Mr. Trump said on social media.
預計雙方年內還將再次會晤。川普向習近平表示，他將出席11月在中國深圳舉行的亞太經合組織(APEC)峰會。他隨後補充稱，習近平已同意出席12月在佛羅里達州舉行的二十國集團峰會。「期待我們的下一次聚會！」川普在社交平台上寫道。
Mr. Trump insisted on social media that the meetings with Mr. Xi were “very productive.”
川普在社群媒體上強調，與習近平的會晤「富有建設性」。
The one example he cited in that post was Mr. Xi’s seemingly supporting his rebrand of artificial intelligence as “super intelligence.”
他在該帖文中舉出的唯一例證，是習近平似乎贊同他將人工智慧改稱為「超級智能」(super intelligence)。
廣告
“That would be ‘SUPER!’” Mr. Trump said.
「那真是『超』棒！」川普寫道。

Zolan Kanno-Young是時報白宮記者，報導川普總統及其政府。
