AI巨頭與習近平共進晚餐，川普脫稿演講

Dining With A.I. Moguls, Billionaires and Xi Jinping, Trump Went Off Script

SHAWN McCREESH • 2026-09-26
It was just a fun little dinner for the people deciding the future of humanity.
這不過是為決定人類未來者舉辦的一場輕鬆愉快的小型晚宴。
The amount of power concentrated at the head table alone was dizzying. The American president and the Chinese president, dining by candlelight with Elon Musk, Jensen Huang and Tim Cook, all of them sitting in the State Dining room of the White House at this moment that many in Silicon Valley warn could be the last before mankind stumbles into the A.I. apocalypse.
光是主桌上匯聚的權力就令人目眩。美國總統和中國國家主席與伊隆·馬斯克、黃仁勛和蒂姆·庫克在燭光下共進晚餐。此刻，他們全都坐在白宮的國宴廳裡，而矽谷的許多人警告說，這可能是人類陷入人工智慧末日之前的最後時刻。
Standing before a room chockablock with A.I. moguls, billionaires and politicians, President Trump decided to depart from his prepared remarks. Now that he finally had these people together in one place, he could talk about his important plans. The ones he’s been working on nonstop ever since he returned to the White House.
站在擠滿人工智慧大亨、億萬富翁和政界人士的房間裡，川普總統決定脫離準備好的講稿。既然他終於把這些人聚在了一起，他就可以談談自己的重要計劃——自從他重返白宮以來就一直不遺餘力推進的計劃。
“If I could go off-script, just for a second,” he said.
「請允許我脫稿一下，就一小會兒，」他說。
廣告
The sound of rich people clapping filled the room. Suspense!
富人們熱烈的掌聲響徹全場。有懸念！
“I just want to say …” the president continued.
「我只是想說……」總統繼續道。
“… I’d like to invite everyone in the room to visit through that beautiful curtain in the back — The door, it’s all lit up, and looks beautiful. It’s one year away from completion, the new ballroom that you’ve heard about.”
「……我想邀請在座各位穿過後面那道美麗的帷幕去參觀——那扇門，亮著燈，看起來很漂亮。你們聽說過的那個新宴會廳，距離完工還有一年的時間。」
Oh. He was inviting them to take a walk through the construction site in back of the house.
哦。原來他是在邀請大家去白宮後面的建築工地走走。
“You’ll be the first people to step foot on that,” he told guests including Jeff Bezos, Mark Zuckerberg and Sam Altman, “and we’ll do that maybe after we’re finished with the entertainment tonight.”
「你們是第一批踏上那裡的人，」他對包括傑夫·貝佐斯、馬克·祖克柏和薩姆·奧特曼在內的賓客們說，「也許等今晚的娛樂活動結束，我們就去那兒轉轉。」
There was not a single mention of A.I. in his speech. Mr. Trump doesn’t buy into the A.I. panic. He insists it’s mostly just a problem of branding. A.I. needs better P.R.
在他的講話中，根本沒有提及人工智慧。川普先生並不相信人工智慧恐慌。他堅持認為這主要只是一個品牌包裝問題。人工智慧需要更好的公關。
If any of the tech titans and members of Chinese Politburo who showed up to the White House used the state visit to get on the same page about the direction of this terrifying new technology, they haven’t shared their designs with the public. This was a roomful of people who have many disagreements about the way forward.
就算出現在白宮的任何科技巨頭和中國政治局委員利用這次國事訪問，就這項可怕的新技術的發展方向達成了什麼共識，他們也沒有向公眾透露他們的構想。這個房間裡的人對於未來的發展方向存在諸多分歧。
The American president, for his part, just seemed happy to be playing maître d’ to the mighty.
就美國總統而言，他似乎只是樂於充當這些權貴們的領班角色。
“You will see something that I think you will really love,” Mr. Trump told them about the ballroom site. “Tonight, if we had it, we would have had way more than a thousand people, with standing room only. And this room has about 145, that’s about it.”
「你們會看到一些我覺得你們會非常喜歡的東西，」川普在談到宴會廳場地時對他們說。「今晚，如果我們有那個場地，到場人數肯定遠遠超過一千人，擠得水泄不通。而這個廳只能裝145人左右，就這樣了。」
“Anybody who wants to walk into a construction site that’s nice and clean and sparkling, it’s our honor to have you do so,” he said. “This is in honor of President Xi, Madam Peng.”
「任何想要走進一個整潔、乾淨、閃閃發光的建築工地的人，我們都很榮幸能請你來參觀，」他說。「這是為了向習近平主席和彭夫人致敬。」
廣告
Mr. Trump never seems more pleased than when he is hosting wealthy and powerful people at his table. The aspects of the job that so clearly delight him concern pageantry and set design, and Mr. Xi’s visit provided opportunities for plenty of it.
川普最開心的時候似乎就是邀請富人和權貴坐在他的餐桌旁。這份工作中最能讓他樂在其中的莫過於盛大的儀式和精心設計的場景，而習近平的訪問為此提供了大量的機會。
Earlier in the day, Mr. Trump took Mr. Xi around the back to show off a new helipad and the slick new driveway. “He’s an expert on stone, aside from many others, and he loves good granite,” Mr. Trump told reporters as the pair walked back inside.
當天早些時候，川普帶著習近平繞到後面，向他展示新建的直升機停機坪和光鮮的新車道。「他精通很多領域，還是石材專家，而且他特別喜歡上好的花崗岩，」兩人走回室內時，川普對記者說。
Mr. Xi announced a gift of two giant pandas, named Ping Ping and Fu Shuang, who will live at the Atlanta zoo. At the dinner, Mr. Trump unveiled a rather large statue of a bald eagle to give to the Chinese autocrat.
習近平宣布贈送兩隻大熊貓作為禮物，它們名叫「平平」和「福雙」，將會生活在亞特蘭大動物園。在晚宴上，川普揭幕了一座相當大的白頭海雕雕像，作為送給這位中國專制統治者的禮物。
“We hope it’ll find a nice place, a wonderful place, in Beijing,” Mr. Trump said.
「我們希望它能在北京找到一個好地方，一個絕好的位置，」川普說。
Sadly for Mr. Trump, almost none of this made it onto the television networks he watches so closely. The Chinese state visit has collided with the consequences of the decision he made in a fit of pique last Friday to ban CNN, MS NOW and Politico from the White House campus. A judge ordered the Trump administration to let the banned reporters back Thursday, but the TV networks are still refusing to take Mr. Trump’s carnival live. Until his administration formally agrees to let CNN back into the White House TV pool rotation, he is not going to see those blinking red lights he loves so much.
令川普遺憾的是，這一切幾乎都沒有出現在他密切關注的電視上。此次中國國家元首的訪問恰好與他上週五一氣之下做出的決定所帶來的後果發生了衝突——他當時下令禁止CNN、MS NOW和Politico進入白宮區域。週四，一名法官下令川普政府允許被禁記者重返白宮，但各大電視網仍拒絕直播川普的這場嘉年華。除非他的政府正式同意讓CNN重新加入白宮電視聯合採訪團輪換，否則他將無法看到那些他深深喜愛的閃爍紅燈。
Shortly before the dinner began, he posted one of his diatribes on his website.
就在晚宴開始前不久，他在自己的網站上發布了一篇抨擊文章。
CNN and MS NOW were “fake,” he wrote, because they wouldn’t cover “one of the Biggest and Most Beautiful Events to take place in many years, the arrival at the White House of the Highly Respected President of China, Xi Jinping, and his beautiful wife, Madam Peng."
他寫道，CNN和MS NOW是「假新聞」，因為它們拒絕報導「多年來最盛大、最美麗的活動之一——備受尊敬的中國國家主席習近平和他美麗的妻子彭夫人抵達白宮。」
廣告
The president continued: “The event was magnificent — Actually made for Television but, because they’re Fake News, and because they knew how good it would be, they didn’t want credit to be given to ‘TRUMP,’ they refused to be there. That’s why they have such poor Ratings.”
總統還寫道：「這場活動非常盛大——實際上就是為電視量身定做的，但因為它們是假新聞，而且因為它們知道這會有多棒，它們不想把功勞歸於『川普』，所以它們拒絕出席。所以它們的收視率才這麼糟糕。」
He concluded: “Tonight’s State Dinner at the White House, for President Xi, will be spectacular! The Media will be in attendance.”
他總結道：「今晚在白宮為習近平主席舉行的國宴將會非常壯觀！媒體屆時也會到場。」
Yes, the White House complex was crawling with journalists Thursday evening as Mr. Xi pulled up to the front door of the residence. But, in the spirit of solidarity with those targeted by Mr. Trump’s ban, some just ignored the spectacle.
是的，週四傍晚，當習近平抵達白宮官邸正門時，白宮建築群內確實到處都是記者。但是，為了聲援那些遭到川普禁令針對的人士，一些人對這個壯觀的場面無動於衷。

Shawn McCreesh是時報白宮記者，報導川普政府新聞。
