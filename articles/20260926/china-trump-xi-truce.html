中美貿易休戰延長為習近平贏得喘息之機

For China, a Truce With Trump Buys Valuable Time

KEITH BRADSHER • 2026-09-26
For China and its top leader, Xi Jinping, the biggest prize from a summit in Washington, notably free from confrontation, may be simple: time.
對於中國及其最高領導人習近平而言，這場在華盛頓舉行、顯著缺乏對抗色彩的峰會帶來的最大收穫或許很簡單：時間。
Time to cope with a housing market crash that has wiped away the savings of tens of millions of Chinese families. Time to deal with local governments running short of money as land-use revenue collapses. Time to make China even less vulnerable to economic pressure from the United States and other industrialized countries.
這是一段應對房市崩盤的時間，這場危局已蒸發了數以千萬計中國家庭的積蓄；這是一段理順地方財政的時間，隨著土地出讓收入銳減，地方政府普遍面臨資金匱乏；這也是一段讓中國進一步提升抗壓能力的時間，使其面對美國及其他工業化國家的經濟施壓時不再脆弱。
And perhaps most consequentially, time to prepare for the once-in-five-years Communist Party national congress next autumn, which will choose a new slate of top leaders to work under Mr. Xi.
而最具深遠意義的是為明年秋季召開的五年一度的中共全國代表大會做準備。屆時大會將產生新一屆高層領導集團，在習近平的領導下開展工作。
“By trying to stabilize relations with the U.S., China can better concentrate on its own priorities,” said Wu Xinbo, a leading American studies scholar at Fudan University in Shanghai.
上海復旦大學知名美國問題學者吳心伯表示：「通過力求穩定對美關係，中國能夠將精力更好地集中於國內的核心議題。」
When President Trump returned to office last year, Beijing had little reason to expect such breathing room.
去年川普剛剛重返白宮的時候，北京恐怕不會奢望能獲得如此寶貴的喘息空間。
Mr. Trump had campaigned on imposing very steep tariffs on imports from China as part of an effort to rebuild America’s industrial base. Three months after taking office, he briefly raised tariffs to 145 percent, while his administration imposed stringent new restrictions on technology trade with China.
在競選期間，川普曾誓言對中國進口商品加徵極高關稅，以期重塑美國的工業基礎。就職僅三個月後，他一度將關稅增至145%，同時其政府還實施了新一輪嚴苛的對華技術貿易限制。
But the confrontation did not unfold the way Beijing had initially feared.
然而，這場正面交鋒並未向北京最初擔憂的方向演變。
China demonstrated that it had economic weapons of its own, most importantly its dominance of rare-earth metals and magnets essential to the production of cars, electronics and weapons.
中國展現出了自身掌握的經濟反制手段，其中最為關鍵的是其對汽車、電子產品及武器製造不可或缺的稀土金屬與磁體的主導地位。
After Beijing restricted rare-earth exports to the United States, Washington backed away from some of its most aggressive measures. Tariffs fell from their peaks, some export controls were eased, and the two governments began negotiating a series of temporary truces.
在北京收緊對美稀土出口後，華盛頓撤回了部分最為激進的舉措。關稅自高位回落，部分出口管制得到鬆綁，兩國政府開始就一系列臨時休戰展開談判。
Economic brinkmanship continues between the two countries, especially in emerging industries such as artificial intelligence and robotics. But the Trump administration has shifted from the extraordinary escalation of its first months to a less confrontational relationship in which China has shown that it, too, can inflict considerable pain on the American economy.
兩國間的經濟邊緣博弈仍在延續，特別是在人工智慧與機器人等新興產業領域。不過，川普政府已從執政初期不同尋常的局勢升級策略轉向了一種對抗性較低的相處模式；在這種模式下，中國同樣證明了自身具備給美國經濟造成重創的能力。
廣告
Many China experts in the United States now see the Trump administration as pursuing a less combative policy toward Beijing than the posture favored by many Democratic and Republican leaders.
如今，許多美國國內的中國問題專家認為，川普政府對北京採取的政策比許多民主黨和共和黨領袖所主張的立場更具妥協色彩。
“I see the current policy as far too weak and contrary to the views of senior leaders in both parties,” said R. Nicholas Burns, who served as ambassador to China under President Joseph R. Biden Jr. and previously held senior posts elsewhere under President George W. Bush.
曾在小約瑟夫·R·拜登政府擔任駐華大使、並在小布希政府中身兼要職的R·尼古拉斯·伯恩斯指出：「在我看來，當前的政策顯得過於軟弱，且與兩黨資深領導人的共識背道而馳。」
Trade frictions with the United States have consequently receded as an immediate concern in China after seeming to pose a severe threat early in Mr. Trump’s second term. Beijing, however, has not slowed in its longstanding push to become less dependent on the rest of the world for technology or energy.
因此，在川普第二任期初期，中美貿易摩擦一度對中國構成嚴重威脅，但如今已不再是北京亟待解決的燃眉之急。然而，北京並未放慢技術與能源對外依賴度這一既定戰略的步伐。
“They want time and stability because it helps them build a more resilient, more self-reliant economy,” said Evan Medeiros, a professor at Georgetown University and a former senior Asia director on the National Security Council under President Barack Obama. “It gives them time to reduce their exposure to the U.S.”
喬治城大學教授、歐巴馬政府國家安全委員會前亞洲事務高級主任麥艾文(Evan Medeiros)分析道：「他們渴望贏得時間和穩定，因為這有助於他們打造一個更有韌性、更自給自足的經濟體系。這為他們減少對美風險敞口爭取了時間。」
For two decades, Beijing has pursued a consistent policy of replacing high-tech imports with domestic production. That push has helped China become a dominant producer of everything from batteries and solar panels to telecommunications equipment and electric vehicles. It has also made China less vulnerable to foreign economic pressure while leaving other industrialized countries increasingly dependent on Chinese supplies of key raw materials or manufactured goods.
二十年來，北京始終貫徹以本土生產替代高科技進口的堅定政策。這一政策的推行助力中國成為了從電池、太陽能電池板到電信設備、電動汽車等眾多領域的主導生產國。這不僅降低了中國面對外部經濟施壓時的脆弱性，也讓其他工業化國家日益依賴中國關鍵原材料與工業品的供給。
Beijing has pursued a similar strategy of energy self-reliance. Its enormous investments in electric vehicles, wind and solar power, as well as its national electricity grid, have reduced its dependence on imported fossil fuels, helping insulate the economy from high oil prices and supply shortages caused by the war in Iran.
在能源自給自足方面，北京同樣採取了類似的戰略。其在電動汽車、風電與光伏以及國家電網構建上的巨額投入，降低了對進口化石燃料的依賴，幫助本國經濟躲過了因伊朗戰爭引起的高油價與能源短缺衝擊。
廣告
China is also using the lull in trade tensions productively.
中國亦在高效利用貿易局勢緩和帶來的喘息之機。
Its overall trade surplus has surged since Mr. Trump returned to office and is on course to match or surpass last year’s record of roughly $1.2 trillion. Chinese exporters have expanded rapidly into Southeast Asia, Latin America and other markets, reducing their direct dependence on the United States even as many goods reach American consumers through third countries.
自川普重返白宮以來，中國的整體貿易順差大幅攀升，有望追平甚至突破去年約1.2萬億美元的歷史紀錄。中國出口商迅速開闢東南亞、拉丁美洲等新市場，降低了對美國的直接依賴；與此同時，大量商品正通過第三方國家間接輸往美國消費者手中。
Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said at the start of the summit that China and the United States had agreed to extend their trade truce until Jan. 10. China’s Ministry of Commerce confirmed an extension, without specifying a duration.
財政部長史考特·貝森特在峰會開幕時透露，美中雙方已同意將貿易休戰期延長至明年1月10日。中國商務部隨後確認了延長休戰的決定，但未指明具體截止日期。
The relatively short extension could reflect a calculation in Beijing that its bargaining position may improve with time, especially if the Republican Party loses badly in the midterm elections.
這一相對短期的休戰延長，或許反映出北京方面的策略盤算：隨著時間的推移，其談判籌碼可能會進一步增強，特別是在共和黨若在期中選舉中遭遇重挫的情況下。
“Suddenly, the negotiation price changes because Trump will be on his back foot, he’ll be weak against China, and he’ll need China even more than he does today,” Mr. Medeiros said.
麥艾文指出：「屆時談判的門檻將發生改變，因為川普將陷入被動，在對華交鋒中處於弱勢，對中國的需求也會比現在更加迫切。」
For U.S. negotiators, the extension allows them to continue seeking a deal as soon as possible after the midterm elections.
對美國談判代表而言，休戰延期使他們能夠在期中選舉結束後繼續爭取儘快達成全面協議。
廣告
The summit has also given Mr. Xi a chance to portray himself at home as a powerful and respected world leader. Mr. Trump welcomed Mr. Xi as he disembarked from his plane, whereas Mr. Xi sent Vice President Han Zheng to the airport to welcome Mr. Trump to Beijing in May.
這場峰會也讓習近平可以趁機在國內塑造自己強大且受尊重的全球領袖形象。在此次峰會中，川普親自前往舷梯前迎接下飛機的習近平；而在今年5月川普訪華時，習近平是派副主席韓正前往接機。
A display of Mr. Xi’s stature — and, by extension, China’s — is particularly useful as Beijing seeks a tranquil international environment ahead of next year’s Communist Party congress. Mr. Xi appears highly likely to remain in charge even as leaders around him may change. But the gathering will still require extensive maneuvering over senior appointments and policy priorities.
在北京力求在明年中共全國代表大會前營造平穩外部環境之際，展現習近平以及中國的國際影響力尤為重要。儘管他身邊的高層人事可能迎來調整，但習近平繼續執政幾乎已成定局。即便如此，這場盛會仍需要圍繞高層人事布局與政策優先事項展開密集的內部協調。
Repeated meetings with Mr. Trump could help keep China’s most important foreign relationship stable as Mr. Xi turns his attention to domestic politics.
在習近平將精力轉回國內政治之際，與川普保持頻密會晤有助於夯實中國最重要的一對雙邊關係。
And few domestic problems are more pressing than the country’s housing market crash.
而在當前，沒有哪一項國內難題比房地產市場的全面崩盤更為緊迫。
The property downturn has created a cascade of problems. Falling apartment prices have eaten into household savings, while the collapse in property development has gutted the land-use revenue many local governments depended on. Apartment prices have already fallen twice as far as they did in the United States during the American housing market debacle nearly two decades ago.
樓市持續低迷引發了一連串連鎖反應。房價下挫侵蝕了居民家庭的積蓄，房企倒閉潮則抽幹了許多地方政府賴以維繫財政的土地出讓收入。當下的住宅價格跌幅已達近二十年前美國次貸危機時期房價跌幅的兩倍。
Chinese families have responded by cutting back sharply on spending. Restaurants, shopping malls and car dealerships are struggling, while weak domestic demand has left China increasingly reliant on its export industries to sustain growth.
中國家庭對此做出的直接反應是急劇緊縮開支。餐飲、商場以及汽車銷售行業陷入苦撐，疲軟的內需迫使中國經濟更加依賴出口產業來維持增長底線。
廣告
Mr. Trump returned to office threatening tariffs severe enough to reshape China’s export economy. Nearly halfway through the American president’s second term, those tariffs are modest, China’s trade surplus shows no signs of slowing, and Beijing has demonstrated considerable economic leverage.
川普重返白宮時曾威脅要加徵足以重塑中國出口經濟的嚴厲關稅。然而，在其第二任期即將過半之際，這些關稅措施顯得相對溫和，中國的貿易順差擴大勢頭絲毫沒有放緩的跡象，反而展示出了相當可觀的經濟制衡槓桿。
For Mr. Xi, then, another summit that ends without the steep American tariffs once threatened against Chinese exports may represent the biggest victory of all.
因此，對於習近平而言，又一場沒有以美國加徵高昂關稅收場的峰會——這樣的關稅一度威脅到中國的出口——或許本身就是一種至關重要的勝利。
