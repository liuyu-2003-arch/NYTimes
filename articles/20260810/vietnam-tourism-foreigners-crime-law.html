遊客激增考驗越南司法系統：如何應對「不良」外國人

When Misbehaving Foreigners Meet Communist Policing

DAMIEN CAVE • 2026-08-10
He looked like one of the many digital nomads appearing in Vietnam these days as he plopped his bag on a table near the back of a stylish cafe and ordered a coffee.
他那時看起來就像如今在越南出現的眾多「數字遊民」中的一員，把包扔在一家時尚咖啡館後方附近的桌子上，點了一杯咖啡。
Then he ripped off his shirt and ordered everyone to leave.
然後，他扯掉自己的襯衫，並命令所有人離開。
Security footage captured the man, Shaymus Lilly, 34, an Australian brand strategist, clearing counters and shattering windows at Gé Cafe in the coastal city of Da Nang. He smashed a stranger’s phone and choked a young Vietnamese man before police officers hauled him away.
監控錄像顯示，34歲的澳洲品牌策略師沙伊穆斯·利利在沿海城市峴港的Gé Cafe咖啡館大鬧，把櫃檯上的東西扔到地上，砸碎窗戶。在警察把他拖走之前，他砸了一名陌生人的手機，並勒住了一名年輕越南男子的脖子。
Witnesses wondered if he was mentally ill and followed him to the station. The only person who could translate was the man Mr. Lilly had attacked. Worried about mistreatment, the victim volunteered to interpret for his assailant, according to Nguyen Hang, 31, the cafe’s manager.
目擊者懷疑他患有精神疾病，並跟著他去了警局。現場唯一能翻譯的人是被利利襲擊的那名男子。咖啡館經理、31歲的阮姮（Nguyen Hang，音）表示，由於擔心對方受到不公待遇，他自願為襲擊他的兇手擔任翻譯。
廣告
It was a striking illustration of the paradox of today’s Vietnam, which has eagerly welcomed foreigners with promises of natural beauty, fun, friendliness and affordability — but remains a Communist state that prioritizes social order and control. When things go wrong for visitors, open-minded warmth often gives way to a legal system built for a colder, less global era.
這鮮明地展現了當今越南的悖論：一方面，它以自然美景、樂趣、友好和親民的價格熱情歡迎外國人；另一方面，它依然是一個將社會秩序與控制放在首位的國家。當遊客惹出麻煩時，開放包容的熱情往往會讓位於一個為更冷酷的、沒那麼全球化的時代所構建的法律體系。
A record 22.8 million international visitors came to Vietnam in 2025, and this year the country is on track to receive around 15 percent more. Though most are well-behaved, foreigners accused of offenses from gun violence, drunken driving and drugs to illegal businesses have been packed into detention centers, fueling a fierce debate over what kind of country Vietnam really is — and wants to be — as it tries to supercharge its economy with international integration.
2025年，來到越南的國際遊客達到了創紀錄的2280萬人次，今年有望實現約15%的進一步增長。儘管大多數人遵紀守法，但被指控犯有從槍枝暴力、酒後駕駛、毒品到非法經營等罪行的外國人被關進拘留所，這引發了一場激烈辯論：在試圖通過國際一體化推動經濟高速發展的過程中，越南究竟是一個怎樣的國家——以及想成為怎樣的國家。
Vietnamese officials are facing intense diplomatic pressure to enforce international norms, and upgrade a criminal justice system that is ill-equipped for the country’s rising popularity.
越南官員面臨著巨大的外交壓力，要求他們執行國際規範，改進刑事司法系統——隨著越南越來越受歡迎，這個系統難以應對。
Official translators are scarce. Gaining access to lawyers or consular officers can take weeks or months. Penalties for even minor infractions — such as overstaying visas, bar scuffles or using small amounts of marijuana — can be severe, including hefty fines and jail time, according to interviews with former detainees, relatives of people recently arrested, lawyers and diplomats from nearly a dozen countries.
官方翻譯人員十分匱乏。聯繫律師或領事官員可能需要數週甚至數月。根據對曾被拘留或最近被捕的人員的親屬、來自近十個國家的律師和外交官的採訪，即使是對輕微違法行為——例如簽證逾期滯留、酒吧打架或使用少量大麻——的處罰可能也會十分嚴厲，包括高額罰款和監禁。
“It’s a whole different world, a different paradigm,” said a person close to a foreigner detained in Vietnam, who, like others interviewed for this story, insisted on anonymity to avoid antagonizing Vietnamese authorities. “Over there, you’re presumed guilty.”
「這是一個完全不同的世界，一種不同的範式，」一位與在越南被拘留的外國人關係密切的人士說，與本故事採訪的其他受訪者一樣，他堅持匿名以避免激怒越南當局。「在那個地方，你會被推定為有罪。」
Vietnam’s Ministry of Public Security acknowledged in a recent public statement that “foreign nationals’ exploitation of relaxed visa policies to enter and reside in Vietnam and engage in unlawful activities has become increasingly complex.”
越南公安部在最近的一份公開聲明中表示，「外國公民利用寬鬆的簽證政策進入並在越南定居、從事違法活動的現象正變得日益複雜。」
廣告
A legislative proposal presented this year to local lawmakers in Hanoi, the capital, reported that the city’s detention facility where foreigners are held was under enormous strain, and called for more funding, a rare admission of systemic struggle.
今年向首都河內的地方立法者提交的一份立法建議報告稱，河內關押外國人的拘留設施正面臨巨大壓力，並呼籲提供更多資金，這是對系統性困境的一次罕見承認。
Foreign diplomats raised the issue with the highest levels of Vietnam’s government earlier this year, according to five international officials with knowledge of the meeting. Ambassadors’ frustrations have grown as more of their citizens have languished in crowded cells, sometimes for months, lacking regular contact with relatives, consular officials or lawyers.
據五名知情國際官員透露，外國外交官今年早些時候與越南政府最高層討論了這個問題。隨著越來越多的本國公民被關押在擁擠的牢房裡，有時長達數月，缺乏與親屬、領事官員或律師的定期聯繫，大使們的不滿在加劇。
Mr. Lilly’s case has been public enough that Australian officials in Vietnam were notified, and they confirmed they are trying to help. But his fate remains uncertain. Da Nang police announced on June 9 that he would be detained for at least three months while the authorities investigate public disorder charges against him, which could lead to several years in prison.
利利的案件引起了足夠多的公眾關注，澳洲駐越南官員已經知悉此事，並證實正在努力提供幫助。但他的命運依然懸而未決。峴港警方於6月9日宣布，他將被拘留至少三個月，期間當局將調查針對他的擾亂公共秩序指控，這可能會導致長達數年的監禁。
His family in Australia could not be reached for comment. A relative there told a local news outlet that he had bipolar disorder and appeared to be experiencing an episode when he damaged the cafe. At the police station, Ms. Hang recalled, Mr. Lilly said that he had been depressed and not taken his medication.
本報無法聯繫到他在澳洲的家人置評。一位親屬告訴當地新聞媒體，他患有雙相情感障礙，在破壞咖啡館時似乎正處於發作期。阮姮回憶道，在警局時，利利說他一直很抑鬱且沒有吃藥。
At some point that night, he posted on Facebook: “I need help.”
在那天晚上的某個時刻，他在Facebook上發帖：「我需要幫助。」
Not Thailand 2.0
不是泰國2.0
廣告
Vietnam has made clear with state-run media heavily covering foreigners’ crimes that it does not want to be another Thailand — where visitors have long flocked for cheap long-term stays, seeking not just the beaches and food but also red light districts and legal marijuana.
隨著官方媒體對外國人犯罪的大量報導，越南明確表示不想成為另一個泰國。長期以來，遊客大量湧入泰國尋求廉價的長期逗留，不僅為了沙灘和美食，還為了紅燈區和合法大麻。
Part of Vietnam’s challenge is that Thailand also wants to shed that image. The Thai government announced in May that it was cutting most tourist visas to 30 days from 60.
越南面臨的部分挑戰在於泰國也希望擺脫這一形象。泰國政府在5月宣布，將把大多數旅遊簽證從60天縮短至30天。
Steven Foukal, 28, a digital wellness entrepreneur from Chicago who I met outside the Lighthouse Cafe in Da Nang, where foreigners with laptops occupied nearly every seat, told me he was one of many who recently left Thailand for Vietnam.
我在峴港Lighthouse Cafe外遇到了28歲的史蒂芬·福卡爾，他是一名來自芝加哥的數字健康創業者。當時咖啡館幾乎完全被使用筆記型電腦的外國人佔據。他告訴我，他是最近離開泰國來到越南的許多人之一。
“More and more people want an escape,” he said.
「越來越多的人想要一個避世的地方，」他說。
Crackdowns on scam centers in neighboring Cambodia, along with Vietnam’s looser visa rules for several countries, including Russia, may also be drawing in nomads and ne’er-do-wells.
鄰國柬埔寨對詐騙中心的打擊、越南對包括俄羅斯在內的多個國家更寬鬆的簽證規則也可能吸引了遊民和不良分子。
A few weeks before Mr. Lilly’s rampage on the night of May 29, the police in another beach town arrested a Russian man who hit and killed a 70-year-old Vietnamese woman with his motorbike while driving drunk. In June, 83 Chinese nationals were detained after taking over a Ho Chi Minh City hotel for what the police described as “transnational fraud.”
在利利於5月29日晚上發狂的前幾週，另一個海灘小鎮的警方逮捕了一名俄羅斯男子，他在酒後駕駛摩托車時撞死了一名70歲的越南女子。6月，83名中國公民因在胡志明市的一家酒店從事警方所稱的「跨國詐騙」而被拘留。
廣告
Many Vietnamese are not jumping straight to cynicism. Near Da Nang’s My Khe beach on a recent night, Ho Sy Hoang, 76, and his wife, Nguyen Thi Tuyet, 72, sat in front of the building where they live, down the block from a bar full of foreigners pounding shots and playing pool.
許多越南人並沒有直接轉向抵觸情緒。最近的一個晚上，在峴港美溪沙灘附近，76歲的黃胡士（Ho Sy Hoang，音）和72歲的妻子阮氏雪（Nguyen Thi Tuyet，音）坐在他們家門前，不遠處有一家酒吧，裡面都是在痛飲和打檯球的外國人。
It was quiet until four years ago, the couple said, when the city rezoned the area for tourist nightlife. On the plus side, Mr. Hoang said, his property value has risen. The biggest negative: all-night partying.
這對夫婦說，直到四年前這裡都很安靜，當時市政府將該區域重新規劃為遊客夜生活區。從好的方面來看，黃先生說，他的房產價值上升了。最大的負面影響是：通宵達旦的派對。
“In the morning when I open my door, so many times, I see people drunk, aimless or asleep on the street,” he said.
「好幾次早晨我推開門，看到有人喝醉了，漫無目的地遊盪或者睡在街上，」他說。
He and his wife insisted nonetheless that choosing fear over friendliness was not very Vietnamese.
儘管如此，他和妻子堅持認為，選擇恐懼而不是友好並不是很符合越南人的性格。
“They’re just part of the neighborhood,” Mr. Hoang said, pointing to foreigners nearby. “It’s good because before Vietnam was at war and closed to the world. Now we welcome everyone.”
「他們只是社區的一部分，」黃先生指著附近的外國人說。「這很好，因為以前越南在打仗並且對世界封閉。現在我們歡迎所有人。」
Crime and Punishment
罪與罰
廣告
The police in Da Nang have chosen a more confrontational response, inspecting visas at hotels and showing up at foreigners’ apartments at 3 a.m. to make them take drug tests.
峴港警方的反應則更具對抗性：在酒店檢查簽證；在凌晨3點出現在外國人的公寓裡，讓他們接受毒品檢測。
Nationally, the tension between hospitality and suspicion has deep roots. Ho Chi Minh, Vietnam’s revolutionary leader, often accused French colonial rulers of spreading drugs and gambling to keep the Vietnamese people from standing on their own.
在這個國家，好客與懷疑之間向來有一種張力。越南革命領導人胡志明經常指責法國殖民統治者散布毒品和賭博，以阻止越南人民自立自強。
After the Vietnam War ended in 1975, the country codified a lot of that thinking in law. Drug trafficking, even for small amounts, carries the death penalty. Anyone caught “organizing the use of drugs” — a broad charge that can include making plans to share a joint — faces up to 20 years in prison.
1975年越南戰爭結束後，該國將這一思維固化在了法律條文中。販毒——即使是極小數量，也可判處死刑。任何被抓到「組織吸毒」的人都將面臨長達20年的監禁，這是一個寬泛的罪名，可以包括計劃分享大麻煙捲。
Legal scholars say justice, for citizens and visitors alike, remains shaped by Communist Party politics and a bias toward guilt.
法律學者表示，無論是對本國公民還是遊客而言，正義依然受到共產黨政治和有罪推定的影響。
The party sets targets — officials recently declared that Ho Chi Minh City, the country’s largest, will be drug-free by 2030 — and members of the police compete for promotions with prosecutions. Officers send judges “dossiers” about alleged offenders, including a description of the crime and the recommended sentence, and “the assumption is that it’s correct,” said John Gillespie, an expert in Vietnamese law at Monash University in Australia.
共產黨設定了目標——官方最近宣布，該國最大城市胡志明市將在2030年前實現無毒品——辦案起訴的數量成為警員們競逐晉陞機會的籌碼。澳洲莫納什大學的越南法律專家約翰·吉勒斯皮表示，警官們會向法官提交關於涉嫌違法者的「案卷」，其中包括對犯罪的描述和量刑建議，「並且假設這些案卷是正確的」。
Human rights groups have long criticized the Vietnamese authorities for staging show trials and depriving their own citizens of due process. International laws designed to protect foreign detainees — requiring regular consular access and swift notification to their governments — are routinely violated in Vietnam, according to foreign officials from 10 countries. Lawyers and relatives also have little to no access to defendants, especially during pretrial detention, which can last for more than a year.
人權組織長期以來一直批評越南當局舉行示眾審判並剝奪本國公民的正當程序。據來自10個國家的外國官員稱，旨在保護外國被拘留者的國際法（要求定期的領事接觸並迅速通知其政府）在越南經常被違法。律師和親屬也幾乎無法或很難接觸被告，尤其是在可能持續一年以上的審前拘留期間。
“The old Soviet system has remained somewhat intact,” Mr. Gillespie said.
「舊的蘇聯體系在很大程度上保持完好，」吉勒斯皮說。
Vietnam’s Ministry of Public Security and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs did not respond to written requests for interviews about the country’s approach.
越南公安部和外交部沒有回應關於應對方法的書面採訪請求。
In July, the security ministry posted a video of its chief spokesman, Maj. Gen. Nguyen Quoc Toan, emphasizing its commitment to simplifying procedures so foreigners “who come to Vietnam for legitimate purposes” experience “a welcoming, efficient and well-governed country.”
7月，公安部發布了其首席發言人阮國全（Nguyen Quoc Toan，音）少將的影片，強調致力於簡化程序，以便「出於合法目的來到越南」的外國人能體驗到一個「熱情、高效和管理良好的國家」。
There are hints that some officials think the detention system needs updating. The document presented to Hanoi’s lawmaking body noted that the average meal allowance of about $1 a day for detainees, in place since 2000, “is no longer suitable given inflation and the physical needs of foreign nationals.”
有跡象表明，一些官員認為需要改進拘留系統。提交給河內立法機構的文件指出，自2000年開始實施的被拘留者每天約1美元的平均伙食津貼，「考慮到通貨膨脹和外國人的身體需求，已不再合適。」
The man that Mr. Lilly had put in a chokehold went to the police station because of concerns for the Australian’s welfare, according to Ms. Hang, the cafe manager.
據咖啡館經理阮姮稱，被利利鎖喉的男子去警局是出於擔心這名澳洲人會受到不公對待。
“We were all just trying to get out of it safely — and the customer didn’t want the guy to be harmed,” she said.
「我們當時只是想安全地擺脫困境——而且顧客並不希望那個人受到傷害，」她說。
Gé Cafe reopened a few days later, after paying for repairs. At the entrance when I visited in June, near new windows, customers were greeted with a poster displaying the flags of Australia and Vietnam side by side.
Gé Cafe自己出錢進行維修，在幾天後重新開業。我6月去的時候，在靠近新窗戶的門口可以看到一張將澳洲和越南國旗並排展示的海報。
But in early August, Ms. Hang said she and the cafe’s owner were disappointed because they still had not heard from Mr. Lilly’s family. Instead of a lengthy prison sentence, they would like an apology and compensation.
但在8月初，阮女士表示，她和咖啡館的老闆很失望，因為他們仍然沒有收到利利家人的消息。他們希望得到道歉和賠償，而不是讓對方被判處漫長的監禁。
