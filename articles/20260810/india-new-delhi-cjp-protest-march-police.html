印度警方如何鎮壓青年抗議者

Pellet Guns, Sticks and Tear Gas: How India’s Police Halted a Youth March

ANUPREETA DAS, Showkat Nanda, JIAWEI WANG • 2026-08-10
When thousands of unarmed students marched toward the Indian Parliament building last month in a rare challenge to the government, they were met by a wall of riot police officers determined to stop them.
上個月，數以千計手無寸鐵的學生向印度議會大樓遊行，對政府發起罕見的挑戰，他們遭遇了決心阻止他們的防暴警察人牆。
Officials had blocked internet in the area, initially slowing the emergence of details of the crackdown. But videos taken by protesters and witnesses began circulating, revealing that security forces beat people with sticks and fired tear gas and pellet guns at them. The footage led to accusations of excessive force being wielded against peaceful demonstrators.
當局封鎖了該地區的互聯網，起初延緩了鎮壓細節的披露。但抗議者和目擊者拍攝的影片開始流傳，畫面顯示安全部隊用棍棒毆打民眾，向他們發射催淚瓦斯和彈丸槍。這些錄像引發對安全部隊向和平示威者過度使用武力的指責。
The New York Times has verified and analyzed images and video footage shared by protesters, along with witness accounts, to examine how the Delhi Police, the Rapid Action Force and other paramilitary forces handled the crowds. Several Times reporters were also present at the scene on July 20, the day of the march.
《紐約時報》核實並分析了抗議者分享的圖像和影片片段，結合目擊者的敘述，審視了德里警察局、快速行動部隊及其他准軍事部隊是如何對待人群的。7月20日遊行當天，幾名《紐約時報》記者也在現場。
The evidence suggests that security forces exceeded many guidelines for law enforcement in peaceful demonstrations established by the United Nations and also those from India’s home ministry, which state that force “should be used when it is absolutely necessary, it should be minimum and proportional to the situation.”
證據表明，安全部隊違反了聯合國制定的關於和平示威的執法準則以及印度內政部的相關規定。這些規定明確指出，武力「應在絕對必要時使用，且應降至最低限度，並與當時的情況相稱」。
廣告
While police brutality is not unusual in India, especially against minority groups, there was public outrage over how the authorities repressed a vast crowd of young people demanding accountability for the nation’s flawed education system. Protest organizers said at least 150 participants were hospitalized and many more injured. Rahul Gandhi, the leader of the opposition in the lower house of Parliament, has condemned the use of “brute” force, saying, “the assault on students is a stain on our democratic country.”
儘管警察暴力在印度並不罕見（尤其是針對少數群體），但當局鎮壓大批要求對該國弊病叢生的教育體系問責的年輕人依然引發了公眾的強烈憤慨。抗議組織者表示，至少有150名參與者被送往醫院，受傷人數更多。議會下院反對黨領袖拉胡爾·甘地譴責了這種使用「野蠻」武力的行為，他表示：「對學生的襲擊是我們這個民主國家的污點。」
The Delhi Police has said that no permission had been sought or granted for the march, which demonstrators called for after several weeks of peaceful protest at a site near Parliament. The police said the march was “strictly prohibited” and that force had been used as a last resort. They said that 118 police personnel had been injured and that 70 people had been detained for violations of law and order. The Delhi Police did not respond to requests for comment on the video findings documented by The Times.
德里警方表示，這場遊行並未申請許可，也沒有獲得批准。此前，示威者已在議會附近地點進行了數週的和平抗議，隨後才發起了這場遊行。警方表示，此次遊行「被嚴格禁止」，使用武力是最後的手段。他們表示，有118名警務人員受傷，70人因違反法律和秩序被拘留。關於時報記錄的影片調查結果，德里警方未回應置評請求。
Batons and Bamboo Sticks
警棍與竹棍
Security forces used lathis — long batons made out of bamboo or polycarbonate — to hit protesters, a tactic commonly used to control crowds if they become unruly and don’t respond to whistles or warnings delivered over loudspeakers. Security forces are advised to avoid overarm strikes and to hit people on their arms or legs, according to U.N. guidelines. But multiple accounts and videos verified by The Times appear to show police personnel, and a man in plainclothes, aiming for protesters’ torsos.
安全部隊使用「拉提」——一種由竹子或聚碳酸酯製成的長警棍——毆打抗議者。這是一種常用的控制人群的手段，在人群失控且對哨聲或擴音器發出的警告不予理會時採用。根據聯合國的指導方針，建議安全部隊避免手舉過肩揮打，且只應擊打人們的手臂或腿部。但經《紐約時報》核實的多份陳述和影片似乎顯示，警務人員以及一名便衣男子將擊打目標對準抗議者們的軀幹。
“The policemen treated us like criminals,” said Dheeraj Kataria, an undergraduate student at Delhi’s Hansraj College who had joined the march and said a police officer had struck him on the shoulder several times. “They hurled all kinds of abuse at us and beat us as if we were animals.”
「警察把我們當罪犯一樣對待，」德里漢斯拉吉學院的本科生迪拉吉·卡塔裡亞說。他參加了遊行，並稱一名警察多次擊打他的肩膀。「他們對我們破口大罵，像對待動物一樣打我們。」
Mr. Kataria, 24, continued to visit the protest site until July 25, when India’s education minister resigned, leading to the end of the demonstrations. By then, three long welts on Mr. Kataria’s shoulder had turned into dark bruises.
24歲的卡塔裡亞繼續前往抗議現場，直到7月25日印度教育部長辭職示威活動才宣告結束。到那時，他肩膀上三道長長的傷痕已經變成了深色瘀青。
Videos shared by protesters on Instagram, verified by The Times, showed others with scars they said were from similar beatings, including the man below.
抗議者在Instagram上分享、並經《紐約時報》核實的影片顯示，還有其他人身上留有傷痕，他們聲稱也是遭到類似毆打所致，其中包括下方的這名男子。
Arushi Dave, 19, who had her head bandaged, said she had been struck by a police baton “when dozens of policewomen charged at protesters just outside the protest site.” She said she had been frightened at the sight of her own blood as it soaked her white T-shirt. Ms. Dave was among the 2.2 million aspiring doctors who had taken a medical college-entrance exam in May that was later canceled because a question paper had been leaked, igniting the protests.
19歲的阿魯希·戴夫頭纏繃帶，她說，當時「幾十名女警從抗議現場外向抗議者衝過來」，她被警棍擊中。她說，看到自己的鮮血浸透了白色T恤，她感到非常害怕。戴夫是220萬名參加了5月份醫學院入學考試的立志從醫者之一，該考試後來因試題洩漏而被取消，這也是引發此次抗議活動的導火線。
Unarmed Protesters
手無寸鐵的抗議者
Several members of India’s Rapid Action Force, a riot-control unit of an armed police force, were also filmed hitting protesters with batons.
快速行動部隊是印度武裝警察部隊下屬的防暴單位，其數位成員也被拍到用警棍毆打抗議者。
The R.A.F. was established in 1992 as an anti-riot force to prevent violent clashes, including during demonstrations in 1990 when students were protesting a new policy that reserved jobs for people in certain castes. The unit chose the color blue for its uniforms because it denoted peace.
該部隊成立於1992年，是一支旨在防止暴力衝突的防暴部隊。建立該部隊的背景是為了避免再次發生類似1990年示威期間的暴力衝突，當時學生們抗議一項為特定種姓保留工作崗位的政策。該部隊選擇藍色作為制服顏色，因為藍色象徵和平。
At least three R.A.F. personnel wearing helmets and carrying shields took turns hitting an unarmed man who was not fighting back.
至少三名戴著頭盔、手持盾牌的快速行動部隊人員輪流毆打了一名手無寸鐵且未進行還擊的男子。
Tear Gas
催淚瓦斯
Tear gas, which irritates the eyes, throat and lungs, was widely used to break up large crowds gathered in the areas adjacent to the designated protest site. U.N. guidelines say tear gas should never be used to disperse peaceful protesters. Typically, the canisters should be fired in an arc and not directly toward a crowd, but videos verified by The Times showed security forces releasing tear gas by hand at a lower trajectory.
催淚瓦斯會刺激眼睛、喉嚨和肺部，當局廣泛使用這種武器來驅散聚集在指定抗議場地周邊區域的大批人群。聯合國指導方針指出，絕不應使用催淚瓦斯驅散和平抗議者。通常情況下，催淚彈應以弧線軌跡發射，而不是直接射向人群，但經《紐約時報》核實的影片顯示，安全部隊手持催淚瓦斯罐，以較低角度釋放了催淚彈。
Pellet Guns
彈丸槍
The R.A.F. personnel also fired pellet guns, first used in India in 2010 against protesters in the Indian-controlled part of Kashmir, a disputed region also claimed by Pakistan. These guns, which spray dozens of small metal pellets, or birdshot, at a time, can cause severe injuries.
快速行動部隊人員還使用了彈丸槍，這種武器於2010年在印度首次被用於對付印控克什米爾地區的抗議者（巴基斯坦也聲稱對這一爭議地區擁有主權）。這種槍一次能噴射出數十個小金屬彈丸（即鳥霰彈），可造成嚴重傷害。
In one video, an R.A.F. officer is seen pointing his gun at someone out of the camera frame, and the sound of firing can be heard.
在一段影片中，可以看到一名快速行動部隊軍官將槍口對準鏡頭外的人，並能聽到開火的聲音。
In another video, a man standing in the area where the R.A.F. officer was seen firing a pellet gun shows at least 18 small red puncture wounds on his abdomen.
在另一段影片中，一名站在該軍官發射彈丸槍區域的男子腹部可見至少18處紅色小刺傷。
The Delhi Police has said no pellet guns were used, but local media have reported that it has since begun an inquiry into their use. The R.A.F. has not commented on whether the guns were used. Gyanendra Pratap Singh, the director general of the Central Reserve Police Force, under which the R.A.F. sits, said in a public statement that he would bear responsibility for actions conducted in the “bona fide discharges of duty.”
德里警方曾表示沒有使用彈丸槍，但當地媒體報導稱，此後警方已開始對彈丸槍的使用展開調查。快速行動部隊尚未就其是否使用了這種槍枝發表評論。快速行動部隊隸屬的中央後備警察部隊總幹事吉安南德拉·普拉塔普·辛格在一份公開聲明中表示，對於在「正當履行職責」過程中所採取的行動，他將承擔責任。
The Fallout
事態餘波
廣告
Many protesters who were detained or targeted by security forces in Delhi or elsewhere have filed petitions with the Supreme Court, accusing the security forces of brutality and seeking punishment for those involved. The leaders of the Cockroach Janta Party — a once satirical group that led the youth movement — have urged protesters to preserve videos and photos as evidence, saying that dozens of people detained could face charges despite government assurances that they would not be.
許多在德里或其他地方被安全部隊拘留或作為目標打擊的抗議者已經向最高法院提交了請願書，指控安全部隊的暴行，並尋求對涉事人員進行懲罰。「蟑螂人民黨」（一個曾經的諷刺團體，也是這場青年運動的領導者）領導人敦促抗議者保留影片和照片作為證據，他們表示，儘管政府保證不會對被拘留者提起訴訟，但仍有數十人可能面臨指控。
While hearing petitions filed by protesters on July 28, the Supreme Court of India said that there should be an “evidence-based inquiry” into whether excessive force was used.
7月28日，印度最高法院在審理抗議者提交的請願書時表示，應該對是否使用了過度武力進行「基於證據的調查」。

Anupreeta Das為時報報導印度和南亞新聞。她常駐新德里。

Jiawei Wang是《紐約時報》影片記者，常駐首爾。
