美國在伊朗消耗武器，俄中或坐收漁利

The U.S. Is Burning Through Weapons in Iran. Russia and China Are Taking Note.

ANTON TROIANOVSKI, JONATHAN SWAN, ERIC SCHMITT, JULIAN E. BARNES • 2026-08-10
President Trump’s war against Iran has depleted U.S. weapons stockpiles to troubling levels, siphoning supplies from Asia and Europe, according to senior officials.
高級官員表示，川普總統對伊朗的戰爭已將美國的武器庫存消耗至令人擔憂的水平，並抽取了原本用於亞洲和歐洲的物資儲備。
The rapidly shrinking supply of air-defense missiles and other critical weaponry in Europe and Asia not only leaves the U.S. military less equipped, but could also leave Russia and China feeling emboldened should they contemplate a potential conflict with the United States, experts said.
專家指出，歐洲和亞洲防空導彈及其他關鍵武器供應的迅速減少不僅削弱了美軍的裝備水平，還可能使俄羅斯和中國在考慮與美國發生潛在衝突時感到更加有恃無恐。
Bombing Iran burned through thousands of multimillion-dollar missiles that contain precision parts from a global web of suppliers and can take years to produce. Defending against Iran’s retaliatory barrages has worn down U.S. stocks of air-defense missiles to an extent that has privately alarmed top officials in the Trump administration and has led to the United States’ delaying deliveries to European and Asian customers.
轟炸伊朗消耗了數以千計價值數百萬美元的導彈，這些導彈中包含來自全球供應商網路的精密部件，生產週期可能長達數年。為防禦伊朗的報復性打擊，美國的防空導彈庫存損耗已經到了極高的程度，令川普政府高層在私下裡感到震驚，並導致美國推遲向歐洲和亞洲客戶的交付。
The Iran campaign has forced the Pentagon to rush ships, aircraft and air-defense units from across Europe and Asia to the Middle East, an effort with cascading consequences for equipment maintenance and troop morale, U.S. officials said. The result is a significant erosion of American firepower in those regions, a shift with long-term implications that are a growing concern in the military and intelligence communities, senior U.S. and Western officials said.
美國官員表示，針對伊朗的軍事行動迫使五角大廈將艦艇、飛機和防空部隊從歐洲和亞洲各地緊急調往中東，這一舉措對裝備維護和部隊士氣產生了連鎖反應。美國及西方高級官員稱，其結果是美國在這些地區的火力遭到嚴重削弱，這種轉變具有長期影響，正日益引發軍方和情報界的擔憂。
Karoline Leavitt, the White House press secretary, said the United States has “more than enough firepower to carry out any operation the president orders at any time and place.” But experts say the munitions crisis — part of which Mr. Trump inherited from President Joseph R. Biden Jr. — is almost certain to outlast his presidency.
白宮新聞秘書卡羅琳·萊維特表示，美國「擁有充足的火力，足以在任何時間和地點執行總統下達的任何行動」。但專家指出，這場彈藥危機——其中一部分是川普從拜登總統那裡繼承而來的——幾乎肯定會持續到川普的總統任期結束之後。
廣告
Tom Karako, a senior fellow at the Center for Strategic and International Studies in Washington, said the depletion of munitions was a “generational annihilation of the means of conventional deterrence.” The impact on how decisions are made in Beijing and Moscow, he said, could resonate for years.
華盛頓戰略與國際問題研究中心高級研究員湯姆·卡拉科表示，彈藥的耗儘是對「常規威懾手段的一次代際性毀滅」。他說，這對北京和莫斯科決策方式的影響可能會持續多年。
“It’s impossible for that not to have a significant effect on the decision calculus of Russia and China,” said Mr. Karako, who has been tracking U.S. weapons stockpiles. “They may do something quite deliberate and at the time of their choosing, because keep in mind — we’re going to take years to reconstitute.”
「這不可能不對俄羅斯和中國的決策考量產生重大影響，」一直關注美國武器庫存的卡拉科說，「他們可能會在自己選擇的時間採取相當深思熟慮的行動，因為請記住——我們需要數年時間才能重建儲備。」
In meetings in the lead-up to the Iran war, Gen. Dan Caine, the chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff, informed the president of the risks of dipping deeper into drained stockpiles, according to two officials with knowledge of the exchanges. Mr. Trump still decided to go ahead, believing the war would be over quickly. A spokesman for General Caine declined to comment on his private conversations with the president.
據兩名知情官員透露，在伊朗戰爭爆發前的會議上，參謀長聯席會議主席丹·凱恩將軍告知了總統進一步動用已經大量消耗的軍火儲備所帶來的風險。川普仍決定繼續推進，他認為戰爭會很快結束。凱恩的發言人拒絕就凱恩與總統的私人談話發表評論。
After attacking Iran alongside Israel on Feb. 28, Mr. Trump repeatedly promised victory within weeks. More than five months later, he remains unable to bend Iran to his will, or even to find a way to end the war. The munitions shortage is said to have played a role in persuading Mr. Trump to put aside a major bombing campaign late last month. But it also shows how the president’s decision to attack Iran has left the United States and its allies more vulnerable to other threats.
在2月28日與以色列聯手襲擊伊朗後，川普一再承諾將在幾週內取得勝利。五個多月過去了，他仍然無法讓伊朗聽從他的意願，甚至找不到結束戰爭的方法。據稱，彈藥短缺在促使川普上個月末擱置一場大規模轟炸行動中起了一定作用。但這也表明，總統攻擊伊朗的決定令美國及盟國在面對其他威脅時變得更加脆弱。
The Trump administration has worked with military contractors to try to accelerate and expand production. But even accounting for those efforts, it could take more than two years for the United States to replenish the more than 1,500 Patriot air-defense interceptor missiles that have been used in the Iran war. The current U.S. inventory of those missiles is fewer than 1,700, according to two people briefed on the stockpile who spoke on the condition of anonymity to discuss sensitive military details.
川普政府已與軍事承包商合作，試圖加快並擴大生產。但即使考慮到這些努力，美國也可能需要兩年多的時間才能補充在伊朗戰爭中使用的1500多枚「愛國者」防空攔截導彈。據兩名因討論敏感軍事細節而要求匿名的了解庫存情況的人士透露，美國目前庫存的該型導彈不足1700枚。
Mr. Trump has been enraged about news stories covering the munitions shortages. He has ordered a massive leak hunt across his government and is demanding criminal prosecutions. He has told advisers in private that any discussion of the munitions crisis signals weakness to the country’s enemies, and he continues in public to downplay the issue, deny it exists or blame his predecessor.
川普對報導彈藥短缺的新聞感到憤怒，下令在整個政府範圍內展開大規模的洩密清查，並要求對相關人員提起刑事訴訟。他私下裡告訴顧問，任何關於彈藥危機的討論都會向國家的敵人示弱；而在公開場合，他繼續淡化這一問題，否認其存在，或將責任推給他的前任。
廣告
“Our defense companies are making more munitions than ever before, while rapidly expanding their plants and equipment at record levels,” Ms. Leavitt, the White House press secretary, said in a statement. “Anyone entrusted with access to classified military information who leaks it to the media is betraying that trust and breaking federal law, and should be prosecuted to the fullest extent of the law.”
白宮新聞秘書萊維特在聲明中表示：「我們的國防公司正在生產比以往任何時候都多的彈藥，同時以創紀錄的水平迅速擴建工廠和設備。任何被授權接觸機密軍事信息並將其洩漏給媒體的人，都是在背叛這種信任並違反聯邦法律，應當依法予以最嚴厲的起訴。」
Sean Parnell, the chief Pentagon spokesman, said reports of weapons shortages are false, adding that the United States has “everything required to strike at the time and place of the president’s choosing.”
五角大廈首席發言人肖恩·帕內爾表示，有關武器短缺的報導是虛假的，並稱美國擁有「一切必要資源，可在總統選擇的時間和地點發動打擊」。
The most immediate impact of the munitions shortage is being felt in Kyiv, the Ukrainian capital. President Volodymyr Zelensky of Ukraine said this week that the number of air-defense missiles being provided to his country by Western allies had dropped threefold this year compared with 2025. On Wednesday, Ukraine failed to intercept any of the 28 missiles fired by Russia, which killed at least 17 people. More interceptors, Mr. Zelensky said, “could have saved the lives of those killed today.”
彈藥短缺帶來的最直接影響正在烏克蘭首都基輔顯現。烏克蘭總統澤連斯基本週表示，西方盟國今年向該國提供的防空導彈數量已降至2025年的三分之一。週三，烏克蘭未能攔截俄羅斯發射的28枚導彈中的任何一枚，導致至少17人死亡。澤連斯基說，如果有更多的攔截導彈，「本可以拯救今天那些遇難者的生命。」
The fundamental problem, analysts say, is that Russia has ramped up its missile production to a greater extent than Ukraine’s Western supporters have been able to manufacture air defenses. The Iran war exacerbated the mismatch.
分析人士指出，根本問題在於，俄羅斯增加導彈產量的幅度超過了烏克蘭的西方支持者製造防空武器的能力。伊朗戰爭則加劇了這種失衡。
Representative Jason Crow, a Colorado Democrat who sits on the House Armed Services and Intelligence Committees, said the “antiquated” U.S. defense procurement process “doesn’t allow us to actually keep pace with the rapidly evolving nature of warfare.”
眾議院武裝部隊和情報委員會成員、科羅拉多州民主黨眾議員傑森·克羅表示，美國「陳舊」的國防採購流程「使我們無法真正跟上迅速演變的戰爭性質」。
“Frankly, our adversaries don’t have those hindrances,” Mr. Crow said.
「坦率地說，我們的對手沒有這些障礙，」克羅說。
廣告
The United States and its allies have begun to hoard air-defense missiles amid the global supply crunch. Mr. Trump, asked by a reporter in the Oval Office on Thursday about Mr. Zelensky’s plea for more missiles, shot back: “We want missiles too.”
在全球供應緊縮的背景下，美國及其盟國已經開始囤積防空導彈。上週四，在橢圓形辦公室，當被記者問及澤連斯基請求提供更多導彈一事時，川普反擊說：「我們也需要導彈。」
Long-range missiles would be critical in a war against China or Russia, countries with far more advanced air defenses than Iran. Both governments are tracking U.S. stockpiles closely, estimating them through public sources like the congressional budget process and defense contractor announcements, as well as via their spy satellites and other surveillance systems, according to a U.S. official familiar with defense intelligence.
在與中國或俄羅斯的戰爭中，遠程導彈將至關重要——這兩個國家的防空系統遠比伊朗先進。據一位熟悉國防情報的美國官員稱，中俄兩國政府都在密切跟蹤美國的庫存情況，通過國會預算流程和國防承包商公告等公開來源，以及間諜衛星和其他監視系統來進行估算。
“The situation is shaping up very favorably,” a popular Russian pro-war blogger, Aleksandr Kots, recently wrote. “The less precision-guided weaponry the U.S. has, the less will reach Ukraine.”
「局勢的發展非常有利，」俄羅斯知名主戰部落客亞歷山大·科茨最近寫道。「美國的精確制導武器越少，能抵達烏克蘭的就越少。」
Even if Mr. Trump’s successor wants to ramp up aid to Ukraine, Mr. Kots added, “doing so will be impossible with empty arsenals.”
科茨還補充說，即使川普的繼任者想要增加對烏克蘭的援助，「面對空虛的軍火庫，也將是不可能的。」
The long-term impact of the depletion may be greatest in Asia, where the United States has long been preparing for the possibility of a Chinese invasion of Taiwan, the self-governing island that Beijing claims as its own. The U.S. military’s Indo-Pacific Command became a major source of munitions for the Iran war, with many of its Tomahawks and other long-range missiles in the region — critical to a fight with China — transferred from its stocks.
庫存耗盡的長期影響在亞洲可能最為嚴重。長期以來，美國一直在為中國可能入侵台灣（北京聲稱這個自治島嶼屬於自己）做準備。美軍的印太司令部成為了伊朗戰爭的主要彈藥來源地，其在該地區的許多「戰斧」巡航導彈和其他遠程導彈已從其庫存中被調走——這些彈藥對與中國的交戰至關重要。
Hundreds of advanced air-defense interceptors have been sent to the Middle East from South Korea and elsewhere in Asia, while a carrier strike group and some of the Navy’s highest-end destroyers were deployed from the region to support the Iran campaign. U.S. troops in South Korea would be more vulnerable in a war with North Korea because the Pentagon has redirected tactical surveillance assets to the Middle East and withdrawn air defenses, a U.S. official said.
數以百計的先進防空攔截彈已從韓國和亞洲其他地區被送往中東，同時一個航母打擊群和部分海軍最先進的驅逐艦也被從該地區調離，以支持對伊朗的軍事行動。一位美國官員表示，一旦與朝鮮開戰，駐韓美軍將面臨險境，因為五角大廈已將戰術監控資產重新部署到了中東，並撤走了防空系統。
廣告
The depletion has wide-ranging effects. In Asia, some U.S. officials fear that South Korea and Japan will doubt the ability of the United States to protect them with nonnuclear defenses, and will be more inclined to seek their own nuclear weapons to establish more credible deterrence. In Europe, U.S. allies are already looking to increase weapons purchases from other countries, given the production backlog in the United States.
這種消耗產生了廣泛的影響。在亞洲，一些美國官員擔心，韓國和日本將懷疑美國用非核防禦系統保護它們的能力，並會更傾向於尋求獲取自己的核武器，以建立更可信的威懾力。在歐洲，鑒於美國的生產滯後，美國的盟友已經在尋求增加從其他國家的武器採購。
Russia and China have long understood that the U.S. stockpile is limited, officials say, and now understand that inventories will take time to be refilled to levels that would allow for the execution of certain war plans. In that sense, a more drawn-out Iran conflict benefits Moscow and Beijing, some analysts said.
官員們表示，俄羅斯和中國早就明白美國的庫存是有限的，並且現在也了解到，將庫存恢復到可以執行某些戰爭計劃的水平需要時間。一些分析人士認為，從這個意義上說，一場更持久的伊朗衝突對莫斯科和北京是有利的。
“The longer this war runs and the more we continue to exchange fire with Iran, the worse the munitions problem becomes for the United States, and the larger and more visible this window of vulnerability becomes,” said Todd Harrison, a defense analyst at the American Enterprise Institute.
美國企業研究所的國防分析師托德·哈利森表示：「這場戰爭持續的時間越長，我們與伊朗繼續交火的次數越多，美國的彈藥問題就越嚴重，這個脆弱的窗口期也就變得越大、越明顯。」
Dara Massicot, an expert on the Russian military at the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace, said that Moscow had already been reassessing its assumptions about U.S. military stockpiles before the start of the Iran war. The four years of conflict in Ukraine, she said, might have shown Russian military planners that the United States was slower at ramping up weapons production than they had believed.
卡內基國際和平基金會研究俄羅斯軍隊的專家達拉·馬西科特表示，在伊朗戰爭爆發之前，莫斯科就已經在重新評估其對美軍庫存的假設。她說，四年的烏克蘭衝突可能已經向俄羅斯的軍事規劃者表明，美國的武器生產提速比他們想像的要慢。
“That’s the wrong kind of math to go into the Russian system, because then they start changing their perception of their own power vis-à-vis NATO,” said Ms. Massicot, who recently returned from a trip to Ukraine. “It causes them to come to conclusions that we may not want them to.”
「俄羅斯人的系統要是納入了這種考量就糟糕了，因為隨後他們就會開始改變自己相對北約實力的認知，」剛從烏克蘭歸來的馬西科特說。「這會導致他們得出我們可能不希望看到的結論。」
