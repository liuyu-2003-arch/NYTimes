台灣的無人機「地獄景象」能抵禦中國入侵嗎？

How a Drone ‘Hellscape’ Might Stop a Chinese Invasion of Taiwan

儲百亮, AMY CHANG CHIEN • 2026-08-10
To repel any future Chinese invasion, Taiwan is building fleets of flying drones and unmanned attack boats.
為抵禦未來可能發生的中國入侵，台灣正在打造大規模的無人機和無人攻擊艇編隊。
The goal is to use them to help locate and attack Chinese forces approaching the island, relying on Taiwanese soldiers trained to move quickly before China can strike them. U.S. commanders who developed this strategy call it a “hellscape”: overwhelming an invading force with waves of drones, missiles and artillery, and making an amphibious assault so risky that China’s leaders are deterred from trying.
其目標是藉助這些裝備協助發現和打擊向該島逼近的中國軍隊，依託經過快速機動訓練的台灣士兵，在中國發動打擊之前搶先行動。提出這一戰略的美國指揮官將其稱為「地獄景象」：以一波又一波的無人機、導彈和火炮壓倒入侵部隊，使兩棲登陸風險大到足以令北京領導層望而卻步。
Inspired by lessons from Ukraine and other recent war zones, Taiwan is now accelerating efforts to put that strategy into practice.
受烏克蘭及近期其他戰區經驗的啟發，台灣目前正在加快將這一戰略付諸實踐的步伐。
“Our drone and counter-drone efforts started relatively late, and initially we were trying things out as we went along,” Lt. Gen. Huang Wen-chi, the director general of strategic planning for the Taiwanese Defense Ministry, told a committee of lawmakers on Wednesday. That has begun to change over the past two years, he added.
「無人機跟反制無人機系統的這個部分，事實上我們起步是比較晚的，所以在初期的時候，我們基本上是一個邊試邊走的狀態，」台灣國防部戰略規劃司司長黃文啟中將週三對立法院的一個委員會表示。他還說，過去兩年裡，這種情況已開始改變。
廣告
On Thursday, Taiwan’s military allowed reporters to watch as troops in central Taiwan practiced using drones for reconnaissance, as part of the island’s annual Han Kuang military exercises, which began on Wednesday and will run for 10 days.
週四，台灣軍方開放媒體前往台灣中部，觀摩部隊在年度漢光演習中開展無人機偵察的訓練。此次演習於週三開始，將持續10天。
Maj. Gen. Lu Wen-yuan, who helped plan the exercises, said earlier that they would also train troops to take out enemy drones.
參與此次演習籌劃的陸軍少將呂文遠此前表示，演習還將訓練部隊如何摧毀敵方無人機。
Many militaries across the world are embracing the drone revolution, but Taiwan faces a particularly urgent threat: China, which is roughly 110 miles away, claims the self-governed island as its territory. Chinese leaders say they want to absorb Taiwan peacefully, but will not rule out using force. They are backing that threat with expanding fleets of warships, planes, missiles and drones, as well as more frequent coast guard patrols and landing exercises.
世界各地的許多軍隊都在積極投入無人機革命，但台灣面臨的威脅尤其緊迫：中國距離台灣大約180公里，北京聲稱對這座自治島嶼擁有主權。中國領導人表示希望實現和平統一，但不排除使用武力。他們正通過不斷擴充的軍艦、飛機、導彈和無人機編隊，以及更加頻繁的海警巡邏和登陸演習來抵抗這些威脅。
Taiwanese officials have noted Ukraine’s drone-driven gains on the battlefield, and they are trying to emulate Ukraine’s David-versus-Goliath approach: using relatively cheap and nimble drones to level the playing field against a much larger adversary.
台灣官員注意到烏克蘭在戰場上憑藉無人機取得的戰果，並試圖效仿烏克蘭以弱勝強的思路：利用相對廉價靈活的無人機與規模遠大於自己的對手較量時扳平劣勢。
“Drones are entering a new chapter,” said Max Lo, the chairman of the Taiwan National Drone Industry Association. “Taiwan, for its own survival and national defense, is in urgent need of these drones. But it’s the same everywhere in the world right now — that’s an opportunity for Taiwan.”
「無人機又進到新的一個篇章，」亞洲無人機創新產業園區廠商協進會理事長羅正方表示。「台灣為了自己本身的生存，也為了這個國家的防衛，急著需要有這些無人機。但是全球其他地方也都一樣。對台灣而言，這個也是一個機會。」
Aiming to make the island a drone industry superpower, the government says it wants Taiwan to make 100,000 drones a month by 2030, with around half exported. It also wants the military to acquire over 210,000 flying and seaborne drones.
台灣政府希望把台灣打造成為無人機產業強國，並表示計劃到2030年每月生產10萬架無人機，其中大約一半用於出口。台灣還希望軍方採購超過21萬架無人機和無人艇。
Taiwan has “realized that drones will be important in all the domains, in all the phases,” in a potential war with China, said Mick Ryan, a retired Australian major general who has studied Ukraine and Taiwan.
曾深入研究烏克蘭和台灣的澳洲退役少將米克·萊恩表示，台灣已「認識到，無論在哪個領域、哪個階段」，無人機都將在與中國可能發生的戰爭中發揮重要作用。
Ukraine’s recent success at longer-range drone strikes suggests that Taiwan could also attack ships leaving Chinese ports if Beijing launched an amphibious assault, said Mr. Ryan, now a senior fellow at the Lowy Institute in Sydney.
現任雪梨洛伊研究所高級研究員的萊恩指出，烏克蘭近期在遠程無人機打擊方面取得的成果表明，如果北京發動兩棲攻擊，台灣也可以利用無人機攻擊從中國港口出發的船隻。
廣告
China’s army is not yet strong enough to easily invade Taiwan, many experts believe, and recent purges in the People’s Liberation Army’s high command have undermined its readiness. But like Taiwan’s military, it has been building up fleets of crewless planes and boats, and is investing heavily in technology to detect and jam drones.
許多專家認為，中國軍隊目前尚不具備輕鬆入侵台灣的實力，而解放軍高層近期的大清洗更是進一步削弱了其戰備狀態。但與台灣軍隊一樣，解放軍同樣在擴充無人機和無人艇編隊，並投入大量資金研發探測和干擾無人機的技術。
“Remember, the Chinese are also watching everything the Ukrainians are doing,” Mr. Ryan added.
「別忘了，中國人也在密切觀察烏克蘭人正在做的一切，」萊恩補充說。
A Long Road to Mastering Drones
掌握無人機作戰的漫長之路
For Taiwan, acquiring the drones may be the straightforward part. To master drone warfare, Taiwan must also retool its military organization, training and doctrine. It must learn to operate swarms of drones, rapidly assess and share the intelligence they provide and prevent an enemy from disabling them, according to experts. This will require troops to be more mobile and self-sustaining.
對於台灣來說，採購無人機或許反而是相對簡單的一步。要真正掌握無人機作戰，台灣還必須重新調整軍隊的組織架構、訓練方式和作戰理念。專家表示，台灣必須學會如何操作無人機蜂群，快速評估和共享無人機提供的情報，同時防止敵方讓這些無人機失效。這將要求部隊具備更強的機動性和自我保障能力。
“The army’s frontline military operations must undergo a total change from the past,” said Shu Hsiao-Huang, an associate research fellow at the Institute for National Defense and Security Research in Taipei, which is supported by Taiwan’s Defense Ministry. “In the Russia-Ukraine War, if you fire one round, you won’t have a chance to fire a second, if you just stay lined up there without moving on,” he said.
「現在陸軍前線作戰的方式必須是一個完全不同於以往的作戰方式，」具有官方背景、位於台北的國防安全研究院副研究員舒孝煌說道。「在俄烏戰爭中，你能夠打得出一發，不會有（機會打出）下一發——如果是一字排開，完全不動。」
Unlike Ukraine, though, Taiwan lacks the pressure of an actual invasion to mobilize resources and force changes. “It’s very difficult, because they haven’t experienced the crucible of war,” Mr. Shu said.
不過，與烏克蘭不同，台灣並沒有實際入侵的壓力來調動資源、推動變革。「很難，因為他們還沒有真正經歷戰爭的洗禮，」舒孝煌說。
But military units have been working to learn drone tactics, which are a focus of the continuing Han Kuang exercises.
不過，各軍事單位一直在努力鑽研無人機戰術，這也是正在進行的漢光演習的重點內容之一。
The Taiwanese army also released pictures from a drill last month in which a unit scrambled to defend islands off Taiwan’s east coast by using drones to hunt for enemy forces. On Saturday, Taiwan’s president, Lai Ching-te, watched an exercise in the island’s south where troops tested two types of prototype drones designed to strike invading forces.
台灣陸軍還發布了上個月一次演習的照片，照片中一支分隊在台灣東岸離島緊急布防，利用無人機搜尋敵軍。上週六，台灣總統賴清德在台灣南部觀摩了一場演習，部隊在演習中測試了兩款用於打擊入侵力量的無人機原型。
Taiwan also recently created a Littoral Combat Command, integrating drones, mobile missiles and artillery into a coastal shield against potential Chinese invasion.
台灣近期還成立了濱海作戰指揮部，將無人機、機動導彈和火炮整合起來，構建一道抵禦中國潛在入侵的沿海防線。
廣告
Still, Taiwan is a long way from meeting the complex demands of 21st-century warfare, many experts said. It risks becoming “focused on the technology more than the equally essential organizational and doctrinal changes that are needed to implement a hellscape concept,” said Stacie Pettyjohn, a senior fellow at the Center for a New American Security and an author of a recent study of Taiwan’s drone warfare strategy.
不過，許多專家表示，台灣距離滿足21世紀戰爭的複雜需求仍有相當長的路要走。新美國安全中心高級研究員斯塔西·佩蒂約翰表示，它有可能變得「過於關注技術本身，而忽視同樣至關重要的組織和作戰理念的變革，而這些變革正是實施『地獄景象』概念所必不可少的」。斯塔西·佩蒂約翰最近撰寫了一份關於台灣無人機作戰戰略的研究報告。
Building an Indispensable Drone Industry
打造不可或缺的無人機產業
In the event of an invasion, China is likely to try to seal off Taiwan from external support. To survive a blockade, Taiwan needs to build stockpiles and expand homegrown drone production, especially “cheaper, shorter-range one-way attack drones,” Ms. Pettyjohn said.
如果發生入侵，中國很可能試圖切斷台灣與外部世界的聯繫。佩蒂約翰說，為了在封鎖中生存下來，台灣需要建立庫存儲備，並擴大本土無人機的生產能力，尤其是生產「成本較低、射程較短的單程攻擊型無人機」。
Both sides of Taiwan’s polarized political landscape agree on building more drones, even if they disagree on the details. Taiwan’s government has proposed spending $6.5 billion over five years to buy drones. But two opposition parties that hold the majority in its legislature rejected the plan, arguing that it could attract graft and create waste. The main opposition Nationalist Party has proposed spending $7.4 billion over six years, with requirements for growing levels of domestic production.
雖然台灣政壇對立嚴重，但在製造更多無人機這一點上大家意見一致，只是在具體細節上存在分歧。台灣政府提議在五年內投入65億美元採購無人機。但在立法院占多數席位的兩個反對黨否決了這一計劃，認為這可能滋生貪腐並造成浪費。主要反對黨國民黨則提出在六年內投入74億美元，並要求逐步提高本土生產的比例。
As lawmakers debate spending, Taiwanese and U.S. drone makers are competing for potential orders.
在立法機構就相關軍費展開爭論之際，台灣和美國的無人機製造商也在爭奪潛在訂單。
Thunder Tiger, a Taiwanese manufacturer, and Shield AI, an American defense technology company, last month staged a trial run for two models of sea drones that could scout for enemy vessels and blow them up. But Chen Kwan-ju, the chairman of Thunder Tiger, said the company is seeking more policy certainty before it expands production partnerships.
台灣製造商雷虎科技與美國國防科技公司Shield AI上月對兩款無人艇進行了試驗，它們可用於偵測敵方艦艇並將其摧毀。但雷虎科技董事長陳冠如表示，在擴大生產合作之前，公司需要更明確的政策保障。
At the same time, Taiwan is rapidly increasing its rate of drone exports, seeking to make itself an indispensable supplier of components for other democratic states that want to reduce their reliance on Chinese parts, which raise concerns about supply disruptions, or espionage.
與此同時，台灣正在快速擴大無人機出口規模，致力於成為其他民主國家減少對中國零部件依賴的關鍵供應方。後者既存在供應中斷的風險，也引發了間諜活動的擔憂。
廣告
In the first three months of 2026, Taiwan exported drones worth $115 million, exceeding its total drone exports for all of 2025, according to the island’s Ministry of Economic Affairs. The top destination is the Czech Republic, though many of the drones find their way to nearby Ukraine, research shows.
根據台灣經濟部的數據，2026年前三個月，台灣無人機整機出口達1.15億美元，已經超過2025年全年出口總額。最大的出口目的地是捷克共和國，不過研究顯示，其中許多無人機最終流向了鄰近的烏克蘭。
Taiwan’s exports of drone components are probably even higher. Demand for these parts, such as motors and optical equipment, is likely to grow, according to representatives of U.S. drone companies.
台灣的無人機零部件出口額可能更高。美國無人機公司代表表示，對電機、光學設備等零部件的需求預計將持續增長。
“Taiwan sits at the center of one of the most important industrial and strategic competitions of our time,” Michael Robbins, the president of the Association for Uncrewed Vehicle Systems International, wrote in an email. “It is one of the few places positioned to produce trusted drone components at scale.”
「台灣處於當今時代最重要的產業與戰略競爭之核心，」國際無人系統協會主席麥可·羅賓斯在一封電子郵件中寫道。「它是全球少數幾個有能力大規模生產可信賴無人機零部件的地方之一。」

儲百亮(Chris Buckley)是《紐約時報》首席中國記者，自台北報導中國和台灣問題，重點關注政治、社會變革以及安全和軍事問題。

Amy Chang Chien是《紐約時報》記者/研究員，常駐台北，報導台灣和中國新聞。
