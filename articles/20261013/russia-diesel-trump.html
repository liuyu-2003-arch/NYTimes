俄烏經濟戰，川普助普丁一臂之力

Trump Gives Putin a Hand in Russia’s Economic War With Ukraine

CONSTANT MÉHEUT • 2026-10-13
With the front lines largely frozen, Russia and Ukraine have turned the war into a contest of economic endurance. Kyiv has gone after Russia’s oil refineries. Moscow has hit Ukraine’s ports and heavy industries.
隨著前線基本陷入僵局，俄羅斯和烏克蘭已將戰爭變成一場經濟耐力競賽。基輔打擊俄羅斯煉油廠。莫斯科則打擊烏克蘭港口和重工業。
On Friday, President Trump sided with Russia in this all-important battle.
上週五，川普總統在這場至關重要的較量中站在了俄羅斯一邊。
The American leader announced a deal to buy Russian diesel fuel and stepped up pressure on Ukraine to stop striking Russian refineries. The diesel agreement, if it goes ahead, could send some $4 billion of American money into the Russian economy, energy experts said. A halt to refinery strikes, if Ukraine accedes to one, could be much more valuable to the Kremlin.
這位美國領導人宣布了一項購買俄羅斯柴油的協議，並加大對烏克蘭施壓，要求其停止襲擊俄羅斯煉油廠。能源專家稱，如果該柴油協議推進，可能將有約40億美元美國資金流入俄羅斯經濟。如果烏克蘭同意停止對煉油廠的襲擊，這對克里姆林宮來說可能價值更大。
Mr. Trump is enlisting Russian help in his effort to bring down sky-high diesel prices, caused mostly by the U.S.-Israeli war against Iran, before the midterm election early next month. Ukraine is expected to get nothing in exchange, even as the American president demands an end to Kyiv’s refinery attacks, its most potent tool for depriving the Russian war machine of income.
川普正尋求俄羅斯幫助，以期在下月初期中選舉前壓低飛漲的柴油價格，柴油價格飆升主要是由美以對伊朗的戰爭造成的。美國總統要求基輔停止對煉油廠的襲擊——這是烏克蘭切段俄羅斯戰爭機器收入來源的最有力工具，但預計烏克蘭得不到任何回報。
Moscow remains free to continue hitting Ukrainian ports, steel factories, warehouses, power plants and data centers, a campaign that is gradually pushing Ukraine’s economy toward a breaking point. Such attacks destroy nearly $10 billion in business assets each year, Ukraine’s economy minister has said.
莫斯科仍可繼續打擊烏克蘭港口、鋼鐵廠、倉庫、發電廠和數據中心，這一行動正逐漸將烏克蘭經濟推向崩潰邊緣。烏克蘭經濟部長表示，此類襲擊每年摧毀近100億美元的商業資產。
President Volodymyr Zelensky of Ukraine has condemned Mr. Trump’s decision to buy Russian diesel, saying in a social media post on Friday that “Russia will ‘repay’ the diesel with further terror and perfidy.”
烏克蘭總統澤連斯基譴責川普購買俄羅斯柴油的決定，他上週五在社群媒體發帖稱，「俄羅斯將用進一步的恐怖和背信棄義來『回報』這些柴油。」
Mr. Trump, who lifted sanctions to allow Russia to sell the fuel to the United States, struck an economic deal with Mr. Putin even as Russian attacks have driven civilian casualties in Ukraine to what the United Nations called a “new horrifying level.”
川普解除制裁，允許俄羅斯向美國出售該燃料，與普丁達成經濟協議，儘管俄羅斯的襲擊已使烏克蘭平民傷亡達到聯合國所稱的「新的駭人程度」。
廣告
More than 100 Ukrainian civilians were killed in the past week alone. Moscow is striking Ukraine with an ever growing arsenal of increasingly powerful and precise weapons, taking advantage of gaps in Ukrainian air defenses.
僅過去一週，就有100多名烏克蘭平民喪生。莫斯科正利用烏克蘭防空體系的漏洞，使用日益龐大、威力更強且精度更高的武器庫對烏克蘭發動襲擊。
Mr. Zelensky said on Monday that Ukraine had refrained from attacks on Russian oil refineries overnight. But he has said that Ukraine will agree to an extended cease-fire only if Moscow stops attacking Ukrainian energy infrastructure. There are fears of a humanitarian crisis in Ukraine this winter if Russia succeeds in its goal of taking down the Ukrainian power and water systems.
澤連斯基週一表示，烏克蘭在過去一晚未對俄羅斯煉油廠發動襲擊。但他表示，只有莫斯科停止襲擊烏克蘭能源基礎設施，烏克蘭才會同意延長停火。人們擔心，如果俄羅斯成功實現摧毀烏克蘭電力和供水系統的目標，烏克蘭今冬將出現人道主義危機。
The diesel deal is another sign of Mr. Trump’s willingness to tip the scales toward Russia, the aggressor in the conflict. This time, he is offering the Kremlin assistance in an economic battle that could help decide the fate of the war, with both armies largely bogged down at the front.
這筆柴油交易再次表明，川普願意讓天平向這場衝突中的侵略者俄羅斯傾斜。這一次，他在這場可能決定戰爭命運的經濟戰中向克里姆林宮提供援助，與此同時，俄烏兩軍在前線基本陷入僵局。
Ukraine’s campaign against Russian oil infrastructure began in 2024, with strikes on oil depots and refineries. The effort at first had little effect. But as Ukraine expanded its fleet of attack drones and learned how to target critical components that are hard to repair, the effects increased.
烏克蘭對俄羅斯石油基礎設施的行動始於2024年，當時襲擊了油庫和煉油廠。起初效果甚微。但隨著烏克蘭擴大攻擊無人機機隊規模，並掌握了如何鎖定那些難以修復的關鍵部件，行動效果逐漸增強。
Ukrainian attacks on Russian refineries, combined with those on other businesses, have cost Russia about 1 percent of its gross domestic product, President Vladimir V. Putin acknowledged this month.
普丁總統本月承認，烏克蘭對俄羅斯煉油廠及其他企業的襲擊已使俄羅斯損失約1%的國內生產總值。
Reported gasoline output is down by 20 percent compared with 2025 levels, and diesel production is estimated to have fallen by nearly 30 percent, the International Energy Agency said in a report last month. The Ukrainian attacks prompted Russia to impose restrictions on diesel exports that Moscow now says it will lift to sell fuel to the United States.
國際能源署在上月一份報告中稱，俄羅斯汽油產量較2025年水平下降了20%，柴油產量估計下降了近30%。烏克蘭的襲擊促使俄羅斯對柴油出口實施了限制，但莫斯科現在表示將解除這些限制，以便向美國出售燃料。
廣告
The energy agency added that Russia’s oil tax revenue from January to August was nearly 17 percent below the level of the same period in 2025.
國際能源署還稱，俄羅斯1月至8月的石油稅收入比2025年同期低近17%。
Should Kyiv be forced to halt its refinery strikes, Russia’s advantage in the two countries’ contest to starve the other of money will only grow.
如果基輔被迫停止對煉油廠的襲擊，在兩國通過經濟手段相互施壓的較量中，俄羅斯的優勢只會擴大。
Nowhere is that imbalance clearer than in the near closure of Ukraine’s ports.
這種不平衡在烏克蘭港口瀕臨關閉一事上表現得最為明顯。
For months, Russia has attacked commercial ships sailing in and out of the ports in the southern region of Odesa, aiming to discourage them from calling there. The ports are a lifeline for Ukraine’s economy, accounting for 60 percent of the country’s total export volume. They are used to export grain from the country’s fertile farmland, a key source of revenue.
數月來，俄羅斯一直襲擊進出敖德薩南部地區港口的商船，目的是阻止它們停靠那裡。這些港口是烏克蘭經濟的生命線，占該國總出口量的60%。它們用於出口該國肥沃農田產出的穀物，這是烏克蘭的重要收入來源。
Because of the Russian attacks, traffic through the three Odesa ports was down nearly 95 percent in August and September, compared with the same period last year, according to port data obtained by The New York Times. In September, only 12 ships made it out of the ports, compared to 266 in the same month last year.
根據《紐約時報》獲得的港口數據，由於俄羅斯的襲擊，8月和9月通過敖德薩三個港口的交通量較去年同期下降近95%。9月，只有12艘船駛出這些港口，而去年同期為266艘。
Ukraine has tried to redirect some of the traffic to its ports on the Danube River, which connects the country to Romania. Ships can travel down the river through Romania and back into the Black Sea, sailing off the coast of NATO countries toward Turkey. But the Danube ports can handle only about half of the cargo that normally moves through the Odesa ports.
烏克蘭試圖將部分交通轉向其多瑙河港口，該河連接烏克蘭與羅馬尼亞。船隻可以順河而下，穿過羅馬尼亞，再進入黑海，沿著北約國家海岸駛向土耳其。但多瑙河港口只能處理通常由敖德薩港口運輸貨物的大約一半。
廣告
And the Danube route is now under threat, too. Last week, drones struck three ships off the coasts of Romania and Bulgaria, sinking two and killing several crew members. All three vessels had carried Ukrainian grain in the past, according to Romanian authorities and port data.
多瑙河路線現在也受到威脅。上週，無人機襲擊了羅馬尼亞和保加利亞沿海的三艘船，擊沉兩艘，造成數名船員死亡。根據羅馬尼亞當局和港口數據，這三艘船過去都曾運輸烏克蘭糧食。
Ukraine has accused Russia of carrying out the attacks, though Romania and Bulgaria have steered clear of assigning blame. Russia did not comment on the attacks.
烏克蘭指責俄羅斯實施了這些襲擊，但羅馬尼亞和保加利亞均未明確歸咎於任何一方。俄羅斯未對襲擊置評。
The halt to Ukraine’s seaborne exports could have catastrophic consequences for the country’s economy, with effects across many sectors. Ukraine’s agriculture minister said this month that farmers were unable to export about 30 million tons of agricultural products, worth about $30 billion.
烏克蘭海運出口的中斷可能對該國經濟造成災難性後果，並影響許多行業。烏克蘭農業部長本月表示，農民無法出口約3000萬噸農產品，價值約300億美元。
Russia has also suffered from Ukrainian strikes on its Black Sea ports, which have constrained its agricultural exports. But economists say the impact is not comparable because Russia has more export routes than Ukraine, including through the Baltic and Caspian Seas, which Ukrainian weapons cannot reach as easily.
俄羅斯也因烏克蘭對其黑海港口的襲擊而受損，其農產品出口受到限制。但經濟學家表示，影響不可同日而語，因為俄羅斯比烏克蘭擁有更多出口路線，包括通過波羅的海和裏海，而烏克蘭的武器難以輕易觸及這些區域。
In a sign of that imbalance, Russia became the world’s largest exporter of sunflower oil in the 2025-26 marketing year, overtaking Ukraine for the first time in two decades, according to estimates from the U.S. Department of Agriculture.
這種不平衡的一個跡象是，根據美國農業部的估計，俄羅斯在2025-26銷售年度成為世界最大葵花籽油出口國，20年來首次超過烏克蘭。
Russia appears to be following a methodical approach to dismantling Ukraine’s economic base, striking all kinds of assets that an economy needs to function, including export routes, factories and energy facilities.
俄羅斯似乎正在有條不紊地瓦解烏克蘭的經濟基礎，打擊經濟運轉所需的各種資產，包括出口路線、工廠和能源設施。
廣告
The fate of Metinvest, Ukraine’s largest steel manufacturer, is a case in point.
烏克蘭最大鋼鐵製造商梅廷維斯特的命運就是一個例證。
Its factories, including two large steel plants in southeastern Ukraine, were already suffering from power outages caused by Russian strikes on the grid. Then came attacks on the ports, which blocked a key export route, and on the railways, which left its products stranded inside the country. Finally, strikes on the plants themselves forced them to shut down last month.
該公司的工廠此前已因俄羅斯對電網的打擊而遭受停電困擾，包括烏克蘭東南部的兩家大型鋼廠。隨後，港口遭到襲擊，一條關鍵出口路線被阻斷；鐵路也遭到襲擊，使其產品滯留在國內。最後，針對工廠本身的襲擊迫使它們在上月關閉。
The collapse of Ukraine’s steel industry, which contributed more than 7 percent of Ukraine’s economic output last year, will have severe economic consequences, said Oleksandr Myronenko, Metinvest’s chief operating officer. The company is planning layoffs for administrative personnel, and towns that depend on steel plants will see their budgets shrink.
梅廷維斯特首席運營官亞歷山大·米羅年科表示，烏克蘭鋼鐵業的崩潰將帶來嚴重經濟後果；該行業去年貢獻了烏克蘭超過7%的經濟產出。該公司正計劃裁減行政人員，而那些依賴鋼鐵廠的城鎮將面臨財政預算縮減。
In September, for the first time in a century, not a single metric ton of steel was produced in Ukraine for one week.
9月，烏克蘭出現了百年來首次一週內鋼鐵產量為零的情況。

Constant Méheut是時報記者，報導烏克蘭戰爭。
