全球首富的崛起之路：馬斯克紀錄片講了些什麼

What to Know About the New Elon Musk Documentary

MATTHEW HUFF • 2026-10-13
Alex Gibney opens his documentary about the tech titan Elon Musk with a question: “How could Musk assume so much power, and why had we given it to him?”
亞歷克斯·吉布尼關於科技巨頭伊隆·馬斯克的紀錄片以這樣一個問題開場：「馬斯克怎麼能獲得這麼大的權力，而我們又為什麼把這些權力交給了他？」
The Oscar-winning filmmaker, whose previous subjects include Enron and Scientology, spends nearly four hours answering that question in “Musk,” tracing the rise of the Tesla chairman and chief executive from his childhood in South Africa to the I.P.O. of SpaceX in June, which made Musk the world’s first trillionaire. (The movie, in theaters, has a 10-minute intermission baked in.)
這位曾經拍過安然公司和山達基教的奧斯卡獲獎導演在《馬斯克》一片中用近四個小時的時間來回答這個問題，追溯了這位特斯拉董事長兼首席執行官的崛起之路，從他在南非的童年一直講到今年6月SpaceX首次公開募股——這讓馬斯克成為世界上首位身家達到萬億美元的富豪。（這部正在影院上映的電影安排了10分鐘的中場休息。）
When news of Gibney’s project broke back in 2023, Musk took to Twitter (now X) to preemptively call it a “hit piece.” Combining interviews with ex-wives and former colleagues and clips from Musk’s 30-plus years in the spotlight with commentary from an A.I. version of Musk constructed from an archive of his own words, the film follows his rise from Silicon Valley start-ups to his brief tenure as the de facto head of the Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE).
2023年，吉布尼正在拍攝這部紀錄片的消息傳出後，馬斯克就在Twitter（現稱X）上先發制人，稱其為「抹黑之作」。影片採訪了馬斯克的前妻和前同事，穿插他過去30多年間在公眾視野中留下的影像資料，還利用他過去的語音檔案建立了一個人工智慧版馬斯克，讓這個虛擬人物對影片內容發表評論。影片記錄了馬斯克從矽谷創業到短暫擔任政府效率部實際負責人的崛起歷程。
Here are some takeaways.
以下是影片的幾個要點。
Musk’s privileged childhood was marred by violence
馬斯克家境優渥的童年籠罩著暴力陰影
“Musk” paints a harrowing portrait of Elon’s youth in apartheid-era South Africa. In one interview, his wealthy businessman father, Errol Musk, claims to have shot and killed three intruders, covering the floor of their home in a “swimming pool” of blood. In another, he describes Elon riding in a car alongside the body of a family friend who had just “dropped dead.” After Elon’s mother, Maye Musk, separated from Errol, claiming physical and mental abuse (charges her former husband has denied), Elon continued to live with his father. When Elon was bullied so badly at school that he was hospitalized for a week, his father, he later said, sided with his attackers.
《馬斯克》描繪了伊隆在當時實行種族隔離制度的南非度過的童年歲月，揭示了一段令人不寒而慄的往事。在一次採訪中，他的父親、富商埃羅爾·馬斯克聲稱自己曾開槍打死三名闖入者，鮮血流了一地，在家中地板上積成了一個「游泳池」。在另一次採訪中，他描述伊隆坐在一輛車裡，旁邊是一位剛「猝死」的家族朋友的屍體。伊隆的母親梅耶·馬斯克與埃羅爾分居時，指控丈夫對她實施身體和精神虐待（她的前夫否認了這些指控），但伊隆仍然選擇和父親一起生活。有一次，伊隆在學校遭到嚴重霸凌，以至於住院一週。據他後來回憶，父親當時卻站在欺負他的那些人一邊。

He’s made a habit of failing upward
他屢屢因失敗而獲利
Gibney suggests that Elon Musk’s business blunders have been as lucrative as his successes. After a failed bid to become the chief executive of his first start-up, Zip2, Musk was pushed out of the company but earned a $22 million buyout. Only a few years later, the entrepreneur and venture capitalist Peter Thiel pushed Musk out of PayPal with a $250 million parting gift.
吉布尼認為，伊隆·馬斯克在商業上的失誤給他帶來的財富並不亞於成功為他帶來的財富。他曾試圖成為自己創辦的第一家初創企業Zip2的首席執行官，但未能如願，隨後被趕出公司，卻拿到了2200萬美元的收購所得。僅僅幾年後，企業家兼風險投資人彼得·蒂爾又將馬斯克趕出了貝寶，而馬斯克離開時獲得了2.5億美元。
More recently, according to Gibney, Twitter (now X) lost 80 percent of its value after Musk purchased it (though other estimates vary). Musk then rolled X and his A.I. start-up xAI into SpaceX for the largest I.P.O. on record to date, prompting Gibney to ask, “How can you keep screwing up and still make millions?”
吉布尼稱，更近的例子是，Twitter（現X）在馬斯克收購後損失了80%的價值（不過，不同機構的估算並不一致）。馬斯克隨後將X和他的AI初創公司xAI併入SpaceX，完成了有記錄以來最大的IPO，這讓吉布尼不禁發問：「你怎麼能一次又一次把事情搞砸，卻依然賺得盆滿缽滿？」
Tesla is a lesson in entrepreneurial sleight of hand
特斯拉背後的商業障眼法
While Musk claims to be a founder of Tesla, the automotive company was actually started by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning in 2003. However, Musk negotiated the title of co-founder for himself as part of a 2009 legal settlement. Later, he claimed that Tesla drivers could easily swap out their 1,000-pound car batteries, which helped him garner more than $100 million worth of environmental credits from the state of California, though he had only built a single battery swap facility in the desert.
雖然馬斯克自稱是特斯拉的創始人，但這家汽車公司實際上由馬丁·艾伯哈德和馬克·塔彭寧於2003年創立。不過，在2009年達成的一項法律和解協議中，馬斯克通過談判為自己爭取到了聯合創始人的頭銜。後來，他又宣稱，特斯拉車主可以輕鬆更換重達450公斤的汽車電池，這幫助他從加利福尼亞州獲得了價值超過1億美元的環保積分，儘管他僅在沙漠裡建了一個電池更換站。
Gibney also dedicates substantial time to Tesla’s self-driving Autopilot feature, which has been linked to deaths and injuries. In 2016, Musk oversaw the creation of a promotional video depicting a self-driving Tesla, causing the company’s stock to rise. However, it was later revealed that humans had controlled the car at certain points during filming and that the car had actually crashed.
吉布尼還用了相當長的篇幅討論特斯拉的自動駕駛Autopilot系統，該系統被指與多起造成人員傷亡的事故有關。2016年，馬斯克主導製作了一段展示特斯拉汽車自動駕駛的宣傳影片，推動了公司股價上漲。然而，後來有消息披露，拍攝過程中，汽車在某些時刻實際上由人控制，而且那輛車還發生了撞車事故。
He sees increasing the birthrate as a “national emergency”
他把提高出生率視為「國家緊急事務」
“Musk” includes extended interviews with Justine Musk, Elon’s first wife, and Ashley St. Clair, a former conservative influencer who dated Elon from 2023 to 2025 and gave birth to one of his 14 known children. St. Clair claims to have received messages from multiple women on social media whom Musk offered to impregnate. “It’s like a Gatling gun of sperm in his DMs,” she says in one of the documentary’s more evocative lines.
《馬斯克》對伊隆的第一任妻子賈斯廷·馬斯克以及曾經的保守派網紅阿什莉·聖克萊爾進行了長時間採訪。聖克萊爾在2023年至2025年間與伊隆交往，並為他生下一個孩子（馬斯克為公眾所知的孩子共有14個）。聖克萊爾稱，她在社群媒體上收到多名女性發來的私信，她們都說馬斯克曾提出要讓她們懷孕。她在紀錄片中用了一句格外生動的比喻：「他在私信裡到處播種，簡直就像一挺噴射精子的加特林機槍。」

Despite Musk’s preoccupation with procreating, both women suggest that Musk sustains little interest in his children or their mothers. Justine remembers Elon telling her, “I am the alpha in this relationship” at their wedding reception, and later telling her, “If you were my employee, I’d fire you.” St. Clair claims Musk openly called X Æ A-Xii (whose mother is Musk’s former partner Claire Boucher, the musician known as Grimes) his favorite and didn’t care to spend time with his other offspring.
儘管馬斯克對生育如此執著，但這兩名女性都表示，他對自己的孩子及孩子的母親幾乎沒有持續的關心。賈斯廷回憶說，伊隆曾在兩人的婚宴上對她說：「在這段關係裡，我是老大。」後來又對她說：「如果你是我的員工，我早就把你開除了。」聖克萊爾則稱，馬斯克曾公開表示，X Æ A-Xii（其母是馬斯克之前的伴侶、音樂人格萊姆斯，本名克萊爾·布歇）是他最喜歡的孩子，她還說他不怎麼願意花時間陪伴其他子女。
Starlink and DOGE gave him global reach
星鏈和政府效率部讓他的影響力遍及全球
According to Gibney, Musk has been able to consolidate political power via Starlink, the satellite arm of SpaceX, which provides internet access from space. While Musk supplied Ukrainian forces with necessary tech during their war with Russia, he’s also able to control and withhold access to it. That military bargaining chip has given Musk remarkable leverage in lucrative business deals around the world.
據吉布尼說，馬斯克得以通過星鏈鞏固政治權力。星鏈是SpaceX的衛星部門，從太空提供互聯網接入。雖然馬斯克在俄烏戰爭中向烏克蘭軍隊提供了必要的技術，但他同樣能夠控制甚至切斷對這項技術的使用。這一軍事籌碼讓馬斯克在全球利潤豐厚的商業交易中擁有了非同尋常的議價能力。
As part of his work with DOGE, Musk dispatched gatekeepers and regulations designed to check the A.I. industry. With those barriers removed, Gibney speculates that Musk could use A.I. to amass even more power.
在政府效率部任職期間，馬斯克清除了旨在約束人工智慧行業的監管把關機制和規定。吉布尼推測，隨著這些障礙被移除，馬斯克可能利用人工智慧進一步擴大自己的權力。
