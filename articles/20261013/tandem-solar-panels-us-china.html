美國研發新型太陽能電池板，或挑戰中國壟斷地位

A New Crop of Solar Panels Is the U.S.’s Best Hope of Catching China

IVAN PENN • 2026-10-13
Producers of solar panels in the United States say they are on the cusp of a technological breakthrough that could upend the industry and even threaten China’s grip on it.
美國太陽能板生產商表示，他們即將迎來一項技術突破，這項突破可能顛覆整個行業，甚至威脅到中國在該行業的壟斷地位。
The manufacturers are racing to develop new kinds of panels that can generate 25 percent more energy than most such devices available today. If they are successful, the advance will allow the world to meet the rapidly rising demand for electricity with less land and roof area.
這些製造商正在競相開發新型太陽能板，其發電量比目前市面上大多數同類產品高出25%。如果成功，這一進步將使世界能以更少的土地和屋頂面積來滿足迅速增長的電力需求。
The new technology, known as tandem solar, involves adding second and third layers of material, like a layer cake, to allow panels to convert more of the sun’s radiance into electricity.
這項新技術被稱為疊層太陽能，通過像層層蛋糕一樣添加第二層和第三層材料，使太陽能電池板能夠將更多的太陽輻射轉化為電能。
Researchers have been working to perfect tandem panels for many years, but now several companies, including Tandem PV, First Solar and Hanwha Qcells, say they are preparing to sell such panels commercially soon, after successfully testing the devices and obtaining certifications from technical bodies.
研究人員多年來一直致力於完善疊層太陽能電池板，但現在，包括Tandem PV、First Solar和韓華Qcells在內的幾家公司表示，在成功測試這些設備並獲得技術機構認證後，他們正準備很快將此類電池板投入商業銷售。
廣告
“It is the next frontier,” said Mike Carr, executive director of the Solar Energy Manufacturers for America Coalition. He acknowledged that Chinese companies were also working on tandem solar but said their technology “is not better than ours and maybe even behind us.”
「這是下一個前沿領域，」美國太陽能製造商聯盟執行主任邁克·卡爾說。他承認中國企業也在研發疊層太陽能，但表示中國的技術「不比我們的更好，甚至可能落後於我們」。
But the new tandem panels and their manufacturers will face many challenges. Power producers will be reluctant to buy new devices that are too expensive or until manufacturers can convince them that tandem panels will not degrade more quickly than conventional panels.
但新的疊層太陽能板及其製造商將面臨許多挑戰。電力生產商將不願購買過於昂貴的新設備，除非製造商能讓他們相信，疊層太陽能電池的性能衰減速度不會比傳統太陽能電池更快。
U.S. politics could be another barrier. Republicans in Congress and the Trump administration have made it hard for new solar farms to obtain federal permits while trying to increase the use of fossil fuels and nuclear power plants.
美國政治可能是另一個障礙。國會共和黨人和川普政府一方面試圖增加化石燃料和核電站的使用，一方面讓新的太陽能發電場難以獲得聯邦許可。
Then there is China, which controls about 80 percent of the solar panel market by supplying either panels or their components.
此外還有中國，它通過供應太陽能板及其組件控制著約80%的太陽能板市場。
China devastated the U.S. solar panel manufacturing industry by offering devices at prices far lower than companies in the United States, Germany and Japan — which dominated the industry until around 2010.
中國以遠低於美國、德國和日本公司的價格提供設備，重創了美國太陽能板製造業；此前，美國、德國和日本曾主導該行業直至2010年左右。
The Biden and Trump administrations have tried to revive U.S. solar manufacturing with tariffs, subsidies and other policies. Partly as a result, new factories have been built across the country in recent years. Hanwha, a South Korean company that bought the German solar manufacturer Qcells in 2012, has spent billions on production lines in Georgia, including in Cartersville, north of Atlanta.
拜登和川普政府都試圖通過關稅、補貼和其他政策重振美國太陽能製造業。部分得益於此，近年來美國各地紛紛建起了新工廠。韓國企業韓華在2012年收購了德國太陽能製造商 Qcells，已在喬治亞州的生產線上投入數十億美元，包括亞特蘭大以北的卡特斯維爾。
Energy experts say perfecting tandem panels before the Chinese do would allow the United States, Germany and other countries to reclaim a big share of the global solar industry.
能源專家表示，若能在中國之前完善疊層電池技術，美國、德國及其他國家將有望重新奪回全球太陽能產業的巨大份額。
Qcells has been testing its tandem solar panels in its lab in Germany as well as in fields in the United States, Australia, Cyprus and South Korea to show that they are ready. Among other things, the company has been blasting panels with LED light to see how they will perform. It has also been firing balls of ice — some roughly the size of marbles, others as big as or slightly bigger than golf balls — at panels to see how they handle extreme weather.
Qcells一直在其德國實驗室以及美國、澳洲、塞浦路斯和韓國的實地測試其疊層太陽能電池板，以證明它們已經準備就緒。除其他測試外，該公司一直用 LED 光照射太陽能電池板，以觀察其表現。它還向太陽能電池板發射冰丸——有些大約彈珠大小，另一些與高爾夫球一樣大或略大——以檢驗其應對極端天氣的能力。。
The tests are designed to reassure energy companies, investors and lenders that the new panels will last 20 to 30 years once they are installed in baking deserts or in fields subject to torrential downpours.
這些測試旨在讓能源公司、投資者和貸款方放心：無論安裝在酷熱的沙漠中，還是在常受暴雨侵襲的田野裡，這些新的太陽能電池板都能使用20至30年。
The power industry is “quite conservative in accepting new technologies,” said Fabian Fertig, head of tandem research and development for Qcells, who is based in Leipzig, Germany. “We’ve been focused on stability. We have to convince banks that this new technology can deliver.”
電力行業「在接受新技術方面相當保守」，Qcells疊層研發負責人法比安·費爾蒂希說。他常駐德國萊比錫。「我們一直專注於穩定性。我們必須讓銀行相信，這項新技術能夠兌現承諾。」
廣告
Scientists developed early tandem panels decades ago, but it has taken time to ensure that they could withstand harsh conditions — from heat and humidity to pounding hail.
科學家早在幾十年前就開發出了早期疊層太陽能電池板，但要確保它們能夠承受從高溫、高濕到猛烈冰雹等各種惡劣條件，仍花費了相當長的時間。
“There are all these questions around how long they actually last,” said Chetan Krishna, a principal at RMI, a research organization that specializes in sustainability and was previously known as the Rocky Mountain Institute. “Are there degradation issues that we don’t know yet?”
「圍繞它們實際能用多久存在各種各樣的問題，」RMI（一家專注於可持續發展的研究機構，前身為落基山研究所）負責人切坦·凱瑞希納說。「是否存在我們尚不知道的衰減問題？」
Mr. Krishna and other experts say they believe the tandem solar panels are very close to being ready.
凱瑞希納和其他專家表示，他們認為疊層太陽能電池板已非常接近投入實際應用。
But the technology remains expensive. By some estimates, these panels could cost three to three and a half times as much as conventional devices, which are typically made of one layer of silicon.
但這項技術仍然昂貴。根據一些估計，這些太陽能電池板的價格可能是傳統設備的三到3.5倍，而傳統設備通常由單層硅製成。
The cost of buying and installing conventional panels for solar farms ranges from about 93 cents to $1.06 per watt, according to a report by the Solar Energy Industries Association.
根據太陽能產業協會的一份報告，為太陽能發電場購買和安裝傳統太陽能電池板的成本約為每瓦93美分到1.06美元。
Andrés Gluski, chief executive of AES, a big utility and power company, said price would be crucial to the success of tandem panels because Chinese manufacturers sold solar panels at very low prices. China “could probably supply the world twice over,” he said.
大型公用事業和電力公司AES的首席執行官安德烈斯·格盧斯基表示，價格對疊層太陽能電池板的成功至關重要，因為中國製造商以極低價格銷售太陽能電池板。他說，中國「可能足以滿足全球兩倍的需求」。
廣告
The technology for modern solar panels was created at AT&T’s Bell Labs in 1954. But broad use of it didn’t take off until the 1980s as the devices became more efficient at converting sunshine into electricity. Improvements helped lower costs and have made the technology the cheapest way to generate electricity.
現代太陽能板技術於1954年在AT&T的貝爾實驗室誕生。但直到20世紀80年代，隨著太陽能設備將陽光轉化為電能的效率提高，該技術才開始得到廣泛應用。技術的改進有助於降低成本，並使該技術成為最經濟的發電方式。
In recent years, energy companies have also figured out how to pair solar panels with batteries to supply energy hours after the sun has set. Solar and batteries accounted for 70 percent of the capacity added to U.S. grids in the first half of this year, according to Wood Mackenzie, an energy research firm.
近年來，能源公司還摸索出了將太陽能電池板與電池相結合的方法，以便在日落後數小時仍能供電。根據能源研究公司伍德麥肯茲的數據，今年上半年，太陽能和電池佔美國電網新增容量的70%。
Solar energy made up nearly 9 percent of the electricity produced in the United States in 2025, up from 3 percent in 2020, according to the Energy Information Administration.
根據美國能源信息署的數據，2025年太陽能佔美國發電量的近9%，高於2020年的3%。
Widespread use of tandem panels could significantly increase solar’s share of the country’s electricity, helping to satisfy the surging demand for power from data centers used to develop artificial intelligence.
疊層太陽能板的廣泛使用有望顯著提高太陽能在美國電力中的份額，幫助滿足用於開發人工智慧的數據中心激增的電力需求。
Energy analysts evaluate the dependability of different sources of power by measuring their capacity factors. In theory, a power source that can supply electricity 24 hours a day, 365 days a year would have a capacity factor of 100 percent. But no power plants operate at that level because they require maintenance, upgrades, refueling and so on.
能源分析師通過衡量容量係數來評估不同電源的可靠性。理論上，一種能夠一年365天、每天24小時供電的電源，其容量係數應為100%。但沒有電廠能達到這一水平，因為它們需要進行維護、升級、補給燃料等操作。
Nuclear plants typically offer the highest capacity factors, in the low 90s. Large natural gas power plants generally operate at about 58 percent, while U.S. solar farms average roughly 24 percent, according to the E.I.A. That is primarily because they produce electricity only when the sun is shining and cannot convert all of the light they receive into usable energy.
核電站通常具有最高的容量係數，達到90%出頭。根據美國能源信息署的數據，大型天然氣發電廠一般以約58%的係數運行，而美國太陽能發電場的平均容量係數約為24%。這主要是因為它們只在陽光充足時發電，並且無法將接收到的所有光都轉化為可用能源。
Solar executives assert that tandem panels could have capacity factors of more than 31 percent. As a result, energy companies would be able to generate the same electricity on less land and with fewer materials.
太陽能行業高管堅稱，疊層太陽能電池板的容量係數可能超過31%。因此，能源公司將能夠在更少的土地上、使用更少的材料產生同樣的電力。
The design of tandem panels favors First Solar, one of the few U.S. manufacturers that survived as Chinese producers became dominant. First Solar’s products use a thin film material that other manufactures would most likely need to perfect to make the layers in tandem panels.
疊層太陽能電池板的設計對First Solar很有利，它是在中國生產商佔據主導地位時倖存下來的少數美國製造商之一。First Solar的產品使用一種薄膜材料，其他製造商若要製造疊層電池板中的各層，很可能需要先將這種材料完善。
廣告
“We’ve invested over $2 billion in thin film research and development, including this program,” said Mark Widmar, chief executive of First Solar, in Phoenix.
「我們已在薄膜研發上投資超過20億美元，包括這個項目，」位於鳳凰城的First Solar 首席執行官馬克·維德馬說。
Some energy companies have bought early versions of the tandem panels to ensure that they do not get left behind, said Wayne Li, a principal at the Electric Power Research Institute, a nonprofit research organization. How dependable and durable the panels are will require extensive third-party testing, he added.
非營利研究機構電力研究院的負責人韋恩·李（音）說，一些能源公司已經購買了疊層太陽能電池板的早期版本，以確保自己不被時代淘汰。他還說，這些電池板的可靠性和耐用性還需要經過廣泛的第三方測試。
“Manufacturers are not very open to share the details and the data,” Mr. Li said.
「製造商不太願意分享細節和數據，」韋恩·李說。
