黃仁勛事件折射中美外交背後的激烈情報戰

Behind the Trump-Xi Bonhomie, a Fierce Spy War Rages

DAVID PIERSON • 2026-11-12
As President Trump and China’s leader, Xi Jinping, prepare to meet next week to extend a fragile trade truce, a fresh espionage scandal has shown that behind their diplomacy is a fierce intelligence rivalry.
就在川普總統和中國領導人習近平準備下週會面以延長脆弱的貿易休戰期之際，一樁新的間諜醜聞表明，在他們的外交背後，存在一場激烈的情報較量。
At its center is Jensen Huang, the Nvidia leader and an influential ally of Mr. Trump’s, who has acknowledged having an affair with a Chinese businesswoman whom U.S. intelligence officials suspect of spying for Beijing. China has denied the espionage allegation.
處於事件中心的是英偉達領導人、川普的重要盟友黃仁勛，他已承認與一名中國女商人發生婚外情，而美國情報官員懷疑這名女商人為北京從事間諜活動。中國否認了這一間諜指控。
Under Mr. Xi, the Chinese Communist Party has built a more sophisticated and assertive spy service that regularly highlights the importance of technological security and of threats posed by the United States. At the same time, the Central Intelligence Agency has said that Chinese companies are a legitimate target for espionage in areas like artificial intelligence, semiconductors and biotechnology.
在習近平的領導下，中國共產黨建立了一個更加成熟且強勢的間諜機構，該機構經常強調技術安全的重要性以及美國構成的威脅。與此同時，美國中央情報局表示，在人工智慧、半導體和生物技術等領域，中國企業是間諜活動的合法目標。
Nvidia’s computer chips power the global A.I. boom and sit at the center of Washington’s efforts to keep that technology out of China’s hands. That makes Mr. Huang, who has lobbied Mr. Trump to ease restrictions on the export of advanced chips to China, a prime target, analysts say.
英偉達的計算機晶片推動了全球人工智慧熱潮，也成為華盛頓阻止中國掌握人工智慧技術的核心所在。分析人士指出，這使得曾遊說川普放寬對華先進晶片出口限制的黃仁勛成為了首要目標。
“It is not at all surprising, given the centrality of Nvidia to U.S.-China tech competition, and the importance of tech competition in the overall U.S.-China rivalry, that someone like him would be a top target for Chinese intelligence collection,” said Sheena Chestnut Greitens, an expert on Chinese security at the University of Texas at Austin.
「鑒於英偉達在中美科技競爭中的核心地位，以及科技競爭在整個中美較量中的重要性，像他這樣的人成為中國情報收集的首要目標，一點也不令人驚訝，」得克薩斯大學奧斯汀分校的中國安全問題專家希娜·切斯特納特·格雷滕斯說。
U.S. agencies concluded that the Chinese businesswoman, who goes by Alice Huang, was an asset of the Chinese Ministry of State Security. Nvidia has said that it is conducting an internal investigation. Ms. Huang has not responded to requests for comment.
美國情報機構得出結論認為，這位名叫黃樂(Alice Huang)的中國女商人是中國國家安全部的線人。英偉達表示正在進行內部調查。黃樂尚未對置評請求作出回應。
“It is brazen, and yet I don’t think we should be surprised at all,” said Christopher K. Johnson, a former U.S. government intelligence officer who is now president of China Strategies Group, a risk consultancy. “Whenever the Chinese declare something a national strategic, whole-of-society priority, like they have done with chips and A.I., that implies they will use every tool available to them to work, buy or steal their way to that end.”
 「這種行為雖然明目張膽，但我認為我們完全不應感到驚訝，」前美國政府情報官員、現任風險諮詢公司中國戰略集團總裁的克里斯托弗·K·強生說。「每當中國人宣布某件事是國家戰略、全社會的優先事項時——就像他們在晶片和人工智慧領域所做的那樣，這就意味著他們將使用所有可用的工具，通過工作、購買或竊取的方式來達到這一目的。」
廣告
So far, the scandal has not apparently disrupted plans for Mr. Trump and Mr. Xi to meet next week in the southern Chinese city of Shenzhen. Mr. Trump said on Monday that he did not plan to bring the matter up with Mr. Xi. He has repeatedly downplayed reports of Chinese spying and cyberattacks on the United States, arguing that Washington does the same.
迄今為止，這樁醜聞顯然並未打亂川普和習近平下週在中國南方城市深圳會面的計劃。川普週一表示，他不打算向習近平提及此事。他曾多次淡化有關中國對美國進行間諜活動和網路攻擊的報導，並稱華盛頓也在做同樣的事情。
That is a contrast with the Biden administration, which confronted China in early 2023 about a spy balloon that was spotted drifting across the continental United States. Official relations between the countries froze for months.
這與拜登政府形成了鮮明對比——2023年初，拜登政府曾就一架被發現漂浮穿越美國本土的間諜氣球一事向中國提出交涉。兩國官方關係因此凍結了數月。
Mr. Trump’s approach could embolden China to take bigger risks, said Dennis Wilder, a former U.S. intelligence analyst on China and a senior fellow at Georgetown University.
喬治城大學高級研究員、前美國對華情報分析師韋德寧(Dennis Wilder)表示，川普的做法可能會讓中國膽子更大，願意冒更大的風險。
“The president’s whole laissez-faire attitude toward spying reassures Beijing that the political blowback from espionage operations will be very manageable,” he said.
「總統對間諜活動的整體放任態度讓北京確信，間諜行動帶來的政治反彈將是非常可控的，」他說。
Mr. Huang was until recently also expected to travel to Shenzhen. When Mr. Xi visited the United States in September, the tech executive was a guest at the White House state dinner. He had joined a presidential trip to Beijing in May at Mr. Trump’s invitation.
直到前不久，黃仁勛原本還計劃前往深圳。9月習近平訪美時，這位科技高管曾作為嘉賓出席白宮國宴。5月，他應川普的邀請，隨總統出訪北京。
“The president has over and over dismissed Chinese spying by saying that everyone does it,” Mr. Wilder said. “The problem is that no one else does it to the very damaging effect that the Chinese can and do.”
「總統一次又一次地對中國間諜活動輕描淡寫，說大家都在這麼做，」韋德寧說。「問題在於，其他國家無法也不會像中國那樣造成如此嚴重的破壞性後果。」
廣告
In recent years, Chinese spies have been accused of targeting the phones of American presidential candidates, recruiting U.S. Navy sailors, cultivating political aides, and planting malicious code in power grids and water systems that Americans rely on every day.
近年來，中國間諜被指針對美國總統候選人的手機開展行動，招募美國海軍水手，收買政治助理，並在美國人日常依賴的電網和水務系統中植入惡意代碼。
Beijing also a history of ruthless counterintelligence operations. From 2010 through 2012, the Chinese government killed or imprisoned 18 to 20 of the C.I.A.’s sources in China, crippling American spying there for years, The New York Times reported in 2017.
北京也有開展無情反間諜行動的歷史。據《紐約時報》2017年報導，從2010年到2012年，中國政府殺害或監禁了18到20名中央情報局在中國的線人，導致美國在華間諜活動在此後多年裡陷入癱瘓。
Espionage has repeatedly strained relations, at times erupting into diplomatic crises, as when a U.S. Navy surveillance plane collided with a Chinese fighter jet in 2001 near China’s Hainan Island, killing the Chinese pilot. Beijing condemned Washington for spying and held the 24 American crew members for 11 days.
間諜活動屢屢導致兩國關係緊張，有時還會引發外交危機。例如2001年，一架美國海軍偵察機在中國海南島附近與一架中國戰鬥機相撞，導致中國飛行員喪生。北京譴責華盛頓從事間諜活動，並將24名美國機組人員扣留了11天。
A More Sophisticated Spy Agency
一個更成熟的間諜機構
China’s lead intelligence agency, the Ministry of State Security, has transformed from a service that relied on gathering gossip at embassy dinner parties into a sophisticated spy shop that can go toe-to-toe with the C.I.A. with bigger budgets and modern tools like A.I.
中國的首要情報機構國家安全部曾經只是一個依靠在使館晚宴上收集流言蜚語的機構，如今已經轉變成了有雄厚預算和人工智慧等現代化工具、能夠與中央情報局正面交鋒的成熟間諜機構。
Its political standing has risen as well. In 2022, Mr. Xi elevated the ministry’s then chief, Chen Wenqing, to the Communist Party’s Politburo, a council of the 24 most senior national leaders. He was the first spy chief in decades to ascend to that body.
它的政治地位也有所提高。2022年，習近平將該部時任部長陳文清提拔進了由24名國家最高領導人組成的中共中央政治局。他是幾十年來首位晉陞該機構的情報部門負責人。
The once-shadowy ministry has also stepped into public view, posting a stream of warnings on its WeChat social media account about foreign spies. It has publicized examples of Chinese nationals working for the government and a military contractor in Tokyo and Rome being recruited by C.I.A. officers to provide information about the government or the military.
這個曾經行事隱秘的部門也步入了公眾視野，在其微信帳號上接連發布關於外國間諜的警告。它還公布了一些案例：在東京和羅馬，為政府和軍事承包商工作的兩位中國公民被中央情報局官員招募，以提供有關政府或軍隊的信息。
At the same time, the United States has stepped up its intelligence gathering to include not just military and government targets, but also private Chinese companies, the deputy C.I.A. director, Michael Ellis, told a cybersecurity conference this year. Compared with past geopolitical rivals, China “poses a fundamentally different kind of threat to us because the competition is economic,” Mr. Ellis said.
與此同時，美國中央情報局副局長麥可·埃利斯在今年的一次網路安全會議上表示，美國加強了情報收集工作，不僅包括軍事和政府目標，還包括中國私營企業。埃利斯說，與過去的地緣政治對手相比，中國「對我們構成了根本上不同類型的威脅，因為這種競爭是經濟方面的」。
The C.I.A. has also taken the unusual step of recruiting in public, producing a series of Mandarin-language videos urging disaffected Chinese officials to share secrets with the United States. (The Ministry of State Security’s WeChat account mocked the videos as “crude,” “farcical” and a sign of the C.I.A.’s “baffling incompetence.”)
中央情報局還採取了不同尋常的公開招募舉措，製作了一系列普通話影片，敦促心懷不滿的中國官員向美國透露機密。（中國國家安全部的微信帳號嘲笑這些影片「蹩腳」、「拙劣可笑」，並稱這是中央情報局「無能」的標誌。）
China Pushes Back
中國的反駁
In China, the case has drawn official denials and questions about what Beijing would stand to gain.
在中國，此案引發了官方的否認，並引發了外界對北京方面能從中獲得什麼利益的質疑。
廣告
Ms. Huang had been an executive at a Singapore-based company called Megaspeed that buys advanced Nvidia chips. The United States began restricting sales of advanced chips directly to China in late 2022, but Chinese companies can lease computing power from data centers that use those chips in Southeast Asia, like the ones Megaspeed operates.
黃樂曾是一家名為Megaspeed的新加坡公司的高管，該公司購買先進的英偉達晶片。美國於2022年底開始限制直接向中國銷售先進晶片，但中國公司可以從東南亞使用這些晶片的數據中心（例如Megaspeed運營的數據中心）租賃算力。
At the same time, China has also encouraged domestic companies to make do with less powerful domestically produced chips to promote greater national self-reliance, leading some analysts to question the significance of the Nvidia case.
與此同時，中國也鼓勵國內企業將就使用功能較弱的國產晶片，以促進國家自給自足，這讓一些分析人士對英偉達案的重要性提出質疑。
“China does not want to import so many Nvidia chips,” said Shen Dingli, an international relations scholar in Shanghai. “They are trying to force Chinese companies to use Chinese chips from Huawei.”
「中國不想進口那麼多英偉達晶片，」上海的國際關係學者沈丁立說。「他們正試圖迫使中國企業使用華為的國產晶片。」
The Chinese Foreign Ministry has rejected the espionage allegations surrounding Ms. Huang as “nothing but a smear against China.” On Chinese social media platforms, which are heavily censored, comments about Mr. Huang have centered on his extramarital affair.
中國外交部駁斥了圍繞黃樂的間諜指控，稱其純屬「污衊抹黑」。在審查嚴格的中國社群媒體平台上，有關黃仁勛的評論主要集中在他的婚外情上。
Nationalist commentators in China have described the scandal as an effort that brings together American opponents of China, Mr. Trump, and artificial intelligence.
中國的民族主義評論將這樁醜聞描述為一次將美國反對中國、川普和人工智慧的勢力結合在一起的行動。
Ren Yi, a Harvard-educated Chinese blogger who goes by the pen name “Chairman Rabbit,” wrote: “Once again, anti-China politics weaves America’s disparate political causes together.”
曾在哈佛大學就讀、筆名為「兔主席」的中國部落客任意寫道：「反華再次把美國的各種議題聯繫到了一起。」
