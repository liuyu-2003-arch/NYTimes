英偉達將討論黃仁勛去留

Nvidia’s Board Is Expected to Meet to Discuss Jensen Huang’s Future

TRIPP MICKLE, MAGGIE HABERMAN • 2026-11-12
Nvidia’s board of directors was expected to meet on Wednesday to discuss the future of Jensen Huang, the chief executive of Nvidia, according to two people with knowledge of the situation, after revelations that he had an affair with an executive who reports to him as well as with a woman whom U.S. intelligence has identified as a Chinese spy.
據兩名知情人士透露，在曝出英偉達首席執行官黃仁勛與一名向其彙報的高管以及一名被美國情報部門認定為中國間諜的女性有婚外情後，英偉達董事會預計將於週三召開會議，討論黃仁勛的去留問題。
Nvidia didn’t immediately respond to a request for comment.
英偉達沒有立即回應置評請求。
Mr. Huang’s potential exit would open an uncertain chapter for Nvidia, the premier maker of artificial intelligence chips, and would have widespread ripple effects. The Silicon Valley firm is a linchpin of the A.I. boom and one of the world’s most valuable public companies, controlling an estimated 90 percent of the market for the computer chips used in the big A.I. projects that are driving the U.S. economy.
黃仁勛若離職，將使這家頂尖的人工智慧晶片製造商英偉達迎來充滿不確定性的新篇章，並引發廣泛的連鎖反應。這家矽谷公司是人工智慧熱潮的關鍵支柱，也是世界上最有價值的上市公司之一，據估計，在推動美國經濟發展的重大人工智慧項目所使用的計算機晶片市場中，該公司佔據了90%的份額。
That dominance has made Mr. Huang one of the wealthiest people in the world, as well as an ally of President Trump. Mr. Trump was previously informed about Mr. Huang’s relationship with the woman believed to be a Chinese intelligence asset but took no action. He has defended Mr. Huang over the past week, calling him an “honorable man” who did not know the woman was a Chinese agent.
這種主導地位使黃仁勛成為世界上最富有的人之一，也成為川普總統的盟友。川普此前已被告知黃仁勛與這名被認為是中國情報人員的女性的關係，但沒有採取行動。過去一週，他一直為黃仁勛辯護，稱他是一個「正直的人」，並表示黃仁勛不知道這名女性是中國特工。
廣告
Few Silicon Valley executives, with the exception of Tesla’s Elon Musk and Apple’s Steve Jobs, have been as synonymous with their companies as Mr. Huang, 63. He manages all aspects of Nvidia’s business as chief executive, and his family members have steadily risen through the company’s ranks. He is also Nvidia’s largest individual shareholder with a 3.56 percent stake. He has had such a tight grip on the company that there is no clear successor.
除了特斯拉的伊隆·馬斯克和蘋果的史提夫·賈伯斯，很少有哪位矽谷高管能像63歲的黃仁勛那樣，與自己的公司如此密不可分。作為首席執行官，他管理著英偉達業務的方方面面，他的家庭成員也在公司內部穩步晉陞。他還是英偉達最大的個人股東，持股3.56%。他對公司控制得如此之緊，以至於沒有明確的繼任者。
Public scrutiny of Mr. Huang began after The New York Times first reported last month that American intelligence agencies had discovered that he had developed a close relationship with a Chinese citizen and chip buyer, who U.S. officials concluded was gathering intelligence for Beijing.
公眾對黃仁勛的審視始於《紐約時報》上個月率先發布的報導，報導稱美國情報機構發現他與一名中國公民兼晶片買家發展了密切關係，美國官員認定此人正在為北京收集情報。
The chip buyer was Alice Huang or Huang Le, whose biography said she worked in China as a TV reporter before becoming a top executive at a Singapore-based data center company that had become one of Nvidia’s biggest customers in Southeast Asia. It bought more than $4.8 billion in chips since June 2024.
這名晶片買家是黃樂（音，英文名為Alice Huang）。她的簡歷顯示，她曾在中國擔任電視記者，後來成為一家新加坡數據中心公司的高管，該公司已成為英偉達在東南亞最大的客戶之一。自2024年6月以來，該公司購買了超過48億美元的晶片。
China has denied the espionage claims. Ms. Huang has not responded to requests for comment.
中國否認了間諜指控。黃樂沒有回應置評請求。
On Wednesday, The Times reported that Mr. Huang also had an extramarital relationship with an executive who reports to him. Under Nvidia’s code of conduct, employees are told to “avoid situations where our actions create conflict” including “personal relationships,” and the company states that it “promptly investigates reports of misconduct.” Many corporations have policies against managers having relationships with subordinates.
週三，《紐約時報》報導稱，黃仁勛還與一名向他彙報的女高管有婚外情。根據英偉達的行為準則，員工應「避免因自身行為引發利益衝突的情況」，其中包括「個人關係」，公司聲明「會迅速調查對不當行為的報告」。許多公司都有規定，禁止經理與下屬發生關係。
