黃仁勛婚外情醜聞將其妻子置於聚光燈下

Jensen Huang’s Affair Puts His Wife in the Spotlight

ERIN GRIFFITH, DON CLARK • 2026-11-12
In November 2021, Jensen Huang attended the annual dinner held by the Semiconductor Industry Association in San Jose, Calif. That night, the trade group gave Mr. Huang, the chief executive of Nvidia, its highest honor, named for the Intel co-founder Robert Noyce.
2021年11月，黃仁勛出席了半導體行業協會在加利福尼亞州聖何塞舉辦的年度晚宴。當晚，該行業組織向身為英偉達首席執行官的黃仁勛頒發了以英特爾聯合創始人羅伯特·諾伊斯命名的最高榮譽。
Mr. Huang arrived early with his wife, Lori Huang, and quickly headed to the bar at the pre-dinner reception, saying the event was the first time he had been out of his house since the Covid lockdown began in March 2020. He introduced Ms. Huang to reporters who clustered nearby, but they mainly talked to him.
黃仁勛與妻子洛麗·黃早早抵達，並迅速走向晚宴前招待會的吧台，稱這次活動是他自2020年3月新冠疫情封鎖開始以來首次出門。他將洛麗·黃介紹給聚集在附近的記者，但記者們主要還是與他交談。
By the time Mr. Huang received the award, two hours had passed. The Nvidia leader walked onstage with a bottle of red wine and two glasses, sharing them with a moderator and taking the conversation into unexpected topics. Throughout, Ms. Huang remained in the background.
到黃仁勛領獎時，兩個小時已經過去了。這位英偉達領導者拿著一瓶紅酒和兩個酒杯走上舞台，與主持人分享，並將對話引向意想不到的話題。在此期間，洛麗·黃一直留在幕後。
For more than 40 years, Ms. Huang has been by Mr. Huang’s side as he built his fledgling chip start-up into one of the most valuable companies in the world. Nvidia’s success turned Mr. Huang into an international business star. His face is on sweaters, his signature leather jackets have become collectors items, and his net worth has soared to nearly $150 billion.
40多年來，黃仁勛將他剛剛起步的晶片初創公司打造成全球最具價值的企業，洛麗·黃全程一直陪伴在他身邊。英偉達的成功讓他成為國際商業明星。他的面孔出現在毛衣上，他的標誌性皮夾克成為收藏品，他的凈資產飆升至近1500億美元。
廣告
By contrast, Ms. Huang stayed far from the spotlight.
相比之下，洛麗·黃遠離聚光燈。
That changed this week, when Mr. Huang, 63, revealed he had an affair with an executive identified by the U.S. government as a Chinese spy. In his admission, which he shared in a message to Nvidia employees, Mr. Huang apologized to his wife, as well as his two children, who both work at Nvidia, thrusting them into the limelight.
這種情況在本週發生了改變，63歲的黃仁勛透露他與一名被美國政府認定為中國間諜的高管發生了婚外情。在向英偉達員工分享的信息中，黃仁勛向他的妻子以及都在英偉達工作的兩個孩子道歉，這讓他們突然進入了公眾的視野。
The bombshell news had implications for U.S. national security and the economy. Nvidia’s role as the A.I. industry’s main supplier of chips makes Mr. Huang a crucial linchpin for an industry that has come to dominate the stock market.
這則重磅新聞對美國國家安全和經濟產生了影響。英偉達作為人工智慧行業主要晶片供應商的角色使黃仁勛成為了一個已經主導股票市場的行業的關鍵核心人物。
It also has ramifications for his personal life. Mr. Huang’s admission has fueled speculation about the fate of his 41-year marriage with Ms. Huang, 64. She is president of the Jen-Hsun Lori Huang Foundation, which had $9 billion in assets as of 2024, including $6 billion in fresh funding that year. Of Mr. Huang’s 922 million Nvidia shares, more than half belong to a trust where Mr. Huang and Ms. Huang are the co-trustees, according to financial filings. She is also the sole controller of a trust that owns around $5 billion worth of Nvidia shares.
這也給他的個人生活帶來了影響。他的承認引發了關於他與64歲的洛麗·黃41年婚姻命運的猜測。她是黃仁勛與洛麗·黃基金會的主席，截至2024年，該基金會擁有90億美元資產，其中包括當年60億美元的新增資金。根據財務文件，在黃仁勛的9.22億股英偉達股票中，超過一半屬於一個信託基金，黃仁勛和洛麗·黃是該信託的共同受託人。她還是一個擁有價值約50億美元英偉達股票的信託基金的唯一控制人。
Ms. Huang joins a small group of tech titan spouses who have had to grapple with public infidelity. The list includes MacKenzie Scott, the ex-wife of the Amazon founder Jeff Bezos, and Melinda French Gates, ex-wife of the Microsoft co-founder Bill Gates. The billionaire ex-wife of a tech mogul has become such a fixture in American pop culture that it has inspired characters in “The Devil Wears Prada 2” and the Maya Rudolph comedy “Loot.”
洛麗·黃加入了一小群不得不應對公開不忠行為的科技巨頭配偶的行列。這份名單包括亞馬遜創始人傑夫·貝佐斯的前妻麥肯齊·史考特，以及微軟聯合創始人比爾·蓋茲的前妻梅琳達·弗蘭奇·蓋茲。科技大亨的億萬富翁前妻已經成為美國流行文化中的固定形象，以至於啟發了《穿普拉達的女王2》(The Devil Wears Prada 2)和瑪雅·魯道夫的喜劇《錢錢錢錢》(Loot)中的角色。
Ms. Huang’s public profile stands out among the current and former spouses of powerful business leaders because of how private she has been. She has not promoted her philanthropic work in interviews or on the conference circuit.
在位高權重的現任和前任商業領袖的配偶中，洛麗·黃的公眾形象因其低調私密而顯得與眾不同。她沒有在採訪或會議巡迴活動中宣傳過自己的慈善工作。
廣告
“Lori is unusually modest by the standards of billionaire couples,” said George Rush, who formerly coauthored Rush & Molloy, a long-running gossip column at The Daily News.
「以億萬富翁夫婦的標準來看，洛麗異乎尋常地低調，」曾在《每日新聞》(The Daily News)共同撰寫長期八卦專欄「拉什與莫洛伊」(Rush & Molloy)的喬治·拉什表示。
That adds further intrigue to the political and economic consequences of the affair, Mr. Rush noted. “Bezos and Gates didn’t have this dynamic, where the relationship could move markets and have this global fallout,” he said. “It is one of a kind.”
拉什指出，這使這起婚外情在政治和經濟後果上更加引人關注。「貝佐斯和蓋茲夫婦的關係不存在這種能夠撼動市場併產生全球性影響的特性，」他說。「這是獨一無二的。」
Nvidia declined to comment and Ms. Huang did not respond to a request for comment. (China has rejected the espionage claims. Alice Huang, the Chinese executive accused of spying for Beijing, has not responded to requests for comment.)
英偉達拒絕置評，洛麗·黃也沒有回應置評請求。（中國已否認間諜指控。被指控為北京從事間諜活動的中國高管黃樂未回應置評請求。）
Similar to other tech billionaire spouses like Ms. Scott, Ms. Huang was crucial to the start of her husband’s company.
與史考特等其他科技億萬富翁的配偶類似，洛麗·黃對她丈夫公司的起步至關重要。
She met Mr. Huang as one of a handful of women studying engineering at Oregon State University in the early 1980s. Mr. Huang was her lab partner in an electrical engineering fundamentals class, and he convinced her to join him for a weekly study date. He later called meeting her “the most important, single event of OSU, and of my life.”
20世紀80年代初，作為奧勒岡州立大學少數工程學專業女生之一，她結識了黃仁勛。後者是她在電氣工程基礎課上的實驗室搭檔，他曾說服她加入他每週一次的學習約會。後來他稱遇見她「是奧勒岡州立大學、也是我一生中發生的最重要的一件事」。
After the pair graduated and married, they moved to Silicon Valley to work as microchip designers. Both were known as gifted engineers. Ms. Huang worked at Silicon Graphics, a hardware and software maker, where her salary was higher than Mr. Huang’s at LSI Logic, a semiconductor company, he said in a 2023 profile in The New Yorker.
兩人畢業並結婚後搬到矽谷擔任微晶片設計師。兩人都作為才華橫溢的工程師而聞名。2023年《紐約客》的一篇人物專訪中，黃仁勛表示，洛麗·黃曾在硬件和軟體製造商硅圖公司工作，在那裡的薪水高於黃仁勛在半導體公司LSI邏輯公司的薪水。
But the couple struggled to find the right child care, according to “The Thinking Machine,” a biography of Mr. Huang. Just before he co-founded Nvidia in 1993, Ms. Huang gave up her career to raise their children, Spencer and Madison. While Mr. Huang missed their karate tournaments to build his company, Ms. Huang never complained, he recalled in May on the podcast How I Built This With Guy Raz. “That’s the gift that she gave me.”
但根據黃仁勛傳記《會思考的機器》(The Thinking Machine)所述，這對夫婦一度難以找到合適的幼兒照料服務。在1993年黃仁勛與人創立英偉達之前，洛麗·黃放棄了自己的事業來撫養他們的孩子黃勝斌(Spencer)和黃敏珊(Madison)。黃仁勛在5月份的播客How I Built This With Guy Raz中回憶道，當他為了建立公司而錯過子女的空手道錦標賽時，洛麗·黃從未抱怨過。「那是她給我的禮物。」
Mr. Huang has frequently shared a warm reverence for Ms. Huang in interviews. “It’s good to have someone in your life who unconditionally believes in you, who believes you’re doing your best,” he said in an interview with a publication run by Oregon State University in 2014. “You need people like that, and Lori’s always been that person for me — ever since I was 18, she was there believing in me and knowing I could do it. Even when I had self-doubts, she didn’t.”
在採訪中，黃仁勛經常表達對洛麗·黃深切的尊重。「生命中有一個無條件相信你、相信你正在盡力而為的人是件好事，」他在2014年接受奧勒岡州立大學運營的一家出版物採訪時說。「你需要這樣的人，而洛麗對我來說一直是那個人——從我18歲起，她就在那裡相信我，知道我能做到。甚至當我有自我懷疑時，她也沒有。」
廣告
When Mr. Huang’s Nvidia shares made him wealthy, the family set up an endowment that ballooned alongside their net worth. The foundation has donated to the San Francisco Opera and acquired computing power to donate to universities and nonprofits. The Huangs have sidestepped political donations.
隨著黃仁勛的英偉達股票使他變得富有，一家人設立了一個隨著他們凈資產激增而膨脹的捐贈基金。該基金會向舊金山歌劇院捐款，併購買算力捐贈給大學和非營利組織。兩夫婦不參與政治捐款。
Through it all, Ms. Huang was a frequent presence at Mr. Huang’s public events, including state dinners, conference appearances and celebrations of the couple’s donations to Stanford University and Oregon State University. She generally stayed in the background while he gave speeches and hobnobbed with industry executives and reporters.
在整個過程中，洛麗·黃頻繁出現在黃仁勛的公開活動中，包括國宴、大會亮相以及兩人向史丹佛大學和奧勒岡州立大學捐款的慶祝活動。當他發表演講並與行業高管和記者交談時，她通常留在幕後。
“She’s been to every conference, every speech, you know, and every moment of Nvidia, she was there,” he said in an interview this year with CNA, an Asian news network. “Lori’s been with me for a long time, our whole life.”
「她參加了每一次會議、每一次演講，你知道，英偉達的每一個時刻，她都在場，」黃仁勛在今年接受亞洲新聞網路CNA的採訪中說。「洛麗和我在一起很長時間了，我們的一生。」
She even helped shape his public image. Along with her daughter Madison, she dressed Mr. Huang in a designer leather jacket that has become his trademark look. Madison joined Nvidia in 2020 and Spencer joined the company in 2022. Madison’s husband, Nico Caprez, also works there.
她甚至幫助塑造了他的公眾形象。她和女兒黃敏珊一起讓黃仁勛穿上了一件設計師皮夾克，這成了他的標誌性造型。黃敏珊於2020年加入英偉達，黃勝斌也於2022年入職。黃敏珊的丈夫尼科·卡普雷茲也在那裡工作。
Mr. Huang ended his message to Nvidia employees about the affair with a request: “My family bears no responsibility for my choices. I do. Please give them privacy.”
在向英偉達員工發送的關於婚外情的信件末尾，黃仁勛提出了一個請求：「我的家人對我的選擇不承擔任何責任。責任在我。請給他們隱私。」
