黃仁勛被曝與英偉達一名女高管有染

Jensen Huang Said to Have Had Relationship With Nvidia Executive Who Reports to Him

RYAN MAC, TRIPP MICKLE, GEORGIA GEE • 2026-11-12
Jensen Huang, the leader of Nvidia who is under investigation for an affair with a woman who U.S. intelligence determined is a Chinese spy, also had an extramarital relationship with an executive who reports to him, according to five people who worked at the company.
英偉達領導人黃仁勛因與一名被美國情報機構認定為中國間諜的女子發生婚外情而受到調查，據五名曾在該公司工作的人士稱，他還與一名下屬高管有染。
The relationship, which has not been previously reported, was with Kimberly Powell, Nvidia’s vice president of health care, said the people, who spoke under the condition of anonymity to describe sensitive information. Under Nvidia’s code of conduct, employees are told to “avoid situations where our actions create conflict” including “personal relationships,” and the company states that it “promptly investigates reports of misconduct.” Many corporations have policies against managers having relationships with subordinates.
這些人因描述敏感信息而要求匿名，他們說，這段此前未被披露的婚外情涉及英偉達醫療保健業務副總裁金伯莉·鮑威爾。根據英偉達的行為準則，員工應「避免自身行為造成利益衝突的情況」，其中包括「私人關係」。公司還表示，會「及時調查有關不當行為的舉報」。許多企業都有禁止管理人員與下屬發生親密關係的規定。
Mr. Huang, 63, and Ms. Powell, 48, were captured on video being affectionate at a San Jose, Calif., hotel during a Nvidia conference in March, according to two of the people who worked at the company. The New York Times has not seen the video.
兩名曾在英偉達工作的人士透露，今年3月，在加利福尼亞州聖何塞舉行的一場英偉達會議期間，63歲的黃仁勛和48歲的鮑威爾被拍到在當地一家酒店舉止親密。《紐約時報》未看到這段影片。
Both Mr. Huang and Ms. Powell are married to other people.
黃仁勛和鮑威爾都是已婚人士。
廣告
Mr. Huang, who co-founded Nvidia in 1993 and has turned it into the premier supplier of artificial intelligence chips, has been under intensifying scrutiny. Last month, The New York Times reported that a Chinese executive he had become close to had been identified by U.S. officials as a Chinese intelligence asset.
黃仁勛在1993年跟他人共同創立英偉達，並將其發展為全球領先的人工智慧晶片供應商。他現在受到越來越嚴格的審視。上個月，《紐約時報》報導，一名與他關係密切的中國高管被美國官員認定為中國情報人員。
This month, after Mr. Huang acknowledged the affair, Nvidia’s board opened an investigation into the matter that is being led by the law firm Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz. The Republican leaders of three influential House committees have also asked Mr. Huang to appear for a closed-door briefing on his interactions with the woman, who goes by the name Alice Huang.
本月，在黃仁勛承認婚外情後，英偉達董事會對此事展開調查，由Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz負責調查工作。三個有影響力的眾議院委員會的共和黨領袖還要求黃仁勛就他與這個名為黃樂(Alice Huang)的女子之間的交往情況出席閉門簡報會。
China has denied the espionage claims. Ms. Huang, the Chinese executive accused of spying for Beijing, has not responded to requests for comment.
中國否認了間諜指控。被指控為北京從事間諜活動的中國高管黃樂沒有回應置評請求。
When reached by phone Wednesday and asked about his relationship with Ms. Powell, Mr. Huang said, “I don’t think you should be talking to me directly.” He then hung up.
週三，記者通過電話聯繫黃仁勛，詢問他與鮑威爾女士的關係時，黃仁勛回答說：「我認為你不應該直接跟我談這件事。」隨後掛斷了電話。
Nvidia declined to provide a statement. Ms. Powell did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
英偉達拒絕發表聲明。鮑威爾女士沒有立即回應置評請求。
Mr. Huang’s relationship with Ms. Powell has been discussed inside the company, the five people said.
前述五名知情人士表示，黃仁勛與鮑威爾的關係已在公司內部引發議論。
Ms. Powell joined Nvidia in 2008. She oversees programs that could apply Nvidia’s chip technology to higher education and health care, particularly medical imaging devices, according to the company’s website.
鮑威爾於2008年加入英偉達。據公司網站介紹，她負責英偉達晶片技術在高等教育和醫療保健領域的應用項目，尤其是醫學影像設備方面。
廣告
Ms. Powell has risen through Nvidia’s ranks. She was promoted to senior director of artificial intelligence business development in 2016, according to her LinkedIn. By late 2017, she was appointed vice president of health care. She attends technology conferences around the world, and delivers speeches on behalf of the company.
鮑威爾在英偉達一路晉陞。根據她在領英上的個人資料，2016年，她被提拔為人工智慧業務開發高級總監。到2017年底，她被任命為醫療保健業務副總裁。她經常參加世界各地的科技會議，並代表公司發表演講。
In March, Ms. Powell appeared at GTC, Nvidia’s annual developer conference in San Jose, where she spoke about using A.I. to aid in breakthroughs in health care and biology. Her presentation used cartoon depictions of Mr. Huang.
今年3月，鮑威爾出席了英偉達在聖何塞舉行的年度開發者大會GTC，並就如何利用人工智慧推動醫療保健和生物學領域的突破發表演講。她的演示文稿中使用了黃仁勛的卡通形象。
During the conference, Nvidia executives were at a nearby hotel for meetings. Mr. Huang and Ms. Powell were seen being affectionate at the hotel. One bystander captured the interaction on their phone, three people briefed on the incident said.
大會期間，英偉達高管在附近一家酒店舉行會議。有人看到黃仁勛與鮑威爾在酒店舉止親密。三名知情人士說，一名旁觀者用手機拍下了兩人的互動影片。
Mr. Huang’s security witnessed the bystander with their phone and sequestered them, the people said. They made the person an offer to sign a nondisclosure agreement.
這些人士表示，黃仁勛的安保人員發現這名在場者用手機拍攝後將其帶到一旁，並提出讓此人簽署保密協議。
Mr. Huang is among a long list of chief executives of American companies who have had relationships with subordinates. Brian Krzanich resigned in 2018 from Intel, the chip maker, after acknowledging a “past consensual relationship” with an employee.
黃仁勛只是眾多與下屬有過親密關係的美國企業首席執行官之一。2018年，晶片製造商英特爾的首席執行官布賴恩·克爾扎尼奇承認曾與一名員工存在「雙方自願的親密關係」，隨後辭職。
The following year, McDonald’s removed Steve Easterbrook as its chief executive and later sued him after learning he had violated corporate policies by maintaining sexual relationships with multiple employees.
次年，麥當勞解除了史提夫·伊斯特布魯克的首席執行官職務，後來又對他提起訴訟。此前，公司發現他與多名員工保持性關係，違反了公司政策。
