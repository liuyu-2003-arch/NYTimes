馬斯克等矽谷盟友力挺黃仁勛

Tech Allies Rally Behind Jensen Huang, Calling Him Vital in A.I. Race

EMMY MARTIN • 2026-11-12
Within hours of Jensen Huang’s admission on Sunday that he had been unfaithful to his wife, some of Silicon Valley’s most influential investors closed ranks around him.
黃仁勛週日承認對妻子不忠後數小時內，矽谷一些最有影響力的投資人便紛紛站出來力挺他。
Their messages, delivered mostly on social media, were consistent. Mr. Huang’s relationship with a woman identified by U.S. officials as a Chinese spy was a personal failing. Losing the Nvidia chief executive, they argued, would be a far bigger blow to the country, and America couldn’t win the artificial intelligence race without him.
他們主要通過社群媒體發聲，傳遞的信息高度一致：黃仁勛與一名被美國官員認定為中國間諜的女子有染是個人過失。他們認為，若失去這位英偉達首席執行官，對國家將是大得多的打擊，沒有黃仁勛，美國就無法贏得人工智慧競賽。
“Jensen is one of the greatest entrepreneurs of this generation,” Shaun Maguire, a partner at Sequoia Capital, wrote in a post on Sunday afternoon. He said that Mr. Huang “did something incredibly dumb,” and that it was bad for America, “but my guess is there was no bad intention.” He concluded that Mr. Huang is “irreplaceable for America’s AI leadership.”
「黃仁勛是這一代最傑出的企業家之一，」紅杉資本合伙人肖恩·馬奎爾週日下午發帖寫道。他表示，黃仁勛「做了一件極其愚蠢的事」，這對美國不利，「但我猜他並無惡意」。他最後總結說，黃仁勛「對於美國在人工智慧領域的領導地位而言，是不可替代的」。
About an hour later, Elon Musk quoted the post and added: “I agree.”
大約一個小時後，伊隆·馬斯克轉發了這篇帖文，並補充道：「我同意。」
廣告
The show of loyalty reflects Mr. Huang’s rarefied standing in Silicon Valley. In the three decades since he co-founded Nvidia, he has gone from running a maker of video game graphics cards to presiding over the company at the center of the A.I. boom, now one of the world’s most valuable companies.
這些支持反映了黃仁勛在矽谷舉足輕重的地位。自30多年前與他人共同創立英偉達以來，他從一家電玩遊戲顯卡製造商的掌門人一步步成為人工智慧熱潮核心企業的領導者。如今，英偉達已是全球市值最高的公司之一。
Nvidia’s chips are the scarce resource every A.I. company needs, a position that makes Mr. Huang something of a kingmaker. Decisions about who gets his products, and how many, can shape which start-ups and tech giants pull ahead. Nvidia’s finances are also deeply intertwined with other leading A.I. companies, including OpenAI and Anthropic.
英偉達的晶片是所有人工智慧公司都需要的稀缺資源，這讓黃仁勛在某種程度上成為了「造王者」。誰獲得他的產品、獲得多少都可能影響哪些初創企業和科技巨頭能夠脫穎而出。英偉達的財務也與OpenAI和Anthropic等領先的人工智慧公司有著密切聯繫。
(China has rejected the espionage claims. Alice Huang, the Chinese executive accused of spying for Beijing, has not responded to requests for comment.)
（中國否認了相關指控。被指為北京從事間諜活動的中國女高管黃樂[Alice Huang]尚未回應置評請求。）
Bill Ackman, the billionaire hedge fund manager, called Mr. Huang “an incredible human” who took “full responsibility as he should,” and urged people to “support him and his family.” Later on Sunday, Mr. Ackman added that he was “not suggesting he gets a free pass.”
億萬富豪、對沖基金經理比爾·阿克曼稱黃仁勛是「一個了不起的人」，他「承擔了自己理應承擔的全部責任」，並呼籲人們「支持他和他的家人」。週日晚些時候，阿克曼又補充說，他「並不是說應該讓他免於承擔責任」。
On Monday, the investor Gavin Baker argued that many venture capitalists were “patriotic Americans” who believed Mr. Huang’s policy views served the country against China. He wrote that it would be “a disaster for America — and a gift to China — if Jensen goes down.”
週一，投資人加文·貝克主張，許多風險投資家是「愛國的美國人」，他們相信黃仁勛的政策主張有助於美國在對華競爭中取勝。他寫道，「如果黃仁勛倒下，對美國將是一場災難，卻是送給中國的一份大禮。」
“100% in agreement,” replied Jason Calacanis, a host of the All-In podcast, which is a popular podcast hosted by a group of venture capitalists including David Sacks, President Trump’s former A.I. and crypto czar.
「百分之百同意，」傑森·卡拉卡尼斯回覆道。他是熱門播客節目《All-In》的主持人之一。該節目由一群風險投資家共同主持，其中包括曾任川普總統人工智慧與加密貨幣事務主管的戴維·薩克斯。
廣告
Antonio Gracias, the founder of Valor Equity Partners and a longtime associate of Mr. Musk, agreed on Tuesday: “America would not be ahead in the AI race without Jensen,” he wrote. “He clearly made a mistake. One mistake does not erase a lifetime of leadership.”
週二，Valor Equity Partners創始人、馬斯克的多年商業夥伴安東尼奧·格拉西亞斯也表示贊同。「沒有黃仁勛，美國就不可能在人工智慧競賽中領先，」他寫道。「他顯然犯了一個錯誤。但一次錯誤不能抹殺他一生的領導成就。」
The All-In hosts have been close to Mr. Huang. During an appearance onstage with the podcast’s hosts in September, Mr. Huang took a phone call from Mr. Trump.
《All-In》的幾位主持人與黃仁勛關係密切。今年9月，黃仁勛與該播客的主持人一同登台時，還接到了川普的電話。
Mr. Trump, too, has stood by Mr. Huang. “I really believe him,” he said on Monday, recalling a conversation when Mr. Huang told him he did not know that Ms. Huang was a spy. The Times has reported that Mr. Trump was briefed a year ago about intelligence showing that Ms. Huang was an operative for the Chinese government. Mr. Trump on Tuesday demurred when asked whether he received the briefing last year.
川普也支持黃仁勛。「我真的相信他，」他週一說，並回憶起黃仁勛告訴他不知道黃樂是間諜的那次談話。《紐約時報》報導，川普一年前聽取過情報簡報，顯示黃樂是中國政府特工。週二，當被問及去年是否收到該簡報時，川普沒有正面回應。
Mr. Huang has been among Washington’s most forceful voices against cutting China off from American chips, and has been an ally of Mr. Trump since his return to office. That, Mr. Calacanis acknowledged, makes him and his company “a critical target, and we need to protect them both.”
黃仁勛一直是華盛頓反對切斷中國獲取美國晶片管道的最有力聲音之一，自川普重返白宮以來，他也是川普的盟友。卡拉卡尼斯承認，這使黃仁勛及其公司成為「重點針對的目標，我們需要保護他們兩者」。
In Congress, the Republican leaders of three influential House committees have asked Mr. Huang to appear for a closed-door briefing on his interactions with Ms. Huang.
在國會，眾議院三個具有重要影響力的委員會的共和黨領袖已要求黃仁勛出席一場閉門情況通報會，說明他與黃樂之間的交往情況。
The Times reported last month that U.S. officials had concluded Ms. Huang, a former executive at Megaspeed, a Singapore company that bought billions of dollars of Nvidia chips, had been recruited by China’s Ministry of State Security.
《紐約時報》上個月報導稱，美國官員認定，黃樂已被中國國家安全部招募。她曾在新加坡公司Megaspeed擔任高管，該公司購買了價值數十億美元的英偉達晶片。
廣告
Nvidia’s board has opened an investigation, and says it has no reason to believe national security or company secrets were compromised. Beijing dismissed the allegations as smears.
英偉達董事會已經展開調查，並表示目前沒有理由認為國家安全或公司機密受到損害。北京方面則斥責這些指控是污衊抹黑。
