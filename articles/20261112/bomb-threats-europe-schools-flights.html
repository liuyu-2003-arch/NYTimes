歐洲接連發生炸彈威脅事件

Hundreds of Schools in Italy Are Evacuated as More Bomb Threats Rattle Europe

JONATHAN WOLFE, LEO SANDS, ELISABETTA POVOLEDO, ALI WATKINS • 2026-11-12
Hundreds of schools in Italy and elsewhere in Europe were evacuated on Wednesday after receiving emailed bomb threats that were determined to be hoaxes, officials said, adding to disruptions after flights and news outlets across the continent were targeted with similar threats last week.
官員表示，義大利及歐洲其他地區數百所學校週三收到含有炸彈威脅的電子郵件後實施疏散，這些威脅後被認定為虛假信息。此前一週，歐洲大陸多架航班和多家新聞機構也遭遇類似威脅，進一步加劇了混亂。
The latest messages, which were also sent to schools in Scotland and Northern Ireland, appeared to be part of a coordinated campaign to sow confusion across Europe, officials said, even as they indicated that they did not know who was behind them.
官員表示，最新一批信息還發送給了蘇格蘭和北愛爾蘭的學校，似乎是一場意圖在歐洲各地製造混亂的協同行動的一部分，儘管他們同時表示尚不清楚幕後主使是誰。
Italy’s education minister, Giuseppe Valditara, said that at least 300 schools had received the anonymous threats. He said that Italy’s intelligence agencies were investigating and suggested a link to other recent disruptions in Europe, including apparent cyberattacks on supermarket refrigeration systems and blood banks.
義大利教育部長朱塞佩·瓦爾迪塔拉表示，至少300所學校收到了匿名威脅。他稱義大利情報機構正在調查，並暗示此事與歐洲近期其他干擾事件存在關聯，包括針對超市製冷系統和血庫的疑似網路攻擊。
“It’s not the first time,” Mr. Valditara told “l’Aria che Tira,” a morning talk show on the La7 channel in Italy. “We obviously need to be vigilant, but we must also be aware that in a context where hybrid warfare is increasingly being used as a tool to destabilize nations and economies, we need to prepare ourselves.”
瓦爾迪塔拉對義大利La7頻道早間脫口秀節目《風向》(L’Aria che Tira)表示：「這不是第一次了。我們顯然需要保持警惕，但也必須意識到，在混合戰爭日益被用作破壞國家和經濟穩定工具的背景之下，我們需要做好準備。」
廣告
Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni of Italy called a meeting for Wednesday afternoon with ministers and cybersecurity officials but said that “at this time, the investigations conducted have not revealed any evidence to suggest that a concrete danger exists.”
義大利總理焦爾吉婭·梅洛尼週三下午召集部長和網路安全官員開會，但她表示「目前進行的調查未發現任何證據表明存在具體危險」。
“Rather,” she added in a statement on social media, “they appear to be part of a strategy aimed at causing alarm and concern.”
「倒像是一種製造恐慌和憂慮的策略的一部分，」她在社群媒體聲明中補充道。
The police in Scotland said that “threatening messages were sent to a number of schools” on Wednesday morning. “These messages were immediately assessed and are being treated as hoaxes,” the police said in a statement, adding that they were not advising schools to evacuate.
蘇格蘭警方表示，週三上午「威脅信息被發送給了多所學校」。「這些信息經即時評估後被認定為虛假威脅，」警方在聲明中說，並表示不建議學校疏散。
Similar emails sent to schools across Northern Ireland were “being treated as a hoax,” the country’s Education Authority said in a statement, adding, “We strongly condemn these malicious emails and the distress and disruption caused to school communities.”
北愛爾蘭教育局在聲明中表示，發送給多所學校的類似電子郵件「正被作為虛假威脅處理」，並稱「我們強烈譴責這些惡意郵件及其給學校社區造成的痛苦和混亂」。
Officials have emphasized that the extent of the threats remains limited. The number of flights affected last week, for example, was a fraction of the tens of thousands that operate daily in Europe. But anxiety is running high on the continent, where hybrid warfare attacks and intimidation tactics have shaken air travel and other industries in recent months as Russia’s war against Ukraine spills over its borders.
官員強調，威脅的範圍仍然有限。例如，上週受影響的航班數量僅是歐洲每日數萬架次中的一小部分。但整個歐洲大陸的焦慮情緒持續高漲。近幾個月來，混合戰爭攻擊和恐嚇手段已動搖航空旅行及其他行業，與此同時俄羅斯對烏克蘭的戰爭外溢至其邊境之外。
What happened last week?
上週發生了什麼？
Bomb threats targeted several European airlines, disrupting dozens of flights and forcing emergency landings, some accompanied by military jets. No explosives were found on any of the flights, according to officials.
炸彈威脅針對多家歐洲航空公司，擾亂了數十架次航班並迫使緊急降落，部分航班有戰機伴飛。官員稱，所有航班上均未發現爆炸物。
On Friday, Czech police said that Prague’s main airport had received threats of explosives placed aboard arriving and departing flights. The Hungarian defense minister, Romulusz Ruszin-Szendi, said that Hungarian fighter jets had temporarily escorted one of the flights.
週五，捷克警方表示布拉格主要機場收到威脅，稱進出港航班上被放置了爆炸物。匈牙利國防部長羅穆盧斯·魯辛-森迪表示，匈牙利戰機曾臨時為一架航班護航。
廣告
Officials at Copenhagen’s airport also said that Danish police had responded to threats to flights, without giving details.
哥本哈根機場官員也表示，丹麥警方已對航班所受威脅作出響應，但未提供細節。
A day earlier, officials said that targeted flights included an Aer Lingus plane flying to Rome from Dublin that was diverted to an air base in France; four planes heading to Prague; two LOT Polish Airlines flights, bound for Paris and Brussels; and two Aegean Airlines flights, from Thessaloniki to Milan and from Athens to Vienna. A series of similar threats on Nov. 3 disrupted flights in Poland, Spain, Bulgaria and Italy.
一天前，官員稱受影響的航班包括一架從都柏林飛往羅馬的愛爾蘭航空飛機，該航班備降法國一處空軍基地；四架飛往布拉格的飛機；兩個波蘭航空航班，分別飛往巴黎和布魯塞爾；以及兩個愛琴海航空航班，分別從塞薩洛尼基飛往米蘭、從雅典飛往維也納。11月3日的一系列類似威脅擾亂了波蘭、西班牙、保加利亞和義大利的航班。
Nerves were further rattled on Friday after the offices of several news media outlets in Ireland and other European countries were evacuated following bomb threats that turned out to be hoaxes. Nova, a Bulgarian news media organization, reported that it had received a bomb threat by email that also claimed responsibility for the threats against planes.
週五，愛爾蘭及其他歐洲國家多家新聞媒體機構的辦公室在收到炸彈威脅後實施疏散，這些威脅後被證明是虛假的，進一步加劇了緊張情緒。保加利亞新聞機構Nova報導稱其收到一封電子郵件炸彈威脅，該郵件還聲稱對針對飛機的威脅負責。
What are officials saying?
官員們怎麼說？
On Friday, after the threats to media companies, the Irish government said in a statement: “It seems likely that this is a coordinated campaign of disruption.”
週五，在媒體公司受到威脅後，愛爾蘭政府在聲明中表示：「這似乎很可能是一場協同的擾亂行動。」
Heidi Alexander, Britain’s transport secretary, told British news media last Thursday that the government was taking the “malicious hoax threats” seriously, but that they were not believed to be credible.
英國交通大臣海蒂·亞歷山大上週四對英國新聞媒體表示，政府正在認真對待這些「惡意虛假威脅」，但認為它們並不可信。
廣告
Officials at Aeroporti di Roma, which manages Rome’s two commercial airports, said that one threat arrived Thursday morning in an email written in Italian with the subject line “threat notification.” A spokesman for the airport operator said that the email had been sent from “fighters of the male state” who said they wanted to “turn the skies red” with explosions.
管理羅馬兩座民用機場的羅馬機場公司官員表示，其中一條威脅於週四上午通過一封義大利語電子郵件送達，主題為「威脅通知」。該機場運營方發言人稱，郵件發件人是「男性國家的戰士」，聲稱他們想用爆炸「把天空變成一片紅色」。
Police Scotland noted that the messages on Wednesday were “sent widely to distribution lists across the U.K. and Northern Ireland and our assessment remains that they do not represent a credible threat.” The agency’s Cyber and Fraud Unit was investigating, it said.
蘇格蘭警察局指出，週三的信息「被廣泛發送至英國和北愛爾蘭的郵件列表，我們的評估仍然是它們不構成可信威脅」。該機構表示，其網路與欺詐部門正在調查。
What else has Europe on edge?
歐洲還有哪些事件令人不安？
A number of European countries have been affected by suspected cyberattacks on supermarket refrigeration systems, including France in October and Ireland, Sweden, Belgium and Luxembourg this month.
多個歐洲國家受到針對超市製冷系統的疑似網路攻擊影響，包括10月的法國以及本月愛爾蘭、瑞典、比利時和盧森堡。
On Monday, French officials accused Iran of orchestrating the October attack and issued international arrest warrants for three Iranians “affiliated with the Electronic and Cyber ​​Center of the Islamic Revolutionary Guards Corps.”
週一，法國官員指責伊朗策划了10月的攻擊，並對三名「隸屬於伊斯蘭革命衛隊電子與網路中心」的伊朗人發出國際逮捕令。
And on Nov. 4 France said that a cyberattack had targeted blood banks by raising the temperatures in storage refrigerators.
11月4日，法國表示血庫遭到網路攻擊，手段是提升儲存冰箱的溫度。
廣告
It is not clear whether the cyberattacks are linked to the bomb threats.
目前尚不清楚這些網路攻擊是否與炸彈威脅有關聯。
What is the goal?
目的是什麼？
Neither the identities of those making the threats nor their motives are clear. But some officials and experts have sought to find a logic behind the targets. For example, Ireland — where media companies and the national air carrier, Aer Lingus, received threats — is hosting summits of European and global leaders this week.
威脅實施者的身份和動機均不明確。但一些官員和專家試圖在目標中尋找邏輯。例如，愛爾蘭——媒體公司和國有航空公司愛爾蘭航空均在其境內收到威脅——本週正在主辦歐洲和全球領導人峰會。
Peter Neumann, a professor of security studies at King’s College London, said he saw hallmarks of a hybrid attack: The targets were diffuse, and the goal seemed to be disruption.
倫敦國王學院安全研究教授彼得·諾伊曼表示，他看到了混合攻擊的特徵：目標分散，目的似乎是製造擾亂。
“The aim of a hybrid campaign is to create chaos, uncertainty and a sense of threat,” he said.
「混合行動的目的是製造混亂、不確定性和威脅感，」他說。
(Hybrid warfare involves tactics, including sabotage and disinformation, that are used covertly to destabilize countries and erode trust in institutions without provoking a major military response.)
（混合戰涉及破壞和虛假信息等戰術，被祕密用於破壞國家穩定、侵蝕對機構的信任，同時不引發大規模軍事回應。）
廣告
But analysts agreed that it can be extremely difficult to attribute these kind of threats to any one actor or group with confidence. The Irish government’s statement said it was unclear whether the threats were from “individuals or a centralized or coordinated effort.”
但分析人士一致認為，要將此類威脅明確歸因於任何單一行為者或團體極為困難。愛爾蘭政府的聲明稱，尚不清楚威脅來自「個人還是統一協調的行動」。
“Bomb threats are incredibly cheap, and they are low-risk in the sense that they’re pretty easy to obscure,” said Rhys Jones, a security analyst at Osprey, an aviation risk consultancy, meaning a variety of people are capable of performing them, including teenagers or pranksters.
「炸彈威脅成本極低，而且風險很低，因為它們很容易隱匿，」航空風險諮詢公司Osprey的安全分析師裡斯·瓊斯說，這意味著各類人群都有能力實施，包括青少年或惡作劇者。
Why are the hoaxes so disruptive?
虛假威脅為何如此具有擾亂性？
Marco Chan, a lecturer in aviation operations at Kingston University in London, said that even if a bomb threat is assessed as a probable hoax, it is difficult for the authorities to determine that for certain without carrying out a thorough search.
倫敦金斯頓大學航空運營講師馬可·陳表示，即使炸彈威脅被評估為很可能是虛假的，當局若不進行徹底搜查，也難以確定。
As a result, he said, if a threat is received while a flight is in the air, the authorities have little choice but to ground the aircraft for emergency inspections.
他說，因此，如果航班在空中時收到威脅，當局別無選擇，只能讓飛機降落進行緊急檢查。
“If there is a shred of concern as to whether there is a bomb threat, they should always treat it as genuine,” Mr. Chan said.
「只要對是否存在炸彈威脅有一絲擔憂，他們就應始終將其當作真實威脅來對待，」他說。
