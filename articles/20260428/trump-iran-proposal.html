川普對伊朗和談方案表示不滿意

Trump Is Dissatisfied With Iran’s Plan to Reopen Strait of Hormuz

TYLER PAGER, JULIAN E. BARNES • 2026-04-28
President Trump has told advisers he is not satisfied with Iran’s latest proposal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and end the war, according to multiple people briefed on discussions in the White House Situation Room on Monday.
據多名了解週一白宮戰情室討論情況的人士透露，川普總統對伊朗提出的重新開放霍爾木茲海峽並結束戰爭的最新提議並不滿意。
The proposal also called on the United States to end its naval blockade but would have set aside questions about what to do with Iran’s nuclear program, according to U.S. and Iranian officials familiar with details of the negotiations.
據了解談判細節的美伊官員稱，該提議還要求美國解除海上封鎖，但將如何處理伊朗核計劃的問題擱置一旁。
Iran has repeatedly rejected American proposals to suspend its nuclear program and hand over its stockpile of highly enriched uranium.
伊朗一再拒絕美國提出的暫停核計劃並交出高濃縮鈾儲備的要求。
It is not clear precisely why Mr. Trump is not satisfied with the proposal, but he has repeatedly insisted that Iran cannot have nuclear weapons. A U.S. official also said that accepting it could appear to deny Mr. Trump a victory.
目前尚不清楚川普總統對提議不滿的確切原因，但他一再堅持伊朗不能擁有核武器。一名美國官員還表示，接受該方案可能會讓川普看起來未能取得勝利。
廣告
The White House declined to comment on Mr. Trump’s thinking, but officials noted that discussions would continue over the war and Tehran’s enrichment efforts.
白宮拒絕就川普的具體想法發表評論，但官員們表示，圍繞戰爭以及德黑蘭鈾濃縮活動的討論將繼續進行。
“The United States will not negotiate through the press — we have been clear about our red lines and the president will only make a deal that’s good for the American people and the world,” Olivia Wales, a White House spokeswoman, said in a statement.
「美國不會通過媒體進行談判——我們已明確了我們的紅線，總統只會達成對美國人民和世界有利的協議，」白宮發言人奧利維亞·威爾斯在一份聲明中表示。
Iran’s proposal to open the strait has been subject to a vigorous debate inside the administration over whether the United States or Iran has more leverage, and which country is better positioned to endure the economic hardship the closure of the waterway has created.
圍繞是否接受伊朗提出的開放海峽的方案，政府內部展開了激烈討論，爭論的焦點在於美國和伊朗誰擁有更多籌碼，以及在這條水道關閉所帶來的經濟壓力下，哪一方更有能力承受。
Mr. Trump reviewed the proposal with advisers on Monday after Iran’s foreign minister, Abbas Araghchi, delivered it to Pakistan on Sunday. Mr. Trump rejected another proposal from Iran last week and called off a round of peace talks in Islamabad over the weekend.
在伊朗外長阿拉格奇週日向巴基斯坦轉達該提議後，川普總統週一與顧問們一起對其進行了審議。川普上週已拒絕了伊朗的另一項提議，並在週末取消了在伊斯蘭瑪巴德舉行的一輪和談。
U.S. officials say Iran’s leadership has not authorized its negotiators to make concessions on the nuclear deal, frustrating any attempts to forge a compromise or peace agreement.
美國官員表示，伊朗領導層並未授權其談判代表在核協議問題上做出讓步，這使得任何促成妥協或和平協議的嘗試都陷入僵局。
Mr. Trump has expressed his frustration with Iran and its inability to negotiate with his administration.
川普對伊朗及其缺乏談判能力感到失望。
“Iran is having a very hard time figuring out who their leader is! They just don’t know!” Mr. Trump wrote on Truth Social on Thursday. “The infighting is between the ‘Hardliners,’ who have been losing BADLY on the battlefield, and the ‘Moderates,’ who are not very moderate at all (but gaining respect!), is CRAZY!”
「伊朗現在連誰是他們的領導人都弄不清楚！他們根本不知道！」川普週四在Truth Social上寫道。「『強硬派』在戰場上輸得很慘，『溫和派』其實一點也不溫和（但正在贏得尊重！），它們之間的內鬥簡直瘋狂！」
Delaying nuclear talks could have been a way to reach a quick agreement to ease pressure on global energy and financial markets. But any decision to halt nuclear talks, even temporarily, would be a sign that the war had failed to achieve one major goal: increasing pressure on Tehran to make a deal over its enrichment program.
推遲核談判本可以是一種儘快達成協議、緩解全球能源和金融市場壓力的方式。但任何暫停核談判的決定，即便是暫時的，都將意味著戰爭未能實現一個主要目標：加大對德黑蘭的壓力，迫使其在鈾濃縮計劃問題上達成協議。
廣告
Talks focused on reopening the strait would be fraught as well. The U.S. blockade has sought to cut off Iran’s ability to export its oil. But Iranian threats to attack ships that fail to pay a toll have drastically reduced other oil traffic.
圍繞重新開放海峽的談判同樣充滿風險。美國的封鎖意在切斷伊朗的石油出口能力，但伊朗威脅對未繳納通行費的船隻發動攻擊，已導致其他產油國經由該海峽的石油運輸大幅減少。
Iranian officials have insisted that any deal to open the strait should allow them to continue to impose a tax or fee on ships that transit it. Historically, the United States has opposed any such restrictions on freedom of navigation in international waterways or straits, but the Trump administration has sent mixed messages.
伊朗官員堅稱，任何開放海峽的協議都應允許他們繼續對過往船隻徵收稅款或費用。從歷史上看，美國一直反對對國際水道或海峽航行自由施加此類限制，但川普政府在這一問題上釋放了相互矛盾的信號。
At the heart of the debate over whether to accept the Iranian proposal were discussions in the Trump administration about the issue of economic leverage and what further American military operations would be needed to get Tehran to make significant concessions in negotiations, according to U.S. officials.
據美國官員稱，川普政府內部關於是否接受伊朗提議的辯論核心在於對經濟籌碼問題的討論，以及還需要採取哪些進一步軍事行動才能迫使德黑蘭在談判中做出重大讓步。
Some administration officials believe that continuing the blockade for two more months would cause significant long-term damage to Tehran’s energy industry. Oil wells cannot be turned on and off, and they would be damaged if they are forced to shut down, incurring costly repairs. Iran, these officials argue, will make a deal to avoid such long-term problems.
一些政府官員認為，將封鎖再維持兩個月，將對德黑蘭的能源產業造成嚴重的長期損害。油井不能隨意開關，如果被迫停產，油井將受損，需要高昂的維修費用。這些官員認為，伊朗會為了避免這種長期問題而達成協議。
But others in the administration have said the assessment is flawed, noting that Iran’s positions have hardened, and that the Islamic Revolutionary Guards Corps has only solidified its hold on power.
但政府內部也有人認為這種看法有誤，指出伊朗的立場已趨強硬，而且伊斯蘭革命衛隊對權力的掌控反而更加穩固了。
The U.S. government has assessed that Iranian negotiators have not been authorized — either by the supreme leader or by senior Revolutionary Guards officials — to make concessions on the nuclear program. Without a resumption of military action, there is little reason to think the Iranian position will shift.
美國政府評估認為，伊朗談判代表未獲得最高領袖或革命衛隊高層的授權，無法在核計劃上做出讓步。如果不恢復軍事行動，幾乎沒有理由相信伊朗的立場會發生改變。
廣告
Even if bombing resumed, there is little evidence that would alter Iran’s decision-making process. To increase pressure, Mr. Trump would need to authorize the attacks on civilian infrastructure.
即便恢復轟炸，也幾乎沒有證據表明這會改變伊朗的決策過程。即便恢復轟炸，也幾乎沒有證據表明這會改變伊朗的決策過程。若要進一步施壓，川普將需要授權對民用基礎設施發動攻擊。
Some administration officials have been skeptical that Iran is ready to make concessions and say making a deal to open the strait is the best way forward.
一些政府官員對伊朗是否準備讓步持懷疑態度，他們表示，目前最好的做法是先達成開放海峽的協議。
