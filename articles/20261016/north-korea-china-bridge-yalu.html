中朝貿易關係升溫

North Korea and China Open Their Biggest Border Bridge

CHOE SANG-HUN • 2026-10-16
North Korea and China on Thursday opened the New Yalu River Bridge, the largest border crossing between the two countries, a sign of growing trade despite international sanctions aimed at constraining North Korea’s economy and nuclear weapons program.
週四，朝鮮與中國開通了兩國間最大的邊境通道——新鴨綠江大橋。儘管旨在遏制朝鮮經濟及核武器計劃的國際制裁依然存在，此舉表明雙邊貿易正在不斷增長。
In recent weeks, travelers returning from the river border reported that trucks and cars were already crossing the four-lane, nearly two-mile long bridge from China into North Korea. The bridge connects the Chinese city of Dandong to Sinuiju, a North Korean city on the Yalu River that forms much of the border between the two countries.
最近幾週，從邊境江段返回的旅客反映，已有卡車和客車經由這座雙向四車道、長約三公里的大橋從中國駛入朝鮮。這座橋樑連接中國丹東市與坐落於鴨綠江畔的朝鮮城市新義州，鴨綠江構成了兩國間的大部分邊界。
Its opening, which was reported by the Chinese state media, comes just a month after North Korea and Russia inaugurated their first — and much smaller — road bridge across the Tumen River border. The consecutive bridge openings underscore Pyongyang’s deepening ties with its two main allies.
據中國官方媒體報導，這座橋樑通車距朝俄啟用橫跨圖們江邊境、規模小得多的首座公路橋僅相隔一個月。兩座跨境橋樑的相繼啟用凸顯出平壤正在深化與這兩大主要盟友的關係。
In a video address at the opening ceremony, Premier Li Qiang of China said the new bridge “will not only strongly promote bilateral economic and trade cooperation but also further strengthen people-to-people exchanges,” according to Chinese state media. Prime Minister Pak Thae-song of North Korea also delivered a speech via video, it said.
據中國官方媒體報導，中國國務院總理李強在開通儀式上發表影片致辭時表示，新大橋「不僅將有力促進兩國經貿合作，也將進一步密切雙方人員往來」。報導稱，朝鮮內閣總理朴泰成亦通過影片致辭。
廣告
Russia built its road bridge to supplement its lone rail link with North Korea after Pyongyang sent weapons and troops to aid Moscow’s war against Ukraine. As North Korea’s relationship with Russia has strengthened, China has moved to expand its own trade with Pyongyang in an effort to maintain its economic influence over its neighbor.
隨著平壤向對烏克蘭作戰的莫斯科提供武器和軍事人員援助，俄羅斯建造了公路橋，以彌補其與朝鮮間單一鐵路連接的不足。隨著朝俄關係不斷強化，中國也著手擴大對朝貿易，力求保持對該鄰國的經濟影響力。
Moscow and Beijing had already weakened the international sanctions against North Korea when they worked together in 2024 to dissolve the United Nations panel responsible for monitoring the enforcement of sanctions. Both countries have also urged Washington to ease restrictions on Pyongyang, arguing that the sanctions have failed to halt the country’s nuclear weapons development.
早在2024年，莫斯科與北京聯手解散了負責監督制裁執行情況的聯合國專家小組，從而削弱了針對朝鮮的國際制裁。兩國還敦促華盛頓放寬對朝限制，主張制裁併未能阻止朝鮮研發核武器。
Analysts warn that these new bridges could serve as a trade route for North Korea, whose economy has been strained by U.N. sanctions that prohibit key exports, including coal and labor, and severely restrict fuel imports.
分析人士警告稱，這些新建橋樑可能成為朝鮮的貿易通道。受制於聯合國禁止煤炭和勞工等關鍵出口並嚴控燃料進口的制裁措施，朝鮮經濟此前已承受重壓。
Until now, Sinuiju and Dandong were linked primarily by the 83-year-old Sino-Korean Friendship Bridge, which features just a single rail line and one road lane. The aging structure could not hold trucks weighing over 20 tons and had a strict three-mile-per-hour speed limit. In 2009, China proposed building the wider New Yalu River Bridge as part of broader efforts to bring North Korea further into its economic sphere.
此前，新義州與丹東主要依靠擁有83年歷史的中朝友誼橋相連，該橋僅設一條單軌鐵路和一條公路。這座老化的橋樑無法承載20噸以上的貨車，且需嚴格保持每小時約4.8公里的限速。2009年，中國提議建造橋面更寬的新鴨綠江大橋，作為將朝鮮進一步納入自身經濟輻射範圍的更廣泛舉措之一。
Construction was completed in 2014, with China footing the bill estimated at $329 million. But the opening was delayed for 12 years, as North Korea took time building connecting roads, customs stations and supporting facilities.
該工程於2014年竣工，耗資約3.29億美元且全額由中方承擔。但因朝方耗時修建連接道路、海關檢查站及配套設施，通車時間被推遲了12年。
But Pyongyang was also concerned about becoming even more dependent on China. Decades of international sanctions have left North Korea reliant on China for nearly all of its external trade. After the U.N. Security Council blacklisted North Korea’s top exports, the country’s trade deficit with China ballooned.
平壤方面也擔憂對華依賴進一步加深。數十年的國際制裁使朝鮮在幾乎所有對外貿易上均依賴中國；在聯合國安理會將朝鮮主要出口產品列入黑名單後，該國對華貿易逆差急劇攀升。
廣告
“From North Korea’s perspective, the opening of the New Yalu River Bridge could allow Chinese goods to enter more quickly and in larger volumes, with no guarantee that North Korea could significantly increase its own exports to China,” said Kim Gyu-beom, an analyst at the Seoul-based Institute for National Security Strategy. “There is little incentive for North Korea to welcome a scenario in which its trade deficit and dependence on China continue to deepen.”
位於首爾的國家安保戰略研究院分析師金奎范（音）表示：「從朝鮮的角度來看，新鴨綠江大橋的開通可能促使中國商品更快捷、更大規模地進入，卻無法確保朝鮮能大幅增加對華出口。」他指出：「若貿易逆差與對華依賴持續加深，這沒有理由成為朝鮮所樂見的前景。」
Analysts suggested that before agreeing to open the bridge, North Korea may have sought concessions that would facilitate exports to China and create opportunities for more North Korean workers to be employed there.
分析人士指出，在同意大橋通車前，朝鮮可能爭取過某些優惠條件，以便利本國對華出口，並為更多朝鮮勞工在華務工創造機會。
North Korea’s leader, Kim Jong-un, has faced the challenge of importing the Chinese raw materials and equipment needed to rebuild his economy while also raising hard currency. North Korea has largely accomplished the latter through illicit means such as smuggling, sending workers overseas, cyber operations and cryptocurrency theft.
朝鮮領導人金正恩面臨著雙重挑戰，既要進口重建其經濟所需的中國原材料和設備，又需籌集硬通貨。朝鮮主要通過走私、向海外輸出勞工、網路行動以及盜竊加密貨幣等非法手段來實現了外匯目標。
Mr. Kim pushed his country to produce more consumer goods domestically while cracking down on the smuggling of such products from China, using the Covid-19 pandemic as justification. He has also sought to reduce the chronic trade imbalance by attracting Chinese tourists to newly built coastal and mountain resorts across North Korea.
金正恩以新冠疫情為由嚴打自華走私消費品，同時要求國內增產此類產品。他還試圖通過吸引中國遊客前往朝鮮各地新建的沿海與山區度假勝地，來緩解長期的貿易失衡狀況。
China accelerated its re-engagement with North Korea after Pyongyang grew closer to Moscow during the Ukraine war. China’s leader, Xi Jinping, invited both Mr. Kim and President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia to a military parade in Beijing last year. Rail service between Beijing and Pyongyang, suspended during the pandemic, resumed in March, and Mr. Xi visited Pyongyang in June.
隨著平壤在烏克蘭戰爭期間向莫斯科靠攏，中國加快了與朝鮮重新接觸的步伐。去年，中國領導人習近平邀請金正恩與俄羅斯總統弗拉基米爾·V·普丁出席北京的閱兵式。疫情期間暫停的京平客運列車已於3月恢復運行，習近平6月訪問了平壤。
Official trade between North Korea and China reached $1.5 billion in the first half of this year, according to Chinese data, hitting its highest level since comprehensive U.N. sanctions were fully implemented in 2017.
中國官方數據顯示，今年上半年朝中官方貿易額達15億美元，創下2017年聯合國全面制裁完全落地以來的最高水平。

Choe Sang-Hun是時報駐首爾首席記者，報導韓國和朝鮮新聞。
