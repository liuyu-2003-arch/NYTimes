中國車企成巴黎車展焦點，加速布局歐洲市場

Chinese Carmakers, Once a Sideshow, Take Center Stage

JAMES PATON • 2026-10-16
With the spotlight on car giants like General Motors and Toyota, visitors to the Paris Motor Show in 2006 might have missed two newcomers: Landwind and Great Wall Motor from China.
2006年的巴黎車展上，通用汽車和豐田等汽車巨頭備受矚目，參觀者們可能錯過了兩個新面孔：來自中國的陸風和長城汽車。
Two decades later, no one could possibly overlook Chinese carmakers at the biennial gathering. China’s ambition is palpable throughout the sprawling Paris Expo Porte de Versailles this week.
20年後，在這個兩年一度的展會上，再也沒有人會忽視中國汽車製造商。本週，在佔地廣闊的巴黎凡爾賽門展覽中心，中國的雄心隨處可見。
More than 20 Chinese brands are showing off their designs and seeking to strengthen their European ties. One company, GAC, publicized a partnership with Chelsea F.C., the Premier League soccer club. Another, BYD, promoted its sponsorship deal with the hometown team, Paris St.-Germain. Geely introduced a hybrid electric-gasoline sport utility vehicle with a “born for Europe” slogan.
20多個中國品牌在此展示其設計，並尋求加強與歐洲的聯繫。廣汽宣傳與英超足球俱樂部切爾西的合作。另一家公司比亞迪則宣傳其與這座城市的主場球隊巴黎聖日耳曼的贊助協議。吉利推出了一款油電混合動力運動型多用途車，宣傳口號是「為歐洲而生」。
Despite Europe’s efforts to curb an influx of China’s low-cost cars, Chinese companies show no signs of slowing down. Shut out of the United States and facing weak demand at home, they are aiming to build on their rapid gains in Europe and expand local production to stay ahead of tougher trade restrictions.
儘管歐洲努力遏制中國低成本汽車的湧入，中國公司卻沒有放緩腳步的跡象。它們被擋在美國之外，又面臨國內需求疲軟，因此正致力於鞏固在歐洲的快速增長勢頭，並擴大當地生產，以應對日益嚴厲的貿易限制。
廣告
Xpeng is one example. The Chinese company is taking an “in-Europe, for-Europe” approach, He Xiaopeng, a co-founder, said in a speech on Tuesday. The company plans to focus on “localizing” production in Europe as it moves toward a target of selling about 500,000 vehicles a year in the region by 2030. Xpeng is open to sharing its technology with European companies, he said.
小鵬就是一個例子。這家中國公司的聯合創始人何小鵬在週二的演講中表示，公司正在採取「在歐洲、為歐洲」的方式。該公司計劃專注於在歐洲實現「本地化」生產，朝著到2030年在該地區年銷量約50萬輛的目標邁進。他說，小鵬願意與歐洲公司分享其技術。
“We want to have deeper collaboration in Europe,” he said.
「我們希望在歐洲進行更深入的合作，」他說。
Anne-Laure Paulic is one potential customer. She and her family might replace their BMW — and buy their first Chinese car. Ms. Paulic, a consultant, and her husband took the train to Paris from their home in Brittany in northwest France to check out the newest models.
安娜-洛爾·波利克就是一位潛在客戶。她和家人可能會換掉他們的寶馬，併購買他們的第一輛中國汽車。波利克是一名顧問，她和丈夫從法國西北部布列塔尼的家中乘火車到巴黎去看最新車型。
“It’s a nice surprise,” she said as she stepped out of a gleaming Xpeng G9L, which is produced in Austria and has an array of features increasingly common in Chinese cars: a 21-inch, ceiling-mounted screen; climate-controlled seats with massage; and a refrigerator. “I love the gadgets.”
「這是一個不錯的驚喜，」她在走出閃亮的小鵬G9L時說道。該車在奧地利生產，配備了一系列在中國汽車中越來越常見的功能：21英寸吸頂螢幕、帶按摩的溫控座椅和一台冰箱。「我喜歡這些小玩意。」
Spain has emerged as a prime destination for Chinese carmakers seeking partnerships with European and American rivals. Another electric car on display, Leapmotor’s B10, is produced at the Chinese company’s first European manufacturing facility, in Zaragoza, and developed with Stellantis.
西班牙已成為中國汽車製造商尋求與歐美競爭對手建立合作關係的首選目的地。展覽上的另一款電動汽車零跑B10在中國公司位於薩拉戈薩的首個歐洲製造工廠生產，與斯泰蘭蒂斯共同開發。
Geely is working with Ford Motor at the U.S. company’s plant in Valencia, SAIC plans to manufacture cars in Galicia and Chery has joined with a Spanish brand to produce cars in Barcelona.
吉利正與福特汽車在這家美國公司位於瓦倫西亞的工廠合作，上汽計劃在加利西亞生產汽車，奇瑞已與一個西班牙品牌合作，在巴賽隆納生產汽車。
Other countries are attracting Chinese companies, too. BYD, China’s dominant maker of electric vehicles, plans to produce cars in Hungary and is considering more factories, possibly in France. The trend is set to continue as struggling European companies turn to Chinese competitors to fill underused plants.
其他國家也在吸引中國公司。中國領先的電動汽車製造商比亞迪計劃在匈牙利生產汽車，並正在考慮更多工廠，可能選址在法國。隨著陷入困境的歐洲公司轉向中國競爭對手以填補產能利用率不足的工廠，這一趨勢勢必會繼續。
廣告
Chinese companies are aiming to produce more cars in Europe as the region’s policymakers try to curtail their exports. European Union officials said a deal with Beijing on Friday could halve the number of Chinese hybrid cars sent to the bloc in the next four years, helping to alleviate pressure on the European car industry. Officials are also discussing proposed rules that would prioritize European companies when public money is at stake.
中國公司正尋求在歐洲生產更多汽車，而歐洲政策制定者試圖限制中國公司的出口。歐盟官員表示，週五與北京達成的一項協議可能使中國在未來四年向歐盟出口的混合動力汽車數量減半，有助於緩解歐洲汽車工業的壓力。官員們還在討論一項擬議規則，在涉及公共資金時優先考慮歐洲公司。
France’s president, Emmanuel Macron, who visited the event on Monday, has said Europe’s industries face a “life or death” moment.
法國總統馬克宏週一參觀了展會，他曾表示歐洲工業正面臨「生死攸關」的時刻。
Still, efforts to stem the flow could be just “a temporary respite, as in the medium term we see Chinese manufacturers near-shoring more to the E.U.,” said Matthias Schmidt, an analyst in Germany. Restricting supplies could also allow Chinese companies to raise prices, benefiting those manufacturers in Europe over time.
不過，德國分析師馬蒂亞斯·施密特說，遏制這股潮流的努力可能只是「暫時的喘息，因為從中期來看，我們看到中國製造商更多地向歐盟近岸外包」。限制供應也可能讓中國公司提高價格，隨著時間推移，這會使它們在歐洲的製造商受益。
China’s rise is a new chapter for the Paris show, first held at the Jardin des Tuileries in 1898. Although companies such as Renault are keen to demonstrate their prowess, one prominent European company — BMW — opted not to attend.
中國的崛起是巴黎車展的新篇章。該車展於1898年首次在杜樂麗花園舉辦。此次儘管雷諾等公司急於展示其實力，但一家知名的歐洲公司——寶馬——此次卻選擇缺席。
Chinese companies “have a big foot in the door,” said Serge Gachot, the director of the event and a former executive at Toyota and other companies. “They are very disruptive and fast moving.”
車展總監、曾任豐田等公司高管的塞爾日·加肖說，中國公司「已經一隻腳牢牢踏進門內」。「它們極具顛覆性，而且行動迅速。」
BYD’s sales in Europe have more than doubled this year, while sales of Chery and Leapmotor have grown at an even faster clip. After selling 700,000 cars back home, Xiaomi sees Germany as the foundation of its European expansion plans.
比亞迪今年在歐洲的銷量增長了一倍多，奇瑞和零跑的銷量增長更快。在中國本土售出70萬輛汽車後，小米將德國視為其歐洲擴張計劃的基礎。
廣告
Chinese cars already account for about one in every 10 cars sold in Europe. And by teaming up with Chinese companies to keep their factories running, European manufacturers could end up helping their rivals. Yet they may have little choice but to cooperate and learn from their Chinese counterparts, said Ferdinand Dudenhöffer, an analyst and former industry executive.
中國汽車目前已佔歐洲汽車銷量的約十分之一。而歐洲製造商若為了維持工廠運轉而與中國企業合作，最終可能會助長競爭對手的勢頭。但分析師、前行業高管費迪南德·杜登赫費爾說，它們可能別無選擇，只能合作，並向中國同行學習。
“You can’t stand alone,” he said.
「你不能單打獨鬥，」他說。
One of Zeekr’s mottos, “the power of a giant and a European soul,” is emblazoned on the wall of its exhibit. Sitting in the back of one of the Geely brand’s electric cars, its director in France, Rémy Aybaly, said he did not expect trade hurdles to hamper Zeekr’s ambitions.
極氪的口號之一「巨人的力量與歐洲的靈魂」醒目地展示在其展台牆上。坐在吉利品牌一款電動汽車的后座，該公司法國總監雷米·艾巴利表示，他不認為貿易障礙會阻礙極氪的雄心。
As for the Chinese companies, “you can see it as a threat, or you can see it as a chance” for Europe, he said.
他說，至於中國公司，「你可以將其視為威脅，也可以將其視為歐洲的機遇。」

James Paton是時報國際商業和經濟記者，常駐倫敦。
