塞爾維亞大選暴露右翼民粹潮流新弱點

Serbia’s Election Shows New Vulnerability for Nationalist Populists

ANDREW HIGGINS • 2026-10-27
When voters in Hungary rejected their seemingly invincible populist leader Viktor Orban in April, Andrej Borovac, a young designer in neighboring Serbia, was ecstatic, but he was downbeat about his own country’s populist strongman suffering the same fate.
今年4月，匈牙利選民拒絕了看似不可戰勝的民粹主義領導人維克托·歐爾班時，鄰國塞爾維亞的年輕設計師安德烈·博羅瓦茨欣喜若狂，但對於自己國家的民粹強人是否會遭遇同樣命運，他卻感到悲觀。
“That can never happen here,” he recalled thinking. Aleksandar Vucic, Serbia’s dominant political figure since 2012, looked immovable, given his grip — established over more than a decade in power — on security forces, nearly all major media outlets, the electoral commission and many other institutions. Or so he thought.
「這種事在塞爾維亞永遠不會發生，」他回憶自己當時的想法。亞歷山大·武契奇自2012年以來一直是塞爾維亞政壇的主導人物。憑藉十多年執政建立起來的對安全部隊、幾乎所有主要媒體、選舉委員會和許多其他機構的控制，他看上去不可撼動。至少博羅瓦茨當時是這麼想的。
On Sunday night, Mr. Borovac, 38, and two friends joined tens of thousands of people gathered near Parliament in Belgrade, the Serbian capital, to celebrate Mr. Vucic’s defeat in a pivotal election for a new legislature.
週日晚上，38歲的博羅瓦茨和兩個朋友加入了數萬人的行列，聚集在塞爾維亞首都貝爾格勒議會附近，慶祝武契奇在一場決定新立法機構的關鍵選舉中落敗。
“The Hungarian Spring has come to Serbia in the fall,” Mr. Borovac said late Sunday night, surrounded by revelers waving Serbian flags and setting off red flares. Nearby, a group of young Serbs danced for joy.
「匈牙利之春在秋天來到了塞爾維亞，」博羅瓦茨週日深夜說。周圍是揮舞塞爾維亞國旗、點燃紅色信號棒的歡慶者。附近，一群年輕塞爾維亞人歡快地跳舞。
At a time of rising liberal fears of surging support for right-wing nationalists, the formerly Communist East, an early pioneer of populist politics after the collapse of Soviet hegemony in 1989, is showing that tides can turn.
在自由派日益擔憂右翼民族主義者支持率飆升之際，曾經是共產主義陣營的東歐——作為1989年蘇聯霸權崩潰後民粹主義政治的早期先驅——正在表明潮流可以逆轉。
Elections this year in Hungary and Serbia, and in Poland in 2023, have given new life to mostly forgotten liberal hopes voiced in “The Power of the Powerless,” a 1978 book by Vaclav Havel, the Czech dissident who would later become president and a global icon for believers in liberal democracy.
今年匈牙利和塞爾維亞的選舉，以及2023年波蘭的選舉，讓瓦茨拉夫·哈維爾1978年所著的《無權者的權力》一書中提出的、很大程度上已被遺忘的自由主義希望重獲新生。哈維爾是捷克異見人士，後來成為總統，並成為自由民主信仰者的全球偶像。
廣告
Anger at rampant corruption helped glue together disparate and sometimes contradictory political forces, allowing them to campaign on an issue that resonated with a wider population upset by the conspicuous wealth of people connected to the governing party.
對普遍腐敗的憤怒幫助凝聚了不同且有時相互矛盾的政治力量，使他們能夠圍繞一個議題競選，而這個議題引起了更廣泛民眾的共鳴——這些民眾對執政黨相關人士顯赫的財富深感不滿。
In Germany, France and Italy and elsewhere in Western Europe, the anti-establishment nationalist camp has taken on the appearance of an invincible juggernaut, and it may yet show strongly in a raft of elections next year. But for the moment, in Eastern Europe and beyond, it is newly showing signs of strain.
在德國、法國、義大利和西歐其他地方，反建制民族主義陣營呈現出不可戰勝的巨無霸形象，並可能在明年一系列選舉中表現強勁。但就目前而言，在東歐及其他地區，該陣營新近顯示出承壓跡象。
On the same day as Serbia’s election, voters in Brazil narrowly rejected an all but preordained victory for the hard right and handed a win to the leftist president, Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva.
在塞爾維亞選舉的同一天，巴西選民以微弱優勢拒絕了強硬右翼幾乎預定的勝利，將勝利交給了左翼總統盧拉。
The Serbian election will also add to the discomfit of President Trump’s MAGA movement, which for now lags in many polls ahead of what is expected to be a difficult election next week. It had looked to the Serbian leader as a kindred spirit committed to defending national sovereignty and resisting European liberals. Mr. Trump’s family eyed it for business deals.
塞爾維亞選舉也將加劇川普總統MAGA運動的尷尬處境。在預計將於下週舉行的一場艱難選舉前夕，該運動目前在許多民調中均處於落後地位。這場運動曾把這位塞爾維亞領導人視為志同道合者，認為他致力於捍衛國家主權、抵抗歐洲自由派。川普家族還將塞爾維亞視為開展商業交易的潛在對象。
A few weeks before the election, the Conservative Political Action Conference, or CPAC, a MAGA-aligned American organization, announced that it would hold its first gathering in the Balkan region, to be held in Belgrade in November and “headlined by President Aleksandar Vucic.”
選舉前幾週，與美國MAGA結盟的組織「保守派政治行動會議」(CPAC)宣布，將於11月在貝爾格勒舉辦其在巴爾幹地區的首次集會，屆時「亞歷山大·武契奇總統將作為主講嘉賓出席」。
CPAC had previously focused its European activities on Hungary, where it held a big annual gathering, now in doubt following Mr. Orban’s defeat and the end of state funding by the new government to foundations that helped bankroll various ventures by “anti-woke” warriors from abroad.
CPAC此前將其歐洲活動集中在匈牙利，在那裡舉行大型年度集會。如今，在歐爾班落敗、新政府停止向那些曾資助海外「反覺醒」鬥士各類行動的基金會提供國家撥款，這一集會能否舉行已成疑問。
廣告
Even in Eastern Europe, the momentum has not been all one-way, and it often zigzags. Parliamentary elections in Slovenia this year and the Czech Republic last year as well as a presidential vote in Poland all saw nationalist forces prevail, albeit by tiny margins.
即便在東歐，勢頭也並非全是單向的，而且常常曲折反覆。今年斯洛文尼亞和去年捷克共和國的議會選舉，以及波蘭的總統選舉，都是民族主義力量獲勝，儘管優勢微弱。
Also, the forces that have proved ascendant against the nationalist right are themselves a mixed bag that are far from being liberal globalists. Mr. Orban was defeated by a new party led by Peter Magyar, a conservative former Orban loyalist, and Mr. Vucic by a slate of student-selected candidates, nearly all newcomers to politics.
此外，那些在與民族主義右翼的較量中佔據上風的力量本身也是五花八門，遠非自由派全球主義者。歐爾班被彼得·馬扎爾領導的新政黨擊敗，馬扎爾是保守派、歐爾班的前忠實擁護者；武契奇則被一份由學生選出的候選人名單擊敗，這些人幾乎全是政壇新人。
Thorsten Benner, director of the Global Public Policy Institute in Berlin, said the lesson from both Hungary and Serbia was that “at some point, anti-incumbency bias or rage hits these authoritarians,” though the alternative “won’t be picture-perfect liberals.”
柏林全球公共政策研究所所長托爾斯滕·本納說，匈牙利和塞爾維亞的教訓是，「到了某個時候，反對現任者的偏見或憤怒會擊中這些威權主義者」，儘管替代者「也不會是完美無缺的自由派」。
“The real positive lesson,” he added “is that these authoritarians can actually lose elections and concede defeat.”
他還說，「真正積極的教訓是，這些威權主義者實際上可能輸掉選舉並承認失敗。」
The political party that underpinned Mr. Vucic’s long ascendancy is called the Serbian Progressive Party, hardly a right-wing outfit. It has railed against privatization and vowed to keep big chunks of the economy, which has grown robustly, under state control.
支撐武契奇長期執政的政黨叫塞爾維亞進步黨，很難算是一個右翼團體。該黨一直抨擊私有化，並誓言將經濟中增長強勁的大部分領域保持在國家控制之下。
After a record turnout of nearly 70 percent, his party won 39 percent of the vote compared with 52 percent for its main opponent, a new political grouping that emerged from nearly two years of student-led protest called Student List-Students Win.
在投票率創下近70%的歷史新高後，武契奇的政黨獲得39%的選票，而其主要對手贏得52%。主要對手是一個新的政治團體，誕生於近兩年學生領導的抗議，名為「學生名單—學生獲勝」。
廣告
The Student List has itself embraced nationalist themes. Many of the people rejoicing Sunday night on the street flashed the three-finger Serbian salute, a patriotic gesture used by marauding ethnic Serb troops during the Balkan wars of the 1990s.
「學生名單」本身也接納了民族主義主題。週日晚上在街頭歡慶的許多人比出塞爾維亞三指禮，這是20世紀90年代巴爾幹戰爭期間，橫行霸道的塞族部隊使用的一種愛國手勢。
Miljana Pejic, 33, a youth trainer who joined Sunday night’s street celebrations, said the salute in its current use was not a throwback to dark nationalist passions but a “reclaiming and reframing of Serb symbols” that had been hijacked.
33歲的青年培訓師米利亞娜·佩伊奇加入了週日晚上街頭的慶祝活動。她說，這種手勢在當下的使用並不是要回到黑暗的民族主義激情，而是「重新奪回和重新界定」被劫持的塞爾維亞象徵。
“We love our country so perhaps we are nationalist,” she said, “But everything the students advocate — the rule of law, anti-corruption, independent institutions — is in line with the values of the European Union,” she said.
「我們熱愛我們的國家，所以也許我們是民族主義者，」她說，「但學生們倡導的一切——法治、反腐敗、獨立機構——都符合歐盟的價值觀。」
Serbia applied to join the European bloc in 2009, but its application largely stalled under Mr. Vucic, whose brand of nationalism and illiberal politics closely resembled that of his ally Mr. Orban in Hungary.
塞爾維亞於2009年申請加入歐盟，但在武契奇治下，其申請基本停滯。武契奇的民族主義和非自由主義政治路線與他的盟友、匈牙利的歐爾班非常相似。
In the end, both men nonetheless conceded defeat when it became obvious that the groundswell of discontent from voters had overwhelmed their best, years-old attempts to tilt the political playing field their way, not least by manipulating the media.
最終，選民不滿的浪潮明顯壓倒了兩人多年來為使政治競爭環境偏向自己而作出的最大努力——尤其是操縱媒體，隨後這兩位領導人還是承認了失敗。
In an early concession speech Sunday evening, Mr. Vucic lamented that “they toppled us,” blaming his defeat on shadowy foreign forces. The election result, he said, “was the verification of a revolution carried out with direct support from various European countries and the American deep state — the one opposed to President Trump.”
在週日晚上早些時候的承認敗選演講中，武契奇哀嘆「他們推翻了我們」，並將失敗歸咎於神秘的外國勢力。他說，選舉結果「驗證了一場革命，這場革命得到各種歐洲國家和美國深層政府的直接支持——那個反對川普總統的深層政府」。
But his biggest problem, as was Mr. Orban’s, was that the election largely became a referendum on himself: he had stopped being popular and, accused of ties to gangsters, become instead a hate figure for large segments of the population, no matter what their political orientations.
但正如歐爾班一樣，他最大的問題是，這場選舉在很大程度上變成了對他本人的公投：他已不再受歡迎，被指控與黑幫有牽連，被各種政治取向的廣大民眾所仇恨。
廣告
Dimitar Bechev, a senior fellow at Carnegie Europe and an expert on the Balkans, said he was surprised by the opposition’s wide margin of victory but added: “You overstay your welcome after many years.”
卡內基歐洲高級研究員、巴爾幹問題專家迪米塔爾·貝切夫說，他對反對派以如此大的優勢獲勝感到驚訝，但他還說：「在位多年後，就會讓人感到厭倦。」
In both Hungary and Serbia, he said, “when you have a strongman figure, you have a coalition from liberals to people on the far right fed up.”
他說，在匈牙利和塞爾維亞，「一旦出現一個強人領袖，就會出現一個從自由派到厭倦的極右翼人士的聯盟」。
Bozo Prelevic, an opposition-aligned lawyer, on Monday compared Mr. Vucic to a malignant tumor. “Serbia has freed itself after a long and serious illness, a cancer that was destroying both society and the state, setting citizens against one another and invoking war,” he told N1, an opposition-friendly television chancel.
週一，與反對派結盟的律師博佐·普雷列維奇將武契奇比作惡性腫瘤。「塞爾維亞在經歷漫長而嚴重的疾病後解放了自己，這是一種正在摧毀社會和國家的癌症，它讓公民相互對立，並煽動戰爭，」他在親反對派的N1電視台上說。
It is unclear what the Student List’s victorious candidates want to do in terms of policy once they take their seats in a new Parliament, which must convene within 30 days of the announcement of the final results. Their campaign revolved around a single issue: getting rid of Mr. Vucic and uprooting what they called his corrupt “mafia.”
目前尚不清楚「學生名單」的獲勝候選人在進入新議會後想推行什麼政策。新議會必須在最終結果公布後30天內召開。他們的競選圍繞一個單一議題：趕走武契奇，並根除他們所稱的腐敗「黑手黨」。
The slate of candidates selected by student activists includes no students and comprises academics, musicians, activists, actors, athletes and others from a wide range of backgrounds. Most have no experience in politics or government.
由學生活動人士選出的候選人名單中沒有學生，而是由學者、樂手、活動人士、演員、運動員和其他來自廣泛背景的人組成。大多數人沒有政治或政府經驗。
The first candidate on the list was Ilija Srdanovic, a cardiologist from the city of Novi Sad, but he is not the head of a hierarchical party with power to set policy and tell fellow Student List members of Parliament what to do.
名單上的第一候選人是諾維薩德市的心臟病專家伊利亞·斯爾達諾維奇，但他並非某個等級森嚴的政黨的領導人，沒有權力制定政策並告訴「學生名單」的其他議員該怎麼做。
廣告
In his first public remarks after the election, Mr. Srdanovic chided those who had questioned whether such an amorphous political movement could defeat Mr. Vucic.
在選舉後的首次公開講話中，斯爾達諾維奇斥責了那些質疑如此鬆散的政治運動能否擊敗武契奇的人。
“We believed not just in victory, but in a victory so clear that it could not be concealed,” he told RT Balkan, a Russian state-funded news outlet aimed at Serbia.
「我們相信的不只是勝利，而是一場清晰到無法掩蓋的勝利，」他告訴面向塞爾維亞的俄羅斯國家資助新聞機構RT巴爾幹頻道。
