盧拉逆轉贏得巴西總統大選，打擊拉美右翼浪潮

Lula Wins Brazil’s Presidency, in Stunning Blow to Latin America’s Right

ANA LONOVA, ADAM B. ELLICK • 2026-10-27
Brazilian voters on Sunday handed the leftist president, Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, a fourth term in office and a surprise victory over Flávio Bolsonaro, his right-wing challenger, in a striking rebuke to a conservative surge sweeping Latin America.
巴西選民週日給予左翼總統盧拉第四個任期，他出人意料地擊敗其右翼挑戰者博索納羅，這是對席捲拉丁美洲的保守浪潮引人注目的回擊。
Mr. Bolsonaro refused to accept the outcome and said it was the result of foul play, seizing on a narrative that his father also employed after his own electoral loss four years ago.
博索納羅拒絕接受這一結果，稱這是舞弊所致，他沿用了其父四年前競選失敗後也曾使用過的說法。
President Trump on Monday also criticized the results of the vote,  telling reporters at Joint Base Andrews in Maryland that “it looked like another rigged election.”
週一，特朗​​普總統也批評了投票結果，他在馬里蘭州安德魯斯聯合基地告訴記者，「這看起來像是又一次被操縱的選舉。」
The rejection by voters of Mr. Bolsonaro, the son of a former president jailed for attempting a coup four years ago, is also a loss for President Trump, who had embraced the Bolsonaro family and the region’s rising right.
選民對博索納羅的拒絕也意味著川普總統的失利。博索納羅是四年前因試圖發動政變而入獄的前總統之子，而川普曾與博索納羅家族及該地區崛起的右翼勢力關係密切。
Mr. Lula, in contrast to many world leaders, has often and defiantly clashed with Mr. Trump, and his victory highlights that Latin America’s left-wing forces still wield influence despite a string of losses across the hemisphere. This is the first leftist victory following seven straight wins by right-wing leaders in the region over the last 18 months.
與許多世界領導人不同，盧拉經常與川普針鋒相對。他的勝利凸顯出，儘管拉美左翼力量在整個半球遭遇一連串失敗，但仍具有影響力。這是該地區過去18個月右翼領導人連續七次獲勝後首次出現左翼勝利。
“I would like to thank the Brazilian people for the generosity they have shown me for a long time,” Mr. Lula said, during a victory speech in São Paulo, flanked by his wife, running mate and allies.
「我要感謝巴西人民長期以來對我的慷慨，」盧拉在聖保羅發表勝選演講時說，他身旁站著妻子、競選搭檔和盟友。
The result capped a chaotic final stretch of the race, as polling places were ordered to remain open after public transportation delays and torrential rains disrupted voting. The extended polling hours, which both candidates asked for but came right as voting was set to conclude, opened up the possibility that the results would be disputed.
這一結果為一度混亂的競選最後階段畫上句號。此前，由於公共交通延誤和暴雨擾亂投票，投票站被下令延長開放時間。延長投票時間這一舉措雖是兩位候選人共同提出的，但恰好在投票即將結束時才實施，帶來了選舉結果可能被質疑的可能性。
廣告
Mr. Bolsonaro called an order by Brazil’s judiciary to extend voting a “dirty trick” and demanded the election be annulled.
博索納羅稱巴西司法機構延長投票的命令是「骯髒伎倆」， 並要求宣布選舉無效。
“There’s a lot that needs to be cleared up before Brazil simply accepts this foul play that took place in these elections,” he said during a news conference in Brasília, the capital.
「在巴西簡單地接受這些選舉中發生的舞弊之前，有很多問題需要澄清，」他在首都巴西利亞的新聞發布會上說。
Mr. Bolsonaro’s party will now likely petition electoral authorities for a review of the result.
博索納羅的政黨現在可能會向選舉當局請願，要求對結果進行複核。
Since Brazil’s return to democracy, no presidential election has ever been annulled. In 2014, the losing party requested an audit, but that review found no evidence of fraud and the results were upheld.
自巴西恢復民主以來，從未有總統選舉被宣布無效。2014年，敗選政黨要求審計，但審查未發現舞弊證據，選舉結果得以維持。
In 2022, Mr. Bolsonaro’s Liberal Party similarly challenged his father’s loss in the election, but the court ultimately fined the party more than $4 million for failing to provide evidence to support its allegations.
2022年，博索納羅的自由黨同樣質疑其父在選舉中的失敗，但法院最終因該黨未能提供支持其指控的證據，對其處以超過400萬美元的罰款。。
Among Mr. Bolsonaro’s supporters on Sunday, the sentiment was one of sadness and indignation. “We know we’ve been robbed again,” said Valéria Maria de Melo, a 49-year-old housewife, bursting into tears.
週日，博索納羅的支持者們情緒低落，充滿憤慨。「我們知道我們又被洗劫了，」49歲的家庭主婦瓦萊裡婭·瑪麗亞·德梅洛說，她淚流滿面。
Mr. Lula headed into Sunday’s vote as the underdog after finishing 2 percentage points behind his opponent in the first round of voting earlier this month.
盧拉在週日投票前處於劣勢，在本月早些時候的第一輪投票中，他落後對手二個百分點。
But on Sunday, Mr. Lula achieved a stunning comeback, winning 51.8 percent of the vote versus 48.2 percent for Flávio Bolsonaro, with nearly all the ballots counted.
但週日，盧拉實現了驚人的逆轉，在幾乎所有選票清點完畢後，他獲得了51.8%的選票，而弗拉維奧·博索納羅僅獲得48.2%選票。
Mr. Lula’s margin of victory on Sunday was roughly double the margin four years ago when he narrowly defeated Mr. Bolsonaro’s father. It was the first time in Brazil’s history that a candidate who lost the first round of voting came back to win the second.
盧拉週日的勝選優勢大約是四年前的兩倍，當時他險勝博索納羅的父親。這是巴西歷史上首次有候選人在第一輪投票中落後，但在第二輪投票中實現逆轉獲勝。
Mr. Lula’s victory was even more surprising given that several of the candidates who failed to make it out of the first round were conservatives whose votes were expected to broadly shift to Mr. Bolsonaro.
考慮到幾位未能通過首輪投票的候選人都是保守派，其選票預計將大致流向博索納羅，盧拉的勝利就更令人驚訝。
Mr. Lula, speaking at a hotel surrounded by cheering allies and supporters, thanked those who had voted for him. In an indirect swipe at Mr. Trump, he said the mass rallies in Brazilian cities in the days leading up to the vote were “a demonstration that this country refuses to be anyone’s backyard.”
盧拉在酒店發表講話，周圍是歡呼的盟友和支持者。他感謝投票給他的人。他間接抨擊川普說，投票前數日，巴西城市的大規模集會「表明這個國家拒絕成為任何人的後院」。
廣告
“I want to thank the generosity of the Brazilian youth, who took to the streets to defend democracy,” he added.
「我要感謝巴西青年的慷慨，他們走上街頭捍衛民主，」他還說。
The results showed that far more voters had turned up to cast a ballot on Sunday than in the earlier round, a highly unusual shift. Typically, the number of voters drops from the first round to the second. In total, more than 128 million Brazilians voted on Sunday, compared to 125 million in the first round.
結果顯示，週日出來投票的選民遠多於第一輪，這是一個極不尋常的變化。通常，從第一輪到第二輪，投票人數會有所下降。週日共有超過1.28億巴西人投票，而第一輪為1.25億。
An intense social media campaign that included a wildly popular channel on WhatsApp, a platform long dominated by Mr. Bolsonaro’s right-wing movement, played a major role in mobilizing Mr. Lula’s supporters in the final stretch of the race.
一場激烈的社群媒體運動——包括一個在WhatsApp上極受歡迎的頻道，該平台長期由博索納羅的右翼運動主導——在競選最後階段動員盧拉支持者方面發揮了重要作用。
As he celebrated in Rio de Janeiro, Fernando Porto said that he and his friends never gave up hope as they took to social media and to the streets to rally support for Mr. Lula over the last three weeks. “This comeback was truly incredible,” said Mr. Porto, 24, a psychology student.
在里約熱內盧慶祝的費爾南多·波爾圖說，過去三週，他和朋友們從未放棄希望，他們在社群媒體和街頭為盧拉爭取支持。「這次逆轉真的不可思議，」24歲的心理學學生波爾圖說。
In São Paulo, a massive celebration that had already erupted long before the results came grew quickly with news of Mr. Lula’s victory. Supporters of the president broke down in tears of disbelief and set off fireworks, while cars honked in a deafening chorus of horns.
在聖保羅，在結果出爐前就已爆發的大規模慶祝活動隨著盧拉獲勝的消息迅速擴大。總統的支持者們因難以置信而流下眼淚，並燃放煙花，與此同時，汽車鳴笛聲此起彼伏，響徹雲霄。
Mr. Lula’s comeback win capped a roller-coaster career for the president that has traversed soaring popularity, scandal and prison, and now an election that deeply divided Brazil, forcing voters to choose between two starkly different visions for the future of Latin America’s largest nation.
盧拉的逆轉勝利將這位總統過山車般的職業生涯推向高潮。他的生涯經歷了極高的支持率、醜聞和監禁，如今又經歷了一場令巴西社會嚴重分裂的選舉，迫使選民在拉丁美洲最大國家未來的兩種截然不同的願景之間做出選擇。
A stalwart of Brazilian politics, Mr. Lula lifted millions out of poverty through social programs in earlier years as president, though his popularity waned in his third term as he failed to address anxieties over crime and the economy.
作為巴西政壇的中堅人物，盧拉早年在擔任總統期間，通過社會項目幫助數以百萬計的人脫貧，儘管在第三個任期內，由於未能解決人們對犯罪和經濟的焦慮，他的支持率有所下降。
His political legacy has also been tarnished by links to corruption scandals, including a new one unfolding on his watch that has implicated much of Brazil’s political and judicial elite, including Mr. Bolsonaro himself.
他的政治遺產也因與腐敗醜聞的關聯而受損，其中包括在他任期內爆發的最新一起醜聞，涉及巴西大部分政治和司法精英，包括博索納羅本人。
Yet Sunday’s narrow tally makes clear that Mr. Lula, 80, faces a daunting task: leading a polarized nation plagued by a spiraling debt crisis, high grocery prices and growing fears about organized crime. And Mr. Lula is bound to face resistance at every turn, after voters elected an overwhelming number of Mr. Bolsonaro’s political allies to the Legislature.
然而，週日的微弱票數差距表明，80歲的盧拉麵臨艱巨任務：領導一個兩極分化的國家，這個國家飽受債務危機螺旋上升、食品價格高企和對有組織犯罪的日益擔憂等困擾。而且，在選民將壓倒性數量的博索納羅政治盟友選入立法機構後，盧拉勢必處處遭遇阻力。
廣告
And the strong showing of Mr. Bolsonaro, 45, highlights that the right-wing leader’s ideas resonate with millions of working-class Brazilians who may have supported Mr. Lula in the past, but have become disillusioned with him.
45歲的博索納羅表現強勁，凸顯出這位右翼領導人的理念引起成百上千萬工人階級巴西人的共鳴。這些人過去可能支持盧拉，但如今已對他失望。
Ultimately, though, a majority of voters appears to have heeded Mr. Lula’s warnings about handing power to another Bolsonaro, just four years after former President Jair Bolsonaro orchestrated a plan to hold onto the presidency and inspired supporters to storm Brazil’s halls of power.
不過，最終多數選民似乎聽取了盧拉的警告：不要在前總統雅伊爾·博索納羅策劃保住總統職位並煽動支持者衝擊巴西權力機構僅四年後，再把權力交給另一個博索納羅。
Last year, the elder Mr. Bolsonaro was sentenced to 27 years in prison for trying to stage a coup after the 2022 election, in a plot that included plans to assassinate Mr. Lula, Mr. Lula’s running mate and a Supreme Court justice.
去年，老博索納羅因在2022年選舉後企圖發動政變被判處27年監禁，其陰謀包括暗殺盧拉、盧拉的競選搭檔和一名最高法院法官的計劃。
The younger Mr. Bolsonaro had said he planned to seek amnesty for his father and the rioters, impeach the Supreme Court justice who oversaw their jailing, and change the nation’s constitution.
小博索納羅曾表示，他計劃為父親和騷亂者尋求特赦，彈劾監督他們入獄的最高法院法官，並修改國家憲法。
Mr. Lula’s resounding win on Sunday signals that the majority of Brazilians want to protect their democracy, said Christian Lynch, a political scientist at the State University of Rio de Janeiro.
里約熱內盧州立大學政治學家克里斯蒂安·林奇說，盧拉週日的壓倒性勝利表明，多數巴西人希望保護他們的民主。
“People here realized they don’t want to surrender democracy,” he said. “It was a big surprise.”
「這裡的人們意識到，他們不想放棄民主，」他說。「這是一個巨大的驚喜。」
Mr. Lula’s win will most likely complicate Mr. Trump’s plans for dominance in the region: The Brazilian leader has repeatedly defied Mr. Trump over issues like trade, social media and the treatment of the Bolsonaros.
盧拉的勝利很可能使川普在該地區謀求主導地位的計劃複雜化：這位巴西領導人在貿易、社群媒體和博索納羅家族待遇等問題上多次違抗川普。
Mr. Trump remained silent about Mr. Lula’s win on Sunday. While he had not formally endorsed Mr. Bolsonaro, his administration had appeared willing to help him in other ways, such as by labeling Brazil’s largest drug gangs as terror groups. Last year, the American president also tried, and failed, to help Mr. Bolsonaro’s father avoid jail by hitting Brazil with tariffs and sanctions.
雖然川普未正式支持博索納羅，但其政府似乎願意以其他方式幫助博索納羅，例如將巴西最大的毒品幫派列為恐怖組織。去年，這位美國總統還試圖利用關稅和制裁幫助老博索納羅免於入獄，但未能成功。
Turning Brazilian sovereignty into a rallying cry, Mr. Lula repeatedly urged voters to reject the younger Mr. Bolsonaro, painting him as a puppet of the United States.
盧拉將巴西主權作為集結口號，一再呼籲選民拒絕小博索納羅，將他描繪成美國的傀儡。
廣告
Sunday’s result signaled that many Brazilians had rejected Mr. Bolsonaro’s cozy ties with the United States.
週日的結果表明，許多巴西人拒絕了博索納羅與美國之間的密切關係。
Despite Mr. Lula’s disagreements with Mr. Trump, Brazil’s economy was largely unscathed by American tariffs during his third term, and unemployment dropped to record-low rates. But rising food prices and mounting household debt have weighed heavily on Mr. Lula’s approval ratings.
儘管盧拉與川普存在分歧，但在盧拉的第三個任期內，巴西經濟基本未受美國關稅影響，失業率降至歷史低點。但食品價格上漲和家庭債務增加嚴重拖累了盧拉的支持率。
Many Brazilians are also eager to see if Mr. Lula makes good on his promises for a judicial overhaul amid a crisis of confidence in Brazil’s top court. The next president will have unusually broad power to shape the court, which has 11 members, by filling up to four seats freed by justices reaching the mandatory retirement age.
許多巴西人也渴望看到盧拉是否能在巴西最高法院信任危機中兌現司法改革承諾。下一任總統將擁有異常廣泛的權力，來塑造這個由11名成員組成的法院，因為他可以填補多達四個因法官達到強制退休年齡而空出的席位。
“Most people want change,” said Thomas Traumann, a political analyst and press secretary for a former leftist Brazilian president, “but not the Bolsonaro change.”
「大多數人想要改變，」政治分析師、巴西一位左翼前總統的新聞秘書托馬斯·特勞曼說，「但不是博索納羅式的改變。」
