Anthropic上市關乎什麼？幾乎所有一切

What’s Riding on Anthropic’s Market Debut? Nearly Everything.

ERIN GRIFFITH, LAUREN HIRSCH • 2026-10-27
When Anthropic goes public as soon as next month, its initial offering will be more than just a coming-out party for the artificial intelligence company. It will also be a litmus test for the economy, with trillions of dollars at stake.
Anthropic最早將於下個月上市，屆時它的首次公開募股不僅僅會是一家人工智慧公司的亮相派對，還將成為對經濟的一次試金石考驗，關乎數萬億美元的利益。
Anthropic, which makes the Claude chatbot, is a tent pole of the A.I. industry. Valued in the private markets at nearly $1 trillion and backed by more than $130 billion in funding, it could raise $100 billion in its offering and promptly become one of the world’s biggest public companies. But the stakes are even higher than that.
製造Claude聊天機器人的Anthropic是人工智慧行業的一根台柱。它在私募市場估值近1萬億美元，並獲得超過1300億美元融資支持，可能在其發行中籌集1000億美元，從而迅速成為世界上最大的上市公司之一。而此事還關係到更大的利益。
Anthropic and OpenAI, its chief rival, sit atop a pyramid of business partners and suppliers — infrastructure providers, chipmakers, energy companies and A.I. start-ups — that have all spent heavily over the past four years because they believe demand for A.I. products will keep growing.
Anthropic及其主要競爭對手OpenAI位於一個由商業夥伴和供應商組成的金字塔頂端——基礎設施提供商、晶片製造商、能源公司和人工智慧初創企業——它們在過去的四年裡都投入巨資，因為它們相信對人工智慧產品的需求將持續增長。
Anthropic’s listing will provide the first in-depth look at the health of a key business underlying the A.I. boom — and will be a test of its durability. Is there unending appetite for A.I. services to justify the spending on chips, data centers and other infrastructure? Or will Anthropic’s performance show that there are limits?
Anthropic的掛牌上市，將讓外界首次得以深度審視支撐此輪人工智慧繁榮的關鍵企業的健康狀況，同時也將檢驗其增長的持續性。市場對人工智慧服務的需求是否真如無底洞般旺盛，足以證明在晶片、數據中心及其他基建上的龐大開支是合理的？抑或Anthropic的表現將表明，這一切是有局限的？
廣告
“For the market, it’s a bellwether,” said Umesh Padval, an investor at Seligman Ventures, who has worked in tech for nearly 40 years.
在科技界從業近40年的塞利格曼風投公司投資人烏梅什·帕德瓦爾指出：「對整個市場而言，這是一個關鍵的風向標。」
If Anthropic disappoints in its public debut, that could hurt investor confidence and cascade into a wider market sell-off, casting doubts on how much longer the A.I. boom can go. A successful offering could lift the market and pave the way for more A.I. companies to go public.
若Anthropic上市首秀不及預期，不僅可能打擊投資者信心，引發更大範圍的市場拋售，還會讓外界對人工智慧繁榮還能維繫多久產生懷疑。反之，一次成功的募股則有望提振市場，並為更多人工智慧企業上市鋪平道路。
Stock markets are already on edge, with technology stocks broadly falling this month, led by a sell-off in the companies that design and produce the computer chips that power A.I. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index is in a bear market, defined as a decline of 20 percent or more from a recent high and a sign of significant investor angst, after falling 26 percent from its June peak.
此時的股票市場本已處於緊張狀態，科技板塊本月整體走低，主要受設計與生產人工智慧晶片的企業遭遇拋售所拖累。費城半導體指數較6月峰值下跌26%，已進入熊市——即較近期高點下跌20%或以上，是投資者嚴重焦慮的信號。
Over the past couple of weeks, Microsoft canceled a deal with Nscale, a provider of data centers. A report from the research firm Gartner predicted that 80 percent of “neoclouds,” the companies that provide data and computing power for A.I. firms, could be forced out of business. And Ken Griffin, the billionaire owner of the hedge fund Citadel, said A.I. investors were “almost certainly” in a bubble.
過去幾週內，微軟取消了與數據中心提供商Nscale的一項合作協議。研究機構高德納在報告中預測，為人工智慧公司供應數據與算力的「新興雲公司」中，多達80%恐將被迫退出市場。對沖基金城堡投資的億萬富翁所有者肯·格裡芬亦公開表示，人工智慧投資者「幾乎肯定」身處泡沫之中。
Those negative signals have added to fears about the huge piles of money being spent on A.I. infrastructure. Amazon, Meta, Google and Microsoft have said they plan to spend at least $740 billion on A.I. infrastructure, including data centers, this year. Private market investors have poured $1.1 trillion into Anthropic, OpenAI and other A.I. companies since 2022, according to PitchBook, which tracks investments.
這一連串負面信號加劇了市場對投入人工智慧基礎設施的巨額資金的擔憂。亞馬遜、Meta、谷歌與微軟此前均表示，今年在數據中心等人工智慧基礎設施領域的開支計劃合計不少於7400億美元。另據投資監測機構PitchBook統計，自2022年以來，私募市場投資者向Anthropic、OpenAI及其他人工智慧企業注入的資金總規模已達1.1萬億美元。
As spending and investment have risen in private markets, public investments have grown, too, increasing the stock market’s reliance on the performance of the A.I. industry. The top 10 stocks in the S&P 500 are worth more than $1 trillion together, with eight of them being tech companies. Combined, the top 10 account for over 40 percent of the index, up from less than 20 percent a decade ago.
隨著私募市場的支出與投資雙雙攀升，公開市場投資也同步增長，導致股市對人工智慧產業表現的依賴進一步加深。標普500指數排名前十的成分股均跨過萬億美元市值門檻，其中八家為科技公司。這十家企業合計占該指數權重的40%以上，較十年前不足20%的比重翻倍。
When Anthropic is added to the index, it is likely to displace either Berkshire Hathaway or Eli Lilly, the only non-tech companies with valuations above $1 trillion, from the top 10.
待Anthropic正式納入標普500指數後，很可能將前十名中僅有的兩家估值超過1萬億美元的非科技公司——伯克希爾·哈撒韋或禮來公司——擠出前十名。
廣告
“There is a lot riding on this one,” said Brad Gastwirth, the global head of research and market intelligence at Circular Technology, a supply chain services firm. “If it goes bad, it’s going to bring down the market.”
供應鏈服務機構圓環科技全球研究與市場情報主管布拉德·加斯特沃思表示：「這場IPO牽涉極廣。一旦出現閃失，將拖累整個大盤。」
On Monday, Anthropic filed its offering prospectus, revealing details of its fast revenue growth and plans to spend up to $807 billion on data centers, chips and cloud computing over the next decade.
週一，Anthropic正式遞交招股說明書，公布了其營收飛速增長的具體細節，並披露未來十年內計劃在數據中心、晶片與雲計算領域支出最高可達8070億美元。
The filing did not include Anthropic’s performance from the third quarter, which it presented to investors at its San Francisco headquarters this month. The company’s revenue hit $16.8 billion in the third quarter, up 45 percent from the prior quarter. But investors compared that with the even faster growth of 141 percent that the second quarter had from the first quarter.
該文件並未包含第三季度的業績，不過Anthropic本月已在舊金山總部向投資者作了通報。該公司第三季度營收達168億美元，環比增長45%；但與第二季度環比第一季度高達141%的爆髮式增速相比，投資者已注意到增速顯著放緩。
How Anthropic explains that slowdown will be important. If it was because the company lost market share to OpenAI or companies using cheaper open-source A.I. models — the technology that powers chatbots and other tools that generate videos, text and computer code — that means the overall demand for A.I. services remains healthy. If Anthropic’s growth slowed because the entire market saw less demand for A.I. products, the reaction could be more dramatic.
Anthropic如何解釋這一降速將至關重要。若增速放緩是因為市場份額被OpenAI或使用更廉價開源人工智慧模型（即驅動聊天機器人以及生成影片、文本和代碼工具的底層技術）的公司搶食，則意味著市場對人工智慧服務的整體需求依舊健康；但若其放緩根源於全行業對人工智慧產品的需求疲軟，市場的反應恐將劇烈得多。
Anthropic declined to comment. (The New York Times has sued OpenAI and Microsoft, claiming copyright infringement of news content related to A.I. systems. The two companies have denied those claims.)
對此，Anthropic方面不予置評。（《紐約時報》此前已起訴OpenAI及微軟，指控兩家公司侵犯與人工智慧系統相關的新聞內容版權；兩家公司均否認了上述指控。）
For the past year, Anthropic has been Silicon Valley’s hottest company, partly because of Claude Code, the service that transformed how software engineers write code. The company’s revenue soared, causing private market investors to drive up its valuation to $965 billion, topping OpenAI.
過去一年中，Anthropic憑藉革新軟體工程師編程方式的Claude Code服務，成為矽谷最受矚目的明星企業。公司營收呈爆髮式增長，推動私募市場投資者將其估值推高至9650億美元，超越OpenAI。
To meet the demand for its A.I. products, Anthropic struck deals worth hundreds of billions to lease computing power from Google, Amazon and Microsoft. It has also spent on chips from Nvidia and data centers from Nscale and others. The companies serving Anthropic are, in turn, shelling out money to their suppliers.
為滿足對其人工智慧產品的需求，Anthropic達成了價值數千億美元的協議，從谷歌、亞馬遜和微軟租用算力，同時還在英偉達的晶片和Nscale等企業的數據中心上耗費巨資。而服務於Anthropic的上下游企業，反過來又向各自的供應商投入大量資金。
But over the summer, some Anthropic customers concluded they were spending too much on “tokens,” or units of data processed and generated by A.I. systems. They sought cheaper options. Meta, one of Anthropic’s largest customers, projected this year that it would spend as much as $10 billion a year on the company’s technology, but pulled back as it switched to other A.I. models.
然而入夏以來，部分Anthropic客戶意識到自己在人工智慧系統處理與生成的數據單元（即「詞元」）上的開支過於龐大，轉而尋求低成本替代方案。Anthropic最大的客戶之一Meta今年曾預計每年將在該公司的技術上花費高達100億美元，但隨著轉向其他人工智慧模型，這項支出已大幅收縮。
廣告
Stocks of some A.I. companies that went public this year have wilted. Shares of Cerebras, a Silicon Valley maker of A.I. chips, have fallen roughly 40 percent from their offering price in May. Shares of Accelevation, an A.I. infrastructure company, are trading about a third lower than their $18 offering price in September. Elon Musk’s SpaceX, which went public in June, has also fallen far from its peak, though it remains roughly 9 percent above its offering price.
與此同時，今年上市的部分人工智慧企業股票已顯現頹勢。矽谷人工智慧晶片製造商Cerebras的股價自5月發行以來已下挫約40%；人工智慧基礎設施企業Accelevation的交易價格較其9月18美元的發行價低了約三分之一；伊隆·馬斯克旗下的太空探索技術公司雖於6月上市且仍較發行價高出約9%，但已自高點深度回撤。
Against this backdrop, the market for initial public offerings has slowed. Holtec, which provides nuclear energy to the A.I. sector, withdrew its listing last month. SB Energy, a data center company controlled by SoftBank, one of OpenAI’s biggest backers, and Aggreko, a power company that works with data centers, have delayed their plans to go public.
在此背景下，首次公開募股市場已明顯降溫。向人工智慧行業供應核能電力的霍爾泰克於上月撤回了上市申請；獲OpenAI最大金主之一軟銀控股的數據中心企業軟銀能源(SB Energy)、主營數據中心供電的亞力克(Aggreko)也紛紛推遲了IPO計劃。
A.I. infrastructure is a “dominant theme” of the I.P.O. market this year, said Matthew Kennedy, a senior strategist at Renaissance Capital, which tracks public listings. “It’s also been among the hardest hit by the sell-off these past few weeks.”
追蹤新股上市動態的復興資本高級策略師馬修·甘迺迪直言，人工智慧基礎設施是今年首次公開募股市場的「主導主題」，「但在過去幾週的拋售潮中，該板塊也是受創最嚴重的領域之一。」
Bankers have warned companies that they may not get the valuations they were contemplating just two months ago. In an earnings call this month, David Solomon, the chief executive of Goldman Sachs, said that while the market for public offerings was “wide open,” companies seeking valuations close to those discussed this summer might choose to wait.
投資銀行家已警告各擬上市企業，它們可能無法獲得僅兩個月前設想的高估值。高盛首席執行官大衛·所羅門在本月的財報電話會議上表示，儘管首次公開募股市場依然「大門敞開」，但那些尋求接近今夏討論估值的企業可能會選擇等待。
“Things got a little bit softer in September,” he said.
「9月的行情確實偏軟了一些，」他說。
Anthropic’s revenue numbers have added to the doubts. The company had been expected to go public at a valuation as high as $2 trillion. But after seeing the slowing growth and the market doldrums, some investors have started discussing a valuation of $1.5 trillion to $1.7 trillion, according to one person with knowledge of the matter.
Anthropic的營收數據進一步加重了外界的疑慮。該公司此前曾被預期以高達2萬億美元的估值上市；但據知情人士透露，在看到增速放緩和市場低迷後，部分投資者已開始探討將估值目標調降至1.5萬億至1.7萬億美元區間。
廣告
A.I. executives have been trying to provide reassurance. Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company, which supplies chips to the A.I. sector, saw “no ceiling” to its profits, its chief executive, C.C. Wei, said this month.
面對市場擔憂，人工智慧產業界高管極力釋出定心丸。為人工智慧行業供應晶片的台積電首席執行官魏哲家本月表示，公司盈利「看不見天花板」。
Late last week, amid growing concerns about A.I. spending, the markets fell further. The tech-heavy Nasdaq experienced its largest drop since June.
上週晚些時候，在對人工智慧基礎設施支出的擔憂日益加劇之際，市場進一步下跌。以科技股為主的納斯達克指數創下了6月以來的最大跌幅。
