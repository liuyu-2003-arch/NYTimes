泰國豪門醜聞：弟弟指控哥哥性侵，家族試圖封口

He Accused His Brother of Sexual Abuse. Then He Took on His Family.

黃瑞黎 • 2026-09-16
For years, the Thai public knew Siranudh Scott as a scion of Thailand’s largest beer dynasty and a high-profile marine conservationist who publicly criticized government officials for ignoring environmental violations.
多年來，泰國公眾都知道西拉努·史考特是出身泰國最大啤酒王朝的富家子弟，也是一位高調的海洋保護主義者，曾公開批評政府官員忽視環境違法行為。
But Siranudh, 30, was also fighting a personal battle. This year, he decided he could no longer stay silent, and made a bombshell accusation.
但30歲的西拉努也在進行一場個人鬥爭。今年，他決定不再沉默，並提出了一項爆炸性指控。
Siranudh, who is also known as Psi, said his older brother, Sunit Scott, and his nanny had sexually assaulted him as a child — and that his mother attempted to cover up the abuse and buy his silence. The fallout was swift. He said that as punishment for speaking out, he was cut off from the $1.3 billion Singha family fortune and thrown out of the family beach house.
被人稱作Psi的西拉努說，哥哥蘇尼特·史考特和他的保姆在他小時候對他實施了性侵犯，母親則試圖掩蓋虐待並用錢封他的口。他的舉動很快招致後果。他說，作為發聲的懲罰，他將無緣勝獅家族的13億美元財富，並被趕出了家族的海濱別墅。
His accusation — which his brother denied — unleashed a rare public reckoning over sexual assault in Thailand, where the #MeToo movement had failed to make an impact. Many Thais have responded with accounts of sexual assault within families and by powerful people.
在連「#我也是」(#MeToo)運動都無法產生影響的泰國，他的指控（他的哥哥予以否認）引發了一場罕見的性侵犯公開清算。許多泰國人紛紛講述了他們在家庭內部和被權勢人物性侵犯的經歷。
His mother has disputed his account of what she knew and how she acted, in a written response to The New York Times, her first public comment on the allegations. Sunit, 36, did not respond to multiple requests for comment. In a social media video posted after his brother made his accusations public, he denied the abuse and described what happened as roughhousing. The nanny did not respond.
他的母親在給《紐約時報》的書面回覆中首次就這些指控發表公開評論，就自己的知情程度和行為對他的說法予以反駁。36歲的蘇尼特沒有回應多次置評請求。在指控公開後，他發布了一段社群媒體影片，否認存在施虐，並將發生的事情描述為打鬧。保姆沒有回應。
The New York Times interviewed Siranudh on multiple occasions and spoke with seven of his friends and supporters. We also reviewed a contract signed between Siranudh and his family, as well as an audio clip of what Siranudh said was a conversation between the brothers that he secretly recorded.
《紐約時報》多次採訪西拉努，並與他的七位朋友和支持者交談。我們還查閱了西拉努與其家人簽署的一份合同，以及一段據西拉努稱是兄弟之間對話的錄音片段，該錄音是他祕密錄製的。
The support from the Thai public has given Siranudh something he says he long wanted from his family: acknowledgment. Having the accusations out in the open was like a weight lifting from him, he said.
泰國公眾的支持給了西拉努一些他說自己長期以來一直想從家人那裡得到的東西：承認。他說，將指控公開就像從他身上卸下了一個重擔。

“Some form of justice has come in its exposure,” he added.
「某種形式的正義已經在曝光中到來，」他補充說。
To Be a Bhirombhakdi
成為比龍帕迪家族的一員
Siranudh described a childhood in which his parents, who were divorced, were distant. The household staff took care of him, and even though he was not close to his brother, they would play together sometimes.
西拉努描述了一個與離異父母疏遠的童年。他由家中傭人照看，儘管與哥哥不親近，他們有時也會一起玩。
That made the abuse so hard to understand, he said, because it came from those he trusted.
他說，正因為如此，虐待行為才更令人難以理解，因為施虐者是他信任的人。
He said he barely saw his mother and met his father for dinner once a week. His grandfather, Chamnong Bhirombhakdi, was the longtime chair of Boon Rawd Brewery, which owns Singha beer and holds the largest share of Thailand’s lucrative beer market. The country’s wealthy elite regularly visited his grandfather’s house.
他說他幾乎見不到母親，每週與父親見一次面吃晚飯。他的祖父昌農·比龍帕迪任汶洛啤酒廠董事長多年，該廠擁有勝獅啤酒，並持有泰國利潤豐厚的啤酒市場的最大份額。該國富有的精英經常拜訪他祖父。
Closest to him was his nanny, who had cared for him since he was a baby.
與他最親近的是從他嬰兒時就照顧他的保姆。
But once, when he was 8, Siranudh said, his nanny took her pants off and sat on top of him. He was confused about what happened. (The nanny did not respond to requests for comment.)
但西拉努說，在他八歲時，有一次保姆脫下褲子坐在他身上。他對發生的事情感到困惑。（保姆沒有回應置評請求。）
The bigger betrayal, he said, came when he was 9. His older brother had returned from boarding school in England for summer vacation. Siranudh wanted to spend time with him. Instead, he said, his brother forced him to give him oral sex.
他說，更大的背叛發生在他九歲時。他的哥哥從英國的寄宿學校回來過暑假。西拉努想和他共度時光。結果，他說，哥哥強迫他為他口交。
“The first time he grabbed my head, I remember being really repulsed,” Siranudh said.
「他第一次抓住我的頭時，我記得我感到非常厭惡，」西拉努說。
The abuse continued until Siranudh was 13, he said.
他說，虐待一直持續到西拉努13歲。
Siranudh said he told no one because he felt responsible for protecting his family.
西拉努說他沒有告訴任何人，因為他覺得有責任保護他的家人。
“I just felt early on that I had to undermine my reality to let other people live,” he said. “I didn’t speak up also because I didn’t think I was worthy or that I was loved.”
「我很早就覺得，為了讓別人好好生活，我必須否定自己的現實，」他說。「我沒有說出來，也因為我不認為自己有價值，或者被愛。」
As a teenager, Siranudh struggled with depression. He found refuge in drawing, and left home in 2014 to study animation in California. He moved to London in 2020 and started seeing a therapist, whom he confided in.
十幾歲時，西拉努與憂鬱症作鬥爭。他在繪畫中找到了避難所，並於2014年離家前往加利福尼亞學習動畫。他於2020年搬到倫敦，並開始見一位心理諮詢師，向其傾訴。

He said the therapist helped him see the gravity of his situation. He said before that, he thought an older brother abusing a younger brother was normal.
他說，諮詢師幫助他看到了自己處境的嚴重性。他說，在那之前，他認為哥哥虐待弟弟是正常的。
“The minute that I was able to start saying that yes, it was rape, I was able to start planning like how I was going to go forward,” he said.
「當我能夠開始說，是的，那是強姦的那一刻，我就能夠開始計劃我將如何前進，」他說。
Breaking the Silence
打破沉默
Siranudh said he did not set out to make his allegations public. He first tried to address them with his family. What followed, he said, convinced him that he would need proof, and public pressure.
西拉努說，他並沒有打算將他的指控公之於眾。他首先試圖與家人解決這些問題。他說，接下來發生的事情讓他相信，他需要證據和公眾壓力。
In 2020, Siranudh told his mother, Chiranuj Bhirombhakdi, that he had been sexually abused by his nanny.
2020年，西拉努告訴他的母親七拉努·比龍帕迪，他曾被保姆性侵犯。
When Chiranuj confronted the nanny, Siranudh said, the nanny told her that he had been abused by his older brother. His mother asked Siranudh about it then told him not to tell anyone, he said. She fired the nanny.
西拉努說，當七拉努質問保姆時，保姆告訴她，西拉努曾被哥哥虐待。他說，他的母親問了他這件事，然後叫他不要告訴任何人。她解僱了保姆。
廣告
Siranudh saw what happened next as punishment for speaking out. Within months, he said, Chiranuj threw him out of the family’s beach house in Hua Hin. She said he had damaged the property. This was the start of his estrangement from his mother and brother.
西拉努將接下來發生的事情視為發聲的懲罰。他說，幾個月內，七拉努將他趕出了在華欣的家族海濱別墅。她說他損壞了房產。這是他與他母親和哥哥疏遠的開始。
Siranudh’s estrangement from his mother deepened when he learned that she had rehired his nanny and continued taking vacations with his older brother.
當西拉努得知母親重新僱用他的保姆並繼續與他的哥哥一起度假時，他與母親的關係更加疏遠。
In August 2023, he said he found the courage to do what he had thought was impossible. In a series of Facebook posts, he wrote that he was “raped by someone in the family,” that his nanny had sexually abused him when he was a child, and that his mother had thrown him out.
2023年8月，他說他找到了勇氣去做他曾認為不可能的事情。在一系列Facebook帖子中，他寫道，他「被家族中的某個人強姦」，他的保姆在他小時候對他實施了性侵犯，他的母親將他趕了出去。
The posts drew widespread attention. Three months later, Siranudh’s aunt invited him to her house for a meeting with his relatives. His brother, Sunit, was there.
這些帖子引起了廣泛關注。三個月後，西拉努的姑姑邀請他去她家與親戚會面。他的哥哥蘇尼特在那裡。
Siranudh asked to speak alone with his brother. Siranudh told the Times he recorded the conversation without his brother’s knowledge because he wanted evidence if he ever made his allegations public.
西拉努要求與他的哥哥單獨交談。西拉努告訴《紐約時報》，他在他哥哥不知情的情況下錄下了對話，因為如果最終要將他的指控公之於眾，他需要證據。
The Times reviewed the 20-minute recording, the full version of which has not been made public. In it, Siranudh is heard confronting the other speaker, whom Siranudh identifies as Sunit.
《紐約時報》核查了這段20分鐘的錄音，其完整版本尚未公開。在錄音中，可以聽到西拉努與另一位說話者對峙，西拉努稱此人為蘇尼特。
廣告
“It’s nothing that a brother should ever feel. It’s not just sexual assault, Pi,” he said. “It’s like incest, and I had no responsibility in it at all ’cause I was a kid.”
「這不是一個兄弟應該產生的感受。這不僅僅是性侵犯，Pi，」他說。「這就像亂倫，而我對此完全沒有責任，因為我是個孩子。」
The other speaker says he did not know what he was doing at the time. He does not appear to deny Siranudh’s accusations. He says: “What I’ve done I can never take back.”
另一位說話者說，他當時不知道自己在做什麼。他似乎沒有否認西拉努的指控。他說：「我所做的事情，我永遠無法收回。」
Sunit did not respond to requests for comment sent through his mother’s lawyer as well as a crisis communications consultant hired by the lawyer of the Bhirombhakdi family. A Times reporter who went to his home and asked to speak with him was turned away.
蘇尼特沒有回應通過他母親的律師以及比龍帕迪家族律師聘請的危機溝通顧問發出的置評請求。《紐約時報》的一名記者前往他家，要求採訪他，但被拒之門外。
At the family meeting, Siranudh challenged his mother to tell their relatives about what he said his brother had done, but the relatives refused to listen, he said. His mother and aunt offered to give him financial support if he signed an agreement to “uphold the reputation, honor, and dignity as a member of a ‘Bhirombhakdi,’” according to a copy of the contract viewed by the Times.
在家族會議上，西拉努要求母親告訴親戚們他所說的哥哥所做的事情，但他說，親戚們拒絕傾聽。根據《紐約時報》查閱的合同副本，他的母親和姑姑提出，如果他簽署一份協議，以「維護作為『比龍帕迪』家族成員的聲譽、榮譽和尊嚴」，就給他經濟支持。
Specifically, he was told not to post “extra content” about his mother on social media. In return, he would receive an annual allowance of 5 million baht, and a one-time payment of 10 million baht ($300,000). He signed.
具體來說，他被告知不要在社群媒體上發布關於他母親的「更多內容」。作為回報，他將獲得每年500萬泰銖的津貼，以及一次性支付1000萬泰銖（30萬美元）。他簽了字。
But he refused to be silenced. He reiterated his complaints about his family on television. In January, his mother sued him under a Thai law that allows parents to take legal action against “ungrateful” children who defame or insult them.
但他拒絕被噤聲。他在電視上重申了對家人的不滿。今年1月，他的母親根據一項泰國法律起訴他，該法律允許父母對誹謗或侮辱他們的「忘恩負義」子女採取法律行動。
廣告
Then, in May, Siranudh made his accusations against his brother public. After his brother denied them, he released excerpts from the recording on social media, saying he felt his family would otherwise never acknowledge what happened.
然後，在5月，西拉努公開了他對哥哥的指控。在哥哥否認後，他在社群媒體上發布了錄音摘錄，說他覺得如果不這樣做，他的家人永遠不會承認發生了什麼。
Chiranuj disputed much of his account in written answers to questions from the Times.
在對《紐約時報》提問的書面回答中，七拉努對西拉努的大部分敘述提出了異議。
She said she had not heard the accusations against Sunit until Siranudh made them public; Siranudh says he told her in 2020.
她說，她是在西拉努公開後才聽說針對蘇尼特的指控；西拉努說他在2020年就告訴了她。
She rejected his description of the agreement as hush money, saying it was meant to support her son financially, on one condition. “He has to behave and uphold our names and reputations of ‘Bhirombhakdi’,” she wrote. Chiranuj said the lawsuit was necessary because he had damaged the beach house and was “causing trouble.”
她拒絕了他將協議描述為封口費的說法，稱其目的是在經濟上支持她的兒子，但有一個條件。「他必須守規矩，維護我們『比龍帕迪』的名聲和聲譽，」她寫道。七拉努說，訴訟是必要的，因為他損壞了海濱別墅，並且「製造麻煩」。
A National Reckoning
一場全國性的清算
By releasing the recordings, Siranudh generated public pressure that his family, and the brewery tied to its fortune, could not ignore.
通過發布錄音，西拉努製造了公眾壓力，他的家人以及與其財富相關的啤酒廠無法忽視。
In Thailand, the #MeToo movement had not gained traction because confrontation is often avoided and criminal defamation laws can make people reluctant to name those they accuse of assault. The topic of sexual abuse within families was especially sensitive, but that is changing.
在泰國，「#我也是」運動沒有產生多少影響，因為這裡往往會迴避正面對峙，而刑事誹謗法可能使人們不願提出具名的指控。家庭內部的性虐待話題尤其敏感，但這種情況正在改變。
Jaray Singhakowinta, an assistant professor at the National Institute of Development Administration in Bangkok, said Siranudh’s story challenged the expectation that family comes before the individual, and the silence surrounding male victims.
位於曼谷的泰國國立發展管理學院的助理教授賈拉伊·辛加科溫塔說，西拉努的故事挑戰了家庭先於個人以及男性受害者應保持沉默的觀念。
“A young man crying on camera about childhood abuse gave other men permission to speak, and many did,” he said.
「一個年輕人在鏡頭前為童年虐待哭泣，給了其他男性發聲的許可，許多人確實這樣做了，」他說。
As the response to Siranudh’s account grew, the family and the business sought to distance themselves from the dispute.
隨著對西拉努敘述的回應增多，家族和企業試圖與這場糾紛保持距離。
Boon Rawd Brewery, the maker of Singha beer, announced that Sunit, who worked for the company, had resigned. It published his resignation letter, in which he apologized for “the incident and the impact it has caused,” and said that he was stepping down until the matter was clarified. The company also expressed its sympathy to Siranudh.
勝獅啤酒的製造商汶洛啤酒廠宣布，在該公司工作的蘇尼特已經辭職。公司公布了他的辭職信，他在信中為「該事件及其造成的影響」道歉，並表示他將辭職，直到事情得到澄清。該公司還對西拉努表示了同情。
In a statement, the Bhirombhakdi family acknowledged the “significant public concern” around Siranudh’s allegations and said its members were committed to addressing the issue responsibly. The broader family said it did not know about the contract.
在一份聲明中，比龍帕迪家族承認圍繞西拉努指控的「重大公眾關注」，並表示其成員致力於負責任地解決這個問題。家族成員大多表示不知道那份合同。
In July, Chiranuj announced that she was dropping the lawsuit against Siranudh, saying she hoped that ending the litigation would allow the family to heal.
7月，七拉努宣布她將撤銷對西拉努的訴訟，說她希望結束訴訟能讓家庭癒合。
Siranudh said reconciliation was no longer his goal. He is now preparing to file criminal complaints against his brother. He has no plans to speak to his mother.
西拉努說，和解不再是他的目標。他現在正準備對他的哥哥提起刑事控告。他沒有與母親對話的打算。
