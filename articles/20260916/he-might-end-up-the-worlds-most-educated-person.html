全球頂尖高校「學位收割機」：他為何一直求學？

He Might End Up the World’s Most Educated Person

SCOTT CACCIOLA • 2026-09-16
Early in the fall semester of the 2025-26 academic year, Lisa Belkin, an adjunct professor at Columbia University’s journalism school, realized that one of her students, Jamie Beaton, had yet to show up for class.
2025-26學年秋季學期初，哥倫比亞大學新聞學院兼職教授麗莎·貝爾金髮現，她的一名學生傑米·比頓還沒來上過課。
Belkin, who avoids combing the internet for information about her students, knew nothing about Beaton. She was curious about his absence, though, so she reached out to a dean, who sent an email.
從不上網搜索學生信息的貝爾金對比頓一無所知。但她對他的缺席感到好奇，於是聯繫了一位院長，院長髮了一封郵件。
Beaton was apologetic in his reply, saying he had mixed up his schedule. He was in New Zealand, teaching a course in entrepreneurship at the University of Auckland, but would be back in New York the next week.
比頓在回覆中致歉，說他日程有衝突。他當時在紐西蘭，在奧克蘭大學教一門創業課程，但下週會回紐約。
That was when Belkin decided to break her rule. Who was this guy?
這時，貝爾金決定破例去了解一下，這傢伙是誰？
廣告
Beaton, she learned, had graduated magna cum laude from Harvard University in 2016, picking up a master’s degree in applied mathematics along the way. He had gone on to collect an MBA from Stanford University. Then a law degree from Yale University. That was followed by a doctor of philosophy in public policy from Oxford, where he was a Rhodes scholar — all before age 28.
她得知比頓2016年以優異成績從哈佛大學畢業，獲得一個應用數學碩士學位。之後他在史丹佛大學獲得MBA，在耶魯大學獲得法律學位，又在牛津大學獲得公共政策哲學博士學位，且是羅德學者——這一切都在28歲前完成。
Attending classes remotely and in person, he earned additional master’s degrees from — deep breath — the University of Pennsylvania (education entrepreneurship), Stanford (education technology), Princeton University (finance), Cornell University (healthcare policy and economics), Dartmouth College (implementation science), King’s College London (war studies) and Tsinghua University in Beijing (global affairs).
通過遠程和線下課程，他還在——深吸一口氣——賓夕法尼亞大學（教育創業）、史丹佛大學（教育技術）、普林斯頓大學（金融）、康乃爾大學（醫療政策與經濟學）、達特茅斯學院（實施科學）、倫敦國王學院（戰爭學）和清華大學（全球事務）獲得了碩士學位。
Still, there was more: Beaton, who was born and raised in New Zealand, was a founder and the CEO of Crimson Education, a college admissions counseling service that has been valued at more than $600 million.
還有：在紐西蘭出生和長大的比頓是Crimson Education的創始人和首席執行官，這家大學招生諮詢公司估值超過6億美元。
A week after his apologetic reply, Beaton made his first appearance in Belkin’s classroom. As the semester went on, he did not miss an assignment.
回覆致歉一周後，比頓第一次出現在貝爾金的課堂上。整個學期，他沒有錯過一次作業。
“He was a joy to get to know,” Belkin said. “But it was also the weirdest relationship I’ve ever had with a student.”
「認識他是一件愉快的事，」貝爾金說。「但這也是我與學生有過的最奇怪的關係。」
‘Passions and Curiosities’
「激情與好奇心」
A 5-foot-6 personification of naked ambition whose LinkedIn profile has been the subject of memes and Reddit threads, Beaton has gone to the head of a rarefied class — perpetual students.
一個身高1米67的赤裸野心化身，其領英個人資料一直是網路迷因和Reddit帖子的主題，比頓已進入一個稀有的階層——永久學生。
If he continues collecting advanced degrees at such a swift pace, he will surpass Benjamin B. Bolger, who has racked up 16 at age 50. He might even beat Michael Nicholson, an octogenarian forever scholar with more than 30 degrees to his name.
如果他繼續以如此快的速度收集高級學位，他將超過本傑明·B·博爾格，後者50歲時已積累16個學位。他甚至可能擊敗麥可·尼科爾森，一位擁有30多個學位的八旬永久學者。
廣告
Away from his studies, Beaton, 31, has become a player in the field of college admissions counseling. His clients pay between $30,000 and $200,000 for a multiyear program designed to enhance their children’s chances of getting into top universities.
學業之外，31歲的比頓已成為大學招生諮詢領域的參與者。他的客戶支付3萬至20萬美元，參加一個多年期項目，旨在提高子女進入頂尖大學的機會。
His own academic background has raised some questions, starting with: How can this be real? Is it education as performance art? An elaborate marketing ploy for his business?
他自己的學術背景引發了一些問題，首先是：這怎麼可能是真的？這是把教育當成行為藝術了嗎？是給他的企業精心設計的營銷策略嗎？
The truth, Beaton said, is more prosaic.
比頓說，真相比較無聊。
“The first five degrees had a clear skill acquisition component to them,” he said. “Since then, it’s really been about following different passions and curiosities.”
「前五個學位有明確的技能獲取成分，」他說。「從那以後，真的是關於追隨不同的激情和好奇心。」
We met on a recent afternoon in Manhattan, not far from where he lives with his wife, Priscilla Beaton. His day was crowded with appointments, including an evening event at the Harvard Club in Midtown, where he and his Crimson Education colleagues would hold a meet-and-greet with potential clients.
最近一個下午，我們在曼哈頓見面，離他和妻子普裡西拉·比頓住的地方不遠。他的日程很滿，包括在市中心哈佛俱樂部的晚間活動，他和Crimson Education同事將在那裡與潛在客戶舉行見面會。
“I love the action, and I love a jam-packed day,” he said. “It’s good fun.”
「我喜歡行動，喜歡排得滿滿的一天，」他說。「這很有趣。」
廣告
So many things are, according to Beaton, “good fun.” An abridged list: Helping students decode the college admissions process. Studying the viability of distressed companies. Grabbing snacks in the Amtrak cafe car. Reading big books in one gulp. Riding the fastest roller coaster in the world on a recent trip to Saudi Arabia.
根據比頓的說法，很多事情都是「有趣的」。一份簡略清單：幫助學生解讀大學招生過程。研究陷入困境的公司的生存能力。在國鐵咖啡車廂裡吃零食。一口氣讀完大部頭書籍。在最近一次沙烏地阿拉伯之旅中玩世界上最快的過山車。
He was cheerful and accommodating. He spoke rapidly, in fully formed paragraphs. Shortly before we met, he emailed me a 61-page legal notice stating that Crimson Education had initiated a $28 million hostile takeover of Kip McGrath, an international tutoring provider.
他開朗而隨和。他說話很快，一說就是有頭有尾的一整段。在我們見面前不久，他給我發了一封61頁的法律通知，稱Crimson Education已對Kip McGrath發起2800萬美元的敵意收購，後者是一家國際輔導服務提供商。
He keeps up with his studies even while traveling the world on business. A few weeks ago, he was in India when he submitted his end-of-semester project for a course at Brown University, where he is working toward a master’s in data science. He said he tries to get six hours of sleep a night.
即使在世界各地出差時，他也跟得上學業。幾週前，他在印度提交了布朗大學一門課程的期末項目，他在那裡攻讀數據科學碩士學位。他說他每晚盡量睡六個小時。
“Oh, he’s lying,” John Key, a former prime minister of New Zealand, said in a telephone interview.
「哦，他在撒謊，」紐西蘭前總理約翰·基在電話採訪中說。
Key, the chair of Crimson Education, considers Beaton the second-brightest New Zealander, behind the noted scientist Ernest Rutherford, who died nearly 90 years ago.
基是Crimson Education的董事長，他認為比頓是第二聰明的紐西蘭人，僅次於著名科學家歐內斯特·盧瑟福，後者在近90年前去世。
“And I’m only saying that because it’s hard to take it away from the guy who split the atom,” Key said.
「我這麼說只是因為，總不能跟那個劈開原子的人爭第一吧，」基說。
廣告
Amy Chua, the author of “Battle Hymn of the Tiger Mother,” a 2011 memoir about her hard-driving parenting style, was one of Beaton’s professors at Yale Law School. In recent years she has written more letters of recommendation for him than she can count.
在2011年出版的回憶錄《虎媽戰歌》(Battle Hymn of the Tiger Mother)中講述自己嚴厲育兒風格的蔡美兒(Amy Chua)是比頓在耶魯法學院的教授之一。她為他寫了無數推薦信。
“He can’t stop applying to things,” she said.
「他沒完沒了地申請各種東西，」她說。
‘I Want to Work’
「我想工作」
The making of the boy who wanted to know everything began in Auckland, New Zealand, where he was the only child of a single mother, Paula Beaton. She was pregnant when she and Jamie’s father separated, she said. They later divorced.
這個想知道一切的男孩的成長始於紐西蘭奧克蘭，他是單身母親寶拉·比頓的獨生子。她說，她和傑米的父親分開時她正懷孕。他們後來離婚了。
Jamie, who took his mother’s surname, recalled “odd experiences” when he was young, like when his mother joined him for father-son breakfasts at his primary school. But family was important — his maternal grandparents, John and Sarah Beaton, lived with them — and so was education. Jamie’s grandfather, a tradesman, always seemed to be leafing through a newspaper or an Andy McNab thriller.
傑米隨了母親的姓，他回憶起小時候的「奇怪經歷」，比如母親參加他小學的父子早餐會。但家庭很重要——他的外祖父母約翰和莎拉·比頓與他們住在一起——教育也很重要。外祖父是一名手藝人，似乎總是在翻閱報紙或安迪·麥克納布的驚悚小說。
“Academics was our religion,” Jamie said.
「求學是我們的宗教，」傑米說。
廣告
His mother said that one of her goals had been to expose her son to myriad opportunities. So he played tennis and basketball. He went camping, swimming, kayaking. He was 9 when his mother introduced him to a chess coach named Glenn Harry Kwok. Kwok and Paula soon began dating and are now long-term partners.
他的母親說，她的目標之一是讓兒子接觸無數機會。所以他打網球和籃球。他去露營、游泳、劃皮划艇。他九歲時，母親把他介紹給一位名叫格倫·哈利·郭的國際象棋教練。郭和寶拉很快開始約會，現在是長期伴侶。
As a teenager, Jamie dreamed of attending a top college in the United States. In his final year of high school, he took more than twice the number of required courses. He was admitted to 25 universities, including Harvard, Yale and Princeton.
十幾歲時，傑米夢想在美國上頂尖大學。高中最後一年，他選修了所需課程數量的兩倍多。他被25所大學錄取，包括哈佛、耶魯和普林斯頓。
Before leaving home, he co-founded the counseling service that would become Crimson Education, billing it as a way to help international students navigate their way to U.S. universities. After all, he had done it himself.
離家前，他與人創立了後來成為Crimson Education的諮詢服務公司，將其宣傳為幫助國際學生進入美國大學的一種途徑。畢竟，他自己做到了。
At Harvard, he felt all alone at first. Then he joined 15 student organizations, including the debate club and the undergraduate economics association. Brett Davis, one of Beaton’s roommates at Harvard, grew accustomed to his 2 a.m. business calls.
在哈佛，他起初感到非常孤獨。然後他加入了15個學生組織，包括辯論俱樂部和本科生經濟學協會。布雷特·戴維斯——比頓在哈佛的室友之一——對他凌晨2點的商務電話習以為常。
“That was certainly a clue that this guy was thinking beyond the script of what is normal to do or achieve in college,” Davis said.
「這顯然能表明，這個人的思考超越了大學裡正常要做或實現的事情的劇本，」戴維斯說。
Beaton was a sophomore when he emailed Julian Robertson, the founder of Tiger Management, a Wall Street hedge fund. Following a practice he had used with other traders at his firm, Robertson sent him off to be evaluated by a psychoanalyst — “Good fun,” Beaton said — before bringing him aboard.
比頓大二時給朱利安·羅伯遜發了郵件，後者是華爾街對沖基金老虎管理公司的創始人。遵循他在公司與其他交易員使用過的做法，羅伯遜讓他接受一位精神分析師的評估——「很有趣，」比頓說——然後才讓他加入。
Between classes at Harvard, Beaton spent two days a week at the Tiger Management offices in New York. Robertson, who died in 2022, and his son Alex, who now runs the firm, became mentors. Tiger Management was an early investor in Crimson Education.
在哈佛上課期間，比頓每週有兩天會去紐約的老虎管理公司辦公室。羅伯遜（2022年去世）以及他的兒子、如今經營該公司的亞歷克斯都成了比頓的導師。老虎管理公司還是Crimson Education的早期投資者之一。
Alex Robertson recalled urging Beaton to have more work-life balance: Why not spend a week in Cancún with his buddies? “Honestly, Alex,” Beaton told him, “I can’t think of anything worse than sitting on a beach for five days when I want to work.”
亞歷克斯·羅伯遜回憶說，他曾勸比頓應該更好地平衡工作與生活：為什麼不和朋友們去坎昆玩一週呢？「說真的，亞歷克斯，」比頓告訴他，「我想不出還有什麼事情比我明明想工作，卻要在海灘上待五天更糟糕的了。」
Beaton was among the dozens of students who took a class on entrepreneurship taught by David Ager, a senior lecturer at Harvard Business School. Beaton stood out, not only because of his prominent spot in the amphitheater — “In the front, three rows up,” Ager said — but also because he was such an active participant.
比頓是數十名選修哈佛商學院高級講師戴維·阿格教授的創業課程的學生之一。比頓之所以格外引人注意不僅因為他坐在階梯教室裡一個非常顯眼的位置——「前排，往上數第三排，」阿格說——還因為他在課堂上非常活躍。
“I say this with love,” Ager said, “but I’m sure there was a little bit of frustration that he had to share the time with other people.”
「我是帶著善意這麼說的，」阿傑爾說，「但我敢肯定，他多少會有點不耐煩，因為他不得不和其他人分享課堂時間。」
Throughout his journey, Beaton has juggled academics and business. Belkin recalled that he often had his laptop open during class to check work emails or his stock portfolio. “Yet everything came in on time,” Belkin said.
一路走來，比頓一直兼顧學業和生意。貝爾金回憶說，他上課時經常打開筆記型電腦查看工作郵件或者自己的股票投資。「但他每次都能按時完成作業，」貝爾金說。
Beaton was multitasking in other ways, too, often talking with the student seated next to him, Priscilla Putzer. “There was a lot of whispering and giggling,” Belkin said.
比頓還在以其他方式同時處理多件事情，他經常和坐在旁邊的學生普裡西拉·普策聊天。「他們經常竊竊私語，還咯咯笑個不停，」貝爾金說。
Beaton and Putzer were married in April at a ceremony on the beach in Santa Monica, California.
比頓和普策於今年4月在加利福尼亞州聖莫尼卡的海灘舉行了婚禮。
A Company Grows
一家公司的成長
Crimson Education has not been immune from criticism. Early on, it faced pushback for using aggressive sales tactics and employing many college students as tutors and counselors.
對於Crimson Education，也並非沒有批評的聲音。早期，它因使用咄咄逼人的銷售策略以及僱用許多大學生作為導師和顧問而遭到質疑。
As the company grew, Beaton said, it shifted to employing more full-time counselors — the company now has 1,200 full-time staff members, he said — and hired an accounting firm to vet its numbers. Of the 10,000 students who have used its services, more than 2,000 have been accepted by Ivy League universities, Stanford or the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, he said.
比頓說，隨著規模不斷壯大，公司逐漸轉向聘用更多全職顧問——他說公司現在有1200名全職員工——並聘請了一家會計師事務所對公司的數據進行審核。他說，在使用了其服務的1萬名學生中，已有超過2000人被常春藤盟校、史丹佛大學或麻省理工學院錄取。
The company has also found itself the subject of a broader critique — that its high price tag effectively self-selects students who already have a good chance of getting into elite universities.
公司還面臨著更廣泛的批評：其高昂的收費實際上會自行篩選出那些本來就很有希望進入頂尖大學的學生。
Jeff Selingo, a journalist who has written several books about college admissions, said that private counselors can be a helpful resource for those who can afford them. “But it’s not going to get you into a place that you can’t get into otherwise,” he said.
傑夫·塞林哥是一名記者，曾撰寫過數本關於大學招生的書。他說，對於那些負擔得起費用的人來說，私人升學顧問確實可以是有用的資源。「但這不會讓你進入一個你本來進不去的地方，」他說。
Hamish Coates, an education expert at the Australian National University and Beaton’s thesis adviser at Tsinghua University, praised Beaton as someone who understood “the power of higher education.” But Coates posed a hypothetical: “Do you really want your child to be grooming themselves for the Ivy League from the age of 5, when 95% fail to get in? Or are you better off doing some finger painting, reading ‘Frog and Toad’ and having a good life?”
澳洲國立大學教育專家、比頓在清華大學的論文導師哈米什·科茨稱讚比頓理解「高等教育的力量」。但科茨提出了一個假設性的問題：「你真的希望自己的孩子從五歲起就開始為進入常春藤盟校做準備嗎？畢竟95%的人最終都進不去。還是說，你更應該讓他們塗塗畫畫手工、讀讀《青蛙和蟾蜍》，過快樂的生活？」
Beaton has heard that argument before — the “pressure-cooker environment” on one side and the “chill, happy childhood” on the other. He doesn’t buy the premise. “The students who try harder in high school are often the happiest and most engaged,” he said.
比頓以前也聽過這種說法——一邊是「高壓鍋般的環境」，另一邊是「輕鬆、快樂的童年」。但他並不認同這種前提。「那些在高中階段更加努力的學生往往也是最快樂、最投入的學生，」他說。
His own future appears to be a work in progress. For now, he said, he is focused on “education and entrepreneurship,” along with starting a family. But several of his friends said they could see him entering politics. He has worked with New Zealand’s center-right National Party, which was once headed by Key.
至於自己的未來，比頓似乎還沒有完全確定。目前，他說自己專注於「教育和創業」，同時也在考慮生子。但他的幾位朋友說，他們可以想像他將來進入政界。他曾與紐西蘭中右翼政黨國家黨合作，而該黨過去曾由約翰·基領導。
“If you said to me, ‘In 35 years’ time, Jamie will still be the CEO of Crimson,’ I don’t see it,” Key said. “I think he’s got other, bigger things that he’s going to move on to.”
「如果你對我說，『35年後，傑米仍將是Crimson的首席執行官』，我不信，」基說。「我認為他還有其他更大的事情要去做。」
