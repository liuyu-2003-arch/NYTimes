美司法部稱兩家中國公司利用必安洗錢

Chinese Firms Used Binance to Launder Iranian Oil Money, Prosecutors Say

DAVID YAFFE-BELLANY, 傅才德 • 2026-09-16
Two Chinese companies used accounts on the cryptocurrency exchange Binance to launder illegal proceeds from the sale of Iranian oil, the Justice Department said in court papers.
美國司法部在法庭文件中說，兩家中國公司利用加密貨幣交易所幣安上的帳戶洗錢，這些資金來自出售伊朗石油的非法收益。
In a civil forfeiture claim filed in federal court in Manhattan on Monday, prosecutors said that the two companies had moved funds intended to finance the Iranian military, including the Islamic Revolutionary Guards Corps, which the United States has classified as a terrorist organization. Binance was not charged with any wrongdoing.
在週一提交給曼哈頓聯邦法院的一項民事沒收申請中，檢察官說，這兩家公司轉移了旨在資助伊朗軍方的資金，包括美國列為恐怖組織的伊斯蘭革命衛隊。幣安未被指控有任何不當行為。
A group of crypto wallets linked to Iran received more than $1.5 billion of the illegal proceeds, the complaint said, though it wasn’t clear exactly how much of that money had moved through Binance, the world’s largest crypto trading platform. The Justice Department plans to seize $61 million, currently held in a set of accounts overseen by Tether, another crypto firm, the complaint said.
起訴書說，一組與伊朗有關的加密幣錢包收到了超過15億美元的非法收益，但尚不清楚其中有多少資金流經了幣安這個全球最大加密貨幣交易平台。起訴書說，司法部計劃沒收目前由另一家加密幣公司Tether掌管的一組帳戶中的6100萬美元。
The flow of illicit funds through Binance was flagged internally last year by a group of the exchange’s compliance officials, according to reports in The New York Times and other outlets. Binance fired or suspended the officials after they pointed out the Iran transactions; the company said the discipline had nothing to do with their warnings.
據《紐約時報》和其他媒體報導，流經幣安的非法資金去年被該交易所的多名合規官員在內部標記。幣安在這些官員指出伊朗交易後將他們解僱或停職；該公司說，這些處罰與他們提出的警告無關。
廣告
The reports in The Times raised concerns in Congress, where a top Senate Democrat opened an investigation into Binance. For years, the company has struggled to eliminate financial crime on its platform. It paid $4.3 billion in penalties in 2023 after pleading guilty to violating U.S. financial laws.
《紐約時報》的報導在國會引發擔憂，一位參議院民主黨高層啟動了對幣安的調查。多年來，該公司一直在努力消除其平台上的金融犯罪。它在2023年承認違反美國金融法後支付了43億美元罰款。
A Binance spokeswoman said in a statement that the exchange had “zero tolerance for sanctions violations or illicit activity.”
幣安一位發言人在聲明中說，該交易所對「違反制裁或非法活動零容忍」。
“This case was not filed against Binance and does not allege wrongdoing by Binance,” she said. “Binance did not permit any transactions with sanctioned individuals.”
「本案不是針對幣安提起的，也沒有指控幣安有不當行為，」她說。「幣安沒有允許與受制裁個人的任何交易。」
Binance has tried to stop illegal activity on its platform since the 2023 settlement, pledging to work with law enforcement around the world to root out financial crime. Its founder, Changpeng Zhao, stepped down as chief executive and served four months in prison.
自2023年和解以來，幣安一直試圖阻止其平台上的非法活動，承諾與世界各地執法部門合作，根除金融犯罪。其創始人趙長鵬辭去首席執行官職務，並服刑四個月。
Last year, Binance forged a business relationship with World Liberty Financial, the crypto start-up founded by President Trump and his three sons. Mr. Trump granted Mr. Zhao a pardon in October.
去年，幣安與World Liberty Financial建立了商業關係，這家加密貨幣初創公司由川普總統及其三個兒子創立。川普在10月赦免了趙長鵬。
Around that time, the compliance officials at Binance raised internal alarms over the movement of funds to Iran, identifying the same two companies named in the complaint. One, Hexa Whale, a firm based in Hong Kong, used Binance to send $490 million to crypto wallets tied to Iranian entities, the employees reported. They found that another $1.2 billion had flowed to Iran-linked entities from an account owned by the financial firm Blessed Trust, a Binance business partner in Hong Kong.
大約在那時，幣安的合規官員就流向伊朗的資金髮出內部警報，確認了起訴書中提到的兩家公司。其中一家是總部位於香港的Hexa Whale，員工報告說，它利用幣安向與伊朗實體有關的加密幣錢包發送了4.9億美元。他們發現，另外還有12億美元從金融公司Blessed Trust擁有的一個帳戶流向了與伊朗有關的實體，該公司是幣安在香港的業務夥伴。
The Binance investigators also learned that an account belonging to Blessed Trust’s chief executive and one controlled by a group of Binance employees had both been accessed from the same electronic device. In the exchange’s internal systems, accounts belonging to Blessed Trust and its chief executive were marked with a note: “Don’t block. Internal accounts,” The Times reported in March.
幣安調查人員還了解到，屬於Blessed Trust首席執行官的帳戶和一個由一群幣安員工控制的帳戶，都曾從同一台電子設備訪問。《紐約時報》在3月報導，在交易所的內部系統中，屬於Blessed Trust及其首席執行官的帳戶被標記了一條注釋：「不要封鎖。內部帳戶。」
Binance has disputed many of the employees’ findings. The company said this year that Blessed Trust was “one of several vendors for Binance” and that the exchange did not control it. Blessed Trust did not immediately respond to a request for comment. Hexa Whale has ceased operations.
幣安否認了這些員工的許多發現。該公司今年說，Blessed Trust是「幣安的幾個供應商之一」，交易所並不控制它。Blessed Trust沒有立即回應置評請求。Hexa Whale已停止運營。
In the court filing on Monday, the Justice Department said that Blessed Trust and Hexa Whale had used Binance to launder the proceeds from black market sales of Iranian oil to buyers in China. The money was funneled to the Iranian government, the complaint said, and used to finance “terrorist activities,” a violation of U.S. sanctions.
在週一的法庭文件中，司法部說，Blessed Trust和Hexa Whale利用幣安清洗在黑市向中國買家出售伊朗石油的收益。起訴書說，這些錢被輸送給伊朗政府，並用於資助「恐怖活動」，違反了美國制裁。
Blessed Trust misrepresented to crypto and financial firms that it provided “wealth management” and other seemingly legitimate services, the complaint said.
起訴書說，Blessed Trust向加密貨幣和金融公司提供虛假陳述，稱其提供「財富管理」和其他看似合法的服務。
“Today’s action demonstrates our determination to deprive the Government of Iran and its terrorist proxies of the illegal money they rely on,” Deputy U.S. Attorney Sean S. Buckley said in a statement. “The Government of Iran relies on black-market sales of sanctioned crude oil to fund its military and foster terrorism in the Middle East and around the world.”
「今天的行動表明我們決心剝奪伊朗政府及其恐怖代理人所依賴的非法資金，」聯邦副檢察官肖恩·S·巴克利在聲明中說。「伊朗政府依賴受制裁原油的黑市銷售來資助其軍隊，並在中東和世界各地扶植恐怖主義。」
