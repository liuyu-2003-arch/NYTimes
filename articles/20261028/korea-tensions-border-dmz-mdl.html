朝韓邊境線爭端引發半島新緊張局勢

Where Is the Korean Border? A Decades-Old Dispute Fuels New Tensions.

CHOE SANG-HUN • 2026-10-28
Last month, three South Korean soldiers were injured in land mine explosions on the western edge of the boundary with North Korea. The blasts were followed by angry exchanges between the two governments, military warning shots and an elevated risk of armed clashes.
上個月，在與朝鮮接壤的邊界西端，三名韓國士兵因地雷爆炸受傷。爆炸發生後，兩國政府之間爆發了激烈交鋒，雙方軍隊進行了警告射擊，爆發武裝衝突的風險隨之升高。
Fueling these tensions is a deceptively simple question: Where, exactly, does the line dividing the two countries lie?
加劇這些緊張局勢的是一個看似簡單卻暗藏玄機的問題：兩國之間的分界線究竟在哪裡？
South Korea says the mines were laid in its territory by North Korea, which rejects the claim and accuses the South of fabricating the episode. This week, North Korea moved artillery pieces out of hiding along its western coastal border and pointed them south, a show of force it has often used during periods of heightened tensions.
韓國稱，這些地雷是朝鮮在其領土上埋設的。朝鮮拒絕這一說法，並指責韓國捏造了這一事件。本週，朝鮮將其西部沿海邊境的火炮從隱蔽處調出並指向南方，這是它在緊張局勢加劇時期經常使用的武力展示手段。
“If a jackboot or a spark of fire from the Republic of Korea goes even a millimeter beyond the border to violate our territory, our border guard forces will take immediate and powerful counteraction,” North Korea warned earlier this month, using South Korea’s official name.
「如果大韓民國的一隻軍靴或一點火花越過邊界哪怕一毫米，侵犯我國領土，我們的邊防部隊將立即採取強有力的反制行動，」朝鮮本月早些時候警告稱，並使用了韓國的官方名稱。
Historians often describe the Korean border as the final flashpoint of the Cold War. Nearly two million troops stand ready to resume the Korean War, which was halted 73 years ago by a truce. Yet the boundary has never been clearly delineated, let alone with the precision implied by North Korea’s warning about a millimeter.
歷史學家常把朝鮮半島邊境描述為冷戰的最後一個衝突點。近200萬軍隊隨時準備重啟73年前因停戰協定而暫停的朝鮮戰爭。然而，邊界從未被明確劃定，更遑論像朝鮮所警告的那樣精確到毫米。
When the American-led United Nations Command, North Korea and China signed the 1953 armistice, they established a 2.5-mile-wide buffer known as the Demilitarized Zone, or DMZ. Through its center, they drew the 150-mile-long Military Demarcation Line, or MDL, a temporary divider to keep opposing forces apart as long as the truce lasted. Because the war was never formally resolved with a peace treaty, the MDL was never intended to be an official international border.
當美國領導的聯合國軍司令部、朝鮮和中國簽署1953年停戰協定時，建立了一個寬四公里的緩衝區，稱為非軍事區。在非軍事區中心劃定了約248公里長的軍事分界線，這是一條臨時分界線，在停戰協定持續期間將敵對雙方部隊隔開。由於戰爭從未以和平條約正式解決，軍事分界線原本就並非作為正式的國際邊界而設立。
But the MDL was drawn by hand in pencil on a 1:50,000-scale map attached to the armistice agreement. On the ground, that pencil stroke translated into a fuzzy strip about 165 feet wide. To clarify the boundary, the warring parties installed 1,292 yellow temporary markers across hills, valleys and marshes to indicate the MDL’s location. They maintained the markers regularly until 1973, when a bloody clash during a repair operation left dozens of soldiers dead on both sides.
但軍事分界線是用鉛筆手繪在附於停戰協定的1:50,000比例地圖上的。在實地，這道鉛筆線轉化為一條大約500米的模糊地帶。為澄清邊界，交戰各方在山丘、山谷和沼澤中安裝了1292個黃色臨時標記，以指示軍事分界線的位置。他們定期維護這些標記，直到1973年，一次維修行動中的血腥衝突導致雙方數十名士兵死亡。

Yellow Flags
黃旗
In the decades since, most of those markers have vanished, swallowed by winds, rains, floods and landslides. Today fewer than 200 rusting posts remain, leaving the MDL virtually indistinguishable. Both sides developed their own versions of the line without sharing them. Instead, they spent years accusing each other’s troops of repeated border incursions.
此後幾十年，大多數標記已經消失，被風雨、洪水和山體滑坡吞沒。如今只剩下不到200根銹跡斑斑的柱子，使軍事分界線幾乎無法辨認。雙方各自發展了自己版本的邊界線，卻不與對方分享。相反，他們多年來一直指責對方軍隊屢次越界。
The depth of this confusion became clear in 2018. During a brief thaw in inter-Korean relations, both militaries agreed to demolish several guard posts within the DMZ to lower tensions. When officers from both sides tried to meet on the boundary to inspect the work, they could not agree on where that boundary actually was. Nor could they share common geolocation coordinates because they used different satellite systems: South Korea used the American-operated GPS, while North Korea relied on the Russian-owned Glonass.
這種混亂的嚴重程度在2018年變得清晰。在朝韓關係短暫緩和期間，兩國軍方同意拆除非軍事區內幾個哨所，以緩和緊張局勢。當雙方軍官試圖在邊界會面檢查工作時，他們無法就邊界實際位置達成一致。雙方也無法共享共同的地理位置坐標，因為他們使用不同的衛星系統：韓國使用美國運營的GPS，朝鮮則依賴俄羅斯擁有的格洛納斯系統。
“So we had to raise yellow flags” at proposed meeting spots, recalled Cho Yong Kun, a former North Korea policy coordinator at South Korea’s Defense Ministry. “There is no MDL inside the DMZ.”
「所以我們不得不在提議的會面地點升起黃旗，」韓國國防部前朝鮮政策協調員趙容根（音）回憶說。「非軍事區內沒有軍事分界線。」
The stakes soared after North Korea’s leader, Kim Jong-un, labeled South Korea as his country’s “most hostile enemy state” in 2023, and the following year, ordered his military to fortify the southern border, meaning the version the North had drawn for itself.
2023年，朝鮮領導人金正恩將韓國稱為朝鮮的「最敵對的敵國」，局勢隨後驟然升級；次年，他下令軍隊加固南部邊境，即朝鮮自行劃定的邊境線。
North Korean troops moved into the DMZ, constructing new military roads, barbed-wire fences, concrete barriers, guard posts and minefields along the length of its version of the MDL. In several locations, the South Korean military spotted North Korean soldiers planting anti-personnel mines dozens of feet south of what it considered the correct MDL.
朝鮮軍隊進入非軍事區，沿著其單方面劃定的軍事分界線，全線修建新的軍事道路、鐵絲網圍欄、混凝土屏障、哨所和雷區。在幾個地點，韓國軍方發現朝鮮士兵在其認定正確的軍事分界線以南數十米處埋設殺傷人員地雷。

“According to our manuals, we broadcast warnings when they approached the MDL and fired warning shots when they crossed it,” said Brig. Gen. Kim Sung Yoon, a chief of intelligence analysis at the South Korean military. “When we did that, we saw them consulting their own map, but they did not appear to believe they had violated the MDL.”
「根據我們的手冊，當他們接近軍事分界線時，我們廣播警告；當他們越過時，我們鳴槍示警，」韓國軍方情報分析負責人金成允（音）准將說。「當我們採取這些行動時，看到他們正在查閱自己的地圖，但他們似乎並不認為自己違反了軍事分界線。」
North Korea has also deployed increasingly powerful artillery and missile systems, some capable of carrying nuclear warheads, transforming the border into an even more formidable military divide.
朝鮮還部署了越來越強大的火炮和導彈系統，其中部分系統能夠攜帶核彈頭，將邊境變成更加難以逾越的軍事分界。
‘Firing Positions’
「射擊陣地」
The MDL conundrum had devastating consequences on Sept. 21, when 18 South Korean troops ventured into a lush wilderness inside the DMZ where no South Korean soldier had set foot for decades. They were tasked with clearing a path and sweeping for mines so that United Nations officials could inspect an area where North Korea was suspected of planting mines in the South’s territory. They had just reached the site when two mines exploded. Three soldiers were injured, including a battalion commander who lost his left foot.
9月21日，軍事分界線難題造成了毀滅性後果。18名韓國士兵冒險進入非軍事區內一片茂密的荒野，幾十年來沒有韓國士兵踏足那裡。他們的任務是清理道路並掃雷，以便聯合國官員能夠檢查朝鮮涉嫌在韓國領土埋設地雷的地區。他們剛到達現場，兩枚地雷就爆炸了。三名士兵受傷，其中一名營長左腳被炸斷。
South Korea accused the North of planting the mines on its side of the border and demanded an apology. Its military warned that the North must clear the explosives or the South would remove them itself and “take firm punitive action” if the North interfered.
韓國指責朝鮮在韓國一側邊界埋設地雷，並要求道歉。其軍方警告，朝鮮必須清除爆炸物，否則韓國將自己清除，如果朝鮮干涉，將「採取堅決懲罰行動」。
Pyongyang dismissed the claims as a “farce” and warned the South that its “new firepower units have taken their firing positions.”
平壤將這些說法斥為「鬧劇」，並警告韓國，「新的火力部隊已進入發射陣地」。
North Korea’s aggressive engineering inside the DMZ appears aimed at hardening a temporary truce line into a permanent, official border, according to Hong Min, an analyst at the Seoul-based Korea Institute for National Unification.
首爾韓國統一研究院分析師洪敏（音）說，朝鮮在非軍事區內的激進工程似乎旨在將臨時停戰線固化為永久、正式的邊界。
That analysis aligns with Mr. Kim’s new doctrine of total isolation from South Korea. By no longer treating the South as a partner for eventual reunification, but as an enemy state to be shunned, contained and defeated — with nuclear weapons, if necessary — Mr. Kim can more easily justify expanding his nuclear arsenal and shielding his citizens from South Korea’s cultural influence.
這一分析符合金正恩與韓國徹底隔絕韓國的新方針。通過不再將韓國視為最終統一的夥伴，而是視為應被迴避、遏制和擊敗的敵國——必要時使用核武器——金正恩可以更容易地為擴大核武庫、讓公民免受韓國文化影響而辯護。
North Korea has ignored an offer for dialogue. Instead, it has vowed to push forward with its border fortifications, threatening to resume psychological warfare with loudspeakers if South Korea continued to harass North Korean troops working along the border with warning broadcasts.
朝鮮無視對話提議。相反，它誓言推進邊境加固，威脅如果韓國繼續用警告廣播騷擾在邊境工作的朝鮮軍隊，將重啟擴音器心理戰。

The North’s goal is to make the border “eternally impregnable” against “the most despicable and disgusting enemy in the world,” Kim Yo-jong, the North Korean leader’s sister and regime spokeswoman, said last week.
朝鮮領導人金正恩的妹妹、政權發言人金與正上週說，朝鮮的目標是，面對「世界上最卑鄙、最令人厭惡的敵人」時，讓邊境變得「永遠堅不可摧」。

Choe Sang-Hun是時報駐首爾首席記者，報導韓國和朝鮮新聞。
