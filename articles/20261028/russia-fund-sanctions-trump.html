川普考慮解除對普丁「小金庫」的制裁

The U.S. Called It Putin’s ‘Slush Fund.’ Now Trump May Drop Sanctions on It.

ANTON TROIANOVSKI • 2026-10-28
The Trump administration is exploring a new concession to Russia: lifting sanctions on the country’s flagship government investment fund, long viewed by U.S. officials as one of the Kremlin’s most powerful tools of international influence.
川普政府正在研究對俄羅斯做出新妥協：解除對該國旗艦主權投資基金的制裁。長期以來，美國官員一直將該基金視為克里姆林宮最強有力的國際影響力工具之一。
Russian officials have pushed for the move as part of broader talks about the fund’s involvement in shipping Russian diesel fuel to the United States, according to people familiar with the negotiations.
據知情人士透露，俄羅斯官員極力推動這一舉措，將其作為更廣泛談判的一部分，該談判涉及該基金參與向美國運送俄羅斯柴油事宜。
A U.S. official confirmed that the administration was assessing the fund’s application for sanctions to be removed through established procedures as part of an effort to “improve U.S.-Russia relations and help end the war.” The official, who spoke on the condition of anonymity because of the sensitivity of the talks, praised the fund’s chief, Kirill Dmitriev, as “a helpful envoy” to U.S. negotiators in discussions to end the conflict.
一名美國官員證實，作為「改善美俄關係並推動停戰」努力的一部分，美政府正在依既定程序評估該基金提出的解除制裁申請。因談判事涉敏感，該官員要求匿名，並稱讚了該基金負責人基里爾·德米特里耶夫，稱他在結束衝突的談判中成為美方談判人員眼中的「得力特使」。
The consideration of more sanctions relief for Russia, which has not been previously reported, shows how the Kremlin is using a recent flurry of diplomacy over the war in Ukraine to advance an agenda that appears to have little to do with securing peace.
考慮為俄羅斯提供進一步制裁寬免的消息此前未見報導。這表明克里姆林宮正在利用近期圍繞烏克蘭戰事掀起的外交熱潮，推進一項看似與締造和平關係不大的訴求。
廣告
In recent weeks, Mr. Trump’s envoys and Mr. Putin have discussed the sale of part of a Russian oil giant to a well-connected investor group; an effort to produce natural gas in Alaska, from which several executives resigned amid concerns about hidden Russian financing; and a proposal to increase Russian gas exports to Europe that has stunned European governments.
最近數週，川普的特使與普丁磋商了多項議題，包括將一家俄羅斯石油巨頭的部分股權出售給一個背景深厚的投資財團；推進阿拉斯加天然氣開發項目（該項目數名高管因擔憂存在隱秘的俄羅斯資金注入而辭職）；以及提出一項令歐洲各國震驚的擴大俄羅斯對歐天然氣出口提案。
Removing sanctions on the multibillion-dollar fund, called the Russian Direct Investment Fund, would be a significant unwinding of the yearslong U.S. effort to use economic pressure to get Russia to change its behavior. The fund has invested billions of dollars in Russian companies, often alongside investors from the Middle East and elsewhere, pushing to expand Russia’s global influence.
解除對掌握數百億資金的俄羅斯直接投資基金的制裁將對美國政策構成重大逆轉，華盛頓多年來通過經濟施壓促使俄羅斯改變行徑。該基金已向俄企注資數十億美元，力求擴大俄羅斯的全球影響力，並且常常是與中東及其他地區的投資者聯手。
The Biden administration imposed sanctions on the fund days after Russia’s invasion of Ukraine in 2022, calling it “a slush fund” for President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia that was “emblematic of Russia’s broader kleptocracy.”
拜登政府在2022年俄羅斯入侵烏克蘭數日後便對該基金實施制裁，指責其為俄羅斯總統弗拉基米爾·V·普丁的「小金庫」，「集中體現了俄羅斯更廣泛的盜賊統治制度。」
Eric Green, who oversaw Russia policy as part of the White House’s National Security Council at the time, said targeting the fund was an early priority because its access to the Western financial system could help Russia finance the war and because of its “association with projects closely identified with Putin.”
當時在白宮國家安全委員會分管對俄政策的埃里克·格林指出，打擊該基金是早期的優先事項，因為其接觸西方金融體系的管道可能為俄羅斯籌措戰爭資金提供便利，且其「與普丁高度相關的項目存在牽連」。
Now the fund has taken a lead role in implementing Mr. Trump’s diesel deal, helping to arrange the first shipments of Russian diesel to the United States after Mr. Trump suspended sanctions on it on Oct. 9, a key Russian official has said. The fund’s head, Mr. Dmitriev, has acted as Mr. Putin’s economic envoy to the Trump administration, leading the Kremlin’s effort to convince U.S. officials of the great potential of business deals with Russia.
俄羅斯一名核心官員表示，在川普於10月9日暫緩對俄柴油制裁後，該基金目前在執行川普的柴油協議中發揮了主導作用，協助安排了運往美國的首批俄羅斯柴油。該基金負責人德米特里耶夫充任普丁面對川普政府的經濟特使，牽頭克里姆林宮的行動，力圖讓美方官員確信與俄羅斯開展商業往來的巨大潛能。
“It’s been really, really good dealing with Kirill,” Steve Witkoff, Mr. Trump’s special envoy for peace missions, told Mr. Putin at the negotiating table in St. Petersburg, Russia, last week.
川普的和平特使史提夫·威特科夫上週在俄羅斯聖彼得堡的談判桌上對普丁表示：「與基里爾打交道非常、非常順利。」
廣告
In the Trump team’s view, negotiating business deals with Mr. Putin now can help end the war in Ukraine by showing the Russian president that the United States is serious about building a new and prosperous relationship with Moscow. To the many critics of that approach, including some Republican lawmakers, Mr. Trump is simply ceding economic benefits to Mr. Putin without pressuring him to end the war.
在川普團隊看來，眼下與普丁商談商業交易有助於結束烏克蘭戰事，因為這能向這位俄羅斯總統表明，美國正認真考慮與莫斯科構建全新的繁榮關係。但在該方案的眾多批評者（包括部分共和黨議員）看來，川普純粹是在未對普丁施壓促其停戰的情況下拱手出讓經濟利益。
Mr. Green, the former Biden administration official, said lifting sanctions on the fund would be a “real strong message that we are relaxing the previous position that we weren’t going to relax the economic sanctions and other costs until there was a settlement in Ukraine.”
前拜登政府官員格林表示，解除對該基金的制裁將是一個「極其強烈的信號，表明我們正在鬆動原先的立場，即在烏克蘭達成和平協議前絕不放寬經濟制裁和其他的施壓」。
Speaking at a conference in Moscow on Oct. 16, Mr. Dmitriev boasted that the diesel deal was among the “concrete examples” starting to convince Americans that “sanctions are, in principle, philosophically wrong.” He said that his fund was prepared to handle U.S. payments for the Russian diesel, despite “a sea of sanctions restrictions.”
德米特里耶夫10月16日在莫斯科的一場會議上公開聲稱，這筆柴油交易是開始讓美國人確信「制裁在原則和哲學層面都是錯誤的」的一個「切實範例」。他表示，儘管面臨「重重製裁限制」，他的基金已做好準備處理美方採購俄羅斯柴油的款項結算。
Mr. Dmitriev left unmentioned that he was trying to get sanctions removed on his own fund amid the talks over the diesel deal, according to the people familiar with the negotiations. Mr. Dmitriev’s fund offered to serve as the go-between for Russian diesel being sold to the United States, while also asking for American sanctions on the fund to be relaxed, the people said.
知情人士透露，德米特里耶夫在柴油協議談判中對爭取解除自身基金制裁一事未作聲張。他們表示，德米特里耶夫的基金主動提出作為向美國出售俄羅斯柴油的中間機構，但同時要求美方放寬對該基金的制裁。
The talks played out as Mr. Dmitriev helped put in motion an effort to hand Mr. Trump a visible win at a time of record high diesel prices.
這場談判推進之際，正值德米特里耶夫協助啟動一項行動，旨在柴油價格創歷史新高之際為川普奉上一場顯眼的勝利。
Three tankers carrying Russian diesel have set sail for the United States this month, according to Russian officials. The shipments were made “with the significant support” of Mr. Dmitriev’s fund, a Russian vice prime minister, Aleksandr Novak, said on Friday.
俄羅斯官員稱，本月已有三艘載有俄羅斯柴油的油輪啟航開赴美國。俄羅斯副總理亞歷山大·諾瓦克週五表示，這批運輸是在德米特里耶夫所在基金的「鼎力支持」下完成的。
The diesel was shipped without any U.S. buyers having paid for it, according to some of the people familiar with the negotiations. One of the tankers, the Agioi Fanendes, was scheduled to arrive in the United States on Nov. 7, after leaving Primorsk, Russia, on Oct. 23, according to Kpler and TankerTrackers.com, two ship tracking firms. The ship recently changed its destination from New York to “for orders,” meaning it had not yet received instructions on where to discharge.
部分知情人士透露，這批柴油起運時並無任何美國買家先行付款。據船舶追蹤機構Kpler和TankerTrackers.com的數據，其中一艘名為Agioi Fanendes的油輪於10月23日駛離俄羅斯普裡莫爾斯克港，原定於11月7日抵達美國。該船近期將其目的港由紐約更改為「等候指令」，這意味著它尚未收到具體的卸貨指示。
As Mr. Dmitriev’s fund sought sanctions relief amid the diesel talks, the Treasury Department took the position that the fund already had all the permission it needed to handle diesel transactions thanks to the Treasury’s Oct. 9 announcement suspending sanctions on Russian diesel.
在德米特里耶夫的基金借柴油談判謀求制裁寬免之際，美國財政部所持立場是，鑒於其10月9日公布的聲明已暫停對俄柴油的制裁，該基金實際上已具備處理柴油交易所需的一切許可。
Instead, the Treasury is going through its normal process in assessing the fund’s applications for getting sanctions removed, meaning that it would need to prove that the reasons for which it was originally sanctioned are no longer in effect, people familiar with the process said.
知情人士指出，財政部目前仍按常規程序評估該基金的解禁申請，這意味著該基金必須證明當初對其施加制裁的依據現已不復存在。
廣告
Despite Mr. Trump’s friendly rhetoric toward Mr. Putin, he has until now left almost all of the Biden-era sanctions on Russia in place.
儘管川普對普丁態度親善，但截至目前，他仍幾乎全盤保留了拜登時期施加的所有對俄制裁措施。
But Mr. Dmitriev, a financier fluent in English who studied at Harvard and Stanford, has shown significant clout with the Trump administration. He has spearheaded the Kremlin’s effort to float potential economic deals to Washington, a strategy apparently aimed at getting Mr. Trump to focus more on doing business with Russia and less on pressuring it to stop its invasion of Ukraine.
然而，畢業於哈佛大學和史丹佛大學、英語流利的金融家德米特里耶夫在川普政府面前展示了不容小覷的能量。他帶頭落實克里姆林宮向華盛頓拋出經濟合作方案的策略，該策略顯然旨在促使川普把注意力轉向與俄做生意，而非施壓迫使其停止對烏侵略。
“The ice has finally broken,” Mr. Dmitriev said at the Oct. 16 conference, emphasizing that he was discussing deals to be done with the United States “right now” rather than only after the end of the war.
德米特里耶夫在10月16日的會議上表示：「破冰之日終於到來。」他還強調自己目前正探討與美國在「當下」即可開展的交易，而非必須拖到戰後。
Mr. Dmitriev’s wife is close with a daughter of Mr. Putin, people who know them say, and he has done business with Gulf leaders like Mohammed bin Salman, the crown prince of Saudi Arabia, who also have the ear of Mr. Trump.
知情人士稱，德米特里耶夫的妻子與普丁的一位女兒關係密切，且他曾與沙烏地阿拉伯王儲穆罕默德·本·薩勒曼等海灣國家領導人打交道，這些人同樣對川普極具影響力。
During the pandemic, Mr. Dmitriev and his fund financed the development of Russia’s Covid-19 vaccine, spearheaded its distribution around the world and often criticized Western vaccines. Since Mr. Trump’s re-election two years ago, Mr. Dmitriev has taken to X almost daily, in English, and echoed White House talking points.
疫情期間，德米特里耶夫及其掌管的基金注資研發了俄羅斯新冠疫苗，力推該疫苗在全球推廣，並頻頻批評西方疫苗。自川普兩年前再次當選以來，德米特里耶夫幾乎每天都在社交平台X上用英文發帖，呼應白宮的說法。
“Ideological warmongering Biden-think is being rejected as destructive and inefficient,” he wrote about Mr. Trump’s diesel deal last week.
談及川普推動的柴油協議時，他上週在社交平台寫道：「帶有意識型態色彩的好戰拜登思維，正因其破壞性和低效而遭到唾棄。」
廣告
Mr. Dmitriev’s primary U.S. interlocutors have been Mr. Witkoff, who is also a real-estate developer and friend of Mr. Trump, and Jared Kushner, Mr. Trump’s son-in-law. In St. Petersburg last week, Mr. Witkoff approvingly told Mr. Dmitriev that “our Treasury Department is working with you now independently.”
德米特里耶夫在美方的主要聯絡人是身為房地產開發商兼川普密友的威特科夫，以及川普的女婿賈里德·庫什納。威特科夫上週在聖彼得堡向德米特里耶夫讚許地表示，「我們的財政部眼下正獨立與你們接洽。」
“I think that’s leading to a sense of what the possibilities are between our two nations,” Mr. Witkoff went on. “We even talk about it now openly with the Ukrainians, to give them some sort of sense about what the possibilities will be when this whole conflict is ended.”
威特科夫接著表示：「我認為這讓外界看到了兩國合作的潛能。我們現在甚至正公開同烏克蘭方面談及這些前景，讓他們對衝突徹底結束後的種種可能性心中有數。」
