「機器人健身房」：歐洲在機器人領域追趕中國

In the A.I. ‘Robogym,’ Machines Train to Be More Like Humans

JAMES PATON • 2026-10-28
Neura Robotics is not a household name. It’s not even the most prominent business in Metzingen, a German town of 22,000 people that is also home to the fashion brand Hugo Boss.
紐鼐機器人(Neura Robotics)並非家喻戶曉的名字，甚至不是梅青根最顯眼的企業——這座人口2萬餘人的德國小鎮同時也是時尚品牌雨果博斯(Hugo Boss)的總部所在地。
But in just a few months, Neura’s founder, David Reger, has attracted investment from some of the world’s most powerful technology companies, including Nvidia and Amazon, and received invitations to meet the leaders of Europe’s three largest economies.
然而短短數月間，紐鼐創始人戴維·雷格爾(David Reger)已獲得英偉達(Nvidia)、亞馬遜(Amazon)等全球最具實力的科技公司投資，並受邀與歐洲三大經濟體的領導人會面。
The company’s rise underscores the importance of artificial intelligence and robotics, technologies that could help Europe transform its ailing manufacturing sector and take on Chinese competition. Neura is among companies at the forefront of Europe’s efforts to seize the potential economic gains as A.I. makes the leap from the digital world to the physical.
這家公司的崛起凸顯出人工智慧與機器人技術的重要性：它們可能幫助歐洲改造萎靡不振的製造業，並應對來自中國的競爭。隨著人工智慧從數字世界跨入物理世界，歐洲正力圖抓住這一潛在經濟收益，而紐鼐充當著開路先鋒的角色。
“We are not efficient anymore,” said Mr. Reger, who raised as much as $1.4 billion in June. “But I do believe that this can be solved.”
「我們已不再高效，」6月籌得高達14億美元的雷格爾說。「但我確實相信，這個問題能夠解決。」
廣告
Whether A.I.-powered robots can live up to their promise remains unclear. Companies need to teach them to perform complex tasks, a challenge that requires the collection of vast amounts of data.
人工智慧驅動的機器人能否兌現承諾，眼下仍不明朗。企業必須教會它們執行複雜任務，而這一挑戰需要收集海量數據。
European robots are also far outnumbered.
歐洲的機器人數量也遠遠落後。
After rolling out 2.3 million industrial robots, almost half the global fleet, China is moving into the next frontier. Unlike the mostly one-armed machines that have been used for decades to perform repetitive tasks, the next generation of robots is expected to see, hear, feel and — most important — learn.
在部署了230萬台工業機器人、幾乎佔全球總量一半之後，中國正在邁向下一個前沿。過去數十年間用於執行重複性任務的機器大多只有單臂，而下一代機器人預計將具備視覺、聽覺和觸覺，還有最重要的學習能力。
One of the keys to Europe’s strategy is a tech hub on the edge of Munich Airport. Inside the facility, as many as 100 humanoids and other robots have embarked on a 24-7 training mission. On a recent morning, a day after joining business leaders in Berlin for a discussion with Chancellor Friedrich Merz of Germany and Prime Minister Andy Burnham of Britain, Mr. Reger opened the “robogym” to a journalist for the first time. He declined to provide details of the meetings with the government leaders.
歐洲戰略的關鍵一環是慕尼黑機場邊緣的一座技術中心。設施之內，多達100台人形機器人及其他機器人已投入全天候訓練。不久前，雷格爾在柏林與商界領袖一道同德國總理弗里德裡希·默茨和英國首相安迪·伯納姆會談。第二天上午，他首次向一名記者開放了這座「機器人健身房」。他拒絕透露與政府領導人會面的細節。
Wearing a black blazer, a black shirt and black pants, Mr. Reger, 38, introduced the team. The robots picked up car parts and placed them into containers, stacked boxes and carried out other tasks, mimicking the movements of human trainers who guided them remotely. One robot practiced tightening its grip on a glass, learning how to respond when water is added.
現年38歲、身著黑色外套、襯衫和長褲的雷格爾介紹了團隊。機器人拿起汽車零件放入容器，堆疊箱體，並完成其他任務，模仿著遙控它們的人類訓練員的動作。一台機器人反覆練習握緊玻璃杯，學習杯中加水後該如何應對。
When one robot learns a new skill, they all get smarter as the data is fed into a cloud-based platform, or a shared brain. Neura has signed up dozens of automotive, logistics, pharmaceutical and other companies to carry out their own training in the gym, declining to identify them. More than 2,600 have applied for space in one of the sites, Neura’s chief said.
當一台機器人掌握新技能，數據會被送入一個雲端平台——或者說一個共享大腦——所有機器人便隨之變得更聰明。紐鼐已簽約數十家汽車、物流、製藥等領域的公司，讓它們在健身房中進行各自的訓練，但拒絕披露這些公司的名稱。紐鼐首席執行官稱，已有超過2600家企業申請在其中一個場地獲得空間。
The training center, developed with the Technical University of Munich, is part of a wider plan. By the end of the year, Neura aims to have five gyms operating globally, including one already opened in Hangzhou, China. Ten more are expected to follow.
這座由紐鼐與慕尼黑工業大學共同開發的培訓中心是一項更大計劃的一部分。紐鼐的目標是在年底前在全球運營五座健身房，其中一座已在中國杭州開設，預計還將新增十座。
Unlike generative A.I. models trained on enormous amounts of internet data, physical A.I. depends on the costly collection of real-world data. Mr. Reger likened it to learning how to swim. You can’t simply watch videos; you have to get in the water.
生成式人工智慧模型依靠海量互聯網數據訓練，物理人工智慧則不同，它依賴於成本高昂的現實世界數據採集。雷格爾將其比作學游泳：光看影片不夠，你必須下水。

China, however, is building most of the pools.
然而，大多數的泳池正由中國建造。
More than 90 humanoid data collection centers are running across China, according to Interact Analysis, a consulting firm. And the campaign, fueled by state support and a need to replace retiring workers, shows no signs of slowing down. At least 50 more sites are under development. Europe has fewer than 10 facilities.
據諮詢公司Interact Analysis統計，中國各地已有90餘個人形機器人數據採集中心投入運行。在政府支持與替換退休工人的需求推動下，這場運動毫無放緩跡象，至少還有50個場所正在建設中。歐洲的設施則不足10個。
It’s not just the scale of China’s operation that is making headlines. A Chinese humanoid in August broke Usain Bolt’s record in a 100-meter sprint, while dozens of others ran a half-marathon in Beijing. At the Paris Motor Show, robots developed by China’s Agibot danced to the Dua Lipa song “Levitating.”
登上頭條的不只是中國行動的規模。8月，一台中國人形機器人在百米短跑中打破了尤塞恩·博爾特的紀錄，另有數十台在北京跑完半程馬拉松。在巴黎車展上，中國智元機器人(Agibot)研發的機器人隨著杜阿·利帕的歌曲《Levitating》起舞。
“The skill is undeniable, but a lot of it, especially in the humanoid sector, is around what we’d call entertainment robots versus industrial robots,” said Arnaud Robert, the president of Hexagon Robotics, a Swiss company that is working on a pilot with BMW at one of the German automaker’s plants in Leipzig.
「這種技能是毋庸置疑的，但其中許多——尤其是在人形機器人領域——屬於我們所說的娛樂機器人，而非工業機器人，」海克斯康機器人(Hexagon Robotics)總裁阿諾·羅伯特說。這家瑞士公司正與寶馬在其位於萊比錫的一家工廠開展試點合作。
That leaves an opening for European companies, he said.
他說，這給歐洲公司留下了空間。
A.I. robots are hitting the factory floor in Europe for the first time, with Germany emerging as the main training ground. Robots are expected to move into production at the BMW site by the first quarter of next year. Agile Robots, in Munich, is working with partners including Google DeepMind. Another company, Humanoid, aims to integrate robots into the operations of Schaeffler, a German auto supplier, by the end of 2026.
人工智慧機器人正首次進入歐洲的工廠車間，德國正在成為主要訓練場。預計到明年第一季度，機器人將在寶馬基地投入生產。總部位於慕尼黑的思靈機器人(Agile Robots)正與谷歌DeepMind等夥伴合作。另一家公司Humanoid則計劃在2026年底前將機器人整合進德國汽車供應商舍弗勒(Schaeffler)的運營。
廣告
While Neura advances with the gyms, it is teaming up with another supplier, Bosch, and other companies, equipping more than 1,000 workers with sensor suits to record their movements and capture other data during their regular shifts.
在推進健身房業務的同時，紐鼐還與另一家供應商博世(Bosch)及其他公司合作，為1000多名工人配備傳感器套件，在正常輪班中記錄他們的動作並採集其他數據。
European companies want to be judged on their ability to develop technology that translates into tangible results. But they will need to prove that their robots can operate safely and efficiently — and at a low enough cost to make them economically viable.
歐洲公司希望外界關注的是它們將開發技術轉化為切實成果的能力。但它們必須證明自己的機器人能夠安全、高效地運行——而且成本足夠低，具備經濟可行性。
Smart robots in factories, warehouses, hospitals and hotels are forecast to play a critical role as countries cope with aging populations and shrinking work forces. The number of humanoids deployed worldwide could rise to one million in 2030 from 15,000 last year, if costs fall and the technology does not encounter regulatory headwinds, said Zornitsa Todorova, a researcher at Barclays.
隨著各國應對人口老齡化和勞動力萎縮，工廠、倉庫、醫院和酒店中的智能機器人預計將發揮關鍵作用。巴克萊(Barclays)研究員佐爾尼察·托多羅娃表示，如果成本下降且技術不遭遇監管阻力，全球部署的人形機器人數量可能從去年的1.5萬台增至2030年的100萬台。
“The next 10 years will be the decade of the robot — and at the moment, it looks like that decade might belong to China,” Ms. Todorova said. Still, Europe’s focus on safety and expertise in hardware, such as actuators that enable robots to move, provide an edge, she said.
「未來十年將是機器人的十年——而眼下，這個十年看起來可能屬於中國，」托多羅娃說。不過她認為，歐洲對安全的重視以及在硬件領域的專長——例如讓機器人得以運動的執行器——構成了一種優勢。
Neura raised funds this year from several prominent companies, including existing partners Nvidia and Amazon. Leading the investment was Tether, the cryptocurrency company, which is valued at more than $180 billion and has faced scrutiny from lawmakers and regulators. Neura said part of the $1.4 billion was tied to milestones that it was on track to achieve.
紐鼐今年從多家知名企業籌得資金，包括現有合作夥伴英偉達和亞馬遜。領投方是加密貨幣公司泰達(Tether)，這家估值超過1800億美元的公司正受到立法者和監管機構的審視。紐鼐表示，14億美元中的一部分是與它有望達成的里程碑掛鈎的。
Growing up as the eldest of 11 siblings in Rottweil, 50 miles from Metzingen, Mr. Reger dreamed of living overseas, eventually heading to California. Instead of finding a tech job in Silicon Valley, however, he got his start working with a Christian organization to help homeless people in San Francisco.
雷格爾在距梅青根50英里的羅特韋爾長大，是11個兄弟姐妹中的長子。他夢想移居海外，最終去了加利福尼亞。但他一開始並沒有在矽谷找到科技工作，而是與一家基督教組織合作幫助舊金山的無家可歸者。
After returning home, he developed a deeper appreciation for Europe and its health and social systems. Rather than attending a university, he taught himself engineering and got a job in Switzerland, where he built his first robots.
回國之後，他對歐洲及其醫療和社會保障體系有了更深的認同。他沒有上大學，而是自學了工程，在瑞士找到一份工作，並在那裡造出了自己的第一批機器人。
廣告
Mr. Reger, who also met with France’s president, Emmanuel Macron, in September, said he aimed to become “Europe’s largest taxpayer,” combining A.I. and robotics to help create an economic engine for Europe that is even bigger than the car industry. Failing to reap the gains, he said, would be costly.
雷格爾在9月與法國總統埃馬紐埃爾·馬克宏會面。他表示，自己的目標是成為「歐洲最大的納稅人」，把人工智慧與機器人技術結合起來，為歐洲打造一個比汽車業更大的經濟引擎。他說，如果不能收穫這些收益，代價將十分高昂。
“This is a competition,” Mr. Reger said. “If you see other countries get more efficient through robotics and we don’t, it’s going to be very difficult to come out of this situation.”
「這是一場競爭，」雷格爾說。「如果其他國家通過機器人技術變得更高效，而我們沒有，那麼要走出這種局面將非常困難。」

James Paton是時報國際商業和經濟記者，常駐倫敦。
