川普應對失敗的「經典」方式：製造混亂

Trump Trots Out His Chaotic Greatest Hits to a Weary Public

KATIE ROGERS • 2026-11-11
Coping with loss looks different for everyone, and President Trump has processed the country’s nearly wholesale rejection of the Republican Party in the midterm elections in his own way.
每個人應對失敗的方式都不一樣，而川普總統正以他自己的方式來消化這個國家在期中選舉中對共和黨近乎全面的否定。
The last week has seen the president renew false claims about rigged elections, float a mixed martial arts executive as his new press secretary, muse about the beams in the White House ballroom, oust his chief of staff and call unannounced into C-SPAN.
過去一週裡，總統重新拋出了關於選舉被操縱的虛假指控，提議讓一名綜合格鬥賽事高管擔任他的新任新聞秘書，念叨著白宮宴會廳的橫樑，解僱了他的幕僚長，並在未事先通知的情況下和C-SPAN（公共事務衛星有線電視網，實況轉播美國國會現場等——編注）進行了電話連線。
In other words, this is all unfolding exactly as expected. The news has been bad, and Mr. Trump knows it. Cue the mayhem.
換言之，一切都和預料的一樣。壞消息接踵而至，川普對此心知肚明。於是，混亂開場了。
“We were having a very bad press day,” Mr. Trump told reporters on Monday about why he suggested on social media that the chief executive of the Ultimate Fighting Championship should turn the briefing room into his personal octagon. “I said, you know, I think I’m going to get Dana White to be my new press secretary.”
「我們度過了新聞報導非常糟糕的一天，」關於為什麼他在社群媒體上建議UFC首席執行官應把簡報室變成其個人的八角籠，川普週一向記者解釋道。「我說，你知道的，我想我要讓達納·懷特來當我的新一任新聞秘書。」
廣告
Mr. Trump loves to change an unflattering headline by whipping out a diversion. But his usual tricks seem particularly discordant in the face of the urgent challenges facing the White House. There is an effort to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, wars in Ukraine and Iran that the president promised to resolve months or years ago, and an incoming Congress whose Democratic majority is hungry to hold administration officials to account.
川普喜歡通過突然拋出一個轉移注意力的噱頭來扭轉不利的新聞。但面對白宮眼下面臨的緊迫挑戰，他慣用的伎倆似乎顯得尤為不合時宜。目前有人正推動彈劾國防部長彼特·海格塞斯；總統數月乃至數年前承諾解決的伊朗和烏克蘭戰爭仍在繼續；此外，即將上任的民主黨多數國會正急於追究政府官員的責任。
But so far, Mr. Trump’s answer to the pressure has been to take an unserious approach to some of the most serious topics. In recent days, he has left the country guessing what he may do, what he may say, or even when he may show up in public.
但到目前為止，川普應對壓力的對策是對一些最嚴肅的話題採取並不嚴肅的態度。最近幾天，他讓整個國家都在猜測他可能會做什麼、說什麼，甚至猜測他什麼時候會在公開場合露面。
His advisers say the president thrives on chaos. The problem is that the public may not.
他的顧問們表示，總統在混亂中如魚得水。問題在於，公眾可能並非如此。
“We always talk about Donald Trump as the executive producer in chief,” said Doug Heye, a Republican strategist. “TV shows sort of run out of steam before they get to season six. That’s a big part of where voters are. They’re just exhausted.”
「我們總是把唐納德·川普稱作『首席執行製片人』，」共和黨戰略家道格·海伊說。「電視節目往往在播到第六季前熱度就不行了。很大程度上，這也正是選民當下的狀態。他們只是筋疲力盡了。」
This extends to Mr. Trump repeating his baseless claims about election fraud. “It’s just not a credible argument, to the point that nobody’s paying attention,” Mr. Heye added.
這種疲態也體現在人們對川普重複其關於選舉欺詐的無端指控上。「這根本不是一個可信的論點，以至於已經沒人關注了，」海伊補充道。
Indeed, after the president on Monday revived claims that the midterm vote had been manipulated, the conservative commentator Megyn Kelly had a two-word retort: “Just stop.”
事實上，在總統週一重新提起期中選舉投票被操縱的指控後，保守派評論員梅根·凱只回了寥寥幾字：「得了吧。」
廣告
Beni Rae Harmony, a White House spokeswoman, defended the president’s focus and productivity.
白宮發言人貝尼·雷·哈莫尼為總統的專注度及工作效率進行了辯護。
“President Trump never stops working, and his momentum has only increased in the past week,” she said in a statement, adding that he had held seven policy meetings, met with the Qatari prime minister and signed several bills into law, including one that designated a museum in Missouri as a national historic landmark and one that changes the bankruptcy threshold for small businesses.
「川普總統從未停止工作，在過去的一週裡他的動力只增不減，」她在一份聲明中表示，並補充說總統舉行了七次政策會議，會見了卡達首相，並將數項法案簽發為法律，其中包括一項將密蘇里州的一家博物館指定為國家歷史地標的法案，以及一項修改小企業破產門檻的法案。
One adviser, who spoke on the condition of anonymity to describe the environment in the White House, called this moment a “natural transition phase.”
一位要求匿名以描述白宮當前氛圍的顧問，將這一時刻稱為「自然的過渡階段」。
This is a sanguine descriptor, given the abrupt departure of Susie Wiles, the chief of staff Mr. Trump replaced with Stephen Miller, a hard-line loyalist who has driven many of the policies voters rejected last week. A Republican fratricide has also been playing out on social media, where current and former Trump officials have spent the past several days blaming each other for the midterm failures, trading accusations of corruption and grift, and threatening lawsuits.
這是一個頗為樂觀的描述，因為前幕僚長蘇西·懷爾斯剛遭遇突然解職，而川普用斯蒂芬·米勒接替了她。米勒是一名強硬的忠誠派，曾推動了許多在上週遭選民抵制的政策。同時，一場共和黨人的內鬥也正在社群媒體上上演，在過去的幾天裡，現任和前任川普政府官員一直在為期中選舉的失利互相指責，相互指控對方腐敗和斂財，並威脅要提起訴訟。
It is the Republican version of the scene in the 2004 movie “Mean Girls,” in which the main antagonist makes public a book full of private insults, unleashing bedlam inside a high school.
這就像是 2004 年電影《賤女孩》(Mean Girls)中某個場景的共和黨翻版：在那場戲中，大反派公開了一本寫滿私人侮辱言辭的日記本，在一所高中裡引發了軒然大波。
After spending a weekend at Mar-a-Lago golfing and delivering impromptu remarks at a conservative gathering on his property, Mr. Trump sought to distract from the bad headlines and the mounting congressional support for the impeachment of his defense secretary. On Monday, he startled reporters with an appearance in the White House briefing room.
在馬阿拉歌度過了一個高爾夫週末、在那裡的一場保守派集會上發表即興講話後，川普試圖轉移人們對負面新聞以及國會支持彈劾其國防部長的呼聲不斷高漲的關注。週一，他出人意料地現身白宮簡報室，令記者們感到驚訝。
廣告
Mr. Trump riffed about his gambit of offering the press secretary gig to Mr. White, who accompanied him to greet reporters, and then began fielding questions of national and international import.
川普隨口拿他提議懷特擔任新聞秘書的把戲開起了玩笑——當時懷特也隨他一起前來和記者們打招呼，隨後便開始回答一些重要的國內外問題。
Asked about the admission by Jensen Huang, the chief executive of Nvidia, of an affair with a Chinese businesswoman U.S. officials concluded was an intelligence operative for Beijing, the president seemed unconcerned, saying he bought Mr. Huang’s explanation.
當被問及英偉達首席執行官黃仁勛承認與一名中國女商人有染（美國官員斷定該女子為北京的情報人員）一事時，總統似乎毫不關心，表示自己相信黃仁勛的解釋。
“He said, ‘I had no idea that she was a spy,’ and I really believe him,” Mr. Trump said.
「他說，『我完全不知道她是間諜，』而我真的相信他，」川普說。
The president also discussed the death toll in the Russia-Ukraine war, framing his assessment of the carnage in terms he understood: crowd sizes.
總統還討論了俄烏戰爭的死亡人數，用他最熟悉的詞彙——觀眾群規模，來表達他對這場慘烈殺戮的評估。
“I just think that Ukraine and Russia are tired of fighting. They’re tired of seeing dead young soldiers, mostly soldiers. Most of the people are soldiers. Think of it. They’re losing 25,000 — Dana, can you imagine 25,000? That’s like a stadium, Madison Square Garden. You have your fights, 25,000. That’s what it holds, and they’re losing that every single month, dead, young beautiful people, and it’s — it’s got to stop.”
「我只是認為烏克蘭和俄羅斯都厭倦了戰鬥。他們厭倦了看到年輕士兵死去，主要是士兵。死的大多是士兵。想想看。他們損失了2.5萬人——達納，你能想像2.5萬人嗎？那就是個體育場了，像麥迪遜廣場花園那樣的。你辦比賽，2.5萬人。那就是它的容量，而他們每個月都在失去這麼多人，死去的、年輕漂亮的人，而這——這必須停止。」
The fun and games continued later on Monday when Mr. Trump called into the premiere of a C-SPAN show, apparently without notice. A woman could be heard giggling in the background as the president’s call connected.
這場玩笑和遊戲在週一晚些時候還在繼續。當時川普似乎在沒有預先通知的情況下，撥通了C-SPAN一檔首播節目的熱線。當總統的電話接通時，可以聽到背景中有一名女子在咯咯地笑。
廣告
For eight minutes, Mimi Geerges, the host of the program, tried to press Mr. Trump about news he had made in several other interviews since the midterm elections. What about that immigration deal he had suggested he could strike with Democrats? What about amnesty for some migrant workers, as he had suggested on “60 Minutes” over the weekend?
在八分鐘的通話裡，該節目主持人米米·格爾吉斯試圖就川普自期中選舉以來在其他幾次採訪中引出的話題追問他。他之前暗示可能與民主黨人達成的那項移民協議進展如何了？他週末在《60分鐘》節目中暗示的、給予部分移民人員的特赦又是怎麼回事？
His responses were not clarifying.
他的回答並未起到任何澄清作用。
“Frankly, a big problem we had is the mental institutions being dumped into our country. Some very dangerous people were released into our country, to put it mildly. So we have not put the same, uh, pressure, obviously, and, you know, the, uh, we call them the worst of the worst,” Mr. Trump said, by way of an answer. “We haven’t put the same pressure on waitresses and, and on farm workers, et cetera. Not even close.”
「坦率地說，我們面臨的一個大問題是精神病院裡的人被傾倒進我們的國家。說得委婉點，一些極其危險的人被釋放到了我們的國家。所以，我們顯然沒有施加同樣的，呃，壓力，而且，你知道的，那個，呃，我們稱他們為最壞中的最壞，」川普如此回答道。「我們沒有對女服務員和農場工人等人群施加同等的壓力。差得遠了。」
Natural transition phase or not, many Republicans are worried that the White House will not be able to deliver on the request made by voters last week, which was to get serious about addressing economic concerns, an unchecked war and accusations of rampant corruption among those in Mr. Trump’s orbit.
無論這是否屬於「自然的過渡階段」，許多共和黨人都擔心，白宮將無法滿足選民上週提出的要求——即認真應對經濟擔憂、一場不受控制的戰爭，以及針對川普核心圈子裡猖獗腐敗的指控。
The best-case scenario, said the G.O.P. strategist Whit Ayres, would be if the administration followed through “on Trump’s initial instinct to do something with the Democrats who are going to be running the place shortly.”
共和黨策略師惠特·艾爾斯表示，最好的情況將是，政府能夠順應「川普最初的直覺，與不久後即將接管這裡的民主黨人一起合作做些什麼」。
But, he added, “I think the chances of that happening are somewhere between slim and none.”
但是，他補充道，「我認為這種情況發生的機率微乎其微。」

Katie Rogers是時報白宮記者，報導川普總統。
