川普訪華前曾被告知黃仁勛與疑似間諜有親密關係

Despite Denial, Trump Was Told on Trip to China About Spying on Nvidia Leader

MAGGIE HABERMAN, JONATHAN SWAN, JULIAN E. BARNES, ANA SWANSON, TRIPP MICKLE • 2026-11-11
President Trump was told aboard Air Force One in May en route to China that the United States had obtained sensitive intelligence showing that Nvidia’s chief executive was in a relationship with a woman believed to be a Chinese spy, people with knowledge of the matter said.
據知情人士透露，今年5月，川普總統在乘坐「空軍一號」前往中國的途中被告知，美國獲得了敏感情報，顯示英偉達首席執行官與一名據信為中國間諜的女性有戀愛關係。
The message was delivered by Marco Rubio, his secretary of state and national security adviser, shortly before the Nvidia leader, Jensen Huang, joined the presidential trip to Beijing at Mr. Trump’s invitation. It marked at least the second time in seven months that the president had been told of intelligence about Mr. Huang’s involvement with the woman.
這一消息由國務卿兼國家安全顧問魯比奧傳達，時間就在英偉達掌門人黃仁勛應川普邀請加入總統訪華行程前不久。這是在七個月內，總統至少第二次被告知有關黃仁勛與該女性有牽連的情報。
The existence of the previously unreported update given to Mr. Trump contradicts the president’s assertion on Monday that he had not known at the time of the trip about Mr. Huang’s relationship with the woman, who U.S. officials had concluded was working for China’s Ministry of State Security.
這次向川普的通報此前並未被報導，它與總統週一的說法相矛盾。川普當時稱，在訪華時他並不知道黃仁勛與該女性的關係。美國官員已認定該女性為中國國家安全部工作。
“No, I didn’t hear about it then,” Mr. Trump said when asked at a news conference whether he knew about Mr. Huang’s ties to the woman when he headed to a summit meeting with Xi Jinping, China’s leader.
當在新聞發布會上被問及前去與中國領導人習近平舉行峰會時是否知道黃仁勛與該女性的關係時，川普說：「不，我當時沒聽說。」
The disclosures about the relationship have rocked the company and driven down its stock price. They have focused new attention on the intensity of the competition between China and the United States for supremacy in artificial intelligence — a rivalry in which Nvidia’s advanced chips are integral to the current American advantage. And they have put a new spotlight on Mr. Huang’s influence with Mr. Trump and the administration on A.I. policy and issues affecting Nvidia.
有關這段關係的披露震動了英偉達，並壓低了其股價。此事使外界重新關注美中爭奪人工智慧主導權競爭的激烈程度——在這場競爭中，英偉達的先進晶片是美國當前保持優勢的關鍵所在。這也再次凸顯了黃仁勛對川普及政府在人工智慧政策和對英偉達相關問題上的影響力。
Ever since American officials first warned him last year that a woman having an affair with Mr. Huang was believed to be working for Chinese intelligence, Mr. Trump has played down or dismissed the concerns. When first briefed on the issue late last fall, Mr. Trump chose to take no action, The New York Times reported last month.
自美國官員去年首次警告川普，與黃仁勛有婚外情的女性被認為為中國情報機構工作以來，川普一直淡化或無視這些擔憂。據《紐約時報》上個月報導，去年晚秋首次聽取該問題簡報時，川普選擇不採取任何行動。
When pressed on Monday about whether he had received a briefing on the issue last year, Mr. Trump, who has embraced Nvidia as key to U.S. technology competitiveness and a driver of the stock market’s rise over the past several years, said he would have to check.
週一，當被追問去年是否收到過有關該問題的簡報時，川普表示他需要核實一下。川普一直將英偉達視為美國技術競爭力的關鍵，以及過去幾年股市上漲的推動力。
廣告
“I’d certainly check with the intelligence people,” he said. “They could have been mentioned. Certainly, it didn’t make much of an impact on me.”
「我當然會向情報人員核實，」他說。「他們可能提到過。當然，這對我沒有產生多大影響。」
Mr. Trump is scheduled to visit China again next week. He initially indicated he might invite Mr. Huang again, but on Monday he said the Nvidia executive will not accompany him on the trip.
川普定於下週再次訪問中國。他最初暗示可能再次邀請黃仁勛，但週一，他表示這位英偉達高管不會隨行。
But the president continued to vigorously defend Mr. Huang, saying he had spoken to him and asked him if he knew that the woman, known as Alice Huang, was a spy. Mr. Huang said he did not, Mr. Trump said. The president said he did not plan to raise the matter with Mr. Xi when he meets the Chinese leader next week.
但總統繼續大力為黃仁勛辯護，稱他曾與黃仁勛談過，並問他是否知道這個名為黃樂（音，其英文名為Alice Huang）的女性是間諜。川普說，黃仁勛表示自己並不知情。總統說，他並不打算在下週會見中國領導人習近平時提及此事。
“I just know Jensen very well,” Mr. Trump said on Monday. “I just think he’s a very honorable man.”
「我只是非常了解黃仁勛，」川普週一說。「我只是認為他是一個非常正直的人。」
Mr. Trump’s previous trip to China, in May, has come under new scrutiny since the disclosures about the Nvidia leader’s relationship with Ms. Huang, who had been an executive at a Singapore-based company called Megaspeed that purchases advanced Nvidia chips. The United States began restricting sales of advanced chips directly to China in late 2022, but it still allows Chinese companies to rent that computing power at data centers in Southeast Asia, like the ones Megaspeed operates.
自有關英偉達高管與黃樂關係被披露以來，川普今年5月對中國的上次訪問再次受到審視。黃樂曾是一家名為Megaspeed的新加坡公司的高管，該公司購買英偉達先進晶片。美國從2022年底開始限制直接向中國出售先進晶片，但仍允許中國公司在東南亞的數據中心租用這種算力，比如Megaspeed運營的數據中心。
In the weeks before the trip, Mr. Rubio had overseen the drafting of a memo about Mr. Huang, which was circulated to a small number of top administration officials. It was not intended for Mr. Trump, according to one person with knowledge of the matter, and it was not shown to him aboard Air Force One.
在此行前幾週，魯比奧負責監督起草了一份關於黃仁勛的備忘錄，並分發給少數政府高級官員。據一名知情人士說，這份備忘錄並非為川普準備，也沒有在「空軍一號」上給他看。
Mr. Huang, among the most pivotal figures in A.I., was initially left off the invitation list of business and tech leaders traveling to China in May, a fact that generated headlines before Mr. Trump departed the United States. With little warning, Mr. Trump invited Mr. Huang to meet up with Air Force One during a refueling stop in Alaska. Mr. Rubio was among more than a dozen administration officials on Air Force One for the trip.
作為人工智慧領域最關鍵人物之一的黃仁勛最初並未被列入5月赴華的商界和科技界領袖邀請名單。這一事實在川普離開美國前就已引發媒體關注。川普幾乎未作任何預告，便邀請黃仁勛在「空軍一號」於阿拉斯加進行空中加油停靠期間與之會面。魯比奧是「空軍一號」上的十多名政府官員之一。
A U.S. government official, who spoke on the condition of anonymity because they were not authorized to discuss the matter publicly, confirmed that when Mr. Rubio learned that Mr. Huang would be joining the trip in Alaska, he “briefed the president on the issue.”
一位因未獲授權公開討論此事而要求匿名的美國政府官員證實，當魯比奧得知黃仁勛將在阿拉斯加加入行程時，他「就這一問題向總統作了簡報」。
廣告
Mr. Trump was not particularly moved by what he heard from his national security adviser, and continued with the plan to pick up Mr. Huang, the people with knowledge of the events said.
知情人士說，川普對國家安全顧問的彙報並未特別在意，仍然堅持接上黃仁勛的計劃。
Mr. Huang, once aboard Air Force One, saw the president “very briefly” but “remained in the separate part of the plane for almost all of the trip,” the official said.
這名官員說，黃仁勛登上「空軍一號」後「非常短暫地」見了總統，但「幾乎整個行程都待在飛機的另一個區域」。
The State Department declined to comment. Asked to comment, the White House press office declined to do so on the record. Instead, the office emailed a statement on background that pointed to Mr. Trump’s remarks from Monday. The email disputed the reporting for this article, without elaborating.
國務院拒絕置評。白宮新聞辦公室被要求置評時，拒絕正式表態。該辦公室通過電子郵件發送了一份背景聲明，援引了川普週一的講話。該郵件對本文報導提出異議，但沒有詳細說明。
After The Times reported last month that U.S. officials had determined that Ms. Huang was a Chinese intelligence asset and that she was in a relationship with Mr. Huang, Nvidia executives and Mr. Huang both insisted that the relationship was a routine engagement with a customer. On Sunday, Mr. Huang admitted to a romantic relationship with Ms. Huang. Soon after, Nvidia announced that the company would conduct an independent investigation.
在《紐約時報》上個月報導美國官員認定黃樂是中國情報人員、且與黃仁勛有戀愛關係後，英偉達高管和黃仁勛都堅稱這段關係是與客戶的日常接觸。週日，黃仁勛承認與黃樂有戀愛關係。不久後，英偉達宣布公司將進行獨立調查。
Mr. Huang has long favored a more expansive policy on chip sales, well before Mr. Trump returned to the White House in 2025. His defenders have often noted that he has publicly said the U.S. must lead China in the A.I. race. Mr. Trump has largely sided with Mr. Huang and other technology executives and investors, amid a growing divide about the global risks of A.I.
早在川普2025年重返白宮之前，黃仁勛就長期主張更寬鬆的晶片銷售政策。他的支持者經常指出，他公開表示，美國必須在人工智慧競賽中領先中國。在圍繞人工智慧全球風險分歧日益加劇之際，川普在很大程度上站在黃仁勛和其他科技高管及投資者一邊。
Earlier this year, at an investor conference, Mr. Huang reiterated that China should not be able to access Nvidia’s top-tier products, and that the United States should have “the first, the most and the best.” But, he added, “Simultaneously, all American companies should compete globally.”
今年早些時候，在一次投資者會議上，黃仁勛重申，中國不應獲得英偉達的頂級產品，美國應該擁有「第一、最多和最好」。但他還說，「與此同時，所有美國公司都應該在全球競爭。」
廣告
It was unclear whether the Rubio memo from last spring played a role in Mr. Huang’s having been excluded from the initial invitation list for the subsequent China visit.
目前不清楚魯比奧去年春天的備忘錄是否導致黃仁勛被排除在隨後訪華行程的初步邀請名單之外。
At the time, the president was considering opening sales to China of Nvidia’s most advanced Blackwell chip. Mr. Huang, along with some other major tech leaders, has supported such a move, although many of Mr. Trump’s advisers opposed it. The administration has not approved the sale of Blackwell chips to China, although the U.S. government ultimately permitted the sale of less advanced H200 chips to approved purchasers.
當時，總統正在考慮向中國開放銷售英偉達最先進的Blackwell晶片。黃仁勛和其他一些主要科技領袖支持此舉，儘管川普的許多顧問對此持反對意見。政府尚未批准向中國出售Blackwell晶片，但美國政府最終允許向獲批買家出售較不先進的H200晶片。
