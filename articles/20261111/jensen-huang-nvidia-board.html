順從的董事會：黃仁勛危機考驗英偉達公司治理

Nvidia’s Board Has a History of Letting Jensen Huang Get His Way

KALLEY HUANG, DON CLARK • 2026-11-11
In 1993, Sequoia Capital and Sutter Hill Ventures led a $2 million funding round for Nvidia, a start-up that made chips for video games. Mark A. Stevens, an investor at Sequoia, and Tench Coxe, an investor at Sutter Hill, then joined Nvidia’s board of directors. They watched the company struggle as it burned cash and its earliest products failed.
1993年，為電玩遊戲製造晶片的初創公司英偉達獲得一輪200萬美元的融資，領投的是紅杉資本和薩特希爾風投。隨後，紅杉投資人馬克·A·史蒂芬斯和薩特希爾投資人滕奇·考克斯加入了英偉達董事會。他們看著這家公司消耗大量資金，最初的產品遭遇失敗，境況堪憂。
“It wasn’t clear if Nvidia was ever going to, sort of, have escape velocity,” Mr. Stevens recalled on a podcast in 2023. “We were very concerned about, are we going to lose this company or not?”
「當時並不清楚英偉達是否能在某種程度上達到逃逸速度，」史蒂芬斯在2023年的一檔播客中回憶道。「我們當時非常擔心，我們會不會失去這家公司？」
Three decades later, under the leadership of Jensen Huang, its co-founder and chief executive, Nvidia is one of the most valuable companies in the world. Its chips are an essential component of the giant artificial intelligence projects that are driving the U.S. economy.
三十年後，在聯合創始人兼首席執行官黃仁勛的領導下，英偉達已成為全球最有價值的公司之一。其晶片是推動美國經濟發展的巨型人工智慧項目不可或缺的組成部分。
Now, Nvidia’s 10-member board of directors — a collection of early investors, longtime partners and other business executives — faces a daunting task: investigating their celebrity C.E.O. On Sunday, Mr. Huang admitted to an extramarital relationship with a businesswoman whom U.S. intelligence agencies have determined was a spy for China.
如今，由10名成員組成的英偉達董事會——包括早期投資者、長期合作夥伴及其他商企高管——正面臨一項艱巨任務：調查他們聲名顯赫的首席執行官。週日，黃仁勛承認與一名女商人存在婚外情，而美國情報機構已認定這名女商人是中國間諜。
廣告
(China has rejected the espionage claims. Alice Huang, the Chinese executive accused of spying for Beijing, has not responded to requests for comment.)
（中國已否認間諜指控。被控為北京做間諜的中國高管黃樂[音，其英文名為Alice Huang]尚未回應置評請求。）
Nvidia’s board is coming into this investigation with a track record of both loyalty and deference to Mr. Huang, exposing them to criticism that they were unprepared for him to become the Silicon Valley company’s biggest vulnerability.
這個將展開調查的董事會一貫對黃仁勛表現出忠誠和順從，這使他們面臨批評，因為他們對黃仁勛成為這家矽谷公司最大漏洞的狀況毫無準備。
“Substantial experience does not always translate to willingness to provide full oversight, especially if that long tenure has built strong connections to the C.E.O.,” said Allison Henry, a clinical assistant professor of management and organizations at Northwestern University’s Kellogg School of Management.
「豐富的經驗並不總能轉化為提供全面監督的意願，尤其是如果這種長期的任職已經建立起了與首席執行官的緊密聯繫，」西北大學凱洛格管理學院管理與組織學臨床助理教授艾利森·亨利表示。
Nvidia does not appear to even have a succession plan for Mr. Huang, and it does not have an obvious internal candidate to replace him. Mr. Huang, 63, said in 2023 that he believed he could run the company for three or four more decades.
英偉達甚至似乎沒有黃仁勛繼任者的規劃，並且也沒有一個明顯的內部候選人來取代他。63歲的黃仁勛在2023年曾表示，他相信自己還可以再管理這家公司三四十年。
None of Nvidia’s board members responded to requests for comment on Monday, and a company spokesman declined to comment.
週一，沒有任何英偉達董事會成員對置評請求作出回應，一名公司發言人也拒絕置評。
Setting up a succession plan for Mr. Huang was internally discussed over the years, former Nvidia executives said, but Mr. Huang resisted.
前英偉達高管們表示，多年來公司內部曾討論過為黃仁勛制定繼任計劃，但他表示抵制。
“He didn’t like the idea,” said Rene Haas, the chief executive of the chip design company Arm Holdings, who worked at Nvidia from 2006 to 2013. He discussed Mr. Huang’s management style in a 2024 interview. “He felt it would create competition among executives and be divisive.”
「他不喜歡這個想法，」晶片設計公司安謀首席執行官雷內·哈斯說，他曾在2006年至2013年間供職於英偉達。他在2024年的一次採訪中談及黃仁勛的管理風格時說。「他覺得這會在高管之間製造競爭，並導致分裂。」
廣告
Many of Nvidia’s board members — with a combined 161 years of serving on the board — have a long history with Mr. Huang, who has shown that his big bets usually pay off for his company and the people backing him.
許多英偉達董事會成員——他們在董事會的任期總計達161年——與黃仁勛有著深厚的淵源，而他也已經證明，那些巨大賭注通常能為公司和支持他的人帶來回報。
One of the first examples was Synopsys, a Silicon Valley company that supplied key software needed to design chips. Two former Synopsys leaders joined Nvidia’s board of directors in the 1990s: Harvey C. Jones, the software company’s onetime chief executive, and A. Brooke Seawell, its former chief financial officer. The Nvidia shares of the two former Synopsys executives were worth a combined $1.86 billion as of June.
最早的例子之一是新思科技，這是一家提供晶片設計所需關鍵軟體的矽谷公司。兩名前新思科技的領導人在20世紀90年代加入了英偉達董事會：公司的前首席執行官哈維·C·瓊斯，以及其前首席財務官A·布魯克·西威爾。截至6月，這兩名前新思科技高管持有的英偉達股票總價值達18.6億美元。
Mr. Huang is by far the largest individual shareholder in Nvidia with a 3.56 percent stake, making him one of the wealthiest people in the world.
憑藉3.56%的股份，黃仁勛是英偉達無可爭議的最大個人股東，使他成為全球最富有的人之一。
Among other individual shareholders, Mr. Stevens and Mr. Coxe, his early investors and board members, are second and third on the list. When Mr. Stevens last sold shares in the company, in September, his stake was worth $6.22 billion. When Mr. Coxe last gave away shares, in November, his stake was worth $5.62 billion.
在其他個人股東中，他的早期投資者兼董事會成員史蒂芬斯和考克斯分別位列第二和第三。史蒂芬斯在9月份最近一次出售該公司股票時，股份價值為62.2億美元。考克斯在11月份最近一次轉讓股份時，股份價值為56.2億美元。
The other board members are:
其他董事會成員包括：
• Dawn Hudson, the former chief marketing officer of the National Football League;
·唐·哈德森，前NFL首席營銷官；

• Stephen C. Neal, the former chief executive of the law firm Cooley LLP;
·斯蒂芬·C·尼爾，前科律律師事務所首席執行官；
• John O. Dabiri, an engineering professor;
·約翰·O·達比裡，工程學教授；
• Aarti Shah, the former chief information officer at the drugmaker Eli Lilly;
·阿爾蒂·沙阿，前製藥商禮來公司首席信息官；
• Melissa B. Lora, the former president of Taco Bell;
·梅麗莎·B·洛拉，前Taco Bell總裁；
• Suzanne Nora Johnson, the former vice chairman of Goldman Sachs.
·蘇珊·諾拉·強生，前高盛副董事長。
Mr. Huang has viewed Nvidia’s board primarily as a governance requirement, rather than as an important part of the company’s strategy or execution, according to three former employees.
據三名前員工稱，黃仁勛主要將英偉達的董事會視為一項治理要求，而非公司戰略或執行的重要組成部分。
He has taken that same go-it-alone approach to building his executive team, emphasizing an anti-bureaucratic management philosophy designed to make big decisions quickly. He has more than 60 direct reports, putting him at the center of the company’s day-to-day operations, despite a hectic travel schedule that often takes him from California to Washington to Asia and back.
在組建高管團隊時，他也採取了同樣的獨立行事的方式，強調旨在快速做出重大決策的反官僚管理理念。他有60多名直接下屬，這使他處於公司日常運營的中心，儘管他繁忙的旅行日程經常讓他往返於加利福尼亞、華盛頓和亞洲之間。
But Nvidia’s current predicament presents considerable challenges for a company so dependent on one man. Nvidia’s independent board members will choose an outside counsel to investigate. A company spokesman said that Mr. Huang would not be part of the selection process, but he will continue to lead Nvidia during the investigation.
但英偉達目前的困境對一家如此依賴一個人的公司構成了巨大的挑戰。英偉達的獨立董事會成員將聘請外部法律顧問進行調查。一名公司發言人表示，黃仁勛不會參與這一選拔過程，但在調查期間他將繼續領導英偉達。
“The best way to weather this kind of storm in the world of public opinion is to have the C.E.O. or whoever is caught out apologize publicly and cede control at least temporarily to another party,” said Jo-Ellen Pozner, associate professor of management at Santa Clara University’s Leavey School of Business.
「在公眾輿論領域，度過這類風暴的最佳方式是讓涉事首席執行官或其他相關人員公開道歉，並至少暫時將控制權讓渡給第三方，」聖克拉拉大學利維商學院管理學副教授喬-艾倫·波茲納說。
廣告
Mr. Huang has also given his children substantial power and authority at Nvidia, according to five current and former employees.
據五名現任及前任員工透露，黃仁勛在英偉達內部也賦予了他的子女巨大的權力和權威。
His son, Spencer, is a director of product management and plays a leading role in Nvidia’s robotics efforts. His daughter, Madison, is a senior director of product marketing who also works on robotics, as well as communications and marketing. And his son-in-law, Nico Caprez, works on business development for the company. That has created the impression among some at the company that he is nurturing his children to eventually succeed him.
他的兒子黃勝斌(Spencer)擔任產品管理總監，在英偉達的機器人項目中發揮著主導作用。他的女兒黃敏珊(Madison)擔任產品營銷高級總監，同樣涉足機器人業務以及企業傳播和營銷工作。而他的女婿尼科·卡普雷茲則負責公司的商業開發。這給一些員工留下一種印象：他正在培養他的子女，以便他們最終接替自己。
In an interview last month, when asked whether Madison was in line to succeed him, Mr. Huang said: “I don’t wish the job on her.”
在上個月的一次採訪中，當被問及黃敏珊是否有望接替他時，黃仁勛說：「我不希望她接手這份工作。」
“If the C.E.O. doesn’t do his job according to 12 or 15 board members, the C.E.O. is let go,” Mr. Huang said in January. But, he added, “there will never be one like me, and the reason for that is because I was raised by the company.”
「如果首席執行官沒有按照12或15名董事會成員的要求做好本職工作，首席執行官就會被解僱，」黃仁勛在1月份表示。但他補充道，「永遠不會有像我這樣的人，原因在於我是由這家公司撫養長大的。」
It wouldn’t be easy to pick a manager inside Nvidia that could temporarily run the company, said Jeff Christian, chief executive of Christian & Timbers, an executive recruiting firm that frequently works with Silicon Valley companies.
經常與矽谷企業合作的高管招聘公司Christian & Timbers首席執行官傑夫·克里斯蒂安表示，在英偉達內部挑選一名能夠暫時管理公司的經理並非易事。
Most current executives have responsibilities for specific products or technology areas, not broad responsibilities with thousands of people reporting to them. The question is, he said, is whether directors will at some point feel compelled to ask Mr. Huang to step aside.
大多數現任高管僅負責特定的產品或技術領域，而非擁有數千人向其彙報的廣泛職責。他說，問題在於董事們是否會在某一時刻感到不得不要求黃仁勛退居二線。
廣告
“He’s such a big personality,” he said. “It’s almost like trying to fire the owner.”
「他是一個個性如此鮮明的大人物，」他說。「這簡直就像是試圖解僱東家一樣。」
