黃仁勛事件中的「女主角」黃樂是誰？

The Woman in the Middle of Nvidia’s Spy Scandal

孟建國, ANA SWANSON, TRIPP MICKLE, 傅才德 • 2026-11-11
In December 2023, a Chinese executive who goes by Alice Huang met with Nvidia’s chief executive, Jensen Huang, and a small group of colleagues at an Italian restaurant in Singapore.
2023年12月，一位名為黃樂（音，其英文名為Alice Huang）的中國高管在新加坡一家義大利餐廳與英偉達首席執行官黃仁勛及幾名同事會面。
In a photo of the previously unreported meeting, which was confirmed by The New York Times, Ms. Huang smiled as she posed next to Mr. Huang, who was in his trademark black leather jacket. He held a glass of red wine.
《紐約時報》核實了這場此前未見報導的會面照片。照片中，黃樂微笑著站在黃仁勛身旁，後者穿著他標誌性的黑色皮夾克，手裡拿著一杯紅酒。
For Ms. Huang, there was reason to celebrate. Backed by wealthy investors in Shanghai, she had just restructured a company that would soon buy billions of dollars in Nvidia artificial intelligence chips. The U.S. government had barred Chinese companies from buying the advanced chips, but Ms. Huang had sidestepped this prohibition by creating a firm, Megaspeed, through a Singaporean shell company.
黃樂確實有理由慶祝。在上海一群富有投資者的支持下，她剛剛完成一家公司的重組。這家公司很快就將斥資數十億美元購買英偉達的人工智慧晶片。美國政府已經禁止中國企業購買這些先進晶片，但黃樂通過一家新加坡空殼公司成立Megaspeed，繞過了這一禁令。 
Her close relationship with Nvidia’s leader also appeared to smooth the way. Four days after the ownership change Megaspeed was named a Nvidia cloud partner, giving it access to the coveted components.
她與英偉達掌門人的密切關係似乎也為此鋪平了道路。在公司所有權變更四天後，Megaspeed被列為英偉達雲合作夥伴，從而獲得了採購這些搶手晶片的管道。
廣告
Their relationship is now at the center of a global geopolitical competition between China and the U.S. government to dominate A.I. It has also shaken and captivated the tech industry on both sides of the Pacific, surfacing often invisible details of the fierce technological race, like spy craft and the human vulnerabilities of A.I. kingmakers like Mr. Huang.
如今，兩人的關係已成為中美兩國政府爭奪人工智慧主導地位的全球地緣政治競爭的焦點。這段關係也震動了太平洋兩岸的科技行業，引發廣泛關注，使這場激烈技術競賽中一些通常不為人知的細節浮出水面，包括間諜手段，以及黃仁勛這樣左右人工智慧行業格局的關鍵人物在人性上的弱點。
The meeting in Singapore was a sign of how Ms. Huang’s relationship with Mr. Huang goes back further than previously known. It also speaks to the close social and business ties that grew during years of tech collaboration between China and the United States, and how they have persisted despite the icy geopolitical divide of the past decade.
新加坡的這次會面表明，黃樂與黃仁勛的關係可以追溯到比此前所知更早的時期。這也體現出中美兩國在多年科技合作中建立的密切私人交往和商業聯繫，以及這些關係如何在過去十年兩國地緣政治關係日益緊張的情況下延續至今。
The Times was the first to report Mr. Huang’s dealings with Megaspeed and Ms. Huang last year. Last month, The Times reported that U.S. officials had concluded she was a Chinese intelligence asset who had developed a close relationship with the Nvidia leader, and that President Trump had taken no action when briefed on the situation last year. Last week, The Times reported that Ms. Huang had been recruited to spy by China’s Ministry of State Security. On Sunday, Mr. Huang, 63, acknowledged that he had an affair with her, and Nvidia said it was conducting an internal investigation.
《紐約時報》去年率先報導了黃仁勛與Megaspeed及黃樂之間的往來。上個月，《紐約時報》報導，美國官員已得出結論，黃樂是中國的一名情報人員，並與英偉達掌門人建立了密切關係，而川普總統去年聽取相關簡報後並未採取行動。上週，《紐約時報》又報導，黃樂受中國國家安全部招募從事間諜活動。週日，63歲的黃仁勛承認與她有過婚外情，英偉達表示正在展開內部調查。 
Many details of the relationship between Mr. Huang and Ms. Huang, including when they met and when she may have been recruited by Chinese intelligence, are not known. But Ms. Huang’s work with Megaspeed put her in the middle of a sprawling effort to accumulate A.I. chips across Southeast Asia and provide access to the technology to China’s companies.
兩人關係的許多細節仍不清楚，包括他們何時相識，以及黃樂可能在何時被中國情報機構招募。但黃樂在Megaspeed的工作使她置身於一場橫跨東南亞的大規模行動的核心。該行動旨在大量獲取人工智慧晶片，並為中國企業提供獲取該技術的管道。
Photos from Ms. Huang’s social media, which were verified by The Times, show that she and Mr. Huang met across the globe over the past three years, including in Shanghai; at Nvidia’s Silicon Valley headquarters; at a golf resort south of San Jose, Calif., and in Taiwan.
《紐約時報》核實了黃樂社群媒體上的照片，發現過去三年裡，她與黃仁勛曾在世界多地見面，包括上海、英偉達位於矽谷的總部、加利福尼亞州聖何塞以南的一家高爾夫度假村，以及台灣。
After the 2023 meeting in Singapore, Ms. Huang, whose Chinese name is Huang Le, used her experience and connections in the world of Shanghai finance to work her way into tech circles. She turned Megaspeed into a destination for Nvidia technology, working with a longtime business partner who was often introduced as her husband.
2023年新加坡會面後，黃樂利用自己在上海金融界的經驗和人脈逐漸打入科技圈。她與一名長期商業夥伴合作，將Megaspeed發展成為英偉達技術的重要買家。這名商業夥伴經常被介紹為她的丈夫。
The success attracted the attention of authorities. By 2024, the United States had dispatched a Commerce Department official to check on Ms. Huang’s company. She was monitored by Taiwan’s government during her visits to the island, including when she appeared publicly with Mr. Huang. For part of 2025, she was not allowed to leave Singapore because of an investigation.
這一成功引起了當局注意。2024年，美國商務部派出一名官員，對該公司進行實地檢查。台灣政府在她訪問台灣期間對其進行監控，包括她與黃仁勛公開露面的場合。2025年部分時間，她因一項調查被禁止離開新加坡。
Behind Megaspeed was a network of wealthy tech investors in China with links to domestic data centers, the government and the defense industry. Between 2024 and 2026, the firm bought billions of dollars of Nvidia’s advanced A.I. technology, bringing Ms. Huang further into Mr. Huang’s orbit.
Megaspeed背後是一個由中國富有的科技投資者組成的網路，這些投資者與國內數據中心、政府和國防工業都有聯繫。2024年至2026年間，該公司購買了價值數十億美元的英偉達先進人工智慧技術，使黃樂進一步進入黃仁勛的圈子。
Details about Ms. Huang, much of which have not been previously reported, are based on a Times analysis of 15 years of corporate documents, as well as interviews with more than half a dozen people and government officials familiar with her and her business.
關於黃樂的許多細節此前從未見諸報導，這些信息來自《紐約時報》對15年公司文件的分析，以及對六名以上熟悉黃樂及其業務的人士和政府官員的採訪。
Ms. Huang did not respond to a request for comment. Nvidia and Megaspeed declined to comment. China has denied the espionage claims.
黃樂沒有回應置評請求。英偉達和Megaspeed拒絕置評。中國否認了間諜指控。
廣告
A Life in Finance
金融界生涯
For many years, Ms. Huang’s life was rooted in the high-flying world of Shanghai finance. She mingled easily with tech executives from China and the United States alike, according to people who have met her.
多年來，黃樂的生活一直圍繞著上海繁華的金融圈展開。據見過她的人士透露，她與中美兩國的科技企業高管都能輕鬆自如地打交道。
In China, Ms. Huang is closely linked to a business partner named Kang Wei. Mr. Kang, a 51-year-old fund manager, has been introduced as Ms. Huang’s husband to Chinese investors and Megaspeed employees, according to people familiar with the matter. The Times could not verify whether the two are married.
在中國，黃樂與一名叫康偉的商業夥伴關係密切。據知情人士透露，這名現年51歲的基金經理在中國投資者和Megaspeed員工面前一直被介紹為黃樂的丈夫。《紐約時報》無法核實兩人是否確實結婚。
Mr. Kang rose to prominence at a young age, earning a reputation as a savvy stock market strategist in the 2000s. Chinese financial media treated him as a sort of boy wonder. By the 2010s, he had moved into private equity, including investing in online gaming firms.
康偉年紀輕輕便嶄露頭角，2000年代就以精明的股市策略師聞名。中國的財經媒體將他視為一名少年奇才。到了2010年代，他開始涉足私募股權投資，包括投資網路遊戲公司。
China’s securities regulator has fined Mr. Kang at least twice for failing to disclose his stakes in listed companies. He has also struggled with fellow investors for corporate control, according to regulatory filings. Mr. Kang has bragged about Ms. Huang’s connections to Nvidia’s Mr. Huang and used those ties to raise money for their businesses, according to one of the people.
根據監管文件，中國的證券監管機構曾至少兩次因康偉未披露其在上市公司的持股情況而對他處以罰款。他也曾與其他投資者爭奪公司的控制權。一名知情人士表示，康偉曾炫耀黃樂與英偉達黃仁勛的關係，並利用這種關係為他們的企業籌集資金。 
Mr. Kang did not respond to a request for comment.
康偉沒有回應置評請求。
廣告
Ms. Huang’s career mirrored Mr. Kang’s focus on finance. According to her online résumé, she spent four years as a television reporter before working at HSBC’s private bank and CITIC Securities, a Chinese state-backed investment bank.
黃樂和康偉一樣，職業生涯也主要集中在金融領域。根據她的網上簡歷，她曾在電視台做了四年記者，之後先後在滙豐私人銀行和有中國國資背景的投資銀行中信證券工作。
Occasionally, she appeared with Mr. Kang. In an August 2023 photo, the two visited a state-backed, auto-tech company in the eastern city of Nanjing.
她偶爾與康偉一起公開露面。2023年8月的一張照片顯示，兩人曾參觀位於中國東部城市南京的一家有國資背景的汽車科技公司。
The earliest corporate records bearing Ms. Huang’s name trace back to a small “sports and leisure club” company registered in 2010 inside Beihu No. 9, a private golf club in Beijing that drew officials and featured themed saunas, steam rooms and a buffet of foie gras, hairy crabs and Häagen-Dazs.
最早帶有黃樂名字的公司記錄可追溯到2010年，是一家小型「體育休閑俱樂部」公司，註冊地在北京的私人高爾夫俱樂部北湖九號內。這是一家會有官員光顧的俱樂部，設有主題桑拿、汗蒸以及包含鵝肝、大閘蟹和哈根達斯冰淇淋的自助餐。
Over the next few years, Ms. Huang branched out, investing in a Chinese medical equipment maker that would list its shares in Hong Kong. She acquired her stake through an offshore company, corporate records show.
接下來幾年，黃樂拓展業務，投資了一家後來在香港上市的中國醫療設備製造商。公司記錄顯示，她通過一家離岸公司獲得了股份。
By 2020, Ms. Huang and Mr. Kang were known in some of Shanghai’s investment circles. They operated an interconnected web of companies to carry out corporate takeovers and invest in companies. That spring, their network landed a major project, helping build and operate an A.I. computing center in Shanghai for the internet giant Tencent.
到2020年，黃樂和康偉已在上海一些投資圈中為人所知。他們運營著一個相互關聯的公司網路，用來進行公司收購和投資。那年春天，他們的網路獲得了一個重大項目，參與互聯網巨頭騰訊在上海的一個人工智慧計算中心的建設和運營。
That set the stage for Ms. Huang’s next effort: a push abroad to acquire critical chips.
這為黃樂的下一步行動奠定了基礎：向海外擴張以獲取關鍵晶片。
Building Megaspeed
打造Megaspeed
In late 2022, the U.S. government began instituting restrictions on the sale of advanced A.I. chips to China. The United States feared that the components could potentially give Beijing an economic and military edge.
2022年底，美國政府開始對向中國出售先進人工智慧晶片實施限制。美國擔心這些組件可能給北京帶來經濟和軍事優勢。
廣告
To have any hope of getting the chips, a company had to be domiciled outside China and have no obvious ownership ties leading to the country.
要想有希望獲得這些晶片，公司必須註冊在中國境外，並且沒有明顯指向中國的所有權關係。
In 2023, Ms. Huang used a British Virgin Islands company to buy a Singaporean subsidiary of an online gaming company that was linked to her Shanghai network, called 7Road. It was renamed Megaspeed. It would become her first major venture into chips.
2023年，黃樂利用一家英屬維爾京群島公司收購了一家在線遊戲公司的新加坡子公司。該公司與她上海的網路有關，名為7Road。它被更名為Megaspeed。這將是她首次大舉進軍晶片領域。
Later that year, Megaspeed was transferred to a Singaporean shell company, Singapore records show. After the transfer, Ms. Huang remained in control of Megaspeed’s day-to-day operations, according to people familiar with the company.
新加坡記錄顯示，當年晚些時候，Megaspeed被轉讓給一家新加坡空殼公司。知情人士說，轉讓後，黃樂仍控制Megaspeed的日常運營。
By June 2024, Ms. Huang was close enough to Mr. Huang that she bragged about her access to Mr. Huang at a tech event in Taipei. Just after midnight, she was mingling with a crowd of tech executives when she offered to ask Mr. Huang to join them, recalled Parker Schmitt, who attended.
到2024年6月，黃樂與黃仁勛的關係已足夠密切，她在台北一場科技活動上吹噓自己能接觸到黃仁勛。參加活動的帕克·施密特回憶，剛過午夜，她正在與一群科技高管交際，並提出請黃仁勛加入他們。
“She said, ‘I bet you guys I can get Jensen here,’ and then Jensen immediately showed up,” said Mr. Schmitt, who is chief executive of NetThunder, a data software provider. Shortly after, Mr. Huang arrived and drank a whiskey shot with the group.
「她說，『我敢打賭我能把傑森（黃仁勛英文名）叫來』，然後傑森立刻出現了，」數據軟體提供商NetThunder的首席執行官施密特說。不久後，黃仁勛到場，與這群人喝了一杯威士忌。
Mr. Huang was with two other Nvidia executives and had planned to attend the party, said a person close to the company.
一名接近該公司的人士說，黃仁勛當時與另外兩名英偉達高管在一起，原本就計劃參加聚會。
廣告
Mr. Schmitt, who stayed in touch with Ms. Huang, said her social media was littered with photos of her and Mr. Huang, including pictures of them at a golf course and at restaurants drinking wine. She wanted to be seen as a talented sales executive, Mr. Schmitt said, and “photos with Jensen are a point of pride” in the tech industry.
與黃樂保持聯繫的施密特說，她的社群媒體上滿是與黃仁勛的合影，包括他們在高爾夫球場和餐廳喝酒的照片。施密特說，她想被視為一名有才華的銷售高管，而在科技行業，「與傑森的合影是驕傲的資本。」
Business Takes Off
業務起飛
That same month, Ms. Huang’s fledgling business had a series of breakthroughs. Megaspeed began receiving large shipments of Nvidia’s most advanced A.I. chips, eventually securing more than $4 billion of the precious hardware.
同月，黃樂初創新業務取得一系列突破。Megaspeed開始收到大批英偉達最先進的人工智慧晶片，最終獲得超過40億美元的珍貴硬件。
The company also landed a major new customer: the Chinese e-commerce giant Alibaba. Months after its sale to the Singaporean shell company, Megaspeed had created a Malaysian subsidiary, which then pledged its servers and intellectual property to a subsidiary of Alibaba called Apex. Alibaba became Megaspeed’s dominant customer, according to a person familiar with the business.
該公司還獲得一個重要新客戶：中國電商巨頭阿里巴巴。在被出售給新加坡空殼公司幾個月後，Megaspeed創建了一家馬來西亞子公司，該子公司隨後將其伺服器和知識產權抵押給阿里巴巴旗下子公司Apex。一名知情人士說，阿里巴巴成為Megaspeed的主要客戶。
Before long, Ms. Huang’s large A.I. chip orders began to attract attention. In late 2024, the U.S. Commerce Department sent an official to inspect a Megaspeed data center and confirm that the Nvidia chips delivered were on site. Some Nvidia equipment was still packed in boxes, raising concerns that it was being shipped in or out and was not installed at the location.
不久，黃樂的大額人工智慧晶片訂單開始引起注意。2024年底，美國商務部派一名官員檢查Megaspeed的一個數據中心，確認交付的英偉達晶片在現場。一些英偉達設備仍裝在箱子裡，這引起了擔憂：設備正被運進或運出，並未在該地點安裝。
That year, Taiwanese authorities began to monitor Ms. Huang’s trips to the island at the suggestion of the U.S. government, a person familiar with the matter said. She entered Taiwan on a Hong Kong passport and made trips beyond her public appearances with Mr. Huang.
一名知情人士說，那年，在美國政府建議下，台灣當局開始監控黃樂的訪台行程。她持香港護照進入台灣，並在與黃仁勛公開露面之外還有行程。
In March 2025, Ms. Huang traveled to the United States and visited Mr. Huang at Nvidia’s offices with other executives, according to a Times analysis of photos from the meeting. Two months later, she was pictured dining with him and others in Taipei, where she posed for a photo and kissed him on the cheek.
根據《紐約時報》對會面照片的分析，2025年3月，黃樂前往美國，與其他高管一起在英偉達辦公室拜訪黃仁勛。兩個月後，她被拍到在台北與他及其他人共進晚餐，合影時她親吻了他的臉頰。
Yet trouble was brewing in Singapore, where officials had also been looking into Megaspeed. The Singaporean government prohibited Ms. Huang from leaving the country and brought her in for questioning in 2025 as part of an investigation into false declaration of imports, according to a person familiar with the investigation.
然而，麻煩正在新加坡醞釀，當地官員也在調查Megaspeed。一名知情人士說，作為對虛假進口申報調查的一部分，新加坡政府2025年禁止黃樂離開該國，並對她進行了問訊。
There was insufficient evidence to suggest that Ms. Huang was complicit in the false declarations and investigations against her were concluded, the person said. Singaporean authorities have said they are still investigating Megaspeed.
該知情人士說，沒有足夠證據表明黃樂是虛假申報的共謀，對她的調查已結束。新加坡當局表示仍在調查Megaspeed。
Ms. Huang subsequently flew to Shanghai and a new boss began giving orders to Megaspeed employees, said a person familiar with the company.
一名知情人士說，黃樂隨後飛往上海，一位新老闆開始向Megaspeed員工發號施令。
Megaspeed has pressed on. It is nearing completion of the first phase of a data center in Malaysia, for which A.I. chips have been secured. In Ms. Huang’s absence, another executive tied to her network has visited the Malaysia office to help obtain permits and ensure work continues, a person familiar with the operation said.
Megaspeed繼續推進。它即將完成馬來西亞一個數據中心的第一階段，已為其獲得人工智慧晶片。一名熟悉運營的人士說，在黃樂缺席期間，另一名與其網路有關的高管訪問了馬來西亞辦公室，幫助獲得許可並確保工作繼續。
Other help has come from China. Shanghai Shuoyao, another company in Ms. Huang’s network, has sent large groups of Chinese engineers to work on the project, the person said.
還有一些幫助來自中國。這名人士說，黃樂網路中的另一家公司上海碩耀派出大批中國工程師參與該項目。
In Shanghai, local officials have held up Shuoyao as a model. A July report from the Songjiang district government showcased the company’s new A.I. server factory, where, it said, more than 100 technicians can repair servers down to the chip level and reconfigure them.
在上海，地方官員將碩耀樹立為典範。松江區政府7月的一份報告展示了該公司的新人工智慧伺服器工廠。報告稱，那裡有100多名技術人員能夠進行晶片級伺服器維修和重新配置。
