伊朗多次襲擊、美國不願介入：沙烏地阿拉伯面臨「最壞情況」

Saudi Arabia Faces ‘Worst-Case Scenario’ After Being Rebuffed by Trump

VIVIAN NEREIM • 2026-09-15
Saudi Arabia has increasingly found itself caught in the middle of the war between the United States and Iran. Now, the kingdom’s leadership is assessing dwindling options on how to respond.
沙烏地阿拉伯越來越發現，自己夾在美國與伊朗的戰爭中間。如今，沙烏地阿拉伯領導層正在權衡不斷減少的應對選項。
In recent weeks, the Iran-allied Houthi militia in Yemen has repeatedly attacked the kingdom, threatening to throttle its ability to export oil and piling pressure on the global economy. But Saudi Arabia has been left frustrated and disappointed by President Trump’s reluctance to confront the Houthis, former diplomats and analysts say.
過去幾週，與伊朗結盟的葉門胡塞武裝多次襲擊沙烏地阿拉伯，威脅要掐斷沙烏地阿拉伯的石油出口能力，並給全球經濟進一步施壓。但據前外交官和分析人士稱，川普總統不願對胡塞武裝採取行動，讓沙烏地阿拉伯方面既惱火又失望。
The kingdom’s diplomatic efforts to engage with Iran have also had limited results. A meeting planned on Monday between officials from Iran, Saudi Arabia and other Gulf countries was abruptly postponed on Sunday night, as the gap between their positions remained vast.
沙烏地阿拉伯試圖與伊朗的外交接觸也收效有限。伊朗、沙烏地阿拉伯和其他海灣國家官員原定於週一舉行會議，但由於各方立場分歧仍然巨大，在週日晚間突然推遲。
“Right now the Saudis are really frustrated,” said Michael Ratney, a former U.S. ambassador to Saudi Arabia. “In some sense it’s their worst-case scenario.”
「現在沙烏地阿拉伯人真的非常沮喪，」美國駐沙烏地阿拉伯前大使麥可·拉特尼說。「從某種意義上說，這正是他們最不願看到的局面。」
廣告
The Saudi government’s Center for International Communication, which fields queries from journalists, did not respond to a request for comment.
沙烏地阿拉伯政府負責回應記者詢問的國際傳播中心沒有回應置評請求。
The kingdom’s conundrum underscores the heavy costs that the U.S.-Israeli war with Iran has imposed across the Middle East, including on countries whose leaders opposed the war or have sought repeatedly to bring it to an end.
沙烏地阿拉伯的困境凸顯了美以與伊朗的戰爭給整個中東地區帶來的沉重代價，包括那些反對這場戰爭或多次試圖促成戰爭結束的國家。
In the first few months of the fighting, Saudi Arabia was relatively untouched by Iran’s retaliatory attacks, compared with its neighbors. Now, with no end to the hostilities in sight, Iran is seeking greater leverage over the United States, and Saudi Arabia — the world’s largest oil exporter until the war knocked it down the list — has become a primary target.
在衝突最初幾個月，與鄰國相比，沙烏地阿拉伯相對未受伊朗報復性襲擊影響。如今，隨著敵對行動看不到結束的跡象，伊朗正尋求對美國施加更大的籌碼，沙烏地阿拉伯已成為主要目標，該國在戰爭爆發前曾是全球最大石油出口國。
Over the past two weeks alone, Saudi Arabia has faced Iran-backed attacks from the east, the south and the north.
僅過去兩週，沙烏地阿拉伯就在東、南、北三個方向受到親伊朗勢力的襲擊。
A Saudi oil tanker was struck in the Strait of Hormuz at the end of August, killing two sailors.
8月底，一艘沙烏地阿拉伯油輪在霍爾木茲海峽遭到襲擊，造成兩名船員死亡。
On Friday, the Houthi militia, which had already declared a blockade on Saudi oil shipments through the Red Sea, seized territory on an important global shipping route and trounced Saudi-allied forces in Yemen.
週五，已宣布封鎖沙烏地阿拉伯經紅海石油運輸的胡塞武裝奪取了一條重要全球航運路線上的領土，並擊潰了沙烏地阿拉伯支持的葉門部隊。
廣告
The same day, the Saudi authorities announced that they had been forced to shut down a critical oil pipeline, blaming a drone attack by an Iran-backed Iraqi militia. The pipeline had become a key alternative export route for Saudi Arabia to get its oil to the Red Sea, after Iran effectively choked off the Strait of Hormuz.
同一天，沙烏地阿拉伯當局宣布被迫關閉一條至關重要的輸油管道，歸咎於伊朗支持的伊拉克民兵發動的無人機襲擊。在伊朗事實上切斷霍爾木茲海峽之後，這條管道已經成為沙烏地阿拉伯將石油輸往紅海的一個重要替代出口通道。
“The reason a country like Saudi Arabia invests so heavily in developing a security partnership with the United States is for just this sort of eventuality,” said Mr. Ratney, the former ambassador.
「像沙烏地阿拉伯這樣的國家之所以投入如此巨大的資源來發展與美國的安全夥伴關係，就是為了應對這種可能出現的情況，」前大使拉特尼說。
Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman of Saudi Arabia, the country’s de facto ruler, had reasons to be confident in that partnership. For years, his government has invested billions of dollars and extensive political capital in building personal relationships with Mr. Trump and his family.
沙烏地阿拉伯王儲穆罕默德·本·薩勒曼是該國事實上的統治者，他原本有理由對這種夥伴關係充滿信心。多年來，沙烏地阿拉伯政府投入數十億美元以及大量政治資本，與川普及其家族建立私人關係。
But when the crown prince asked Mr. Trump last week to take military action against the Houthis, the president declined, according to officials with knowledge of the conversations.
但據知情人士透露，上週王儲請求川普對胡塞武裝採取軍事行動時，總統拒絕了。
Saudi officials and pundits have been clear that the kingdom does not want to fight the Houthis alone. That is especially true because an earlier military campaign that Saudi Arabia led against the group turned into a decade-long quagmire.
沙烏地阿拉伯官員和評論人士已明確表示，沙烏地阿拉伯不想單獨與胡塞武裝作戰。尤其是因為，此前由沙烏地阿拉伯領導的針對胡塞武裝的一場軍事行動最終陷入長達十年的泥潭。
“This demands international support, not only from Saudi Arabia and regional countries,” Abdulrahman al-Rashed, a columnist close to the Saudi government, wrote in a Saudi-owned newspaper on Saturday.
「這需要國際社會的支持，而不能只靠沙烏地阿拉伯和地區國家，」與沙烏地阿拉伯政府關係密切的專欄作家阿卜杜勒拉赫曼·拉希德週六在一家沙烏地阿拉伯擁有的報紙上寫道。
廣告
Mr. Trump, however, appears reluctant to get involved.
然而，川普似乎不願介入。
“Already confronting one open-ended conflict with Iran, with an overstretched Navy and a diminishing munitions stockpile, it’s unsurprising that he does not want to open this new front,” said Dan Shapiro, a former top Middle East policy official in the Pentagon during the Biden administration. “But it is bitterly disappointing to the Saudis.”
「已經在與伊朗進行一場沒有止境的衝突，海軍不堪重負，彈藥庫存不斷減少，因此他不想再開闢一個新的戰線，這並不令人意外，」拜登政府時期五角大廈前中東政策高級官員丹·夏皮羅說。「但這讓沙烏地阿拉伯人深感失望。」
On Saturday, Mr. Trump told journalists on a trip to Ireland that the Houthis had “called us and they don’t want to fight with us.”
週六，川普在訪問愛爾蘭期間對記者說，胡塞武裝已經「給我們打了電話，他們不想與我們作戰」。
“There’s just one country that they’re not too happy with and we’ll get that straightened out,” he added, referring to Saudi Arabia.
他隨後補充說：「只有一個國家讓他們不太滿意，我們會把這件事處理好。」他指的是沙烏地阿拉伯。
Mohammed al-Bukhaiti, a Houthi political official, denied that the group had reached out to the United States. “We did not communicate with Trump, nor did we ask him not to attack us,” he said.
胡塞武裝政治官員穆罕默德·布哈伊蒂否認該組織曾與美國接觸。「我們沒有與川普溝通，也沒有要求他不要攻擊我們，」他說。
Whether the United States gets involved in Yemen is still a matter of discussion, and Washington is continuing to assist Saudi Arabia with intelligence, said Vice Admiral John W. Miller, a retired U.S. Navy commander in the Middle East.
曾指揮美國駐中東海軍部隊的退役海軍中將約翰·米勒說，美國是否介入葉門仍在討論中，華盛頓正在繼續向沙烏地阿拉伯提供情報協助。
Saudi Arabia is now likely to turn to diplomacy, according to Barbara Leaf, a former assistant secretary of state for Near Eastern affairs at the State Department. Despite years of Saudi, U.S. and Israeli bombing campaigns against the Houthis, “no one has come up with a military solution” for dealing with them, she said.
美國國務院負責近東事務的前助理國務卿芭芭拉·利夫說，沙烏地阿拉伯現在很可能會轉向外交手段。儘管沙烏地阿拉伯、美國和以色列多年來一直對胡塞武裝展開轟炸，「沒有人找到過解決胡塞問題的軍事方案」，她說。
廣告
Saudi Arabia could also work with its neighbors and Iran to hammer out an “ugly set of arrangements” to bring the war with Iran to an end, she said.
她說，沙烏地阿拉伯也可以與鄰國以及伊朗合作，通過談判達成一套「並不甚體面的安排」，以結束與伊朗的戰爭。
The kingdom could look to China to try to find a solution, but Beijing’s previous diplomatic efforts in the region have not led to lasting peace deals, she noted. China brokered a rapprochement between Saudi Arabia and Iran in 2023 that has since frayed.
她指出，沙烏地阿拉伯可能會向中國求助，以尋找解決方案，但北京此前在該地區的外交努力並未帶來持久和平協議。中國曾在2023年促成沙烏地阿拉伯與伊朗和解，但此後雙方關係已經出現裂痕。
For now, Saudi officials are unlikely to complain publicly about the United States, however frustrated they feel, analysts said.
分析人士說，就目前而言，無論沙烏地阿拉伯官員多麼沮喪，他們都不太可能公開抱怨美國。
“They’re not going to show some sort of distance,” she said. “They’re going to have to generate some other diplomatic effort.”
「他們不會表現出某種疏遠的姿態，」她說。「他們將不得不開展其他外交努力。」

Eric Schmitt自華盛頓、Shuaib Almosawa自葉門薩那對本文有報導貢獻。

Vivian Nereim是時報報導阿拉伯半島各國的首席記者，常駐沙烏地阿拉伯利雅得。
