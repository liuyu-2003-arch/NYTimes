《黃色臉孔》作者匡靈秀在台北最喜歡的五個地方

‘Yellowface’ Author R.F. Kuang’s 5 Favorite Places in Taipei

CLARISSA WEI • 2026-09-15
When the novelist R.F. Kuang spent two months in 2023 in Taipei for an intensive Mandarin course, she found herself inadvertently sketching the outlines of a new novel. Her grandfather had recently died, and she was keeping a diary. “I was trying to remember as many details and document the texture and flavor of what grief felt like,” she said in a video interview.
2023年，小說家匡靈秀在台北生活了兩個月，參加一個高強度漢語課程，期間她發現自己無意中勾勒出了一部新小說的輪廓。她的祖父剛去世不久，她正在寫日記。「我試圖盡可能多地記住細節，記錄悲傷的質地和味道，」她在一次影片採訪中說。
Those entries became the raw material for her novel “Taipei Story,” publishing on Tuesday, which follows Lily Chen, a 19-year-old Chinese American contending with mistranslation and bereavement during a summer in the city.
這些日記成為她的小說《台北故事》的原始素材。這本於週二出版的書講述了19歲的華裔美國人陳麗麗在這座城市度過一個夏天、面對誤譯和喪親之痛的故事。
A doctoral candidate in East Asian languages and literatures at Yale, Ms. Kuang has built a devoted following for her meticulously researched novels about language, empire and power. “Taipei Story” is her seventh novel and her second work of literary fiction after her 2023 publishing-world satire “Yellowface.” It returns to the questions of identity and belonging that have long animated her work.
匡靈秀是耶魯大學東亞語言與文學專業博士生，憑藉關於語言、帝國和權力、經過嚴謹考據的小說積累了一批忠實讀者。《台北故事》是她的第七部小說，也是繼2023年關於出版界的諷刺小說《黃色臉孔》之後的第二部純文學作品。它回歸了長期激發她創作的身份與歸屬問題。
Lily Chen is fictional, but the novel’s emotional and physical settings are inspired by Ms. Kuang’s study-abroad stint. She built her protagonist’s Taipei from her own experiences, like studying in kitschy cafes near National Taiwan University, where she was enrolled; slipping into movie screenings between classes; and hiking Elephant Mountain at the edge of the city. “I wanted to explore the sort of circuit of daily experiences that somebody spending a summer at N.T.U. would have had,” Ms. Kuang said.
陳麗麗是虛構的，但小說的情感和實體場景受到匡靈秀留學經歷的啟發。她用自身經歷構建了主人公的台北：在她就讀的台灣大學附近俗氣的咖啡館裡學習，課間溜進電影放映廳，在城市邊緣的象山徒步。「我想探索一個在台大度過夏天的人會有的那種日常，」匡靈秀說。
廣告
Although Ms. Kuang has not returned to Taipei since that summer, certain corners of the city have stayed with her. Here are five:
儘管匡靈秀自那個夏天之後沒有再回台北，但這座城市的某些角落一直伴隨著她。以下是其中的五個：
1. Qingtian Tea House
1. 青田茶館
“I had a classmate whose hobby was obsessively trying tea everywhere around the city,” Ms. Kuang said. She was occasionally roped into these quests, one of which led to Qingtian Tea House, a charming oasis that occupies a Japanese-era wooden home near her campus at N.T.U. in the Da’an District. It specializes in Taiwanese oolongs, but the detail Ms. Kuang remembers the most fondly wasn’t on the menu. It was the giant mango tree in the courtyard. During her visit, a rainstorm began knocking mangoes onto the roof with startling thuds. “At some point, we stopped paying attention to our tea and started counting the mango bangs,” she said. The final tally: roughly 85.
「我有一個同學熱衷於在城市各處嘗試茶飲，」匡靈秀說。她偶爾被拉進這些探索之旅，其中一次帶她來到了青田茶館——位於大安區台大校園附近一座日據時期木屋中的迷人綠洲。這裡專營台灣烏龍茶，但匡靈秀記憶最深刻的細節不在菜單上，而是院子裡那棵大芒果樹。有一次在店裡，一場暴雨開始把芒果砸到屋頂上，發出驚人的重擊聲。「我們一度不再注意茶，開始數芒果的撞擊聲，」她說。最終計數：大約85次。
2. Vegetarian Tomato Beef Noodle Restaurant
2. 素食茄汁牛肉麵餐廳
Beef noodle soup — braised chunks of beef in a rich broth — is one of Taiwan’s most emblematic dishes. Thousands of restaurants across the country specialize in it, and Taipei even holds an annual competition for who can cook the best bowl. Ms. Kuang, who does not eat meat, had never tried the dish — or even a vegetarian version — until she stumbled on this restaurant in the Xinyi District. “It’s this hole-in-the-wall place where they seemed totally uninterested in having any customers,” she said. Their version swaps the beef with lion’s head mushroom, served with springy noodles in a tomato-centric soup. “It was incredible,” she said.
牛肉麵——使用大塊牛肉燉煮的濃郁湯底——是台灣最具標誌性的菜肴之一。全台有數千家餐廳專營牛肉麵，台北甚至每年舉辦比賽評選最佳。不吃肉的匡靈秀從未嘗試過這道菜，甚至素食版本也沒有，直到她在信義區偶然發現了這家餐廳。「就是那種不起眼的小館，似乎對有沒有顧客完全不在意，」她說。他們的版本用猴頭菇替代牛肉，配上筋道的麵條，放在以番茄為主的湯裡。「太不可思議了，」她說。
3. Nationwide Healthy Vegetarian
3. 全國素食
Before she moved to Taipei, some of Ms. Kuang’s friends warned her that the city would be difficult for a vegetarian. “That’s just completely not true,” she said. She became a regular at Nationwide Healthy Vegetarian, a cafeteria-style buffet chain with locations all over the city where diners load up on freshly prepared dishes like crispy fried tofu, braised eggplant, stir-fried ferns, bamboo shoot salad and multigrain rice, then pay by weight.
來到台北前，匡靈秀的一些朋友警告她，這座城市對素食者來說會很困難。「完全不是這樣，」她說。她成了「全國素食」的常客——這是一家自助餐式連鎖店，全市各地都有分店。食客們揀選新鮮烹製的菜肴，如酥炸豆腐、紅燒茄子、炒蕨菜、竹筍沙拉和雜糧飯，然後按重量付費。
4. N.T.U. Agricultural Products Sales Center
4. 台大農產品展示中心
Some of Ms. Kuang’s favorite discoveries came from tips shared in class — including this campus shop, which sells fresh milk and ice cream sandwiches made with dairy from the university’s experimental farm. The milk, which her classmates described as unusually rich, drew lines every day. The sandwiches, consisting of a thick slab of ice cream pressed between two savory soda crackers, also had a cultish following. “I don’t know if we were just starving, but it was some of the best ice cream I ever had,” she said.
匡靈秀最喜歡的一些發現來自課堂上分享的推薦，包括這家校園商店。它出售鮮奶和用大學實驗農場乳製品製作的冰淇淋三明治。她的同學形容這種牛奶口感異常濃郁，每天都吸引人們排起長隊。用兩塊鹹味蘇打餅乾夾著厚厚一片冰淇淋的冰淇淋三明治，也有一批狂熱追隨者。「我不知道是不是我們太餓了，但那是我吃過的最好的冰淇淋之一，」她說。
5. SPOT Huashan
5. 光點華山
Taiwanese cinema made its international breakthrough in the 1980s. Though its heyday has passed, the island’s cinephile culture endures. “Taiwan has a really fun repertory indie cinema scene,” Ms. Kuang said. She spent hours at the SPOT Huashan in the Zhongzheng District, an art-house theater where obscure international films screen alongside domestic hits. One stood out: The Japanese filmmaker Ryusuke Hamaguchi’s “Passion,” which follows a group of college friends through a night of romantic revelations. Ms. Kuang was delighted by the interplay of Japanese dialogue and English, Japanese and Mandarin subtitles. “In the film, they’re jumping between languages, yet communication is still sometimes impossible because we have so many ways of hiding how we actually feel and what we want to express,” she said. She ended up weaving that experience — and those themes — into a scene in “Taipei Story.”
台灣電影在1980年代取得國際突破。儘管鼎盛時期已過，這座島嶼的影迷文化依然存續。「台灣有一個非常有趣的藝術影院生態，」匡靈秀說。她在中正區的光點華山度過了許多時光——這是一家藝術影院，冷門國際電影與本土熱門影片一同放映。其中一部格外突出：日本導演濱口龍介的《激情》(Passion)，講述一群大學朋友在一個充滿浪漫啟示的夜晚的故事。日語對白與英語、日語和國語字幕之間的相互作用讓匡靈秀欣喜。「在電影中，他們在語言之間跳躍，但溝通有時仍然不可能，因為我們有太多的辦法去隱藏真正的感受和想要表達的東西，」她說。她最終將那次經歷——以及那些主題——編織進了《台北故事》的一個場景中。
