川普斥AI安全擔憂為「騙局」，無意加強監管

Trump Says a Smart President Is All That’s Needed to Rein In A.I.

JONATHAN SWAN, MICHAEL GOLD • 2026-09-15
President Trump on Monday rejected calls from leading artificial intelligence executives for new limits on the technology, writing on social media that the industry already has the only guardrail it needs: “a STRONG AND SMART (High IQ!) PRESIDENT.”
川普總統週一拒絕了主要人工智慧高管提出的對該技術實施新限制的呼籲，他在社群媒體上寫道，該行業已經擁有了唯一所需的護欄：「一位強大且聰明（高智商！）的總統。」
He dismissed safety concerns as a “hoax” that would give China the upper hand in developing the technology. He argued that “the people that say AI is going to destroy the World” are the same ones warning about climate change — which he also insists is a hoax.
他將安全擔憂斥為「騙局」，認為這會讓中國在這項技術的開發方面佔據上風。他爭辯說，「那些說人工智慧將毀滅世界的人」與警告氣候變化的人是同一批人——他同樣堅稱氣候變化也是個騙局。
With the posts, Mr. Trump inserted himself into an intensifying national debate over how to govern a rapidly evolving technology. Researchers and industry leaders warn it could bring mass unemployment, a new wave of biological weapons and autonomous warfare. The president did not address those risks directly. Instead, he questioned the sincerity of the executives who have been calling to slow the technology’s development.
通過這些帖子，川普介入了關於如何治理這項快速發展技術日益激烈的全國性辯論。研究人員和行業領袖警告說，人工智慧可能會帶來大規模失業、引發新一波生物武器威脅和自主戰爭。總統沒有直接回應這些風險，而是質疑那些一直呼籲放緩該技術發展的高管們的誠意。
“The only control or ‘guardrails’ that AI needs is a STRONG AND SMART (High IQ!) PRESIDENT, and the U.S.A. has that, in spades!” Mr. Trump posted on Monday morning. “The Trump Administration has stopped AI ‘people’ from doing bad, or potentially bad, ‘things,’ like Dario (Anthropic!), who is now pretending to be a ‘perfect little angel’ — and we will continue to do so!
「人工智慧需要的唯一控制或『護欄』是一位強大且聰明（高智商！）的總統，而美國擁有這樣一位總統，毫無疑問！」川普週一早上發帖稱。「川普政府已經阻止了人工智慧『人士』做壞事，或者可能的壞『事』，比如達里奧（Anthropic公司！），他現在正假裝成一個『完美的小天使』——我們還要繼續這麼做！
廣告
“We already have tremendous CRIMINAL and REGULATORY power over these companies!” he added.
「我們對這些公司已經擁有了巨大的刑事和監管權力！」他還寫道。
It was not clear what authority Mr. Trump was referring to, or what actions he believes his administration has already blocked. The United States does have laws in place to prosecute deliberate hacking into other systems, but it is not clear that rogue A.I. agents’ hacking into networks would be covered by a law designed to go after human hackers.
目前尚不清楚川普指的是什麼權力，也不清楚他認為其政府已經阻止了哪些行動。美國確實有法律來起訴蓄意駭客攻擊其他系統的行為，但是旨在打擊人類駭客的法律是否能夠涵蓋失控的人工智慧智能體侵入網路的行為目前尚不明確。
Similarly, as the companies go public, they will be subject to regulations that govern publicly traded firms in the United States. But generally the industry is subject to very little existing regulation.
同樣，隨著這些公司上市，它們將受到美國針對上市公司制定的監管法規約束。但總的來說，該行業受到的現有監管非常少。
The White House did not respond to a request for comment.
白宮沒有回應對此置評的請求。
Mr. Trump’s own grasp of the technology is superficial, according to people close to him. He is an extreme late adopter of tech: He began text messaging only in 2022, and his staff prints out social media posts for him to read.
據川普身邊的人士透露，他本人對這項技術的了解很膚淺。他在使用技術方面極度滯後：他直到2022年才開始發短訊，他的工作人員還會將社群媒體帖子打印出來供他閱讀。
The president’s statement followed a weekend essay from Dario Amodei, the chief executive of Anthropic, calling for a global slowdown of artificial intelligence development. Days earlier, one of Anthropic’s researchers quit and went public over his fears about the safety of the technology.
總統發表上述聲明之前，Anthropic首席執行官達里奧·阿莫迪在週末發表了一篇文章，呼籲全球放緩人工智慧的發展。幾天前，Anthropic的一名研究人員辭職，並公開表達了他對該技術安全性的擔憂。
廣告
The Trump administration has clashed with Anthropic before. Earlier this year, the company refused to give the Pentagon unfettered access to its A.I. system without safeguards it wanted. Mr. Trump then ordered the federal government to stop using Anthropic’s A.I. technology and labeled the company a security risk, a designation that a federal judge later ruled was unlawful.
川普政府以前曾與Anthropic發生過衝突。今年早些時候，該公司拒絕在沒有其所希望的保護措施的情況下，讓五角大廈不受限制地訪問其人工智慧系統。川普隨後命令聯邦政府停止使用Anthropic的人工智慧技術，並將該公司列為安全風險，後來一名聯邦法官裁定這一認定是非法的。
Other A.I. chiefs have voiced concerns of their own in recent days. Demis Hassabis, the chairman of Google DeepMind; Elon Musk of SpaceX; and Sam Altman of OpenAI have all endorsed calls for a slower pace.
近日來，其他人工智慧領域的主管也表達了他們自己的擔憂。谷歌DeepMind董事長傑米斯·哈薩比斯、SpaceX的伊隆·馬斯克以及OpenAI的薩姆·奧特曼都支持放慢發展步伐的呼籲。
Vice President JD Vance has repeated the president’s warnings about China leaping ahead and has also echoed Mr. Trump’s skepticism about A.I. company executives’ calls for their own regulation. But he has also described certain uses of artificial intelligence as “satanic” and has been more open about his concerns regarding the technology’s risks.
副總統J·D·萬斯重申了總統關於中國可能超越美國的警告，也附和了川普對人工智慧公司高管呼籲自我監管的懷疑態度。但他也將人工智慧的某些用途描述為「極其邪惡」， 並對這項技術帶來的風險表達了更為直率的擔憂。
“It’s something that we’re concerned by, but we want to make sure that we regulate smartly,” Mr. Vance told a reporter on Monday morning before leaving for an event in Kansas City. “There are obviously risks to A.I.; there are benefits to A.I. I have to say, personally, I feel a little bit weird about the fact that we have so many frontier A.I. tech companies kind of talking to the government and begging the government to regulate them. It feels a little bit to me like a bit of a Trojan horse, so I think we just have to be careful about this.”
「這是我們關注的問題，但我們想確保我們能夠明智地進行監管，」週一早上，萬斯在啟程前往堪薩斯城參加一場活動前告訴記者。「人工智慧顯然存在風險；人工智慧也帶來了好處。我不得不說，就我個人而言，看到有這麼多前沿的人工智慧科技公司跟政府交談、懇求政府對它們進行監管這件事，我感覺有點奇怪。這讓我覺得有點像特洛伊木馬，所以我認為我們在這一點上必須小心。」
“Any technology has risks,” he added, saying later, “I think what the president has said is we want to be smart about making sure we stay on top of those risks.” The best approach, he said, would be “consensus between both Congress and the administration.”
「任何技術都有風險，」他還說，他後來又說，「我認為總統想表達的是，我們要明智地確保我們能夠掌控這些風險。」他說，最好的辦法是「國會和政府之間達成共識」。
Speaker Mike Johnson has shared Mr. Trump’s warnings that China could overtake the United States, but he has been more open to congressional action. Over the weekend, he urged a cautious approach to regulation and said executives of the leading A.I. companies should take part in any talks about a federal framework. On Monday he told reporters at the Capitol that he hoped to convene such a meeting as soon as the end of next week, though he would not commit to a date. “It will be soon,” he said.
眾議院議長邁克·強生贊同川普關於中國可能超越美國的警告，但他對國會採取行動持更開放的態度。上週末，他敦促在監管方面採取謹慎態度，並表示領先的人工智慧公司的高管應參與有關聯邦框架的談判。週一，他在國會大廈對記者表示，他希望最快在下週末召開這樣一次會議，儘管他不願承諾具體日期。「會很快的，」他說。
廣告
Though Mr. Johnson has called A.I. regulation a top priority for Congress, lawmakers have done little. Under his leadership, the House passed a provision in the sweeping tax cuts and domestic policy bill that would have blocked states from writing their own A.I. rules. It drew bipartisan opposition, and the Senate stripped it.
儘管強生稱人工智慧監管是國會的重要優先事項，但立法者們卻鮮有作為。在他的領導下，眾議院在全面的減稅和國內政策法案中通過了一項條款，該條款將阻止各州制定自己的人工智慧規則。這招致了兩黨的反對，參議院隨後將其刪除。
That legislation, which Mr. Trump calls his “big, beautiful bill,” also expanded major tax breaks for research costs, new investment and rural development projects. Those provisions benefit artificial intelligence players looking to build new data centers, invest in chip-making facilities and increase their power supply.
這項被川普稱為「大而美法案」的立法還擴大了針對研究成本、新投資和農村發展項目的重大稅收減免。這些條款有利於那些希望建設新數據中心、投資晶片製造設施和增加電力供應的人工智慧企業。
On Monday, Democrats rejected Mr. Trump’s position on A.I. Senator Chuck Schumer, Democrat of New York and the minority leader, called for Congress to pass legislation on the issue, saying on the Senate floor, “even if we had a high IQ president right now, and we surely don’t, it wouldn’t be enough.” Representative Hakeem Jeffries, the Democratic leader in the House, said that lawmakers should not leave Washington for a planned recess without passing regulations on A.I.
週一，民主黨人拒絕了川普在人工智慧問題上的立場。紐約州民主党參議員、少數黨領袖查克·舒默呼籲國會就此問題通過立法，並在參議院發言時說：「即使我們現在有一位高智商的總統——當然我們並沒有——那也是不夠的。」眾議院民主黨領袖、眾議員哈基姆·傑弗里斯表示，立法者不應在未通過人工智慧監管法規的情況下離開華盛頓休會。
Even if a meeting with A.I. executives happens, the House has all but run out of time to act before the midterm elections. Mr. Johnson has canceled votes scheduled for later this month, and lawmakers will leave Washington this week without returning until November.
即使與人工智慧高管的會晤得以舉行，眾議院也幾乎沒有時間在期中選舉前採取行動。強生已經取消了定於本月晚些時候舉行的投票，議員們本週將離開華盛頓，直到11月才會回來。
Mr. Trump knew almost nothing about artificial intelligence until the 2024 campaign, when OpenAI executives gave him a demonstration in Las Vegas. According to the book “Regime Change,” by the New York Times reporters Maggie Haberman and Jonathan Swan, Mr. Trump was struck by ChatGPT’s capabilities, telling the executives that the chatbot’s ability to write instant poetry was “beautiful.” He then turned, in the same meeting, to how the technology worked and how a president might clear regulatory hurdles to let the industry grow.
在2024年競選期間OpenAI的高管在拉斯維加斯向川普進行演示之前，他對人工智慧幾乎一無所知。據《紐約時報》記者瑪吉·哈伯曼和喬納森·斯旺所著的《政權更迭》(Regime Change)一書，川普對ChatGPT的功能感到震驚，他告訴高管們，這個聊天機器人即時寫詩的能力「很美」。隨後，在同一次會議上，他又轉而詢問這項技術的工作原理，以及總統可以如何清除監管障礙，從而推動該行業發展。
Since returning to office, he has aligned himself with A.I. accelerationists pressing the government to dismantle obstacles to that growth. He has been influenced in particular by Jensen Huang, the chief executive of the chipmaker Nvidia, and David Sacks, the Silicon Valley investor who has shaped much of the administration’s A.I. policy. Both argue that new guardrails on American companies would let China “win” the race.
自重返白宮以來，他與敦促政府消除阻礙該行業增長障礙的人工智慧加速主義者站在了一起。他特別受到了晶片製造商英偉達首席執行官黃仁勛和矽谷投資者大衛·薩克斯的影響，後者對本屆政府的人工智慧政策產生了很大影響。兩人都認為，對美國公司設置新的護欄會讓中國在這場競賽中「獲勝」。
As if to dramatize his alliance with the A.I. evangelists, Mr. Trump phoned Mr. Huang on Monday while Mr. Huang was onstage at a tech conference in Los Angeles. The president, who talks to the Nvidia chief regularly, declared that concerns about A.I. safety were overblown. “It’s a hoax,” the president said after Mr. Huang put him on speaker and held a microphone beside his phone for the benefit of the audience and the cameras. “The robots are not going to be taking over the world.”
似乎是為了戲劇化地展示他與人工智慧佈道者們的結盟，川普週一給黃仁勛打去了電話，當時後者正在洛杉磯的一個科技會議的舞台上。這位經常與英偉達總裁交談的總統宣布，對人工智慧安全的擔憂被誇大了。「這是一場騙局，」 總統在黃仁勛打開免提、把麥克風放在手機旁以便觀眾和鏡頭都能聽清後說道。「機器人不會接管世界。」
Mr. Trump returned to the subject of A.I. later Monday, dismissing the risks outright. “AI taking over the World, destroying Humanity, and all other things bad, is a HOAX,” he wrote, comparing it to the Russia investigation and his two impeachments. He questioned the motives of the executives asking to be reined in: “When, in the History of Business, did anyone see the Leaders of an Industry call for Regulation that, if strongly implemented, will drive them into oblivion and bankruptcy?”
川普週一晚些時候再次談到人工智慧話題，徹底否認相關風險。「人工智慧接管世界、毀滅人類以及所有其他壞事都是騙局，」他寫道，並將其與俄羅斯調查和他的兩次彈劾相提並論。他質疑那些呼籲加強監管的高管的動機：「在商業史上，什麼時候有人見過一個行業的領袖呼籲實施如果得到強有力執行，將把他們推向滅亡和破產境地的監管？」
廣告
Mr. Trump has dismissed the domestic backlash as well. In one of Monday’s posts, he portrayed widespread opposition to data center construction — a major issue emerging across the country ahead of the midterms — as part of a “conspiracy” rather than genuine public anxiety, and predicted the A.I. industry would be “the Greatest Economic Development Engine in History — Bigger than Oil, Gold, Diamonds, or even the Internet.”
川普也對國內的強烈反對不屑一顧。在週一的一篇帖子中，他將圍繞數據中心建設的廣泛反對——這是期中選舉前全國範圍內浮現的一個重大問題——描繪為「陰謀」的一部分，而不是真正的公眾焦慮，並預測人工智慧行業將成為「歷史上最偉大的經濟發展引擎——比石油、黃金、鑽石甚至互聯網還要大」。
“There is a SICK conspiracy going on against AI and Data Centers, and the only one that is happy about it is China,” he wrote in his post earlier on Monday. “WHOEVER WINS AI, WINS! We are leading China, and all others, and will continue to do so. Conspiracy Theorists, Treasonists, Traitors, and Leakers, BEWARE!”
「針對人工智慧和數據中心的陰謀非常病態，而唯一對此感到高興的是中國，」他在週一早些時候的帖子中寫道。「誰贏得人工智慧，誰就贏得一切！我們正在領先中國和所有其他人，並將繼續如此。陰謀論者、叛國者、叛徒和洩密者，當心！」
Abroad, governments are moving the other way. Prime Minister Mark Carney of Canada told Bloomberg News on Monday that he favored the creation of a global “technology stability” body to oversee artificial intelligence.
在國外，各國政府正朝著相反方向行動。加拿大總理馬克·卡尼週一告訴彭博新聞社，他贊成建立一個全球性的「技術穩定」機構來監督人工智慧。
“There’s a need for coordination,” he said. “Ultimately, a technology stability board, in our view, along the lines of the Financial Stability Board would make sense.”
「有必要進行協調，」他說。「在我們看來，建立一個類似於金融穩定理事會的技術穩定理事會，最終是合理的。」
Beijing, too, has been sounding the alarm. Chen Yixin, China’s security minister, recently warned that the leading A.I. models pose risks to the Chinese system and called for stricter government oversight — suggesting that the country Mr. Trump invokes as a reason to deregulate is weighing the opposite course.
北京也一直在發出警報。中國國家安全部部長陳一新最近警告稱，領先的人工智慧模型對中國體制構成風險，並呼籲實施更嚴格的政府監管——這表明，這個被川普引用作為放鬆監管理由的國家正在考慮相反的路線。

Jonathan Swan是《紐約時報》白宮記者，報導川普總統。
