中國國安部長：人工智慧可能對中共執政構成威脅

China’s Top Spy Chief Warns A.I. Is a Threat to Party Rule

郭莉莉 • 2026-09-15
China’s top spy chief has warned that artificial intelligence could pose a direct threat to the Chinese Communist Party’s hold on power, in what is the highest-level and most detailed articulation yet of how Beijing sees the technology’s security risks.
中國最高情報官員警告稱，人工智慧可能對中國共產黨的執政地位構成直接威脅，這是迄今為止北京方面就該技術安全風險所作出的最高層級、最詳盡的表述。
The unusually blunt assessment by Chen Yixin, the head of China’s Ministry of State Security, is in contrast to the warnings dominating the A.I. debate in the United States, which have focused on the possibility that A.I. could slip out of human control or pose a threat to humanity itself.
中國國家安全部部長陳一新這一異常直白的評估與主導美國人工智慧辯論的警告形成了鮮明對比，後者的焦點在於人工智慧可能脫離人類控制，或對人類本身構成威脅。
In an article published on Sunday in the state-run magazine China Cyberspace, Mr. Chen called for more party control over A.I. and stricter government oversight. This, he said, was necessary to protect political stability at home; to defend against increasingly sophisticated cyberattacks and misinformation campaigns from “hostile forces”; and to compete militarily with countries like the United States.
在週日發表於官方雜誌《中國網信》的文章中，陳一新呼籲黨加強對人工智慧的控制，並實施更嚴格的政府監管。他表示，這是為了維護國內政治穩定、抵禦「敵對勢力」日益複雜的網路攻擊和虛假信息運動，以及在軍事上與美國等國家競爭。
Mr. Chen referred specifically to U.S. tech companies and their A.I. models — like Claude Mythos and GPT-5.5-Cyber — as examples of new threats to cybersecurity. He also described the danger that foreign intelligence agencies might use A.I. for “large-scale espionage” and attacks on China’s critical infrastructure.
陳一新特別提到了美國科技公司及其人工智慧模型——如Claude Mythos和GPT-5.5-Cyber——作為網路安全新威脅的例子。他還描述了外國情報機構可能利用人工智慧造成境內數據「大規模外洩」，以及對中國關鍵基礎設施發動攻擊的危險。
廣告
The warning from the powerful security agency makes clear that China sees foreign A.I. models as an active threat to China, according to Henry Gao, a law professor at Singapore Management University. That effectively draws a red line before talks between American and Chinese officials on A.I. safety risks, expected ahead of a meeting between President Trump and China’s leader, Xi Jinping, on Sept. 24 in Washington.
新加坡管理大學法學教授高樹超表示，這家強勢安全機構的警告清楚地表明，中國將外國人工智慧模型視為對中國的現實威脅。這實際上在美中官員就人工智慧安全風險舉行會談之前划下了一道紅線，預計該會談將在川普總統與中國領導人習近平9月24日於華盛頓舉行會晤之前進行。
“Beijing is going into these talks viewing A.I. safety not as a shared humanitarian mission, but through an adversarial lens,” Mr. Gao said. It is making clear to Chinese tech firms and foreign rivals that “data sovereignty and political security will never be traded away for international safety accords,” he added.
「北京帶著這樣一種視角參加這些會談：不把人工智慧安全視為一項共同的人道主義使命，而是通過對抗的視角來看待，」高樹超表示。他還說，這向中國科技企業和外國競爭對手明確傳達了一個信息：「絕不會為了國際安全協議而犧牲數據主權和政治安全。」
Mr. Chen’s remarks are not the first time the security agency has spoken about the dangers of A.I., but they are the most comprehensive and signal Beijing’s growing concern about the industry. The security chief outlined several ways that A.I. could pose a threat to China:
陳一新的言論並非該安全機構首次談及人工智慧的危險，但這些言論是最為全面的，並且標誌著北京對該行業日益增長的擔憂。這位安全主管概述了人工智慧可能對中國構成威脅的幾種方式：
• Undermining the security of the Communist Party. The government’s top priority has long been maintaining the power of the ruling party. Mr. Chen’s article warns that those with “ulterior motives” could use A.I. to generate fake videos and audio to wage a “propaganda war” against China.
• 損害共產黨的安全。長期以來，政府的首要任務始終是維護執政黨的權力。陳一新的文章警告稱，那些「別有用心」的人可能會利用人工智慧生成虛假音影片，對中國發動「輿論戰」。
• “They’re worried, among other things, of say a deepfake of a Chinese official saying something that wasn’t actually said, and that spreading rapidly and destabilizing everything,” said Kyle Chan, a fellow at the Brookings Institution who studies Chinese technology and industrial policy. “Someone thinks that Xi Jinping made some announcement which he didn’t actually. That could sow chaos.”
• 「他們擔心的其中一件事是，比如出現一段中國官員的深度偽造影片，影片中官員說了一些實際上並未說過的話，並且該影片迅速傳播，破壞了一切穩定，」布魯金斯學會研究中國科技與產業政策的研究員凱爾·陳說。「如果有人以為習近平發布了某種實際上他並沒有發布的公告。這可能會製造混亂。」
• Attacks on China’s critical infrastructure. Mr. Chen, the spy chief, wrote that the technical and financial barriers to launching cyberattacks had been drastically lowered because of A.I. This, he said, posed “serious risks” to China’s information infrastructure. Last week, a small team of researchers in California demonstrated that risk when they disclosed a hacking tool that could take control of millions of users’ accounts on WeChat, China’s most widely used messaging app.
• 針對中國關鍵基礎設施的攻擊。情報部門負責人陳一新寫道，由於人工智慧，發動網路攻擊的技術和資金門檻已大幅降低。他表示，這對中國的信息基礎設施構成了「嚴重風險」。上週，加利福尼亞州的一個小型研究團隊展示了這種風險，當時他們披露了一款可以控制中國最廣泛使用的即時通訊應用微信上成百上千萬用戶帳戶的駭客工具。
廣告
• Leaks of sensitive information. Chinese users using foreign models could cause large-scale data leaks, the article noted. Anthropic, the company behind Claude and other A.I. models, said last week that the Chinese start-up Moonshot AI had routed queries by its users to Claude. Those queries included sensitive data, including video surveillance linked to the Chinese military, as well as proprietary information from high-profile Chinese technology companies.
• 敏感信息洩漏。文章指出，中國用戶使用外國模型可能會導致大規模數據洩漏。開發了Claude及其他人工智慧模型的Anthropic公司上週表示，中國初創公司月之暗面曾將其用戶的查詢轉發給Claude。這些查詢包含敏感數據，包括與中國軍方相關的影片監控信息，以及來自知名中國科技公司的專有信息。
• More intense military competition. The U.S. military’s use of A.I. in the U.S.-Israeli war with Iran was an example of how warfare has been “fundamentally transformed,” Mr. Chen wrote. He says that whoever can harness the power of tools such as algorithms to achieve more precise sensing and targeting, “will gain the initiative on the battlefield.”
• 更激烈的軍事競爭。陳一新寫道，美軍在美以與伊朗的戰爭中使用人工智慧，這是戰爭如何被「根本性變革」的例子。他說，誰能利用算法等工具的力量來實現更精確的感知和目標定位，「誰就能掌握戰場主動權。」
The warning adds to a growing drumbeat of concern from Chinese officials about the risks of A.I. Under Mr. Xi, China has deemed A.I. a strategic technology that is critical to the country’s development and ability to compete with the United States. At the same time, officials have begun to issue warnings and new guidelines for regulating the industry.
這一警告加劇了中國官員對人工智慧風險越來越強烈的擔憂。在習近平領導下，中國已將人工智慧視為對國家發展及與美國競爭能力至關重要的戰略技術。與此同時，官員們已開始發布警告並出台新的行業監管指南。
Previous warnings from Chinese regulators have tended to focus on specific issues, such as when the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology said in July that it had detected a security risk in some versions of Anthropic’s coding tool, Claude Code. Regulators have also repeatedly warned domestic users about the risks of A.I. assistants like OpenClaw.
以前中國監管機構的警告往往集中在具體問題上，例如工業和信息化部曾在7月份表示，在Anthropic的編程工具Claude Code的某些版本中檢測到了安全風險。監管機構也曾多次警告國內用戶警惕OpenClaw等人工智慧助手的風險。
“This is now a much more broad-based sort of warning, not about a specific threat from this one model or this one vulnerability, but a broader paradigm shift in how A.I. will create new risk for China,” said Mr. Chan of Brookings.
「現在，這是一種更為廣泛的警告，不是關於這個單一模型或這個單一漏洞帶來的具體威脅，而是關於人工智慧將如何為中國製造新風險的更廣泛的範式轉變，」布魯金斯學會的凱爾·陳說。
“It’s about taking these new powerful tools seriously and realizing there are a lot of vulnerabilities and China needs to move faster, and also use A.I. to patch those vulnerabilities, and defend against these new powerful cyber-actors,” he said.
「這意味著要認真對待這些強大的新工具，並認識到存在許多漏洞，中國需要加快步伐，同時還要利用人工智慧來修補這些漏洞，並抵禦這些新型的強大網路行為體，」他說。
廣告
Even as China’s security chief was articulating the country’s concerns about A.I., its Foreign Ministry was accusing U.S. companies of hyping the dangers of the technology.
就在中國安全主管闡明該國對人工智慧的擔憂之際，中國外交部卻在指責美國企業炒作該技術的危險性。
That was in response to calls by some Western A.I. executives like Dario Amodei, the chief executive of Anthropic, for Washington to clamp down on Chinese companies.
這是針對Anthropic首席執行官達里奧·阿莫迪等一些西方人工智慧高管呼籲華盛頓對中企採取嚴厲措施所作出的回應。
In an open letter over the weekend, Mr. Amodei said that democracies must keep their lead over China. He backed restrictions on selling powerful A.I. chips to the country, among other measures to slow China’s progress enough to widen America’s lead.
在週末的一封公開信中，阿莫迪表示，民主國家必須保持對中國的領先地位。他支持限制向該國出售強大的人工智慧晶片，並支持採取其他措施來放緩中國的進步，從而擴大美國的領先優勢。
Asked if China’s A.I. industry posed a threat to the United States, Guo Jiakun, a spokesman for China’s Foreign Ministry, said on Monday that such “threat narratives” and “malicious competition” would only make it harder for countries to work together on the regulation of A.I.
當被問及中國的人工智慧產業是否對美國構成威脅時，中國外交部發言人郭嘉昆在週一表示，這種「威脅敘事」和「惡性競爭」只會讓各國更難以在人工智慧的監管方面開展合作。
In an editorial on Sunday, the Communist Party-run tabloid, the Global Times, said that China played a central role as one of the world’s main suppliers of A.I. tools. It described Mr. Amodei’s call for the United States to contain China’s development as an “unrealistic delusion.”
在週日的一篇社論中，由共產黨開辦的小報《環球時報》表示，中國作為世界上主要的人工智慧工具供應國之一發揮著核心作用。該報將阿莫迪呼籲美國遏制中國發展的做法描述為「根本無法實現的妄想」。

郭莉莉(Lily Kuo)是《紐約時報》報導中國記者，常駐台北。
