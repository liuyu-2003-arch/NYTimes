北京最高樓「中國尊」遭小型飛機撞擊

Small Plane Crashes Into Tallest Building in Beijing

KEITH BRADSHER, MEAGHAN TOBIN, PEI-LIN WU • 2026-06-27
A small aircraft flew into the tallest building in Beijing on Friday, sending huge hunks of debris and plane parts plummeting onto the streets below and prompting crowds to flee.
週五，一架小型飛機撞擊北京最高的建築，巨大的碎片和飛機零件墜落到街道上，引發人群逃散。
Video shared on social media and verified by The New York Times showed debris falling from the skyscraper, as people ran to safety. The footage showed broken windows and debris, including what appeared to be the tail of a small aircraft, falling dozens of stories into the street below.
發布在社群媒體上並經《紐約時報》核實的影片顯示，碎片從這座摩天大樓上掉落，人們紛紛逃離。影片顯示，破碎的窗戶和碎片，包括一個疑似小型飛機尾翼的物體，從數十層高的地方墜落到下面的街道上。
The building is in the capital’s busy central business district, which was clogged with cars at evening rush hour. It was not immediately clear how many people were injured, if any were. The whereabouts and identity of the pilot were also unknown.
這棟建築位於中國首都繁忙的中央商務區，晚高峰時段交通壅堵。目前尚不清楚是否有人受傷，或有多少人受傷。飛行員的身份和下落也尚不明確。
Photos posted to social media from the site showed parts of an aircraft that appeared to be a lightweight model with only a few seats, registered to the airline Shuangyue General Aviation, a regional flight training provider. Calls to the company went unanswered.
社群媒體上發布的現場照片顯示，飛機的部件似乎屬於一架僅有幾個座位的輕型飛機，該飛機註冊在雙悅通用航空名下，這是一家區域性飛行培訓服務提供商。記者致電該公司，但無人接聽。
廣告
Data from FlightRadar, a flight tracking website, showed that the plane took off from an airfield in Beijing’s eastern Pinggu district. The plane circled the area before flying west into the city center.
航班追蹤網站FlightRadar的數據顯示，這架飛機從北京東部平谷區的一個機場起飛。飛機在該地區盤旋後，向西飛往市中心。
It was unclear if the crash was an accident or intentional. It was also unclear how the pilot was able to fly into the city center apparently unobstructed. Calls to the Civil Aviation Authority of China and a nearby police station in Beijing rang unanswered.
目前尚不清楚這次墜機是意外事故還是人為造成的。同樣不清楚的是，飛行員是如何能夠在似乎暢通無阻的情況下飛入市中心的。致電中國民航局和事發附近北京一個派出所的電話均無人接聽。
“This aircraft is normally used for pilot training east of Beijing,” said Ian Petchenik, a spokesman for FlightRadar. He said that this flight was “out of character” with those types of flights, adding: “I don’t think we can rule anything out at this point.”
「這架飛機通常在北京東部用于飛行員培訓，」FlightRadar的發言人伊恩·佩錢尼克表示。他稱這次飛行與此類飛行通常的特徵「不符」，並補充道：「我認為目前我們不能排除任何可能性。」
There was significant police presence near the building on Friday night. The police closed at least one major road near the building, setting up a folding metal grille gate, and a police car with flashing lights was parked on the street. High above, bright lights occasionally flashed around a hole in the side of the building as repair work got underway.
週五晚上，該建築附近有大量警察駐守。警方關閉了附近至少一條主要道路，設置了摺疊式金屬柵欄門，一輛閃爍著警燈的警車停在街上。大樓上方，隨著維修工作的展開，大樓側面的一個破洞周圍不時閃爍著強光。
The skyscraper is called the Citic Tower and is also known as China Zun. It is the headquarters of Citic Group, one of China’s biggest state-owned financial conglomerates.
這座大樓名為中信大廈，也被稱為「中國尊」，是中國最大的國有金融集團之一中信集團的總部所在地。
An employee of a nearby gym, who identified himself by the surname Zhang, said that he was walking with friends in the area when he saw an aircraft hit the tower around 6 p.m. local time. A woman with a head injury was later taken away in an ambulance, he said. A worker at a nearby restaurant reported hearing a loud noise around the same time.
附近一家健身房的一名自稱姓張的員工表示，下午6點左右，他和朋友在附近散步時看到一架飛機撞上了大樓。他說，隨後一名頭部受傷的女子被救護車拉走。附近一家餐廳的員工也報告稱，大約在同一時間聽到了巨大的聲響。
廣告
The accounts of injuries could not be immediately verified. But in a sign of the authorities’ sensitivity about the news, social media posts about the crash and the building itself appeared to be censored online in China.
有關受傷人員的說法無法立即得到核實。但關於此次墜機以及該建築本身的社群媒體帖子在中國網路上似乎遭到了審查，這是官方對此新聞高度敏感的一個跡象。
A resident of the neighborhood who gave his surname, Wang, said that just before 6 p.m., he heard loud noises coming from the street and walked toward the tower, where he saw a hole, which was briefly on fire, in the side of the building. He also said he saw the wreckage of an aircraft, also on fire, at the base of the building. He could not determine whether there were any casualties before the police hurried onlookers away, he said.
一位自稱姓王的附近居民表示，下午將近6點，他聽到街上傳來巨大的聲響，於是走向中信大廈。他在那裡看到大樓側面有一個破洞，並短暫起火。他還表示，在大樓底部看到了同樣在起火的飛機殘骸。他表示自己無法確定是否有人員傷亡，後來警察趕走了圍觀者。
An office worker who gave only his first name, Richard, told a Times reporter that he had been in a meeting on a high floor in an office building across from the Citic Tower when he realized something had happened. “I saw some white pieces of paper fluttering through the air,” he said, though he couldn’t see or hear anything else. He said he was surprised when he emerged from his office building later to find a crowd outside.
一名只透露自己英文名叫理查德的公司職員告訴時報記者，當他意識到出事了的時候，他正在中信大廈對面一座寫字樓的高層開會。「我看到一些白色的紙片飄在空中，」他說，儘管除此之外他看不到也聽不到任何其他動靜。他說，當他稍後走出辦公樓時，很驚訝地發現外面聚集了很多人。
Later in the evening, about 200 people had gathered across the street from the Citic Tower, taking pictures. The police were waving people away from entering the street on the east side. The avenue past the south side of the building remained open to pedestrians, bicyclists and motor scooters.
當晚晚些時候，約有200人聚集在中信大廈對面的街道上拍照。警方揮手示意人們不要進入東側的街道。大樓南側的街道仍然對行人、單車和電動單車開放。
The iconic tower was completed in 2018 and can be seen from across the city. It is across the street from another well-known Beijing architectural landmark, the headquarters of China Central Television, where nearly 20 police cars were parked on Friday night.
這座標誌性的大樓於2018年竣工，從北京的各個角落都可以看到它。它與北京另一處著名的建築地標——中國中央電視台總部隔街相望，週五晚上，那裡停靠了近20輛警車。
