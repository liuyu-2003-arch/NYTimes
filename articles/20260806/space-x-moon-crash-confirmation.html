SpaceX火箭殘骸撞擊月球，會發生什麼？

A Four-Ton SpaceX Rocket Stage Hit the Moon, but Almost No One Saw It

KENNETH CHANG • 2026-08-06
If a rocket stage crashes into the moon and no one sees it, did it really crash?
如果一枚火箭的某一級撞上月球卻沒有人觀測到，那它真的撞上月球了嗎？
A discarded upper stage of a SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket slammed into the surface of the moon around 2:35 a.m. Eastern time on Wednesday at a speed of 5,400 miles per hour.
美國東部時間週三凌晨2點35分左右，SpaceX公司「獵鷹9號」運載火箭的一個廢棄上面級以每小時5400英里（約合8690公里）的速度猛烈撞擊了月球表面。
However, it appears no one has yet reported any obvious signs of the impact.
然而，目前似乎還沒有人報告發現任何明顯的撞擊跡象。
Astronomers hoped that the collision would create a bright enough flash of light and a big enough cloud of dust and debris that it could be observed through telescopes from Earth. But at the appointed time, the moon looked like the moon.
天文學家曾希望，這次碰撞會產生足夠明亮的閃光以及足夠龐大的塵埃和碎片雲，可以通過地球上的望遠鏡進行觀測。但在預定時間，月球看起來依然如常。
廣告
More subtle observations taken at the Lowell Discovery Telescope in Arizona and the Very Large Telescope in Chile could reveal evidence of the collision, like specific wavelengths of light emitted or absorbed by the plume of debris, but those data need to be analyzed.
在亞利桑那州的「洛厄爾發現望遠鏡」和智利的「甚大望遠鏡」進行的更精細觀測可能會揭示此次碰撞的證據，例如碎片羽流發射或吸收的特定波長光，但這些數據還需要進行分析。
“We were able to observe despite intermittent thick clouds, but need to do some quick reduction on the data to know if we saw anything,” said Patrick Lierle, a Boston University astronomer involved with the Lowell Discovery observations. “So the answer is nothing yet.”
「儘管有間歇性的厚雲層，我們還是能夠進行觀測，但需要對數據進行一些快速處理，才能確定我們是否觀測到了什麼，」參與「洛厄爾發現望遠鏡」觀測的波士頓大學天文學家派翠克·利爾說。「所以答案是，目前還沒有發現。」
The most promising observations so far come from Chile.
迄今為止，最有希望的觀測結果來自智利。
Another Boston University scientist, Carl Schmidt, used the Very Large Telescope to measure sodium and lithium in a plume a few tens of kilometers in size that lasted five to 10 minutes after impact.
波士頓大學的另一位科學家卡爾·施密特利用「甚大望遠鏡」測量了撞擊後持續五到10分鐘、規模達數十公里的羽流中的鈉和鋰。
Dr. Schmidt said he was “100 percent confident” that the readings came from the impact.
施密特表示，他「100%確信」這些測量讀數來自於此次撞擊。
However, it seems that no one was able to directly see or photograph the collision.
然而，似乎沒有人能夠直接看到或拍攝到這次碰撞。
“I’ve so far only heard negatives from imaging folks,” Mr. Lierle said.
「到目前為止，我從從事成像工作的人那裡聽到的都是否定的結果，」利爾說。
The anticlimactic demise of the rocket stage was not a surprise.
火箭殘骸以這種平淡的方式收場並不令人意外。
Computer simulations calculated the effect of the rocket stage striking the moon in a vertical orientation, which would have produced the most debris, but the stage would more likely have belly-flopped at an angle instead. (The vertical case was the only one simple enough to be computed.)
計算機模擬計算了這一上面級以垂直方向撞擊月球的效果——這種撞擊會產生最多的碎片，但它更有可能是以一定傾斜角度摔落的。（垂直撞擊是唯一簡單到足以進行計算的情況。）
But even in the case of a vertical impact, the predicted flash and plume were at the edge of what might be visible, and the dynamics depended on other unknown factors like whether the stage hit solid bedrock or loose lunar soil.
但即使是在垂直撞擊的情況下，預測的閃光和羽流也處於可能被看見的極限邊緣，而且動力學過程還取決於其他未知因素，比如它撞擊的是堅硬的基岩還是鬆散的月壤。
廣告
The Korean spacecraft Danuri passed over the expected crash site about eight hours after impact. Even if it successfully captured an image of the crater, it could take days or weeks to identify it. That is because the process of locating the newly disrupted moonscape is like finding a needle in a haystack. Confirmation will require comparing the gouge with an earlier photograph that shows a pristine surface at that location before impact.
韓國航天器「賞月號」在撞擊發生約八小時後飛越了預期的墜毀地點。即使它成功拍到了撞擊坑的圖像，也可能需要幾天或幾週的時間才能識別。這是因為，定位新近被破壞的月球地貌，過程就像大海撈針一樣困難。要確認這一撞擊坑，需要將其與一張顯示撞擊前該位置原始地表的早期照片進行對比。
In six days, NASA’s Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter will also pass over the site, providing additional photographs to aid the search.
六天後，美國宇航局的月球勘測軌道飛行器也將飛越該地點，提供更多照片協助搜尋。
Is it possible that the predictions were wrong, and that the Falcon 9 stage missed the moon?
預測是否可能有誤，「獵鷹9號」的廢棄上面級有沒有可能並未擊中月球？
Scientists are confident that the collision happened. Like everything flying around in the solar system, the rocket stage moved according to Newton’s laws of motion with only slight deviations arising from additional forces like the solar wind.
科學家們確信碰撞已經發生。就像在太陽系中飛行的所有物體一樣，這枚火箭的殘骸按照牛頓運動定律運行，只有受到太陽風等額外作用力時才會產生輕微偏差。
The uncertainty in where the impact would occur was just a few miles.
撞擊發生位置的不確定範圍只有幾公里。

Kenneth Chang是《紐約時報》科學記者，報導NASA和太陽系，以及一些更近地球的研究。
