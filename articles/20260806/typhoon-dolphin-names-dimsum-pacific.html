「點心」風暴來襲？揭秘太平洋颱風命名法則

Coming Soon to the Asia Pacific: A Storm Named Dim-sum

FRANCESCA REGALADO • 2026-08-06
If you see that a storm named Dim-sum is gathering steam in the Pacific Ocean, it’s not an error.
如果你看到一個名叫「點心」的風暴在太平洋上生成，那並不是筆誤。
In June, as the Pacific typhoon season began, the Hong Kong Observatory said it had chosen the city’s bite-sized cuisine as its latest contribution to the list of names for tropical cyclones that form in the Pacific.
6月，隨著太平洋颱風季的到來，香港天文台表示，它選擇這座城市的小吃美食來為太平洋生成的熱帶氣旋命名名單貢獻一個新名字。
It’s not the first time that Hong Kong has named a storm after a beloved symbol. Typhoon Dolphin, which is forecast to approach Japan and Taiwan this weekend, takes its name from the city’s animal mascot.
這並不是香港第一次用一個深受喜愛的城市象徵為風暴命名。預計本週末將逼近日本和台灣的「白海豚」颱風，其名字就來源於香港的動物吉祥物。
The names are compiled by the Typhoon Committee, which was formed in 1968 by the United Nations and the World Meteorological Organization to help Asia Pacific countries prepare for tropical storms. The committee maintains an active list of 140 names, divided into five columns, that it cycles through every four or five years, depending on the frequency of storms.
這些名字由颱風委員會編製。該委員會由聯合國和世界氣象組織在1968年共同成立，旨在幫助亞太國家做好應對熱帶風暴的準備。該委員會維護著一份包含140個名字的現行名單，分為五組列表，根據風暴發生頻率，大約每四到五年循環使用一次。
廣告
Each of the committee’s 14 members — including the United States, China, the Koreas, Hong Kong and Malaysia — contributes 10 names. They are drawn from each member’s language and culture, resulting in storms named for animals, plants, historical and mythological figures, and, of course, food.
委員會的14個成員——包括美國、中國、朝鮮、韓國、香港和馬來西亞——各自提供10個名字。這些名稱來自各成員的語言和文化，因此一些颱風的名字取自動物、植物、歷史人物和神話人物，當然，也有食物。
That means Dim-sum will eventually be joined by typhoons such as Gaeguri (frog, in Korean), Burapha (the Thai word for “east”) and Tirou (a Micronesian greeting), according to the committee’s latest list of names released in March.
這意味著，根據委員會今年3月公布的最新名單，「點心」最終還會與「簡古維」（朝鮮語的「青蛙」之意）、「布拉帕」（泰語中的「東方」之意）以及「蒂魯」（密克羅尼西亞語中的一種問候語）等颱風名字一起出現在名單上。
Here’s how the system works.
這個命名體系是這樣運作的。
U.S. forecasters started the naming practice.
美國氣象預報員開創了颱風命名的做法。
Tropical cyclones form when warm ocean water, moist air and light winds persist long enough to combine into systems that produce violent winds, large waves and torrential rain. Those that form over the western Pacific Ocean and affect Asia are called typhoons.
熱帶氣旋的形成始於溫暖的洋面、潮濕的空氣和微弱的風持續足夠長的時間，待它們匯聚成氣象系統後，便能產生狂風、巨浪和暴雨。在太平洋西部形成並影響亞洲的熱帶氣旋被稱為颱風。
During World War II, meteorologists in several Asian countries were prevented from working by occupying Japanese forces because weather forecasts were considered sensitive military intelligence, Rey Lirios, a historian at the Philippine Atmospheric, Geophysical and Astronomical Services Administration, said in an interview. As a result, those countries relied on American military forecasters in the U.S. Pacific territory of Guam.
菲律賓大氣地球物理天文局的歷史學家雷·利里奧斯在接受採訪時說，第二次世界大戰期間，亞洲一些國家的氣象學家因為日軍佔領而無法正常工作，因為天氣預報被視為敏感的軍事情報。因此，這些國家不得不依靠駐紮在美國太平洋屬地關島的美軍氣象預報員。
廣告
To distinguish among storms, American forecasters first compiled a list of female names, often drawn from their wives and girlfriends, according to meteorologists. In the 1970s, amid the gender equality movement, the U.S. government began adding male names, according to Lam Chiu Ying, a former director of the Hong Kong Observatory, in a 2017 video interview.
據氣象學家介紹，為區分不同風暴，美國的預報員最初彙編了一份由女性名字組成的名單，通常取自他們的妻子和女友之名。香港天文台原台長林超英在2017年一段影片採訪中說，進入上世紀70年代，隨著性別平等運動興起，美國政府開始在名單中加入男性名字。
Some naming processes are open to the public.
部分命名程序向公眾開放。
Members of the Typhoon Committee pushed to replace American names with regional ones in the 1990s. Some opened the process to the public.
颱風委員會成員在20世紀90年代推動以具有地區特色的名字取代美國式的名字。一些成員還將提名過程向公眾開放。
“We wanted to leave all of the old names behind in the 20th century,” said Mr. Lirios, who created the criteria for the Philippines’ public submissions in 1999.
「我們希望把所有舊名字都留在20世紀，」利里奧斯說。他曾在1999年為菲律賓建立了公眾提名標準。
He said the Typhoon Committee rejected names that were indecent or carried negative connotations in other languages. Storms also cannot be named for public figures, including politicians and celebrities.
他說，颱風委員會拒絕那些在其他語言中不雅或帶有負面含義的名字。颱風也不能以公眾人物命名，包括政治人物和名人。
In 2005, the committee rejected two names chosen by the public in Hong Kong. Taichi, the ancient Chinese martial art, could be mistaken as a Japanese man’s name, while Kapok, the cotton tree, sounded like a slang term for sexual organs in some languages, Mr. Lam said.
林超英說，2005年的時候，委員會拒絕了香港公眾選出的兩個名稱：太極(Taichi)——中國古老武術，但它也可能會被誤認為是日本男性的名字；木棉(Kapok)——它在某些語言中聽起來像是對性器官的俚語稱呼。
廣告
Dim-sum, by contrast, was uncontroversial. Lui Yuk Sing, a spokesman for the Hong Kong Observatory, said it was selected by the public in 2024, along with Sparrow and Shui-sin, the Cantonese word for daffodil.
相比之下，「點心」則毫無爭議。香港天文台發言人呂旭昇表示，這個名字是香港公眾在2024年選出的，與它一起入選的還有「麻雀」和「水仙」。
The names reflect regional culture.
這些名字體現了不同地區的文化。
Aside from Dim-sum and Dolphin, Hong Kong has submitted Banyan, named for the tree, and Lionrock, for a mountain overlooking the city.
除了「點心」和「白海豚」，香港以前還提交了「榕樹」和「獅子山」這兩個名字，前者取自樹木名，後者則來自俯瞰香港的一座山。
The Japan Meteorological Agency said in an email that it typically contributes the Japanese names of constellations, such as Koinu (“puppy,” for Canis Minor) and Koguma (“bear cub,” for Ursa Minor). The committee’s list also includes mythological figures such as the Chinese folk heroine Mulan, the Micronesian nature goddess Sinlaku and the Vietnamese mountain god Son Tinh.
日本氣象廳在一封電子郵件中表示，日本通常提供星座的日語名稱，如「小犬」（對應小犬座）和「小熊」（對應小熊座）。該委員會的名單還包括神話人物，如中國民間傳說中的女英雄「木蘭」、密克羅尼西亞自然女神「森拉克」和越南的「山神」。
Malaysia submits the names of native flora and fauna that are easy to pronounce, like the Cempaka flower, said Mazly Bin Mohamed, a spokesman for the Malaysian Meteorological Department. Other countries also draw inspiration from nature, including Vietnam’s Ha Long Bay, Thailand’s Khanun (meaning “jackfruit”) and the Philippines’ Mount Amuyao.
馬來西亞氣象部門發言人穆罕默德·馬茲利·本·穆罕默德表示，馬來西亞提交的是本地動植物的名稱，易於發音，如「查帕卡」。其他國家也從自然中汲取靈感，包括越南的「夏浪」、泰國的「卡努」（菠蘿蜜果）和菲律賓的「阿穆丘」。
Because the Philippines is typically one of the first countries in a typhoon’s path, Filipino meteorologists assign storms a local name, Mr. Lirios said.
利里奧斯說，由於菲律賓通常是颱風最早經過的國家，因此菲律賓氣象學家還會給颱風另取一個當地名稱。
廣告
For example, Typhoon Jangmi, which caused heavy flooding in the Philippines and Japan in early June, was known in the Philippines as Domeng, a local diminutive of Domingo.
例如，今年6月初給菲律賓和日本造成嚴重洪災的「薔薇」颱風在菲律賓就被稱為「Domeng」，這是當地對「Domingo」這個人名的一種暱稱形式。
The names of deadly storms are often removed from the list.
造成重大傷亡的颱風名稱通常會從名單中被刪除。
Some names are retired after becoming associated with particularly devastating or deadly storms.
一些颱風名在與特別具有破壞性或造成重大傷亡的災難性風暴產生關聯後，會被正式撤銷。
The Philippines stopped using the local name Yolanda after the 2013 typhoon that killed more than 6,000 people. The Typhoon Committee retired the storm’s official name, Haiyan, in 2015.
2013年的颱風「Yolanda」造成逾6000人罹難，此後菲律賓停止使用這一本地名稱。颱風委員會則於2015年正式撤銷了該颱風的官方名稱「海燕」。
This year, the committee added Dim-sum to the list to replace Man-yi, which was retired after the storm caused more than a dozen deaths in the Philippines in 2024.
今年，委員會將「點心」加入名單，以取代「萬宜」——後者2024年在菲律賓造成十餘人死亡後被永久除名。

Francesca Regalado是《紐約時報》記者，報導突發新聞。
