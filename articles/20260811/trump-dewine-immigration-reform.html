移民改革會是川普的「尼克森訪華」時刻嗎？

Mike DeWine: Trump Would Shock Everyone With This Reform

MIKE DEWINE • 2026-08-11
In 1972, President Richard Nixon did something monumental and unexpected when he traveled to the People’s Republic of China and met with the leaders of its Communist regime, resulting in the establishment of formal diplomatic relations between our two countries just a few years later.
1972年，理查德·尼克森總統做出了一個影響深遠且出人意料的舉動：他前往中華人民共和國，並與中國共產黨領導人會面。短短几年，兩國便建立起了正式的外交關係。
At that time, few others could have done what Nixon did for fear of being labeled an appeaser. But Nixon’s lifelong fight against Communism gave him the credibility and public trust he needed to make the bold move.
在當時，鑒於害怕被貼上妥協綏靖的標籤，很少有人能做到尼克森所做的事。但尼克森一生反共的立場賦予了他所需的公信力和公眾信任，使他得以邁出這大膽的一步。
President Trump has an equally historic opportunity.
川普總統正面臨著一個同樣具有歷史意義的契機。
For decades, this country’s failure to seal its southern border and stem the tide of illegal immigration has hamstrung our ability to agree upon a rational legal immigration policy.
數十年來，美國未能封鎖南部邊境並遏制非法移民潮，這阻礙了我們就理性的合法移民政策達成一致的能力。
廣告
But the Department of Homeland Security reports that in June, Border Patrol apprehensions at our southern border were 94 percent lower than the monthly average during the Biden administration. Having demanded and led this remarkable turnaround, Mr. Trump has the standing to lead an effort to reform our legal immigration system — to make changes that prioritize our work force needs while respecting our history of welcoming people fleeing oppression and humanitarian crises.
但美國國土安全部報告稱，今年6月，邊境巡邏隊在南部邊境逮捕的非法移民人數比拜登政府期間的月平均水平降低了94%。在要求並領導了這一非凡的逆轉之後，川普擁有足夠的威望來領導對我們合法移民體系的改革——進行相關調整，優先考慮我們的勞動力需求，同時尊重我們接納受壓迫者和人道危機難民的歷史傳統。
As Ohio’s governor, I understand the need for a sensible immigration policy. In our state, very simply, we need people.
作為俄亥俄州州長，我深知制定一項明智移民政策的必要性。簡而言之，我們州需要人。
The national birthrate has been trending down since 2007 and is well below the replacement rate. As our population grows more slowly, it will also trend older. The Congressional Budget Office projects that starting in 2030, deaths will outpace births and all of our population growth will come from net immigration.
自2007年以來，全國出生率一直呈下降趨勢，且遠低於世代更替率。隨著人口增長放緩，我們的老齡化趨勢也將愈發明顯。國會預算辦公室預測，從2030年開始，死亡人數將超過出生人數，我們所有的人口增長都將來自於凈移民。
Despite declining birthrates, Ohio’s population grew between 2014 and 2024, with about 60 percent of the increase coming from those born outside the United States. Because our employment rate for prime-age workers is near its historic peak, Ohio must attract people of all skill levels to expand our work force.
儘管出生率有所下降，但俄亥俄州的人口在2014年至2024年間依然實現了增長，其中約60%的增長來自於出生在美國境外的人群。由於我們黃金年齡工作者的就業率接近歷史最高點，俄亥俄州必須吸引各個技能水平的人才來擴充我們的勞動力隊伍。
Immigrants help fill work force gaps. Foreign-born workers represent nearly 30 percent of our country’s long-term direct-care work force. They provide one-third of the workers who allow older Americans to remain in their homes.
移民有助於填補勞動力的空缺。外國出生的工作者占我國一線照護勞動力的近30%。在讓老年美國人得以繼續留在自己家中生活的護理人員中，他們佔了三分之一。
As of 2023, 22 percent of Ohio’s physicians, 12 percent of our STEM workers and 8 percent of our entrepreneurs were foreign-born. Looking to the future, where advanced manufacturing, aerospace and health care will drive our economy, we need both native-born workers and immigrants.
截至2023年，俄亥俄州22%的醫師、12%的STEM（科學、技術、工程和數學）領域從業者以及8%的創業者均出生於國外。展望未來，先進制造、航空航天和醫療衛生將驅動我們的經濟發展，我們既需要本國出生的工作者，也需要移民。
廣告
By taking on jobs that are often physically demanding and hard to fill, immigrants in many instances create opportunities for native-born Americans to hold more specialized, and higher paying, jobs. Immigrants and native-born Ohioans are building a better economy together.
通過承擔往往對體力要求高且難以招到人的崗位，移民在許多情況下為本國出生的美國人創造了從事更專業、薪酬更高工作的機會。移民與俄亥俄州本土居民正在共同建設一個更美好的經濟體。
This is the case in many parts of our country, where immigrants contribute to the economy, including many employed in the agricultural, meat and seafood processing, hospitality, construction and medical care fields.
在我們國家的許多地方也是如此，移民為經濟做出了貢獻，包括許多受雇於農業、肉類和海鮮加工、餐飲住宿、建築以及醫療護理領域的移民。
Like the president, I want everyone who comes to this country to do it legally — and I applaud his focus on and success at bringing us closer to that reality. However, despite his administration’s success in stemming illegal immigration, its approach to those here legally threatens to derail economic momentum in my state and elsewhere by deporting hard-working individuals and shutting the door to future talent.
和總統一樣，我希望每一個來到這個國家的人都是合法合規的——我也讚賞他專注於並成功地使我們更接近這一現實。然而，儘管他的政府在遏制非法移民方面取得了成功，其對待合法居留人員的方式卻威脅到我州及其他地方的經濟增長勢頭，不僅驅逐勤勞的個體，還將未來的優秀人才拒之門外。
According to one estimate, international students added nearly $55 billion to the nation’s economy in 2024. Yet the administration has drastically reduced our international student population — a sharp departure from decades of open-ended academic stays that supported research and spurred innovation.
根據一項估計，國際學生在2024年為本國經濟貢獻了近550億美元。然而政府卻大幅減少了我們的國際學生群體——這是對數十年來支持科研並激發創新、不設上限的學術居留政策的徹底背離。
In January, the State Department froze immigrant visa approvals for applicants from 75 countries. (In July, a Federal District Court judge ruled that this was unlawful.) Those visas have in recent years accounted for nearly half of all U.S. immigrant visas.
今年1月，國務院凍結了對來自75個國家的申請人的移民簽證批准。（7月，一名聯邦地區法院法官裁定此舉違法。）近年來，這些簽證佔到了美國所有移民簽證的近一半。
The administration also reduced the annual cap on refugees allowed into the United States to 7,500 from 125,000, before raising it to 17,500, with most of those slots reserved for white South Africans.
政府還將每年允許進入美國的難民上限從12.5萬人削減至7500人，之後才回調至17500人，且大部分名額預留給了南非白人。
廣告
In Springfield, Ohio, Haitian nationals have played a major role in the city’s economic comeback. They’ve filled jobs, repaired homes, paid taxes, started businesses and supported their families. But the administration has ended their Temporary Protected Status, a move I have vocally opposed.
在俄亥俄州的斯普林菲爾德，海地籍人士在該市的經濟復甦中發揮了重要作用。他們填補了崗位空缺、維修房屋、繳納稅款、開辦企業並撫養家庭。但政府終止了他們的「臨時保護身份」，我明確反對這一舉措。
It’s time for Mr. Trump’s Nixon-goes-to-China moment. Having established himself as a staunch opponent of illegal immigration, he should begin a thoughtful, forward-thinking discussion with members of Congress in both parties about enacting a legal immigration policy that strengthens national security, bolsters our economy and keeps decent, hard-working people here.
現在是時候迎來川普的「尼克森訪華」時刻了。在確立了自己作為非法移民堅決反對者的地位後，他應該與國會兩黨成員展開一場周密且具前瞻性的討論，以頒佈一項能夠加強國家安全、促進經濟繁榮並留住品行端正、勤勞的人的合法移民政策。
Among the reforms he should consider supporting: Establishing new pathways to extend the legal status of foreign students who graduate with advanced STEM degrees, essentially stapling a green card to their diplomas; reworking the H-1B visa program to better prioritize workers whose skills and experience are essential to American businesses; and equipping employers with modern, reliable tools to verify work eligibility.
他應當考慮支持的改革包括：建立新的途徑，延長獲得STEM高級學位的外國留學生的合法身份，本質上相當於把綠卡釘在他們的畢業證書上；重塑H-1B簽證項目，以便更好地優先考慮那些掌握對美國企業至關重要的技能和經驗的工作者；以及為僱主提供現代、可靠的工具來驗證其工作資格。
My great-great grandfather, Dennis DeWine, left County Galway on a cattle boat in the 1840s to escape the Irish potato famine. He worked on the railroad being built in Yellow Springs, Ohio, where he settled and raised his family.
19世紀40年代，我的高祖父丹尼斯·德瓦因乘坐運牛船離開愛爾蘭的高威郡以逃避大饑荒。他在俄亥俄州黃泉鎮的鐵路工地打工，並在那裡定居，成家立業。
With only big dreams and guts, he took a chance on America, and America took a chance on him. That’s the American story.
僅憑著宏大的夢想和膽量，他選擇在美洲大陸孤注一擲，而這片土地也接納了他。這就是美國的故事。
President Ronald Reagan, who signed a sweeping immigration reform bill in 1986, chose immigration as the topic for his final speech before leaving office. He reflected on America’s spirit of openness and opportunity, reminding us that “we lead the world because, unique among nations, we draw our people — our strength — from every country and every corner of the world.” He said:
羅納德·雷根總統於1986年簽署了一項全面移民改革法案，他在離任前的最後一次演講中選擇了移民作為主題。他反思了美國開放與機遇的精神，提醒我們，「我們之所以領跑世界，是因為在所有國家中唯獨我們能夠從世界上的每一個國家、每一個角落汲取我們的人民——也就是我們的力量。」他說：
廣告
Thanks to each wave of new arrivals to this land of opportunity, we’re a nation forever young, forever bursting with energy and new ideas, and always on the cutting edge, always leading the world to the next frontier. This quality is vital to our future as a nation. If we ever closed the door to new Americans, our leadership in the world would soon be lost.
「由於一批批湧向這片機遇之地的新移民，我們是一個永遠年輕、永遠迸發著活力與新思想的國家，並且始終處於前沿，始終引領世界走向下一個前沿。這種特質對我們作為一個國家的未來至關重要。如果我們向新美國人關上大門，我們將很快失去在世界上的領導地位。」
The president should seize the moment to achieve real immigration reform that recognizes this same American spirit.
總統應當抓住這一時機，實現彰顯這種美國精神的真正移民改革。

Mike DeWine曾任美國參議員和眾議員。
