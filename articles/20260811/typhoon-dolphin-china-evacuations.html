颱風「白海豚」來襲，中國百萬人被疏散

Typhoon Dolphin Barrels Into China, Prompting Mass Evacuations

MAX KIM • 2026-08-11
A powerful tropical cyclone buffeted China on Sunday evening, prompting the authorities to evacuate more than a million people on the country’s eastern coast and cancel hundreds of flights, as the storm continued its long path of destruction across the Pacific Ocean.
週日傍晚，一場強大的熱帶氣旋襲擊了中國。迫使當局對東部沿海地區超過100萬人進行疏散，並取消了數百個航班，而這場風暴仍在太平洋上繼續漫長的破壞之旅。
After forming near the Marshall Islands last month and traveling what meteorologists said was an unusually long distance, Dolphin pummeled Okinawa, Japan, injuring six people and cutting power to thousands over the weekend.
「白海豚」颱風上個月在馬紹爾群島附近形成，經過氣象學家所稱的異常長距離的移動，於週末重創了日本沖繩，造成六人受傷，數以千計的人遭遇停電。
It made landfall on Sunday evening near Yuhuan, a city in Zhejiang Province on China’s southeastern coast. The storm had winds as strong as 94 miles per hour, or 151 kilometers per hour. More than 1,000 flights in Shanghai were canceled as the authorities warned that the eastern coast would continue to experience heavy rains this week.
週日傍晚，風暴在中國東南沿海浙江省的玉環市附近登陸。該風暴的最高風速達每小時151公里。上海有1000多個航班被取消，當局警告稱，東部沿海地區本週將繼續遭遇暴雨。
As residents wrangled umbrellas that were being flipped inside out by the powerful winds, the Shanghai Tower — the third-tallest building in the world — was stabilized by a mass damper, an ornate pendulum-like weight suspended on one of its upper floors, according the Chinese state broadcaster, CCTV.
據中國國家廣播電視機構中央電視台報導，居民們在強風中與被吹翻外翻的雨傘艱難搏鬥，世界第三高樓上海中心大廈憑藉質量阻尼器保持了穩定，這是一種懸掛在高層的華麗鐘擺狀配重。
The storm also intensified monsoon rains in the Philippines, disrupting maritime transportation and causing some areas to flood.
這場風暴還加劇了菲律賓的季風降雨，擾亂了海上交通，導致部分地區發生洪災。
In some parts of Manila, residents waded through waist-deep waters, where some children also played, while others sheltered in emergency evacuation centers.
在馬尼拉的部分地區，居民們在齊腰深的水中涉水而行，一些兒童在水中嬉戲，還有人在緊急疏散中心避難。
The Philippine meteorological agency warned in a Monday advisory that certain regions could still get up to 200 millimeters, or about eight inches, of rain that day.
菲律賓氣象局在週一的預警中警告稱，當天部分地區降雨量仍可能高達200毫米。
“Numerous flooding events are likely, especially in areas that are urbanized, low lying, or near rivers,” the agency said. “Landslide likely in moderate to highly susceptible areas.”
「極有可能發生多起洪澇事件，特別是在城市化地區、低洼地帶或靠近河流的地區，」該機構表示。「在中度至高度易發地區，很可能發生山體滑坡。」
廣告
Dolphin, which takes its name from the Chinese white dolphins that live in Hong Kong’s waters, is one of three tropical storms currently active in the northwest Pacific. Joining it are Chan-hom (a type of sandalwood tree in Laos) and Peilou (a migratory bird in Macau).
「白海豚」這個名字來源於生活在香港水域的中華白海豚，它是目前在西北太平洋活躍的三場熱帶風暴之一。與它同時活躍的還有「燦鴻」（寮國的一種檀香樹）和「琵鷺」（澳門的一種候鳥）。
All three monikers were picked by an international typhoon committee that allows its 14 member countries to take turns naming storms.
這三個名字均由國際颱風委員會挑選，該委員會允許其14個成員國輪流為風暴命名。
