人工智慧推波助瀾，網路攻擊席捲日本

Ransomware Attacks Have Swept Japan. A.I. May Be Making Them Easier.

TIFFANY MAY, KIUKO NOTOYA • 2026-10-14
A wave of cyberattacks has wreaked havoc in Japan over the past few weeks, leaving virtually no industry untouched and the government searching for answers about how to bolster its defenses.
過去幾週，一波網路攻擊席捲日本，造成嚴重混亂，幾乎沒有哪個行業能夠倖免。日本政府正在設法尋找加強網路防禦的對策。
The hackers have tampered with servers, taken local government websites offline and gained access to large tranches of consumer data. Masaaki Taira, Japan’s former digital minister who leads a cybersecurity committee within the ruling Liberal Democratic Party, declared on Friday that Japan was in “a state of emergency in cyberspace.”
駭客篡改伺服器、使地方政府網站陷入癱瘓，並獲取了大量消費者數據。日本前數字大臣、執政黨自民黨網路安全委員會負責人平將明週五宣稱，日本處於「網路空間緊急狀態」。
Experts said that the attackers were most likely motivated by money. After paralyzing operations, they could attempt to extort local governments and companies into paying large ransoms, or sell the stolen information.
專家表示，攻擊者最有可能是出於經濟利益的驅動。他們在癱瘓相關機構的運營後，可能試圖勒索地方政府和企業索取巨額贖金，或出售竊取的信息。
“Their actions may result in the suspension or degradation of systems and services that are important to society,” said Katsunari Yoshioka, a professor at Yokohama National University and an expert in systems security.
橫濱國立大學教授、系統安全專家吉岡克成表示：「他們的行為可能導致對社會至關重要的系統和服務中斷或性能下降。」
廣告
A study by TrendAI, a cybersecurity software company, found 600 cyberattacks were reported in Japan in the first nine months of 2026, more than the total number in 2025.
網路安全軟體公司TrendAI的一項研究發現，2026年前九個月，日本報告了600起網路攻擊，超過2025年全年總數。
In the past two weeks, a public university had to cancel classes for a week after its servers were attacked. A car-sharing service said that more than one million personal documents had been leaked, including headshots on driver’s licenses and home addresses. A ransomware attack on a cloud service operator owned by SoftBank affected almost 500 companies and local governments, including a rail operator and a police headquarters.
過去兩週內，一所公立大學的伺服器遭到攻擊後不得不停課一週；一家汽車共享服務公司表示，超過100萬份包含個人信息的文件遭到洩漏，其中包括駕照上的大頭照和家庭住址；軟銀旗下的一家雲服務運營商遭到勒索軟體攻擊，波及近500家企業和地方政府，其中包括一家鐵路運營商和一個警察總部。
Ransomware attacks have caused major problems in recent years well before A.I., but experts said artificial intelligence may have made these attacks easier.
早在人工智慧出現之前，勒索軟體攻擊就已在近年造成嚴重問題，但專家表示，人工智慧可能讓發動這些攻擊變得更加容易。
Toshiharu Furukawa, Japan’s minister of digital transformation, said at a news conference on Friday in Tokyo that the cyberattacks were reaching “across all sectors.”
日本數字轉型大臣古川俊治週五在東京的新聞發布會上說，網路攻擊正波及「所有行業」。
Why Japan?
為什麼是日本？
As the world’s fourth-largest economy that produces many of the world’s robots, cars and electronics, Japan has long been an attractive target for hackers. Even though it was an early adopter of technology, its cybersecurity infrastructure is underdeveloped, according to experts.
日本是世界第四大經濟體，也是全球許多機器人、汽車和電子產品的生產國，長期以來它一直是駭客眼中的理想攻擊目標。專家指出，儘管日本很早就開始採用新技術，但其網路安全基礎設施仍然不夠完善。
廣告
A Microsoft report found that, in 2026, Japan was second only to Taiwan as the most frequently affected by cyberthreats in Asia. The International Institute for Strategic Studies, a London think tank, placed Japan in the lowest of three tiers ranking countries on their cyber capabilities in a 2021 assessment. The report noted that Japan’s military had avoided offensive cybersecurity strategies in the past, in part because of the pacifist constitution the country enacted after World War II.
微軟的一份報告發現，2026年，日本是亞洲遭受網路威脅最頻繁的地區之一，僅次於台灣。倫敦智庫國際戰略研究所2021年對各國網路能力進行評估時，將日本列入三個等級中的最低一級。報告指出，日本軍方過去一直避免採取進攻性的網路安全戰略，部分原因是日本在第二次世界大戰後制定的和平憲法。
Artificial intelligence has also turbocharged the work of hackers, experts say. Automated systems can now scan websites for vulnerabilities around the world and around the clock.
專家表示，人工智慧還大大提高了駭客的攻擊效率。自動化系統現在可以全天候掃描全球網站，尋找安全漏洞。
Takahiro Anno, a technologist and founder of a political party focused on A.I., said while the Japanese language had once hindered international hackers, A.I.-generated translations have made phishing campaigns more persuasive.
技術專家、一個以人工智慧為重點的政黨的創始人安野貴博表示，過去日語曾是國際駭客發動攻擊的一道障礙，但人工智慧生成的翻譯讓網路釣魚攻擊更具迷惑性。
“Until recently — specifically up to about five years ago — Japan seemed relatively insulated from ransomware attacks and similar threats” he said in an interview. “It is very clear in emails or on phone calls when someone cannot speak Japanese fluently, so phishing campaigns were not that successful.”
「直到最近——具體說大約五年前——日本似乎相對不受勒索軟體攻擊和類似威脅影響，」他在採訪中說。「如果一個人日語說得不流利，無論是在電子郵件還是電話中，都很容易被察覺，因此網路釣魚攻擊的成功率並不高。」
Ko Fujii, a technology expert and the founder of Makaira, a public affairs agency, said the recent wave of cyberattacks was unlikely to be part of a coordinated, state-sponsored geopolitical attack, according to his agency’s preliminary analysis.
公共事務諮詢公司Makaira創始人、技術專家藤井宏一郎說，根據該機構初步分析，最近這波網路攻擊不太可能是一次由國家支持、經過協調的地緣政治攻擊行動。
“Japan suffered a high volume of breaches simply because its unpatched infrastructure yielded the highest return on investment” for hackers, he said.
他說：「日本之所以遭遇大量網路入侵，純粹是因為其基礎設施中的漏洞沒有得到修補，讓駭客能夠獲得最高的投入回報。」
廣告
It is unclear what threats the hackers had made, and how the companies have responded to any demands. The Japanese police said last week that the Russian ransomware group Qilin had been responsible for attacks on 53 Japanese companies over the past year and that a suspect had been extradited to Germany. It is unclear if Qilin was responsible for some of the recent cyber attacks.
目前尚不清楚駭客提出了哪些威脅，以及遭到攻擊的企業如何回應他們的要求。日本警方上週表示，俄羅斯勒索軟體組織「麒麟」在過去一年中對53家日本企業發動了攻擊，一名嫌疑人已被引渡至德國。目前尚不清楚「麒麟」是否參與了最近的部分網路攻擊。
How is the government responding?
政府如何應對？
The government has issued alerts with updated guidelines on handling personnel information, Mr. Furukawa said, including removing personal data that is no longer needed and requiring two-factor authentication.
古川表示，政府已發布警報，並更新了處理人事信息的指南，包括刪除不再需要的個人數據，並要求雙重認證。
The government was still investigating the causes of the attacks, including whether A.I. was used. “Progress in clarifying the details of these incidents has been slow,” he said.
政府仍在調查攻擊原因，包括是否使用了人工智慧。「查明這些事件細節的進展緩慢，」他說。
A new cyberdefense law took effect earlier in October that allows the national police and Japan’s military to monitor and even disable foreign servers and infrastructure to protect national security interests. The law also makes it mandatory for companies to report breaches.
一項新的網路防禦法規於10月初生效，它允許日本警察和軍方監控甚至禁用境外伺服器和相關基礎設施，以維護國家安全利益。該法律還強制企業必須報告網路安全遭入侵事件。
Professor Yoshioka, the systems security specialist, who advised the government as it developed the new law, called for international collaboration, as the infrastructure behind the attacks was unlikely to be confined to Japan.
系統安全專家吉岡教授曾在新法制定過程中為政府提供建議。他呼籲開展國際合作，因為攻擊背後的基礎設施不太可能僅限於日本境內。
廣告
Hans van der Heijden, a Melbourne-based digital forensics specialist at the global risks consultancy firm Control Risks, said that while Japan has made significant strides in its cybersecurity infrastructure, the process will take time and investment.
全球風險諮詢公司Control Risks駐墨爾本數字取證專家漢斯·范德海登說，雖然日本在網路安全基礎設施方面取得了顯著進展，但這一過程需要時間和投資。
“Some organizations continue to face challenges in patching vulnerabilities, replacing outdated technology, and implementing modern security controls,” Mr. van der Heijden wrote in an email.
范德海登在一封電子郵件中寫道：「一些機構在修補安全漏洞、更換過時技術以及實施現代化安全控制措施方面，仍然面臨挑戰。」
In the meantime, the cyberthreats will continue, he said.
他說，與此同時，網路安全威脅仍將持續存在。
