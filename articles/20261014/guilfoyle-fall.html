從川普「準兒媳」到被解職的美國駐希臘大使

‘Honey Please’: The Steep Rise and Sudden Fall of Kimberly Guilfoyle.

ELISABETH BUMILLER • 2026-10-14
It started out so well for her.
起初，她的一切都那麼順利。
Kimberly Guilfoyle, the U.S. ambassador to Greece until she was effectively fired on live television on Tuesday when the new White House press secretary said that President Trump had asked for her resignation, was at one point engaged to Donald Trump Jr., the president’s eldest son. The couple owned a $9.7 million home in Florida, appeared at Trump rallies together and sent each other adoring posts on Instagram.
金伯莉·吉爾福伊爾曾是美國駐希臘大使，直到週二，白宮新任新聞秘書在電視直播中宣布川普總統已要求她辭職，她實際上就這樣被公開解職。吉爾福伊爾曾與總統的長子小唐納德·川普訂婚。兩人在佛羅里達州擁有一棟價值970萬美元的豪宅，一起出席川普的競選集會，還經常在Instagram上向彼此公開示愛。
“Falling in love with my fiancé more and more every day,” Ms. Guilfoyle posted on Instagram on Valentine’s Day 2022. “@donaldjtrumpjr, you’re my best friend, my support system and my forever guy.”
「我每天都比前一天更加愛我的未婚夫，」吉爾福伊爾在2022年情人節的一則Instagram帖子中寫道。「@donaldjtrumpjr，你是我最好的朋友，是我的精神支柱，也是我想相伴一生的男人。」
“Thanks for always being there no matter what the haters are throwing our way,” he posted in 2024. “I love you.”
「謝謝你始終陪在我身邊，無論那些討厭我們的人怎樣攻擊我們，」小川普在2024年發帖寫道。「我愛你。」
廣告
Today, the junior Mr. Trump is married to a Florida socialite and model. And Ms. Guilfoyle, who was nominated as ambassador to Greece by the president soon after her engagement to his son was broken off two years ago, is out of a job after a series of damning reports in The Wall Street Journal, including that she asked a Republican donor to pay off $100,000 on her American Express credit card just before her Senate confirmation hearing in July 2025.
如今，小川普已經娶了一名來自佛羅里達州的名媛兼模特。吉爾福伊爾則在兩年前與他解除婚約後不久獲得川普總統提名，出任美國駐希臘大使，如今卻丟了工作。此前，《華爾街日報》接連發表了多篇對她不利的報導，其中一篇披露，2025年7月，就在參議院舉行她的大使任命確認聽證會之前，她曾要求一名共和黨捐款人替她償還美國運通信用卡上的10萬美元欠款。
The rise and fall of Ms. Guilfoyle, 57, was not a surprise at the White House, where many saw her as a flamboyant party girl with questionable judgment.
現年57歲的吉爾福伊爾經歷了從風光無限到黯然失勢的轉變，在白宮，很多人對此並不意外，他們認為她是一個作風張揚、熱衷派對、判斷力令人質疑的女人。
Her supporters said she was in fact a substantive person underneath the glitz. Ms. Guilfoyle, a former Fox News host and a former prosecutor in San Francisco and Los Angeles, was once married to Gavin Newsom, who would go on to become governor of California and one of the Democratic Party’s most outspoken critics of Mr. Trump.
她的支持者說，在浮華外表之下，她其實是個有內涵的人。吉爾福伊爾曾是福克斯新聞主持人，也曾在舊金山和洛杉磯擔任檢察官，還曾與加文·紐森有過一段婚姻。紐森後來成為加利福尼亞州州長，也是民主黨內最直言不諱的川普批評者之一。
Either way, Ms. Guilfoyle was a Trump insider with a prominent role in the family and its political operation. The president’s swiftness in firing her showed his willingness to cut off anyone who becomes a liability and an embarrassment. But it also left her with little reason to remain loyal to the president and his family at a time when Mr. Trump’s political authority is ebbing.
無論如何，吉爾福伊爾曾是川普圈內人，在這個家族及其政治運作中扮演著重要角色。總統如此迅速地將她解職，表明他願意毫不猶豫地與任何成為負擔、令他難堪的人切割。但與此同時，在川普政治權威日漸衰落之際，此舉也讓吉爾福伊爾幾乎沒有理由繼續效忠總統及其家族。
In announcing her resignation, she decried what she called false accusations and said she would vigorously defend her reputation. Speaking to reporters outside the White House, Mr. Trump did little to suggest he would continue to keep her close.
在宣布辭職時，她譴責了她所稱的不實指控，並表示將竭盡全力維護自己的聲譽。而川普在白宮外對記者發表講話時，幾乎沒有流露出繼續與她保持親近關係的意思。
“She wasn’t a member of my family,” he said. “She, I guess, dated my son a long time ago.”
「她又不是我的家人，」他說。「我猜，她很久以前跟我兒子交往過。」
廣告
Ms. Guilfoyle, who arrived in Greece less than a year ago, was a highly unusual choice for the role to begin with.
吉爾福伊爾女士不到一年前抵達希臘，而從一開始，她就不是這個職位的尋常人選。
In a sense, it was Don Jr. who got her the job, indirectly. In December 2024 he and the socialite and model, Bettina Anderson, were seen holding hands as they left a Palm Beach restaurant. The next day, President-elect Trump announced that Ms. Guilfoyle was his pick to be ambassador to Greece.
從某種意義上說，是小唐納德間接讓她得到了這份工作。2024年12月，有人看到他和社會名媛兼模特貝蒂娜·安德森手牽手離開棕櫚灘一家餐廳。次日，當選總統川普宣布，吉爾福伊爾是他駐希臘大使的人選。
The timing suggested the president wanted her out of the country at an awkward time for Don Jr., who married Ms. Anderson this year. Ms. Guilfoyle’s allies said that was not the case, although Greeks at the time wondered if they were simply getting the woman who had been jilted by the president’s son.
這一時機表明，總統想在小唐納德尷尬之際把她送出國外。小唐納德今年與安德森結婚。吉爾福伊爾的盟友否認了這種說法，但當時希臘人也不禁懷疑，美國是不是只不過把一個被總統兒子拋棄的女人派給了他們。
In Athens, Ms. Guilfoyle became a sensation as soon as she arrived, on the private jet of a multimillionaire Greek American businessman. She wore a partly sheer black evening dress to a Thanksgiving week dinner, joked with the Greek president when she presented her credentials that she was working on finding a new husband and stayed out until the small hours of the morning at Athens nightclubs.
在雅典，吉爾福伊爾乘坐一位希臘裔美國億萬富翁商人的私人飛機抵達後，立刻引起轟動。感恩節那一週，她穿著一襲局部透視的黑色晚禮服出席晚宴。在遞交國書時與希臘總統開玩笑說，她正在努力尋找新丈夫。她還經常流連雅典的夜總會，直到凌晨才離開。
“Oh, I wasn’t late, it was like before 3,” Ms. Guilfoyle said in an interview with The New York Times last year, referring to how she had turned up at a nightclub for the 1:30 a.m. premiere show of her friend, one of the country’s most famous pop singers, Konstantinos Argiros.
「哦，不算晚，大概不到3點，」吉爾福伊爾去年接受《紐約時報》採訪時說，指的是自己曾在凌晨1點半趕到一家夜總會，觀看好友、希臘最著名的流行歌手之一康斯坦丁諾斯·阿爾吉羅斯的首演。
Paparazzi followed her everywhere, but Greeks at the time said Ms. Guilfoyle worked as hard as she played. By the nature of her position and what they saw as her access to the White House, they viewed her as one of the most important people in the country. Mostly forgotten were her words on Fox during the Greek financial crisis in 2015, when she called Greeks “freeloaders” who should be punished like a dog that “pees on the rug.”
狗仔隊到處跟著她，但當時希臘人說，吉爾福伊爾玩得凶，工作也努力。由於她的職位性質以及他們認為她能接觸白宮，他們把她視為該國最重要的人物之一。人們大多忘了她在2015年希臘金融危機期間在福克斯新聞說過的話——當時她稱希臘人是「白吃白喝的人」，應該像「在地毯上撒尿」的狗一樣受到懲罰。
Early on, Ms. Guilfoyle announced two important business deals, the development of a U.S.-backed port near Athens and the exploration for natural gas off the island of Corfu. Both were in the works long before she arrived in Athens, but Greek officials said she had jumped into the plans months earlier and wanted them announced on her watch.
上任不久，吉爾福伊爾便宣布了兩項重要的商業協議：在雅典附近開發一個由美國支持的港口，以及在科孚島附近勘探天然氣。這兩個項目早在她抵達雅典之前就已開始籌劃，但希臘官員表示，她在幾個月前就已參與其中，並希望在自己的任期內宣布這些項目。
廣告
Adonis Georgiadis, Greece’s health minister and a vice president of the governing conservative party, was among those impressed. “When she was first named, the people were, ‘What’s that?’” he said. But if she delivers, he said, “who cares if she has a see-through dress?”
希臘衛生部長、執政的保守黨副主席阿多尼斯·喬治亞迪斯便是對她印象深刻的人士之一。「她剛獲得提名時，人們都在問：『這是什麼情況？』」他說。但他表示，只要她能幹出成績，「誰還在乎她是不是穿了一件透視裝？」
Her downfall began last month, after The Journal reported about Ms. Guilfoyle and her potential conflicts of interest over energy deals in the Balkans. But it accelerated with the report this month that she had secretly begged a Republican donor to pay her credit card debt while she was waiting to be confirmed as ambassador.
吉爾福伊爾的失勢始於上個月，當時《華爾街日報》報導了她與巴爾幹地區能源交易之間可能存在的利益衝突。但真正加速她垮台的是該報本月的一篇報導，這篇報導披露，在等待大使任命獲得確認期間，她曾私下懇求一名共和黨捐款人替她償還信用卡債務。
“Honey please I need you to get this done for me today,” she wrote in what appeared to be a text message to the donor, Eric Deters. Mr. Deters provided what appeared to be screenshots of their correspondence to The Times after the initial report by The Journal.
「親愛的，拜託，我需要你今天替我把這件事辦妥，」她在一則似乎是發給捐款人埃里克·迪特斯的短訊中寫道。在《華爾街日報》最初報導後，迪特斯向《紐約時報》提供了據稱是兩人通信記錄的截圖。
It is unclear what Ms. Guilfoyle will do now. Once a legal analyst for CNN and ABC in New York, her career at Fox ended in 2018, when two people familiar with her exit told The Times on the condition of anonymity that she had been forced to leave over questions about inappropriate workplace behavior.
目前尚不清楚吉爾福伊爾接下來會做什麼。她曾在紐約擔任美國有線電視新聞網和美國廣播公司的法律分析師。2018年，她在福克斯新聞的職業生涯告終。當時，兩名了解她離職內幕的人士以匿名為條件告訴《紐約時報》，她因涉嫌不當職場行為被迫離開。
The New Yorker reported two years later that Ms. Guilfoyle had been forced out because of sexual harassment allegations brought by a female assistant, including that she had showed the assistant photographs of genitalia of men with whom Ms. Guilfoyle had had sexual relations.
《紐約客》兩年後報導說，吉爾福伊爾因一名女助理提出性騷擾指控而被迫離職，其中包括她向該助理展示與她發生過性關係的男性的生殖器照片。
The magazine reported that the assistant had received a settlement of more than $4 million. Ms. Guilfoyle denied the allegations through her lawyer at the time, and declined to comment in the Times interview last year.
該雜誌報導說，這名助理獲得了超過400萬美元的和解金。吉爾福伊爾當時通過律師否認了這些指控，去年接受《紐約時報》採訪時拒絕就此置評。
廣告
She was immensely proud of her ambassadorship. At the end of the interview, she pointed out a framed document near the entrance to her official residence, Jefferson House, that listed all the American ambassadors to Greece since 1948. Every one until Ms. Guilfoyle was a man.
吉爾福伊爾對自己能夠出任大使感到無比自豪。採訪結束時，她指著大使官邸傑斐遜府入口附近的一份裝裱起來的文件，上面列出了自1948年以來歷任美國駐希臘大使的名字。在吉爾福伊爾之前，所有大使都是男性。
“Here I am,” she said, pointing to her name. “I almost cried.”
「看，我在這裡，」她指著自己的名字說。「我當時差點哭出來。」
