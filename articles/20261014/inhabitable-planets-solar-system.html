比地球更適宜居住的星球？確實存在

Worlds More Habitable Than Earth? They’re Out There.

NATALIE WOLCHOVER • 2026-10-14
A few years ago, in a lab in Berlin, the astrobiologist Iva Vilović was thrilled when she observed garden cress sprouting under a light designed to mimic a “K-dwarf” star — an orange-hued star that’s cooler and slower burning than our yellow sun. To her surprise, the cress grew even faster and taller than it did under simulated sunlight.
幾年前，在柏林一間實驗室裡，天體生物學家伊娃·維洛維奇觀察到花園水芹在模擬「K型矮星」光照的條件下發芽，興奮不已。K型矮星是一種橙色調恆星，比我們的黃色太陽溫度更低、燃燒更慢。令她意外的是，水芹的生長速度甚至比在模擬太陽光照下更快，植株也更高。
Her results suggested that photosynthesis, the process that supplies the energy for Earth’s entire food web, can happen on planets orbiting these orange stars. “K-dwarf light is a very, very interesting light source, and it should be considered, if not prioritized, for any type of search for extraterrestrial life, in my opinion,” said Dr. Vilović, now at the Leibniz Institute for Astrophysics in Potsdam, Germany.
她的結果表明，光合作用——為地球整個食物網提供能量的過程——可以在圍繞這些橙色恆星運行的行星上發生。「K型矮星光是一種非常非常有趣的光源，在我看來，任何類型的外星生命搜索中都應該考慮——甚至優先考慮——這個因素，」現就職於德國波茨坦的萊布尼茨天體物理研究所的維洛維奇說。
In particular, alien life may thrive in the light of these stars on planets that are slightly larger than Earth, known as super-Earths. Scientists like Dr. Vilović believe super-Earths that orbit K-dwarfs might, in fact, be the liveliest places in the galaxy — perhaps even more suitable for life than here.
尤其值得注意的是，在這些恆星的光照下，外星生命可能在比地球稍大的行星上繁盛生長，這類行星被稱為超級地球。像維洛維奇這樣的科學家認為，圍繞K型矮星運行的超級地球或許才是銀河系中最有生機的地方，甚至可能比地球更適合生命。
Scientists call these “superhabitable worlds,” planets that would allow carbon-based life to persist longer and become even more complex and diverse than on Earth.
科學家們將這些行星稱為「超級宜居世界」，即允許碳基生命比在地球上存續更久、變得更複雜和更多樣的行星。
廣告
A superhabitable world — as scientists imagine it — is about 20 to 30 percent wider and twice as massive as Earth, and is placed at the right distance from its star for water to flow on its surface. The stronger gravity of this bigger Earth would hold on tighter to ambient molecules than Earth does, giving the planet a thicker atmosphere and a warmer and more stable climate, fueling the growth of extraterrestrial plants and animals (or their equivalents). The super-Earth’s bigger iron core would erect a stronger magnetic field around it, better blocking cosmic radiation.
按科學家的想像，一個超級宜居世界比地球寬約20%至30%，質量是地球的兩倍，與恆星的距離恰到好處，使水能在其表面流動。這個更大的「地球」引力更強，比地球更緊地留住周圍分子，賦予該行星更厚的大氣層和更溫暖、更穩定的氣候，從而推動外星植物和動物（或它們的等同物）的生長。超級地球更大的鐵核會在其周圍形成更強的磁場，更好地阻擋宇宙輻射。
As for the superhabitable planet’s surface, picture paradise. An enlarged orb would be rounder and smoother than Earth, as its stronger gravity erodes continental shelves and fills in trenches. Water would spill evenly over its surface as shallow lakes and seas.
至於超級宜居行星的表面，想像一下天堂。一個更大的球體會比地球更圓、更平滑，因為其更強的引力侵蝕大陸架並填平海溝。水會以淺湖和淺海的形式均勻地漫溢在其表面。
On Earth, tropical islands and shallow seas are among the most biodiverse habitats. Now imagine a whole world of balmy archipelagos and reefs. The islands would burst with biological activity, their diverse topographies and geographic isolation fueling explosions of distinct species. Light would reach clear to the seafloor, powering abundant alien algae and plants to feed a booming marine ecosystem.
在地球上，熱帶島嶼和淺海是生物多樣性最豐富的棲息地之一。現在想像一個遍布溫暖群島和珊瑚礁的完整世界。這些島嶼會迸發出旺盛的生物活動，其多樣的地形和地理隔離推動著獨特物種的爆發。光線會一直到達海底，驅動豐富的異星藻類和植物，供養一個蓬勃的海洋生態系統。
As for that light, it might be the orange glow of the planet’s K-dwarf star.
至於那光，或許就是該行星的K型矮星發出的橙色輝光。
It was in the 2010s when astronomers began contemplating planets that are a little bigger, and a little nicer, than Earth, and where to find them. The whole idea was spurred in part by a question from John Armstrong a professor at Weber State University in Utah, who asked in the comments section of an astrobiology seminar on YouTube: “Shouldn’t there be planets more habitable than Earth?”
天文學家在2010年代開始思考比地球稍大一點、稍好一點的行星，以及在哪裡能找到它們。整個想法部分源於猶他州韋伯州立大學教授約翰·阿姆斯壯的一個問題。他在YouTube上一個天體生物學研討會的評論區問道：「難道不應該存在比地球更宜居的行星嗎？」
That got another astronomer, René Heller, who is now at the Max Planck Institute for Solar System Research in Germany, thinking. In the quest to discover chemical signs of life on distant worlds, he thought, astronomers have focused on finding “Earth twins,” rocky, watery, Earth-size planets orbiting sun-like stars. But when it comes to hosting life, Earth may not be the ideal archetype. After all, the fact that we’re not located at a special place in the cosmos (that we revolve around the sun, not that it revolves around us) is what the Copernican revolution was all about.
這引起了現供職於德國馬克斯·普朗克太陽系研究所的天文學家勒內·海勒的思考。他認為，在尋找遙遠世界生命化學跡象的過程中，天文學家一直專注於尋找「地球雙胞胎」，即圍繞類太陽恆星運行的岩石質、有水、地球大小的行星。但就承載生命而言，地球可能並非理想的範本。畢竟，我們並不位於宇宙中一個特殊位置（我們繞太陽轉，而不是太陽繞我們轉）——這正是哥白尼革命的全部意義所在。
廣告
“It occurred to me that currently, humanity is looking for Earthlike planets with the same anthropocentric bias,” Dr. Heller said. “Of course, Earth is special to us. But from a universal perspective, why should Earth be a reference?”
「我想到，目前人類正以同樣的人類中心主義偏見尋找類地行星，」海勒說。「當然，地球對我們來說是特殊的。但從宇宙視角來看，為什麼要拿地球當參照？」
In fact, Earth is only marginally habitable. Most astrobiologists think water must be present on a planet’s surface for life to arise there, and indeed the first bacteria appeared on Earth almost as soon as water pooled on its cooling surface roughly four billion years ago. But if Earth were just 2 percent closer to the sun, that pooling wouldn’t have happened. Instead, all of Earth’s water would have boiled away. We got lucky. But our luck will run out. The sun is expanding and brightening. Scientists calculate that in another half-billion years or so, Earth will be too hot to sustain complex life like plants and animals.
事實上，地球只是勉強宜居。大多數天體生物學家認為，行星表面必須有水存在才能孕育生命，而事實上，大約40億年前水在地球冷卻表面匯聚後不久，第一批細菌就出現了。但地球離太陽只需要再近2%，那種匯聚就不會發生。相反，地球所有的水都會沸騰蒸發。我們很幸運。但運氣終會用完。太陽正在膨脹和變亮。科學家計算，再過大約五億年，地球將熱得無法維持植物和動物這樣的複雜生命。
For Dr. Heller, the hunt for more habitable worlds is about “finding out if we are alone, if we could have been found, if there’s someone to talk to,” he said.
對海勒來說，尋找更宜居世界關乎「弄清我們是否孤獨，我們是否可能被找到，有沒有誰能跟我們交談」，他說。
“Although I am not, at least in my everyday life, a very spiritual person,” he added, “it’s nevertheless something that at least drives me — this question, Could there be someone?”
「儘管我——至少在日常生活中——不是一個非常注重靈性的人，」他補充道，「但這個問題仍然至少驅動著我——有沒有這樣的一種存在？」
He contacted Dr. Armstrong, and together they wrote a 2014 paper introducing the hypothesis of superhabitable worlds, which the two envisioned as archipelago-covered super-Earths. They were proposing a new target for telescopes.
他聯繫了阿姆斯壯，兩人在2014年共同發表了一篇論文，提出超級宜居世界的假說，將其設想為被群島覆蓋的超級地球。當時他們是在為望遠鏡提出一個新目標。
“Superhabitable worlds must be considered for future follow-up observations of signs of extraterrestrial life,” they wrote.
「超級宜居世界必須被考慮用於未來對外星生命跡象的後續觀測，」他們寫道。
廣告
In the paper, Dr. Heller and Dr. Armstrong reasoned that cool, petite K-dwarfs would be better host stars for superhabitable worlds than sun-like stars because they burn through their hydrogen fuel more slowly and brighten much more gradually. Our sun has only five billion years’ worth of hydrogen left before it will suddenly balloon and redden, becoming a red giant that will engulf the Earth. K-dwarfs, by contrast, shine steadily for tens of billions of years. This means life on a superhabitable planet orbiting a K-dwarf has far longer to evolve into ever more complex life forms.
在論文中，海勒和阿姆斯壯論證，涼爽小巧的K型矮星比類太陽恆星更適合作為超級宜居世界的宿主恆星，因為它們燃燒氫燃料更慢，變亮的過程也更慢。我們的太陽只剩五十億年的氫燃料，之後將突然膨脹變紅，成為一顆紅巨星，吞沒地球。相比之下，K型矮星能穩定閃耀數百億年。這意味著圍繞K型矮星的超級宜居行星上的生命，有更多的時間來演化成漸趨複雜的生命形式。
What kinds of life forms? We can only imagine. All we know is earthlings have grown increasingly complex and sophisticated over the past four billion years, culminating so far in human-level intelligence.
什麼樣的生命形式？我們只能想像。我們只知道，地球居民在過去40億年裡變得越來越複雜和精密，迄今為止達到了人類級別的智能。
For Dr. Vilović, the pursuit of superhabitable planets is, in part, “an idealistic dream of a world that has beautiful conditions that are not being influenced by humans in a negative way,” she said. She thinks it’s also about a “drive to find extremes” — the most extreme version of the kind of life we know and understand.
對維洛維奇來說，追求超級宜居行星部分是「一個理想主義的夢想，關於一個擁有美麗條件、不被人類以負面方式影響的世界」，她說。她認為這還關乎「尋找極端的驅動力」——我們所知、所理解的生命類型的最極端版本。
As for whether the dream is also a reality, Dr. Vilović feels good about the odds. “I like to believe that statistically speaking, in the realm of the universe, it is likely that life exists — especially on superhabitable planets,” she said.
至於這個夢想是否也是現實，維洛維奇覺得還是有機會的。「我樂於相信，從統計上講，在宇宙領域中，生命很可能存在——尤其是在超級宜居行星上，」她說。
