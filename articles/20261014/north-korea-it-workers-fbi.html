美國聯邦調查局搗毀涉朝鮮IT詐騙網路

F.B.I. Disrupts Pyongyang Revenue Stream: North Koreans in U.S. Tech Jobs

DUSTIN VOLZ • 2026-10-14
The F.B.I. last month disrupted a widespread network of American facilitators accused of aiding the North Korean government in securing remote information technology jobs that helped fund the reclusive country’s nuclear and ballistic weapons programs, U.S. officials said Tuesday.
美國官員週二表示，聯邦調查局上個月搗毀了一個由美國人組成的廣泛網路，這些人被指控幫助朝鮮政府獲取遠程信息技術工作崗位，從而為這個與世隔絕的國家的核武器和彈道導彈計劃提供資金。
The operation included the dismantling of more than 40 so-called laptop farms across Texas and the seizure of nearly 200 computer devices, the officials said, marking one of the largest efforts to date by U.S. authorities to block a key revenue stream that has helped North Korea evade harsh international sanctions and generate billions in cash in recent years.
官員們表示，此次行動包括在得克薩斯州各地搗毀了40多個所謂「筆記型電腦農場」，並查獲近200台計算機設備。這是美國當局迄今為止為阻斷這一關鍵收入來源所採取的最大規模行動之一。近年來，這一收入來源幫助朝鮮規避了嚴厲的國際制裁，並創造了數十億美元的現金。
The Justice Department separately unsealed indictments in Ohio on Tuesday against two U.S. nationals and 10 China-based North Koreans accused of committing identity theft to earn paychecks from unwitting American companies that hired them. Prosecutors said that scheme bilked 70 companies out of more than $3 million and ran from 2019 to 2025. The identities of two additional defendants remained under seal.
另外，美國司法部週二在俄亥俄州解封了針對兩名美國公民和10名駐華朝鮮人的起訴書，指控他們通過盜用身份信息，從不知情的美國僱主處騙取薪資。檢察官表示，該計劃從2019年持續到2025年，騙取了70家公司超過300萬美元。另外兩名被告的身份仍處於保密狀態。
The moves were the latest by the United States in an intensifying campaign to try to cut off a stealthy and evolving digital threat from North Korea whose scale is not entirely clear but likely vast. U.S. intelligence officials in both the Biden and second Trump administrations have said the I.T. worker scams are a primary method of funding Pyongyang’s weapons program, alongside thefts of cryptocurrency.
這些舉措是美國在不斷升級的行動中採取的最新手段，旨在試圖切斷來自朝鮮的隱秘且不斷演變的數字威脅，這種威脅的規模尚不完全清楚，但可能非常龐大。拜登政府和第二屆川普政府的美國情報官員都曾表示，IT人員詐騙與竊取加密貨幣一樣，是為平壤武器項目提供資金的主要方式。
廣告
North Korea is deploying “thousands of state-controlled I.T. workers to infiltrate companies in the United States and allied countries,” Brett Leatherman, the head of the F.B.I.’s cyber division, said in an interview. “Every one of these jobs is funding a stream for a nuclear and ballistic missiles program obtained with the stolen identity of an American and the trust of an American employer.”
「朝鮮正在部署數以千計受國家控制的IT人員，滲透美國及其盟國的企業，」聯邦調查局網路部門負責人布雷特·萊舒曼在接受採訪時表示。「他們利用竊取的美國人身份和美國僱主的信任獲得了這些工作，而每一份這樣的工作都在為核武器和彈道導彈項目提供資金來源。」
The bureau worked closely with the U.S.-based cybersecurity firm CrowdStrike to identify and disrupt the network, Mr. Leatherman said. He added that the coordinated actions were an early example of the F.B.I.’s new cyber strategy, which was unveiled last month and promised closer collaboration with the private sector to target hackers in ways that impede or thwart malicious activity. The strategy is part of a broader effort across the Trump administration to work more closely with American companies to strike hackers with cyberattacks, a pivot cautiously welcomed by some security experts.
萊舒曼說，聯邦調查局與總部位於美國的網路安全公司CrowdStrike密切合作，查明並搗毀了這個網路。他還說，這些協調行動是聯邦調查局新網路戰略的早期範例，該戰略於上個月公布，承諾與私營部門加強合作，以阻礙或挫敗惡意活動的方式打擊駭客。該戰略是川普政府更廣泛舉措的一部分，旨在與美國企業更緊密地合作，通過網路攻擊打擊駭客，一些安全專家對此轉變表示謹慎歡迎。
U.S. officials and cybersecurity analysts have said the North Korean I.T. worker scheme is among the most serious ongoing digital threats facing businesses today, and that it remains underappreciated as a risk. Adam Meyers, who oversees counteradversary operations at CrowdStrike, said the North Korean schemes to get hired for jobs have been “increasing both in prevalence and complexity.”
美國官員和網路安全分析師表示，朝鮮IT人員騙局是當今企業面臨的最嚴重的持續性數字威脅之一，但其風險仍被低估。負責CrowdStrike反制對手行動的亞當·邁耶斯表示，朝鮮獲取僱傭的騙局「在普遍性和複雜性方面都在不斷增加」。
It is difficult to know how widespread laptop farms are in the United States, Mr. Meyers said, but noted that CrowdStrike identified an average of two new cases per day throughout the month of September — which is likely only the tip of the iceberg.
邁耶斯說，目前尚難以確定筆記型電腦農場在美國的普及程度，但他指出，CrowdStrike在整個9月份平均每天發現兩起新案例——而這可能只是冰山一角。
For more than two years, U.S. intelligence officials and cybersecurity researchers have warned that North Korea has been using stolen American identities — often acquired from compromised private information, like Social Security numbers, on the dark web — and artificial intelligence tools to get hired for remote information technology jobs at companies across the United States, Europe and elsewhere. Experts have said companies ranging from small start-ups to Fortune 500 firms have been victimized.
兩年多來，美國情報官員和網路安全研究人員一直警告稱，朝鮮一直在利用竊取的美國人身份——通常是從暗網上洩漏的隱私信息中獲取（如社會安全號碼）——以及人工智慧工具，在美國、歐洲及其他地方的公司獲得遠程信息技術工作。專家表示，從小微初創企業到財富500強公司，各類企業都已成為受害者。
A crucial aspect of the fraud campaign is support from people in the United States who are recruited by North Korea to help bypass corporate security measures that detect fraudulent hires, according to investigators. Facilitators help enable the fraud by receiving and setting up work laptops at addresses that appear like legitimate residences to corporate human-resources teams. North Korean operators then use software to remotely tunnel into those laptops and work on them, posing as Americans to the companies that hired them.
調查人員表示，這場欺詐活動的一個關鍵環節是來自美國境內人員的協助，他們被朝鮮招募，幫助繞過企業用於檢測欺詐性僱傭的安全措施。協助者通過在看似合法住所的地址接收並設置工作筆記型電腦，從而為欺詐行為提供便利，使企業人力資源團隊誤以為這些地址是合法住所。然後，朝鮮操作員使用軟體遠程登錄這些筆記型電腦並在其上工作，向僱傭他們的公司冒充美國人。
廣告
The appeal for facilitators is simple: jobs that look like easy cash and, at least at first, might not require much of them. “They get kind of hooked on it initially, and then more gets asked of them, and they’re kind of in too deep at that point,” Mr. Meyers said.
對協助者來說，誘惑很簡單：這些工作看似能輕鬆賺錢，而且至少在最初可能不需要他們做太多事情。「他們一開始有點上癮，然後被要求做得更多，到那個時候，他們就已經陷得太深了，」邁耶斯說。
Mr. Leatherman said that the Texas sweep did not include any arrests of facilitators, but that authorities warned them they could face criminal penalties should they continue to assist with remote fraud. The decision stemmed in part from a desire to move quickly to upend the network rather than wait for prosecutors to file charges, he said.
萊舒曼說，得克薩斯州的掃蕩行動中並沒有逮捕任何協助者，但當局警告他們，如果繼續協助遠程欺詐，可能會面臨刑事處罰。他說，這一決定部分源於希望迅速採取行動瓦解該網路，而不是等待檢察官提起指控。
Mr. Leatherman said the laptop farms often operate like a “mini data center” for the North Koreans, where one facilitator hosts dozens or even hundreds of laptops used to fill scores of jobs. A woman in Arizona sentenced last year to more than eight years in prison was accused of hosting dozens of laptops at her home to help North Koreans connect to more than 300 U.S. companies.
萊舒曼表示，這些筆記型電腦農場的運作模式往往就像一個朝鮮人的「迷你數據中心」， 一名協助者會託管數十台甚至數百台筆記型電腦，用於處理大量工作任務。去年一名女子在亞利桑那州被判處八年多監禁，她被控在其家中託管數十台筆記型電腦，幫助朝鮮人連接到300多家美國公司。
The tactics of North Korea’s I.T. worker army have steadily evolved to evade detection. That includes increased reliance on artificial intelligence to create fake résumés, videos and photos, and to navigate virtual job interviews, officials said. The fraudsters are also relying more on subcontractors in places like Pakistan and Nigeria to further obscure North Korean involvement, Mr. Leatherman said.
為逃避偵查，朝鮮IT工作者大軍的戰術在不斷演變。官員們表示，這包括越來越多地依賴人工智慧來偽造簡歷、影片和照片，以及應對虛擬求職面試。萊舒曼說，欺詐者還日益依賴巴基斯坦和奈及利亞等地的分包商，以此進一步掩蓋朝鮮的參與。
In an advisory released on Tuesday, the F.B.I. said the North Koreans were paying Americans and other Western accomplices to “perform a growing variety of identity and location obfuscation tasks” that included appearing on camera, participating in job interviews or meetings, verifying fraudulent identities, shipping packages, and even completing urinalysis tests or fingerprint submissions.
在週二發布的一份建議中，聯邦調查局表示，朝鮮正在付錢給美國人和其他西方同謀，讓他們「執行日益多樣化的身份和位置混淆任務」，其中包括出境、參加求職面試或會議、驗證虛假身份、寄送包裹，甚至完成尿檢或指紋採集。
