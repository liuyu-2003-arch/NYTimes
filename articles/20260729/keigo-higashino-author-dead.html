暢銷推理小說作家東野圭吾去世，終年68歲

Keigo Higashino, Best-Selling Japanese Mystery Writer, Dies at 68

FRANCESCA REGALADO, HISAKO UENO • 2026-07-29
Keigo Higashino, a best-selling author of mystery novels who published more than 100 books in a prolific career, many of which inspired screen adaptations in his native Japan and beyond, died on Thursday. He was 68.
暢銷推理小說作家東野圭吾於週四去世，終年68歲。他的職業生涯著述頗豐，出版了100多本作品，其中許多作品在他的祖國日本及海外被改編為影視作品。
His longtime publisher in Japan, Kodansha, said in a statement on Monday that the cause was colorectal cancer.
他在日本多年的出版商講談社週一發表聲明稱，東野圭吾的死因為結腸癌。
Mr. Higashino was the first Japanese author to be nominated for an Edgar Award in the United States and a Dagger Award in Britain, two of the most prestigious prizes in mystery and crime writing.
東野圭吾是首位同時獲得美國愛倫·坡獎和英國匕首獎提名的日本作家，這兩個獎項都是懸疑與犯罪寫作領域最負盛名的獎項。
His English-language breakthrough was a translation of his 2005 novel, “The Devotion of Suspect X.” Time magazine named it one of the 100 best mystery and thriller books of all time, and it was a finalist for the Edgar Award for Best Novel in 2012. The novel has been adapted into films in Japan, China, South Korea and India.
他真正打開英語世界市場的作品是2005年的小說《嫌疑人X的獻身》的英譯本。《時代》雜誌將其評為有史以來百大最佳懸疑與驚悚小說之一；該書還入圍了2012年愛倫·坡獎最佳長篇小說獎的決選名單。小說後來被日本、中國、韓國和印度改編成電影。
廣告
Mr. Higashino wrote 106 books that have sold about 109 million copies in Japan, in addition to several co-written works. His books have been published in 41 countries and territories, according to Kodansha, and translated into several languages including English, Chinese, Korean and French.
東野圭吾共創作了106本書，在日本銷量約1.09億冊，他還與人合著了數本書。據講談社稱，他的作品已在41個國家和地區出版，並被翻譯成英語、中文、韓語、法語等多種語言。
His characters were often regular people such as schoolteachers, young children and pawnbrokers, who perpetrated or suffered acts of violence hidden by their mundane lives. His 2009 novel, “Newcomer,” which revolved around the murder of a middle-aged woman who lived alone in Tokyo and seemed to have no enemies, was nominated for a Dagger Award in 2019.
他筆下的角色往往都是普通人，如學校教師、小孩子、當鋪老闆等，他們要麼犯下暴力罪行，要麼成為暴力的受害者，而這一切都隱藏在平凡的日常生活之下。其2009年小說《新參者》圍繞一名看似沒有仇敵的東京獨居中年女性被害案展開，該作品於2019年獲得匕首獎提名。
“I always have a defense attorney, a prosecutor and a judge within me, and perhaps even a defendant,” Mr. Higashino said about his detached writing style, which never allowed any single character to dominate the narrative, in a 2006 interview with the Mainichi Shimbun, a Japanese newspaper. “This mind-set prevents me from writing solely based on one side’s perspective.”
「我內心總是有一位辯護律師、一位檢察官和一位法官，或許甚至還有一位被告，」在2006年接受日本《每日新聞》採訪時，東野圭吾談到自己冷靜克制的寫作風格，這種風格從不允許任何一個角色主導整部作品的敘事。「正是這種心態讓我不會僅憑某一方的視角來寫作。」
One of the earliest international film adaptations of his works was “The Secret,” a 2007 English-language French production starring David Duchovny. It was a remake of a 1999 Japanese movie based on Mr. Higashino’s novel of the same name.
他首個被外國改編成電影的作品之一是2007年大衛·杜楚尼主演的《祕密》。這部法國製作的英語對白影片翻拍自1999年根據東野圭吾同名小說改編的日本電影。
His final novel, “Eternal Memory,” is set to be published on Aug. 5 by the Tokyo-based publishing house Bungeishunju, according to Kodansha’s statement.
據講談社的聲明，他的最後一部小說《永恆的記憶》定於8月5日由東京的文藝春秋出版社出版。
Mr. Higashino was born on Feb. 4, 1958, in Osaka, Japan. His family was not wealthy and he grew up wearing clothes handed down from his two older sisters, according to a biography provided by the publisher Vertical Inc..
東野圭吾於1958年2月4日出生於日本大阪。根據出版社Vertical Inc.提供的傳記，他家境並不富裕，他從小穿兩位姐姐的舊衣服長大。
廣告
He disliked books as a child, he said in 2006 when he won Japan’s Naoki Prize, an award for popular fiction.
他在2006年獲得日本大眾文學獎直木獎時表示，自己小時候並不喜歡讀書。
“What I always think about is wanting to write novels that even my book-hating self from back then would enjoy reading,” Mr. Higashino said at the time.
「我一直想著，要寫出連當年那個討厭讀書的自己都會覺得好看的小說，」東野圭吾當時說。
He graduated with a degree in electrical engineering from Osaka Prefecture University, which is now Osaka Metropolitan University. Soon afterward, he began working for Denso, a Japanese manufacturer of automotive parts, but continued writing in the evenings and on weekends, he told The Wall Street Journal in 2011.
他畢業於大阪府立大學（現為大阪公立大學）電氣工程專業。他在2011年接受《華爾街日報》採訪時回憶道，畢業後不久，他進入日本汽車零部件製造商電裝公司工作，同時利用晚上和週末堅持寫作。
He decided to become a full-time writer in 1985 when his debut novel, “After School,” won the Edogawa Rampo Prize, an award for unpublished mystery novels presented by the Mystery Writers of Japan. The novel, about a string of violent attacks at a girls’ high school, was inspired by his experiences at university and his former wife’s work as a teacher.
1985年，他決定成為全職作家，當時他的處女作《放學後》獲得了江戶川亂步獎——該獎項由日本推理作家協會頒發給未出版的推理小說。這部小說講述一所女子高中發生的一系列暴力襲擊事件，靈感源自他的大學經歷以及前妻的教師工作。
Mr. Higashino and his wife divorced in 1997. Information about his survivors was not immediately available.
東野圭吾在1997年與妻子離婚。關於其遺屬的信息，目前尚未公布。
In 2023, the Japanese government awarded Mr. Higashino the Medal with Purple Ribbon, an honor for individuals who have contributed to art and academia.
2023年，日本政府授予東野圭吾紫綬褒章，該獎授予在藝術和學術領域做出傑出貢獻的人士。
廣告
When he received the medal, he said he still aimed to entertain his readers and to encourage them to read.
獲獎時，他表示，自己的目標依然是娛樂讀者，並讓更多人愛上閱讀。
“The feeling is similar to climbing a mountain,” he said. “I believe that if I just keep moving forward, I will eventually reach the top.”
「這種感覺就像在登山，」他說。「我相信只要一直向前走，終究會登頂。」

Francesca Regalado是《紐約時報》記者，報導突發新聞。

Hisako Ueno是常駐東京的記者/研究員，撰寫日本政治、商業、勞工、性別及文化相關議題。
