科技股暴跌，市場對AI支出擔憂加劇

Tech Stocks Tumble on Worries About A.I. Spending and China’s Chip Competition

RIVER AKIRA DAVIS • 2026-07-29
Technology stocks tumbled on Tuesday, dragged down by fears of the hefty bills for the build-out of artificial intelligence systems and concerns over intensifying competition from China in memory chips — a critical component of A.I. long dominated by South Korea and its peers.
週二，科技股大幅下挫，拖累因素包括市場擔憂建設人工智慧系統所需投入極其龐大，以及對來自中國在存儲晶片領域競爭加劇的憂慮。存儲晶片是人工智慧的關鍵組件，長期以來由韓國等國主導。
South Korea’s benchmark Kospi index plunged more than 10 percent on Tuesday, at one point triggering a temporary trading halt. Benchmark indexes in Japan and Taiwan both fell around 4 percent. Stocks in China fell over 2 percent.
韓國基準股指Kospi週二暴跌逾10%，一度觸發臨時停牌。日本和台灣的基準指數均下跌約4%。中國股市跌幅超過2%。
Futures for the tech-heavy Nasdaq fell about 1 percent. The American chip company Micron was down 6 percent in premarket trading. Nvidia, the leading chip maker, was also lower, as was SpaceX, Elon Musk’s rocket and A.I. company. In Europe, the German chipmaker Infineon fell 4 percent and the Dutch semiconductor equipment maker ASML shed 3 percent.
以科技股為主的納斯達克指數期貨下跌約1%。美國晶片公司美光盤前跌6%，晶片龍頭英偉達同樣走低，伊隆·馬斯克旗下的火箭和人工智慧公司SpaceX也出現下跌。歐洲方面，德國晶片製造商英飛凌下跌4%，荷蘭半導體設備製造商ASML跌3%。
The latest A.I.-related market tremor began on Monday, when the shares of China’s leading memory chipmaker, ChangXin Memory Technologies, began trading after a blockbuster initial public offering. The company’s stock soared nearly 500 percent on its market debut, quickly making it the most valuable company on the Shanghai exchange. It slipped 4 percent on Tuesday.
最新一輪與人工智慧相關的市場震盪始於週一。當天，中國領先的存儲晶片製造商長鑫科技在完成轟動性的首次公開募股後開始交易。該公司股價上市首日飆升近500%，迅速成為上海證券交易所市值最高的公司。週二股價回落4%。
廣告
The listing prompted widespread fears that intensifying industry competition could threaten rival global memory chipmakers in South Korea, Japan, Taiwan and the United States.
此次上市引發了廣泛擔憂，即行業競爭加劇可能威脅到韓國、日本、台灣及美國的全球存儲晶片製造競爭對手。
The declines in Asia began as soon as stocks started trading on Tuesday, with A.I. and semiconductor stocks the hardest hit. Japanese flash memory maker Kioxia fell more than 18 percent. South Korean semiconductor company Samsung Electronics dropped about 13 percent.
週二亞洲股市開盤即跌，人工智慧和半導體板塊跌幅最大。日本閃存製造商鎧俠下跌逾18%，韓國半導體公司三星電子下跌約13%。
Asian markets have been trending downward since late June, weighed down by recurring concerns over the sustainability of this year’s A.I. rally.
自6月底以來，亞洲股市整體一直處於下行趨勢，市場持續擔憂今年這輪人工智慧行情是否具有可持續性。
The staggering scale of spending required to keep up in the global A.I. race was highlighted this week by OpenAI, the parent of ChatGPT, nearing a deal to lease a $500 billion data center in Ohio, supported by $250 billion in financial backing from Nvidia. Last week, earnings reports by Tesla and Alphabet, Google’s parent company, shook the markets as they revealed their own enormous A.I. spending needs.
本週，ChatGPT母公司OpenAI接近達成一項租用俄亥俄州價值5000億美元數據中心的協議，並獲英偉達2500億美元資金支持，凸顯了全球人工智慧競賽中保持領先所需的驚人支出規模。上週，特斯拉和谷歌母公司「字母表」的財報因披露其龐大的人工智慧支出需求而引發市場震動。
On Monday, the Chinese start-up Moonshot publicly released the details of its latest A.I. model, adding to investor concerns about an A.I. arms race between China and the United States.
週一，中國初創公司月之暗面發布了其最新人工智慧模型的技術細節，加劇了投資者對中美人工智慧競賽的擔憂。
The latest market turmoil is an unwelcome turn for a region where the A.I. boom has served as a rare economic bright spot. In markets like South Korea and Taiwan, rising chip exports and soaring A.I. valuations have masked broader weakness across their domestic economies, which remain heavily dependent on energy imports from the Middle East.
對於亞洲而言，最新這輪市場動盪無疑來得不是時候。人工智慧熱潮一直是該地區少數的經濟亮點之一。在韓國、台灣等市場，晶片出口增長和人工智慧概念股估值飆升掩蓋了經濟整體依然疲弱的現實，而這些經濟體仍高度依賴來自中東的能源進口。
廣告
The war in Iran, another major worry for investors, has rattled markets recently by restricting oil exports from the Persian Gulf and Red Sea, pushing up oil and gas prices. But President Trump said on Monday that there was “a good chance” that a new round of diplomatic talks could yield a breakthrough in the monthslong war.
伊朗戰爭是投資者的另一大隱憂，近期波斯灣和紅海的石油出口受限推高了油氣價格，從而擾動市場。但川普總統週一表示，這場持續數月的戰爭「很有可能」通過新一輪外交談判取得突破。
A recent pause in fighting has helped ease oil prices, with Brent crude, the international benchmark, falling to around $84 a barrel on Tuesday. Still, the cost of crude is up more than 15 percent since the start of the war, feeding into higher prices for gasoline and a wide range of products derived from petroleum.
近期戰事暫停有助於緩解油價，國際基準布倫特原油週二跌至每桶約84美元。不過，自戰爭爆發以來，原油價格已上漲逾15%，推高了汽油及多種石油衍生產品的價格。
And anxiety about the prospects for tech stocks will be tested again soon, with Microsoft and Meta scheduled to report earnings Wednesday, alongside South Korea semiconductor giant SK Hynix. On Thursday, Apple and Amazon report earnings, as does Samsung Electronics.
對科技股前景的擔憂將很快再次面臨檢驗——微軟、Meta以及韓國半導體巨頭SK海力士定於週三發布財報。週四，蘋果、亞馬遜和三星電子將公布業績。
