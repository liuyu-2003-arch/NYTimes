熊本發生6.8級強震，購物中心坍塌或有人員被困

What to Know About the Kumamoto Earthquake in Japan

JOHNNY DIAZ • 2026-07-29
A powerful 6.8 magnitude earthquake struck southwestern Japan on Tuesday afternoon, damaging buildings and homes, igniting fires and injuring dozens, prompting a search for people who may have been trapped in rubble.
日本西南部週二下午發生6.8級強烈地震，造成多棟建築物和住宅受損，並引發火災，還導致數十人受傷。當局已展開搜救行動，尋找可能被困在廢墟中的人員。
Here’s what to know.
以下是目前已知情況。
Where did the earthquake happen?
地震發生在哪裡？
The earthquake hit around 4:30 p.m. local time in Kumamoto Prefecture on Kyushu island, the southernmost of Japan’s main islands.
地震發生於當地時間下午4時30分左右，震中位於日本九州島的熊本縣。九州是日本主要島嶼中最南端的一個。
廣告
The earthquake’s intensity led to buildings collapsing in the region. Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi of Japan said people were trapped inside some structures. Fires also erupted in Kumamoto City, the regional capital, which has a population of more than 700,000.
地震造成當地多棟建築倒塌。日本首相高市早苗表示，一些建築物內有人被困。人口超過70萬的熊本縣首府熊本市還發生了多起火災。
The epicenter of the quake was near Uto, a smaller coastal city around 30 miles south of Kumamoto City, according to the U.S. Geological Survey
據美國地質調查局數據，震中位於熊本市以南約48公里的沿海小城宇土市附近。
The U.S. agency had earlier reported a magnitude measurement of 7.1 for the earthquake before it adjusted the figure to 6.8.
該機構此前報告地震震級為7.1級，隨後修正為6.8級。
Where was the most severe damage?
受災最嚴重的是哪裡？
The second floor of a shopping mall in southern Kumamoto City collapsed, injuring two people. Crews were working to rescue people who may have been trapped inside. Video footage posted on social media showed debris scattered inside and outside the mall.
熊本市南部一家購物中心二樓發生坍塌，造成兩人受傷。救援人員正在搜救可能被困在內部的人員。社群媒體上的影片顯示，商場內外散落著大量瓦礫。
Aerial footage showed a flipped train carriage on tracks in the city and widespread damage to buildings and bridges. Other images recorded by the public broadcaster NHK also captured elevated sections of the Kyushu Expressway that had split into two pieces, as other footage showed a wall that had partly collapsed at Kumamoto Castle, a landmark in the city’s center.
航拍畫面顯示，市內一節列車車廂在軌道上發生側翻，多棟建築和橋樑受損。公共廣播機構日本廣播協會(NHK)拍攝的畫面還顯示，九州高速公路部分高架路段斷裂成兩截；還有畫面顯示，位於熊本市中心的地標性建築——熊本城有城牆局部坍塌。
The prime minister said the authorities were still assessing the severity of the damage to buildings and bridges.
日本首相表示，當局仍在評估建築和橋樑的受損嚴重程度。
Was anyone killed or injured?
是否有人傷亡？
At least one person died in a hospital after being found in a collapsed house in Kumamoto, according to the police.
據警方消息，在熊本，一名在倒塌房屋中發現的人員送醫後死亡。
At least 50 people were being treated for injuries at a hospital in Hikawa, a town to the south of Kumamoto City, according to NHK.
據NHK報導，位於熊本市以南的冰川町一家醫院已收治至少50名傷者。
廣告
The last major quake in Japan in 2024, centered on the Noto peninsula farther north, killed hundreds of people, with more succumbing to disaster-linked causes afterward, according to NHK.
據NHK報導，日本上一次重大地震發生於2024年，震中位於更北部的能登半島，那次地震造成數百人死亡，之後又有不少人因災害相關原因不幸去世。
In April 2016, the Kumamoto region also experienced days of deadly seismic events that killed at least nine and injured hundreds, according to the U.S. Geological Survey.
2016年4月，熊本地區也曾連續發生多次致命地震。據美國地質調查局資料，當時至少九人死亡、數百人受傷。
What is the status of the area’s nuclear power plant?
當地核電站情況如何？
The Sendai Nuclear Power Plant appeared to be secure and had no reports of abnormalities, Minoru Kihara, a top Japanese cabinet official, said during a news briefing.
日本內閣官房長官木原稔在新聞發布會上表示，川內核電站目前運行正常，未發現任何異常情況。
The power plant, which has two reactors and supplies electricity to the Kyushu region, is in Kagoshima, south of Kumamoto.
該核電站位於熊本以南的鹿兒島縣，它擁有兩座反應堆，為九州地區供電。
