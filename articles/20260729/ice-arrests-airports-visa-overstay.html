美國加強打擊簽證逾期者

ICE Arrests Surge at Airports, Opening New Front in Deportation Drive

HAMED ALEAZIZ, MIRIAM JORDAN • 2026-07-29
The Trump administration has begun arresting foreign citizens with expired U.S. visas as they travel through airports, including spouses of Americans, according to documents obtained by The New York Times and interviews with immigration lawyers — a tactic that opens up a vast new pool of people for deportation.
根據《紐約時報》獲得的文件及對移民律師的採訪，川普政府已開始在機場逮捕持過期美國簽證的外國公民，其中包括美國公民的配偶——這一策略大幅擴大了可被驅逐出境的人群範圍。
Immigration agents in plain clothes have whisked away targets at check-in counters and arrival gates, with enforcement actions in at least 15 airports in recent weeks. Some of the arrests have occurred quietly, while others have happened as angry fellow passengers filmed the encounters.
近幾週來，身著便衣的移民執法人員已在至少15座機場採取行動，在值機櫃檯和到達出口將目標人員帶走。有些逮捕行動悄無聲息地進行，另一些則被憤怒的其他乘客拍下現場畫面。
The recent arrests appear to be an expansion of an arrangement between the Transportation Security Administration and Immigration and Customs Enforcement. The Times reported in December that ICE officers were arresting people with deportation orders at airports with the help of information provided by airlines to the T.S.A.
近期的逮捕行動似乎是運輸安全管理局(TSA)和移民與海關執法局(ICE)合作安排的擴展版本。《紐約時報》去年12月曾報導，ICE利用航空公司向TSA提供的信息，在機場逮捕已收到驅逐令的人員。
Now, through a review of Homeland Security Department documents and interviews with lawyers for more than 25 people from more than a dozen countries recently detained at airports, The Times has learned that the program apparently now includes people with expired visas — a much larger group.
現在，通過查閱國土安全部文件，以及對近期在機場被拘留的、來自十多個國家的25名人士的律師進行採訪，《紐約時報》了解到，該計劃顯然已將持過期簽證者納入目標——這是一個規模大得多的群體。
廣告
Hundreds of thousands of immigrants and visitors overstay visas each year. Many with expired visas occupy a legal gray area, as they are awaiting visa extensions or green cards and have been issued work permits. Such individuals were not prioritized for deportation in the past unless they had committed crimes, and they were rarely detained while waiting for visa extensions or green cards. But that has changed under President Trump, whose administration has undertaken a campaign of mass deportation and views all visa overstays as illegal.
每年有數十萬移民和訪客逾期滯留。許多持過期簽證者處於法律灰色地帶：他們正在等待簽證延期或綠卡，並已獲得工作許可。過去，此類人員除非有犯罪記錄，否則不屬於遣返優先對象，且在等待簽證延期或綠卡期間極少遭到拘押。但在川普總統任內，情況發生變化，政府開展大規模驅逐行動，將所有簽證逾期者視為非法居留。
“This administration is working diligently to ensure that aliens in our country illegally can no longer fly unless it is out of our country to self-deport,” a spokesperson for the Homeland Security Department said in a statement. The agency did not confirm the expansion of the program.
國土安全部發言人在一份聲明中表示：「本屆政府正全力確保非法滯留我國的外國人今後不能再乘坐飛機，除非他們乘坐的是離開美國、自行遣返的航班。」不過，該部並未證實這項計劃已經擴大。
The department, which oversees ICE, has not disclosed how many people have been detained at airports as overstays under the new initiative. The arrests come during a period in which the White House has pushed ICE for more arrests across the country, and Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin has pledged to turn “up the heat on the streets.” To that end, the administration has set a target of 2,000 immigration arrests a day, roughly double the pace from earlier this year.
負責監管ICE的國土安全部沒有透露根據這項新舉措在機場拘留了多少逾期居留者。這些逮捕發生之際，白宮正要求ICE在全國加大執法力度，國土安全部長馬克韋恩·馬林也承諾要進一步「加大街頭執法力度」。為此，政府已將每日移民逮捕目標設定為2000人，約為今年早些時候的兩倍。
Interviews with more than a dozen immigration attorneys, advocacy organizations and an airline employee reveal a broad cross-section of targets: an engineer awaiting a work-visa extension, several newlyweds married to U.S. citizens, and a former au pair.
對十多名移民律師、倡導組織及一名航空公司員工的採訪顯示，目標群體十分廣泛：一名等待工作簽證延期的工程師、數名與美國公民新婚的人士，以及一名前互惠生。
Footage of encounters between travelers and ICE officers have flooded social media. In one widely circulated video, Chantal Morales Rojas, a 27-year-old from Ecuador, was detained by plainclothes officers as she boarded a Southwest Airlines flight from Denver to Oakland, Calif., on July 20. She had been spending the weekend with the family for whom she had worked as an au pair, according to the family.
ICE執法人員與旅客之間的衝突影片在社群媒體上大量流傳。在一段廣為傳播的影片中，7月20日，27歲的厄瓜多女子尚塔爾·莫拉萊斯·羅哈斯在從丹佛飛往加州奧克蘭，她在登上西南航空公司的航班時被便衣特工攔下帶走。據她曾擔任互惠生的那戶人家稱，事發時她正和他們一起過週末。
When Ms. Morales Rojas scanned her boarding pass, an alarm sounded and the gate agent asked her to wait. Moments later, two plainclothes agents intercepted her in the jetway.
當莫拉萊斯·羅哈斯掃描登機牌時，警報驟然響起，登機口工作人員要求她稍等。幾分鐘後，兩名便衣執法人員在廊橋處將她截住。
廣告
“They said they were going to be respectful, that they would be polite,” Alicia Dantzker, a friend of the family, who was on the same flight and filmed part of the encounter, told The Times in an interview. The video captures the men encircling Ms. Morales Rojas, and then ushering her through a side door and down steps to the tarmac as Ms. Dantzker screams for help.
「他們說會尊重她，會禮貌對待她。」這戶人家的朋友、當時也在同一航班上的艾麗西亞·丹茨克在採訪中告訴《紐約時報》。她拍攝了部分過程，影片顯示，兩名男子將莫拉萊斯·羅哈斯圍住，隨後帶著她穿過一道側門，沿樓梯走向停機坪，丹茨克則不斷高喊求救。
Also among those detained was a Ugandan woman in a wheelchair who has sickle cell anemia and an active asylum application, according to her lawyer.
另一名被拘留者是一名坐輪椅的烏干達女性，她患有鐮狀細胞貧血症，且有一份正在處理中的庇護申請。她的律師證實了這一情況。
In another case, ICE attempted to arrest an Australian citizen who had overstayed his visa at the Las Vegas airport, according to the Department of Homeland Security. The scene was recorded by a bystander whose video of the encounter drew wide attention. Officers let him go “to de-escalate the situation and for officer safety,” but ICE ended up taking him into custody a day later when he tried to board a flight in Los Angeles,the department said in a statement.
另一起案例中，國土安全部表示，移民執法局曾試圖在拉斯維加斯機場逮捕一名簽證過期的澳洲公民。一名旁觀者拍攝了事發經過，這段影片引起了廣泛關注。國土安全部在聲明中表示，為「緩和局勢、確保執法人員安全」，暫時將其釋放，但第二天當他在洛杉磯試圖登機時，ICE最終將其拘留。
“In 38 years practicing immigration law, I had never seen this,” said Charles Kuck, who is based in Atlanta and is representing an Indian engineer who was detained while awaiting a work-permit extension. “And I know it’s happening to lots of people.”
「從事移民法律執業38年來，我從未見過這種情況，」亞特蘭大的移民律師查爾斯·庫克表示，他正代理一名在等待工作許可延期時被拘留的印度工程師。「而且我知道這正發生在很多人身上。」
Shannon Shepherd, an immigration lawyer who is vice chair for the Chicago chapter of the American Immigration Lawyers Association, said she and other lawyers were revising longstanding advice about domestic air travel.
美國移民律師協會芝加哥分會副主席、移民律師香農·謝潑德表示，她和同行都正在修改長期以來關於國內乘機出行的建議。
“Before, I was saying, ‘As long as you have an ID, you can travel domestically,’” Ms. Shepherd said. “Now I am turning around and saying, ‘If you are in the process of changing status, avoid any travel.’”
「以前，我都會說：『只要有身份證件，就可以乘坐美國國內航班，』」謝潑德說，「現在我卻要反過來說：『如果你正在辦理身份變更，最好避免任何旅行。』」
廣告
The original collaboration between T.S.A. and ICE began in May 2025, when the airport security agency started sharing information with the immigration agency for enforcement purposes, according to a document obtained via the Freedom of Information Act from the government transparency group American Oversight and shared with The Times.
根據政府透明倡導組織"美國監督"依據《信息自由法》獲取並與本報共享的一份文件，TSA與ICE的最初合作始於2025年5月。當時，機場安檢機構開始向移民執法機構共享信息，以便進行執法行動。
ICE shared names of individuals with deportation orders with the airport agency, which would run them through its databases and report any hits to ICE for potential arrest. It was a remarkable shift from past practices, when the federal government avoided involving the T.S.A. in immigration enforcement for fear of disrupting domestic travel.
ICE將身負驅逐令的人員名單提供給機場安全機構，後者在其數據庫中比對，並將匹配結果報告ICE以便實施逮捕。這是對以往做法的重大轉變——為避免擾亂國內出行秩序，聯邦政府過去刻意迴避將TSA捲入移民執法。
Now, “the net they are casting for people they want to detain is far wider,” said Ghassan Shamieh, an immigration lawyer in San Francisco.
「他們如今撒網捕人的範圍要廣泛得多，」舊金山移民律師加桑·沙米耶說。
“It’s not people with criminal records” being targeted, Mr. Shamieh said. “You are talking about people with legitimate pending applications.”
「他們針對的並不是有犯罪記錄的人，」沙米耶說。「而是那些有合法待審申請的人。」
Mr. Shamieh has four clients who were detained at airports in the last 10 days, including two women who recently married Americans and are in the process of obtaining permanent residency through their spouses. One of the husbands was with his wife when she was taken into custody after they landed in Burbank, Calif.
沙米耶表示，過去10天內，他有四名當事人在機場遭到拘留，其中包括兩名剛與美國公民結婚、正在通過配偶申請永久居民身份的女性。其中一名女性在抵達加州伯班克機場後被拘留，當時她的丈夫就在她身邊。
On Tuesday morning, an immigration judge granted her release on a $35,000 bond. But D.H.S. lawyers reserved the right to appeal within 30 days, and she is likely to remain detained until they decide.
週二上午，一名移民法官准予她以3.5萬美元保釋金獲釋。但國土安全部律師保留30天內提出上訴的權利，因此在他們決定是否上訴之前，她很可能仍會繼續被拘留。
廣告
Ms. Morales Rojas, the au pair, entered the United States lawfully in January 2023 as a J-1 exchange visitor, a legal status that allowed her to work for two years, according to her lawyer. Her visa expired Jan. 4, 2025, according to D.H.S. Before the exchange program ended, Ms. Morales Rojas filed an application with immigration authorities to remain in the country, said the lawyer, Laura Lichter.
互惠生莫拉萊斯·羅哈斯的律師勞拉·利希特介紹，她於2023年1月持J-1交流訪問者簽證合法入境美國，該簽證允許其在美工作兩年。國土安全部文件顯示，她的簽證於2025年1月4日到期。律師表示，在交流項目結束前，莫拉萊斯·羅哈斯已向移民當局提交了留美申請。
“During the last three and a half years, Morales Rojas lived openly in the United States with the government’s knowledge of her pending case, passed repeated background checks, and received work authorization from D.H.S. while waiting for her application to be reviewed,” Ms. Lichter said. “The process she’s pursuing is completely lawful.”
利希特說：「在過去三年半裡，莫拉萊斯·羅哈斯一直公開生活在美國，政府也知道她的申請正在處理中。她通過了多次背景審查，並且在等待申請審核期間獲得了國土安全部批准的工作許可。她正在進行的程序完全合法。」
But homeland security officials said in a statement that “in violation of our nation’s laws, she overstayed her visa.” On Tuesday afternoon, a judge granted her release on a $3,000 bond, and she is likely to leave detention by Wednesday.
但國土安全部官員在聲明中表示，她「違反美國法律，逾期滯留」。週二下午，一名法官裁定準予她以3000美元保釋金獲釋，她有望在週三前離開拘留所。
Ms. Lichter has filed suit challenging the government’s decision to arrest and detain Ms. Morales Rojas without a proper arrest warrant and without an individualized determination that detention was necessary.
利希特已提起訴訟，質疑政府在沒有適當逮捕令、也未對拘留必要性進行個案裁定的情況下逮捕和拘留莫拉萊斯·羅哈斯的決定。
Lauren and Scott Cahr, her former employers, said they had hired Ms. Morales Rojas when their first child, Silas, was 6 months old. The Cahrs said in an interview that they had flown Ms. Morales Rojas to Denver during an Ultimate Frisbee tournament that they attended, and she had helped care for Silas and his little brother.
她以前的僱主勞倫和史考特·卡爾夫婦表示，他們在第一個孩子西拉斯6個月大時僱用了莫拉萊斯·羅哈斯。卡爾夫婦在採訪中說，他們帶莫拉萊斯·羅哈斯一起飛往丹佛參加一場極限飛盤比賽，她在那裡幫助照顧西拉斯以及他的弟弟。
The family formed a deep bond, and the Cahrs created a custom photo book titled “The Adventures of Chanty and Gordo,” the nicknames Ms. Morales Rojas and their son called each other, as a parting gift.
這個家庭與她建立了深厚的情感紐帶，卡爾夫婦還製作了一本定製相冊，題為《尚蒂和戈多的冒險》——這是莫拉萊斯·羅哈斯和他們的兒子彼此之間的暱稱——作為臨別禮物。
“She is an amazing young woman who we were so lucky to have in our lives,” Ms. Cahr said. “We have tried to maintain a consistent relationship with her.”
「她是一位非常優秀的年輕女性，我們非常幸運能夠讓她進入我們的生活，」卡爾女士說。「我們一直努力保持與她的聯繫。」
廣告
Ms. Cahr has visited Ms. Morales Rojas in detention, while members of the Cahrs’ Ultimate Frisbee community have launched a GoFundMe campaign to help cover legal costs.
卡爾女士曾去拘留所探望莫拉萊斯·羅哈斯，卡爾夫婦所在的極限飛盤社群成員已發起GoFundMe眾籌活動，幫助支付她的法律費用。

Hamed Aleaziz是時報報導國土安全部和移民政策的記者。

Miriam Jordan從基層視角報導移民及他們對美國人口結構、社會和經濟的影響。
