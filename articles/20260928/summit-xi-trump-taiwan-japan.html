習近平「試探」美國立場

At Summit, Xi Sought to Tilt Trump’s Stance on America’s Place in Asia

儲百亮, DAVID PIERSON • 2026-09-28
China’s leader, Xi Jinping, is not known for lightheartedness. Yet during his White House summit with President Trump, he sometimes smiled and even laughed, projecting a warmth unusual for a meeting between superpower rivals.
中國領導人習近平素來不以輕鬆詼諧著稱。但在與川普總統的白宮峰會上，他時而微笑，時而甚至開懷大笑，展現出超級大國對手間的會晤中並不常見的暖意。
Behind the show of geniality, Mr. Xi’s carefully chosen words revealed a cooler calculus: an effort to steer Mr. Trump toward Beijing’s positions on Taiwan, Japan and the broader security order in Asia.
在親切表象的背後，習近平精心選擇的措辭透露出更為冷靜的盤算：試圖將川普引向北京在台灣、日本以及更廣泛的亞洲安全秩序問題上的立場。
On Taiwan, Mr. Xi pressed Mr. Trump to limit American support for the self-ruled island that Beijing claims as its territory. On Japan, Mr. Xi invoked history to portray Tokyo, an American ally, as a resurgent military threat, while reminding the president that China and the United States had once fought on the same side.
在台灣問題上，習近平向川普施壓，要求限制美國對這個北京聲稱擁有主權的自治島嶼的支持。在日本問題上，他援引歷史，將美國的盟友東京描繪成一個重新崛起的軍事威脅，同時提醒川普，中美兩國曾經並肩作戰。
Mr. Xi appeared to be testing for opportunities to shape Mr. Trump’s views, rather than hoping for a decisive breakthrough, Matt Pottinger, who served as a deputy national security adviser to Mr. Trump in his first term as president, said in an interview. Mr. Xi and Mr. Trump have met three times since October last year, and they may meet two more times by the end of this year.
曾在川普首個總統任期內擔任副國家安全顧問的博明(Matt Pottinger)在接受採訪時表示,習近平似乎是在試探是否有機會影響川普的看法，而不是期待取得決定性突破。自去年10月以來，習近平和川普已會面三次，年底前可能還會再見兩次。
廣告
“Xi sees frequent meetings with his American counterpart as an opportunity to probe for weaknesses in the U.S. positions,” said Mr. Pottinger, now the chairman of the China Program at the Foundation for Defense of Democracies.
現任捍衛民主基金會中國項目主席的博明說：「習近平把與美國總統頻繁會晤視為一個機會，可以藉此試探美國立場中的薄弱之處。」
Outwardly, the summit between Mr. Xi and Mr. Trump achieved little.
從表面上看，習近平與川普的此次峰會成果寥寥。
China and the United States agreed to extend their truce in the trade war, but only by two months. The leaders discussed the wars in the Middle East and Ukraine but announced no apparent advances on ending either conflict. They toasted each other, and spoke loftily of partnership and stability in relations, yet announced no concrete agreements on the most contentious of issues: the trade imbalance, technology restrictions and Taiwan.
中美雙方同意延長貿易戰休戰期，但僅延長兩個月。兩位領導人討論了中東和烏克蘭的戰爭，但並未宣布在結束其中任何一場衝突方面取得明顯進展。他們彼此舉杯致意，高談夥伴關係和雙邊關係的穩定，但在最棘手的幾個問題——貿易失衡、技術限制和台灣——上，沒有宣布任何具體協議。
The White House did announce that it had reached a deal with China to reduce tariffs on $30 billion of “nonsensitive goods.” The two nations also agreed to continue to discuss supply-chain issues with critical minerals, as well as concerns over artificial intelligence. But the White House did not detail specific plans on those more contentious points.
白宮確實宣布，已與中國達成協議，將降低對300億美元「非敏感商品」的關稅。兩國還同意繼續就關鍵礦產的供應鏈問題以及人工智慧方面的擔憂進行討論。但白宮沒有就這些更具爭議的問題公布體計劃。
The trip was certainly a propaganda boost for Mr. Xi, who has sought to cast himself as an equal to Mr. Trump on the world stage.
這次訪問無疑對習近平來說是一次宣傳上的助力。長期以來，他一直試圖把自己塑造成在世界舞台上與川普平起平坐的領導人。
Coverage of the summit was splashed across the front page of the People’s Daily, the Communist Party’s mouthpiece, with images of Mr. Xi being honored at the White House with a military flyover and being greeted by Mr. Trump at Joint Base Andrews. A top trending hashtag on Chinese social media was about how rare it was for an American president to receive a world leader on the tarmac.
中共機關報《人民日報》在頭版大篇幅報導了這次峰會，並刊登了習近平在白宮接受軍機飛行致敬、在安德魯斯空軍基地受到川普迎接的照片。中國社群媒體上的一個熱門話題是，美國總統機場迎接外國領導人多麼罕見。
廣告
“This is historic validation of China’s strength. It’s seen as a point of arrival and adds to Xi Jinping’s effort to portray China as an equal partner, which has been the brass ring of Chinese foreign policy” for decades, said Evan Medeiros, a professor of Asian studies at Georgetown University who was senior Asia director on the National Security Council under President Barack Obama.
喬治敦大學亞洲研究教授、歐巴馬總統任內曾任國家安全委員會亞洲事務高級主任的麥艾文(Evan Medeiros)說：「這是對中國實力具有歷史意義的認可。人們把它視為一個抵達目標的時刻，也為習近平把中國塑造成平等夥伴的努力增添了分量——幾十年來，這一直是中國外交政策夢寐以求的目標。」
The summit served Mr. Xi’s purposes in a more subtle but important way. He used verbal finesse to suggest that Mr. Trump was sympathetic to Beijing’s positions — even if members of Mr. Trump’s own administration disagree with them.
這場峰會還以一種更為微妙卻同樣重要的方式契合了習近平的目的。他運用言辭上的巧妙分寸暗示川普對北京的立場抱有同情，即使川普政府內部的一些成員實際上並不認同這些立場。
Most notably, Mr. Xi appeared to imply that Mr. Trump had shifted closer to Beijing’s views on Taiwan, including that the island’s government is scheming for independence. Continued stability in relations between China and the United States, he indicated, rested on Mr. Trump heeding Chinese positions.
最值得注意的是，習近平似乎在暗示，川普在台灣問題上的立場已經更接近北京，包括認同台灣當局正在謀求獨立這一說法。他還暗示，中美關係能否繼續保持穩定，取決於川普是否聽取中方立場。
“I hope that the United States adheres to the correct position of opposing ‘Taiwan independence’ and handles the Taiwan issue prudently,” Mr. Xi said in his meeting with Mr. Trump on Thursday, according to an official summary issued by Chinese news outlets. That approach would, Mr. Xi said, “lay a solid foundation for U.S.-China strategic cooperation and the development of bilateral relations.”
據中國官方通稿，習近平週四與川普的會晤中表示：「希望美方堅持反對『台獨』的正確立場，慎重處理台灣問題。」習近平說，這種做法將「為中美戰略合作和兩國關係發展奠定堅實基礎」。
Successive U.S. administrations have said they do not support outright Taiwanese independence. But U.S. presidents have generally rejected Chinese calls for Washington to declare that it flatly “opposes” independence. Such a shift, experts say, could suggest that the United States believes that Taiwan’s policies, and not Beijing’s threat of forcible unification, are the primary source of cross-strait tensions.
歷屆美國政府都表示不支持台灣正式宣布獨立。但美國總統通常拒絕接受中方要求華盛頓明確宣布「反對」台灣獨立的主張。多位專家指出，如果美國作出這樣的轉變，可能意味著華盛頓認為，兩岸緊張局勢的主要根源在於台灣自身的政策，而非北京方面的武統威脅。
Mr. Xi’s use of the word “adhere” (“jianchi” in Chinese) appeared intended to create the impression that “Beijing believes Trump has already absorbed some of its red lines on Taiwan,” said Craig Singleton, a China Program senior director at the Foundation for Defense of Democracies. “Beijing is steadily wrapping its preferred language around the summit.”
捍衛民主基金會中國項目高級主任克雷格·辛格頓說，習近平使用「堅持」一詞，似乎是為了營造這樣一種印象：「北京認為，川普已經接受了中方在台灣問題上劃定的紅線。」他說：「北京正在穩步地把自己偏好的措辭套在這次峰會上。」
廣告
The official Chinese summary of Mr. Xi’s remarks did not go into specifics, but the implied warning was clear, several experts said. If Mr. Trump went ahead and approved a proposal to sell $14 billion worth of weapons to Taiwan, he could damage or even undo the fragile détente between Beijing and Washington.
中方發布的這份官方通稿並未就習近平的表態展開具體闡述，但多位專家指出，其中隱含的警告意味已相當明顯。倘若川普執意批准一項價值140億美元的對台軍售提案，就可能損害、甚至徹底破壞北京和華盛頓之間脆弱的緩和關係。
The package includes missiles, anti-drone equipment and air-defense systems. It would follow a separate $11 billion package Mr. Trump approved late last year. After his last summit with Mr. Xi in Beijing in May, though, Mr. Trump said that the arms sales were a “very good negotiating chip” with China. He has since held off on approving the new package, raising unease in Taiwan and across Asia about how far he could go in subordinating U.S. security commitments to better ties with China.
這批軍售包括導彈、反無人機設備和防空系統。此前，川普已於去年年底批准另一批價值110億美元的對台軍售。不過，今年5月在北京與習近平舉行上一次峰會後，川普說，對台軍售是與中國談判時「一張非常好的籌碼」。此後，他一直沒有批准這批新的軍售，這也在台灣以及整個亞洲地區引發了不安，人們擔心他可能在多大程度上為了改善對華關係而把美國的安全承諾置於次要位置。
“What is important is in practice the U.S. should stop selling arms to Taiwan and be very careful in the dealing with Taiwan,” said Wu Xinbo, an American studies scholar at Fudan University in Shanghai who advises China’s Foreign Ministry.
「重要的是，在實踐中，美國應停止向台灣出售武器，並在非常謹慎地處理台灣問題，」上海復旦大學美國研究學者、為中國外交部提供諮詢的吳心伯說。
Many experts expect Mr. Trump to eventually sign off on more arms sales, however. China could retaliate with military intimidation of Taiwan or economic punishment of the United States, such as further restrictions of rare earth exports to U.S. companies.
不過，許多專家預計，川普最終仍會批准更多對台軍售。中國可能通過對台灣進行軍事恫嚇或者對美國實施經濟懲罰來報復，例如進一步限制向美國企業出口稀土。
Taiwan’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs said Mr. Xi’s comments “distorted the facts” in an effort to support Beijing’s claim over the island.
台灣外交部表示，習近平的言論「扭曲事實」，目的是為北京對台灣的主權主張提供支持。
Ali Wyne, a senior researcher on U.S.-China ties at the International Crisis Group, said Mr. Xi recognizes that Mr. Trump was “not going to dwell on semantic considerations, especially if he can frame them as part of an appeal to Trump’s ego.”
國際危機組織的美中關係高級研究員阿里·韋恩說，習近平意識到，川普「不會糾結於語義上的考量，尤其是如果他能把這些措辭包裝成一種迎合川普自尊的說法」。
For that, Mr. Xi leaned into a familiar maritime metaphor that appealed to Mr. Trump’s preference for leader-to-leader diplomacy. “I am ready to work with you to steer the giant ship of the China-U.S. relationship on a steady course,” Mr. Xi told Mr. Trump.
為此，習近平再次使用了一個熟悉的航海比喻，迎合川普偏好領導人之間直接外交的風格。習近平對川普說：「我們應當共同扛起這個歷史重任，推動中美關係這艘巨輪沿著正確航道平穩前行。」
廣告
Mr. Trump said on Friday that he would attend the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit in Shenzhen, China, in November, and that Mr. Xi would attend the Group of 20 summit in Florida in December.
川普週五表示，他將出席11月在中國深圳舉行的亞太經合組織峰會，而習近平將出席12月在佛羅里達舉行的二十國集團峰會。
Mr. Xi also used his time to try to drive a wedge between Washington and one of its stalwart allies in Asia — Japan.
習近平還利用這次會晤，試圖在華盛頓與日本之間製造裂痕，日本是美國在亞洲最堅定的盟友之一。
He reminded Mr. Trump that the United States and China fought together against a common Japanese enemy during World War II. He described Imperial Japan as “fascist” and “militarist” aggressors, reviving an image of Japan as an “enemy state” that cannot be trusted on the global stage.
他提醒川普，第二次世界大戰期間，美中曾共同抗擊日本這個共同敵人。他把日本帝國稱為「法西斯」和「軍國主義」侵略者，重新喚起一種把日本描繪成全球舞台上不值得信任的「敵國」的形象。
His remarks were in line with a monthslong effort by Beijing to punish Japan over comments Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi made in defense of Taiwan in November.
他的這些言論與北京幾個月來針對日本展開的施壓行動一脈相承。去年11月，日本首相高市早苗發表了支持台灣的言論，此後北京一直試圖讓日本為此付出代價。
“The message is, ‘They are the bad guys, and we are the good guys that are trying to change the world order, and the Americans should cooperate with us,’” said Akio Takahara, a visiting professor at Tokyo Woman’s Christian University who specializes in Chinese politics.
專攻中國政治的東京女子大學客座教授高原明生說：「傳遞的信息是：『他們是壞人，而我們才是試圖改變世界秩序的好人，美國人應該與我們合作。』」
The risk for Japan, he said, is that Mr. Xi’s narrative could gain traction if repeated enough times.
他說，對日本而言，風險在於，如果習近平不斷重複這種敘事，可能會獲得越來越多的認同。
廣告
Ms. Takaichi told reporters in Tokyo on Friday that Japan had achieved “genuine reconciliation” with the United States and they now had a “relationship of mutual trust as allies bound by very strong bonds.”
高市早苗週五在東京對記者說，日本已經與美國實現了「真正的和解」，如今是「以非常牢固的紐帶相連、彼此信賴的盟友關係」。
