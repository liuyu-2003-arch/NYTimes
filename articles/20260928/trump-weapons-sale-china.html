美駐華大使：川普曾提議對華軍售

Trump Offered to Sell Arms to China, U.S. Ambassador Says

黃安偉 • 2026-09-28
President Trump has asked Xi Jinping, the leader of China, whether he would like to buy American weapons, David Perdue, the American ambassador to China, said in a television interview on Sunday.
美國駐華大使龐德偉(David Perdue)在週日的電視採訪中表示，川普總統曾詢問中國領導人習近平是否願意購買美國武器。
“President Trump continues to say, ‘Hey, we sell arms to other people around the world,’” Mr. Perdue said on “Fox News Sunday” while discussing the state visit by Mr. Xi to Washington last week. “He actually asked President Xi would he like to buy some at one point.”
「川普總統一直說，『嘿，我們向世界各地的其他人出售武器。』他實際上問過習主席，是否願意買一些，」龐德偉在《福克斯週日新聞》上討論習近平上週對華盛頓的國事訪問時說。
Mr. Perdue did not give further details and did not reveal Mr. Xi’s reply.
龐德偉沒有提供更多細節，也沒有透露習近平的答覆。
An admission that the American president offered to sell arms to China is extraordinary. The Communist-ruled nation is considered by most American policymakers and legislators of both parties to be the greatest military, economic and diplomatic rival of the United States — the only real challenger to America as a superpower.
承認美國總統曾提出向中國出售武器，這是極不尋常的。這個由共產黨執政的國家被美國兩黨大多數政策制定者和立法者視為美國在軍事、經濟和外交上的最大對手——也是美國作為超級大國的唯一真正挑戰者。
廣告
When asked for comment, the White House said through a U.S. official that the United States had no plans to sell arms to China. The State Department said in a statement that “U.S. law prohibits arms sales to China and there is no offer or plan to sell arms to China.”
在被要求置評時，白宮通過一名美國官員表示，美國沒有向中國出售武器的計劃。美國國務院在聲明中表示，「美國法律禁止向中國出售武器，並且沒有向中國出售武器的提議或計劃。」
The Chinese Embassy in Washington did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
中國駐華盛頓大使館沒有立即回應置評請求。
China has the world’s fastest-growing military, and for decades U.S. officials across administrations, including in the current one, have warned of a potential military clash between the two nuclear-armed powers in the Asia-Pacific region, particularly over the island of Taiwan or China’s aggressive actions in territory claimed by U.S. allies in the South and East China Seas.
中國擁有世界上發展最快的軍隊，幾十年來，包括現任政府在內的歷屆美國政府官員都曾警告，這兩個核武大國之間在亞太地區可能發生軍事衝突，特別是圍繞台灣問題，或是由於中國在美國盟友聲稱擁有主權的南海和東海領土採取的強硬行動。
Chinese defense companies, with the approval of the Chinese government, are supporting adversaries of the United States, including Iran and Russia, by selling them dual-use equipment and satellite imagery, as well as planning the secret export of weapons, according to U.S. officials. The United States has imposed sanctions on some Chinese companies.
據美國官員稱，在得到中國政府批准的情況下，中國國防企業正在通過出售軍民兩用設備和衛星圖像，以及策劃祕密出口武器，來支持包括伊朗和俄羅斯在內的美國對手。美國已經對部分中國企業實施了制裁。
The United States has been the dominant military power in the Asia-Pacific region, but that strength is fading relative to China’s, especially as the Trump administration diverts resources from the region for use in its war against Iran. In recent years, the U.S. defense industry has also struggled to manufacture weapons systems at a rate considered sufficient for global needs by American officials.
美國一直是亞太地區的主導軍事力量，但相對於中國，這種力量正在衰退，尤其是隨著川普政府將該地區的資源調往對伊朗的戰爭中。近年來，美國國防工業也一直難以按照美國官員認為能滿足全球需求的速度來製造武器系統。
“If there was any ambiguity about whether President Trump views China as a security threat, this disclosure by @USAmbChina Perdue puts the question to rest,” Ryan Hass, a former career diplomat and White House national security official who is director of the China center at the Brookings Institution, wrote on social media.
「如果說對於川普總統是否將中國視為安全威脅還存在含糊之處，那麼@USAmbChina（美國駐華大使）龐德偉的這一披露讓這個問題有了定論，」前職業外交官、白宮國家安全官員、布魯金斯學會中國中心主任何雷恩(Ryan Hass)在社群媒體上寫道。
廣告
“Trump’s offer to sell weapons to China will be unsettling to American partners at the pointy edge of China’s spear,” he added.
「川普向中國出售武器的提議將讓處於中國矛頭最前沿的美國合作夥伴感到不安，」他還說。
Those partners include the Philippines, Japan and Taiwan, the de facto independent island and democracy that the Chinese Communist Party seeks to rule.
這些合作夥伴包括菲律賓、日本和台灣，台灣是一個事實上的獨立島嶼和民主政體，是中國共產黨企圖統治的對象。
Mr. Trump has been withholding a $14 billion sale of weapons to Taiwan, even though the U.S. government has been required by law since 1979 to send arms of a defensive nature to the island. The latest arms package was put together by State Department officials and was approved by congressional leaders early this year, but has since languished at the department, The New York Times reported.
川普一直擱置向台灣出售價值140億美元的武器，儘管自1979年以來，美國政府在法律上被要求向該島提供防禦性武器。據《紐約時報》報導，最新的軍售一攬子計劃由國務院官員擬定，並在今年早些時候獲得了國會領導人的批准，但此後便一直被擱置在國務院。
Senators sent a bipartisan letter in May to Mr. Trump urging him to move forward with the sales. On Wednesday, hours before Mr. Xi arrived in Washington, four Republican senators led by Sen. Mitch McConnell of Kentucky sent a letter to Secretary of State Marco Rubio and Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth asking for the same thing.
今年5月，參議員們向川普發送了一封兩黨聯名信，敦促他推進軍售。週三，就在習近平抵達華盛頓前幾個小時，由肯塔基州參議員米奇·麥康乃爾牽頭的四位共和黨參議員致信國務卿馬可·魯比奧和國防部長彼特·海格塞斯，提出了同樣的要求。
They also pressed the two men to allocate $300 million in aid for purchasing arms that Congress had already approved for Taiwan, as well as $1 billion for the Taiwan Security Cooperation Initiative, a U.S. defense program.
他們還敦促這兩位官員撥出國會已經批准用於台灣購買武器的3億美元援助，以及用於美國國防項目「台灣安全合作倡議」的10億美元。
When a Fox News interviewer asked Mr. Perdue on Sunday about the widespread concerns over withheld weapons sales and military aid to Taiwan, Mr. Perdue pointed out that Mr. Trump had approved $11 billion of arms sales to Taiwan last year. And he said “there are continuing conversations about other sales. He and President Xi talk about that every time they get together.”
週日，當福克斯新聞的一位採訪記者就外界對暫緩對台軍售和軍事援助的廣泛擔憂向龐德偉提問時，龐德偉指出，川普去年批准了對台110億美元的軍售。他說，「關於其他軍售的對話仍在繼續。他和習主席每次見面都會談論這個問題。」
It was then that Mr. Perdue said Mr. Trump had argued that the United States sells arms to “other people around the world,” and offered such sales to Mr. Xi.
就在這個時候，龐德偉說，川普曾主張美國向「世界各地的其他人」出售武器，並向習近平提出了此類軍售提議。
廣告
China has said Mr. Xi has told Mr. Trump that he opposes the United States selling weapons to Taiwan. In May, after leaving a Beijing summit with Mr. Xi, Mr. Trump told reporters he had discussed Taiwan arms sales with Mr. Xi “in great detail.” Such conversations — the existence of which Mr. Perdue underscored in his remarks on Sunday — are a violation of a 1982 document in which the United States agreed to broaden its support of Taiwan; one of the assurances said Washington “has not agreed to consult with” China on arms sales to Taiwan.
中國表示，習近平曾告訴川普，他反對美國向台灣出售武器。今年5月，在結束與習近平在北京舉行的峰會後，川普告訴記者，他與習近平「非常詳細地」討論了對台軍售問題。龐德偉在週日的採訪中強調了這些對話的存在，而這些對話違反了1982年的一份文件，在該文件中，美國同意擴大對台灣的支持；其中一項保證指出，華盛頓「未同意」與中國就對台軍售問題進行「磋商」。
When asked in May about that commitment, Mr. Trump replied: “Well, I think the 1980s is a long way. That’s a big, far distance.”
今年5月被問及該承諾時，川普回答說：「嗯，我認為1980年代是很久以前的事了。那是很遠很遠的距離。」
In a Fox News interview recorded in May while he was in Beijing, Mr. Trump said he was holding back Taiwan arms sales because it was a “very good negotiating chip” with China.
在5月訪問北京期間錄製的福克斯新聞採訪中，他表示，他之所以暫緩對台軍售，是因為這是與中國談判的「一張非常好的籌碼」。
After the summit last week. Mr. Trump said he and Mr. Xi did not talk much about Taiwan. “Right now, it’s fine,” he said. “It’s just moving along.”
在上週的峰會之後。川普表示他和習近平沒有太多談論台灣。「現在情況還不錯，」他說。「一切正在向前發展。」
On Sunday, Taiwan’s de facto ambassador to the United States, Alexander Yui, said on CBS News’s “Face the Nation” that he felt confident, based on public remarks last week by administration officials, that “there’s no change in Taiwan’s position for the United States.” He added, though, that he hoped Taiwan could get the $14 billion withheld arms package “as soon as possible.”
週日，台灣事實上的駐美大使俞大㵢在CBS新聞的《面向全國》節目中表示，基於美國政府官員上週的公開講話，他感到很有信心，「美國對台灣的立場沒有改變」。不過他還說，他希望台灣能「儘快」獲得被擱置的140億美元武器方案。
On Wednesday, Mr. Rubio was asked by reporters in New York about the withholding of the Taiwan arms package. He said the sale was being reviewed in light of the need for “balancing our industrial capacity and our military-industrial base” and determining whether the United States needed the arms for itself. It is unclear how Mr. Trump’s offer to sell arms to China is consistent with that reasoning.
週三，魯比奧在紐約被記者問及暫緩對台軍售一事。他說，考慮到需要「平衡我們的工業能力和軍工基地」，並確定美國自身是否需要這些武器，該軍售正在接受審查。目前尚不清楚川普提出向中國出售武器的提議如何與這一理由保持相符。
廣告
Mr. Trump said last week that he would meet with Mr. Xi again in China in November during a regional leadership summit, and that the Chinese leader would return to the United States in December, also for a multilateral summit. Analysts say it is unlikely that Mr. Trump will approve any sales of arms to Taiwan before the end of the year, for fear of upsetting Mr. Xi before those planned meetings.
川普上週表示，他將於11月在中國舉行的區域領導人峰會上再次與習近平會晤，後者也將於12月重返美國，參加一場多邊峰會。分析人士表示，川普不太可能在年底前批准對台任何軍售，以免在這些計劃中的會晤之前惹惱習近平。

黃安偉(Edward Wong)為《紐約時報》報導全球事務、美國外交政策和國務院新聞。
