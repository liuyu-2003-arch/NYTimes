中國商界領袖缺席白宮國宴，這說明了什麼？

Missing From the Trump-Xi State Dinner: China’s Business Leaders

黃瑞黎 • 2026-09-28
When China’s leader, Xi Jinping, traveled to the United States in 2015 for his first state visit, he brought a delegation of Chinese chief executives to meet their American counterparts.
2015年，中國領導人習近平首次對美國進行國事訪問時，他隨行帶了一支由中國企業的負責人組成的代表團，與美國同行會面。
Disputes over human rights, cyberattacks and trade were straining relations. But Washington and Beijing still shared an undeniable common interest: making money in each other’s markets.
當時，人權、網路攻擊和貿易爭端正在令兩國關係日趨緊張。但華盛頓和北京仍有一個不可否認的共同利益：在彼此的市場賺錢。
A decade later, the contrast with this week’s summit between President Trump and Mr. Xi could not be more stark. No prominent Chinese chief executives attended Thursday night’s state dinner at the White House.
十年後，本週川普總統與習近平的峰會與之形成了再鮮明不過的對比。週四晚的白宮國宴上，沒有一位中國知名企業負責人出席。
“It’s a rather depressing marker of how bad the relationship has gotten,” said Emily Kilcrease, a senior fellow and director at the Center for a New American Security focused on the U.S.-China economic relationship.
「這相當令人沮喪地表明兩國關係已經惡化到了什麼程度，」新美國安全中心高級研究員兼主任、專注於美中經濟關係的艾米麗·基爾克里斯說。
廣告
“One of the highlights of this summit was supposed to be some level of announcement of commercial deals,” said Ms. Kilcrease, a former deputy assistant U.S. trade representative. “But it seems like we can’t even get to agreement on some of that.”
曾任美國貿易代表副助理的基爾克里斯說：「這次峰會原本的一大看點應該是會在某種程度上宣布一些商業協議。但現在看來，我們甚至連其中一些協議都無法達成一致。」
The absence of Chinese business leaders was a small but telling illustration of how thoroughly the economic relationship between the two countries has changed. In 2015, high-profile tech executives were among those traveling with Mr. Xi, including Alibaba’s Jack Ma and Tencent’s Pony Ma. Their companies were expanding rapidly, and both sides saw enormous opportunities in deeper commercial ties.
中國商界領袖的缺席是一個雖小卻意味深長的例子，說明兩國經濟關係已經發生了多麼徹底的變化。2015年，隨中國領導人出訪的人中包括阿里巴巴的馬雲和騰訊的馬化騰等知名科技公司高管。他們的公司當時正在迅速擴張，而中美雙方都認為，進一步深化商業聯繫蘊藏著巨大機遇。
That era is over.
那個時代已經結束。
Beginning in Mr. Trump’s first term, the two countries have imposed waves of tariffs on each other. Washington restricted access to some of America’s most advanced technology and limited U.S. investments in parts of China’s high-tech sector. The United States slapped 100 percent tariffs and imposed restrictions on internet-connected vehicles from China, making it financially unviable for the country’s electric vehicles to enter the United States.
從川普第一任期開始，兩國相互加徵了一輪又一輪關稅。華盛頓限制中國獲取美國部分最先進的技術，並限制美國對中國部分高科技行業的投資。美國對來自中國的聯網汽車徵收100%的關稅並實施限制，使中國電動汽車進入美國市場在經濟上變得幾乎不可能。
Beijing retaliated with its own trade restrictions, including controls on exports of rare-earth metals and rare-earth magnets essential to U.S. manufacturers.
北京則以自己的貿易限制措施予以反擊，包括對稀土金屬和稀土磁體的出口管制，而這些材料對美國製造商至關重要。
At the same time, Chinese companies have evolved from prospective customers and partners for American businesses into some of their most formidable competitors.
與此同時，中國企業已經從美國企業眼中的潛在客戶和合作夥伴變成了它們最難對付的競爭對手之一。
廣告
And many of China’s most prominent entrepreneurs now come from industries like artificial intelligence, robotics and electric vehicles — the exact technologies Washington and Beijing are competing most intensely for.
中國許多最傑出的企業家如今來自人工智慧、機器人技術和電動汽車等行業，恰恰也是華盛頓和北京競爭最激烈的那些技術領域。
It would be difficult to imagine, for example, seeing the DeepSeek co-founder Liang Wenfeng showcased alongside the OpenAI chief executive, Sam Altman, at a state dinner.
舉例來說，難以想像在一場國宴上，DeepSeek聯合創始人梁文鋒會與OpenAI首席執行官山姆·奧特曼一起亮相。
Yet until days before the summit, Chinese officials expected they might bring a business delegation to Washington, according to two people familiar with the discussions who spoke on the condition of anonymity because they were not authorized to disclose details of the private conversations.
然而，據兩名知情人士透露，直到峰會前幾天，中國官員仍預計可能會帶一支商業代表團前往華盛頓。由於無權披露這些私人談話的細節，兩人要求匿名。
It was unclear which executives were under consideration, although reports had mentioned Wang Chuanfu, founder of the electric carmaker BYD, and Robin Zeng, chief executive of the battery maker CATL. Their presence would have allowed Mr. Xi to highlight China’s dominance in battery technology and renewable energy.
目前尚不清楚哪些高管曾被考慮，但有報導提到電動汽車製造商比亞迪創始人王傳福和電池製造商寧德時代首席執行官曾毓群。他們的出席本有助於習近平強調中國在電池技術和可再生能源領域的主導地位。
In the end, none received invitations.
最終，沒有人收到邀請。
Several top Chinese executives arrived in Washington shortly before the summit and held meetings outside the official gathering.
多位中國頂級高管在峰會前不久抵達華盛頓，並在官方聚會之外舉行了會議。
“There are a number of important Chinese C.E.O.s in Washington, waiting on the sidelines, like: ‘Do I get tapped or not?’” said Jeremy Chan, a senior China analyst at Eurasia Group and a former American diplomat who worked in China. “The whole thing is crazy. It would be comical if it weren’t so tragic.”
「華盛頓有多位重要的中國企業首席執行官在場外觀望，心裡都在想：『會不會叫到我？』」歐亞集團的中國問題高級分析師、曾在中國工作的前美國外交官傑瑞米·陳（音）說。「整件事太瘋狂了。要不是因為太可悲，這件事甚至顯得有點滑稽。」
廣告
There was another complication: Some prominent Chinese companies face U.S. restrictions, making their appearance at a White House state dinner politically fraught. In June, the U.S. Department of Defense had included Alibaba and BYD in its expanded list of firms it identified as “Chinese military companies.”
還有另一個複雜因素：一些中國知名企業受到美國方面的限制，相關企業的負責人如果出現在白宮國宴上，在政治上會相當棘手。今年6月，美國國防部把阿里巴巴和比亞迪列入了擴大後的所謂「中國軍工企業」名單。
“It would actually be seen by some in Washington as provocative to bring these companies along,” said Ilaria Mazzocco, a deputy director at the Center for Strategic and International Studies specializing in Chinese business and economics.
專門研究中國商業和經濟問題的戰略與國際研究中心副主任伊拉里婭·馬佐科說：「讓這些公司參與進來實際上會被華盛頓一些人視為挑釁。」
The contrast was especially striking compared with the scene when Mr. Trump visited Beijing in May.
與川普今年5月訪問北京時的場面相比，這種反差尤其鮮明。
Apple’s Tim Cook, Tesla’s Elon Musk and other executives of top American companies stood on the steps of Beijing’s Great Hall of the People just behind senior U.S. officials. Mr. Trump told Mr. Xi that he had brought “the greatest businessmen, the biggest, and I guess the best, in the world.”
蘋果的蒂姆·庫克、特斯拉的伊隆·馬斯克以及其他美國頂尖企業的高管站在北京人民大會堂的台階上，緊挨在美國高級官員身後。川普對中國領導人說，他帶來了「世界上最偉大的商人，最了不起的，我想也是最優秀的」。
Mr. Trump added, “I didn’t want the second or the third in the company, I wanted only the top, and they’re here today to pay respects to you and to China.”
川普還說：「我不要公司的二三把手，我要第一把手，所以他們都來了，表示出對中國和對您的尊重。」
On Thursday, many of the same executives from U.S. firms were present again. Alongside Mr. Cook and Mr. Musk were Nvidia’s Jensen Huang, Meta’s Mark Zuckerberg, Amazon’s Jeff Bezos, OpenAI’s Mr. Altman, Microsoft’s Satya Nadella and General Motors’ Mary Barra.
週四，不少來自同一批美國企業的高管再次出席。與庫克和馬斯克一同在場的還有英偉達的黃仁勛、Meta的馬克·祖克柏、亞馬遜的傑夫·貝索斯、OpenAI的奧特曼、微軟的薩蒂亞·納德拉和通用汽車的瑪麗·博拉。
Earlier in the day, Mr. Xi said in a speech that China “welcomes American companies wanting to invest and do business there.” Then, in an apparent reference to the U.S. restrictions on Chinese firms operating in America, he added: “We hope Chinese companies are treated fairly here in the United States.”
當天早些時候，習近平在講話中說，中國「歡迎美國企業來華投資興業」。隨後，他顯然是暗指美國對中國企業在美運營的限制，又補充說：「希望美方公平對待中國企業。」
廣告
While American companies have not given up on China, they are struggling to compete against its companies. American automakers have lost ground to Chinese electric vehicle makers. Apple faces intense competition from Huawei. Nike is battling increasingly popular Chinese brands.
雖然美國企業並沒有放棄中國市場，但它們正越來越難以與中國企業競爭。美國汽車製造商已經在與中國電動汽車製造商的競爭中節節敗退。蘋果正面臨華為的激烈競爭。耐吉也在應對越來越受歡迎的中國品牌的挑戰。
Yet China remains too large and too deeply embedded in global manufacturing for many American companies to ignore. A 2026 survey by the U.S.-China Business Council found that 80 percent of American companies still regarded the Chinese market as either “very important” or “important” because of its scale and industrial ecosystem.
但對許多美國企業來說，中國市場規模太大，而且與全球製造業嵌合太深，因此難以忽視。美中貿易全國委員會2026年的一項調查發現，80%的美國企業仍認為中國市場「非常重要」或「重要」，原因在於其市場規模和產業生態體系。
For Chinese companies, the calculation about the United States is increasingly different. Tariffs, political scrutiny, investment restrictions and national security rules have made entering or expanding in the United States far more difficult, particularly for companies in strategic industries.
對中國企業而言，是否進入美國市場的考量正越來越不同。關稅、政治審查、投資限制和國家安全規定，使得進入美國或在美國擴張變得困難得多，尤其是對那些處於戰略性行業的企業而言。
Joerg Wuttke, a partner at DGA-Albright Stonebridge Group, a Washington-based consultancy, said seeing so many American business executives “kissing Xi’s ring” underscored how lopsided the relationship had become.
華盛頓諮詢公司奧爾布賴特石橋集團合伙人伍德克(Joerg Wuttke)說，如此多美國企業高管「向習近平俯首示好」，凸顯出這段經濟關係已經變得多麼失衡。
“Is there anybody from China interested in doing business in America?” Mr. Wuttke said. “No, because it’s not possible. So why should they waste their time?”
伍德克說：「中國還有誰有興趣去美國做生意嗎？沒有，因為不可能。那他們為什麼還要浪費時間？」
