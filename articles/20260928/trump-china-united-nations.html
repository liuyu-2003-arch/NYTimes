習近平缺席聯大凸顯聯合國尷尬地位

China’s Xi Snubs a Troubled U.N. for Trump, Overshadowing Its Big Week

MICHAEL CROWLEY • 2026-09-28
World leaders spoke, diplomats bartered and gossiped, and motorcades with sirens blaring snarled traffic. In some ways, the annual United Nations General Assembly in New York this week felt like the center of the world.
世界各國領導人發表講話，外交官們交涉和閑聊，鳴著警笛的車隊導致交通癱瘓。在某些方面，本週在紐約舉行的年度聯合國大會彷彿成了世界的中心。
But it was ultimately overshadowed by a meeting more than 200 miles away in Washington, where President Trump hosted President Xi Jinping of China at the White House.
但這場會議最終被300多公里外華盛頓的一場會議掩蓋了光芒——在那裡，川普總統在白宮接待了中國國家主席習近平。
Even if their summit did not produce much in the way of tangible action, the Trump-Xi meeting felt at least as consequential as the diplomatic frenzy at the U.N. Mr. Trump and Mr. Xi met at a moment when their countries wield huge power over the global economy and the future of technology, including the dizzyingly — and perhaps dangerously — fast development of artificial intelligence.
儘管他們的峰會並未產生多少實質性成果，但川普與習近平的會晤令人感覺至少與聯合國的這場外交狂潮同等重要。川普和習近平會面之際，兩國對全球經濟和技術的未來擁有著巨大影響力，包括人工智慧令人眼花撩亂、甚至可能相當危險的飛速發展。
The U.N. is faltering, dysfunctional and largely ineffectual as world powers increasingly circumvent or ignore it. It was telling that Mr. Xi did not bother to stop in New York on his way to see Mr. Trump. When Mr. Xi visited the Obama White House in September of 2015, he also attended and spoke at that year’s U.N. General Assembly.
隨著世界大國日益規避或忽視聯合國，該組織正陷入搖搖欲墜、失能且基本無效的困境。耐人尋味的是，習近平前往會見川普的途中甚至沒有順道在紐約停留。2015年9月習近平訪問歐巴馬時期的白宮時，還曾出席當年的聯合國大會並發表講話。
The symbolism of Mr. Xi’s absence “could hardly be more devastating for the U.N.,” said Jeffrey Prescott, who served as the U.S. ambassador to the United Nations offices in Rome during the Biden administration. “The train between Washington and New York is slower than the one in China would be. But Xi is not far away, and yet he’s not taking the time to go there.”
拜登政府時期曾任美國駐羅馬聯合國機構大使的傑弗里·普雷史考特表示，習近平缺席的象徵意義「對聯合國而言，其破壞性再大不過」。「華盛頓和紐約之間的火車速度比中國國內的車速要慢。但習近平離得並不遠，卻不願抽時間去那裡。」
“I think that tells you something about how important the U.N. platform is right now,” added Mr. Prescott, who also served as an adviser on Asia to Vice President Joseph R. Biden Jr. and is now a senior fellow at the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace.
「我認為這說明了聯合國平台目前的重要性到底如何，」普雷史考特還說，他曾擔任副總統小約瑟夫·R·拜登的亞洲顧問，現為卡內基國際和平基金會的高級研究員。
廣告
Mr. Trump spoke at the U.N. gathering and held meetings with visiting counterparts on Tuesday. But several other leaders also skipped the event, including Prime Minister Narendra Modi of India, the world’s most populous country.
週二，川普在聯合國大會上發表演講，並與到訪的各國領導人舉行了會晤。但還有幾位領導人也缺席了此次盛會，包括世界上人口最多的國家印度的總理納倫德拉·莫迪。
The U.N. has had little influence on many of the top issues of concern at this year’s gathering, including the U.S.-Israeli war against Iran, the conflict in Gaza and A.I.
在本次大會關注的許多重大議題上，聯合國幾乎沒有發揮什麼影響力，包括美以針對伊朗的戰爭、加薩衝突以及人工智慧。
The global body, founded in 1945, has been paralyzed in recent years by divisions on the U.N. Security Council, which holds the power to adopt legally binding resolutions and authorize sanctions or military force.
這個成立於1945年的全球組織近年來因聯合國安理會的內部分歧而陷入癱瘓，安理會擁有通過具有法律約束力的決議，以及授權制裁或使用武力的權力。
Disputes involving the United States, China and Russia, all of whom hold veto power on the council, have repeatedly blocked substantive action on matters like the conflicts in Iran, Ukraine, Gaza, Yemen and South Sudan.
美國、中國和俄羅斯均在安理會擁有否決權，它們的分歧反覆阻礙了針對伊朗、烏克蘭、加薩、葉門和南蘇丹等衝突問題的實質性行動。
But Mr. Xi has real power in Ukraine and Iran. China has helped to sustain Russia’s war machine with economic and political support. China is also Iran’s top trading partner, providing desperately needed cash despite Mr. Trump’s call for all nations to cut off trade with Iran completely.
但習近平在烏克蘭和伊朗問題上擁有實質影響力。中國通過經濟和政治支持幫助維持了俄羅斯的戰爭機器。儘管川普曾呼籲所有國家完全切斷與伊朗的貿易往來，中國仍是伊朗的最大貿易夥伴，為其提供了急需的現金。
The secretary general of the U.N., António Guterres, who has sought to mobilize action on the potential dangers of a technological arms race, led a discussion this week on global governance of A.I. But the United States rejects talk of international A.I. regulation, and while China says it is open to global A.I. cooperation, it opposes calls to dramatically constrain its development.
聯合國秘書長安東尼奧·古特雷斯一直致力於動員各方採取行動應對技術軍備競賽的潛在危險，本週他主持了一場關於人工智慧全球治理的討論。但美國拒絕討論國際人工智慧監管問題，中國雖表示對全球人工智慧合作持開放態度，卻反對大幅限制人工智慧發展的呼籲。
Many experts welcome a conversation about global controls on A.I., such as a ban on its use to create biological weapons. But they concede that any meaningful near-term effort to ensure the technology does not escape human control is likely to require an agreement between Washington and Beijing reminiscent of Cold War nuclear arms control deals between the United States and the Soviet Union.
許多專家歡迎就人工智慧的全球管控展開討論，例如禁止將其用於製造生物武器。但他們承認，任何旨在確保該技術不脫離人類控制的、具有實質意義的近期舉措都可能需要美中之間達成一項協定，類似於冷戰時期美蘇之間的核軍備控制協議。
Mr. Trump said he planned to discuss A.I. with Mr. Xi, although they announced no immediate agreement on the matter.
川普表示計劃與習近平討論人工智慧問題，儘管雙方並未就此達成任何即刻協議。
And in fairness to the dignitaries who gathered in New York, for all the attention the Trump-Xi summit drew, it ended with little more than a promise by both men to meet again soon.
公平地說，儘管川普與習近平的峰會吸引了大量關注，但對齊聚紐約的各國政要而言，此次峰會最終僅以兩國領導人承諾不久後再次會晤告終。
廣告
Nor does Mr. Xi consider the U.N. entirely irrelevant. Under his rule, China has increased its funding for the global body and sought more influence throughout its ranks, in what China hawks in the U.S. insist is an attempt to manipulate the organization and steer it away from Western countries.
習近平也並非認為聯合國完全無關緊要。在他的領導下，中國增加了對這個全球機構的資金支持，並尋求在聯合國各層級中獲得更大影響力，美國的對華強硬派堅稱這是在企圖操縱該組織，使其偏離西方國家。
Some observers also contend that Mr. Xi’s failure to appear in New York was less about the U.N.’s stature and more about demonstrating that China is an equal of the United States. Combining a trip to Washington with one to the U.N. might have diluted the importance of his White House visit.
一些觀察人士還認為，習近平未在紐約露面與其說關乎聯合國的地位，不如說是為了彰顯中國與美國平起平坐的地位。若將訪問華盛頓與出席聯合國大會結合起來，可能會削弱習近平白宮之行的分量。
Mr. Trump for his part has proved less hostile to the U.N. than many of its supporters once feared. He has slashed U.S. funding for the body and withdrawn from dozens of its issue-specific organizations. But the Trump administration notified Congress in August that it would pay $850 million of some $5 billion it owes to the organization.
就川普而言，他對聯合國的敵意並未如許多支持者曾擔心的那樣強烈。他大幅削減了美國對該機構的資助，並退出了數十個專門機構。但川普政府在8月通知國會，它將支付欠聯合國約50億美元中的8.5億美元。
Still, he made no significant effort to win U.N. support before launching a war on Iran, arresting President Nicolás Maduro of Venezuela, or mounting a campaign to force Cuba’s government into major concessions.
儘管如此，在對伊朗發動戰爭、逮捕委內瑞拉總統尼古拉斯·馬杜羅，或發起強迫古巴政府做出重大讓步的運動之前，他並沒有做出重大努力來爭取聯合國的支持。
On Gaza, Mr. Trump created an independent Board of Peace, with a stated goal to resolve the conflict between Israel and Hamas and put into place a hoped-for plan for security, governance and reconstruction of the territory. (The board won the U.N. Security Council’s endorsement last year but does not answer to it.)
關於加薩問題，川普建立了一個獨立的「和平委員會」，其明確目標是解決以色列與哈馬斯之間的衝突，並落實一項旨在保障該地區安全、治理和重建的計劃。（該委員會去年獲得了聯合國安理會的認可，但不對其負責。）
Many experts worry that the U.N. is being pushed aside in a world where international norms are eroding and powerful states feel more emboldened to do as they please — such as the attacks on Ukraine and Iran and perhaps, many fear, a potential Chinese invasion of Taiwan.
在一個國際規範不斷遭到侵蝕、強國愈發肆無忌憚地為所欲為的世界裡，許多專家擔心，聯合國正被邊緣化——比如對烏克蘭和伊朗的襲擊，以及許多人所擔心的中國可能入侵台灣。
廣告
In a recent essay for the journal Just Security, three U.N. experts at the International Crisis Group wrote that the body “is in poor shape,” citing among other things its “political irrelevance in major crises from Ukraine to Sudan to the Persian Gulf.”
在期刊《Just Security》最近發表的一篇文章中，國際危機組織的三位聯合國專家寫道，聯合國「狀況不佳」，並特別提到該組織「在從烏克蘭到蘇丹再到波斯灣等重大危機中已失去政治影響力」。
Formally opening the U.N.’s 81st session earlier this month, Khalilur Rahman, the foreign minister of Bangladesh, acknowledged that observers of the U.N. “are asking whether it works.”
本月早些時候，孟加拉國外交部長哈利勒·拉赫曼在聯合國第81屆會議開幕式上致辭時承認，聯合國觀察人士「正在質疑它是否還在發揮作用」。
Mr. Rahman lamented the U.N.’s failure to prevent or end numerous conflicts, and conceded that it required reform.
拉赫曼對聯合國未能阻止或結束眾多衝突感到遺憾，並承認聯合國需要改革。
But he insisted that the U.N. still performs vital work, noting its recent security and development efforts in such places as Myanmar, Iraq and Haiti. For Haiti, the U.N. approved the deployment last fall of a beefed-up police force to battle marauding gangs.
但他堅持認為，聯合國仍在開展至關重要的工作，並提到了聯合國近期在緬甸、伊拉克和海地等地為維護安全與促進發展所做的努力。在海地，聯合國去年秋天批准部署了一支加強型警察部隊，以打擊橫行霸道的黑幫。
The U.N. has long been accused of failure and irrelevance, Mr. Rahman said. “Yet, through it all, the United Nations has persevered. Not because it is perfect, but because it is irreplaceable, and because the alternative is simply unfathomable.”
拉赫曼表示，聯合國長期以來一直被指責為失敗和無關緊要。「然而，經歷了這一切，聯合國依然堅持下來。不是因為它完美，而是因為它不可替代，沒有它後果將不堪設想。」

Michael Crowley是時報報導美國國務院和外交政策的記者。他曾在近三十個國家進行報導，並經常隨國務卿出訪。
