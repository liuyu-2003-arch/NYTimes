對韓國而言，美沙核協議暴露美國雙重標準

For South Korea, U.S.-Saudi Nuclear Deal Exposes a Double Standard

CHOE SANG-HUN • 2026-07-24
For decades, South Korea was an example of the United States’ unyielding stance on nuclear nonproliferation.
幾十年來，韓國一直是美國在核不擴散問題上保持堅定立場的一個典型例子。
In the 1970s, the United States pressured South Korea, a steadfast ally with whom it shared nuclear technology, to give up a clandestine nuclear weapons program. Then, it barred the country from enriching its own reactor fuel. The restriction held firm even after Seoul joined a nonproliferation treaty, accepted rigorous safeguards, and built one of the world’s largest nuclear power industries. Nor did the policy waver as North Korea built a nuclear arsenal on the South’s doorstep.
上世紀70年代，美國曾向韓國施壓，要求其放棄祕密核武器計劃——儘管韓國是與其共享核技術的堅定盟友。此後，美國又禁止韓國自行濃縮反應堆燃料。即便韓國加入了不擴散核武器條約、接受了嚴格的保障監督，並建成了規模居世界前列的核電產業，這一限制也未曾鬆動。即便朝鮮在韓國門口建成了核武庫，美國的這一政策也未動搖。
Now, the Trump administration has apparently shattered that standard. By striking a deal that could lead to Saudi Arabia enriching uranium on its own soil, Washington has prompted an inevitable question in Seoul: Why Saudi Arabia, and not us?
如今，川普政府似乎打破了這一標準。華盛頓與沙烏地阿拉伯達成一項可能導致其在境內進行鈾濃縮的協議，這在首爾引發了一個不可避免的問題：為什麼是沙烏地阿拉伯，而不是我們？
South Korea has long negotiated with the United States for enrichment rights. While its Foreign Ministry declined to comment directly on the U.S.–Saudi accord, it said on Thursday that it was closely monitoring “trends in U.S. foreign policy on the nuclear energy sector” for potential impacts on its own talks. While the deal raises expectations for progress in South Korea’s negotiations with Washington, it could also seed deep friction in the seven-decade-old alliance if Washington continues to ignore Seoul’s demands.
長期以來，韓國一直與美國就鈾濃縮權進行談判。雖然韓國外交部拒絕就美沙協議直接置評，但週四表示正在密切關注「美國核能領域外交政策趨勢」對韓美談判可能產生的影響。雖然該協議提升了人們對韓美談判取得進展的預期，但如果華盛頓繼續無視首爾的要求，也可能在兩國長達70年的同盟關係中埋下深刻分歧的種子。
廣告
“It is clear that the Trump administration is willing to make major geopolitical bargains with less emphasis on international norms and institutions,” said Leif-Eric Easley, an international studies professor at Ewha Womans University in Seoul.
首爾梨花女子大學國際研究教授萊夫·埃里克·伊斯利表示：「很明顯，川普政府願意進行重大的地緣政治交易，而不太重視國際規範和制度。」
On Thursday, President Trump said the agreement is contingent on Saudi Arabia normalizing relations with Israel, putting its immediate future in doubt.
週四，川普總統表示，這項協議的前提條件是沙烏地阿拉伯與以色列實現關係正常化，這使協議的近期前景充滿不確定性。
Still, the contrast between Washington’s dealings with Saudi Arabia and South Korea exposes a stark double standard in American nuclear policy. Washington appears to have paved the way for enrichment rights for Saudi Arabia for power plants it has yet to build, while denying them to South Korea, which already operates 26 reactors and plans to build more to meet surging energy demands for the A.I. revolution. It has exempted Saudi Arabia from intrusive inspections by the International Atomic Energy Agency, which South Korea has long submitted to to assure the international community that no technology is diverted to weapons projects.
然而，華盛頓對待沙烏地阿拉伯和韓國的不同方式暴露出美國核政策中明顯的雙重標準。華盛頓似乎為沙烏地阿拉伯尚未建成的核電站鋪平了鈾濃縮的道路；與此同時，它卻拒絕給予韓國同樣的權利，後者已在運行26座核反應堆，並計劃建設更多核電設施，以滿足人工智慧革命帶來的不斷增長的能源需求。美國還豁免沙烏地阿拉伯接受國際原子能機構的侵入性檢查，而韓國長期以來一直接受這類檢查，向國際社會證明其核技術不會被轉用於核武項目。
Many in South Korea find this American concession especially jarring given the geopolitical risks. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman of Saudi Arabia has openly warned that his kingdom would build a bomb if Iran did. By contrast, South Korea’s president, Lee Jae Myung, has repeatedly promised that Seoul will not pursue nuclear weapons, choosing instead to rely on the U.S. security guarantees for deterrence as North Korea expands its nuclear arsenal.
許多韓國人認為，考慮到地緣政治風險，美國的這一讓步尤其令人難以接受。沙烏地阿拉伯王儲穆罕默德·本·薩勒曼曾公開警告，如果伊朗造出核彈，沙烏地阿拉伯也會跟進。相比之下，韓國總統李在明已多次承諾首爾不會追求核武器，而是在朝鮮不斷擴大核武庫的情況下，繼續依靠美國提供的安全保障來實現威懾。
“The danger of a breakout by Seoul is likely lower because of its continuous close coordination with Washington on extended deterrence,” Professor Easley said, adding that unlike in the Middle East, “major war on the Korean Peninsula has been deterred for decades.”
「首爾核突破的風險可能更低，因為它與華盛頓在延伸威懾方面持續密切協調，」伊斯利教授表示。他還指出，與中東不同，「朝鮮半島幾十年來一直沒有發生大規模戰爭。」
As South Korea was pressured to abandon its weapons program, it signed a treaty with the United States in 1972 that barred enrichment without American consent. Even during a 2015 revision of the agreement, Washington mandated that any enrichment require written bilateral agreement.
在韓國被迫放棄核武器計劃的時候，它在1972年與美國簽署了一項條約，未經美國同意不得進行鈾濃縮。即使在2015年修訂該協議時，華盛頓仍規定任何濃縮活動都需經雙邊書面同意。
廣告
Consequently, South Korea imports all of its uranium fuel. Seoul now wants independent enrichment capabilities to secure its fuel supply chain. It also wants to recycle spent fuel to reduce waste storage volume — a massive political headache in a densely populated country with a vocal environmental movement.
因此，韓國目前所有鈾燃料都依賴進口。如今，首爾希望獲得獨立的鈾濃縮能力，以保障燃料供應鏈安全。同時，它也希望能夠對乏燃料進行再處理，以減少核廢料儲存量——這對於一個人口密集、環保運動活躍的國家而言是一個巨大的政治難題。
The U.S.-Saudi deal will likely take center stage when South Korea and the United States resume talks to implement the comprehensive investment and security agreement struck by Mr. Trump and Mr. Lee in November.
 當韓國和美國重啟談判，以落實川普和李在明去年11月達成的全面投資和安全協議時，美沙協議很可能成為焦點。
Back then, Washington appeared to address Seoul’s grievances by promising to support a “process that will lead to” civil enrichment of uranium and reprocessing of spent fuel by South Korea. He also approved South Korea’s plan to build nuclear-powered attack submarines, pledging American help to secure uranium fuel for the vessels.
當時，華盛頓回應了韓國的不滿，承諾支持一個「將促成」韓國進行民用鈾濃縮和乏燃料再處理的進程。川普還批准了韓國建造核動力攻擊潛艇的計劃，並承諾美國將幫助韓國確保這些潛艇所需的鈾燃料供應。
However, the broad wording left critical details unresolved, including whether South Korea could operate enrichment or reprocessing facilities on its own soil and whether those facilities would also supply fuel for nuclear submarines.
然而，協議中的寬泛措辭留下了關鍵問題尚未解決，包括韓國是否可以在本國領土上自行運營鈾濃縮或乏燃料再處理設施，以及這些設施是否也能為核潛艇提供燃料。
Talks aimed at resolving such questions were sidetracked by a separate trade dispute, and working-level negotiations only resumed last month.
旨在解決這些問題的談判因貿易爭端而中斷，工作層面的談判上月才重新恢復。
South Korea aims to launch its first domestic nuclear submarine within a decade, using low-enriched fuel to ease proliferation fears. But the pathway remains unclear.
韓國計劃在十年內讓第一艘國產核動力潛艇下水，並使用低濃縮鈾燃料，以降低核擴散方面的擔憂。但目前實現這一目標的路徑仍不明確。
廣告
In 2004, the I.A.E.A. investigated South Korean scientists for unauthorized enrichment experiments. Today, many in South Korea openly view uranium enrichment as a vital path to “nuclear latency” — the ability to rapidly develop weapons if Mr. Trump’s transactional diplomacy renders the U.S. security guarantee, known as the nuclear umbrella, unreliable.
2004年，國際原子能機構曾調查韓國科學家進行未經授權的鈾濃縮實驗一事。如今，韓國國內許多人公開將鈾濃縮視為成為「核門檻」國家的重要途徑。「核門檻國家」意味著，如果川普式的交易型外交導致美國的安全承諾（即核保護傘）變得不再可靠，韓國能夠迅速發展核武器。
Despite the South Korean government’s repeated denials of any intent to build atomic weapons, public and political backing for a sovereign nuclear deterrent has increased in recent years, while North Korea’s nuclear threats have escalated. In December, Pyongyang unveiled the hull of what it described as its first nuclear-powered strategic guided missile submarine.
儘管韓國政府多次否認有意發展核武器，但近年來，隨著朝鮮核威脅不斷升級，韓國公眾和政界對建立自主核威懾力量的支持正在增加。去年12月，平壤曝光了一艘它稱之為首艘核動力戰略制導導彈潛艇的艇體。

Choe Sang-Hun是時報駐首爾首席記者，報導韓國和朝鮮新聞。
