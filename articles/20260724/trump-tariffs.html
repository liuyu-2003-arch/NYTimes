美國將對80多個國家加徵約10%關稅

U.S. to Impose Tariffs on Over 80 Nations, Setting New Grounds for Trade Policy

ANA SWANSON • 2026-07-24
The Trump administration will impose tariffs of around 10 percent on goods from more than 80 countries on Friday, its latest effort to put in place an expansive protectionist policy that has been repeatedly challenged in court.
川普政府將於週五對來自80多個國家的商品加徵約10%的關稅，這是其落實一項大規模保護主義政策的最新舉措，而該政策此前已多次在法院受到挑戰。
The tariffs will range from 10 percent to 12.5 percent and take effect at 12:01 a.m. on Friday, replacing a global 10 percent duty set to lapse at the same time. Mr. Trump issued that earlier tariff in February, after the Supreme Court struck down duties he imposed last year.
關稅稅率將在10%至12.5%之間，於週五凌晨0時01分生效，取代屆時到期的全球統一10%關稅。川普於今年2月宣布了此前的這項關稅，在此之前，最高法院推翻了他去年實施的關稅。
The move provides fresh evidence of Mr. Trump’s intention to transform global trade, despite numerous court challenges and the protests of U.S. consumers and businesses that have been stuck with higher tax bills. Over the past 17 months, the Trump administration has cycled through trade law after trade law as it tries to build a system to shield the U.S. economy from foreign competition.
此舉再次表明，儘管法院多次提出挑戰，且美國消費者和企業因稅負增加而抗議，川普仍有意改變全球貿易格局。過去17個月來，川普政府不斷嘗試各種貿易法，試圖構建一套保護美國經濟免受外國競爭影響的體系。
The duties will be issued under Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974, which allows the president to impose tariffs on foreign countries that engage in unreasonable or discriminatory trade practices. The administration has cited the failure of foreign countries to pass or enforce laws banning the importation of goods made by forced labor into their own countries, saying that disadvantages U.S. businesses that do follow such laws.
此次關稅將依據1974年《貿易法》第301條徵收。該條款允許總統對存在不合理或歧視性貿易做法的外國徵收關稅。川普政府聲稱：外國未能通過或執行禁止進口使用強迫勞動生產的商品的法律，這使遵守此類法律的美國企業在競爭中處於不利地位。
廣告
Canada, which will be subject to a 10 percent tariff under the arrangement, already prohibits importing forced labor goods. The European Union, also at 10 percent, has a ban that is scheduled to take effect in December 2027. But Trump officials say that the governments have not effectively enforced those laws.
根據這一安排，加拿大將被徵收10%的關稅。事實上，加拿大早已禁止進口強迫勞動產品。歐盟同樣適用10%的關稅，而其禁止進口強迫勞動產品的法規計劃於2027年12月生效。但川普政府官員表示，這些政府並沒有切實執行這些法律。
The United States has arguably the strongest restrictions on forced labor imports of any country. It has prohibited the importation of goods made with slave labor for nearly a century, though it still allows prison labor under conditions that labor organizations consider coercive. In 2021, the United States passed a law banning imports from a region of China where it had found forced labor to be rampant.
可以說，美國對強迫勞動產品進口的限制是世界上最嚴格的國家之一。近一個世紀以來，美國一直禁止進口奴隸勞動生產的商品，不過，它仍允許勞工組織認為具有強迫性質的監獄勞動。2021年，美國通過一項法律，禁止進口來自中國一個被認定強迫勞動現象普遍存在地區的產品。
The Trump administration also pushed Canada and Mexico to adopt a prohibition on imports made by forced labor as part of prior trade negotiations, and 10 other countries have committed to bans as part of trade deals negotiated over the past year, a senior administration official said.
一位政府高級官員表示，在此前的貿易談判中，川普政府還推動加拿大和墨西哥採納禁止進口強迫勞動產品的規定；此外，在過去一年達成的貿易協議中，還有另外10個國家承諾實施類似禁令。
But critics say the United States, too, has shortfalls on its labor protections. And some say that the administration has seized on forced labor as merely the most convenient way to reimpose tariffs struck down by the Supreme Court.
但批評人士指出，美國自身的勞工保護也存在不足。還有人表示，川普政府不過是借強迫勞動作為最方便的藉口，以重新恢復被最高法院推翻的關稅政策。
Peter Harrell, a visiting scholar at Georgetown Law School and a former Biden administration official, said that the small differential in tariffs between Canada and the European Union on one hand and China on the other “just brings home that USTR is using this forced labor investigation as a pretext to impose tariffs that Trump wants to impose for his own economic theories and preferences.”
喬治城大學法學院訪問學者、前拜登政府官員彼得·哈雷爾表示，加拿大和歐盟與中國之間的關稅差異很小，「這恰恰說明美國貿易代表辦公室只是將強迫勞動調查作為藉口，徵收那些川普基於自己的經濟理論和政策偏好本來就想徵收的關稅。」
“It’s not really about forced labor,” he added.
「這實際上與強迫勞動無關，」他補充道。
廣告
While Jamieson Greer, the U.S. trade representative, and other U.S. officials have publicly said they cannot prejudge the outcome of the trade investigations, administration officials have privately reassured multiple foreign governments that their tariff rates will end up being the same as in deals negotiated last year, people familiar with the discussions said.
儘管美國貿易代表賈米森·格里爾及其他美國官員公開表示，他們不能預先判斷貿易調查的結果，但據知情人士透露，政府官員私下已向多個外國政府保證，它們最終適用的關稅稅率將與去年談判達成的協議一致。
“Trump’s next trade scheme is ordering USTR to reconstruct his illegal global tariffs under the guise of addressing forced labor,” Senator Ron Wyden, Democrat of Oregon, said in a Congressional hearing Wednesday. “If the administration wants to get serious about forced labor, the first step is to look at its own enforcement record.”
「川普的下一個貿易計劃是命令美國貿易代表辦公室以解決強迫勞動問題為幌子，重新構建他那些已被裁定違法的全球關稅，」奧勒岡州民主党參議員榮恩·懷登在週三的國會聽證會上表示。「如果政府真想嚴肅對待強迫勞動問題，第一步就是審視自身的執法記錄。」
The new tariffs will exempt oil and gas and certain national resources, as well as goods already covered under the United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement, or national security-related tariffs Mr. Trump has imposed on cars, steel and other goods.
新關稅將豁免石油、天然氣及部分國家資源，以及美墨加協定涵蓋的商品或川普已對汽車、鋼鐵等商品徵收的國家安全相關關稅。
More duties are likely to be on the way in the coming weeks. The administration has proposed another batch of tariffs, also using Section 301, on 15 countries and the European Union to offset what the White House calls unfair practices in their manufacturing sectors. An administration official said Thursday that an investigation was continuing.
未來數週可能還會有更多關稅出台。政府已提議依據第301條對15個國家和歐盟再徵收一批關稅，用於補償白宮所稱這些國家製造業中的「不公平做法」。一名政府官員週四表示，相關調查仍在進行中。
Mr. Trump has long maintained that U.S. tariffs on imports were unfairly low, and he came into office wanting to transform that system. But his efforts have been met with plenty of stops, starts and setbacks, reflecting the fact that a president’s legal authority over tariff policy is in some ways limited.
川普長期以來一直認為，美國對進口商品徵收的關稅過低，因此他上任後便希望徹底改變這一體系。但他的努力屢屢受阻、反覆啟動又受挫，這也反映出，總統在關稅政策上的法律權限在某些方面其實是有限的。
The Constitution gives Congress the power to regulate trade, but legislators have written numerous laws that allow the president to issue tariffs in certain circumstances. But typically, those laws are designed to help the president address unfair trade practices in certain countries or industries, not to replace the U.S. tariff system wholesale.
憲法賦予國會監管貿易的權力，但國會也制定了許多法律，允許總統在特定情形下徵收關稅。不過，這些法律通常都是為了幫助總統應對某些國家或行業的不公平貿易行為，而不是讓總統全面取代美國現行的關稅體系。
廣告
Mr. Harrell said that Mr. Trump’s use of Section 301 was much broader that the statute intended, and that it likely would be challenged in court. While Section 301 was supposed to be used to create leverage to push a country to address an unfair trade practice, he said, “Trump is reinterpreting the statute to try to impose perpetual tariffs on almost all imports.”
哈雷爾表示，川普對第301條的運用遠遠超出了這項法律原本的立法目的，很可能會再次受到法院挑戰。他說，第301條本應被用來向他國施壓，迫使其糾正不公平貿易行為，而「川普正在重新解讀該條款，試圖對幾乎所有進口商品徵收永久性關稅」。
“They are showing that on tariffs, they can kind of run faster than the courts will, and they’re going to force the courts to keep playing catch up,” he added.
「他們表明，在關稅問題上，他們的行動速度可以比法院更快，並迫使法院不斷疲於追趕，」他補充道。
The Supreme Court invalidated Mr. Trump’s preferred legal tool in February, when it said his use of an international emergency law to impose tariffs was unlawful and ordered roughly $160 billion in tariff revenue to be refunded. Mr. Trump had used the emergency law to announce his “Liberation Day” tariffs on foreign countries last year, and to penalize Canada, Mexico and China for their real or alleged role in channeling fentanyl to the United States.
今年2月，最高法院否定了川普最常使用的法律工具，裁定他依據一項國際緊急經濟權力法徵收關稅屬於違法，並下令退還約1600億美元的關稅收入。川普此前正是依據這部緊急狀態法於去年宣布了針對外國的「解放日」關稅，並以加拿大、墨西哥和中國在吩坦尼流入美國問題上的真實或所謂責任為由施以懲罰。
After the Supreme Court decision, Mr. Trump turned to Section 122 of the Trade Act of 1974 as a stopgap, a law which had never been used to impose tariffs before. Section 122 allows a president to impose a tariff to address balance of payments issues, but it has a 150-day time limit that is set to expire early Friday.
最高法院作出裁決後，川普轉而援引1974年《貿易法》第122條作為過渡措施。這項法律此前從未被用於徵收關稅。第122條允許總統為解決國際收支問題徵收關稅，但規定最長只能實施150天，而該時限將於本週五凌晨到期。
Mr. Trump’s use of Section 122 has also faced legal challenges. A group of small businesses and a coalition of states each sued the administration, claiming that the government did not satisfy the law’s strict criteria. In May, a majority of judges on a federal trade court agreed, handing down the second major tariff defeat against Mr. Trump.
川普援引第122條的做法同樣遭遇了法律挑戰。一批小企業和多個州的聯盟相繼對政府提起訴訟，聲稱政府未能滿足該法律的嚴格適用條件。今年5月，聯邦貿易法院多數法官支持了這一立場，這是川普第二次在關稅問題上遭遇重大挫折。
The administration appealed the decision, and the courts have allowed the government to continue collecting the 10 percent tax on imports as the appeal progresses.
川普政府隨後提起上訴，而法院允許政府在上訴審理期間繼續徵收這項10%的進口關稅。
廣告
The provision that the administration is turning to next, Section 301, is more battle tested. Mr. Trump used it to impose tariffs on China in his first term, and his use of it has survived multiple challenges in court. But it has never before been used in such a sweeping way, to issue tariffs on dozens of countries simultaneously.
政府接下來準備援引的第301條經過了更多的司法檢驗。川普在第一任期內曾援引該條款對中國徵收關稅，且經受住了多輪法律挑戰。但以此條款同時對數十個國家大規模徵收關稅尚屬首次。
The administration also turned to another obscure trade law earlier this week, when Mr. Trump signed orders to impose a 50 percent tariff on billions of dollars of Canadian exports. The law, the Tariff Act of 1930, also known as the Smoot-Hawley Tariff Act, was written by Congress to shield American businesses during the onset of the Great Depression, though many historians believe it actually deepened the crisis. The Section 338 provision of the act that the administration used had never been used to impose tariffs.
本週早些時候，川普政府還援引了另一部鮮為人知的貿易法律，當時川普簽署命令，對價值數十億美元的加拿大出口產品徵收50%的關稅。他所依據的是1930年《關稅法》，也就是《斯穆特—霍利關稅法》。這部法律是國會在大蕭條初期制定的，旨在保護美國企業，但許多歷史學家認為，它實際上加劇了當時的經濟危機。而川普政府此次援引的該法第338條此前也從未被用來徵收關稅。
Testifying in Congress Wednesday, Mr. Greer said that the administration remained intent on imposing tariffs, regardless of the legal approach.
格里爾週三在國會作證時表示，無論採用何種法律依據，政府都將繼續徵收關稅。
“The specific authorities this administration is using have changed, but the trade strategy has not,” Mr. Greer said. “We are committed to continuing to use tariffs and to negotiate deals to support the re-industrialization of our economy, protect American workers and increase their wages and shrink our trade deficit.”
「這屆政府具體援引的法律依據在變，但貿易戰略沒有變，」格里爾說。「我們將繼續堅定地運用關稅，並通過談判達成協議，以支持我們經濟的再工業化，保護美國工人，提高他們的薪資水平，縮減貿易赤字。」
