高市早苗捍衛「恩師」安倍，推進民族主義議程

Japan’s Leader Invokes Her Hero, Shinzo Abe, to Drive Nationalist Agenda

赫海威 • 2026-07-24
Inside a hushed hall in Tokyo on a recent afternoon, Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi of Japan stood before a giant portrait of her mentor, Shinzo Abe, the slain Japanese leader, and bowed.
近日的一個下午，在東京一座安靜的大廳裡，日本首相高市早苗站在她的恩師、遇刺身亡的日本領導人安倍晉三的巨幅畫像前，鞠躬致敬。
It had been four years since Mr. Abe, a giant in Japanese politics and the country’s longest-serving prime minister, was shot and killed while giving a speech outside a train station. Ms. Takaichi, his protégé, had come to pay her respects — and to vow to carry on his vision. She told the crowd of right-wing politicians, activists, journalists and business leaders that she sometimes still imagined him sitting on the couch of the prime minister’s office.
安倍晉三是日本政壇巨頭，也是該國在任時間最長的首相。他在車站外發表演講時遇刺身亡，至今已過去四年。作為他的門生，高市早苗前來致哀，誓言繼承他的遺志。她告訴在場的右翼政治人士、活動人士、記者和商界領袖，她有時仍會想像他坐在首相辦公室的沙發上。
“I could never fill Prime Minister Abe’s shoes; my days are filled with nothing but criticism when compared to him,” she said. “Even so, I want to be a fighter politician like him. I tell myself that I must be.”
「我永遠無法取代安倍首相；與他相比，我的每一天都充斥著批評，」她說。「即便如此，我也想成為像他一樣不停奮戰的政治家。我告訴自己，我必須做到。」
Since winning election nine months ago, Ms. Takaichi, Japan’s first female prime minister, has frequently leveraged her ties to Mr. Abe to rally support for her nationalist agenda, often referring to him and his policies in major speeches.
自九個月前贏得選舉以來，日本首位女首相高市早苗頻繁利用她與安倍的關係來為她的民族主義議程爭取支持，並經常在重要演講中提及他以及他的政策。
She has hailed his vision of a more muscular Japan that is a driving force for strength and stability across Asia. She has invoked his legacy to call for greater military spending and the establishment of Japan’s first intelligence agency since World War II. She has dropped his name while wooing foreign leaders such as President Trump and Prime Minister Narendra Modi of India.
她讚揚安倍晉三關於建立一個更強大的日本並使之成為整個亞洲力量與穩定驅動力的願景。她援引他的政治遺產，呼籲增加軍事開支，並建立日本自二戰以來的首個情報機構。在爭取川普總統和印度總理莫迪等外國領導人的支持時，她也曾提及他的名字。
廣告
Yet Mr. Abe remains a divisive figure in Japan because of his break with decades of postwar pacifism; his support for changing how the country’s World War II-era history is taught in schools; and a rise in economic inequality during his tenure. His killing, by a 41-year-old man who was protesting the ruling Liberal Democratic Party’s ties to the Unification Church, a fringe religious group, prompted a range of reactions. While many people were outraged, some sympathized with the assassin, noting his difficult childhood. Others said that a state funeral for Mr. Abe was a waste of money. (His assassin, Tetsuya Yamagami, was sentenced to life in prison earlier this year.)
然而，安倍晉三在日本仍是一個引發分歧的人物，因為他打破了數十年的戰後和平主義；支持改變學校對二戰時期歷史的教學方式；在他的任期內，經濟不平等現象出現加劇。他被一名41歲男子刺殺，該男子以此抗議執政的自民黨與邊緣宗教團體「統一教會」的關係。這一事件引發了一系列反應。雖然許多人感到憤怒，但也有人理解兇手，同情他艱難的童年。還有人說為安倍晉三舉行國葬是浪費金錢。（今年早些時候，刺殺他的兇手山上徹也被判處終身監禁。）
Despite Mr. Abe’s complicated legacy, Ms. Takaichi has emerged as one of his chief defenders, hailing him as a transformative leader who elevated Japan’s stature on the global stage.
儘管安倍晉三留下的政治遺產錯綜複雜，但高市早苗已成為他的主要捍衛者之一，她稱讚安倍晉三是一位具有變革精神的領導人，提升了日本在全球舞台上的地位。
This triumphant account of Mr. Abe’s tenure has been on display recently across Japan, including at a sold-out exhibition by the Shinzo Abe Digital Museum, an organization founded by Mr. Abe’s allies in business and government. The exhibition featured more than 100 objects from his life, including the microphone he was holding when he was shot on July 8, 2022, in the ancient city of Nara, Ms. Takaichi’s hometown.
最近，對安倍任期的這種頌揚式敘述在日本各地展示，其中包括「安倍晉三數字博物館「舉辦的一場門票售罄的展覽，「安倍晉三數字博物館「由安倍在商政兩界的盟友創辦。該展覽的展品包括他生前的100多件物品，包括2022年7月8日他在古城奈良遇刺時手裡拿著的麥克風，那裡也是高市早苗的故鄉。
Mr. Abe’s widow, Akie Abe, said during a visit to the exhibition in Nara on Sunday that her husband’s message “has been entrusted to” Ms. Takaichi.
安倍的遺孀安倍昭惠週日參觀在奈良舉行的展覽時表示，她丈夫的使命「已託付給」高市早苗。
Same Playbook, New Challenges
相同的策略，全新的挑戰
Ms. Takaichi, who met Mr. Abe in the 1990s when they were both rising politicians and later served in his cabinet, has sought to portray herself as the inheritor of his legacy, at a time when Japan is facing rising tensions with China, Russia and North Korea.
高市早苗在20世紀90年代結識安倍晉三，當時兩人都是冉冉升起的政治新星，她後來還在安倍內閣中任職。在日本與中、俄、朝三國緊張局勢不斷升級之際，她一直試圖將自己塑造成安倍晉三政治遺產的繼承人。
廣告
“She’s embracing what Abe did and running with it,” said Sheila A. Smith, a senior fellow at the Council on Foreign Relations.
「她正在全盤接受安倍晉三的一切，並以此為基礎繼續前進，」美國外交關係委員會高級研究員希拉·史密斯說。
The question now, analysts say, is whether Ms. Takaichi can adapt Mr. Abe’s vision to new challenges. Among them:
分析人士表示，現在的問題是，高市早苗能否將安倍晉三的願景進行調整，以適應新的挑戰。其中包括：
• Beijing is rapidly expanding its military and economic influence in Asia, and has led a monthslong campaign against Ms. Takaichi to punish her for a comment she made in support of Taiwan, which China claims as its territory.
• 北京正迅速擴大其在亞洲的軍事和經濟影響力，並針對高市早苗發起了一場持續數月的攻勢，以懲罰她發表的支持台灣的言論。中國聲稱台灣是其領土。
• Japan’s economic outlook has grown more complicated as the country grapples with inflation for the first time in decades and a stubbornly weak yen.
• 隨著日本幾十年來首次面臨通貨膨脹，加上日元持續疲軟，該國的經濟前景變得更加複雜。
• The country’s demographic decline is also accelerating, hurting the economy, causing labor shortages and undermining defense priorities like military recruitment.
• 日本的人口衰退也在加速，這拖累了經濟，導致了勞動力短缺，並削弱了徵兵等國防優先事項。
Mieko Nakabayashi, a political science professor at Waseda University in Tokyo, said Ms. Takaichi was clinging to Mr. Abe’s playbook while ignoring new realities. She pointed to Ms. Takaichi’s support for expansionary fiscal policies, which some analysts say could exacerbate inflation.
東京早稻田大學政治學教授中林美惠子表示，高市早苗一味沿襲安倍晉三的策略，卻忽視了新的現實。她指出，高市早苗支持擴張性財政政策，一些分析人士稱這可能會加劇通貨膨脹。
“Her popularity depends on Mr. Abe’s legacy,” Ms. Nakabayashi said. “Her biggest selling point is that she is trying to realize everything that Mr. Abe wanted to do, at whatever cost.”
「她的支持率依賴於安倍的政治遺產，」中林美惠子說。「她最大的賣點在於，無論付出什麼代價，她都致力於實現安倍想要做的一切。」
Ms. Takaichi worked for Mr. Abe during his first stint as prime minister in 2006, and during his second term, which began in 2012. She rose to the powerful position of minister for internal affairs and communications.
高市早苗曾在2006年安倍晉三首次擔任首相期間以及始於2012年的第二個任期內為他工作。她一路晉陞，升任總務大臣這一要職。
During that time she became a fierce defender of his policies, including efforts to revise Japan’s Constitution to unfetter its military, and his economic program, which emphasized cheap cash and government stimulus efforts.
在那期間，她成為安倍晉三政策的堅定捍衛者，包括旨在修改日本憲法以解除軍隊束縛的努力，以及強調低成本資金和政府刺激措施的經濟計劃。
廣告
“I was deeply drawn not only to his qualities as a politician but also to his character as a human being,” Ms. Takaichi said of her work with Mr. Abe, in a letter on display at the new exhibition.
在此次新展覽中展出的一封信中，高市早苗如此描述她與安倍晉三共事的經歷：「我不僅深深地被他作為政治人士的品質所吸引，也被他作為一個人的人格魅力所吸引。」
Tobias Harris, the founder of the advisory firm Japan Foresight and the author of a book on Mr. Abe, said that it remained to be seen whether Ms. Takaichi had fully grasped the lessons of his tenure. He said it was notable that she identified with Mr. Abe’s fighting spirit, given that Mr. Abe’s success in his second term came in part from tempering those impulses.
諮詢公司「日本遠見」的創始人、一部有關安倍晉三書籍的作者托比亞斯·哈利斯表示，高市早苗是否充分吸取了安倍晉三任期內的教訓目前還有待觀察。他說，值得注意的是，她認同安倍晉三的戰鬥精神，而事實上，安倍晉三在第二任期的成功部分源於他剋制了那些衝動。
“We have seen her willingness to bulldoze her opponents over and over again,” Mr. Harris said. “But as Abe learned, there is a cost to pay for that.”
「我們一次又一次地看到她不惜碾壓對手的意願，」哈利斯說。「但正如安倍所意識到的那樣，這種做法是要付出代價的。」
The Shinzo Abe Digital Museum raised more than $338,000 from individual donors for the exhibition. On a recent day, visitors left notes on a wall with messages such as “You were our hope!” and “We will carry forward your mission.” A shop sold Abe-themed calendars and playing cards that included scenes of him golfing with President Trump.
「安倍晉三數字博物館」從個人捐助者那裡籌集了超過33.8萬美元用於此次展覽。前不久的一天，參觀者在牆上留下了諸如「您曾是我們的希望！」和「我們將繼承您的使命」之類的留言。一家商店出售以安倍晉三為主題的日曆和撲克牌，其中包括他與川普總統打高爾夫球的場景。
The exhibition became a gathering spot for his supporters, drawing nearly 7,000 visitors in Tokyo and Nara, including more than a half dozen of Ms. Takaichi’s cabinet officials.
展覽成為安倍晉三支持者的聚集地，在東京和奈良吸引了近7000名參觀者，包括超過六名高市內閣官員。
Mr. Abe’s supporters are hopeful about Ms. Takaichi, though they said she would face different challenges than Mr. Abe, including disruptions to the energy supply, foreign wars and political volatility in the United States.
安倍晉三的支持者對高市早苗抱有希望，儘管他們表示，她將面臨與安倍晉三不同的挑戰，包括能源供應中斷、外國戰爭以及美國的政治動盪。
廣告
Fumiyo Kato, 48, who runs a weather data company in Tokyo, cried during a recent visit to the exhibition as she viewed Mr. Abe’s personal artifacts: a childhood notebook, a portrait of his father, a wedding photo. She said that Ms. Takaichi appeared to be carrying on his legacy, but it was too early to say whether she would succeed.
48歲的加藤文代（音）在東京經營一家氣象數據公司。最近參觀展覽時，她看到安倍的個人物品——一本童年筆記本、一張他父親的肖像、一張結婚照——不禁流下了眼淚。她說高市早苗似乎繼承了他的遺志，但現在斷言她是否會成功還為時過早。
“Abe was a great man,” she said. “He can’t be easily replaced.”
「安倍是一位偉人，」她說。「他不能被輕易取代。」
