澤連斯基稱川普試圖迫使烏克蘭投降

Trump Is Pushing Ukraine to Surrender to Russia, Zelensky Says

CONSTANT MÉHEUT • 2026-10-20
President Trump is trying to force Ukraine to surrender in the war, President Volodymyr Zelensky of Ukraine said in an interview published on Sunday, as a deepening feud between the two leaders drives their relations to new lows.
烏克蘭總統弗拉基米爾·澤連斯基在週日發表的一篇採訪中表示，川普總統正試圖迫使烏克蘭在戰爭中投降，與此同時，兩位領導人之間不斷加深的不和正驅使兩國關係跌至新低。
“I believe steps have been taken to convince Ukraine to capitulate,” Mr. Zelensky told Bloomberg News. He was referring to a string of measures in recent days by the Trump administration that could bolster Russia’s war machine while squeezing Ukraine’s ability to fight.
「我認為已經採取了一些措施來勸說烏克蘭投降，」澤連斯基對彭博新聞社表示。他指的是川普政府最近幾天採取的一系列措施，這些措施可能會增強俄羅斯的戰爭機器，同時削弱烏克蘭的戰鬥能力。
Mr. Zelensky added that he did not want to believe that Washington was seeking a quicker end to the war at Ukraine’s expense. “But that’s how it looks,” he said.
澤連斯基還說，他不想相信華盛頓正在以犧牲烏克蘭為代價尋求更快地結束戰爭。「但看起來就是這樣，」他說。
His comments capped a week in which the leaders traded some of their harshest words yet. Mr. Zelensky said that Mr. Trump had an instinctive affinity for autocrats, a pointed allusion to President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia, while the U.S. leader urged Ukrainians to vote Mr. Zelensky out of office.
他的這番言論為本週雙方領導人之間最尖銳的言辭交鋒畫上了句號。澤連斯基表示，川普對威權領導人有一種本能的親近感，這顯然是指俄羅斯總統普丁；而美國領導人則敦促烏克蘭人通過投票將澤連斯基趕下台。
廣告
Mr. Zelensky on Sunday appeared to tell the NBC show “Meet the Press” that he would run in a wartime presidential election, if one took place. It was the first time he suggested this publicly, after leaving the question open for months.
週日，澤連斯基在接受NBC節目《與媒體見面》採訪時似乎表示，如果舉行戰時總統選舉，他將會參選。此前數月他一直對此問題持保留態度，這是他首次公開表態。
But he played down the prospect of holding a vote during the war. Russia has repeatedly refused a cease-fire that would allow people to cast ballots, and a vast majority of Ukrainians oppose holding elections in wartime.
但他淡化了在戰爭期間舉行投票的可能性。俄羅斯一再拒絕達成停火協議，而停火本可讓民眾參與投票，而且絕大多數烏克蘭人反對在戰時舉行選舉。
The war of words between Mr. Zelensky and Mr. Trump began on Oct. 9, when the American president announced that the United States would buy Russian diesel in an effort to bring down fuel prices before the midterm elections, a deal that could send billions of U.S. dollars into Russia’s economy. He then demanded that Kyiv stop striking Russian refineries, one of its main levers in the war, so that Russian oil could return to the market.
澤連斯基與川普之間的口水戰始於10月9日，當時美國總統宣布美國將購買俄羅斯柴油，以期在期中選舉前壓低燃料價格，這筆交易可能會將數十億美元注入俄羅斯經濟。隨後，他要求基輔停止打擊俄羅斯煉油廠——這是烏克蘭在戰爭中的主要籌碼之一——以便讓俄羅斯石油能夠重返市場。
In a social media post on Sunday night, Mr. Trump openly sided with Russia, the aggressor in the conflict, as he explained why he was offering the Kremlin incentives to negotiate a peace deal. He attacked Mr. Zelensky, saying he “doesn’t want to end this war, and probably never did.”
在週日晚上的社群媒體帖子中，川普公開站在了衝突的侵略者俄羅斯一邊，並解釋了他為何向克里姆林宮提供激勵以談判和平協議。他抨擊澤連斯基，稱他「不想結束這場戰爭，而且可能從來都不想」。
On Monday, the Kremlin spokesman, Dmitri S. Peskov, said that Mr. Putin, Mr. Trump and the Chinese leader, Xi Jinping, could meet next month in Shenzhen, China. The statement fed fears in Ukraine that Mr. Trump is only encouraging Mr. Putin to continue the conflict, and that the two men are conspiring to eventually impose an unfavorable settlement of the war on Kyiv.
週一，克里姆林宮發言人德米特里·佩斯科夫表示，普丁、川普和中國領導人習近平下個月可能會在中國深圳會面。這一聲明加劇了烏克蘭的擔憂，即川普只是在鼓勵普丁繼續衝突，而且兩人正在密謀，最終將一個不利的戰爭解決方案強加給基輔。
Ukraine has accused Washington of restricting the Ukrainian military’s access to a U.S.-provided secure satellite internet network that is important to frontline operations. Ukraine has also complained about what it has described as lapses in U.S. intelligence sharing, which provides early warnings of incoming Russian ballistic missiles.
烏克蘭指責華盛頓限制烏克蘭軍方訪問美國提供的一個安全衛星互聯網網路，該網路對前線行動至關重要。烏克蘭還抱怨稱，美國在情報共享方面存在疏漏，這些情報本應為即將到來的俄羅斯彈道導彈提供預警。
廣告
Ukrainian officials said they received no such warnings on two occasions last week. Mr. Zelensky said it happened again on Sunday night. Ukraine, he wrote in a social media post, got “no early warning from our partners” before missiles struck Kyiv, the Ukrainian capital.
烏克蘭官員表示，上週他們有兩次沒有收到此類預警。澤連斯基說，這種情況在週日晚上再次發生。他在社群媒體帖子中寫道，在導彈襲擊烏克蘭首都基輔之前，烏克蘭「沒有從我們的合作夥伴那裡得到預警」。
U.S. officials have denied halting the sharing of intelligence with Ukraine on ballistic missiles. Secretary of State Marco Rubio told reporters that the lack of warnings last week resulted from “a software problem involving their early warning system” and that he could not speak to what happened on Sunday night.
美國官員否認停止與烏克蘭共享有關彈道導彈的情報。國務卿魯比奧告訴記者，上週缺乏預警是由於「涉及其預警系統的軟體問題」所致，並稱無法就週日晚上發生的情況發表評論。
Yurii Ihnat, a spokesman for the Ukrainian Air Force, confirmed that no early warning came before a first salvo of missiles struck Kyiv at 11 p.m. local time on Sunday. He noted that early warnings did arrive before the next salvo, around 3 a.m. on Monday.
烏克蘭空軍發言人尤裡·伊格納特證實，在當地時間週日晚上11點第一波導彈襲擊基輔之前，沒有收到任何預警。他指出，在週一凌晨3點左右的下一波襲擊之前，確實收到了預警。
Ukraine said Russia had launched 16 ballistic missiles at Kyiv and the surrounding region, along with nearly 270 drones. The Air Force said it had brought down more than 240 of the drones. It did not say whether it had shot down any of the missiles, part of a new policy not to release interception rates for ballistic missiles as Kyiv suffers from interceptor shortages.
烏克蘭表示，俄羅斯向基輔及周邊地區發射了16枚彈道導彈，以及近270架無人機。空軍稱擊落了超過240架無人機。空軍沒有說明是否擊落了任何導彈，這是一項新政策的一部分——在基輔面臨攔截彈短缺的情況下，不公布彈道導彈的攔截率。
The depletion of U.S.-made Patriot interceptors, Ukraine’s only reliable defense against Russian ballistic missiles, stems from surging global demand, most notably in the Middle East. Mr. Zelensky told Bloomberg that Washington was delaying deliveries of the interceptors.
美國製造的「愛國者」攔截彈是烏克蘭防禦俄羅斯彈道導彈的唯一可靠武器，其庫存告急源於全球需求的激增，尤其是在中東地區。澤連斯基告訴彭博社，華盛頓正在延遲交付這些攔截彈。
The shortage can be heard in the way attacks now unfold. When Kyiv had enough interceptors, residents could hear the thud of air defenses firing at incoming missiles. Now, most of what they hear is the blast of missiles striking their targets, unopposed.
從現在襲擊展開的方式中，可以聽出這種短缺的影響。當基輔有足夠的攔截彈時，居民們能聽到防空系統向來襲導彈開火的沉悶聲。現在，他們聽到的大多是導彈毫無阻擋地擊中目標的爆炸聲。
廣告
That was the pattern on Sunday night, too. The first salvo sent explosions reverberating across Kyiv’s city center. Then, when the attack ended, the blasts gave way to an eerie hush.
週日晚上的情況也是如此。第一波齊射使得爆炸聲在基輔市中心迴盪。然後，當襲擊結束時，爆炸聲被一種令人毛骨悚然的寂靜所取代。
In one part of downtown Kyiv, seconds after the strike, a song rose from an apartment, filling the night. It was “How Can I Not Love You, Kyiv of Mine,” a 1962 lyrical ode to the Ukrainian capital that has become an anthem of the city’s defiance under fire.
在基輔市中心的某個地方，襲擊發生幾秒鐘後，一首歌曲從一間公寓裡飄出，迴盪在夜空中。那是《我怎能不愛你，我的基輔》，這是一首創作於1962年、獻給烏克蘭首都的抒情頌歌，如今已成為這座城市在戰火中堅強不屈的讚歌。
