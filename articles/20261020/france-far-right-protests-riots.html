法國騷亂令極右翼獲益

France’s Far Right Seizes the Moment as Rioting Stuns the Country

CATHERINE PORTER • 2026-10-20
After riots erupted this weekend in cities across France, its centrist government blamed the main far-left party, accusing its politicians of inciting the violence. That party blamed the government, accusing it of stirring public anger through years of bad governance and heavy-handed policing.
在週末法國多座城市爆發騷亂後，該國中間派政府將責任歸咎於主要極左翼政黨，指責該黨政治人物煽動暴力。該黨則指責政府，稱其多年治理不善和強硬警察執法激起了公眾憤怒。
What did the main far-right party do? It blamed both, and launched into campaign mode.
主要極右翼政黨做了什麼？它把雙方都指責了一番，並且進入了競選模式。
“We are paying for decades of being soft on crime, of weak authority, of the moral, psychological, and material disarmament of our internal security forces,” Jordan Bardella, the president of the far-right National Rally party, said after emerging from a police station in central Paris on Sunday. He said had gone there to support police officers.
「我們正在為幾十年來對犯罪軟弱、權威缺失，以及對我們國內安全力量在道德、心理和物質上的『解除武裝』付出代價，」極右翼國民聯盟主席若爾當·巴爾代拉週日從巴黎市中心一所警察局走出後說。他說自己去那裡是為了支持警察。
Then he addressed France’s “honest citizens” who were stunned on Sunday by the smashed storefronts and burned-out businesses destroyed by rioters the night before. He said they had an vital role to play in reinstating national stability. And that would be by voting in the coming election for his party.
隨後，他向法國的「誠實公民們」發表講話。這些公民週日看到前一晚騷亂者砸毀的店面和燒毀的商鋪時，都感到震驚。他說這些公民在恢復國家穩定方面發揮著至關重要的作用。而要做到這一點，就是在即將到來的選舉中投票支持他的政黨。
廣告
“People need to wake up now,” Mr. Bardella said. “They need to make the right political choices, and in any case, we stand ready to restore order in the country.”
「人們現在需要醒過來，」巴爾代拉說。「他們需要做出正確的政治選擇，而無論如何，我們隨時準備恢復國家秩序。」
Sunday was a day of mourning, shock and finger pointing in France. The day before, huge peaceful protests for better school conditions and against rising living costs were overshadowed by black-clad and often masked agitators of unclear affiliation who attacked government buildings and shops across the country, sometimes stealing wine or electronics.
週日對法國來說是哀悼、震驚和相互指責的一天。前一天，要求改善學校條件、反對生活成本上漲的大規模和平抗議被一群身份不明、身穿黑衣且往往蒙面的煽動者蒙上了陰影，他們襲擊了全國各地的政府建築和商鋪，有時還偷走葡萄酒或電子產品。
For the National Rally, which bills itself as the party of law and order as well as an advocate for expelling unauthorized immigrants, Sunday was also a day of political opportunity. It is six months before a presidential election that the party’s candidate, Marine Le Pen, is the favorite to win.
對於自詡為法律與秩序之黨、同時主張驅逐非法移民的「國民聯盟」而言，週日也是一個政治良機。距離總統大選還有六個月，該黨候選人瑪麗娜·勒龐被視為熱門。
“The picture of France emerging this morning, in light of all the destruction, plays well into their hands,” said Bruno Cautrès, a political scientist at Sciences Po, a university in Paris. “We can clearly see that the National Rally is not a loser here.”
「鑒於所有這些破壞，今早呈現出的法國景象正中他們下懷，」巴黎政治學院政治學者布魯諾·科特雷說。「我們可以清楚看到，國民聯盟在這件事裡不是輸家。」
The party’s approach mirrors how far-right groups across Europe are presenting themselves as the solution to different kinds of social and governmental challenges. In July, Spain’s government briefly lost control of the borders of a Spanish territory in North Africa, allowing a surge of migrants and handing the country’s far-right party, Vox, a new issue to campaign on. In Germany, the far-right Alternative for Germany party, or AfD, is leading national polls, in part by channeling anxiety over the country’s identity.
該黨的做法映照出歐洲各地極右翼團體如何把自己塑造為不同類型社會和政府挑戰的解決方案。7月，西班牙政府一度失去對該國在北非一塊領土邊界的控制，導致移民大量湧入，並給該國極右翼政黨呼聲黨提供了新的競選議題。在德國，極右翼正當德國選擇黨在民調中領先，部分原因在於該黨利用了民眾對國家身份認同的焦慮情緒。
In France this week, as Mr. Bardella criticized the government’s policing, Ms. Le Pen seized the moment to criticize Jean-Luc Mélenchon, the leader of the main far-left party, France Unbowed, and one of her main rivals for the presidency. She said “legitimate social demands” had been “hijacked by the far left and the rhetoric of Jean-Luc Mélenchon” and his colleagues, whom she accused of fostering chaos by “legitimizing violence” — an accusation that the party denies.
本週在法國，當巴爾代拉批評政府警務時，勒龐抓住時機，批評主要極左政黨不屈法國領導人、也是她總統競選的主要對手之一讓-呂克·梅朗雄。她說「合法的社會訴求」 已被「極左翼以及讓-呂克·梅朗雄的言論」及其同僚「劫持」，她指責他們通過「為暴力正名」助長混亂——該黨否認這一指控。
廣告
The riots arrived at a sensitive time in France, with the country already dealing with a daunting budget deficit and a deadlocked Parliament in which no party has a majority.
騷亂髮生在法國的敏感時刻，該國已在應對嚴峻的預算赤字，以及無一政黨占多數的僵局議會。
“The far right are sure to gain from this,” said Alexis Veller, 53, a filmmaker, who stopped his motorcycle on Sunday morning to take photos of broken storefront windows and tagged walls in central Paris. “We are a very fractured country,” he said. “The situation is very, very worrying.”
「極右翼肯定會從中獲益，」53歲的電影製片人亞歷克西斯·韋勒說。週日早晨，他停下摩托車，在巴黎市中心拍攝破碎的店面窗戶和被塗鴉的牆壁。「我們是一個非常分裂的國家，」他說。「局勢非常、非常令人擔憂。」
Political analysts cautioned that the six months until the election is a long time in politics, and that voters’ motivations are complex and rarely focused on a single issue.
政治分析人士提醒，距選舉還有六個月，在政治領域，這是很長一段時間，而且選民的動機複雜，很少只集中於單一議題。
Still, the violence perfectly aligned with the image the National Rally has long painted of a France divided into two and battling for survival, said Vincent Martigny, a political science professor at the University of Nice, Côte d’Azur.
不過，尼斯蔚藍海岸大學政治學教授樊尚·馬蒂尼說，這場暴力與國民聯盟長期以來描繪的法國形象完全吻合：一個分裂為二、為生存而戰的法國。
The party has portrayed the country as split between the France of the city centers — filled with woke students and rioters, it says — and the “good France” in the quiet countryside, he said.
他說，該黨把國家描繪成分裂為兩部分：一部分是市中心地帶的法國——它聲稱這裡充斥著覺醒派的學生和騷亂者——另一部分是寧靜鄉村中的「好法國」。
“They try to oppose these two Frances, for an obvious reason, because their electorate is not in the city centers, nor in the suburbs, they are clearly in this other France,” Mr. Martigny said, adding that “this fuels the feeling decay, of decline.”
「他們試圖把這兩個法國對立起來，原因很明顯，因為他們的選民不在市中心，也不在郊區，他們顯然都在那個『另一個法國』，」馬蒂尼說，他還說：「這助長了衰敗和沒落的感覺」。
Whether the unrest offers a long-term bump in the National Rally’s fortunes will depend, in part, on if the protests continue, he said.
他說，這場動盪是否會給國民聯盟的命運帶來長期提振，部分取決於抗議是否持續。
廣告
On Sunday evening, Ms. Le Pen released a video address in which she painted this very picture of a divided France. She was filmed sitting behind a wooden desk, with the French flag behind her, hands clasped — the image of a president in waiting.
週日晚上，勒龐發布了影片講話，描繪的正是這個分裂法國的圖景。影片中，她坐在木桌後，身後是法國國旗，她雙手交握，宛如候任總統的形象。
“I condemn the hijacking of this anger by thugs who are sowing chaos in the cities of France,” she said.
「我譴責那些在法國城市製造混亂的暴徒，他們劫持了這種憤怒，」她說。
“In six months,” she said, “the French people will be able to choose a different path: That of restored authority and security.”
「六個月後，」她說，「法國人民將能夠選擇另一條道路：一條恢復權威與安全的道路。」
The National Rally supports a bigger, stronger police force, and campaigned in legislative elections in 2024 for mandatory minimum sentences and an end to reduced sentences, as well as building more prisons. It also pledged to lower the age when defendants can be tried as adults by two years, to 16, and to cut state subsidies to the parents of juvenile criminals.
國民聯盟支持規模更大、更強的警察力量，並在2024年立法選舉中主張實行強制最低刑期、廢除減刑制度，以及建造更多監獄。它還承諾將被告可作為成年人受審的年齡降低兩歲，至16歲，並削減對未成年罪犯者父母的國家補貼。
Since the current wave of protests broke out in working-class suburban high schools, Mr. Bardella has condemned students causing damage to schools and expressed support for police officers injured in confrontations with protesters.
鑒於此輪抗議浪潮始於工人階級郊區的高中，巴爾代拉譴責破壞學校的學生，並對在與抗議者衝突中受傷的警察表示支持。
Across the Atlantic, President Trump and Vice President JD Vance, who have both expressed support for Ms. Le Pen in the past, weighed in on the unrest in ways that amplified the National Rally’s critiques. Though the identities of the black-clad and masked rioters are largely unknown, the American leaders portrayed the unrest as caused by immigrants.
在大西洋彼岸，總統川普和副總統萬斯——兩人過去都曾對勒龐表示支持——對這場動盪發表看法，這些言論進一步強化了國民聯盟的批評。儘管黑衣蒙面騷亂者的身份基本不明，兩位美國領導人卻將騷亂歸咎於移民。
“France is burning!” Mr. Trump said on social media. “The immigration of people from an entirely different world, people with hatred in their hearts, has shattered that once great country. Close your Borders and take control, before it is too late!”
「法國在燃燒！」川普在社群媒體上說。「來自截然不同世界、心中充滿仇恨的人們的移民已經摧毀了那個曾經偉大的國家。關閉你們的邊境並且控制局面，趁著還不晚！」
廣告
Mr. Vance blamed the violence on “Western liberals” who “imported the third world into France and other countries in the name of diversity and compassion. They ignored fundamental questions of assimilation and compatibility.”
萬斯將暴力歸咎於「西方自由派」，稱他們「以多樣性和同情之名，把第三世界輸入法國和其他國家。他們忽視了同化和相容性的根本問題」。
He added: “I hope Americans are paying attention. This is the future congressional Democrats seek for your country.”
他還說：「我希望美國人能注意。這就是國會民主黨人試圖為你們的國家爭取的未來。」
Given how unpopular Mr. Trump’s government is in France, it was not clear whether those messages would help or hinder the National Rally.
鑒於川普政府在法國極不受歡迎，這些言論究竟會對國民聯盟有利還是有害，尚不明確。
At the police station on Sunday, Mr. Bardella was confronted by the area’s Green lawmaker, Pouria Amirshahi, who was touring his constituency wearing the tricolor sash of an elected official. It was scandalous, he said, that while he was working, Mr. Bardella was out campaigning.
週日，在警察局，巴爾代拉與該選區的綠黨議員普裡亞·阿米爾沙希對峙。阿米爾沙希當時正佩戴著民選官員的三色綬帶巡視自己的選區。他說，令人憤慨的是，當他在工作時，巴爾代拉卻在外競選。
“They spend their time, as you can see, rubbing their hands together over this violence as if their time has come,” Mr. Amirshahi said.
「正如你所見，他們把時間花在對這場暴力幸災樂禍上，彷彿他們的時代已經到來，」阿米爾沙希說。
Mr. Amirshahi then turned to the camera.
阿米爾沙希隨後轉向鏡頭。
廣告
“We in France should unite,” he said. “We should stand together in the face of the challenge ahead of us, and not fall into this pattern of division and this pornography of violence that seems to excite him right now.”
「我們法國應該團結起來，」他說。「我們應該共同面對眼前的挑戰，而不是陷入這種分裂模式，也不是陷入這種暴力色情化——這似乎正是現在讓他興奮的東西。」
But Mr. Bardella wasn’t listening. He had already hopped into a car and left.
但巴爾代拉沒有在聽。他已經鑽入汽車離開了。
