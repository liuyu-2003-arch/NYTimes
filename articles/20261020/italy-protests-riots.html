義大利南部與北部同時爆發抗議浪潮

Italy Faces Parallel Protests in the North and South

JOSEPHINE DE LA BRUYÈRE • 2026-10-20
Italy was rocked over the weekend by two separate waves of protests, as students in the north and farmers and truckers in the south demanded government action to stanch rising costs.
上週末，兩個互不相干的抗議浪潮震動了義大利：北部學生與南部農民、卡車司機分別要求政府出手遏制不斷上漲的生活成本。
In cities across northern Italy on Saturday and Sunday, thousands of student demonstrators marched in protest of low wages, high taxes, rising grocery and gas prices and cuts to public funding. A number of the marches turned violent, as groups of masked protesters looted stores, set fires and lobbed projectiles toward the police, who responded with water cannons and tear gas.
週六和週日，義大利北部多座城市有數千名學生示威者遊行，抗議低工資、高稅負、食品雜貨和汽油價格上漲以及公共資金削減。部分遊行釀成暴力事件：成群蒙面抗議者搶劫商店、縱火併向警察投擲物品，警方則以水炮和催淚瓦斯回應。
In the country’s south, farmers and truckers have since last week blockaded ports and major highways to protest high fuel prices triggered by the war in Iran, causing shortages at supermarkets and gas pumps. They say price hikes have chipped away at their slim margins and are demanding a stronger government response.
在義大利南部，農民和卡車司機自上週起封鎖港口和主要高速公路，抗議伊朗戰爭引發的高油價，造成超市和加油站出現短缺。他們表示，價格上漲侵蝕了本已微薄的利潤，並要求政府作出更強有力的回應。
Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni, who has made law and order a pillar of her government, acknowledged her “great respect” for peaceful protesters in a post on social media on Saturday but condemned the violence: “Protesting is a right. Setting our cities ablaze is not.”
總理喬治婭·梅洛尼一直將法律與秩序作為其政府的支柱。她週六在社群媒體發帖，表示對和平抗議者「高度尊重」，但譴責暴力：「抗議是一項權利。縱火焚燒我們的城市則不是。」
She met with government and law officials on Monday to consider harsher penalties for violent protesters.
她週一與政府和執法官員會面，考慮對暴力抗議者施加更嚴厲的懲罰。
The protests in both north and south were the latest in a wave of similar movements across Europe. The eviction of an 87-year-old woman in Madrid last month spurred Spaniards to take to the streets to protest rising rents, plunging the government of Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez into a political crisis. Anger in Albania about a planned luxury development by Jared Kushner has swelled into public outrage over perceived political corruption and worsening living standards. In France, students protesting the conditions in their schools unleashed a tide of frustration that has swept the country, leading this weekend to the country’s worst riots in decades.
南北兩地的抗議是席捲歐洲的一波類似運動中的最新一起。上個月，馬德里一名87歲老婦被驅逐，促使西班牙人走上街頭抗議房租上漲，使總理佩德羅·桑切斯的政府陷入政治危機。阿爾巴尼亞民眾對賈里德·庫什納計劃中的豪華開發項目的不滿已升級為對政治腐敗和生活水平惡化的公開憤怒。在法國，學生抗議學校狀況，釋放出一股沮喪情緒，席捲全國，並在週末演變為該國數十年來最嚴重的騷亂。
Italy’s youth protests were much more modest and had wrapped up by Sunday night. About 2,000 people demonstrated in Milan, where the largest protest took place, the city’s police force said. Marches also occurred in Bologna, Turin, Brescia and Bergamo, among other cities.
義大利的青年抗議規模要小得多，並於週日晚間結束。米蘭警方稱，約2000人在米蘭示威，當地發生了最大規模的抗議。博洛尼亞、都靈、布雷西亞和貝加莫等城市也舉行了遊行。

“There isn’t an electoral solution to our misery,” read a post by the anonymous social media account created earlier this month that issued the initial call to action, urging widespread anti-government demonstrations to “bring the birth of a new Italy and a new Europe.”
「我們的苦難沒有選舉解決方案，」本月早些時候創建、發出最初行動呼籲的匿名社群媒體帳號發帖寫道，敦促廣泛的反政府示威「帶來一個新義大利和一個新歐洲的誕生」。
Though the majority of protesters in Milan marched peacefully, clashes with the police led to the arrests of 32 people, most of them minors. The police said 14 officers had been injured, one of whom was hit on the head with a cobblestone. A Milan business association estimated on Monday that the unrest had caused more than one million euros, or about $1.1 million, of damage.
儘管米蘭多數抗議者和平遊行，但與警方的衝突仍導致32人被捕，其中大多數是未成年人。警方稱14名警察受傷，其中一人被鵝卵石擊中頭部。米蘭一家商業協會週一估計，騷亂造成的損失超過100萬歐元（約合人民幣750萬元）。
In a statement, Giuseppe Sala, the city’s left-wing mayor, called “actions and scenes like those we witnessed on Saturday and Sunday, directed against law enforcement and against public spaces and businesses, intolerable and unjustifiable.”
該市左翼市長朱塞佩·薩拉在一份聲明中稱，「我們週六和週日目睹的針對執法部門、公共空間和企業的行動和場景不可容忍，也不可辯護」。
Images and videos of the disorder prompted strong negative reactions across the country. “Unacceptable,” read Monday’s front page of Il Messaggero, one of Italy’s national dailies.
騷亂的畫面在全國引發強烈負面反應。「不可接受，」義大利全國性日報之一《信使報》週一頭版寫道。
But some urged Italians to look past the violence. “Young people are angry,” Marco Bascetta wrote on Saturday in Il Manifesto, Italy’s communist daily. “If a body of water is neglected and choked for decades, it can’t be expected not to overflow.”
但一些人敦促義大利人不要只盯著暴力。「年輕人很憤怒，」馬爾科·巴斯切塔週六在義大利共產主義日報《宣言報》上寫道。「一片水域若被忽視和堵塞數十年，就不能指望它不溢出。」
Italy’s mainstream press has covered the protests in northern Italy significantly more than it has the longer-running ones in the poorer south — another sticking point for the farmers, truckers and their supporters. “The news doesn’t talk about southern Italy because the government isn’t interested in southern Italy,” read a headline in Scomodo, an online magazine.
義大利主流媒體對北部抗議的報導明顯多於較貧窮南部持續時間更長的抗議——這是另一個令農民、卡車司機及其支持者不滿的地方。「新聞不談義大利南部，因為政府對義大利南部不感興趣，」在線雜誌《Scomodo》的一則標題寫道。
Matteo Salvini, Ms. Meloni’s minister of transport and infrastructure, had agreed to meet with representatives of the southern truckers next week. But over the weekend, as the Italian press began reporting on the fallout from the blockades, he announced he would move the meeting up to Tuesday. The government will “do everything within our power” to appease the truckers, he said in a radio interview.
梅洛尼的交通和基礎設施部長馬泰奧·薩爾維尼已同意下週與南部卡車司機代表會面。但週末隨著義大利媒體開始報導封鎖的後果，他宣布將會議提前到週二。他在一次電台採訪中表示，政府將「盡我們所能」安撫卡車司機。
