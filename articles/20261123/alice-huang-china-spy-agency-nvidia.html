情報披露黃樂與中國國安部接觸時間線

U.S. Tracked Alice Huang’s Contacts With China Spy Agency During Nvidia Affair

JONATHAN SWAN, JULIAN E. BARNES, MAGGIE HABERMAN • 2026-11-23
The Chinese woman at the heart of the Nvidia scandal kept in contact with an official of China’s top spy agency for more than a year while she was carrying out a secret affair with Jensen Huang, according to multiple people with knowledge of U.S. intelligence reports that describe the encounters.
據多名了解相關美國情報報告的人士透露，處於英偉達醜聞中心的中國女子黃樂(Alice Huang)在與黃仁勛祕密保持婚外情期間，曾與中國最高情報機構的一名官員保持了一年多的聯繫。
The woman, Alice Huang, was in touch with an officer of China’s Ministry of State Security from the spring of 2025 until the fall of this year, the people with knowledge of the intelligence said. That period overlaps with at least a portion of the time she was in a relationship with Mr. Huang, then the head of the world’s leading maker of advanced artificial intelligence chips.
知情人士稱，從2025年春季到今年秋季，黃樂一直與中國國家安全部的一名官員保持聯繫。這段時間與她和黃仁勛保持親密關係的時期至少有部分重合。黃仁勛當時還在掌管全球領先的先進人工智慧晶片製造商英偉達。
U.S. intelligence agencies have also gathered evidence that Ms. Huang’s business partner, Kang Wei, who was often introduced as her husband, worked with the same Chinese spy agency, the people said.
這些人士說，美國情報機構還收集到證據表明，黃樂的商業夥伴康偉——常被介紹為她的丈夫——也曾與同一個中國情報機構合作。
The extent and specific timing of Ms. Huang’s contacts with the spy agency — and Mr. Kang’s work for the same intelligence service — have not been previously reported.
此前尚無報導披露黃樂與中國情報機構接觸的程度和具體時間，也沒有報導過康偉為同一情報機構工作的情況。
廣告
Ms. Huang’s contacts with the Chinese spy agency last year and up through recent months help to flesh out how U.S. officials came to believe that Mr. Huang, who resigned as Nvidia’s chief executive earlier this month, was the target of an intelligence operation.
黃樂從去年直到最近幾個月裡與中國間諜機構的接觸有助於進一步說明，為何美國官員會認為黃仁勛是一起情報行動的目標。黃仁勛於本月早些時候辭去了英偉達首席執行官職務。
They correspond not just to the time when he was conducting the affair but also to at least part of the period when Ms. Huang’s company, Megaspeed, was acquiring large amounts of advanced Nvidia chips, some of which went to data centers in Southeast Asia serving Chinese technology companies like Alibaba and some of which are suspected of being smuggled to China.
這些接觸不僅發生在黃仁勛與黃樂發生婚外情期間，也與黃樂的公司Megaspeed大量採購英偉達先進晶片的時期至少部分重合。其中一些晶片流向了東南亞為阿里巴巴等中國科技公司服務的數據中心，還有一些被懷疑走私到中國。
Allies of the former Nvidia chief, including President Trump, have sought to minimize the scandal and questioned whether Ms. Huang was spying for Chinese intelligence. But the newly revealed information underscores the continuous contacts that she had with the Ministry of State Security, even as she was in a romantic relationship with Mr. Huang.
包括川普總統在內的黃仁勛支持者一直試圖淡化這起醜聞，並質疑黃樂是否真在為中國情報機構從事間諜活動。但最新披露的信息凸顯了一個事實：即使在與黃仁勛保持戀愛關係期間，她仍持續與中國國家安全部保持聯繫。 
It also helps to flesh out why some American officials have been worried that Ms. Huang’s role is just one element of a much broader push by China in that technological battle. The Ministry of State Security has prioritized focusing on acquiring high-end chips and advancing China’s A.I. ambitions.
這也有助於說明，為什麼一些美國官員擔心，黃樂所扮演的角色只是中國在這場技術爭奪戰中更廣泛行動的一部分。中國國家安全部已將獲取高端晶片、推動中國實現人工智慧發展目標列為優先任務。
U.S. officials are investigating the role of Ms. Huang and Mr. Kang in a network of Southeast Asian companies, including Megaspeed, that have been linked to the possible smuggling of Nvidia chips to China, to evade U.S. export control laws. U.S. officials are still working to determine the full extent and duration of their work with Chinese intelligence.
美國官員正在調查黃樂和康偉在一個東南亞企業網路中扮演的角色。這個網路包括Megaspeed在內的多家公司，它們被認為可能參與了向中國走私英偉達晶片的活動，以規避美國的出口管制法律。美國官員仍在努力查明兩人與中國情報機構合作的完整範圍及持續時間。
The intelligence gathered by the U.S. contains evidence — including shipping records — demonstrating the role of Ms. Huang and Mr. Kang in that network of companies, people with knowledge of the intelligence reports said. The New York Times has previously reported that Ms. Huang and her family have a web of connections to Chinese defense contractors.
了解情報報告的人士表示，美國收集的情報包含運輸記錄等證據，顯示黃樂和康偉在這一企業網路中發揮了作用。《紐約時報》此前曾報導，黃樂及其親屬與中國軍工承包商之間存在錯綜複雜的關係。
Mr. Huang initially described his relationship with Ms. Huang as normal interactions with a business customer. He acknowledged an affair only after The Times revealed last month that Ms. Huang had been identified by the United States as a Chinese intelligence asset and that the Nvidia chief had been picked up on electronic surveillance targeting her.
黃仁勛最初將自己與黃樂的關係描述為與商業客戶之間的正常交往。直到《紐約時報》上個月披露，美國已認定黃樂是一名為中國情報機構提供情報的線人，而且這位英偉達負責人的活動也被針對黃樂的電子監控捕捉到，他才承認兩人有婚外情。 
He stepped down this month after The Times subsequently reported that he had an affair with a subordinate at Nvidia. Administration officials never provided him with a so-called defensive briefing about Ms. Huang, a heads-up that the woman he was in a relationship with had been linked to Chinese intelligence.
此後，《紐約時報》又報導了黃仁勛與英偉達一名下屬有染的消息，他隨後於本月辭職。政府官員從未向黃仁勛提供所謂的「防範性情報通報」，提醒他與之保持親密關係的女子跟中國情報機構有關聯。
廣告
A spokesman for Mr. Huang declined to comment. The White House, the State Department, the Chinese Embassy in Washington, Ms. Huang and Mr. Kang did not respond to requests for comment on Sunday.
黃仁勛的發言人拒絕置評。白宮、美國國務院、中國駐華盛頓大使館、黃樂和康偉均未回應週日發出的置評請求。
Before the scandal broke into public view, advisers to Mr. Trump told him on at least two occasions that U.S. intelligence had uncovered Ms. Huang’s links to the Chinese spy agency and that she was in a close relationship with Mr. Huang while he was still running Nvidia, according to U.S. officials with direct knowledge of the briefings.
據直接了解相關通報情況的美國官員透露，在這起醜聞曝光之前，川普的幕僚至少兩次向他報告，美國情報機構發現了黃樂與中國情報機構之間的聯繫，而且在黃仁勛仍執掌英偉達期間，黃樂與他關係密切。
Both times, including when his national security adviser discussed it with him, the president downplayed the concerns and made clear he did not want to intervene in the matter. The officials said Mr. Trump regarded Mr. Huang as a valuable ally and Nvidia as essential to the health of the American economy and stock market. Mr. Trump has recently suggested that Nvidia should consider bringing Mr. Huang back as chief executive, a possibility the company’s board has rejected.
這兩次，川普都淡化了相關擔憂，並明確表示不想干預此事，其中一次是他的國家安全顧問向他彙報時。據這些官員稱，川普認為黃仁勛是一位重要盟友，而英偉達對美國經濟和股市的健康發展至關重要。川普最近還表示，英偉達應該考慮讓黃仁勛回來重新擔任首席執行官，但公司董事會已經拒絕了這一可能性。 
“We have to protect our brilliant people. Jensen is one of them,” the president said at a White House news conference on Friday. “I think the company if they were smart they’d take that brilliant man, accept an apology and let him go back to work.”
「我們必須保護我們的傑出人才。黃仁勛就是其中之一，」川普週五在白宮的新聞發布會上說。「我認為，英偉達如果夠聰明，就應該接受這位傑出的人才，接受他的道歉，讓他回去工作。」
Mr. Trump has claimed that no one in his government told him of any intelligence indicating that Ms. Huang was a Chinese intelligence asset.
川普聲稱，政府中沒有任何人向他報告過表明黃樂是中國情報人員的線人。
“I don’t know that she is a spy,” he said. “Usually, what happens with that is that any woman coming to the country that’s a beautiful woman that comes from China, they say she’s a spy.”
「我不知道她是間諜，」他說。「通常情況是，任何來到這個國家的漂亮女人，只要來自中國，他們就說她是間諜。」 
In fact, his administration has been monitoring Ms. Huang’s contacts with the Chinese spy agency for more than a year, a period when the Ministry of State Security has been intensifying its new role in seeking to give China an advantage in artificial intelligence.
事實上，他的政府一年多以來一直在監控黃樂與中國間諜機構的聯繫。在此期間，中國國家安全部在強化其新角色，試圖幫助中國在人工智慧領域取得優勢。
“This network of companies working to procure and smuggle Nvidia chips lines up precisely with the M.S.S.’s recently stated core mission of circumventing and undermining U.S. export controls on semiconductors and other key technologies,” said Matt Pottinger, who served as deputy national security adviser during Mr. Trump’s first term, and who is chief executive of the open-source research firm Garnaut Global.
 「這個採購和走私英偉達晶片的企業網路與中國國家安全部最近宣布的核心任務完全吻合，即規避和破壞美國對半導體及其他關鍵技術實施的出口管制，」川普第一任期內擔任副國家安全顧問、現任開源研究公司Garnaut Global首席執行官的博明(Matt Pottinger)說。
廣告
The Ministry of State Security has for decades declared that its mission is to combat espionage and to eliminate political threats to Communist Party rule in China. The minister of state security, Chen Yixin, gave a similar five-point statement as recently as late last year. Then in April, he unveiled a new, sixth mission — overcoming the “blockades” choking China’s access to key technologies like artificial intelligence.
幾十年來，中國國家安全部一直宣稱，其使命是打擊間諜活動，消除對中國共產黨統治的政治威脅。直到去年年底，國家安全部部長陳一新仍在發表類似的五項任務聲明。但今年4月，他又提出了第六項新任務：突破阻礙中國獲取人工智慧等關鍵技術的「封鎖」。 
“Deepen the struggle against blockades, and comprehensively advance artificial intelligence innovation, industry development and applications,” Mr. Chen wrote in a party journal. “Focus on tackling the ‘choke holds’ of critical technologies.”
「深入開展反封鎖鬥爭，全面推進人工智慧科技創新、產業發展和賦能應用，」陳一新在一份中共刊物上寫道。「聚焦攻克『卡脖子』關鍵技術。」
“To assume the M.S.S. wasn’t at the center of this operation would be to assume that they are incompetent,” Mr. Pottinger added. “That’s not an assumption I would make.”
「如果認為國安部不是這次行動的核心，那就等於認為他們無能，」 博明補充說。「我不會做這種假設。」
American spy agencies are still working to understand the role played by Mr. Kang, the business partner of Ms. Huang, whom she would introduce to associates as her husband. Multiple people with knowledge of the intelligence said the United States had collected evidence that Mr. Kang worked with the Ministry of State Security.
美國情報機構仍在努力了解黃樂的商業夥伴康偉所扮演的角色。黃樂會向合作夥伴介紹康偉是自己的丈夫。多名了解相關情報的人士表示，美國已收集到康偉與中國國家安全部合作的證據。
Ms. Huang also appears to have met with officials of China’s Ministry of Public Security, the nation’s main law enforcement agency, according to multiple people with knowledge of the U.S. intelligence reports.
多名了解美國相關情報報告的人士說，黃樂似乎還與中國公安部官員會面。公安部是中國主要的執法機構。
Ms. Huang, Mr. Kang, Ms. Huang’s sister and companies associated with them have emerged as a significant branch of the Chinese effort to build data centers and acquire access to Nvidia chips to train powerful A.I. models. Ms. Huang’s network has been linked to efforts to supply the powerful chips to Chinese-state owned military contractors doing work for the People’s Liberation Army.
黃樂、康偉、黃樂的親姐妹以及與他們有關聯的企業已成為中國建設數據中心、獲取英偉達晶片以訓練強大人工智慧模型努力的一支重要力量。黃樂的企業網路還被發現與向中國國有軍工承包商供應高性能晶片的活動存在關聯，這些承包商為中國人民解放軍承擔項目。
廣告
Little of the intelligence about Ms. Huang and her network has been provided to Capitol Hill, but lawmakers from both parties are demanding briefings.
有關黃樂及其企業網路的情報很少提供給國會，但兩黨議員都在要求政府舉行情報簡報會。 
A briefing this month for top lawmakers by John Ratcliffe, the C.I.A. director, and Jay Clayton, the director of national intelligence, was originally scheduled to discuss Iran, but The Times’s reporting on Nvidia led the lawmakers to ask questions about Mr. Huang and Alice Huang. Their answers raised more questions and left some lawmakers puzzled.
本月，中央情報局局長約翰·拉特克利夫和國家情報總監傑伊·克萊頓為國會高層議員舉行了一次簡報會，原定討論伊朗問題，但《紐約時報》關於英偉達的報導促使議員們就黃仁勛和黃樂的情況紛紛提問。他們的回答引發了更多疑問，也讓一些議員感到困惑。
Trump administration officials postponed a follow-up briefing for top national security staffers scheduled for last Wednesday, in which senior officials were set to share details of the intelligence collected on Ms. Huang.
川普政府官員推遲了原定上週三為最高國家安全工作人員舉行的後續簡報會，按照原計劃，政府高級官員將在會上分享針對黃樂收集的情報細節
The intelligence about Ms. Huang was closely held inside the government until The Times reported the existence of the information in late October. Behind the scenes, Secretary of State Marco Rubio, who is also the national security adviser, had written a confidential memo, dated March 30 this year, laying out Ms. Huang’s ties to Chinese intelligence.
直到《紐約時報》10月底披露這些情報的存在之前，相關信息一直在美國政府內部受到嚴格保密。私下裡，國務卿兼國家安全顧問魯比奧撰寫了一份日期標註為3月30日的機密備忘錄，詳細說明了黃樂與中國情報機構之間的聯繫。
