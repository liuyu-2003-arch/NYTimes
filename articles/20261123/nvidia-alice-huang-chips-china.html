黃樂親屬及商業夥伴與中國軍工承包商有廣泛聯繫

Spying Suspect’s Chip Network Has Ties to Chinese Military Suppliers

JULIAN E. BARNES, 孟建國, ANA SWANSON • 2026-11-23
Alice Huang, the woman at the center of the Nvidia scandal, has extensive ties through her family and associates to China’s defense industry, according to an independent research firm and corporate filings reviewed by The New York Times.
據一家獨立研究公司以及《紐約時報》查閱的企業備案文件顯示，處於英偉達醜聞中心的黃樂(Alice Huang)通過親屬和商業夥伴與中國國防工業有著廣泛聯繫。
The findings show a web of relationships, most of them indirect, linking Ms. Huang’s family and companies that acquired Nvidia chips to Chinese state-owned defense contractors that supply the People’s Liberation Army.
調查結果揭示了一個關係網路——其中大多數關係是間接的——將黃樂的親屬以及購買英偉達晶片的公司與向中國人民解放軍提供裝備的中國國有軍工承包商聯繫起來。
The nature of those relationships, including the vital question of whether Nvidia chips ended up being used to support Chinese military projects, remains murky. But the revelations, along with an investigation by The Times published on Wednesday, underscore how the Chinese effort to acquire access to the computing power of Nvidia’s chips is intertwined with Beijing’s desire to harness artificial intelligence to bolster its military capabilities.
這些關係的性質，包括一個關鍵問題——英偉達晶片最終是否被用於支持中國軍事項目——仍然模糊不清。但這些新披露的情況，連同《紐約時報》週三發表的一篇調查報導，凸顯了中國獲取英偉達晶片算力的努力與北京希望利用人工智慧增強軍事實力的目標之間的密切聯繫。
That outcome has long been feared by U.S. national security officials, given that the technology could help China develop more sophisticated weapons, carry out cyberattacks and integrate A.I. into the battlefield.
美國國家安全官員長期以來一直擔心這種結果，因為該技術可能幫助中國開發更先進的武器、實施網路攻擊，並將人工智慧應用於戰場。
廣告
A confidential analysis by Strider Technologies, an independent intelligence firm that employs former intelligence officers and experts on China and specializes in corporate intelligence, shows that about $3.6 billion in Chinese government money flowed through companies associated with Ms. Huang’s family or associates. The government money funded technology projects, including data centers that received Nvidia chips.
獨立情報公司Strider Technologies的一份機密分析顯示，約有36億美元的中國政府資金流經與黃樂的親屬或商業夥伴有關的公司。這些政府資金用於資助技術項目，包括獲得英偉達晶片的數據中心。Strider Technologies僱用前情報官員和中國問題專家，專門從事企業情報調查。
The New York Times reviewed the Strider findings. Many of the corporate ties revealed by Strider were independently confirmed by The Times.
《紐約時報》查閱了Strider的調查結果，並獨立核實了它所披露的許多企業關聯關係。
The Times reported last month that U.S. officials believe Ms. Huang, a Chinese citizen also known as Huang Le, is a Chinese intelligence asset who was providing information to the Ministry of State Security. The Strider Technologies analysis and the review of corporate filings by The Times show that she; her sister, Huang Yu; and an investor named Kang Wei, who was Alice Huang’s business partner and often introduced as her husband, are tied to a web of companies that connect official Nvidia partners, which can buy advanced Nvidia A.I. chips, to state- and military-linked projects.
《紐約時報》上個月報導，美國官員認為，中國公民黃樂是中國情報機構的線人，一直向國家安全部提供情報。Strider Technologies的分析和《紐約時報》對企業備案文件的查閱顯示，黃樂和她的親姐妹黃瑜（音）以及一名叫康偉的投資者與一個龐大的企業網路存在關聯。康偉是黃樂的商業夥伴，經常被介紹為她的丈夫。通過這一網路，能夠購買英偉達先進人工智慧晶片的官方合作夥伴與具有政府及軍方背景的項目建立了聯繫。
Nvidia’s co-founder, Jensen Huang, stepped down as the company’s chief executive after acknowledging an affair with Alice Huang, who is no relation.
英偉達聯合創始人黃仁勛在承認與黃樂有婚外情後辭去了公司首席執行官職務。
Companies in the Huang family’s network co-own a cybersecurity venture with an arm of China’s main military-electronics conglomerate, lease Nvidia-powered servers financed by an affiliate of its largest military aircraft maker and in one case share a parent company with a maker of radar and missile components.
黃樂及其親屬的企業關係網與中國主要軍用電子設備集團旗下的一家機構共同持有一家網路安全企業的股份，租用配備英偉達晶片的伺服器，相關設備由中國最大軍用飛機製造商的一家關聯企業提供融資；其中一家企業還與一家雷達和導彈零部件製造商同屬一家母公司。 
Two companies that have partnerships with Nvidia, Megaspeed and Century Litong, are connected to Ms. Huang. Ms. Huang ran Megaspeed, a Singapore-based data center firm that is under investigation by the Justice Department for smuggling advanced chips into China. Megaspeed has said Ms. Huang, whose involvement with Mr. Huang corresponded with a period when it was acquiring large numbers of Nvidia chips, no longer works for the company.
與黃樂有關聯的兩家公司Megaspeed和世紀利通均與英偉達建立了合作關係。黃樂曾經營總部位於新加坡的數據中心公司Megaspeed。該公司因涉嫌向中國走私先進晶片正受到美國司法部調查。Megaspeed表示，黃樂已不再為公司工作。她與黃仁勛交往的那段時間，恰逢Megaspeed大量採購英偉達晶片。
廣告
Ms. Huang’s family members had prominent roles and significant investments in the data center firm that founded Century Litong, which is based in Shanghai and rents out computing power to major Chinese companies and military contractors. The family members include Ms. Huang’s sister and Mr. Kang.
黃樂的家人在創辦世紀利通的數據中心公司中擔任重要職務，並持有大量股份。這些家人包括黃瑜和康偉。世紀利通總部位於上海，向中國大型企業和軍工承包商出租算力。
U.S. officials are also working to learn more about Ms. Huang and her efforts to help supply chips to data centers. The nature and extent of her ties to the P.L.A. and the companies it controls are a focus of that work, according to people briefed on the American efforts.
美國官員也在努力進一步了解黃樂及其幫助向數據中心供應晶片的努力。據了解美國相關調查工作的人士透露，調查的重點是她與中國人民解放軍及其控制的企業究竟有何聯繫、聯繫有多廣泛。
The ties are complex and often indirect. The research does not show whether the P.L.A. used computing power in the data centers that the family’s companies helped build. But it shows how deeply Ms. Huang and her family are intertwined with the Chinese state, and the inextricable connections between China’s tech industry and its military.
這些聯繫錯綜複雜，而且往往是間接的。研究並未顯示黃樂及其親屬的企業參與建設的數據中心是否曾為中國人民解放軍提供算力。但研究揭示了黃樂及其親屬與中國政府之間聯繫之深，以及中國科技產業與軍方之間難以割裂的關係。
“Beijing has spent years building these networks that reach deep into Western economies,” said Greg Levesque, the chief executive of Strider. “Follow the money and, too often, you find the P.L.A. at the end of it.”
「北京多年來一直在建設這些深入西方經濟體的網路，」Strider首席執行官格雷格·萊韋斯克說。「順著資金流向追查，很多時候最終都會發現解放軍。」
Mr. Levesque said China was exploiting more Western technology, capital and talent than ever before to build its defense industrial base. “This is happening on a scale we simply weren’t seeing a few years ago,” he said.
萊韋斯克說，中國正在利用比以往更多的西方技術、資本和人才來建設其國防工業基礎。「這種活動的規模之大，是我們幾年前根本沒有見過的，」他說。
Nvidia declined to comment. Megaspeed did not respond to a request for comment. Ms. Huang could not be reached for comment.
英偉達拒絕置評。Megaspeed未回應置評請求。記者未能聯繫到黃樂置評。
廣告
From late last year into early this year, Century Litong appears to have been one of only a few sources able to supply Nvidia chips to China at the vast scale needed for its A.I. build-out, according to Strider.
Strider的分析顯示，從去年年底到今年年初，世紀利通似乎是少數幾家能夠向中國大規模供應英偉達晶片的企業之一，其供應能力足以滿足中國大舉建設人工智慧基礎設施的需求。
Those chips were key to building the data centers that drive China’s A.I. development. Investigators are trying to determine how many of the chips that were used in those data centers were banned under U.S. export controls and, in essence, smuggled into the country. Companies controlled by Ms. Huang and her relatives were instrumental in supplying the chips, Strider found.
這些晶片對於建設推動中國人工智慧發展的數據中心至關重要。調查人員正在設法查明，這些數據中心使用的晶片中，有多少受到美國出口管制禁令限制，實際上是被走私進入中國的。Strider發現，黃樂及其親屬控制的公司在供應這些晶片方面發揮了關鍵作用。
“Essentially, those entities worked around the controls to continue to meet the chip demands of the customers they already had, including at new data centers,” Mr. Levesque said.
「實際上，這些企業繞過了管制措施，繼續滿足既有客戶對晶片的需求，包括用於新建數據中心的晶片需求，」萊韋斯克說。
On Thursday, the House Foreign Affairs Committee took the unusual step of releasing confidential information on Nvidia’s license applications to legally sell H200 chips to Chinese firms. The list showed that Nvidia had sought permission to sell more than 40,000 H200s to the Hong Kong subsidiary of Century Litong.
週四，眾議院外交事務委員會採取了一項不同尋常的舉措，公布了英偉達為合法向中國企業出售H200晶片而提交的許可證申請中的機密信息。名單顯示，英偉達曾申請向世紀利通的香港子公司出售逾4萬枚H200晶片。
An Nvidia spokesman said that, despite receiving some licenses, it had already stopped shipping H200s to China, and Nvidia had not and would not ship to that firm.
英偉達發言人表示，儘管獲得了部分許可證，但此前已經停止向中國發貨H200晶片，而且過去沒有、將來也不會向這家公司供貨。 
Century Litong has said it has no business dealings with Ms. Huang. But the company was founded in 2023 by Century Longteng, a data center operator that was then 27 percent owned by an investment firm controlled by Ms. Huang’s sister. Mr. Kang, Ms. Huang’s presumed husband, had been Century Longteng’s executive director since February 2022, according to Strider.
世紀利通曾表示，它與黃樂沒有業務往來。然而，該公司於2023年由數據中心運營商世紀龍騰創辦。當時，一家由黃瑜控制的投資公司持有世紀龍騰27%的股份。據Strider的分析，據信是黃樂丈夫的康偉自2022年2月起一直擔任世紀龍騰的執行董事。
廣告
In 2023, Jiangsu Lettall Electronic, a Shanghai-listed maker of metal parts for flat-screen TVs, bought 90 percent of Century Litong, and owned all of it by 2024. Lettall also owns 80 percent of Nanjing Jinning Microwave, a maker of components that Lettall has said are used in Chinese radar and missile systems. Strider said Jinning’s parts go into the P.L.A.’s DF-series missiles, including intercontinental ballistic missiles and anti-ship “carrier killers,” as well as the J-20 stealth fighter plane.
2023年，在上海證券交易所上市、生產液晶電視金屬結構件的江蘇利通電子收購了世紀利通90%的股份，並於2024年實現全資控股。利通電子還持有南京金寧微波80%的股份。利通電子曾表示，金寧微波生產的零部件用於中國的雷達和導彈系統。Strider稱，金寧微波的零部件被用於中國人民解放軍的東風系列導彈，包括洲際彈道導彈和被稱為「航母殺手」的反艦導彈，以及殲-20隱形戰鬥機。
China’s defense industry wants advanced A.I. chips to design better missiles and build other military software, experts have said.
專家指出，中國國防工業希望獲得先進人工智慧晶片，以設計性能更好的導彈，並開發其他軍事軟體。
Lettall did not respond to an emailed request for comment. Asked about Ms. Huang’s network of companies and their efforts to acquire chips, the spokesman for the Chinese Embassy in Washington said he was not familiar with the situation. “We have made clear more than once our principled position on U.S. chip exports to China,” said the spokesman, Liu Chang.
利通電子沒有回覆通過電子郵件發出的置評請求。當被問及黃樂的企業網路及其獲取晶片的活動時，中國駐華盛頓大使館發言人劉暢表示，他不了解相關情況。「我們已經不止一次明確闡述了中方在美國對華晶片出口問題上的原則立場，」他說。 
Century Litong delivered servers containing Nvidia’s H800 chips to a supercomputing center in Shanghai in October 2023, according to Strider. It is unclear where Century Litong obtained those chips, but they may have been obtained legally, since U.S. restrictions on the sale of the H800 to China did not go into effect until that same month.
據Strider稱，2023年10月，世紀利通向上海一個超級計算中心交付了裝有英偉達H800晶片的伺服器。目前尚不清楚世紀利通從何處獲得這些晶片，但可能是通過合法管道獲得的，因為美國直到當月才開始實施限制向中國銷售H800晶片的規定。
Century Litong now runs servers at the Shanghai supercomputing center under a lease of about $54 million from a leasing arm of the Aviation Industry Corporation of China, China’s state aerospace and defense conglomerate.
世紀利通目前根據與中國國有航空航天和國防工業集團——中國航空工業集團旗下的租賃部門簽訂的約5400萬美元租約，在上海超級計算中心運行伺服器。
The Trail to Huawei and DeepSeek
通往華為和DeepSeek的關係網路
廣告
Century Litong’s reach extends elsewhere. When Lettall bought into the company in 2023, it said all of Century Litong’s computing business would be housed in two places: data centers in Shanghai and Shenzhen.
世紀利通的影響力還延伸到其他地方。2023年利通電子入股該公司時表示，世紀利通的所有計算業務將設在上海和深圳兩個地方的數據中心。
The Shenzhen center was built for the cloud unit of Huawei, the Chinese telecom giant that has long been under sanctions by the United States over national security concerns. Huawei awarded the project in 2022 to a company controlled by Century Longteng, according to corporate filings.
深圳的數據中心是為中國電信巨頭華為的雲業務部門建設的。長期以來，美國一直以國家安全問題為由對華為實施制裁。根據企業備案文件，華為於2022年將這一項目授予了一家由世紀龍騰控制的公司。
Century Litong’s customers have also included a unit of China Electronics Technology Group, or C.E.T.C., a state-owned military electronics maker. The United States has included C.E.T.C. on its list of companies that it publicly identifies as doing work with the Chinese military, banning the firm from doing work with Pentagon suppliers.
世紀利通的客戶還包括中國電子科技集團旗下的一家企業，這是一家國有軍用電子設備製造商。美國已將其列入一份公開認定與中國軍方合作的企業名單，並禁止該集團與五角大廈的供應商開展業務。 
Another Century Litong partner is Hangjin Technology, a listed company whose subsidiaries make chips for China’s military.
世紀利通的另一個合作夥伴是航錦科技，這是一家上市公司，它旗下的子公司為中國軍方生產晶片。
Until last December, Hangjin controlled Chaoqing, an Nvidia partner whose website once listed High-Flyer, the hedge fund behind the Chinese A.I. start-up DeepSeek, as a partner. Others on the list included the military-linked Zhejiang Lab and its co-founder, Alibaba.
直到去年12月，航錦科技一直控制著英偉達合作夥伴超擎數智。超擎數智的網站曾將中國人工智慧初創公司DeepSeek背後的對沖基金幻方量化列為合作夥伴。該名單上還包括與軍方有關聯的之江實驗室及其聯合創辦者阿里巴巴。 
DeepSeek has attracted attention for its cutting-edge A.I. models. But lawmakers in the United States have also accused it of presenting national security risks, and the federal government has banned employees from using it. The Information earlier reported ties between Ms. Huang’s network and DeepSeek.
DeepSeek因其尖端人工智慧模型而受到關注。但美國議員也指責該公司構成國家安全風險，聯邦政府已禁止僱員使用其產品。《The Information》此前報導了黃樂的企業網路與DeepSeek之間的聯繫。
Hangjin did not respond to an emailed request for comment. It has said it does no business with DeepSeek.
航錦科技未回應通過電子郵件發送的置評請求。該公司此前表示，它與DeepSeek沒有業務往來。
