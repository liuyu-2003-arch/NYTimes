伊朗襲擊貨船，霍爾木茲航運再次中斷

Iran Strikes Ship in Strait of Hormuz, Undermining Efforts to Restore Traffic

PETER EAVIS, FARNAZ FASSIHI, JENNY GROSS, EUAN WARD • 2026-06-26
Iran’s armed forces struck a container ship that was passing through the Strait of Hormuz on Thursday, according to U.S. and Iranian officials, undermining efforts to restore shipping traffic through the crucial waterway.
據美伊雙方官員透露，伊朗武裝部隊週四在霍爾木茲海峽襲擊了一艘途經此處的集裝箱船，使恢復這條關鍵水道航運的努力遭受重挫。
The attack came hours after Iran, demonstrating its hold over the strait, had warned ships that the only route through the vital pathway for oil and natural gas was through its waters. Many ships had been using a route on the southern side of the strait, hugging the Omani coast.
此次襲擊發生數小時前，伊朗為了展示自己對霍爾木茲海峽的控制權，警告所有船隻：要穿越這條對石油和天然氣運輸至關重要的水道，唯一許可航線必須經過伊朗水域。此前，許多船隻一直選擇沿著海峽南側、貼近阿曼海岸的航道航行。
The strike halted traffic through the crucial waterway, contradicting President Trump’s claim that Iran did not control the strait and his assurances that it was open once again open to shipping. Oil prices jumped after the attack, with the cost of Brent crude, the global benchmark for oil, rising over 2 percent to about $75 a barrel. West Texas Intermediate crude, the U.S. benchmark, also rose over 2 percent, to around $72 a barrel.
襲擊導致這條重要水道的航運再次中斷，這與川普總統此前宣稱「伊朗並不控制霍爾木茲海峽」以及海峽已重新向航運開放的保證相矛盾。襲擊發生後油價跳漲，全球基準布倫特原油價格上漲超過2%，至每桶約75美元。美國基準西得克薩斯中質原油也上漲超過2%，至每桶約72美元。
A U.S. official, who spoke anonymously in order to share details of the strike, said the vessel had been hit by a drone. The attack prompted the International Maritime Organization, a United Nations agency, to suspend its plan to evacuate seafarers from hundreds of ships that had been stranded in the Persian Gulf.
一名美國官員在匿名情況下透露了襲擊細節，稱該船遭到無人機打擊。此次襲擊還迫使聯合國機構國際海事組織暫停了將數百艘滯留在波斯灣船隻上的海員撤離的計劃。
廣告
It was not clear how the strike would affect the ongoing negotiations between the United States and Iran over control of the strait and over Iran’s nuclear program. Secretary of State Marco Rubio met on Thursday with Gulf Arab leaders in Bahrain to try to allay their security concerns.
目前尚不清楚這次襲擊將對美伊兩國正在進行的關於海峽控制權和伊朗核計劃的談判產生怎樣的影響。國務卿魯比奧週四在巴林會見了海灣阿拉伯國家領導人，努力消除他們的安全顧慮。
Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guards Corps had warned ships earlier on Thursday that they must coordinate with its navy and that they should not take an alternative route, in an apparent reference to Omani territorial waters. The threats came just as shipping in the waterway was surging this week after months of near-paralysis.
伊朗伊斯蘭革命衛隊週四早些時候曾發出警告，要求所有船隻必須與伊朗海軍協調，不得選擇其他航線——這顯然指的是阿曼領海。這些威脅發出之際，該水道的航運在經歷了數月近乎癱瘓後，本週剛開始迅速恢復。
“This route is unacceptable and extremely dangerous,” the Revolutionary Guards’ Navy said early on Thursday in a statement carried by Tasnim, an Iranian news agency tied to the Guards. “We warn all vessels to strictly refrain from any movement outside the designated routes,” it added, warning that action would be taken against vessels that did not heed its instructions.
「這條航線是不可接受且極其危險的，」革命衛隊海軍週四早些時候在伊朗塔斯尼姆通訊社（該機構有革命衛隊背景）發表的一份聲明中稱。「我們警告所有船舶嚴格避免在指定航道之外航行，」聲明補充道，並放話將對不服從的船隻採取行動。
The United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations organization, which is administered by Britain’s Royal Navy, reported that a cargo vessel had been hit by an “unknown projectile,” damaging the bridge. The episode happened 7.5 nautical miles southeast of Dahit, Oman, where vessels were transiting.
由英國皇家海軍管理的英國海上貿易行動辦公室報告稱，一艘貨船被「不明拋射物」擊中，駕駛台受損。事件發生在阿曼達希特東南方向7.5海裡處，正是過往船隻通行的區域。
The ship struck, the Ever Lovely, is owned by Evergreen Marine, a shipping company based in Taiwan, according to Equasis, a ship database.
據船舶數據庫Equasis顯示，遭襲的「長麗輪」隸屬台灣船運公司長榮海運。
Arsenio Dominguez, the secretary general of the International Maritime Organization, did not name the attacked ship in a news release on Thursday, but said it had not been part of the agency’s evacuation operation.
國際海事組織秘書長阿塞尼奧·多明格斯在週四的新聞稿中未點名遭襲船隻，但表示該船未包括在該組織的撤離行動中。
The Wall Street Journal initially reported the strike on the vessel in the strait.
《華爾街日報》最早報導了這起霍爾木茲海峽船隻遇襲事件。
廣告
Jakob Larsen, the chief security officer at BIMCO, the world’s largest shipping association, said in a statement on Thursday that the attack underscored the importance of having clear agreements between the United States and Iran on the resumption of shipping through the strait. The current agreement is “not sufficiently clear,” he said.
全球最大航運協會——波羅的海國際航運公會的首席安全官雅各布·拉森週四發表聲明稱，此次襲擊凸顯了美伊兩國就恢復海峽航運達成明確協議的重要性。他表示，目前的協議「不夠明確」。
The Iranian official, who asked not to be named because he was not authorized to speak publicly, said Oman’s providing an alternative route to vessels for transiting the strait had angered Iran and undermined its control over the passageway, therefore it had decided to act.
一名因無權公開發言而要求匿名的伊朗官員表示，阿曼向過往船隻提供替代航線一事令伊朗深感不滿，因為這削弱了伊朗對海峽的控制，因此它決定採取行動。
The official said Oman was in a difficult position, working with Iran to create a management system over the strait and under pressure from the United States to reject all monetization and open the waterway. He added that Oman did not have the ability to provide security guarantees to vessels without Iran on board, and that Iran, which was insisting on maintaining its control over the area, would not tolerate a second- or third-party intervention.
該官員還說，阿曼處境兩難——一方面要與伊朗合作建立海峽管理機制，另一方面又面臨美國的壓力，要求其拒絕任何收費安排，確保水道暢通開放。他補充說，阿曼在沒有伊朗配合的情況下無力為過往船隻提供安全保障，而堅持維護本地區控制權的伊朗也不會容忍任何第二方或第三方介入。
Though Iran agreed last week to let vessels pass through the strait safely, it is also seeking to gain more influence over shipping traffic in the waterway, said an analyst, Noam Raydan, a senior fellow at the Washington Institute for Near East Policy.
儘管伊朗上週同意讓船隻安全通過海峽，但它也在尋求獲得對水道航運的更多影響力，華盛頓近東政策研究所高級研究員諾姆·雷丹分析道。
“Iran wants this navigational order to be according to its own terms,” she said.
「伊朗希望按照自己的條件來確立這套航行秩序，」她說。
Traffic through the strait — an energy route that Iran and the United States blockaded during the war — had jumped this week as vessels stranded for months in the Persian Gulf began leaving. Shipping companies are seeking to take advantage of a temporary two-month window to move Middle East crude oil.
霍爾木茲海峽曾在戰爭期間遭到伊朗和美國封鎖。本週，隨著此前滯留波斯灣數月的大批船隻陸續駛離，海峽航運量大幅增加。各大航運公司正試圖利用目前這兩個月的窗口期，加快中東原油運輸。
About 70 vessels transited the waterway on Wednesday, including 29 tankers, making it the busiest day since March 1, according to figures offered on Thursday by Kpler, a maritime data company. Before the war, 130 or more ships passed through the strait every day.
據海事數據公司Kpler週四公布的數據，週三共有約70艘船隻通過海峽，其中包括29艘油輪，創下3月1日以來的單日新高。而在戰前，每天通過海峽的船隻超過130艘。
廣告
Bahrain is the final stop of a three-country tour for Mr. Rubio, which also took him to the United Arab Emirates and Kuwait. At a meeting with representatives of the six-member Gulf Cooperation Council in the Bahraini capital, Manama, he sought to assuage concerns among regional allies over a framework peace agreement with Iran that has left some of their central security questions unaddressed.
巴林是魯比奧此次三國海灣之行的最後一站，此前他已訪問阿聯酋和科威特。在巴林首都麥納麥與海灣合作委員會六國代表會面時，他著力安撫地區盟友對美伊框架和平協議的顧慮——這份協議並未解決他們最關心的一些安全問題。
“We want to ensure that in any decisions that are made throughout this negotiating process, the interest of our partners and our allies in the region are always taken into account,” Mr. Rubio told the council’s representatives on Thursday.
「我們希望確保在整個談判過程中所作出的任何決定都能夠充分考慮到我們地區夥伴和盟友的利益，」魯比奧週四對海合會代表表示。
The deal should not undermine “the security, the stability, or the prosperity of any of our partners in the Gulf region,” he added.
他還表示，這項協議不應損害「海灣地區任何一個夥伴的安全、穩定和繁榮」。
Gulf Arab states have broadly welcomed efforts to end the war but remain uneasy about provisions of the preliminary agreement. The deal does not address Iran’s missile and drone programs, which Tehran used to attack Gulf countries — some hosting American military bases — after the conflict with the United States and Israel began in late February.
海灣阿拉伯國家總體上對終結戰爭的努力表示歡迎，但對初步協議的部分內容仍存有疑慮。協議未涉及伊朗的導彈和無人機項目——今年2月下旬美以對伊開戰後，伊朗正是利用這些武器對包括駐有美國軍事基地的海灣國家在內的多個目標發動了攻擊。
The crucial question of how shipping will be managed through the Strait of Hormuz also remains unresolved.
霍爾木茲海峽航運管理方式這一關鍵問題同樣懸而未決。
The naval arm of the Revolutionary Guards, in its latest threat, repeated that coordination with the guards was “mandatory” for vessels seeking to transit the strait. Iran has insisted on this since the framework U.S.-Iran peace deal was announced last week.
革命衛隊海軍在其最新威脅中重申，與革命衛隊協調是希望通過海峽的船隻「必須」做的事。自上週美伊框架和平協議宣布以來，伊朗一直堅持這一要求。
Several container ships, some of which were stranded for more than three months, have been able to leave the Persian Gulf.
目前，已有多艘集裝箱船陸續駛離波斯灣，其中有些已滯留超過三個月。
The Danish shipping giant Maersk said two of its vessels left the Gulf between late Wednesday and early Thursday after “security assessments” and consultations with regional security partners. Three Maersk vessels remain in the Gulf and are expected to leave later, the company said.
丹麥航運巨頭馬士基表示，其兩艘船隻在經過「安全評估」並與地區安全合作夥伴磋商後，於週三晚間至週四凌晨先後離開波斯灣。該公司表示，另有三艘馬士基船隻仍留在海灣，預計稍後也將駛離。
廣告
Since early May, the U.S. military has helped ships sail through the strait on routes close to Oman. More than 500 ships and 250 million barrels of crude have been moved under that effort, according to U.S. Central Command, which is behind the operation.
5月初以來，美國軍方一直協助船隻沿阿曼的航線穿越海峽。據負責該行動的美國中央司令部統計，迄今已有逾500艘船隻、2.5億桶原油藉助這一行動順利轉移。
Mr. Rubio, during his meeting with Gulf Cooperation Council foreign ministers on Thursday, rejected Iran’s continued claim to control passage through the strait.
魯比奧週四在與海合會各國外長舉行的會議上明確拒絕了伊朗繼續聲稱擁有霍爾木茲海峽通行控制權的說法。
“International waterways do not belong to any nation state,” he said. “This is a foundational principle in the world today, without which the world would be in total chaos.”
「國際水道不屬於任何國家，」他說。「這是當今世界的一項基本原則，如果不是這樣，世界將陷入徹底混亂。」
