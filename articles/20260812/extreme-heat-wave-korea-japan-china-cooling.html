東亞極端天氣催生「防暑神器」新經濟

A.C. Vests and UV Parasols: East Asia Deploys Heat-Fighting Gadgets

MAX KIM • 2026-08-12
Stroll through Tokyo on a summer day and you’ll see hand-held fans everywhere. But the light-blue one that Eimi Nakayama was holding on a recent afternoon was a more high-tech variety. Outfitted with a small metal plate that runs on the same science behind semiconductors, it uses electrical currents to generate a chill.
夏日漫步在東京街頭，手持風扇隨處可見。不過，中山詠美（音）在近日一個下午拿著的那把淺藍色風扇屬於科技含量更高的款式。它配備了一塊小金屬板，採用與半導體相同的科學原理，用電流來產生涼意。
The sensation is “kind of like holding a cold drink against your neck,” said Ms. Nakayama, a 20-year-old university student. “Maybe because of the plate, the air flow feels a bit cooler, too.”
現年20歲的大學生中山表示，這種感覺「有點像拿著冰飲貼在脖子上」。「也許是因為這塊金屬板，吹出來的風感覺也更涼爽了一點。」
The product has lately been sweeping through cities in East Asia, where a deadly heat wave this month has led to canceled baseball games in South Korea and a new meteorological term in Japan: kokushobi, or “cruel heat day.”
這類產品最近席捲了東亞各大城市。本月一場致命的熱浪導致韓國取消了棒球比賽，日本則出現了一個新的氣象術語：「酷暑日」(kokushobi)。
East Asia’s summers are becoming hotter and longer, and such “personal cooling” has become a ubiquitous part of daily life. A market once dominated by inexpensive hand-held fans now includes a growing array of more sophisticated products, from air-conditioned apparel to cooling sprays that claim to lower your body temperature within seconds.
東亞的夏天正變得越來越熱、越來越漫長，而這種「個人降溫」已成為日常生活中無處不在的一部分。這個曾經由廉價手持風扇主導的市場如今涵蓋了越來越多更為尖端的產品，從空調服裝到聲稱能在幾秒鐘內降低體溫的降溫噴霧，應有盡有。
廣告
Sales of cooling products are soaring, and employers and governments increasingly see them as a necessity rather than a luxury.
降溫產品的銷量正在飆升，僱主和政府也日益將其視為必需品而非奢侈品。
As in Japan, the market for such products in South Korea has grown dramatically over the past two years, said Lee Joo-young, an expert in the relationship between clothing and thermal environments at Seoul National University. She said a major factor is that motors and batteries have become cheaper and more effective, making powerful new cooling products accessible.
首爾大學服裝與熱環境關係專家李周泳（音）表示，與日本一樣，過去兩年韓國此類產品的市場增長極其迅猛。她指出，一個主要因素是電機和電池變得更加便宜且高效，使得強力的新型降溫產品得以普及。
In Japan, the birthplace of the fan-cooled jacket — invented by Hiroshi Ichigaya, a former Sony electrical engineer, in 2004 — the online market for “heat countermeasure” products has expanded more than sixfold since 2020, according to Rakuten, one of the country’s biggest e-commerce platforms. It now lists roughly 800,000 products in that category.
日本是空調服的發源地——該產品是2004年由前索尼電子工程師市谷弘司發明的。據日本最大的電商平台之一樂天的數據，自2020年以來，該平台上的「防暑對策」產品在線市場規模增長了五倍以上，目前列出的該品類產品約有80萬件。
Mr. Ichigaya’s invention has since evolved considerably. One apparel company recently introduced a vest studded with the same metal plates now appearing on hand-held fans.
市谷的發明已經有了很大改變。最近，一家服裝公司推出了一款背心，上面鑲嵌著與新款手持風扇上相同的金屬板。
But these gadgets are no longer just aimed at consumers looking for quick relief during their commutes. Increasingly, they are becoming part of how governments and employers are adapting to extreme heat.
但這些小裝置不再僅僅針對那些希望在通勤途中尋求短暫清涼的消費者。它們正日益成為政府和僱主適應極端酷熱方式的一部分。
Chinese delivery platform Meituan is piloting “air-con jackets” — another spin on Mr. Ichigaya’s invention — for its delivery workers in Chongqing, a city in the country’s southwest. In Hubei Province in central China, the state-owned power company has equipped some of its workers with waist-mounted mini fans.
中國外賣平台美團正在西南城市重慶進行外賣騎手空調服試點——這是市谷的發明的又一種變體。在中國中部的湖北省，國家電網公司為其部分工人配備了腰掛式微型風扇。
In Japan, the Ministry of the Environment and Tokyo’s metropolitan government have promoted parasols as an effective way to reduce heat stress, while some South Korean municipalities have been outfitting their street cleaners with neon cooling vests.
在日本，環境省和東京都政府大力推廣遮陽傘，將其作為減輕熱應力的有效途徑；而在韓國，一些地方政府一直在為其環衛工人配備熒光色降溫背心。
In South Korea, the need for such technologies is especially great on farms, said Kim In-soo, a researcher at the country’s National Institute of Agricultural Sciences. He recently designed his own cooling vest for greenhouse workers that is being piloted at 350 farms around the country.
韓國國立農業科學院研究員金仁秀（音）表示，在韓國，農場對此類技術的需求尤為迫切。他最近為溫室大棚工人設計了一款自研的降溫背心，目前正在全國350個農場進行試點。
廣告
“Greenhouses are one of the worst places to be during a heat wave,” he said. “It’s hot and humid and a lot of the work is time-sensitive harvesting that can’t be stopped at will.”
「在高溫天氣裡，溫室大棚是最糟糕的地方之一，」他說。「那裡又熱又濕，而且很多工作都是有時效性的採摘，無法隨心所欲地停下來。」
The vest, which relies on a technology known as a “vortex tube,” is hooked up to a compressor that shoots cold, dry air into thin tubes, which then distribute it across the skin through tiny perforations running throughout the vest.
這款背心依賴於一種被稱為「渦流管」的技術，它與一台壓縮機相連，壓縮機將乾燥的冷空氣吹入細管中，隨後通過布滿整個背心的微孔將冷氣散布到皮膚上。
Seo Hye-young, the 35-year-old founder of a South Korean start-up that sells cooling neck bands online, has built a business around the growing appetite for cooling products, a trend she first noticed a few years ago.
徐惠英（音）現年35歲，是韓國一家在線銷售降溫頸圈的初創企業的創始人。她圍繞著人們對降溫產品日益增長的需求建立了一家企業，這是她在幾年前首次察覺到的趨勢。
At around $35 apiece, Ms. Seo’s product — a thick, squishy collar filled with coolant — is among the pricier options, but promises shorter freezing times and “explosive” cooling effects. She is on track to sell around 60,000 units this year.
她的產品——一種充填有冷卻劑的厚實、柔軟的項圈——單價約為35美元（約合236元人民幣），屬於較為昂貴的選擇，但承諾降溫更快，且有「爆髮式」的降溫效果。今年的銷量有望達到約6萬件。
“But it got so hot so quickly this year that we’re struggling to keep up with the sudden demand,” she said.
「但是今年天氣熱得太快了，我們正在努力跟上這突如其來的需求，」她說。
The race to beat the heat has spawned more unusual inventions. In Japan, one company has developed a human-sized refrigerated booth that offers quick relief at construction sites, universities and evacuation shelters for natural disasters. Across East Asia, manufacturers are serving up solar-powered fan hats, hands-free parasols and air-conditioned pet houses, among other specialized devices.
這場戰勝酷暑的競賽催生出了更多與眾不同的發明。在日本，一家公司研發了一種人體大小的冷藏艙，為建築工地、大學和自然災害避難所提供快速清涼。而在東亞各地，製造商們正提供太陽能風扇帽、無需手持的遮陽傘和空調寵物屋等各種專業設備。
But Ms. Lee, the professor, said cooling gadgets should not be treated as a cure-all.
但李周泳表示，降溫小裝置不應被視為萬靈藥。
廣告
“When the temperature is above 33 degrees Celsius or so, fans just blow hot air onto your skin,” she said. And the cooling neck bands? They lose their chill too quickly to make a big difference, she added.
「當氣溫達到33攝氏度左右以上時，風扇吹向你皮膚的就只是熱風，」她說。至於降溫頸圈？涼意很快會消退，無法起到太大的作用。
For most people, she said, the low-tech options are often most effective: drinking cold beverages; keeping your feet — among the body’s most notorious heat-trappers — uncovered and cool; and shielding yourself from the sun with parasols, many of which now have black undersides that block UV rays.
她指出，對大多數人來說，低科技含量的選擇往往最有效：飲用冰飲；保持雙腳——人體中最惡名昭彰的集熱部位之一——裸露和涼爽；以及用遮陽傘遮擋陽光，其中許多遮陽傘現在都配有可阻擋紫外線的黑色內襯。
Among the recent converts to parasols is Lee Se-jin, a 36-year-old consultant in Seoul. Though they were primarily seen as a feminine item just a few years ago, he said, in heat like this, such notions of gender have largely gone out the window.
現年36歲的首爾顧問李世鎮（音）是最近開始使用遮陽傘的人之一。他說，雖然就在幾年前遮陽傘還主要被視為女性用品，但在這樣的酷暑下，這類關於性別的固有觀念很大程度上早已煙消雲散。
“These days it really doesn’t feel strange to use a parasol as a man,” Mr. Lee said. “It’s been the biggest difference-maker in terms of actual cooling benefits.”
「如今作為一個男人撐遮陽傘真的不覺得奇怪了，」他說。「這種降溫手段的實際效果是最好的。」
