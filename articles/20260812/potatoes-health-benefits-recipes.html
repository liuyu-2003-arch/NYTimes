How Healthy Are Potatoes?

ALEXANDRA PATTILLO • 2026-08-12
Sweet potatoes tend to get most of the nutritional glory, but regular potatoes (russets, fingerlings, red and Yukon Golds) deserve another look. The tubers help to support your immune system, aid in various bodily functions and keep you energized.
甘薯往往因營養價值備受推崇，但普通馬鈴薯（如褐皮馬鈴薯、拇指馬鈴薯、紅皮馬鈴薯和育空金馬鈴薯）同樣值得我們重新審視。這種塊莖類蔬菜有助於增強免疫系統，促進身體各項機能，讓你保持精力充沛。
“Potatoes often get a bad reputation” because they’re commonly eaten in the form of French fries or potato chips, said Tal Kleiner, a clinical nutrition manager at NYU Langone Health. But when they’re prepared in a healthy way, potatoes are an “amazing, nutrient-dense” food, she added.
紐約大學朗格尼健康中心的臨床營養經理塔爾·克萊納表示：「馬鈴薯經常名聲不佳」，因為人們通常以炸薯條或薯片的形式食用它們。但她補充道，如果以健康的方式烹飪，馬鈴薯其實是一種「非常棒、營養豐富」的食物。
Here’s what to know, along with some recipes from New York Times Cooking.
以下是您需要了解的知識，以及來自《紐約時報》烹飪頻道的一些食譜。
They stoke your energy.
馬鈴薯能夠補充能量

Potatoes are high in carbohydrates (a medium-size peeled russet potato has about 35 grams), but that doesn’t make them “inherently bad,” explained Tara Schmidt, a dietitian and instructor of nutrition at the Mayo Clinic in Rochester, Minn. — it just means they may not be considered an “unlimited” food.
明尼蘇達州羅切斯特市的梅奧診所的營養師兼營養學講師塔拉·施密特解釋說，馬鈴薯含有大量的碳水化合物（一個中等大小、去皮的褐皮馬鈴薯約含35克碳水化合物），但這並不意味著它們「天生不好」，只是說明它們不能被視為可以「無限量食用」的食物。
Potatoes are complex carbs that take longer for the body to break down and digest than simple carbs, so they provide a steadier source of energy, which helps you feel fueled and fuller for longer, Ms. Kleiner said.
克萊納表示，馬鈴薯屬於複合碳水化合物，身體分解和消化它們的時間比簡單碳水化合物更長，因此它們能夠提供更穩定的能量來源，幫助你更持久地保持精力充沛和飽腹感。
But certain varieties do have a high glycemic index, meaning they can rapidly spike blood sugar, Ms. Kleiner said. Diets rich in foods with a high G.I. have been linked to an increased risk of diabetes and heart disease. Waxy potatoes, like fingerlings and red potatoes, tend to have a lower G.I. than russet or Idaho potatoes, she explained, and boiled and roasted potatoes have a lower G.I. than fried or mashed ones.
但克萊納也指出，某些品種的升糖指數確實很高，這意味著它們會導致血糖迅速飆升。富含高升糖指數食物的飲食習慣已被證實會增加患糖尿病和心臟病的風險。她解釋說，蠟質馬鈴薯（如拇指馬鈴薯和紅皮馬鈴薯）升糖指數通常低於褐皮馬鈴薯或愛達荷馬鈴薯，而且煮馬鈴薯和烤馬鈴薯的升糖指數也低於炸馬鈴薯或馬鈴薯泥。
To help manage blood sugar spikes and slow digestion, Ms. Schmidt suggested “meal balancing” by pairing potatoes with lean protein, healthy fats and nonstarchy vegetables.
為了幫助控制血糖驟升並減緩消化，施密特建議通過「飲食平衡」的方式，將馬鈴薯與瘦肉蛋白、健康脂肪和非澱粉類蔬菜搭配食用。
They support a healthy immune system.
馬鈴薯有助於維持健康的免疫系統
Potatoes are a surprisingly solid source of vitamin C. One medium peeled russet potato offers about 25 percent of the recommended daily amount. Vitamin C is essential for the growth and repair of tissue. It also acts as an antioxidant, keeping your immune system strong and helping to protect against chronic diseases such as heart disease and cancer.
令人驚訝的是，馬鈴薯是維生素C的優質來源。一個中等大小、去皮的褐皮馬鈴薯大約能提供每日推薦攝入量的25%。維生素C對組織的生長和修復至關重要。它還具有抗氧化作用，保持免疫系統強健，有助於預防心臟病和癌症等慢性疾病。

Potatoes are also full of phenolics, a type of polyphenol that helps reduce inflammation, regulate immune function and protect cells from damage.
馬鈴薯還富含酚類物質，是一種有助於減輕炎症、調節免疫功能並保護細胞免受損傷的多酚。
And they contain a modest amount of vitamin B6, which is needed for cardiovascular, immune and mental health, said Mary Ellen Camire, a professor of food science and human nutrition at the University of Maine who has studied potatoes’ health benefits.
此外，緬因大學食品科學與人類營養學教授瑪麗·艾倫·卡米爾說，它們還含有適量的維生素B6，這對於心血管、免疫系統和心理健康都是必不可少的，，她曾深入研究過馬鈴薯的健康益處。
They have more potassium than a banana.
馬鈴薯的鉀含量比香蕉還高
Potassium is an electrolyte that the cells in your nerves and muscles need to function, and it helps keep blood pressure in check. But few people get enough potassium in their diet.
鉀是一種電解質，是神經和肌肉細胞維持正常功能所必需的，它也有助於控制血壓。但很少有人能夠通過日常飲食攝入足夠的鉀。
One medium peeled russet potato has about 900 milligrams, compared with 650 milligrams in a similar serving of banana. That’s roughly 30 percent of the recommended daily amount.
一個中等大小、去皮的褐皮馬鈴薯大約含有900毫克鉀，大約佔到每日推薦攝入量的30%，相比之下，同等份量的香蕉含量只有650毫克。
And don’t forget the skin.
別忘了馬鈴薯皮

Potato skins contain much of the vegetable’s phenolics, as well as its fiber. One medium baked potato with the skin on has about 10 to 20 percent of the recommended daily amount of fiber, depending on a person’s age and sex.
馬鈴薯皮中含有它大部分的酚類物質以及膳食纖維。一個帶皮烤制的中等大小馬鈴薯大約含有每日推薦膳食纖維攝入量的10%到20%（具體比例取決於個人的年齡和性別）。
The darker the color of the skin and flesh, the more nutritious the potato, said Mary Ellen DiPaola, a senior outpatient dietitian at the University of California, San Francisco.
加州大學舊金山分校的高級門診營養師瑪麗·艾倫·迪保羅表示，馬鈴薯的皮和肉顏色越深，營養就越豐富。
Purple and red potatoes are packed with pigments called anthocyanins, which can promote cardiovascular health, slow starch digestion and help stave off cognitive problems, Dr. Camire said.
卡米爾博士說，紫色和紅色馬鈴薯富含一種叫做花青素的色素，可以促進心血管健康、延緩澱粉消化，並有助於預防認知障礙。
Is there a best way to eat potatoes?
吃馬鈴薯有最佳方法嗎？
To fully reap the nutritional benefits, preparation is the key. When baked, roasted or boiled with minimal add-ons, potatoes are virtually fat-free. But piling them with butter or sour cream or frying them can quickly torpedo their nutritional profile. Instead, experts recommend a drizzle of olive oil or hot sauce.
要想充分獲得其營養價值，烹飪方法是關鍵。若以烘烤、炙烤或水煮，且僅添加極少配料，馬鈴薯的脂肪含量幾乎為零。但若在其上堆滿黃油、酸奶油，或使用油炸，就會立刻破壞其的營養成分。專家建議，淋上少許橄欖油或辣醬即可。
Potatoes are a more nutrient-dense option than processed white starches like rice or pasta, Ms. DiPaola said. “And who doesn’t like potatoes?”
迪保羅表示，與大米或意大利麵等加工過的白色澱粉相比，馬鈴薯是一種營養密度更高的選擇。「況且，誰不愛吃馬鈴薯呢？」
