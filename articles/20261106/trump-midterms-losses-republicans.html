「敵人是我們自己」：揭秘川普團隊的歷史性崩潰

‘I Have Seen the Enemy, and It Is Us’: Inside Team Trump’s Historic Collapse

SHANE GOLDMACHER, JONATHAN SWAN, MAGGIE HABERMAN • 2026-11-06
Six days before the election, more than two dozen of President Donald Trump’s top strategists logged onto a private video call.
選舉前六天，唐納德·川普總統的二十多名高級策略師連入了一場私密的影片通話。
The mood was grim. The discussion began with talk of cyanide.
氣氛十分嚴峻。談話以氰化物開場。
“Do you want the white pill first, or the black pill first?” asked Tony Fabrizio, the president’s chief pollster.
「你們是想先吃白藥丸，還是黑藥丸？」總統的首席民調專家托尼·法布裡齊奧問道。
Tim Saler, one of the Republican Party’s top data consultants, laid out two scenarios. In the first, the $400 million MAGA Inc. super political action committee was reaching the voters that it had wanted and expected to vote. “That’s the optimistic scenario,” he said.
共和黨頂級數據顧問之一蒂姆·薩勒給出了兩種可能情形。第一個是擁有4億美元經費的超級政治行動委員會「MAGA公司」(MAGA Inc.)有效動員了他們想動員且預計會投票的選民。「這是樂觀的設想，」他說。
廣告
The other was a wipeout.
另一個則是全軍覆沒。
“I’m getting my affairs in order,” James Blair, who left the White House to oversee the president’s midterm strategy, said to laughter, according to four people on the call. “I suggest everybody else do the same.”
據參與通話的四人透露，離開白宮轉而負責監督總統的期中選舉策略的詹姆斯·布萊爾在笑聲中說：「我正在安排後事。我建議大家也早做準備。」
For roughly 30 minutes, the call ran on gallows humor: funeral arrangements, prison food, presidential pardons, even a brief riff on fleeing the country.
在大約30分鐘的時間裡，通話內容充斥著黑色幽默：葬禮安排、監獄伙食、總統赦免，甚至還一度聊到流亡海外。
“At least Bibi won. He’ll protect us,” Blair said sarcastically of Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, whose country had voted the day before. (Netanyahu’s bloc fell short of a majority, and his party soon claimed the election had been stolen.)
「至少比比贏了。他會保護我們的，」在提及以色列總理本雅明·納坦尼雅胡時，布萊爾諷刺地說道，納坦尼雅胡的國家在前一天舉行了投票。（納坦尼雅胡的陣營未能獲得多數席位，其黨派隨後聲稱選舉存在舞弊。）
“Maybe we could get asylum in Israel,” Fabrizio said.
「也許我們能在以色列獲得庇護，」法布裡齊奧說。
Collapse at the Polls
投票站的崩潰
The scope of the Republican wreckage would become clear a week later.
一周後，共和黨潰敗的規模才真正清晰起來。
Democrats stormed to a huge House majority and won control of the Senate, a remarkable feat powered by victories in Republican strongholds — Kansas, South Carolina, Texas — that few could have imagined a year ago.
民主黨人以壓倒性優勢奪得眾議院多數席位，並贏得了參議院的控制權。這是一場了不起的勝利，得益於民主黨人在堪薩斯州、南卡羅來納州和得克薩斯州等共和黨重鎮的勝利。一年前幾乎無人能夠預料到這樣的結果。
This account of the Republican Party’s collapse is based on interviews with more than 70 people — among them lawmakers, political strategists, party leaders, pollsters, White House officials and congressional aides — who were directly involved in the midterm elections, many of whom were granted anonymity to describe private conversations and decisions.
本文記述的共和黨崩潰基於對70多人的採訪，受訪者包括直接參与期中選舉的議員、政治策略師、黨派領袖、民調專家、白宮官員和國會助手。其中許多人獲准匿名發言，以便披露不為外界所知的談話內容和決策過程。
The scale of the defeat was far from inevitable.
如此重大的失敗遠非不可避免。
Republicans had record fundraising, the world’s richest man, a favorable Supreme Court ruling on campaign finance and a demoralized, unpopular opposition.
共和黨人擁有創紀錄的籌款規模、世界首富的支持、最高法院一項有利於競選資金的裁決，並且面對著一個士氣低落且不受歡迎的反對黨。
廣告
In the Senate, they had a map that required Democrats to compete in states they hadn’t won in decades. 
在參議院，目前的競選版圖使得民主黨人不得不在幾十年未曾贏過的州展開競爭。
And in the House, the Trump team demolished the tradition of redrawing congressional lines only once a decade in order to carve up new districts to help the GOP bolster its narrow majority.
而在眾議院，川普團隊打破了每十年重劃一次國會選區的傳統，試圖通過劃定新選區來幫助共和黨鞏固其微弱的多數席位。
What Republicans could not overcome was a president who lost the public’s trust on the economy and so much more. And what they had not counted on was an unpopular war in the Middle East — one that would close the Strait of Hormuz and push gas prices past $4 a gallon — that would rage all the way through the election.
共和黨人無法克服的難關在於，總統在經濟及其他諸多問題上失去了公眾信任。他們未曾料到的是，一場在中東發生的不受歡迎的戰爭——這場戰爭導致霍爾木茲海峽關閉，並將汽油價格推高至每加侖4美元以上——會一路蔓延，貫穿整個選舉。
And Democrats were ready, this time, to seize on the Republican stumbles.
而這一次，民主黨人做好了準備，抓住了共和黨人的失誤。
A Stark Warning on the Iran War
關於伊朗戰爭的嚴厲警告
The slide deck that Blair had circulated over the summer to officials in the West Wing was stark.
布萊爾今夏分發給白宮西翼官員的演示文稿展現出了嚴峻的形勢。
廣告
One slide linked Republicans’ polling numbers to an Iran war timeline.
其中一張幻燈片將共和黨的民調數據與伊朗戰爭的時間表聯繫在了一起。
It showed how the Democratic advantage had ballooned during active hostilities, shrunk during talks over a “memorandum of understanding” and then spiked again when the ceasefire collapsed.
它展示了民主黨的優勢如何在激烈的敵對行動期間擴大，在「諒解備忘錄」談判期間縮小，隨後在停火破裂時再度飆升。
By the end of July, Democrats had a 10-point edge on the congressional ballot.
截至7月底，民主黨人在國會選票上已經佔據了10個百分點的優勢。
The “bottom line” was hard to miss. It was in highlighter yellow: Republicans could still conceivably win the House if Democrats had only a 4-point edge. “But that won’t happen without an extended ceasefire, Hormuz open & gas prices falling,” the slide warned. 
最終的「結論」顯而易見。內容用熒光黃標出：如果民主黨人只有4個百分點的優勢，共和黨人仍然有可能贏得眾議院。「但這必須在停火延長、霍爾木茲海峽開放且汽油價格下降的情況下才會發生，」幻燈片警告稱。
“A final result approaching D+10 would likely cost us the Senate.”
「接近D+10的最終結果可能會讓我們失去參議院。」
The results Tuesday were even worse: Trump’s party was on track to lose the national popular congressional vote by about 13 points, according to estimates by The New York Times. It is shaping up to be the worst loss in a half-century, since the aftermath of the Watergate scandal.
週二的實際結果甚至更糟：根據《紐約時報》估計，川普的共和黨在全國國會選舉總票數上預計將以約13個百分點的劣勢落敗。這將是自​​水門事件之後，半個世紀以來最慘重的敗局。
廣告
Even before the polls had closed, Trump had begun to ruminate on what might have been.
甚至在投票站關閉之前，川普就已經開始反覆回想原本可能發生的情況。
“If we never went into Iran, what do you think would happen in the midterms?” the president had asked privately, according to a person who spoke to him.
「如果我們當初沒有捲入伊朗，你認為期中選舉會發生什麼？」據一位與總統交談過的人士透露，總統曾私下這樣問道。
An Unchecked Second Term
不受限制的第二任期
What made the war such a potent force is that it told a simple story for voters.
這場戰爭之所以成為如此巨大的影響因素，是因為它向選民講述了一個簡單的故事。
As a candidate, Trump had promised no new wars. He started a new war. 
作為候選人，川普曾承諾不發動新戰爭，但他發動了一場新戰爭。
He promised lower prices. Gas prices soared and inflation rose.
他承諾降低物價，但汽油價格飆升，通貨膨脹加劇。
廣告
The story of the Republicans’ midterm implosion was not only a story of broken promises; it was also a story of radical overreach. After Trump came back from pariah status after Jan. 6, 2021, some advisers believed he had convinced himself that the rules of politics had been suspended for him alone. That illusion was obliterated Tuesday night.
共和黨期中選舉崩盤的故事不僅是一個打破承諾的故事，也是一個激進越權的故事。在川普從2021年1月6日之後的政治棄兒狀態中回歸後，一些顧問認為他已經開始相信，政治規則唯獨對他不再適用。那種錯覺在週二晚上被徹底粉碎了。
Over nearly two years, hubris had become the governing philosophy of Trump’s second presidency.
近兩年裡，傲慢已經成為川普第二任期的治理哲學。
There was a seemingly endless string of investigative pieces revealing that Trump, his family and their close associates had used the presidency to enrich themselves brazenly. Those reports began to resonate with voters; an early October poll from Fox News showed corruption as the top voter concern. (Anna Kelly, a White House spokesperson, said: “The President only acts in the best interest of the American people. There are no conflicts of interest.”)
層出不窮的調查報導揭露，川普、他的家人及其親密夥伴利用總統職位明目張膽地為自己斂財。這些報導開始引起選民的共鳴；福克斯新聞10月初的一項民調顯示，腐敗是選民最關心的問題。（白宮發言人安娜·凱利表示：「總統行事只考慮美國人民的最大利益。不存在利益衝突。」）
In 2025, Trump began a trade war with the entire world — the pain of which was still being felt more than a year later on farms all over the suddenly competitive states of Kansas, Texas and Iowa.
2025年，川普對全世界發動了一場貿易戰——一年多後，堪薩斯州、得克薩斯州和愛荷華州的農場依然能真切感受到由此導致的痛苦，這些州的競選爭奪也突然間激烈起來。
Some members of his team grew exasperated as Trump spoke seemingly more about his new ballroom than the border, golf courses than grocery prices, interior decorations than inflation.
他的團隊的一些成員感到惱火，因為比起邊境問題，川普似乎更多地在談論他的新宴會廳；比起食品價格，他更關心高爾夫球場；比起通貨膨脹，他更在意室內裝飾。
On the eve of the midterms, he focused his closing pitch on his refurbishment of the Reflecting Pool on the National Mall, joking that he was considering renaming it for himself rather than Abraham Lincoln.
在期中選舉前夕，他將最後的陳述重點放在他對國家廣場倒影池的翻修上，並開玩笑說他正考慮用自己的名字取代亞伯拉罕·林肯的命名。
Unlike in the past, Democrats mostly ignored the daily Trump distractions and sought to connect corruption to costs. They also told a story of a government focused on elites while average people struggled — from Jeffrey Epstein’s protected associates to the president’s $2.2 billion in personal profits.
與過去不同的是，民主黨人很大程度上忽略了川普每天轉移注意力的舉動，而是試圖將腐敗與生活成本聯繫起來。他們還講述了一個政府只關注精英、普通人卻在苦苦掙扎的故事——這其中包括受保護的傑弗里·愛潑斯坦同夥，以及總統個人賺取的22億美元。
In the House, Democrats quickly and unexpectedly countered Trump’s redistricting push and pursued strong candidates deep into red territory to spread the map. In the Senate, they found a series of blue-chip recruits who put once unthinkable states into play. In the final, frenzied weeks of the campaign, Republicans were redirecting cash from the usual swing states of North Carolina and Georgia to try to shore up traditional strongholds in South Carolina and even Mississippi.
在眾議院，民主黨人迅速而意外地反擊了川普的重劃選區計劃，並派遣強有力的候選人深入紅色陣營以擴大版圖。在參議院，他們找到了一批優質的候選人，讓曾經不可撼動的州也變得充滿懸念。在競選最後那狂亂的幾週裡，共和黨人不得不將資金從北卡羅萊納州和喬治亞州等傳統的搖擺州轉移出來，試圖穩固南卡羅來納州甚至密西西比州的傳統基本盤。
The war in Iran, said Rep. Hakeem Jeffries, D-N.Y., the minority leader and now the speaker-in-waiting, helped “supercharge” the contrast between the two parties.
少數黨領袖、如今已是候任議長的紐約州民主黨眾議員哈基姆·傑弗里斯表示，伊朗戰爭加劇了兩黨之間的對比。
Republicans had tried to frame the midterms as a choice between “common sense and crazy,” relentlessly attacking the rising democratic socialist wing of the left.
共和黨人曾試圖將期中選舉定性為「常識與瘋狂」之間的選擇，並無情地攻擊左翼中不斷崛起的民主社會主義派。
Yet Trump’s own pollster’s final survey, taken two weeks before the election, showed that while both the MAGA Republican brand and democratic socialists were unpopular with voters, MAGA carried more baggage. When Republicans who were not planning to cast ballots were asked what it would take to earn back their vote, their top two answers were ending the war and addressing inflation.
然而，川普自己的民調專家在選舉前兩週進行的最終調查顯示，儘管MAGA共和黨的品牌和民主社會主義者在選民中都不受歡迎，但MAGA背負了更多的包袱。當不打算投票的共和黨人被問及需要什麼才能贏回他們的選票時，他們排名前兩位的回答是結束戰爭和解決通貨膨脹。
Alex Pfeiffer, a spokesperson for the MAGA Inc. super PAC, said that the group’s “sober and clear-eyed view of the political environment” led to investing in House races Republicans were leading in, in Maine, Florida, Tennessee, New York and Texas.
「MAGA公司」超級政治行動委員會發言人亞歷克斯·法伊弗表示，該組織「對政治環境的清醒客觀認識」促使他們將資金投入到了共和黨在緬因州、佛羅里達州、田納西州、紐約州和得克薩斯州處於領先的眾議院競選中。
“The people peddling these made up quotes and false gossip not only demean and belittle the historic and generous investment by the president into the party’s midterm effort, but they also slander the hard work of the many who contributed to this effort,” he said in a statement. “They are cowards and losers.”
「那些兜售這些捏造的引語和虛假流言的人不僅貶低和輕視了總統為該黨期中選舉所作的歷史性的、慷慨的投資，還誹謗了許多為這一努力做出貢獻的人的辛勤工作，」他在一份聲明中表示。「他們是懦夫和失敗者。」
A White House official, who declined to speak on the record, said in response to the reporting for this article: “President Trump was not on the ballot, and it’s proven that Trump voters show up when he is on the ballot. Candidate quality matters, and President Trump did a better job promoting many of the candidates on the ballot than the candidates did themselves.”
一位拒絕具名的白宮官員在回應本文的報導時說：「川普總統不在選票上，而事實證明，當他在選票上時，他的選民就會出來投票。候選人的素質很重要，而川普總統在推介選票上的許多候選人方面做得比候選人自己還要出色。」
No candidate proved more costly to the party than Ken Paxton, the scandal-ridden Texas attorney general. Trump endorsed him over Sen. John Cornyn, the Republican incumbent, ignoring a year of warnings that Paxton would put the seat in jeopardy. Republicans were ultimately forced to spend close to $300 million in Texas — money that could have gone to shore up candidates in other states. Paxton lost by 12 points.
事實證明，沒有哪個候選人比醜聞纏身的得克薩斯州總檢察長肯·帕克斯頓讓共和黨付出的代價更大。川普選擇支持帕克斯頓而不是在任共和黨參議員約翰·科寧，儘管一年來一直有人在警告，帕克斯頓會讓這個席位陷入險境。共和黨最終被迫在得州投入近3億美元，這筆錢本可用於鞏固其他州的候選人。帕克斯頓以12個百分點的差距落敗。
On another recent call, the Trump team mocked the haplessness of some of the Republicans they were trying to elect.
在最近的另一次通話中，川普團隊嘲笑了他們試圖助選的一些共和黨人的倒霉相。
“I have seen the enemy,” Blair told the group a week earlier. “And it is us.”
「我看到了敵人，」布萊爾一週前對該團隊說。「那正是我們自己。」
Fabrizio replied: “In many places.”
法布裡齊奧回答道：「在很多地方都是這樣。」
The Democrats’ Playbook
民主黨人的劇本
In May 2025, Schumer arrived at Wildflower Farms, an upscale resort in New York’s Hudson Valley, for a secretive retreat with donors and party officials. He desperately needed to expand the Senate map.
2025年5月，舒默來到位於紐約哈德遜河谷的高檔度假村野花農場，與捐助者和黨派官員進行了一次祕密閉門會議。他迫切需要擴大參議院的選舉版圖。
To win control of the Senate, Democrats needed to hold all of their seats and flip at least four, including in states that Trump won handily in 2024.
為了奪取參議院控制權，民主黨人需要保住他們現有的所有席位，並至少翻盤四個席位，其中包括川普在2024年輕鬆獲勝的州。
Schumer spent months wooing Roy Cooper, North Carolina’s popular Democratic former governor, and took at least three trips to Ohio to coax former Sen. Sherrod Brown, who had lost in 2024 but had a populist brand that matched the country’s mood.
舒默花了數月時間爭取北卡羅萊納州頗受歡迎的民主黨前州長羅伊·庫珀，並至少三次前往俄亥俄州，勸說前參議員謝羅德·布朗出山，布朗雖在2024年敗選，但他的民粹主義風格恰好契合了當時全國的民情。
“If you retire and we lose the majority by one vote, you won’t enjoy your retirement,” Schumer recalled telling Brown, 73.
 「如果你退休了，而我們以一票之差失去多數席位，你將無法安享退休生活，」舒默回憶起他對73歲的布朗說的話。
Both ran and won.
兩人都參選並獲勝了。
In the House, where Democrats had fallen three seats short in 2024, recruiters went hunting in districts Trump had carried by 10 points or more, betting early that the emboldened president would overreach.
在眾議院，民主黨人在2024年以三席之差落敗。這次招募人員前往川普曾以10個百分點或更大優勢拿下的選區尋找候選人，他們早早押注這位氣焰囂張的總統將會越權行事。
They set about recruiting candidates with distinctive biographies: military veterans, doctors, farmers, a smokejumper, a Tejano music star — just about anyone who didn’t scream professional politician.
他們開始招募具有獨特背景的候選人：退伍軍人、醫生、農民、空降消防員、特哈諾音樂明星——幾乎涵蓋了任何不具有典型職業政治人士特徵的人。
Redistricting Wars
重劃選區戰爭
Trump, meanwhile, was trying to redraw the House battlefield itself.
與此同時，川普正試圖重繪眾議院選區版圖本身。
In the spring of 2025, Blair, then his deputy chief of staff, brought the president a plan to redraw congressional maps mid-decade to make seats safer for Republicans. The gerrymandering push started in Texas and soon spread to other Republican-led states.
2025年春，時任副幕僚長布萊爾向總統提交了一項在十年中期重新劃定國會選區版圖的計劃，以確保共和黨人的席位更加穩固。這場選區劃分操縱行動始於得克薩斯州，很快蔓延至其他由共和黨主導的州。
From the start, Blair was cocksure about his redistricting plan, and he and the president dismissed Republicans who worried about the maneuver backfiring.
從一開始，布萊爾對他的重劃選區計劃就非常自信，他和總統都無視了那些擔心此舉會適得其反的共和黨人的擔憂。
But by April 2026, Democrats had good reason to believe that they had neutralized the threat by redrawing districts in several blue states. Jeffries held a celebratory news conference featuring a poster with the faces of Trump and Blair and an anonymous quote that had appeared in an article about redistricting in the Times: “Maximum warfare everywhere all the time.”
但到了2026年4月，民主黨人有充分理由相信，他們通過在幾個藍州重劃選區已經化解了這一威脅。傑弗里斯舉行了一場慶祝新聞發布會，展示了一張印有川普和布萊爾面孔的海報，上面還印著《紐約時報》一篇關於重劃選區文章中的匿名引語：「隨時隨地進行最高級別的戰爭。」
When Blair saw the poster, he texted one of Jeffries’ top aides, Gideon Bragin: “Can you send a copy of this to my office?”
當布萊爾看到這張海報時，他給傑弗里斯的一位高級助手吉迪恩·布拉金髮短訊說：「你能把這海報複印一份送到我辦公室嗎？」
Instead, it remained inside House Democratic headquarters through Election Day, when a blue tidal wave washed over even many of the districts drawn to favor Republicans.
他未能如願，這張海報一直留在眾議院民主黨總部，直到選舉日那天，一股藍色的浪潮甚至席捲了許多原本被劃定為有利於共和黨的選區。
The Unforced Errors
非受迫性失誤
As 2026 began, the administration’s immigration enforcement surge turned violent in Minneapolis when two protesters were shot and killed by federal agents. Both killings were captured on video.
2026年伊始，政府加強移民執法的行動在明尼阿波利斯演變成了暴力事件，兩名抗議者被聯邦特工開槍打死。這兩起槍殺事件都被影片記錄了下來。
Soon after Minneapolis, Democrats’ internal polls showed the party taking the edge on immigration for the first time. What had been one of Trump’s perennial strengths was for a period his top liability, according to one of the president’s senior strategists.
明尼阿波利斯事件發生後不久，民主黨內部的民調顯示，該黨首次在移民問題上佔據了優勢。據總統的一位高級策略師表示，曾是川普長期優勢之一的議題在一段時間內成了他最大的包袱。
Then, about five weeks after the second shooting, Trump took the country to war in Iran.
隨後，在第二起槍擊事件發生大約五周後，川普帶領國家在伊朗捲入了戰爭。
Blair at first shared his boss’s optimism that the war would be a quick success. He reassured skeptical colleagues that the conflict would not be the political disaster some feared because some Defense Department officials had assured the White House it would be resolved rapidly.
布萊爾起初認同他老闆的樂觀情緒，認為戰爭會迅速取得勝利。他向持懷疑態度的同事保證，衝突不會像某些人擔心的那樣成為一場政治災難，因為一些國防部官員曾向白宮保證，衝突會迅速得到解決。
But the president’s 4-to-6-week timeline stretched into months.
但總統承諾的四至六週時間表卻拖延成了數月。
In public, Trump crowed about a decisive military triumph. In private, advisers briefed him on the closure of the Strait of Hormuz, spiking prices of gas and fertilizer and an alarming depletion of U.S. weaponry.
在公開場合，川普大肆宣揚取得了決定性的軍事勝利。在私下裡，顧問們卻向他彙報了霍爾木茲海峽關閉、汽油和化肥價格飆升以及美國武器庫驚人消耗的情況。
Trump knew the war was draining his support, according to advisers, but for a brief moment it seemed that he had found himself a way out of the mess. By mid-June, Vice President JD Vance had negotiated a memorandum of understanding with Iran, and the Trump political team exhaled. Fabrizio’s first poll of the proposal found that voters aware of the deal favored it by 41 percentage points, and 67% thought it would bring down gas prices.
據顧問們透露，川普知道戰爭正在消耗他的支持率，但有那麼短暫的一刻，他似乎為自己找到了一個擺脫困境的出路。到6月中旬，副總統萬斯與伊朗談判達成了一份諒解備忘錄，川普的政治團隊終於鬆了一口氣。法布裡齊奧針對該提案的首次民調發現，了解該協議的選民以41個百分點的優勢表示支持，67%的人認為它將降低汽油價格。
But GOP Iran hawks, including Sen. Ted Cruz of Texas, pressed Trump to keep the pressure on Tehran. The deal fell apart and the war dragged on.
但包括得克薩斯州參議員泰德·克魯茲在內的共和黨對伊鷹派施壓川普繼續對德黑蘭保持高壓。協議最終破裂，戰爭繼續拖延。
‘My Money’
「我的錢」
Trump never stopped raising money after the 2024 election, assembling a roughly $400 million war chest that he made clear to advisers he saw as “my money.”
自2024年大選以來，川普從未停止籌款，積攢了大約4億美元的競選資金，他向顧問們明確表示，他將這些資金視作「我的錢」。
By August, with the election less than 100 days away, Republican Senate strategists still had no clarity on where Team Trump would spend. One described being “shocked at how ill-prepared they were.”
到了8月，距離大選已不到100天，共和党參議院的策略師們仍然不清楚川普團隊將在哪裡投入資金。一位戰略家表示，「對他們準備得如此不充分感到震驚」。
A Trump adviser said a plan existed but could not be shared because the president had not approved it.
一位川普顧問表示，計劃是存在的，但由於總統尚未批准而無法分享。
Some days, Trump told advisers privately: “We’ve gotta win. Anything we gotta do.” On others, he sounded as if he couldn’t care less. “It says something very nice about me that they just can’t win without me,” he told aides.
有些時候，川普私下告訴顧問：「我們必須贏。不惜一切代價。」而有些時候，他聽起來好像毫不在乎。「這說明我真的很棒，沒有我他們就是贏不了，」他對助手們說。
Blair and others had to make the case to the president to sign off on the spending. Trump withheld his approval until the end of the summer.
布萊爾和其他人必須向總統陳述理由，以獲得他的支出批准。川普直到夏末才予以批准。
The delay was costly: Ad prices soar as elections near.
這種拖延代價高昂：隨著選舉臨近，廣告價格一路飆升。
Typically, endangered lawmakers distance themselves from an unpopular president in midterm elections. Team Trump wanted to do the opposite.
通常情況下，在期中選舉中處境危險的議員會與不受歡迎的總統保持距離。川普團隊卻想反其道而行之。
“We have taken that old construct and flipped it on its head,” said Chris LaCivita, the co-manager of Trump’s 2024 campaign.
「我們打破了那種舊的模式，把它徹底顛覆，」川普2024年競選活動的聯合經理克里斯·拉奇維塔說。
Thus, the first modern midterm convention was born, a two-day celebration of the president in Dallas in September that few members of his party had wanted.
於是，首屆現代期中選舉大會應運而生。這是9月在達拉斯舉行的一場總統慶典，為期兩天，而該黨內鮮有成員對此表示支持。
The convention did bring one piece of good news: money. On the first day, Elon Musk sent $75 million to the Senate Leadership Fund, the super PAC led by allies of the Senate majority leader, John Thune of South Dakota. It was one of the largest single contributions in American history.
這次大會確實帶來了一個好消息：資金。第一天，伊隆·馬斯克向參議院領導基金捐贈了7500萬美元。該超級政治行動委員會由參議院多數黨領袖、南達科他州的約翰·圖恩的盟友領導。這也是美國歷史上最大的單筆捐款之一。
At the convention, Paxton made his way into the fund’s suite, where Alex Latcham, the super PAC’s executive director, made a somewhat cryptic remark to him.
在大會上，帕克斯頓走進了該領導基金的套房。在那裡，該超級政治行動委員會的執行董事亞歷克斯·拉查姆對他說了一句略顯隱晦的話。
“You’re going to be happy tomorrow.”
「明天你就會高興的。」
The ads were about to begin.
廣告即將開始投放。
Musk would spend more than $450 million to aid Republicans in the midterms, much of it in Texas to try to save Paxton. The super PAC he funded spent tens of millions on ads attacking the Democratic nominee, James Talarico, for his support of transgender rights, mirroring a line of attack that the Trump team had used effectively in 2024.
馬斯克將在期中選舉中花費超過4.5億美元幫助共和黨人，其中大部分資金投向了得克薩斯州，試圖拯救帕克斯頓。他資助的超級政治行動委員會花費數千萬美元投放廣告，攻擊民主黨提名人詹姆斯·塔拉里科支持跨性別權利，這重現了川普團隊在2024年曾有效運用的一套攻擊策略。
In other races, the focus on transgender ads became a point of disagreement between officials at the Senate Leadership Fund and Trump’s operation, which argued that the 2026 election was fundamentally about the economy and that running ads on other topics would be less effective. Senate GOP strategists saw economic messages falling flat in the current political environment in some states.
在其他選區，對跨性別廣告的關注成為參議院領導基金官員和川普競選團隊之間的分歧點。後者爭辯說，2026年的選舉從根本上說是關於經濟的，在其他話題上投放廣告效果欠佳。參議院共和黨的戰略家們則認為，在當前的政治環境下，有關經濟的信息在某些州並沒有引起共鳴。
The fight to save the Senate was hampered by party leadership that could barely work together. Trump and Senate GOP leaders, Thune among them, never grew close. The president endorsed Paxton over their objections. And when Trump advisers met with officials from the top Republican super PAC at Camp David in March 2026 to plan for the midterms, no one from the National Republican Senatorial Committee was invited.
這場保住參議院席位的鬥爭因黨內領導層幾乎無法協同工作而受阻。川普與參議院共和黨領導人（包括圖恩在內）從未建立起密切關係。總統不顧他們的反對，支持了帕克斯頓。此外，當川普的顧問們在2026年3月於戴維營與來自頂級共和黨超級政治行動委員會的官員會面，以規劃期中選舉時，全國共和党參議院委員會竟無一人受邀。
The Collapse
崩潰
Not long after the Republican convention in Dallas, diesel prices hit all-time highs.
在達拉斯的共和黨大會後不久，柴油價格創下歷史新高。
In the Senate, it got so bad for the GOP that by early October Republicans were ordering up polls in seemingly unthinkable states, like South Dakota and Idaho.
在參議院，共和黨的處境變得極為糟糕，以至於到了10月初，共和黨人開始在南達科他州和愛達荷州等看似固若金湯的州進行民調。
A month before the election, the Senate Leadership Fund pulled ads from North Carolina, ceding the state to redirect money to Kansas, then South Carolina and eventually Mississippi — a state Trump had carried by nearly 23 points two years ago.
選舉前一個月，參議院領導基金撤回了在北卡羅萊納州的廣告，放棄了該州，將資金轉投向堪薩斯州，隨後是南卡羅來納州，最後是密西西比州——川普在兩年前曾在這個州以近23個百分點的巨大優勢獲勝。
“Battleground Mississippi,” Trump’s advisers called it mockingly on a call.
「密西西比戰場州，」川普的顧問在一次通話中嘲諷地這樣稱呼它。
In the House, the deterioration was perhaps worse.
在眾議院，情況也許更糟。
At one point, top officials with the Congressional Leadership Fund and Trump’s super PACs had decided to give up trying to flip Rep. Henry Cuellar’s South Texas seat — only to realize that Speaker Mike Johnson was set to be in the district the day the ads were going to come off the air. They delayed the announcement for a day to spare Johnson an embarrassing episode.
國會領導基金和川普超級政治行動委員會的高級官員一度已決定放棄爭奪眾議員亨利·庫埃利亞爾在南得克薩斯州的席位，卻發現議長邁克·強生定於廣告停播當天前往該選區。為了避免讓強生陷入尷尬境地，他們將消息宣布推遲了一天。
Cuellar won reelection in a landslide.
庫埃利亞爾以壓倒性優勢贏得連任。
In the end, the map shifted so far left that House Republicans — who polled more than 100 seats — could not keep up. It surprised even some leading Democrats.
最終，選舉版圖如此大幅度地向左移動，哪怕眾議院共和黨人對100多個席位做了民調監控，也根本招架不過來。這甚至讓一些民主黨的主要人物都感到驚訝。
At a September dinner, Rahm Emanuel, who ran the House Democrats’ campaign arm in the 2006 wave, asked the party’s top recruits how much Trump had won their districts by. When Zach Dembo, a military veteran and former federal prosecutor running in Kentucky’s 6th Congressional District, said 15 points, Emanuel looked at him in disbelief.
在9月的一次晚宴上，曾操盤2006年民主黨眾議院「藍色浪潮「大勝的拉姆·伊曼紐爾問該黨的主要新晉候選人，川普曾在他們的選區贏了多少個百分點。在肯塔基州第六國會選區競選的退伍軍人兼前聯邦檢察官扎克·登博回答，是15個百分點，伊曼紐爾難以置信地看著他。
Dembo won.
登博贏了。
The last Sunday before Election Day, Jeffries arranged for a symbolic final stop to a battleground district.
選舉日之前的最後一個星期日，傑弗里斯安排了一次象徵性的最後一站，前往一個戰場選區。
He went to Alabama, where Republicans had redrawn Rep. Shomari Figures’ district to favor their party after a Supreme Court decision weakened the Voting Rights Act. It was important to go there, Jeffries said, because “he was facing a renewed effort to subject the African American community to Jim Crow-like tactics.”
他去了阿拉巴馬州。在最高法院的一項裁決削弱了《投票權法》之後，共和黨人在那裡重新劃定了眾議員肖馬利·菲格斯的選區，以利於他們自己的黨派。傑弗里斯表示，去那裡很重要，因為「他正面臨著新一輪試圖使非裔美國人社區遭受類似《吉姆·克勞法》策略的企圖」。
“It’s fair to say that Shomari and I both felt the weight of history given the fact that the civil rights movement led by Dr. King, Rosa Parks, and so many others began in the very place where we were ending this all important 2026 campaign,” Jeffries said.
「可以說，肖馬利和我都感受到了歷史的沉重，因為由馬丁·路德·金博士、羅莎·帕克斯以及其他許多人領導的民權運動，正是在我們結束這場至關重要的2026年競選的地方拉開帷幕的，」傑弗里斯說。
Figures won his race Tuesday.
菲格斯在週二贏得了選舉。
On the way to the airport, Jeffries made an unpublicized stop at the Freedom Monument Sculpture Park that remembers the history of enslaved people in America.
在去機場的途中，傑弗里斯低調造訪了紀念美國被奴役者歷史的自由紀念雕塑公園。
In January, Jeffries is now set to become the first Black speaker in American history.
明年1月，傑弗里斯即將成為美國歷史上首位黑人眾議院議長。
