剝削與掌控：NewJeans祕密合同揭露K-pop陰暗面

Secret Deals Reveal K-Pop’s Firm Grip on a Top Girl Group

SARAH HURTES, JIN YU YOUNG • 2026-11-06
Two years ago, the five members of the record-shattering girl group NewJeans, some still in their teens, did what no K-pop stars of their stature had done in a generation: They quit their record label.
兩年前，屢創紀錄的韓國女子組合NewJeans的五名成員做出了一件近一代人以來從未有同等知名度的韓流明星做過的事：與公司解約。當時，部分成員還只有十幾歲。
“Staying at the company will only prolong our psychological pain,” one of the members, Hanni Pham, told reporters, about a year after their album “Get Up” debuted at No. 1 on the U.S. charts.
「留在公司只會延長我們的精神痛苦，」成員之一范玉欣(Hanni Pham)對記者說。那是她們的專輯《Get Up》登頂美國排行榜約一年後。
What followed was one of the most explosive controversies in K-pop history, a yearslong legal and power struggle over artistic freedom in an industry where companies tightly control aspiring stars starting from early adolescence. Executives warned that if NewJeans could walk away, the multibillion-dollar K-pop industry might collapse.
隨後發生的是K-pop歷史上最具爆炸性的爭議之一：一場圍繞藝術自由的、長達數年的法律與權力鬥爭。在這個行業裡，娛樂公司從藝人十幾歲起就對其嚴密控制。高管們警告說，如果NewJeans可以一走了之，價值數十億美元的K-pop產業可能崩潰。
Underpinning this fight are the secret contracts that the singers signed while ages 14 to 17, when they were mostly unknowns, dreaming of stardom. The contracts, which were obtained by The New York Times, offer a rare inside look at the strict control that K-pop labels place on even the biggest acts. (The contracts contain redactions of names and home addresses. The Times made further redactions to protect personal information.)
這場爭端的核心是五名成員在14歲至17歲時簽署的祕密合同。當時，她們大多沒沒無聞，懷揣著明星夢。《紐約時報》獲得了這些合同，得以罕見地窺見韓國的娛樂公司如何嚴密控制旗下藝人，即便是最紅的明星也不例外。（合同中的姓名和家庭住址等信息已被隱去。《紐約時報》為保護個人隱私，又進一步遮蓋了部分信息。）
The Korean government has a K-pop contract template that guarantees the right to education and to physical and mental health. The NewJeans contracts stripped away that language. The contracts banned such behavior as having an “insincere attitude,” entering “inappropriate romantic relationships” or criticizing the record label. The agreements included steep exit clauses and allowed the label to sell NewJeans music after the end of the deal while slashing payments to the band. And it gave the label the right to use artificial intelligence-generated NewJeans voices indefinitely.
韓國政府有一套K-pop合同模板，保障受教育權以及身心健康權。NewJeans的合同刪去了這些表述。合同禁止諸如「態度不真誠」、進入「不適當的戀愛關係」或批評廠牌等行為。協議包含高額退出條款，允許廠牌在合同結束後繼續銷售NewJeans的音樂，同時削減支付給組合的款項。它還賦予廠牌無限期使用人工智慧生成的NewJeans聲音的權利。
K-pop has long been dogged by allegations of exploitation. Labels recruit children, put them through years of intensive training, then often bill them for those costs. Performers have described near-starvation diets and lingering debt, even while selling out stadiums. Several stars, known as idols, have died by suicide.
長期以來，K-pop一直受到剝削藝人的指控。廠牌招募兒童，讓他們接受多年高強度訓練，之後往往還要求他們償還培訓費用。一些藝人表示，即使能夠在座無虛席的體育場演出，他們仍被迫節食到近乎挨餓的程度，並背負長期債務。已有多名韓流「愛豆」自殺。
The NewJeans contracts show that despite years of promised overhauls and government intervention, little has changed, even for one of the most successful groups of its time.
NewJeans的合同表明，儘管多年來業界不斷承諾改革，政府也出手干預，但實際情況並沒有多少改變，即使對於當今最成功的組合之一也是如此。
“This contract is unusually aggressive,” said Jong-eon Roh, a Seoul-based entertainment lawyer who was one of five experts who reviewed the NewJeans deal for the Times. “It restricts the artists’ freedom to a degree rarely seen, even in recent major-label contracts.”
「這份合同苛刻得異乎尋常，」首爾的娛樂行業律師盧宗彥（音）說。他是為《紐約時報》審閱NewJeans合同的五名專家之一。「它對藝人自由的限制程度即使在近年來大廠牌的合同中也極為罕見。」 
At the heart of the NewJeans controversy is the band’s fight with Ador, a label under the entertainment conglomerate Hybe, the K-pop giant behind bands including BTS and Katseye.
NewJeans爭議的核心是該組合與廠牌Ador的鬥爭。Ador是娛樂集團Hybe旗下的廠牌，Hybe是K-pop巨頭，旗下擁有BTS和Katseye等組合。
廣告
Hybe made four spokespeople available for a lengthy interview and sent a written statement, both on the condition that information be attributed to company representatives.
Hybe安排四名發言人接受了一次長時間採訪，並提供了一份書面聲明，但要求採訪和聲明中的信息均以公司代表的名義發布。
Hybe said its labels’ contracts were legal and negotiated in good faith, with terms to protect the artist and the company’s financial investment. The Korean government’s template is a nonbinding guideline, and Hybe said that adjustments were accepted by the performers’ parents.
Hybe表示，旗下廠牌的合同合法，談判也秉持誠信原則，條款旨在保護藝人及公司的財務投資。韓國政府的合同範本不具有約束力，Hybe表示，合同中的修改得到了成員家長的認可。
Contract disputes in the music industry are not new. Lance Bass said recently that he lived on $35 a day during ’NSync’s heyday. Taylor Swift famously lost control of her master recordings.
音樂行業的合同糾紛並不新鮮。男子組合’NSync成員蘭斯·巴斯最近表示，在組合最紅的時候，他每天只有35美元生活費。泰勒·斯威夫特也曾因失去對音樂母帶的控制權而引起廣泛關注。 
But Dina LaPolt, an entertainment lawyer whose clients have included Britney Spears and Cardi B, said that such contracts were largely relics in the United States. Not so for K-pop. Entertainment companies act as labels, managers and agents, and wield control over all aspects of a performer’s life.
但曾代理布蘭妮·斯皮爾斯和Cardi B等藝人的娛樂行業律師律師迪娜·拉波爾特表示，這類合同在美國基本已成為歷史遺物，在K-pop業界則不然。韓國的娛樂公司同時扮演唱片公司、經紀人和代理人的角色，控制藝人生活的方方面面。
“In K-pop, you either do what they say, dress as they say, do your hair as they say, or you’re going to get replaced,” LaPolt said.
「在K-pop，你要麼按他們說的做，按他們說的穿，按他們說的做頭髮，要麼就會被換掉，」拉波爾特說。
Child Protection Language Was Stripped Away
保護未成年人的條款遭刪除
By the time they signed their NewJeans contracts, the performers — Pham, Minji Kim, Hyein Lee, Haerin Kang and Danielle Marsh — had spent years training. They started in dorms, one as young as 12, with no guarantee that they would make it into a pop group.
在簽署合同時，NewJeans的五名成員范玉昕、金玟池(Minji Kim)、李惠仁(Hyein Lee)、姜諧潾(Haerin Kang)和牟智慧(Danielle Marsh)已經接受了多年訓練。她們從練習生宿舍開始自己的演藝之路，最小的一個入住時只有12歲，而且沒有人能保證她們最終能夠出道。 
廣告
Rehearsing until 2 a.m. was not uncommon, according to interviews with people close to the group who spoke on the condition of anonymity because of the fear of retribution from the label.
據多名與該組合關係密切的人士透露，排練到凌晨兩點是司空見慣的事情。由於擔心遭到公司報復，他們要求匿名。
Video cameras monitored training areas, and three people close to the group said that taking breaks was discouraged. Four of the five members dropped out of school during training.
訓練區域安裝了監控攝像頭。三名與該組合關係密切的人士表示，訓練期間，不鼓勵她們休息。五名成員中有四人在訓練期間輟學。
Hybe representatives said that the company fully complies with child-protection laws and works hard to ensure that performers work and train in safe environments. The company said it does not pressure artists to drop out of school or penalize them for resting.
Hybe的代表表示，公司完全遵守兒童保護法律，並努力確保藝人在安全的環境中工作和訓練。公司表示，從不施壓要求藝人輟學，也不會因為藝人休息而懲罰他們。
To protect artists, the South Korean government’s template guarantees rights to physical and mental health, education and sleep. It bars labels from pressuring children to work excessive hours.
為保護藝人，韓國政府的模板保障身心健康、教育和睡眠權。它禁止唱片公司強迫未成年人超時工作。
The NewJeans contracts removed that language. Doing so makes it harder for singers to challenge their working conditions, experts said.
但NewJeans的合同刪除了這些內容。專家表示，這使組合成員更難就工作條件提出異議。
Emails obtained by the Times show that contract terms were essentially nonnegotiable. In January 2022, the mother of one member requested several changes before signing.
《紐約時報》獲得的電子郵件顯示，合同條款基本上沒有協商餘地。2022年1月，一名成員的母親在簽約前提出了幾處修改要求。
廣告
“If you do not agree to the current contract draft, including the points above, it may be best to reconsider the contract itself,” Ador’s vice president responded.
Ador副總裁回覆說：「如果您無法接受目前的合同草案，包括上述幾點，那麼最好重新考慮是否簽署這份合同。」 
The parents all signed.
最終，所有家長都簽了字。
Financial Terms and Cosmetic Surgery
財務條款與整容手術
Performers everywhere change their looks, but K-pop labels often arrange plastic surgery for their artists. That causes little comment in South Korea, which has one of the highest cosmetic surgery rates in the world.
世界各地的藝人都會調整自己的外在形象，但K-pop的娛樂公司經常為旗下藝人安排整容手術。這在韓國很少引起爭議，因為韓國是全球整容手術比例最高的國家之一。
K-pop labels urge singers, sometimes in their early teens, to get nose jobs or eyelid surgery, according to interviews with former idols. Labels often pay for the procedures and bill the performers later.
據對前偶像的採訪，K-pop廠牌敦促歌手做鼻整形或眼瞼手術，哪怕有時對方只有十幾歲。廠牌通常先墊付手術費用，之後再向藝人收費。
The NewJeans contract required members to repay a significant share of what the label spent on plastic surgery and other beauty treatments while they were trainees.
NewJeans的合同規定，成員必須償還公司在她們擔任練習生期間為整容手術及其他美容項目支付的相當一部分費用
廣告
Before NewJeans debuted as a band, photographers took pictures from multiple angles to study how light would hit the singers onstage. The label then suggested cosmetic procedures to several of them, according to two people close to the group.
NewJeans正式出道前，攝影師從多個角度為成員拍照，以便研究舞檯燈光照在她們身上的效果。據兩名與組合關係密切的人士透露，隨後公司向其中幾名成員建議接受美容整形。
Hybe’s representatives said in a written statement that it does not require plastic surgery. “We do not even recommend cosmetic surgery or procedures against an individual’s wishes.” The two people close to the group said that in an industry and country where cosmetic surgery was commonplace, the suggestions were not taken as coercive.
Hybe代表在書面聲明中表示，公司不要求藝人整容。「我們甚至不會違背個人意願推薦整容手術或美容項目。」上述兩名知情人士也表示，在整容十分普遍的韓國及其娛樂行業，這些建議並不被視為強迫。
The Times is not revealing the medical histories of the NewJeans members.
《紐約時報》不會披露NewJeans成員的醫療史。
Cosmetic surgery often contributes to what is known in K-pop as trainee debt. Many singers start their careers owing their labels for training and other expenses, then work off the debt by performing.
整容手術往往造成K-pop所謂的「練習生債務」。許多歌手職業生涯開始時，欠下訓練和其他費用，然後通過演藝收入還債。
In this regard, NewJeans was an exception. Hybe absorbed the group’s early costs, meaning that members started earning right away, without debt. The company’s representatives said that trainee expenses were handled at the discretion of its labels — in this case, Ador. (Hybe is in a legal fight with Ador’s former CEO.)
在這方面，NewJeans是個例外。Hybe承擔了組合早期的費用，因此成員從出道起便能獲得收入，沒有債務。公司代表表示，練習生費用如何處理由旗下各廠牌自行決定，NewJeans的情況由Ador決定。（Hybe目前正與Ador前首席執行官打官司。） 
From a financial standpoint, the NewJeans contract was fairly standard in the K-pop industry, experts said. After the label deducted expenses, the group received about half the profits from concerts, advertisements and promotional appearances, plus a small share of record sales and streaming.
專家表示，從財務角度看，NewJeans的合同在K-pop行業相當常見。扣除開支後，組合可以獲得演唱會、廣告和宣傳活動約一半的利潤，外加唱片銷售和流媒體播放收入中的一小部分分成。
廣告
LaPolt said that such terms would be “horrific” in the United States, where artists hire their own managers and agents and keep most of their touring revenue.
拉波爾特說，這樣的條款在美國會被認為「極其惡劣」，因為美國藝人可以自行聘請經紀人和代理，並保留大部分巡演收入。
No ‘Insincere Attitude’ Allowed
不允許「態度不真誠」
In October 2024, after NewJeans became the fastest K-pop act to hit 1 billion Spotify streams, Pham testified before the South Korean parliament that she believed that Ador hated her and her fellow singers. She accused the label’s employees of insulting the group on social media, playing down their success in the media and at times giving them the silent treatment.
2024年10月，在NewJeans成為Spotify平台上最快達到10億次播放量的K-pop藝人後，范玉欣在韓國國會作證時表示，她認為Ador憎恨她和其他成員。她指責公司員工在社群媒體上侮辱這個組合，在媒體報導中淡化她們的成就，有時甚至對她們不理不睬。 
Her contract made no allowance for saying such things publicly. Singers were prohibited from saying “true or false facts” that could damage the company’s reputation. The contract also prohibited underage drinking and drug use, plus vague behavior and actions such as “insincere attitude,” “neglect of self-management” and “inappropriate romantic relationships.”
但她的合同並不允許她公開發表這樣的言論。合同禁止歌手傳播可能損害公司聲譽的「真實或虛假事實」，還禁止未成年人飲酒、吸毒，以及一些定義模糊的行為，例如「態度不真誠」「疏於自我管理」和「不適當的戀愛關係」。
Experts said those terms would give labels wide latitude to claim that artists had violated their contract.
專家表示，這些條款給了唱片公司很大的解釋空間，可以據此指控藝人違約。
Weeks after Pham’s testimony, NewJeans members accused Ador of leaking training videos to the media and urged the company to treat them better.
范玉欣作證幾周後，NewJeans成員指責Ador向媒體洩漏訓練影片，並敦促公司改善對她們的待遇。
When discussions fizzled, NewJeans members called a news conference and said they were quitting the label. The rebellion sent shock waves through the industry. Ador sued, and a monthslong legal battle followed. In March 2025, almost a year after the group’s announcement, judges sided with the company, saying that NewJeans could not strike out on its own.
雙方協商無果後，NewJeans成員召開記者會，宣布退出該公司。這場反抗震動了整個行業。Ador隨後提起訴訟，雙方展開了長達數月的法律爭鬥。2025年3月，該組合宣布退出近一年後，法官作出有利於公司的裁決，認定NewJeans不能自行開展演藝活動。
Under their contract terms, the attempted insurrection had exposed the stars to potential lawsuits and millions of dollars in damages.
根據合同條款，這次試圖反抗公司的舉動使成員面臨潛在訴訟和數百萬美元賠償的風險。
Family members moved to smooth things over and asked the company not to retaliate, according to a meeting transcript filed in a Korean court.
根據提交韓國法院的一份會議記錄，成員家屬曾試圖緩和矛盾，要求公司不要報復。
The company offered no such assurances, saying that the hiatus had already cost it a lot of money.
公司沒有給出這樣的保證，稱停擺已經讓其損失了很多錢。
Penalties for Leaving, Even if You’re Pushed Out
即使被公司解約，也要支付賠償金
Consequences came quickly.
後果很快顯現。
In December 2025, the label terminated its contract with one NewJeans member, Marsh, who was 20 at the time, and sued her. The company said that she had broken her contract by refusing work and by doing unauthorized solo projects and photo shoots, as well as by signing a deal with a third party.
2025年12月，公司終止了與NewJeans成員牟智慧的合同，並對當時20歲的她提起訴訟。公司稱，她拒絕工作、未經授權獨自開展項目和拍攝照片，還與第三方簽訂協議，違反了合同。
Marsh’s lawyer said the label was punishing her for the attempted revolt.
牟智慧的律師表示，公司是在懲罰她參與此前的反抗行動。
The lawsuit revealed a K-pop peculiarity. Contracts often allow labels to demand exit fees, even when the company, not the singer, ends the relationship.
這起訴訟揭示了K-pop行業的一項特殊做法：合同通常允許公司要求支付解約金，即使是由公司而非歌手單方面終止合作關係也是如此。
In Marsh’s case, the company sued her for roughly $22 million, most of it in exit fees, claiming that her behavior had effectively severed the contract.
在牟智慧的案件中，公司向她索賠約2200萬美元，其中大部分是解約賠償金。公司聲稱，她的行為實際上已導致合同關係破裂。
The government template suggests a payout formula based on average monthly earnings, projected over the months left in the deal. The NewJeans contracts used a similar calculation but with a twist. Instead of averaging all months, the contracts only considered months when the band made money — a change that lawyers who reviewed the terms said could inflate the penalty.
韓國政府的合同範本建議，根據藝人的平均月收入和合同剩餘月數計算賠償金額。NewJeans的合同採用了類似公式，但有所修改：計算平均收入時，不是把所有月份都計入，而是只計算組合有收入的月份。審閱合同的律師表示，這種修改可能抬高賠償金額。
Hybe representatives said that its exit penalties are in line with industry standards. They said that, in practice, the formula does not increase penalties because its performers make money every month.
Hybe的代表表示，公司的解約賠償條款符合行業標準。他們還稱，實際操作中，這種計算方法不會增加賠償金額，因為旗下藝人每個月都有收入。
Marsh’s lawyer, Jung Jong-chae, complained to the South Korean Fair Trade Commission, accusing Hybe and Ador of trying to end her career.
牟智慧的律師鄭鍾采（音）向韓國公平交易委員會提出投訴，指控Hybe和Ador試圖終結她的演藝事業。
Jung argued that the minor outside work was a pretext. The goal, he said, was to send a warning across the industry.
鄭鍾采認為，牟智慧在公司以外開展的少量工作不過是藉口，他表示，公司的真正目的是向整個行業發出警告。
“If you try to escape Hybe’s control,” he said, “this is the price you will pay.”
「如果你試圖擺脫Hybe的控制，這就是你要付出的代價，」他說。
The Label Owns Their Voices and Faces
公司擁有藝人的聲音和肖像
Under the government template, a label’s rights to a performer’s name, image and voice end with the contract. Not so with NewJeans.
根據韓國政府的合同範本，公司使用藝人姓名、肖像和聲音的權利應隨合同終止而結束。但NewJeans的合同並非如此。
The singers are under contract until 2029. After that, the contract allows the label to keep using and licensing their identities. NewJeans members must still be paid, but they will not control the use of their faces and voices. That includes any NewJeans voice clones or chatbots created by artificial intelligence.
成員的合同將持續到2029年。此後，合同仍允許公司繼續使用她們的身份形象，並將相關使用權授權給第三方。NewJeans成員仍有權獲得報酬，但將無法控制自己的肖像和聲音如何被使用，包括人工智慧生成的NewJeans聲音複製和聊天機器人。
How broadly those contract provisions would hold up in court is unclear, legal experts said.
法律專家表示，這些合同條款究竟能在多大程度上得到法院支持，目前尚不清楚。
Hybe can also sell and repackage NewJeans music after the contract ends, while slashing the amount of money due to the singers. Legal experts called that clause unusual.
Hybe還可以在合同結束後繼續出售和重新包裝NewJeans的音樂作品，同時大幅削減應支付給成員的報酬。法律專家稱，這一條款並不尋常。
The future for NewJeans remains uncertain. The label has teased the possibility of a new album, but the group has not performed in over a year.
NewJeans的未來仍充滿不確定性。公司曾暗示可能推出新專輯，但組合已經一年多沒有登台演出。
