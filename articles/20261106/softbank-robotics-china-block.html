中國阻止軟銀收購ABB機器人業務

China Blocks SoftBank’s $5.4 Billion Robotics Takeover, Impeding Foreign Deals

ADAM SATARIANO • 2026-11-06
​Chinese authorities thwarted a multibillion-dollar effort by the Japanese tech giant SoftBank to expand further into robotics, showing Beijing’s willingness to intervene in global business deals that may impact its own technology ambitions.
中國監管部門阻止了日本科技巨頭軟銀一項價值數十億美元的機器人業務擴張計劃，顯示出北京願意干預可能影響自身技術發展雄心的全球商業交易。
On Thursday, ABB, a Swiss-Swedish industrial company, said that the sale of its robotics division to SoftBank for $5.4 billion, which was announced last year, had been blocked by a Chinese regulator that conducts national security reviews of foreign investments.
週四，瑞士-瑞典工業公司ABB表示，去年宣布的以54億美元將其機器人部門出售給軟銀的交易遭到中國一家負責對外國投資進行國家安全審查的監管機構否決。
China’s approval was needed for the SoftBank acquisition to move ahead in part because so much of ABB’s sales come from the country. At a factory in Shanghai, ABB produces a variety of products, mainly used for manufacturing and automating tasks like handling heavy materials and polishing.
軟銀的這筆收購需要獲得中國批准，部分原因是ABB有很大一部分銷售收入來自中國。ABB在上海設有一家工廠，生產多種產品，主要用於製造業，以及重型物料搬運、拋光等作業的自動化。
China is taking an aggressive approach to reviewing foreign deals involving key technology and sensitive industries. In April, regulators forced Meta to unwind its $2 billion acquisition of the Chinese A.I. start-up Manus.
中國正採取更為強硬的方式審查涉及關鍵技術和敏感行業的外國企業交易。今年4月，監管機構迫使Meta撤銷了以20億美元收購中國人工智慧初創企業Manus的交易。
廣告
To block the Manus deal, authorities cited national security concerns, a move seen as signaling the government’s determination to keep important technology and talented engineers from being acquired by foreign companies.
在阻止Manus收購案時，中國當局以國家安全問題為由採取行動。外界認為，此舉表明中國政府決心防止重要技術和優秀工程人才被外國企業收購。
China’s scrutiny of foreign deals is raising concerns about operating in the country as technology development takes on more strategic importance in government planning. Regulators in the United States, European Union and Britain have also intervened in deals involving semiconductors, quantum computing and other technology.
隨著技術發展在政府規劃中的戰略地位日益突出，中國對外國企業交易的嚴格審查也讓企業對在華經營產生了更多顧慮。美國、歐盟和英國的監管機構同樣曾干預涉及半導體、量子計算及其他技術領域的交易。
ABB, based in Zurich, has decades-long ties to Chinese manufacturing businesses, which gives it access to valuable data on robotics technology. China is ABB’s largest robotics market and about 90 percent of its machines built for Chinese customers are manufactured locally. The company employs thousands of people in the country.
ABB總部位於蘇黎世，與中國製造業企業有著長達數十年的聯繫，因此能夠獲取寶貴的機器人技術數據。中國是ABB最大的機器人業務市場，該公司為中國客戶生產的機器約有90%在當地製造。ABB在中國僱用了數千名員工。
Chinese authorities were concerned about robotics data being transferred overseas and used to train A.I. models, according to The Financial Times, which first reported that the deal was blocked.
據最先報導這筆交易遭到否決的《金融時報》稱，中國當局擔心機器人相關數據被轉移至海外，用於訓練人工智慧模型。
China’s intervention is a major blow to SoftBank and its founder, Masayoshi Son, one of the tech industry’s biggest believers in artificial intelligence. He has staked his company’s future on the technology and has viewed robots as the essential link between the technology and the physical world.
中國的干預對軟銀及其創始人孫正義是一個重大打擊，他是科技行業最堅定的人工智慧支持者之一。他把公司的未來押在這項技術上，並認為機器人是連接人工智慧與現實物理世界的關鍵紐帶。
Yet concerns have grown that Mr. Son has put too much emphasis on A.I. SoftBank has invested roughly $65 billion in OpenAI, the maker of ChatGPT, and billions more into data center businesses.
然而，外界越來越擔心孫正義過度押注人工智慧。軟銀已向ChatGPT的開發商OpenAI投資約650億美元，並向數據中心業務投入了數十億美元。
廣告
On Thursday, shares of SoftBank fell almost 7 percent and are down nearly 30 percent over the past year. The Democratic sweep in the U.S. midterm elections on Tuesday also hit the shares of some big tech firms, as many of the winning governors and legislators have pushed for stricter oversight of the A.I. build-out, which could slow its expansion.
週四，軟銀股價下跌近7%，過去一年累計跌幅接近30%。民主黨在週二美國期中選舉中的全面勝利，也拖累了一些大型科技公司的股價，因為許多勝選的州長和議員一直主張加強對人工智慧基礎設施建設的監管，這可能拖慢相關項目的擴張步伐。
A spokesman for SoftBank did not respond to messages seeking comment.
軟銀發言人沒有回覆置評請求。

Adam Satariano是《紐約時報》科技記者，常駐倫敦。
