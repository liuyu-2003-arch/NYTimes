眾議院領導人要求黃仁勛出席簡報會

House G.O.P. Leaders Ask Nvidia’s Huang to Speak in Closed-Door Briefing

TRIPP MICKLE • 2026-11-06
The Republican leaders of three influential House committees on Thursday asked Jensen Huang, the chief executive of Nvidia, to voluntarily appear for a closed-door briefing on his interactions with an executive at a Singapore-based company, who American officials determined was funneling information to Chinese intelligence.
三個有影響力的眾議院委員會的共和黨領導人週四要求英偉達首席執行官黃仁勛自願出席一場閉門簡報會，說明他與一家新加坡公司一名高管的往來。美國官員認定，這名高管向中國情報機構輸送信息。
The request came from Brian Mast, chairman of the House Committee on Foreign Affairs; John Moolenaar, who leads the House Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party; and Rick Crawford, who leads the select committee on intelligence.
提出這一要求的是眾議院外交事務委員會主席布賴恩·馬斯特、眾議院中共問題特別委員會主席約翰·穆勒納爾，以及情報特別委員會主席裡克·克勞福德。
The New York Times reported last week that Mr. Huang had a close relationship with Alice Huang, a top executive at Megaspeed, a company that the U.S. government suspected of helping China evade U.S. prohibitions and gain access to Nvidia’s A.I. chips.
上週《紐約時報》報導，黃仁勛與Megaspeed公司高管愛麗絲·黃（中文名黃樂，音）關係密切。美國政府懷疑該公司幫助中國規避美國禁令，獲取英偉達的人工智慧晶片。
The House leaders asked Mr. Huang to appear on Nov. 18 and discuss his interactions with Ms. Huang as well as Nvidia’s security policies for guarding against espionage by a foreign adversary.
這些眾議院領導人要求黃仁勛於11月18日出席簡報會，就其與黃樂的往來，以及英偉達為防範外國對手間諜活動而制定的安全政策進行說明。
廣告
“It is vital Congress determines the extent to which U.S. national security has been placed at risk,” they wrote.
他們寫道：「國會必須確定美國國家安全所面臨的風險程度，這一點至關重要。」
John Rizzo, a spokesman for Nvidia, declined to comment on whether Mr. Huang would appear. “Our chief executive has an outstanding security team that accompanies him where needed,” Mr. Rizzo said in a statement.
英偉達發言人約翰·裡佐拒絕評論黃仁勛是否會出席。「我們的首席執行官有一支優秀的安全團隊，在需要時會隨行，」裡佐在聲明中說。
The request represented a shift for Republican congressional leaders, who have largely followed President Trump’s lead in taking a hands-off approach to the technology industry. Mr. Huang has developed a close relationship with Mr. Trump over the last two years, and the two often speak about technology issues.
這一要求標誌著共和黨國會領導人的轉變。此前，他們大多追隨川普總統，對科技行業採取不干預態度。過去兩年，黃仁勛與川普建立了密切關係，兩人經常談論技術問題。
The move by the three Republicans also followed a midterm election where voters made clear that they were concerned about the tech industry’s impact on their lives, from A.I.’s potential to eliminate jobs to the construction of giant data centers in their communities.
這三名共和黨人的舉動也發生在期中選舉之後。選民在選舉中明確表示，他們擔心科技行業對自身生活的影響，從人工智慧可能消除工作崗位，到社區內大型數據中心的建設。
Anti-tech sentiment largely favored largely favored Democratic candidates, and the move by the Republicans on Thursday was an early indication that they not want to cede that politically potent issue to Democrats.
反科技情緒大體上有利於民主黨候選人，而共和黨人週四的舉動是一個早期跡象，表明他們不想把這一具有政治影響力的議題拱手讓給民主黨。
American spy agencies learned of the relationship through surveillance of Ms. Huang, raising alarms inside the federal government because of the possibility that she could be gleaning vital trade or national security information from Mr. Huang.
美國間諜機構通過對黃樂的監視得知了這一關係，這在聯邦政府內部引發警報，因為她可能從黃仁勛那裡搜集重要的貿易或國家安全信息。
In April 2025, Mr. Moolenaar and the House Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party, which focuses on national security threats from Beijing, opened an investigation into Nvidia’s sale of chips in Asia.
2025年4月，穆勒納爾和專注於北京國家安全威脅的眾議院中共問題特別委員會對英偉達在亞洲銷售晶片展開調查。
廣告
But in the months that followed, the Trump administration rolled back restrictions on Nvidia’s chip sales to China.
但在隨後幾個月裡，川普政府放寬了對英偉達向中國銷售晶片的限制。
Mr. Huang later joined Mr. Trump on the president’s first diplomatic trip to the Middle East, where the president spoke of a major chip sale to the United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia. Nvidia also donated more than $20 million to the White House ballroom and promised to invest $500 billion in U.S. manufacturing.
在川普總統的首次中東外交之行中，黃仁勛陪同在側，期間總統提到了向阿聯酋和沙烏地阿拉伯出售大規模晶片的交易。英偉達還向白宮宴會廳廳捐贈超過2000萬美元，並承諾在美國製造業投資5000億美元。
In June, Mr. Huang declined a request to appear before the Senate from Elizabeth Warren, a Democrat from Massachusetts and the ranking member of the Senate Banking Committee, which oversees rules about chip sales abroad.
6月，黃仁勛拒絕了麻薩諸塞州民主党參議員伊麗莎白·沃倫的要求，未出席參議院聽證會。該委員會監督有關海外晶片銷售的規則。
Last week, Ms. Warren renewed her call for testimony after The Times reported on Mr. Huang’s relationship with Ms. Huang.
上週，在《紐約時報》報導黃仁勛與黃樂的關係後，沃倫再次呼籲黃仁勛出席聽證會。
In 2025, Megaspeed, a little-known company, emerged to buy billions of dollars in A.I. chips. Ms. Huang, who is a Chinese citizen, was listed as its managing director for its first eight months before removing herself from that role in corporate paperwork. The company later installed a director from Singapore, who is based in Shanghai.
2025年，一家鮮為人知的公司Megaspeed橫空出世，購買數十億美元的人工智慧晶片。黃樂是中國公民，在公司成立最初八個月被列為董事總經理，後來在公司文件中不再擔任該職務。公司後來任命了一名來自新加坡、常駐上海的董事。
Megaspeed’s sister company in Malaysia bought at least $4.8 billion of advanced A.I. equipment containing Nvidia chips and technology from June 2024 to February 2026, according to trade records obtained from ImportGenius, a trade platform.
根據從貿易平台ImportGenius獲得的貿易記錄，從2024年6月到2026年2月，Megaspeed在馬來西亞的姊妹公司購買了至少48億美元包含英偉達晶片和技術的先進人工智慧設備。
The U.S. government has been investigating Megaspeed since late 2024, when a Commerce Department official visited its data center in Malaysia and found the site to be suspicious. The Nvidia equipment was still packed in boxes, suggesting it was being shipped in or out rather than installed there.
美國政府自2024年底以來一直在調查Megaspeed。當時一名商務部官員訪問了該公司的馬來西亞數據中心，發現該場所情況可疑。英偉達設備仍裝在箱子裡，表明設備是被運進或運出，而非安裝在那裡。
廣告
Nvidia has said that it complies with the law and that there is no evidence of its chips being smuggled to China. In a statement last week, Mr. Rizzo said Mr. Huang’s “interactions with Ms. Huang are not unusual or unexpected.” He added, “Meetings with customers are part of a C.E.O.’s job; Jensen talks with thousands of customer executives every year.”
英偉達表示，公司遵守法律，沒有證據表明其晶片被走私到中國。裡佐上週在一份聲明中說，黃仁勛「與黃女士的互動並非不同尋常或出人意料」。他還說：「與客戶會面是首席執行官工作的一部分；黃仁勛每年與數以千計客戶高管交談。」

Tripp Mickle報導英偉達、谷歌和蘋果等全球最大的科技公司。他也報導整個科技行業的趨勢，包括裁員、生成式AI和機器人計程車等。
