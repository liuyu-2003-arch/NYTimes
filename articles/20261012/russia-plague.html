鼠疫疑雲超一週，俄羅斯仍未作出官方解釋

A Week Later, Russia Hasn’t Said What Pathogen Killed Plague Researcher

PAUL SONNE, JULIAN E. BARNES • 2026-10-12
More than a week after a young plague researcher’s mysterious death from pneumonia, Russia has still not publicly identified the pathogen that killed her.
一名年輕的鼠疫研究人員因肺炎神秘死亡已超過一週，俄羅斯至今仍未公開確認導致她死亡的病原體。
The possibility that the researcher contracted pneumonic plague in her lab led to global fears of an outbreak. Those worries have begun to recede as no other known cases have surfaced in the Irkutsk region, where Darya Shipilova, a 28-year-old researcher at the Anti-Plague Institute of Siberia and the Far East, died on Oct. 2.
由於這名研究人員可能是在實驗室感染肺鼠疫的，這引發了全球對疫情暴發的擔憂。不過，隨著伊爾庫茨克地區未出現其他已知病例，這種擔憂已經開始消退。10月2日，28歲的達裡婭·希皮洛娃在該地區去世。她生前是西伯利亞及遠東地區抗鼠疫研究所的一名研究人員。
But the absence of an official explanation for Ms. Shipilova’s death, despite thousands of tests conducted on others in the community and on her own remains, has stoked concerns about what, if anything, Russia’s authoritarian government might be hiding.
然而，儘管當局對當地其他居民以及希皮洛娃的遺體進行了數千次檢測，卻始終未對她的死因給出官方解釋，這不禁令人懷疑，俄羅斯威權政府是否有所隱瞞，如果確實如此，它究竟在隱瞞什麼？
According to Rospotrebnadzor, the Russian public health regulator, Ms. Shipilova’s remains did not test positive for any of the dangerous pathogens she was working on at the institute, which included the bacterium that causes the plague. The regulator has said she died of pneumonia of an unknown origin. Pneumonia can be caused by numerous viruses, bacteria and fungi.
據俄羅斯公共衛生監管機構——聯邦消費者權益保護和公益監督局稱，希皮洛娃的遺體沒有檢測出她在研究所接觸的任何危險病原體呈陽性，其中包括引起鼠疫的細菌。該監管機構說，她死於來源不明的肺炎。肺炎可由多種病毒、細菌和真菌引起。
廣告
The World Health Organization is still seeking to understand the “causative agent” of the fatal pneumonia, whether or not it was the plague, Maria Van Kerkhove, the W.H.O.’s director of epidemic and pandemic management, said at a news conference on Wednesday.
世界衛生組織負責流行病和大流行病管理的主任瑪麗亞·范凱爾克霍夫週三在新聞發布會上表示，世衛組織仍在設法查明這起致命肺炎病例的「致病因子」，包括確認它是否由鼠疫引起。
“If that is ruled out — they say that is ruled out — what did she actually die from?” Ms. Van Kerkhove asked.
「如果鼠疫的可能性被排除了——他們說已經排除了——那麼她究竟是因為什麼死的？」范凱爾克霍夫問道。
Experts said they believed that with modern testing technology, the Russian authorities should know the answer by now.
專家表示，他們認為，憑藉現代檢測技術，俄羅斯當局現在應該已經知道答案。
“Presumably, they have the capability within days to do a simple genome sequence,” said Raj Panjabi, who served as a White House public health official during the Biden administration. “Presumably, they’ve done that, but they’ve not shared the results, and that’s concerning.”
「按理說，他們有能力在幾天內完成簡單的基因組測序，」拜登政府時期曾擔任白宮公共衛生官員的拉傑·潘賈比說。「按理說，他們已經做了測序，但沒有公布結果，這令人擔憂。」
The lingering questions reflect a broader lack of global public trust that stems in part from unanswered questions about the origins of the Covid-19 pandemic.
這些揮之不去的疑問反映出全球公眾普遍存在的信任缺失，而這種不信任在一定程度上源於新冠疫情的起源至今仍未得到解答。
More than six years after Covid upended the world, questions remain about whether the lab that was studying bat coronaviruses in the Chinese city where the pandemic started played a role in the outbreak. China swiftly rejected the possibility and impeded a global investigation into the matter.
新冠疫情給世界帶來巨大衝擊，六年多過去了，人們仍有諸多疑問尚未得到解答，其中包括：在疫情最初暴發的那座中國城市，一家從事蝙蝠冠狀病毒研究的實驗室是否與疫情暴發有關。中國迅速否認了這種可能性，並阻礙國際社會對此展開調查。
廣告
In Russia, any possible case of a pathogen escaping a lab attracts outsize attention globally in part because the country is one of the few in the world that continues to work on biological weapons, according to the U.S. intelligence community’s annual threat assessment.
在俄羅斯，任何一起疑似病原體從實驗室洩漏的事件都會引起國際社會的高度關注。部分原因是，根據美國情報界的年度威脅評估，俄羅斯是世界上少數幾個仍在開展生物武器研究的國家之一。
That has led to greater concern among officials and experts in the United States, who point to the nation’s vulnerabilities.
這加劇了美國官員和專家的擔憂，他們指出，美國在應對這類威脅方面存在薄弱環節。
“The United States does not have a licensed plague vaccine,” said Andrew Weber, a senior fellow at the Council on Strategic Risks and a former Pentagon official in charge of biodefense. “Given that Russia has an illegal bioweapons program, it is very concerning.”
「美國沒有獲得許可的鼠疫疫苗，」戰略風險委員會高級研究員、曾在五角大廈負責生物防禦事務的安德魯·韋伯說。「鑒於俄羅斯有一個非法生物武器項目，這非常令人擔憂。」
There is no indication that the Irkutsk institute was doing bioweapons research, people briefed on U.S. intelligence said, speaking on the condition of anonymity to give a candid assessment.
聽取過美國情報簡報的人士表示，沒有跡象表明伊爾庫茨克的這家研究所正在開展生物武器研究。這些人士要求匿名，以便坦率地作出評估。
Ms. Shipilova was involved in testing which antibiotics and doses worked against Y. pestis, the bacterium that causes the plague, according to the summary of a study presented this year at a Moscow infectious disease conference.
根據今年在莫斯科一場傳染病會議上發表的研究摘要，希皮洛娃參與了針對鼠疫耶爾森菌的研究，測試哪些抗生素及多大劑量能夠有效對抗這種引發鼠疫的細菌。
The plague, which is endemic to Siberia and some other regions of the world, caused the “Black Death” that ravaged Eurasia in the 14th century, but it is treatable with antibiotics if caught quickly. Pneumonic plague is considered the most dangerous of the three main forms of infection, because it spreads from person to person and causes deadly, rapid-onset pneumonia.
鼠疫在西伯利亞和世界其他一些地區屬於地方性流行病。14世紀肆虐歐亞大陸的「黑死病」就是由鼠疫引起的，但如果及早發現，可用抗生素治療。肺鼠疫被認為是鼠疫三種主要感染類型中最危險的一種，因為它可以在人與人之間傳播，並迅速引發致命性肺炎。
廣告
Referring to Ms. Shipilova as “the patient,” the Russian authorities have said that she was vaccinated against the dangerous microorganisms she was working with, and that two reviews of the institute ruled out any “emergency situations” at the lab. The quarantine measures that the Russian authorities took in the Irkutsk region were put in place out of caution, owing to the nature of the patient’s work, they said.
俄羅斯當局將希皮洛娃稱為「患者」，並表示她已經接種了針對其工作中接觸的危險微生物的疫苗，對研究所進行的兩次調查也排除了實驗室存在任何「緊急情況」。他們說，之所以在伊爾庫茨克地區採取隔離措施，是考慮到患者的工作性質，出於謹慎而採取的預防措施。
Plague vaccines have long existed, but experts have raised serious doubts about their effectiveness and safety.
鼠疫疫苗早已問世，但專家對其有效性和安全性一直存在嚴重質疑。
Beyond what pathogen led to Ms. Shipilova’s pneumonia, many other questions remain unanswered, including where she was in the days leading up to her death, what she was working on at the institute at the time, and how and when her illness first showed itself and then progressed.
除了是什麼病原體導致希皮洛娃患上肺炎之外，還有許多問題沒有答案，包括她去世前幾天在哪裡、當時在研究所做什麼，以及她的病症最初何時出現、有哪些表現，之後又是如何發展的。
Labs around the world working with deadly germs take extensive precautions to keep workers from being infected, but accidents have occurred.
世界各地研究致命病原體的實驗室都會採取嚴格的防護措施，避免工作人員受到感染，但事故仍有發生。
Though Russia took swift action to quarantine and test individuals who had come into contact with the researcher, Rospotrebnadzor has not held any news briefings and has provided information only in periodic public statements. The Kremlin has declined to field specific questions, urging the public to listen only to public health officials.
儘管俄羅斯迅速採取行動，對與這名研究人員有過接觸的人實施隔離和檢測，但俄聯邦消費者權益保護和公益監督局沒有舉行過任何新聞發布會，只是不定期發布公開聲明。克里姆林宮則拒絕回答具體問題，敦促公眾只聽取公共衛生官員發布的信息。
Russia has been slow to reveal the details of other incidents with possible public health implications.
對於其他可能影響公眾健康的事件，俄羅斯也曾遲遲不肯披露相關細節。
廣告
In 2019, Moscow concealed critical information about the failed underwater recovery of a nuclear-powered missile in the White Sea, which caused an explosion and left seven people dead. The authorities did not initially say that a nuclear device was involved, acknowledge a spike in radiation or warn doctors that they were treating irradiated patients.
2019年，俄羅斯在白海打撈一枚核動力導彈失敗，引發爆炸，造成七人死亡。莫斯科隱瞞了這一事件的關鍵信息。俄羅斯當局最初既沒有說明事件涉及核裝置，也沒有承認輻射水平曾急劇上升，更沒有提醒醫生，他們正在救治的患者受到了輻射。
Nondisclosure has long been the modus operandi of the Soviet and now Russian bureaucracy, said Alexander Kelle, a senior researcher at the Institute for Peace Research and Security Policy at the University of Hamburg in Germany. He cautioned against reading too much into the paucity of details.
德國漢堡大學和平研究與安全政策研究所高級研究員亞歷山大·凱勒表示，不披露信息長期以來一直是蘇聯乃至如今俄羅斯官僚機構的慣常做法。他提醒人們，不要因為披露的細節有限就過度解讀。
“Just because there is a lack of information, we have to be careful not to equate that with something nefarious,” Mr. Kelle said, noting that there were many legitimate public health reasons for researchers to work on dangerous pathogens.
「正因為信息缺乏，我們就必須謹慎，不能因此認定背後有什麼不可告人的勾當，」凱勒說，並指出研究人員研究危險病原體有許多正當的公共衛生理由。
To posit any link to Russian biowarfare, he said, would “be stretching the very limited evidence that has been presented too far.”
他說，如果據此推斷這起事件與俄羅斯的生物戰研究存在任何聯繫，那就「遠遠超出了目前極其有限的證據所能支持的範圍」。
In a video message on Thursday, the governor of the Irkutsk region, Igor Kobzev, sought to close the book on the episode. He said that the medical monitoring of people in the region had concluded and thanked his fellow Russian officials for taking swift action under intense scrutiny.
伊爾庫茨克州州長伊高爾·科布澤夫週四發布影片講話，試圖為這一事件畫上句號。他說，對該地區相關人員的醫學監測已經結束，並感謝俄羅斯其他官員在受到密切關注的情況下迅速採取行動。
Mr. Kobzev said the W.H.O. had publicly confirmed that there were no risks to public health, though the organization has said it has not received enough information from Russia to make a risk assessment.
科布澤夫稱，世衛組織已公開確認不存在公共衛生風險。然而，世衛組織表示，尚未從俄羅斯獲得足夠的信息，無法進行風險評估。
廣告
Some people commenting on Mr. Kobzev’s video in his Telegram channel weren’t having it.
但在科布澤夫的Telegram頻道上，一些給他的影片留言的人並不買賬。
“What caused the pneumonia that killed the healthy young woman?” one asked.
「究竟是什麼引發了肺炎，奪走了這名健康年輕女性的生命？」一人問道。
“Are there really still naïve, gullible people in our city who would believe this?” another commenter said.
另一名評論者說：「難道我們這座城市裡真的還有這麼天真、這麼輕信別人的人，會相信這一套嗎？」
A third wrote: “X-Files, nothing to see here.”
還有人寫道：「簡直就是《X檔案》：沒什麼好看的，都散了吧。」
