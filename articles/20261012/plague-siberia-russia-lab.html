從新冠到疑似鼠疫：我們能遏制下一場瘟疫嗎

What Keeps Me Up at Night About the Russia Plague Scare

GIGI KWIK GRONVALL • 2026-10-12
A 28-year-old woman who worked at an anti-plague research laboratory in Siberia died on Oct. 2, raising concerns and many questions. Russian officials have said that the woman died from a pneumonia of unknown origin and that no “emergency situations” had occurred at the laboratory where she worked. They stated they’d almost finished the investigation of her contacts to monitor for illness, with no concerning findings.
10月2日，一名在西伯利亞一家鼠疫研究實驗室工作的28歲女性去世，引發了人們的擔憂和諸多疑問。俄羅斯官員表示，這名女性死於病因不明的肺炎，她所在的實驗室沒有發生任何「緊急事件」。他們還表示，對其接觸者進行的健康監測調查已接近尾聲，目前沒有發現任何令人擔憂的情況。
The Russian pronouncements contrasted with furious speculation elsewhere about what happened. Initial, unverified reports from local media suggested that the lab worker had accidentally broken a test tube containing Yersinia pestis, the bacterium that causes the plague. Local hospitals, including a maternity hospital, limited visitors and instituted enhanced health checks. A local factory required masks for its workers.
俄羅斯官方的說法與其他地方對事件真相的激烈猜測形成鮮明對比。當地媒體最初未經證實的報導稱，這名實驗室工作人員意外打碎了一支裝有鼠疫耶爾森菌的試管，這種細菌會引發鼠疫。當地多家醫院，包括一家婦產醫院，限制了訪客，並加強了健康檢查。當地一家工廠還要求員工佩戴口罩。
The World Health Organization has said, based on the unofficial information available, that “the public health risk to the general population appears to be low.” Indeed, the available evidence does not point to a global plague emergency. But this tragic death should prompt Americans to question whether we have the capacity to recognize and contain the next disease threat.
世界衛生組織表示，根據現有的非官方信息，「普通公眾面臨的公共衛生風險似乎較低」。確實，現有證據並不表明全球正面臨鼠疫疫情緊急事態。但這起悲劇性的死亡事件應該促使美國人思考：我們是否有能力及時發現並遏制下一場疾病威脅。
While the plague is of course deadly, it is treatable with antibiotics if identified quickly. The form of the plague the Siberian lab worker is rumored to have contracted is called pneumonic plague. It infects the lungs and can spread between people — unlike bubonic plague, the likely cause of the 14th-century Black Death, which is primarily transmitted by flea bites.
鼠疫固然致命，但如果及早確診，它是可以用抗生素治療的。據傳，這名西伯利亞實驗室工作人員感染的是肺鼠疫。肺鼠疫會侵襲肺部，並且可人際傳播，這與腺鼠疫不同。腺鼠疫很可能是14世紀黑死病的元兇，主要通過跳蚤叮咬傳播。

But even pneumonic plague has relatively limited transmissibility. Historically, one person infected with pneumonic plague has transmitted the disease to an average of 1.3 other people; by way of comparison, measles may transmit to 12 to 18 people in an unvaccinated population. Transmission of the pneumonic plague before symptoms appear has not been documented, so caregivers and close contacts have been the most at risk of infection. Masks, contact tracing and isolation and other established infection control procedures should work well to limit the spread of pneumonic plague.
但即便是肺鼠疫，其傳播力也相對有限。歷史數據顯示，一名肺鼠疫感染者平均會傳染1.3個人；相比之下，麻疹在未接種疫苗人群中可能傳染12到18人。目前尚無肺鼠疫患者在出現癥狀前傳播疾病的記錄，因此，護理人員和密切接觸者面臨的感染風險最高。佩戴口罩、追蹤接觸者、實施隔離以及其他既有的感染控制程序應該能夠有效限制肺鼠疫的傳播。
Still, we should get better answers about what happened in Siberia than those that Russia has so far provided. Was there a laboratory accident? If so, when was it recognized, and what treatment did the exposed lab worker receive? What prompted the extensive monitoring of her contacts? Outsiders’ concerns were in part fueled by speculation about biological weapons. The U.S. intelligence community assesses Russia to have a clandestine biological weapons program. The former Soviet Union included Yersinia pestis, the causative agent of plague, in its armamentarium.
儘管如此，俄羅斯迄今提供的信心遠遠不夠，我們仍有必要弄清西伯利亞究竟發生了什麼。實驗室是否發生了事故？如果是，事故是在什麼時候被發現的？受到暴露的實驗室工作人員接受了什麼治療？是什麼原因促使當局對她的接觸者展開大規模健康監測？外界的擔憂在一定程度上受到生物武器相關猜測的推動。美國情報界評估認為，俄羅斯擁有一個祕密生物武器項目。前蘇聯曾將引發鼠疫的鼠疫耶爾森菌納入其生物武器庫。
Disease surprises could come from many places. That’s why it’s dispiriting that the United States has so isolated itself. After withdrawing from the W.H.O. in January, and calling back the U.S. experts on loan to the organization, America now has less direct information about potential disease emergencies. The dismantling of the United States Agency for International Development cut off scientific collaborations and relationships through which emerging threats could be identified. Cuts and restrictions on infectious disease research have weakened American scientists’ ability to help establish safe laboratory practices internationally. We are diminishing our access to important public health information as well as our influence over what happens next.
疾病帶來的意外威脅可能來自許多地方。因此，美國如今如此孤立於國際社會之外令人沮喪。自今年1月退出世界衛生組織並召回借調給該組織的美國專家以來，美國如今能夠直接獲取的潛在疾病緊急事件信息減少了。美國國際開發署遭解散也切斷了原本有助於識別新興疾病威脅的科研合作及合作關係。對傳染病研究的經費削減和限制削弱了美國科學家協助國際社會建立安全實驗室操作規範的能力。我們不僅正在減少獲取重要公共衛生信息的管道，也在削弱自身對未來事態發展的影響力。
The Trump administration fired thousands of people at the Department of Health and Human Services, including pandemic experts at the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention and the Administration for Strategic Preparedness and Response. Losing this expertise has weakened capabilities that took years to build. No quick fix — and no artificial intelligence model — can replace the professional judgment, experience and trusted relationships needed to investigate and assess what is truly going on in a disease outbreak anywhere in the world. Infectious diseases do not recognize borders.
川普政府解僱了衛生與公眾服務部數千名工作人員，其中包括疾病控制與預防中心以及戰略準備與應對管理局的疫情防控專家。這些專業人才的流失削弱了美國耗費多年建立起來的應對能力。要調查和評估世界任何地方暴發的疫情究竟是怎麼回事都需要專業判斷、豐富經驗和長期建立的信任關係。任何權宜之計，乃至任何人工智慧模型，都無法取代。傳染病不認國界。
During the Covid-19 pandemic, American biotechnology and biological expertise helped deliver vaccines, diagnostics and treatments. The response had profound shortcomings, especially the failure to ramp up diagnostic testing and the lack of personal protective equipment available to medical staff in the earliest days of the pandemic. We should be building on our capacity and correcting those failures. Instead, we are weakening our public health infrastructure while thousands of Americans, including many children, suffer from preventable infections like measles. We are also undermining preparation for future potential threats, such as H5N1 influenza — the bird flu. That virus was recently detected at a U.S. mink farm, and remains a potential pandemic pathogen. The Trump administration canceled a contract to develop an mRNA H5N1 vaccine for humans.
新冠疫情期間，美國的生物技術和生物醫學專業能力幫助推動了疫苗、診斷檢測手段和治療方法的開發與應用。但美國的疫情應對存在嚴重缺陷，尤其是未能迅速擴大診斷檢測規模，以及疫情初期醫護人員缺乏個人防護裝備。我們本應在現有能力的基礎上繼續加強建設，並糾正這些失誤。然而，我們卻在削弱公共衛生基礎設施，與此同時，包括許多兒童在內的成千上萬美國人正遭受麻疹等本可預防的傳染病侵襲。我們也在削弱應對未來潛在威脅的準備工作，例如H5N1流感，也就是禽流感。最近，美國一家水貂養殖場檢測出了這種病毒，而它仍是一種可能引發大流行的病原體。川普政府還取消了一項為人類研發H5N1信使核糖核酸疫苗的合同。
The lesson from an unexplained death in Siberia should be that disease emergencies demand evidence, expertise and cooperation. They demand continued investment in the tools that can be used to investigate and limit disease outbreaks. Most important, they demand investment in the people who can use those tools.
西伯利亞這起死因不明的事件應該帶給我們的教訓是，應對突發疾病事件需要證據、專業知識和合作。我們必須對疫情的調查及控制工具進行持續投資。最重要的是，我們必須投資於那些能夠運用這些工具的專業人才。

Gigi Kwik Gronvall是一位免疫學家，也是約翰·霍普金斯大學彭博公共衛生學院環境健康與工程系的教授。她即將出版著作《我們的生物學未來：生命、健康和地球的交叉路口》(Our Biological Future: A Crossroads for Life, Health, and the Planet)。
