一家中國銀行如何幫助俄羅斯規避制裁

How a Small Chinese Bank Gave Moscow Access to Wall St.

AARON KROLIK • 2026-10-12
The Bank of America account ending in 0252 was not what you might expect.
這個尾號為0252的美國銀行帳戶並非你想像的那樣。
It did not belong to a person or a business. In fact, the money did not belong to any single entity.
它不屬於某個人或某家企業。事實上，帳戶裡的錢不屬於任何單一實體。
The account had funds pooled from countless customers of Zhejiang Chouzhou Commercial Bank, a regional lender in the eastern Chinese city of Yiwu.
帳戶彙集了眾多浙江稠州商業銀行客戶的錢款。這家地區性銀行位於中國東部城市義烏。
And among Chouzhou Bank’s customers were Russian front companies that had burrowed into the global financial system as part of a sprawling effort to evade Western sanctions, a New York Times analysis of thousands of financial documents and bank transactions found.
《紐約時報》對數千份金融文件和銀行交易的分析發現，稠州商業銀行的客戶包括俄羅斯的幌子公司。它們作為規避西方制裁的龐大行動的一部分，已潛入全球金融體系。
廣告
Chouzhou Bank provides what are known as correspondent services, a common banking practice that allows local banks to offer international services across many currencies to customers without opening branches abroad.
稠行提供所謂的代理行服務。這是一種常見的銀行業務，允許本地銀行無需在海外開設分行，就能為客戶提供多種貨幣的國際服務。
Chouzhou is one of many institutions that have found a lucrative business as middlemen in places where Western banks are reluctant to serve. Often, smaller banks hire banks like Chouzhou that have correspondent accounts at big Wall Street firms, creating a nesting doll effect in the financial system that can mask illicit activity.
在西方銀行不願服務的地區，許多機構充當中間人，找到了有利可圖的業務，稠行便是其中之一。通常，較小的銀行會聘用像稠行這樣在華爾街大公司擁有代理帳戶的銀行，從而在金融系統中形成一種套娃效應，可以掩蓋非法活動。
Since the invasion of Ukraine four years ago, finding and exploiting weak links in the correspondent system has become an important part of Russia’s effort to sidestep U.S. economic restrictions, the Times analysis shows.
時報分析顯示，自四年前入侵烏克蘭以來，尋找並利用代理行系統中的薄弱環節已成為俄羅斯規避美國經濟限制努力的重要組成部分。
In addition to legitimate clients, more than 50 companies that sold restricted electronics, industrial chemicals, and other goods and services to Russia in the first four months of 2025 held accounts at Chouzhou.
除了合法客戶外，2025年前四個月向俄羅斯出售受限電子產品、工業化學品及其他商品和服務的50多家公司也在稠行持有帳戶。
Many of the documents reviewed by The Times were generated by A7, a Moscow-based financial services firm. A trove of its records from early 2025 was posted online by hackers. In addition, The Times obtained and analyzed thousands of business transactions and other documents from internal databases that the firm had left unsecured online. Chouzhou Bank appeared repeatedly throughout the documents, providing a detailed example of the role that correspondent services play in sanctions evasion.
時報審閱的許多文件來自總部位於莫斯科的金融服務公司A7。駭客在網上發布了該公司2025年初的大量記錄。此外，時報還獲取並分析了數千筆商業交易和其他文件，這些來自該公司在網上未受保護的內部數據庫。稠行在文件中反覆出現，為代理行服務在制裁規避中所起的作用提供了一個詳細例證。
A7 did not respond to a request for comment. The Times reported last month that A7 helps Russian businesses evade economic restrictions by sending money abroad to buy goods, often through shell companies that have little purpose other than to mask such purchases.
A7未回應置評請求。時報上個月報導，A7通過將資金匯往國外購買商品，幫助俄羅斯企業規避經濟限制，通常是通過除了掩蓋此類採購外別無其他目的的空殼公司。
廣告
One shell company working on behalf of A7 in Kyrgyzstan made more than $10 million of restricted purchases during that period through a bank that held an account at Chouzhou.
通過一家在稠行持有帳戶的銀行，一家在吉爾吉斯斯坦代表A7工作的空殼公司在此期間進行了超過1000萬美元的受限採購。
In other instances, Russian companies bought industrial goods, including precision grinding machines that could be used to manufacturer components for weapons.
還有一些俄羅斯公司購買了工業產品，包括可用於製造武器部件的精密磨床。
In January 2025, transaction documents show, a Russian company bought $73,000 of components used to build electronic warfare systems. The parts were supplied by a Hong Kong distributor with a history of sales to Russian buyers, according to trade data.
交易文件顯示，2025年1月，一家俄羅斯公司購買了價值7.3萬美元的組件，用於製造電子戰系統。貿易數據顯示，這些部件由一家有向俄羅斯買家銷售歷史的香港分銷商供應。
To complete the sale, First Abu Dhabi Bank, an Emirati bank, arranged to have the money sent to Chouzhou’s account at Bank of America.
為完成這筆交易，阿聯酋的第一阿布達比銀行安排將資金匯至稠行在美國銀行的帳戶。
As is standard practice in correspondent transactions, Bank of America received money transfer orders, but not details about the purchase itself. That allowed the nature of the goods, and who was buying them, to be obscured as the sale worked its way through the international banking system.
按照代理行交易的標準做法，美國銀行收到了匯款指令，但不會得到有關採購本身的細節。這使得貨物的性質以及買家的身份在交易通過國際銀行系統進行時得以被掩蓋。
Without explanation in June 2025, Bank of America ended its relationship with Chouzhou Bank, closing Account 0252, whose existence was disclosed on a Kyrgyz bank website. Chouzhou has continued to provide access to the dollar — through Citibank, JPMorgan Chase and Standard Chartered — for dozens of banks that otherwise lack direct access to the U.S. financial system, including in countries that have close economic ties to Russia, The Times found.
2025年6月，美國銀行在沒有解釋的情況下終止了與稠行的關係，關閉了0252帳戶。該帳戶的存在曾在一家吉爾吉斯斯坦銀行網站上披露。時報發現，稠行繼續通過花旗銀行、摩根大通和渣打銀行為數十家原本無法直接進入美國金融系統的銀行提供美元通道，其中包括與俄羅斯有密切經濟聯繫的國家。
廣告
Chouzhou did not respond to detailed requests for comment. Reached in person at a banking conference in Miami in late September, a manager for the bank’s correspondent banking division declined to comment.
稠行未回應詳細的置評請求。9月下旬在邁阿密舉行的一次銀行業會議上，本報得以面見該行代理行部門的一位經理，對方拒絕置評。
Representatives from Bank of America, Citigroup, Standard Chartered and First Abu Dhabi all said that they worked closely with law enforcement officials and governments. The representatives added that their banks regularly monitored correspondent activity and reported suspicious activity to the authorities in order to identify and help disrupt illicit banking networks.
美國銀行、花旗集團、渣打銀行和第一阿布達比銀行的代表均表示，他們與執法官員和政府密切合作。這些代表補充說，他們的銀行定期監控代理行活動，並向當局報告可疑活動，以識別並幫助破壞非法銀行網路。
While a spokeswoman for Citi and a spokesman for First Abu Dhabi said they would not comment on specific customers, all four banks emphasized that they maintained rigorous internal controls to combat money laundering, sanctions evasion and other financial crimes. JPMorgan declined to comment.
儘管花旗和第一阿布達比的發言人都表示不會對特定客戶置評，但四家銀行均強調，他們保持著嚴格的內部控制，以打擊洗錢、制裁規避和其他金融犯罪。摩根大通拒絕置評。
An essential piece of plumbing
必不可少的基礎設施
The correspondent banking system is an opaque and antiquated yet essential piece of financial plumbing that underpins international finance. It has played a crucial role in making the dollar the dominant currency for international trade and giving the United States unmatched leverage over financial transactions.
作為國際金融的支柱，代理行系統是一個不透明且陳舊但又必不可少的金融管道。它在使美元成為國際貿易主導貨幣以及賦予美國在金融交易中無可比擬的影響力方面發揮了至關重要的作用。
Here’s how it works: Imagine that a fabric exporter in rural China wants to sell its goods to customers abroad. It would be impractical for the exporter, or even its local bank, to maintain accounts in every country and in every currency that its customers might want to use.
其運作方式如下：想像一下，中國農村的一家面料出口商想向海外客戶出售商品。對於該出口商，甚至其當地銀行來說，在客戶可能想要使用的每個國家和每種貨幣中維護帳戶是不切實際的。
廣告
Correspondent banking solves this problem. The exporter’s local Chinese bank holds dollar, euro and other currency accounts at a select few global banks. Through those accounts, the Chinese bank gains access to the broad networks of those international banks, allowing its customers to send and receive international payments and convert proceeds into local currencies.
代理行解決了這個問題。出口商的當地中資銀行在少數全球銀行持有美元、歐元和其他貨幣帳戶。通過這些帳戶，中資銀行獲得了這些國際銀行的廣泛網路，使其客戶能夠收發國際支付，並將收益兌換成本地貨幣。
By its nature, however, the correspondent system can add distance between big banks and small companies seeking to circumvent their controls. Big banks often delegate the vetting of customers and transactions to smaller banks further down the correspondent chain.
然而，代理行系統本質上會在大銀行與試圖規避其控制的小公司之間增加距離。大銀行通常將客戶和交易的審查委託給代理鏈下游的小銀行。
When new regulations were put in place to stop terror financing after the Sept. 11, 2001, attacks, banks began cutting correspondent relationships around the globe.
2001年9月11日襲擊後為阻斷恐怖主義資金管道而實施了新規，銀行隨即開始在全球範圍內削減代理行關係。
Sudan, which the United States labeled a state sponsor of terrorism, was hit especially hard. Many banks stopped handling payments from the country, raising fears that it could be cut off from the global financial system and pushed toward shadow banking networks in order to supply its citizens with even basic necessities.
被美國列為支持恐怖主義國家的蘇丹受到的打擊尤其嚴重。許多銀行停止處理來自該國的付款，引發其被隔絕於全球金融體系之外的擔憂，哪怕國民的最基本需求都被迫要轉向影子銀行網路。
Since then, regulators have tried to balance the risk of sanctions evasion with the goal of keeping the dollar used around the world. In 2016, the Treasury Department issued guidance that said banks did not need to individually vet their correspondent’s clients, a practice called “know your customer’s customer.”
自那以後，監管機構一直試圖在制裁規避風險與保持美元在全球使用這一目標之間取得平衡。2016年，美國財政部發布指引稱，銀行無需逐一審查其代理行客戶——也就是所謂「了解你的客戶的客戶」的原則。
When transactions pass through an American bank, the Treasury Department has broad jurisdiction to regulate and police the activity. Last month, for example, the U.S. government issued economic restrictions against two foreign banks that used the correspondent system to help Iran gain access to the dollar system in order to launder money and evade sanctions on behalf of the Islamic Revolutionary Guards Corps.
當交易通過美國銀行時，美國財政部擁有廣泛的管轄權來監管和監督該活動。例如，上個月，美國政府對外國銀行實施經濟限制，這兩家銀行利用代理行系統幫助伊朗進入美元體系，以便為伊斯蘭革命衛隊洗錢和規避制裁。
廣告
U.S. banks can face legal liability for processing restricted transactions even when acting only as a correspondent for another financial institution. However, the Treasury Department has discretion over the cases it pursues.
即使僅作為另一家金融機構的代理行處理受限交易，美國銀行也可能面臨法律責任。然而，美國財政部對追查哪些案件擁有自由裁量權。
Depending on the extent of the illicit activity and the level of the bank’s due diligence, it would be difficult to imagine an American bank being penalized for unwittingly playing a role in a sophisticated sanctions evasion scheme, according to a former senior Treasury official who was not authorized to speak publicly about enforcement procedures.
據一位未獲授權公開談論執法程序的前財政部高級官員稱，很難想像一家美國銀行會因在複雜的制裁規避計劃中無意中發揮作用而受到處罰，具體要看非法活動的情節和銀行盡職調查的水平。
A critical lifeline
一條關鍵的生命線
Chouzhou was founded in Yiwu in 1987 and grew as the midsize Chinese trading city blossomed into a manufacturing hub for goods that are sent around the world.
稠州商業銀行於1987年在義烏成立，隨著這座中國中型貿易城市發展成為向世界各地輸送商品的製造業中心而成長。
Over the years, it carved out a niche by offering correspondent services to dozens of banks in countries identified by Western officials as high risk for money laundering or sanctions evasion, including Afghanistan, Yemen and South Sudan, according to the documents obtained by The Times and data from BankCheck, a correspondent banking compliance platform.
據時報獲得的文件以及代理行合規平台BankCheck的數據，多年來，通過向西方官員認定為洗錢或制裁規避高風險的數十個國家的銀行提供代理行服務，這家銀行開闢了一個利基市場，這些國家包括阿富汗、葉門和南蘇丹。
Chouzhou has an especially large presence in Kyrgyzstan, Georgia and Uzbekistan — countries with close economic ties to Russia that have become important hubs for rerouted trade.
稠行在吉爾吉斯斯坦、喬治亞和烏茲別克斯坦尤其活躍，這些國家與俄羅斯有密切經濟聯繫，已成為改道貿易的重要樞紐。
According to the BankCheck data, Chouzhou provides access to dollar accounts to nearly 70 other banks, most of which otherwise lack direct access to the U.S. financial system. Those banks, in turn, market dollar accounts to their customers.
根據BankCheck數據，稠行為近70家其他銀行提供美元帳戶通道，其中大多數原本無法直接進入美國金融系統。這些銀行反過來又向客戶推銷美元帳戶。
By comparison, Industrial and Commercial Bank of China, the largest bank in the world by assets, provides dollar access to 20 banks. HSBC Bank in London provides just seven dollar correspondent accounts to other banks, BankCheck data shows.
相比之下，按資產計全球最大的銀行中國工商銀行為20家銀行提供美元通道。BankCheck數據顯示，倫敦的滙豐銀行僅向其他銀行提供七個美元代理帳戶。
In 2024, Chouzhou processed more than $200 billion in international transactions, according to a bond prospectus, nearly four times the amount of its total assets. But that is likely a small fraction of its activity now: At the financial services conference in Miami last month, Chouzhou advertised relationships with more than 1,000 banks and the ability to process payments in 27 currencies.
根據一份債券招股說明書，2024年，稠行處理了超過2000億美元的國際交易，將近其總資產的四倍。但這可能只是其當前活動的一小部分：在上個月邁阿密的金融服務會議上，稠行宣傳其與1000多家銀行保持著關係，有能力處理27種貨幣支付。
Chouzhou stopped processing transactions directly with Russian and Belarusian banks in early 2024, according to news releases and trade documents. But it has continued working with customers and banks in countries considered high risk for Russian sanctions evasion.
據新聞稿和貿易文件，稠行於2024年初停止直接處理與俄羅斯和白俄羅斯銀行的交易。但它繼續與被視為俄羅斯制裁規避高風險國家的客戶和銀行合作。
Soon after Bank of America ended its relationship with Chouzhou last year, the Chinese bank opened two new dollar accounts at Citibank and Standard Chartered, BankCheck data shows.
BankCheck數據顯示，去年美國銀行終止與稠行的關係後不久，這家中國銀行在花旗銀行和渣打銀行開設了兩個新的美元帳戶。
The accounts remain open.
這些帳戶至今仍正常使用。
