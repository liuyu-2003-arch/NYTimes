習近平的APEC「高光時刻」會否蒙塵？

Will a Spy Scandal, Trade Friction and a Surprise Guest Ruin Xi’s Big Moment?

DAVID PIERSON • 2026-11-18
When China’s then-paramount leader, Deng Xiaoping, toured Shenzhen in 1992, he used the southern Chinese city to signal an embrace of open markets, setting off decades of rapid growth that would make his country the world’s factory floor.
1992年時任中國最高領導人鄧小平視察深圳時，他利用這座中國南方城市釋放了擁抱開放市場的信號，開啟了數十年的快速增長，最終使他的國家成為了世界工廠。
Since then, Shenzhen has grown from a sleepy town into a gleaming metropolis that dwarfs its more established neighbor, Hong Kong. Apple’s main assembler, Foxconn, runs a sprawling campus there, and Walmart manages its China business from the city. It is home to many Chinese brands like Huawei, BYD and the drone maker DJI.
自那時起，深圳從一個偏僻小鎮發展成為閃耀的大都市，甚至令早已聲名顯赫的鄰居香港相形見絀。蘋果的主要組裝商富士康在這裡運營著一個龐大的園區，沃爾瑪在此管理其中國業務。它也是華為、比亞迪和無人機製造商大疆等眾多中國品牌的總部所在地。
This week, China’s leader, Xi Jinping, is hosting a regional economic summit in the same city, using it to make the case that China’s growth can benefit the world. Shenzhen has been primped for the occasion, with bus shelters plastered with the slogan: “Building an Asia-Pacific Community to Prosper Together.”
本週，中國領導人習近平在這座城市主辦一場區域經濟峰會，以此來證明中國的增長可以惠及世界。深圳已為此場合進行了精心布置，公車候車亭貼滿了標語：「建設亞太共同體，促進共同繁榮。」
But some of that message may be difficult to square for many of China’s trading partners. To them, Shenzhen also embodies much of what makes the world uneasy about China today: a flood of cheap exports; tech giants that pose security risks; and a Communist Party that has traded Mr. Deng’s freewheeling experimentation for more state control.
但對於中國的許多貿易夥伴來說，其中一些信息可能難以接受。對他們而言，深圳也同樣體現了當今世界對中國感到不安的諸多因素：廉價出口商品的泛濫、構成安全風險的科技巨頭，以及一個用更多的國家控制取代了鄧小平的自由試驗的共產黨。
廣告
Mr. Xi’s moment in the spotlight at the Asia Pacific Economic Cooperation forum will also be clouded by allegations of a Chinese spy scandal at the American chip giant, Nvidia, that has already prompted Washington to tighten Chinese access to advanced American chips.
習近平在亞太經合組織論壇上備受矚目的時刻也將因美國晶片巨頭英偉達的中國間諜醜聞指控蒙上陰影，該指控已經促使華盛頓收緊中國獲取先進美國晶片的管道。
And he is set to meet for the first time with President Trump and President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia, a gathering that could unsettle smaller powers like Ukraine. (Mr. Trump has also suggested that he might see the North Korean dictator Kim Jong-un while in China.)
他將首次與川普總統和俄羅斯總統弗拉基米爾·V·普丁舉行會晤，這次聚會可能會讓烏克蘭等小國感到不安。（川普也曾暗示，他在中國期間可能會與朝鮮獨裁者金正恩見面。）
Here’s what is on China’s agenda when Mr. Xi takes center stage in Shenzhen:
以下是習近平在深圳登上舞台中心時中國方面的議程：
Showcase of China’s Tech Ambitions
展示中國的科技雄心
In some ways, the setting itself could be read as a statement of defiance.
從某種意義上說，選擇深圳作為舉辦地本身就可以被解讀為一種反抗的聲明。
Shenzhen is home to companies Washington has labeled national security risks, like Huawei and ZTE, as well as Chinese artificial intelligence labs racing to close the gap with American rivals. Many of the companies have been subjected to American sanctions and blacklists.
深圳是華為、中興等被華盛頓貼上國家安全風險標籤的公司的總部，也是許多中國人工智慧實驗室的所在地，這些實驗室正競相縮小與美國競爭對手的差距。許多公司都受到了美國的制裁併被列入黑名單。
Mr. Xi wants to show that “China can do well despite America’s all-out effort to kill it” with those trade restrictions, said Minxin Pei, a professor at Claremont McKenna College who studies Chinese leadership politics.
研究中國領導層政治的克萊蒙特·麥肯納學院教授裴敏欣表示，習近平希望表明，儘管美國竭盡全力通過這些貿易限制手段進行打壓，但「中國依然可以發展得很好」。
That is an image that Beijing has tried to cultivate after Washington began restricting direct sales of advanced chips to China in 2022, pointing to Huawei’s homegrown processors and DeepSeek’s and other Chinese labs’ breakthrough A.I. models as proof that U.S. restrictions have failed.
自華盛頓於2022年開始限制向中國直接銷售先進晶片以來，北京一直試圖樹立這種形象，將華為的國產處理器以及DeepSeek等中國實驗室的突破性人工智慧模型作為美國限制措施沒有見效的證明。
Beijing is also casting itself as a partner to other countries in technological advancement, offering Chinese cooperation and know-how on A.I., an approach that competes with the United States’ attempts to unite partners around A.I. supply chains independent of China.
北京還將自己塑造成其他國家在技術進步方面的合作夥伴，提供中國在人工智慧方面的合作和專業知識，抗衡美國試圖團結合作夥伴、建立不依賴中國的人工智慧供應鏈的策略。
廣告
What Espionage?
哪有什麼間諜活動？
For China, the news had threatened to distract from the upcoming summit. Less than three weeks before the gathering, The New York Times reported that a Chinese businesswoman who had a close relationship with Jensen Huang, the chief executive of Nvidia, was found to be passing intelligence to Beijing. In the days since, Mr. Huang has stepped down and the company’s board has said it would conduct an investigation.
對中國而言，近期的新聞可能會轉移人們對即將召開的峰會的注意力。在距離會議還不到三週的時候，《紐約時報》報導稱，一名與英偉達首席執行官黃仁勛關係密切的中國女商人被發現向北京傳遞情報。此後的幾天裡，黃仁勛已經辭職，公司董事會表示將展開調查。
The allegations, which China has denied, could have scuttled talks with Mr. Trump and put the countries’ fragile trade truce at risk. The claim of such a bold espionage plot was also a potential source of embarrassment for a country hosting business and government leaders at a major event.
這些被中國否認的指控原本可能會破壞與川普的會談，並危及兩國脆弱的貿易休戰。對於一個要在重要活動中接待商界和政府領導人的國家來說，如此直白的間諜陰謀指控可能也會引起尷尬。
China has carried on as if it were business as usual. And in a way, Beijing has already scored a win: The controversy has failed to derail the scheduled talks between Mr. Xi and Mr. Trump, who has downplayed the alleged spy plot.
中國在繼續推進議程，彷彿什麼都沒有發生。在某種程度上，北京已經取得了一場勝利：這一爭議未能破壞習近平與川普之間的預定會談，川普已經淡化了被指存在的間諜陰謀。
Rather than declaring that he would confront China about the Nvidia scandal, Mr. Trump has said he does not intend to bring it up. “I don’t think you want to insult China just before you get there,” he told reporters outside the White House on Monday when asked about divisions within his administration about how tough to be on China.
川普並未宣布他將就英偉達醜聞與中國對抗，而是表示他不打算提及此事。週一，當在白宮外被問及其政府內部在對華強硬程度上的分歧時，他告訴記者：「我認為你不應該在去中國之前去得罪它。」
But the scandal is already costing China. On Monday, the Trump administration issued a rule to stop Chinese companies from accessing advanced Nvidia chips through data centers in third countries, and to require licenses for chip exports to companies in Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, Indonesia and Brazil.
但這項醜聞已經讓中國付出了代價。週一，川普政府發布了一項規定，阻止中國公司通過第三國的數據中心獲取先進的英偉達晶片，並要求向馬來西亞、新加坡、泰國、印度尼西亞和巴西的公司出口晶片必須獲得許可。
廣告
China Needs to Ease Trade Concerns
中國需要緩解貿易擔憂
China’s export machinery is practically unbeatable. This month, China reported that its exports exceeded its imports by more than $1 trillion in the first 10 months of this year, putting it on track to eclipse last year’s record.
中國的出口機器幾乎不可戰勝。中國本月報告稱，今年前10個月貿易順差超過1萬億美元，有望打破去年的紀錄。
But as its factories have churned out solar panels, electric cars and other goods for consumers globally, China has faced pushback not only from the United States but from around the world. It needs to head off efforts by trading partners like the European Union to block its exports.
但隨著中國工廠大量生產太陽能電池板、電動汽車和其他面向全球消費者的商品，中國不僅面臨來自美國的抵制，還面臨來自世界各地的抵制。它需要阻止歐盟等貿易夥伴阻礙其出口的努力。
That’s why China is billing the event as an opportunity for the region to champion free trade and oppose protectionism.
這就是為什麼中國將此次活動宣傳為該地區倡導自由貿易和反對保護主義的一個機會。
In reality, China has been turning inward. Beijing has sought to squeeze out foreign technology, electronics and auto brands in favor of Chinese firms. The Communist Party, which under Mr. Xi has tightened its grip on the economy, vowed last month to expand its presence inside internet companies. Shen Weizhong, a former policy planner with China’s Foreign Ministry, called the approach “manageable openness,” in which security weighs as heavily as growth.
實際上，中國一直在轉向內向型發展。北京試圖擠壓外國技術、電子產品和汽車品牌的市場，扶持中國企業。在習近平領導下加強了對經濟控制的共產黨上個月誓言要擴大其在互聯網公司內部的影響力。前中國外交部政策規劃官員沈偉中稱這種做法是「可管控的開放」，其中安全與增長同等重要。
Mr. Xi’s willingness to turn the world’s dependence on Chinese goods into leverage has also alienated other countries.
習近平意在將世界對中國商品的依賴轉化為籌碼，這也疏遠了其他國家。
廣告
Beijing imposed controls on exports of rare earths last year, and it has recently moved to restrict the exports of dozens of technologies, including drones, artificial intelligence models and agents, and know-how about advanced medicines and solar panels. China says these measures are a response to U.S. blacklists and export restrictions.
北京去年對稀土出口實施了管制，最近又採取行動限制數十項技術的出口，包括無人機、人工智慧模型和智能體，以及有關先進藥物和太陽能電池板的專業知識。中國表示，這些措施是對美國黑名單和出口限制的回應。
“Chinese leaders recognize that the golden era of globalization is over, and geoeconomic competition has become the new normal,” said Yuen Yuen Ang, a professor of political economy at Johns Hopkins University.
「中國領導人認識到，全球化的黃金時代已經結束，地緣經濟競爭已成為新常態，」約翰斯·霍普金斯大學政治經濟學教授洪源遠表示。
Putin, Trump … and Kim?
普丁、川普……還有金正恩？
Mr. Xi is expected to sit down with Mr. Trump and Mr. Putin on Thursday, a meeting that is without precedent. China’s Foreign Ministry on Monday called the meeting one “of groundbreaking significance.”
預計習近平將於週四與川普和普丁舉行會談，這是一次史無前例的會議。中國外交部週一稱這次會議「具有開創性意義」。
For Beijing, the gathering casts China as a power broker that can bring Washington and Moscow to the same table. Mr. Xi wants to show the world, and especially his own people, that China under his leadership is “consequential, influential and highly respected, with enormous convening power,” said Mr. Pei of Claremont McKenna.
對北京來說，這次聚會將中國塑造成一個能夠讓華盛頓和莫斯科坐到同一張談判桌前的權力中間人。克萊蒙特·麥肯納學院的裴敏欣表示，習近平希望向世界、特別是向本國人民展示，在他領導下的中國是「舉足輕重、有影響力且備受尊重的，擁有巨大的號召力」。
But the optics are awkward.
但輿論觀感有些尷尬。
Mr. Trump said on Monday that he planned to discuss the war in Ukraine in Shenzhen. Beijing is reluctant to take the lead on peace talks, said Zhao Long, a senior research fellow at the Shanghai Institutes for International Studies, lest it create “the wrong impression” that “major powers are dividing up the interests of other countries.”
川普週一表示，他計劃在深圳討論烏克蘭戰爭。上海國際問題研究院高級研究員趙隆表示，北京不願在和平談判中牽頭，以免造成「大國正在瓜分其他國家利益」的「錯誤印象」。
And Mr. Trump has raised the possibility of another guest: the North Korean dictator, Kim Jong-un. He told reporters on Monday as he left the White House: “And we may meet with Kim Jong Un, also. So, that’ll be good. We could — we have a chance to accomplish a lot.”
川普還提出了另一位客人的可能性：朝鮮獨裁者金正恩。他週一離開白宮時告訴記者：「我們可能還會見金正恩。所以，那會很好。我們可以——我們有機會取得很多成就。」
廣告
A spokesman for China’s foreign ministry, Guo Jiakun, asked on Tuesday if Mr. Kim would be attending, said: “I do not have information on this.” Russian state news agencies reported that Mr. Putin could meet with Mr. Kim if the North Korean leader travels to China, citing Dmitri S. Peskov, the Kremlin’s spokesman.
中國外交部發言人郭嘉昆週二在被問及金正恩是否會出席時表示：「我們沒有這方面的信息。」俄羅斯國家通訊社援引克里姆林宮發言人德米特里·S·佩斯科夫的話報導稱，如果這位朝鮮領導人前往中國，普丁可能會與他會面。

Berry Wang、Keith Bradsher、Ruoxin Zhang和Siyi Zhao對本文有報導和研究貢獻。
