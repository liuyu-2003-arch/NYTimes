川普向習近平和普丁獻媚不會有好結果

Trump’s Ego Trip With Xi and Putin Will End Badly

KURT CAMPBELL • 2026-11-18
A vexing feature of President Trump’s foreign policy is his unfailing attraction to the world’s two dominant strongmen, President Xi Jinping of China and President Vladimir Putin of Russia. Like a deer drawn toward the intriguing smell of the hunters’ blind, Mr. Trump seems compelled to engage with both authoritarian leaders.
川普總統外交政策中一個令人費解的特點是，他總是被世界上兩位處於主導地位的強人所吸引，也就是中國國家主席習近平和俄羅斯總統弗拉基米爾·普丁。就像鹿被獵人掩體散發出的迷人氣息所吸引一樣，川普似乎情不自禁地要與這兩位威權領導人來往。
Mr. Trump’s desire has produced generally anticlimactic bilateral engagements so far in his second term. The 2025 Alaska summit with Mr. Putin made no headway on ending the war in Ukraine. The three recent meetings with Mr. Xi made little progress in easing trade tensions or increasing artificial intelligence safeguards. Now, all three leaders are set to convene in an unusual trilateral forum on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation in Shenzhen, China, this week.
迄今為止，在川普的第二任期內，他的這種意願所帶來的雙邊接觸總體上令人掃興。2025年，他與普丁在阿拉斯加舉行的峰會在結束烏克蘭戰爭方面沒有取得任何進展。近期與習近平的三次會晤在緩解貿易緊張局勢或增加人工智慧安全保障方面進展甚微。如今，本週在中國深圳舉行的亞太經合組織會議間隙，這三位領導人將在一場不同尋常的三邊論壇上齊聚一堂。
For Americans and their like-minded partners, little that is beneficial can come from the meeting.
對於美國人及其志同道合的夥伴來說，這次會議幾乎不會帶來任何有益的結果。
Most American multilateral gatherings in recent decades have convened states that largely shared our national interests, like the maritime democracies of the so-called QUAD — India, Japan, Australia and the United States. This would be the first time that a sitting American president has jointly met with the leaders of China and Russia, who represent systems and approaches to global affairs that are widely considered antithetical to American interests.
近幾十年來，美國的大多數多邊集會召集的都是在很大程度上與我們享有共同國家利益的國家，比如四方安全對話(QUAD)中的海洋民主國家——印度、日本、澳洲和美國。這將是現任美國總統首次與中俄領導人共同會晤，這兩位領導人代表的體制和處理全球事務的方式被廣泛認為與美國利益背道而馳。
廣告
Mr. Trump’s spokesmen and sycophants say doing so will advance peace in Europe and buy time while the United States builds resilience in its critical minerals stockpiles. It is difficult for them to explain the president’s obvious zeal for the gathering when he is so evidently unenthusiastic about Group of 7 summits with the seven richest democracies, or meetings with European Union leaders. It is even harder given what Mr. Xi and Mr. Putin are doing behind the scenes to undermine America.
川普的發言人和阿諛奉承者表示，這樣做將促進歐洲的和平，並為美國在關鍵礦產儲備上形成韌性爭取時間。他們很難解釋總統為什麼對此次集會表現出巨大的熱情，對由七個最富有的民主國家組成的七國集團峰會或與歐盟領導人的會晤卻明顯缺乏興趣。而考慮到習近平和普丁在幕後為削弱美國所做的一切，這一切就更加難以理解了。
China is the principal backer of Russia’s war against Ukraine, providing essential supplies that keep Moscow’s missiles and drones flying. Russia is giving Iran satellite imaging and overhead targeting data even as Iran strikes U.S. military assets in the Gulf region. Chinese companies have provided Iran with crucial missile components. Joint Chinese and Russian military exercises in the western Pacific are increasingly active around Japan and near contested island territories. Beneath the appearance of accord at the trilateral summit, Mr. Trump will be making nice with opponents who have daggers drawn against our allies and partners in three of the world’s most dangerous spots.
中國是俄羅斯對烏戰爭的主要支持者，為莫斯科的導彈和無人機提供維持運轉的關鍵物資。即使在伊朗打擊美國在海灣地區的軍事資產之際，俄羅斯仍在向伊朗提供衛星成像和高空目標數據。中國企業向伊朗提供了關鍵的導彈部件。中俄在西太平洋的聯合軍事演習在日本週邊和有爭議的島嶼領土附近日益活躍。在三邊峰會表面上的和諧之下，川普實際上是在向對手示好，它們正在世界上最危險的三個地區對我們的盟友和夥伴拔刀相向。
The Trump administration completely misunderstands the complexities of the Russia-China relationship. In that, it is not alone: There are few bilateral relationships that American strategists have historically so thoroughly misdiagnosed. In the 1960s, the best and the brightest in the United States were very late in realizing the opportunity presented by the Sino-Soviet split; now they underestimate just how significant the Russia-China partnership has become.
川普政府完全誤解了俄中關係的複雜性。在這方面，它並不孤單：在歷史上，很少有哪對雙邊關係被美國戰略家誤診得如此徹底。在20世紀60年代，美國最優秀、最聰明的人遲遲沒能意識到中蘇交惡帶來的機遇；如今，他們又低估了俄中夥伴關係已經變得多麼重要。
Since Mr. Xi came to power in 2012, he and Mr. Putin have met officially about 50 times, an extraordinary tempo of high-level diplomacy. In theory, Russia and China, with their history of distrust, border disputes and conflicting ambitions, should not get along. In practice, these two like-minded men have built one of the most durable security partnerships on the planet. Each side no doubt worries about the future of that collaboration, but for now it is deeply consequential and immune to efforts to split them apart.
習近平自2012年上台以來已經與普丁舉行了約50次正式會晤，這是一種不同尋常的高層外交節奏。理論上，兩國的歷史充斥著不信任、邊界爭端和相互衝突的野心，本不應如此和睦相處。但在實踐中，這兩位志同道合的人建立起了地球上最持久的安全夥伴關係之一。毫無疑問，雙方都對這種合作的未來感到擔憂，但就目前而言，這種合作影響深遠，並且不受試圖將他們分開的努力的影響。
What will the three leaders discuss at this summit? China is rapidly and quietly building up its nuclear arsenal, which is much smaller than Russia’s or America’s, and so it will seek to avoid any negotiations that would keep it from catching up. Russia will talk only in ideological generalities about peace in Europe. The United States will be loath to beg for assistance in ending the war in Iran, particularly from the two countries most actively supporting Tehran. Russia will not want to talk much about A.I., as doing so will highlight how it is badly behind. Boosting global energy supplies is an issue tangled up in the disruptions in the Persian Gulf. Global development needs would be an awkward topic now that the U.S. has sharply reduced its humanitarian assistance efforts.
這三位領導人將在這次峰會上討論什麼？中國正在迅速而悄悄地擴充其核武庫，其規模遠小於俄羅斯或美國，因此它將尋求避免任何會阻止其迎頭趕上的談判。俄羅斯將只會在意識型態的泛泛之談中討論歐洲和平問題。美國將不願乞求協助結束伊朗戰爭，尤其考慮到這是兩個最積極支持德黑蘭的國家。俄羅斯不會想多談人工智慧，因為這樣做會凸顯其嚴重落後的情況。增加全球能源供應是一個與波斯灣局勢動盪糾纏不清的問題。鑒於美國已大幅削減其人道主義援助努力，全球發展需求現在將成為一個尷尬的話題。
The lack of a clear agenda for the trilateral summit is going to make Mr. Xi uncomfortable, and he is unlikely to agree to any spontaneous deals. He is focused on achieving a fourth five-year term in office next year, the longest stretch in power of any recent Chinese leader, and he does not like surprises. Given Mr. Trump’s proclivity for improvisation, Mr. Xi will be wary of any attempt at a diplomatic breakthrough.
三邊峰會缺乏明確議程會讓習近平感到不安，他不太可能同意任何即興達成的協議。他正專注於明年獲得第四個五年任期——近年中國領導人的最長任期，而且他不喜歡意外。鑒於川普傾向於即興發揮，習近平將對任何在外交上尋求突破的企圖保持警惕。
廣告
So the primary consequence of the trilateral summit in Shenzhen may be the message conveyed by three would-be strongmen standing together astride the planet, surveying the scene. This will send shivers among America’s traditional allies — clearly a plus for Mr. Putin and Mr. Xi, and probably irrelevant to Mr. Trump. Even if the discussions focus only on the stonework in Mr. Trump’s new ballroom, the flight time from Moscow to Shenzhen or the quality of the local Cantonese cuisine, projecting the image of these three leaders meeting together may well be the real purpose at work in Moscow and Beijing.
因此，深圳三邊峰會的主要結果可能是三位熱衷於鐵腕統治的領導人並肩屹立、審視全局，這將讓美國的傳統盟友感到不寒而慄——這對普丁和習近平來說顯然是個加分項，而對川普來說可能無關緊要。即使討論只集中在川普新宴會廳的石材工藝、從莫斯科到深圳的飛行時間，或是當地粵菜的質量上，展示這三位領導人齊聚一堂的形象很可能才是莫斯科和北京方面真正在努力達成的目的。
