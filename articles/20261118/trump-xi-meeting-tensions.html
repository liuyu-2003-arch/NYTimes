中美經濟裂痕繼續擴大

As Trump and Xi Mingle, Their Governments Are Arming Up

艾莎, MURPHY ZHAO • 2026-11-18
In front of the cameras, President Trump and China’s Xi Jinping have showcased a budding friendship, cemented by state visits, friendly pats on the arm and the occasional shared laugh.
在鏡頭前，川普總統與中國國家主席習近平展現出一種日漸升溫的友誼，國事訪問、友好的輕拍手臂以及偶爾的開懷大笑鞏固了這段友誼。
But the public cordiality masks a different reality. Their governments are hardening their economic defenses and putting up new barriers to trade and investment. It has become more difficult for the two countries to invest, trade or do business with each other.
但公開的友好掩蓋了另一種現實。兩國政府正在強化各自的經濟防禦並設置新的貿易和投資壁壘。兩國之間的投資、貿易或商業往來變得更加困難。
More good will is expected when Mr. Trump arrives in the Chinese city of Shenzhen this week for his third meeting with Mr. Xi this year. Yet the divide between their governments is becoming harder to ignore. Each meeting has been preceded by fresh economic offensives that are driving the world’s two largest economies further apart.
本週川普抵達中國深圳，與習近平舉行今年內的第三次會晤時，外界預期雙方將釋放更多善意。然而，兩國政府間的分歧正變得愈發難以忽視。每次會晤前都出現了使得世界兩大經濟體漸行漸遠的新一輪經濟攻勢。
Shortly before Mr. Trump was scheduled to land in Asia, his administration moved to restrict China’s access to advanced artificial intelligence chips through data centers outside its borders, proposing a new rule to close a loophole that experts said would further inflame tensions. On Tuesday, China called the rule a “textbook case of economic coercion and long-arm jurisdiction” that “disrupts the stability of global industrial and supply chains.”
就在川普按計劃抵達亞洲前不久，華盛頓採取行動，限制中國通過境外數據中心獲取先進人工智慧晶片，提議制定新規以堵住漏洞，專家稱此舉將進一步加劇緊張局勢。週二，中國稱該規定是「典型的經濟脅迫和長臂管轄」，「破壞全球產業鏈供應鏈穩定」。
廣告
It was the latest in a series of moves from both China and the United States in recent weeks to label new sectors of their economies off limits in the name of national security. Beijing has designated more technologies as strategically vital, placing tighter controls on their export. Washington has proposed barring American investment in Chinese biotechnology, hypersonic weapons and artificial intelligence.
這是中美兩國近幾週以國家安全為由將各自經濟的新領域列為禁區的一系列舉措中的最新一項。北京已將更多技術指定為具有戰略重要性，並對其出口實施了更嚴格的控制。華盛頓則提議禁止美國投資中國的生物技術、高超音速武器和人工智慧領域。
The confrontation has spilled beyond their two countries’ borders into global supply chains. China is building a fortress around technologies and critical materials on which much of the world depends, forcing other countries and multinational companies to contend with the fallout.
這種對抗已經蔓延至兩國邊界之外的全球供應鏈。中國正在圍繞世界上許多國家所依賴的技術和關鍵材料建立堡壘，迫使其他國家和跨國公司應對由此產生的影響。
“There are more barriers than bridges being built,” said Ben Kostrzewa, a partner at the law firm Hogan Lovells in Hong Kong. “Each of these moves on its own is another brick in the wall and it is becoming harder and harder to invest in the other country.”
霍金路偉律師事務所(Hogan Lovells)駐香港合伙人本·科斯特澤瓦表示：「建起的壁壘多於橋樑。這些舉措中的每一項本身都是牆上的一塊磚，在對方國家投資正變得越來越困難。」
The steady buildup of those barriers began nearly a decade ago as a tit-for-tat tariff war during Mr. Trump’s first term. It has evolved into a far broader showdown for economic and technological supremacy.
這些壁壘的穩步建立始於近十年前川普首個任期內的一場針鋒相對的關稅戰。如今，它已演變成一場在經濟和技術主導權方面更為廣泛的較量。
China, in particular, has shifted its tactics. Once largely content to answer American tariffs and sanctions with reciprocal measures that were often symbolic, Beijing is now going on the offensive. It is wielding its dominance in manufacturing and critical supply chains to remind Washington — and the rest of the world — of the costs of confronting it.
中國方面已經改變了策略。北京曾經在很大程度上滿足於用往往只是象徵意義上的對等措施來回應美國的關稅和制裁，但現在正採取攻勢。中國正利用其在製造業和關鍵供應鏈中的主導地位，提醒華盛頓以及世界其他地區與其對抗的代價。
The rivalry reached a low point last year, when both sides imposed tariffs so steep they amounted to a de facto trade embargo. China then threatened to tighten its controls on rare earths, the essential materials used in everything from smartphones and electric vehicles to fighter jets.
競爭在去年降至冰點，當時雙方徵收的關稅過高，等同於事實上的貿易禁運。隨後，中國威脅要收緊對稀土的控制，稀土是智慧型手機、電動汽車以及戰鬥機等各種產品中必不可少的材料。
廣告
In October 2025, Mr. Trump and Mr. Xi eased tensions by agreeing to a truce and reciprocal state visits.
2025年10月，川普和習近平通過同意休戰和相互進行國事訪問緩解了緊張局勢。
The two visits this year were heavy on ceremony. When Mr. Trump traveled to Beijing in May, he was greeted by military bands and cheering children. When Mr. Xi landed in Maryland in September, he was welcomed on the tarmac by Mr. Trump. There have been raised glasses, shared walks and moments of banter.
今年的兩次訪問都注重禮節。當川普5月前往北京時，受到了軍樂隊和歡呼的兒童的迎接。當習近平9月降落在馬里蘭州時，川普在停機坪上迎接了他。雙方曾舉杯慶祝、一同散步，並進行了輕鬆愉快的交談。
After Mr. Xi’s visit two months ago, the two governments agreed to extend their yearlong truce and reduce tariffs on $60 billion worth of goods. Yet neither side formally confirmed the extension until hours before the agreement was set to expire, just a week before Mr. Trump’s planned arrival in China.
在習近平兩個月前訪問之後，兩國政府同意將為期一年的休戰期延長，並降低價值600億美元商品的關稅。然而，直到協議到期前幾個小時，也就是川普計劃抵達中國前一週，雙方才正式確認延期。
Even then, the reprieve was limited. The renewed truce, which suspended certain tariffs and export controls, runs only through January.
即使如此，這種喘息之機也是有限的。新的休戰協議暫停了部分關稅和出口管制，但僅持續到明年1月。
And despite Beijing’s pledge to ease restrictions on exports of rare-earth metals and other critical minerals, some American companies still struggle to obtain supplies, said Erin Browne, the U.S. Treasury Department’s under secretary for international affairs.
美國財政部負責國際事務的副部長埃琳·布朗表示，儘管北京承諾放寬對稀土金屬和其他關鍵礦產的出口限制，但一些美國公司在獲取供應方面仍面臨困難。
“We’re making sure that we’re getting equal treatment across companies, across sectors, but also across materials or rare earths,” Ms. Browne said. “The strength of the relationship between our two presidents goes a long way in helping to move that along.”
布朗表示：「我們正確保我們在不同企業、不同行業，以及不同材料或稀土方面獲得同等待遇。兩位領導人之間的融洽關係在很大程度上有助於推動這一進程。」
廣告
Yet the personal rapport has done little to restrain an emboldened China, which has signaled it is no longer afraid of escalating economic warfare.
然而，這種個人交情幾乎無法約束更加自信的中國，中國已發出信號，不再畏懼經濟戰的升級。
Beijing has strengthened its arsenal of countermeasures against American blacklists of its companies. It has created new legal mechanisms to block foreign sanctions, and Chinese companies have brought legal actions against foreign businesses, including JPMorgan Chase and Citibank, in Chinese courts for complying with American restrictions.
隨著美國將中國企業列入黑名單，中國也加強了反制。它建立了新的法律機制來阻斷外國制裁，而且中國企業已經在中國法院對遵守美國限制措施的外國企業（包括摩根大通和花旗銀行）提起訴訟。
This year, Beijing introduced sweeping rules empowering regulators to inspect corporate records and prevent executives from leaving China if their companies are found to be helping to shift supply chains out of the country.
今年，北京出台了廣泛的規定，授權監管機構檢查企業記錄，並且如果發現企業協助將供應鏈轉移出中國，可阻止高管離境。
It has also established a national security review system for foreign investments, using it to block Meta’s $2 billion acquisition of Manus and SoftBank’s $5.4 billion takeover of ABB Robotics.
它還建立了一套外商投資國家安全審查制度，並以此阻止了Meta以20億美元收購Manus以及軟銀以54億美元收購ABB機器人的交易。
Together, these developments reflect “a very prominent change” in China’s approach, said Zhao Minghao, a professor at Fudan University. Beijing is assembling legal and regulatory tools built around economic and technological security, he said, and “exploring using actual cases to give these laws ‘teeth,’ so that they can truly be implemented and put to use.”
復旦大學教授趙明昊表示，這些都反映了中國策略中「一個非常顯著的變化」。他表示，北京正在整合圍繞經濟和技術安全的法律和監管工具，並「探索通過實際案例讓這些法律『長牙齒』，從而使其能夠真正落地並投入使用」。
Its new strategy aims to protect control over the technologies and industries that give it a competitive edge.
新戰略旨在保護對賦予其競爭優勢的技術和行業的控制權。
Beijing has recently started restricting exports of dozens of technologies related to artificial intelligence, biotechnology and solar power. Under the rules, Chinese companies need government approval before transferring or sharing the covered technologies overseas.
北京最近開始限制幾十項與人工智慧、生物技術和太陽能相關技術的出口。根據規定，中國企業在向海外轉讓或分享受限技術前需要獲得政府批准。
For its part, Washington is seeking to cut off the capital that helps finance China’s technological ambitions. A Treasury Department proposal to expand those investment limits could make investing in broad swaths of China’s technology sector nearly impossible.
就華盛頓而言，它正試圖切斷有助於中國技術雄心的資金。財政部一項擴大這些投資限制的提議可能會使投資中國科技廣泛領域變得幾乎不可能。
廣告
“We’re in the early stages of financial decoupling,” said H.K. Park, managing director and national security expert at Crumpton Global.
克倫普頓環球公司(Crumpton Global)董事總經理兼國家安全專家H·K·朴(H.K. Park)表示：「我們正處於金融脫鉤的早期階段。」
Analysts in both countries see little prospect of reversing the ever-wider restrictions on trade, technology and capital.
兩國的分析人士都認為，在貿易、技術和資本方面日益廣泛的限制幾乎沒有逆轉的可能。
That leaves Mr. Xi and Mr. Trump with a shrinking set of issues on which they can strike deals. When the two leaders meet in Shenzhen, they may find room to bargain over tariffs. But the technologies, investments and critical materials at the heart of their rivalry are becoming harder to negotiate.
這使得習近平和川普能夠達成協議的議題範圍不斷縮小。當兩位領導人在深圳會晤時，他們可能會找到在關稅問題上討價還價的空間。但處於競爭核心的技術、投資和關鍵材料正變得越來越難以談判。
“Tariffs are a minor issue,” said Zhou Yong, a partner at the JunHe Law Offices in Beijing. They are among the few areas where both governments can still reach agreement.
北京君合律師事務所合伙人周勇表示：「關稅是次要問題。」它們是兩國政府仍能達成一致的少數領域之一。
“The real sticking points are competition in A.I., high tech, and chips, and China’s export curbs on rare earths,” he said.
「真正的癥結在於人工智慧、高科技和晶片領域的競爭，以及中國對稀土的出口限制，」他表示。
“These are higher-stakes bargaining chips that both sides feel difficult to negotiate now.”
「這些是更重大的交易籌碼，雙方現在都覺得難以談判。」

艾莎(Alexandra Stevenson)是《紐約時報》記者，主要報導中國經濟及其在全球的影響。
