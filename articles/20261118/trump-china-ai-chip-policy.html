英偉達醜聞加深川普政府內部裂痕

Trump Administration Divide on China Policy Breaks Into Public View

JONATHAN SWAN, MAGGIE HABERMAN, ANA SWANSON, SHAWN McCREESH • 2026-11-18
President Trump headed on Monday for a trip to Asia to meet President Xi Jinping of China with his administration openly divided over how hard to squeeze that country on artificial intelligence technology.
川普總統週一啟程前往亞洲，準備與中國國家主席習近平舉行會晤。與此同時，川普政府內部對於如何在人工智慧技術領域向中國施壓存在公開分歧。
Mr. Trump noted before he left that he has not wanted to make moves that “insult China.”
川普在出發前表示，他不想採取「冒犯中國」的舉動。
The public airing of the fight over China policy inside Mr. Trump’s cabinet shows how much the fallout of the Nvidia scandal has deepened fissures inside the government, forcing into view a debate about how the United States should reckon with Beijing.
川普內閣內部對華政策之爭公開化表明，英偉達醜聞的餘波進一步加深了政府內部的裂痕，也使美國應該如何應對北京的爭論浮出水面。
Mr. Trump, who is due to meet Mr. Xi and President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia at a summit in the Chinese tech hub of Shenzhen this week, has struggled to balance national security concerns about America’s most powerful rival against his desire to prop up Nvidia’s chips business and avoid another trade war with Mr. Xi.
川普定於本週在中國科技中心深圳舉行的峰會上與習近平和俄羅斯總統普丁會晤。他既要應對美國頭號競爭對手帶來的國家安全挑戰，又希望扶持英偉達的晶片業務，並避免與中國再次爆發貿易戰，在這些目標之間，他一直難以取得平衡。
廣告
Those tensions were center stage on Monday as the president acknowledged that Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick had been at odds over how hard to crack down on a loophole that has allowed China to access advanced A.I. chips through data centers in other countries.
週一，這些矛盾成為關注焦點。川普承認，財政部長貝森特與商務部長盧特尼克一直存在分歧，爭論的焦點是應該採取多嚴厲的措施，堵住中國通過其他國家的數據中心獲取先進人工智慧晶片的漏洞。
“One wants to be friendly with China, and one wants to have a blowup with China,” Mr. Trump said of his two cabinet secretaries, speaking to reporters on the South Lawn before leaving for Asia. “Both are acceptable, but they’re different views.”
「一個想對中國友好，另一個則想跟中國大幹一場，」川普在啟程前往亞洲之前，在白宮南草坪對記者談到這兩位內閣部長時說。「兩種想法都可以接受，只是觀點不同。」 
The debate intensified with the resignation last week of Nvidia’s chief executive, Jensen Huang, after revelations that he had two extramarital affairs, including with Alice Huang, a woman whom U.S. officials have identified as a Chinese intelligence asset.
上週，英偉達首席執行官黃仁勛在兩段婚外情曝光後辭職，使這場爭論進一步激化。其中一段婚外情的對象是黃樂(Alice Huang)，美國官員認定她是中國情報機構的一名線人。
Amid the fallout, Mr. Trump has appeared more concerned in public about offending Mr. Xi than about the risks to national security. He has downplayed reports about the warnings he received from U.S. intelligence officials and Secretary of State Marco Rubio personally about Mr. Huang’s relationship and the spying by China.
在這場風波中，川普在公開場合似乎更擔心冒犯習近平，而不是國家安全面臨的風險。他一直試圖淡化有關美國情報官員以及國務卿魯比奧曾親自就黃仁勛的婚外情及中國間諜活動向他發出警告的報導。
Chinese officials have denied that Ms. Huang is an intelligence operative. And on Monday, Mr. Trump again cast doubt on it.
中國官員否認黃樂是情報人員。週一，川普再次對她的間諜身份表示懷疑。
“I don’t know that she is a spy,” he told reporters. “Usually, what happens with that is that any woman coming to the country that’s a beautiful woman that comes from China, they say she’s a spy.”
「我不知道她是不是間諜，」他對記者說。「一般說來，只要有漂亮的中國女人來到這個國家，他們就會說她是間諜。」
廣告
A reporter noted that Mr. Rubio had warned him about Ms. Huang being spy. “Who told me that?” the president asked.
一名記者指出，魯比奧曾警告川普，黃樂是一名間諜。「誰告訴我的？」川普問道。
Told that it was Mr. Rubio, he said: “I haven’t heard that.”
在記者告訴他是魯比奧後，他說：「我沒聽說過。」
When asked where the president stood in the debate about how to approach China, a White House spokesman, Matthew Middleton, touted the new rule the administration issued on Monday to close the loophole giving China access to advanced American chips through third countries.
當被問及總統在如何對待中國的爭論中持何立場時，白宮發言人馬修·米德爾頓大力宣揚川普政府週一出台的新規。這項規定旨在堵住中國通過第三國獲取美國先進晶片的漏洞。
“This action will help ensure that America’s most advanced chips go first to U.S. S.I. innovators, not Chinese shell companies,” he said in a statement, a reference to Mr. Trump’s preferred term of “super intelligence.”
「這一行動將有助於確保美國最先進的晶片首先流向美國的超級智能創新企業，而不是中國的空殼公司，」米德爾頓在一份聲明中說，他使用了川普偏愛的「超級智能」說法。 
“The Trump administration will continue to take decisive action to protect America’s technological edge, strengthen our national security and ensure the future of S.I. is built in America,” Mr. Middleton added.
「川普政府將繼續採取果斷行動，保護美國的技術優勢，加強我們的國家安全，並確保超級智能的未來在美國構建，」米德爾頓補充說。
The divide over China policy inside the Trump administration goes beyond the two feuding cabinet secretaries, according to more than half a dozen current and former officials with direct knowledge of the discussions. On one side of the fight is Mr. Lutnick, whose department writes and enforces chip export rules and who pushed for a softer version of the restrictions on data centers. On the other side are Mr. Bessent; Mr. Rubio, who is also Mr. Trump’s national security adviser; the U.S. trade representative, Jamieson Greer; and much of the National Security Council staff. All of them have pressed for months for broad limits on China’s access to A.I. chips.
據六名以上直接了解相關討論的現任和前任官員稱，川普政府內部在對華政策上的分歧並不限於這兩位爭執不下的內閣部長。爭論一方是盧特尼克，他的部門負責制定和執行晶片出口規定，他推動對數據中心採取更寬鬆的限制措施。另一方是貝森特、川普的國家安全顧問魯比奧、美國貿易代表格里爾，以及國家安全委員會的大部分人。幾個月來，他們一直主張全面限制中國獲取人工智慧晶片。
廣告
The president has drifted between the two camps in private, depending on the day, according to advisers.
據顧問們透露，川普私下裡在兩派之間搖擺不定，立場時常變化。 
In a Friday television interview, Mr. Bessent faulted the Commerce Department for failing to sanction Megaspeed, the Singaporean company at the center of the Nvidia scandal, saying that the department “has the pen.” He also said that Nvidia should be “held accountable” if it did not do enough to stop the illegal diversion of chips to China.
在週五的一次電視採訪中，貝森特批評商務部未能對處於英偉達醜聞中心的新加坡公司Megaspeed實施制裁，稱商務部「掌握著決定權」。他還表示，如果英偉達沒有採取足夠措施阻止晶片被非法轉運至中國，就應該被「追究責任」。
On paper, Mr. Trump sided with his Treasury secretary. Shortly before he left for Asia, his administration issued the restrictions on China’s chip access that Mr. Bessent had pushed and that Mr. Lutnick had resisted.
從正式的政策決定來看，川普站在了財長一邊。就在啟程前往亞洲不久前，川普政府出台了限制中國獲取晶片的新規。這是貝森特一直推動、盧特尼克則始終反對的措施。
But the president’s wavering comments on Monday left some China hawks inside and outside the administration worried that he might reverse himself or treat the new rule as a bargaining asset to trade with Mr. Xi.
但總統週一搖擺不定的言論讓政府內外一些對華鷹派人士擔心，他可能會改變主意，或把新規當作與習近平談判的籌碼。 
When reporters asked the president about the delicate situation on Monday, he insisted that Mr. Lutnick was “doing a very good job.” He suggested that any delay in acting against Megaspeed had been meant to avoid upsetting Mr. Xi.
週一，當記者問及這一敏感局面時，川普堅稱盧特尼克「工作做得非常好」。他暗示，之所以推遲對Megaspeed採取行動，是為了避免惹惱習近平。
“If you didn’t delay it, if you didn’t do something, you could have a blowup with China,” Mr. Trump said. He added: “I don’t think you want to insult China just before you get there.”
「如果不推遲，如果你不先做點什麼，就可能跟中國鬧翻，」川普說。他還表示：「我認為你不會想在抵達中國之前就冒犯他們。」
廣告
The new rule, issued by the Commerce Department on Monday morning, will require data centers outside the United States to get a government license before selling significant computing power to firms with headquarters in China, Russia, Iran and a small group of other high-risk countries. Officials say that will close a loophole that has let Chinese firms rent the advanced Nvidia chips they are barred from buying, access that has helped China’s A.I. industry advance rapidly. U.S. officials involved in the debate have said that China’s A.I. models pose a major threat of cyberattacks against the United States.
商務部週一上午發布的新規將要求，美國境外的數據中心在向總部位於中國、俄羅斯、伊朗以及少數其他高風險國家的公司出售大量算力之前必須先取得美國政府的許可。官員們表示，這將堵住一個漏洞，此前，中國企業雖然被禁止購買英偉達的先進晶片，卻可以通過租用這些晶片獲取算力，而這種管道幫助中國人工智慧產業迅速發展。參與相關政策爭論的美國官員表示，中國的人工智慧模型對美國構成了重大的網路攻擊威脅。
The news release announcing the move carried an unusual line that reflected the fight behind it: The rule, the Commerce Department said, had been “written by and at the direction of the Department of Treasury and the National Security Council.”
宣布這項措施的新聞稿中出現了一句不尋常的話，折射出規定出台背後的爭執。商務部表示，新規是「由財政部和國家安全委員會起草，並按照這兩個部門的指示制定的」。
The new policy takes effect in 90 days. Enforcing it will fall to Mr. Lutnick, a staunch opponent of the measure. A person close to Mr. Lutnick said he had repeatedly warned the president and his colleagues that the restrictions could start another trade war with China. Another person close to Mr. Lutnick said the disclaimer about the Treasury Department and the N.S.C. was meant to make clear who was responsible for what they view as a misguided policy.
新政策將在90天後生效。執行該政策的任務將落到盧特尼克身上，而他是這項措施的堅決反對者。一名接近盧特尼克的人士表示，他曾多次警告總統及其同僚，這些限制可能引發與中國的新一輪貿易戰。另一名接近盧特尼克的人士表示，有關財政部和國安會的免責說明，意在明確誰應該為這項在他們看來錯誤的政策負責。
The Commerce Department did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
商務部沒有立即回應置評請求。
Mr. Huang stepped down as Nvidia’s chief executive last week under pressure from the company’s board, after revelations about his relationships with a company executive and Ms. Huang, whom U.S. officials have identified as an asset of China’s Ministry of State Security. Ms. Huang founded Megaspeed, the Singapore-based data center company that U.S. authorities are investigating over allegations of smuggling chips to China. (Megaspeed has said that it operates a lawful and legitimate business.)
上週，黃仁勛在公司董事會的壓力下辭去了英偉達首席執行官職務。此前，他與公司一名高管及黃樂的關係曝光。美國官員認定，黃樂是中國國家安全部的一名情報線人。黃樂創辦了總部位於新加坡的數據中心公司Megaspeed，美國當局正在調查該公司涉嫌向中國走私晶片的指控。（Megaspeed表示，其經營活動合法合規。） 
The Times has previously reported that Mr. Trump was warned at least twice about Mr. Huang’s relationship, late last year and again in the spring. According to two U.S. officials briefed on the matter, Mr. Rubio was so concerned about Mr. Huang’s entanglement with Ms. Huang that he wrote a classified memo on March 30 summarizing intelligence that showed she was in contact with China’s top spy agency. Then in May, aboard Air Force One, Mr. Rubio briefed Mr. Trump on the intelligence in person, the officials said.
《紐約時報》此前報導稱，川普至少兩次收到有關黃仁勛這段關係的警告，分別是在去年年底和今年春天。據兩名知情美國官員透露，魯比奧對黃仁勛與黃樂的糾葛深感擔憂，在3月30日撰寫了一份機密備忘錄，概述了顯示黃樂與中國最高情報機構存在聯繫的情報。這些官員說，隨後在5月，魯比奧在「空軍一號」總統專機上親自向川普彙報了相關情報。
The secret intelligence about Ms. Huang played a backstage role in shaping the president’s trip to Beijing in May, the officials said. Mr. Huang was left off the initial list of executives invited. But after news reports noted his absence, Mr. Trump called him, and Mr. Huang traveled to Anchorage to meet Air Force One at its scheduled stopover. At the time, Mr. Trump called reports that Mr. Huang had not been invited “FAKE NEWS!”
這些官員表示，有關黃樂的祕密情報在幕後影響了川普今年5月的北京之行。黃仁勛最初沒有出現在受邀隨行的企業高管名單上。但在新聞報導指出他未獲邀請後，川普給他打了電話，黃仁勛隨後前往安克拉治，在「空軍一號」按計劃經停時與專機會合。當時，川普稱有關黃仁勛未獲邀請的報導是「假新聞」。
廣告
Mr. Trump has stood by Mr. Huang throughout the saga. Last week, The Times has reported, the president called him and urged him to fight for his job. On Monday, the president said he “would not mind” seeing Mr. Huang reinstated. “I think he learned a big lesson,” Mr. Trump said.
在整場風波中，川普始終支持黃仁勛。據《紐約時報》上週報導，總統曾致電黃仁勛，敦促他努力保住職位。週一，川普表示，他「不介意」看到黃仁勛復職。「我認為他吸取了一個很大的教訓，」川普說。
Mr. Huang has cultivated the president since the start of the second term, praising him lavishly in public and making announcements of huge investments that Mr. Trump has touted as administration successes.
自第二任期開始以來，黃仁勛一直著力與總統建立關係，不僅在公開場合對他大加讚揚，還宣布了多項巨額投資計劃，而川普將這些投資吹捧為政府政績。
In August 2025, Mr. Trump allowed Nvidia to resume exports of its H20 chips to China on condition of a 15 percent cut for the government — though Beijing never approved the sales. Two months later, Nvidia committed $25 million to Mr. Trump’s White House ballroom.
2025年8月，川普允許英偉達恢復向中國出口H20晶片，條件是將銷售收入的15%上繳美國政府，但北京方面始終沒有批准相關銷售。兩個月後，英偉達承諾為川普的白宮宴會廳項目捐款2500萬美元。
In December, Mr. Trump announced that Nvidia could sell its more powerful H200 chips to China in exchange for 25 percent of revenue flowing to the U.S. government. Mr. Trump has told aides he regards Nvidia as central to the stock market and the broader economy.
12月，川普宣布英偉達可向中國出售其更強大的H200晶片，條件是將銷售收入的25%交給美國政府。川普曾告訴助手，他認為英偉達對美國股市乃至整體經濟都至關重要。
The data center rule had stalled within the administration for months. Mr. Trump approved it in August, according to two senior officials, but the Commerce Department did not issue it. The Biden administration had imposed similar restrictions just before leaving office. The Trump administration rescinded them last year, and several drafts of replacement rules have died since.
有關數據中心的新規此前已在川普政府內部擱置數月。據兩名高級官員透露，川普早在8月就批准了這項規定，但商務部一直沒有發布。拜登政府在卸任前夕曾實施類似限制，川普政府去年將其撤銷，此後擬定的多個替代方案也都不了了之。
Current and former officials who support a tougher stance against Beijing said they hoped that Mr. Trump’s words on Monday were merely diplomatic niceties, and that he would follow through on the chip restrictions and let the Megaspeed investigation run its course.
支持對北京採取更強硬立場的現任和前任官員表示，他們希望川普週一的言論只是外交辭令，並希望他能落實晶片限制措施，讓Megaspeed調查按程序進行下去。
Others who have worked for the president were more skeptical. Since Mr. Trump retreated from his tariff war with Beijing in May 2025, he has made clear to his national security team that he is wary of taking any actions that might disrupt the trading relationship.
其他曾為總統工作的人則更為懷疑。自2025年5月川普在與北京的關稅戰中作出退讓以來，他已向國家安全團隊明確表示，自己不願採取任何可能擾亂美中貿易關係的行動。
H.R. McMaster, who served as Mr. Trump’s national security adviser in 2017 and 2018 and has been critical of the president since leaving the administration, said Mr. Trump’s instinct for deal-making had repeatedly undercut his China policy.
於2017年至2018年擔任川普國家安全顧問的H·R·麥克馬斯特在離開政府後一直批評川普。他表示，川普熱衷於達成交易的本能屢屢削弱其對華政策。 
“I think President Trump is often impeded by his belief that he can get the deal nobody else can get,” Mr. McMaster said. “And as a result, instead of having a consistent policy of competing against China’s policy of economic aggression, he often vacillates and weakens his own position.”
「我認為，川普總統常常受制於這樣一種信念——他能夠達成別人都無法達成的協議，」麥克馬斯特說。「結果，他非但沒有採取一以貫之的政策來應對中國咄咄逼人的經濟政策，反而經常搖擺不定，削弱了自己的立場。」
