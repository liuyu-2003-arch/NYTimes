川普短暫出席東盟峰會，淡化國內外擔憂

President Trump Starts Asia Trip by Shrugging Off U.S. Concerns: ‘It’s Fine’

KATIE ROGERS, DAVID E. SANGER • 2026-11-18
President Trump arrived in Manila on Tuesday, at the tail end of a diplomatic summit in which world leaders had arranged to hold part of the gathering at his hotel. His agenda was supposed to be about inking trade deals and embracing regional partnerships, but several problems from home had followed him overseas.
川普總統週二抵達馬尼拉，當時一場外交峰會已接近尾聲，世界各國領導人此前已安排在他下榻的酒店舉行部分會議。他的行程本應圍繞簽署貿易協議和推進區域夥伴關係展開，但國內的幾個問題卻隨他到了海外。
He was pelted with questions. He seemed to meet each with a shrug.
他面對一連串的提問。他似乎對每一個問題都聳聳肩予以回應。
Did he have any worries about the aviation-system meltdown afflicting travelers across the United States, or a technological outage affecting several large banking systems?
他是否擔憂正在困擾全美旅客的航空系統癱瘓、或是影響多家大型銀行系統的技術故障？
“It’s life,” Mr. Trump said. “When you’re big and powerful and you have all the money, things like that happen. It’s fine.”
「這就是生活，」川普說。「當你強大且富有，擁有大量金錢，就會發生這種事。這沒關係。」
廣告
Mr. Trump is not shy about prizing photo opportunities. But his appearance showcased just how little he seemed to be interested in netting diplomatic wins or easing concerns about American leadership — his leadership — as he made this quick stop at the Association of Southeast Asian Nations.
川普向來不吝於利用拍照機會。但他的露面表明，在東南亞國家聯盟進行短暫逗留時，他似乎既無意爭取外交成果，也無意緩解外界對美國領導力——也就是他本人領導力——的擔憂。
His appearance was on the way to the main event later in the week: a meeting on the edges of the APEC economic summit in Shenzhen, China, on Thursday with Presidents Xi Jinping of China and Vladimir V. Putin of Russia, two authoritarians whom Mr. Trump openly admires.
這次露面是他此行的途中一站，重頭戲則是本週晚些時候：週四在中國深圳舉行的亞太經合組織經濟峰會期間，他將與中國國家主席習近平和俄羅斯總統普丁舉行會晤，這兩位威權領導人都是川普公開推崇的。
Mr. Trump will be meeting Mr. Xi for the third time this year, and there is another likely next month at Mr. Trump’s Doral resort in Miami, where he gets to play the host (and has invited Mr. Putin, ending the Russian leader’s isolation for invading Ukraine). The three-way meeting between China, Russia and the United States would be the first of its kind.
這將是川普今年第三次會見習近平，下個月兩人可能會在川普位於邁阿密的多拉度假村舉行另一次會晤，屆時他是東道主（他還邀請了普丁，結束了這位俄羅斯領導人因入侵烏克蘭而受到的孤立）。這樣的中俄美三方會晤將是史無前例的。
Mr. Trump’s visit to Manila amounted to a layover. When he arrived, he trudged off the plane like all weary long-haul travelers do, and slowly made his way down the stairs of Air Force One. Mr. Trump was then whisked to his hotel, where several world leaders had gathered to bring the diplomatic summit to his doorstep.
川普對馬尼拉的訪問相當於一次中轉。抵達時，他像所有疲憊的長途旅客一樣步履沉重地走出飛機，慢慢地走下「空軍一號」的舷梯。隨後川普被迅速送往他下榻的酒店，幾位世界領導人聚集在那裡，將外交峰會帶到了他的門口。
Alongside members of the so-called Quad — the informal grouping that includes the United States, India, Japan and Australia — his comments were brief, cryptic and nearly mechanical: “Quad is important. We’re working on things that will surprise people.”
在與「四方安全對話」（Quad，包括美國、印度、日本和澳洲在內的非正式機制）成員會面時，他的發言簡短、隱晦且幾乎是機械式的：「Quad很重要。我們正在做一些會讓人感到驚訝的事情。」
On the way to Manila, Mr. Trump floated a meeting with another leader, Kim Jong Un, the North Korean leader and a dictator he has talked warmly about, perhaps in Shenzhen. It isn’t clear why he wants to see Mr. Kim: The three meetings he held in his first term ended in failure, and today the North has more nuclear weapons, and more ways to launch them, than ever before.
前往馬尼拉途中，川普提出可能會與另一位領導人——朝鮮領導人金正恩會面，也許就在深圳。他曾熱情地談論過這位獨裁者。目前尚不清楚他為什麼要見金正恩：他在第一任期內與金正恩舉行的三次會晤均以失敗告終，而朝鮮如今擁有比以往任何時候都多的核武器，以及更多發射核武器的方式。
廣告
If he holds the meeting it would also surprise his staff. Last week, White House officials said that there was no chance of a Trump-Kim meeting. On Tuesday, the White House did not explain whether plans for such a meeting were taking shape.
如果他舉行這次會晤，他的幕僚都會感到驚訝。上週，白宮官員表示川普與金正恩沒有會面的可能。週二，白宮沒有解釋舉行此類會晤的計劃是否正在成型。
During an address to Quad leaders on Tuesday evening, Mr. Trump again focused on personal relationships over policy. He called Prime Minister Narendra Modi of India, a member of the group, “a great leader and a friend of mine, highly respected by everybody all over the world.” The president also praised Mr. Modi as a “very tough man.” The countries are at odds over India’s purchases of Russian oil, and trade talks have dragged on, frustrating both parties.
在週二晚上向四方機制領導人發表講話時，川普再次將重點放在了個人關係而非政策上。他稱該組織成員、印度總理納倫德拉·莫迪是「一位偉大的領導人和我的朋友，受到全世界所有人的高度尊重」。總統還稱讚莫迪是一個「非常強硬的人」。美印兩國因印度購買俄羅斯石油而產生分歧，貿易談判被拖延，這讓雙方都感到沮喪。
Later, the Quad leaders issued a joint statement, affirming the group as “a force for good and a partnership of action, committed to peace, stability, and prosperity” and outlining several plans, including maritime flyovers of the Indo-Pacific, cooperation on combating cyberattacks, and accelerating work on A.I. initiatives.
隨後，四方機制領導人發表了一份聯合聲明，肯定該機制是「一股向善的力量和行動的夥伴關係，致力於和平、穩定與繁榮」，並概述了多項計劃，包括印太地區的海上飛越、在打擊網路攻擊方面的合作以及加速人工智慧倡議的工作。
Before ending his day, Mr. Trump delivered remarks at a dinner of ASEAN leaders, and praised his host, President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr. of the Philippines.
在結束一天的行程之前，川普在東盟領導人晚宴上發表了講話，並讚揚了東道主菲律賓總統小費迪南德·馬科斯。
“I don’t want to date myself, but I knew your parents very well. They were great,” Mr. Trump said. “I believe in genetics. They did a good job. You have very good genetics, I can tell you that.”
「我不想暴露我的年齡，但我非常了解你的父母。他們很偉大，」川普說。「我相信遺傳。他們做得很好。你擁有非常好的基因，我可以告訴你這一點。」
It was not the first time Mr. Trump has praised the family of Mr. Marcos, who is the son of the dictator Ferdinand E. Marcos, who ruled the Philippines for 20 years, imposed martial law, and was ousted in 1986.
這不是川普第一次讚揚馬科斯家族。小馬科斯是獨裁者費迪南德·馬科斯的兒子，老馬科斯曾統治菲律賓20年，實施戒嚴令，並於1986年被推翻。
On Wednesday, Mr. Trump will stay in Manila long enough to have lunch at Malacañang Palace and a bilateral meeting with Mr. Marcos before departing for Shenzhen.
週三，川普將在馬尼拉停留足夠長的時間，在馬拉卡南宮吃午餐並與馬科斯舉行雙邊會晤，然後啟程前往深圳。
廣告
In China, Mr. Trump needs to extend a soon-expiring trade truce that limited tariffs on Chinese goods entering the United States in return for a Chinese commitment not to cut off rare earths and magnets.
在中國，川普需要延長一項即將到期的貿易休戰協議，該協議限制了進入美國的中國商品的關稅，以換取中國不切斷稀土和磁鐵供應的承諾。
The president is also going to focus on the issue of nuclear proliferation in his meeting with Mr. Xi and Mr. Putin, though White House officials do not expect any significant breakthroughs.
總統在與習近平和普丁的會晤中也將聚焦核擴散問題，儘管白宮官員並不期待取得任何重大突破。
Also lingering over Mr. Trump’s trip to Asia is the fallout from the resignation of Jensen Huang, the chief executive of the chip maker Nvidia, after revelations he had engaged in extramarital affairs, including with a woman whom U.S. officials have identified as a Chinese intelligence asset.
黃仁勛在塑造總統對華技術競爭觀點方面一直具有影響力，他認為，讓中國依賴美國製造的半導體晶片和技術有助於維持美國對北京的主導地位。
Mr. Huang has been influential in shaping the president’s views about technological competition with China, and made the argument that keeping China dependent on American-made semiconductor chips and technology could help maintain U.S. dominance over Beijing.
黃仁勛的辭職以及商務部在放緩旨在限制向海外數據中心銷售英偉達晶片的規定方面所採取的做法暴露了川普政府內部由來已久的矛盾。商務部長霍華德·盧特尼克和財政部長史考特·貝森特陪同川普出訪，盧特尼克主張對亞洲晶片出口實施較寬鬆限制，貝森特則在近日公開指責商務部未能限制先進的英偉達技術的銷售。
Mr. Huang’s resignation — and the approach the Commerce Department took to slowing rules that would clamp down on the sale of Nvidia chips to overseas data centers — has exposed a long-burning feud within the Trump administration. Howard Lutnick, the commerce secretary, who has argued for less strict limits on chip exports to Asia, joined Mr. Trump for the trip, as did Scott Bessent, the Treasury secretary, who in recent days publicly blamed the Commerce Department for failing to clamp down on the sale of advanced Nvidia technology.
啟程前往馬尼拉之前，川普似乎並不擔心這樁震動了對人工智慧行業至關重要的企業巨頭的醜聞。當被問及間諜活動時，他通常的反駁是所有人都在這麼做，他似乎並沒有特別感到不安。如果他確實感到不安，他也不會表現出來。
Before leaving for Manila, Mr. Trump did not seem concerned about the scandal that has rocked a corporate juggernaut that is critical to the A.I. industry. His usual retort when asked about spying is that everyone does it, and he did not seem especially perturbed. If he was, he would not show it.
「你知道，我們要去中國，我認為你不會想在到達中國之前就侮辱中國，」川普說。
