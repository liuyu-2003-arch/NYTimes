中加達成貿易協議進一步削減關稅

Carney and Xi Announce Trade Pact Ahead of Asia Summit

IAN AUSTEN • 2026-11-18
Prime Minister Mark Carney of Canada and China’s leader, Xi Jinping, have struck an agreement to reduce some tariffs between their countries, a largely symbolic move that shows relations between the two countries are warming as Canada’s ties to the United States falter.
加拿大總理卡尼和中國領導人習近平達成了一項降低兩國間部分關稅的協議，這在很大程度上是一個象徵性的舉措，表明隨著加拿大與美國的聯繫出現動搖，中加兩國關係正在回暖。
While the expanded trade between China and Canada resulting from the accord in the Chinese tech and financial hub Shenzhen will be modest, involving just $280 million in exports, the pact — as well as the seemingly amicable meeting — illustrates a contrast to Mr. Carney’s relationship with President Trump. Earlier this year, Mr. Carney said that the United States was “at war” with Canada after trade negotiations between the two countries collapsed in acrimony.
這項在中國的科技和金融中心深圳達成的協議將使中加貿易規模有所擴大，但增幅有限，僅涉及2.8億美元的出口。不過，這項協議以及此次顯得頗為友好的會晤與卡尼和川普總統的關係形成了鮮明對比。今年早些時候，美加貿易談判在激烈爭吵中破裂後，卡尼曾表示美國正在與加拿大「交戰」。
Talks are restarting between Canada and the United States but there is no indication that Washington will back away from key irritants including tariffs of up to 50 percent on Canadian autos, steel and aluminum.
加拿大和美國正在重啟談判，但沒有跡象表明華盛頓會在主要摩擦因素上讓步，包括對加拿大汽車、鋼鐵和鋁徵收高達50%的關稅。
Since entering politics last year, Mr. Carney has been traveling the world to diversify Canada’s economic and political ties away from the United States. Those efforts include current talks to make Canada the European Union’s first associate member and Mr. Carney’s second formal meeting this year with Mr. Xi.
自去年進入政界以來，卡尼一直在世界各地周遊，致力於使加拿大的經濟和政治關係多元化，減少對美國的依賴。這些努力包括目前旨在使加拿大成為歐盟首個准成員國的談判，以及卡尼今年與習近平的第二次正式會晤。
廣告
“The stabilization of our bilateral trade arrangement is indicative of how we can work together more broadly, pragmatically, to solve problems,” Mr. Carney said at the start of the meeting with Mr. Xi on Tuesday.
「雙邊貿易安排的穩定表明，我們可以在更廣泛、更務實的層面上合作解決問題，」卡尼在週二與習近平會晤開始時說。
He added that Canada is ahead of schedule with its goal of a 50 percent increase in exports to China by the end of the decade.
他還說，加拿大在實現本十年末對華出口增長50%的目標方面進度超前。
China and Canada were in a trade war after the Canadian government under Justin Trudeau, Canada’s former prime minister, followed the lead of the Biden administration and imposed a 100 percent tariff on Chinese-made electric vehicles. China responded with tariffs on a variety of Canadian products, particularly canola, an oil seed.
加拿大前總理杜魯道領導的加拿大政府效仿拜登政府，對中國製造的電動汽車徵收100%的關稅，此後中加兩國曾陷入貿易戰。作為回應，中國對多種加拿大產品徵收關稅，特別是油料作物油菜籽。
That dispute was largely resolved in January during the first formal meeting between Mr. Xi and Mr. Carney. China lifted its canola tariff after Canada agreed to allow a relatively small number of Chinese E.V.s into the country at a minimal tariff rate.
這一爭端在今年1月習近平和卡尼的首次正式會晤中基本得到解決。在加拿大同意以極低的關稅稅率允許相對少量的中國電動汽車進入該國後，中國取消了對油菜籽的關稅。
Before Tuesday’s meeting, Mr. Xi attempted to put the frosty relations firmly in the past, which were worsened by China’s arrest in 2018 of two Canadians in retaliation for Canada’s detaining a Chinese telecommunications executive under an extradition request from the United States.
在週二的會晤之前，習近平試圖將冰冷的關係徹底留在過去——2018年，中國逮捕了兩名加拿大人，以報復加拿大根據美國的引渡請求拘留一名中國電信高管，這使得兩國關係進一步惡化。
“The development of Chinese-Canadian relations over the years has provided us with an important lesson,” Mr. Xi said at the start of the meeting. “We must respect each other, pursue equality and mutual benefit and seek common ground while setting aside differences.”
 「中加關係這些年發展歷程給我們的重要啟示，就是相互尊重、平等互利、求同存異。中方願同加方一道努力，」習近平在會晤開始時說。
Tuesday’s announcement will largely eliminate tariffs on frozen Canadian pork and several seafoods. Both were caught up in China’s E.V. retaliation. Canada, in turn, will continue to suspend tariffs on some Chinese steel and aluminum products that were set to expire at the end of the year. It will also expand the list of products covered by the suspension.
週二的公告將在基本取消對加拿大冷凍豬肉和若干海產品的關稅。這兩種產品都被捲入了中國對電動汽車的報復行動中。相應地，加拿大將繼續暫停對部分中國鋼鐵和鋁產品的關稅，這些關稅原定於年底到期。加拿大還將擴大暫免關稅的產品清單。
廣告
A Canadian government document said that since March car plants in China had exported 16,211 vehicles to Canada under an annual quota of 49,000. Half of those autos, it said, are priced at under 35,000 Canadian dollars (about $24,500). It said that a second Chinese automaker had been given safety approval by Canada for its cars. That company, which the government will not name, plans to offer only cars that cost less than 35,000 dollars, Canada’s price limit target.
一份加拿大政府文件稱，自3月以來，中國汽車工廠在4.9萬輛的年度配額下向加拿大出口了16211輛汽車。文件稱，其中一半汽車的售價低於3.5萬加元（約合2.45萬美元）。文件還指出，第二家中國汽車製造商的汽車已獲得加拿大安全認證。該公司（政府未透露其名稱）計劃僅銷售價格低於3.5萬加元的汽車，這正是加拿大的價格上限目標。
On Monday in Phnom Penh, Cambodia, Mr. Carney said that Canada would turn to China, Japan and Europe to save its auto industry if it was unable to eliminate Mr. Trump’s tariffs.
週一在柬埔寨金邊，卡尼表示，如果無法取消川普的關稅，加拿大將轉向中國、日本和歐洲來拯救其汽車工業。
The meeting between Mr. Carney and Mr. Xi came ahead of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation forum, which is being held in Shenzhen later this week.
卡尼和習近平的會晤是在亞太經濟合作組織論壇之前舉行的，該論壇將於本週晚些時候在深圳舉行。
Since his address to the World Economic Forum in Davos, Switzerland, in January, Mr. Carney has become something of a champion for nations with advanced economies that are not super powers — a group he calls “middle powers.”
自今年1月在瑞士達佛斯世界經濟論壇發表演講以來，卡尼已成為那些非超級大國的發達經濟體國家的代言人，他將這一群體稱為「中等強國」。
But the main event of the APEC summit will be a meeting between Mr. Xi, Mr. Trump and Vladimir V. Putin of Russia.
但亞太經合組織峰會的主要議程將是習近平、川普和俄羅斯總統普丁之間的會晤。
When asked earlier in his Asia trip about the superpower summit, Mr. Carney repeated Canada’s support for Ukraine following Russia’s invasion.
在此次亞洲之行的早些時候，當被問及這場超級大國峰會時，卡尼重申了加拿大在俄羅斯入侵後對烏克蘭的支持。
廣告
Canadian officials said they do not expect that Mr. Trump and Mr. Carney will meet in Shenzhen. Mr. Carney is also unlikely to meet Mr. Putin. He said over the weekend that he would not boycott a summit group photo including Mr. Putin, but only out of respect for China.
加拿大官員表示，他們預計川普和卡尼不會在深圳會面。卡尼也不太可能與普丁會面。他週末表示，他不會抵制包括普丁在內的峰會合影，但這僅僅是出於對中國的尊重。
