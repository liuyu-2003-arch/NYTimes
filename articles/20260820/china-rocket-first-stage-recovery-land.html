中國首次實現火箭陸地回收

Chinese Start-Up Lands Reusable Rocket for the First Time

ADEEL HASSAN • 2026-08-20
A Chinese aerospace company landed the bottom part of a rocket that went to space for the first time, state media reported on Wednesday, marking an advance for the country in its ability to partly reuse rockets for future missions.
據中國官方媒體週三報導，一家中國航天公司首次成功在陸地回收了一枚升入太空的火箭的一子級，標誌著中國實現火箭部分重複使用的能力又向前邁進了一步。
The success follows the recovery of a different rocket’s booster stage, or first stage, which returned upright to a platform at sea last month. That vehicle was operated by a state-owned corporation, while Tuesday’s breakthrough was achieved by a private start-up, LandSpace.
此次成功緊隨上個月另一枚火箭的一子級成功實現海上平台回收。那枚火箭由一家國有企業運營，而週二的突破則由民營初創企業藍箭航天完成。
Reusing rocket boosters would allow Chinese satellite makers to reduce the cost of getting to orbit, and better compete in the global space market.
火箭一子集的重複使用將使中國衛星製造商降低進入軌道的成本，並在全球航天市場中更具競爭力。
“China is already prepped and primed to scale different space capabilities, like satellite communications, but they just needed reusable launch to really unlock the economics of that,” Kathleen Curlee, a senior research analyst at Georgetown University’s Center for Security and Emerging Technology, said in an interview on Wednesday.
「中國已經為提高各項航天能力做好了準備，比如衛星通信，但他們一直缺少可重複使用的運載火箭，而這正是釋放這些業務經濟效益的關鍵，」喬治城大學安全與新興技術中心高級研究分析師凱瑟琳·柯利週三接受採訪時說。
廣告
“Now that they’ve had multiple instances with two separate entities landing reusable rockets,” she said, “it just gives them even more power and capability to scale these companies that will be competitors for American companies.”
她說：「現在，他們已經由兩家不同的企業實現了可重複使用火箭的回收，這讓他們有更大的力量和更強的能力去擴大這些公司的規模，而這些企業未來將成為美國公司的競爭對手。」
China still lags far behind its American competitors, she said, especially SpaceX, the private space juggernaut founded by Elon Musk. It first achieved a booster landing with its Falcon 9 vehicle in 2015. The Falcon 9 now launches and lands reused boosters on an almost daily basis. A second company, Blue Origin, started by the Amazon founder Jeff Bezos, landed the booster of its New Glenn rocket for the first time last year.
不過，她也指出，中國仍然遠遠落後於美國的競爭對手，尤其是伊隆·馬斯克的私營航天巨頭SpaceX。SpaceX於2015年首次實現「獵鷹9」號火箭一子級的回收。如今，「獵鷹9」幾乎每天都在執行重複使用一子級的發射和回收任務。由亞馬遜創始人傑夫·貝索斯創立的藍色起源也於去年首次成功回收其「新格倫」火箭的一子級。
It is part of China’s core state strategy to pursue dominance in space, Ms. Curlee said, and Wednesday’s launch and booster landing was another step toward that goal.
柯利表示，追求太空主導地位是中國核心國家戰略的一部分，而週三的發射和一子級回收是朝著這一目標邁出的又一步。
In the morning, approximately 2 minutes and 20 seconds after liftoff, the first and second stages of the reusable rocket, Zhuque-3, separated, the Chinese state news agency Xinhua reported.
據中國官方通訊社新華社報導，當天上午，可重複使用火箭朱雀三號在升空約兩分20秒後，一二級成功分離。
The first stage of the rocket touched down “smoothly,” Xinhua said, at the designated recovery site in Gansu Province in northwest China.
新華社稱，火箭一子級在位於中國西北部甘肅省的指定回收點「平穩」著陸。
The second stage continued to fly and deployed a satellite into orbit.
二子級繼續飛行，並將一顆衛星送入軌道。
廣告
LandSpace is one of several Chinese launch companies, Ms. Curlee said.
柯利表示，藍箭航天是中國幾家商業發射公司之一。
The company is trying to achieve profitability, “so they’re on a very aggressive path toward commercializing reusable launch,” she said.
她說，該公司正努力實現盈利，「因此正全力推進可重複使用火箭的商業化」。
Chinese companies have been launching satellites with single-use rockets, letting their parts fall back to Earth or into the ocean. In July, the China Academy of Launch Vehicle Technology, a government entity, recovered the first stage of its Long March 10B rocket using nets on a boat in the South China Sea.
此前，中國公司一直使用一次性火箭發射衛星，部件隨後墜落，返回陸地或海洋。今年7月，政府實體中國運載火箭技術研究院在南中國海通過網系捕獲方式回收了長征十號乙運載火箭的一子級。
China is aiming to match the United States as a space power, with an ambitious program to put astronauts on the moon. It is also building a pair of satellite constellations that it hopes will rival SpaceX’s Starlink satellites in orbit.
中國正力爭成為與美國匹敵的航天強國，並制定了雄心勃勃的載人登月計劃。與此同時，中國還在建設兩個衛星星座，希望讓它們成為能夠與SpaceX的星鏈衛星相抗衡的系統。
The more than 10,000 Starlink vehicles account for about two-thirds of all active satellites in orbit, many of which provide high-speed broadband to remote and underserved areas globally. So far, the two Chinese constellations have only 400 satellites between them.
目前在軌的星鏈衛星超過1萬顆，約佔全球所有在軌運行活躍衛星總數的三分之二。其中許多衛星為全球偏遠地區和服務不足地區提供高速寬帶網路。截至目前，中國這兩個衛星星座合計只有約400顆衛星。
The partly reusable SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket, whose first stage returns to the launchpad upright, has revolutionized the space industry. It allows the company to send satellites into orbit in rapid succession.
部分可重複使用的SpaceX「獵鷹9」號火箭（一子級能夠垂直返回發射場）已經徹底改變了航天產業。它使SpaceX能夠以很短的間隔連續將衛星送入軌道。
廣告
China’s recent booster recovery breakthroughs are putting its space industry on a path to closing the gap.
中國近期在一子級回收方面的突破正推動其航天產業走上縮小這一差距的道路。
“This matters because it makes China’s satellite communications industry much more competitive,” Ms. Curlee said, drawing a comparison to the rise of Huawei, the Chinese tech conglomerate that makes telecommunications equipment and smartphones.
柯利說：「這很重要，因為它使中國的衛星通信產業更具競爭力。」她將此比做中國科技巨頭華為的崛起。華為是一家製造電信設備和智慧型手機的企業集團。
“Huawei was able to scale and provide services all over the world, cheaply and in a robust way,” she said. “Countries and different users were willing to look over the security risks of using a Chinese system for communications,” she said, because it offered a quality, cheaper alternative.
她說：「華為能夠擴大規模並在全球範圍內提供服務，價格低廉且方式穩健。」她還表示，「各國和不同用戶願意忽略使用中國通信系統所帶來的安全風險」，因為它提供了一個質量可靠且更便宜的替代選擇。
Ms. Curlee said that once a Chinese company fields internet satellite constellations, other countries may think of it as an “alternative Starlink.”
柯利說，一旦中國企業建立起互聯網衛星星座，其他國家可能會把它視為「星鏈的替代方案」。
Still, one or two successful launches doesn’t spell success.
不過，一兩次成功的發射並不等於最終成功。
“There still is going to be some time where they have to continue to innovate, continue working on it, and prove that this is a repeatable instance and not just a fluke,” she said.
她說：「他們還需要一段時間繼續創新、繼續努力，並證明這是一種可以重複實現的能力，而不僅僅是偶然的運氣。」

Adeel Hassan是一名常駐紐約的時報記者，報導突發新聞和其他議題。
