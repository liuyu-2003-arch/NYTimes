恆大創始人許家印被判無期徒刑

China Sentences Evergrande Founder to Life in Prison

YAN ZHUANG, 艾莎 • 2026-08-20
A court in China on Thursday sentenced the billionaire founder of Evergrande to life in prison, years after the real estate giant’s collapse brought China’s financial system to the brink.
週四，中國一家法院判處恆大集團億萬富翁創始人無期徒刑。數年前，這家房地產巨頭的崩盤曾將中國金融體系推向崩潰邊緣。
Hui Ka Yan, the company’s founder and former chairman, pleaded guilty in April to several charges, including fund-raising fraud, misuse of funds and securities fraud.
恆大創始人兼前董事長許家印於今年4月對多項指控表示認罪，包括集資詐騙、違法運用資金和欺詐發行證券。
The Shenzhen Intermediate People’s Court found that between 2016 and 2021, Mr. Hui and Evergrande engaged in large-scale financial fraud and committed crimes including misuse of funds, fund-raising fraud and illegally taking public deposits, according to an announcement the court posted on social media.
根據深圳市中級人民法院在社群媒體上發布的通告，法院認定，在2016年至2021年期間，許家印及恆大集團進行了大規模財務造假，並犯有違法運用資金、集資詐騙和非法吸收公眾存款等罪行。
The court ordered Mr. Hui’s assets seized and fined Evergrande Group and its subsidiary, Evergrande Real Estate Group, a combined $2.35 billion. His appearance in court, dressed in a navy blue shirt with a flash of white hair, was the first time Mr. Hui had been seen since he disappeared from public view in 2023.
法院下令沒收許家印的資產，並對恆大集團及其子公司恆大地產處以總計158.2億元的罰款。許家印身穿藏藍色上衣出現在法庭上，滿頭白髮，這是他自2023年從公眾視野消失以來首次公開露面。
廣告
Evergrande was once China’s fastest-growing property developer, but its collapse in 2021 under more than $300 billion of debt pushed China’s financial system to the edge and exposed the vulnerabilities of the country’s economy — especially its dependency on real estate as a driver of growth.
恆大曾是中國增長最快的房地產開發商，但其在2021年因超過2萬億元的債務破產，將中國金融體系推向邊緣，並暴露了中國經濟的脆弱性，尤其是對房地產作為經濟增長引擎的依賴。
Few companies played a bigger role in China’s staggering real estate boom. Evergrande’s crash helped usher in a prolonged housing crisis that is still being felt across the economy.
在中國房地產市場驚人的繁榮時期，很少有公司能像恆大那樣扮演如此重要的角色。它的崩盤加劇了持續至今的房地產危機，其影響仍然波及整個經濟。
The court found that Mr. Hui bore responsibility for Evergrande Group’s operations and should be punished severely because his actions and those of the company “seriously disrupted the socialist market economic order,” according to its social media post.
根據法院發布的社群媒體通告，法院認定許家印對恆大集團的運營負有責任，應當予以嚴懲，因為他及該公司的行為「嚴重破壞社會主義市場經濟秩序」。
“The amount involved in the crime is exceptionally large, the circumstances are particularly heinous, exceptionally heavy economic losses have been caused, and the harm to society is extremely grave,” the court said.
法院表示：「犯罪數額特別巨大，情節特別惡劣，造成特別重大經濟損失，社會危害特別嚴重。」
Mr. Hui founded Evergrande in 1996 in the southern city of Guangzhou, just as China was beginning to urbanize and move people from the countryside into the cities. The company’s success turned Mr. Hui into one of the world’s richest people and he expanded his empire to include a soccer team and an electric vehicle company.
1996年，許家印在南方城市廣州創立了恆大集團，當時中國正開始城市化進程，大量人口從農村湧入城市。公司的成功使許家印成為全球最富有的人之一，隨後他將商業帝國拓展至足球隊和電動汽車公司等領域。
But by 2020, Beijing was growing worried that its property boom — the biggest the world has ever seen — was threatening the financial system because property developers like Evergrande had borrowed so heavily from local governments and banks. To cool the market, officials began restricting how much developers could borrow, cutting debt-burdened companies like Evergrande off from the cash they needed to fund their operations.
但到了2020年，北京日益擔心這場世界上規模最大的房地產繁榮正威脅到金融體系，因為像恆大這樣的房企向地方政府和銀行大量借債。為給市場降溫，官方開始限制開發商的借貸額度，切斷了像恆大這樣負債累累的公司維持日常運營所需的資金來源。
When Evergrande collapsed in 2021, much of the blame fell on Beijing and its abrupt crackdown. Home buyers protested in the streets in cities across China. But the company had been borrowing beyond its means for years and had engaged in questionable accounting.
2021年恆大倒閉時，很多人將其歸咎於北京及其突如其來的嚴厲整頓。中國多座城市的購房者走上街頭抗議。然而，該公司多年來一直在進行超出自身償付能力的借貸，並存在可疑的會計操作。
廣告
As its cash crunch deepened, the company even turned to its own employees, pressuring them to put up money for short-term loans. Several months after Evergrande defaulted, the company announced that banks had seized $2 billion.
隨著資金鏈危機加深，公司甚至向自家員工施壓，迫使他們出資作短期借貸。違約幾個月後，恆大宣布銀行已扣押其20億美元資金。
An internal investigation later found that executives had engineered a plan in late 2020 to circumvent borrowing restrictions by arranging for third parties to take out loans using Evergrande subsidiaries as collateral. Such practices would lead to the company’s decline and, ultimately, the arrest of Mr. Hui and other executives.
隨後的一項內部調查發現，高管們曾在2020年底精心策划了一項方案，通過安排第三方以恆大子公司為抵押申請貸款，以此規避借貸限制。正是此類做法導致了公司的衰敗，並最終導致許家印及其他高管被捕。
A Hong Kong court ordered Evergrande into liquidation in 2024. On Thursday, two courts in Shenzhen also sentenced 56 other people, including Mr. Hui’s two sons, to terms ranging from 22 months to 18 years.
2024年，香港一家法院下令對恆大進行清盤。週四，深圳的兩家法院還對包括許家印兩個兒子在內的另外56人作出了判決，刑期從22個月到18年不等。
Evergrande’s rise was part of a property boom that enriched a system of local governments that leased land to developers, banks that lent them money, and the Wall Street firms that collected fees as dozens of Chinese real estate companies went public.
恆大的崛起是房地產繁榮的一部分，這一繁榮讓向開發商出讓土地的地方政府、為開發商提供貸款的銀行，以及在數十家中國房地產企業上市過程中賺取手續費的華爾街公司都賺得盆滿缽滿。
Its collapse exposed the system’s excess and vulnerabilities. It set off a cascade of defaults and helped trigger a property downturn that has weighed on the country’s economy for years, eroding a major source of wealth for the country’s middle class.
恆大的崩潰暴露了系統的無度與脆弱。它引發了一連串的違約潮，並助推了拖累中國經濟多年的房地產衰退，侵蝕了該國中產階級的主要財富來源。
For millions of Chinese families, apartments had been more than places to live. They were seemingly safe investments and a vehicle for building wealth — a bet that unraveled as prices tumbled and developers failed.
對數百萬中國家庭而言，住房曾不僅僅是安居之所。它們曾是看似安全的投資，也是積累財富的工具。然而，隨著房價暴跌和開發商紛紛倒閉，這場賭局以崩盤收場。
