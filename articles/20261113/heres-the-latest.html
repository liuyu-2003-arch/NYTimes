黃仁勛辭任英偉達CEO

Here’s the latest.

TRIPP MICKLE, MAGGIE HABERMAN • 2026-11-13
Jensen Huang, the co-founder and chief executive of the Silicon Valley chip giant Nvidia, resigned on Thursday, according to the company’s board of directors, capping a swift, stunning fall of one of the most powerful figures in the A.I. boom. His departure came after revelations that he had extramarital affairs with an executive who reported to him and with a woman whom U.S. intelligence agencies concluded was spying for China.
據英偉達董事會消息，這家矽谷晶片巨頭的聯合創始人兼首席執行官黃仁勛於週四辭職。黃仁勛是人工智慧熱潮中最具影響力的人物之一，這一消息為其在英偉達的職業生涯畫上了驟然且令人震驚的句號。此前他被披露與一名向其彙報工作的公司高管以及一名被美國情報機構認定為中國間諜的女性有婚外情。
President Trump, who had defended Mr. Huang over the past week, praised him for doing a “fantastic job” atop the Nvidia, which entered a moment of uncertainty without the celebrity C.E.O. whose departure immediately reverberated through the global economy.
美國總統川普過去一週以來一直為黃仁勛辯護，他稱讚黃仁勛在執掌英偉達期間「幹得非常出色」。隨著這位明星首席執行官的離任，英偉達步入了一個充滿不確定性的時期，他的離任也立即在全球經濟中引發震動。
Colette Kress, Nvidia’s chief financial officer, will serve as interim chief executive while the search for a permanent replacement is carried out, Nvidia’s 10-member board said in a statement.
英偉達由10名成員組成的董事會在一份聲明中表示，在尋找永久繼任者期間，公司首席財務官科萊特·克雷斯將擔任臨時首席執行官。
Nvidia controls an estimated 90 percent of the market for the advanced chips that are an essential component of artificial intelligence projects. Mr. Huang’s exit, which followed a lengthy board meeting on Wednesday, will have widespread ripple effects. Shares of Nvidia, one of the world’s most valuable public companies, plunged at the start of trading, weighing on the broader market.
英偉達控制著人工智慧項目核心組件——高端晶片約90%的市場份額。黃仁勛在週三一場漫長的董事會會議後決定離職，此舉將產生廣泛的連鎖反應。作為全球市值最高的上市公司之一，英偉達股價在開盤時大幅下跌，拖累了整個大盤走勢。
廣告
Nvidia, long a maker of graphics chips, became a linchpin of A.I. development in recent years, which put it at the center of the U.S.-China tussle for dominance in the sector.
長期以來以製造圖形處理晶片聞名的英偉達近年來已成為人工智慧發展的中流砥柱，這也使其處於中美兩國爭奪該領域主導權的漩渦中心。
A series of reports by The New York Times showed that Mr. Huang, 63, had an affair with a Chinese businesswoman who goes by Alice Huang, who had been recruited by China’s main spy agency to gather information on Mr. Huang. The Times also reported that Mr. Trump had been warned about the relationship but took no action.
《紐約時報》的一系列報導顯示，現年63歲的黃仁勛曾與一位名叫黃樂(Alice Huang)的中國女商人有染，該女子已被中國主要情報機構招募，負責收集有關黃仁勛的情報。《紐約時報》還報導稱，川普此前曾收到過關於這段關係的警告，但並未採取任何行動。
U.S. officials suspect that Megaspeed, the Singaporean company where Ms. Huang was a top executive, has helped smuggle restricted chips into China. Ms. Huang has not responded to requests for comment, and China has rejected the espionage claims and called the smuggling allegations “smears.”
美國官員懷疑，黃樂擔任高管的新加坡公司Megaspeed曾協助向中國走私受限制的晶片。黃樂未回應置評請求，而中國否認了間諜指控，並稱走私晶片的指責是「抹黑」。
Nvidia’s board, which has had a long track record of deference to its chief executive, did not mention Mr. Huang’s conduct in its statement. It called his contributions to Nvidia “profound” and thanked him for his “immeasurable contributions to the company and the industry.”
長期以來對首席執行官頗為順從的英偉達董事會在聲明中並未提及黃仁勛的行為。董事會稱他對英偉達的貢獻「影響深遠」，並感謝他為「公司和整個行業作出的不可估量的貢獻」。
While the board begins its search for the company’s next leader, Mr. Trump had his own idea: Tim Cook, the former chief executive of Apple. “A Legend replaces a Legend!” he wrote on social media.
在董事會開始物色公司下一任掌門人之際，川普提出了自己的想法：蘋果公司前首席執行官蒂姆·庫克。他在社群媒體上寫道：「一位傳奇接替另一位傳奇！」
