接替黃仁勛的七名潛在人選

7 People Nvidia Could Consider for Its Next C.E.O.

DON CLARK • 2026-11-13
Finding a successor to Jensen Huang at Nvidia is being compared to replacing Steve Jobs at Apple. In many ways, it’s actually harder.
為英偉達尋找黃仁勛的繼任者正被拿來和在蘋果接替史提夫·賈伯斯相提並論。而在很多方面，前者實際上更難。
Mr. Huang, 63, has much deeper technical expertise than the late Mr. Jobs did, with a keen understanding of the field of artificial intelligence and what chips and other technologies are needed to support it.
63歲的黃仁勛比已故的賈伯斯擁有更深厚的專業技術知識，他對人工智慧領域有著敏銳的洞察力，並深諳支持該領域所需的晶片及其他技術。
Unlike Mr. Jobs, Mr. Huang also forged close friendships throughout the industry, creating a complex network of partners, customers and suppliers. He turned Nvidia into a major player in Washington, built on a close relationship with President Trump and technology vital to U.S. national security.
與賈伯斯不同，黃仁勛還在整個行業建立了密切友誼，編織出一個由合作夥伴、客戶和供應商組成的複雜網路。憑藉與川普總統的密切關係，以及對美國國家安全至關重要的技術，他把英偉達打造成華盛頓的重要力量。
And Mr. Huang shaped a culture at Nvidia that drove rapid-fire innovation while attracting some of the most accomplished engineers.
而且，黃仁勛在英偉達塑造了一種文化，既推動快速創新，又吸引了一些最有成就的工程師。
廣告
“They have to get someone who is an inspirational leader, that people love,” said Jeff Christian, chief executive of Christian & Timbers, an executive recruiting firm that frequently works with Silicon Valley companies. “This person has to be a great talent engineer, and a great talent magnet.”
「他們必須找到一個能鼓舞人心、讓人們愛戴的領導者，」高管獵頭公司Christian & Timbers首席執行官傑夫·克里斯蒂安說。該公司經常與矽谷公司合作。「這個人必須是一位傑出的技術工程師，同時也是吸引人才的磁石。」
Any candidate would also need an impeccable record for integrity and honesty, Mr. Christian and others noted.
克里斯蒂安等人指出，任何候選人都必須在誠信和正直方面擁有無可挑剔的記錄。
The other issue facing the search effort is that few candidates could be expected to maintain Mr. Huang’s ultra-flat structure at Nvidia, where an estimated 60 people were reporting directly to him. Anything more than 12 is problematic, said Craig Stevens, a managing partner at the search firm Boyden. “Things have to change structurally immediately,” he said.
此次人才搜尋工作面臨的另一個問題是，預計很少有候選人能夠維持黃仁勛在英偉達推行的超扁平化組織結構。據估計，有60人直接向黃仁勛彙報。獵頭公司博伊登的管理合伙人克雷格·史蒂芬斯說，直接彙報人數超過12人，就會出現問題。「結構必須立即改變，」他說。
Which candidates could fit the bill? Here are some who have been suggested by headhunters, analysts and others.
哪些候選人可能符合條件？以下是獵頭、分析師及其他人士提出的一些人選。
Colette Kress
科萊特·克雷斯
Ms. Kress, Nvidia’s chief financial officer, was appointed acting chief executive on Thursday while a search for a permanent successor is completed. She has a handle on the company’s operations and strong ties to Wall Street, leading each conference call after Nvidia reports earnings. But Ms. Kress, 59, lacks the deep technical expertise needed for a company that has been driving the evolution of A.I.
克雷斯是英偉達首席財務官，週四被任命為臨時首席執行官，同時公司將繼續尋找正式繼任者。她熟悉公司運營，並與華爾街關係牢固，每當英偉達公布財報後，她都會主持電話會議。但59歲的克雷斯缺乏一家推動人工智慧演進的公司所需的深厚技術專長。
Ian Buck
伊恩·巴克
Mr. Buck, an Nvidia vice president, gets much of the credit for the underlying technology that allowed the company’s chips to move from video game graphics to A.I. He invented the CUDA software foundation that underlies many A.I. applications and now handles relations with big companies running thousands of Nvidia chips in data centers. Though Mr. Buck lacks broad management experience, he has testified in Congress and developed other contacts in Washington.
作為英偉達副總裁，巴克在推動該公司晶片技術從電玩遊戲圖形領域向人工智慧領域轉型方面功不可沒。他發明了支撐許多人工智慧應用的CUDA軟體框架。目前他負責與在數據中心運行數以千計英偉達晶片的大型企業保持聯繫。儘管巴克缺乏廣泛管理經驗，但他曾在國會作證，並在華盛頓建立了其他聯繫。
Tim Cook
蒂姆·庫克
Tim Cook, who recently left the chief executive role at Apple, was immediately suggested by Mr. Trump as the obvious successor for Mr. Huang. But Mr. Cook hasn’t been deeply engaged in the A.I. revolution. And at 66, he is older than most people appointed to lead major public companies. Mr. Stevens suggested that Mr. Cook, if selected, might serve in a longer-term interim role after Ms. Kress.
最近卸任蘋果首席執行官的蒂姆·庫克，被川普立即點名為接替黃仁勛的不二人選。但庫克並未深度參與人工智慧革命。而且他現年66歲，比大多數被任命領導大型上市公司的候選人都要年長。史蒂芬斯認為，如果庫克被選中，他可能在克雷斯之後擔任較長期的過渡角色。
Johny Srouji
約翰尼·斯魯吉
Mr. Srouji, Apple’s hardware chief, has deep semiconductor expertise. The Israeli engineer has helped keep Apple on the leading edge of chip design, incorporating A.I. features into its custom-built silicon. But he has not developed much of a public presence, besides speaking at Apple events. And Apple’s chips have mainly been targeted at personal computers, tablets and smartphones, not the data centers where most A.I. transactions are carried out.
作為蘋果公司的硬件主管，斯魯吉擁有深厚的半導體專業知識。這位以色列工程師幫助蘋果在晶片設計領域保持領先地位，將人工智慧功能融入其定製晶片。但除了在蘋果活動上發言外，他鮮少在公眾場合露面。而且蘋果晶片主要面向個人電腦、平板電腦和智慧型手機，而非大多數人工智慧交易所在的數據中心。
Rene Haas
雷內·哈斯
Mr. Haas, 64, the chief executive of the chip technology licensing company Arm Holdings, has a deep familiarity with Nvidia. He worked there from 2006 to 2013. He also engaged in many interactions with Mr. Huang and others at the Silicon Valley company when Arm was preparing to be acquired by Nvidia in 2020, a deal that collapsed in 2022 because of government opposition.
64歲的哈斯是晶片技術授權公司安謀控股首席執行官，對英偉達非常熟悉。他於2006年至2013年在那裡工作。2020年，安謀準備被英偉達收購時，他還曾與黃仁勛以及這家矽谷公司的其他高管進行過多次接觸。由於政府反對，這筆交易最終於2022年告吹。
Forrest Norrod
福雷斯特·諾羅德
廣告
An executive vice president at the Nvidia rival Advanced Micro Devices, Mr. Norrod is highly regarded in the industry and seems to possess many leadership attributes that Nvidia needs, Mr. Christian said. Mr. Norrod oversees AMD’s business in selling chips for A.I. data centers, where his company has emerged as a solid No. 2 to Nvidia.
諾羅德是英偉達競爭對手超威半導體的執行副總裁，在業內備受推崇，似乎具備英偉達所需的許多領導特質，克里斯蒂安說。諾羅德負責超威銷售人工智慧數據中心晶片的業務，在該領域，其公司已穩居英偉達之後的第二位。
Charlie Kawwas
查理·考瓦斯
Mr. Kawwas, the president of Broadcom’s semiconductor business, is another well-regarded executive in Silicon Valley with deep experience in A.I. Broadcom has emerged as a key player in helping companies like Google develop their own chips in addition to buying from Nvidia.
考瓦斯是博通半導體的業務總裁，也是矽谷備受推崇、在人工智慧領域經驗豐富的高管。博通已成為關鍵參與者，幫助谷歌等公司在從英偉達購買晶片之外，開發自己的晶片。
