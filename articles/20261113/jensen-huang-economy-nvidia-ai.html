黃仁勛下台會成為人工智慧泡沫破裂的開端嗎

Jensen Huang’s Downfall Costs the A.I. Boom a Top Backer

BEN CASSELMAN • 2026-11-13
As investors have pumped trillions of dollars into artificial intelligence companies, chipmakers and data center developers, the economy has come to rely on the industry as its primary — some would argue only — reliable driver of growth.
隨著投資者向人工智慧公司、晶片製造商和數據中心開發商投入數萬億美元，人工智慧行業已成為美國經濟增長最主要的可靠動力，甚至有人認為是唯一的可靠動力。 
No company has been more central to that growth than Nvidia, whose chips fill the data centers that power A.I. and whose money helps to finance them. And, at least until this week, it was impossible to imagine Nvidia without Jensen Huang, its high-flying, leather-clad co-founder and chief executive.
在這一增長過程中，沒有哪家企業比英偉達更為核心。為人工智慧提供算力的數據中心裡布滿了英偉達的晶片，英偉達的資金也為這些數據中心的建設提供了支持。而且，至少在本週之前，人們很難想像英偉達可以沒有黃仁勛：這位身穿皮夾克、風頭正勁的聯合創始人兼首席執行官。
All of which means that Mr. Huang’s resignation on Thursday, following a made-for-Hollywood series of scandals, matters not just for the company and its investors, but for virtually every American.
所有這些意味著，在一連串堪比好萊塢劇情的醜聞之後，黃仁勛週四辭職所產生的影響不僅關係到英偉達及其投資者，而且也與幾乎每一個美國人息息相關。
Or, as Aaron Klein, an expert in financial regulation at the Brookings Institution, put it: “Can one C.E.O.’s sex life take down the economy?”
正如布魯金斯學會金融監管專家亞倫·克萊因所說：「一位首席執行官的性生活能把整個經濟拖垮嗎？」
廣告
The answer, in a literal sense, is almost certainly no. No one believes that Mr. Huang’s resignation will, by itself, bring the A.I. boom to an end, let alone plunge the economy into a recession. Nvidia’s stock price tumbled on Thursday, but the broader market barely budged.
從字面意義上說，答案幾乎肯定是否定的。沒人認為黃仁勛的辭職本身就會終結人工智慧熱潮，更不用說讓經濟陷入衰退了。英偉達股價週四大跌，但大盤幾乎沒有波動。
But Mr. Klein’s quip spoke to the unique role that Mr. Huang played at Nvidia, and that his company has played in the A.I. build out.
不過，克萊因這句半開玩笑的話道出了黃仁勛在英偉達、英偉達在人工智慧基礎設施建設中所扮演的獨特角色。
Nvidia controls about 90 percent of the market for the most advanced chips used to train and run A.I. models. The turmoil at the company could create an opening for competitors such as AMD and Intel to encroach on that market share. But there is little reason to think that Mr. Huang’s resignation will threaten Nvidia’s ability to develop and produce the chips it has promised its customers.
英偉達在用於訓練和運行人工智慧模型的最先進晶片市場上佔據約90%的份額。公司的動盪可能為AMD和英特爾等競爭對手創造機會，蠶食其市場份額。但幾乎沒有理由認為，黃仁勛辭職會危及英偉達研發和生產已向客戶承諾交付的晶片的能力。
But Nvidia isn’t just the dominant chipmaker. It is also the “central bank of A.I.,” helping to finance the industry’s growth through a series of complex deals with its customers and suppliers.
然而，英偉達不僅是佔據主導地位的晶片製造商，它還是「人工智慧產業的中央銀行」，通過與客戶和供應商之間一系列複雜的交易安排為整個行業的發展提供融資支持。
When OpenAI signed a lease for a 10-gigawatt data center in Ohio in August, it was Nvidia that provided $105 billion in loan guarantees. Days later, the company teamed up with several Wall Street firms to create a $500 billion fund to help finance A.I.-related infrastructure. Nvidia owns a more than 10 percent stake in CoreWeave, a big data center operator, and has guaranteed billions of dollars in its loans.
今年8月，OpenAI簽約租用俄亥俄州一座規模達10吉瓦的數據中心時，英偉達提供了1050億美元的貸款擔保。幾天後，英偉達又與多家華爾街公司合作，設立一隻規模達5000億美元的基金，為人工智慧相關基礎設施提供融資。英偉達還持有大型數據中心運營商CoreWeave超過10%的股份，並為其數十億美元的貸款提供了擔保。
Its loan commitments and financing arrangements would make Nvidia the 10th largest bank in the country, according to an estimate from Ryan Cummings, the chief of staff for the Stanford Institute for Economic Policy Research.
根據史丹佛經濟政策研究所主管萊恩·卡明斯的估計，英偉達的貸款承諾和融資安排將使其成為美國第十大銀行。 
“Nvidia is at the center of this circular financing, and obviously at the center of Nvidia is Jensen Huang,” Mr. Cummings said. “He has been the driving force, pretty inarguably, behind a lot of the A.I. build-out.”
「英偉達處於這種循環融資的中心，顯然英偉達的中心是黃仁勛，」卡明斯說。「無可爭辯，他一直是大量人工智慧建設背後的驅動力。」
廣告
Without Mr. Huang in charge, Nvidia’s role could change, Mr. Cummings said. And while it is possible that other companies will step in to fill the gap, that isn’t guaranteed.
卡明斯表示，黃仁勛不再掌舵後，英偉達所扮演的角色可能發生變化。其他企業或許會填補它留下的空缺，但這並非板上釘釘。
Mr. Huang’s departure comes at a delicate time for the A.I. industry. There have been signs in recent months that investors are growing more skeptical that companies will deliver returns that justify the huge investments they are making in A.I., or at least that those returns could take longer than expected to materialize.
黃仁勛離職之際，人工智慧產業正處於一個敏感時期。近幾個月來，已有跡象表明，投資者越來越懷疑，企業能否從人工智慧領域的巨額投入中獲得足以證明這些投資合理的回報，即便最終能夠獲得這樣的回報，所需的時間也可能比預期更長。
And Democrats’ sweeping victories in last week’s midterm elections could lead to new restrictions on data center construction, which could jeopardize the industry’s boom.
而民主黨在上週期中選舉中的大勝可能導致對數據中心建設的新限制，這可能危及該行業的繁榮。 
“At a point when people are already on edge about this trade, it’s another piece of uncertainty,” Mr. Cummings said.
「在人們已經對這波投資行情感到緊張不安的時候，這又增添了一層不確定性，」卡明斯說。
In the past, when confidence in the A.I. story has wavered, Mr. Huang and Nvidia have been the ones to calm doubts, reporting huge sales figures or stepping in to provide financing to data center projects whose future seemed in jeopardy. This time, it isn’t clear who will fill the void.
過去，每當市場對人工智慧的發展前景產生疑慮，總是黃仁勛和英偉達出面打消這些疑慮：要麼公布亮眼的銷售業績，要麼為前景堪憂的數據中心項目提供融資支持。而這一次，誰能填補他們留下的空缺尚不得而知。
“Could the single largest A.I. company C.E.O.’s impropriety be the start of the pop of the bubble?” Mr. Klein asked. “Yes, as crazy as that sounds. Particularly when one company is so central.”
「全球最大人工智慧公司的首席執行官行為失當會不會成為泡沫破裂的導火線？」克萊因問。「有可能。聽起來再瘋狂不過，但當一家公司處於如此核心的地位時，確實可能發生。」
廣告
Bubbles are inherently unpredictable: It isn’t clear, ahead of time, what will flip the psychological switch that causes them to burst. The muted market reaction of financial markets to the news on Thursday suggests that investors aren’t losing faith in the A.I. trade.
泡沫本身就難以預測：事先誰也無法確定，究竟什麼會觸動投資者心理上的開關，導致泡沫破裂。金融市場對週四消息的溫和反應表明，投資者並未對人工智慧交易失去信心。
But Mr. Klein said the Nvidia mess should be a warning that the complex web of A.I. financing arrangements is threatening financial stability.
但克萊因表示，英偉達的這場風波應該敲響警鐘：人工智慧產業錯綜複雜的融資關係正在威脅金融穩定。
“It looks like the financial regulators are sleeping through the alarm clock,” he said.
「金融監管機構似乎連鬧鐘響了都還在睡覺，」他說。
If Nvidia’s new leadership takes a more cautious approach to financing, that could have implications for the broader economy. The data center boom has helped to prop up the construction and manufacturing industries, at a time when tariffs, high interest rates and uncertainty have limited demand from other sectors.
如果英偉達的新管理層在融資方面採取更加謹慎的態度，可能會對整體經濟產生影響。在關稅、高利率和不確定性抑制其他行業需求之際，數據中心建設熱潮一直在支撐建築業和製造業的發展。
Still, the breakneck pace of the A.I. build-out has also pushed up inflation and raised borrowing costs. Many economists argue that a modest slowdown would be healthy.
不過，人工智慧基礎設施建設的狂飆突進也推高了通貨膨脹和借貸成本。許多經濟學家認為，適度放緩反而有益於經濟健康。
The more immediate risk, economists say, is that a loss of confidence in the A.I. story leads to a steep drop in the stock market. The strength of the market has helped prop up consumer spending even as wage growth has cooled.
經濟學家指出，更迫在眉睫的風險是，投資者對人工智慧前景失去信心，導致股市大幅下跌。儘管工資增長已經放緩，股市的強勁幫助支撐了消費者支出。 
廣告
If the A.I. trade unravels because of an abrupt curtailment in investment, “that is more recessionary,” said Pooja Sriram, an economist at Barclays. “That’s when there is a more material shakeout in the stock market that transcends to how consumers feel. Balance sheets are hit and that could escalate very quickly into a downturn.”
巴克萊經濟學家普賈·斯裡拉姆說，如果人工智慧交易因投資突然大幅縮減而瓦解，「那將更容易引發經濟衰退」。「屆時，股市將出現更為嚴重的震盪，並進一步影響消費者信心。人們的資產負債狀況受到衝擊，局面可能迅速惡化，最終演變成經濟衰退。」
