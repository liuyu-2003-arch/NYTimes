黃仁勛醜聞引發人工智慧「玩梗」熱潮

Nvidia C.E.O. Scandal Sets Off a Blitz of A.I. Memes

ZANE IRWIN • 2026-11-13
Jensen Huang and his leather-clad likeness had become synonymous with the technological giant he co-founded, Nvidia. They were so entwined that, this year, the company sold $178 sweaters with a pixel-art version of Mr. Huang on the front.
黃仁勛和他那身標誌性的皮夾克裝扮早已成為他聯合創立的科技巨頭英偉達的代名詞。兩者之間的聯繫如此緊密，以至於今年英偉達還推出了售價178美元的毛衣，胸前印著像素藝術風格的黃仁勛肖像。
Now, in the wake of Mr. Huang’s resignation on Thursday after back-to-back cheating scandals, including an extramarital affair with a woman the U.S. government believes is a Chinese spy, the internet has taken ahold of his image to unleash a flood of memes.
現在，在接連爆出出軌醜聞後，黃仁勛於週四辭職。其中一起婚外情的對象是一名被美國政府認為是中國間諜的女子。網友拿他的形象大做文章，製造出鋪天蓋地的迷因。
In many cases, they are being made by A.I. enthusiasts who revered Mr. Huang.
這些迷因中有不少出自曾經崇拜黃仁勛的人工智慧愛好者之手。
Images of Mr. Huang have appeared in online parodies of music videos, Bollywood movies, American sitcoms and kiss-cams at Coldplay concerts — many generated using artificial intelligence, the technology that became widely available through the chips Nvidia produces.
黃仁勛的形象出現在各種網路惡搞作品中，從MV、寶萊塢電影、美國情景喜劇到酷玩樂隊演唱會上的「親吻抓拍」(kiss-cam)，不一而足。其中許多作品都是利用人工智慧生成的，而正是英偉達生產的晶片讓這項技術得以廣泛普及。
廣告
The revelations about Mr. Huang, 63, one of the most powerful figures in the A.I. boom, are part of a long list of infidelity scandals that became memes.
63歲的黃仁勛是人工智慧熱潮中最有權勢的人物之一。如今，他的婚外情醜聞也成為又一起被網友製成迷因廣泛傳播的名人出軌事件。
Last year, the married chief executive of a tech start-up was caught on a kiss-cam at a Coldplay concert embracing the married but separated head of his company’s human resources department.
去年，一家科技初創公司的已婚首席執行官在酷玩樂隊的演唱會上被現場鏡頭拍到正擁抱公司的人力資源部門主管。後者雖然也已婚，但當時已與配偶分居。
Mr. Huang’s image was superimposed on that viral moment by Peter Dedene, a Belgian software engineer. He said in a phone interview on Thursday that he was up late this week on his phone, scrolling the headlines about Nvidia.
比利時軟體工程師彼得·德德內把黃仁勛的形象移植到了這段廣為流傳的影片中。他週四在電話採訪中說，本週他熬夜看手機，刷關於英偉達的新聞。
Without leaving his bed, Mr. Dedene, 43, used an open-source generative A.I. tool to substitute the couple from the Coldplay concert with images of Mr. Huang and Alice Huang, the Chinese businesswoman with whom Mr. Huang has admitted having an affair.
43歲的德德內躺在床上，利用一款開源生成式人工智慧工具，把酷玩樂隊演唱會影片中的男女主角替換成了黃仁勛和黃樂(Alice Huang)。黃樂是那名中國女商人，黃仁勛已承認與她有過婚外情。
“I didn’t think a lot of it,” Mr. Dedene said of his meme.
談到自己製作的這個迷因，德德內說：「我當時沒太當回事。」
When he woke up on Sunday morning, he said, the post had over a million impressions. As of Thursday afternoon, it had nearly seven million.
他說，週日早上醒來時，這條帖文的曝光量已經超過100萬次。截至週四下午，曝光量已接近700萬次。
廣告
“I really looked up to this guy,” Mr. Dedene said of Mr. Huang, adding, “It’s sort of hilarious that, in the end, the technology he cocreated is now enabling these kinds of jokes.”
「我以前真的非常崇拜這個人，」德德內談到黃仁勛時說。「到頭來，他參與創造的技術被用來開他的玩笑，挺諷刺的。」 
Social media users have used A.I. to place Mr. Huang in iconic music videos about cheating.
社群媒體用戶還利用人工智慧把黃仁勛放進了一些以出軌為主題的經典音樂錄影帶中。
In one post, Mr. Huang, donning his signature black leather jacket, takes Usher’s place in his music video for “Confessions, Pt. II.” (The lip-syncing is better in the original version.)
在一個帖子裡，黃仁勛穿著標誌性的黑色皮夾克，在Usher的《Confessions, Pt. II》音樂影片中取代了Usher。（不過，論對口型還是原版更勝一籌。）
Thomas Unise, an A.I. consultant in Minneapolis who made the video, said it took him five minutes to find four images of Mr. Huang on Google and feed them into an A.I. tool to substitute Mr. Huang’s image for Usher’s in the video.
這段影片的製作者、明尼阿波利斯的人工智慧顧問托馬斯·尤尼斯表示，他只花了五分鐘就在谷歌上找到了四張黃仁勛的照片，然後輸入一款人工智慧工具，用黃仁勛的形象替換了音樂影片中的Usher。
Mr. Unise, 40, said the proliferation of memes using Mr. Huang’s image illustrated how rapidly the technology had advanced.
40歲的尤尼斯說，黃仁勛形象的迷因大量湧現說明這項技術的發展速度有多麼驚人。 
“A year and a half ago, Will Smith eating spaghetti looked like a bunch of smears all over the screen,” he said of the actor and musician. “You can watch a video now and be like, ‘Wow, this actually looks pretty dang real.’”
「一年半以前，人工智慧生成的威爾·史密斯吃意大利麵的影片畫面還是模糊不清的一團，」他談到這位演員、音樂人時說。「現在你看一段影片，會覺得，『哇，這看起來還真挺像真的。』」
廣告
In another video, Mr. Huang plays the character Neo in a scene from the movie “The Matrix.” President Trump, as the character Morpheus, lectures him on the importance of maintaining strategic competition with China and not falling for the woman in the red dress.
在另一段影片中，黃仁勛扮演電影《駭客任務》中的尼奧，川普總統則化身墨菲斯，對他大談與中國保持戰略競爭的重要性，以及不要被穿紅裙子的女人迷住。
Mr. Huang’s avatar turns around to realize that the woman, replaced with an image of Ms. Huang, has become Xi Jinping, the Chinese leader, brandishing a gun.
黃仁勛的虛擬形象轉過身來，發現那個被替換成黃樂模樣的紅衣女子，竟然變成了舉著槍的中國領導人習近平。
Elon Musk also makes a cameo in the meme, which as of Thursday had more than six million views on X.
伊隆·馬斯克也在這段惡搞影片中客串亮相。截至週四，該影片在X上的觀看次數已超過600萬。 
On the theme of high-stakes deceit in Silicon Valley, some users placed Mr. Huang into “You Can See Everything,” the documentary about Elizabeth Holmes, the Theranos founder who went to prison for defrauding investors.
圍繞矽谷那些涉及巨額利益的欺詐事件，一些網友還把黃仁勛的形象植入了紀錄片《你可以看見一切》。這部紀錄片講述的是血液檢測公司Theranos創始人伊麗莎白·霍姆斯的故事。霍姆斯因欺詐投資者入獄。
With the help of generative A.I., users recast a montage from the documentary’s trailer in which Ms. Huang — as Ms. Holmes — unblinkingly insists, “Of course I’m not deceiving you.”
藉助生成式人工智慧，網友重新製作了紀錄片預告片中的一組鏡頭，讓黃樂化身霍姆斯，眼睛都不眨一下地堅稱：「我當然沒有騙你。」
Many of the videos have labels identifying them as artificially generated.
這些影片中，有不少都標註了人工智慧生成的說明。
廣告
But it’s “still surprising,” Mr. Dedene said of his video, how many people asked: “‘Is this real?’”
但德德內說，令他「依然感到驚訝」的是，竟然有那麼多人看了他製作的影片後問：「這是真的嗎？」
