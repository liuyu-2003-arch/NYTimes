What to Know About Colette Kress, Nvidia’s New Interim Chief Executive

ADAM SATARIANO, KALLEY HUANG • 2026-11-13
Nvidia said on Thursday that Colette Kress, the company’s chief financial officer, would be the company’s interim chief executive officer, following the resignation of its longtime leader, Jensen Huang, who admitted to an extramarital affair with a businesswoman U.S. intelligence agencies believe to be a spy for China.
英偉達週四表示，在該公司長期領導人黃仁勛辭職後，首席財務官科萊特·克雷斯將擔任臨時首席執行官。黃仁勛承認與一名被美國情報機構認為是中國間諜的女商人有婚外情。
“The board has full confidence in Colette and the leadership team,” said Harvey C. Jones, the newly named chair of Nvidia’s board of directors in a statement. “We expect the company’s operations, customer commitments and product roadmap to continue without interruption.”
「董事會對科萊特和領導團隊充滿信心，」新任英偉達董事會主席哈維·C·瓊斯在一份聲明中表示，「我們預計公司的運營、客戶承諾和產品路線圖將繼續推進，不會中斷。」
In a note to Nvidia’s employees, Ms. Kress said she believed “deeply in this company” and called its employees “our greatest strength.” She said Nvidia would have a companywide meeting on Nov. 18 to discuss its future.
克雷斯在致英偉達員工的一份備忘錄中表示，她「深信這家公司」，並稱員工是「我們最大的優勢」。她表示，英偉達將於11月18日召開全公司會議，討論公司未來。
She will remain Nvidia’s chief financial officer while serving as its interim chief executive officer until the board names a permanent successor. The board said on Thursday that it had started a search for that replacement and would consider internal and external candidates. Nvidia did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
在董事會任命正式繼任者之前，她將在擔任臨時首席執行官的同時繼續兼任英偉達首席財務官。董事會週四表示，已開始物色該繼任人選，並將考慮內部和外部候選人。英偉達未立即回應置評請求。
廣告
Ms. Kress, who joined Nvidia in 2013, is leading the company as it enters an uncertain next chapter. Under the leadership of Mr. Huang, Nvidia grew from a niche maker of computer chips used for video games to one of the most valuable public companies in the world and the linchpin of the artificial intelligence boom. Nvidia controls an estimated 90 percent of the market for the chips used in the big A.I. projects that are driving the U.S. economy.
2013年加入英偉達的克雷斯正帶領這家公司步入充滿不確定性的下一篇章。在黃仁勛的領導下，英偉達從一家生產影片遊戲電腦晶片的小眾製造商成長為全球市值最高的上市公司之一，以及人工智慧熱潮的關鍵支柱。在推動美國經濟的大型人工智慧項目所使用的晶片市場中，英偉達據估計佔據了90%的份額。
Ms. Kress, 59, faces a challenging road. She is temporarily taking over a company that has been tightly controlled by her predecessor. Mr. Huang co-founded Nvidia in 1993 and had managed all aspects of its business with a flat structure that placed him at the center of its day-to-day operations.
現年59歲的克雷斯面臨著一條充滿挑戰的道路。她暫時接管的是一家此前一直由其前任嚴密掌控的公司。黃仁勛於1993年與人一同創立了英偉達，並通過一種將他置於日常運營核心的扁平化架構管理著公司業務的方方面面。
His family members had steadily risen through its ranks in recent years. And while Ms. Kress is already familiar to Wall Street, she does not have Mr. Huang’s relationships with Nvidia’s sprawling supply chain or Washington.
近年來，他的家庭成員在公司內部穩步晉陞。而儘管克雷斯已為華爾街所熟知，她並不具備黃仁勛與英偉達龐大供應鏈或華盛頓方面建立的關係。
Ms. Kress has a bachelor’s degree in finance from the University of Arizona and a master’s degree in business administration from Southern Methodist University. She previously worked in finance at Texas Instruments, Microsoft and Cisco, before joining Nvidia in 2013.
克雷斯擁有亞利桑那大學金融學學士學位和南衛理公會大學工商管理碩士學位。在2013年加入英偉達之前，她曾在德州儀器、微軟和思科從事財務工作。
Mr. Huang interviewed 22 candidates before hiring Ms. Kress as chief financial officer. At the time, Ms. Kress’s young sons, who were avid gamers, encouraged her to join Nvidia. During her first week on the job, Ms. Kress asked Mr. Huang how long he thought he would want her in the role.
在聘請克雷斯擔任首席財務官之前，黃仁勛面試了22名候選人。當時，克雷斯的幾個年幼的兒子都是狂熱的遊戲玩家，他們鼓勵她加入英偉達。入職第一週，克雷斯曾問黃仁勛，他預計希望她在這個職位上工作多久。
“I said, ‘For as long as we shall live and death do us part,’” Mr. Huang said on a podcast this year.
「我說：『只要我們活著，直到死亡將我們分開，』」黃仁勛今年在一檔播客節目中說道。
廣告
As chief financial officer, Ms. Kress was responsible for communicating with Nvidia’s investors and Wall Street. Over the past four years, Ms. Kress delivered a rosy message on quarterly earnings calls as Nvidia’s revenue and profits soared, though she also expressed concerns about the financial impact of American policies that restrict the sale of advanced chips to China.
作為首席財務官，克雷斯負責與英偉達投資者和華爾街溝通。過去四年來，隨著英偉達營收和利潤飆升，克雷斯在季度財報電話會議上一直傳遞出樂觀的信息，不過她也曾對美國限制向中國出售先進晶片的政策所帶來的財務影響表示擔憂。
In 2013, Nvidia’s revenue was $4.28 billion. Its revenue in its most recent fiscal year was more than 50 times that, at nearly $215.94 billion. In August, Ms. Kress told analysts and investors that Nvidia expects to grow 70 percent next year.
2013年，英偉達的營收為42.8億美元。該公司最近一個財年的營收是這一數字的50多倍，達到近2159.4億美元。8月，克雷斯告訴分析師和投資者，英偉達預計明年將增長70%。
Ms. Kress has benefited from Nvidia’s skyrocketing stock price in recent years. As of March, she owned more than 4.4 million shares in the company, according to its most recent annual report, a stake that was worth about $780.4 million at the time. That makes Ms. Kress one of the largest individual shareholders of Nvidia. Its largest individual shareholder, with a 3.56 percent stake, is Mr. Huang.
近年來，克雷斯受益於英偉達飆升的股價。根據該公司最新年報，截至3月，她持有公司超過440萬股股票，這些股份當時價值約7.804億美元。這使克雷斯成為英偉達最大的個人股東之一。該公司最大的個人股東是黃仁勛，持股比例為3.56%。
Ms. Kress’s transition — as well as that of the permanent successor to Mr. Huang — could be complicated by how Mr. Huang managed Nvidia. He led the company with an executive team of more than 60 people who directly reported to him, many of whom are longtime loyalists. That flat structure will be difficult to unwind. On a podcast in January, Mr. Huang described his executive team as a group of “60 people who could be world-class C.E.O.s for many other companies.”
黃仁勛管理英偉達的方式可能會使克雷斯——以及黃仁勛的正式繼任者——的過渡變得複雜。他領導公司時擁有一個由60多名直接向他彙報的高管組成的團隊，其中許多人是長期追隨他的親信。這種扁平化架構將難以拆解。1月在一檔播客節目中，黃仁勛將他的高管團隊形容為「60個可以成為其他公司的世界級CEO的人」。
For the time being, Ms. Kress will be C.E.O. for Nvidia.
目前，克雷斯將擔任英偉達首席執行官。
