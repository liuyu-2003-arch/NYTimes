黃仁勛因何跌落神壇？傲慢與自負

I Wrote Jensen Huang’s Biography. Here’s What Led to His Downfall.

STEPHEN WITT • 2026-11-13
We live in the age of artificial intelligence — an age that Jensen Huang, perhaps more than any other single person, has made possible. The systems we are building with Nvidia’s equipment will transform our society and culture in ways we can’t imagine. But Mr. Huang’s unraveling is not a technology story. It is a timeless tale of masculine hubris, of a man undone by reckless overconfidence. Some things never change.
我們生活在人工智慧的時代——而這個時代之所以成為可能，黃仁勛或許比任何其他個人貢獻都大。藉助英偉達設備構建的系統，我們將以無法想像的方式改變社會和文化。但黃仁勛的崩盤不是一個關於技術的故事。這是一個關於男性傲慢的永恆故事，講述了一個因魯莽自負而自取滅亡的男人。有些事永遠不會改變。
When I wrote my biography of Mr. Huang, I titled it “The Thinking Machine.” The name referred to the microchips that powered A.I., but also to Mr. Huang’s marauding, relentless intellect, which never slowed down or turned off. In the wake of his stunning departure, spurred by revelations that he carried on at least two affairs, one with a woman whom U.S. intelligence determined is a Chinese spy, and another with one of his direct reports, I should write a sequel: “The What-Were-You-Thinking?! Machine.”
當我撰寫黃仁勛的傳記時，我把它命名為《思考機器》（The Thinking Machine，中譯《黃仁勛：英偉達之芯》）。這個名字既是指驅動人工智慧的微晶片，也是指黃仁勛那種所向披靡、永不疲倦的才智——它從不減速，也永不停機。在他令人震驚地離職之後——導火線是他被爆出至少有兩段婚外情，一段是與一名被美國情報機構認定為中國間諜的女性，另一段是與一名直接向他彙報的下屬，我應該寫一部續作：《你當時到底在想什麼？！機器》。
Let’s start with Mr. Huang’s affair with Alice Huang, an executive who managed a Singapore-based company that owns data centers in Southeast Asia. These data centers are suspected of helping Chinese A.I. developers circumvent U.S. restrictions on sales of Nvidia equipment. A recent report in The Information suggests they were used as a way station to illicitly smuggle some $7 billion of equipment into China. Since U.S. intelligence captured evidence of Mr. Huang in a romantic relationship with Ms. Huang, it’s hard to believe that he did not understand what was going on.
讓我們從黃仁勛與黃樂(Alice Huang)的婚外情說起。她是一名高管，管理一家在新加坡的公司，該公司在東南亞擁有數據中心。這些數據中心涉嫌幫助中國人工智慧開發者規避美國對英偉達設備銷售的限制。《The Information》最近的一篇報導指出，這些數據中心被用作中轉站，將約70億美元設備非法走私到中國。既然美國情報機構已掌握黃仁勛與黃樂戀愛的證據，很難相信他不知道自己捲入了什麼。
Now consider Mr. Huang’s reported involvement with Kimberly Powell, an executive in the health care group at his firm and his direct subordinate. A former Nvidia employee told me this affair was common knowledge in the company — the same company that employs both of Mr. Huang’s children in executive roles. It’s a stunning and disrespectful display of arrogance. I asked the former employee why Mr. Huang would behave this way, right in front of his kids. The former employee said that it got him high — a narcissist’s high.
再來看看黃仁勛據報與金伯利·鮑威爾的交往。鮑威爾是他公司醫療健康部門的高管，也是他的直接下屬。一名前英偉達員工告訴我，這段婚外情在公司裡是公開的祕密，而黃仁勛的兩個孩子都在公司擔任高管。這是一種令人震驚且毫無尊重的傲慢表現。我問這名前員工，黃仁勛為什麼會在自己孩子眼皮底下做這種事。這名前員工說，這讓他興奮——一種自戀者的興奮。

I started hearing rumors of Mr. Huang’s infidelities earlier this year, but was unable to confirm them. Inside Nvidia, there was a culture of omertà that was difficult to break, and this enabled Mr. Huang to be his worst self. I sensed this deference, at times, in the sycophantic laughter of his subordinates when he was around, and the little things they muttered once he left the room. Ultimately, it took government intelligence to pierce Nvidia’s culture of silence — along with the reporters at this newspaper. But now that containment is shattered, and I will not be surprised if more women come forward. No one will be surprised.
今年早些時候，我開始聽到黃仁勛不忠的傳言，但無法證實。英偉達內部有一種難以打破的緘默文化，這讓黃仁勛得以得以肆無忌憚地展現自己最糟糕的一面。有時，從下屬在他身邊時那種諂媚的笑聲中，以及他離開房間後他們低聲嘟囔的小話裡，我都能感受到這種諂媚。最終，是政府情報部門——以及時報的記者們——共同打破了英偉達的這種沉默文化。但現在這層遮掩已被打破，如果有更多女性站出來，我也不會驚訝。沒有人會驚訝。
Mr. Huang went to considerable lengths to cloak his arrogance. When I first met him in 2023, he and his handlers were pushing the idea that he was a humble man. In our conversations, Mr. Huang was constantly deflecting attention away from his success, always quick to assign credit to his employees and executive staff members. He also liked to glad-hand service people. He’d been a dishwasher himself once, a point he never let you forget. He loved visiting small, hole-in-the-wall restaurants and leaving extravagant tips. I’d met him for breakfast at Denny’s, and he tipped our waitress $1,000 in hundred-dollar bills. He didn’t make a show of it, either. I had to track her down to ask.
黃仁勛費了很大力氣掩蓋自己的傲慢。2023年我第一次見他時，他和他的團隊正極力塑造他謙遜的形象。在我們的交談中，黃仁勛不斷把注意力從他的成功上移開，總是迅速把功勞歸於員工和高管團隊。他還喜歡跟服務人員熱情寒暄。他自己當過洗碗工，這一點他從不讓你忘記。他喜歡光顧那些不起眼的小餐館，留下巨額小費。有一次我在丹尼餐廳和他吃早餐，他給了女服務員1000美元小費，全是百元鈔票。他並沒有刻意炫耀。是我後來特意找到那位女服務員才得知的。
Over time, though, I began to suspect that this was an act, one performed not for the observer, but to flatter himself. In truth, Mr. Huang was quite arrogant. He had little tolerance for disagreement and almost no capacity for open discourse. When you contradicted him, even over the most trivial matter, it was like lighting a fuse. Stick to your position and soon he exploded, screaming at you for your idiocy. A harangue from Mr. Huang could last an hour or more; his executive staff appeared genuinely afraid of him. Listening to their stories, I was reminded of the abusive music instructor from the movie “Whiplash.”
然而隨著時間推移，我開始懷疑這是一種表演——不是演給觀察者看，而是為了恭維自己。事實上，黃仁勛相當傲慢。他幾乎不能容忍異議，也幾乎沒有開放討論的能力。如果你反駁他，哪怕只是最微不足道的小事，都如同點燃導火線。如果你堅持自己的立場，他很快就會爆發，對你大喊大叫指責你愚蠢。黃仁勛的一頓訓斥可以持續一小時甚至更久；他的高管團隊似乎真的很怕他。聽著他們的講述，我不禁想起了電影《爆裂鼓手》(Whiplash)裡那個苛刻的音樂老師。
Mr. Huang went to even greater lengths to present himself as a family man. Here, the act was harder to detect and must have involved a great deal of self-deception. He spoke of his wife, Lori, and his kids, Madison and Spencer, all the time. Unlike his flimsy man-of-the-people shtick, this affection for his family seemed genuine. He traveled with them often, all over the world. He encouraged his kids to leave careers in unrelated fields to join him at his microchip company. Several weeks ago, at a wedding in Hawaii, he gave away his daughter Madison to Nico Caprez, another Nvidia executive.
黃仁勛更是不遺餘力地把自己塑造成一個顧家的好男人。在這方面，表演更難識破，而且一定也夾雜著大量的自我欺騙。他總談起妻子洛麗和孩子黃勝斌、黃敏珊。與他虛偽的「平民」人設不同，他對家人的感情似乎是真誠的。他經常帶他們週遊世界。他鼓勵孩子們離開不相關領域的職業，加入他的微晶片公司。幾週前，在夏威夷舉行的婚禮上，他把女兒黃敏珊交給另一位英偉達高管尼科·卡普雷斯。
Mr. Huang met Lori when he was 17. The two were lab partners in an electrical engineering course and Mr. Huang was so shy it took him six months to ask her out. She was his first girlfriend and they married in their early 20s. She left the work force to raise their kids so Mr. Huang could pursue his dream of founding a company. He has spoken often of the sacrifices she made for him. His betrayal of her is absolute.
黃仁勛17歲時遇見洛麗。兩人是一門電氣工程課的實驗搭檔，黃仁勛害羞到花了六個月才邀請她約會。她是他的第一個女朋友，兩人在20歲出頭結婚。她離開職場撫養孩子，讓黃仁勛追求創立公司的夢想。他經常談起她為他做出的犧牲。他對她的背叛是徹底的。
While reporting his biography, I spoke to some of Jensen and Lori’s oldest friends. These were people they knew in their 20s, long before the money and the fame. I sometimes dropped little hints: Was there any chance of infidelity, perhaps? Any rumors of marital friction? Any “arrangements”? The response I got was that the Huangs’ marriage was happy, even ideal — something to be emulated.
在寫他傳記的報導過程中，我採訪了黃仁勛與洛麗一些最老的朋友。這些人都是他們20多歲時結識的，那時他們還遠未擁有財富和名望。我有時會丟出小暗示：是否可能有過出軌？有沒有婚姻不和的傳言？有沒有什麼「安排」？我得到的回答是，黃氏夫婦的婚姻幸福，甚至堪稱理想，是值得效仿的典範。

In a divorce, Lori Huang could become one of the richest women in America. It’s possible the Huangs signed a prenuptial agreement, or have some other agreement, but I doubt it. When they married, in the mid-1980s, neither had much in assets. The hundreds of millions of Nvidia shares they jointly own are held in family trusts. They recently donated $75 million toward a flagship building at Vanderbilt University’s new campus in San Francisco. Another building they helped fund at Oregon State University, where they met, is nearing completion.
如果離婚，洛麗·黃可能成為美國最富有的女性之一。黃氏夫婦可能簽了婚前協議或其他協議，但我對此表示懷疑。1980年代中期他們結婚時，兩人都沒多少資產。他們共同擁有的數億股英偉達股票由家族信託持有。他們最近捐贈7500萬美元，用於范德堡大學舊金山新校區的旗艦建築。他們資助的另一座位於奧勒岡州立大學（兩人相識之地）的建築也即將竣工。
The hypocrisy is almost overwhelming. In the early days — the 1990s — Nvidia had been a boys’ club. The staff played video games and drank beer in the office, and almost no women worked there. Their mascot, for a time, was “Dawn,” an underdressed, large-breasted C.G.I. pixie. Dawn was memory-holed in 2020, around the time Mr. Huang started to talk about empowering women within the company. In 2023, while at Nvidia’s campus, I saw Mr. Huang make an impassioned call for gender parity at Nvidia, where men still comprise 80 percent of its employees. Maybe he had an ulterior motive.
這種虛偽幾乎到了令人窒息的地步。早年間，也就是1990年代，英偉達幾乎是男人的天下。員工在辦公室打電動、喝啤酒，公司裡幾乎沒有女性員工。有一段時間，英偉達的吉祥物是一個叫「黎明」(Dawn)的電腦製作的小精靈，衣著暴露、胸部豐滿。2020年左右，黃仁勛開始談論如何賦予公司女性員工更多權力，「黎明」也在那時被悄悄抹去了蹤跡。2023年，我在英偉達園區親眼看到黃仁勛慷慨激昂地呼籲實現性別平等，儘管男性仍占公司員工總數的80%。也許他另有所圖。
I was surprised — and, frankly, amazed — when Mr. Huang became President Trump’s close ally in 2025. He’d never shown much interest in politics and had limited Washington connections. He didn’t attend Mr. Trump’s inauguration. But soon, in a masterly display of social engineering, he’d gained the president’s trust, mostly by flattering him. By the time of the president’s tech summit at the White House this September, Mr. Huang was seated at Mr. Trump’s right arm.
2025年黃仁勛成為川普總統的親密盟友時，我感到驚訝——坦率說，簡直震驚。他從未對政治表現出多大興趣，在華盛頓人脈有限。他沒參加川普的就職典禮。但很快，憑藉一套高超的社交手腕，他贏得了總統的信任，主要是通過奉承。到今年9月總統在白宮舉行科技峰會時，黃仁勛坐在川普的右手邊。
Mr. Trump and Mr. Huang have a fair amount in common: the bluster, the narcissism and, I suppose, the womanizing. But it was politics that triggered Mr. Huang’s downfall. He convinced the president to lift export restrictions on Nvidia chips to China, and this put him in conflict with the more hawkish elements on the president’s staff — especially Secretary of State Marco Rubio, whose sharp criticisms of China at one point got him barred from that country. It was Mr. Rubio who oversaw the memo that exposed Mr. Huang.
川普和黃仁勛有不少共同點：愛說大話、自戀，恐怕還有拈花惹草。不過，真正導致黃仁勛垮台的還是政治。他說服總統取消了對華出口英偉達晶片的限制，卻因此與川普幕僚中對華態度更為強硬的人士發生衝突，尤其是國務卿馬爾科·魯比奧。魯比奧曾因嚴厲批評中國而被禁止入境。正是魯比奧負責監督編寫了那份揭露黃仁勛的備忘錄。
Now Nvidia must find a successor. That’s going to be difficult: Mr. Huang never groomed one and has no second-in-command. Colette Kress will serve as interim chief executive, but she comes from the world of finance, not electrical engineering, so I don’t think she can provide a long-term solution.
現在英偉達必須找繼任者。這將很困難：黃仁勛從未培養過繼任者，也沒有二把手。科萊特·克雷斯將擔任臨時首席執行官，但她出身金融領域，而非電氣工程，因此我認為她無法成為長期的解決人選。
Whoever replaces Mr. Huang must be a world-class computer scientist, someone who can close deals with powerful customers and also someone who can get on the phone to Taiwan and start screaming when the chips are delayed. Nvidia without Jensen Huang is like the Bulls without Michael Jordan. He built Nvidia from nothing but, through his arrogance and toxic self-regard, he has left an empty space that might be impossible to fill.
無論誰接替黃仁勛，都必須是一位世界級的計算機科學家，既能與實力雄厚的大客戶敲定交易，也能在晶片交付延誤時拿起電話，對著台灣那頭大發雷霆。沒有黃仁勛的英偉達，就像沒有麥可·喬丹的芝加哥公牛隊。他白手起家創立了英偉達，卻因傲慢和病態的自負留下了一個可能無法填補的空缺。

Stephen Witt是黃仁勛傳記《黃仁勛：英偉達之芯》(The Thinking Machine)的作者。
