尼泊爾中國邊境山洪暴發

Here’s the latest.

《紐約時報》 • 2026-08-27
 A giant torrent of mud, water and ice that began high in the Himalayas tore a devastating path through a mountain valley on the border between Nepal and Tibet on Wednesday, smashing buildings and sweeping away fleeing people caught by surprise by its speed and destructive force.
週三，一場發源於喜馬拉雅山脈高處，由泥石、水和冰組成的巨大洪流在尼泊爾和西藏交界的山谷中肆虐，造成毀滅性破壞，摧毀了建築物，逃生人群因這股洪流的速度和破壞力而措手不及，被捲入其中。
At least 160 people were killed and hundreds more were missing after the flood, which snapped bridges into pieces and pulled multistory structures off their foundations. The deluge also engulfed dozens of men, women and children who were overtaken as they tried to run. The death toll was expected to rise.
Initial assessments by geologists and glaciologists indicated that an enormous chunk of ice — possibly a half-mile wide — had snapped off a glacier at an altitude of 17,000 feet and tumbled thousands of feet into a river valley below. Scientists studying the disaster said the impact registered as the equivalent of a 5.2-magnitude earthquake and crushed the ice into a fast-moving mix of water, ice and rock that pulverized everything in its path.
洪流造成至少160人死亡，另有數百人失蹤。它將橋樑沖得支離破碎，並將多層建築從地基上連根拔起，吞噬了數十名在逃生時被追上的男女老少。預計死亡人數還會上升。地質學家和冰川學家的初步評估表明，一塊巨大的冰塊——可能有大約800米寬——從約5000米高的冰川上斷裂，翻滾著墜落數千米，落入下方的河谷中。研究這場災難的科學家表示，撞擊產生的威力相當於5.2級地震，將冰塊碾碎成由水、冰和岩石組成的快速移動的混合物，摧毀了沿途的一切。
Officials in Nepal reported 157 deaths. Chinese officials reported only three deaths in the Tibet region of China, but the Chinese state news agency Xinhua said there had been “significant” casualties at the Gyirong port of entry, at the border with Nepal.
尼泊爾官員報告有157人死亡。中國官員報告中國西藏地區僅有三人死亡，但中國國家通訊社新華社表示，在尼泊爾邊境的吉隆口岸出現了「重大」傷亡。
Surveillance footage circulating on social media and verified by The New York Times shows crowds running up a road near the port before torrents of water and thick clouds engulf the parking lot and surrounding buildings, destroying the area.
在社群媒體上流傳並經《紐約時報》核實的監控錄像顯示，人群沿著口岸附近的一條道路奔跑，隨後洶湧的洪水和濃密的雲霧吞噬了停車場及周圍的建築物，摧毀了這個地區。

“The bottom looks like a bomb exploded — it’s just deposits from the broken-up glacier,” said Daniel Shugar, a geologist at the University of Calgary in Canada who studied satellite imagery to analyze what happened. He said other factors may have also contributed to the landslide.
「底部看起來就像炸彈爆炸了一樣——那全是破碎冰川的沉積物，」加拿大卡爾加里大學研究衛星圖像以分析事發情況的地質學家丹尼爾·舒格說。他表示，其他因素可能也促成了這場山體滑坡。
As many as 391 tourists, including 291 foreigners, had been reported missing in Nepal, the country’s tourism board said in a statement. They included more than 100 people on a pilgrimage from India, a dozen Britons and at least three Americans. Citizens from Australia, the Netherlands, Malaysia and South Africa were also among the missing.
尼泊爾旅遊局在一份聲明中表示，尼泊爾據報有多達391名遊客失蹤，其中包括291名外國人。這其中包括100多名來自印度的朝聖者、十幾名英國人和至少三名美國人。失蹤者中還有來自澳洲、荷蘭、馬來西亞和南非的公民。
Thousands of people live in riverside settlements downstream from the flooding, including along the Bhote Koshi and Trishuli rivers in the Rasuwa district. The authorities have not estimated the number of people missing and the extent of damage but said that bridges, schools and hydropower projects had been swept away.
數以千計的民眾居住在洪水下游的河畔聚居地，包括拉蘇瓦區的波特科西河和特里蘇裡河沿岸。當局尚未估計失蹤人數和受損程度，但表示橋樑、學校和水電項目已被沖走。
Chinese state media reports ‘significant’ casualties at a port on the border with Nepal.
中國官方媒體報導稱，與尼泊爾接壤的一個口岸出現「重大」傷亡。
While the Nepali authorities reported that dozens of people had been killed and hundreds of people were missing on Wednesday after devastating flooding, there was less information about casualties on the Chinese side of the border, in the territory of Tibet.
尼泊爾當局報告稱，在週三毀滅性的洪水過後，已有數十人死亡、數百人失蹤，但關於邊境中國一側西藏境內的傷亡情況，目前尚無更多信息。
The Chinese state news agency Xinhua reported that there had been “significant” casualties at the Gyirong Port in China, where footage released by state media shows surging waters overwhelming buildings, taking out trees and rendering roads impassable.
中國國家通訊社新華社報導稱，中國吉隆口岸出現了「重大」傷亡，官方媒體發布的影片顯示，洶湧的洪水淹沒了建築物，沖毀了樹木，導致道路無法通行。

China’s leader, Xi Jinping, ordered local authorities to “make every effort” to search for and rescue missing residents. Mr. Xi issued a statement emphasizing the need for flood prevention, and other members of the Politburo’s Standing Committee in Beijing made similar remarks.
中國領導人習近平下令地方當局「千方百計」搜救失蹤居民。習近平發表指示，強調了防汛工作的必要性，中央政治局常委的其他成員也發表了類似講話。
Drone footage broadcast on China’s state television news showed buildings buried at Gyirong port, where goods are traded across the international border.
中國國家電視新聞播放的無人機畫面顯示，在進行跨國貿易的吉隆口岸，建築物被掩埋。
Surveillance footage circulating on social media and verified by The New York Times shows the moment a landslide rips through the port. In one video, crowds of people are running up a road near the port. Seconds later, torrents of water and thick clouds engulf the parking lot and surrounding buildings, destroying the area.
在社群媒體上流傳並經《紐約時報》核實的監控錄像，顯示了土石流滑坡席捲該口岸的瞬間。在一段影片中，人群正沿著口岸附近的一條道路奔跑。幾秒鐘後，洶湧的洪水和濃密的雲霧吞噬了停車場及周圍的建築物，摧毀了該地區。
Another surveillance video shows the moment the landslide crashes through the building that serves as the port of entry, as dozens of people run in the other direction. Debris flies as successive waves flood the port, filling the camera’s field of vision.
另一段監控影片顯示，當土石流衝破作為入境口岸的建築物那一刻，數十人朝相反方向奔跑。隨著一波接一波的土石流淹沒口岸，碎片四處飛濺，充滿了攝像頭的整個視野。
Most of those at the port were likely local Tibetans, migrant workers from other parts of China or traders from Nepal. Gyirong, a rural border town, is within the Tibetan Autonomous Region, an area that the Chinese government keeps under tight control as part of efforts to quash any dissent over its control of Tibet.
口岸裡的大多數人可能是當地藏民、來自中國其他地區的農民工或來自尼泊爾的商人。吉隆是一個邊境鄉村小鎮，位於西藏自治區內，中國政府對該地區實施嚴密管控，以此壓制任何針對其對西藏管轄權的異議。
The Chinese authorities invested in the port as part of efforts to incorporate Tibet into China’s signature Belt and Road infrastructure initiative through trade with Nepal and India.
中國當局對該口岸進行投資，旨在通過與尼泊爾和印度的貿易將西藏納入中國標誌性的「一帶一路」基礎設施倡議。

Here’s what else we’re covering:
以下是我們的其他相關報導：
• Initial Assessment: A preliminary analysis of satellite imagery indicates that an ice avalanche set off the flooding, said Simon Cook, a glaciologist at the University of Dundee in Scotland who is part of a team of scientists studying the disaster. According to their initial assessment, he said, a large chunk of ice broke off from a high-altitude glacier and tumbled thousands of feet toward the Bhotekoshi River in Nepal, releasing vast amounts of energy that pulverized the ice into a significant volume of flood water.
· 初步評估：蘇格蘭鄧迪大學的冰川學家、參與研究這場災難的科學家團隊成員西蒙·庫克表示，對衛星圖像的初步分析表明，是冰崩引發了這場洪水。他說，根據他們的初步評估，一大塊冰從高海拔冰川上脫離，向尼泊爾的波特科西河滾落數千英尺，釋放出巨大的能量，將冰塊粉碎成大量的洪水。
• Rescue Effort: Security teams led by the Nepali Army have been carrying out rescue operations. The army has mobilized 12 helicopters, set up a temporary hospital in the flood zone and evacuated at least 13 seriously injured people to Kathmandu, Nepal’s capital.
· 救援行動：由尼泊爾軍方領導的安全團隊一直在進行救援行動。軍方出動了12架直升機，在洪災區建立了一所臨時醫院，並將至少13名重傷員送至尼泊爾首都加德滿都。
• Nepal’s Government: The disaster adds to the woes of Nepal’s recently elected government, and its 36-year old prime minister, Balendra Shah, who swept to power on the back of a youth-led protest movement last year. Nepal’s education minister, Sasmit Pokharel, said the country had sought help from both its giant neighbors, China and India, to strengthen its rescue operations.
· 尼泊爾政府：這場災難讓尼泊爾新當選的政府及其36歲的總理巴倫德拉·沙阿面臨的困境雪上加霜。沙阿是在去年一場由青年領導的抗議運動的支持下上台的。尼泊爾教育部長薩斯米特·波卡雷爾表示，該國已尋求其兩個毗鄰大國——中國和印度的幫助，以加強救援行動。
