現場影片揭示尼泊爾山洪有多致命

Maps and Images Show the Devastation in Nepal’s Flooding

PABLO ROBLES, LEANNE ABRAHAM, JOSH HOLDER • 2026-08-27
Floodwaters swept away villages near the border between Nepal and the Tibet region of China on Wednesday, leaving at least 160 people dead and hundreds missing across a 40-mile path of destruction. An initial assessment of satellite imagery indicates that a collapsing glacier may have caused the flooding. The death toll is expected to rise.
週三，一場洪水襲擊了尼泊爾與中國西藏邊境附近的村莊。洪水沿途綿延約60餘公里，造成了毀滅性的破壞，目前已導致至少160人遇難、數百人失蹤。根據衛星圖像的初步評估，洪水可能是由一處冰川崩塌引發的。預計死亡人數還會進一步上升。
The images below are screenshots from surveillance footage that circulated on social media and were verified by The New York Times. They show the moment the landslide ripped through Gyirong Port, a small town at the border between China and Nepal.
以下圖片是社群媒體上流傳的監控錄像的截圖，《紐約時報》對其進行了核實。畫面顯示了山體滑坡襲擊中尼邊境小鎮吉隆口岸的一刻。
In the footage, people are seen trying to escape the area, and then a massive wave of mud and a thick cloud of debris engulf a parking structure and adjoining buildings. Within seconds, the building collapsed and at least a dozen vehicles were buried in mud.
影片顯示，人們正試圖逃離該區域，隨後一股巨大的泥浪裹挾著濃密的瓦礫雲席捲而來，將一座停車場及周邊建築吞沒。幾秒鐘之內，建築物坍塌，至少十幾輛汽車被泥漿掩埋。
The mudslide continued downstream for at least 45 miles along the Trishuli River, destroying buildings and bridges and flooding surrounding areas. The map below shows the path of destruction, from Gyirong Port deep into Nepal.
土石流隨後繼續沿特里蘇裡河向下游奔涌了至少70餘公里，沿途摧毀建築物和橋樑，並淹沒周邊地區。下方地圖展示了這場災難造成破壞的路徑，從吉隆口岸一直深入尼泊爾境內。
Satellite imagery captured on Wednesday shows flood damage about 25 miles southwest of Gyirong Port.
週三拍攝的衛星圖像顯示，在吉隆口岸西南約40公里處也可以看到洪災造成的破壞。
The force of the landslide caused a seismic signal that registered a magnitude of 5.2, according to the U.S. Geological Survey.
據美國地質調查局測定，此次山體滑坡產生的衝擊力引發了地震信號，震級為5.2級。
