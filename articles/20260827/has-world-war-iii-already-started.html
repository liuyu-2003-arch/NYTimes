第三次世界大戰已經爆發了嗎？

Has World War III Already Started?

BRET STEPHENS • 2026-08-27
Shortly after Russia’s full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, I wrote a column under the headline “This Is How World War III Begins.” More than four years on, I wonder how much I got right.
2022年俄羅斯全面入侵烏克蘭後不久，我寫了一篇題為《第三次世界大戰將如何開始？》的專欄文章。四年多過去了，我想知道我的預測有多少是準確的。
My argument was based on the origins of World War II, which did not simply start with Germany’s invasion of Poland on Sept. 1, 1939. Instead, the war was an accumulation of regional wars and crises, beginning with Japan’s invasion of Manchuria in 1931, which gradually gathered into a single global conflict.
我的論點基於第二次世界大戰的起源——二戰並非單純地始於1939年9月1日德國入侵波蘭。這場戰爭是一系列局部戰爭和危機的不斷累積，從1931年日本入侵滿洲開始，最終匯聚成一場全球衝突。
That gathering, abetted by the converging ambitions of the totalitarian states and the divisions and vacillations of the democracies, led to catastrophe. Added to this was a revolution in military technology and tactics that gave the dictatorships an early advantage in offensive combat. Also an advantage: their willingness to inflict — and endure — a level of brutality and suffering their adversaries were initially reluctant to match.
這種匯聚在極權國家的勃勃野心以及民主國家的分歧與動搖的推波助瀾下最終導致了災難。此外，軍事技術和戰術的革命使得獨裁政權在初期進攻中佔據了優勢。他們還有一個優勢：他們所願意施加——並且承受——的殘酷和苦難是他們的對手在一開始無法比擬的。
In 2022, I saw a similar pattern unfolding: Decades of Western appeasement of Vladimir Putin had given the Russian strongman a sense that he could get away with nearly anything. His burgeoning ties with North Korea, Iran and especially China — memorialized by the pledge of a “no limits” partnership with Beijing announced weeks before the invasion — gave him additional confidence. So did the spectacle of American weakness during our shambolic withdrawal from Afghanistan just six months earlier.
2022年，我看到了類似的模式正在重演：西方幾十年來對弗拉基米爾·普丁的綏靖政策讓這位俄羅斯強人產生了一種「幾乎可以為所欲為而無需承擔後果」的錯覺。他與朝鮮、伊朗，以及特別是和中國之間日益密切的關係——在入侵前幾週宣布與北京建立「無上限」的夥伴關係就是明證——給了他額外的信心。而在六個月前我們從阿富汗混亂撤軍期間表現出的美國虛弱景象也同樣起到了壯膽作用。

But Ukraine did not, as I had feared, turn out to be the France of 1940. It was more like Britain, prepared to fight, as Churchill put it that May, “however long and hard the road may be.” The Biden administration, for all its fatal hesitation about letting Kyiv take the war to Russia, at least helped ensure that Ukraine didn’t lose. Ukraine has also held the technological edge, pioneering equipment and tactics that are finally stimulating a long-overdue rethink about how wars of the future will be fought.
但烏克蘭並沒有如我擔心的那樣淪為1940年的法國。它更像英國，做好了戰鬥的準備，正如邱吉爾在當年5月所說的那樣，「無論道路多麼漫長和艱難。」拜登政府儘管在允許基輔將戰火燒向俄羅斯本土方面存在致命的猶豫，但至少幫助確保了烏克蘭不會失敗。烏克蘭還佔據了技術優勢，其首創的裝備和戰術最終引發了人們對未來戰爭打法的長期反思。
That’s the good news. There’s far more by way of bad news.
這些是好消息。而壞消息則要多得多。
First, as in the 1930s and ’40s, the spheres of conflict have multiplied and are merging.
首先，就像在1930年代和40年代一樣，衝突範圍正在倍增並相互交織。
Russia’s battlefield reversals seem to have done nothing to diminish Putin’s appetite for risk as he expands his campaign, in barely concealed attacks and threats, against Moldova, Poland and Germany, among other states, while his political allies repeatedly call for direct strikes on Europe and the use of nuclear weapons against Ukraine. Hamas’s assault on Israel in 2023 became, in stages, a war between Israel and Hezbollah, the Houthis and Iran; then a war between Iran, the United States and the Gulf states. More recently, Ukraine has directed long-range strikes in the Caspian Sea to disrupt Iranian military supplies to Russia. And decisions in one theater directly affect outcomes in the other: The expenditure of missile interceptors in the Middle East means that few can now be spared for Ukraine.
俄羅斯在戰場上遭遇的逆轉似乎絲毫沒有削弱普丁對風險的胃口。他正在擴大其針對摩爾多瓦、波蘭和德國等國的行動，進行幾乎毫不掩飾的攻擊和威脅；與此同時，他的政治盟友不斷呼籲直接打擊歐洲，並對烏克蘭使用核武器。2023年哈馬斯對以色列的襲擊已逐步演變成以色列與真主黨、胡塞武裝及伊朗之間的戰爭；進而演變成伊朗、美國和海灣國家之間的戰爭。最近，烏克蘭在裏海實施了遠程打擊，以切斷伊朗向俄羅斯的軍事補給。而且，一個戰區的決策會直接影響另一個戰區的結果：在中東的攔截導彈消耗意味著現在幾乎沒有富餘的彈藥能留給烏克蘭。
Second, the Axis of Authoritarians has become a fully operational military alliance.
其次，威權軸心已演變為一個全面運作的軍事聯盟。
Russia is providing North Korea with help to modernize its armed forces, while the North has returned the favor by sending millions of artillery rounds and is now reportedly prepared to send thousands of additional troops to fight for Russia in Ukraine. China supplies Russia with most of the electronic components used in its drones and other military hardware. Russia has given Iran critical targeting intelligence to strike U.S. bases and ships in the Middle East. In the event of a Chinese attack on Taiwan, Beijing could call in its chips with Moscow by engaging the aid of Russia’s powerful Pacific submarine fleet, as detailed in a new paper from the Australian Strategic Policy Institute.
俄羅斯正在幫助朝鮮實現其武裝部隊的現代化，作為回報，朝鮮已送去數百萬發炮彈，據報導目前還準備再派遣數千名士兵前往烏克蘭為俄羅斯作戰。中國向俄羅斯提供了其無人機和其他軍事硬件所需的大部分電子元件。俄羅斯向伊朗提供了關鍵的目標情報，以打擊美國在中東的基地和船隻。如果中國對台灣發動攻擊，正如澳洲戰略政策研究所的一份新報告所詳述的那樣，北京可能會要求莫斯科提供協助，即出動俄羅斯強大的太平洋潛艇艦隊。

Third, the West is a mess.
第三，西方陣營一片混亂。
President Trump complains of our allies’ weakness. The allies complain of Trump’s bullying, capriciousness, callowness, mendacity and incompetence. Both sides are right. The latest U.S. tariff salvo on Canada is a case in point: politically ill advised, economically self-defeating, strategically enervating.
川普總統抱怨我們盟友的軟弱。盟友們則抱怨川普的恃強凌弱、反覆無常、缺乏經驗、虛偽和無能。雙方都沒錯。美國最近對加拿大發起的關稅攻勢就是一個恰當的例子：此舉在政治上是不明智的，在經濟上是作繭自縛，在戰略上則是對自身力量的削弱。
Yet none of that erases the fact that Canada, a trade-dependent nation with the world’s longest coastline, barely has a navy to speak of, or that its government’s “new strategic partnership” with China is immoral, unwise and unsound — unless Mark Carney, the prime minister, really thinks that Trump’s idiotic outbursts are a graver long-term threat to Canada and the free world writ large than the patient designs of the People’s Republic.
然而，這一切都無法抹殺這樣一個事實：加拿大作為一個擁有世界上最長海岸線的貿易依賴國，卻幾乎沒有一支值得一提的海軍；或者說，其政府與中國建立的「新戰略夥伴關係」是不道德、不明智且不穩妥的——除非馬克·卡尼總理真的認為川普那些白痴般的突發奇想對加拿大和整個自由世界構成的長期威脅要比中國的徐徐圖之要嚴重得多。
Fourth, we are militarily unprepared.
第四，我們在軍事上毫無準備。
The war against Iran revealed not just the munitions shortages, which helped bring the war to a premature close, but also America’s acute sensitivity to military fatalities and economic pain, which gave Iran’s leaders an asymmetric advantage given their own tolerance for human suffering. The war in Ukraine has demonstrated that even the most sophisticated U.S. military kit is entering obsolescence thanks to the advent of A.I.-assisted drone warfare. The tanks we and our allies supplied Kyiv during the Biden administration “now mostly sit idle, hidden in forests and collecting cobwebs,” as The Times reported this week.
對伊朗的戰爭不僅暴露了彈藥短缺（這促使戰爭提前結束），還暴露了美國對軍人傷亡和經濟痛苦的極度敏感。鑒於伊朗領導人自己對人類苦難的容忍度，這賦予了他們一種不對稱的優勢。烏克蘭戰爭證明，由於人工智慧輔助的無人機戰爭的出現，即使是美國最先進的軍事裝備也正在走向過時。正如《紐約時報》本週報導的那樣，我們和盟友在拜登政府期間提供給基輔的坦克「現在大部分都閑置著，隱藏在森林裡積滿蛛網」。
Yet Pete Hegseth, our testosterone-obsessed defense secretary, last week decided to phase out an Army drone unit, seemingly because it was championed by generals he didn’t like. The travails of the U.S.S. Abraham Lincoln during the carrier’s long deployment are a national scandal. But they should call attention to the historically small size of the fleet and our depleted industrial base that struggles to build and maintain ships. Democrats blocking the defense appropriations bill aren’t helping things, either.
然而，我們那位睾酮上腦的國防部長彼特·海格塞斯上週決定逐步淘汰一支陸軍無人機部隊，原因似乎僅僅是因為這支部隊得到了他不喜歡的將軍們的支持。亞伯拉罕·林肯號航母在漫長部署期間的種種困境簡直是國家醜聞。但這些問題理應讓人們注意到，艦隊規模正處於歷史低點，而我們空心化的工業難以建造和維護船隻。民主黨人阻撓國防撥款法案的做法更是雪上加霜。

Fifth, we may be near the economic brink.
第五，我們可能正處於經濟崩潰的邊緣。
One great difference between the 1930s and the present is that, unlike then, we are not in the midst of a worldwide depression. But the warning signs of a sharp and abrupt downturn are everywhere. What happens if or when the A.I. boom starts to run out of steam, much as the dot-com boom did at the beginning of the century? Or if the currency crisis in Japan causes it to start selling off its U.S. Treasury bonds? Stanley Druckenmiller, the legendary investor, recently warned in The Wall Street Journal that the Treasury Department’s bond buybacks were all but destined to fail: Ultimately, the price of a $40 trillion national debt will be felt in sharply higher interest rates and the risks they pose to every sector of the economy that’s depended for too long on cheap credit.
1930年代與現在的一個巨大不同之處在於，我們目前並未處於全球性蕭條之中。但嚴重且急劇衰退的警告信號無處不在。如果人工智慧熱潮開始退去，就像本世紀初的互聯網泡沫破裂時那樣，會發生什麼？或者如果日本的貨幣危機促使它開始拋售其持有的美國國債，又會怎樣？傳奇投資者斯坦利·德魯肯米勒最近在《華爾街日報》上警告說，財政部的債券回購註定會失敗：最終，40萬億美元國家債務的代價將體現為急劇上升的利率，以及這些債務給依賴廉價信貸多時的經濟各部門構成的風險。
A deep U.S. recession, or a global financial panic, will inevitably worsen every trend mentioned above. In periods of economic distress, democracies almost always become more risk-averse, while dictatorships become risk-prone. That’s a pattern of history worth recalling as we continue to drift, only dimly aware, toward the gathering storm.
深度衰退的美國或全球金融恐慌將不可避免地導致上述所有趨勢惡化。在經濟困難時期，民主國家幾乎總是變得更加規避風險，而獨裁政權則變得更加傾向於冒險。這是值得銘記的歷史規律，因為我們正繼續在半夢半醒中，隨波逐流地駛向那場正在醞釀的風暴。
