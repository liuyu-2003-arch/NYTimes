哪些國家限制青少年使用社群媒體？

A Movement to Restrict Social Media for Children Has Taken Hold Globally

EPHRAT LIVNI • 2026-08-27
Meta’s landmark settlement of up to $17.1 billion with U.S. states and territories on Wednesday over claims it endangered children with addictive social media platforms ended one struggle with regulators.
Meta週三與美國各州和領地達成了一項高達171億美元的里程碑式和解協議，以解決有關其令人上癮的社群媒體平台危及兒童的指控，從而結束了與監管機構的一場爭鬥。
But Meta — and its peers across the industry — still face many others around the world.
但Meta及其整個行業的同行仍在世界各地面臨著許多其他類似問題。
The company, which owns Facebook and Instagram, and other tech giants have faced a slew of bans and restrictions internationally in the last year, as the movement to shield young people from social media’s potential harms seems to be gaining ground globally.
過去一年裡，這家擁有Facebook和Instagram的公司以及其他科技巨頭在國際上面臨一系列禁令和限制，因為保護年輕人免受社群媒體潛在傷害的運動似乎正在全球範圍內聲勢漸長。
Still, those bans and restrictions have faced setbacks and obstacles. Even some world leaders who say they may support such bans in theory are questioning the approach or its effectiveness.
儘管如此，這些禁令和限制也遇到了挫折和障礙。甚至一些表示在原則上可能支持此類禁令的世界領導人也在質疑這種做法或其有效性。
廣告
These are some of the countries where social media for young people has already been banned or restricted and some that have been contemplating similar steps over the last year.
以下是一些已經禁止或限制年輕人使用社群媒體的國家，以及過去一年中一直在考慮採取類似措施的部分國家。
Denmark
丹麥
Last year, the Danish government announced a plan to bar anyone under 15 from using certain platforms. Denmark’s ministry for digital affairs said the goal was for children to stay off social media until age 15, giving them more time for “peace, play and development.”
去年，丹麥政府宣布了一項計劃，禁止15歲以下人群使用某些平台。丹麥數位化部表示，此舉旨在讓兒童在15歲之前遠離社群媒體，給他們更多的時間用於「平靜、玩耍和發展」。
Australia
澳洲
In December, the world’s first social media ban for young teenagers went into effect in Australia. The country undertook a sweeping governmental effort to shield children from its potential harms, blocking those under 16 from platforms including Facebook, Instagram, Kick, Reddit, Snapchat, Threads, TikTok, Twitch, X and YouTube.
去年12月，世界上首個針對青少年的社群媒體禁令在澳洲生效。該國採取了全面的政府行動來保護兒童免受潛在傷害，禁止16歲以下人群使用包括Facebook、Instagram、Kick、Reddit、Snapchat、Threads、TikTok、Twitch、X和YouTube在內的平台。
But young people quickly found workarounds. By spring, surveys showed most parents of children under 16 who already had social media accounts before the ban — about 70 percent — said the teenagers still had access to one of the age-restricted services. In June, the government said that it would take steps to strengthen the law, acknowledging its limited effectiveness.
但年輕人很快就找到了繞過限制的方法。到了春天，調查顯示，在禁令實施前就已經擁有社群媒體帳戶的16歲以下兒童的父母中，大多數人（約70%）表示，這些青少年仍然可以訪問其中一項受年齡限制的服務。今年6月，政府表示將採取措施加強該法律，並承認其效果平平。
廣告
Spain
西班牙
Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez said in February that Spain would ban social media for anyone under 16, but added that the move would require parliamentary approval.
西班牙首相佩德羅·桑切斯在2月表示，西班牙將禁止16歲以下人群使用社群媒體，但他補充說，此舉需要獲得議會批准。
Indonesia
印尼
In March, a ban in Indonesia on social media use by children under 16 on platforms deemed “high-risk,” including YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, X and Roblox, was introduced. That same month the ban went into effect, and Indonesian authorities summoned officials from Google and Meta over what they said was a failure to comply with the new law.
今年3月，印尼出台了一項禁令，禁止16歲以下兒童使用被視為「高風險」的社群媒體平台，包括YouTube、TikTok、Facebook、Instagram、Threads、X和Roblox。該禁令於同月生效，印尼當局還傳喚了谷歌和Meta的官員，稱其未能遵守這項新法律。
Brazil
巴西
Since March, social media users under 16 in Brazil have been required to link their accounts to a legal guardian for supervision, and platforms have been prohibited from using addictive features such as infinite scroll, among other restrictions.
自今年3月以來，巴西16歲以下的社群媒體用戶被要求將其帳戶與法定監護人綁定以接受監督，此外，各大平台被禁止使用無限下拉等令人上癮的功能，並面臨其他限制。
廣告
Austria
奧地利
Austria said in March that it also planned to ban social media use for children under 14.
奧地利在3月表示也計劃禁止14歲以下兒童使用社群媒體。
Turkey
土耳其
In April, Turkey passed a law restricting social media for children under 15, which is being rolled out gradually. The measure did not specify which platforms were affected or how users’ ages would be verified, but Turkish officials said the login process would require the use of an online portal run by the government, a step that free-speech advocates say could threaten online privacy and freedom of expression.
今年4月，土耳其通過了一項限制15歲以下兒童使用社群媒體的法律，該法律目前正在逐步實施。這項措施沒有明確說明哪些平台會受到影響，也沒有說明將如何驗證用戶的年齡，但土耳其官員表示，登錄過程需要使用政府運營的在線門戶網站。言論自由倡導者表示，這一步驟可能會威脅在線隱私和言論自由。
Malaysia
馬來西亞
In June, a measure barring children under 16 from social media platforms with over eight million users in Malaysia went into effect in that country, restricting the use of TikTok, Facebook, Instagram and YouTube. The restrictions have not entirely blocked children from using the platforms, however, and — as in Australia and Indonesia — parents and officials have lamented the difficulties of implementing the new rules.
今年6月，一項禁止16歲以下兒童使用社群媒體平台的措施在馬來西亞生效，禁令涉及所有在該國用戶數超過800萬的平台，包括TikTok、Facebook、Instagram和YouTube。然而，這些限制並未完全阻止兒童使用這些平台，而且與澳洲和印尼一樣，家長和官員都對實施新規的困難感到苦惱。
廣告
United Arab Emirates
阿拉伯聯合酋長國
The Emirates in June announced that the minimum age for social media accounts would be 15 and restricted some activities for teenagers ages 15 and 16. The rules apply to all platforms operating in the Emirates, but companies have 12 months to comply.
阿聯酋在6月宣布，社群媒體帳戶的最低年齡限制為15歲，並對15歲和16歲青少年的一些活動進行了限制。這些規定適用於在阿聯酋運營的所有平台，但企業有12個月的時間按規定進行整改。
Britain
英國
The British government in June announced a plan to ban social media for children under 16, which will take effect early next year. The government said that it also planned to block children under 16 from livestreaming and from communicating with strangers on a wider range of online services, including game sites, and that it would be “looking in more detail at overnight curfews and breaks in infinite scrolling” for people under 18.
英國政府於6月宣布了一項禁止16歲以下兒童使用社群媒體的計劃，將於明年初生效。此外政府還計劃禁止16歲以下兒童在更廣泛的在線服務（包括遊戲網站）上進行直播和與陌生人交流，並且將為18歲以下人群「更詳細地研究夜間宵禁和中斷無限下拉的功能」。
France
法國
In July, France passed a law restricting access to social media for anyone under 15, becoming the first European country to do so. It planned to put elements of the law into place as soon as September, but this month, the country’s highest court blocked it, citing concerns about curtailing freedom of expression. President Emmanuel Macron has vowed to pass a new version before he leaves office next spring.
今年7月，法國通過了一項限制15歲以下人群訪問社群媒體的法律，成為首個這樣做的歐洲國家。該國原計劃最快在9月份實施該法律的部分內容，但本月，該國最高法院以對限制言論自由的擔憂為由否決了該法律。總統埃馬紐埃爾·馬克宏誓稱要在明年春天卸任前通過一個新版本。
廣告
New Zealand
紐西蘭
Prime Minister Christopher Luxon of New Zealand has called for restrictions on social media and this week said the government was introducing such a measure. But the difficulties other countries have faced in implementation have bolstered the position of those in New Zealand who oppose the limits, and it is not clear when or if any legislation may pass.
紐西蘭總理克里斯托弗·盧克森呼籲對社群媒體實施限制，並在本週表示政府正在出台一項此類措施。但其他國家在實施過程中遇到的困難增強了紐西蘭國內反對這些限制的人士的立場，目前尚不清楚相關立法何時或是否能獲得通過。
