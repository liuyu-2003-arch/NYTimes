社群媒體成癮訴訟：Meta以最高171億美元達成和解

Meta to Pay Up to $17.1 Billion in Landmark Settlement Over Social Media Addiction Claims

CECILIA KANG, ELI TAN • 2026-08-27
Meta on Wednesday reached a landmark settlement with 47 states, the District of Columbia and U.S. territories, agreeing to pay up to $17.1 billion in penalties and make major changes to its products over claims it endangered children with addictive social media platforms.In a dramatic capitulation, the owner of Facebook and Instagram agreed to the financial penalties for violating federal child privacy and states’ consumer protection laws, the states announced. Meta also agreed to limit how long teenagers can spend on its platforms and to bans on features that stoke mental health issues, striking at the heart of the company’s business of engagement for advertising.
週三，Meta與47個州、哥倫比亞特區及美國各屬地達成了一項具有里程碑意義的和解協議，同意支付最高達171億美元的罰款，並對其產品進行重大調整，該公司被指控通過令人上癮的社群媒體平台危害兒童。各州宣布，這家擁有Facebook和Instagram的公司同意因違反聯邦兒童隱私法和各州消費者保護法接受經濟處罰，這是一次戲劇性的讓步。Meta還同意限制青少年在其平台上的使用時長，並禁止使用會引發心理健康問題的功能，這直接打擊了該公司以用戶參與度換取廣告收入的核心業務。
“The focus of this case was to protect our kids: stopping notifications and alerts at night and when they are in school, encouraging them to take breaks from social media, protecting them against harmful features,” said Colorado’s attorney general, Phil Weiser, in a statement. The agreement exceeded what most courts might order, he added.
「此案的重點是保護我們的孩子：在夜間和他們在校期間停止推送通知和提醒，鼓勵他們在使用社群媒體時休息片刻，保護他們免受有害功能的影響，」科羅拉多州總檢察長菲爾·韋瑟在聲明中表示。他補充說，該協議超出了大多數法院可能作出的裁決範圍。
The settlement effectively ends a bellwether federal trial in the U.S. Northern District of California in Oakland, where California, Colorado, Kentucky and New Jersey were seeking roughly $200 billion over accusations that Meta harmed children. The states filed their agreement with Meta on Wednesday morning in that court, and Judge Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers approved it.
這項和解實際上結束了在美國加州北區聯邦地區法院（位於奧克蘭市）一場具有風向標意義的聯邦審判。在該案中，加利福尼亞州、科羅拉多州、肯塔基州和紐澤西州指控Meta傷害兒童，尋求約2000億美元的賠償。週三上午，各州在該法院提交了與Meta達成的協議，法官伊馮娜·岡薩雷斯·羅傑斯批准了該協議。
Separately, Meta said on Wednesday that it settled with Texas for about $1 billion over similar allegations. The company still faces numerous other lawsuits from school districts and individuals, some of which are scheduled for trial in the coming months.
此外，Meta在週三表示，它已就類似指控與得克薩斯州達成了約10億美元的和解。該公司仍面臨來自學區和個人的眾多其他訴訟，其中一些定於未來幾個月內開庭審理。
廣告
Meta’s stock rose on the news, closing up just over 1 percent. The company is valued at $1.47 trillion and most recently generated $60.8 billion in quarterly revenue.
受此消息影響，Meta的股價上漲，收盤漲幅略超1%。該公司估值為1.47萬億美元，最近一個季度的營收為608億美元。
The settlement could signal an inflection point for a social media industry that has largely escaped regulatory scrutiny over the harms its products have caused children. The amount, paid in installments over 10 years, is one of the highest ever reached by a tech company to states.
這項和解可能標誌著社群媒體行業的一個轉折點，此前該行業的產品對兒童造成傷害的問題在很大程度上逃脫了監管審查。這筆分10年分期支付的款項是科技公司向各州支付的最高和解金額之一。
“Meta wouldn’t settle unless it sees the writing on the wall and feels really exposed,” said Nora Freeman Engstrom, a law professor at Stanford University.
史丹佛大學法學教授諾拉·弗里曼·恩格斯特羅姆說：「除非Meta覺得大勢已去，並感到自己真的已經無處可藏，否則它不會和解。」
The full value of Meta’s payout depends on whether other social media companies also settle with the states and agree to financial penalties and product changes. Meta will initially pay about $12 billion. It will pay an additional $5 billion if Snap, TikTok and YouTube also settle with the states and agree to financial penalties and product changes.
Meta最終需支付的總額取決於其他社群媒體公司是否也與各州達成和解，並同意接受經濟處罰和產品調整。Meta最初將支付約120億美元。如果Snap、TikTok和YouTube也與各州達成和解，並同意接受經濟處罰和產品調整，Meta還將額外支付50億美元。
In a call with reporters on Wednesday, Meta’s legal team said the company negotiated the terms of its settlement to require the other companies to join, setting industry standards that would not single out Meta.
在週三與記者的電話會議中，Meta的法律團隊表示，該公司在協商和解條款時要求其他公司加入，以確立不單獨針對Meta一家的行業標準。
Meta wants to ensure “teens have a safe and productive experience on our platforms,” and it “partnered with state attorneys general to set a new industry standard,” C.J. Mahoney, Meta’s chief legal officer, wrote in a blog post. He implored other companies to settle.
Meta希望確保「青少年在我們的平台上得到安全、富有成效的體驗」，並且它「與各州總檢察長合作制定了新的行業標準」，Meta首席法務官CJ·馬霍尼在一篇部落格文章中寫道。他懇請其他公司也達成和解。
“This framework will only work if all our peers join us,” he added. “Because teens move fluidly across dozens of apps, we need an industrywide solution.”
「只有所有同行都加入我們，這一框架才能奏效，」他還說。「因為青少年會在幾十個應用程序之間流暢地切換，我們需要一個全行業的解決方案。」
廣告
The agreement may also play into other legal claims against Meta, TikTok, YouTube and Snap, the owner of Snapchat. States, schools and teenagers have filed thousands of lawsuits against the tech companies, accusing them of targeting young users with product features that are as addictive as cigarettes or digital casinos, drawing inspiration in part from a legal playbook used against Big Tobacco in the 1990s.
該協議也可能會對針對Meta、TikTok、YouTube以及Snapchat的所有者Snap的其他法律訴求產生影響。各州、學校和青少年已經對這些科技公司提起了數以千計的訴訟，指控它們針對年輕用戶推出具有成癮性的產品功能，其成癮性堪比香煙或數字賭場。這些訴訟在一定程度上借鑒了20世紀90年代針對大型煙草公司所採用的法律策略。
The tech companies have argued that they have added safety features for children and are protected by a law, Section 230 of the Communications Decency Act, which shields companies from liability for what their users post.
科技公司則辯稱，他們已經為兒童增加了安全功能，並受到《通信規範法》第230條的保護，該條款免除了公司對用戶發布內容的責任。
Some of the lawsuits were grouped into a series of bellwether personal injury cases brought by individual teenagers in California state court; some are scheduled for trial in October.
其中部分訴訟被歸入加利福尼亞州法院一系列由青少年個人提起、具有風向標意義的人身傷害案件；部分案件定於10月開庭審理。
A separate group of federal cases is being heard in Oakland, of which some states were a part. School districts have also brought cases accusing the companies of public nuisance for the costs that schools have shouldered from social media addiction.
另一組聯邦案件正在奧克蘭審理，上述部分州也參與了其中。學區也提起訴訟，指控這些公司構成了公共妨害，因為學校承擔了由社群媒體成癮帶來的成本。
Meta has faced an uphill battle with some of these lawsuits. In March, Meta and YouTube lost their first personal injury case, paying $6 million in damages. Separately, a New Mexico judge ordered Meta this month to pay penalties totaling nearly $1 billion in a case brought by the state attorney general for violations of consumer protection laws.
Meta在應對其中一些訴訟時面臨艱巨挑戰。今年3月，Meta和YouTube在首起人身傷害訴訟中敗訴，支付了600萬美元的賠償金。另外，新墨西哥州的一名法官本月在一樁由州總檢察長提起的違反消費者保護法的案件中，裁定Meta支付總計近10億美元的罰款。
Meta said it will continue to fight the other lawsuits, and that it is confident it can quash any additional personal injury claims.
Meta表示，它將繼續對抗其他訴訟，並有信心駁回任何額外的人身傷害索賠。
The company’s decision to settle acknowledges its vulnerability as the trials have shaped a negative narrative about its treatment of young users. Mark Zuckerberg, Meta’s chief executive, has had to defend himself against evidence that he knew of harms caused to children. He had been expected to testify again at the trial in Oakland.
該公司決定和解，相當於承認了自身的脆弱性，因為這些庭審已塑造出關於它對待年輕用戶方式的負面輿論。Meta首席執行官馬克·祖克柏不得不針對「他知曉對兒童造成的傷害」這一證據進行辯護。他原定還將在奧克蘭的庭審中再次作證。
Last month, Meta said that it had spent about $2 billion in the second quarter alone to handle its legal challenges.
上個月，Meta表示，僅在第二季度，它就花費了約20億美元來應對法律挑戰。
廣告
The agreement also ends a trial brought by Tennessee’s state attorney general against Meta for consumer protection violations.
這項協議還結束了田納西州總檢察長因Meta違反消費者保護法對其提起的訴訟。
The settlement will effectively force Meta to make major product changes for all U.S. teen users. The company agreed to interrupt endless scrolling and to impose two-hour daily time limits on Instagram and Facebook. To avoid addictive use and sleep interruptions, the company will limit usage between midnight and 6 a.m. and silence notifications during school hours of 8 a.m. to 3 p.m.
這項和解協議將有效迫使Meta針對所有美國青少年用戶實施重大產品調整。該公司同意中斷無限頁面滾動功能，並對Instagram和Facebook設置每天兩小時的使用時間限制。為了避免成癮使用和睡眠中斷，該公司將限制午夜至早上6點之間的使用，並在早上8點至下午3點的上課時間屏蔽通知。
Meta will also limit features that psychologists link to negative social comparisons, such as beauty filters and a tallying of the “like” button clicks. It will also strengthen age verification tools and parental controls.
Meta還將限制被心理學家認為與負面社會攀比有關的功能，例如美顏濾鏡和「點讚」次數統計。它還將加強年齡驗證工具和家長控制功能。
The agreement between Meta and the states also imposes tougher penalties and higher costs if other social media companies reach similar agreements. For example, if YouTube and TikTok also settle litigation previously brought by the states, Meta would reduce its daily time limits on Instagram and Facebook to one hour from two.
Meta與各州達成的協議還規定，若其他社群媒體公司也達成類似和解，Meta將面臨更嚴厲的處罰和更高的成本。例如，若YouTube和TikTok也就此前各州提起的訴訟達成和解，Meta將把Instagram和Facebook的每日使用時長限制從兩小時減至一小時。
The aim is to pressure the other social media companies into agreeing to the same rules, creating an industry standard that doesn’t overly penalize or single out Meta.
這樣做的目的是向其他社群媒體公司施壓，迫使它們同意相同的規則，從而創建一個不會過度懲罰或單獨針對Meta的行業標準。
“Meta, while they are a major player in the industry and they have visited enormous mental health harms on kids through their products and their designs, they are not the only player in the industry doing so, and others rightfully must be held accountable,” California Attorney General Rob Bonta said in a call with reporters on Wednesday.
「Meta雖然是該行業的主要參與者，並且通過其產品和設計對兒童造成了巨大的心理健康損害，但他們並不是業內唯一這樣做的公司，其他公司理應承擔相應責任，」加利福尼亞州總檢察長羅布·邦塔在週三與記者的電話會議上表示。
廣告
Snap and YouTube, which is owned by Alphabet, declined to comment. TikTok did not immediately respond to requests for comment.
Snap和Alphabet旗下的YouTube拒絕置評。TikTok沒有立即回應置評請求。
A settlement with dozens of states would indicate that for Meta, “the cost of maintaining infinite scroll, auto play, filters, etc., is just too big a risk for the company,” said Stuart Benjamin, a professor at Duke School of Law and the co-director of Duke’s Center for Innovation Policy. “And they have concluded that they’ve just got to end that risk one way or another.”
杜克大學法學院教授兼杜克創新政策中心聯合主任斯圖爾特·本傑明說，與數十個州達成和解表明，對Meta來說，「維持無限滾動、自動播放、濾鏡等功能的代價對公司來說是一個太大的風險」。「而且他們已經得出結論，必須想方設法消除這一風險。」
Separately, Meta’s settlement with Texas requires the company to make product changes similar to those in the agreement reached with other states. The $1 billion will help fund things like youth mental health services and grants for Texas schools, the state said in news release.
另外，Meta與得克薩斯州達成的和解協議要求該公司對其產品進行類似於與其他州達成的協議中的調整。得州在新聞稿中表示，這10億美元將幫助資助青少年心理健康服務和面向得州學校的撥款等項目。
That agreement brings Meta’s combined settlements with states to over $18 billion.
這一協議使Meta與各州達成的和解總金額超過了180億美元。
