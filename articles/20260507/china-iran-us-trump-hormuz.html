王毅在北京會見伊朗外長

In Talks With Iran, China Calls for Opening of Strait of Hormuz

儲百亮 • 2026-05-07
China’s top diplomat met with Iran’s foreign minister on Wednesday and called for greater efforts to open the Strait of Hormuz and for an end to the war, after the Trump administration pressed Beijing to help end Tehran’s chokehold over the waterway.
中國最高外交官週三與伊朗外長會面，呼籲各方加大努力推動霍爾木茲海峽重新開放，還呼籲停止戰爭。此前，川普政府曾向北京施壓，要求其協助結束德黑蘭對霍爾木茲海峽的封鎖。
Meeting in Beijing with his Iranian counterpart, Abbas Araghchi, Foreign Minister Wang Yi of China said that stopping the war was a matter of urgency, according to an official summary of their meeting issued by Xinhua, China’s main state news agency.
據中國主要的官方通訊社新華社發布的新聞通稿，在與伊朗外長阿巴斯·阿拉格奇於北京會晤時，中國外長王毅表示，全面止戰刻不容緩。
While Mr. Wang was careful not to blame Iran for the crisis over the strait — a shipping route vital for global oil, natural gas and other cargo — he emphasized China’s concerns about the blockade. The standoff over the route has sent oil prices to new highs, with broader global economic shocks.
雖然王毅強調了中方對封鎖的關切，但措辭謹慎，避免將這場危機的責任直接歸咎於伊朗。霍爾木茲海峽是全球石油、天然氣及其他貨物運輸的重要航線，這場對峙已將油價推至新高，並對全球經濟造成了更廣泛的衝擊。
“China believes that a comprehensive cessation of hostilities should not be delayed, any reigniting of hostilities is even less desirable, and sticking to negotiations is particularly important,” Mr. Wang said in the talks, according to the Xinhua summary.
據新華社通稿，王毅在會談中表示：「中方認為，全面止戰刻不容緩，重啟戰端更不可取，堅持談判尤為重要。」
Mr. Wang said that the “international community shares a common concern for restoring normal, safe passage” through the Strait of Hormuz, and that China “hopes the parties concerned will respond to the strong calls from the international community” over reopening the strait.
王毅表示，「國際社會對恢復海峽正常安全通行有共同關切」，中國「希望當事方儘快回應國際社會的強烈呼聲」，推動海峽重新開放。
Mr. Araghchi is the first senior Iranian official to visit China since the United States and Israel’s war with Iran broke out in late February. His meeting with Mr. Wang was another step in Beijing’s measured efforts to encourage an end to the war, while seeking to avoid being entangled in the conflict.
阿拉格奇是自2月底美國及以色列對伊朗開戰以來，首位訪華的伊朗高級官員。他與王毅的此次會面是北京在鼓勵結束戰爭的同時，努力避免捲入衝突的又一審慎舉措。
The Chinese account of their talks suggested that Beijing is holding to that position, even as it becomes more worried about the economic damage from the blockade.
中方對雙方會談的表述表明，儘管北京對封鎖造成的經濟損失日益擔憂，但仍堅守這一立場。
Mr. Wang also reiterated Beijing’s position that Iran should have the right to peaceful development of nuclear energy, while not acquiring nuclear weapons, and said: “China supports Iran’s efforts to safeguard its national sovereignty and security.”
王毅還重申了北京的立場，即伊朗有和平利用核能、但不發展核武器的正當權利，並表示：「中方支持伊方維護國家主權和安全。」
Mr. Araghchi sought to reassure his Chinese hosts that Iran is committed to diplomacy, saying in a statement about his meeting with Mr. Wang that he briefed his counterpart on the status of efforts to end the war, including the peace talks mediated by Pakistan.
阿拉格奇試圖向東道主表明，伊朗致力於通過外交途徑解決問題。他在介紹與王毅會面情況的聲明中表示，已就結束戰爭的努力進展向王毅作了通報，包括由巴基斯坦斡旋的和談。
Iran was as “serious and steadfast” in negotiations as it has been in its defending itself militarily, the statement from Mr. Araghchi’s office said.
阿拉格奇辦公室的聲明稱，伊朗在談判中與在軍事自衛上同樣「認真而堅定」。
Beijing is preparing to host President Trump for a summit with President Xi Jinping next week, during which the leaders are likely to discuss the Iran war and the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz to allow commercial ships and oil tankers through.
北京正在為川普總統下週與習近平的峰會做準備，兩國領導人屆時可能就伊朗戰爭及重新開放霍爾木茲海峽、允許商船和油輪通行等議題展開討論。
Iran has effectively blocked the strait for two months since the war started. In addition, the U.S. Navy has imposed a blockade on ships going to or from Iran. On Tuesday, Secretary of State Marco Rubio told reporters in Washington that China should press Mr. Araghchi to open the waterway during his visit.
自戰爭爆發以來，伊朗已實際封鎖該海峽長達兩個月。與此同時，美國海軍也對進出伊朗的船隻實施封鎖。週二，美國國務卿魯比奧在華盛頓向記者表示，中國應在阿拉格奇訪華期間向其施壓，要求開放這條水道。
廣告
“I hope the Chinese tell him what he needs to be told, and that is that what you are doing in the straits is causing you to be globally isolated,” Mr. Rubio said, referring to Mr. Araghchi. “It is in China’s interest that Iran stop closing the straits. It’s harming China as well.”
「我希望中方告訴他那些他該聽的話，那就是，你在海峽的所作所為正導致你們陷入全球孤立，」魯比奧在談及阿拉格奇時表示。「讓伊朗停止封鎖海峽符合中國的利益。封鎖海峽同樣也在損害中國。」
On Tuesday, Mr. Trump announced that he was briefly pausing the day-old U.S. operation to escort commercial ships through the strait, citing what he said was progress toward an agreement with Iran.
週二，川普宣布暫停剛啟動一天的美軍護送商船通過海峽的行動，他表示，這是因為與伊朗的協議談判取得了進展。
Iran has allowed ships carrying Iranian oil to pass, including ones bound for China. Even so, surging global energy prices have hurt China’s economy. Foreign officials have said that China played a backroom role in persuading Iran to accept the terms of the brittle cease-fire that was reached in April.
伊朗已允許運載伊朗石油的船隻通行，包括開往中國的船隻。即便如此，全球能源價格的飆升仍對中國經濟造成了衝擊。外國官員表示，中國在幕後勸說伊朗接受了4月達成的那份脆弱的停火協議條款。
Even as China tries to keep ties with the United States steady ahead of Mr. Trump’s summit with Mr. Xi, it has pushed back against U.S. pressure over Iran. After the U.S. Treasury Department imposed sanctions on an independent Chinese refinery that buys large amounts of Iranian oil, China told its companies not to comply with the U.S. sanctions.
中國在努力維護中美關係穩定、為川普與習近平的峰會創造條件之際，也在頂住美國就伊朗問題的施壓。美國財政部對一家大量購買伊朗石油的中國民營煉油廠實施制裁後，中國要求本國企業不得遵守美方的制裁措施。
