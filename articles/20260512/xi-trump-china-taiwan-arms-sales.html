習近平或就對台軍售問題向川普施壓

Xi Poised to Press Trump on Arms Sales to Taiwan

儲百亮 • 2026-05-12
The United States’ stance on Taiwan has rested for decades on a complex latticework of policies designed to support the island democracy while avoiding treating it officially as an independent country, a step that would enrage Beijing.
幾十年來，美國的對台政策一直建立在一個複雜的政策網路之上，其目的是支持這個島嶼民主政體，同時避免正式將其視為一個獨立國家——這一步將激怒北京。
Many in Taiwan are holding their breath for what may happen to that delicate structure when President Trump, with his off-the-cuff, transactional ways, meets China’s leader, Xi Jinping, in Beijing for a two-day summit starting Thursday. Mr. Xi appears poised to lecture Mr. Trump on U.S. support for Taiwan, especially weapons sales.
許多台灣人正屏息以待，當川普總統以其即興、交易式的作風於週四開始在北京與中國領導人習近平舉行為期兩天的峰會時，這個微妙的結構將會發生什麼。習近平似乎已準備好就美國對台灣的支持——尤其是武器銷售——向川普發出告誡。
Mr. Trump and his officials have said that his trip to Beijing will be focused on trade and investment. But China’s foreign minister, Wang Yi, and other officials have indicated that they also expect the two presidents to discuss Taiwan, the issue that could most likely ignite a war between their countries. China claims Taiwan is its territory, and could use armed force to take it, while the United States says it could intervene to defend Taiwan, a longtime partner.
川普及其官員表示，他此次北京之行將重點關注貿易和投資。但中國外交部長王毅和其他官員已表示，他們也期待兩位總統討論台灣問題——這是最有可能引發兩國之間戰爭的議題。中國聲稱台灣是其領土，並可能動用武力奪取它；而美國則表示，可能介入以保衛台灣這個長期夥伴。
Beijing’s Expectations
北京的期待
廣告
“The Taiwan question is at the very core of China’s core interests,” Lin Jian, a spokesman for China’s Foreign Ministry said last week when asked if the issue would be a priority for Mr. Xi at the summit.
中國外交部發言人林劍上週在被問及台灣問題是否會成為習近平在峰會的優先事項時表示：「台灣問題是中國核心利益中的核心。」
Taiwan often comes up in talks between Chinese and U.S. leaders. But the summit’s setting in Beijing will give Mr. Xi more opportunity and time to make his case, said Ryan Hass, a former director for China at the National Security Council.
台灣問題經常出現在中美領導人會談中。但曾任美國國家安全委員會中國事務主任的何雷恩(Ryan Hass)指出，在北京舉行的這次峰會將給習近平更多機會和時間來闡述中方立場。
Mr. Xi may try to persuade Mr. Trump to say that he opposes Taiwanese independence. That could be a setback for Taiwan’s president, Lai Ching-te, though he has said he has no plans to declare independence. Successive U.S. presidents have said they “do not support” independence for Taiwan. Declaring “opposition” may suggest greater sympathy with Beijing’s view that Mr. Lai’s government is the side stirring up trouble, experts said.
習近平可能會試圖說服川普公開表示反對台灣獨立。這可能對台灣總統賴清德構成打擊，儘管他已表示沒有計劃宣布獨立。歷屆美國總統都表示「不支持」台灣獨立。而專家們說，明確表示「反對」可能會顯得更傾向於北京的觀點，即賴清德的政府是挑起事端的一方。
“It could be easily used by China to claim that the U.S. is taking Beijing’s side, and even create new diplomatic pressure on Taiwan,” said Chen Kuan-ting, a lawmaker from Mr. Lai’s Democratic Progressive Party.
賴清德所在的民進黨立法委員陳冠廷表示：「這容易被中國拿來宣傳成美方站到北京立場，甚至對台灣形成新的外交壓力。」
Large U.S. Arms Purchases at Stake
巨額美國軍售面臨風險
Mr. Xi’s main focus in raising Taiwan will probably be to persuade Mr. Trump to slow, or ultimately reduce, U.S. arms sales to the island, experts from China and Washington have said. In a phone call in February, Mr. Xi urged Mr. Trump to handle the issue of arms sales with “extreme caution.”
來自中國和華盛頓的專家表示，習近平提出台灣問題的主要重點，可能是說服川普放緩、甚至最終減少美國對台灣的武器銷售。今年2月的一次通話中，習近平敦促川普「慎重」處理武器銷售問題。
廣告
The Trump administration approved $11 billion in arms sales to Taiwan late last year, drawing condemnation from Beijing, which quickly held a two-day military exercise near Taiwan. Another arms sale package worth around $14 billion is awaiting final approval from Mr. Trump, who has held off making a decision for months already.
川普政府去年底批准了對台價值110億美元的武器銷售，引發北京強烈譴責，並迅速在台灣附近舉行了兩天的軍事演習。另一批價值約140億美元的武器銷售方案正等待川普最終批准，他已經拖延數月，尚未作出決定。
China wants Mr. Trump to delay approval, and ultimately to persuade him and his successors to roll back broader military support to Taiwan, said Xin Qiang, the director of the Center for Taiwan Studies at Fudan University in Shanghai.
上海復旦大學台灣研究中心主任信強表示，中國希望川普推遲批准，並最終說服他及其繼任者縮減對台灣的更廣泛軍事支持。
“But China also knows that’s difficult right now,” he said, “so they hope at least for delays and then a reduction — a reduction in the monetary value of the arms, in the scale, in the quality of the weapons sold.”
「但中國也知道目前這很困難，」他說，「所以他們至少希望能拖延，然後逐步減少——減少武器銷售的金額、規模和武器質量。」
China’s Economic Levers
中國的經濟槓桿
Professor Xin acknowledged that coaxing Mr. Trump to significantly curtail U.S. arms sales would be a long shot. But if the U.S. sells more weapons to Taiwan, he added, “China certainly won’t be as enthusiastic about purchasing goods from the United States like agricultural products or Boeing aircraft.”
信強承認，要說服川普大幅削減對台軍售是一項長期而艱難的任務。但他補充說，如果美國繼續向台灣出售更多武器，「從美國採購農產品啊，購買波音飛機啊，那肯定也不會那麼積極。」
Many in Washington, including from both parties in Congress, support weapons sales to Taiwan. Taiwanese lawmakers last week approved a $25 billion special budget to cover the two big weapons packages, including the one waiting in the wings, adding to pressure on Mr. Trump. In a letter sent on Friday, a bipartisan group of eight U.S. senators urged Mr. Trump to move ahead on it.
華盛頓的許多人士，包括國會兩黨議員，都支持對台武器銷售。台灣立法委員上週批准了一項250億美元的特別預算，用於支付這兩筆大型武器採購方案（包括正在等待批准的那一筆），這給川普增加了壓力。上週五，由八名兩党參議員組成的小組致信川普，敦促他推進這筆140億美元的武器銷售案。
廣告
Secretary of State Marco Rubio, who will accompany Mr. Trump to Beijing, last week brushed off speculation that the summit could produce a shift in U.S. policy toward Taiwan. “The Chinese understand our position on that topic. We understand theirs,” Mr. Rubio told a news conference at the White House.
將陪同川普前往北京的國務卿馬可·魯比奧上週在白宮記者會上淡化了峰會可能改變美國對台政策的猜測。「中國人理解我們在這個議題上的立場。我們也理解他們的立場，」魯比奧說。
Taiwanese officials have taken reassurance from such comments.
台灣官員從這些表態中獲得了一定安心。
“The U.S. continues to reiterate in both public and private settings that its policy toward Taiwan has not changed,” Tsai Ming-yen, the director-general of Taiwan’s National Security Bureau, told lawmakers in Taipei last week.
台灣國家安全局局長蔡明彥上週在台北告訴立法委員：「美方在這種公開或私下的場合也不斷重申對於台灣的政策並沒有改變。」
Still, Mr. Xi could hold out the lure of economic deals and more summits later this year, including in Washington, to try to get Mr. Trump to hold back on the latest proposed arms package, said Mr. Hass, the former National Security Council official, now a China expert at the Brookings Institution.
儘管如此，前國家安全委員會官員、現布魯金斯學會中國專家何雷恩表示，習近平仍可能以今年晚些時候更多經濟協議和在華盛頓舉行更多峰會的誘惑，來試圖讓川普暫緩最新提出的武器銷售案。
“As a result, I have low expectations that Trump will approve another tranche of arms sales to Taiwan between now and this fall,” he said.
「因此，我認為從現在開始到今年秋季這段時間，川普不太會批准另一批對台軍售，」他說。

儲百亮(Chris Buckley)是《紐約時報》首席中國記者，自台北報導中國和台灣問題，重點關注政治、社會變革以及安全和軍事問題。
