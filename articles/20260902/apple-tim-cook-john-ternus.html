特納斯正式接任蘋果CEO，面臨考驗

Apple Begins Its Next Balancing Act as New C.E.O. Takes Over

KALLEY HUANG • 2026-09-02
John Ternus became Apple’s chief executive on Tuesday, succeeding Tim Cook, the company’s leader for the last 15 years. The long-anticipated handoff, Mr. Cook has said, will be “perfectly smooth.”
約翰·特納斯於週二正式成為蘋果公司首席執行官，接替了過去15年一直領導該公司的蒂姆·庫克。庫克曾表示，這場備受期待的權力交接將「無比順利」。
But the changing of the guard could still have some bumps. Mr. Ternus will have to guide Apple through an unusual amount of leadership churn. He will have to stem an exodus of talent to companies like OpenAI. And he will have to distinguish himself from Mr. Cook, who will remain at Apple as its executive chair.
但這輪交接可能仍遇到一些波折。特納斯不得不帶領蘋果度過非同尋常的高管層動盪期。他必須遏制人才大量流失到向OpenAI等公司。同時，他還必須展現出與庫克不同的個人特質，後者將繼續留在蘋果擔任執行董事長。
Most of all, Mr. Ternus will have to perform a balancing act: displaying the financial predictability and discipline that have been a hallmark of the Cook era while showing that Apple can still create new products that capture the imagination of consumers.
最重要的是，特納斯必須把握好一種平衡：既要展現出庫克時代標誌性的財務可預測性和紀律性，又要證明蘋果依然能夠創造出令消費者心馳神往的新產品。
Mr. Ternus will have to do so while steering Apple through arguably the biggest technology shift in a generation. At its annual hardware event this month, Apple is expected to introduce a foldable iPhone — its most significant change in 19 years to its most important product. The company is also taking another stab at weaving artificial intelligence through its products.
與此同時，特納斯還必須引領蘋果度過這一代人中堪稱最重大的技術變革。在本月的年度硬件發布會上，蘋果預計將推出一款可摺疊iPhone——這是其19年來對其最核心產品做出的最重大改變。該公司也在再次嘗試將人工智慧技術全面融入其產品線中。
廣告
“Apple is going to have to face this idea of ‘What’s next?’” said Michael Gartenberg, a consumer technology analyst who worked as a product marketer at Apple from 2013 to 2016. The task for Mr. Ternus, he added, is to “bring Apple back to being a cool brand even though it’s a multitrillion-dollar company, show that you are able to innovate.”
「蘋果將不得不面對『下一步是什麼？』這個問題，」曾在2013年至2016年擔任蘋果產品營銷人員的消費科技分析師麥可·加滕伯格表示。他還說，特納斯的任務是「讓蘋果重新成為一個酷炫的品牌，儘管它已經是一家價值數萬億美元的公司，要證明你仍然有能力進行創新」。
Apple, which has waffled over how aggressively to embrace A.I., will tackle those questions with a changing management team. Over the past four months, Mr. Ternus, 51, has started to install his own executives, mixing a roster of Apple veterans with newcomers.
蘋果公司此前在是否應積極擁抱人工智慧的問題上舉棋不定，如今將由一個不斷調整的管理團隊來應對這些問題。過去四個月裡，51歲的特納斯已經開始任命自己的高管，組建了一個由蘋果資深員工和新面孔混合組成的團隊。
“Apple is a company that’s used to having a consistent brain trust,” said Jennifer Chatman, the dean of the Haas School of Business at the University of California, Berkeley, and a director of its Center for Workplace Culture and Innovation. “The challenge there is, are you getting enough new ideas in?”
「蘋果是一家習慣於擁有穩定智囊團的公司，」加州大學柏克萊分校哈斯商學院院長兼職場文化與創新中心主任珍妮佛·查特曼表示。「面臨的挑戰在於，你是否引進了足夠多的新想法？」
Apple declined to comment.
蘋果公司拒絕置評。
This summer, Apple hired Nate Gatten from American Airlines to lead government affairs, replacing Kate Adams, who will retire this year. Laura Legros, a hardware engineering vice president and deputy of Mr. Ternus’s before retiring from Apple in 2022, has rejoined the company, three people familiar with her hiring said, speaking on the condition of anonymity. Ms. Legros, who reports to Mr. Ternus, could act as his adviser and emissary to various parts of the company, the three people said.
今年夏天，蘋果從美國航空聘請了內特·加滕來領導政府事務部門，接替將於今年退休的凱特·亞當斯。三位要求而匿名發言的知情人士透露，2022年從蘋果退休的勞拉·萊格羅斯現已重返公司，她曾擔任硬件工程副總裁併曾是特納斯的副手。這三名知情人士表示，向特納斯彙報工作的萊格羅斯可能會擔任他的顧問，並作為其與公司各部門之間的聯絡人。
More new leaders could soon join Mr. Gatten and Ms. Legros. Nearly half of Mr. Ternus’s executive team is nearing the traditional retirement age, although none of the members have publicly said they are looking to step down. There have been executive departures in addition to Ms. Adams, including Jennifer Bailey, the head of Apple Pay, who will retire this year. She joined the company in 2003.
可能會有更多新的領導者很快加入加滕和萊格羅斯的行列。特納斯的高管團隊中近一半的人已接近傳統的退休年齡，儘管目前還沒有成員公開表示希望卸任。除了亞當斯之外，還有其他高管離職，其中包括將於今年退休的Apple Pay負責人珍妮佛·貝利。她於2003年加入公司。
廣告
A number of the management changes go to the heart of Apple’s important hardware organization, which was restructured in April. When Apple named Mr. Ternus as its next chief executive, the company also promoted Johny Srouji, a chips leader, to chief hardware officer. Apple’s hardware engineering team, which Mr. Ternus led and works on devices like the iPhone, has since merged with its hardware technologies teams, which Mr. Srouji led.
許多管理層變動觸及了蘋果重要硬件部門的核心，該部門在4月進行了重組。蘋果任命特納斯為下一任首席執行官時，還將晶片業務負責人約翰尼·斯魯吉晉陞為首席硬件官。此前由特納斯領導、負責iPhone等設備研發的蘋果硬件工程團隊現已與斯魯吉領導的硬件技術團隊合併。
Mr. Srouji’s ascent coincided with departures from the hardware organization. Brian Lynch, who led hardware for Apple’s smart home products, joined the smart ring maker Oura in March. Paul Meade, who led hardware for Apple’s augmented and virtual reality products, joined OpenAI in July.
斯魯吉的晉陞恰逢硬件部門出現人員離職潮。負責蘋果智能家居產品硬件的布萊恩·林奇於今年3月加入了智能戒指製造商Oura。負責蘋果增強現實和虛擬現實產品硬件的保羅·米德則於今年7月加入了OpenAI。
Bloomberg previously reported on aspects of the transition at Apple.
彭博社此前曾報導過蘋果公司過渡期的相關細節。
Apple has seen a rash of rank-and-file departures, as well. More than 400 of its former employees work at OpenAI, according to an intellectual property lawsuit that Apple filed against OpenAI in July.
蘋果也出現了大量普通員工離職的情況。根據蘋果在7月份對OpenAI提起的知識產權訴訟顯示，超過400名蘋果前員工目前在OpenAI工作。
Many of those exits happened in the months after OpenAI acquired IO, a design studio co-founded by Apple’s former longtime design chief, Jony Ive. OpenAI’s hardware division, led by another former Apple executive and IO founder, has hired scores of engineers from Apple, according to the lawsuit. OpenAI moved to dismiss the lawsuit in August.
其中許多人員流失發生在OpenAI收購IO工作室之後的幾個月裡，IO是由蘋果前長期設計主管喬尼·艾夫聯合創立的設計工作室。訴訟文件指出，OpenAI的硬件部門由另一位前蘋果高管兼IO創始人領導，該部門已從蘋果聘請了數十名工程師。OpenAI在8月份提出動議，要求駁回該訴訟。
(The New York Times has sued OpenAI and Microsoft, claiming copyright infringement of news content related to A.I. systems. The two companies have denied those claims.)
（《紐約時報》已起訴OpenAI和微軟，指控其在人工智慧系統方面侵犯了新聞內容的版權。這兩家公司均否認了這些指控。）
廣告
Mr. Ternus will also have to define himself as chief executive, even with Mr. Cook as executive chair. Apple has said Mr. Cook, 65, “will assist with certain aspects of the company, including engaging with policymakers around the world.”
即便有庫克擔任執行董事長，特納斯也必須確立自己作為首席執行官的威信。蘋果公司表示，65歲的庫克「將協助處理公司的某些事務，包括與世界各地的決策者進行溝通交流」。
Silicon Valley companies have long had chief executives step down and remain as board chairs, though the setup has yielded mixed results, said David Yoffie, a professor at Harvard Business School who has written case studies about Apple.
哈佛商學院撰寫過蘋果案例研究的教授戴維·尤菲表示，矽谷公司長期以來一直有首席執行官卸任後繼續留任董事會主席的傳統，儘管這種安排成效不一。
The test for Mr. Ternus and Mr. Cook, Mr. Yoffie added, will come if they diverge on strategy or their vision for Apple. For example, Mr. Ternus could decide to invest more deeply in A.I.; to restart Apple’s car project, which Mr. Cook killed in 2024; or to end Apple’s work on the Vision Pro, the mixed-reality headset released in 2024.
尤菲還說，如果特納斯和庫克在戰略或對蘋果的願景上出現分歧，真正的考驗就會到來。例如，特納斯可能會決定在人工智慧領域進行更深度的投資；重新啟動庫克於2024年終止的蘋果汽車項目；或者停止蘋果在2024年發布的混合現實頭顯Vision Pro上的開發工作。
“The question is, is Tim going to be comfortable with those changes?” Mr. Yoffie said. “Will John feel uncomfortable doing those things because Tim is still there?”
「問題在於，蒂姆能否接受這些變化？」尤菲說。「約翰會不會因為蒂姆還在，從而在推行這些事情的時候感到不安？」
So far, Mr. Cook and Mr. Ternus appear to be on the same page. In July, they attended the Sun Valley Conference in Idaho, a gathering of finance, media and technology leaders. That month, at Apple TV’s season premiere of “Ted Lasso” in Los Angeles, they both wore nearly matching navy suits with yellow pins on the right lapel.
到目前為止，庫克和特納斯似乎保持著步調一致。今年7月，他們共同出席了在愛達荷州舉行的太陽谷會議，這是一場匯聚金融、媒體和科技界領袖的盛會。同月，在洛杉磯舉行的Apple TV劇集《足球教練》(Ted Lasso)的新一季首映式上，他們倆穿著幾乎同款的藏青色西裝，右側翻領上別著黃色徽章。
“We really set this bar for the transition to be the best ever,” Mr. Cook said in an interview during the premiere. “We want it to be a textbook example, and that is exactly how it’s going. I feel so great about how it’s going.”
「我們確實為這次交接設定了『史上最佳』的標準，」庫克在首映式的採訪中說道。「我們希望它能成為一個教科書般的範例，而目前事情也正是這樣發展的。我對目前的進展感覺好極了。」

Kalley Huang是時報駐舊金山記者，報導蘋果公司和科技行業。
