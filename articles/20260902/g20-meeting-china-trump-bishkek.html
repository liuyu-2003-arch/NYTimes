兩場峰會折射中美博弈

China and the U.S. Offer Competing Showcases of Global Power

DAVID PIERSON • 2026-09-02
The Chinese leader Xi Jinping arrived in Central Asia this week with a message for a world increasingly unsettled by President Trump: China has friends and influence, and is prepared to help shape what comes next.
中國領導人習近平本週訪問中亞，向因川普總統而日益不安的世界傳遞出明確信號：中國擁有朋友與影響力，並已做好參與塑造未來的準備。
The scene unfolded at a security summit in Bishkek, the capital of Kyrgyzstan, where Mr. Xi gathered with the leaders of Russia and Iran. He presented Beijing as a source of stability and a champion of an “orderly multipolar world” that would replace the current U.S.-dominated order.
在吉爾吉斯斯坦首都比什凱克舉行的一場安全峰會上，習近平與俄羅斯和伊朗領導人會面。他將北京塑造為一個穩定的源泉，倡導用「有序多極世界」取代目前由美國主導的秩序。
If the Bishkek summit was designed to project solidarity, a meeting of the world’s most powerful countries in the mountains of Asheville, N.C., offered an image of fracture.
如果說比什凱克峰會旨在展現團結，那麼在北卡羅萊納州阿什維爾山區舉行的世界最強大國家的會議則呈現出分裂的景象。
European officials attending that meeting of the Group of 20 nations’ finance ministers and central bankers expressed outrage this week that the Trump administration had invited Russia, despite its war against Ukraine. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent claimed that Canada was too small to fight a trade war with the United States. And officials were already confronting the economic fallout from the U.S.-Israeli war with Iran, which disrupted the flow of oil and pushed global energy prices up and threatened to slow growth.
在本週的二十國集團財長和央行行長會議上，歐洲官員對川普政府不顧俄烏戰爭而邀請俄羅斯表示憤怒。美國財政部長史考特·貝森特稱，加拿大體量太小，無法與美國打貿易戰。同時，官員們還在應對美以對伊戰爭帶來的經濟衝擊，這場衝突干擾了石油供應，推高了全球能源價格，可能導致經濟增長放緩。
The simultaneous gatherings happening across the world reflected the broader contest over who will define the next phase of global order. China is using its solidarity with Russia and Iran, two countries at war with the West, to project its influence, giving Mr. Xi leverage as he prepared for a summit with Mr. Trump later this month. The United States, meanwhile, was straining the alliances and economic relationships that have underpinned decades of global leadership.
在全球兩地同時舉行的會議折射出關於誰將定義下一階段全球秩序的更廣泛博弈。中國正在利用與俄伊這兩個與西方交戰國家的團結來展現其影響力，為習近平本月晚些時候與川普舉行峰會積累籌碼。與此形成對比的是，美國正在損害數十年來作為其全球領導力基礎的同盟和經濟關係。
“China’s theory of the case for bending the international political order in its preferred direction is to amass support from the ‘global majority,’” said Ryan Hass, a former career diplomat and White House national security official now at the Brookings Institution.
「中國想要按照自己的意願重塑國際政治秩序，其核心策略是爭取『全球多數』國家的支持，」前職業外交官、前白宮國家安全官員、現任職於布魯金斯學會的何雷恩(Ryan Hass)表示。
廣告
“This is why Xi’s participation in these groupings is so important to Beijing,” he added. “It builds a narrative around China gaining momentum in building support for playing a more central role on the world stage.”
「這就是為什麼習近平參與這些集團對北京如此重要，」他補充說。「這種參與構建了一套敘事，即中國在贏得支持者方面勢頭漸強，逐步在世界舞台上發揮更核心的作用。」
In Bishkek, at the summit of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization, Mr. Xi met with the leaders of Kyrgyzstan, Mongolia and Uzbekistan, but his talks with President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia carried the most weight. Just days earlier, John Ratcliffe, the C.I.A. director, had made a secret trip to Moscow to urge the Kremlin to end the war in Ukraine. Mr. Xi told Mr. Putin: “The foundation of China-Russia relations will become ever stronger.”
在比什凱克舉行的上海合作組織峰會上，習近平會見了吉爾吉斯斯坦、蒙古和烏茲別克斯坦的領導人，但他與俄羅斯總統弗拉基米爾·V·普丁的會談最為關鍵。就在幾天前，中央情報局局長約翰·拉特克利夫剛剛祕密訪問莫斯科，敦促克里姆林宮結束烏克蘭戰爭。習近平對普丁表示：「中俄關係發展的根基將更加牢固。」
President Masoud Pezeshkian of Iran also attended the summit. Iranian media reported that he met with Mr. Xi on the sidelines, but they did not appear to have scheduled talks.
伊朗總統馬蘇德·佩澤希齊揚也出席了峰會，伊朗媒體報導稱，他與習近平在峰會期間見了面，但雙方似乎沒有安排會談。
Mr. Putin, however, held a meeting with the Iranian president, praising the Iranian people’s “courage and resilience” in fighting for its national interests. The Russian leader underscored how Moscow was coming to Tehran’s aid amid the conflict with the United States.
但普丁與伊朗總統舉行了會晤，讚揚伊朗人民在捍衛國家利益方面展現出的「勇氣和韌性「。這位俄羅斯領導人強調，在伊朗與美國發生衝突之際，莫斯科正向德黑蘭伸出援手。
“During this difficult time, we are trying to provide you with the assistance you need,” Mr. Putin said. “We have discussed this with you and are supplying you with the necessary goods.”
「在這段艱難時期，我們正努力為你們提供所需的幫助，」普丁表示，「我們已經就此進行了討論，並正在向你們供應必要的物資。」
Earlier in the day, Mr. Putin praised the consolidation of a new power center in the Shanghai Cooperation Organization, calling the grouping “one of the independent and influential centers of the emerging multipolar world.” Its member countries, he noted, are home to almost half of the planet’s population and more than a third of its gross domestic product.
當天早些時候，普丁讚揚了上海合作組織這一新權力中心的鞏固，稱該組織是「新興多極世界中獨立且有影響力的中心之一」。他指出，上合組織成員國擁有全球近一半的人口，經濟總量佔全球三分之一以上。
廣告
By comparison, the United States appears to be drifting away from its historical allies and weakening America’s ability to project strength globally. Mr. Trump has opened up a trade war with Canada and reduced war games with South Korea. Concerns are also growing that the United States is depleting its munitions in Iran and unable to meet its defense commitments around the world.
相比之下，美國似乎正在疏遠其傳統盟友，削弱了自身在全球投射實力的能力。川普對加拿大挑起了貿易戰，並減少了與韓國的軍演。人們還日益擔心美國正在伊朗耗盡其彈藥儲備，無法履行其在全球的防禦承諾。
Lars Klingbeil, the German vice chancellor and finance minister, told reporters at the G20 that it was “troubling” that the Trump administration had invited Russia’s finance minister, Anton Siluanov. Mr. Klingbeil said he scolded Mr. Siluanov during a session on Monday over Russia’s war in Ukraine. Many Europeans were also angered that the Treasury Department did not invite representatives from South Africa, a longtime G20 member and Africa’s largest economy.
德國副總理兼財政部長拉爾斯·克林拜爾在G20會議上告訴記者，川普政府邀請俄羅斯財長安東·西盧安諾夫的決定「令人不安」。克林拜爾表示，自己在週一的一場會議上就俄羅斯對烏克蘭的戰爭嚴厲指責了西盧安諾夫。許多歐洲人也對美國財政部沒有邀請來自南非（G20長期成員及非洲最大經濟體）的代表感到憤怒。
An Opportunity for China
中國的機會
Beijing is happy to seize the opportunity that Mr. Trump is presenting by alienating Washington’s partners. Wu Xinbo, an American studies scholar at Fudan University in Shanghai who advises China’s Foreign Ministry, said that Beijing had two objectives: to build a consensus so that the United States could no longer dictate the agenda alone and to take a larger role in powering the world economy. (China also attended the G20 meeting.)
北京樂於抓住川普疏遠華盛頓夥伴帶來的機會。上海復旦大學美國研究學者、中國外交部諮詢顧問吳心伯表示，北京有兩個目標：一是建立共識，使美國不再能獨霸主導議程；二是在推動世界經濟增長中發揮更大作用。（中國也出席了G20會議。）
“Now that the American engine is losing steam — or even generating negative side effects — China, as an economic engine, must step up and play a greater role in driving and leading global growth,” he said.
「既然美國發動機正在失去動力，甚至產生負面副作用，中國作為經濟發動機，必須挺身而出，在推動和引領全球增長方面發揮更大作用，」他說。
The meeting in Bishkek was one stop in a broader diplomatic campaign by Mr. Xi to work both sides of the emerging divide in global politics. He has hosted many Western leaders — including those from Germany, Britain and Canada — this year. He will head to Cairo next and is also expected to attend a summit of BRICS nations, a group of emerging economies led by China and India, in New Delhi.
比什凱克會議只是習近平更廣泛外交行動中的一站，他正在全球政治日益顯現的分歧兩側同時展開斡旋。今年以來，他已接待了包括德國、英國和加拿大在內的多位西方領導人。他接下來將前往開羅，預計還將出席在新德里舉行的金磚國家峰會（這是一個由中國和印度領銜的新興經濟體集團）。
As much as Mr. Xi would like to cast such groupings as a unified counterbalance to the West, disagreements and tensions still abound. Beijing and New Delhi have traditionally been at odds in BRICS over how to rival the United States, with India reluctant to be too confrontational.
儘管習近平希望將這些集團塑造為抗衡西方的統一力量，但分歧和對峙依然充斥其中。在金磚國家內部，北京和新德里傳統上就如何與美國競爭存有分歧，印度不願意表現得過於對抗。
廣告
In another sign of a crack, Prime Minister Narendra Modi of India urged Mr. Putin during talks on the sidelines of the summit in Kyrgyzstan to end his war in Ukraine for the sake of “humanity.” Mr. Putin told Mr. Modi that Russia was “moving in this direction,” according to Russian state media.
陣營存在裂痕的另一個跡象是，在吉爾吉斯斯坦峰會間隙，印度總理納倫德拉·莫迪出於「人道」考量敦促普丁結束烏克蘭戰爭。據俄羅斯官方媒體報導，普丁告訴莫迪，俄羅斯正「朝這個方向努力」。
The Bishkek summit also exposed the limits of the group’s solidarity. The Shanghai Cooperation Organization is not a military alliance. Beijing can offer diplomatic backing, but has been unwilling to commit forces to defend Russia or Iran.
比什凱克峰會也暴露了該集團團結的局限性。上海合作組織並不是一個軍事聯盟。北京可以提供外交支持，但一直不願意承諾出兵保衛俄羅斯或伊朗。
To China, that is the point of the arrangement, argues Zhou Bo, a retired People’s Liberation Army senior colonel who is now at Tsinghua University in Beijing.
中國人民解放軍退役大校、現任職於北京清華大學的周波認為，對中國來說，這正是該機制的關鍵。
“It is not based on alliance relationships, so it is not provocative,” he said. “That’s why people have no problem accepting it or engaging with it.”
「它不是建立在同盟關係的基礎上，因此不具挑釁性，」他說。「這就是為什麼大家樂於接受它或與之接觸。」
As Washington retreats and the world becomes increasingly multipolar, countries will choose to join more pragmatic forums with less strings attached, Mr. Zhou said. “Naturally everyone is hedging their bets,” he said.
隨著華盛頓後退以及世界變得日益多極化，周波說，各國將選擇加入附加條件更少、更為務實的論壇。「大家自然都在對衝風險，」他說。
Building Leverage Before Trump Summit
在川普峰會前積累籌碼
China’s diplomatic show of strength also reflects Mr. Xi’s need to widen his options before he meets with Mr. Trump in Washington later this month.
中國展示外交實力的舉動也反映出習近平需要在本月晚些時候於華盛頓會見川普之前拓寬其選擇。
廣告
Despite striking an economic truce with China, the Trump administration has continued to apply pressure by banning imports of Chinese robots and expanding sanctions on Chinese companies that import Iranian oil.
儘管與中國達成了經濟休戰，川普政府仍繼續施壓，禁止進口中國機器人，並擴大對進口伊朗石油的中國企業的制裁。
Trump officials have also moved to slow China’s advancement in artificial intelligence by threatening to restrict Chinese A.I. models that collect data from American ones. The Trump administration is preparing to warn dozens of countries not to participate in a Chinese A.I. initiative or risk being shut out of an American A.I. coalition called Pax Silica, according to a letter written by the State Department and seen by The New York Times. (It is unclear if the letters have been sent. The State Department did not respond to a request for comment).
川普政府官員還採取行動阻礙中國在人工智慧領域的進步，威脅要限制從美國模型獲取數據的中國人工智慧模型。根據《紐約時報》看到的一份由美國國務院撰寫的信函，川普政府準備警告數十個國家不要參與中國的AI倡議，否則將面臨被排除在美國名為「硅和平」(Pax Silica)的AI聯盟之外的風險。（尚不清楚信件是否已寄出。美國國務院未回應置評請求。）
China is facing potential trade restrictions from Europe, too. Lawmakers in Brussels are trying to slow the deluge of Chinese products like electric vehicles, solar panels and industrial chemicals flooding the European Union and accelerating the loss of tens of thousands of jobs.
中國也面臨著來自歐洲的潛在貿易限制。布魯塞爾的立法者正試圖放慢電動汽車、太陽能電池板和工業化學品等中國產品湧入歐盟的速度，這些產品正加速導致數萬個工作崗位的流失。
Then there are Mr. Xi’s domestic vulnerabilities. A more immediate one is a flood along the border with Nepal that has left thousands dead and missing and is raising questions about China’s preparedness. Another is the yearslong property crisis that has suppressed the Chinese economy, forcing Beijing to rely more on exports to power growth, even as that strategy has frayed relations with trading partners.
此外習近平還要面對國內的困境。一個更為直接的問題是尼泊爾邊境沿線的洪水，導致數千人死亡和失蹤，並引發了對中國防災準備的質疑。另一個問題是持續多年的房地產危機，這場危機拖累了中國經濟，迫使北京更加依賴出口拉動增長，即使這一策略損害了與貿易夥伴的關係。
“China is facing headwinds, so is the United States,” said Yun Sun, director of the China program at the Stimson Center in Washington. “The right balance is between getting enough support and endorsement at the S.C.O. without poking Trump directly in the eye.”
「中國面臨著逆風，美國也是如此，」華盛頓史汀生中心中國項目主任孫韻表示。「正確的平衡點在於，在上合組織獲得足夠的支撐與背書，同時又不直接刺激到川普。」
