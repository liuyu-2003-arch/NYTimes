中國反對G20峰會對其出口戰略批評

China Rejects G20 Criticism of Its Export Strategy

ALAN RAPPEPORT, JIM TANKERSLEY • 2026-09-02
Top economic officials from the Group of 20 nations were unable to agree on a joint statement on Tuesday because of resistance from China, which opposed language criticizing its export strategy.
由於中國反對包含批評其出口戰略內容的表述，20國集團各國經濟高官週二未能就一份聯合聲明達成一致。
The dissent from China frustrated the United States, which was hosting the meetings in North Carolina, and underscored a major rift between the world’s two largest economies. The United States has for years been growing impatient by China’s excess industrial capacity, warning that its export strategy is creating economic imbalances around the world.
中國的異議讓擔任東道主、在北卡羅萊納州主辦此次會議的美國感到受挫，也凸顯了全球最大兩個經濟體之間的巨大分歧。多年來，美國對中國過剩的工業產能日益感到不滿，並警告稱其出口戰略正在世界各地造成經濟失衡。
“We believe that nonmarket-based economies pushing out a never-ending stream of cheap exports is not sustainable,” Mr. Bessent said at a news conference at the conclusion of the meetings. “It is clear that the country with the world’s largest and unsustainable current account surplus, People’s Republic of China, was the dissenter.”
貝森特在會議結束後的新聞發布會上表示：「我們認為，非市場經濟體源源不斷地輸出廉價出口產品是不可持續的。很明顯，擁有全球最大且不可持續的經常帳戶順差的國家——中華人民共和國——就是持異議的一方。」
In the absence of a joint statement, or communiqué, the Treasury Department released a chair statement outlining the areas of agreement from the meeting. China rejected parts of that statement that included language on ensuring efficient supply chains for critical minerals, the risks of an overreliance on exports for growth and the importance of speeding up sovereign debt restructuring.
由於未能發表聯合聲明或公報，美國財政部發布了一份主席聲明，概述了此次會議達成共識的領域。中國拒絕了該聲明中的部分內容，其中包括關於確保關鍵礦產供應鏈高效運轉、過度依賴出口實現增長的風險，以及加快主權債務重組重要性的相關表述。
廣告
The Treasury secretary said that he was encouraged that the other 19 countries in attendance were in agreement about global imbalances. The summit was focused on accelerating economic growth, increasing business investment and restructuring debt in developing economies.
這位財政部長表示，他對出席會議的其他19個國家在全球失衡問題上達成一致感到鼓舞。此次峰會的重點是加速經濟增長、增加商業投資以及重組發展中經濟體的債務。
Geopolitics overshadowed some of the economic dialogue, however, as European countries expressed what Mr. Bessent described as a “sour taste” at the fact that Russia’s finance minister, Anton Siluanov, attended the meetings in person.
然而，地緣政治給部分經濟對話蒙上了陰影。正如貝森特所描述的，歐洲國家對俄羅斯財政部長安東·西盧安諾夫親自出席會議感到「不快」。
“If both fighters go in a corner, then there will never be a resolution to this horrible conflict,” Mr. Bessent said, explaining that engagement with Russia and Ukraine was critical to ending the war.
貝森特在解釋為何與俄羅斯和烏克蘭的接觸對結束戰爭至關重要時說：「如果交戰雙方都躲進角落，那麼這場可怕的衝突將永遠無法得到解決。」
The biggest issue haunting the global economy is the war in Iran, which has driven up energy prices and fanned inflation. Mr. Bessent said that he had received significant support for the United States’ initiative to strangle Iran’s economy.
困擾全球經濟的最大問題是伊朗戰爭，這場戰爭推高了能源價格、加劇了通膨。貝森特表示，美國旨在扼殺伊朗經濟的倡議獲得了廣泛支持。
Speaking on the sidelines of the two-day summit, Mr. Bessent expressed optimism that the European Union, Britain and the United Arab Emirates were prepared to join the United States as it embarks on “Operation Economic Outcast.” The Trump administration unveiled the campaign last week and has warned that countries that fail to sever economic ties with Iran will face U.S. sanctions and other economic penalties.
在為期兩天的峰會間隙，貝森特樂觀地表示，歐盟、英國和阿拉伯聯合酋長國已準備好加入美國啟動的「經濟孤立行動」。川普政府上週公布了這項行動，並警告稱，未能切斷與伊朗經濟聯繫的國家將面臨美國制裁及其他經濟懲罰。
“We are going to economically asphyxiate this regime,” Mr. Bessent said in an interview with Larry Kudlow on Fox Business.
貝森特先生在福克斯商業頻道接受拉里·庫德洛採訪時表示：「我們要在經濟上讓這個政權窒息。」
The Treasury secretary went on to say that Iran reminds him of the snakes he encountered as a child growing up in South Carolina. He recalled that poisonous snakes used to continue wiggling their tails after being decapitated.
財政部長還說，伊朗讓他想起了他小時候在南卡羅來納州長大時遇到的蛇。他回憶說，毒蛇被砍掉頭後，尾巴仍會繼續扭動。
“We are burying the head of the Iranian snake,” Mr. Bessent said. “The snake doesn’t know it’s dead yet, but it will stop wiggling when the sun goes down.”
「我們正在埋葬這條伊朗毒蛇的頭，」貝森特說。「這條蛇還不知道自己已經死了，但太陽下山時它就會停止扭動。」
廣告
Mr. Bessent said that the Treasury Department would announce additional sanctions on banks that Iran relies upon to facilitate transactions that fund its economy. He also said that the department was working to seize Iranian leaders’ assets abroad, including, specifically, money in bank accounts in the British Virgin Islands.
貝森特表示，財政部將宣布對伊朗賴以促進交易、為其經濟提供資金的銀行實施額外製裁。他還表示，該部門正致力於沒收伊朗領導人在海外的資產，特別是存放在英屬維爾京群島銀行帳戶中的資金。
Last week, the United States shut down the Dubai branch of an Egyptian bank that the Treasury Department said had helped Iran evade sanctions.
上週，美國關閉了一家埃及銀行在杜拜的分行，財政部稱該分行曾幫助伊朗逃避制裁。
The Treasury secretary expressed optimism that the disruptions caused by the conflict with Iran would be temporary. He predicted that oil transport routes would quickly adjust to Iran’s blockade of the Strait of Hormuz, a consequence of the war.
這位財政部長樂觀地表示，與伊朗的衝突造成的干擾將是暫時的。他預測，石油運輸路線將很快對伊朗封鎖霍爾木茲海峽（戰爭的後果之一）作出調整。
“In two years, the Strait of Hormuz will be a worthless piece of water,” Mr. Bessent said.
貝森特說：「兩年後，霍爾木茲海峽將成為一片毫無價值的水域。」
While the United States is trying to marshal global support to confront Iran, it is facing blowback from American allies over President Trump’s trade policies.
就在美國試圖凝聚全球力量以對抗伊朗之際，川普總統的貿易政策卻招致了美國盟友的強烈反彈。
The newly announced tariffs on Canadian goods have come under repeated criticism from other attendees at the conference. On Tuesday morning, Lars Klingbeil, the German vice chancellor, told reporters that “tariff conflicts waged by the U.S.A., such as currently with Canada, destroy trust” and “ultimately harm everyone involved.”
美國新近宣布的對加拿大商品徵收關稅遭到了與會其他代表的反覆批評。週二上午，德國副總理拉爾斯·克林拜爾告訴記者，「美國發起的關稅衝突——比如目前與加拿大之間的衝突——破壞了信任」，並且「最終會傷害到所有相關方」。
廣告
Mr. Bessent dismissed the retaliatory measures that Canada, which is the United States’ second-largest trading partner, has taken. He suggested in an interview on CNBC that the northern neighbor of the United States was too small to wage a trade war.
加拿大是美國的第二大貿易夥伴，對於該國採取的報復性措施，貝森特不以為意。他在接受CNBC採訪時暗示，美國的這個北方鄰國體量太小，無法發動一場貿易戰。
“I don’t think you can be in a tit-for-tat with someone who’s 13 times larger than you are,” Mr. Bessent said, referring to the size of the countries’ economies.
「我不認為你可以和一個體量比你大13倍的對手針鋒相對，」貝森特在提到兩國經濟體量時說道。
After meeting on Tuesday with his Canadian counterpart, François-Philippe Champagne, Mr. Bessent said that the trade dispute would ultimately have to be resolved by the leaders of the two countries.
週二，貝森特在與加拿大財政部長商鵬飛(François-Philippe Champagne)會面後表示，貿易爭端最終必須由兩國領導人來解決。

Alan Rappeport是時報經濟政策記者，常駐華盛頓，負責報導財政部，並撰寫有關稅收、貿易和財政問題的文章。
