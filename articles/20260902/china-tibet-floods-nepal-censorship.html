中國嚴格控制西藏洪災災情敘事

Punishment and Silence: China Pulls Out the Disaster Playbook in Tibet

艾莎 • 2026-09-02
Rescue workers were only just starting to assess the scope of loss and destruction from deadly Himalayan floods last week when Chinese authorities turned to another challenge: unapproved narratives of the devastation.
上週那場致命的喜馬拉雅洪災發生後，救援人員才剛剛開始評估災情和損失的規模，中國當局就已經著手應對另一項挑戰：未經批准的災情敘事。
Censors quickly deleted videos and eyewitness accounts from social media. Journalists working for state media and social media influencers were warned not to report on casualties or missing people unless their reports aligned with official announcements. People who questioned the official death toll were given “administrative penalties,” punishment that typically involves either a fine of up to $150 or up to 10 days in prison.
審查人員迅速刪除了社群媒體上的影片和目擊者描述。官方媒體的記者和自媒體創作者接到警告，傷亡或失蹤人員信息須以官方通報為準，不得擅自報導。質疑官方死亡人數的人被處以「行政處罰」——通常包括最高1000元的罰款或最高10天的拘留。
When some foreign news outlets noted the contrast in coverage between Tibet, where no foreign journalist is allowed to report, and Nepal, where international media have been able to travel freely, the Chinese government called the reporting “malicious speculation.”
一些外國新聞機構指出，西藏與尼泊爾的報導情況形成鮮明對比：外國記者不被允許進入西藏進行報導，但國際媒體可以自由前往尼泊爾。對此，中國政府稱這些報導屬於「惡意揣測」。
The immediate and widespread crackdown on speech reflects two challenges facing China’s ruling Communist Party, which tightly controls information across the country.
這種迅速且廣泛的言論管控折射出中共面臨的兩大挑戰，北京對全國的信息傳播實行嚴格控制。
The crisis has focused attention on Tibet, a region with a history of unrest and protest centered around demands for more autonomy that are sensitive to Beijing. And it has hit both China and its neighbor, Nepal, where just across the border dozens of journalists have arrived to report witness accounts and talk to families and local residents. That difference has raised questions about what is happening in China.
這場危機將關注焦點引向了西藏——這個地區歷史上曾發生過多次動盪和抗議活動，而要求擴大自治的訴求一直是北京高度敏感的問題。這場災害同時衝擊了中國及其鄰國尼泊爾，在尼泊爾一側的邊境地帶，已有數十名記者抵達當地，採訪目擊者，遇難者家屬和當地居民交談。這種反差引發了人們對中國境內究竟發生了什麼的質疑。
“With this one, even if the Chinese side has information control, on the Nepal side there is a lot of information coming out,” said Xiao Qiang, the editor in chief of China Digital Times, a nonprofit that collects data on censorship and archives work that has been censored on China’s internet. “It makes the Chinese government feel that controlling information around this event is harder.”
「這次的情況是，即便中方實施信息管控，尼泊爾那邊也會流出大量信息，」中國數位時代總編輯蕭強說。這是一個收集審查數據並歸檔中國互聯網上被刪內容的非營利組織。他表示，「這讓中國政府感到，控制這次事件的相關信息變得更加困難。」
Tibet, which was annexed by Beijing in the 1950s, is rigidly governed and has been a place of protest, including self-immolations by Tibetans. Foreign journalists are not allowed to travel there unless invited on heavily monitored and choreographed official tours. Tibetans risk detention for speaking to reporters.
西藏於20世紀50年代被北京納入版圖，此後一直對這裡實行嚴格管控。這裡曾發生過多次抗議，包括藏人自焚事件。除非受邀參加由官方組織、行程安排嚴密且全程受到監控的訪問活動，否則外國記者不得前往西藏。藏人若與記者交談，可能面臨拘留風險。
When official memorials were held on Tuesday in Gyirong County, Tibet, as workers continued to clear roads and search for bodies, China’s state media showed images of doctors, police officers, emergency workers and officials standing in mourning.
週二，西藏吉隆縣舉行官方悼念活動時，道路清理和遺體搜尋工作仍在繼續。中國官方媒體發布的畫面中，可以看到醫生、警察、救援人員和官員肅立哀悼。
“Throughout the rescue efforts, cadres and party members stood on the front lines, stepping up to take full responsibility,” the state-controlled People’s Daily wrote in a front-page article last week. “United as one, braving all perils,” the article went on.
「搶險救援中，廣大黨員幹部衝鋒在前、挺膺擔當，」官方報紙《人民日報》上週在頭版文章中寫道。「眾志成城，不畏艱險，」文章繼續寫道。
Largely absent from the reporting have been images of the injured, the dead and their families.
報導中幾乎看不到傷者、遇難者及其家屬的畫面。
廣告
The flooding killed at least 987 people in Nepal and 16 in Tibet, according to official tallies. Nearly 4,000 people were missing in Nepal as of Tuesday, according to the country’s disaster agency. In Tibet, 546 people were still missing, according to Chinese state media, which did not publish any names. China’s most recent figures are from Aug. 29.
官方統計顯示，洪災在尼泊爾造成至少987人死亡，在西藏造成16人死亡。截至週二，尼泊爾災害管理部門稱仍有近4000人失蹤。而在西藏，據中國官方媒體報導，仍有546人失蹤，但未公布任何姓名。中國方面的最新數據截至8月29日。
“Search and rescue and emergency relief work in the disaster area are being carried out in an intense and orderly manner,” said Guo Jiakun, a spokesman for China’s foreign ministry. “Our release of information is public, transparent, and timely.”
「當前，災區人員搜救和搶險救災工作正緊張有序開展。」中國外交部發言人郭嘉昆說，「中方信息發布公開、透明、及時。」
One day after floodwaters ravaged villages around the Himalayas, government censors were already removing videos from social media platforms. The cybersecurity department of China’s Ministry of Public Security quickly tracked down people posting specific numbers of dead and missing and accused them of spreading false information online. The department announced 10 cases in five different provinces. In one case, a user was penalized for posting that the disaster was “not that severe, probably around 500 people, and the missing might all be dead.”
洪水席捲喜馬拉雅地區的村莊後第二天，政府審查人員已開始從社群媒體平台刪除相關影片。中國公安部網路安全部門迅速追查發布具體傷亡數字的用戶，並指控他們傳播虛假信息。該部門通報了涉及五個省份的10起案件。其中一起案件中，一名用戶因為發布這樣的內容而受到處罰：「不算嚴重，可能死500個人左右、失蹤人口可能全部都死了。」
This week, the authorities in Tibet issued a notice to social media influencers, warning them not to go to the disaster zone or to spread rumors, in order to “support the overall rescue and relief efforts and jointly create a rational, orderly, and clean online public opinion environment.”
本週，西藏有關部門向自媒體創作者發出通知，警告他們不得前往災區或傳播謠言，以「為全力服務保障搶險救援大局，共同營造理性、有序、清朗的網路輿論環境」。
Journalists accredited to work at Chinese state media were ordered not to interview survivors or their families, said Maya Wang, the deputy Asia director at Human Rights Watch.
人權觀察亞洲區副主任王松蓮說，官媒記者被要求不得採訪倖存者或者他們的家人。
“What authoritarian governments fear most are questions,” she said. “The playbook is to control other narratives, extinguish any questions which inevitably lead to seeking accountability.”
「威權政府最害怕的就是問題，」她說，「它們慣用的做法就是控制敘事，撲滅一切問題，因為這些問題最終不可避免地會指向追責。」
廣告
The trumpeting of rescue efforts and the exclusion of stories of death and injury is part of China’s playbook when it comes to major disasters.
中國政府應對重大災難的一貫做法是大力宣傳救援工作，同時排除傷亡故事。
The crackdown in Tibet echoes the government’s response to other crises, like the Covid-19 outbreak and a huge earthquake in the southern Chinese province of Sichuan in 2008 that killed thousands. In both cases, officials moved fast to blot out public comments about the government response and punish people who tried to expose lapses in it.
這次關於西藏的言論管控與政府應對其他危機時的做法如出一轍，例如新冠疫情，以及2008年四川造成數萬人死亡的強烈地震。在這兩起事件中，官員都迅速採取行動，壓制公眾對政府應對措施的評論，並懲罰那些試圖揭露政府失誤的人。
The true scale of devastation from the latest flooding in Tibet is expected to be far worse than official state accounts, according to the International Campaign for Tibet, a nonprofit advocacy group.
非營利倡議組織「國際聲援西藏運動」認為，此次西藏洪災的真實破壞規模預計遠比官方公布的情況嚴重。
China has not said which villages have been affected on the Tibetan side. But the group, citing sources on the ground, said some of the hardest hit included Sale, Serchung, Labi and Rizi, where some 300 homes have been destroyed and many people were still missing.
中國政府沒有說明西藏一側究竟有哪些村莊受災。但該組織援引當地消息人士的話稱，受災最嚴重的地區包括薩勒、色瓊、拉比和裡孜，當地約有300棟房屋被毀，許多人仍然失聯。
Some survivors have been moved to Gyirong Town and are being held under supervision and tight security, said Ryan Fioresi, the executive director of the International Campaign for Tibet.
「國際聲援西藏運動」執行主任彭德安(Ryan Fioresi)說，部分倖存者已經被轉移到吉隆，並受到監控和嚴密安保。
“Almost any unrestricted information from Tibet can become politically sensitive because it challenges the party’s monopoly over the global narrative over how Tibet is represented and governed,” Mr. Fioresi said.
「幾乎任何來自西藏、未經限制的信息都可能成為政治敏感信息，因為它挑戰了中共對於西藏如何呈現、如何治理的全球敘事壟斷，」彭德安說。
廣告
“Even during a humanitarian disaster, spontaneous relief efforts or independent reporting can show that Tibetans have their own networks, leadership and sense of collective responsibility outside party structures,” he added.
他還說：「即便是在一場人道主義災難中，自發的救援行動或獨立報導也可能讓人看到，藏人擁有黨組織體系之外的網路、領導力量以及集體責任意識。」

艾莎(Alexandra Stevenson)是《紐約時報》上海分社社長，報導中國經濟和社會新聞。
