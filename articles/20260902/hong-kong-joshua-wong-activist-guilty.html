黃之鋒就「勾結外國勢力」認罪，最高可判終身監禁

Joshua Wong, Jailed Hong Kong Activist, Pleads Guilty to Foreign Collusion

DAVID PIERSON • 2026-09-02
Joshua Wong, a prominent Hong Kong pro-democracy activist, pleaded guilty on Wednesday to collusion for lobbying Western lawmakers, a case that shows how China treats international outreach as a national security crime.
週三，香港知名民主派活動人士黃之鋒就涉及遊說西方議員的勾結外國勢力案表示認罪。此案表明中國已將對外聯絡行為視為危害國家安全的犯罪。
The maximum penalty for colluding with foreign forces is life in prison.
勾結外國或者境外勢力罪的最高刑罰為終身監禁。
Mr. Wong was accused of conspiring with other campaigners to lobby lawmakers, particularly those in the United States like Nancy Pelosi and Marco Rubio, to impose sanctions on Hong Kong and China. The campaigners sought to pressure officials in Beijing and Hong Kong for greater democratic rights in the semiautonomous territory.
黃之鋒被指控與其他倡議者串謀，遊說包括南希·佩洛西與馬爾科·魯比奧在內的美國議員對香港和中國實施制裁。這些倡議者旨在向北京和香港官員施壓，以在該半自治地區爭取更多民主權利。
Mr. Wong, who turns 30 next month, was one of the youngest activists in a democracy movement that was quashed in 2020 after Beijing introduced a sweeping national security law that effectively outlawed political dissent in Hong Kong.
2020年北京出台全面國家安全法、實際上等於取締了香港的政治異見後，當地民主運動被鎮壓，下個月將滿30歲的黃之鋒曾是該運動中最年輕的活動人士之一。
廣告
In a sign of the sensitivity around the case, police officers in tactical vests and police vehicles surrounded the courthouse where Mr. Wong’s case was being heard on Wednesday.
週三，身著防彈衣的警察與警車包圍了審理黃之鋒案件的法院，凸顯出此案的敏感程度。
He is already serving an almost five-year prison term for his role in an unofficial primary election in 2020 organized by the city’s pro-democracy camp that the authorities deemed subversive. That case, the city’s largest national security trial, led to the conviction of Mr. Wong and more than 40 other former politicians and activists.
因參與2020年由民主派陣營組織、被當局認定具顛覆性的非官方初選，黃之鋒目前已在服近五年的刑期。該案是香港規模最大的國家安全審判，導致黃之鋒及40多名其他前政治人物和活動人士被定罪。
Mr. Wong was arrested on the foreign collusion charge in June last year while in prison. On Wednesday, his lawyers urged the judge, William Tam, to consider a lighter sentence, saying Mr. Wong had matured substantially and hoped to one day leave prison so that he could take care of his aging parents.
去年6月，仍在服刑中的黃之鋒因涉嫌勾結外國勢力罪被捕。週三，其辯護律師敦促法官譚耀豪考慮輕判，表示黃之鋒已日趨成熟，希望終能出獄照顧年邁的雙親。
Mr. Wong’s repeated prosecutions reflect the drastic political changes that have taken place in Hong Kong, a former British colony, since it was returned to China in 1997. At the time, Beijing agreed to preserve Western-style civil liberties that are not available in mainland China, such as freedom of speech, for 50 years.
黃之鋒屢遭起訴折射出這個前英國殖民地自1997年回歸中國以來發生的劇烈政治變遷。彼時，北京曾同意將言論自由等中國大陸所不具備的西式公民自由保留50年。
Today, the city’s legislature is devoid of any significant opposition and independent media and members of civil society have largely been muzzled. Bookstores accused of carrying subversive books have been closed.
如今，香港立法機關已無任何實質性的反對派，獨立媒體與公民社會成員大多被噤聲，被指售賣顛覆性書籍的書店也已停業。
Mr. Wong’s plea on Wednesday came seven months after a court sentenced Jimmy Lai, a pro-democracy media tycoon, to 20 years in prison on charges of conspiracy to collude with foreign forces and conspiracy to publish seditious materials under the national security law.
在黃之鋒週三認罪的七個月前，法院根據國家安全法判處民主派媒體大亨黎智英20年監禁，黎智英被控串謀勾結外國勢力及串謀發布煽動刊物罪。
廣告
Mr. Wong rose to prominence in 2012, when at he age of 14 he led a student movement to oppose the introduction of a Chinese national education curriculum in Hong Kong. He later became one of the most internationally-recognized faces of the 2014 Umbrella Movement protests that called for people in Hong Kong to be given the right to vote for their leader. He would appear on the covers of Western magazines and star in a Netflix documentary, “Joshua: Teenager vs. Superpower.”
黃之鋒於2012年嶄露頭角，年僅14歲的他當時領導了一場學生運動，反對在香港推行國民教育課程。隨後，他在2014年爭取行政長官普選的「雨傘運動」中成為最受國際關注的代表人物之一。他曾多次登上西方雜誌封面，並成為Netflix紀錄片《黃之鋒：熱血青年vs超級強權》(Joshua: Teenager vs. Superpower)的主角。
Western legal experts say that Mr. Wong and other pro-democracy activists have not been given fair trials by Hong Kong’s judges, who are handpicked by the city’s chief executive to handle national security cases.
西方法律專家指出，負責審理國家安全案件的香港法官均由行政長官指定，黃之鋒及其他民主派活動人士未能獲得公正審判。
“It’s clear that the Hong Kong government has made a decision to keep Joshua behind bars more or less indefinitely,” said Thomas E. Kellogg, the executive director of the Georgetown Center for Asian Law. “They’re doing this precisely because he is a prominent political figure, someone whom Hong Kongers around the world respect and admire.”
喬治城大學亞洲法中心執行主任托馬斯·E·凱洛格表示：「很明顯，香港政府已決定將黃之鋒近乎無限期地監禁下去。他們之所以這樣做，恰恰是因為他是一位知名政治人物，深受全球香港人的尊敬與愛戴。」
“The Hong Kong government may hope that Hong Kongers will forget about him if they put him away for another decade or more,” he continued. “But I don’t think that will happen.”
他補充道：「香港政府或許希望，只要再將他關押十年甚至更久，香港人就會漸漸遺忘他。但我認為這不會發生。」

David Pierson報導中國外交政策和中國與世界的經濟與文化交互。他從事新聞工作已超過20年。
