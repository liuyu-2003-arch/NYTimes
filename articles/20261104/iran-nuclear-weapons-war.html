更多伊朗人呼籲發展核武器對抗美國

On Cusp of Renewed War, More Iranians Are Calling for Nuclear Weapons

ERIKA SOLOMON • 2026-11-04
From pulpits at Friday prayers to street rallies, Iranian clerics and officials are increasingly urging the country’s leaders to reverse their longtime pledge not to pursue nuclear weapons.
從週五禮拜時的佈道講壇到街頭集會，越來越多的伊朗神職人員和官員呼籲國家領導人改變長期以來不尋求發展核武器的承諾。 
This demand, once seen as a fringe position held by hard-liners, is moving into mainstream public debate, and the shift could have dramatic consequences for Iran, whose government has for decades argued that its nuclear program was solely for peaceful purposes.
這一訴求過去被視為強硬派中的邊緣立場，如今正逐漸進入主流公共討論。這一轉變可能給伊朗帶來重大影響。幾十年來，伊朗政府一直堅稱，其核計劃完全用於和平目的。
“We must be equipped with nuclear weapons — so that we can fight the criminal America with strength, God willing,” Ali Sepahvand, the Friday prayer leader in Iran’s western city of Kuhdasht, said last week, according to a widely distributed video.
據一段廣泛傳播的影片顯示，上週，伊朗西部城市庫赫達什特的週五禮拜領拜人阿里·塞帕萬德說：「我們必須擁有核武器，這樣才能在真主的保佑下，有力量對抗罪惡的美國。」
As his voice echoed through a prayer hall, covered in photographs of commanders killed in the war with the United States and Israel this past year, the congregants chanted: “Nuclear deterrence is our inalienable right!”
他的聲音在掛滿過去一年與美國和以色列戰爭中陣亡指揮官照片的禮拜堂裡迴盪，信眾高呼：「核威懾是我們不可被剝奪的權利！」
廣告
The growing calls for Iran to pursue nuclear weapons come as the country’s leaders engage in diplomatic efforts to stave off renewed war with the United States and Israel, an outcome widely considered likely after the American midterm elections on Tuesday.
伊朗國內要求追求核武器的呼聲日益高漲之際，該國領導人正開展外交努力，以避免與美國和以色列重新爆發戰爭——外界普遍認為，週二美國期中選舉結束後，戰爭很可能再次爆發。
For decades, Iran’s officials have publicly endorsed the fatwa, or religious edict, from its supreme leader, Ayatollah Ali Khamenei, which forbade the development and use of nuclear weapons. Issued in his capacity as the head of Iran’s theocratic state, his decree barred Iranian institutions, including the military and Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, from pursuing a nuclear bomb.
幾十年來，伊朗官員一直公開支持最高領袖阿亞圖拉阿里·哈梅內伊頒佈的法特瓦（即宗教敕令），禁止研發和使用核武器。他以伊朗神權國家元首身份發布的這項法令禁止包括軍隊和伊斯蘭革命衛隊在內的伊朗機構研製核彈。 
Yet at the same time, Western intelligence agencies in past years reported signs Iran was enriching uranium to levels close to those that could be used to develop a bomb. Some officials in Israel and the United States accused Iran of preparing to build one, regardless of the fatwa.
然而，與此同時，西方情報機構過去幾年曾報告，有跡象表明伊朗正在將鈾濃縮至接近可用於製造核彈的丰度。以色列和美國的一些官員指責伊朗不顧這一宗教敕令，仍在為製造核彈做準備。
To curb this threat, the United States and other world powers reached an agreement with Iran in 2015 to restrict its nuclear activities. Three years later, President Trump pulled out of that agreement.
為遏制這一威脅，美國和其他世界大國於2015年與伊朗達成協議，限制其核活動。三年後，川普總統退出了該協議。
Ayatollah Khamenei’s strategy, regional analysts say, had been to keep Iran’s nuclear intentions ambiguous in hopes of deterring attacks on Iran without it actually becoming a nuclear power.
地區分析人士表示，哈梅內伊的戰略一直是讓伊朗的核意圖保持模糊，以期在不實際成為核大國的情況下威懾對伊朗的攻擊。 
But that approach failed to prevent the two wars Israel and the United States have waged against Iran since June 2025 — including the one in which Mr. Khamenei was himself killed in February.
但這一做法未能阻止以色列和美國自2025年6月以來對伊朗發動的兩場戰爭，其中包括今年2月導致哈梅內伊本人喪生的那場戰爭。
廣告
“He created this neither in nor out situation, where they paid for a bomb they didn’t have,” said Mohammad Ali Shabani, an Iran analyst who edits the independent regional news site Amwaj.media. “Now, there is this sense that unless you have this ultimate weapon, you could end up at the mercy of not just the U.S., but Israel, at any point.”
「他製造了一種既沒有真正擁有核彈、又沒有完全放棄核彈的局面，結果伊朗付出了擁核的代價，卻沒有真正擁有核彈，」獨立地區新聞網站Amwaj.media的編輯、伊朗問題分析人士穆罕默德·阿里·沙巴尼說。「現在，人們越來越覺得，如果沒有這種終極武器，伊朗隨時可能任由美國甚至以色列擺布。」
President Trump’s own comments may strengthen this assessment, analysts say.
分析人士表示，川普總統本人的言論可能進一步強化這種判斷。
“If they had a nuclear weapon, I’d be calling the Supreme Leader and I’d be saying, ‘Mr. Supreme Leader, how are you, sir? Is there anything we can do for you?’ As opposed to bombing the crap out of him,” Mr. Trump said at the Republican midterm convention in September.
「如果他們擁有核武器，我會打電話給最高領袖，我會說：『最高領袖先生，您好嗎，先生？我們能為您做些什麼嗎？』而不是把他炸得稀巴爛，」川普在9月共和黨期中選舉大會上說。 
Inside Iran, calls for withdrawing from the Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons, or N.P.T., which prohibits development of a nuclear weapon, have been growing ever since Israel and the United States attacked in 2025.
在伊朗國內，自2025年以色列和美國發動攻擊以來，要求退出《不擴散核武器條約》的呼聲不斷高漲。該條約禁止發展核武器。 
In recent weeks, an increasing number of Iranian political and religious figures have added their voices to this chorus, suggesting to some Iran analysts that there is an effort orchestrated, or at least enabled, by the country’s leadership.
最近幾週，越來越多的伊朗政治和宗教人物加入了這一行列。一些伊朗問題分析人士因此認為，這股輿論浪潮可能由伊朗領導層組織推動，至少也是在其默許下形成的。
It is unclear whether serious debate over Iran’s nuclear doctrine has reached the new supreme leader, Mojtaba Khamenei. But on Saturday, Iran’s top national security figure, Mohsen Rezaei, said in an interview on state television that Iran’s leaders were discussing it.
目前尚不清楚關於伊朗核政策的嚴肅討論是否已進入新任最高領袖穆傑塔巴·哈梅內伊的決策層面。但週六，伊朗最高國家安全官員穆赫辛·雷扎伊在接受國家電視台採訪時表示，伊朗領導人正在討論這一問題。
廣告
“No new decision has been made yet,” he said. “Of course, the debate now is whether we want to keep the same doctrine we had before the war, if war breaks out again.”
「目前還沒有作出新的決定，」他說。「當然，現在討論的是，如果再次爆發戰爭，我們是否還要堅持戰前的核政策。」
Advocating for withdrawal from the N.P.T. was once a minority position within Iran’s leadership, said Mohammad Ghaderi, a foreign affairs expert in Tehran.
德黑蘭外交事務專家穆罕默德·加德里說，主張退出《不擴散核武器條約》曾是伊朗領導層中的少數派立場。
Now, he argued, it is becoming a majority view. “They argue that if the military threats from the United States and Israel persist, withdrawing from the N.P.T. represents a crucial step toward establishing deterrence,” he said.
他認為，如今這正在成為多數派意見。「他們主張，如果來自美國和以色列的軍事威脅持續存在，退出《不擴散核武器條約》就是建立威懾的關鍵一步，」他說。
Raising the nuclear prospect at a time when Iran could be at the cusp of another possible attack by the United States or Israel may itself be a strategy, said Omid Memarian, an Iran analyst at DAWN, a Washington-based Middle East research group.
華盛頓中東研究機構DAWN的伊朗問題分析人士奧米德·梅馬利安表示，在伊朗可能即將再次遭到美國或以色列攻擊之際，提出發展核武器的可能性本身也可能是一種策略。
Mr. Rezaei’s entry into the debate, while Qatar has been leading intensive mediation efforts between Washington and Tehran, may be an attempt to push the United States to reach a deal, Mr. Memarian said.
梅馬利安說，在卡達正積極斡旋華盛頓與德黑蘭之間的談判之際，雷扎伊加入這場討論可能是為了向美國施壓，促使其達成協議。
“They are playing with this card: If the negotiation fails, we will seek a different path, and it will be costly for you,” he said.
「他們在打這張牌：如果談判失敗，我們將尋求另一條道路，而這對你們來說代價高昂，」他說。
廣告
Especially notable has been the number of Friday prayer leaders adding their voices to the calls and effectively challenging the late ayatollah’s fatwa.
尤其值得注意的是，越來越多的週五禮拜領拜人加入呼籲，這實際上是在挑戰已故哈梅內伊頒佈的宗教敕令。
“The taboo is gone,” said Mr. Shabani. “A religious foundation for the shift is here. The question is whether a strategic or political shift will follow.”
「禁忌已經消失，」沙巴尼說。「轉變的宗教基礎已經存在。問題是，戰略或政治轉變是否會隨之而來。」
Some of Iran’s leading reformist figures, like the president Masoud Pezeshkian and former president Hassan Rouhani, continue to back Iran’s peaceful nuclear doctrine.
伊朗一些主要改革派人物，如總統馬蘇德·佩澤希齊揚和前總統哈桑·魯哈尼，繼續支持伊朗的和平核原則。 
In Iran’s leading reformist newspaper, Shargh, a former diplomat, Kourosh Ahmadi, warned this week that abandoning that doctrine poses grave risks. The plethora of pro-weapon statements from prominent figures, he said, “is dangerous because it makes the work of officials trying to prevent another war more difficult.”
本週，伊朗主要改革派報紙《東方報》刊登了前外交官庫魯什·艾哈邁迪的警告。他指出，放棄這一政策將帶來嚴重風險。他說，知名人士發表的支持核武的言論層出不窮，「十分危險，因為這會讓那些試圖阻止另一場戰爭的官員更加難以開展工作」。 
And until Iran could manage to build a bomb, he argued, the chances of being attacked would actually increase.
他還認為，在伊朗真正製造出核彈之前，這種轉變反而會增加其遭到攻擊的可能性。
After U.S. and Israeli strikes wiped out most of Iran’s top leadership and battered its infrastructure, it is harder for critics of the nuclear option to make their case, said Sina Toossi, an Iran expert at the Center for International Policy, a Washington-based think tank.
華盛頓智庫國際政策中心的伊朗問題專家西納·圖西表示，美國和以色列的空襲清除了伊朗大部分高層領導人，並重創其基礎設施之後，反對發展核武器的人如今更難說服其他人接受自己的立場。
廣告
“The central problem for advocates of restraint is explaining what Iran gained from remaining below the nuclear threshold,” he said.
「主張克制的人面臨的核心問題是，他們如何解釋伊朗堅持不越過核門檻，究竟得到了什麼好處，」他說。
At the same time, he said, a new round of U.S. attacks “could strengthen the very arguments for the nuclear weapons that Washington says it wants to prevent.”
與此同時，他指出，美國如果再次發動攻擊，「反而可能強化伊朗國內支持發展核武器的理由，而這恰恰是華盛頓聲稱要阻止的」。
At public prayers in Tehran on Friday, the prayer leader, Mohammad Javad Haj Ali Akbari, declared that changing Iran’s nuclear doctrine was “a national demand,” according to a video posted on social media.
根據社群媒體上發布的一段影片，週五在德黑蘭的公開禮拜中，領拜人穆罕默德·賈瓦德·哈吉·阿里·阿克巴里宣布，改變伊朗的核政策已成為「全國性的訴求」。
His congregants immediately responded with a refrain common at government-sponsored rallies from decades past: “Nuclear energy is our inalienable right!”
參加禮拜的民眾立即高喊一句幾十年來經常出現在政府組織的集會上的口號：「核能是我們不可被剝奪的權利！」 
“This is an old slogan,” he told them, and smiled: “We have gone way beyond that.”
「這是老口號了，」他微笑著對眾人說：「我們已經遠遠超越了那個階段。」
