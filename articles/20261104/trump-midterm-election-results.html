川普表示將接受選舉結果

Trump Says in Interview That He Will Accept Election Results

ERICA L. GREEN, TYLER PAGER • 2026-11-04
President Trump said on Tuesday night that he was “absolutely willing” to accept the results of the midterm elections, and was prepared to work with Democrats.
川普總統週二晚間表示，他「絕對願意」接受期中選舉結果，並準備與民主黨人合作。
“We’ll, you know, look forward to working with them,” he said in a phone interview with The New York Times. “We’ll probably work something out.”
「我們，你知道，期待跟他們合作，」他在接受《紐約時報》電話採訪時說。「我們大概能商量出一些辦法。」
Mr. Trump blamed Republican losses on the fact that he was not on the ballot, and called his decision to go to war with Iran a “very wise decision.”
川普把共和黨的失利歸咎於自己未出現在選票上，並稱自己對伊朗開戰的決定是「非常明智的決定」。
He said he had no regrets about endorsing candidates such as Senator Darline Graham, Republican of South Carolina, and Ken Paxton, the Republican attorney general of Texas, who both lost their Senate bids.
他說，自己對支持南卡羅來納州共和黨參議員達琳·格雷厄姆和得克薩斯州共和黨總檢察長肯·帕克斯頓等人毫不後悔，儘管這兩位候選人最終均在參議院競選中落敗。
廣告
Mr. Trump spoke to The Times shortly before 10:30 p.m. on Tuesday, while he was watching election results at the White House. Democrats were on track to take control of the House and the Senate.
週二晚上10點30分前不久，川普在白宮觀看選舉結果時接受了《紐約時報》的採訪。當時民主黨正有望掌控眾議院和參議院。
“I’m just looking at these results as I’m talking,” he said. “I see that Darline lost. Wow, that’s amazing. How do you think Lindsey would have done if he ran?” he added, referring to Senator Lindsey Graham, who died in July.
「我正一邊說話一邊看結果，」他說。「我看到達琳輸了。哇，太驚人了。你覺得如果林賽參選，他的表現會怎麼樣？」他還說。這裡他指的是今年7月去世的參議員林賽·格雷厄姆。
Mr. Trump added that he believed that Mr. Graham most likely would have prevailed in his race.
川普還表示，他認為林賽·格雷厄姆很可能在選舉中獲勝。
In the short conversation with The Times, the president sounded subdued as he absorbed decisive Democratic wins across the country.
在與《紐約時報》的簡短交談中，面對民主黨在全國範圍內的壓倒性勝利，總統顯得情緒低落。
But he insisted that he regretted nothing about the first two years of his second term, despite the widespread Republican defeats. “I consider it to be the most successful two-year period in the history of any president,” he said.
但他堅稱，儘管共和黨遭遇了全面挫敗，他對第二任期前兩年沒有什麼遺憾。「我認為這是任何總統歷史上最成功的兩年時期，」他說。
Mr. Trump’s pledge to accept the results of the elections followed his sustained efforts over the last year to sow doubt about the security of elections. On Tuesday, the Department of Homeland Security posted a series of social media messages vowing to arrest noncitizen voters, despite the fact that the administration had struggled to find them.
川普承諾接受選舉結果，此前他在過去一年中一直不遺餘力地散布對選舉安全性的質疑。週二，國土安全部在社群媒體上發布了一系列信息，誓言逮捕非公民選民，儘管事實上本屆政府一直難以找到這類選民。
The president’s comments came after he huddled inside the White House’s East Room Tuesday night with some of his top aides, including Vice President JD Vance, Secretary of State Marco Rubio, Attorney General Todd Blanche and Howard Lutnick, the commerce secretary, to watch the results come in. Some in attendance described it as a “dark” and “funereal” atmosphere.
總統發表上述言論前，於週二晚上在白宮東廳與一些高級助手聚在一起觀看計票結果出爐，其中包括副總統萬斯、國務卿魯比奧、司法部長托德·布蘭奇和商務部長霍華德·盧特尼克。一些在場者形容氣氛「陰暗」且「像葬禮一樣」。
Some guests started leaving before 10 p.m., according to people with knowledge of the departures. Other allies of Mr. Trump’s said privately that they were happy they were out of town.
據知情人士透露，一些客人不到晚上10點就開始離場。川普的其他盟友私下表示，他們慶幸自己不在華盛頓。
廣告
Throughout the evening, Mr. Trump vented his frustration at political aides and accused them of giving him bad information about polling in the lead-up to the elections, according to people with knowledge of his remarks, who spoke on the condition of anonymity to describe the private conversations.
據要求匿名描述私下談話的知情人士透露，整個晚上，川普對政治助手發洩不滿，指責他們在選舉前提供了關於民調的糟糕信息。
It was not clear what he meant: Aides to Mr. Trump had spoken openly about the difficult environment for Republicans.
目前不清楚他是什麼意思：川普的助手此前曾公開談到共和黨面臨的艱難局面。
A White House official disputed the idea that the president was frustrated with his advisers.
一名白宮官員駁斥了總統對顧問感到沮喪的說法。
In his interview with The Times, the president said that he believed Republicans suffered losses because he was not on the ballot.
在接受《紐約時報》採訪時，總統說，他認為共和黨之所以失利，是因為他本人不在選票上。
“I wasn’t on the ticket, and because I wasn’t on the ticket, we couldn’t get enough people out to vote,” he said.
「我不在選票上，因為我不在選票上，我們沒法讓足夠多的人出來投票，」他說。
He also said he did not regret the decision to go to war with Iran and “would do that again.” But he acknowledged that voters were unhappy about gas prices.
他還說，他不後悔對伊朗開戰的決定，並且「如果重來一次還會這麼做」。但他承認，選民對汽油價格不滿。
廣告
“They don’t mind the Iran, but they probably weren’t happy — they weren't happy with the oil price,” Mr. Trump said. “So you know, it’s just one of those things. But I don’t regret that decision at all. In fact, you know, that’s a decision that was a very wise decision for the future of the country and the world.”
「他們不介意伊朗，但他們可能不高興——他們對油價不高興，」川普說。「所以你知道，這只是其中一件事。但我一點也不後悔那個決定。事實上，你知道，那是一個對國家的未來和世界的未來都非常明智的決定。」
Mr. Trump said Democrats had already reached out to talk to him about working together, though he declined to identify any of them by name. He said he thought they could work together on the debt ceiling.
川普說，民主黨人已經主動聯繫他，商談合作事宜，但他拒絕說出任何具體人物的名字。他說，他認為雙方可以在債務上限問題上合作。
“I have total veto power, so they have to come to us,” he said. “And if they have some good ideas, I’m certainly open to working.”
「我有完全的否決權，所以他們必須來找我們，」他說。「如果他們有什麼好主意，我當然願意合作。」
The bleak scene capped a day when the president remained out of public view, awaiting the results of a campaign that he insisted should be seen as a referendum on him.
陰沉的場面為這一天畫上句號。總統當天沒有公開露面，等待著選舉結果出爐，而他一直堅稱，這場選舉應被視為對他本人的一次公投。 
Mr. Trump kicked off the day by making a final pitch to voters in a call to a friendly television program, “Fox & Friends,” promoting his administration’s immigration crackdown and claiming that his policies had boosted the economy.
當天早些時候，川普通過電話連線與他關係友好的電視節目《福克斯與朋友們》，向選民作最後的拉票。他宣傳了政府嚴厲打擊非法移民的行動，並聲稱自己的政策提振了經濟。
“Inflation is solved,” Mr. Trump asserted, even though consumer prices had surged since he took office, rising 3.7 percent in September from a year earlier, driven in part by high energy costs stemming from the war with Iran.
「通膨已經解決，」川普斷言，儘管自他上任以來消費價格飆升，9月同比上漲3.7%，部分原因是與伊朗戰爭導致的能源成本居高不下。
廣告
The United States, he added, was “the hottest country in the world.”
他還說，美國是「世界上最炙手可熱的國家」。
The dissonance between the president’s rosy assessments and the affordability worries of many voters underscored Mr. Trump’s incongruous closing argument.
總統對經濟的樂觀評價與許多選民對生活負擔的擔憂形成鮮明反差，凸顯了川普在競選最後階段的論調與現實之間的脫節。
He repeatedly mocked the concerns about affordability, blamed his predecessors for inflation and falsely claimed that grocery and gas prices were falling. He also said that rising gas prices were a negligible price to pay for the war with Iran, which he repeatedly claimed the United States had already won. (On Monday, Mr. Trump dismissed the idea that it was even a war.)
他一再嘲笑人們對生活成本的擔憂，將通膨歸咎於前幾任總統，並錯誤地聲稱食品雜貨和汽油價格正在下降。他還說，汽油價格上漲是與伊朗戰爭可以忽略不計的代價，並一再聲稱美國已經贏得了這場戰爭。（週一，川普甚至否認這是一場戰爭。） 
In one of the strangest turns of the campaign, Mr. Trump made his assessments of the male physique a mainstay of his stump speech, focusing in particular on the backside of Senator Jon Husted, Republican of Ohio.
這場競選最離奇的情節之一，是川普將自己對男性身材的品評變成了競選演講中的固定內容，他尤其關注俄亥俄州共和黨參議員喬恩·赫斯特德的臀部。
Rarely did Mr. Trump substantively address the issues that voters said were the most pressing in their lives, or acknowledge his own unpopularity. Heading into the election, views of his handling of the economy — one of his biggest strengths when he ran two years ago — were underwater. Majorities disapproved of his handling of tariffs, the economy, gas prices, the cost of living and the Iran war.
川普很少認真回應選民所說的生活中最緊迫的問題，也很少承認自己並不受歡迎。選舉臨近時，選民對他處理經濟問題的評價已是負面居多，而這原本是他兩年前競選時最大的優勢之一。多數選民不認可他在關稅、經濟、汽油價格、生活成本和對伊朗戰爭等問題上的表現。
As he traveled the country, the president promised giveaways, floating $500 Obamacare “refund” checks and $5,000 for every American adult — called the “Trump Dividend” — if Republicans retained control of Congress.
在全國各地奔走拉票期間，川普承諾向民眾發放現金福利，提出過每人500美元的歐巴馬醫改「退款」支票，還表示，如果共和黨繼續控制國會，將向每位美國成年人發放5000美元，稱之為「川普紅利」。 
At the center of the president’s pitch was that Americans should think of their votes as going to him. While headlining 30 rallies across the country, Mr. Trump pleaded with his base. “Pretend I’m on the ballot,” he often said.
川普拉票的核心訴求，是希望美國人應當把自己的選票想成是投給他的。在全國主持30場競選集會期間，他不斷懇求自己的基本盤選民。「就當我的名字也在選票上。」他經常這樣說。
But in the waning days of the campaign, Mr. Trump openly complained about the grueling pace. At a rally in Florida on Friday, he said that he thought it would be his last of the cycle, and that he would be able to relax afterward. “Twenty-eight days in a row of this crap is a lot,” the president said.
但在競選進入最後階段時，川普公開抱怨密集的行程令他疲憊不堪。週五在佛羅里達州的一場集會上，他說自己以為這將是本輪選舉週期的最後一場集會，之後終於可以放鬆一下了。「連續28天乾這種破事，實在夠嗆，」川普說。
廣告
The day before the election, Mr. Trump chose to make his final public appearance at the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool, a troubled project that had become emblematic of his administration’s willingness to skirt laws and target dissent to further his efforts to remake the capital.
選舉前一天，川普選擇在林肯紀念堂倒影池進行最後一次公開露面。這個麻煩不斷的工程項目，已經成為川普政府為改造首都而不惜繞開法律、打壓異議的一個典型例子。
“We gave this a very strong shot,” he said of the election.
談到這場選舉，他說：「我們已經盡了很大的努力。」 
“America is ready to go,” Mr. Trump added. “They’re ready to vote. I hope they’re ready to do the right thing.”
「美國準備好了，」川普補充說。「他們準備好投票了。我希望他們準備好做正確的事。」
Throughout Election Day, the White House sent out a flurry of news releases praising Mr. Trump’s accomplishments, including one about his “relentless travel schedule.”
選舉日當天，白宮接連發布大量新聞稿，讚揚川普的政績，其中一篇還特別提到了他「馬不停蹄的行程安排」。
But as the day wore on, Mr. Trump’s agitation burst through in sharply worded social media posts. He lashed out about coverage of his speech at the Reflecting Pool, lobbing insults at Representative Ted Lieu, Democrat of California, and at The Daily Mail for allegedly misrepresenting a joke he had made about wanting to name the landmark after himself.
但隨著時間推移，川普越來越難以掩飾自己的焦躁，在社群媒體上發表了一連串措辭尖銳的帖子。他對媒體報導自己在倒影池的講話大發雷霆，辱罵加利福尼亞州民主黨眾議員劉雲平和英國《每日郵報》，指責他們歪曲了他開玩笑說要以自己的名字重新命名這處地標的言論。
He appeared optimistic at one point in the day, when he called attention to results in Guam, where a Republican was poised to become governor. “Wow, hopefully that’s an indication for things to come!” he wrote.
當天有一度，他似乎頗為樂觀，因為關島的選舉結果顯示，一名共和黨人有望當選總督。他特意提及這一消息，寫道：「哇，希望這是接下來選舉結果的好兆頭！」
By early evening, as the president prepared to host a watch party in the East Room, he turned his ire on Fox News, calling a commentator “DUMB AS A ROCK.” He said that he would change the channel until that “LOSER is off the air.” (He added that he would turn back later.)
到傍晚，總統準備在白宮東廳舉辦觀選派對時，他把怒火轉向福克斯新聞，罵一名評論員「蠢得像塊石頭」。他說他會換台，直到那個「失敗者」不再上節目。（他又說稍後會換回來。）
Shortly after, he was back to railing about the cable channel, decrying its poll questions and complaining that its ratings were low because it spent “too much time covering the Trump Deranged Dumocrats!”
沒過多久，他又開始抨擊這家有線新聞頻道，指責其民調問題設置不當，並抱怨福克斯新聞收視率低迷，是因為它花了「太多時間報導那些患有『川普譫妄症』的民主黨蠢貨！」
