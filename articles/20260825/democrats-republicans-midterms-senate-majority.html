美國期中選舉：民主黨奪回參議院的勝算正在增加？

Can Democrats Win Back the Senate? Why Their Chances Are Improving.

TIM BALK • 2026-08-25
As a tumultuous primary season draws to a close, Democrats have opened up more than one path to winning control of the Senate, according to strategists in both parties and independent analysts, reshaping the battle on a map where Republicans still hold significant advantages.
隨著動盪的初選季接近尾聲，兩黨策略師和獨立分析人士表示，民主黨已為贏得參議院控制權開闢了多條路徑，從而在共和黨仍佔據顯著優勢的選情版圖上重塑了選戰格局。
Voters are uneasy about President Trump’s handling of the economy, and his unpopular war with Iran has driven increases in the costs of fuel, food and health services, putting Republicans on the defensive in states they once saw as much safer terrain.
選民對川普總統處理經濟的方式感到不安，而他不受歡迎的對伊戰爭推高了燃料、食品和醫療服務的成本，使共和黨在那些他們曾視為穩操勝券的州陷入守勢。
The basic contours of the map remain challenging for Democrats. Holding 47 seats in the Senate, they must flip at least four to win a majority. At the same time, they are trying to defend seats in two states Mr. Trump won in 2024. And four of the six most competitive races are in states Mr. Trump won by double digits.
選戰版圖的基本格局對民主黨人來說依然充滿挑戰。他們在參議院擁有47個席位，必須翻盤至少四個席位才能贏得多數席位。與此同時，他們正試圖在川普2024年贏得的兩個州保住席位。而在六場競爭最激烈的競選中，有四場發生在川普以兩位數優勢獲勝的州。
Republicans are also hoping that Democratic divisions in two important battlegrounds, Maine and Michigan, could help offset some of the political headwinds.
共和黨人還希望，在緬因州和密西根州這兩個重要戰場州，民主黨的內部分歧能夠有助於抵消部分政治逆勢。
廣告
Mr. Trump himself has expressed concern about a pattern of Republicans not performing as well in cycles when he is not on the ballot. “Pretend, please, that I’m on the ballot,” he said at a rally in South Carolina on Friday, aiming to motivate his base.
川普本人曾對共和黨在自己未參選的選舉週期中表現欠佳這一現象表示擔憂。「請假裝我也在選票上，」他在週五南卡羅來納州的一場集會上說道，旨在激勵其基本盤。
But Mr. Trump’s approval rating is below 40 percent, unnerving Republicans, who worry that voters in the center will break for Democrats. And rank-and-file Democratic voters are plainly energized: They have been turning out in huge numbers for primaries.
但川普的支持率低於40%，這讓共和黨人感到不安，他們擔心中間派選民會倒向民主黨。而普通民主黨選民顯然士氣高漲，在初選中踴躍投票。
Now the battle for the Senate, once seen as tilting heavily toward the G.O.P., is increasingly up for grabs.
如今，曾經被認為共和黨極有可能大勝的參議院爭奪戰正變得越來越勝負難料。
Democrats Make Recent Gains
民主黨近期取得進展
The National Picture
全國局勢
The latest show of Democratic strength came last week in conservative-leaning Alaska. Former Representative Mary Peltola, a Democrat who had recently fired a top aide and changed campaign managers, led Senator Dan S. Sullivan, the Republican incumbent, by more than five percentage points in the state’s nonpartisan primary, according to incomplete results.
上週，在傾向保守派的阿拉斯加州，民主黨再次展現了其實力。根據不完整的計票結果，民主黨前眾議員瑪麗·佩爾托拉在該州無黨派初選中以超過五個百分點的優勢領先現任共和黨參議員丹·沙利文。佩爾托拉最近解僱了一名高層助手並更換了競選經理。
廣告
“I haven’t seen that kind of energy for a Democrat in Alaska in decades,” said Senator Catherine Cortez Masto of Nevada, a former chair of Senate Democrats’ campaign arm. “Democrats are energized. You see it across the country.”
「幾十年裡，我都沒在阿拉斯加見到過民主黨人展現出這種幹勁，」內華達州參議員、參議院民主黨競選委員會前主席凱瑟琳·科爾特斯·馬斯托表示。「民主黨人士氣高昂。全國各地都能看到這一點。」
By Thursday, the Cook Political Report, a nonpartisan election forecaster, moved races in Texas and Iowa from “Lean Republican” to “Toss Up,” citing polling and the economic environment.
截至週四，無黨派選舉預測機構《庫克政治報告》援引民調和經濟環境，將得克薩斯州和愛荷華州的競選評級從「傾向共和黨」調整為「勢均力敵」。
The shift grew the core Senate battlefield from four states — Alaska, Maine, Michigan and Ohio — to six, widening Democrats’ margin for error.
這一轉變將參議院的核心戰場從四個州（阿拉斯加州、緬因州、密西根州和俄亥俄州）擴大到六個州，拓寬了民主黨的容錯空間。
“It’s a 50-50 tossup,” said Douglas Heye, a Republican strategist. “Republicans are nervous. They’ve watched Trump’s numbers be a constant limbo contest of ‘How low can they go?’”
「這是一場五五開的角逐，」共和黨策略師道格拉斯·海伊說。「共和黨人很緊張。他們看著川普的數據成了一場無休無止的懸念競賽——『還能低到什麼程度？』」
While Republican groups have a vast cash advantage over Democratic ones, individual Democratic Senate candidates are handily out-raising their Republican opponents in most of the key Senate races.
儘管共和黨團體相較於民主黨團體擁有巨大的資金優勢，但在大多數關鍵參議院競選中，民主党參議員候選人個人的籌款額輕鬆超過了他們的共和黨對手。
Alex Latcham, executive director of the top Senate Republican super PAC, the Senate Leadership Fund, said his party still had “advantages in the quality of candidates, in our message and in our ability to raise and spend resources.”
參議院頂級共和黨超級政治行動委員會參議院領導基金的執行董事亞歷克斯·拉查姆表示，共和黨仍然在「候選人質量、我們的主張以及籌集和使用資源的能力方面擁有優勢」。
廣告
As of Friday, Republican advertising spending surpassed that of Democrats in all the top six races except for the one in Texas, according to the ad-tracking firm AdImpact. Democrats, whose national party is entering the fall in debt, argue that spending will not be decisive.
根據廣告跟蹤公司AdImpact的數據，截至週五，除了得克薩斯州之外，在其餘六場主要選戰中，共和黨的廣告支出均超過了民主黨人。民主黨全國委員會在進入秋季時已背負債務，該黨認為支出並非決定性因素。
“The truth is that all the money in the world should not change the fact that Trump and the Republicans in Congress have made life much more difficult,” said Senator Kirsten Gillibrand of New York, chair of Senate Democrats’ campaign arm, adding: “People are fed up. And they’re exhausted. And they can’t afford anything. And they’re going to change who’s in charge.”
「事實是，世界上所有的錢都改變不了川普和國會共和黨人讓民眾生活變得更加艱難這一事實，」參議院民主黨競選委員會主席、紐約州參議員柯斯滕·吉利布蘭德說。她還說：「人們受夠了。他們身心俱疲。他們什麼都買不起。他們要改變執政者。」
Alaska, Iowa and Ohio
阿拉斯加州、愛荷華州和俄亥俄州
Economic Conditions Challenge Republicans
經濟形勢對共和黨人構成挑戰
By May, when the average gas price rose as high as $4.56 a gallon, Mr. Trump had dismissed the economic costs of the war with Iran, saying they were outweighed by national security interests.
到5月份，當平均汽油價格升至每加侖4.56美元的高位時，川普曾輕描淡寫地帶過與伊朗開戰帶來的經濟代價，稱國家安全利益比這些代價更為重要。
“I don’t think about Americans’ financial situation,” he told reporters in mid-May. In a cabinet meeting later that month he declared, “I don’t care about the midterms.”
「我不考慮美國人的財務狀況，」他在5月中旬對記者說。在那個月晚些時候的一次內閣會議上，他宣稱：「我不在乎期中選舉。」
廣告
Gas prices have since dropped somewhat from their spring peaks, but they remain elevated, and the fallout from the fighting has spilled across the economy, driving inflation that has outpaced wage growth. Voters say they feel gloomy about the economy.
此後，汽油價格雖從春季高點有所回落，但仍居高不下，戰事帶來的負面效應已蔓延至整個經濟領域，推高了超過工資增長速度的通膨率。選民表示對經濟前景感到悲觀。
Some states at the center of the Senate battle have been hit hard.
一些處於參議院選戰核心的州受到了重創。
In Alaska, the cost of a gallon of gas on Sunday was $4.82, roughly 70 cents above the national average, according to the AAA motor club.
根據美國汽車協會的數據，週日，阿拉斯加州一加侖汽油的成本為4.82美元，比全國平均水平高出約70美分。
Josh Turek, a centrist Democrat, is trying to flip a Senate seat in Iowa, which has a farming-heavy economy that has struggled under Mr. Trump’s tariff policies in his second term. Those troubles have been exacerbated by fertilizer prices that have soared since the war began.
溫和派民主黨人喬什·圖雷克正試圖在愛荷華州奪取一個參議院席位，該州經濟以農業為主，在川普第二任期內的關稅政策下舉步維艱。自戰爭爆發以來，暴漲的化肥價格更是加劇了這一困境。
The economic pressures have been enough to give Democrats hope in a state where Republicans feel their nominee, Representative Ashley Hinson, a skilled fund-raiser, is a particularly strong candidate.
在該州，儘管共和黨人認為其提名人——擅長籌款的眾議員阿什利·欣森——是一位特別有力的候選人，但經濟壓力已足以讓民主黨人看到希望。
The same economic forces are at play in Ohio, another agriculture-heavy state. There, Sherrod Brown, a former senator and a Democrat, is trying to stage a comeback and has out-raised Senator Jon Husted, the Republican incumbent, by an overwhelming margin.
同樣的經濟因素也在另一個農業重鎮俄亥俄州發揮作用。在那裡，民主黨前參議員肖羅德·布朗正試圖重返政壇，其籌款額以壓倒性優勢超過了現任共和黨參議員強·胡斯特德。
廣告
Dale Butland, a Democratic strategist in Ohio, said the signs of deep voter discontent were plainly visible. “It’s bad here,” Mr. Butland said of economic conditions in Ohio. But politically, he added, “it’s all breaking now in our favor.”
俄亥俄州的民主黨策略師戴爾·布特蘭表示，選民深感不滿的跡象清晰可見。「這裡情況很糟糕，」布特蘭先生在談到俄亥俄州的經濟狀況時說。但他還說，從政治上看，「現在一切都在朝有利於我們的方向發展。」
Maine, Michigan and Texas
緬因州、密西根州和得克薩斯州
Candidate Concerns for Both Parties
兩黨候選人面臨的擔憂
In other areas of the country, Republicans see more encouraging signs.
在全國其他地區，共和黨人看到了更多令人鼓舞的跡象。
In Maine and Michigan, Republicans say that primary season handed them relatively weak Senate opponents who they believe could lose in more Democratic-leaning terrain. In Maine, the candidate the party initially nominated, Graham Platner, was accused of rape a month after the primary.
在緬因州和密西根州，共和黨人表示，初選季給他們帶來了相對較弱的參議院對手，他們認為這些對手可能會在更傾向於民主黨的地區敗北。在緬因州，民主黨最初提名的候選人格雷厄姆·普拉特納在初選一個月後被指控強姦。
Mr. Platner, who denied the allegation, exited the race, requiring Democrats in Maine to scramble to find a last-minute replacement. They settled on Troy Jackson, who finished third in Maine’s primary for governor in June.
普拉特納否認這一指控，並退出了競選，使得緬因州的民主黨人不得不倉促尋找臨時代替人選。他們最終敲定了特洛伊·傑克遜，他在6月緬因州州長初選中排名第三。
Democrats have appeared to quickly coalesce behind Mr. Jackson, and some polls show him leading Senator Susan Collins, the Republican incumbent. But many in the party remain anxious about his electoral track record. And they are concerned that some Democrats captivated by Mr. Platner’s campaign may not turn out for a candidate who did not go through the normal primary process.
民主黨人似乎迅速團結在傑克遜身後，一些民調顯示，他領先於現任共和黨參議員蘇珊·柯林斯。但黨內許多人仍對其過往的選舉記錄感到焦慮。他們還擔心，一些被普拉特納競選活動吸引的民主黨人可能不會為一位沒有經過正常初選程序的候選人投票。
In Michigan, where Mr. Trump won narrowly in 2024, Democrats are rushing to heal the wounds from a bruising primary animated by personal attacks and fierce disagreements over Israel. Some Jewish Democrats in Michigan have declined to immediately back the nominee, Dr. Abdul El-Sayed, who spoke out forcefully against Israel during the primary.
在川普2024年以微弱優勢獲勝的密西根州，民主黨人正急於彌合初選留下的創傷。那場初選因人身攻擊以及圍繞以色列問題的激烈分歧而變得異常激烈。密西根州的一些猶太裔民主黨人拒絕立即支持被提名人阿卜杜勒·埃爾-賽義德博士，他在初選期間曾強烈發聲反對以色列。
The Republican nominee, Mike Rogers, who is trying to flip a Democratic-held seat, has already begun to issue appeals to disaffected Democrats. Dr. El-Sayed, a left-wing insurgent, is trying to defy a trend for activist progressives, who have tended to perform worse electorally than the rest of their party.
共和黨候選人邁克·羅傑斯正試圖奪取一個由民主黨掌控的席位，他已經開始向不滿的民主黨人爭取支持。作為左翼新銳的埃爾-賽義德正試圖顛覆激進進步派在選舉中的表現往往遜於同黨其他成員的趨勢。
In Texas, on the other hand, Democrats believe Republicans offered them an opening by nominating Ken Paxton, the state’s scandal-plagued attorney general.
另一方面，在得克薩斯州，民主黨人認為共和黨人提名了該州醜聞纏身的總檢察長肯·帕克斯頓，從而給他們留出了機會。
Mr. Paxton has been indicted on securities fraud charges (he reached a deal with prosecutors to avoid a criminal trial), has escaped an impeachment trial, has been accused of adultery by his estranged wife and was even caught on video years ago pilfering a prize pen.
帕克斯頓曾因證券欺詐指控被起訴（他與檢方達成協議以避免刑事審判），他在彈劾案審判中脫身，分居的妻子指控他通姦，他甚至在多年前曾被影片拍到順手牽羊拿走一支名貴鋼筆。
Democrats have nominated James Talarico, a Presbyterian seminarian who has made his Christian faith a centerpiece of his campaign. The popular podcaster Joe Rogan once told him to run for president.
民主黨提名了詹姆斯·塔拉里科，他是一位長老會神學生，將自己的基督教信仰作為競選的核心。知名播客主持人喬·羅根曾建議他去競選總統。
The contrast between the candidates has fed Democratic hopes and Republican fears that Texas might slip out of G.O.P. hands for the first time in three decades.
候選人之間的鮮明對比燃起了民主黨的希望，也讓共和黨人擔憂得克薩斯州可能會在三十年來首次脫離共和黨掌控。
Georgia and North carolina
喬治亞州和北卡羅萊納州
Democratic Strength in the South
民主黨在南方的優勢
Nowhere is the Senate picture brighter for Democrats than in the South, where two popular and well-known Democrats have raced out to leads in states Mr. Trump carried two years ago.
對民主黨人而言，參議院選情在南方最為樂觀。川普兩年前曾贏得這些州，但如今兩位廣受歡迎且聲名顯赫的民主黨人已迅速取得領先優勢。
In Georgia, Senator Jon Ossoff, a Democrat and the youngest sitting senator, has a huge war chest and consistently leads his Republican rival, Representative Mike Collins, in the polls. His advantage, which Georgia Republicans attribute in part to his focus on constituent services, has led some Republicans to write off the race, and some Democrats to encourage him to run for president in 2028.
在喬治亞州，現任最年輕參議員、民主黨人喬恩·奧索夫擁有巨額競選資金，並且在民調中持續領先其共和黨對手、眾議員邁克·柯林斯。喬治亞州的共和黨人將他的優勢部分歸因於他專注於選民服務，這導致一些共和黨人放棄了該州競選，而一些民主黨人鼓勵奧索夫在2028年競選總統。
In neighboring North Carolina, the Democratic nominee, former Gov. Roy Cooper, is trying to flip a seat and is running as a tough-on-crime moderate. He has never lost an election. Mr. Cooper consistently leads his Republican opponent, Michael Whatley, in the polls, with some showing him ahead by double digits.
在鄰近的北卡羅萊納州，民主黨提名人、前州長羅伊·庫珀正試圖從共和黨手中奪取一個席位，他正以對犯罪態度強硬的中間派身份參選。他從未輸過任何一場選舉。庫珀在民調中持續領先其共和黨對手麥可·沃特利，部分民調顯示他的領先達到兩位數。
