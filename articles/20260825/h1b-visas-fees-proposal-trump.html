川普政府擬對H-1B簽證徵收逾10萬美元費用

Trump Officials Propose Charging More Than $100,000 for Skilled Worker Visas

MADELEINE NGO • 2026-08-25
The Trump administration on Monday proposed charging a new steep fee for visas granted to skilled foreign workers, in the latest attempt to fundamentally overhaul the H-1B visa program.
川普政府週一提議，對授予高技術外籍工人的簽證收取一筆新的高額費用，這是其從根本上改革H-1B簽證項目的最新嘗試。
Under the proposal, the Department of Homeland Security would attach a $103,265 fee to the visa, mirroring a move by the administration last year to impose a $100,000 fee to new H-1B visas. But that effort ran into legal stumbles. A federal judge threw out the fee in June, a decision that the federal government said it would appeal.
根據這項提案，國土安全部將對每份簽證收取103265美元的費用，這與政府去年對每份新H-1B簽證徵收10萬美元費用的舉措如出一轍。但那次努力遭遇了法律障礙。一位聯邦法官在6月否決了該費用，聯邦政府表示將對這項裁決提出上訴。
Critics of the proposed regulation view it as another attempt by the administration to install the fee, despite the legal challenges. If finalized, the fee would be applied in a substantially broader way and have far-reaching implications for businesses across the country that rely on the H-1B program to fill crucial jobs.
批評該擬議監管規則的人士認為，這仍是政府再次試圖強征該費用，儘管在法律上已經面臨挑戰。若該規定最終敲定，其適用範圍將大幅擴大，並對全國依賴H-1B項目填補關鍵崗位的企業產生深遠影響。
“It’s much bigger in scope,” said Doug Rand, who was a senior official at U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services in the Biden administration. “This regulation, if enacted, would have catastrophic effects on America’s global talent pipeline.”
「其範圍要大得多，」曾在拜登政府時期擔任美國公民及移民服務局高級官員的道格·蘭德表示。「這項法規若得以實施，將對美國的全球人才輸送管道造成災難性影響。」
廣告
The $100,000 fee was imposed by President Trump last September through a proclamation citing the president’s authority to shut out certain foreign nationals from entering the United States. That meant the fee was not triggered by every new H-1B visa issued this year — it applied only to workers outside the United States, meaning that companies that hired workers already in the country were able to skirt the fee.
川普總統去年9月通過一則公告宣布徵收10萬美元費用，該公告利用的是總統禁止某些外國國民入境的權力。這意味著並非今年簽發的每一份新H-1B簽證都需繳納該費用——它僅適用於在美國境外的工人，因此，僱用已在美國境內工人的公司得以規避此費用。
Many firms, including big technology companies, were largely shielded from the changes because they mostly use the H-1B program to recruit international students and other foreign workers who already have some form of status inside the United States. The impact was mostly felt by companies that relied on recruiting workers from overseas, such as I.T. firms that had been among the program’s biggest beneficiaries for years.
包括大型科技企業，許多公司在很大程度上免受這些變化的影響，因為它們主要利用H-1B項目招聘國際學生或其他已在美國境內擁有某種身份的外籍工人。影響主要集中在依賴從海外招聘工人的公司，例如多年來一直是該項目最大受益者之一的IT公司。
But the new proposal appears to apply to workers inside the United States, marking a major shift and potentially requiring more companies to re-evaluate their use of the program.
但新提案似乎也適用於美國境內的工人，這標誌著一個重大轉變，可能迫使更多公司重新評估對H-1B的使用。
In a statement, U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services said the new fee would apply to all petitions subject to the annual cap on H-1B visas. Because demand typically exceeds the small number of visas available each year, most employers must enter a lottery once a year to compete for them. This year, the agency said it received more than 211,000 registrations, far exceeding the 85,000 visas available annually.
美國公民及移民服務局在一份聲明中表示，新費用將適用於所有受H-1B簽證年度配額限制的申請。由於需求通常遠超每年可用的少量簽證，大多數僱主必須每年參加一次抽籤以競爭名額。服務局稱，今年收到了超過21.1萬份登記，遠高於每年8.5萬個簽證的配額。
The Homeland Security Department estimated that the new fee would generate about $8.8 billion annually. The agency said the proposed fee would not apply to employers exempt from the annual cap on the visas, providing some relief for nonprofit universities and hospitals that can apply for H-1B visas year-round.
國土安全部估計，新費用每年可帶來約88億美元收入。該機構表示，擬議費用不適用於不受年度配額限制的僱主，這為非營利性大學和醫院提供了一定緩衝，它們可以全年申請H-1B簽證。
The Trump administration has called for an overhaul of the H-1B visa program, saying it has unfairly allowed companies to sideline Americans and hire foreign workers at lower pay. The program has long faced controversy, garnering calls for reform across the political spectrum.
川普政府呼籲對H-1B簽證項目進行全面改革，稱該項目不公平地讓公司排擠美國人，並以較低的薪酬僱用外籍工人。該項目長期以來一直飽受爭議，各政治派別都呼籲進行改革。
廣告
Still, economists generally agree that the visa holders boost American productivity and raise wages even for American workers.
儘管如此，經濟學家普遍認為，簽證持有者提高了美國的生產率，甚至也提高了美國工人的工資。
Some business groups immediately voiced concern over the administration’s proposal, saying it would prevent smaller businesses from accessing the visas.
一些商業團體立即對政府的提案表示擔憂，稱這會阻礙小企業獲得簽證。
Neil Bradley, chief policy officer at the U.S. Chamber of Commerce, said the fee would make it prohibitive for “even more U.S. employers — especially the thousands of start-ups and small and midsize businesses who rely on H-1Bs — to utilize the program.”
美國商會首席政策官尼爾·布拉德利表示，該費用將使「更多的美國僱主——尤其是成千上萬依賴H-1B的初創企業以及中小型企業——無法使用該項目」。
“The program helps companies expand, innovate, and create jobs across their operations,” Mr. Bradley said in a statement.
「該項目幫助企業擴大規模、創新，並在其運營的各環節創造就業機會，」布拉德利在聲明中表示。
The chamber challenged the $100,000 fee in court last year, saying it would “inflict significant harm on American businesses.” In that lawsuit, a federal judge allowed the administration to move forward with the visa fee, leading the chamber to say that it would appeal the decision. Six months later, a federal judge in Massachusetts reached the opposite decision in a separate lawsuit and voided the policy.
商會去年在法庭上對10萬美元費用提出質疑，稱其將「對美國企業造成嚴重損害」。在那起訴訟中，一名聯邦法官允許政府繼續收取簽證費，商會隨後表示將對該裁決提出上訴。六個月後，麻薩諸塞州的另一名聯邦法官在另一起訴訟中得出了相反結論，並廢止了該政策。
U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services said the fee would help cover the costs of multiple federal agencies under the Homeland Security Department.
公民及移民服務局表示，該費用將有助於支付國土安全部下屬多個聯邦機構的成本。
廣告
“The proposed H‑1B fee is intended to recover the costs incurred across the federal government to adjudicate, vet and support lawful immigration programs that otherwise must be funded by taxpayers,” Zach Kahler, a spokesman for U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services, said in a statement.
「擬議的H-1B費用旨在回收聯邦政府為審批、審查和支持合法移民項目所產生的成本，否則這些成本必須由納稅人承擔，」服務局發言人扎克·卡勒在聲明中表示。
U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services is primarily funded by fees it charges people applying for immigration benefits, such as green cards. But in its proposal, the agency said it would also use the money it collected from the H-1B fee to cover certain costs at other agencies like Immigration and Customs Enforcement, which already receives funding appropriated by Congress.
服務局的資金主要來源於其向申請移民身份（如綠卡）的人收取的費用。但在其提案中，該機構表示還將用從H-1B費用中收取的資金來支付移民及海關執法局等其他機構的某些成本，而後者已獲得國會撥付的資金。
“That seems to be very legally tenuous, if not outright illegal,” said Sarah Pierce, a former policy analyst at Citizenship and Immigration Services, who is now the director of social policy at the center-left think tank Third Way.
「這似乎在法律上非常站不住腳，甚至根本就是違法的，」曾任公民及移民服務局政策分析師、現為中左翼智庫「第三條道路」社會政策主任的莎拉·皮爾斯表示。
