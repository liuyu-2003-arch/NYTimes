人民幣疲軟與出口退稅：中國出口繁榮背後的政策

The Hidden Policies That Power China’s Export Boom

KEITH BRADSHER • 2026-10-08
China has kept its export engine roaring with a combination of tax rebates and a weak currency, helping sustain economic growth at home while sending a surge of Chinese goods into markets abroad.
中國藉助出口退稅與貨幣疲軟的雙重策略維持了其出口引擎的強勁運轉，在支撐國內經濟增長的同時，也將大量中國商品推入海外市場。
But that strategy is fueling tension with its trading partners. And this week, officials from Brussels arriving in Beijing are preparing to press Chinese officials about a European trade deficit with China that exceeds $1.1 billion a day.
但這一策略正加劇中國與其貿易夥伴的緊張關係。本週，前往北京的布魯塞爾官員準備就歐洲對華每日逾11億美元的貿易逆差問題向中國官員施壓。
Europe’s leaders say China tilts the scale with government subsidies and low-interest loans to its manufacturers, flooding European markets with inexpensive goods.
歐洲領導人表示，中國的政府補貼以及向製造商提供低息貸款破壞了公平競爭，使廉價商品充斥歐洲市場。
But subsidies are only part of the advantage Chinese exporters enjoy. The renminbi, China’s currency, remains unusually weak, making China’s goods cheaper overseas. At the same time, Beijing refunds exporters hundreds of billions of dollars a year in taxes, further lowering their costs.
但補貼並非中國出口商的全部優勢。中國貨幣人民幣持續異常疲軟，使中國商品在海外更具價格競爭力。與此同時，北京每年向出口商退還數千億美元稅款，進一步壓低了他們的成本。
廣告
Both policies have become difficult for China to unwind.
如今，兩項政策中國都很難輕易撤回。
The renminbi is down more than 14 percent in inflation-adjusted terms from its early-2022 level against a broad set of currencies. Over roughly the same period, China’s trade surplus has nearly doubled. While the weak currency helps exporters, it also makes foreign goods more costly for Chinese consumers and businesses.
經通膨調整後，人民幣兌一攬子貨幣的匯率較2022年初水平下跌逾14%。在幾乎同一時期，中國的貿易順差接近翻番。疲軟的貨幣在提振出口的同時，也使得外國商品對中國消費者和企業而言變得更加昂貴。
China’s export tax rebates have also surged. Rebates to export companies for value-added taxes reached $318.2 billion last year, equivalent to 1.5 percent of China’s economy, according to data from the country’s Ministry of Finance.
中國的出口退稅規模同樣大幅飆升。根據中國財政部的數據，去年向出口企業返還的增值稅達到3182億美元，相當於中國經濟總量的1.5%。
Together, the weak currency and extensive tax rebates capture the dilemma facing Chinese policymakers. Exports are one of the few bright spots in an economy weighed down by plunging apartment prices and weak consumer spending. Maintaining export momentum is important to Beijing’s goal of 4.5 percent to 5 percent economic growth. But supporting those exports carries high costs at home and abroad.
貨幣疲軟和大規模退稅共同折射出中國決策者面臨的困境。在因房價暴跌和消費者支出疲軟而承壓的經濟大環境下，出口成為為數不多的亮點之一。維持出口勢頭對北京實現4.5%至5%的經濟增長目標至關重要。但支持這些出口也在國內外帶來了高昂代價。
A weak renminbi raises the cost of oil and other imports, squeezing households already reluctant to spend. Export tax rebates drain government revenues when many local governments are struggling with the financial fallout from the housing downturn.
疲軟的人民幣推高了石油及其他進口商品的成本，進一步擠壓了本就不願消費的家庭。在許多地方政府努力應對房地產低迷帶來的財政衝擊之際，出口退稅又進一步消耗了政府收入。
For China’s trading partners, Europe in particular, resentment has been building, prompting more protectionist policies.
對於中國的貿易夥伴，特別是歐洲而言，不滿情緒持續積聚，從而催生了更多的保護主義政策。
廣告
“We need to rebalance our economic ties to ensure they are fair, sustainable and mutually beneficial — the current situation is unsustainable,” said Caspar Veldkamp, the European Union ambassador to China, in a speech on Sept. 22.
「我們需要重新平衡雙方的經濟關係，以確保其公平、可持續且互利互惠——目前的狀況是不可持續的，」歐盟駐華大使卡斯帕·費爾德坎普在9月22日的一場演講中表示。
With China on track to match or exceed last year’s record global trade surplus of $1.19 trillion, allowing the renminbi to strengthen and reducing tax rebates could ease some pressure from overseas. But either step could also damage one of the economy’s few remaining engines of growth.
鑒於中國有望達到或超越去年1.19萬億美元的創紀錄全球貿易順差，允許人民幣升值並削減退稅可能會緩解部分來自海外的壓力。然而，這兩項舉措中的任何一項也都有可能損害中國經濟僅存的少數幾個增長引擎之一。
Some prominent Chinese economists have begun arguing that a stronger currency may benefit China by lowering the cost of oil and other imports priced in dollars. China’s central bank and state-owned banks intervene heavily in currency markets to set the renminbi's value, although market forces also play a role.
一些知名中國經濟學家已開始提出，更為強勁的貨幣可能會通過降低以美元計價的石油等進口商品的成本而使中國受益。儘管市場力量同樣發揮作用，中國央行及大型國有銀行依然會大量干預貨幣市場以設定人民幣匯率。
“We need to reassess the issue of exchange rates: An appreciation of the renminbi is not necessarily entirely negative, while depreciation may not effectively boost exports,” said Shen Guobing, an economics professor at Fudan University in Shanghai, in a speech last December.
「我們需要重新審視匯率問題：人民幣升值並不全然是負面效應，貶值也未必能有效提振出口，」上海復旦大學經濟學教授沈國兵在去年12月的一次發言中指出。
Beijing has been cautious about allowing meaningful appreciation. Policymakers are well aware of Europe’s unhappiness, but remain wary that a rapid rise in the renminbi would slow the country’s vast export machine.
在允許人民幣大幅升值方面，北京一直持謹慎態度。決策者深知歐洲方面的不滿，但依然警惕人民幣的快速升值會拖累該國龐大的出口機器。
The same caution applies to export tax rebates. Before the Covid-19 pandemic, they consumed about a tenth of tax revenue. Now, as exports surge while much of the domestic economy stagnates, they account for nearly one-eighth.
同樣的謹慎態度也體現在出口退稅上。在新冠疫情爆發前，出口退稅消耗了約十分之一的稅收收入。如今，在出口激增而大部分國內經濟陷入停滯的情況下，其佔比已接近八分之一。
廣告
China has announced plans to halt tax rebates for battery exporters next year. Some influential economists are urging the government to go much further.
中國已宣布計劃在明年停止對電池出口商的退稅。一些具有影響力的經濟學家正敦促政府採取更加深入的行動。
“Export tax rebates, which are essentially a form of export subsidy, should be reduced,” said Huang Qifan, a former mayor of Chongqing and an influential Chinese economist, in a speech in May.
「下調出口退稅率相當於削減出口補貼，」前重慶市市長、頗具影響力的中國經濟學家黃奇帆在5月的一場演講中表示。
The reasons for Beijing’s reluctance to jeopardize exports can be seen in Guangzhou, a metropolis in southern China’s Pearl River Delta and the heart of China’s garment export industry.
在中國南方珠江三角洲的大都市、同時也是中國服裝出口行業中心的廣州，可以窺見北京不願危及出口的原因。
On Haizhu Island, dozens of men and women loitered at an outdoor hiring area late one afternoon after waiting hours for anyone willing to pay them $45 to $60 for a day of sewing.
在海珠區一個露天招工區域的傍晚，數十名男女閑坐著。他們已等待了數小時，希望能找到願意支付45至60美元雇他們做一天縫紉工作的人。
“Business has slowed down — the first half of the year was a little better, but things are really bad now,” said a worker at the hiring area who gave only his family name, Liu.
「生意變淡了——今年上半年稍微好一點，但現在情況真的很糟，」招工區一名僅透露自己姓劉的工人說。
China’s housing crash has crippled domestic demand for new clothes, while tariffs and competition from lower-wage countries have hurt exports. Day labor rates occasionally reached as high as $150 a few years ago, when exports and domestic demand were much stronger, said the owner of a sewing machine distributorship whose family name is Yi.
中國房地產市場的崩盤嚴重打擊了國內消費者對購置新衣的需求，而關稅以及來自低工資國家的競爭則對出口造成了傷害。一名姓易的縫紉機經銷店老闆表示，在幾年前出口和內需強勁得多的時候，日結工人的薪水偶爾能高達150美元。
廣告
“Compared with previous years, our business is down by at least a third this year — you could even say it’s close to half,” he said.
「與往年相比，今年我們的生意至少下滑了三分之一——甚至可以說接近減半，」他說。
Such weakness helps explain why Beijing is reluctant to risk policies that could further undermine exports. Until 2022, the currency gradually strengthened in inflation-adjusted terms for many years, according to the Bank for International Settlements in Basel, Switzerland, which has tracked the renminbi’s weakness.
這種疲軟表現有助於解釋為何北京不願冒險採取可能進一步削弱出口的政策。根據追蹤人民幣疲軟態勢的瑞士巴塞爾國際清算銀行的數據，在2022年之前，經通膨調整後的人民幣匯率曾連續多年逐步走強。
That changed after a stringent Covid lockdown began in Shanghai in March 2022. Consumer confidence plunged to the lowest level in 36 years of government record-keeping, while companies and households sought to move savings out of China. The renminbi started a long decline that eventually took it down about 20 percent.
這一情況在2022年3月上海實施嚴格的新冠封控後發生了改變。消費者信心暴跌至有政府記錄36年來的最低水平，同時企業和家庭試圖將儲蓄轉移出中國。人民幣自此開啟了長期的下跌，最終累計貶值約20%。
The slide coincided with the collapse of China’s housing market. Prices for existing homes have fallen about 40 percent since the summer of 2021, eroding household savings and further weakening consumer spending.
這一跌勢與中國房地產市場的崩盤同步發生。自2021年夏季以來，現房價格下跌了約40%，侵蝕了家庭儲蓄並進一步削弱了消費者支出。
The renminbi has strengthened somewhat over the past 15 months, but it remains far below pre-Covid levels after adjusting for inflation.
在過去15個月裡，人民幣匯率有所回升，但在經通膨調整後，依然遠低於疫情前的水平。
Currency movements can take time to alter decisions companies make about where they buy components and other supplies. American importers tend not to look for new suppliers unless a currency moves sharply against the dollar, said Joe Jurken, the managing director of ABC Group, a Milwaukee-based supply chain consultancy.
貨幣波動往往需要一定時間才能改變企業在何處採購零部件和其他耗材的決策。總部位於密爾瓦基的供應鏈諮詢機構ABC集團董事總經理喬·尤肯表示，除非某種貨幣兌美元出現劇烈波動，否則美國進口商通常不會去尋找新的供應商。
廣告
“A 15 percent appreciation would open up eyes and start some discussions,” he said.
「15%的升值幅度才會有人正眼看待，開始討論對策，」他說。
A weak currency is not the only reason for China’s export strength. Chinese companies have become formidable competitors through innovation in sectors such as batteries, electric vehicles and solar panels. Fierce domestic competition has also pushed manufacturers to cut costs and prices.
貨幣疲軟並非中國出口強勁的唯一原因。憑藉在電池、電動汽車和太陽能電池板等領域的創新，中國企業已成為極具實力的競爭對手。激烈的國內競爭也迫使製造商不斷壓低成本和價格。
But some economists argue that those low prices also reflect weaknesses in the domestic economy, including pressure on wages and corporate profits.
但部分經濟學家指出，這些低廉的價格同樣反映了國內經濟的疲軟，包括在工資和企業利潤方面承受的壓力。
“The high quality and low price of Chinese goods is a global triumph, but it is built on the suppressed wages and squeezed margins of Chinese workers and enterprises,” wrote Zhao Jian, the founding dean of the Xijing Research Institute, a commercial research foundation, in a commentary on Sept. 22 in Caixin, a Chinese business publication.
「中國商品物美價廉是一場全球性的勝利，但這是建立在中國工人和企業被壓抑的工資和被擠壓的利潤空間之上的，」商業研究機構西京研究院創始院長趙建在9月22日發表於中國商業出版物《財新》的一篇評論文章中寫道。
Bert Hofman, a former China country director for the World Bank, said reducing tax rebates might have a bigger impact than allowing the renminbi to appreciate in terms of freeing up government revenue for domestic priorities.
前世界銀行中國局局長郝福滿(Bert Hofman)表示，就騰出政府資金用於國內優先事項而言，減少退稅可能比允許人民幣升值產生更大的影響。
“China benefits by increasing revenues, which they can use to increase government spending on health and education,” he said.
「中國能通過增加財政收入獲益，他們可以利用這些收入來提升政府在醫療和教育領域的支出，」他說。
