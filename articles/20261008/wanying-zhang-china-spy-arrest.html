加州女子被指控幫中國監視賴清德家人

U.S. Citizen Accused of Surveilling Taiwanese Family on China’s Behalf

JACEY FORTIN, AMY QIN, VIK JOLLY • 2026-10-08
A California woman charged with surveilling relatives of the Taiwanese president on China’s behalf made her first appearance before a federal judge on Monday.
週一，一名涉嫌為中國監視台灣領導人親屬的加利福尼亞州女子首次出庭接受聯邦法官審訊。
The woman, Wanying Zhang, of Irvine, was about to board a flight to China when she was taken into custody at Los Angeles International Airport on Sunday, officials said. According to the F.B.I., the targets of the surveillance were the son of President Lai Ching-te of Taiwan and his family members.
官員稱，該女子是來自爾灣的張婉瑩（音）。週日，她在洛杉磯國際機場準備登上飛往中國的航班時被拘捕。據聯邦調查局稱，其監視目標是台灣領導人賴清德的兒子及其他家庭成員。
Ms. Zhang, 34, was charged with acting as an unregistered agent of a foreign government, a charge that has previously been used to prosecute Chinese influence cases. The Justice Department has referred to the charge as “espionage lite” because it has a lower bar for evidence.
34歲的張女士被控充當未註冊的外國政府代理人。這項指控此前曾被用於起訴涉及中國影響力的案件。由於該指控的證據門檻較低，美國司法部將其稱為「輕量版間諜罪」。
Ms. Zhang appeared at a federal courthouse in Los Angeles on Monday but could not be arraigned because she was deemed ineligible for a court-appointed lawyer, meaning she will have to retain private counsel. The federal defense lawyer who was initially assigned to represent her declined to comment on Monday, and efforts to reach Ms. Zhang’s family members were unsuccessful.
週一，張女士在洛杉磯一家聯邦法院出庭，但因被認定不具獲得法庭指定律師資格而未能完成提審程序，這意味著她必須聘請私人律師。最初被指派代表她的聯邦辯護律師週一拒絕置評，記者試圖聯繫張女士家屬未果。
廣告
Beijing has long sought to reach across borders to influence local American politics, keep tabs on members of the Chinese diaspora and promote the country’s agenda abroad. And while the criminal complaint against Ms. Zhang suggests that federal investigators think she may have been enlisted in those efforts, she has not been charged with espionage, which is harder to prove.
長期以來，北京一直尋求跨國影響美國地方政治、密切監視海外華人，並在海外推進其政治議程。儘管針對張女士的刑事起訴書表明聯邦調查人員認為她可能受雇參與了上述活動，但由於間諜罪更難舉證，她並未受到此項指控。
Instead, she was charged under a statute that makes it illegal to act as an agent of a foreign government without notifying the U.S. attorney general.
對她的起訴所依據的是一項法令，規定在未通知美國司法部長的情況下充當外國政府代理人屬非法行為。
While espionage cases usually require the government to prove a threat to national security, the statute used to charge Ms. Zhang concerns undisclosed relationships with foreign officials, said Ronak D. Desai, an expert in foreign influence law and a partner at the law firm Paul Hastings.
普衡律師事務所(Paul Hastings)合伙人、外國影響力法專家羅納克·D·德賽表示，間諜案件通常需要政府證明國家安全受到威脅，而起訴張女士所依據的法令針對的是未披露與外國官員的關係。
“The activity that the individual was undertaking did not, in itself, have to be illegal,” he added.
「具體此人正在從事的活動，本身並不一定是非法的，」他補充道。
The complaint said that Ms. Zhang, who the authorities said is a naturalized U.S. citizen and also goes by the name Heather, conducted surveillance in Seattle in September 2025 by sitting in a car outside of her target’s home and taking photos and video footage of him and his family.
起訴書指出，張女士是一名歸化美國公民，亦名海瑟(Heather)。2025年9月，她在西雅圖進行監視活動，坐在目標住所外的車內拍攝了目標及其家人的照片和影片。
“She coordinated the trip with an associate and used tradecraft to avoid detection,” Bill Essayli, the top federal prosecutor in Los Angeles, said in a post on social media. “She never notified the Attorney General she was acting for a foreign government, a federal offense.”
洛杉磯最高聯邦檢察官比爾·埃薩利在社群媒體的一篇帖子中表示：「她與一名同夥協調了這次行程，並使用了特工手段以避免被發現。她從未向司法部長通報自己正為外國政府行事，這是一項聯邦罪。」
廣告
The criminal complaint said that she had attended the University of International Relations in Beijing and was licensed to work as a real estate agent in Southern California.
刑事起訴書顯示，她曾就讀於北京的國際關係學院，並持有南加州房地產經紀人執照。
Ms. Zhang had been communicating with at least two Chinese government officials on WeChat, a Chinese messaging app, the complaint said.
起訴書稱，張女士一直通過中國通訊軟體微信與至少兩名中國政府官員保持聯繫。
The WeChat messages suggest that Ms. Zhang met with government officials in China and then conducted surveillance after she returned to the United States, according to the complaint. She got a flight from Los Angeles to Seattle along with another person who has not been named publicly. That person paid for a rental car to take them to the target’s home, the complaint said.
根據起訴書，微信記錄表明，在中國會見政府官員後，張女士返回美國開始實施監視。她與另一名未公開姓名的人士一同乘機從洛杉磯前往西雅圖，後者支付了租車費用並驅車帶他們前往目標住所。
It added that Ms. Zhang sent video footage of the target’s home, as well as license plate numbers, to one of the Chinese government officials she had been corresponding with.
起訴書還稱，張女士將目標住所的影片片段以及車牌號碼發送給了與她聯繫的一名中國政府官員。
Mr. Essayli was named acting U.S. attorney by President Trump and has been critical of Democratic officials in California, including Gov. Gavin Newsom and Mayor Karen Bass of Los Angeles. Mr. Essayli’s critics have argued that he lacks the legal standing to prosecute crimes. A spokesman for his office pointed to a December federal court ruling in his favor.
由川普總統任命為代理聯邦檢察官的埃薩利此前一直對加州民主黨官員持批評態度，包括加州州長加文·紐森和洛杉磯市長凱倫·巴斯。埃薩利的批評者認為，他缺乏發起刑事指控的法律資格。其辦公室發言人則引用了去年12月的一項對他有利的聯邦法院裁決。
China has long claimed the self-governed island of Taiwan as its territory, and its officials are staunchly opposed to Mr. Lai and his Democratic Progressive Party, which rejects Beijing’s claims to sovereignty.
中國長期宣稱實行自治的台灣島是其領土。由於賴清德及其所屬的民進黨拒絕接受北京的主權主張，中國官員對他們抱著強烈反對的態度。
廣告
Although the United States does not recognize Taiwanese sovereignty, Taipei has relied on American military power to counter threats from Beijing. Chinese officials regularly press the American government to limit its support for the island, including last month, when China’s leader, Xi Jinping, met with President Trump during a White House summit.
美國不承認台灣的主權，但台北一直依賴美國的軍事力量來對抗來自北京的威脅。中國官員經常向美國政府施壓，要求其收斂對台灣的支持；上個月中國領導人習近平在白宮峰會期間會見川普總統時，也提出了這一要求。
In statements on Monday, Taiwanese officials thanked the U.S. authorities for prosecuting Ms. Zhang.
在週一發表的聲明中，台灣當局官員感謝美國當局起訴張女士。
“This is a typical case of transnational repression,” said Karen Kuo, a spokeswoman for the presidential office in Taipei. “Our country expresses its strongest condemnation of this barbaric act that disregards human rights and national sovereignty, aiming to suppress, threaten or even harm those who advocate freedom and democracy or their families.”
「這是典型的跨國鎮壓，」台灣領導人辦公室發言人郭雅慧表示。「台灣對於這類無視人權與各國主權，甚至是傷害主張自由民主人們或其家人的野蠻行徑，表達最嚴厲譴責。」
The Chinese embassy in Washington did not immediately respond to a request for comment. Beijing’s foreign ministry said that it was “not familiar” with the situation and that it was opposed to sensationalized reports of “so-called ‘Chinese ⁠spies’ to slander and smear China.”
中國駐華盛頓大使館未立即回應置評請求。中國外交部表示對相關情況「不了解」，並反對炒作「所謂『中國間諜』」對中國進行污衊抹黑。
Earlier this year, in a separate case brought by Mr. Essayli, Eileen Wang, the mayor of Arcadia, Calif., pleaded guilty to acting as an illegal foreign agent for promoting pro-Beijing views on a news website at the direction of Chinese government officials.
今年早些時候，在埃薩利發起的另一起檢控中，加州阿卡迪亞市市長王愛琳(Eileen Wang)承認充當非法外國代理人，受中國政府官員指示在新聞網站上宣揚親北京的觀點。
Similar cases have also been brought in San Francisco and New York.
舊金山和紐約也曾提起過類似案件。
廣告
Audrye Wong, a senior fellow at the American Enterprise Institute who studies Chinese government influence in the United States, said that the allegations against Ms. Zhang, if true, appeared to be part of Beijing’s playbook of incentivizing marginal, sometimes amateur, members of the Chinese diaspora to undertake minor tasks in pursuit of its broader foreign policy goals.
美國企業研究所研究中國政府在美影響力的高級研究員黃韻琪(Audrye Wong)表示，針對張女士的指控如果屬實，這似乎將屬於北京策略的一部分：即以激勵手段推動海外華人邊緣群體——有時為業餘人士——執行次要任務，從而推進其更廣泛的外交政策目標。
Such actions, Ms. Wong said, can have a chilling effect across the diaspora.
她表示，此類行動可能會在整個海外華人社群中產生寒蟬效應。
“It demonstrates that the Chinese government’s reach is quite powerful,” Ms. Wong said. “And it creates a sense among overseas Chinese that the Chinese government is always there, and we don’t know who is working on behalf of them.”
「這表明中國政府的觸及面之廣，」黃韻琪說。「也讓海外華人產生了一種感覺：中國政府無處不在，而我們不知道誰在為他們效力。」
