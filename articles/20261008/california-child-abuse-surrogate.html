中國移民夫婦被控虐待十餘名代孕子女

Couple Charged With Conspiring to Abuse Numerous Surrogate Children

AMY QIN, SOUMYA KARLAMANGLA, LAUREN HERSTIK • 2026-10-08
A Southern California couple were arrested and charged on Tuesday with crimes related to the abuse of 15 of their children, nearly all of whom were born to surrogate mothers, prosecutors said.
檢察官表示，南加州一對夫婦週二被捕並遭到起訴，罪名涉及虐待他們的15名子女。這對夫婦的孩子幾乎全部來自代孕。
The couple, Guojun Xuan, 66, and Silvia Shasha Zhang, 39, of Arcadia, Calif., were living in a 10,287-square-foot mansion in the Los Angeles suburb where they employed multiple nannies to supervise and physically discipline their many children, some of whom were as young as 2 months old at the time, according to the felony complaint.
根據重罪起訴書，這對夫婦是66歲的宣國軍和39歲的張莎莎（音），住在洛杉磯郊區亞凱迪亞市一棟面積為10287平方英尺（約956平方米）的豪宅中。他們雇了多名保姆，負責照看他們眾多的孩子，並對孩子實施體罰。案發時，有些孩子只有兩個月大。
The Los Angeles County District Attorney’s Office accused Mr. Xuan and Ms. Zhang of directing the physical punishment and laughing at times while watching it happen.
洛杉磯縣地方檢察官辦公室指控，宣國軍和張莎莎指使保姆體罰孩子，有時還在一旁觀看時發笑。
Nannies used shoes and wooden sticks to strike toddlers, according to prosecutors. In at least one instance, prosecutors said, Mr. Xuan participated in the physical abuse by lifting a child into the air with one arm and repeatedly delivering blows to the child’s head.
檢察官稱，保姆用鞋子和木棍毆打幼兒。檢方表示，至少有一次，宣國軍親自參與了虐待行為，他用一隻手將一名孩子提起來，並反覆擊打孩子的頭部。
廣告
“This house could only be described as a house of horrors,” Nathan Hochman, the Los Angeles County district attorney, said in a news conference on Tuesday. “The discipline was nothing short of physical abuse, beatings and punishment on a daily basis.”
「這棟房子只能用恐怖屋來形容，」洛杉磯縣地方檢察官內森·霍克曼週二在新聞發布會上說。「所謂的管教完全就是身體虐待，孩子們每天都遭到毆打和懲罰。」
On Tuesday, Mr. Xuan and Ms. Zhang appeared in a courthouse in Pasadena, Calif., and were arraigned on felony charges of conspiracy to commit child abuse. Mr. Xuan also faces one felony count of child abuse.
週二，宣國軍和張莎莎在加州帕薩迪納的一家法院就共謀實施兒童虐待的重罪指控出庭接受提訊。宣國軍還面臨一項虐待兒童的重罪指控。
If convicted on all charges, Mr. Xuan faces up to 17 years in prison, and Ms. Zhang faces up to 11 years and 8 months.
如果所有罪名成立，宣國軍最高可被判處17年監禁，張莎莎最高可被判處11年零8個月監禁。
The couple, who have 27 children in total, made their fortune in commercial real estate, The New Yorker has reported. Investigators last year found a spreadsheet maintained by Ms. Zhang with the names of 21 children, and since then, the couple has had another six children via surrogates, according to the magazine. Most of the children were taken into state custody last year and remain in foster care.
據《紐約客》報導，這對夫婦靠商業地產發家，共有27個孩子。調查人員去年發現了一份由張薇薇保存的電子表格，上面有21個孩子的名字。據該雜誌報導，此後這對夫婦又通過代孕生了六個孩子。大多數孩子去年已被州政府接管，目前仍由寄養家庭照顧。
Mitchell Krems, a lawyer for Mr. Xuan, told reporters on Tuesday that his client had never abused any children. He said that Mr. Xuan had moved to the United States for the freedom to have a large family and that he and Ms. Zhang were being profiled unjustly.
宣國軍的律師米切爾·克雷姆斯週二對記者表示，他的當事人從未虐待過任何子女。他說，宣國軍移居美國是為了享有組建大家庭的自由，而他和張莎莎正遭到不公正的針對。
If “they weren’t Chinese, they wouldn’t experience this level of scrutiny,” Mr. Krems said. “He is being unfairly scrutinized because he has so many children. There’s no law against having all these children.”
「如果他們不是中國人，就不會受到如此程度的審查，」克雷姆斯說。「他因為孩子多而受到不公平的審查。擁有這麼多孩子並不違法。」
Most of the actions alleged by prosecutors were captured by the couple’s extensive security system, which consisted of about 32 motion-activated cameras that recorded video and audio throughout the home, according to the complaint.
根據起訴書，檢察官指控的大多數行為都被這對夫婦家中遍布各處的監控系統拍了下來。該系統包括約32個動作感應攝像頭，記錄整棟房子的影片和音頻。
The children spent multiple hours a day in makeshift classrooms in the home, prosecutors said. Multiple nannies, who were not charged, were also recorded carrying children into a room that did not have a camera, but from which children could be heard screaming once inside, the complaint said.
檢察官稱，孩子們每天有好幾個小時待在家中臨時設置的教室裡。起訴書稱，監控錄像還拍到多名保姆將孩子帶進一個沒有安裝攝像頭的房間，但孩子們一進去，就能聽到裡面傳出尖叫聲。這些保姆並未受到起訴。
廣告
Criminal scrutiny of Mr. Xuan and Ms. Zhang began in May 2025, when staff members at a Los Angeles children’s hospital notified the police that a 2-month-old boy had been brought in with a serious head injury that appeared to be inconsistent with Ms. Zhang’s explanation that he had fallen off the bed.
針對宣國軍和張莎莎的刑事調查始於2025年5月。當時，洛杉磯一家兒童醫院的工作人員報警稱，一名兩個月大的男嬰因嚴重頭部受傷被送醫，而傷情似乎與張莎莎所稱孩子從床上摔下來的說法不符。
After a police investigation, one of the couple’s nannies, Chunmei Li, was charged with child abuse that was likely to cause death related to the baby’s injuries. She fled to China, prosecutors said on Tuesday, and there is an outstanding warrant for her arrest.
警方調查後，這對夫婦的一名保姆李春梅（音）因涉嫌虐待該男嬰、實施可能致死的行為而受到起訴。檢察官週二表示，李春梅已逃往中國，針對她的逮捕令仍然有效。
The couple were arrested on suspicion of child endangerment last year but were not charged by prosecutors and were released after a few days in jail.
這對夫婦去年曾因涉嫌危害兒童安全被捕，但檢察官當時沒有提出起訴，兩人在監獄被關押數日後獲釋。
On Tuesday, Mr. Xuan and Ms. Zhang were each charged with being an accessory after the fact in connection with what prosecutors say were their attempts to help Ms. Li evade arrest.
週二，宣國軍和張莎莎還分別受到一項事後從犯罪指控。檢察官稱，兩人曾試圖幫助李春梅逃避逮捕。
Mr. Xuan and Ms. Zhang were also charged on Tuesday with multiple counts of dissuading a witness. Prosecutors said the couple had instructed the children not to disclose to investigators that they had been beaten and to deny that they had been coached on how to answer questions. On Tuesday, Judge Kerry White of Los Angeles County Superior Court set bail for each of them at $20 million. Mr. Xuan’s lawyer argued that the couple were not a flight risk, given that he recently had a bone-marrow transplant. He has leukemia, he said, and appeared in court in a wheelchair.
宣國軍和張莎莎週二還受到多項妨礙證人作證的指控。檢察官稱，這對夫婦曾告訴孩子們不要向調查人員透露自己遭到毆打的事實，還要求他們否認曾有人教他們如何回答問題。週二，洛杉磯縣高等法院法官凱瑞·懷特將兩人的保釋金分別定為2000萬美元。宣國軍的律師辯稱，這對夫婦不存在潛逃風險，因為宣國軍最近剛接受了骨髓移植。他說，宣國軍患有白血病。宣國軍當天坐著輪椅出庭。
MacKenzie Teymouri, the deputy district attorney, said in court that detectives were actively searching the couple’s residence. They had so far found antique sculptures, millions of dollars worth of alcohol, cash, and bank statements for 200 banks accounts and 70 corporate accounts. They also found multiple real and fake passports.
副地方檢察官麥肯齊·泰穆裡在法庭上表示，警探正在全力搜查這對夫婦的住所。到目前為止，他們發現了古董雕塑、價值數百萬美元的酒類、現金，以及涉及200個銀行帳戶和70個公司帳戶的銀行對帳單。他們還發現了多本真實和偽造的護照。
廣告
Judge White also ordered that the couple surrender all of their passports, submit to home detention and electronic monitoring and have no contact with any surrogacy agencies. He also ordered no contact with most of the children.
懷特法官還下令，這對夫婦必須交出所有護照，接受居家監禁和電子監控，不得與任何代孕機構接觸。法官還禁止他們與大多數孩子接觸。
In 2021, shortly after Mr. Xuan and Ms. Zhang moved into the Arcadia house, they established a company called Mark Surrogacy Investment LLC, according to the complaint. Through the company, the couple reached out to prospective surrogates across the United States. They often provided false information to the women, claiming, for example, that Ms. Zhang was a single mother who was struggling to conceive.
根據起訴書，2021年，宣國軍和張莎莎搬進亞凱迪亞的豪宅後不久，便成立了一家名為馬克代孕投資有限責任公司的企業。通過這家公司，兩人聯繫了美國各地有意擔任代孕母親的女性。他們經常向這些女性提供虛假信息，例如聲稱張莎莎是一名難以受孕的單身母親。
At least 14 of the children who were living in the residence at the time the police came in May 2025 were born to surrogate mothers, prosecutors said. The children ranged in age from 2 months to around 5 years old, Mr. Hochman said.
檢察官稱，2025年5月警方進入這棟住宅時，住在裡面的孩子至少有14個是由代孕母親所生。霍克曼說，這些孩子的年齡從兩個月到五歲左右不等。
Rijon Charne, a lawyer who represents some of the surrogates recruited by Ms. Zhang and Mr. Xuan, said she was glad to see charges brought against the couple.
律師裡瓊·查恩代理部分張莎莎和宣國軍招募的代孕母親，她表示，很高興看到這對夫婦受到起訴。
“Anyone who abuses a child should be prosecuted and punished to the fullest extent of the law,” she said in an email.
「任何虐待兒童的人都應該受到起訴，並依法受到最嚴厲的懲罰，」她在一封電子郵件中說。

Amy Qin是《紐約時報》國內新聞記者，主要撰寫亞裔美國人社群新聞。

Soumya Harlamangla是《紐約時報》記者，報導加州新聞。她常駐灣區。
