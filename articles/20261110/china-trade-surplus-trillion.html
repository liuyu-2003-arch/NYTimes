中國前10個月貿易順差突破1萬億美元

China’s Trade Surplus Passes $1 Trillion at Record Pace

KEITH BRADSHER • 2026-11-10
China’s trade surplus surged past $1 trillion in the first 10 months of this year, eclipsing last year’s record-setting pace as the country’s exports continued to climb steeply, the Chinese government announced on Tuesday.
中國政府週二宣布，由於出口持續急劇攀升，今年前10個月，中國貿易順差激增突破1萬億美元，超越了去年創紀錄的速度。
From solar panels to electric cars, China has found strong global demand for green energy products, as Iranian attacks on ships in the Strait of Hormuz have driven up international oil prices. China has also profited from the boom in artificial intelligence data centers, for which it manufactures much of the equipment, except for the most powerful semiconductors.
隨著伊朗在霍爾木茲海峽襲擊船隻推高了國際油價，從太陽能電池板到電動汽車，中國發現全球對綠色能源產品存在強勁需求。中國還從人工智慧數據中心的繁榮中獲利，中國製造了除最強大的半導體外的大部分數據中心設備。
China’s trade surplus reached $1.04 trillion in the first 10 months of the year, the General Administration of Customs said, bolstered by a surplus of $105.3 billion in October. The monthly figure narrowed from September as oil prices remained high. In addition, China restricted exports of diesel, jet fuel and other refined oil products to nearby countries so as to conserve its own supplies.
海關總署表示，今年前10個月中國貿易順差達到1.04萬億美元，其中10月出口額超過進口額1053億美元，推動貿易順差大幅增長。由於油價居高不下，10月數據較9月有所收窄。此外，為保障自身供應，中國限制了對周邊國家的柴油、航空煤油和其他成品油出口。
Exports surged 35.2 percent last month from a year earlier, including strong shipments of cables, racks and other equipment for other countries’ data centers. Imports jumped 41.6  percent, including a lot of electronics for China’s own A.I. infrastructure buildout.
上個月出口額同比激增35.2%，其中電纜、機架和其他用於其他國家數據中心的設備出貨量強勁。進口額跳升41.6%，包括大量用於中國自身人工智慧基礎設施建設的電子產品。
廣告
The figures came a day after Washington and Beijing took steps to ease tariffs and restrict the use of export controls through Jan. 10, putting into effect an agreement reached by senior officials in September ahead of a Washington summit between President Trump and Xi Jinping, China’s top leader.
這些數據發布的前一天，華盛頓和北京採取了多項措施，在1月10日前放寬關稅並限制出口管制的使用，從而使兩國高級官員於9月川普總統與中國最高領導人習近平的華盛頓峰會前達成的一項協議生效。
China’s ever-rising trade surplus partly reflects the remarkable competitiveness of its exports. Low labor costs and heavy investment in the latest automation keep production costs down. World-class infrastructure makes it inexpensive to ship goods from towns and cities across China to international markets. China’s weak currency has also helped keep Chinese goods affordable in distant markets.
中國不斷擴大的貿易順差部分反映了其出口的顯著競爭力。低廉的勞動力成本和對最新自動化技術的巨額投資維持了較低的生產成本。世界一流的基礎設施使得將貨物從中國各地運往國際市場的成本低廉。中國疲軟的貨幣也有助於中國商品在遙遠的市場中保持價格優勢。
China also helps exporters through tax rebates, which now cost the government almost one-eighth of its tax revenues.
中國還通過退稅為出口商提供幫助，這目前消耗了政府近八分之一的稅收收入。
The trade surplus is also a sign, however, of the weakness of China’s domestic economy. A slow-motion collapse of the country’s housing market, with prices down roughly 40 percent nationwide over the past five years, has wrecked the life savings of much of the middle class.
然而，貿易順差也是中國國內經濟疲軟的一種表現。該國房地產市場正在緩慢崩潰，過去五年全國房價下跌了約40%，這摧毀了許多中產階級的畢生積蓄。
Distrusting stocks, bonds and mutual funds, Chinese households had invested roughly four-fifths of their savings into apartments, only to find that real estate was not as safe an investment as widely believed. This has prompted many Chinese families to economize by cutting back on purchases of everything from cars to household appliances.
由於不信任股票、債券和共同基金，中國家庭曾將大約五分之四的儲蓄投資於公寓，結果卻發現房地產並不像人們普遍認為的那樣是一項安全投資。這已促使許多中國家庭通過削減從汽車到家用電器等各種商品的購買來節約開支。
At the same time, China’s national campaign for self-reliance has further reduced its need for imports. “As supply chains continue to build out in China, there is less need for foreign components,” said Deborah Elms, the head of trade policy at the Hinrich Foundation, a Singapore-based group that favors free trade.
與此同時，中國的國家自力更生運動進一步減少了其對進口的需求。「隨著供應鏈繼續在中國建立，對外來零部件的需求正在減少，」總部位於新加坡、支持自由貿易的機構韓禮士基金會貿易政策負責人黛博拉·埃爾姆斯說。
廣告
China’s rising trade surpluses with Europe, in particular, have fueled rapidly growing concern there about job losses in industry. China reported on Tuesday that its surplus with Europe was $33.4 billion last month, and $320.8 billion for the first 10 months of this year.
中國對歐洲不斷上升的貿易順差尤其迅速加劇了後者對工業領域失業的擔憂。中國週二報告稱，上個月其對歐洲的順差為334億美元，今年前10個月為3208億美元。
China’s rapidly rising exports have allowed it to accomplish a remarkable feat: installing extensive automation in its factories to expand production and improve efficiency without suffering heavy factory job losses. Manufacturing employment has remained mostly steady at about 71 million jobs since 2020, even as the trade surplus has climbed steeply, according to official data. Factory employment had contracted somewhat before then.
中國迅速增長的出口使其得以實現一項了不起的壯舉：在工廠中大規模部署自動化技術以擴大生產並提高效率，同時又未遭遇嚴重的工廠裁員。官方數據顯示，儘管貿易順差急劇攀升，自2020年以來製造業就業人數仍基本穩定在約7100萬個崗位。在此之前，工廠就業曾出現過一定程度的收縮。
Manufacturing job losses have occurred instead in countries like France and particularly Germany, which is shedding 14,000 industrial jobs per month. These countries have become large importers of Chinese goods while also losing markets to Chinese competition in developing countries and elsewhere.
相比之下，製造業失業情況卻出現在了法國、德國等國，德國尤其嚴重，目前每月流失1.4萬個工業崗位。這些國家不僅成為了中國商品的進口大國，同時其在發展中國家及其他地區的市場份額又輸給了中國。

Keith Bradsher是《紐約時報》北京分社社長，此前曾任上海分社社長、香港分社社長、底特律分社社長，以及華盛頓記者。他在新冠疫情期間常駐中國進行報導。
