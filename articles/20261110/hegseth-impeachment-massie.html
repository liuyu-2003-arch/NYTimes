美國國防部長海格塞斯面臨彈劾動議

Hegseth Impeachment Charges Say He Violated the Constitution

ROBERT JIMISON • 2026-11-10
Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth could face an impeachment vote in the House as early as this week, as lawmakers return to Washington after an election in which voters delivered a sweeping rebuke of the Trump administration and the president’s handling of the war with Iran.
國防部長彼特·海格塞斯最早可能於本週面臨眾議院的彈劾投票。此前議員們在經歷了一場選舉後重返華盛頓，而選民在選舉中對川普政府以及總統處理與伊朗戰爭的方式給予了廣泛譴責。
The effort is being led by Representative Thomas Massie, Republican of Kentucky, who on Monday called up a resolution accusing Mr. Hegseth of violating federal law and the Constitution, and abusing his authority on a number of fronts.
這項行動由肯塔基州共和黨眾議員托馬斯·馬西牽頭。週一他提出一項決議，指控海格塞斯違反聯邦法律和憲法，並在多個方面濫用職權。
Democrats have signaled broad support for the effort, and several Republicans have expressed discontent with Mr. Hegseth, though the outcome remains uncertain in a narrowly divided House.
民主黨人已表示廣泛支持這一行動，幾名共和黨人也對海格塞斯表達了不滿，但在兩黨席位差距微弱的眾議院，結果仍不確定。
Here is what to know about the charges, the process and what could happen next.
以下是關於指控內容、程序以及後續可能發展的要點。
廣告
The measure charges Mr. Hegseth with violating the Constitution.
該決議指控海格塞斯違反憲法。
Mr. Massie, who lost his primary in May to a challenger backed by President Trump, has introduced eight articles of impeachment against Mr. Hegseth, three of them focused on his conduct of the war with Iran.
馬西在5月的初選中輸給了川普總統支持的挑戰者。他對海格塞斯提出八項彈劾條款，其中三項聚焦於他處理對伊朗戰爭的方式。
The articles accuse the defense secretary of waging war without congressional authorization and continuing the military campaign after Congress voted to direct the president to terminate the conflict or seek authorization to continue it. The resolution also accuses Mr. Hegseth of violating deadlines under the War Powers Resolution, which requires the withdrawal of American forces from unauthorized hostilities after 60 days, with a possible 30-day extension.
這些條款指控國防部長未經國會授權發動戰爭，並在國會投票要求總統終止衝突或尋求得到繼續衝突的授權之後，仍繼續進行軍事行動。該決議還指控海格塞斯違反了《戰爭權力法》規定的期限。該法要求美國軍隊在未經授權的敵對行動60天後撤出（可以延長30天）。
The remaining charges accuse Mr. Hegseth of carrying out unlawful military strikes against suspected drug traffickers; unlawfully capturing Venezuela’s president, Nicolás Maduro; conducting unauthorized military operations in Yemen; disregarding laws intended to minimize civilian casualties; and abusing his power by retaliating against Senator Mark Kelly for protected speech.
其餘指控稱，海格塞斯對涉嫌毒品販運者實施非法軍事打擊；非法抓捕委內瑞拉總統尼古拉斯·馬杜羅；在葉門開展未經授權的軍事行動；無視旨在盡量減少平民傷亡的法律；並濫用職權，對參議員馬克·凱利因受法律保護的言論進行報復。
It is up to Speaker Mike Johnson to decide whether the chamber will vote on each of the eight articles or if they will be considered as a package.
議長邁克·強生將決定是否讓眾議院對全部八項條款逐一投票，或是將它們作為一攬子方案審議。
Mr. Hegseth has rejected the allegations, calling the impeachment effort a political sham. He is scheduled to hold a news conference on Tuesday morning to respond to the charges.
海格塞斯駁斥這些指控，稱此次彈劾行動是政治鬧劇。他定於週二上午舉行新聞發布會回應這些指控。
“Secretary Hegseth will not back down or be deterred by politicians seeking to score partisan points or launch phony investigations where no wrongdoing exists,” Sean Parnell, the Pentagon spokesman, said in a statement on Monday in which he said Mr. Hegseth intended to serve until the end of President Trump’s term.
「海格塞斯部長不會退縮，也不會被那些試圖撈取黨派政治資本，或在毫無不當行為的情況下發起虛假調查的政治人物所嚇倒，」五角大廈發言人肖恩·帕內爾週一在聲明中表示，並稱海格塞斯打算任職到川普總統任期結束。
Mr. Trump has backed his defense secretary and said he intends to keep Mr. Hegseth in his job.
川普已表態支持他的國防部長，並表示打算讓海格塞斯繼續擔任該職。
A majority is required to impeach Mr. Hegseth.
彈劾海格塞斯需要多數票。
The Constitution lays out the process for Congress to impeach and remove presidents, judges and other federal civil officers, including cabinet secretaries.
憲法規定了國會彈劾並罷免總統、法官和其他聯邦文職官員（包括內閣部長）的程序。
廣告
The first step is in the House, which considers articles of impeachment alleging treason, bribery or other high crimes and misdemeanors. A simple majority is required to approve any article. Impeachment amounts to a formal accusation by Congress, which is then sent to the Senate for a trial to determine whether the official should be convicted and removed from office.
第一步在眾議院進行，眾議院審議指控叛國、賄賂或其他重罪和輕罪的彈劾條款。批准任何一項彈劾條款都需要簡單多數。彈劾相當於國會提出的正式指控，隨後送交參議院進行審判，以決定該官員是否應被定罪並免職。
House rules allow any member to propose impeachment articles and require that the body consider them within two legislative days. Congressional leaders — in this case Republicans — have no power to block a vote, but they can try to head off the impeachment by either moving to send the charges to a committee, delaying final action, or to table them, which would effectively kill the effort.
眾議院規則允許任何議員提出彈劾條款，並規定眾議院必須在兩個立法日內審議這些條款。國會領導人——在本案中是共和黨人——無權阻止投票，但他們可以通過將指控送交委員會、推遲最終表決或將議案擱置來阻止彈劾，擱置實際上將使彈劾努力流產。
If the House were to impeach Mr. Hegseth, it would take a two-thirds vote of senators present to convict and remove him.
如果眾議院彈劾海格塞斯，則需要出席參議員的三分之二多數票才能定罪並罷免他。
The outcome of the impeachment vote is uncertain.
彈劾投票的結果尚不確定。
Democrats are widely expected to support impeaching Mr. Hegseth, and Representative Hakeem Jeffries, the minority leader, declared on Sunday that he was a “hard yes.” With Republicans holding a razor-thin majority, as few as three Republican defections or a few absences could be enough to put the resolution over the top.
普遍預計民主黨人會支持彈劾海格塞斯，少數黨領袖哈基姆·傑弗里斯週日宣布他對此「堅決支持」。鑒於共和黨僅以微弱優勢佔據多數席位，只需三名共和黨人倒戈或幾人缺席，就足以讓決議獲得通過。
Apart from Mr. Massie, several Republicans have expressed frustration with the secretary without committing to impeachment. Representative French Hill, Republican of Arkansas, said Sunday that he wanted to hear the arguments but cautioned that poor performance alone did not necessarily constitute an impeachable offense.
除馬西外，另幾名共和黨人也對這位部長表達了不滿，但未承諾支持彈劾。阿肯色州共和黨眾議員弗倫奇·希爾週日表示，他想聽取相關論點，但警告說，僅憑工作表現不佳並不一定構成可彈劾的罪行。
廣告
“I don’t know if we have enough votes,” Mr. Massie told reporters on Monday. “I’m doing this because it’s the right thing to do.”
「我不知道我們是否有足夠的票數，」馬西週一對記者說。「我這樣做是因為，這是正確的事。」
Beyond the political calculations, many lawmakers are considering whether policy decisions ordered by the president warrant impeachment. When House Republicans impeached Alejandro Mayorkas, who served as the homeland security secretary under President Joseph R. Biden Jr., over his handling of immigration enforcement, Democrats opposed the charges, arguing that policy choices did not constitute impeachable offenses. The Republican-led House impeached Mr. Mayorkas, but the Senate dismissed the charges.
拋開政治考量，許多議員還在考慮，由總統下令的政策決定是否值得彈劾。當眾議院共和黨人因國土安全部長亞歷杭德羅·馬約卡斯在拜登總統任內處理移民執法問題而對他發起彈劾時，民主黨人曾反對這些指控，認為政策選擇不構成可彈劾的罪行。當時由共和黨主導的眾議院彈劾了馬約卡斯，但參議院駁回了指控。
There is no fixed timeline for impeachment.
彈劾沒有固定時間表。
If the House were to impeach Mr. Hegseth, he would remain defense secretary while the articles were pending, and it would be up to House leaders to decide when to send the matter to the Senate for a trial.
如果眾議院彈劾海格塞斯，在彈劾條款待決期間，他將繼續擔任國防部長，而何時將此事提交參議院進行審判，將由眾議院領導層決定。
Unlike legislation, however, impeachment resolutions do not die at the end of a Congress, so Democrats, who will control the House and Senate starting Jan. 3, would be able to take up the matter when they assume power.
然而，與立法不同，彈劾決議不會在國會任期結束時失效，因此，從明年1月3日開始控制眾議院和參議院的民主黨人可在掌權後處理此事。
