黃仁勛對川普稱自己不知婚外情對象疑似中國間諜

Nvidia Chief Told Trump He Did Not Know Chinese Woman Was Suspected Spy

LUKE BROADWATER, MAGGIE HABERMAN • 2026-11-10
President Trump said Monday that Jensen Huang, the chief executive of Nvidia, told him that he was not aware that a Chinese businesswoman with whom he was having a romantic relationship was an intelligence operative for Beijing.
川普總統週一表示，英偉達首席執行官黃仁勛告訴他，自己並不知道與其有親密關係的一名中國女商人是北京的情報人員。
“I asked him that question: ‘Did you have any idea that she was a spy?’” Mr. Trump told reporters at the White House. “He said, ‘I had no idea that she was a spy,’ and I really believe him.’”
「我問了他那個問題：『你對她是個間諜一無所知嗎？』」川普在白宮告訴記者。「他說，『我完全不知道她是間諜，』而我真的相信他。」
Mr. Trump’s comments followed a series of reports by The New York Times revealing that U.S. officials had concluded that the woman, who goes by Alice Huang, had been recruited by China’s main spy agency, and that Mr. Trump and other top officials had been warned about the relationship.
在川普發表此番言論前，《紐約時報》的一系列報導披露，美國官員已得出結論，這位名為黃樂（音，其英文名為Alice Huang）的女子已被中國主要間諜機構招募，並且川普及其他高級官員已收到過關於這段關係的警告。
The Times reported last month that Mr. Trump had been briefed roughly a year ago about electronic surveillance showing that Ms. Huang was believed by the U.S. intelligence community to be an operative for China’s government. The president chose not to do anything with the information at the time.
《紐約時報》上月報導稱，大約一年前，川普曾聽取過關於電子監控的簡報，監控顯示美國情報界認為黃女士是中國政府的情報人員。總統當時選擇不對該信息採取任何行動。
廣告
Marco Rubio, the national security adviser and secretary of state, oversaw the drafting of a highly classified memo in the spring, warning top Trump administration officials about Ms. Huang and intelligence assessments that she was a spy for China’s Ministry of State Security, The Times reported Friday.
據《紐約時報》週五報導，國家安全顧問兼國務卿馬爾科·魯比奧在今年春天監督起草了一份高度機密的備忘錄，向川普政府高級官員警告了黃樂的情況，以及關於她是中國國家安全部間諜的情報評估。
On Monday, Mr. Trump said that he did not recall receiving the information.
週一，川普表示他不記得收到過該信息。
Asked whether he knew about the “alleged ties with the Chinese spy” in May when the president invited Mr. Huang aboard Air Force One on a trip to China, the president said, “No, I didn’t hear about it then.”
當被問及5月份邀請黃仁勛登上「空軍一號」前往中國時，他是否知道「與中國間諜的所謂聯繫」，總統表示：「不，我當時沒聽說過此事。」
When pressed about whether he had received a briefing on the issue about a year ago, Mr. Trump demurred.
當被追問大約一年前他是否收到過關於該問題的簡報時，川普未作正面回答。
“I’d have to check,” he said in response to a question about when he first learned of the matter. “I’d certainly check with the intelligence people. They could have been mentioned. Certainly, it didn’t make much of an impact on me.”
「我得查一下，」他在回答關於首次得知此事的時間時說。「我肯定會向情報人員核實。這些事可能被提及過。當然，它並沒有對我產生太大影響。」
Mr. Huang’s relationship with Ms. Huang, which he publicly acknowledged Sunday, has rattled Nvidia, the powerhouse chipmaker that is driving the data center boom powering the U.S. economy
黃仁勛在週日公開承認了他與黃女士的關係，此事震動了英偉達，這家強大的晶片製造商驅動著支撐美國經濟的數據中心繁榮。
The company said on Sunday that it was opening an investigation into Mr. Huang, after he told the board about his relationship with Ms. Huang. (Mr. Huang and Ms. Huang are not related.)
英偉達週日表示，在黃仁勛將他與黃樂的關係告知董事會後，公司正在對他展開調查。（黃仁勛與黃樂沒有親屬關係。）
廣告
“I was unfaithful to my wife,” Mr. Huang said in a message to the company. “My actions hurt her and our children. There is no excuse for what I did. I am deeply sorry.”
「我對妻子不忠，」黃仁勛在給公司的一份消息中說。「我的行為傷害了她和我們的孩子。我的所作所為沒有任何藉口。我深表歉意。」
The president and Mr. Huang have become close allies in Mr. Trump’s second term. Nvidia has donated at least $20 million to a fund for Mr. Trump’s prized White House ballroom project. And Mr. Huang has persuaded the president to roll back a ban on chip sales to China.
在川普的第二任期內，總統與黃仁勛已成為親密盟友。英偉達已為川普珍視的白宮宴會廳項目基金捐贈了至少2000萬美元。此外，黃仁勛已說服總統撤銷了對華晶片銷售禁令。
Speaking to reporters Monday, the president said he was mostly concerned with how Mr. Huang was holding up amid the scandal.
週一向記者發表講話時，總統表示他主要關心的是黃仁勛在這場醜聞中狀態如何。
“I did speak to him today just to see if he’s feeling all right,” Mr. Trump said, adding: “I believe he’s suffering greatly. I read his statement, and I believe he’s suffering greatly. More so because of his family, his wife who he loves and his family, but he is definitely suffering.”
「我今天確實和他通了話，只是想看看他感覺怎麼樣，」川普說，並且「我相信他非常痛苦。我讀了他的聲明，我相信他非常痛苦。更多是因為他的家人，他深愛的妻子和他的家人，但他絕對很痛苦。」
The president said the F.B.I. had been investigating a matter related to Mr. Huang, but he added that he believed the businessman did nothing wrong.
總統表示，聯邦調查局一直在調查一件與黃仁勛有關的事情，但他說，他相信這位商人沒有做錯任何事。
Mr. Trump appeared to be referring to the Justice Department taking over an investigation by the Commerce Department into whether Megaspeed, the company where Ms. Huang was a top executive and which bought $2 billion of Nvidia’s products, was illegally funneling advanced chips into China.
川普似乎指的是司法部接手了商務部的一項調查，該調查旨在查明Megaspeed公司是否在非法向中國輸送先進晶片。黃樂曾擔任該公司的高管，且該公司購買了20億美元的英偉達產品。
廣告
“I know the F.B.I. is looking into it in terms of something else, and I think that he’ll be in good shape,” Mr. Trump said.
「我知道聯邦調查局正在調查其他方面的事情，我認為他會沒事的，」川普說。
Next week, Mr. Trump plans to travel to Shenzhen, China, for an Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit, where he expects to meet with President Xi Jinping of China.
下週，川普計劃前往中國深圳參加亞太經合組織峰會，他預計將在那裡會見中國國家主席習近平。
But Mr. Trump said he didn’t plan to bring up the matter of the suspected Chinese spy.
但川普表示，他不打算提出中國間諜嫌疑的問題。
“I’m sure President Xi will ask me about it,” Mr. Trump said. “I just know Jensen very well. I just think he’s a very honorable man. I mean, he’s a great. He built a great company, American company, and I think he’s a very honorable guy. And I feel very confident that he’ll be in good shape.”
「我確信習主席會問我這件事，」川普說。「我只是非常了解黃仁勛。我只是覺得他是一個非常正直的人。我的意思是，他很了不起。他建立了一家偉大的公司，一家美國公司，我認為他是一個非常正直的人。而且我非常有信心他會沒事的。」

Luke Broadwater為《紐約時報》報導白宮新聞。

Maggie Haberman是《紐約時報》白宮記者，報導川普總統。
