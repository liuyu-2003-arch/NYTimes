黎智英獲刑20年

Here’s the latest.

DAVID PIERSON, FRANCESCA REGALADO • 2026-02-09
Jimmy Lai, the pro-democracy media mogul who spent decades as a defiant thorn in Beijing’s side, was sentenced to 20 years in prison.
數十年來一直是北京眼中釘的親民主媒體大亨黎智英被判20年監禁。
The landmark ruling completes a yearslong effort by Beijing to dismantle the influence of a man it blamed for masterminding Hong Kong’s pro-democracy movement.
這項具有里程碑意義的判決標誌著北京完成了歷時數年的行動——他們指控黎智英是香港民主運動的幕後策劃者，致力於瓦解其影響力。
In December, Mr. Lai, 78, was found guilty of “conspiracy to collude with foreign forces” that stemmed from meetings he had held with politicians in the United States. He was also convicted of conspiracy to publish seditious material in Apple Daily, the now-shuttered Chinese-language pro-democracy newspaper he founded in 1995.
去年12月，78歲的黎智英因與美國政界人士會面被判犯有「串謀勾結外國勢力罪」。他還被判定在《蘋果日報》上串謀發表煽動性材料，該報是他於1995年創辦的親民主中文報紙，現已停刊。
For decades, China has branded Mr. Lai a traitor seeking to undermine the Communist Party rule over Hong Kong and China. They have accused him of being the “black hand” behind the antigovernment protests that engulfed Hong Kong in 2019. Mr. Lai had taken part in some of the demonstrations and supported them through Apple Daily.
數十年來，中國始終將黎智英視為企圖顛覆中共對香港及中國統治的叛徒，並指控他是2019年席捲香港的反政府抗議活動背後的「黑手」。 黎智英曾參與部分示威活動，並通過《蘋果日報》對抗議活動予以支持。
Even in a hyper-capitalistic city filled with self-made millionaires, Mr. Lai’s rags to riches story stood out. He fled a poverty stricken China as a stowaway when he was a boy and worked his way up the city’s garment factories. That led to the launching of his own brand of casual wear in 1981, which earned him his first fortune.
即使在香港這個充斥著草根出身百萬富翁的超級資本主義城市，黎智英白手起家的故事依然格外引人矚目。少年時他偷渡逃離貧困的中國，在香港的制衣廠從底層做起，最終於1981年創立自有休閑服飾品牌，由此積累了第一桶金。
Western governments have joined Mr. Lai’s family in calling for the release of Mr. Lai, a British citizen, describing his trial as politically motivated.
西方各國政府與黎智英的家人共同呼籲釋放這位英國公民，稱其審判出於政治動機。
“Time is running out for my father, so hope and persistence is all we really have,” Sebastien Lai, Mr. Lai’s son, sat at a British parliamentary hearing last week at which he urged the government to do more to seek his father’s release.
「父親的時間所剩無幾，希望與堅持是我們僅有的依靠，」黎智英之子黎崇恩上週出席英國議會聽證會時如是說，他敦促政府加大努力爭取父親獲釋。
President Trump has previously said that he asked China’s top leader, Xi Jinping, to consider releasing Mr. Lai. Before his election last year, Mr. Trump promised “100 percent I’ll get him out. He’ll be easy to get out.” David Perdue, the U.S. ambassador to China, described Mr. Lai’s case as an “ongoing conversation” between Mr. Trump and Mr. Xi in an interview with Bloomberg TV last month.
川普總統此前曾表示，他已要求中國最高領導人習近平考慮釋放黎智英。去年當選前，川普曾承諾「我百分之百會把他弄出來。讓他獲釋很容易」。美國駐華大使龐德偉(David Perdue)上月在接受彭博電視採訪時稱，黎智英的案件是川普與習近平之間「持續對話」的內容。
