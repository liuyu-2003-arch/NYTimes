長鑫科技上市首日股價飆升470%

Chinese Chip Giant’s Stock Soars 470% in Start of Trading, Amid A.I. Race

STEVE LOHR • 2026-07-28
Shares in China’s leading memory chipmaker soared about 470 percent on its first day of trading on Monday, quickly making it the most valuable company on the Shanghai stock exchange.
週一，中國存儲晶片製造龍頭企業的股價在上市首日交易中飆升約470%，迅速成為上海證券交易所市值最高的公司。
ChangXin Memory Technologies, known as CXMT, had already completed Asia’s largest initial public offering this year, which two weeks ago valued the company at more than $85 billion.
長鑫存儲技術有限公司（簡稱CXMT）此前已完成今年亞洲最大規模的首次公開募股(IPO)，兩週前該公司的估值超過850億美元。
Investors catapulted the chipmaker’s valuation on the Shanghai stock exchange to more than $480 billion at closing. That is well ahead of the Industrial and Commercial Bank of China, the previous biggest company, which is valued at about $385 billion.
投資者將這家晶片製造商在上海證券交易所的市值推高至收盤時的4800多億美元。這遠遠超過了此前市值最大的公司——估值約為3850億美元的中國工商銀行。
Trading in CXMT shares opened at 49.50 renminbi, up from its I.P.O. price of 8.66 renminbi. The closing price was 49.00 renminbi.
長鑫科技股票開盤價為49.50元人民幣，高於其8.66元人民幣的IPO發行價。收盤價為49.00元人民幣。
廣告
The stellar trading start followed global volatility and a recent sell-off in tech stocks. Investors have questioned the stratospheric valuations of Silicon Valley companies that are making enormous investments to pursue the development of artificial intelligence.
這一亮眼的交易開局是在全球市場波動和近期科技股拋售之後出現的。為了追求人工智慧的發展，一些矽谷公司進行了巨額投資，而投資者對這些公司極高的估值提出了質疑。
CXMT, a decade-old company little known outside its homeland, is a linchpin in China’s drive to nurture homegrown suppliers to reduce its dependence on foreign companies in the race for A.I. leadership. And it is an ascendant Chinese chipmaker at a time of increasing tension between the United States and China, and anxiety in America that China is catching up fast.
成立十年的長鑫科技在海外鮮為人知，但它是中國致力於培育本土供應商、在爭奪人工智慧領導地位的競賽中減少對外國公司依賴的關鍵力量。在美中緊張局勢不斷加劇，美國擔憂中國正在迅速趕上的時期，它正成為一家正在崛起的中國晶片製造商。
The global race to dominate A.I. has generated a boom in demand for memory chips, which instantaneously shuttle data to and from the processing engines of computers. All computers rely on memory chips, but the demands of massive A.I. data centers being built in the United States and China are all but limitless. As demand has soared, so have prices.
人工智慧領域的全球霸主之爭催生了對存儲晶片的旺盛需求，這些晶片能瞬間將數據在計算機的處理引擎之間進行傳輸。所有計算機都依賴存儲晶片，但在美國和中國正在建設的大型人工智慧數據中心對存儲晶片的需求幾乎是無限的。隨著需求激增，價格也水漲船高。
CXMT’s revenue jumped to nearly $7.5 billion in the first quarter of 2026, up more than 700 percent from a year earlier, according to its I.P.O. prospectus. And the company posted a profit of more than $1 billion last year, up from a loss of $2.8 billion in 2023.
根據其IPO招股說明書，長鑫科技在2026年第一季度的營收躍升至近75億美元，同比增長超過700%。此外，該公司去年實現了超過10億美元的利潤，而2023年則虧損了28億美元。
CXMT is beginning to make inroads in the global memory market, which is dominated by two South Korean companies, Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix, and an American company, Micron Technology. CXMT held 8 percent of the global market in the first quarter of 2026, compared with 3 percent a year earlier, according to Counterpoint Research, a tech market research firm.
長鑫科技正開始進軍全球存儲晶片市場，該市場目前由韓國公司三星電子和SK海力士以及美國公司美光科技主導。據科技市場研究公司Counterpoint Research的數據，長鑫科技在2026年第一季度佔據了全球市場8%的份額，去年同期這一比例為3%。
Two weeks ago, SK Hynix raised $26.5 billion by selling American depositary receipts, in the largest share sale on Wall Street by a non-U.S. company.
兩週前，SK海力士通過出售美國存托憑證籌集了265億美元，這是非美國公司在華爾街進行的規模最大的一次股票發售。
