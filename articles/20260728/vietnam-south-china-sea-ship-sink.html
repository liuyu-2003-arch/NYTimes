越南貨船在南海爭議海域沉沒，中國參與救援

Vietnam Used Ship That Sank in South China Sea in Island-Building Effort

TUNG NGO, MAX KIM • 2026-07-28
A Vietnamese cargo ship that sank in a heavily disputed section of the South China Sea on Saturday was part of island-building projects to assert Vietnamese sovereignty against Chinese claims in the area, according to a Vietnamese official with knowledge of the effort, as rescuers searched for 15 sailors who were still missing.
上週六，一艘越南貨船在南中國海存在激烈主權爭議的海域沉沒。據一名知情的越南官員透露，該貨船當時正參與越南為對抗中國在該地區主權主張而進行的島礁建設項目。目前，救援人員仍在搜尋15名失蹤船員。
The vessel, the Khoi Nguyen 18, was carrying 62 Vietnamese sailors when it began taking on water before sinking near the Spratly Islands, a cluster of over 100 small reefs and other land formations in the South China Sea where numerous countries have competing claims of sovereignty.
這艘名為「魁元18號」的船隻共載有62名越南船員，在南沙海域船體進水並最終沉沒。南沙群島由100多個小島礁和其他地貌組成，多國都宣稱對這裡擁有主權。
A distress signal from survivors in a raft was first spotted by a Chinese rescue vessel in the area on Saturday evening, prompting a search and rescue operation that has so far taken in 47 survivors, Lin Jian, a spokesman for the Chinese Foreign Ministry, said at a news conference in Beijing on Monday. The Vietnamese authorities have thanked their Chinese counterparts for acting quickly. Rescuers from the two countries and the Philippines are still searching for the others.
中國外交部發言人林劍週一在北京舉行的記者會上表示，週六傍晚，一艘在該海域執行任務的中國救援船首先發現了一個救生筏上倖存者發出的求救信號，並隨即展開搜救行動，目前已救起47名倖存者。越南方面已對中方迅速施救表示感謝。來自中越兩國以及菲律賓的救援人員仍在繼續搜尋其餘失蹤人員。
Accounts from China and Vietnam both place the location of the ship’s sinking between Fiery Cross Reef, which is controlled by China, and Discovery Great Reef, which is controlled by Vietnam.
據中越雙方的說法，沉船地點位於中國控制的永暑礁與越南控制的大現礁之間。
廣告
The reefs are part of the Spratly Islands, which, along with the Paracel Islands, have for years been the site of intense competition between China and Vietnam. Both countries seek to assert their ownership by reclaiming vast tracts of land around uninhabited islets and reefs, and building military facilities on top of them.
這兩個島礁均屬於南沙群島，南沙群島與西沙群島多年來一直是中越激烈競爭的焦點。兩國均通過在無人島礁周圍填海造地並在上面修建軍事設施來宣示主權。
The Khoi Nguyen 18 had been working on one such project when it sank, a Vietnamese official with direct knowledge of the matter told The New York Times. The official requested anonymity because he was not authorized to speak to the media.
一名了解一手情況的越南官員向《紐約時報》透露，「魁元18號」沉沒時，正在執行其中一個項目的建設工程。該官員以無權對外發言為由要求匿名。
The Vietnamese authorities have publicly attributed the sinking to bad weather, but have not said where the ship was headed or what kind of cargo it was transporting.
越方表示，貨船沉沒是由於惡劣天氣造成的，但並未說明船隻當時駛往何處，也未透露運載的是什麼貨物。
A U.N. resolution governing maritime safety — of which Vietnam is a signatory — states that all large cargo ships must have their automatic identification system, or AIS, transponder on at all times. However, tracking data showed that the Vietnam-flagged ship has not transmitted location data for months. The ship was built in 2017, is about 230 feet long and has a total carrying capacity of about 1,900 ⁠metric tons.
越南作為簽署國的一項聯合國海事安全決議規定，所有大型貨船必須隨時開啟自動識別系統應答器。然而，追蹤數據顯示，這艘懸掛越南旗幟的船隻已有數月未傳輸位置數據。該船建於2017年，船身約70米長，總載重量約1900公噸。
The official said that the ship had been shuttling regularly between Cam Ranh, where the Vietnamese Navy has a major port, to the Spratly Islands.
這名官員表示，這艘船此前定期往返于越南海軍擁有大型港口的金蘭灣與南沙群島之間。
The registered owner of the ship is the Khoi Nguyen Trading, Import-Export and Transportation Company, according to Vietnamese government data. Reached on the phone, a representative for the company, who did not give his full name, said that the ship had been deployed to a high-priority project in the Spratlys being overseen by the Dacinco Construction Investment Company, which has been tied to island-building in the area.
越南政府資料顯示，這艘貨船的註冊所有者為魁元貿易進出口運輸公司。一名不願透露全名的公司代表在電話中表示，該船被調派參與南沙群島一項由Dacinco建設投資公司負責的重點工程，而這家公司一直在參與當地的島礁建設項目。
廣告
Dacinco, which is based in Da Nang, Vietnam, has been purchasing cutter section dredgers from Royal IHC, a Dutch shipbuilding company, since 2022. These are specialized vessels that can suction up sand from the seabed, making them effective for land reclamation projects.
總部位於越南峴港的Dacinco公司自2022年以來多次從荷蘭造船企業Royal IHC採購絞吸式挖泥船。這種專用船舶能夠從海床吸取泥沙，因此非常適合用於填海造地工程。
Calls to Dacinco went unanswered.
記者致電Dacinco公司，但無人接聽。
“These dredgers have been extensively used in the reclamation of Namyit Island, Pearson Reef and Barque Canada Reef, significantly enhancing Vietnam’s efficiency,” SCSPI, a Beijing-based maritime affairs think tank, said last year.
北京海事智庫「南海戰略態勢感知計劃」去年曾表示，「絞吸式挖泥船被廣泛使用在鴻庥島、畢生礁和柏礁等島礁的擴建中，讓越南的填海造陸效率大大提升。」
In June 2024, Vietnamese state media reported that a Dacinco worker was injured on a site on Barque Canada Reef, one of the formations comprising the Spratlys that Vietnam has been building out.
2024年6月，越南官方媒體報導稱，一名Dacinco員工在柏礁施工現場受傷。柏礁是南沙群島的一部分，也是越南近年來持續擴建的島礁之一。
The company has also been actively recruiting workers for these projects on social media, where its job listings call for crane operators and truck drivers for “offshore construction projects” in the Spratlys.
該公司還在社群媒體上積極招募項目工人，招聘信息顯示，南沙群島「近海建設項目」招募吊車操作員和卡車司機。
Vietnam’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
越南外交部未立即回應置評請求。
廣告
Both China and Vietnam have repeatedly protested the other’s island-building projects over the years, while efforts by U.S. officials to cool the expansionary fervor have been unsuccessful.
多年來，中越雙方均多次抗議對方的島礁建設，而美國官員試圖為這場不斷升級的擴張降溫的努力也未取得成功。
Last year, as Vietnam ramped up its projects in the Spratlys, where China had previously reclaimed 3,200 acres of land over a period of about three years, China’s Foreign Ministry criticized what it called an “illegal occupation.” Vietnam has leveled similar criticisms at China, which has lately focused its efforts on the Paracel Islands, which Vietnam claims in their entirety.
去年，隨著越南加快推進南沙造島工程——此前中國曾在約三年時間內圍填約1300公頃的土地——中國外交部批評越方是在進行「非法佔領」。越南也曾對中國提出類似指責。近年來，中國將建設重點更多放在越南主張全部主權的西沙群島。
But these tensions were nowhere to be seen over the weekend, as the search for the missing crew members dragged on.
不過，在剛剛過去的這個週末，這些緊張關係並未顯現出來，各方都在持續搜尋失蹤船員。
On Sunday, Pham Thu Hang, a spokeswoman for Vietnam’s Foreign Ministry, thanked Chinese rescuers for their efforts.
週日，越南外交部發言人范秋姮對中方施援表示感謝。
“We would like to express our gratitude to the Chinese authorities for their swift assistance to Vietnam in the search and rescue efforts,” she said. “And we hope that both sides will continue to cooperate closely to find the remaining people.”
她說：「我們向中方有關部門和人員的及時施援表示衷心感謝，希望雙方繼續密切協作，搜尋失蹤人員。」

Berry Wang對本文有報導貢獻。

Tung Ngo是《紐約時報》記者/研究員，常駐越南河內。
