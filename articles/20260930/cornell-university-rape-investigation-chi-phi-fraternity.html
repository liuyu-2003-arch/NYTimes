康乃爾大學兄弟會強姦案調查重啟：目前我們所知的

What We Know About the Cornell Rape Investigation

SHARON OTTERMAN • 2026-09-30
Prosecutors have reopened an investigation into a 2024 report by a Cornell University student who said she was raped at a fraternity house, raising questions about how the case was initially handled by law enforcement officials and the school.
檢方重新啟動了對康乃爾大學一名學生2024年報案稱自己在兄弟會會舍遭強姦一案的調查，這也引發了外界對執法部門和學校當初對此案處理的質疑。
Her allegations surfaced again two weeks ago after the student, identified as Jane Doe in a lawsuit, sued seven men, the university, her sorority and the fraternity. The case has led to fierce debate across the internet and drawn attention to the persistent problem of sexual assault on college campuses.
兩週前，這名學生——在訴訟中以「無名氏」代稱——起訴了七名男子、康乃爾大學、她所在的姐妹會以及涉事兄弟會，她的指控因此再次浮出水面。此案在網上引發激烈爭論，也讓大學校園性侵這一長期存在的問題再度受到關注。
The district attorney for Tompkins County, home to Cornell in Ithaca, N.Y., said he would consider presenting the case to a grand jury. In 2024, immediately after her report, he declined to do so.
紐約州湯普金斯縣（康乃爾大學位於該縣的伊薩卡市）地方檢察官表示，他會考慮將案件提交大陪審團。而在2024年，也就是這名學生報案後不久，他曾決定不這樣做。
Here’s what we know about the Cornell case.
以下是我們目前了解到的康乃爾大學這起案件的相關情況。
廣告
What do we know about Jane Doe?
關於「無名氏」的情況
Because she is anonymous in the lawsuit, most of the details about her life are unknown. Her lawyer has said she is from a small town, about 1,000 miles away from Cornell. She arrived at the university as an undergraduate in August 2022, then dropped out after she said she was raped in 2024. She was 20 years old at the time and had been a member of the Tri-Delta sorority.
由於她在訴訟中匿名，關於她生活的大部分細節都不為人知。她的律師說，她來自一個距離康乃爾大學約1600公里的小鎮。她於2022年8月進入康乃爾大學讀本科，在2024年聲稱自己遭到強姦後退學。當時她20歲，是Tri-Delta姐妹會成員。
Her lawyer said she is struggling now with the aftermath of the incident and not in college.
律師說，她目前仍在應對事件帶來的後續影響，也沒有繼續上大學。
What does the lawsuit say happened?
訴訟對事情經過的描述
On Oct. 19, 2024, according to the lawsuit, the woman was raped repeatedly over nearly seven hours at the Chi Phi fraternity house in Ithaca, N.Y.
根據訴訟，2024年10月19日，這名女子在紐約州伊薩卡的Chi Phi兄弟會會舍內多次遭到強姦，持續了近七個小時。
The lawsuit states that she started off her night drinking vodka at the sorority house. At around 8:30 p.m., she took an Uber to an event her sorority was hosting at a bar. Although the bouncer correctly identified her as underage, she claims she was served at least one alcoholic beverage. By about 11 p.m., she had decided to walk about a mile to Chi Phi’s fraternity house to see a man she considered a friend.
訴訟稱，她當晚先在姐妹會會舍喝了伏特加。晚上8點30分左右，她乘Uber前往姐妹會在某酒吧舉辦的一場活動。雖然保鏢正確識別出她未達法定飲酒年齡，但她聲稱，自己仍被提供了至少一杯酒精飲料。到晚上11點左右，她決定步行約1.6公里前往Chi Ph兄弟會會舍，去見一名她視為朋友的男子。
Once she arrived, according to the lawsuit, the man and his fraternity brothers gave her a beer, marijuana, rum and ketamine. In the lawsuit, she claims she had not tried ketamine before and was pressured into snorting it. The lawsuit then details a series of sexual assaults and continued drug use involving her friend and six other men.
訴訟稱，她抵達後，那名男子和他的兄弟會兄弟給了她啤酒、大麻、朗姆酒和氯胺酮（俗稱K粉）。她在訴訟中稱，自己以前從未用過K粉，是被迫吸食的。訴訟隨後詳細描述了一系列性侵行為，以及她與這名朋友和其他六名男子持續使用毒品的情況。
At about 1:42 a.m., one of the men sent a message to the Chi Phi Snapchat group inviting others to join in, using a crude name for female genitalia, according to a screenshot included in the lawsuit.
訴訟所附的一張截圖顯示，凌晨1點42分左右，其中一名男子在Chi Phi兄弟會的 Snapchat群組裡發消息邀其他人加入，並使用了女性生殖器的粗俗稱呼。
廣告
How did the police respond?
警方的回應
With the encouragement of friends, Jane Doe reported the incident to Cornell’s police department on Nov. 8, 2024.
在朋友們的鼓勵下，「無名氏」於2024年11月8日向康乃爾大學警察局報案。
The Cornell police sent a crime alert to campus, stating that a victim had reported being “sexually assaulted by several males and coerced into consuming ketamine and other drugs” at a fraternity house. In a message to campus before that Thanksgiving, Cornell’s president said that the university police department was continuing its criminal investigation, and that a second, administrative investigation into the allegations was underway.
康乃爾警方向校園發布犯罪警報，稱一名受害者報告自己在兄弟會會舍「遭多名男性性侵，並被強迫吸食氯胺酮和其他毒品」。在那個感恩節前發給校園的信息中，康乃爾校長表示，大學警察局正在繼續刑事調查，同時針對這些指控的第二項行政調查也在進行中。
Some time that month, Cornell police asked the Tompkins County district attorney’s office to review a statement from Jane Doe. Criminal investigators advised the police that they did not think they could bring criminal charges under New York State law based on the statement. Jane Doe’s lawyer said that after she gave her initial statement, no police or criminal investigators ever contacted her again.
當月某個時候，康乃爾警方要求湯普金斯縣地方檢察官辦公室審查「無名氏」的一份陳述。刑事調查人員告知警方，他們認為根據紐約州法律，僅憑這份陳述無法提起刑事指控。「無名氏」的律師說，她作出最初陳述後，再也沒有任何警察或刑事調查人員聯繫過她。
It is unclear whether the police interviewed the men involved, or whether Jane Doe was ever told that her criminal case was not being pursued.
目前尚不清楚警方是否訊問過涉案男子，也不清楚「無名氏」是否曾被告知，她的刑事案件不會繼續推進。
On Monday, the district attorney — who has released only excerpts from Jane Doe’s initial six-page statement — said that the statement did not make clear that she was forced into the sexual acts, or that she was incapacitated, though she was under the influence of drugs and alcohol.
週一，地方檢察官表示——他目前只公布了「無名氏」最初那份六頁陳述中的部分內容——該陳述沒有明確表明她是被迫發生性行為的，也沒有明確表明她當時已喪失行為能力，儘管她當時確實受到毒品和酒精影響。
廣告
How did Cornell respond?
康乃爾大學當時的回應
The university immediately placed the fraternity, and the seven men she named in the lawsuit, on interim suspensions, the suit said.
訴訟稱，校方立即對涉事兄弟會以及她在訴訟中點名的七名男子作出臨時停學處理。
Meanwhile, Cornell’s administrative Title IX justice process swung into motion to determine any potential discipline for the accused men. In May 2025, at least 12 hearings were held before a panel of faculty and staff, according to the woman’s lawsuit.
與此同時，康乃爾大學依據《教育法修正案第九條》開展的校內行政程序也隨即啟動，以決定是否應對這些被指控的男子作出處分。根據這名女子的訴訟，2025年5月，由教職員工組成的一個審議小組舉行了至少12場聽證。
The accused men “were afforded the opportunity to mitigate their conduct by submitting essays to Cornell,” according to the lawsuit. Cornell does not deny that essays — which it calls reflection papers — were part of the punishment that the panel decided on for some of the men. But it stated Monday that it was not the only consequence for any of them.
訴訟稱，被告男子「獲得了通過向康乃爾提交書面材料來減輕其行為後果的機會」。康乃爾並不否認這些書面材料——校方稱之為檢討——是小組決定對部分男子實施的處罰之一。但校方週一表示，對於任何一個當事人來說，這並非唯一的處分。
Cornell, citing student privacy rules, declined to give details about how the individual men were punished. It said Monday that it gave the men “a range of sanctions, which included expulsions and suspensions.” Thomas Giuffra, the lawyer who represents Jane Doe, has said in interviews that only two of the men were expelled, and that his client did not feel justice had been done.
康乃爾大學以學生隱私規定為由，拒絕透露對這些男子各自的具體處分。校方週一表示，對他們採取了「一系列處分措施，其中包括開除和停學」。代表「無名氏」的律師托馬斯·朱弗拉在採訪中稱，只有兩名男子被開除，而他的當事人認為正義並未得到伸張。
What has been the fallout so far from the lawsuit?
此案的反響
廣告
Millions of people have viewed the lawsuit or heard influencers and celebrities talk about the allegations online, bringing attention to the ongoing problem of sexual assault at Cornell and other college campuses. The mayor of Ithaca said Monday that he is proposing a ban on permits for Cornell events until the university reforms its sexual assault policies.
已有數百萬人看過這份訴訟文件，或在網上聽網紅和名人談論這些指控，這令康乃爾大學以及其他大學校園長期存在的性侵問題受到更多關注。伊薩卡市市長週一表示，他將提議，在康乃爾大學對性侵事件處理政策進行改革之前，禁止批准該校的活動舉辦許可。
The pressure and attention pushed the Tompkins County district attorney to reopen the criminal case. A criminal investigator has reached out to Jane Doe’s lawyer, meaning that there could still be criminal charges in the case. Gov. Kathy Hochul has called for an independent investigation of Cornell’s handling of the case.
輿論壓力和關注促使湯普金斯縣地方檢察官重啟刑事調查。一名刑事調查人員已經聯繫了「無名氏」的律師，這意味著此案仍有可能提出刑事指控。紐約州州長凱西·霍格爾已要求對康乃爾大學處理此案的方式展開獨立調查。
The names and photographs of the seven accused men are widely circulating online, as part of a mass movement that some see as a grass-roots effort to bring accountability, and others view as a form of harassment of men not yet charged with any crime.
七名被告男子的姓名和照片在網上廣泛傳播。有人將這場大規模行動視為草根式的追責努力，也有人認為這是對尚未被控任何罪名的男子的騷擾。
