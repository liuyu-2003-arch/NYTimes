川普要求人工智慧巨頭自我監管

At White House, Trump Asks A.I. Titans to Police Themselves

KATIE ROGERS, CECILIA KANG • 2026-09-30
President Trump summoned the world’s most powerful technology executives to the White House on Tuesday, and praised the assembled billionaires as “very brilliant” minds who wanted to make the world a better place through job creation, the construction of data centers and the rapid expansion of artificial intelligence.
川普總統週二將全球最有權勢的科技高管召集到白宮，稱讚與會的億萬富翁們是「非常傑出」的人才，稱他們希望通過創造就業機會、建設數據中心以及快速擴張人工智慧讓世界變得更美好。
As president, Mr. Trump faces questions about whether A.I. should be regulated, and particularly whether restrictions should be put in place to protect humans from technology that has learned to outmaneuver existing safeguards.
作為總統，川普面臨著人工智慧是否應該受到監管的質詢，特別是是否應該實施限制措施，避免人類受到已學會規避現有安全防護措施的技術的傷害。
But in the end, convening all of those billions and all of that brain power did not result in a commitment to formal regulation. Instead, they signed a voluntary agreement to adopt safety policies that include risk reviews, auditing and evaluations of systems by third parties. They also agreed to adopt Mr. Trump’s preferred rebranding of A.I.: “super intelligence.”
但最終，召集這些億萬富翁和頂尖大腦並沒有帶來對正式監管的承諾。取而代之的是，他們簽署了一項自願協議，承諾採取安全政策，包括由第三方對系統進行風險審查、審計和評估。他們還同意採用川普偏愛的人工智慧新名稱：「超級智能」。
“This is going to be all for the good, and we’re going to have it be nice and safe,” Mr. Trump said on Tuesday afternoon, when he and the tech leaders took questions outside the White House. “And they’re really going to be policing each other, and that’s the way it works.”
「這一切都將是為了更好的未來，我們將讓它變得美好且安全，」川普在週二下午與科技領袖們在白宮外接受提問時說道。「而且他們真的會互相監督，這就是它的運作方式。」
廣告
Asked if the agreement would be binding, Mr. Trump said, “I think it’s morally binding.”
當被問及該協議是否具有約束力時，川普說：「我認為它具有道德約束力。」
Inside the White House luncheon, Mr. Trump’s seating chart (which he posted on social media) revealed a powerful assemblage of executives who are deeply divided on the need for A.I. safeguards. The meeting also included top officials, such as Speaker Mike Johnson and Vice President JD Vance, who believe that formalized safeguards would hurt American competitiveness.
在白宮午餐會上，川普公布的座位表（由他本人發布在社群媒體上）顯示，與會的高管們實力雄厚，但在是否需要制定人工智慧保障措施的問題上存在嚴重分歧。與會者還包括眾議院議長邁克·強生和副總統萬斯等高官，他們認為正式的安全措施會損害美國的競爭力。
In the middle of it all was Mr. Trump, who has punted to a group of executives to decide how best to wrangle the rapidly evolving technology, or whether it should be wrangled at all.
處於中心位置的是川普，他把決定如何最好地管控這項快速發展的技術，甚至是否應該對其進行管控的問題，推給了一群高管。
“There’s going to be a tremendous self-policing aspect,” Mr. Trump said.
「將會包含大量自我監管的成分，」川普說。
The one-page document is short on details and relies on the companies to follow through with their promises. It is not an official order, and it is unclear who would enforce the guidance. It was signed by Mr. Trump and the leaders of Anthropic, Google, Meta, Nvidia, OpenAI and SpaceXAI.
這份只有一頁紙的文件缺乏細節，主要依賴於各公司履行其承諾。它不是一項官方命令，也不清楚誰將強制執行該指導方針。它由川普以及Anthropic、谷歌、Meta、英偉達、OpenAI和SpaceXAI的領導人共同簽署。
The agreement echoes voluntary safety commitments by A.I. companies made during the Biden administration. The companies agreed to test their systems for safety and to report safety and security incidents. But the commitments were nonbinding and largely lost at the start of the Trump administration, when the president rolled back safety requirements.
該協議呼應了拜登政府期間人工智慧公司做出的自願安全承諾。這些公司當時同意測試其系統的安全性，並報告安全和安保事件。但這些承諾是不具約束力的，並在川普政府初期基本失效，當時總統撤銷了安全要求。
The business leaders invited to the White House on Tuesday included Dario Amodei, the chief executive of Anthropic, who has called for federal safety regulations, including the testing of models. Earlier this month, Mr. Amodei called for an industrywide slowdown of A.I. development, citing safety concerns.
週二受邀前往白宮的商界領袖包括Anthropic的首席執行官達里奧·阿莫代伊，他曾呼籲制定聯邦安全法規，包括對模型進行測試。本月早些時候，阿莫代伊以安全擔憂為由，呼籲整個行業放緩人工智慧的發展。
廣告
The company has also been embroiled in a legal fight with the administration, and has been barred from working with the Pentagon. The two sides disagreed in negotiations over a $200 million contract, with the company’s insisting that its A.I. technology not be used in mass surveillance of Americans or for autonomous lethal weapons. Speaking to reporters on Tuesday, Mr. Trump called Mr. Amodei “fantastic.”
該公司還捲入了與政府的法律糾紛，並被禁止與五角大廈合作。雙方在一項價值2億美元的合同談判中存在分歧，該公司堅持要求其人工智慧技術不得用於對美國人的大規模監控或用於自主致命武器。週二對記者講話時，川普稱阿莫代伊「非常出色」。
Also on the guest list was Jensen Huang, the chief executive of Nvidia and a close Trump ally, who has called the safety concerns exaggerated. Mr. Huang has implored the administration and lawmakers to keep a hands-off approach to regulating the industry, saying rules would allow rival China to speed past the United States on A.I. leadership.
出席嘉賓名單中還有英偉達首席執行官、川普的親密盟友黃仁勛，他曾稱這些安全擔憂被誇大了。黃仁勛曾懇求政府和立法者對該行業的監管保持不干涉態度，稱相關規則將使競爭對手中國在人工智慧領導地位上迅速超越美國。
When the group appeared with reporters, the president called Mr. Huang a “very handsome guy” and asked him to speak.
當這群人與記者見面時，總統稱黃仁勛是個「非常英俊的傢伙」，並請他發言。
“We’re going to advance this technology that is so consequential to humanity, so consequential to the future prosperity of our nation,” Mr. Huang told reporters. “We’re going to do it responsibly and safely.”
「我們將推進這項對人類如此重要、對我們國家未來繁榮都非常重要的技術，」黃仁勛告訴記者。「我們將以負責任且安全的方式推進這項工作。」
Others invited to the luncheon included Jeff Bezos, the founder of Amazon; Greg Brockman, the president of OpenAI; Alex Karp, the chief executive of Palantir; Sundar Pichai of Google; Mark Zuckerberg of Meta; and Elon Musk of SpaceXAI. The White House chief of staff, Susie Wiles, and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent were there. David Sacks, Mr. Trump’s former top adviser on A.I. and a venture capitalist, also attended.
受邀參加午餐會的人士還包括亞馬遜創始人傑夫·貝佐斯；OpenAI總裁格雷格·布羅克曼；Palantir首席執行官亞歷克斯·卡普；谷歌的桑達爾·皮查伊；Meta的馬克·祖克柏；以及SpaceXAI的伊隆·馬斯克。白宮辦公廳主任蘇西·威爾斯和財政部長史考特·貝森特也在場。川普的前首席人工智慧顧問、風險投資家戴維·薩克斯也出席了會議。
Before the luncheon, Mr. Trump told the audience at an event unveiling America.gov, an A.I.-powered search tool, that the U.S. government was championing the technology in an effort to counter China, a major rival in the race to develop A.I.
在午餐會之前，川普在人工智慧驅動搜索工具America.gov的發布活動上告訴聽眾，美國政府正在擁護這項技術，努力抗衡人工智慧發展競賽中的主要對手中國。
廣告
“We don’t want to stifle growth,” the president said.
「我們不想扼殺增長，」總統說。
Mr. Trump, who hosted a state visit last week for Xi Jinping, the Chinese leader, said that the two did not spend much time discussing opportunities for partnerships or issues related to competition. Mr. Xi said in remarks at the White House that both nations shared the responsibility to develop A.I. with “the well-being of the people” in mind.
上週剛剛接待中國領導人習近平進行國事訪問的川普表示，兩人並沒有花太多時間討論合作機會或與競爭相關的議題。習近平在白宮發表講話時表示，兩國在發展人工智慧時，都應以「造福於民」為出發點，並肩承擔起這一責任。
But in front of a crowd of executives and cabinet members, who were gathered in an auditorium for a presentation that took on the veneer of an Apple product-launch event, the president reiterated a winner-take-all mentality.
但在眾多高管和內閣成員面前——他們聚集在禮堂裡，舉行一場如同蘋果新品發布會般的演講——總統再次重申了贏家通吃的思維方式。
“You’re going to have a winner, or you’re going to have a loser,” Mr. Trump said.
「你要麼成為贏家，要麼成為輸家，」川普說。
Mr. Trump has argued that American dominance over A.I. technology is imperative for national security and economic growth.
川普一直主張，美國在人工智慧技術上的主導地位對國家安全和經濟增長至關重要。
With his embrace of A.I. and support for the development of data centers throughout the country, Mr. Trump is also increasingly out of step with the public. Recent polling shows that a majority of Americans are growing more concerned about A.I. and want the federal government to do more to regulate the technology.
隨著他大力擁抱人工智慧並支持在全國範圍內建設數據中心，川普的步調也同公眾愈發不一。近期的民意調查顯示，多數美國人對人工智慧日益擔憂，並希望聯邦政府採取更多措施來監管這項技術。
廣告
On Tuesday, Mr. Trump again came down on the side of data centers, calling concerns about them a Democratic hoax. Flanked by executives at the luncheon, Mr. Trump predicted that the public would eventually embrace the facilities.
週二，川普再次為數據中心發聲，稱對數據中心的擔憂是民主黨的騙局。午餐會上，在企業高管們的簇擁下，川普預測公眾最終會接受這些設施。
“You’re going to see data centers are going to be very popular, because this is the group, and they’re the ones that want to, and they’re also the ones that want to see communities that are safe and happy,” Mr. Trump said. “Otherwise, they’ll be forced to go overseas or other locations.”
「你們會看到數據中心將變得非常受歡迎，因為這就是那群人，他們是想要建設這些中心的人，他們也是希望看到社區安全和快樂的人，」川普說。「否則，這些數據中心將被迫遷往海外或其他地方。」
As the public has soured on A.I. and the prospect of data centers mushrooming around the country, the debate over technology safety has intensified in recent weeks. Several rogue A.I. clients and models have worked together to breach websites, steal data and then cover their tracks, all while appearing to show an understanding that what they were doing was wrong. Senior leaders in Congress have acknowledged the need for A.I. safety guardrails.
隨著公眾對人工智慧以及數據中心在全國各地如雨後春筍般湧現的前景感到反感，近幾週來，關於技術安全的辯論愈發激烈。一些失控的人工智慧客戶端和模型曾協同入侵網站、竊取數據，然後掩蓋其蹤跡，而在此過程中，它們似乎還表現出對自身行為錯誤性的認知。國會的高層領導人已經承認了設置人工智慧安全護欄的必要性。
In April, Anthropic announced it was holding back the release of one of its models, Mythos, because it was too dangerous for the public. In July, OpenAI models broke out of a restricted testing environment and hacked the A.I. start-up Hugging Face. In early September, leaders of Anthropic, OpenAI and Google DeepMind called for slowing development of advanced A.I. models.
今年4月，Anthropic宣布暫停發布其一個名為Mythos的模型，理由是該模型對公眾而言過於危險。今年7月，OpenAI的模型突破了受限的測試環境，入侵了人工智慧初創公司Hugging Face。在9月初，Anthropic、OpenAI和谷歌DeepMind的領導人們呼籲放緩高級人工智慧模型的開發。
Last week, an OpenAI model was found to have meddled with U.S. government websites including the Education Department, the Securities and Exchange Commission and Commerce Department’s census site. And this week, OpenAI said it would not release its latest model, GPT-6.1 Astra, over safety concerns.
上週，一個OpenAI模型被發現干擾了包括美國教育部、證券交易委員會以及商務部人口普查網站在內的美國政府網站。而本週，OpenAI表示出於安全考慮，將不會發布其最新模型GPT-6.1 Astra。
Within the White House, senior leaders are at odds on how to regulate the industry. Mr. Bessent has floated the idea of an open dialogue with China on safety standards. He has also stressed that A.I. companies must be held accountable for harms created by their models.
在白宮內部，高層領導人對如何監管該行業存在分歧。貝森特提出了就安全標準與中國進行公開對話的想法。他還強調，人工智慧公司必須為其模型造成的危害承擔責任。
As his day with the billionaires wrapped up on Tuesday, Mr. Trump said that he was considering a committee of about 10 people focused on A.I. safety, and that he would name an A.I. czar in the coming days.
隨著週二與億萬富翁們共度的一天走向結束，川普表示，他正在考慮成立一個由大約10人組成、專門負責人工智慧安全的委員會，並且他將在未來幾天內任命一位人工智慧領域的事務專員。
He ended his final appearance with reporters alongside Mr. Musk, another attendee who has worked himself back into Mr. Trump’s good graces after a spat last year over domestic policy legislation. He was seated near Mr. Xi and Mr. Trump at last week’s state dinner, and was at Mr. Trump’s side at the luncheon on Tuesday.
當天他最後一次面見記者時，陪伴在他身邊的是馬斯克。馬斯克也是與會者，在去年因國內政策立法發生口角後，如今已重新贏得了川普的青睞。在上週的國宴上，他坐在習近平和川普附近，在週二的午餐會上，他也坐在川普身邊。
廣告
Mr. Xi, the president said, “will take every data center that we don’t want.”
總統說，習近平「會接手所有我們不想要的數據中心。」
Standing nearby, Mr. Musk agreed.
站在旁邊的馬斯克表示同意。
“He would love that, every single one, because you know why? They’re smart,” Mr. Trump continued. “They want every data center. They want it because that’s great for China. Well, it’s great for us, and we’re leading by a lot, and we're going to keep it that way.”
「他會很樂意的，每一個都要，因為你知道為什麼嗎？他們很聰明，」川普繼續說道。「他們想要每一個數據中心。他們想要是因為這對中國大有好處。好吧，這對我們也很有好處，而且我們遙遙領先，我們會繼續保持這種優勢。」

Kalley Huang contributed reporting.
