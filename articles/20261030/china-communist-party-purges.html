習近平清洗行動繼續

China Confirms Expulsion of 33 Officials, as Xi’s Purge Deepens

儲百亮 • 2026-10-30
China’s ruling Communist Party announced on Thursday that it had formally expelled 33 senior political and military officials, including a prominent former diplomat and a military secretary of Xi Jinping, the country’s top leader.
中國執政的共產黨週四宣布，已正式開除33名黨內高級政界和軍界官員，其中包括一名知名的前外交官，以及中國最高領導人習近平的一名軍方秘書。
The announcement laid bare the depth of Mr. Xi’s anticorruption purges as he prepares to secure a fourth term in power next year.
隨著習近平準備在明年謀求第四個任期，這一公告揭示了其反腐清洗行動之深。
The party said the expulsions had been made earlier by the Politburo, a council of top officials. They were confirmed by the party’s Central Committee, which concluded a four-day meeting in Beijing on Thursday.
中共表示，這些開除黨籍的決定此前已由中央政治局作出。週四在北京結束為期四天會議的中共中央委員會確認了這些決定。
The communiqué released by the Central Committee confirmed the fate of some officials who had disappeared for months or longer without official explanation. They included Liu Jianchao, a senior foreign affairs official who was most recently the head of the party’s International Department, until he abruptly left that job last year.
中央委員會發布的公報證實了一些官員的命運。這些官員此前已消失數月甚至更長時間，官方一直沒有作出解釋。其中包括高級外事官員劉建超。他此前擔任中共中央對外聯絡部部長，直到去年突然離任。
廣告
Another was Zhong Shaojun, a senior secretary to Mr. Xi who had served for over a decade as his eyes and ears in the Central Military Commission, which controls the People’s Liberation Army.
另一人是鍾紹軍。他是習近平的一名高級秘書，十多年來一直在掌管中國人民解放軍的中央軍委中充當習近平的耳目。
“This meeting marked a comprehensive purge by Xi Jinping of officials he had promoted since taking office” in 2012, said Wen-Hsuan Tsai, a research fellow at Academia Sinica, an institute in Taiwan, who studies the Chinese Communist Party.
研究中國共產黨的台灣中央研究院研究員蔡文軒說：「這次會議是習近平對他執政之後提拔的幹部一次整體性的清算。」
The sparse benches at this year’s meeting of the Central Committee, a policy-setting body that meets roughly once a year, told their own story.
作為制定政策的機構，中央委員會大約每年召開一次全體會議。今年會議上座席空了很多，這已透露出不少信息。
Only 279 officials attended the meeting, the party’s statement said. When the current committee was formed at a Communist Party congress in 2022, it had 376 members.
中共的聲明稱，只有279名官員出席了此次會議。2022年中共全國代表大會選出本屆中央委員會時，委員會共有376名成員。
That suggests that nearly 100 officials have been removed from the committee over the past four years.
這意味著過去四年間，有近100名官員被中央委員會中除名。
“This is the biggest purge in history,” Mr. Tsai said, referring to the numbers removed from the committee.
「這是歷史上規模最大的一次清洗，」蔡文軒談到中央委員會除名人數時說。
廣告
The party also confirmed that it would hold its next congress some time in the second half of 2027. At that congress, Mr. Xi is virtually certain to win a fourth five-year term as party general secretary, a feat that would extend his rule to at least 20 years and seal his status as China’s most powerful leader in many decades.
中共還證實，下一次全國代表大會將於2027年下半年某個時間舉行。屆時，習近平幾乎肯定會贏得第四個為期五年的中共中央總書記任期。這將使他的執政時間延長至至少20年，並鞏固他作為數十年來中國最有權勢領導人的地位。 
Since he came to power nearly 14 years ago, Mr. Xi has waged campaigns to eradicate corruption and disloyalty in the party, military and security services. But he has recently complained that still too many officials remain disloyal and are tempted by bribes.
自近14年前上台以來，習近平一直在黨內、軍隊和安全部門開展運動，以根除腐敗和不忠誠行為。但最近，他抱怨仍有太多官員不夠忠誠，容易受到賄賂誘惑。
This week’s Central Committee meeting focused on instilling stricter discipline in officials.
本週的中央委員會會議重點討論了對官員實施更加嚴格的紀律約束。
“We must maintain a high-pressure stance in punishing corruption and strengthen checks and balances on the exercise of power,” the communiqué from the meeting said. But the party leadership has made similar promises before, including at a Central Committee meeting on the same anticorruption theme a decade ago.
會議公報稱：「要保持懲治腐敗高壓態勢，加強對權力運行的制約和監督。」但中共領導層此前也作出過類似承諾，包括十年前一次同樣以反腐為主題的中央委員會會議。
The meeting approved only one major promotion, that of Zhang Shengmin, the general who has overseen sweeping investigations and removals for corruption and disloyalty in the military. He was elevated into the Politburo, the party’s council of over 20 top officials.
此次會議只批准了一項重要人事晉陞，即將張升民上將提拔進入中央政治局。張升民此前一直負責軍隊內部針對腐敗和不忠誠行為展開的大規模調查和清洗。中央政治局是由20多名中共最高層官員組成的機構。 
Of the 33 Central Committee members whose expulsions were confirmed at the meeting this week, 16 came from the military, including Zhang Youxia, formerly China’s most senior general until he was detained for investigation in January.
本週會議確認開除黨籍的33名中央委員會成員中，有16人來自軍方，其中包括張又俠。他曾是中國軍方級別最高的將領，直到今年1月被帶走接受調查。
廣告
“This signifies a further strengthening of the military’s disciplinary inspection system,” Mr. Tsai, the Taiwanese scholar, said of General Zhang’s promotion. “It’s possible that more senior officers will be purged in the future.”
台灣學者蔡文軒在談到張升民的晉陞時說：「這意味著軍隊紀檢體系的強化。未來軍隊將領可能還會有人被整肅。」

儲百亮(Chris Buckley)是《紐約時報》首席中國記者，自台北報導中國和台灣問題，重點關注政治、社會變革以及安全和軍事問題。
