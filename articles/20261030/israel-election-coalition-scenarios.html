無人獲勝的以色列大選，誰將成為總理

With No Clear Winner, How Could Israel Get a Prime Minister?

JOHNATAN REISS, ADINA RENNER, PABLO ROBLES • 2026-10-30
Israel’s high-stakes parliamentary election on Tuesday produced no clear winner after most of the votes were counted.
以色列週二舉行的關鍵議會選舉在大部分選票統計完畢後，未能產生明確的獲勝者。
That in itself was not unexpected — but there were nevertheless some surprises.
這本身並不出人意料——但確實出現了一些意外。
Mr. Netanyahu’s right-wing, populist party, Likud, outperformed many pre-election polls to become the largest party in Parliament, preliminary results show. It won fewer seats than in 2022, though, when the last national election was held.
初步結果顯示，納坦尼雅胡領導的右翼民粹主義政黨利庫德集團表現好於許多選前民調，成為議會第一大黨。不過，其贏得的席位少於2022年的上一次全國選舉。
More noteworthy was the turnout by supporters of Israel’s Arab parties, which ended Election Day with a historic boost.
更值得注意的是以色列阿拉伯政黨支持者的投票率，選舉日結束時這一投票率實現了歷史性增長。
Israel’s parliamentary system as a rule requires parties to find enough common ground to assemble a 61-seat majority bloc in Israel’s 120-seat Parliament. Over the years, that has produced some unlikely alliances — but also shaky, short-lived governments.
以色列議會制度通常要求政黨達成足夠多的共識，在120席的議會中組成61席的多數派陣營。多年來，這一制度促成了一些出人意料的聯盟，但也催生了搖搖欲墜的短命政府。
In less than four years, from 2019 to 2022, Israelis were sent to the polls five times as parties failed to hold a majority together, or to form one in the first place.
2019年至2022年不到四年間，以色列人五次走向投票站，因為政黨要麼未能維持多數，要麼從一開始就未能組成多數。
廣告
In the coming days, party leaders will be back at it again. With the provisional election results showing no bloc with a straightforward path to a coalition, it could mean bitter negotiations — or deadlock, with the very real possibility of another election.
未來幾天，各黨領導人將再次為此展開活動。臨時選舉結果顯示，沒有任何陣營擁有通向聯盟的明確路徑，這可能意味著激烈的談判——或者僵局，而再次選舉的可能性也是真實存在的。
Here’s how parties may align for Israelis to get a government and avoid another trip to the polls.
以下是各黨可能如何組合，以便以色列人產生一個政府並避免再次投票。
Scenario
可能的情形
A historic Jewish-Arab alliance
歷史性的猶太-阿拉伯聯盟
For Mr. Netanyahu’s opponents, securing a majority increasingly appears to depend on support from at least some Arab lawmakers.
對納坦尼雅胡的反對者而言，要確立多數地位，似乎越來越多地取決於至少部分阿拉伯議員的支持。
If the opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot’s coalition were to include both Arab parties — the Joint List, an ideologically diverse alliance, and Raam, a pragmatic Islamist party — he would have a significant majority in Parliament.
如果反對派領導人加迪·艾森科特的聯盟同時納入兩個阿拉伯政黨——意識型態多元的「聯合名單」和務實的伊斯蘭主義政黨「拉姆」——他將在議會中擁有顯著多數。
Such a coalition would carry political risks.
這樣的聯盟將伴隨政治風險。
Mr. Eisenkot has rejected alliances with parties that do not support Israel’s Jewish character or the defeat of militant group Hamas in the Gaza Strip. Many of his voters could see an alliance with Arab parties as a broken campaign promise, a liability for Mr. Eisenkot in a political landscape in which another election could be imminent.
艾森科特拒絕與不支持以色列的猶太屬性、或不支持在加薩地帶擊敗激進組織哈馬斯的政黨結盟。他的許多選民可能將與阿拉伯政黨的聯盟視為背棄競選承諾，在可能很快再次選舉的政治格局中，這對艾森科特而言是一個不利因素。
Another possibility for the Eisenkot bloc is to leave Arab lawmakers out of the coalition but secure their agreement to support a minority government. Such a government can take office as long as more lawmakers vote for it than against it, even if the parties that make it up hold fewer than 61 seats.
艾森科特陣營的另一種可能是將阿拉伯議員排除在聯盟之外，但確保他們同意支持一個少數政府。只要支持票多於反對票，這樣的政府即可就職，即便組成政府的政黨所持席位不足61席。
廣告
For the Jewish anti-Netanyahu bloc, based on provisional results, that would mean support from at least six lawmakers from the Arab parties in the initial confidence vote. But it is unclear whether those parties would agree to support a government from which they would be excluded, or how sustainable such an arrangement would be.
根據目前暫時的結果，對猶太反納坦尼雅胡陣營而言，這意味著在首次信任投票中至少需要阿拉伯政黨六名議員的支持。但這些政黨是否會同意支持一個將自己排除在外的政府，以及這種安排能否持續，尚不清楚。
Scenario
情景
A unity government
團結政府
A unity government led by Mr. Netanyahu and Mr. Eisenkot could offer a different path to a clear majority.
由納坦尼雅胡和艾森科特共同領導的團結政府可能提供另一條通向明確多數的路徑。
But such an alliance could prove uneasy for both leaders.
但這種聯盟對兩位領導人而言都可能並不安穩。
Mr. Eisenkot’s entire campaign focused on the urgent need to unseat Mr. Netanyahu, whom he called unfit to serve as prime minister. Mr. Netanyahu may not be keen to share power, or delegate significant authority, to his fierce rivals, potentially at the expense of longtime allies.
艾森科特的整個競選活動聚焦於將納坦尼雅胡趕下台的緊迫性，他稱後者不適合擔任總理。納坦尼雅胡可能不願與自己的激烈對手分享權力或授予其重要權限，而這可能以長期盟友為代價。
廣告
One possible unity government could bring together Mr. Netanyahu’s Likud party; Mr. Eisenkot’s Yashar party; Together, an alliance between the right-wing Naftali Bennett and the centrist Yair Lapid; and Yisrael Beiteinu, a right-wing party whose leader, Avigdor Liberman, broke with Mr. Netanyahu and has long been his rival on the right.
一種可能的團結政府是彙集納坦尼雅胡的利庫德、艾森科特的亞沙爾黨、右翼的納夫塔利·貝內特與中間派的亞伊爾·拉皮德的「團結」聯盟，以及右翼政黨「以色列是我們的家園」——其領導人阿維格多·利伯曼已與納坦尼雅胡決裂，多年來一直是其在右翼的對手。
Preliminary results give such a bloc 66 seats, a clear governing majority, if a shaky one.
初步結果表明這樣一個陣營有66席，構成明確的執政多數，儘管並不穩固。
Scenario
情景
Defections
倒戈
The coming weeks of political wrangling could open other paths to a government. Defections — not uncommon in Israeli politics — could allow one bloc to prevail at the expense of the other, a risk that exists in both political camps.
未來幾週的政治角力可能打開通往政府的其他路徑。倒戈——在以色列政治中並不罕見——可能讓一個陣營以另一個陣營為代價佔據上風，這一風險在兩個陣營中都存在。
Parties that allied with Mr. Eisenkot during the campaign, or even individual lawmakers, could be coaxed into supporting a government led by Mr. Netanyahu.
競選期間與艾森科特結盟的政黨，甚至個別議員，可能被拉攏去支持納坦尼雅胡領導的政府。
廣告
For his part, Mr. Eisenkot could seek the support of the two ultra-Orthodox parties, which are longtime allies of Mr. Netanyahu but not without grievances against him. That might prove difficult, given that the opposition has long opposed military draft exemptions for ultra-Orthodox men.
艾森科特則可能尋求兩個極端正統派政黨的支持。這兩個政黨是納坦尼雅胡的長期盟友，但對他並非沒有不滿。鑒於反對派長期反對極端正統派男性的兵役豁免，這一努力可能十分困難。
Only one thing appears clear: However much Israelis may have regarded this election as a turning point for their country, neither Mr. Netanyahu nor his chief rival got the mandate they sought.
似乎只有一件事是清楚的：無論以色列人可能在多大程度上將此次選舉視為國家的轉折點，納坦尼雅胡及其主要對手都沒有獲得他們所尋求的授權。
Now, any path to power will require ideological compromises, uncomfortable alliances or both. Otherwise, it’s back to the voting booth.
如今，任何通往權力的道路都需要意識型態妥協、令人不安的聯盟，或兩者兼有。否則，就只能回到投票站。
