川普幫助博索納羅逃離巴西前往美國

Trump and His Aides Helped Bolsonaro Flee Brazil for the U.S.

黃安偉, ANA LONOVA, HAMED ALEAZIZ, TYLER PAGER • 2026-10-30
Flávio Bolsonaro, the Brazilian presidential candidate who faces multiple criminal investigations, is staying in Florida on a temporary status that was given following orders by President Trump, and will likely be granted permanent residency later, U.S. officials said.
美國官員表示，面臨多項刑事調查的巴西總統候選人弗拉維奧·博索納羅目前以川普總統下令授予的臨時身份滯留佛羅里達州，並有望隨後獲得永久居留權。
Mr. Bolsonaro fled Brazil after losing an election this week. U.S. border agents allowed him and his family to enter the United States on what is called parole, usually given for humanitarian reasons, after they landed in Miami on a private jet on Wednesday morning, a U.S. official said that day.
博索納羅本週競選失敗後逃離巴西。一名美國官員當天說，週三上午，博索納羅及其家人乘坐私人飛機抵達邁阿密後，美國邊境人員允許他們以所謂「暫准入境」身份入境，這種身份通常出於人道主義原因授予。
The jet had been arranged by a White House official. President Trump said later that afternoon that Mr. Bolsonaro had been given “a pass” to enter the country.
這架飛機由一名白宮官員安排。川普總統當天下午晚些時候說，博索納羅獲得了入境美國的「通行許可」。
The White House and the State Department referred questions about Mr. Bolsonaro’s parole status and Mr. Trump’s involvement to the Department of Homeland Security. That agency said Mr. Bolsonaro and his family “were met and inspected by U.S. Customs and Border Protection in accordance with applicable immigration laws and procedures. At the conclusion of the inspection, Mr. Bolsonaro and his accompanying family members were released.”
白宮和國務院將有關博索納羅暫准入境身份及川普參與的問題轉給國土安全部。國土安全部表示，博索納羅及其家人「依照適用的移民法律和程序，接受了美國海關與邊境保護局的接待和檢查。檢查結束後，博索納羅及其隨行家庭成員被放行」。
廣告
A public U.S. government record suggests that Mr. Bolsonaro presented a canceled Brazilian diplomatic passport on arrival, according to a review by The New York Times. The record lists a document with a number issued in Brazil that public disclosures show is linked to Mr. Bolsonaro’s diplomatic passport.
《紐約時報》查閱的一份美國政府公開記錄顯示，博索納羅抵達時出示了一本已作廢的巴西外交護照。該記錄列出一個在巴西簽發的證件編號，公開披露信息顯示該號碼與博索納羅的外交護照有關。
The State Department said through an official on Tuesday that it planned to issue visas to Mr. Bolsonaro and his family allowing them to enter the country. The White House said the same thing that day, citing an administration official.
國務院週二通過一名官員表示，計劃向博索納羅及其家人發放簽證，允許他們入境美國。白宮當天也援引一名政府官員的話作出同樣表示。
The U.S. government grants parole status to people “for urgent humanitarian reasons or significant public benefit,” according to a federal website. The status allows a person to remain in the United States while “treated as an applicant for admission,” the site says.
據聯邦政府網站稱，美國政府會「出於緊急人道主義原因或重大公共利益」給予人們暫准居留身份。該網站說，這一身份允許一個人留在美國，同時「被視為入境申請人」。
Mr. Bolsonaro is expected to apply at some point for permanent resident status in the United States, known as a green card, a U.S. official said. That normally would happen after he is given an immigrant visa. Mr. Bolsonaro’s application for a green card would be processed quickly, similar to that of his younger brother before him, the official said.
一名美國官員說，博索納羅預計將在適當時候申請美國永久居民身份，即綠卡。這通常要在他獲得移民簽證之後進行。該官員說，博索納羅的綠卡申請將很快得到處理，類似他弟弟此前的情況。
The officials spoke on the condition of anonymity to discuss sensitive diplomatic matters.
這些官員要求匿名討論敏感外交事務。
Mr. Bolsonaro’s younger brother, Eduardo Bolsonaro, lives in Texas and maintains close ties to the Trump administration, and was granted permanent resident status in July. The process takes years for most immigrants, but the younger Bolsonaro got expedited approval, a U.S. official said.
博索納羅的弟弟愛德華多·博索納羅住在得克薩斯州，與川普政府關係密切，並於7月獲得永久居民身份。一名美國官員說，對大多數移民來說，這一過程需要數年，但愛德華多獲得了加急批准。
廣告
Flávio Bolsonaro set off for the United States after Brazilian police asked him to turn over his passports on Monday because he is being investigated in relation to a money laundering scheme.
弗拉維奧·博索納羅是在巴西警方週一要求他交出護照後前往美國的，因為他正因涉嫌參與洗錢計劃接受調查。
While Mr. Bolsonaro, a right-wing senator who has held elected office for more than two decades, has not been formally charged with a crime, he will be stripped of his parliamentary immunity in early 2027 because he lost the presidential election on Sunday. He could then potentially face prosecution in relation to multiple criminal investigations.
博索納羅是一名右翼參議員，擔任民選公職超過20年。雖然他尚未被正式指控犯罪，但由於他在週日的總統大選中落敗，其議會豁免權將於2027年初被剝奪。屆時他可能因多項刑事調查面臨起訴。
He flew late Tuesday from Argentina to Ecuador for refueling and finally to Miami, all on a private jet arranged by Jason Miller, a White House communications official, according to a person familiar with the logistics. Mr. Bolsonaro’s younger brother Eduardo has been in touch with Mr. Miller. The plan was put into motion on Monday, after Mr. Bolsonaro was told to turn in his passports, the person said.
據一名熟悉後勤安排的人士說，他週二深夜從阿根廷飛往厄瓜多加油，最終飛抵邁阿密，全程乘坐由白宮通訊官員賈森·米勒安排的私人飛機。博索納羅的弟弟愛德華多一直與米勒保持聯繫。這名人士說，該計劃是在週一博索納羅被告知交出護照後啟動的。
On Sunday, Mr. Bolsonaro was decisively defeated in the election by the incumbent, President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva.
週日，博索納羅在選舉中被現任總統盧拉決定性地擊敗。
Mr. Miller, who is listed as a special U.S. government employee, declined to comment, and the White House referred only to public remarks by Mr. Trump, which did not address the private jet. The Wall Street Journal reported earlier on Mr. Miller’s involvement. He is an ally of the Bolsonaro family, and has said he was briefly detained on a visit to Brazil in 2021.
米勒被列為美國政府的特殊僱員，他拒絕置評，白宮僅提及川普的公開言論，而這些言論未涉及那架私人飛機。《華爾街日報》此前報導了米勒參與的情況。他是博索納羅家族的盟友，並說自己曾在2021年訪問巴西時被短暫拘留。
Mr. Bolsonaro, in a lengthy statement on social media on Wednesday afternoon, said he had left Brazil because he faced political persecution and thanked Mr. Trump and President Javier Milei of Argentina. After initially declaring himself the “legitimate president-elect” of Brazil, Mr. Bolsonaro deleted his statement and reposted it using the word “leader” instead.
博索納羅週三下午在社群媒體上發表長篇聲明，稱他離開巴西是因為面臨政治迫害，並感謝川普和阿根廷總統哈維爾·米萊。他最初宣稱自己是巴西「合法當選總統」，隨後刪除了聲明，並重新發布，改用「領導人」一詞。
廣告
“I address the Brazilian people and the international community as the leader of Brazil,” Mr. Bolsonaro said. Without offering credible evidence, he claimed the election that he lost was fraudulent and that he was the subject of a “witch hunt” by Brazil’s judiciary.
「我作為巴西領導人向巴西人民和國際社會講話，」博索納羅說。在沒有提供可信證據的情況下，他聲稱自己敗選的選舉存在舞弊，並稱自己是巴西司法機構「政治迫害」的對象。
The White House’s assistance to Mr. Bolsonaro could escalate tensions between the Trump administration and Mr. Lula, who has accused Mr. Trump of interfering in Brazil’s internal affairs.
白宮對博索納羅的援助可能加劇川普政府與盧拉之間的緊張關係。盧拉指責川普干涉巴西內政。
When asked about Mr. Bolsonaro on Wednesday, Mr. Trump said, “We gave him a pass to come into the country, so we’ll see. But I won’t get involved. It was a very strange election, that’s for sure.”
週三被問及博索納羅時，川普說：「我們給了他入境我國的通行許可，所以看看會怎樣吧。但我不會介入。那場選舉非常奇怪，這是肯定的。」
On Thursday, Mr. Trump declined to say who was the election’s legitimate winner. He said he had not taken sides in the vote because Mr. Lula had asked him to refrain from doing so. “I thought the election was a very great embarrassment to the people of Brazil,” he said.
週四，川普拒絕表態誰是選舉的合法贏家。他說他沒有在投票中選邊站，因為盧拉曾要求他不要這樣做。「我認為這次選舉對巴西人民來說很難堪，」他說。
This year, the State Department under Secretary of State Marco Rubio took actions on Brazil after lobbying by the two Bolsonaro sons, The New York Times reported.
據《紐約時報》報導，今年，在博索納羅兄弟的遊說下，國務卿魯比奧領導的國務院對巴西採取了行動。
The two are sons of a former right-wing president, Jair Bolsonaro, who is serving a lengthy sentence under house detention for attempting a coup. Flávio Bolsonaro conceded this week’s election, but seized on a change in the voting hours on Sunday to demand the outcome be annulled.
這兩兄弟是前右翼總統雅伊爾·博索納羅的兒子。雅伊爾·博索納羅因試圖發動政變正在接受長期居家監禁。弗拉維奧·博索納羅承認了本週選舉的結果，但利用週日投票時間的變化，要求宣布選舉結果無效。
廣告
The change in hours, though, affected only 0.03 percent of the vote and Brazil’s electoral authorities said it did not make any difference to the result, given Mr. Lula’s winning margin of 4.4 million votes. International independent observers have certified the integrity of the election and dozens of countries have recognized the result. The United States and Argentina are not among them.
不過，投票時間變化僅影響0.03%的選票，巴西選舉當局表示，鑒於盧拉以440萬票的優勢獲勝，這對結果沒有任何影響。國際獨立觀察員已認證選舉的公正性，數十個國家已承認選舉結果。美國和阿根廷不在其中。
Mr. Bolsonaro, in his statement on Wednesday, doubled down on his complaints. He also insisted that Mr. Lula had unfairly used government funds to win, citing the president’s move to raise a welfare stipend in the last stretch of the campaign. (Mr. Lula’s government has said the increase was an overdue inflation adjustment.)
博索納羅在週三的聲明中再次重申了他的指控。他還堅稱盧拉不公平地利用政府資金贏得競選，提到總統在競選最後階段提高福利補貼的舉措。（盧拉政府表示，這一提高是遲來的通膨調整。）
Mr. Bolsonaro crossed from Brazil to Argentina by land on Tuesday night with his wife and two daughters, Argentina’s National Directorate of Migration said Wednesday. It said that he then boarded a private fight to Guayaquil, Ecuador, and from there went on to the United States.
阿根廷國家移民局週三說，博索納羅週二晚上與妻子和兩個女兒從巴西陸路進入阿根廷。該局說，他隨後登上一架飛往厄瓜多瓜亞基爾的私人飛機，並從那裡前往美國。
On Monday, Brazilian authorities asked Mr. Bolsonaro to hand over his passports within 24 hours because of investigations into his links to a Brazilian lawyer accused of laundering more than $1 billion through a scheme involving online gambling.
週一，巴西當局要求博索納羅在24小時內交出護照，因為正在調查他與一名巴西律師的關係。該律師被控通過涉及在線賭博的計劃洗錢超過10億美元。
Separately, Mr. Bolsonaro is the subject of a police inquiry over millions of dollars he raised to finance a biopic about his father from a disgraced banker at the center of a huge corruption scheme.
另外，博索納羅還因從一名身處巨大腐敗案中心的失勢銀行家那裡籌集數以百萬計的美元，用於拍攝關於其父親的傳記電影，因而受到警方調查。
When Mr. Bolsonaro did not turn in his passports by Tuesday morning, Brazilian authorities canceled the documents and issued an alert to immigration authorities barring Mr. Bolsonaro’s exit from the country. It is not clear how he was able to cross into Argentina.
由於博索納羅截至週二上午仍未交出護照，巴西當局註銷了他的護照，並向移民當局發出警報，禁止博索納羅出境。目前尚不清楚他是如何越過邊境進入阿根廷的。
