孫悟空愛上林黛玉：中國AI短劇怎麼成了「不當婚戀」

A Monkey King and a Maiden Fell in Love. For China’s Regulator, It Crossed a Line.

TIFFANY MAY • 2026-10-30
In wholesome videos that have delighted the Chinese internet in recent weeks, the Monkey King in a yellow martial arts outfit and a maiden swathed in embroidered silks walk hand in hand. They frolic on sandy beaches and lush forest trails. They ride roller coasters and sample ice cream in a lantern-lit night market.
近幾週，一系列溫馨治癒的影片在中國互聯網上引發熱議。影片中，身穿黃色練功服的孫悟空與一位身披繡花綢緞的少女手牽著手，在沙灘和鬱鬱蔥蔥的林間小道上嬉戲。他們一起坐過山車，還在燈籠點亮的夜市裡品嘗冰淇淋。
The unlikely couple are Sun Wukong and Lin Daiyu, who hail from “Journey to the West” and “Dream of the Red Chamber,” classic Chinese novels that were turned into television shows in the 1980s.
這對意想不到的情侶是孫悟空和林黛玉，分別來自中國古典名著《西遊記》和《紅樓夢》，它們都在上世紀80年代被改編成電視劇。
The pair had never met in literature — or onscreen — until now. Their love story has been concocted by fans, some using artificial intelligence and some splicing old TV footage together.
此前，這兩個人物從未在文學作品中或熒屏上相遇。他們的愛情故事由粉絲杜撰，有人使用人工智慧，有人則把老版電視劇的片段剪輯在一起。
While they are popular with audiences in China, the videos have attracted the attention of the authorities, who believe they take too much creative license by bringing characters from disparate worlds together.
這些影片雖然受到中國觀眾的歡迎，卻也引起了當局的注意。他們認為，將來自不同世界的人物放在一起，太過天馬行空。
廣告
An editorial this month in People’s Daily, a Communist Party mouthpiece, acknowledged the appeal of rewriting the destiny of the characters, but said it risked eroding the meaning of the original stories, which are considered among the four most important Chinese classical novels. The editorial called for red lines to be drawn over videos depicting “malicious alterations that ridicule heroes.”
本月，中共機關報《人民日報》發表評論文章，承認改寫人物命運具有吸引力，但同時警告，這可能損害原著的精神內核。《西遊記》和《紅樓夢》位列中國四大古典名著。評論呼籲劃定紅線，禁止那些「調侃英雄……的惡意篡改」影片。
Citing a government directive to remove A.I. animations that “seriously violate” the spirit of original works of classical literature and their characters, platforms said in October that they had taken down thousands of videos featuring the Monkey King and Lin Daiyu as a couple, one citing “improper romantic associations” between them.
根據政府要求清理「扭曲」原著精神、顛覆角色設定的AI魔改影片的指令，多家網路平台在10月表示，已下架數千條將孫悟空和林黛玉塑造成情侶的影片。其中一家平台稱，兩人之間存在「不當婚戀關聯」。
The two 1980s televised adaptations were the childhood staples of a generation of Chinese millennials, and the characters are beloved for being misfits unbound by convention. The novels have also been reinterpreted into films and operas.
這兩部20世紀80年代的電視劇改編作品是中國千禧一代的童年記憶，劇中人物因不循常規、不受世俗束縛的性格深受喜愛。兩部原著小說也曾多次被改編成電影和戲曲。
Sun Wukong, the Monkey King from “Journey of the West,” was the irrepressible companion of a guileless monk seeking nirvana. He often found himself in trouble with the gods.
《西遊記》中的美猴王孫悟空是一個不受約束的角色，陪伴著一位天真淳樸的僧人踏上追求涅槃的旅程。他常常與天庭眾神發生衝突。
Lin Daiyu was an orphaned girl who had been taken in by wealthy relations in “Dream of the Red Chamber.” Sentimental and sickly, she was prone to sulky ripostes. Ultimately, she died of a broken heart after her lover was married off to another woman.
《紅樓夢》中的林黛玉自幼失去雙親，被有錢的親戚收養。她多愁善感、體弱多病，常常以帶著怨氣的言語反唇相譏。最終，她深愛的人被安排迎娶另一名女子，而她心碎而死。
Liang Ge, a lecturer in sociology at the University of Manchester in Britain, said that the Chinese government demanded a reverential interpretation of the classics and, while the novels had always been rewritten, these retellings seemed to have crossed that line.
英國曼徹斯特大學社會學講師葛亮表示，中國政府要求人們以尊崇的態度詮釋經典作品。儘管這些小說歷來不斷被改編，但這些再創作似乎越過了那條界限。
The use of the phrase “improper romantic association” by the video platform Kuaishou was telling, the academic added, saying that the authorities seemed concerned by “unauthorized authorship of intimacy at scale.”
葛亮還說，影片平台快手使用「不當婚戀關聯」這一說法頗能說明問題。在他看來，當局似乎擔心的是「大規模未經授權地創作親密關係敘事」。 
廣告
Some adaptations of the classics have been encouraged by the Communist Party. China’s leader, Xi Jinping, has urged a revival of traditional culture, saying that classic literature is part of the foundation of modern Chinese identity, along with revolutionary heroes. The blockbuster video game, “Black Myth: Wukong,” with hyper-realistic depictions also drawn from “Journey of the West,” was hailed as such a soft power triumph that the tourism authorities in Shanxi Province released a promotional video featuring landmarks that appeared in the game.
一些經典改編作品得到了共產黨的鼓勵。中國領導人習近平一直呼籲復興傳統文化，稱古典文學與革命英雄一道，構成現代中國身份認同的基礎。採用高度逼真的視覺呈現、同樣取材於《西遊記》的現象級電玩遊戲《黑神話：悟空》被譽為軟實力的一大勝利，以至於山西省旅遊部門發布了一段推廣影片，展示遊戲中出現的當地名勝古蹟。
Yucong Hao, an assistant professor of Asian studies at Vanderbilt University, said in an email that the freedom to reinterpret Chinese history, mythology and classics remained bounded by “ideological correctness.”
范德堡大學亞洲研究助理教授郝宇驄透過電子郵件表示，重新詮釋中國歷史、神話和經典的自由仍受「意識型態正確性」的約束。
“The boundary between telling China’s stories well and telling them in ways that may be deemed subversive is often ambiguous,” she added.
她還說：「講好中國故事以及可能被視為顛覆性的方式講述中國故事，二者之間的界限往往模糊不清。」 
In January, the National Radio and Television Administration launched a campaign to remove A.I. animations that it said maligned revolutionary heroes, presented counterfactual history and misrepresented the spirit of characters from classic literature.
今年1月，國家廣播電視總局啟動一項整治行動，清理其稱為詆毀革命英雄、歪曲歷史事實、歪曲古典文學人物精神的AI魔改影片。
Social media and video streaming apps, including WeChat and Douyin, said in October that they had deleted A.I.-generated videos referencing classical literature that violate the rules.
包括微信和抖音在內的社群媒體及影片平台在10月表示，已刪除違反相關規定、涉及古典文學作品的人工智慧生成影片。
Afra Wang, a technology writer and cultural critic from China, said that the Lin Daiyu and Monkey King pairing was so popular “because of how different those two characters are, and at the same time, how deeply those two character are baked in the psyche of the generation.”
來自中國的科技作者和文化評論人士阿芙拉·王（音）表示，林黛玉和孫悟空這對組合之所以如此受歡迎，「是因為這兩個人物有著天壤之別，同時又都深深植根於這一代人的集體記憶之中」。
廣告
Viewers seemed to enjoy the characters finding happiness together and seeing Lin Daiyu finally content, Ms. Wang said. The most liked comment under the People’s Daily editorial was: “May all great people have great lives. But reality is often so cruel.” Even the Guangzhou Railway posted a commercial comparing its relationship with customers to the chemistry between the pair.
她說，觀眾似乎很喜歡看到這兩個人物在一起獲得幸福，尤其是看到林黛玉終於過上稱心如意的生活。《人民日報》那篇評論下獲贊最多的一條評論寫道：「願所有美好的人都能有美好的人生。但現實往往如此殘酷。」就連廣州鐵路部門也發布了一則廣告，將其與乘客之間的關係比作孫悟空與林黛玉之間的默契。
Internet users have largely mocked the removal of the videos. One joked that all relationships that make women happy are labeled “inappropriate romantic associations.”
對於這些影片遭到下架，網民大多報以嘲諷。有人開玩笑說，凡是能讓女性感到幸福的關係，都會被貼上「不當戀愛關係」的標籤。 
Eileen Chengyin Chow, a Sinologist at Duke University, said that with Mao Zedong identifying himself as a havoc-wreaking Monkey King in 1961, reinterpreting the canon was a political exercise dating back to the early days of the People’s Republic of China.
杜克大學漢學家周成蔭表示，早在1961年，毛澤東就曾自比大鬧天宮的孫悟空。這說明，重新詮釋經典作品作為一種政治行為，可以追溯到中華人民共和國成立初期。
“Fans feel — rightly so — that it is very much in their rights to reinvent the classics,” she wrote in an email. “After all, ‘who doesn’t want Daiyu to be happy?’ seems to be the most popular sentiment!”
她在電子郵件中寫道：「粉絲們覺得——而且理直氣壯地覺得，他們完全有權重新演繹經典。畢竟，『誰不希望黛玉幸福呢』似乎是最普遍的心聲！」

Siyi Zhao自北京對本文有研究貢獻。

Tiffany May是時報駐香港記者，報導香港和更廣泛地區的政治、商業和文化議題。
