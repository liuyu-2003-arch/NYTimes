玻璃摩天大樓：壯麗的城市天際線有多「熱」？

Shiny Glass Skyscrapers Are a Hot Thing. Literally.

CHICO HARLAN, ZUNAIRA SAIEED • 2026-10-30
One thousand feet above city traffic, from her new 74th-floor apartment, Anastasia Zeeman has the kind of enchanting views that only floor-to-ceiling glass can offer. She looks out on a skyline and horizon where storms and sunsets play out like big-screen movies.
城市車流上方約300米處，阿納斯塔西婭·齊曼在她位於74層的新公寓裡欣賞著只有落地玻璃才能提供的迷人景色。她眺望天際線和地平線，風暴與日落交織的景象宛如大銀幕電影般上演。
But Malaysia’s tropical sun sometimes delivers what Ms. Zeeman calls a “wall of heat” radiating from her windows.
但馬來西亞的熱帶陽光有時會帶來齊曼所說的「熱牆」，從她的窗戶輻射進來。
More than any other form of architecture, glass skyscrapers testify to the human desire for sweeping views and a sense of rising above the fray. In some of the world’s fastest developing places — Dubai, Mumbai, Jakarta, Kuala Lumpur — they have become city-defining showpieces, signaling sophistication and rising financial clout.
與其他任何建築形式相比，玻璃摩天大樓最能體現人類對開闊視野的嚮往，以及凌駕於塵囂之上的追求。在杜拜、孟買、雅加達、吉隆坡等全球發展最迅速的城市中，它們已成為定義城市風貌的標誌性建築，象徵著精緻和日益增強的金融影響力。
And yet glass tends to be ill-suited for keeping out the heat, and its overuse often makes buildings more energy intensive to cool. That’s fueling a debate about whether these symbols of modernity make sense in a world growing hotter because of climate change.
然而，玻璃往往不適合阻隔熱量，其過度使用常常使建築冷卻所需能耗更高。這引發了一場辯論：在因氣候變化而日益炎熱的世界裡，這些現代性的象徵是否還有意義。

Some argue that the ambition and innovation that spawned the high-rise era can help modern skyscrapers fend off rising temperatures while maintaining the million-dollar views. Others say the all-glass aesthetic is at odds with the environmental goal of reducing greenhouse-gas emissions. They say it is time to rethink city skylines.
一些人認為，憑藉催生摩天大樓時代的雄心和創新，現代摩天大樓能夠在保持價值百萬美元的壯麗景觀同時抵禦氣溫上升。另一些人說，全玻璃美學與減少溫室氣體排放的環境目標相悖。他們說，是時候對城市天際線進行重新思考了。
When he was New York City mayor in 2019, Bill de Blasio briefly floated the idea of banning the “glass and steel” structures altogether. Philip Oldfield, an Australian architecture scholar who wrote a book on sustainable high rises, calls the glass towers “environmentally criminal.” Ken Shuttleworth, a prominent British architect who three decades ago helped design London’s Gherkin building, now says he’d do it differently, without quite as much glass all the way around.
2019年擔任紐約市長時，比爾·德布拉西奧曾短暫提出全面禁止「玻璃和鋼鐵」結構的想法。寫過一本關於可持續高層建築書籍的澳洲建築學者菲利普·奧德菲爾德稱，玻璃摩天樓「在環境上是犯罪」。30年前曾參與設計倫敦「小黃瓜」大樓的英國著名建築師肯·沙烏地阿拉伯爾斯沃思表示，現在他會採用不同方案，不會四周都使用那麼多玻璃。
“You really just need to have smaller windows,” said Mr. Shuttleworth. He said his approach to skyscraper design has changed as he’s come to realize that “we are burning a hole in the planet.”
「你其實只需要把窗戶做小一些，」沙烏地阿拉伯爾斯沃思說。他說，隨著他逐漸意識到「我們正在把地球燒出一個洞」，他對摩天大樓設計的思路已經改變。
Despite that criticism, the glass skyscraper’s appeal endures.
儘管有這些批評，玻璃摩天大樓的魅力依然不減。
The form, which originated in American cities like Chicago and New York, has now spread across China, the Middle East and Southeast Asia. Over the past decade, the world has constructed 25 times the number of buildings 200 meters (roughly 655 feet) or taller that it did in the 1980s, according to the Council on Tall Buildings and Urban Habitat. Some 91 percent of the world’s skyscrapers of that height have been built since the turn of the 21st century.
這種形式起源於芝加哥和紐約等美國城市，如今已傳播到中國、中東和東南亞。根據高層建築與城市人居委員會的數據，過去十年，全球建造的200米或更高建築數量是1980年代的25倍。世界上約91%的這一高度摩天大樓是在進入21世紀以來建造的。
The “vast majority” have facades largely of glass, said Antony Wood, who was the president of that council until last year.
該委員會任期至去年的前主席安東尼·伍德說，「絕大多數」建築的外立面主要是玻璃。
廣告
Proponents of glass-dominant design say this building spree can still be responsible if strategic choices are made.
支持玻璃為主設計方案的人士認為，只要做出戰略選擇，這種建築熱潮仍然可以做到環保。
Because the sun doesn’t hit a building equally from all directions, buildings can boost their energy efficiency simply with the right orientation. Architects can also add features like fins or undulations so less sunlight directly hits the glass. Stringing greenery on glass facades is also a trend.
因為太陽並非從所有方向均勻照射建築，建築只需通過合理的朝向設計，就能提高能源效率。建築師還可以添加翅片或起伏等特徵，減少陽光直射玻璃。在玻璃外立面上布置綠植也是一種趨勢。
Glass can also be treated with gossamer-thin metallic coatings that reflect away radiation. Most buildings use several layers of glass to impede the heat. One high-end option involves sandwiching automated blinds between them.
玻璃還可以塗覆薄如蛛絲的金屬塗層，以反射輻射熱。大多數建築使用多層玻璃來阻擋熱量。一種高端選擇是在玻璃之間夾入自動百葉窗。
“It’s not as simple as people think it is,” said Gordon Gill, an architect at Adrian Smith + Gordon Gill. He has helped design numerous skyscrapers, including a 1,000-meter tower in Saudi Arabia that will be the world’s tallest once completed. “I’m not a big fan of, ‘Aha, we found the answer: We’re just going to use less glass.’ I don’t believe in panaceas. Your entire process is about balance.”
「這並不像人們想的那麼簡單，」阿德里安·史密斯+戈登·吉爾建築事務所的建築師戈登·吉爾說。他幫助設計了眾多摩天大樓，包括沙烏地阿拉伯一座1000米高的塔樓，建成後將成為世界最高建築。「我不太贊同那種『啊哈，我們找到答案了：少用點玻璃就行』的想法。我不相信萬靈藥。你的整個過程都關乎平衡。」
Most studies conclude that cutting down on glazing, as glass is sometimes called, does help reduce energy for cooling. A 2019 analysis of 1,000 American office buildings found that structures with larger window ratios had higher air-conditioning loads — though window size didn’t predict a building’s overall energy use. A research paper from Saudi Arabia concluded that glazing “should be minimized in hot regions,” with windows ideally amounting to 20 to 30 percent of the facade.
大多數研究得出結論，減少玻璃確實有助於降低冷卻能耗。2019年對1000棟美國辦公樓的分析發現，窗戶比例越大的建築，空調負荷越高——儘管窗戶大小並不能預測建築的整體能耗。沙烏地阿拉伯的一篇研究論文得出結論，在炎熱地區「應盡量減少」玻璃的使用，理想情況下，窗戶應占外立面的20%到30%。
But skyscrapers, of course, are so much more than objects meant to maximize efficiency. Big windows let in natural light, which limits the need for artificial lighting.
但摩天大樓當然不僅僅是為了最大限度提高能效而存在的。大窗戶能引入自然光，從而減少對人工照明的需求。
They also help units sell.
它們也有助於房屋銷售。
Ms. Zeeman, for example, had looked at seven or eight properties before walking into Oxley Towers, one of which is Kuala Lumpur’s sixth-tallest building. She’d moved from the Dutch countryside with her husband, who’d gotten an engineering job in the city. They saw the panorama and paid over their budget.
例如，齊曼女士在走進豪利大廈之前看過七八處房產，其中一棟是吉隆坡第六高的建築。她和丈夫從荷蘭鄉村搬來，丈夫在這座城市找到了一份工程工作。他們看到全景後，以超出預算的價格買下了房子。
“To wake up with this view, it’s a completely different mood,” she said. “It inspires me to do more.”
「每天清晨醒來就能看到這樣的景色，心情完全不一樣，」她說。「它激勵我做更多事情。」

‘Beautiful to the eye’
「賞心悅目」
Glass has helped give Malaysia the very thing its prime minister said he wanted three decades ago: buildings that evoke development and are “beautiful to the eye.”
玻璃幫助馬來西亞實現了其總理30年前期盼的目標：既象徵著發展又「賞心悅目」的建築。
The country started in 1996 by completing the twin Petronas Towers, the tallest buildings in the world at the time. Then it kept going, constructing hundreds more skyscrapers — including two taller than Petronas. Malaysia, with just 35 million people, now has more buildings 200 meters or higher than any country but China, the United States and the United Arab Emirates.
馬來西亞於1996年建成了雙子塔，當時它們是世界最高建築。從那以後，它繼續建造了數百座摩天大樓，其中包括兩座比雙子塔更高的建築。馬來西亞只有3500萬人口，如今其200米及以上高層建築數量僅次於中國、美國和阿聯酋，位居世界第四。
Some of them, particularly ones with top-end technology, have good energy efficiency numbers, said Sarly Adre Sarkum, an architect and the chief executive of Malaysia’s Green Building Index. But Mr. Sarkum said the overall quality of the towers is “still not so good.” He said that in Malaysia’s climate, extensive glass facades carry “significant energy consequences.”
其中一些建築，尤其是採用尖端技術的建築，能源效率指標相當不錯，馬來西亞綠色建築指數首席執行官、建築師薩利·阿德雷·薩爾庫姆說。但他指出，這些高樓的整體質量「仍然不太理想」。在馬來西亞的氣候條件下，大面積使用玻璃幕牆會帶來「顯著的能源消耗問題」。
Roughly 60 percent of the building energy use in Malaysia stems from air-conditioning, Mr. Sarkum said. “When buyers purchase these, they think they get great views,” Mr. Sarkum said. “But you need to keep the curtains closed.”
薩爾庫姆說，馬來西亞約60%的建築能耗來自空調。「買房的人覺得，這樣可以欣賞到絕佳的風景，」他說。「但實際上，你得一直拉著窗簾。」
The Oxley Towers, where Ms. Zeeman lives, represents one of the city’s heaviest uses of glass. Even the bathrooms next to the gym have floor-to-ceiling windows.
齊曼女士居住的奧克斯利大廈是這座城市玻璃使用量最大的建築之一。甚至健身房旁邊的洗手間也有落地窗。
廣告
The Oxley was designed by a firm founded by David Mizan Hashim, a self-described “idealist” who obtained his master’s degree from Harvard in 1986 and launched his company, Veritas, soon after. Veritas says it accepts only projects that aim for green certifications.
奧克斯利大廈由戴維·米贊·哈希姆創立的公司設計。哈希姆自稱「理想主義者」，1986年在哈佛獲得碩士學位，不久後創立了Veritas。該公司表示，只承接以獲得綠色建築認證為目標的項目。
But Dr. Hashim acknowledged that his idealism doesn’t always play out, given the trade-offs over aesthetics and performance. “This is the struggle of my life,” he said.
但哈希姆承認，考慮到建築美觀與性能之間需要作出取捨，他的理想主義並不總能付諸實踐。「這是我一生都在應對的難題，」他說。
For one thing, developers of residential high rises don’t wind up paying the energy bills. “So there is less of an incentive for them to go all-out,” Dr. Hashim said.
其中一個問題是，住宅高樓的開發商最終並不需要支付能源帳單。「因此，他們缺乏全力提高能效的動力，」哈希姆說。
The Singapore-based company that developed Oxley said in a sustainability report that it works “continuously” to improve the efficiency of its properties, and uses energy-efficient air-conditioning systems and motion-sensor lighting where possible. It says Oxley Towers have “high-quality glazing” that “reduces the need for extensive air-conditioning.”
開發奧克斯利大廈的新加坡公司在一份可持續發展報告中表示，公司「持續」致力於提高旗下物業的能源效率，並在條件允許的情況下使用節能空調系統和感應式照明。公司稱，奧克斯利大廈採用了「高品質玻璃」，能夠「減少對大量使用空調的需求」。
Even so, Dr. Hashim said, the building is “not as cutting-edge as it could be.” That’s because the development company wanted a relatively unadorned look, which removed the possibility of adding shading features. “That was an aesthetic choice,” Dr. Hashim said.
即便如此，哈希姆說，這棟建築「沒有達到它本可以有的前沿水平」。這是因為開發單位想要相對樸素的外觀，因此無法增設遮陽設施。「那是出於美觀作出的選擇，」哈希姆說。
The development company, Oxley Holdings Limited, did not respond to a request for comment.
開發公司豪利控股沒有回應置評請求。
廣告
The towers will likely obtain a silver rating from Malaysia’s rating index, the third-highest level, he said, though Dr. Hashim had hoped it could be gold. Ultimately, he said, “it is a building that is being sold.”
哈希姆說，這幾棟大樓很可能獲得馬來西亞綠色建築評級體系的銀級認證，這是該體系的第三高等級，儘管他原本希望能達到金級。歸根結底，他說，「這是一棟要拿來賣的樓」。
The power of air-conditioning
空調如何改變一切
The glass skyscraper became viable only with the advent of air-conditioning.
直到空調問世，玻璃摩天大樓才成為可能。
Before then, high rises were built primarily out of heavy masonry, with windows as little cutouts. That aesthetic now looks old-fashioned. But in a 2023 study comparing the energy efficiency of nine New York City high rises, the oldest skyscraper — the Chrysler Building, constructed in 1929 — was by far the best performer.
在那之前，高層建築主要用厚重的磚石建造，窗戶只是牆面上一個個小小的開口。這種建築風格如今看起來已經過時。然而，2023年的一項研究比較了紐約市九棟高層建築的能源效率，結果發現，其中歷史最悠久的摩天大樓——建於1929年的克萊斯勒大廈——能效表現遙遙領先。
Even in Malaysia, the first modestly tall buildings looked as if they were in an almost defensive stance against the sun.
即使在馬來西亞，最早的那些不算太高的高層建築看起來也幾乎像是在對太陽嚴防死守。
The 18-story parliament building of the 1960s, Malaysia’s tallest at the time, came wrapped in a honeycomb-like lattice. The Dayabumi Complex, built in the 1980s, had its windows shrouded behind an ornate white veil inspired by Islamic motifs.
1960年代的18層國會大廈是當時馬來西亞最高的建築，外面包裹著蜂窩狀格柵。1980年代建成的達雅布米綜合體，其窗戶隱藏在受伊斯蘭圖案啟發的華麗白色遮擋物後面。
Stellios Plainiotis, the chief executive of a facade consulting company in Kuala Lumpur, said Malaysia was “effectively experimenting with what a skyscraper for the tropics should look like.” Some of those ideas now feel “remarkably current,” he said.
吉隆坡一家外立面諮詢公司首席執行官斯特利奧斯·普萊尼奧蒂斯說，馬來西亞「實際上一直在試驗熱帶摩天大樓應該是什麼樣子」。他說，其中一些想法現在感覺「非常入時」。
Critics of the all-glass approach say air-conditioning excused architects to ignore other design lessons about contending with heat. Those include techniques honed over centuries in warm environments, such as the white, sun-reflective facades of Greece or the open, traditional homes of Southeast Asia.
全玻璃的批評者說，空調讓建築師有藉口忽視其他應對高溫的設計經驗。這些經驗包括數百年在炎熱環境中磨練出來的技術，比如希臘白色的反光外立面，或東南亞的開放式傳統住宅。
“Our ancestors, they didn’t go to school. And yet they could build buildings that respond to the climate,” said Abdul Razak Bin Sapian, a professor of architecture at the International Islamic University Malaysia.
「我們的祖先沒有上過學。但他們能建造適應氣候的建築，」馬來西亞國際伊斯蘭大學建築學教授阿卜杜勒·拉扎克·本·薩皮安說。
One such example can be seen from the upper floors of the Oxley: a neighborhood, just a half-mile away, that looks from above like a toy village. Surrounded by high rises, it is an island of one- and two-story homes, preserved almost by accident because of colonial-era laws that created an enclave for ethnic Malay ownership.
從豪利大廈的高處可以看到一個這樣的例子：半英里外的一個街區，從上面看像玩具村莊。它被高樓環繞，是一兩層住宅組成的島嶼，幾乎像是偶然保留下來的，因為殖民時代的法律為馬來族所有權創造了一塊飛地。
That has left the neighborhood, Kampung Baru, as an architectural time capsule. People hang out in open-air restaurants under ceiling fans, and many homes have large overhangs that ward off the sun. Small windows are covered by horizontal louvers.
這使甘榜巴魯街區成為一個建築時間膠囊。人們在吊扇下的露天餐廳消遣，許多住宅有寬大的屋檐遮陽。小窗戶被水平百葉窗覆蓋。
One of the oldest homes in the neighborhood belongs to Jamiyah Kesot, 71. Constructed of dark timber with a high-pitched roof, it relies on the cooling features from a different time. It is built on stilts, allowing airflow from below. Ms. Kesot’s monthly energy bill is around $15. She uses two floor fans and no air-conditioning.
該街區最古老的住宅之一屬於71歲的賈米亞·凱索特。這棟房子由深色木材建造，屋頂高聳，依賴的是另一個時代的降溫特性。房子有高腳，讓空氣從下方流通。凱索特每月的能源帳單約15美元。她使用兩台落地扇，不用空調。
“I don’t need it,” said Ms. Kesot, a grandmother of seven who worked on a Texas Instruments assembly line for 37 years before retiring in 2011.
「我不需要空調，」凱索特說。她有七個孫輩，曾在得克薩斯儀器公司裝配線上工作37年，2011年退休。
Most days last summer, the outdoor temperature rose into the 90s. And Ms. Kesot’s home, at least to an unacclimated visitor, still felt punishingly humid. But one afternoon a thunderstorm rolled in. Wind rippled through the louvers, flapping the curtains, and the home filled with fresh air.
去年夏天大多數日子，室外溫度在攝氏32度以上。凱索特的家，至少對一個不適應的訪客來說，仍然感覺悶熱難耐。但一天下午，一場雷暴襲來。風穿過百葉窗，吹動窗簾，家中充滿新鮮空氣。
From her living room, as the thunder crackled, Ms. Kesot had no panoramic view of the soaring buildings all around her or the dramatic skies about Kuala Lumpur. For that, she’d have to go outside.
在客廳裡，雷聲噼啪作響，凱索特沒有周圍高聳建築的那種全景視野，也看不到吉隆坡那動人心魄的天空。要看的話，她得走到外面去。

Chico Harlan是《紐約時報》的全球氣候記者。
