卡尼訪華，中國會是加拿大擺脫美國依賴的答案嗎

Canada’s Leader, Once a Curiosity at Asia Summits, Is Now a Star

IAN AUSTEN • 2026-11-17
The importance of Europe to Prime Minister Mark Carney’s plan to diversify Canada’s economy and politics away from its reliance on the United States was underscored last week by his travel schedule.
上週，馬克·卡尼總理的行程凸顯了歐洲對於他推動加拿大經濟和政治多元化、減少對美國依賴的計劃的重要性。
Mr. Carney added a stop in Dublin, joining a meeting of European leaders before the start of a previously planned trip to Asia.
卡尼在先前計劃的亞洲之行開始前增加了都柏林一站，參加歐洲領導人會議。
Last month Mr. Carney announced that Canada is officially seeking to become the first “associate member” of the European Union. But experts say that Asia offers the greater economic opportunity.
上月，卡尼宣布加拿大正式尋求成為歐盟首個「准成員國」。但專家表示，亞洲提供更大的經濟機遇。
“Prime Minister Carney certainly seems to have a strong focus on Europe, which gives an impression that he is not looking toward Asia,” said Kazuto Suzuki, a professor at the University of Tokyo’s graduate school of public policy. “Nevertheless, if Canada is to avoid dependence on the U.S. market and form a coalition of middle powers, cooperation with Asia is essential.”
「卡尼總理似乎確實非常關注歐洲，這給人一種他不看向亞洲的印象，」東京大學公共政策研究生院教授鈴木一人說。「然而，如果加拿大要避免依賴美國市場並組建中等強國聯盟，與亞洲合作至關重要。」
Last year, Mr. Carney was something of a curiosity at the meetings of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations and the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation forum. But his speech in January at the World Economic Forum in Davos, Switzerland, and President Trump’s addition of energy security threats to his trade war with the world have made Mr. Carney and his energy-rich country a marquee attraction at both.
去年，卡尼在東南亞國家聯盟和亞太經合組織論壇的會議上還是個新鮮面孔。但他今年1月在瑞士達佛斯世界經濟論壇上的演講，以及川普總統將能源安全威脅加入其與世界的貿易戰，使卡尼和他資源豐富的國家成為這兩個場景下備受矚目的焦點。
Before the APEC meeting in Shenzhen, China, this week, Mr. Carney will have his second formal meeting this year with China’s leader, Xi Jinping. And Mr. Carney, who is as fond of free trade agreements as Mr. Trump is of tariffs, announced in Manila before the ASEAN meetings opened that Canada had completed the broad negotiations for a deal with the Philippines. Talks are also underway on a wider trade arrangement with all 11 ASEAN countries.
本週在中國深圳舉行的亞太經合組織會議前，卡尼將與中國領導人習近平舉行今年第二次正式會晤。卡尼對自由貿易協定的熱衷程度恰如川普對關稅的熱衷，在東盟會議開幕前，他在馬尼拉宣布，加拿大已完成為與菲律賓達成協議的廣泛談判。此外，與所有11個東盟國家達成更廣泛貿易安排的談判也在進行中。
On Saturday, Mr. Carney said that at both summits he would press his call for “middle powers” to unite and would present Canada as a reliable alternative to an increasingly unpredictable United States as a supplier of important products like oil and gas.
週六，卡尼表示，在兩個峰會上，他都將呼籲「中等強國」團結起來，並將加拿大塑造成一個可靠的替代選擇——在石油和天然氣等重要產品的供應方面，加拿大可以替代日益難以預測的美國。
“It’s part of our strategy, absolutely, 100 percent,” Mr. Carney said at a news conference after meeting with President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr. of the Philippines. “We’re very transparent about it. It’s a part of our strategy to develop this dense web of connections to make us resilient, to make us more independent, to make us sovereign so we can live our lives as we choose, so we’re not being told what to do by some hegemon or a hyperscaler.”
「這絕對是我們戰略的一部分，百分之百，」卡尼在與菲律賓總統小費迪南德·馬科斯會晤後的新聞發布會上說。「我們對此非常透明。這是我們戰略的一部分——發展這張密集的聯繫網路使我們有韌性，使我們更獨立，使我們擁有主權，以便我們能按自己選擇的方式生活，而不是被某個霸權或超級巨頭指手畫腳。」
“It is to varying degrees part of the strategies of many, many countries,” he added, “and that’s one of the reasons why you’re seeing so much progress.”
「這在不同程度上是許多許多國家戰略的一部分，」他還說，「這也是你們看到如此多進展的原因之一。」
Between the Asia summits, Mr. Carney made a quick stop in Cambodia, which is hosting a meeting of the International Organization of Francophonie, an association that promotes French language and culture. There, he received noticeably more applause than anyone else when the leaders were introduced.
在兩個亞洲峰會之間，卡尼短暫造訪柬埔寨。柬埔寨正在主辦國際法語國家組織會議，該組織致力於推廣法語和法國文化。會上，在領導人介紹環節，他獲得的掌聲明顯比其他任何人都多。
During the APEC meeting, however, Mr. Carney will come up hard against superpower politics. Mr. Xi will host a meeting with President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia and Mr. Trump. Mr. Putin’s appearance, after he skipped last year’s APEC meeting in South Korea, is a stark contrast with the European Political Community summit in Dublin. There, Mr. Carney and his European counterparts reaffirmed their unqualified support for Ukraine at a meeting also attended by its leader, President Volodymyr Zelensky.
然而，在亞太經合組織會議期間，卡尼將直面超級大國政治的嚴峻考驗。習近平主席將主持一場由俄羅斯總統普丁和川普共同出席的會議。普丁缺席了去年韓國亞太經合組織會議，他此次出席將使這次會議與都柏林歐洲政治共同體峰會形成鮮明對比。在都柏林峰會上，卡尼及其歐洲同行們重申了對烏克蘭的無條件支持，烏克蘭領導人澤連斯基總統也出席了該會議。
Mr. Carney said he would pose for a group photograph of the APEC leaders with Mr. Putin “out of respect for the host.”
卡尼說，他將「出於對東道主的尊重」，與普丁一起參加亞太經合組織領導人合影。
But that appears to be Mr. Carney’s limit. He again noted on Saturday that Canada would bar Russian and Belarusian teams from entering the country for the Men’s World Junior Hockey Championship at the end of the year.
但這似乎是卡尼的極限。週六他再次指出，加拿大將禁止俄羅斯和白俄羅斯隊入境參加年底的男子世界青年冰球錦標賽。
廣告
“Canada stands firmly with Ukraine, firmly with democracy, territorial integrity, rule of law,” he said. “All of those put us on the other side of Vladimir Putin’s Russia.”
「加拿大堅定地與烏克蘭站在一起，堅定地與民主、領土完整、法治站在一起，」他說。「所有這些都使我們站在弗拉基米爾·普丁的俄羅斯的對立面。」
Canada’s relationship with China, the biggest potential market for replacing trade with the United States, was sulfurous before Mr. Carney, a former governor of the Bank of Canada and the Bank of England, entered politics last year.
在曾任加拿大央行行長和英格蘭銀行行長的卡尼去年進入政壇之前，加拿大與中國的關係一直十分緊張，中國是取代與美國貿易的最大潛在市場。
It collapsed after China arrested two Canadians in what was widely seen in Canada as hostage-taking in retaliation for Canada’s arrest of a Chinese telecommunications executive on a U.S. extradition request. An inquiry also concluded that China had tried to interfere in Canadian politics.
在中國逮捕兩名加拿大公民後，兩國關係崩潰。加拿大普遍認為這是人質扣押行為，是對加拿大應美國引渡請求逮捕一名中國電信行業高管的報復。一項調查還得出結論，中國試圖干預加拿大政治。
Polls suggest that Canadians’ good will toward China has risen since Mr. Carney re-established relations. At the same time, their views of the United States under Mr. Trump, who started a trade war and continues to suggest that Canada should be the 51st American state, have moved decidedly in the other direction.
民調顯示，自卡尼重建關係以來，加拿大人對中國的好感有所上升。與此同時，他們對川普治下美國的看法明顯朝相反方向轉變——川普發動貿易戰，並不斷提出加拿大應成為美國第51個州。
But Mr. Carney will be limited in how far he can go with China. A poll by the nonprofit Angus Reid Institute found that only 14 percent of Canadians favor closer trade ties with China. By contrast, 58 percent of respondents want to increase trade ties with Europe.
但卡尼與中國交往的程度將受到限制。非營利機構安格斯·里德研究所的一項民調發現，只有14%的加拿大人支持與中國建立更密切的貿易關係。相比之下，58%的受訪者希望增加與歐洲的貿易關係。
In January, after meeting with Mr. Xi, Mr. Carney predicted that Chinese automakers would make substantial investments in Canada within three years.
今年1月與習近平會晤後，卡尼預測，中國汽車製造商將在三年內在加拿大進行大量投資。
廣告
On Saturday, however, he was more circumspect. While the government wants “a competitive Canadian auto sector that produces good-paying jobs, good union jobs for Canadian workers,” he said, it also wants Canadians to have access to affordable electric cars.
然而，週六他表現得更為謹慎。他說，雖然政府希望「一個有競爭力的加拿大汽車行業，為加拿大工人提供高薪工作、良好的工會工作」，但也希望加拿大人能買得起價格可負擔的電動汽車。
“There are multiple variables, and there’s multiple ways to achieve those objectives,” he said. “It depends partially on — and importantly on — where the U.S. trading relationship settles out.”
「有多個變量，也有多種方式實現這些目標，」他說。「這部分取決於——而且重要的是取決於——與美國的貿易關係最終如何落定。」
