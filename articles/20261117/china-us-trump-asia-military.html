川普的對華豪賭正適得其反

Trump’s China Gamble Is Backfiring

MIRA RAPP-HOOPER, ELY RATNER • 2026-11-17
Since returning to the White House, President Trump has gambled that if Washington took a more accommodating stance toward China’s ambitions in Asia, Beijing would respond with restraint.
自重返白宮以來，川普總統一直抱著這樣一種僥倖心理：如果華盛頓對中國在亞洲的野心採取更包容的立場，北京方面就會以克制作為回應。
The Trump administration has scaled back key military operations in the region and withheld support for Taiwan and U.S. allies facing Chinese coercion. The hope has been that such measures would help avert a potential clash with China. But nearly two years into his second term, Mr. Trump’s approach is backfiring, emboldening China to throw its weight around Asia and raising the risk of a military crisis.
川普政府縮減了在亞太地區的關鍵軍事行動，並對面臨中國脅迫的台灣及其他美國盟友拒絕提供支持。其初衷是，此類舉措將有助於避免與中國發生潛在衝突。但第二任期已近兩年，川普的做法正在適得其反，不僅讓中國在亞洲更加肆無忌憚，還增加了爆發軍事危機的風險。
The Democratic victory in the U.S. midterm elections offers a critical opportunity for change. With control of both chambers of Congress, Democrats will have the power to put guardrails around the president’s failing China policy — and they may not have to do it alone.
民主黨在美國期中選舉中的勝利為改變現狀提供了關鍵契機。憑藉對國會兩院的控制權，民主黨人將有能力為總統失敗的對華政策設置護欄，而且他們或許不必孤軍奮戰。
For years, Republicans in Congress were among the strongest voices for standing up to Beijing, yet many have stayed quiet to avoid challenging the president. The unmistakable rebuke that voters delivered to Mr. Trump this month may change that calculus by giving Republicans more leeway to work with Democrats toward a course correction on China.
多年來，國會共和黨人一直是主張對抗北京的最強烈聲音之一，然而為了避免挑戰總統，許多人保持了沉默。選民本月對川普發出的明確斥責可能會改變這一考量，讓共和黨人有更多與民主黨人合作以糾正對華路線的餘地。
廣告
The new Congress must move quickly to assert its constitutional power over spending, oversight and confirmation of presidential appointments to limit the damage the administration can do in its final two years and build an approach to Asia that allies can count on and that Beijing has to take seriously.
新一屆國會必須迅速採取行動，行使憲法賦予其在支出、監督和總統任命確認方面的權力，以限制政府在最後兩年任期內可能造成的破壞，並制定出一套讓盟友能夠依賴、也讓北京必須認真對待的亞洲政策。
Breaking with past administrations, the president has played down the notion of strategic competition with China in favor of what both Beijing and Washington now describe as a “constructive relationship of strategic stability.” That shift will frame the conversation during a series of summits in Asia that Mr. Trump will attend this week, including a meeting of Southeast Asian leaders in Manila, followed by a wider Asia-Pacific gathering in China where he is expected to meet President Xi Jinping for the third time this year.
與歷屆政府不同，總統淡化了對華戰略競爭的概念，轉而支持目前北京與華盛頓雙方都稱為「建設性戰略穩定關係」的主張。這一轉變將為本週川普出席的亞洲一系列峰會定下基調，其中包括在馬尼拉舉行的東南亞領導人會議，以及隨後在中國舉行的一場更廣泛的亞太地區會議——預計他將在會上與習近平主席進行今年的第三次會晤。
China’s leaders have long made clear that they want the United States to keep its warships away from China’s coast, end arms sales to Taiwan, leave Beijing’s expansive maritime claims unchallenged, pull American forces from the region and refrain from siding with allies who cross China.
中國領導人長期以來一直明確表示，希望美國軍艦遠離中國海岸，停止對台軍售，不對北京廣泛的海洋主張提出挑戰，從該地區撤出美國軍隊，並避免與觸怒中國的盟友站在一起。
Over the past two years, the Trump administration has granted much of that.
過去兩年中，川普政府滿足了其中的大部分要求。
It has canceled or downgraded defense talks with Taiwan and is holding up a $14 billion arms package for the island. The U.S. Navy is on track to make only half as many publicly announced transits through the Taiwan Strait as it did during Mr. Trump’s first term and has reported fewer freedom of navigation patrols in the South China Sea — activities that have long been important in signaling America’s commitment to regional security and challenging China’s spurious maritime claims. Mr. Trump’s open-ended war with Iran also has played into Beijing’s hands by drawing U.S. missile defenses, Marines and aircraft carriers out of the Indo-Pacific region to support the Middle East conflict.
政府取消或降級了與台灣的防務會談，並擱置了對該島價值140億美元的武器援助計劃。美國海軍目前公開宣布穿越台灣海峽的次數僅為川普第一任期內的一半，其報告在南海進行的「航行自由」巡邏次數也有所減少——長期以來，這些活動一直是展示美國對地區安全承諾以及挑戰中國站不住腳的海洋主張的重要信號。川普與伊朗之間無休止的戰爭也正中北京下懷，這場衝突將美國的導彈防禦系統、海軍陸戰隊和航空母艦調離了印太地區，以支援中東衝突。
The White House seems to believe that keeping tensions with China in check will buy time for America to reduce its dependence on Chinese rare earths, rebuild the U.S. defense industrial base and manage the war with Iran.
白宮似乎相信，控制與中國的緊張局勢將為美國贏得時間，以減少對中國稀土的依賴、重建美國國防工業基礎，並應對與伊朗的戰爭。
廣告
Yet Beijing has continued to press its advantage.
然而，北京方面繼續乘勢施壓。
Last November, China launched a campaign of economic pressure against Japan after Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi suggested her country might intervene in a conflict over Taiwan. In December, China deployed more than 100 naval and coast guard vessels in East Asian waters in a show of force, with Chinese fighters twice locking their targeting radar on Japanese aircraft. That same month, China conducted live-fire military drills closer to Taiwan than ever before, simulating a blockade of the island’s major ports. It has expanded its presence on disputed islands and reefs in the South China Sea and in late September unveiled a new defense outpost in Laos.
去年11月，在日本首相高市早苗暗示可能介入因台灣而起的衝突後，中國對日本發起了一場經濟施壓運動。去年12月，中國在東亞水域部署了100多艘海軍和海警艦艇以展示武力，期間中國戰鬥機兩次使用火控雷達鎖定日本軍機。同月，中國在有史以來最靠近台灣的地方舉行了實彈軍事演習，模擬封鎖該島的主要港口。中國在南海擴大了在爭議島嶼和島礁上的存在，並於9月底在寮國設立了一處新的防務前哨基地。
Past U.S. presidential administrations forcefully challenged Beijing over such encroachments, opposing China’s militarization of the South China Sea, standing by allies under coercion and working to block China’s push for military bases abroad.
美國歷屆政府都曾對此類進犯行為向北京發起過強有力的挑戰，反對中國在南海的軍事化，堅守在受脅迫的盟友身邊，並努力阻止中國在海外建立軍事基地的圖謀。
The Trump administration, on the other hand, has largely stood by. The ill-conceived conflict with Iran has left Americans understandably weary of war, and pulling back from Asia may be tempting. But each time Washington stands aside while Beijing presses an ally, China has more reason to believe it can go further, raising the odds that it will cross a line Washington cannot ignore. And as China’s military acts more aggressively, a single accident at sea or in the air could spark a crisis.
川普政府則在很大程度上袖手旁觀。與伊朗這場考慮不周的衝突已讓美國人對戰爭產生合理的厭倦，因此從亞洲抽身可能頗具誘惑力。但是，每當北京向盟友施壓而華盛頓作壁上觀時，中國就有更多理由相信自己可以得寸進尺，從而增加了其跨越華盛頓無法忽視的底線的機率。此外，隨著中國軍隊的行動日益強勢，海上或空中的單一意外事件就可能引發一場危機。
U.S. presidents retain broad latitude over foreign policy. But Congress’s armed services and foreign affairs committees should hold hearings as soon as possible to press the administration to proceed with the Taiwan arms package and return U.S. forces to Asia from the Middle East. If the administration stonewalls, Congress can hold up spending on other White House priorities. In budget negotiations, lawmakers should lock in more security assistance for Asian partners and accelerate much-needed upgrades to bases in Japan and the Philippines that U.S. forces would rely on in a conflict.
美國總統在外交政策上保留著廣泛的自由裁量權。但國會軍事和外交事務委員會應儘快舉行聽證會，敦促政府推進對台武器援助計劃，並將美軍從中東調回亞洲。如果政府拖延阻撓，國會可以擱置白宮其他優先事項的支出。在預算談判中，立法者應鎖定更多對亞洲夥伴的安全援助，並加快對日本和菲律賓諸多美軍在衝突中依賴的基地進行亟需的升級。
The Senate also can make confirmation of pending and future appointments to the Pentagon and State Department contingent on a commitment to restoring regular U.S. Navy transits of key waterways in the region and supporting allies facing Chinese intimidation. In next year’s annual defense bill, Congress should commit more money to the Pacific Deterrence Initiative, a program it established in 2021 to strengthen America’s military position in the region but which came with no dedicated funding.
參議院還可以要求政府承諾恢復美國海軍在地區關鍵水道的定期航行，並支持面臨中國恐嚇的盟友，以此作為五角大廈和國務院當前及未來任命確認的先決條件。在明年的年度國防法案中，國會應向「太平洋威懾倡議」(Pacific Deterrence Initiative)投入更多資金，國會於2021年設立了這一項目以加強美國在亞太地區的軍事地位，但當時並未撥付專項資金。
廣告
A bipartisan approach is more likely to succeed and endure. Leading Democratic and Republican members of Congress have already called on Mr. Trump to release the stalled Taiwan arms package.
兩黨合作的方法更有可能取得成功並持久有效。國會兩黨的主要領導人已經呼籲川普啟動被擱置的對台武器援助計劃。
The midterms offer a chance to go further in restoring America’s commitment to its allies in Asia and rebuilding an approach to the region that actually keeps the peace.
期中選舉提供了一個機會，可以在恢復美國對亞洲盟友的承諾，以及重建一種真正能夠維護地區和平的政策。

Mira Rapp-Hooper是布魯金斯學會的訪問高級研究員。她此前曾擔任美國國家安全委員會總統特別助理兼東亞和大洋洲高級主任。

Ely Ratner是馬拉松倡議組織的負責人。他此前曾擔任負責印太安全事務的國防部助理部長。
