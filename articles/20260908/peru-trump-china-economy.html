華盛頓希望南美各國疏遠中國，秘魯證明這絕非易事

Peru Shows the Limits of Washington’s Anti-China Push

GENEVIEVE GLATSKY, SARA WAYRA • 2026-09-08
Peru’s ambassador in Washington, Luis Miguel Castilla, received a stern phone call in 2015 from the State Department, after U.S. officials learned that a Chinese firm would finance a sprawling new Pacific Coast port project.
2015年，美國官員獲悉一家中國企業將資助太平洋沿岸一個規模龐大的新港口項目，隨後，秘魯駐華盛頓大使路易斯·米格爾·卡斯蒂利亞接到美國國務院的一通嚴厲電話。
Why, they asked, was Peru handing Beijing a strategic foothold in South America?
他們質問，秘魯為何要將南美洲的一個戰略據點拱手讓給北京？
Mr. Castilla’s answer was blunt: Western investors had already passed.
卡斯蒂利亞的回答很直白：西方投資者早已放棄了這個項目。
The port’s Peruvian private sector promoters “came to knock on doors here and in Europe, and no one opened the door,” Mr. Castilla said. “So, any space left behind is space that someone else takes.”
該港口的秘魯私營部門發起人「曾經來到這裡和歐洲，到處敲門，但沒人開門，」卡斯蒂利亞說。「因此，任何留下的空白，都會被其他人填補。」
廣告
Today, the $1.3 billion Chinese-backed mega-port in Chancay, north of Lima, the capital, has become the most visible symbol of China’s expanding economic presence in Peru.
如今，這座位於首都利馬以北的錢凱、由中國出資13億美元支持的超級港口已成為中國在秘魯不斷擴大的經濟存在的最醒目標誌。
It is also a key reason the country, which Secretary of State Marco Rubio is planning to visit this week, has emerged as a testing ground for one of President Trump’s core objectives in Latin America: rolling back Beijing’s influence in the Western Hemisphere.
這也是該國成為川普總統在拉丁美洲核心目標試驗場的關鍵原因，他旨在削弱北京在西半球的影響力。國務卿魯比奧計劃本週訪問秘魯。
Senior administration officials have warned while offering no evidence that Chinese-financed projects threaten Peru’s sovereignty and, in the case of the port, could give Beijing a military foothold.
美國政府高級官員警告稱（儘管未提供任何證據），中國融資的項目威脅到秘魯的主權，並且該港口可能會給北京提供一個軍事立足點。
But in Peru, Washington’s pressure collides with a stubborn reality. China has become Peru’s largest trading partner and a major investor in mining, energy and infrastructure — a relationship that has helped underpin one of South America’s most resilient economies.
但在秘魯，華盛頓的施壓與一個難以改變的現實發生了碰撞。中國已成為秘魯最大的貿易夥伴，也是採礦、能源和基礎設施領域的主要投資者——這種關係幫助支撐了南美洲最具韌性的經濟體之一。
Even the inauguration in July of President Keiko Fujimori, a staunch conservative who has pledged closer security cooperation with Washington, is unlikely to deliver the anti-China shift the Trump administration seeks.
即便是今年7月就職的總統藤森慶子——一位堅定的保守派，承諾加強與華盛頓的安全合作——也不太可能帶來川普政府所尋求的對華政策轉向。
She has given no indication she will scale back ties to China. In her first speech to Congress, Ms. Fujimori described Peru as a “bridge” between Latin American and Asia.
她並未表現出將縮減與中國的關係的跡象。在對國會的首次演講中，藤森慶子將秘魯描述為連接拉丁美洲與亞洲的「橋樑」。
Over the past decade, Peru has cycled through nine presidents, four impeachments, repeated waves of mass protests, one attempted self-coup and countless corruption scandals.
在過去十年裡，秘魯經歷了九任總統、四次彈劾、反覆爆發的大規模抗議浪潮、一次未遂的自發政變，以及無數的腐敗醜聞。
Yet it has maintained steady economic growth, low inflation and strong investor confidence, avoiding the debt crises and sharp downturns that have plagued several of its neighbors.
然而，該國卻保持了穩定的經濟增長、低通膨和強勁的投資者信心，避免了困擾多個鄰國的債務危機和急劇衰退。
The benefits, however, have been uneven. Millions of Peruvians still live in poverty and rely on underfunded schools and health care.
不過，這些紅利並未得到均衡分配。數以百萬計的秘魯人仍然生活在貧困之中，依賴資金不足的學校和醫療保健服務。
廣告
Experts attribute Peru’s economic stability largely to an independent central bank and successive governments that left economic policy to career economists, insulating it from politics.
專家們認為，秘魯的經濟穩定主要歸功於獨立的中央銀行，以及歷屆政府將經濟政策交由職業經濟學家負責，使其免受政治的干擾。
In addition, China has become a crucial pillar of Peru’s economy. When the 2008 financial crisis cratered U.S. and European demand for minerals, China’s construction boom continued absorbing Peruvian copper and iron. China eventually surpassed the United States as Peru’s largest trading partner and today buys roughly a third of the country’s exports.
此外，中國已成為秘魯經濟的關鍵支柱。當2008年金融危機導致美國和歐洲對礦產的需求暴跌時，中國的建築熱潮繼續吸收著秘魯的銅和鐵。中國最終超越美國成為秘魯最大的貿易夥伴，目前購買了該國約三分之一的出口產品。
“China has been the perfect partner during these years because they bought everything,” said Eduardo Dargent, a Peruvian political analyst.
「這些年來，中國一直是一個完美的合作夥伴，因為他們什麼都買，」秘魯政治分析師愛德華多·達根特表示。
Beyond trade, Chinese firms have steadily expanded into sectors Western businesses have retreated from — Chinese state-owned enterprises have bought Peruvian infrastructure companies from U.S. and European firms. Today, Chinese companies control Lima’s electricity distribution system and play a role in transportation, banking and telecommunications.
在貿易之外，中國企業還穩步進軍西方企業已經撤出的領域——中國國有企業從美國和歐洲公司手中收購了秘魯的基礎設施公司。如今，中國公司控制著利馬的配電系統，並在交通、銀行和電信領域發揮著作用。
Experts describe China as a patient partner, with state-owned firms that often think on longer timelines than private Western companies under pressure to deliver quarterly profits to stockholders. President Xi Jinping of China inaugurated the port in November 2024, calling it “a new Asia-Latin America land-sea corridor for the new era.”
專家們將中國描述為一個耐心的合作夥伴，其國有企業往往著眼於更長遠的時間跨度，而受制於向股東交付季度利潤壓力的西方私營企業則不然。中國國家主席習近平於2024年11月為該港口揭幕，稱其為「新時代亞拉陸海新通道」。
To Washington, however, China’s presence represents something ominous. U.S. officials have repeatedly warned that the Chancay port could eventually provide access for the Chinese navy. After it opened, a Trump administration envoy proposed a 60 percent tariff on cargo routed through Chinese-controlled ports in Latin America, though it has not come to pass.
然而，對華盛頓而言，中國的存在代表著某種不祥之兆。美國官員曾多次警告說，錢凱港最終可能會為中國海軍提供通道。該港口開放後，川普政府的一名特使提議，對通過中國控制的拉美港口運輸的貨物徵收60%的關稅，儘管這一提議並未實現。
“China’s investment advances the Chinese Communist Party’s political agenda abroad, often at the expense of the receiving nation,” the State Department said in a statement. “Through President Trump’s leadership, the United States is working to address risks posed to the security and prosperity of our hemisphere.”
美國國務院在一份聲明中表示：「中國的投資在海外推進了中國共產黨的政治議程，往往以犧牲接受國為代價。在川普總統的領導下，美國正致力於應對對我們半球安全與繁榮構成的風險。」
Diplomatic friction escalated this year after a legal dispute erupted over what authority Peru’s transportation regulator maintained over the privately operated port.
今年，圍繞秘魯交通監管機構對這家私營港口擁有何種管轄權爆發了一場法律糾紛，導致外交摩擦升級。
廣告
“Peru could be powerless to oversee Chancay,” which is controlled by “predatory Chinese owners,” the State Department wrote on X in February. “Let this be a cautionary tale for the region and the world: cheap Chinese money costs sovereignty.”
「秘魯可能無力監管錢凱港，」該港口由「掠奪性的中國所有者」控制，美國國務院今年2月在X平台上寫道。「願這成為該地區乃至全世界的警示：廉價的中國資金要以主權為代價。」
But Peruvian officials say the port is regulated by the port authority, police, customs, transportation ministry and others.
但秘魯官員表示，該港口受到港務局、警察、海關、交通部及其他部門的監管。
“We face constant and meticulous oversight,” said Paola Fune, who oversees government affairs for the port.
「我們面臨著持續且細緻的監管，」負責該港口政府事務的保拉·富內表示。
The Chancay port, operated by the Chinese state-owned shipping giant COSCO, has reduced shipping times to China from as many as 40 days to 23, port officials say, and is expected to become a major trade hub, increasing Peru’s integration with Asian markets.
港口官員表示，由中國國有航運巨頭中國遠洋海運運營的錢凱港將通往中國的航運時間從長達40天縮短至23天，預計這裡將成為一個主要的貿易樞紐，從而加深秘魯與亞洲市場的融合。
The port features autonomous cranes, driverless trucks, remote-controlled cargo operations and a mile-long tunnel routing container traffic beneath the city.
該港口配備了自動化起重機、無人駕駛卡車、遠程控制的貨物作業系統，以及一條長約1.6公里、在城市地下引導集裝箱交通的隧道。
Hotels and restaurants are rapidly expanding in Chancay, while universities and health care networks have begun buying land for satellite campuses and offices.
在錢凱，酒店和餐館正在迅速擴張，而大學和醫療網路也已開始購買土地，用於建設衛星校區和辦事處。
Peru’s political and technocratic elite view the port as a major win for the country and bristle at being told by the Trump administration how to manage their commercial relationships.
秘魯的政治和技術官僚高層將該港口視為國家的重大勝利，並對川普政府指手畫腳、干涉其商業關係管理的方式感到不滿。
廣告
“They’ve been taking potshots at it, but they can’t make it stick,” said Cynthia Sanborn, a leading China scholar at Lima’s Universidad del Pacífico. “Peruvians are really proud of that port.”
「他們一直在對它進行明槍暗箭的攻擊，但無法讓這些指責成立，」利馬太平洋大學著名中國問題學者辛西婭·桑伯恩表示。「秘魯人對那個港口感到非常自豪。」
“The U.S. comes along and says, ‘Oh, you’re losing your sovereignty.’ That’s very paternalistic,” Ms. Sanborn said.
「美國跑過來說，『哦，你們正在喪失主權。』這很有大家長作風，」桑伯恩說。
Criticism from Washington rings hollow, analysts say, because many Peruvians see a stark contrast between Chinese and American engagement.
分析人士指出，華盛頓的批評顯得蒼白無力，因為許多秘魯人看到了中美兩國在參與方式上的鮮明對比。
While China has poured money into ports, mines and electricity infrastructure, U.S. engagement has focused on security projects paid for by Peru, including a $1.5 billion modernization of Peru’s naval base and the purchase of American F-16 fighter jets.
當中國大量注資於港口、礦山和電力基礎設施時，美國的參與卻集中在由秘魯自己買單的安全項目上，包括耗資15億美元的秘魯海軍基地現代化改造，以及採購美國F-16戰鬥機。
“The Chinese are investing in Peru and Peru is giving its money to the U.S.,” Ms. Sanborn said.
「中國人在秘魯投資，而秘魯則把錢交給了美國，」桑伯恩說。
U.S. pressure to buy 24 F-16’s provoked widespread criticism over the $3.5 billion cost and the strain on public resources.
美國施壓要求秘魯購買24架F-16戰鬥機的舉動引發了對其35億美元高昂成本及對公共資源造成壓力的廣泛批評。
廣告
“Aren’t there other, more important uses for that money than buying fighter jets?” said Mr. Castilla, now a senior visiting fellow at the London School of Economics.
「比起購買戰鬥機，這筆錢難道沒有其他更重要的用途嗎？」卡斯蒂利亞說，他現在是倫敦政治經濟學院的高級訪問學者。
The tension is visible in the combative posture of the U.S. ambassador, Bernie Navarro, whose sharp rhetoric has alienated Peru’s political class. When it briefly seemed the fighter jet deal would fall through, Mr. Navarro wrote on X that Washington would use “every available tool” to promote its interests “if you deal with the U.S. in bad faith.”
這種緊張局勢在美國大使伯尼·納瓦羅好鬥的姿態中清晰可見，他尖銳的言辭使秘魯政界感到疏遠。當戰鬥機的交易一度看似要泡湯時，納瓦羅在X平台上寫道：「如果你們和美國打交道時不懷好意」，華盛頓將動用「一切可用工具」來推進自身利益。
Across Latin America, Chinese firms have financed more than 300 infrastructure projects worth over $130 billion, and increasingly dominate sectors like electronics and electric vehicles, said Enrique Dussel Peters, an economist at the National Autonomous University of Mexico.
墨西哥國立自治大學經濟學家恩裡克·杜塞爾·彼得斯表示，在整個拉丁美洲，中國企業已資助了300多個基礎設施項目，總價值超過1300億美元，並在電子產品和電動汽車等領域日益佔據主導地位。
Washington has treated trade with China as an ideological question rather than an economic necessity for Latin America, he said, and may not recognize what a gargantuan task it would be to try to reverse China’s “massive presence across the Americas.”
他說，華盛頓將與中國的貿易視為意識型態問題，而不是拉丁美洲的經濟必需，並且可能沒有意識到，試圖扭轉中國「在整個美洲龐大的存在感」將是一項多麼艱巨的任務。
“It is as if people think that by making a decision on a Friday we can just make China disappear by Monday,” Mr. Dussel added.
「就好像人們以為，只要在週五做個決定，到週一我們就能讓中國消失，」杜塞爾還說。
The United States remains a major trading partner for Peru and is the main market for agricultural exports and textiles, sectors that generate far more jobs than some Chinese-dominated industries.
美國仍然是秘魯的主要貿易夥伴，也是其農產品出口和紡織品的主要市場，這些行業創造的就業機會遠遠多於一些由中國主導的行業。
Mr. Castilla said he agreed with longstanding American complaints about smuggling, intellectual property theft and unfair competition from some Chinese firms. But, he added, pressuring countries to choose sides without offering comparable investment alternatives risks alienating allies and pushing countries closer to Beijing.
卡斯蒂利亞表示，他同意美國長期以來對部分中國企業走私、盜竊知識產權和不公平競爭的抱怨。但是，他還說，在不提供同等投資替代方案的情況下，向各國施壓要求選邊站隊可能會疏遠盟友，並將這些國家推向北京。
The United States “is bullying us a bit,” he said.
美國「有點在霸凌我們，」他說。
Yet Latin American leaders — even those closely aligned with Mr. Trump — show no intention of severing lucrative ties to China. They hope their region will benefit from being a battleground for influence between global superpowers.
而拉丁美洲的領導人——甚至那些與川普關係密切的領導人——也絲毫沒有切斷與中國有利可圖之關係的意圖。他們希望自己的地區能從全球超級大國爭奪影響力的角逐場中獲益。
José Tam, a lawyer specializing in Asian investment and former head of the Peruvian-Chinese Chamber of Commerce, said Peru welcomes greater U.S. investment.
專門從事亞洲投資業務的律師、前秘魯中國商會主席譚義勇(José Tam)表示，秘魯歡迎美國增加投資。
“What should not really be our problem is the geopolitical aspect,” he said. “As a developing country, what we need is investment, and it’s all the same to us.”
「地緣政治方面真的不應成為我們的問題，」他說。「作為一個發展中國家，我們需要的是投資，對我們來說，投資都是一樣的。」
