海上航行286天後，5000名美國大兵的「芭提雅假期」

After Months at War, Brief Solace in a Party Town Far From Home

HANNAH BEECH, MUKTITA SUHARTONO • 2026-09-08
After 286 days at sea, the sailors and Marines of the warship Abraham Lincoln savored the pleasures of Pattaya, Thailand. Their indulgences were not all decadent.
在海上航行286天後，亞伯拉罕·林肯號上的水兵和海軍陸戰隊員盡情享受泰國芭堤雅，但他們的享受並非全是放縱。
They ate Double Super Cheeseburgers at Burger King. They shopped for LeBron James jerseys at the mall. They sipped from fresh coconuts at the beach, their backs to the water where they had spent the last nine-plus months.
他們在漢堡王吃雙層超級芝士漢堡，在購物中心選購雷霸龍·詹姆斯球衣，在海灘上啜飲新鮮椰子，背對著他們待了九個多月的大海。
And they danced with scantily clad women at nightclubs, the pounding music seemingly even louder than the aural assault of an aircraft carrier at war.
他們還在夜總會與衣著暴露的女子跳舞，震耳欲聾的音樂似乎比戰時航母的噪音衝擊還要猛烈。
Pattaya is used to welcoming American servicemen. The beach town grew from an R&R refuge for American military personnel during the Vietnam War. For five days beginning on Wednesday, its pool halls, steakhouses and spas, its go-go bars, burrito shacks and trinket stalls have turned their attention to the 5,000 or so service members from the Lincoln, one of four carriers used in the air campaign against Iran.
芭堤雅早已習慣接待美國軍人。這座海灘城市最初是越南戰爭期間美國軍人的休整之地。從週三開始的五天裡，當地的檯球廳、牛排館、水療中心、戈戈舞酒吧、墨西哥卷餅攤和廉價飾品店都把目光投向了林肯號上約5000名軍人，林肯號是對伊朗空襲行動中動用的四艘航母之一。

After a grueling deployment that fed criticism of the Trump administration’s execution of the war, the service members aboard the Lincoln had been instructed not to speak with members of the news media during their time in Pattaya.
在經歷了一次艱苦的部署後，林肯號上的軍人被告知在芭堤雅期間不要接受新聞媒體採訪；他們的這次部署引發了對川普政府戰爭實施方式的批評。
Nevertheless, seven sailors confirmed, on the condition of anonymity, that the carrier’s long months at sea, especially without any of the normal stops at so-called liberty ports, had sapped both supplies and morale. They spoke of clogged toilets and, alarmingly, of multiple suicide attempts. (Navy officials have denied an increase in suicide attempts on the Lincoln.) Amid Iranian missile barrages and drone attacks, the half-day shifts merged into a Sisyphean blur, without the usual relief of care packages from home or treats from the ship store.
儘管如此，七名水兵匿名證實，航母在海上度過的漫長數月——尤其是沒有在「休憩港」進行任何正常停靠——已經耗盡了補給和士氣。他們談到廁所堵塞，還警惕地談到多起自殺未遂事件。（海軍官員否認林肯號上自殺未遂事件有所增加。）在伊朗導彈齊射和無人機襲擊中，原本的半日輪班混成了一團，且沒有了來自家裡的關懷包裹或艦上商店零食帶來的慣常寬慰。
Meals were rationed, they said, and offered up a monotony of carbs. What she was really craving, one sailor said, was a salad, something green and fresh. Was there one to be had in Pattaya? There was.
他們說，餐食實行配給，供應的是一成不變的碳水化合物。一名女水兵說，她真正想吃的是沙拉，一些綠色、新鮮的東西。芭堤雅有嗎？有。
The worst, a sailor from Illinois said, was not knowing how much longer they would be at sea. He stopped counting the days at around 200, despairing that the deployment might never end. Another sailor, a New York native, said that he had gone through a dozen deployments without incident, but this time he had watched junior sailors break, one by one. The price of war, he said, was something he was willing to bear, but not when a logistical crisis, which left the carrier with dwindling supplies, seemed preventable.
來自伊利諾州的一名水兵說，最糟糕的是不知道他們還要在海上待多久。數到大約200天時，他停止計算天數，絕望地認為部署可能永遠不會結束。另一名來自紐約的水兵說，自己經歷過十幾次部署都平安無事，但這一次他看著年輕水兵一個接一個崩潰。他說，戰爭的代價他願意承擔，但一場後勤危機使航母補給日益減少，而且這似乎本可避免，這讓他無法接受。
Did they, he continued — not defining the “they” — think for a moment about the mental health of those aboard the Lincoln? Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth has said that reports of difficult conditions aboard the Lincoln were “misrepresented.” President Trump opined that the Lincoln’s deployment was “not nearly long enough.”
他接著說，他們（沒有說明指誰）是否想過林肯號上那些人的心理健康？國防部長彼特·海格塞思曾表示，有關林肯號上條件艱苦的報導被「歪曲」了。川普總統則認為林肯號的部署「還遠遠不夠久」。
The residents of Pattaya, at least, appeared to understand the hardship borne by the crew of the Lincoln, which will head next toward its home port, San Diego. Pattaya’s mayor turned up to greet the men and women touching dry land for the first time since November. A gem shop offered discounts to the U.S. Navy and Marine Corps personnel. The Sapphire Club, an adult entertainment venue, dispatched three women in strapless black bodysuits to hold placards offering discounts for those with valid U.S. military ID.
至少芭堤雅居民似乎理解林肯號船員承受的艱辛。這艘航母接下來將駛向母港聖地牙哥。芭堤雅市長現身迎接自11月以來首次踏上陸地的男女軍人。一家寶石店向美國海軍和海軍陸戰隊人員提供折扣。成人娛樂場所藍寶石俱樂部派出三名身穿無肩帶黑色連體衣的女性舉著標語牌，向持有有效美國軍人身份證件的人提供折扣。
At the Tahitian Queen, which bills itself as the oldest bar in Pattaya, circa 1978, Mickey Foth, one of the owners and an American military veteran and former Pentagon employee, teared up as he considered the sacrifices made by the Lincoln’s personnel.
在自稱芭堤雅最古老酒吧、約1978年開業的塔希提女王酒吧，老闆之一、美國退伍軍人、前五角大廈僱員米基·福士想到林肯號人員作出的犧牲時熱淚盈眶。
“It’s not just the physical stress, it’s the emotional toll of such a long deployment,” he said, as a line of women in bikinis, one in an American flag pattern, gyrated onstage. “I want to be here, front and center, to say to these men and women, ‘Thank you for your service.’”
「這不僅是身體上的壓力，還有如此長時間部署帶來的情感損耗，」他說，此時一排穿著比基尼的女子在舞台上扭動，其中一人穿著美國國旗圖案。「我想站在這裡，站在最前面最中心的位置，對這些男男女女說：『感謝你們為國效力。』」
On Thursday night, some of the young sailors blushed amid the sexual maelstrom of Pattaya’s Walking Street, with its touts offering intricate shows involving improbably placed pingpong balls. Others, in the throbbing confines of nightclubs, downed shots and leaned close to female companions. Most of the women were Thai, some were Eastern European and Russian, a few were from neighboring countries like Myanmar and Laos or from as far away as Uganda and Uzbekistan.
週四晚上，一些年輕水兵在芭堤雅步行街的色情漩渦中顯得有些靦腆，街上在兜售各種複雜表演，內容包括將乒乓球放在不可思議的位置。還有一些人在夜總會悸動的封閉空間裡灌下烈酒，將身體貼向女性同伴。這些女性大多是泰國人，也有一些來自東歐和俄羅斯，少數來自緬甸、寮國等鄰國，還有烏干達、烏茲別克斯坦等遙遠國家。
Prostitution is illegal in Thailand, but you wouldn’t know it in Pattaya. Ever since American bombing missions took off from a nearby Thai air base during the Vietnam War, the village of Pattaya rapidly expanded its red-light district. Business boomed. With the arrival of the Lincoln, local entrepreneurs talked up the positive effect on Pattaya’s economy.
賣淫在泰國是非法的，但在芭堤雅絲毫看不出這一點。自越南戰爭期間美國轟炸機從附近一個泰國空軍基地起飛以來，芭堤雅這個村莊迅速擴展了紅燈區，生意興隆。隨著林肯號的到來，當地商界人士紛紛宣揚其對芭堤雅經濟的積極影響。

In earlier decades, some women and girls from Thailand’s poor northeast were sold by their families into sex work in Pattaya. While local aid groups say such human trafficking has waned, the criminalization of prostitution, even as it is openly tolerated, can put prostitutes at the mercy of corrupt forces or periodic crackdowns. In the days leading up to the Lincoln’s docking at a nearby port, the police in Pattaya launched an anti-prostitution raid that, like many before it, seemed to have had little lingering effect, apart from stigmatizing the women.
早前幾十年，一些來自泰國貧困東北部地區的婦女和女孩被家人賣到芭堤雅從事性工作。當地援助組織表示，此類人口販運雖已減少，但賣淫被定為犯罪——儘管執行上顯然很寬鬆——可能使性工作者任由腐敗勢力擺布或遭受週期性打擊。在林肯號停靠附近港口前的幾天裡，芭堤雅警方發起反賣淫突擊行動，與以往許多次一樣，除了給這些女性帶來污名外，似乎沒有產生什麼持久影響。
Today, servicemen are a much smaller demographic in Pattaya, compared to tourists from India, China and the Middle East. Amid the sex shows and vendors selling tubes of personal lubricant, vacationing families toted sleeping children, taking in the scene.
如今，與來自印度、中國和中東的遊客相比，軍人在芭堤雅只是一個很小的群體。在性表演和兜售管裝人體潤滑劑的小販之間，度假的家庭背著熟睡的孩子，觀賞著這番喧囂景象。
“Pattaya has been able to develop itself as a tourist destination, not only for sex tourism but also for culture, temples, family,” said Chanan Yodhong, a gender and sexual equality activist.
「芭堤雅已發展成為一個旅遊目的地，不僅面向性旅遊，也面向文化、寺廟和家庭旅遊，」性別與性平等活動人士差南·約東說。
On Thursday night, as some sailors and Marines strolled or sat on the curb reeling from too much to drink, a corps of tourist police stood at attention before scattering into the crowds. There were also volunteer officers, residents who are originally from other parts of Asia, Europe and the Middle East.
週四晚上，一些水兵和陸戰隊員或在路邊漫步，或因飲酒過量搖搖晃晃坐在路邊，一隊旅遊警察立正站立，隨後分散進入人群。還有一些志願警官，他們是原本來自亞洲、歐洲和中東其他地區的居民。
Among them was Hooman Peseshk, a native of Iran, who has lived in Pattaya for 10 years. He recognized the irony of his patrolling the streets with a shipful of American sailors returning from the war in Iran. But he loved Pattaya, he said, for its status as a free port, where almost anything goes.
其中一位是伊朗人胡曼·佩塞什克，已在芭堤雅生活10年。他意識到自己與一船從伊朗戰爭歸來的美國水兵一起走在街上頗具諷刺意味。但他說，他愛芭堤雅，因為這裡是一個幾乎什麼都可以發生的自由港。
“Nobody wants to fight in a war that was started because of our leaders,” he said. “We are all brothers here in Pattaya.”
「沒有人想參加一場因為我們的領導人而爆發的戰爭，」他說。「在芭堤雅，我們都是兄弟。」
Despite the beach town’s reputation, many others from the Lincoln said they had no interest in exploring Play Girlz, KINK or the Harem Ultra go-go club. Instead, they dined poolside at the Hilton. They tried out foot spas where little fish nibbled off calluses. At the Hard Rock Hotel, where the minibar comes stocked with chocolate- and strawberry- flavored condoms, a member of the Lincoln crew said he was planning to spend the night ordering room service for one and watching sports on the big-screen TV.
儘管這個海灘小鎮名聲在外，林肯號上其他許多人表示，他們沒興趣去探索Play Girlz、KINK或Harem Ultra戈戈舞俱樂部。他們選擇在希爾頓酒店泳池邊用餐，嘗試讓小魚啃掉老繭的足部水療。在硬石酒店，迷你吧裡備有巧克力和草莓味避孕套，一名林肯號船員說，他計劃當晚叫一人份客房送餐服務，在大螢幕電視上看體育節目。
One sailor said that after so many months aboard the carrier — with its frantic sirens, clattering pipes, screaming jet engines, explosive catapult launches — what he missed most was silence. Pattaya is many things, but it is not quiet. For that the sailor would have to wait.
一名水兵說，在航母上待了這麼久——伴隨瘋狂的警報、咔嗒作響的管道、尖嘯的噴氣發動機、轟鳴的彈射起飛，他最想念的是寧靜。芭堤雅有很多特點，但唯獨不安靜。要得到寧靜，這名水兵還得等待。
