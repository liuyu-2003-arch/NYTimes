德國極右翼政黨州選舉勝利預示著什麼

Far-Right Victory in German State Seen as Omen Across Europe

MARK LANDLER • 2026-09-08
When a far-right party won a landslide victory on Sunday in a small state in eastern Germany, its reverberations could be felt from Downing Street in London to the Élysée Palace in Paris. Rarely has the result of a regional election been viewed with such portent for the fate of democracy in Europe.
週日，當一個極右翼政黨在德國東部一個小州贏得壓倒性勝利時，其衝擊波從倫敦唐寧街一直迴盪到巴黎愛麗舍宮。一次地方選舉的結果很少會被如此視為關乎歐洲民主命運的預兆。
This wasn’t just any European country; it was Germany, a country that has relentlessly isolated far-right political parties since the calamity of Nazism. And it wasn’t just a strange outlier, but rather the latest step in a steady, decade-long march that has brought the far right ever closer to the fulcrum of power in Europe.
這不是普通歐洲國家，而是德國——一個自納粹主義浩劫以來一直不遺餘力孤立極右翼政黨的國家。這也不是一次反常的例外，而是極右翼在過去十年中穩步推進、日益接近歐洲權力中心的最新一步。
With elections scheduled across Europe next year, the striking success of the party, Alternative for Germany, could be a harbinger of further far-right victories, according to analysts, as well as a template for how they can be engineered. Its populist, anti-immigrant message echoes those of nationalist leaders in Britain, France, Italy and Spain, all countries where voters harbor many of the same grievances as those in Germany.
明年歐洲各國將相繼舉行選舉，分析人士表示，德國選擇黨此次引人注目的成功可能預示著極右翼將取得更多勝利，並為此提供了可資借鑒的範本。該黨的民粹主義、反移民論調與英國、法國、義大利和西班牙的民族主義領導人如出一轍，這些國家的選民有許多與德國選民同樣的不滿。
French voters will elect a new president next spring. An election is due in Spain by August, and in Italy by the end of next year. Far-right candidates are surging in all of them, threatening incumbents blamed for systemic problems with their countries’ economies.
法國選民將於明年春天選出新總統。西班牙最晚將於8月舉行選舉，義大利則是在明年年底。極右翼候選人在所有這些國家都迅猛崛起，對因本國經濟出現系統性問題而備受詬病的現任領導人構成威脅。
“Developed Europe is going through a version of sophisticated state failure,” said Mujtaba Rahman, an analyst who specializes in Europe at Eurasia Group, a political risk consultancy. “Governments are unable to address cost-of-living concerns. They are not able to deliver for their voters.”
政治風險諮詢公司歐亞集團專門研究歐洲事務的分析師穆傑塔巴·拉赫曼表示：「發達歐洲正在經歷某種複雜的國家失靈。政府無法解決生活成本問題，無法回應選民的訴求。」
In Germany, rising prices and high unemployment helped win the far-right party, known by its German initials, AfD, more than twice the number of votes as Chancellor Friedrich Merz’s center-right incumbent party.
在德國，物價上漲和高失業率幫助這個以其德語縮寫AfD聞名的極右翼政黨贏得了比現任總理默茨領導的中右翼執政黨多出一倍以上的選票。
The result laid bare a divide between Europeans who see the far right as a threat to democracy, and a growing number who see it as democracy’s savior. The former warn that the far right will dismantle checks and balances, while treating minorities as second-class citizens. The latter see it as a bulwark against an out-of-touch elite that has eroded their national identity and endangered their livelihoods.
這一結果清楚地揭示出歐洲人的分歧：一些人將極右翼視為對民主的威脅，越來越多的人則將其視為民主的救星。前者警告說，極右翼將拆除制衡機制，同時將少數群體視為二等公民；後者則將其視為抵禦脫離實際的精英階層的堡壘，這些精英侵蝕了他們的民族身份認同並危及了他們的生計。
廣告
Because the AfD appeared to fall just short of an outright majority, according to near-final results, it may not end up governing Saxony-Anhalt, the state where the election was held. Rival parties have vowed that they would not form a coalition government with the AfD, whose leaders have used Nazi slogans and minimized the Holocaust. The AfD is designated as a “suspected” extremist group by German intelligence, partly because the group portrays Germans of immigrant backgrounds as second-class citizens.
根據接近完成的計票結果，由於AfD似乎僅以微弱差距未能獲得絕對多數，因此最終可能無法在選舉所在的薩克森-安哈爾特州執政。競爭對手政黨誓言不會與AfD組成聯合政府，AfD的領導人曾使用納粹口號並淡化大屠殺。德國情報機構將其列為「疑似」極端主義組織，部分原因是該黨將移民背景的德國人描繪成二等公民。
Still, the result could hobble Mr. Merz if members of his party blame him for the loss, at a time when Germany’s leadership in Europe was already faltering. Mr. Merz’s standing has deteriorated so badly that political analysts talk openly of his ouster, a once-far-fetched scenario in German politics.
不過，這一結果仍可能讓默茨陷入困境——如果他的黨內成員將這次失利歸咎於他，尤其是德國在歐洲的領導地位本已日漸式微之際。默茨的政治地位已經嚴重惡化，以至於政治分析人士開始公開討論他下台的可能性，這在德國政壇曾經幾乎是難以想像的。
“It’s a big concern in Europe,” said Mr. Rahman, who previously worked for the European Union’s civil service. “A weak Germany, with a chancellor who is coming unstuck and being forced to deal with domestic problems, is bad and will ultimately subtract from Europe’s ability to confront its challenges.”
「這令歐洲深感憂慮，」曾任職於歐盟公務員體系的拉赫曼表示。「一個虛弱的德國，一個陷入困境、被迫處理國內問題的總理，這對歐洲是壞事，最終將削弱歐洲應對挑戰的能力。」
Worries among centrists about France, where President Emmanuel Macron will step down next spring, are no less acute. Marine Le Pen, the candidate of France’s main far-right party, the National Rally, holds a commanding lead in first-round polls, and has even finished first in recent second-round matchups. That puts her closer to power than at any time in her 14 years of seeking the presidency.
中間派對法國的擔憂也同樣緊迫。法國總統馬克宏將於明年春天卸任。法國主要的極右翼政黨國民聯盟的候選人瑪麗娜·勒龐在首輪民調中遙遙領先，甚至在最近的第二輪模擬對決中也排名第一。在14年來尋求總統之位的過程中，如今她比任何時候都更接近權力中心。
A far-right government in Paris, Mr. Rahman said, would destabilize the European Union in a way that no other national election has, given France’s size, nuclear arsenal and founding role in the European project. It might also bring an end to the so-called cordon sanitaire, under which mainstream parties in France, as in Germany, have refused to enter governing coalitions with the far right.
拉赫曼表示，鑒於法國的體量、核武庫及其在歐洲一體化中的創始角色，一個極右翼政府出現在巴黎對歐盟的破壞力將是其他任何國家選舉都無法比擬的。這還可能終結所謂的「警戒線」(cordon sanitaire)——法國和德國的主流政黨一直拒絕與極右翼組成執政聯盟。
“In both countries, the antifascist front that had prevailed since the end of the Second World War seems on the verge of collapse,” said Philippe Marlière, a professor of French and European politics at University College London.
倫敦大學學院法國和歐洲政治學教授菲利普·馬利耶爾表示：「在這兩個國家，自二戰結束以來一直佔主導地位的反法西斯陣線似乎處於崩潰的邊緣。」
廣告
In Spain, analysts said the far-right Vox Party could elbow its way into the next coalition government, with the current Socialist-led government tarnished by corruption scandals and an immigration crisis. In Britain, the governing Labour Party ousted its unpopular prime minister, Keir Starmer, in July to try to regain traction against a fast-rising far-right party, Reform U.K.
在西班牙，分析人士稱，極右翼的呼聲黨可能擠入下一屆聯合政府——目前由工人社會黨領導的政府因腐敗醜聞和移民危機而蒙塵。在英國，執政的工黨於7月罷免了不受歡迎的首相斯塔默，試圖重新站穩腳跟，以對抗快速崛起的極右翼政黨英國改革黨。
Still, the rise of the far right is not inexorable. Reform’s success has been rudely interrupted by a series of financial scandals involving its firebrand leader, Nigel Farage. That has given the Labour government, under a new prime minister, Andy Burnham, some unexpected breathing room.
儘管如此，極右翼的崛起並非不可阻擋。英國改革黨的勢頭已被一系列涉及其煽動性領袖奈傑爾·法拉奇的金融醜聞狠狠打斷。這給了新任首相安迪·伯納姆領導的工黨政府一些意想不到的喘息空間。
In April, the far-right leader of Hungary, Prime Minister Viktor Orban, was swept out of power after 16 years, a casualty of widespread perceptions that his government was corrupt, inattentive to the economy, and had picked needless battles with the European Union. The defeat of his Fidesz party gave hope to mainstream politicians that the far right, too, is vulnerable.
今年4月，匈牙利極右翼領導人、總理歐爾班·維克托在執政16年後被趕下台，導致他下台的一個重要原因，是公眾普遍認為他的政府腐敗、對經濟問題漠不關心，而且還與歐盟進行了許多不必要的對抗。他的青民盟敗選給了主流政治家希望，即極右翼同樣並非不可撼動。
The extent to which those mainstream politicians “need to worry” about the AfD’s success, said Timothy Bale, a professor of politics at Queen Mary University of London, “is going to depend on whether, if it ends up in office, it behaves relatively normally, and whether or not it’s able to run things competently.”
倫敦瑪麗女王大學政治學教授蒂莫西·貝爾表示，主流政治人物對AfD成功的「擔憂程度將取決於，如果它最終上台是否會表現得相對正常，以及它是否有能力勝任治理」。
Italy’s prime minister, Giorgia Meloni, whose populist party has neofascist roots, offers a playbook to other like-minded leaders. Having modulated her tone on issues like Ukraine and the European Union, she recently celebrated her government’s becoming the longest continuously serving in the history of the Italian Republic.
義大利總理梅洛尼的民粹主義政黨有新法西斯主義淵源，她為其他立場相近的領導人提供了行動範本。在烏克蘭和歐盟等問題上調整了語氣後，她最近慶祝自己的政府成為義大利共和國歷史上連續執政時間最長的政府。
Ms. Meloni is in a decent position to extend that run when she faces voters next summer. But the rising profile of a rival even further to her right, Roberto Vannacci, has led her to harden her positions on issues like immigration. Ms. Meloni heaped criticism on Spain’s prime minister, Pedro Sánchez, after a sudden influx of migrants from Morocco into a Spanish territory in late July.
梅洛尼在明年夏天面對選民時處於有利位置，有望延長這一任期。但比她更右翼的競爭對手羅伯托·范納奇日益上漲的人氣促使她在移民等問題上立場趨於強硬。7月下旬，在摩洛哥移民突然湧入西班牙領土後，梅洛尼對西班牙首相桑切斯大加抨擊。
廣告
Mr. Farage risks being similarly outflanked in Britain. Rupert Lowe, a disaffected member of his party, defected to start an even more right-wing group that is siphoning votes from Mr. Farage. Given that competition, and the proliferating questions about Reform’s finances, Professor Bale said it was difficult to see how Mr. Farage could cobble together enough votes to form a government.
法拉奇在英國面臨類似的風險。他所在政黨一名心懷不滿的成員魯珀特·洛脫離該黨，組建了一個更右翼的團體，正在分流法拉奇的選票。考慮到這種競爭，以及關於英國改革黨財務狀況層出不窮的質疑，貝爾教授表示，很難看出法拉奇要如何拼湊出足夠的選票來組建政府。
In France, Ms. Le Pen’s path to power is more plausible. Her poll numbers are more than double those of the leading centrist, Edouard Philippe, or the leading far-left candidate, Jean-Luc Mélenchon.
在法國，勒龐上台的可能性更大。她的民調支持率是中間派陣營中領先的愛德華·菲利普或極左翼陣營中領先的讓-呂克·梅朗雄的兩倍多。
The big question in France is whether Mr. Philippe or anyone else will be able to gather enough support from the center right and left to emerge as the consensus mainstream candidate. In a second round of voting, analysts said, such a candidate would stand a chance of beating Ms. Le Pen.
法國的最大懸念是，菲利普或其他任何人能否從中間偏右和偏左陣營中爭取到足夠的支持，成為主流共識候選人。分析人士稱，在第二輪投票中，這樣的候選人仍有機會擊敗勒龐。
But if France’s center remains fractured, as it is now, that would give Mr. Mélenchon a shot at making the second round, likely facing off against Ms. Le Pen. In that scenario — one that France’s political establishment views as a nightmare — Ms. Le Pen would be the odds-on victor.
但如果法國的中間派繼續保持目前的四分五裂狀態，那麼梅朗雄就有機會進入第二輪，很可能與勒龐對決。在這種情況下——法國政治建制派視之為噩夢，勒龐將是大熱門贏家。
Like other far-right leaders in Europe, Ms. Le Pen has kept her distance from President Trump. But she and other populist candidates could be buffeted by him anyway, according to analysts. Mr. Trump remains deeply unpopular in Europe and is fast losing popularity in the United States as well.
與歐洲其他極右翼領導人一樣，勒龐與川普總統保持著距離。但分析人士稱，她和其他民粹主義候選人仍可能受到他的波及。川普在歐洲仍然極不受歡迎，在美國的支持率也在迅速下滑。
The lesson that Europe’s beleaguered centrists hope voters will take from America’s turmoil, Mr. Rahman said, is that “if you experiment with non-mainstream parties, you risk making mistakes.”
拉赫曼表示，歐洲受困的中間派希望選民從美國的動盪中吸取的教訓是：「如果你想嘗試非主流政黨，你就有犯錯的風險。」

Mark Landler是時報巴黎分社社長，報導法國以及美國在歐洲和中東的外交政策。他從事新聞工作已超過30年。
