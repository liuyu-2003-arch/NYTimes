美國特使訪問基輔給烏克蘭帶來了什麼

They Went to Ukraine on the ‘Peace Express.’ Detours Are Likely Ahead.

CASSANDRA VINOGRAD, ANDREW E. KRAMER • 2026-09-08
The Americans arrived on a train that the Ukrainians called the “peace express.” Yet nothing about the process of ending the war with Russia has been fast or direct, and there is no sign that will change anytime soon.
美國人是乘著一列烏克蘭人稱為「和平快車」的火車抵達的。然而，結束這場戰爭的過程既不快速也不直接，且沒有跡象表明這一狀況會在短期內改變。
The U.S. envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner went to Ukraine on Sunday fresh off a trip to Moscow bearing what President Trump said was a proposal to end nearly five years of war.
美國特使史提夫·威特科夫和賈里德·庫什納週日抵達烏克蘭，他們此前剛剛結束對莫斯科的訪問，此行帶著川普總統所說的一項旨在結束長達近五年的戰爭的提議。
But the Trump administration itself, consumed by its war with Iran, had allowed the peace process to enter such a deep freeze that the trip was just a preliminary step — talks about how to resume talks. And President Volodymyr Zelensky of Ukraine said it appeared that the war would continue at least into what analysts say could be a devastating winter for the country.
但自身正深陷對伊戰爭泥潭的川普政府已讓和平進程陷入深度凍結，此次行程因此僅是一個先期步驟——討論如何恢復談判。烏克蘭總統弗拉基米爾·澤連斯基表示，戰爭看來將繼續下去，至少要拖入分析人士所稱的、對該國而言可能是毀滅性的冬季。
“It’s a very, very hard problem, but we’re going to try,” Mr. Kushner said of negotiations to end the war. “And I think if we can achieve that, it’d be an incredible thing. That doesn’t mean we will achieve it, but it’s very important to try.”
「這是一個非常、非常難的問題，但我們會嘗試，」庫什納談到結束戰爭的談判時說。「我認為如果我們能做到，那將是一件了不起的事。這不意味著我們一定能做到，但嘗試非常重要。」
廣告
Mr. Kushner said “new ideas” were presented to Mr. Zelensky in a series of discussions on Sunday. He gave no details and also declined to give a timeline for reaching any potential settlement.
庫什納表示，週日在與澤連斯基的一系列討論中提出了「新想法」。他沒有透露細節，也拒絕給出達成任何潛在解決方案的時間表。
The Ukrainian leader offered little more detail about the talks with the Americans. “All the possibilities” for resuming negotiations were discussed, he said at a news conference after the meetings, as were “several genuinely new ideas.”
烏克蘭領導人對與美方的會談基本沒有提供更多細節。會後新聞發布會上，他說討論了恢復談判的「所有可能性」，以及「幾個真正的新想法」。
When pressed on when further talks would be held, the responses from Mr. Witkoff, Mr. Kushner and Mr. Zelensky seemed to follow a pattern: “Soon” or “hopefully soon.”
當被追問後續會談何時舉行時，威特科夫、庫什納和澤連斯基的回應似乎都遵循同一個模式：「很快」或「有望很快」。
Ukrainian officials had been saying before the Americans arrived that they expected negotiations to resume in earnest later this month, after Russian parliamentary elections but before the U.S. midterms.
烏克蘭官員在美方抵達前一直表示，他們預計談判將於本月晚些時候真正恢復，即在俄羅斯議會選舉之後、美國期中選舉之前。
Those talks, if they happen, would be held alongside an escalation in the air war between Moscow and Kyiv, and a Russian push to accelerate its slow territorial gains in the Donbas region of eastern Ukraine. The war, analysts say, is likely to get only uglier in the months to come, as Russia seeks a knockout wintertime blow against Kyiv and the two sides try to gain the upper hand in eventual settlement negotiations.
這些談判如果舉行，將在莫斯科與基輔之間空戰升級的背景下進行，同時俄羅斯正推動加速其在烏克蘭東部頓巴斯地區的緩慢領土擴張。分析人士稱，未來幾個月戰爭只會變得更加殘酷，因為俄羅斯尋求在冬季對基輔發動決定性打擊，而雙方都試圖在最終的解決方案談判中佔據上風。
While it remains to be seen if peace negotiations can be revived after a six-month lull, the visit by Mr. Witkoff and Mr. Kushner was meaningful for Ukraine because it finally brought the two Americans out of a Moscow bubble.
雖然和平談判能否在六個月的停滯後恢復仍有待觀察，但威特科夫和庫什納的此次訪問對烏克蘭意義重大，因為它終於讓兩位美國人走出了莫斯科的「迴音室」。
廣告
The envoys’ visit to Kyiv, which ended late on Sunday, was their first after several trips to Russia. It gave the Ukrainians the chance to show, and not just tell, as they try to make their case and ensure that the Trump administration does not revert to clearly favoring Russia in talks, as it did earlier in the process.
兩位特使對基輔的訪問於週日晚間結束，這是他們多次訪問俄羅斯後的首次基輔之行。此行讓烏克蘭人有機會用實情展示而不只是口頭陳述，從而在試圖闡明立場的同時，確保川普政府不會回到談判進程早期那樣明顯偏向俄羅斯的立場。
The Ukrainians started serving up symbolism before the envoys even reached Kyiv. A refined meal was laid out on the train ride for Mr. Witkoff, a known gourmand, and Mr. Kushner. It included a selection of Crimean Tatar dishes, highlighting Ukraine’s ties to the peninsula that Russia illegally annexed more than a decade ago.
烏克蘭人在特使抵達基輔之前就開始營造象徵意義。火車行程中為嗜好美食的威特科夫和庫什納擺出了一頓精緻的餐食，包括一系列克里米亞韃靼菜肴，凸顯烏克蘭與十多年前被俄羅斯非法吞併的半島之間的聯繫。
Upon arrival, Mr. Zelensky greeted the envoys on the landmark Sophia Square, whose display of tributes to fallen soldiers is impossible to ignore, then brought them into the historic cathedral, a symbol of Ukrainian national identity.
抵達後，澤連斯基在地標性的索菲亞廣場迎接特使，那裡展陳著向陣亡士兵致敬的紀念物，令人無法忽視，隨後帶他們進入這座歷史悠久的教堂——烏克蘭民族身份的象徵。
Mr. Zelensky’s negotiator later wore a joke T-shirt emblazoned with the words “Kyiv Regime” — poking fun at the sneering term used by President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia to refer to Ukraine’s government.
澤連斯基的談判代表後來穿了一件印有「基輔政權」字樣的玩笑T恤，以此嘲諷俄羅斯總統弗拉基米爾·V·普丁用來指代烏克蘭政府的蔑稱。
Mr. Zelensky offered jokes, too, in telling Mr. Witkoff and Mr. Kushner to “come more often,” since the Kremlin agreed to temporarily halt during their trip what have been especially intense all-day strikes on Kyiv.
澤連斯基也開起了玩笑，他對威特科夫和庫什納說「要常來」，因為克里姆林宮同意在兩人訪問期間暫時停止對基輔的打擊——此前這種全天候打擊尤為猛烈。
For many Ukrainians, that was the best and only anticipated result, at least in the near term.
對許多烏克蘭人來說，這至少是近期內最好的、也是唯一可以預期的結果。
廣告
“Thank you dear Steve and thank you dear Jared for giving us this afternoon without air-raid alerts,” a Kyiv tour guide, Dmytro Soloviov, said with a touch of sarcasm while leading a walking tour of the city’s architecture.
「謝謝親愛的史提夫，謝謝親愛的賈里德，給了我們一個沒有空襲警報的下午，」基輔導遊德米特羅·索洛維約夫在帶著人們遊覽城市建築時，用略顯嘲諷的語氣說道。
Yana Vilimetova, a procurement manager, said Ukrainians would use the brief respite to recharge a little. It felt like Groundhog Day to many — another flurry initiated by the Americans, with little expectation of any change in Mr. Putin’s determination to continue fighting.
採購經理亞娜·維利梅托娃表示，烏克蘭人會利用這段短暫的喘息時間。對許多人來說，這感覺像是《土撥鼠之日》，是美國人掀起的又一陣喧囂，而人們並不指望普丁會改變他繼續作戰的決心。
“We’ll just sleep for three days without strikes, reset our mental health during these days, and that’s it,” Ms. Vilimetova said.
「我們只會在沒有空襲的日子睡上三天，恢復一下心理健康，僅此而已，」維利梅托娃說。
While no one expects a lasting cease-fire anytime soon, a placard on the dining car table in the U.S. envoys’ train offered a hopeful thought.
雖然短期內沒有人預計會達成持久停火，但美方特使所乘火車餐車桌上的一塊標語牌提出了一個充滿希望的想法。
“Peace,” it read, “arrives on time.”
「和平，」標語寫道，「準點到達。」

Evelina Riabenko和Kim Barker對本文有報導貢獻。

Andrew E. Kramer是《紐約時報》基輔分社社長，他自2014年起一直報導烏克蘭的戰爭。
