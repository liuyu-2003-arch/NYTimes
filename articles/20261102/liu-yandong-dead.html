劉延東去世：躋身中共高層的少數女性之一

Liu Yandong, Rare Woman Among China’s Political Elite, Dies at 80

艾莎 • 2026-11-02
Liu Yandong, one of the few women to reach the upper echelons of the Chinese Communist Party, but whose career also illustrated the limits for women in China’s ruling elite, died on Wednesday in Beijing. She was 80.
劉延東是極少數幾位躋身中國共產黨高層的女性之一，但其職業生涯也凸顯了中國統治精英階層中女性所面臨的局限。她於上週三在北京逝世，享年80歲。
Her death, after a long illness, was reported by the state news agency Xinhua and confirmed by her family.
據官方通訊社新華社報導，她因長期患病去世，其家屬已證實這一消息。 
Ms. Liu’s revolutionary pedigree, prestigious education and time toiling in a factory helped pave the way for her rise in the 1980s. As one of just six women to have ever served on the Politburo, the party’s elite policymaking body, she demonstrated what women in politics could achieve, spearheading diplomatic efforts and gaining recognition for her personal approach.
劉延東的革命家世、名校教育背景，以及在工廠辛勤工作的經歷為她在20世紀80年代的崛起鋪平了道路。作為迄今為止僅有的六位曾任該黨最高決策機構中共中央政治局委員的女性之一，她展示了女性在政治領域可以取得的成就，不僅領導了外交工作，並因其個人作風而獲得認可。
Yet her portfolio of duties overseeing education, science and technology, health and women’s and children’s affairs — some of which are typically relegated to female politicians — underscored the constraints on that power.
然而，她負責的教育、科技、衛生以及婦女兒童事務——其中部分職責通常被視為女性政界人士的領域——恰恰凸顯了這種權力的局限性。
In its announcement of her death, Xinhua eulogized Ms. Liu as “an excellent Party member, a time-tested and loyal communist fighter” and praised her for her work on the Politburo. But her position on that committee — the party’s second-most powerful group — which she joined in 2007, was not evidence of a liberalizing Communist Party, said Minglu Chen, an associate professor at the University of Sydney who studies gender and politics in China.
在宣布她逝世的訃告中，新華社稱讚劉延東為「優秀黨員，久經考驗的忠誠的共產主義戰士」，並讚揚了她在政治局的工作。但雪梨大學研究中國性別與政治的副教授陳明璐表示，她於2007年任職於政治局——黨內第二大權力機構——並不能證明共產黨正在走向自由化。
“The party leadership has always been a boys’ club,” Ms. Chen said, noting that three of the six women who have reached the Politburo were also charged with overseeing health and women’s and children’s policy, areas that have traditionally been considered softer. Today, there are no women in the Politburo.
「黨的領導層一直是一個男性俱樂部，」陳明璐說。她指出，曾進入政治局的六名女性中，有三名被安排兼管衛生和婦女兒童政策，這些領域傳統上被認為較為軟性。如今，政治局中已沒有女性。
Ms. Liu was born on Nov. 22, 1945, and grew up in Nantong, a city in the coastal province of Jiangsu. As the daughter of Liu Ruilong, a military commander and former vice minister of agriculture, she was what is known as a princeling, or a descendant of a senior party official. That may have given her a keener sense of political strategy than her peers. But she began to carve a path in politics only after studying engineering at Tsinghua University, and then working in factories for a decade in the 1970s.
劉延東出生於1945年11月22日，在沿海省份江蘇省的南通市長大。作為軍事指揮官、前農業部副部長劉瑞龍的女兒，她屬於所謂的「太子黨」，即中共高級官員的後代。這或許使她比同齡人更具政治戰略眼光。但直到她在清華大學學習工程學，隨後在20世紀70年代在工廠工作十年之後，她才開始在政壇開闢自己的道路。

She took a job in the municipal government in Beijing in 1980 and, not long after, joined the Communist Youth League, a training ground for many of China’s top leaders. It was there that she started to be groomed for a political career, and where she got to know Hu Jintao, who was China’s president from 2003 until 2013.
1980年，她在北京市政府工作，不久之後加入了共青團中央，這是許多中國最高領導人的訓練營。正是在那裡，她開始為政治生涯接受培養，並結識了2003年至2013年擔任中國國家主席的胡錦濤。
It was not until 2002, when Ms. Liu became head of the United Front, which works to make alliances outside the party, that she started amassing broader political influence. China had just joined the World Trade Organization, opening the country to international trade and the global economy.
直到2002年，劉延東出任致力於在黨外結成聯盟的統戰部部長後，她才開始積累更廣泛的政治影響力。當時中國剛剛加入世界貿易組織，向國際貿易和全球經濟開放。
Her approach to her own work reflected that outward turn. In the United Front, Ms. Liu took her responsibility for building relationships beyond the party seriously. She drew acclaim during a 2006 visit to Mount Sanqing, in Jiangxi Province, when she insisted on climbing the mountain to the summit, beyond the reach of the cable cars, in order to meet with Taoist priests, who said “they had never seen such a high-ranking official before,” according to state media.
她的工作方式反映了這種向外看的轉變。在統戰部，劉延東認真履行了她在黨外建立關係的責任。2006年，她在訪問江西省三清山時贏得了讚譽，當時她堅持徒步爬上纜車無法到達的山頂去會見道士，據官方媒體報導，這些道士說他們「從未見過這麼大的官」。
The following year, Ms. Liu was promoted to the Politburo, and in 2013 she became a vice premier, among the country’s highest-ranking officials.
次年，劉延東晉陞為政治局委員，並於2013年出任國務院副總理，躋身該國最高級別官員之列。
During that period, as trade between China and the United States was booming and their economies were growing more intertwined, Ms. Liu became known for cultivating deeper cultural ties between the two countries. Together with Hillary Clinton, then the secretary of state, she led an initiative in 2010 to deepen the bilateral relationship beyond trade into areas like education and sports.
在此期間，隨著中美貿易的蓬勃發展，兩國經濟日益交織，劉延東因致力於深化兩國文化聯繫而聞名。2010年，她與時任美國國務卿希拉蕊·柯林頓共同領導了一項倡議，旨在將雙邊關係從貿易深化到教育和體育等領域。
During her visit to Beijing to announce the program, known as the China-U.S. High Level Consultation on People-to-People Exchange, Mrs. Clinton quipped, “I think Madam Liu and I are going to revive the ping pong diplomacy.” She was referring to the visit of American table tennis players to China in 1971 that helped pave the way for the two countries to restore relations after years of Cold War tensions.
在希拉蕊·柯林頓訪問北京，宣布這一被稱為「中美人文交流高層磋商」的項目時，她打趣說：「我想劉女士和我將重振乒乓外交。」她指的是1971年美國乒乓球運動員訪華一事，這為兩國在多年的冷戰緊張局勢後恢復關係鋪平了道路。
Ms. Liu later gave Mrs. Clinton a photograph of her father, telling her that he had rescued American pilots during World War II. Mrs. Clinton said she would hang it in her office.
劉延東後來送給希拉蕊·柯林頓一張自己父親的照片，並告訴她，她父親在二戰期間曾營救過美國飛行員。希拉蕊說她會把照片掛在自己的辦公室裡。
Ms. Liu also oversaw planning for the 2008 Olympic Games in Beijing, and under China’s current leader, Xi Jinping, helped support the development of computer chips, semiconductor manufacturing equipment and high-end manufacturing, helping to lay the foundation for China’s increasingly dominant position in areas like electric vehicles. She also worked to lower the steep cost of drugs and oversaw reforms of public hospitals.
劉延東還曾主管2008年北京奧運會的籌備工作，並在中國現任領導人習近平的領導下，協助支持了計算機晶片、半導體製造設備和高端製造業的發展，幫助中國在電動汽車等領域日益佔據主導地位奠定基礎。她還致力於降低高昂的藥品成本，並主管了公立醫院改革。

She had the leadership style of “a cautious, dependable administrator operating within a strongly hierarchical system,” said Fubing Su, a professor of political science at Vassar College. “She projected steadiness rather than charisma, rarely asserting a distinctive personal identity or an agenda of her own,” he added.
瓦薩學院政治學教授蘇福兵表示，她的領導風格是「在等級森嚴的體制內運作的謹慎可靠管理者」。他還說：「她展現出的是穩健而非個人魅力，很少彰顯鮮明的個人色彩或提出自己的議程。」
To climb the ranks of the Communist Party, officials must demonstrate that they can strengthen the economy in cities and provinces under their purview. Women, however, are almost never given those jobs, said Dali Yang, a professor of political science at the University of Chicago.
為了在共產黨內晉陞，官員們必須證明他們能夠鞏固其管轄下省市的經濟。然而，芝加哥大學政治學教授楊大利指出，女性幾乎從未被委以這類重任。
Despite those limits, Ms. Liu made significant strides. But a woman has never been on the Standing Committee, the highest decision-making body in China.
儘管存在這些限制，劉延東還是取得了重大成就。但是，至今從未有女性進入過中國最高決策機構——政治局常務委員會。
“If she had been a man,” said Professor Yang, “she would most likely have made it to the Politburo Standing Committee.”
「如果她是男性，」楊大利說，「她極有可能進入政治局常委會。」
