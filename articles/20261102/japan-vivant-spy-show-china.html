日劇《Vivant》被指「辱華」，三麗鷗取消聯名合作

How a Japanese Spy Drama Became the Target of China’s Anger

赫海威, HISAKO UENO • 2026-11-02
China’s recent pressure campaign against Japan has involved critical minerals, tourism, seafood and even pandas. Now Beijing is taking the dispute to the cultural sphere, targeting a Japanese television drama.
中國近期對日本施壓的領域涵蓋關鍵礦產、旅遊、海鮮乃至大熊貓。如今北京將爭端延伸至文化領域，瞄準了一部日本電視劇。
The Chinese government has in recent days focused its ire on “Vivant,” one of Japan’s most popular shows, a cross between “24” and “Homeland” that tells the story of Japanese spies working to protect their country from threats overseas.
中國政府近日將矛頭對準《別班》(Vivant)。這部日本最受歡迎的劇集之一風格介於《24小時》與《國土安全》之間，講述日本間諜在海外行動以保衛國家免受威脅的故事。
Beijing says the show, which features a fictional Chinese intelligence agency called “Xinxi” as the antagonist, promotes negative views of China. The second season of “Vivant,” which premiered in July, includes scenes of Chinese agents torturing Japanese operatives. In the first season, the enemy state was not identified.
北京方面稱，該劇將虛構的中國情報機構「信息」(Xinxi)設為反派，宣揚對華負面看法。今年7月首播的《別班》第二季包含中國特工酷刑折磨日本特工的場景。第一季中，敵對國家並未具名。
“This television series once again reveals that there is a force in Japan that attempts to use cultural products to smear China and spread anti-China sentiment,” a spokesman for China’s Foreign Ministry, Guo Jiakun, said at a news conference in Beijing on Thursday.
「這部電視劇再次暴露出日本國內始終存在一股勢力，企圖通過文化產品抹黑中國、散播仇華情緒，」中國外交部發言人郭嘉昆週四在北京的記者會上表示。
廣告
China’s criticism of “Vivant” comes amid a broader effort to penalize Japan for comments made last year by Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi. She suggested that Japan could intervene militarily if China were to attack Taiwan, a self-governed democracy that Beijing claims is part of its territory.
中國對《別班》的批評是懲罰日本的整體行動的一部分，起因是日本首相高市早苗去年的言論。她曾暗示，若中國攻擊台灣（北京聲稱該自治民主政體是其領土一部分），日本可能軍事介入。
The outcry over “Vivant” began on Sunday, when China Central Television, the state broadcaster, condemned the series in a social media post.
對《別班》的聲討始於上週日，國家廣播機構中央電視台在社群媒體發帖譴責該劇。
The criticism grew so intense that Sanrio, the Japanese entertainment juggernaut behind Hello Kitty, announced on Thursday that it was canceling a merchandise partnership with the show. China is one of Sanrio’s fastest-growing markets; the company generated about $200 million in revenue there in the fiscal year that ended in March, almost double the previous year.
批評聲浪愈演愈烈，以至於Hello Kitty背後的日本娛樂巨頭三麗鷗於週四宣布取消與該劇的商品聯名合作。中國是三麗鷗增長最快的市場之一；在截至3月的財年中，該公司在華營收約2億美元，幾乎是前一年的兩倍。
The company said in a statement that it had determined that “certain content associated with this collaboration does not align with Sanrio’s corporate philosophy.”
三麗鷗在聲明中稱，已認定「關於本次聯名相關內容，我們認為其中包含與三麗鷗集團企業理念不符的表達」。
“Vivant,” which premiered in 2023, focuses on a fictional intelligence operation called “Beppan” that is affiliated with Japan’s military.
《別班》於2023年首播，聚焦一個隸屬日本軍方的虛構情報行動「別班」。
The show’s creators have said they did not intend for it to fuel geopolitical tensions. TBS, the network that airs the series, said in a statement that “Vivant” is “completely fictional.”
劇集創作者表示無意藉此加劇地緣政治緊張。播出方TBS電視台在聲明中稱《別班》系「完全虛構」。
廣告
“The main premise is that it does not degrade any particular country,” Masamine Ryuho, the network’s president, said at a news conference on Wednesday.
「該劇的基本前提是不貶低任何特定國家，」TBS社長龍寶正峰週三在記者會上表示。
China responded furiously to Ms. Takaichi’s comments about Taiwan last year, accusing her of crossing a “red line” and demanding a retraction. Since then, Beijing has reduced the export of rare earths to Japan; urged millions of tourists to avoid the country; and increased military patrols in the seas near Japan. China has also shown a growing preoccupation with efforts by Ms. Takaichi to rebuild the military, accusing Japan of reviving World War II-era militarism.
中國去年對高市早苗涉台言論反應激烈，指責其越過「紅線」並要求撤回。此後，北京減少對日稀土出口，敦促數百萬遊客避免赴日，並增加日本附近海域的軍事巡邏。中國還對高市早苗重建軍力的舉措表現出日益關切，指責日本復活二戰時期的軍國主義。
Ms. Takaichi, an outspoken critic of China, has expressed support for “Vivant.” Earlier this month, before China stepped up its propaganda efforts, she said during an appearance before Parliament that she records the show religiously and hopes to binge watch the second season over the New Year holiday.
高市早苗直言批評中國，她對《別班》表示支持。本月早些時候，在中國加大宣傳攻勢之前，她在國會答辯時稱自己每集必錄，希望新年假期一口氣看完第二季。
China has long sought to control how it is portrayed in films and on television produced overseas. The criticism of “Vivant” serves an important propaganda aim for the ruling Communist Party, analysts said: trying to highlight Japan’s record of atrocities during World War II.
中國長期尋求管控海外影視作品中對自身的呈現。分析人士稱，對《別班》的批評服務於執政的共產黨的重要宣傳目的：試圖凸顯日本在二戰期間犯下的暴行。
The People’s Daily, the Chinese Communist Party’s mouthpiece, wrote on Thursday that the series accused China of employing “torture methods used by the Japanese army during its invasion of China” in the run-up to World War II.
中共機關報《人民日報》週四寫道，該劇將二戰前夕侵華日軍「曾經實施的切指、刺腹等暴行」安在中國頭上。
William Chou, a Japan expert and senior fellow at the Hudson Institute, a think tank in Washington, said that “Vivant” was an appealing target for China because of its popularity, and because Beijing felt it could help feed a propaganda narrative.
華盛頓智庫哈德遜研究所日本問題專家、高級研究員威廉·周表示，《別班》因其熱度成了中國的一個有吸引力的目標，同時也因為北京認為它有助於投餵宣傳敘事。
廣告
“The Chinese are hunting for excuses,” he said. “They’re creating reasons to fuel this outrage, this cognitive warfare.”
「中國人在找藉口，」他說。「他們在製造理由來煽動這種憤怒、這場認知戰。」

赫海威(Javier C. Hernández)是《紐約時報》東京分社社長，領導時報對日本及周邊地區的報導。在過去十年的大部分時間裡，他一直在亞洲進行報導，此前曾任駐華記者。

Hisako Ueno是常駐東京的記者/研究員，撰寫日本政治、商業、勞工、性別及文化相關議題。
