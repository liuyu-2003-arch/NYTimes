中國企業推出人工智慧滅蚊設備

A Powerful Mosquito-Hunting Laser Uses A.I. to Zap Winged Pests

KEITH BRADSHER • 2026-11-02
For anyone kept awake by the infernal whine of mosquitoes, artificial intelligence may be coming to the rescue — armed with a laser.
對於那些被蚊子該死的嗡嗡聲折磨得夜不能寐的人來說，人工智慧可能正攜雷射趕來相救。
A Chinese company has started marketing a $1,000 A.I.-enabled device that tracks mosquitoes in flight and burns their wings off. It uses technology found in self-driving cars to pinpoint its targets, while relying on A.I. to decide when it is safe to fire.
一家中國公司已開始推銷一款售價1000美元、搭載人工智慧的設備。它能夠追蹤飛行中的蚊子，並用雷射燒掉它們的翅膀。該設備利用自動駕駛汽車所採用的技術精確鎖定目標，同時依靠人工智慧判斷何時可以安全發射雷射。
During a recent demonstration at the offices of Photon Matrix, the company behind the device, it appeared remarkably effective. The white, nearly brick-size machine quickly zapped mosquito after mosquito, each bursting into a green or blue flame with a tiny pop.
最近，在這款設備的研發公司光之矩智能科技有限公司的辦公室舉行的一場演示中，它展現出了驚人的滅蚊效果。這台大小接近一塊磚頭的白色機器迅速將一隻又一隻蚊子擊落。每隻蚊子被擊中時，都會伴隨著輕微的爆裂聲，迸出綠色或藍色的火光。
For anyone who has ever wished mosquitoes a horrible death, this is a strikingly high-tech way to make that wish a reality. The laser delivers a gruesome end, though not a quick one. Mosquitoes whose wings have burned off can take hours or even days to die, lying on the ground and twitching slightly, said Wang Chuan, chief executive and founder of Photon Matrix, based in Changzhou, about 100 miles northwest of Shanghai.
對於任何希望蚊子不得好死的人來說，這種高科技手段無疑能讓他們如願以償。雷射確實能讓蚊子死得很慘，卻無法讓它們迅速斃命。光之矩的首席執行官、創始人王川說，翅膀被燒掉的蚊子會躺在地上，身體微微抽搐，要過數小時甚至數天才會死亡。該公司位於上海西北約160公里的常州。
After the demonstration, I examined the fallen mosquitoes. The wings appeared to be gone, but their legs and bodies looked intact. None could fly, even when I waved my hand directly over them.
演示結束後，我仔細查看了掉落在地上的蚊子。它們的翅膀似乎已經沒有了，但腿和身體看起來完好無損。即使我把手伸到它們正上方揮動，也沒有一隻蚊子能飛起來。
But there is a potentially dangerous catch. A laser powerful enough to disable a mosquito can also damage the human eye.
但存在一個潛在的危險隱患。強大到足以讓蚊子失去行動能力的雷射，同樣可能傷害人眼。
The device, the Laser Mosquito Air Defense System, uses a Class 4 laser, the most powerful classification, typically reserved for industrial applications. Such lasers can cause retinal injuries in less than a second, and their sale and use are tightly regulated.
該設備名為智能雷射蚊蟲防護系統，使用4類雷射——最高功率等級，通常用於工業應用。這類雷射可在不到一秒內造成視網膜損傷，其銷售和使用受到嚴格監管。
廣告
While the United States leads in developing the most advanced A.I. models, the device shows how quickly China is embedding powerful computing in consumer products. The technology also has military potential. It’s not hard to imagine how a system that tracks and shoots down mosquitoes today could be adapted to target drones tomorrow.
儘管美國在開發最先進的人工智慧模型方面處於領先地位，但這款設備表明，中國正以多麼快的速度將強大的計算能力融入消費產品。這項技術也具有軍事應用潛力。不難想像，一套如今能夠追蹤並擊落蚊子的系統，未來也可能經過改造，用於瞄準無人機。
Mr. Wang said that the company was seeking regulatory approval in China, Europe and the United States and that he believed Chinese approval was close.
王川表示，公司正在中國、歐洲和美國申請監管批准，他相信很快就能獲得中國監管部門的批准。
In the United States, manufacturers can begin selling new laser devices after submitting paperwork to the Food and Drug Administration, which does not test the products, said Ken Barat, a former laser safety officer at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory who oversaw the drafting of the world’s technical standards for research lasers.
曾擔任勞倫斯柏克萊國家實驗室雷射安全官、負責主持起草全球科研用雷射技術標準的肯·巴拉特說，在美國，製造商向食品藥品監督管理局(FDA)提交文件後就可以開始銷售新的雷射設備，而FDA並不檢測產品。
Mr. Barat said Photon Matrix’s multiple safeguards could make the device safe. But he cautioned that a Class 4 laser designed to focus on something as tiny as a mosquito could pack “a lot of energy” into a small space.
巴拉特說，光之矩的多重安全防護措施可能使這款設備達到安全使用的要求。但他同時警告說，專門用於瞄準蚊子這樣微小目標的4類雷射可能會將「大量能量」集中在一個極小的區域。
Photon Matrix initially tested less powerful Class 3 lasers but found they could not reliably disable mosquitoes, Mr. Wang said. The company chose a Class 4 laser, limiting each burst to one-hundredth of a second — long enough, he said, to burn off the wings while reducing, but not eliminating, the risk of eye damage. The wings, which have little ability to absorb heat, burn off before the mosquito’s body is heavily affected.
王川說，光之矩最初測試了功率較低的3類雷射，但發現它們無法可靠地使蚊子失去行動能力。公司選擇了4類雷射，並將每次發射的持續時間限制在百分之一秒。他說，這個時長足以燒掉蚊子的翅膀，同時降低眼部受傷的風險，但無法完全消除風險。翅膀吸收熱量的能力很弱，在身體受到嚴重影響之前，翅膀就已被燒毀。
To protect people and pets, an A.I.-enabled camera recognizes them and prevents the laser from firing. The system also identifies reflective surfaces that could send beams bouncing in unpredictable directions. An infrared system provides illumination so the camera can operate at night. A separate radar system detects movement by people or pets in the laser’s path, even in total darkness.
為了保護人和寵物，設備配備了一台搭載人工智慧的攝像頭，能夠識別他們，並阻止雷射發射。系統還能識別反光表面，以免雷射束反射後射向難以預測的方向。紅外系統提供照明，使攝像頭能夠在夜間工作。此外，一套獨立的雷達系統能夠探測雷射路徑上人員或寵物的活動，即使在完全黑暗的環境中也能發揮作用。
Buyers must also pass a 15-question safety test, including questions like which room is least suitable for the device. (The answer: a bathroom, with high humidity, confined space and reflective surfaces.) Only those who answer every question correctly can register a fingerprint to activate the machine.
購買者還必須通過一項包含15道題目的安全測試，其中一道題要求選出最不適合使用該設備的房間。（正確答案是浴室，因為那裡濕度高、空間狹小，而且存在反光表面。）只有全部答對的人才能登記指紋，激活機器。
The mosquito hunting relies on lidar — an acronym for light detection and ranging, a technology widely used in self-driving cars. The device fires 100,000 extremely faint laser pulses a second to map an area in a flat, two-dimensional plane in front of it, tracking moving objects smaller than a pea as they cross that plane.
滅蚊依賴雷射雷達(lidar)，即「光探測與測距」(light detection and ranging)技術，它廣泛應用於自動駕駛汽車。該設備每秒發射10萬次極其微弱的雷射脈衝，對前方一個平面的二維區域進行掃描測繪，並追蹤穿過該平面、體積比豌豆還小的移動物體。
The idea of shooting mosquitoes out of the air with lasers predates the current A.I. boom. In 2010, Intellectual Ventures, a company founded by a former chief technology officer at Microsoft, unveiled a similar anti-mosquito system but never mass-produced it.
用雷射在空中擊落蚊子的想法早在當前這波人工智慧熱潮興起之前就已出現。2010年，由微軟前首席技術官創辦的公司Intellectual Ventures曾發布過一套類似的雷射滅蚊系統，但從未量產。
廣告
Photon Matrix said it had received 5,000 prepaid orders for the device and had secured a contract manufacturer, with mass production expected to begin within weeks.
光之矩表示，已收到5000份預付訂單，並已確定一家合同製造商，預計幾週內開始量產。
Mr. Wang is already planning a next-generation device that would fire even shorter bursts. With a future model, he hopes, the eye’s moisture will absorb the laser energy before it reaches the retina.
王川已經開始規划下一代設備，屆時雷射脈衝的持續時間將進一步縮短。他希望，未來的型號能夠讓人眼中的水分在雷射能量抵達視網膜之前就將其吸收。
