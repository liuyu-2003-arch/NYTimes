失言、發怒、關注「屁股」：川普的言論變得更粗俗

Threats, Gaffes and Men’s Posteriors: Trump’s Talk Gets Wilder

PETER BAKER, DYLAN FREEDMAN • 2026-11-02
As he opened his speech at a recent campaign rally in Greenville, N.C., President Trump told the story of the Willie Horton crime case that helped sink the presidential campaign of the Democrat Michael S. Dukakis in 1988.
最近在北卡羅萊納州格林維爾一場競選集會上開場時， 川普總統講述了威利·霍頓的罪案故事。該案件曾在1988年重創民主黨人麥可·S·杜卡基斯的總統競選。
And then, 30 minutes later, still speaking at the same rally to the same audience from the same lectern, Mr. Trump told the same Willie Horton story all over again, using some of the same language (and getting the facts wrong both times).
30分鐘後，同一場集會、同一群聽眾、同一個講台，川普又把威利·霍頓的故事講了一遍，部分措辭與之前相同，而且兩次都把事實講錯了。
Mr. Trump, who last year became the oldest person ever inaugurated as president and turned 80 in June, has demonstrated physical stamina as he barnstorms the country in advance of midterm elections. But his performances on the road and at the White House this fall have produced many head-scratching moments that have revived questions about his age and stability.
川普去年就職時成為美國歷史上年齡最大的總統，今年6月年滿80歲。他在期中選舉前奔走全國，展現出身體耐力。但今年秋天他在旅途和白宮的表現中出現了許多令人費解的時刻，重新引發了關於他年齡和精神狀態的疑問。
He can be forgetful and repetitive. He is more long-winded, more profane and more absolutist than when he ran for president a decade ago. His rambling speeches seem even more garbled at times. He makes comments that leave people wondering what he is talking about or that sound like he is saying something he is not. And even for a president who has never fully observed the norms of the office, he seems more willing to say things no other president would say.
他會健忘，會重複。與十年前競選總統時相比，他的表達更冗長、更粗俗、更絕對化。他漫無邊際的演講有時變得愈發難以理解。他的一些言論讓人摸不著頭腦，或者聽起來似乎詞不達意。即便對於一位從未完全遵循總統職位規範的總統來說，他似乎也更願意說出其他總統不會說的話。
廣告
He declared that at an unfenced construction site “only a Hispanic could survive that fall.” He wants to ban immigrants from Norway “because I didn’t get the Nobel Prize.” He says “I don’t give a damn” if he is seen as caring more about oil company profits than struggling workers. He mispronounces the Strait of Hormuz as the “Hormone Strait.” He has told supporters to “cheat like hell” in the Nov. 3 elections. He says military leakers “should be shot.” And then there is his baffling fixation with admiring men’s posteriors.
他聲稱，在一個沒有圍欄的建築工地上，「只有西語裔才能那麼摔都沒摔死」。他想禁止挪威移民，「因為我沒拿到諾貝爾獎」。他說自己「毫不在乎」外界是否認為他更關心石油公司利潤而非處境艱難的工人。他把霍爾木茲海峽念成「荷爾蒙海峽」。他讓支持者在11月3日的選舉中「往死裡作弊」。他說軍方洩密者「應該被槍斃」。還有他那種欣賞男性臀部的令人費解的執念。
Even Mr. Trump seems astonished at times by what comes out of his mouth — and by how much his supporters embrace him nonetheless. “I could say the stupidest things and you’d clap. ‘Oh, I love you so,’” he marveled at a recent rally in Clarksville, Tenn. “But we don’t say the stupidest things,” he quickly added. “We say things of common sense. We say things that make sense.”
有時連川普本人似乎都對自己說出口的話感到驚訝，也對支持者依然如此擁戴他感到驚訝。「我可以說最蠢的話，你們照樣鼓掌。『哦，我好愛你啊，』」他在最近田納西州克拉克斯維爾的一場集會上感嘆道。「但我們不說最蠢的話，」他馬上補充道，「我們講常理。我們說有道理的話。」
For Mr. Trump, making sense often places second to having fun at rallies, and some outrageous remarks appear aimed at generating a laugh. His allies say too much is made of comments that are really just jokes. He has always been a provocative speaker, willing to outrage for the sake of outrage. Mr. Trump defends his stream-of-consciousness rambling as a “brilliant” speaking style that he calls “the weave.”
對川普來說，講道理常常要讓位於在集會上找樂子，一些出格言論似乎就是為了博人一笑。他的盟友說，那些話其實只是玩笑，外界過度解讀了。他一直是個挑釁型演講者，願意為激怒而激怒。川普為自己意識流式的漫談辯護，稱這是一種他叫做「編織」的「出色」演講風格。
But the weave seems to be getting weavier. An A.I.-assisted New York Times review of Mr. Trump’s comments from speeches, rallies, social media, interviews and public meetings found significant changes since he began a run for president at age 69 in 2015, although not as much of a notable shift since 2024 when a similar review was conducted. Broadly speaking, the review concluded, Mr. Trump’s public communication is more vulgar, more uninhibited and less coherent than it was a decade ago.
但「編織」似乎越來越「繞」。《紐約時報》藉助人工智慧查驗了川普在演講、集會、社群媒體、採訪和公開會議中的言論，發現自他2015年69歲開始競選總統以來出現了明顯變化，不過與2024年進行類似查驗時相比變化幅度沒有那麼顯著。總體結論是川普的公共表達比十年前更粗俗、更無所顧忌、更缺乏連貫性。
“He’s saying so many stupid things, even for him, so many outrageous and aggressive things,” said former Representative Francis Rooney, a Republican from Florida. “This aggressive kind of attack language, that’s something I’ve never heard from a president.”
「他說了太多蠢話，就算按他的標準也太多，太多出格和攻擊性的話，」佛羅里達州前共和黨眾議員弗朗西斯·魯尼說。「這種攻擊性語言，我從未從任何一位總統口中聽到過。」
The White House denied that Mr. Trump has lost a step. Davis Ingle, a White House spokesman, hailed his “sharpness, unmatched energy, relentless work ethic and historic accessibility” and dismissed concerns about his provocative comments.
白宮否認川普精力衰退。白宮發言人戴維斯·英格爾稱讚川普「敏銳、精力無人能及、工作態度不懈，在可及性上堪稱前無古人」，並駁斥了外界對其挑釁性言論的擔憂。
廣告
“The president doesn’t care about being politically correct, he cares about making America great again,” Mr. Ingle said. “The American people love how authentic, transparent and effective the president is.”
「總統不在乎政治正確，他在乎的是讓美國再次偉大，」英格爾說。「美國人民熱愛總統的真實、透明和高效。」
The ‘Freaking Genius’
「十足的天才」
Measuring the effects of age can be difficult without a medical exam, although that has not stopped many physicians, political commentators and everyday Americans from drawing conclusions about Mr. Trump based on what they see on television.
沒有醫學檢查，很難衡量年齡帶來的影響，但這並沒有阻止許多醫生、政治評論員和普通美國人根據電視上看到的內容對川普下結論。
The Times review from 2015 through Thursday found that the president in his second term has used 19 percent more all-or-nothing words like “always,” “never,” “everything” and “totally” at rallies than he did a decade ago and 5 percent more than in 2024, a sign of black-and-white thinking that experts say often accompanies advancing age or cognitive decline. He is cruder than he used to be, using nearly 15 swear words per 10,000 words compared with 14 two years ago and six a decade ago, a change consistent with age-related disinhibition.
《紐約時報》從2015年到本週四的回顧發現，川普在第二任期集會上使用「總是」「從不」「一切」「完全」等非黑即白詞語的頻率比十年前高19%，比2024年高5%。專家說，這種黑白思維往往伴隨年齡增長或認知衰退出現。他也比以前更粗魯，每1萬個詞中髒話接近15個，兩年前為14個，十年前為6個，這一變化符合因年齡增長導致自我抑制力減退（脫抑制）的特徵。
The review, relying on an A.I.-assisted metric, found that he makes rambling or confusing digressions in his rallies and speeches two and a half times as often in his second term as in the 2016 campaign, although roughly the same as in 2024. His vocabulary remains about the same, as does the reading level of his speaking, which hovers between fourth and fifth grade. (The average modern president speaks at an eighth-grade level.)
本次回顧採用人工智慧輔助指標，發現川普在第二任期的集會和演講中漫無邊際或令人困惑的離題頻率是2016年競選期間的2.5倍，但與2024年大致相同。他的詞彙量基本沒變，演講的易讀性也大致沒變，徘徊在四五年級水平。（現代總統的平均演講水平為八年級。）
Mr. Trump’s public schedule is thinner, down to 1.3 events per weekday versus 2.1 in the first term. But he is posting online an average of 21 times a day, up from 13 a day in 2016 although down from 34 a day two years ago. The night owl president has posted messages between midnight and 5 a.m. on 30 percent of his nights in his second term and has had 240 bursts of 10 or more posts an hour.
川普的公開日程更稀疏了，從第一任期每個工作日2.1場活動降至1.3場。但他在網上平均每天發帖21次，高於2016年的13次，低於兩年前的34次。這位夜貓子總統在第二任期有30%的夜晚在午夜至凌晨5點之間發帖，並有240次在一小時內發帖10條以上。
廣告
The president’s doctor has said that Mr. Trump is mentally sharp. In a three-page summary released in May, Dr. Sean P. Barbabella, the White House physician, noted that Mr. Trump had scored 30 out of 30 on the Montreal Cognitive Assessment. “Cognitive and physical performance are excellent,” he wrote.
總統的醫生表示川普頭腦敏銳。在5月發布的一份三頁摘要中，白宮醫生肖恩·P·巴爾巴貝拉指出，川普在蒙特婁認知評估中得分30分，滿分30分。「認知和身體表現均出色，」他寫道。
The Montreal assessment is a quick test to screen for Alzheimer’s disease or other cognitive impairment and includes basic questions like identifying a lion, camel and rhinoceros; drawing a clock; repeating words or numbers in order; and naming as many words as possible in one minute that start with the letter F. “Nobody can do this,” Mr. Trump insisted a couple weeks ago, adding: “But I can. You know why? Because I’m a freaking genius!”
蒙特婁評估是一種快速篩查阿爾茨海默病或其他認知障礙的測試，包括辨認獅子、駱駝和犀牛，畫鍾，按順序複述詞語或數字，以及在一分鐘內盡可能多地說出以字母F開頭的詞等基本題目。「沒人做得到這個，」川普幾週前堅稱，隨後補充道：「但我能。知道為什麼嗎？因為我是個十足的天才！」
‘I Don’t Give a Damn’
「我毫不在乎」
While Mr. Trump has been known to nod off during White House meetings, he draws energy from crowds when he is on the road. He speaks and speaks and speaks — twice as long as in 2016, according to the Times review, for an average of 85 minutes per rally compared with 42 minutes a decade ago.
川普以在白宮會議上打瞌睡而出名，但一到外面就從人群中汲取能量。據時報的回顧，他講個不停——時長是2016年的兩倍，平均每場集會85分鐘，十年前為42分鐘。
He is profoundly and proudly undisciplined. He says things that are manifestly unhelpful to him. Even as many Americans are upset about the cost of filling their gas tank, he said in Perry, Ga., the other day that high oil prices were good because the fossil fuel industry was making hundreds of billions of dollars. Even as he said it, he acknowledged it would produce bad headlines. “I don’t give a damn,” he said. “I’m going to say it. We’re making a freaking fortune.”
他極度缺乏自律，而且對此引以為豪。他說一些明顯對自己不利的話。就在許多美國人為加油成本煩惱之際，他前幾天在喬治亞州佩里說，高油價是好事，因為化石燃料行業正在賺取數千億美元。話一出口，他自己也承認這會帶來負面新聞。「我毫不在乎，」他說。「我就是要說。我們正在賺大錢。」
He wanders from thought to thought, often without finishing a sentence, embedding one off-the-cuff story within another. In Pendleton, S.C., he started talking about transgender athletes, interrupted to tell a story about someone he met in Israel, referred to a poll without explaining its results and then riffed on climate change.
他從一個念頭跳到另一個念頭，常常一句話沒說完就嵌入另一個即興故事。在南卡羅來納州彭德爾頓，他先談跨性別運動員，中途插進一段在以色列遇到某人的故事，提到一項民調卻不解釋結果，接著又即興談起氣候變化。

‘He’s All Man’
「他是個純爺們」
Like many people in their ninth decade, he is prone to say things that seem inappropriate. His focus on the male anatomy, for instance, has become remarkably pronounced lately.
像許多80多歲的人一樣，他容易說一些似乎不合時宜的話。比如，他對男性身體的關注最近變得格外突出。
“I don’t do this with men very often,” Mr. Trump said at a rally with Senator Jon Husted, Republican of Ohio, on Oct. 12. “I started examining his body because I didn’t know he was a great athlete. And I looked and I watched up and down. I looked at his ass.”
「我不常對男人這樣，」川普10月12日在俄亥俄州共和黨參議員喬恩·赫斯特德同台的集會上說。「我開始打量他的身體，因為我不知道他是個優秀運動員。我上看下看。我看了他的屁股。」
Five days later, he repeated his appreciation of Mr. Husted’s backside. “I examined his ass,” Mr. Trump said at a rally in Kentucky. “I never did that with, no, I never did that with a man before.” Three days later in North Carolina, he praised Mr. Husted’s derrière again. “His ass is, it’s like a pile of muscle,” he said. In Chillicothe, he did it again: “I started by looking at his ass, and it was strong as hell.”
五天後，他又表達了對赫斯特德的臀部的欣賞。「我打量了他的屁股，」川普在肯塔基州一場集會上說。「我以前從沒這樣過，沒有，我以前從沒對男人這樣過。」三天後在北卡羅萊納州，他再次稱讚赫斯特德的臀部。「他的屁股，就像一堆肌肉，」他說。在奇利科西，他又來了一次：「我先是看他的屁股，強壯得很。」
Mr. Husted is not the only object of Mr. Trump’s manly approval. Among the others:
赫斯特德並非川普男性化讚賞的唯一對象。其他還包括：
Senator Tim Scott, Republican of South Carolina: “As much as I can fall in love with a man, which isn’t that much, I fall in love with him, and his name happens to be Tim Scott.”
南卡羅來納州共和黨參議員蒂姆·史考特：「能讓我愛上的男人能有幾個——沒多少——但我愛上了他，他正好叫蒂姆·史考特。」
廣告
The spouse of Senator Darline Graham, the other South Carolina Republican: “He’s a good-looking husband.”
另一位南卡羅來納州共和黨參議員達琳·格雷厄姆的配偶：「他是個英俊的丈夫。」
Robert F. Kennedy Jr., the health and human services secretary: “He’s got the body of a 30-year-old.”
小羅伯特·F·甘迺迪，衛生與公眾服務部長：「他有一副30歲的身體。」
Representative Mike Collins, Republican of Georgia: “Mike Collins is a good-looking man. And he’s all man.”
喬治亞州共和黨眾議員邁克·柯林斯：「邁克·柯林斯是個英俊的男人。而且他是個純爺們。」
In Michigan on Sunday, Mr. Trump made a point of praising a woman’s backside for a change. “I looked at one hell of a beautiful ass before — Nicki Minaj,” he told the crowd. “That’s hard to miss.”
週日在密西根州，川普破例誇了一位女性的臀部。「我以前看過一個極其漂亮的屁股——妮琪·米娜，」他告訴人群。「想不注意都難。」
A Quick Temper
暴躁易怒
Mr. Trump has always displayed fits of public anger in ways that other presidents have not. But he seems even more prone to pounce these days.
川普向來會當眾發怒，方式與其他總統不同。但他如今似乎更容易突然發難。
He snaps at reporters so often now that he barely waits for their questions before calling them “sick” and ordering, “Quiet!” When a reporter asked on Thursday if he was canceling a trip to Kansas because he was tired of traveling, Mr. Trump accosted his physical appearance. “You are disgusting! Put yourself in shape. You look like hell.”
他現在斥責記者如此頻繁，幾乎不等對方開口就先罵「有病」，再喝令「安靜！」週四，一名記者問他是否因為厭倦出行而取消堪薩斯之行，川普轉而攻擊對方的外貌。「你真噁心！去練練身材。你看起來糟透了。」
His exhortations to supporters to vote have grown more vulgar and violent as well. “If you do that,” he said in Vandalia, Ohio, “we’re going to win and we’re going to win big. And we’re going to shove it up their ass, OK?”
他對支持者投票的鼓動也變得更粗俗、更暴力。「你們要是這麼做，」他在俄亥俄州萬達利亞說，「我們就會贏，會大贏。咱們就給它塞他們的屁股裡，是吧？」
He depicts himself as all-powerful. “I can do whatever I want,” he told reporters in the Oval Office recently. As if to prove it, he regularly tries to rename bodies of water after America or himself. He demanded that tech companies stop using the term artificial intelligence, preferring instead “super intelligence”; those that disobey, he wrote online, are “THE ENEMY!”
他把自己描繪成無所不能。「我想做什麼就做什麼，」他最近在橢圓形辦公室對記者說。彷彿為了證明這一點，他經常試圖以美國或自己的名字重新命名水域。他要求科技公司停止使用「人工智慧」一詞，改用「超級智能」；他在網上寫道，不服從的公司就是「敵人！」
These days, even Mr. Trump seems aware that eyes are on him, watching and waiting. “I love the weave,” he told the midterm Republican convention in Dallas in September. “You know what the weave is? You talk about something, then you bring in a ballroom or you bring in an airplane or you bring in Air Force One. But you notice I always get right back to the same topic.”
如今，連川普本人似乎也意識到大家都在盯著他，看他會有什麼表現。「我喜歡編織，」他9月在達拉斯對中期共和黨大會說。「知道什麼是編織嗎？你談一件事，然後扯進一個宴會廳，或者一架飛機，或者空軍一號。但你會發現我總是回到同一個話題。」
And yet, he went on, “when someday, there’ll be a time when I do the weave is weave, weave. Someday, there’ll be a time when I don’t get back to the original subject, and then you can say he’s had it. OK? You can say then he’s had it.”
但他接著說，「總有一天，會有一個時候，我編織起來就是編織、編織、編織。總有一天，會有一個時候，我沒回到最初的話題，那時候你們就可以說他不行了。好嗎？到時候你們就可以說他不行了。」

方法論

時報利用Roll Call的Factba.se從2015年到今年10月下旬的數據，收集了川普數千份演講、採訪、社群媒體帖子和日程。為量化川普對非黑即白語言和髒話的使用，我們統計了他各總統任期和競選期間的基本詞頻。為識別令人困惑和漫無邊際的段落，我們使用人工智慧模型Claude Opus 5.5，根據我們此前報導中分析川普漫談和粗俗語言的詳細指引，對超過6500段川普演講進行評分。按照這套評分標準——它基於對數百段文字的人工審查以及為確保高準確性所做的評估——一段演講得分至少達到七分（滿分10分），即計為不連貫。

Peter Baker是《紐約時報》首席白宮記者。川普是他報導的第六任美國總統，有時他也撰寫將總統和美國政府置於更宏觀的背景和歷史框架下的分析性文章。
