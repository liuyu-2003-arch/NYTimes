調查記者劉虎及同事因報導貪腐被拘

Two Chinese Journalists Are Detained for Reporting on Corruption

ANDREW HIGGINS, JOY DONG • 2026-02-05
Amid a rolling purge of officials and generals accused of corruption, the Chinese authorities have detained two investigative journalists — for writing about corruption.
在對被控貪腐的官員和軍隊將領進行持續清洗同時，中國當局拘留了兩名調查記者——原因是他們關於貪腐的報導。
China’s leader, Xi Jinping, warned after taking power in 2012 that graft would spell “the end of the party and the end of the state” if left unchecked. He has since presided over a yearslong drive to root out what he sees as wayward officials and military officers.
中國領導人習近平在2012年上台後曾警告，如果任由腐敗蔓延不加遏制，將導致「亡黨亡國」。自那以後，他領導了一場持續多年的運動，旨在肅清他眼中違紀的官員和軍方將領。
But the targets of that effort, which escalated dramatically last month with the purge of a general second only to Mr. Xi in the military command, have all been chosen by the ruling Communist Party, which allows scant space for independent investigations by journalists or others.
但該行動的打擊對象（一名軍中指揮權地位僅次於習近平的將領上個月被清洗，這一行動顯著升級）完全由執政的共產黨選定，它幾乎不給記者或其他人士留有進行獨立調查的空間。
In a sign that the party is determined to keep its monopoly on the policing of corrupt officials, a prominent investigative journalist, Liu Hu, and a colleague, Wu Yingjiao, were detained this week after posting an account of official corruption in the southwestern province of Sichuan.
在網上發表了一篇關於中國西南省份四川的官員腐敗報導後，知名調查記者劉虎及其同事巫英蛟於本週被拘留。這表明中共決心維持對反腐行動的壟斷權力。
廣告
Police in Chengdu, the capital of Sichuan, said in a statement that a 50-year-old man surnamed Liu and a 34-year old man surnamed Wu had been detained on suspicion of “making false accusations” and conducting “illegal business operations.”
四川省會成都警方在聲明中稱，50歲的劉姓男子和34歲的巫姓男子因涉嫌「誣告陷害」和從事「非法經營」，已被依法採取刑事強制措施。
Mr. Liu’s lawyer, Zhou Ze, confirmed the identities of the two detained men on the messaging app WeChat. He wrote that he had visited Mr. Liu on Wednesday at a Chengdu detention center, adding that the case against his client appeared to be based solely on the article he coauthored with Mr. Wu.
劉虎的律師周澤在社交應用微信的朋友圈確認了這兩名被拘留者的身份。他寫道，自己已於週三在成都的一家看守所會見了劉虎，並補充說，針對其當事人的指控似乎完全是基於劉虎與巫英蛟共同署名的那篇文章。
The article, which was published online days before the men were detained, described what they said were the legally dubious, bullying practices of a local official in Pujiang County in Sichuan. The official, they wrote, had a long record of forcibly seizing property from businessmen. The article has since been taken offline by Chinese censors.
這篇文章在兩人被拘留前數日在網上發表，文中描述了他們所稱的四川蒲江縣一名地方官涉嫌違法和霸凌行為。他們寫道，這名官員長期以來存在強佔商人財產的行為。這篇文章隨後被刪除。
The journalists’ detention followed a written warning to Mr. Liu from the Chengdu branch of the Discipline Inspection Commission, a party body responsible for investigating corruption, that “reports against public officials should be made through legal channels and in a legal manner.”
在被拘之前，成都市紀委（負責腐敗調查的黨內機構）曾向劉虎發出書面警告，稱「對於公職人員的舉報要通過合法管道和合理方式進行」。
Mr. Liu responded that “the article we openly published is neither a report nor a complaint, so there’s no need for your reminder.” The New York Times viewed a screenshot of the messages.
劉虎回應稱：「我們公開發布的文章不是舉報，也不屬信訪，無需貴機關提醒。」《紐約時報》查看了相關信息的截圖。
China’s investigative reporters once provided rare voices of accountability and criticism in a society tightly controlled by the party, exposing scandals about babies sickened by tainted formula, shoddy buildings toppled by earthquakes and blood-selling schemes backed by the government.
在這個受黨嚴密控制的社會，中國的調查記者曾提供了罕見的問責和批評之聲。他們曾揭露過毒奶粉致嬰患病醜聞、在地震中倒塌的豆腐渣工程，以及政府背後支持的賣血計劃。
But under Mr. Xi, such independent coverage has largely disappeared, with only a handful of journalists left who struggle to push the boundaries. The authorities have harassed and imprisoned dozens of reporters, and news outlets have cut back on in-depth reporting. Censors routinely scrub the internet of downbeat reports about the state of the Chinese economy, while party-controlled media outlets are filled with cheery accounts of life under Mr. Xi — and scoffing reports about the misery of life in the West.
但在習近平上台後，這類獨立報導已基本消失，只剩下少數記者仍在艱難地試探紅線。當局騷擾並監禁了數十名記者，新聞機構也削減了深度報導。審查機構定期清除互聯網上關於中國經濟狀況的負面報導；與此同時，受黨控制的媒體充斥著對習近平領導下中國生活的讚美，以及對西方水深火熱的嘲諷。
廣告
Mr. Liu once worked for government-backed media in the southern province of Guangdong as an investigative reporter, but he went freelance after being detained in Beijing in 2013. At that time, he was held for nearly a year, accused of “picking quarrels and provoking trouble” and of “fabricating and spreading rumors.”
劉虎曾在中國南方省份廣東一家有政府背景的媒體擔任調查記者，但在2013年於北京被拘留後轉為自由撰稿人。當時，他被指控「尋釁滋事」和「製造傳播謠言」，被關押了近一年。
After his release, his work as a freelancer included an investigation into the corruption case of Zhang Jiahui, the vice president of the Hainan High Court, who was later sentenced to 18 years in prison. He also worked on a series about businessmen who were wrongly arrested as leaders of organized crime gangs and whose assets were seized by cash-hungry local governments. In one such case, a serious charge against a Sichuan developer that Mr. Liu had written about was withdrawn.
出獄後，劉虎以自由撰稿人的身份繼續開展調查，其中包括對海南省高級人民法院副院長張家慧腐敗案的調查。張家慧後來被判處有期徒刑18年。此外，他還撰寫了一系列報導，揭露一些商人被錯誤地指控為有組織犯罪團伙頭目而被捕，其資產被財政吃緊的地方政府收繳。在其中一個案例中，劉虎曾報導過的一名四川開發商面臨的嚴重指控最終被撤銷。
Reporters Without Borders, a group that lobbies for media freedom, denounced the detention of Mr. Liu and his colleague, saying in a statement that it “highlights just how restrictive and hostile China has become toward independent reporting.”
致力於推動新聞自由的無國界記者組織譴責了對劉虎及其同事的拘留，並在聲明中表示，此案「凸顯了中國對獨立報導的環境已變得何等嚴苛且充滿敵意」。
It added, “Under Xi Jinping’s leadership, control over news coverage has tightened to near-totalitarian levels, with independent journalists treated as a threat to the state.”
聲明還稱：「在習近平的領導下，對新聞報導的控制已收緊至近乎極權的程度，獨立記者被視為對國家的威脅。」
Even a news outlet affiliated with the government of a Chinese province, Shandong, raised questions about the detentions, urging the authorities to handle the case fairly. In a commentary published on Tuesday, the outlet wrote that “silence is not conducive to solving problems.”
甚至連一家有山東省政府背景的媒體也對此次拘留提出了質疑，呼籲當局公正處理此案。該媒體在週二發表的評論中寫道：「沉默無益於問題解決。」
Asked about the detentions at a news briefing in Beijing, a spokesperson for the Chinese foreign ministry said, “China is a country ruled by law, and Chinese judicial organs handle cases according to law. Everyone is equal in the face of the law.”
在北京的例行記者會上，當被問及此事時，中國外交部發言人表示：「中國是法治國家，中國司法機關依法辦案。法律面前人人平等。」
廣告
In an interview with The Times several years after his earlier arrest, Mr. Liu lamented the rapidly shrinking space for independent journalists. “We’re almost extinct,” he said, “No one is left to reveal the truth.”
在上一次被捕幾年後接受《紐約時報》採訪時，劉虎曾哀嘆獨立記者生存空間迅速縮小。他說：「調查記者都快要絕種了，很多的真相就沒人去揭露。」

Andrew Higgins是《紐約時報》駐華沙的東歐和中歐分社社長，現臨時派駐上海。
