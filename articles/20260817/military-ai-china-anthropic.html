人工智慧軍備競賽：中國快速進步，美國政策搖擺

The U.S. Military Wants A.I. Dominance. Feuds and China May Thwart It.

DAVID E. SANGER, DUSTIN VOLZ, ANA SWANSON, JULIAN E. BARNES • 2026-08-17
In mid-July, many of the country’s biggest military contractors received a letter from the Air Force with a stern warning: By Sept. 1, all the software they use for weapons and control systems must be free of products built by the A.I. company Anthropic. Failure to comply would put all their business with the Pentagon at risk.
7月中旬，美國眾多大型軍事承包商收到了一封來自空軍措辭嚴厲的信函，信中寫道：9月1日前，所有用於武器和控制系統的軟體必須徹底清除人工智慧公司Anthropic開發的產品。若不遵守這一要求，它們與五角大廈的所有業務都將面臨終止風險。
Within a month, those same contractors received an unexpected reversal: They could — for now — disregard the earlier instructions about purging Anthropic.
然而不到一個月，這些承包商又收到了一紙出人意料的撤回令：他們可以——至少在目前——無視先前關於清除Anthropic產品的指令。
It was one more example of the chaos and contradictions in the tsunami of A.I. disruptions that have swept through the national security establishment in recent months. Agencies like the Pentagon, which still use airplanes designed in the 1950s, are struggling furiously to keep up with A.I. models whose powers multiply in a matter of months.
這再次凸顯出近幾個月來席捲國家安全機構的人工智慧衝擊浪潮帶來的混亂與矛盾。五角大廈這樣的機構至今仍在使用上世紀50年代設計的飛機，卻要拚命追趕以月為單位能力飛速躍升的人工智慧模型。
The tension has been magnified in the case of Anthropic, whose chief executive, Dario Amodei, has been locked in a public feud with Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth over restrictions the company tried to put on the technology’s use by the world’s most powerful military.
在Anthropic的案例中，這種緊張關係進一步加劇。該公司曾試圖對美軍使用其技術施加限制，國防部長彼特·海格塞斯為此與其首席執行官達里奧·阿莫代伊公開交惡。
廣告
Without Anthropic’s powerful Mythos model, it would be far more difficult for the National Security Agency to find and patch vulnerabilities in the Pentagon’s own systems, senior N.S.A. officials argued. And it turned out that, despite the original Anthropic ban, the N.S.A. was still allowed to use its products.
國家安全局高級官員認為，若失去Anthropic強大的Mythos模型，要發現和修復五角大廈自身系統的安全漏洞將困難得多。事實上，儘管最初下令禁用Anthropic，國家安全局仍被允許繼續使用該公司的產品。
Critical tests were underway using Mythos to gauge its potential to hack into highly protected foreign networks, current and former officials said. Shutting down the use of Mythos, an A.I. model whose potential for conducting offensive cyberoperations is already shifting the digital arms race into overdrive, would have amounted to “unilateral disarmament,” according to one recently departed senior U.S. official.
現任及前任官員說，當時正在用Mythos進行關鍵測試，以評估它入侵高度防護的外國網路的潛力。按照一名近期離職的美國高級官員的說法，停止使用Mythos將相當於「單方面解除武裝」。Mythos已經具備實施進攻性網路行動的潛力，這讓數字軍備競賽進一步提速。
From the Department of Defense to the C.I.A. and N.S.A., billions have already been spent on using artificial intelligence to develop weapons that could work with less human control, or to test vulnerable military networks and even nuclear codes to make sure they cannot be cracked by adversarial A.I. systems. Artificial intelligence is critical to the Golden Dome, President Trump’s vision — fanciful to many experts — of a space-based missile shield.
從國防部到中央情報局和國家安全局，政府已投入數十億美元，利用人工智慧研發可減少人工干預的自主武器、測試軍事網路漏洞，甚至測試核密碼，以確保這些系統不會被敵方的人工智慧攻破。人工智慧對川普總統提出的「金穹」太空導彈防禦願景也至關重要——儘管在許多專家看來，這一構想近乎天方夜譚。
But little of that work anticipated the powers of Mythos and its A.I. counterparts, or the prospect that China may be months away from its own formidable and less expensive versions. That could supercharge China’s ability to infiltrate American communications networks, water systems and power grids like those they successfully pierced during the Biden administration in two sweeping attacks called “Volt Typhoon” and “Salt Typhoon.”
但此前的這些工作幾乎都沒有預料到Mythos及其同類人工智慧模型所具備的能力，也沒有充分考慮到中國可能只需幾個月就能擁有強大且成本更低的版本。這可能會大大增強中國滲透美國的通信網路、供水系統和電網的能力——正如中國在拜登政府時期發起的兩次大規模攻擊（「伏特颱風」和「鹽颱風」）中成功做到的那樣。
“These are the kinds of things we were talking about and worrying about before A.I. became a daily headline,” said Jen Easterly, who ran the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency during the Biden administration. “What’s different now, and what makes the problem more urgent, is the potential for A.I. to amplify those capabilities.”
「這些都是我們在人工智慧成為每日頭條之前就在談論和擔憂的事情，」拜登政府時期擔任網路安全與基礎設施安全局局長的珍·伊斯特利說。「而現在之所以不同、問題之所以變得更加緊迫，原因在於人工智慧可能對這些能力產生的放大效應。」
So far the Trump administration has veered wildly in response, at first abandoning its hands-off approach to regulating the industry, then briefly shutting off access for foreigners from Mythos — including some of its inventors — then lifting that ban.
迄今為止，川普政府的應對搖擺不定：先是放棄了對這一行業的不干預態度，隨後一度切斷外國人（包括Mythos的部分發明者）訪問該模型的管道，繼而又解除了這一禁令。
廣告
Earlier this month, the administration called in executives from the largest “frontier” creators, including OpenAI and Anthropic, and told them that new models would be reviewed by the government to assure they could not be used to make biological weapons, or break into classified American systems or the communications networks that control nuclear weapons.
本月早些時候，政府召集了包括OpenAI和Anthropic在內的大型「前沿」人工智慧模型開發企業的高管，告知他們新模型將接受政府審查，以確保它們不能被用於製造生物武器，或入侵美國的機密系統及控制核武器的通信網路。
(The New York Times has sued OpenAI and Microsoft, claiming copyright infringement of news content related to A.I. systems. The two companies have denied those claims.)
（《紐約時報》已對OpenAI和微軟提起訴訟，指控其侵犯與人工智慧系統相關的新聞內容版權。兩家公司否認相關指控。）
But the government review process exempts “open” models — including many of those now produced by Chinese firms — whose code can be customized by users.
但政府審查流程豁免了「開放」模型（包括許多由中國公司開發的模型），這類模型的代碼可由用戶自行修改。
One senior executive of a company briefed on the new policy said it made no sense, noting that the open models can be just as dangerous. Like many in the industry, the executive would not be quoted, for fear of angering administration officials.
一家獲悉新政策的企業高級主管表示，這一政策毫無道理，因為開放模型同樣可能帶來危險。與許多業內人士一樣，該主管不願具名，以免觸怒政府官員。
Pentagon officials reject the idea they are not agile enough. They say the U.S. military has maintained immediate access to the world’s most advanced A.I. systems, despite the Anthropic ban. An official, who discussed Pentagon policies on the condition of anonymity, said the Defense Department had recently integrated a version of a Google Gemini model within four days of its commercial release.
五角大廈否認自身應變不足。他們表示，儘管遭遇了Anthropic禁令，美國軍方依然維持著對全球最先進人工智慧系統的即時訪問能力。一名以匿名為條件討論五角大廈政策的官員表示，國防部近期在谷歌Gemini一款新模型商業發布後四天內完成了整合部署。
More than 1,300 A.I. workers have called for the country to slow development. But others, including inside the Trump administration, have answered that call with a question: “What about China?” They fear losing the roughly six-month lead they believe the United States maintains.
超過1300名人工智慧從業者呼籲美國放緩研發步伐。包括川普政府內部人士在內的其他人則以一個問題回應：「那中國呢？」他們擔心美國會失去據評估目前保持的約六個月領先優勢。
That concern has touched off arguments about whether export controls on some of the most powerful chips made by Nvidia, the leading American maker, are effective in keeping the Chinese from developing the computing power they need to catch up. Controls could also just prompt Chinese makers to obtain the chips on the black market, or double down on development of their own.
這種擔憂也引發了爭論：美國針對領先晶片製造商英偉達部分最強大晶片的出口限制是否能有效阻止中國獲得追趕所需的算力。出口管制也可能適得其反，促使中國企業轉而通過黑市獲取晶片，或者進一步加大自主研發力度。
It is easy to compare this moment in American national security to the early days of the Cold War, when the United States was the only nation to possess nuclear weapons, and it was struggling to delay the day the Soviet Union would match the accomplishment. (The first Soviet bomb was tested in late August of 1949, four years after Hiroshima and Nagasaki.) The C.I.A.’s director, John Ratcliffe, recently reached for just that comparison, saying it would “not be misplaced” to refer to the capabilities of A.I. as “akin to digital nuclear weapons.”
當下的美國國家安全形勢很容易讓人聯想到冷戰初期。當時美國是世界上唯一擁有核武器的國家，並竭力拖延蘇聯趕上。（廣島和長崎遭遇原子彈襲擊四年後，蘇聯在1949年8月底試爆了第一顆原子彈。）中央情報局局長約翰·拉特克利夫最近也援用了這一類比，稱把人工智慧的能力「視為數字核武器」並「不算言過其實」。
廣告
But like most such historical analogies, this one has its limits. Nuclear weapons were solely in the hands of the state back then. And the fundamental technology of building and delivering them has changed little in 80 years since.
但如同大多數歷史類比，這種比較也存在局限。核武器當時完全掌握在國家手中，且製造和投送核武器的基本技術80年來幾乎沒有變化。
A.I. is being built in the private sector, and available to all. Many of the most powerful models are updated weekly, their powers, and unpredictability, discovered along the way. China is perhaps six months or less behind the United States in the development of the most advanced models, U.S. experts estimate, though probably years behind in making the advanced semiconductors needed to power A.I.’s development.
人工智慧卻是在私營部門開發的，並向所有人開放。許多最強大的模型每週都在更新，其能力和不可預測性也是在不斷發展過程中才逐漸被發現。美國專家估計，中國在最先進人工智慧模型的研發方面可能只落後美國六個月甚至更短時間，但在製造支撐人工智慧發展的先進半導體方面，中國可能仍落後美國數年。
But already there is evidence America’s main technological and economic rival is unnerved by the powers being unleashed. Privately some Chinese officials have already been talking about some forms of arms control, even if no one knows how that might work.
但已有證據表明，美國的主要技術和經濟對手也對正在釋放的能力感到不安。一些中國官員私下已在討論某種形式的軍備控制，儘管無人知曉這如何實現。
“They realize there is a gap in capability that may play to our strengths,” said Evan S. Medeiros, a professor at Georgetown University and a former National Security Council official. “On nuclear arms control and on missile defense, we can’t get them to engage. But they appear more interested in the case of A.I.”
「他們意識到存在能力差距，這可能對我們有利，」喬治城大學教授、曾任美國國家安全委員會官員的麥艾文(Evan S. Medeiros)說。「在核軍控和導彈防禦問題上，我們始終無法讓他們坐到談判桌旁。但在人工智慧問題上，他們似乎更有意願。」
Mr. Trump has said that will be a topic of discussion, again, when he meets President Xi Jinping in Washington in late September. The two men are scheduled to see each other again twice, later in the year.
川普曾表示，當他9月底在華盛頓與中國國家主席習近平會面時，人工智慧將再次成為討論議題。預計雙方在今年晚些時候還有兩次會晤。
But when he talks about A.I., Mr. Trump has neither explained his objectives nor indicated any concerns in recent weeks that A.I. agents are finding ways to break out of their confines, or acting autonomously to break into outside companies or networks. When news came out that artificial intelligence was used to design a new virus, not found in nature — a development that could herald real medicinal progress or a biological weapon — the White House said nothing.
但談及人工智慧時，川普從未闡明其政策目標，也未在近期表達對人工智慧代理程序尋求突破自身限制、或自主入侵外部企業和網路等問題的憂慮。當有消息稱人工智慧被用於設計一種自然界不存在的新病毒——這一發展既可能預示真正的醫學進步，也可能預示生物武器，白宮未作任何回應。
廣告
But one thing is clear: Many of the hypothetical scenarios that concerned the national security community a year ago have begun to arrive.
但有一點是明確的：一年前讓國家安全界擔憂的許多假設性情景已經開始成為現實。
At the Pentagon, Punishing Anthropic Goes Astray
在五角大廈，懲罰Anthropic的舉措事與願違
Within the defense industry, the chaos kicked off by Mr. Hegseth and his aides over stripping Anthropic code has only deepened. A month after the Air Force told its contractors to expunge all Anthropic code, it sent another message, telling them to “stand by,” at least for now.
海格塞斯及其助手清除Anthropic代碼在國防業內引發的混亂只增不減。空軍在通知承包商清除所有Anthropic代碼一個月後，又發送了另一條消息，告訴他們「暫緩執行」——至少目前如此。
“At this time, you are not required to remove from your inventory Anthropic products or services that interface with the Department of the Air Force systems and networks,” read the update, a copy of which was seen by The Times. It warned that the latest guidance could be reversed yet again depending on the outcome of litigation.
《紐約時報》獲得的一份更新通知寫道：「目前，不要求你們從庫存中移除與空軍部和空軍系統及網路對接的Anthropic產品或服務。」通知同時警告，最新指引可能根據訴訟結果再次被推翻。
The back-and-forth instructions reflected Mr. Hegseth’s dilemma: He is trying to punish a leading A.I. juggernaut deeply embedded in the defense-industrial complex, without handicapping the military’s national security mission.
這些反覆無常的指令反映了海格塞斯的困境：他試圖懲罰一家深度嵌入軍工複合體的領先人工智慧巨頭，同時又不能妨礙軍方的國家安全使命。
Asked about the apparent about-face, a Pentagon official described the new Air Force guidance as temporary and said the mandate to purge Anthropic across the Department of Defense was absolute.
當被問及這一明顯反轉時，一名五角大廈官員稱新空軍指引是臨時性的，並表示在國防部範圍內清除Anthropic產品的指令不會打折扣。
廣告
Publicly, the Pentagon has refused to relent. In interviews, Pentagon officials have suggested that the animosity between their department and Anthropic began when the Silicon Valley firm began making what the military saw as ridiculous demands about how the Claude for Government model, a version of the popular, publicly available A.I. model, would be used. Senior officials said Anthropic presented them with 25 pages of restrictions.
公開層面，五角大廈拒絕讓步。在接受採訪時，五角大廈官員暗示，該部與Anthropic之間的敵意始於這家矽谷公司開始提出一些軍方認為荒謬的要求——關於Claude for Government模型（一個廣受歡迎的公開人工智慧模型的特殊版本）應如何使用。高級官員稱，Anthropic向他們提交了25頁的限制條款。
After several contentious months, those 25 pages were boiled down to two restrictions. In a February meeting at the Pentagon, Mr. Amodei told Mr. Hegseth that the company would not provide any products to the military unless he had assurances they would not be used for domestic surveillance of Americans or to produce fully autonomous weapons that were not ultimately controlled by human oversight.
經過幾個月的爭執，這25頁被縮減為兩項限制。在2月五角大廈的一次會議上，阿莫代伊告訴海格塞斯，除非軍方明確保證，這些產品不會被用於對美國人的國內監控或生產最終不受人類監督的完全自主武器，否則公司不會向軍方提供任何產品。
It was not enough. The defense secretary got his back up, noting that Raytheon doesn’t dictate limits about how the missiles it produces can be employed against adversaries; those decisions, Mr. Hegseth said, were completely in the realm of government officials, not corporate executives. When Mr. Amodei refused to relent, the Pentagon declared the company was a “supply chain risk” — and banned for military use.
但這並沒有解決問題。國防部長態度強硬，指出雷神公司無權決定其生產的導彈如何用於對付敵人；海格塞斯表示，這些決定完全屬於政府官員的職權範圍，而非企業高管。當阿莫代伊拒絕讓步時，五角大廈宣布該公司構成「供應鏈風險」——並禁止軍事使用。
In May, Emil Michael, the undersecretary of defense for research and engineering, told a Times reporter that Anthropic deserved what it got, and that Pentagon made a mistake by over-relying on a single producer of A.I. programs — even one whose products seemed ahead of both competitors and of China. Asked whether there was anything the company could do to get back in the department’s good graces, Mr. Michael said “not at the Department of War.”
今年5月，負責研究和工程的國防部副部長埃米爾·麥可對《紐約時報》記者表示，Anthropic是自作自受，而五角大廈過度依賴單一人工智慧程序供應商是個錯誤，即使其產品似乎領先於競爭對手和中國。當被問及該公司能做些什麼來重新獲得國防部的好感時，麥可表示：「在戰爭部就別指望了。」
Pentagon officials say they have all but completed the removal of Anthropic from military computing systems. Close to 100 percent of military systems that once used “Claude for Government,” amended for use on classified networks, have now replaced Anthropic’s product with other frontier models, according to officials. In addition, the Pentagon has ceased all use of Anthropic tools in its Maven system, which helps analyze intelligence and suggests targets for airstrikes in the war with Iran.
五角大廈官員稱，他們已基本完成從軍事計算系統中移除Anthropic的工作。據官員稱，曾經使用「Claude for Government」的軍事系統中，已有接近100%的系統用其他前沿模型替換了Anthropic的產品——該版本曾經過修改用於機密網路。此外，五角大廈已在其Maven系統中停止使用所有Anthropic工具——該系統用於分析情報並為對伊朗戰爭中的空襲提供目標建議。
The military contractors affected by the ban have been reticent to discuss it, perhaps in fear of angering their biggest customer. In a statement, a Lockheed spokeswoman declined to comment directly on the letter it received calling for Anthropic’s expulsion from its systems, but said “we follow the president’s and the Department of War’s direction.” The spokeswoman added, without naming Anthropic, that it expected “minimal impacts” to its business because it does not rely on one large-language model for any portion of its work.
受禁令影響的軍工承包商一直不願談論此事，或許是擔心觸怒他們最大的客戶。洛克希德·馬丁公司的一位發言人在聲明中拒絕對其收到的要求清除Anthropic的信件直接置評，但表示「我們遵循總統和戰爭部的指示」。該發言人補充說，預計對其業務「影響極小」，因為其任何工作都不依賴單一大型語言模型，但未點名Anthropic。
But the Pentagon’s break with the company is hardly clean.
但五角大廈與Anthropic的決裂並不徹底。
Barely two months after the blowup between Mr. Amodei and Mr. Hegseth, Anthropic announced Mythos, an A.I. product uniquely suited to find, and exploit, vulnerabilities in software. For a brief period, in what now looks like an overreaction, the Trump administration barred all foreigners from touching the program — only to reverse itself.
在阿莫代伊與海格塞斯鬧翻僅兩個月後，Anthropic發布了Mythos，一款特別適合發現和利用軟體漏洞的人工智慧產品。川普政府曾一度禁止所有外國人接觸該程序——現在看來像是過度反應，隨後又撤銷了禁令。
That left the Pentagon in a fix: It had just barred itself from using what could be the most powerful new cyberweapon in history.
這讓五角大廈陷入了困境：它剛剛禁止自己使用可能是歷史上最強大的新型網路武器。
“Panic set in,” said a former official who recently left the administration.
「恐慌開始了，」一位近期離開政府的前官員說。
While the Pentagon is not allowing Claude to be used for intelligence analysis or code writing, the N.S.A. has been allowed to use versions of Anthropic’s advanced Mythos model on an “experimental” basis, officials said. It is both testing the U.S. military’s computer network defenses and seeking out potential weaknesses in adversarial networks, from China to Russia, North Korea and Iran.
雖然五角大廈不允許Claude用於情報分析或代碼編寫，但官員表示，國安局已被允許以「實驗性」方式使用Anthropic先進Mythos模型的某些版本。該機構既在測試美國軍方計算機網路防禦能力，也在尋找從中國到俄羅斯、朝鮮和伊朗等對手網路中的潛在弱點。
Rethinking Opposition to A.I. Regulation
重新思考對人工智慧監管的反對立場
The inconsistency isn’t limited to the Pentagon; there has been plenty at the Commerce and Treasury Departments, and at the White House. As Mr. Trump’s aides come and go, and departments elbow each other for larger roles, the administration’s decisions on everything from export controls to national security reviews of A.I. models have been driven more by whim and influence than an articulated set of principles.
這種不一致不僅限於五角大樓；商務部、財政部和白宮也存在大量類似情況。隨著川普的助手來來去去，各部門爭相爭取更大話語權，從出口管制到人工智慧模型國家安全審查，本屆政府在各色事務上的決策更多是由一時衝動和內部影響而非一套明確原則驅動。
During its first year, the administration’s approach was hands-off, following the arguments made by David Sacks, the venture capitalist who, until he left the administration earlier this year, ran both artificial intelligence and cryptocurrency policy.
在其任期第一年內，政府的做法是不干預，遵循風險投資家戴維·薩克斯的主張——他在今年早些時候離開政府前一直負責人工智慧和加密貨幣政策。
After publishing an A.I. Action Plan last year, the Trump administration slashed regulation and encouraged exports, hoping to make the world dependent on what Mr. Sacks called an “American tech stack.” In May, Mr. Trump scrapped a draft executive order requiring A.I. companies to submit their models to the government for a safety review, convinced by Mr. Sacks, among others, that the 90-day review requirement was too stringent and would give Chinese competitors an advantage.
在去年發布人工智慧行動計劃後，川普政府大幅削減監管並鼓勵出口，希望讓世界依賴薩克斯所稱的「美國技術棧」。今年5月，在薩克斯等人說服下，川普撤回了一項要求人工智慧公司向政府提交模型進行安全審查的行政命令草案，認為90天的審查要求過於嚴格，會給中國競爭對手帶來優勢。
But Mythos’s powers spooked people inside the administration, officials and others said. By June, the government had imposed the most heavy-handed controls ever in the A.I. industry, cutting off access to Anthropic’s model for foreign citizens, including some of the engineers who developed it. Then, two weeks later, it lifted the ban on foreigners, without public explanation.
但官員和其他人士表示，Mythos的威力讓政府內部人士感到不安。到今年6月，政府實施了人工智慧行業有史以來最嚴厲的管控措施，切斷外國人訪問Anthropic模型的權限，包括一些開發該模型的工程師。兩周後，它在沒有公開解釋的情況下解除了對外國人的禁令。
A similar whipsawing took place in dealing with the Nvidia chips that are critical to developing new models — and building data centers. In April last year, the Trump administration shut down sales of one of the last artificial intelligence chips Nvidia was allowed to offered in China.
在處理對開發新模型和建設數據中心至關重要的英偉達晶片方面也出現了類似的反覆。去年4月，川普政府叫停了一款英偉達人工智慧晶片的對華出口，它是此前獲准在中國銷售的最後一批人工智慧晶片之一。
But President Trump reversed course that July, following a meeting in the Oval Office between Mr. Trump and Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang. Mr. Trump said that the sales, which had formerly been considered a national security threat, would be fine, as long as the U.S. government got a cut of their revenue.
但川普在去年7月改變了立場，此前他在橢圓形辦公室會見了英偉達首席執行官黃仁勛。川普表示，這些此前被視為國家安全威脅的銷售可以放行，只要美國政府能從中分得一杯羹。
Similarly, the administration revoked a rule imposed by the Biden administration that assured the most powerful chips could only be sold freely to close American allies, and limited sales to other countries. Trump officials have said they would replace the measure, but they have clashed on a replacement. In the meantime, some American companies concluded that with the rules revoked, they were free to sell advanced chips to some Chinese firms operating outside of China.
同樣，本屆政府還撤銷了拜登政府時期的一項規定，即確保最強大的晶片只能自由出售給美國的親密盟友，對其他國家的銷售都要受到限制。川普政府官員曾表示將出台替代措施，但他們在替代方案上爭執不下。與此同時，一些美國公司得出結論，既然規定已撤銷，它們可以自由地向在中國境外運營的部分中國企業出售先進晶片。
Mr. Trump later brought Mr. Huang to China for his visit with Xi Jinping, along with other industry executives.
川普後來訪華與習近平會面，黃仁勛和其他行業高管也隨行前往中國。
The administration is now dealing with conflicting security and political currents. If it exercises too much control over the nascent A.I. pioneer labs, and threatens to cut off foreign access, it could drive the Europeans to Chinese models. If it under-regulates, it could invite disaster, especially if there are more incidents that make the public fear that the technology is racing out of control.
本屆政府目前正應對相互衝突的安全考量與政治風向。如果它對新興的人工智慧先鋒實驗室管控過嚴，並威脅切斷外國訪問權限，可能會將歐洲人推向中國的模型。如果監管不到位，則可能招致災難，尤其是若發生更多讓公眾擔憂該技術正失控的事件。
Chris McGuire, a senior fellow at the Council on Foreign Relations and a former Biden official, said that the Trump administration’s posture on export controls had been “incoherent.”
美國外交關係協會高級研究員、前拜登政府官員克里斯·麥圭爾表示，川普政府在出口管制方面的姿態一直「前後不一」。
The administration is now concerned about competition from open-source models from China, but it had walked back many measures that were preventing China’s A.I. advancement, he said. And it was targeting its harshest restrictions on Anthropic, an American company, not a Chinese one.
他說，該政府目前正擔憂來自中國開源模型的競爭，但此前已收回了許多曾阻礙中國人工智慧發展的措施。而且，它將其最嚴厲的限制措施指向了Anthropic——一家美國公司，而非中國公司。
“You’re loosening export controls on China, you’re leaving loopholes, and the first time you use export controls on A.I. is to blast an American company?” Mr. McGuire said.
「你在放鬆對中國的出口管制，你在留下漏洞，而你第一次對人工智慧使用出口管制卻是為了打擊一家美國公司？」麥圭爾說。
