澤連斯基為何對川普展開回擊？

Why Zelensky Took the Gloves Off This Time With Trump

MARIA VARENIKOVA • 2026-10-21
Ukraine has been here before with President Trump. But this time, as the American leader plays down Russian violence and makes it harder for Kyiv to respond, Ukrainian leaders have taken a different tack. They have punched back.
烏克蘭對與川普總統周旋並不陌生。但這一次，在這位美國總統淡化俄羅斯的暴力、讓基輔更難反擊之際，烏克蘭領導人改變了策略。他們選擇了回擊。
A senior Ukrainian official likened it to a last chance to sound an alarm as a tsunami approaches. Even before the latest blowup with Mr. Trump, things were looking difficult for Ukraine. Daily strikes were ravaging its energy infrastructure and economy. Ukrainian air defenses were depleted. A difficult winter was drawing near.
一名烏克蘭高級官員將此比作海嘯逼近時發出警報的最後機會。即便在最近與川普爆發衝突之前，烏克蘭的處境已十分艱難：每日的襲擊正摧毀其能源基礎設施和經濟，防空力量已消耗殆盡，一個艱難的冬天正在逼近。
Then Mr. Trump moved to buy Russian diesel, lifted sanctions on Russia and pressured Ukraine to stop hitting Russian oil refineries. Kyiv began reporting sudden restrictions on a Pentagon-funded satellite internet network, gaps in the sharing of U.S. military intelligence and delays in the delivery of American interceptor missiles. American officials deny any disruptions.
隨後川普採取行動購買俄羅斯柴油，解除對俄制裁，並向烏克蘭施壓要求其停止打擊俄羅斯煉油廠。基輔方面開始報告稱，一個由五角大廈資助的衛星互聯網網路突然受到限制，美國軍事情報共享出現缺口，美國攔截導彈遲遲不交付。美國官員否認存在任何中斷。
The clash reached a new pitch on Monday when Mr. Trump said that “Russia, frankly, has been fine on Ukraine” and that “they haven’t been doing much” — even as Ukrainian civilian casualties from Russian attacks have reached new highs, rising by 85 percent last month compared with a year before, according to the United Nations.
這場衝突在週一達到新的高潮。川普當時稱「坦白講，俄羅斯在烏克蘭問題上一直不錯」，「他們沒做什麼」——而與此同時，據聯合國數據，烏克蘭因俄羅斯襲擊造成的平民傷亡創下新高，上個月同比上升85%。
廣告
There are signs that neither side is ready to fully abandon the relationship. More than a week into his war of words with Mr. Trump, President Volodymyr Zelensky of Ukraine spoke by telephone on Monday with Secretary of State Marco Rubio. Mr. Zelensky then met in Oslo on Tuesday with Steve Witkoff, Mr. Trump’s peace envoy, and during a news conference afterward, the Ukrainian president avoided any direct criticism of the United States.
有跡象顯示，雙方都不打算徹底放棄這一關係。在與川普的口水戰持續一個多星期後，烏克蘭總統澤連斯基於週一與美國國務卿魯比奧通電話。隨後澤連斯基於週二在奧斯陸會見了川普的和平特使威特科夫，在會後的新聞發布會上，這位烏克蘭總統避免了對美國的任何直接批評。
“We are thankful that intelligence works now,” Mr. Zelensky said, “and I’m speaking about early warning, which is very important for our people, for children, adults, civilians, because we can hear when ballistic is coming.”
「我們感謝情報系統現在能正常運轉，」澤連斯基說，「我指的是預警，這對我們的人民、兒童、成年人、平民都至關重要，因為我們能聽到彈道導彈來襲的警報。」
Still, Ukrainian leaders say that Mr. Trump has now gone so far in putting Russia’s interests ahead of Ukraine’s security that holding their tongue would be riskier than speaking up.
儘管如此，烏克蘭領導人表示，川普如今把俄羅斯利益擺在烏克蘭安全之上，已經走得太遠，他們再保持沉默將面臨巨大風險，還不如坦率直言。
“People have to hear reality,” Mr. Zelensky said in an interview with ABC News last week. That reality, as the Ukrainian leader described it, was that Mr. Trump had an affinity for dictators, was on the wrong side of history in siding with President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia, and would only prolong the war by helping him.
「人們必須聽到現實，」澤連斯基上週在接受ABC新聞採訪時說。按照這位烏克蘭領導人的描述，這一現實是：川普對獨裁者有親近感，他選擇站在俄羅斯總統普丁一邊，就是站在歷史的對立面，並且幫助普丁只會延長戰爭。
Mr. Zelensky is not the first world leader and putative ally to have to decide how to respond to an attack from Mr. Trump. Like Prime Minister Mark Carney of Canada and a few others, Mr. Zelensky decided this time to push back. Ukrainian politicians and analysts said that was the right decision, even with the dangers involved.
許多世界領導人和名義上的盟友不得不就川普的攻擊做出回應，這方面澤連斯基並不孤單。與加拿大總理卡尼等人一樣，澤連斯基這次決定反擊。烏克蘭政界人士和分析人士表示，這是正確的決定，儘管存在風險。
“Trump is openly pushing us to capitulate,” said Mykhailo Samus, a Ukrainian expert on security and military affairs. “That is the situation.”
「川普在公開推動我們投降，」烏克蘭安全與軍事事務專家薩穆斯說。「這就是現實。」
Before now, Kyiv had sought to maintain a diplomatic, cautious tone with Mr. Trump. After the Oval Office meltdown in February 2025 between the American and Ukrainian presidents, Kyiv was quick to return to thanks and flattery. Ukraine continued to emphasize the importance of preserving the American partnership as Mr. Trump tried to end the war. Within a month, Kyiv offered an immediate, unconditional cease-fire, though Russia did not accept it.
在此之前，基輔一直尋求對川普保持外交上的謹慎語調。2025年2月美烏總統在橢圓形辦公室爆發衝突後，基輔迅速回歸感謝與恭維的姿態。在川普試圖結束戰爭期間，烏克蘭繼續強調維護美國夥伴關係的重要性。不到一個月，基輔便提出立即無條件停火，儘管俄羅斯並未接受。
Then as now, Ukraine hoped to avoid a cutoff in the crucial military and intelligence support that the United States still provides. Kyiv may now look to move toward de-escalation, having raised the alarm over Mr. Trump and facing the hard reality of a worsening position in the war, even as it holds Russian forces at bay on the front.
與當時一樣，烏克蘭希望避免美國仍在提供的關鍵軍事和情報支持被切斷。如今，基輔在就川普發出警報之後，可能傾向於緩和氣氛，同時面對戰局惡化的嚴酷現實——儘管烏軍在前線仍擋住了俄軍。
After speaking with Mr. Rubio on Monday, Mr. Zelensky wrote on social media: “Thank you to everyone in America who supports fair approaches!” Mr. Trump went unmentioned.
週一與魯比奧通話後，澤連斯基在社群媒體上寫道：「感謝美國所有支持公道的人！」川普未被提及。
Vitaly Portnikov, a political commentator in Ukraine, said Mr. Trump’s attacks had put Mr. Zelensky in a desperate situation. While the Ukrainian leader attacked Mr. Trump’s character at times, he chose to emphasize facts on the ground, with his administration publicly acknowledging the problems Ukraine faced as a result of American restrictions.
烏克蘭政治評論員維塔利·波爾特尼科夫表示，川普的攻擊使澤連斯基陷入絕境。儘管這位烏克蘭領導人有時批評川普的品格，但他選擇強調眼下的現實，其政府公開承認烏克蘭因美國限制而面臨的問題。
廣告
Mr. Zelensky got a chance to reiterate those problems during a video call on Monday with a bipartisan group of U.S. lawmakers, presumably hoping that domestic political pressure would restrain Mr. Trump from going further.
澤連斯基週一在與美國兩黨議員小組的影片通話中獲得了重申這些問題的機會，大概是希望國內政治壓力能阻止川普進一步行動。
The American president has swung away from and back toward Ukraine several times, and another swing is possible. The midterm elections, which are driving some of Mr. Trump’s moves on Russia, could scramble the political landscape again.
這位美國總統已多次在烏克蘭問題上搖擺，再一次出現轉向並非不可能。川普在俄羅斯問題上的一些舉動部分是受到期中選舉的影響，這次選舉可能再次打亂政治格局。
But Mr. Portnikov took Mr. Zelensky’s dialogue with Mr. Rubio, who is currently visiting India, as a good omen.
但波爾特尼科夫將澤連斯基與魯比奧的對話視為好的跡象，後者目前正在訪問印度。
“If the Ukrainian president is making forceful statements directed at the United States while the secretary of state takes the time to speak with him during a trip to Asia, it suggests that the Trump administration is not prepared for a rupture in U.S.-Ukrainian relations,” Mr. Portnikov said.
「如果烏克蘭總統發表了針對美國的強硬聲明，國務卿還是在訪問亞洲期間抽時間與他通話，表明川普政府不準備讓美烏關係破裂，」波爾特尼科夫說。
