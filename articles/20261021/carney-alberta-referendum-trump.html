石油大省選民拒絕脫離加拿大，卡尼取得關鍵勝利

As Alberta Rejects Separatism, Carney Gets Much of the Credit

IAN AUSTEN • 2026-10-21
For Prime Minister Mark Carney of Canada, the decision by oil-rich Alberta to ask voters whether to remain in Canada or hold a second, binding referendum on secession came, as he put it last week, “at the worst possible time.”
對加拿大總理馬克·卡尼而言，石油資源豐富的阿爾伯塔省決定讓選民就「留在加拿大還是舉行第二次具有約束力的獨立公投」做出選擇，用他上週的話說，時機「再糟糕不過」。
But timing aside, Monday’s resounding vote to keep the western province within Canada both strengthens national unity and eliminates a significant distraction as Mr. Carney works to form new global alliances in the face of the trade and diplomatic war that President Trump has waged against Canada since he returned to office.
但拋開時機不論，週一該西部省份以壓倒性多數投票支持留在加拿大，既鞏固了國家團結，也消除了一大幹擾——卡尼正致力於在川普總統重返白宮後對加拿大發起的貿易與外交戰中構建新的全球聯盟。
After months of campaigning, 75.2 percent of Albertans voted to remain in Canada, while 24.8 percent favored holding a second, binding vote on separation, according to unofficial results.
經過數月競選，非官方結果顯示，75.2%的阿爾伯塔選民投票支持留在加拿大，24.8%的人贊成就獨立問題舉行第二次具有約束力的投票。
Neither Mr. Carney nor his Liberal government had a formal role in the referendum, which was called by Danielle Smith, Alberta’s conservative premier. But many analysts attributed the strong vote for Canada to policies Mr. Carney, who was raised in the province, introduced over the past year and to his campaigning for continued unity there.
卡尼及其自由黨政府在公投中並不扮演任何正式的角色。這場公投由阿爾伯塔省保守黨省長丹妮爾·史密斯發起。但許多分析人士將選民對加拿大的強烈支持歸功於卡尼過去一年推出的政策，以及他在當地為維護統一所做的競選活動——卡尼正是在該省長大的。
廣告
“Carney was huge,” said Duane Bratt, a professor at Mount Royal University in Calgary, Alberta, who studies Alberta politics. “His working with Smith really took the wind out of separatism, and that’s why the separatists were just flailing.”
「卡尼的作用至關重要，」研究阿爾伯塔政治的卡爾加里皇家山大學教授杜安·布拉特說。「他與史密斯的合作確實消解了分離主義的勢頭，所以分離主義者只能瞎鬧一氣。」
He added, “Mark Carney is the most popular politician in the province right now.”
他補充道：「馬克·卡尼目前是這個省最受歡迎的政治人物。」
Even after accounting for Mr. Carney’s upbringing in Edmonton, for a Liberal prime minister to enjoy outsize popularity is an anomaly in Alberta, the home of Canadian conservatism, where Conservative candidates frequently win federal elections in landslides.
即便考慮到卡尼在埃德蒙頓的成長經歷，一位自由黨總理在阿爾伯塔享有超常人氣也實屬異數。這裡是加拿大保守主義的故鄉，保守黨候選人經常在聯邦選舉中以壓倒性優勢獲勝。
Even today, many Albertans still complain about a government policy almost 50 years ago under the Liberal prime minister Pierre Elliot Trudeau. His short-lived National Energy Program of 1980, which sought to lower the Canadian price for oil, was popular elsewhere in Canada. But it was deeply resented in Alberta, where it was widely seen as an intrusion on the provinces’ control over natural resources.
時至今日，許多阿爾伯塔人仍在抱怨近50年前自由黨總理皮埃爾·埃利奧特·杜魯道政府的一項政策。他在1980年短暫實施的國家能源計劃旨在壓低加拿大油價，這在其他地方頗受歡迎，卻在阿爾伯塔引發深切不滿——當地人普遍視其為對該省自然資源控制權的侵犯。
When Mr. Carney, former governor of the central banks of Canada and of England, entered politics last year, Ms. Smith seized every opportunity to criticize what she called the federal government’s “nine bad laws,” environmental measures introduced by his predecessor, Justin Trudeau, Pierre’s son. She also relentlessly pushed for a second oil pipeline to the Pacific Coast from her landlocked province.
曾擔任加拿大和英國央行行長的卡尼去年踏入政壇時，史密斯抓住一切機會批評她所稱的聯邦政府「九項惡法」，即其前任、皮埃爾之子賈斯汀·杜魯道推出的環保措施。她還不斷推動從她的這個內陸省份修建第二條通往太平洋海岸的石油管道。
Since then, Mr. Carney has replaced most of the “nine bad laws” with what he calls “cooperative federalism.” The new approach includes a new system that he said would speed up environmental and regulatory approvals of large projects, including oil and gas developments. More significantly, he said the federal government would build and largely finance an oil pipeline to a new ship terminal near Vancouver, British Columbia, the province just west of Alberta.
此後，卡尼用他所謂的「合作聯邦主義」取代了「九項惡法」的大部分。新方案包括一套新系統，他說這將加快大型項目的環境與監管審批，包括石油和天然氣開發。更重要的是，他表示聯邦政府將修建一條石油管道，通往阿爾伯塔以西不列顛哥倫比亞省溫哥華附近的新航運碼頭，並提供該項目大部分資金。
While Mr. Carney’s changes were condemned by some environmentalists and Indigenous communities, they undercut separatist claims that Canada holds Alberta back from its full economic potential.
卡尼的這些改變雖遭到部分環保主義者和原住民社區的譴責，卻削弱了分離主義者的主張——即加拿大阻礙了阿爾伯塔省實現其全部經濟潛力。
As a result, Mr. Bratt said, separatist campaigners were reduced to saying that Mr. Carney “was going to cancel the pipeline the day after the referendum or ask why he wasn’t building it personally by hand — just all sorts of crazy stuff.”
布拉特說，結果，分離主義活動人士只能退而聲稱卡尼「會在公投第二天取消管道，或者質問他為什麼不親手建造——諸如此類的荒唐說法」。
Polls show that Albertans support Mr. Carney’s defiant approach to Mr. Trump, as do Canadians generally. Ms. Smith, by contrast, who became leader of the United Conservative Party and then premier largely with the support of separatists, has frequently urged caution and restraint in dealing with Washington.
民調顯示，阿爾伯塔人支持卡尼對川普的強硬姿態，這和加拿大整體是一致的。相比之下，史密斯——她主要依靠分離主義者的支持成為聯合保守黨領袖並出任省長——則屢屢敦促在處理對華盛頓關係時保持謹慎與剋制。
廣告
She initially joined other provinces’ leaders in banning the sale of American beer, wine and spirits from the provincial liquor distribution system to pressure the Trump administration. But she soon allowed the products’ return, leaving Alberta as the only province without any measures against American alcohol.
她起初與其他省份領導人一道禁止省酒類分銷系統銷售美國啤酒、葡萄酒和烈酒，以向川普政府施壓。但她很快允許這些產品回歸，使阿爾伯塔成為唯一未對美國酒類採取任何措施的省。
Ms. Smith and Mr. Carney also contrasted during the referendum campaign. After a court quashed separatist efforts to force a direct question on leaving through a petition, Ms. Smith changed the law to add the separatism question to a previously planned referendum. She then alienated separatists by saying that she would vote for Canada but not requiring her party’s elected members to follow suit. Her own campaigning for Canada was limited, and she often focused on reasons for leaving the country.
公投競選期間，史密斯與卡尼也形成鮮明對比。法院駁回分離主義者通過請願強制就退出問題直接表決的努力後，史密斯修改法律，將分離主義問題加入此前已計劃好的公投。隨後，她表示自己將投票支持留在加拿大，卻不要求本黨當選議員效仿，此舉疏遠了分離主義者。她本人為留在加拿大所做的競選活動有限，且常常聚焦於脫離加拿大的理由。
Although Mr. Carney acknowledged that Albertans have causes for complaint and that Canada ”isn’t perfect,” he vigorously pushed back against the separatist movement and the referendum. His message was well received. About 7,000 people lined up last week in Calgary, the province’s largest city — some for up to two hours — for a rally where he was the headliner.
儘管卡尼承認阿爾伯塔人有抱怨的理由，也承認加拿大「並不完美」，但他堅決反擊分離主義運動和此次公投。他的訊息得到了積極回應。上週，約7000人在該省最大城市卡爾加里排隊——有些人等了長達兩小時——參加一場集會，而卡尼正是這場集會的主角。
On Tuesday, Mr. Carney made no attempt to hide his delight as he headed into a cabinet meeting in Ottawa.
週二，卡尼在前往渥太華參加內閣會議時毫不掩飾喜悅。
“It’s a great result for Canada, thrilled that Albertans have chosen unity,” he told reporters, adding in French: “It’s a good day for Canada.”
「這對加拿大來說是個極佳的結果，阿爾伯塔人選擇團結，我深感欣慰，」他告訴記者，並用法語補充道：「這對加拿大來說是美好的一天。」
