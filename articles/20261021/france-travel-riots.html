法國多地爆發騷亂，現在去巴黎旅遊安全嗎？

Heading to France? Amid Recent Unrest, Here’s What to Know.

CEYLAN YEGINSU, TARIRO MZEZEWA • 2026-10-21
Anti-government demonstrations across France escalated into widespread violence over the weekend, as thousands of protesters clashed with police, set municipal buildings and cars on fire, and ransacked storefronts.
上週末，法國各地的反政府示威活動升級為大範圍暴力事件，數以千計的抗議者與警察發生衝突，縱火焚燒市政建築和汽車，洗劫沿街商鋪。
The unrest, which began last month as largely peaceful, student-led protests over the quality of the public education system, spiraled into the country’s worst rioting in decades, with more than 2,000 arrests and nearly 1,000 police officers injured, according to the French Interior Ministry. To reassert control, authorities issued curfews in parts of several cities on Monday, including Paris, Nice and Lille.
這場騷亂始於上月，起初主要是學生主導的、針對公共教育體系質量的和平抗議活動，但後來演變成法國數十年來最嚴重的騷亂。據法國內政部稱，已有2000多人被捕，近1000名警察受傷。為重新控制局勢，當局週一在巴黎、尼斯和里爾等幾個城市的部分地區實施宵禁。
More demonstrations have been planned over the next two weeks. Here’s what you need to know if you plan to travel to France soon.
未來兩週還有更多示威活動計劃舉行。如果你計劃近期前往法國旅行，以下是需要了解的信息。
What parts of France have been affected?
法國哪些地區受到影響？
廣告
Paris became the epicenter of demonstrations over the weekend, with major clashes and rioting at Place de la République and surrounding avenues. Many of the shops across Les Halles were looted and vandalized, while the Place de la Bastille and Place de la Concorde became flash points for standoffs between the police and protesters.
巴黎成為週末示威活動的中心，共和國廣場及周邊大道發生重大衝突和騷亂。巴黎大堂一帶許多商店遭到搶劫和破壞，而巴士底廣場和協和廣場則成為警察與抗議者對峙的爆發點。
Marseille, France’s second-largest city and a popular tourist destination, also saw significant unrest, with protesters breaking into City Hall and ransacking the building, leaving graffiti and knocking over the bust of Marianne, a symbol of the French Republic. In response, authorities banned public gatherings in the city between noon and 8 p.m.
法國第二大城市、熱門旅遊目的地馬賽也發生了嚴重騷亂，抗議者闖入市政廳並洗劫了該建築，留下塗鴉，並推倒了法國共和國象徵瑪麗安娜的半身像。作為回應，當局禁止中午至晚上8點在該市舉行公眾集會。
In Lyon, tens of thousands of protesters marched through the city on Saturday, and some set fire to the city’s tourism office. Other major cities like Bordeaux, Lille, Calais and Limoges also experienced violence and damage to public buildings.
在里昂，數萬名抗議者週六在該市遊行，一些人縱火焚燒了該市旅遊局。波爾多、里爾、加來和利摩日等其他主要城市也發生了暴力事件，公共建築遭到破壞。
Is it safe to visit Paris?
去巴黎安全嗎？
While Paris is generally considered safe, government travel advisories, including those from the State Department, urge citizens to avoid protest areas and exercise increased caution because of civil unrest. Travelers have also been advised to follow local media outlets for updates on planned demonstrations.
雖然巴黎通常被認為是安全的，但包括美國國務院在內的政府旅行警告敦促公民避開抗議區域，並因社會動盪而提高警惕。此外，還建議旅行者關注當地媒體，了解計劃中示威活動的最新動態。
The next major protests are scheduled for Oct. 24, Oct. 31 and Nov. 5, with the Yellow Vests, a populist protest movement, calling for gatherings at Place de la Bastille and Place de la République, although times and routes have not been finalized and are awaiting police approval.
接下來的大型抗議活動定於10月24日、10月31日和11月5日舉行。民粹主義抗議運動「黃背心」呼籲在巴士底廣場和共和國廣場集會，但時間和路線尚未最終確定，正在等待警方批准。
廣告
By Tuesday, major streets in central Paris had been cleaned up and Parisians had returned to work, although many storefront windows remained broken, and burned cars and bikes littered the streets. Parks, including Luxembourg Gardens, Tuileries Garden and Parc Monceau, were calm. At cafes, people sat on patios with coffee and wine, and tourists and tour guides went about their days as planned.
到週二，巴黎市中心的主要街道已經清理完畢，巴黎人已返回工作崗位，儘管許多店面窗戶仍然破碎，被燒毀的汽車和單車散落在街道上。盧森堡公園、杜樂麗花園和蒙梭公園等公園都很平靜。在咖啡館裡，人們坐在露台上喝咖啡和葡萄酒，遊客和導遊也照常進行著當天的行程。
Sophie Gacheny, an independent tour guide based in the city, urged visitors to stay on the Left Bank, or the southern bank of the River Seine, which is typically quieter during protests. Like other guides, Ms. Gacheny is planning her tours around the protests and won’t run any on the days when demonstrations are scheduled.
駐該市的獨立導遊索菲·加舍尼敦促遊客留在左岸，即塞納河南岸，那裡在抗議期間通常更安靜。與其他導遊一樣，加舍尼女士正在圍繞抗議活動安排她的導遊行程，在計劃舉行示威活動的日子裡，她不會安排任何導遊活動。
Kiana Tiese, who offers private tours of Paris, said she took a group of visitors out for a tour on Sunday, a day after the protests, and said everything went well.
提供巴黎私人導覽的基安娜·蒂斯說，她在週日（即抗議活動後的第二天）帶了一群遊客出去遊覽，一切順利。
How are tourist attractions affected?
旅遊景點受到什麼影響？
While most major Paris landmarks like the Eiffel Tower and Arc de Triomphe remain open, local authorities can close them on short notice if large-scale protests pass through them. On Monday, the Louvre was forced to shut down after protesters blocked the entrance, causing anger among tourists who had paid for their tickets. (The museum has not announced when it will reopen.)
雖然艾菲爾鐵塔和凱旋門等巴黎大多數主要地標仍然開放，但如果大規模抗議活動經過這些地標，地方當局可以在短時間內關閉它們。週一，羅浮宮在抗議者堵塞入口後被迫關閉，激怒了已購票的遊客。（該博物館尚未宣布何時重新開放。）
The tourism board, Paris Je T’Aime, advises travelers to monitor live updates through its website and dedicated city pass app, which provide real-time updates on operating hours for major landmarks and venues.
巴黎旅遊局建議旅行者通過其網站和專門的城市通票應用程序關注實時更新，這些平台提供主要地標和場所營業時間的實時信息。
廣告
Municipal buildings, shops and outdoor monuments have been damaged across other major cities, too, creating road closures and areas with restricted access. Be sure to check the status of a landmark and surrounding areas before traveling there.
其他主要城市的市政建築、商店和戶外紀念碑也遭到破壞，導致部分道路封閉和區域通行受限。出行前務必查看地標及周邊地區的狀況。
How is local transportation affected?
當地交通受到什麼影響？
French unions have called for a nationwide general strike on Nov. 5, which could affect the national rail, public transit networks and roads. Under French law, transportation workers must file notice of a strike at least 48 hours before the planned action.
法國工會呼籲在11月5日舉行全國總罷工，這可能影響國家鐵路、公共交通網路和道路。根據法國法律，交通工人必須在計劃行動前至少48小時提交罷工通知。
On demonstration days, police shut down metro stations and roads along protest routes, and bus and tram schedules are reduced.
在示威日，警察會關閉抗議路線沿線的地鐵站和道路，公車和有軌電車班次也會減少。
Are there curfews?
有宵禁嗎？
French authorities imposed curfews on Monday across parts of several major cities, including Paris, Lille, Lyon and Nice.
法國當局週一在巴黎、里爾、里昂和尼斯等幾個主要城市的部分地區實施宵禁。
In Paris, the authorities enacted strict curfews in areas where demonstrations turned violent, such as Place de la République and the Les Halles commercial district. Authorities banned public gatherings in those areas between 8 p.m. and 6 a.m., and prohibited people from being in public outdoor spaces across dozens of districts between 11 p.m. Monday and 6 a.m. Tuesday. Police set up checkpoints around the perimeter of the curfew zones to verify the identities of residents and exempt emergency workers. It was unclear how long the curfews would last.
在巴黎，當局在共和國廣場和大堂商業區等示威演變成暴力的地區，實施了嚴格宵禁。當局禁止晚上8點至早上6點在這些地區舉行公眾集會，並禁止人們週一晚上11點至週二早上6點在數十個區的公共戶外空間逗留。警察在宵禁區周邊設置檢查站，核實居民身份，並允許應急人員通行。目前尚不清楚宵禁將持續多久。
廣告
At the five-star Hotel Lancaster Paris, steps from the Champs-Élysées, hotel guests sipped drinks and ate French tapas on Monday night in the lobby’s plush Copperbay cocktail bar. The rest of the neighborhood was equally tranquil, with no police or sirens in sight. Along the tree-shaded grand boulevard, international crowds strolled under the wrought-iron streetlamps, window-shopping at big global stores like Sephora and Zara.
在距香榭麗舍大街僅幾步之遙的五星級蘭卡斯特酒店，客人們週一晚上在大堂豪華的Copperbay雞尾酒吧裡小酌飲品，品嘗法式小吃。附近其他區域同樣寧靜，看不到警察，也聽不到警笛聲。在綠樹成蔭的大道上，國際遊客在鍛鐵路燈下漫步，在絲芙蘭和Zara等全球大型商店櫥窗前瀏覽。
Lauren Bell, 38, who was visiting the city with her sister from Madrid over the weekend, said that while the city felt calm on Tuesday, people also seemed to be preparing for the next wave. “We were shocked to see how quickly and badly the situation got violent and out of control,” she said. “It feels like it’s not over.”
38歲的勞倫·貝爾週末和妹妹從馬德里來巴黎遊玩。她說，雖然週二城市感覺平靜，但人們似乎也在為下一波做準備。「我們震驚地看到局勢如此迅速而嚴重地變得暴力失控，」她說。「感覺這一切還沒有結束。」
Will my travel insurance pay if I decide to cancel?
如果我決定取消行程，旅行保險會賠付嗎？
Whether your travel insurance will cover trip cancellation depends on your policy. Most standard policies don’t cover civil unrest, but if you purchased a “cancel for any reason” policy and meet the conditions, you may get reimbursed for your trip.
你的旅行保險是否覆蓋行程取消取決於你的保單。大多數標準保單不覆蓋內亂，但如果你購買了「無論任何原因均可取消」的保單並符合條件，你可能會獲得旅行費用的退款。
