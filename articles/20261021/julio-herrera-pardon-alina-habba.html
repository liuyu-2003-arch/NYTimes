川普前律師的5000萬美元薪酬與一位富豪的特赦

A Billionaire Wanted a Trump Pardon. His Family Gave Alina Habba a $50 Million Deal.

TYLER PAGER • 2026-10-21
Julio Herrera Velutini really wanted a pardon.
胡里奧·埃雷拉·貝盧蒂尼非常希望獲得特赦。
A billionaire banker from a prominent Venezuelan family, Mr. Herrera faced felony bribery charges in the United States and struck an unusually lenient plea deal in August 2025 for a misdemeanor campaign finance violation. But he felt that reduced charge was still a stain on his record.
這位出身委內瑞拉顯赫家族的銀行家億萬富翁在美國面臨重罪賄賂指控，並於2025年8月達成了一項異常寬鬆的認罪協議，僅承認一項輕罪競選財務違規。但他覺得，即使指控已降級，這仍是他個人記錄上的一處污點。
So Mr. Herrera deployed his lawyer, Christopher M. Kise, the former Florida solicitor general who was on Mr. Trump’s personal legal team, to help with a plea for clemency. His 27-year-old daughter, Isabela Herrera, who is an American citizen, donated $3.5 million to a super PAC tied to Mr. Trump. But as of last fall, the pardon remained elusive.
於是，埃雷拉派出了他的律師克里斯托弗·基斯——前佛羅里達州總檢察長、曾任職於川普私人法律團隊——幫助申請特赦。他27歲的女兒伊莎貝拉·埃雷拉是美國公民，向一個與川普有關聯的超級政治行動委員會捐贈了350萬美元。但截至去年秋天，赦免仍遙不可及。
Then the Herrera family hired Alina Habba, who had been one of Mr. Trump’s personal lawyers and at the time was an adviser at the Justice Department. They offered her $10 million a year for four years along with a range of bonuses. In total, including bonuses, she was guaranteed more than $50 million over four years, according to interviews and documents obtained by The New York Times.
隨後，埃雷拉家族聘用了阿麗娜·哈巴。她曾是川普的私人律師之一，當時在司法部擔任顧問。他們向她提供為期四年、每年1000萬美元的薪酬，外加一系列獎金。根據《紐約時報》獲得的採訪和文件，包括獎金在內，她四年內總計可獲得超過5000萬美元的保證收入。
Under the agreement, Ms. Habba would become general counsel at Concord Partners, a company that Ms. Herrera set up in late October 2025 in Florida. The company has no public profile and its business remains unclear even to some people associated with the Herrera business empire. Ms. Habba finalized her deal days after the company was registered.
根據協議，哈巴將成為康科德合夥公司的總法律顧問。該公司由伊莎貝拉·埃雷拉於2025年10月下旬在佛羅里達州設立。該公司沒有公開信息，其業務甚至對與埃雷拉商業帝國有關的一些人來說也不明確。哈巴在該公司註冊幾天後敲定了她的協議。
At the time, Ms. Habba was also a senior adviser to Attorney General Pam Bondi, overseeing U.S. attorneys. She had an office at the Justice Department and a protective detail from the U.S. Marshals, a holdover from her time serving as the U.S. attorney in New Jersey.
當時，哈巴還是司法部長帕姆·邦迪的高級顧問，負責監督美國聯邦檢察官的工作。她在司法部有一間辦公室，並擁有美國法警局提供的安保團隊，這是她擔任紐澤西州聯邦檢察官時期留下的待遇。
She formally began her work for Concord Partners on Jan. 1. Weeks later, Mr. Trump granted Mr. Herrera a full and unconditional pardon.
她於1月1日正式開始為康科德合夥公司工作。數週後，川普授予埃雷拉全面無條件的赦免。
廣告
The sequence of events raises questions about how Mr. Herrera secured the pardon and who might have helped him. People familiar with Ms. Habba’s contract said they believed that she was brought in to help with Mr. Herrera’s push for a pardon and expressed surprise at the scale of the compensation.
這一系列事件引發了疑問：埃雷拉是如何獲得赦免的，以及誰可能幫助了他。熟悉哈巴合同的人士表示，他們認為她被請來是為了幫助埃雷拉推動赦免，並對薪酬規模表示驚訝。
Ms. Habba denied any role in the pardon.
哈巴否認在赦免一事中扮演任何角色。
“Any insinuation that my employment — all of which has been vetted and disclosed to the D.O.J. — had anything to do with securing a pardon is false,” she said in a statement.
「任何暗示我的僱傭關係——所有這些都經過審查並向司法部披露——與獲得赦免有關的說法都是錯誤的，」她在聲明中說。
Ms. Habba was a special government employee at the time, which allows individuals to work for the federal government for up to 130 days and maintain outside employment with conflict-of-interest restrictions.
哈巴當時是一名特殊政府僱員，該身份允許個人為聯邦政府工作最多130天，同時在遵守利益衝突限制的前提下保留外部工作。
She declined to discuss her work for Concord.
她拒絕討論自己在康科德的工作。
Ms. Herrera and Mr. Herrera did not respond to multiple requests for comment. Neither did their companies. In a statement, the Justice Department said Ms. Habba did not speak to anyone about a pardon.
伊莎貝拉·埃雷拉和胡里奧·埃雷拉沒有回應多次置評請求。他們的公司也沒有回應。司法部在一份聲明中說，哈巴沒有與任何人談論赦免事宜。
A White House official said that Ms. Habba had nothing to do with the pardon and that the administration follows a pardon review process that includes the White House Counsel’s Office and the Justice Department.
一名白宮官員說，哈巴與赦免無關，並表示政府遵循由白宮法律顧問辦公室和司法部參與的赦免審查程序。
In Mr. Trump’s second term, a pardon industry has sprouted up, with wealthy criminals paying well-connected lawyers, fixers and lobbyists to appeal to the administration and its allies in an effort to secure pardons. Many pardon seekers have come to the conclusion that supporting Mr. Trump and his political agenda is the fastest way to secure clemency.
在川普第二任期內，一個赦免產業應運而生，富有的罪犯向人脈廣泛的律師、中間人和遊說者支付高額費用，以向政府及其盟友求助，爭取赦免。許多尋求赦免者得出結論，支持川普及其政治議程是獲得寬恕的最快途徑。
廣告
Some people looking for pardons have reported paying hundreds of thousands of dollars or even $1 million to hire people promising access to the president or influence over the pardon process.
一些尋求赦免的人稱他們支付了數十甚至上百萬美元，聘請承諾能接觸總統或影響赦免程序的人。
For Ms. Habba, the job from the Herrera family came at a pivotal point in her life and career.
對哈巴來說，埃雷拉家族的工作恰逢她人生和職業生涯的關鍵時刻。
She resigned in December 2025 as U.S. attorney in New Jersey after a panel of federal judges ruled that her appointment by Mr. Trump had been carried out unlawfully. Her second marriage was also falling apart.
2025年12月，在一個聯邦合議庭裁定川普對她的任命不合法後，她辭去紐澤西州聯邦檢察官職務。她的第二次婚姻也在走向破裂。
The arrangement with the Herreras provided her with a new level of financial security. She also found new love. Shortly after the pardon was issued, Ms. Habba started dating Turhan Mildon, a Turkish investor.
與埃雷拉家族的安排為她提供了新的財務保障。她還找到了新的愛情。赦免發布後不久，哈巴開始與土耳其投資者圖爾漢·米爾登約會。
They were married last month in a lavish ceremony in New York. The guest list included Attorney General Todd Blanche; the F.B.I. director, Kash Patel; and Will Scharf, the White House counsel.
他們上個月在紐約舉行盛大婚禮。賓客包括司法部長托德·布蘭奇、聯邦調查局局長卡什·帕特爾和白宮法律顧問威爾·沙夫。
The couple is now engaged in high-profile feud with Richard Grenell, a longtime Trump ally. Ms. Habba has accused Mr. Grenell of misallocating a donation from her husband to the John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts, which Mr. Grenell was running at the time. Mr. Grenell has denied her accusations, calling them absurd.
這對夫婦現在與川普長期盟友理查德·格倫內爾陷入高調的爭執。哈巴指責格倫內爾挪用她丈夫向約翰·F·甘迺迪表演藝術中心的捐款，格倫內爾當時是該中心負責人。格倫內爾否認她的指控，稱其荒謬。
Ms. Habba brought those accusations directly to Mr. Trump, who directed Mr. Scharf to investigate them. The Times has previously reported that the investigation centers on a bank transfer from the Democratic Republic of Congo to a Texas defense company.
哈巴直接向川普提出這些指控，川普指示沙夫進行調查。《紐約時報》此前報導，調查集中在從剛果民主共和國向一家得克薩斯州防務公司的一筆銀行轉賬。
Mr. Herrera, who also has Italian citizenship, is a member of the Herrera family, a banking, real estate and fashion dynasty that dates back to the 14th century. The family owns estates and castles in Caracas, Venezuela; the Canary Islands; Spain; and Germany.
同時擁有義大利國籍的埃雷拉是埃雷拉家族的一員。這個銀行、房地產和時尚王朝可追溯到14世紀。家族在委內瑞拉卡拉卡斯、加那利群島、西班牙和德國擁有莊園和城堡。
廣告
Mr. Herrera founded a bank based in Puerto Rico in 2008.
埃雷拉於2008年在波多黎各創立了一家銀行。
In August 2022, he was charged for his role in a scheme to bribe Gov. Wanda Vázquez of Puerto Rico to dismiss a regulator who was investigating the bank. Mr. Herrera, Ms. Vázquez and a former F.B.I. agent who had worked as a consultant for Mr. Herrera all pleaded not guilty to felony charges.
2022年8月，他被控參與賄賂波多黎各總督萬達·巴斯克斯，使其解僱一名正在調查該銀行的監管者的計劃。埃雷拉、巴斯克斯和一名曾為埃雷拉工作的前聯邦調查局探員都對重罪指控表示不認罪。
Mr. Kise, Mr. Herrera’s lawyer, helped negotiate a deal with the Justice Department under which Mr. Herrera pleaded guilty in August 2025 to a single misdemeanor campaign finance charge. Ms. Vázquez and the former F.B.I. agent also pleaded guilty to misdemeanors, disappointing career prosecutors who had pushed for harsher sentences.
埃雷拉的律師基斯幫助與司法部談判達成協議，埃雷拉於2025年8月就一項輕罪競選財務指控認罪。巴斯克斯和那名前聯邦調查局探員也就輕罪認罪，這讓推動更嚴厲判決的職業檢察官感到失望。
All three were pardoned by Mr. Trump at the same time, shortly before they were scheduled to be sentenced for the misdemeanor charges.
三人都在原定的輕罪指控量刑前不久被川普赦免。
In July, Banvelca, the Herreras’ family office since 1781, announced that Ms. Herrera, a member of the eighth generation and the daughter of Julio Herrera Velutini, was assuming leadership of the company. Neither the news release nor her bio on the company’s website makes any mention of Concord Partners, the vehicle employing Ms. Habba, who now goes by Mrs. Mildon.
7月，自1781年以來一直是埃雷拉家族辦公室的Banvelca宣布，第八代成員、胡里奧·埃雷拉·貝盧蒂尼的女兒伊莎貝拉·埃雷拉將領導該公司。新聞稿和公司網站上她的簡歷都沒有提到康科德合夥公司——即聘用哈巴（現在已經是「米爾登夫人」）的實體。
