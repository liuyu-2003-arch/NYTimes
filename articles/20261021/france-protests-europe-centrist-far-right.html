歐洲中間派政黨為何正在失去民心

It’s Not Just France. Europe’s Political Center Is Crumbling.

STEVEN ERLANGER • 2026-10-21
Centrist parties are in trouble in Europe’s largest and most important nations. Anger over low economic growth, high inflation, sizable government debt and poor prospects for the young are feeding protest and populist parties of the far right and far left.
中間派政黨在歐洲最大、最重要的國家正陷入困境。經濟增長低迷、通膨高企、政府債台高築以及年輕人前景黯淡所引發的憤怒正在催生抗議，帶來極右翼和極左翼民粹主義政黨的崛起。
The mass riots in France, which started as protests over underfunded schools, are a shocking example. But the phenomenon has been building for years, with the perceived failure of liberal democracy to make good on its promises of a better future, especially for the young and the salaried working class.
最初只是抗議學校經費不足的法國大規模騷亂就是一個令人震驚的例證。但這一現象已醞釀多年：自由民主制被認為未能兌現創造更美好未來的承諾，對年輕人和受薪工人階級而言尤其如此。
There is a growing perception among many Europeans that traditional parties of the center-left and center-right do not offer real alternatives and are led by an educated elite that has lost its connection to the poor, the workers and the young. Facing rising deficits, expenses and interest rates, governments feel constrained in what they can do to respond.
許多歐洲人日益感到，中左和中右的傳統政黨並未提供真正的替代選項，且由受過教育的精英領導，這些人已與窮人、工人和年輕人脫節。面對不斷上升的赤字、開支和利率，各國政府在回應舉措上感到束手束腳。
Instead, many people are supporting parties like the National Rally of Marine Le Pen, leading the polls for the French presidency on the far right, and France Unbowed of Jean-Luc Mélenchon, on the far-left, running second. In Germany, they are turning to the far-right Alternative for Germany and the far-left Die Linke party.
於是，許多人轉而支持瑪麗娜·勒龐的國民聯盟——該黨作為極右翼在法國總統民調中領先——以及讓-呂克·梅朗雄的不屈法國黨，後者作為極左翼排名第二。在德國，選民轉向極右翼的德國選擇黨和極左翼的左翼黨。
With major elections coming in the next year in four of Europe’s most populous states — Spain, France, Italy and Poland — there are significant challenges coming from more extremist parties. Even in Italy, where protests erupted over the weekend, the right-wing prime minister, Giorgia Meloni, faces a former general, Roberto Vannacci, who is running even further to the right.
明年，歐洲四個人口最多的國家——西班牙、法國、義大利和波蘭——都將迎來重大選舉，來自更極端政黨的重大挑戰正在逼近。甚至在義大利，週末也爆發了抗議，右翼總理喬治婭·梅洛尼要面對立場更靠右的前將軍羅伯托·萬納奇的挑戰。
Hard-right parties also sit in the governments of the Czech Republic, Finland, Slovakia and Croatia, while Slovenia’s government relies on outside support from one, Alberto Alemanno, a professor of European Law at HEC Paris, recently noted. And competition from even farther to the right is already pushing some of them to harden their positions.
巴黎高等商學院歐洲法教授阿爾貝托·阿萊曼諾最近指出，極右翼政黨還進入了捷克、芬蘭、斯洛伐克和克羅埃西亞的政府，而斯洛文尼亞政府則依賴一個此類政黨的外部支持。來自更右翼的競爭已在推動其中一些政黨強化立場。
Satisfaction with how democracy is working has been declining dramatically, according to regular opinion polling from the Pew Research Center. In France, Pew found this year, 69 percent of the population is dissatisfied with democracy. In Spain, it is 68 percent. In Italy, 62 percent. In Poland, the newest democracy after years of Soviet occupation, it is 47 percent. In the United States, it is 69 percent.
皮尤研究中心的定期民意調查顯示，民眾對民主運作的滿意度一直在急劇下降。皮尤今年發現，法國有69%的人口對民主不滿意，西班牙為68%，義大利為62%。經歷多年蘇聯佔領後成為最新民主國家的波蘭，不滿意比例為47%。美國則為69%。

There are many reasons put forward for the decline in optimism and faith in centrist politics. But Daron Acemoglu, a Nobel laureate in economics, puts it simply. “We started taking democracy for granted,” he said.
對於樂觀情緒和對中間派政治的信念為何下降，人們提出了許多解釋。但諾貝爾經濟學獎得主達龍·阿傑姆奧盧說得直白：「我們開始把民主視為理所當然。」
Liberal democracy has failed to keep faith with ordinary citizens, and especially with the larger working class. “If liberalism is to rise again, it must be with the working class at its heart,” he said.
自由民主制未能對普通公民守信，對更廣大的工人階級尤其如此。「如果自由主義要再次崛起，它必須以工人階級為核心，」他說。
Shared Prosperity
共享繁榮
The rapid nationwide spread of the French protests to include workers and pensioners angry at inflation and high interest rates, especially given rising prices for energy and for borrowing costs, points to a larger failure of President Emmanuel Macron’s center-right government and of liberal democracy writ large.
法國抗議活動迅速蔓延全國，工人和退休人員也加入其中，他們對通膨和高利率感到憤怒，尤其是能源價格和借貸成本不斷上升，這指向總統埃馬紐埃爾·馬克宏的中間偏右政府乃至整個自由民主制的更大失敗。
The violence of what became riots in many cities is expected only to help Ms. Le Pen and her anti-liberal complaints about immigration and lawlessness.
在許多城市，抗議演變為騷亂，其暴力預計只會進一步助長勒龐的聲勢，以及她關於移民和治安失序的反自由主義抱怨。
Mr. Acemoglu, a professor at M.I.T., has tried to provide both an analysis and some prescriptions in a recent book, “What Happened to Liberal Democracy?” In it, he outlines “two broad lines of failure” over the last half-century, which have led to widespread alienation and anti-establishment sentiment.
麻省理工學院教授阿傑姆奧盧在新書《自由民主怎麼了？》(What Happened to Liberal Democracy?)中試圖同時提供分析和處方。他在書中概括了過去半個世紀的「兩個廣泛的失敗」，這些失敗導致了普遍的疏離感和反建制情緒。

The first is what he calls the failure to deliver on shared prosperity, especially for a salaried working class hit by automation, globalization and sharply increased inequality, and which now feels deep anxiety about artificial intelligence.
第一條是他所稱的未能兌現共享繁榮，受打擊最重的是受薪工人階級，他們受到自動化、全球化和急劇惡化的不平等的衝擊，如今又對人工智慧深感焦慮。
Leaders have forgotten that a key part of their pact with voters is to “take care of the drains,” he said, to ensure the good functioning of the infrastructure of daily life, from roads to education to health care.
他說，領導人忘記了他們與選民契約的一個關鍵部分是「管好下水道」，即確保日常生活基礎設施的良好運轉，從道路到教育再到醫療保健。
The second, Mr. Acemoglu said in an interview, is “the rise of top-down cultural politics,” whereby a college-educated elite now dominates even traditional Socialist and working-class parties throughout the Western world and in his view patronizes or dismisses the concerns of those who traditionally supported them.
阿傑姆奧盧在採訪中表示，第二條是「自上而下的文化政治的興起」。在此進程中，一個受過大學教育的精英群體如今主導著整個西方世界甚至傳統的社會黨和工人階級政黨，在他看來，這些人對那些傳統上支持他們的人所關切的問題擺出居高臨下的姿態，或不予理會。
As a result, working-class voters are moving toward populist parties on the far left and especially the far right, as in France and Germany, threatening traditional values, shrinking historically “large church” parties of the center and threatening the working of parliamentary democracy.
結果，工人階級選民正轉向極左、極右翼的民粹主義政黨——後者尤甚，法國和德國的情況即是如此。這威脅到傳統價值觀，使歷史上作為中間派「大帳篷」的政黨不斷萎縮，並威脅到議會民主的運作。
In Britain’s 2024 election, the two long-dominant parties, Labour and Conservative, won their lowest combined vote share in more than a century. The far-right Reform UK party surged in the polls last year, though it has since fallen somewhat.
在英國2024年大選中，兩個長期主導的政黨——工黨和保守黨——合計得票率創下一個多世紀以來的最低水平。極右翼的英國改革黨去年在民調中抬頭，儘管此後有所回落。
Centrist parties need to regain the trust of voters that they not only hear them but empathize with them, said Steven E. Sokol, president of the American Council on Germany. “A constant in all these countries are the people who feel left behind, who feel they are no part of the prosperity and security they see all around them,” he said.
美國德國協會主席史蒂芬·E·索科爾表示，中間派政黨需要重新贏得選民的信任，讓選民相信他們不僅聽到了自己的聲音，而且同情自己的處境。「所有這些國家的一個不變因素是那些感到被落下的人，他們覺得自己與周圍所見的繁榮和安全毫無關係，」他說。

The Center Under Stress
承壓的中心
Germany offers another example of the exhaustion and splintering of the center.
德國提供了另一個中間派疲憊和分裂的例子。
The Volksparteien, or “people’s parties” of the center-right and center-left — the Christian Democratic Union (and its partner, the Christian Social Union), and the Social Democratic Party — alternated running democratic Germany since World War II. But they are being hollowed out, said Mr. Sokol, with workers and the young moving to more extreme parties, like the Alternative for Germany (AfD) and Die Linke.
中右和中左的Volksparteien，即「人民黨」——基督教民主聯盟（及其夥伴基督教社會聯盟）和社會民主黨——自二戰以來交替執掌民主化的德國。但索科爾表示，它們正在被掏空，工人和年輕人正轉向更極端的政黨，如德國選擇黨和左翼黨。
Given the difficulty of forming coherent coalitions now, the two Volksparteien are locked together in a squabbling coalition that is increasingly unpopular and perceived as failing to deliver economic growth and security.
鑒於如今組建穩固聯盟的難度，兩個人民黨被鎖在一個爭吵不斷的聯盟中，該聯盟日益不受歡迎，並被外界認為未能帶來經濟增長和安全。
Under former Chancellor Angela Merkel, the C.D.U. moved to the center, but it was cautious, “risking nothing, even in the face of obvious challenges,” said Norbert Röttgen, a prominent legislator in the party.
在前總理安哥拉·梅克爾領導下，基民盟向中間靠攏，但步伐謹慎，「即使面對明顯的挑戰也絕對不冒險，」該黨著名議員諾貝特·勒特根說。
Those challenges included increased migration, a declining export market with China, sharpened tensions with Russia and slow economic growth. The move to the center and Ms. Merkel’s embrace of large-scale immigration, he said, alienated many of the party’s traditional conservative voters.
這些挑戰包括移民增加、對華出口市場下滑、與俄羅斯緊張關係加劇以及經濟增長緩慢。勒特根說，向中間靠攏以及梅克爾對大規模移民的接納疏遠了許多該黨的傳統保守選民。
That gave space to the right, filled by the AfD, which is now attracting traditional C.D.U. voters among the working class, Mr. Röttgen said. “But to seriously deliver on our problems requires majorities, and this,” he said, “is our paradox.”
勒特根表示，這給右翼留下了空間，被德國選擇黨填補，該黨如今正在吸引工人階級中的傳統基民盟選民。「但要認真解決我們的問題需要多數支持，而這，」他說，「就是我們的悖論。」
As for the Social Democrats, they took the path Mr. Acemoglu described of many left-wing parties, where intellectual elites and left-wing academics “conquered the party and disconnected it from its traditional voters,” Mr. Röttgen said.
至於社會民主黨，勒特根說，他們走了阿傑姆奧盧所描述的許多左翼政黨的道路：知識精英和左翼學者「征服了黨，並使其與傳統的選民脫節」。
An average of current opinion polls in Germany shows how hard it is to construct a workable centrist coalition.
德國當前民調的平均值顯示了構建一個可行的中間派聯盟有多麼困難。

The AfD is the most popular party, with some 28 percent support, followed by the C.D.U./C.S.U., with 19.8 percent. The Greens have 15 percent, and the Social Democrats only 13.1 percent, barely ahead of Die Linke, with 11.3 percent.
德國選擇黨是最受歡迎的政黨，支持率約28%，其次是基民盟/基社盟，為19.8%。綠黨有15%，社會民主黨僅13.1%，勉強領先於左翼黨的11.3%。
Both mainstream parties are divided internally about how to proceed — for the Christian Democrats, to move closer to the AfD and not just copy some of its policies; for the Social Democrats, to move farther to the left to defend itself against both the Greens and Die Linke.
兩個主流政黨內部都在如何推進上存在分歧：對基民盟而言，是靠近德國選擇黨而不只是複製其部分政策；對社會民主黨而言，是進一步左轉以同時抵禦綠黨和左翼黨。
A Splintered Europe
分裂的歐洲
With these four important elections and the pull of the far right in each of them “we’re going to have a major transformation,” with fragmented governments, said Ivan Krastev, chairman of the Center for Liberal Strategies in Bulgaria and a fellow at the Institute for Human Sciences in Vienna.
保加利亞自由主義戰略中心主席、維也納人文科學研究所研究員伊萬·克拉斯特夫表示，隨著這四場重要選舉以及極右翼在其中表現出的號召力，「我們將迎來一場重大轉型，」政府將趨於碎片化。
Right-wing coalitions will likely contain small far-right parties “which will keep the mainstream far-right parties hostage and may prevent them from moving to the center.”
右翼聯盟很可能包含小型極右政黨，「這將使主流極右政黨成為人質，並可能阻止它們向中間移動。」
If they do well, “then very quickly you’re trying to normalize them, because otherwise the European Union cannot function, and this is not making people feel more secure,” he said.
他說，如果它們表現良好，「那麼你很快就會試圖將它們正常化，因為否則歐盟無法運轉，而這並不會讓人們感到更安全。」
The French presidential election next spring will be key. A victory by Ms. Le Pen could have major implications for Brussels, for the euro, for NATO and for European support for Ukraine.
明年春天的法國總統選舉將是關鍵。勒龐的勝利可能對布魯塞爾、歐元、北約以及歐洲對烏克蘭的支持產生重大影響。
Mr. Acemoglu argues for “a big-tent populism,” that channels the anger of voters in the name of inclusivity.
阿傑姆奧盧主張「一個大帳篷式的民粹主義」，以包容性的名義引導選民的憤怒。
“The centerpiece has to be creating jobs and wage growth and career paths for people without college degrees and diverse skills,” he said. “There must be an agenda for shared prosperity, which is why artificial intelligence and how it is used is critical.”
「核心必須是為沒有大學學位和多元技能的人創造就業、工資增長和職業路徑，」他說。「必須有一個共享繁榮的議程，這就是為什麼人工智慧及其使用方式至關重要。」
