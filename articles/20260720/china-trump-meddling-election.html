北京駁斥川普指控

Beijing Rejects Trump’s Claim of Meddling, but Avoids a Fight

艾莎 • 2026-07-20
China on Friday rejected President Trump’s claim that it had meddled in American elections as baseless. But its response was relatively restrained, a sign that Beijing did not want to let the accusation derail a thaw in relations that both sides have spent months trying to preserve.
週五，中國駁斥了川普總統關於中國干預美國大選的指控，稱其毫無根據。但北京的回應相對克制，表明它不願讓這一指控破壞雙方數月來共同努力維護的關係回暖勢頭。
Mr. Trump had said in a speech that China had carried out “the largest compromise of election data in history” starting with the 2020 cycle, one of several claims he made that was either overstated or untethered from reality.
川普在一次演講中聲稱，中國從2020年大選週期開始實施了「史上最大規模的選舉數據入侵」，這是他提出的諸多誇大其詞或脫離事實的指控之一。
In Beijing, a foreign ministry spokesman, Lin Jian, said that China had “no interest in interfering in the U.S. elections and has never done so,” adding that the claims “are aimed at vilifying China.” It leveled a vague accusation back at Washington for “wantonly” interfering in other countries.
在北京，外交部發言人林劍表示，中國「沒有興趣也從未干涉美國大選」，並稱這些指控「無端抹黑中國」。與此同時，中方也反過來對華盛頓提出了一項籠統的指責，稱美國「動輒」干涉他國內政。
The response was firm but made no demands for retractions or threats of punishment. On China’s social media, commentators who are usually stridently nationalist were mostly quiet.
中方的回應措辭強硬，但並未要求美方撤回言論，也未發出懲罰威脅。在中國社群媒體上，平時高調的民族主義評論人士大多保持沉默。
廣告
China experts said this showed that Beijing saw Mr. Trump’s speech as an attempt at playing to American voters ahead of midterm elections, rather than a broadside directed at China.
中國問題專家表示，這表明北京方面認為川普的演講是期中選舉前向美國選民進行的政治表演，而非針對中國的猛烈抨擊。
It also underscored a desire on Beijing’s part to maintain a truce that was struck in May during a summit between Mr. Trump and China’s top leader, Xi Jinping.
這也凸顯出北京方面希望維持今年5月川普與中國最高領導人習近平在峰會上達成的休戰局面。
Mr. Trump’s rhetoric on China during his address was “definitely harsh,” said Sun Yun, a scholar of China at the Stimson Center, a research group in Washington. But Mr. Trump had built up so much good will in recent months, she added, “there is some resilience in the relationship.”
華盛頓智庫史汀生中心的中國問題專家孫韻表示，川普在演講中對中國的措辭「確實強硬」。但她又說，川普近幾個月已經積累了大量善意，「雙邊關係具有一定的韌性」。
Playing the China Card
打中國牌
From Beijing’s perspective, Mr. Trump was taking aim at China for domestic political reasons, experts said. Officials in China understand that the Republicans are under pressure ahead of the midterm elections.
專家們表示，從北京的角度來看，川普是出於國內政治目的而將矛頭對準中國。中國官員明白，共和黨在期中選舉前面臨壓力。
Chinese state media commentaries have argued that Mr. Trump has lost some of the support of his political base following his military intervention in Venezuela and the war in Iran. They have noted that he has failed to keep factory jobs at home, while consumers “bear higher fuel costs and inflation.”
中國官方媒體評論稱，在對委內瑞拉進行軍事干預以及發動伊朗戰爭之後，川普已經失去了部分政治基本盤的支持。他們還指出，川普未能把製造業崗位留在國內，同時消費者卻「承受著更高的燃料成本和通貨膨脹」。
廣告
Viewed through this lens, Mr. Trump’s speech was an attempt at mobilizing voters by fanning the idea of foreign intervention, said Wu Xinbo, an American studies scholar at Fudan University in Shanghai.
上海復旦大學美國研究專家吳心伯表示，從這個角度來看，川普的演講是通過渲染外國干預的論調來動員選民。
“Trump played this card mainly for domestic politics, it was not aimed at China-U.S. relations,” said Mr. Wu, adding that this was something Chinese officials would likely not respond to if it was a one-off.
「川普打這張牌主要是為了國內政治，並不是針對中美關係，」吳心伯說。他還表示，如果這只是偶發性的言論，中國官方很可能不會作出回應。
Documents released by the White House that Mr. Trump said bolstered his claims suggest there is evidence of a pro-China influence effort and that Beijing was skeptical of President Trump. But that was a minority perspective inside the intelligence agencies, and the intelligence officers who held that view said they had only low or medium confidence in their assessments.
川普表示，白宮公布的文件為他的指控提供了支持。這些文件顯示，確有證據表明存在一場親華的影響力行動，而且北京方面當時對川普總統也持懷疑態度。不過，這只是美國情報機構內部的一種少數意見，而持這一觀點的情報人員也表示，他們對這一評估只有低到中等程度的信心。
China Wants to Preserve the Stability
中國希望維持關係穩定
The U.S.-China relationship steadied in October after Beijing agreed to suspend its limits on exports of rare earth metals and Washington halved tariffs on China related to fentanyl. “We’re not going to hurt China,” Mr. Trump said at the time.
去年10月，在北京同意暫停對稀土金屬出口的限制、華盛頓將涉及吩坦尼的對華關稅削減一半之後，中美關係趨於平穩。「我們不會傷害中國，」川普當時表示。
After Mr. Trump met with Mr. Xi in Beijing, China said both sides had agreed that U.S. tariffs would not rise further. They agreed on a relationship based on “constructive strategic stability,” a vague term meant to signal that both sides would limit hostilities.
川普在北京與習近平舉行會晤後，中方表示，雙方同意美國不會進一步提高對華關稅。雙方還同意建立一種以「建設性戰略穩定」為基礎的關係。這是一個措辭較為模糊的表述，但意在表明雙方都會限制敵對行為。
廣告
During his trip, Mr. Trump invited Mr. Xi to the White House on Sept. 24, a meeting that officials are now working toward. Beijing expects the upcoming visit to help improve trade ties.
訪華期間，川普邀請習近平於9月24日訪問白宮，目前雙方官員正為此次會晤做準備。北京期待這次訪問能夠進一步改善雙邊貿易關係。
Any disruption to the fragile détente will hinge on whether Mr. Trump decides to take additional action on his claims of election meddling.
這一脆弱的緩和局面是否能維繫下去將取決於川普是否會針對其所謂中國干預美國選舉的指控採取進一步行動。
If Mr. Trump were to take fresh actions against China, like putting new tariffs on Chinese goods, said Mr. Wu, “China would certainly retaliate.”
吳心伯表示，如果川普對中國採取新舉措，比如對中國商品加徵新關稅，「中國肯定會進行反制」。
The Chinese Economy Needs the U.S.
中國經濟需要美國
China may also be tempering its response because it wants to make nice with the United States on trade disputes as its own economy stumbles and as its companies face pushback from foreign governments.
中國之所以保持克制，或許也與其希望在經濟承壓、以及中國企業在海外面臨阻力之際與美國改善貿易關係有關。
Earlier this week, China reported that its economy grew at the slowest pace in three years. It is also facing increased scrutiny in other parts of the world as its exports surge, raising alarm among politicians in Europe and Southeast Asia. Chinese shipments overseas climbed 27 percent over the month of June and its trade surplus with the world climbed to more than $125 billion, its second largest on record.
上週早些時候，中國公布的數據顯示其經濟增速降至三年來最低水平。與此同時，隨著中國出口激增，其不僅在世界其他地區面臨更嚴格的審視，也引發了歐洲和東南亞政界人士的擔憂。6月，中國對外出口同比增長27%，對全球貿易順差攀升至逾1250億美元，創歷史第二高位。
廣告
At home, Chinese families are feeling poorer, consuming less, and have fewer job prospects. That means that China needs markets overseas to keep its factories churning, grow its economy and create new jobs, said Ja Ian Chong, an associate professor of political science at the National University of Singapore.
在國內，中國家庭普遍感到財富縮水，消費意願下降，就業前景暗淡。新加坡國立大學政治學副教授庄嘉穎表示，這意味著中國需要開拓海外市場，以維持工廠運轉、推動經濟增長、創造新的就業崗位。
“Having more access to the U.S. market could also take some of the pressure off China’s economic relationships with a bunch of other countries from Europe to Africa and Asia,” he said.
「獲得更多進入美國市場的機會也有助於緩解中國與歐洲、非洲以及亞洲一系列國家之間經濟關係所承受的部分壓力，」他說。
