習近平繼續反腐清洗

‘Pull Out the Sick Trees’: Xi’s Constant Purges Pave Way for Next Term

儲百亮 • 2026-10-26
For more than a decade, the Chinese leader Xi Jinping has used a storm of corruption probes and loyalty campaigns to bring the country’s officials to heel.
十多年來，中國領導人習近平一直通過一波又一波的反腐調查和忠誠教育運動來約束該國的官員。
Now, as Mr. Xi, 73, prepares to claim five more years in power, he is complaining that some officials are still disloyal, apathetic and corrupt, and has said that tougher measures are needed.
如今，73歲的習近平準備爭取再執政五年，他抱怨一些官員依然不忠誠、麻木不仁且腐敗，並表示需要採取更嚴厲的措施。
The relentlessness of Mr. Xi’s campaign for control was on display this month when the Communist Party announced an investigation into Huang Qifan, the former mayor of Chongqing, a city in southwest China. Mr. Huang remained prominent after leaving office and had survived the downfall of several party officials under whom he had served. Such investigations almost always culminate with the imprisonment of the official.
本月，中國共產黨宣布對中國西南部城市重慶的前市長黃奇帆展開調查，這充分展現了習近平鞏固權力的行動之無情。黃奇帆曾歷經數位曾任其上司的黨內官員倒台而安然無恙，卸任後也仍然保持活躍，地位顯赫。這類調查幾乎總是以官員被判刑入獄而告終。
The news about Mr. Huang came just before the party’s elite Central Committee prepared to gather this week in Beijing. During a four-day meeting, the group of several hundred officials is expected to lay the groundwork for Mr. Xi to secure a fourth term as party leader next year and also approve strict rules for scrutinizing officials.
黃奇帆的新聞傳出之際，正值黨的精英機構中央委員會準備本週在北京舉行會議。在為期四天的會議期間，這個由數百名官員組成的群體預計將為習近平明年開啟第四個黨領導人任期奠定基礎，並批准用於審查官員的嚴格規則。
廣告
The party is preparing for its next party congress, a gathering that takes place once every five years to announce a leadership overhaul. During that congress, in late 2027, Mr. Xi must appoint a new cohort of younger officials to work under him. That backdrop might explain his renewed fixation on enforcing obedience, experts say.
黨正在為其下一屆全國代表大會做準備，每五年舉行一次的大會旨在宣布領導層的改組。將在2027年晚些時候舉行的這次大會上，習近平必須任命一批新的年輕官員在他麾下工作。專家表示，這一背景或許可以解釋他為何重新執著於強化服從。
“What does Xi want his legacy to be? I think first and foremost, it is a stronger Communist Party, perhaps even more than a stronger China,” said Neil Thomas, a senior fellow on Chinese politics at the Asia Society Policy Institute. “The first is the foremost prerequisite for the latter as far as he’s concerned.”
「習近平希望留下怎樣的遺產？我認為，首要的是一個更強大的共產黨，甚至可能比一個更強大的中國更為重要，」亞洲協會政策研究所中國政治高級研究員牛犇(Neil Thomas)表示。「在他看來，前者是後者的首要前提。」
Another five-year term would cement Mr. Xi’s status as China’s most dominant leader in decades, particularly because he has no obvious successor.
再連任五年將鞏固習近平作為中國數十年來最具主導地位的領導人這一地位，尤其是因為他沒有明確的接班人。
But Mr. Xi “remains perpetually disappointed by the people expected to carry out his wishes,” Mr. Thomas and his colleagues wrote in a preview of the party meeting.
但是，牛犇和他的同事在一份關於這次黨內會議的前瞻中寫道，習近平「對那些本應執行其意願的人始終感到失望」。
The current Central Committee had 376 members when it formed at the last party congress in 2022. But Mr. Xi’s purges have taken a toll. At the committee meeting last year, attendance was down to 315 officials, and the number this week could fall to around 300 or fewer, given the officials who have been expelled, investigated or disappeared.
現屆中央委員會在2022年上屆黨代會成立時共有376名成員。但習近平的清洗行動造成了嚴重減員。在去年的委員會會議上，出席人數降至315名官員。考慮到已經被開除、受調查或失蹤的官員，本週的出席人數可能會降至約300人甚至更少。
Mr. Xi said in a speech early this year, which was published this month, that too many officials fail to faithfully implement orders and instead feign obedience. Some were “two-faced,” concealing their disdain for party rules and values behind a mask of loyalty, he said. Likening corrupt officials to diseased trees, he said, the party should “pull out the sick trees that should be pulled out, chop down the ones that should be chopped down.”
習近平在今年早些時候的一篇講話中（該講話於本月發表）表示，有太多的官員未能忠實地執行命令，反而假裝順從。他說，有些人是「兩面人」，在忠誠的面具背後隱藏著對黨的規則和價值觀的蔑視。他將腐敗官員比作有病的樹木，表示黨應該做到「『病樹』該拔就拔、該伐就伐」。
廣告
A big part of Mr. Xi’s agenda is the grinding chore of running the Communist Party with its 101 million members, many of them officials. That party apparatus is how Mr. Xi oversees the world’s second biggest economy and controls 1.4 billion people. Since taking power in 2012 he has revived his party’s power by extending its reach into factories, villages, schools and virtually every corner of society.
習近平議程中的很大一部分是管理擁有1.01億黨員的中國共產黨的繁重日常工作，這些黨員中很多是官員。正是通過這套黨內機制，習近平得以管理世界第二大經濟體並統治14億人口。自2012年上台以來，他將黨的觸角延伸到工廠、村莊、學校以及社會的幾乎每一個角落，重塑了黨的權威。
The Revolution of the Self
自我革命
Mr. Xi appears to believe that the problems in Chinese officialdom are rooted in a lack of discipline and ideological backbone. His remedy for that is “self-revolution,” a program of constant inspections, self-evaluations and criticism sessions intended to keep cadres on their toes.
習近平似乎認為，中國官場的諸多問題根源於紀律鬆懈和思想軟弱。他對此給出的藥方是「自我革命」，這是一項由持續檢查、自我評估和批判會議組成的計劃，旨在讓幹部保持警覺。
“Self-revolution” is not slowing. In 2023, inspectors punished 49 officials in provincial or ministerial-level posts and 24,000 in county-level posts; last year, the corresponding numbers were 69 and 35,000, according to official statistics.
「自我革命」並沒有放緩。根據官方數據，2023年，檢查人員懲處了49名省部級幹部和2.4萬名縣處級幹部；而去年，對應的數字分別為69人和3.5萬人。
Mr. Xi’s purges have lately been most intense in the military. Early this year, China’s topmost general, Zhang Youxia, was detained and accused of corruption and disloyalty. Since 2022, around 100 senior officers have been put under investigation, or simply disappeared, likely under investigation.
習近平的整肅行動最近在軍隊中最為劇烈。今年早些時候，中國最高級別的將領張又俠被拘留，並被指控腐敗和不忠。自2022年以來，已有約100名高級軍官接受調查，或直接失蹤，很可能正在接受調查。
“The anticorruption campaign is not a campaign anymore, because it’s become so normalized,” said Ling Li, a lecturer in Chinese politics and law at the University of Vienna. “On the one hand, it has made the carrot for officials smaller because of the reduced opportunities for corruption. On the other hand, the sticks are getting heavier.”
「反腐運動已經不再是一場臨時運動了，因為它已經變得如此常態化，」維也納大學中國政治與法律講師李玲表示。「一方面，由於腐敗機會的減少，給官員的『胡蘿蔔』變小了。另一方面，『大棒』正變得越來越重。」
The scrutiny of officials is likely to grow ahead of the party congress next year. China’s economy has slowed under the weight of a prolonged housing crash, which has also devastated the revenues of local governments that long relied on land sales. Mr. Xi wants to ensure that despite all that pain, officials stay committed to his rule and to his agenda of building China into a technologically advanced superpower.
在明年的黨代會召開之前，對官員的審查可能會進一步加劇。由於長期的房地產危機，中國經濟有所放緩，也重創了長期依賴土地出讓收入的地方政府財政。習近平希望確保，儘管面臨種種困難，官員們仍能堅定致力於他的領導，以及將中國建設成為技術先進的超級大國的議程。
廣告
“History shows that in times of difficulty, divergent voices may emerge when the party congress congregates,” Ms. Li said. “An injection of fear will make consensus easier to achieve.”
「歷史表明，在困難時期，黨代會召開時可能會出現不同的聲音，」李玲說。「注入恐懼感將使達成共識變得更加容易。」
One jolt of fear may come from the downfall of Mr. Huang, the former Chongqing mayor.
前重慶市長黃奇帆的落馬可能會引發一陣恐慌。
Mr. Huang, 74, retired from major official posts eight years ago, but he enjoyed an outsize public profile, making speeches and writing books on economic issues. But then investigators announced that he was under investigation for “severe violations of party discipline and the law.” Last week, the party also said Xia Linmao, a deputy mayor of Beijing, was under investigation.
74歲的黃奇帆在八年前就從主要公職崗位上退休，但他保持著超乎尋常的高調公眾形象，經常就經濟問題發表演講並撰寫書籍。但隨後調查人員宣布他因「嚴重違紀違法」正在接受調查。上週，黨還宣布北京市副市長夏林茂正在接受調查。
Is a Former Anti-Graft Enforcer in Trouble?
前反腐高官是否陷入麻煩？
Other recent moves by party investigators suggest that Mr. Xi’s suspicions may extend even higher, perhaps to Wang Qishan, 78, the former leader of the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection. Mr. Wang oversaw Mr. Xi’s anticorruption campaign in its first phase, and is now retired.
黨內調查人員最近的其他舉措表明，習近平的懷疑可能延伸到了更高的層級，甚至可能包括78歲的前中央紀律檢查委員會書記王岐山。王岐山曾負責監督習近平反腐行動的第一階段工作，現已退休。
Several of Mr. Wang’s longtime aides — including Zhou Liang, a financial official, and Li Xiaohong, a disciplinary official — have been detained on suspicion of corruption. Dong Hong, another former secretary of Mr. Wang, was imprisoned on corruption charges in 2022. This year, two of Mr. Wang’s former subordinates were demoted from the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection, suggesting that they too might be under a cloud.
王岐山的幾位長期助手已因涉嫌腐敗被拘留，包括金融領域官員周亮和紀檢官員黎曉宏。王岐山的另一位前秘書董宏於2022年因腐敗指控被判刑。今年，王岐山的兩名前下屬被降職，不再擔任中央紀律檢查委員會的職務，這表明他們可能也處於陰影之下。
The reasons that Mr. Wang’s aides have become targets are not clear, and Mr. Wang himself has not been accused of wrongdoing. But the cases have prompted speculation that Mr. Xi is wary of the influence that retired officials could retain through their networks and protégés.
王岐山的助手成為目標的具體原因尚不清楚，王岐山本人也沒有被指控有違法行為。但這些案件引發了人們的猜測，認為習近平對於退休官員可能通過其網路和門生保留的影響力保持警惕。
“I could see Xi thinking that Wang, even though he did so much of Xi’s hatchet work for him, stands as one of the few people with the wherewithal and maybe the sheer chutzpah to be a pain for Xi,” said Jonathan Czin, a researcher on Chinese politics at the Brookings Institution who formerly worked in the Central Intelligence Agency. “It’s about Xi’s mind-set and his perception of risk.”
「我能理解習近平的想法，王岐山即使為他做了那麼多臟活累活，但他依然是少數有能力、甚至有足夠膽量給習近平製造麻煩的人之一，」布魯金斯學會研究中國政治的研究員、曾在美國中央情報局工作過的秦江南(Jonathan Czin)表示。「這關乎習近平的心態以及他對風險的認知。」
The downside of the seemingly endless scrutiny is that Mr. Xi may find it harder to ensure millions of junior officials remain motivated. A recent study has found that officials are often afraid of taking risks. They are sometimes flat-out demoralized.
這種看似無休止的審查帶來的負面效應在於，習近平可能會發現，越來越難確保數以百萬計基層官員保持工作積極性。最近的一項研究發現，官員們往往不敢承擔風險。他們有時感到徹底喪失幹勁。
廣告
“Local governments, especially those in rural areas, don’t have the capabilities and resources to meet all the demands that cascade down from the center,” said Benjamin Hillman, a professor at the Australian National University who was a coauthor of the study. “Local officials sometimes have to fake it.”
「地方政府——尤其是農村地區的地方政府——沒有能力和資源去滿足中央層層下達的所有要求，」該研究的合著者、澳洲國立大學教授本傑明·希爾曼表示。「地方官員有時不得不弄虛作假。」

儲百亮(Chris Buckley)是《紐約時報》首席中國記者，自台北報導中國和台灣問題，重點關注政治、社會變革以及安全和軍事問題。
