Inside a Billionaire’s Paradise With Crypto’s Most Powerful Ex-Convict

I.
One morning in June, the cryptocurrency mogul Changpeng Zhao pulled up to the St. Regis resort in Abu Dhabi in a white Nissan S.U.V. Mr. Zhao is the richest man in crypto, worth an estimated $114 billion, and he lives in a villa nearby, with nine bedrooms, four bodyguards, a chef and a yacht named Da Moon. But he also likes to play the part of a “normal dude,” as he often describes himself, and that day he had offered to give me a personal tour of the city. Outside the St. Regis, Mr. Zhao sat at the wheel of the Nissan, with his iPhone mounted on the windshield, like the world’s wealthiest Uber driver. “My Lamborghini,” he joked as I climbed in.
6月的一個早晨，加密貨幣大佬趙長鵬乘坐一輛白色日產SUV抵達阿布達比的瑞吉酒店。趙長鵬是加密貨幣界首富，身家估計達1140億美元。他住在附近一棟有九間卧室的別墅裡，配有四名保鏢、一名私人廚師和一艘名為「Da Moon」的遊艇。但他也喜歡扮演「普通人」的角色——他經常這樣形容自己——那天他主動提出親自帶我遊覽這座城市。在瑞吉酒店外，趙長鵬坐在日產車的方向盤後，iPhone固定在擋風玻璃上，活像世界上最富有的優步司機。「我的藍寶堅尼，」我上車時他開玩笑說。
Mr. Zhao, to be clear, isn’t a normal dude. Not long ago, the U.S. authorities brought a criminal case against him for his management of Binance, a crypto exchange depicted in court papers as a haven for Russian money launderers, Iranian sanctions evaders and Islamic State terrorists. Mr. Zhao pleaded guilty and escaped with his fortune intact. After serving a four-month prison sentence, he returned to Abu Dhabi, still Binance’s majority shareholder and still the most powerful figure in the industry.
需要明確的是，趙長鵬並不是普通人。不久前，美國當局因他管理幣安而對他提起刑事案件。幣安是一家加密交易所，在法庭文件中被描述為俄羅斯洗錢者、伊朗制裁逃避者和伊斯蘭國恐怖分子的避風港。趙長鵬認罪，但保住了財富。在服完四個月刑期後，他回到阿布達比，仍是幣安多數股東，仍是這個行業最有權力的人物。
These days, Mr. Zhao, who is 49, moves through the world like a head of state, flying around Asia on his private jet to advise governments on crypto regulation; a stop in Islamabad or Manila is often followed by the announcement of a new regulatory breakthrough for Binance. At home, one of his two dogs is a Taigan, an elegant Central Asian breed, likened to the gazelle. It was a gift from the crypto-curious president of Kyrgyzstan, with whom he went skiing in February. “He actually wanted to give me a tent, like a full-sized tent,” Mr. Zhao said. “I said no to the tent, and then he insisted on the dog.”
如今，49歲的趙長鵬像國家元首一樣在世界各地活動，乘坐私人飛機在亞洲各地飛行，就加密貨幣監管問題向各國政府提供建議；每當他造訪伊斯蘭瑪巴德或馬尼拉，往往緊接著就會傳出幣安在監管方面取得新突破的消息。在家裡，他的兩隻狗中有一隻是泰乾犬，一種優雅的中亞品種，常被比作瞪羚。這是對加密貨幣頗感興趣的吉爾吉斯斯坦總統送給他的禮物，今年2月他還和這位總統一起滑雪。「他其實想送我一項帳篷，一頂全尺寸帳篷，」趙長鵬說。「我拒絕了帳篷，然後他堅持要送狗。」

Mr. Zhao’s most influential allies are a pair of powerful ruling families. One is the clan that runs the United Arab Emirates, which wants his help creating a technology powerhouse in the desert. The other is the Trumps. Last year, Binance forged a business relationship with World Liberty Financial, the crypto start-up founded by President Trump and his three sons. World Liberty generated $799 million for Mr. Trump last year — his single most lucrative asset, more remunerative than his real estate portfolio or his social media business. Mr. Trump pardoned Mr. Zhao last October, a move widely criticized as a quid pro quo.
趙長鵬最有影響力的盟友是兩個強大的統治家族。一個是管理阿拉伯聯合酋長國的家族，他們希望趙長鵬幫助他們在沙漠中打造一個科技強國。另一個是川普家族。去年，幣安與世界自由金融公司建立了商業關係，這家加密初創公司由川普總統和他的三個兒子創立。世界自由去年為川普帶來了7.99億美元——這是他最賺錢的單一資產，比他的房地產投資組合或社群媒體業務更賺錢。川普去年10月赦免了趙長鵬，此舉被廣泛批評為利益交換。
Known in the industry as “CZ,” Mr. Zhao often avoids reporters, but he met with me several times this year, eager to put a friendlier gloss on his career at Binance, which still hasn’t stanched the flow of dirty money. He is tall and thin, with short dark hair and a Binance tattoo on his forearm, and lacks the frat-bro swagger of many of his crypto peers. He comes across as polite and attentive, though his interests are almost comically narrow. This year, he self-published a memoir, “Freedom of Money,” in which he describes receiving an after-hours tour of the Louvre. The experience left him cold. The guide might as well have been “playing piano to a cow,” he wrote.
在業內被稱為「CZ」的趙長鵬經常迴避記者，但今年他多次與我會面，渴望為自己在幣安的職業生涯增添一層更友好的光環——儘管幣安至今仍未止住髒錢的流動。他身材高瘦，留著一頭短黑髮，前臂上有幣安的紋身，身上沒有許多加密貨幣同行那種兄弟會成員般的張揚氣勢。他看起來禮貌而專注，儘管他的興趣範圍狹窄得近乎滑稽。今年，他自行出版了回憶錄《金錢的自由》(Freedom of Money)，書中描述了他曾獲邀在閉館後參觀羅浮宮的經歷。這次體驗讓他感到索然無味。他寫道，導遊簡直是在「對牛彈琴」。
For years, his true calling has been what academics term “jurisdictional arbitrage” — moving from country to country in search of a legal framework pliable enough to accommodate his appetite for risk and growth. In Abu Dhabi, he may have found an ideal base of operations. As we drove into the city, Mr. Zhao chatted amiably, pausing to ask his bodyguard for help with directions. (“Yes, boss, stay right.”) We passed a string of impressive compounds and drove toward the presidential palace, Qasr Al Watan, whose striking white dome resembles the Taj Mahal.
多年來，他真正的志向是學者所稱的「管轄套利」——輾轉於各國之間，尋找一個足夠靈活的法律框架，以滿足他對風險和增長的渴望。在阿布達比，他可能找到了理想的運營基地。當我們驅車進入市區時，趙長鵬友好地聊天，不時停下來讓保鏢幫忙指路。（「是的，老闆，靠右。」）我們經過一連串氣派的建築群，駛向總統府卡斯爾·阿爾·瓦坦，其醒目的白色穹頂酷似泰姬陵。
Mr. Zhao was growing animated. “Democracy has been hailed as, like, the holy grail for the last couple hundred years, but democracies are inefficient,” he said as we approached the palace. “If you get a benevolent dictator, it’s actually extremely efficient. There’s very little debate going on. You don’t waste time on a lot of frivolous stuff.”
趙長鵬變得興奮起來。「幾百年來，民主一直被奉為聖杯，但民主效率低下，」我們在接近總統府時他說。「如果你有一個仁慈的獨裁者，那實際上極其高效。幾乎沒有爭論。你不會把時間浪費在許多無聊的事情上。」
Lately, this top-down model of business and politics, simultaneously medieval and ultramodern, has gained traction in the United States. On a trip to Abu Dhabi last year, Mr. Trump called the nation’s ruler, Sheikh Mohammed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, “a very strong man” and “a man of vision like few others.” Zach Witkoff, the son of one of Mr. Trump’s top advisers and himself a kind of MAGA princeling, put it more bluntly at a crypto conference in Dubai in May last year. “We really need to take a page out of His Highness’s and the Emirates’ book,” Mr. Witkoff said. “They are just an amazing example of how you can lead with innovation while also maintaining your family values.”
最近，這種自上而下、同時兼具中世紀和超現代色彩的商業和政治模式在美國獲得了越來越多的支持。去年訪問阿布達比時，川普稱該國統治者謝赫·穆罕默德·本·扎耶德·阿勒納哈揚是「一個非常強大的人」和「少有的有遠見的人」。扎克·威特科夫是川普一位高級顧問的兒子，本身也算得上是「讓美國再次偉大」陣營的王子之一，去年5月，他在杜拜的一次加密會議上說得更直白。「我們真的需要借鑒殿下和阿聯酋的經驗，」威特科夫說。「他們是一個了不起的榜樣，展示了如何在保持家庭價值觀的同時，通過創新來引領發展。」
Mr. Zhao is already living in the future these men crave, nestled in an elite enclave where monarchs and billionaires operate in harmony. As he drove away from Qasr Al Watan, he told me about a private conference he had attended nearby, at the Emirates Palace Mandarin Oriental hotel, a gathering of executives like the hedge fund founder Ray Dalio and the Japanese billionaire Masayoshi Son. While he sat in the audience, Mr. Zhao noticed someone stooping down to speak with him. It was Sheikh Mohammed. He wanted to know how Mr. Zhao was getting along and whether he could do anything to help.
趙長鵬已經生活在這些人夢寐以求的未來中，他棲身在一個精英聚居區，君主和億萬富翁和諧共處。當他驅車離開卡斯爾·阿爾·瓦坦時，他告訴我，他曾在附近酋長宮文華東方酒店參加一個私人會議，與會者包括對沖基金創始人雷·達里奧和日本億萬富翁孫正義等高管。坐在觀眾席時，趙長鵬注意到有人彎下腰來和他說話，正是謝赫·穆罕默德。他想知道趙長鵬過得怎麼樣，以及他是否能幫上什麼忙。

“This is the king,” Mr. Zhao told me, “kneeling down beside you.”
「這是國王，」趙長鵬告訴我，「他跪在你旁邊。」

One day early this year, Mr. Zhao was working from bed — he often does business lying on his back after a long struggle with chronic pain — when the windows began to shake. He got up, he told me, and walked onto his balcony, where he saw a trail of smoke in the sky. Mr. Zhao lives on Saadiyat Island, an enclave of Abu Dhabi across the Persian Gulf from Iran, which had just fired a missile at the Emirates.
今年年初的一天，趙長鵬正躺在床上工作——在與慢性疼痛長期鬥爭後，他經常躺著處理公務——這時窗戶開始震動。他告訴我，他起身走到陽台上，看到天空中有一道煙霧。趙長鵬住在薩迪亞特島，這是阿布達比的一塊飛地，與伊朗隔波斯灣相望，伊朗剛剛向阿聯酋發射了一枚導彈。
It was the start of a geopolitical crisis that has destabilized the region, but Mr. Zhao was not overly concerned. He went back to bed and kept working. “More people die from traffic accidents than from the missiles,” he said later. “Very protected here.”
這是一場地緣政治危機的開始，這場危機已使該地區局勢穩，但趙長鵬並不過分擔心。他回到床上繼續工作。「死於交通事故的人比死於導彈的人多，」他後來說。「這裡非常受保護。」
Until he settled in the Emirates, Mr. Zhao was a man without a country. He was born in China, then moved to Canada with his family at the age of 12. After attending McGill, he worked a series of technology jobs, bouncing among New York, China and Japan. In July 2013, over a poker game in Shanghai, a colleague suggested he check out “this new thing called Bitcoin.”
在定居阿聯酋之前，趙長鵬是一個沒有國家的人。他出生在中國，12歲時隨家人移居加拿大。在麥吉爾大學畢業後，他在紐約、中國和日本從事了一系列技術工作。2013年7月，在上海的一場撲克牌局中，一位同事建議他看看「這個叫比特幣的新東西」。
It was the perfect borderless technology for his borderless lifestyle. After a few months of study, Mr. Zhao made a radical bet. He sold the family home — a three-bedroom apartment in Shanghai where he and his wife were raising two children — and plowed the $900,000 proceeds into Bitcoin. It was a risky move, but Mr. Zhao wasn’t worried. Even if Bitcoin plunged, he figured, he could easily land another high-paying job in traditional finance.
這正是與他這種無國界生活方式完美契合的無國界技術。經過幾個月的研究，趙長鵬下了一個激進的賭注。他賣掉了家庭住宅——上海一套三居室公寓，他和妻子在那裡撫養兩個孩子——將約合90萬美元的收益投入比特幣。這是一個冒險的舉動，但趙長鵬並不擔心。他想，即使比特幣暴跌，他也能輕鬆在傳統金融業找到另一份高薪工作。

Mr. Zhao went on to work at a succession of crypto start-ups in Asia. The industry grew rapidly in the 2010s, with hundreds of new tokens, but the platforms for trading these coins were poorly run and vulnerable to hacks. In July 2017, Mr. Zhao founded Binance. Within a few months it was the largest exchange in the world, a title it has never relinquished.
他隨後在亞洲一系列加密幣初創公司工作。該行業在2010年代迅速發展，出現了數百種新代幣，但交易這些代幣的平台運營不善，容易受到駭客攻擊。2017年7月，趙長鵬創立了幣安。幾個月內，它就成為全球最大的交易所，並一直保持這個地位。
Binance’s success rested on two pivotal insights. The first was that crypto investors, as a species, were drunk on risk and would never be satisfied swapping dollars or yen merely for an equivalent amount of crypto. For years, Binance has operated as a margin exchange: Traders can borrow money to place high-risk, high-reward bets on the prices of Bitcoin and other popular tokens.
幣安的成功基於兩個關鍵洞察。第一，加密幣投資者作為一個物種，沉醉於風險，永遠不會滿足於僅僅用美元或日元換取等量的加密貨幣。多年來，幣安一直作為保證金交易所運營：交易者可以借錢對比特幣和其他熱門代幣的價格進行高風險、高回報的押注。
The second insight was simpler yet more consequential. Many crypto investors didn’t want to identify themselves, and Binance didn’t ask them to. For its first four years, the company allowed customers to open accounts without providing even the most basic K.Y.C. — the “know your customer” information that traditional companies require to prevent financial crime.
第二個洞察更簡單但更為重大。許多加密幣投資者不想透露身份，而幣安也不要求他們這樣做。在最初的四年裡，該公司允許客戶甚至不提供最基本的KYC——傳統公司為防止金融犯罪而要求的「了解你的客戶」信息——就開設帳戶。
Binance was not the only exchange to offer these types of risky services, but Mr. Zhao executed better than any of his competitors. “There was a lot of MySpace in the exchange world, and he built Facebook,” said Austin Campbell, the founder of a crypto consulting firm. The result was an extremely lucrative free-for-all: a platform where unvetted investment products were available to unvetted traders. As the market boomed, Binance took a cut of each transaction; the company quickly reached $1 billion in profits, according to “Freedom of Money.”
幣安並不是唯一提供這類風險服務的交易所，但趙長鵬執行得比任何競爭對手都執行得更好。「交易所世界有很多MySpace，而他建造了Facebook，」加密幣諮詢公司創始人奧斯汀·坎貝爾說。結果是一個利潤極其豐厚的自由競爭場所：一個未經審查的投資產品可供未經審查的交易者使用的平台。隨著市場繁榮，幣安從每筆交易中抽成；根據《金錢的自由》，公司的利潤很快達到10億美元。
Mr. Zhao was suddenly a crypto celebrity. But he has never been widely understood outside the industry, even as he has joined a small cohort of ultrawealthy executives who wield astonishing political influence. When he recounts his most contentious moments, Mr. Zhao portrays himself as a kind of passenger — a crypto nerd overwhelmed by success, a naïve outsider who somehow stumbled into Bond villain status.
他突然成了加密幣名人。但他在行業外從未被廣泛理解，即使他已加入一小群擁有驚人政治影響力的超富高管行列。當講述自己最具爭議的時刻時，趙長鵬把自己描繪成某種乘客——一個被成功淹沒的加密幣書呆子，一個不知何故跌入007系列大反派地位的幼稚局外人。
It is not always a convincing performance. Sitting in his kitchen in late June, Mr. Zhao told me that he had “never really wanted” to break the law in Binance’s explosive early years, when the company’s lax controls allowed a procession of bad actors to launder money. “It’s just my lack of experience in that area,” he said. “I’m a tech guy. I lived in China.”
他的表現並不總是令人信服。6月底，趙長鵬坐在他的廚房裡告訴我，在幣安爆炸性的早期歲月裡，他「從未真正想要」違法，當時公司寬鬆的控制縱容了一連串不良行為者洗錢。「這只是我在那個領域缺乏經驗，」他說。「我是個技術人。我住在中國。」

That image isn’t compatible with the evidence U.S. prosecutors collected in their investigation into Binance. In court, they quoted an admonition that Mr. Zhao had given his underlings: “Better to ask for forgiveness than permission.” Privately, Mr. Zhao had discussed ways to obfuscate that Binance served American users even though it wasn’t licensed in the United States, according to legal filings. “Don’t leave anything in writing,” he told a subordinate.
這一形象與美國檢察官在調查幣安時收集的證據不符。在法庭上，他們引用了趙長鵬對下屬的告誡：「請求原諒比請求許可更好。」根據法律文件，他私下裡談論如何掩蓋幣安為美國用戶服務的事實，儘管公司並未在美國獲得許可。「不要留下任何書面東西，」他告訴一名下屬。
In Abu Dhabi, Mr. Zhao described himself as a “rule follower.” It seems obvious, however, that he made a strategic choice to push the legal limits at a crucial time, when the habits of a new generation of investors were forming and the rules themselves were rather elastic.
在阿布達比，趙長鵬稱自己是一個「循規蹈矩的人」。然而，顯而易見的是，他在一個關鍵時刻做出了挑戰法律極限的戰略決定，當時新一代投資者的習慣正在形成，而規則本身也相當有彈性。
I was reminded of this when he showed me into the entrance of his house, which was stocked with boxes of Binance swag. He urged me to pick out a gift, perhaps a Binance backpack or a yellow-and-black Binance sweater, styled to look like a varsity jacket.
當他帶著我走進他家時，我想起了這一點。家裡堆滿了成箱的幣安周邊商品。他要我挑一件禮物，也許是幣安背包或黃黑相間的幣安毛衣，款式像校隊夾克。
I told him that reporters have protocols about these sorts of things, and that I had to decline any gift over $20. He laughed, and said the price was “probably 19 bucks.”
我告訴他，記者對這類事情有規定，我必須拒絕任何超過20美元的禮物。他笑了，說價格「大概19美元」。

Mr. Zhao paid a visit to the Emirates in 2021, after years of roaming around Asia. In 2017, he and his Binance colleagues had been forced to flee China in the middle of the night, as the government prepared to announce a ban on crypto exchanges. A stint in Japan hadn’t worked out, either; nor had Singapore, where it was difficult to get a work visa. Then Mr. Zhao’s friend Gabriel Abed, a businessman who served as the ambassador to the Emirates for Barbados, made a suggestion. He told Mr. Zhao to check out Dubai.
在亞洲各地漫遊多年後，趙長鵬於2021年去了阿聯酋。2017年，他和幣安同事被迫半夜逃離中國，因為政府準備宣布禁止加密幣交易所。在日本的一段經歷也沒有成功；新加坡也是如此，那裡很難獲得工作簽證。然後他的朋友加布里埃爾·阿貝德——一位擔任過巴巴多斯駐阿聯酋大使的商人——提出了一個建議。他讓趙長鵬去看看杜拜。
廣告
Immediately, Mr. Zhao saw how the Emiratis treated the rich and powerful. He arrived in Dubai on a Saturday morning in October, and within hours, a government minister, Omar Sultan Al Olama, came to visit. He offered Mr. Zhao a so-called golden visa, which grants 10 years of hassle-free residency. A doctor was summoned to conduct the blood test required of all visa holders. Applications often take weeks to process. Mr. Zhao was approved in less than 12 hours.
他立刻看到了阿聯酋人給有錢有勢的人提供的待遇。他於10月一個週六上午抵達杜拜，幾小時內，政府部長奧馬爾·蘇丹·阿爾·奧拉馬就來拜訪。他向趙長鵬提供了一種所謂的黃金簽證，授予10年無憂居留權。一名醫生被召來進行所有簽證持有者所需的血液檢測。申請通常需要數週處理。他在不到12小時內就獲批了。
The friendly reception impressed Mr. Zhao. “On my fourth day in Dubai, I saw two properties,” he told me. “I bought the second one.” By the end of the year, Binance had signed a memorandum of understanding with the local authorities to develop rules for crypto operations; the company dispatched three staff members to help put together a framework. A draft was completed in January 2022 and signed into law a few weeks later. “When you’re the captain of a ship that’s sailing true north, the U.A.E. wants you on their team,” Mr. Abed said. “They did that with CZ. They rallied around him from Day 1.”
友好的接待給趙長鵬留下了深刻印象。「在杜拜的第四天，我看了兩處房產，」他告訴我。「我買了第二處。」到年底，幣安已與地方當局簽署諒解備忘錄，制定加密幣運營規則；該公司派出三名員工幫助制定框架。草案於2022年1月完成，幾周後簽署成為法律。「當你是一艘駛向正北的船的船長時，阿聯酋希望你加入他們的團隊，」阿貝德說。「他們對趙長鵬就是這樣。從第一天起，他們就簇擁著他。」
Not everyone in the Emirates is treated so generously. Over the years, the nation’s leaders have conducted mass surveillance, outlawed even the mildest forms of dissent and overseen a labor market where foreign workers are frequently exploited and abused.
並非阿聯酋每個人都受到如此慷慨的對待。多年來，該國領導人進行了大規模監視，將哪怕最輕微的異見定為非法，並監管著一個外國工人經常被剝削和虐待的勞動力市場。
But for Mr. Zhao, it was the beginning of a comfortable new life. Before founding Binance, he had separated from his wife, who moved to Tokyo with their children. He started a relationship with one of his colleagues, He Yi, a marketing executive known in China as the former host of a travel program called “Beautiful Destinations.” While his older children attended college in the United States, Mr. Zhao and Ms. He raised a young family in the Emirates.
但對趙長鵬來說，這是舒適新生活的開始。在創立幣安之前，他已與妻子分居，妻子帶著孩子搬到了東京。他開始與同事何一交往，何一是一位營銷高管，曾在中國因主持一檔名為《美麗目的地》的旅遊節目而聞名。他的幾個年齡較長的子女在美國上大學，趙長鵬和何一則在阿聯酋撫養年幼的孩子。
Mr. Zhao was ushered into a rarefied world of billionaire networking. He got to know a group of Emirati leaders who were curious about technology — the “crypto sheikhs,” as he calls them. Eventually he worked his way up to the brother of Sheikh Mohammed, Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed Al Nahyan, a jujitsu enthusiast who would host high-powered businessmen at his palace. On the royal dinner circuit, Mr. Zhao was treated as a valued guest. “We’re like, what’s the seating arrangement?’” he said. “Just sit anywhere, it’s fine.”
趙長鵬被引入億萬富翁社交圈的精英世界。他結識了一群對技術好奇的阿聯酋領導人——他稱之為「加密幣酋長」。最終，他一路結識了謝赫·穆罕默德的兄弟謝赫·塔赫農·本·扎耶德·阿勒納哈揚，一位柔術愛好者，會在自己的宮殿接待有權勢的商人。在皇家晚宴圈中，趙長鵬被視為貴賓。「我們就問啊，『座位怎麼安排的呢？』」他說。「隨便坐，無所謂。」
During these early encounters, the sheikhs would pitch Mr. Zhao on the Emirates, asking what they could do to help him. Soon Mr. Zhao was preaching the nation’s virtues to the broader billionaire community. He encouraged the artificial intelligence mogul Sam Altman to set up a corporate presence in the Emirates, warning that other countries would treat him with hostility. “You’re going to face a lot of regulatory issues,” Mr. Zhao told him over coffee.
在這些早期接觸中，酋長們會向趙長鵬推銷阿聯酋，詢問他們能做什麼來幫助他。很快，趙長鵬就開始向更廣泛的億萬富翁群體宣揚這個國家的優點。他鼓勵人工智慧巨頭薩姆·奧爾特曼在阿聯酋設立公司，警告其他國家會對他懷有敵意。「你會面臨很多監管問題，」趙長鵬在一次喝咖啡時告訴奧爾特曼。
Mr. Zhao had more than a passing familiarity with the perils of emerging industries. As he arrived in the Emirates, Mr. Zhao was locked in a vicious competition with a young entrepreneur named Sam Bankman-Fried, the founder of a rival exchange, FTX. The son of Stanford Law School professors, Mr. Bankman-Fried was in many ways the polar opposite of Mr. Zhao: a consummate insider whose personal connections and proximity to the U.S. political elite greased his path to the top of the industry. “He had tricks I didn’t have,” Mr. Zhao wrote in his memoir. Mr. Bankman-Fried would poach Binance employees, offering salaries three to five times as high. Mr. Zhao also heard rumors that Mr. Bankman-Fried was bad-mouthing him in Washington.
趙長鵬對新興產業的風險絕非一知半解。當他抵達阿聯酋時，正與一位名叫薩姆·班克曼-弗里德的年輕企業家陷入激烈競爭，後者是競爭對手交易所FTX的創始人。班克曼-弗里德是史丹佛法學院教授的兒子，在許多方面與趙長鵬截然相反，是一個徹頭徹尾的圈內人，其個人人脈和與美國政治精英的密切關係為他鋪平了通往行業頂峰的道路。「他有我沒有的手段，」趙長鵬在回憶錄中寫道。班克曼-弗里德會挖走幣安員工，提供三到五倍的薪水。趙長鵬還聽到傳言，班克曼-弗里德在華盛頓說他壞話。
In November 2022, a report in the crypto publication CoinDesk raised doubts about the stability of FTX’s finances. Mr. Zhao was in Dubai, on his way to have drinks with Mr. Abed, when he decided to weigh in on Twitter, posting a short but ruthless announcement. He was selling a stash of $500 million in FTT, a digital coin that was a proxy for FTX stock. The post set off a panic, as anxious investors rushed to pull money out of FTX.
2022年11月，加密貨幣媒體CoinDesk的一份報告對FTX財務的穩定性提出質疑。趙長鵬當時在杜拜，正要去和阿貝德喝酒，他決定在Twitter上表態，發布了一條簡短但無情的聲明。他要出售5億美元的FTT，這是一種代表FTX股票的數碼代幣。這條帖子引發恐慌，焦慮的投資者紛紛從FTX撤資。
The money wasn’t there — Mr. Bankman-Fried had stolen $8 billion from his customers. He had no choice but to seek a bailout, and only one man in crypto had the resources to save him. Two days after his tweet, Mr. Zhao announced that he was buying FTX; a day later, he pulled out of the deal, sending FTX deeper into crisis. It all had the feel of a masterful bit of corporate warfare — an act of “blood sport,” as one observer put it. Within six weeks, Mr. Bankman-Fried was arrested at his apartment in the Bahamas and extradited to the United States. He was eventually sentenced to 25 years in prison for fraud.
但資金早已不復存在——班克曼-弗里德從客戶那裡偷了80億美元。他別無選擇，只能尋求救助，而加密貨幣界只有一個人擁有拯救他的資源。發推兩天後，趙長鵬宣布收購FTX；一天後，他退出交易，使FTX進一步陷入危機。這一切感覺像一場高超的企業戰爭——一位觀察者稱之為「血腥運動」。六週內，班克曼-弗里德在巴哈馬的公寓被捕，並被引渡到美國。他最終因欺詐被判處25年監禁。
Mr. Zhao told me that he had never meant to destroy FTX. Slipping into his usual mode of shrugging naïveté, he explained that he had hoped to gradually sell his FTT over a few months. “I had no intention to crash them,” he said. “We lost $500 million.”
趙長鵬告訴我，他從未打算摧毀FTX。他一如既往地聳聳肩，做出一副天真的樣子解釋說，他原本希望在幾個月內逐步拋售自己的FTT。「我無意搞垮他們，」他說。「我們損失了5億美元。」
His vanquished rival sees it differently. “He played me,” Mr. Bankman-Fried said at the time. “He played it well.”
那位被他擊敗的對手並不這麼看。「他耍了我，」班克曼-弗里德當時說。「他幹得很絕。」

IV.
Billionaires gravitate to the Emirates for billionaire reasons. The country doesn’t levy an income tax, and it’s considered extremely safe, at a time when crypto investors have been targeted by kidnappers in Europe and the United States. But for Mr. Zhao, who had just seen his nemesis dragged across borders, the Emirates held another important advantage: It had no extradition treaty with the United States.
億萬富翁青睞阿聯酋自有億萬富翁的原因。該國不徵收所得稅，而且被認為極其安全，尤其是在加密投資者在歐洲和美國成為綁架目標的時候。但對於剛剛目睹宿敵被跨國緝捕的趙長鵬而言，阿聯酋還擁有另一項重要優勢：它與美國沒有引渡條約。
In either late 2022 or early 2023, he recalled, an Emirati royal asked him over dinner whether he wanted citizenship. Mr. Zhao sent in some documents and waited. Months later, a government emissary arrived at his house, holding a box with passports for Mr. Zhao and his family. The Emiratis “would never extradite citizens,” Mr. Zhao told me. “That was a very strong thing.”
他回憶說，在2022年底或2023年初，一位阿聯酋皇室成員在晚宴上問他，是否想要公民身份。趙長鵬提交了一些文件，等待結果。幾個月後，一名政府使者來到他家，拿著一個盒子，裡面是趙長鵬及其家人的護照。阿聯酋人「永遠不會引渡公民，」趙長鵬告訴我。「這分量很重。」
He had reason to be wary: The noose around Binance was tightening. U.S. prosecutors were collecting evidence that the exchange’s lax protocols had allowed scammers, hackers and sanctions evaders to move money. In private messages obtained by the government, Binance employees spoke with astonishing candor about the exchange’s shady clientele. “Is washing drug money too hard these days,” one employee wrote. “Come to Binance we got cake for you.”
他有理由警惕：圍繞幣安周圍的絞索正在收緊。美國檢察官正在收集證據，證明該交易所寬鬆的協議允許騙子、駭客和制裁逃避者轉移資金。在政府獲得的私人信息中，幣安員工以驚人的坦率談論交易所的可疑客戶。「現在給毒品洗錢太難了嗎，」一名員工寫道。「來幣安，我們為你準備了蛋糕。」
Mr. Zhao hired a team of lawyers to broker a deal with the Justice Department. His new passport gave him considerable leverage. After a couple of years in Dubai, he moved an hour and a half south to Abu Dhabi, closer to the nation’s center of political power. While the negotiations unfolded, Mr. Zhao gave serious thought to life as a fugitive; he would have to limit his traveling, but he would be safe in the Emirates, at least in theory.
趙長鵬聘請了一個律師團隊與司法部斡旋達成協議。他的新護照給了他相當大的籌碼。在杜拜生活了幾年後，他向南遷移了一個半小時車程到達阿布達比，更接近國家政治權力中心。談判進行期間，趙長鵬認真考慮過逃亡生活；他必須限制旅行，但在阿聯酋他很安全，至少在理論上如此。
Eventually, his legal team struck a bargain with the U.S. government. Mr. Zhao would plead guilty to violating the Bank Secrecy Act, an anti-money-laundering statute, and step down as chief executive. (The company would pay a $4.3 billion penalty.) Despite the generous terms, he was frustrated with his lawyers and didn’t think he deserved any prison time at all. “I got put in jail because we had a platform that didn’t have proper K.Y.C.,” he told me. “You can vote for the president of the United States government without having an ID. I don’t know how that system came about. I would imagine someone should be put in jail for that, given what I went through.”
最終，他的法律團隊與美國政府達成協議。趙長鵬將對違反反洗錢法規《銀行保密法》認罪，並辭去首席執行官職務。（公司將支付43億美元罰款。）儘管協議條件相當寬鬆，但他對律師們感到不滿，並認為自己根本不應該坐牢。「我坐牢是因為我們的平台沒有實施適當的『了解你的客戶』(KYC)措施，」他告訴我。「在美國，你甚至不需要身份證就能投票選舉總統。我不知道那個系統是怎麼來的。考慮到我的經歷，我覺得應該有人為此坐牢。」
Still, it was an excellent outcome for Binance. At the sentencing hearing in the spring of 2024, Mr. Zhao delivered a contrite statement: “I deeply regret my failure, and I’m sorry.” The judge gave Mr. Zhao a four-month sentence, calling him “a dedicated family man and a giving person.” When the sentence was read aloud in the courtroom, Mr. Zhao’s college-age son quietly pumped his fist.
儘管如此，這對幣安來說是一個極好的結果。在2024年春季的量刑聽證會上，趙長鵬發表了悔過聲明：「我深深後悔我的失敗，我很抱歉。」法官判處趙長鵬四個月監禁，稱他為「盡職的家庭男人和慷慨的人」。當判決在法庭上宣讀時，趙長鵬上大學的兒子悄悄握拳慶祝。
As the rare felon with virtually unlimited resources, Mr. Zhao was ideally positioned to suffer as little as possible. He was assigned to Lompoc II, a low-security facility in California, the same complex where Mr. Bankman-Fried was later housed. Before he reported to prison, Mr. Zhao assembled a team of advisers, who schooled him in the subtleties of cellblock politics. One of them, Michael Santos, exchanged letters with other inmates, who agreed to help Mr. Zhao get his bearings inside. “CZ, you’re a guy who’s understood people and had thousands of employees,” Mr. Santos told him. “You’re not going to lose those skills when you walk in here.”
作為少有的幾乎擁有無限資源的重罪犯，趙長鵬處境理想，可以盡量少受點苦。他被分配到加利福尼亞州低安全級別的洛姆波克II監獄，班克曼-弗里德後來也被關押在同一監獄園區。在報到入獄前，趙長鵬組建了一個顧問團隊，向他傳授了監獄內部政治的種種門道。其中一位顧問麥可·桑托斯與其他囚犯通信，他們同意幫助趙長鵬在獄中熟悉情況。「CZ，你是一個理解別人、擁有數千名員工的人，」桑托斯告訴他。「你走進這裡，也不會失去這些技能。」
Mr. Zhao arrived at Lompoc on the morning of May 30, 2024. He quickly settled into what’s known as a “car” — an informal social group, often established along ethnic lines. The other Asian inmates sat with Mr. Zhao during meals and worked out with him in the yard at recess. They were thrilled to be in the presence of a multibillionaire. “Everybody gave way for him,” said Jaton Aguon, a fellow inmate at Lompoc. “It was like a king walking down the aisle.”
趙長鵬於2024年5月30日上午抵達洛姆波克。他很快融入了一個被稱為「車」的非正式社交團體，這類團體通常按族裔建立。其他亞裔囚犯在用餐時與趙長鵬坐在一起，在放風時和他在院子裡鍛煉。他們為能接近一位億萬富翁而興奮。「每個人都給他讓路，」洛姆波克的同獄犯賈頓·阿古昂說。「就像國王來了一樣。」
Mr. Zhao grew close with a Vietnamese bank robber who had once worked as an engineer. They talked in detail about the robberies, which didn’t involve any of the “Ocean’s Eleven”-style preparation one might expect. He was “very mellow” and “a super-chill dude,” Mr. Zhao told me. “He just walks in, asks for the money and leaves. And he got away with it 11 times.”
趙長鵬與一名曾是工程師的越南銀行劫匪關係密切。他們詳細談論了搶劫案，這些搶劫並不涉及人們可能想像的《十一羅漢》式準備。他「非常溫和」，「一個很平靜的傢伙，」趙長鵬告訴我。「他就直接走進去，要錢，然後離開。他乾成了11票。」
The bank robber was one of a handful of inmates who were interested in the business world. He read Bloomberg and The Wall Street Journal and asked Mr. Zhao about Elon Musk’s astronomical compensation package at Tesla. Other inmates also had questions about business, including one who wanted to understand the mysteries of the crypto market. “He’s like, well, Bitcoin — why did it go up?” Mr. Zhao recalled. “I’m like, wow, I don’t know for sure, either.” Mr. Zhao started a study group that tracked stock prices; one of the prison TVs was tuned to CNBC.
這名銀行劫匪是少數對商業世界感興趣的囚犯之一。他讀彭博社和《華爾街日報》，問趙長鵬關於伊隆·馬斯克在特斯拉的天價薪酬方案。其他囚犯也有商業問題，其中一人想了解加密幣市場的奧秘。「他說，好吧，比特幣——為什麼會漲？」趙長鵬回憶說。「我說，哇，我也不確定。」趙長鵬成立了一個追蹤股價的學習小組；監獄的一台電視調到了CNBC。
Not every one of his messages landed. At Lompoc, Mr. Zhao had a hard time conveying his deeply held belief that the Binance case was essentially victimless, with “no fraud, no nothing.” When he explained the details, the response was dismissive. “They’re like, yeah, that’s what everybody says,” Mr. Zhao recalled.
並非他所有的信息都被人接受。在洛姆波克，趙長鵬很難傳達他深信的幣安案本質上沒有受害者，「沒有欺詐，什麼都沒有。」當他解釋細節時，回應是輕蔑的。「他們說，是啊，每個人都這麼說，」趙長鵬回憶說。
In August 2024, Mr. Zhao left Lompoc for a halfway house. But a complication with his immigration status meant he had to spend his last two weeks at a local jail, passing the time doing push-ups and crunches in his cell. On the day of his release, an officer escorted him outside, where his mother, sister and assistant were waiting. They drove to a nearby airport and deposited him onto a private plane bound for Abu Dhabi.
2024年8月，趙長鵬離開洛姆波克前往過渡教習所。但由於他的移民身份造成的複雜情況，他必須在當地監獄度過最後兩週，在牢房裡做俯卧撐和仰卧起坐打發時間。獲釋那天，一名警官護送他出去，他的母親、妹妹和助手在那裡等候。他們驅車前往附近機場，將他送上飛往阿布達比的私人飛機。
Between the moment Mr. Zhao left custody and the moment his jet took off, only 26 minutes elapsed.
從他離開拘留所到飛機起飛只用了26分鐘。

V.
Mr. Zhao returned to a world that was shifting in his favor. A crypto-friendly administration was poised to take power in Washington. Shortly before the election, Mr. Trump had founded World Liberty as a joint enterprise with the family of Steve Witkoff, a real estate mogul who was preparing to serve as White House envoy to the Middle East. Soon America’s newly empowered dealmaker-diplomats were spending lots of time in the Emirates.
趙長鵬回到了一個正朝著有利於他的方向轉變的世界。一個對加密幣友好的政府即將在華盛頓掌權。選舉前不久，川普與房地產巨頭史提夫·威特科夫的家族共同創立了World Liberty，威特科夫正準備擔任白宮中東特使。很快，美國新近掌權的交易掮客兼外交官們開始花大量時間在阿聯酋。
As he drove me around the city, Mr. Zhao paused to point out the ADNEC Center, a conference hall that hosted a giant gathering of crypto enthusiasts in December 2024. “This is where I met Eric Trump,” he said. At the venue, he recalled, a Trump booster named David Bailey had introduced him to the president’s middle son, a leader of World Liberty. It was a casual conversation, with no official agenda. “He was just saying that he was looking at some real estate deal here,” Mr. Zhao told me.
當他開車帶我遊覽城市時，趙長鵬停下來指著ADNEC中心，這是一個會議廳，2024年12月曾舉辦大型加密幣愛好者聚會。「這是我見到埃里克·川普的地方，」他說。他回憶說，在會場，一位名叫戴維·貝利的川普支持者將他介紹給總統的次子、World Liberty的領導人。這是一次隨意交談，沒有正式議程。「他只是說他在看這裡的一些房地產交易，」趙長鵬告訴我。
Their interests were about to align. The Emirates had been working to transform from a petrostate into a powerhouse in emerging technologies, a project spearheaded by Sheikh Tahnoon, who oversees $1.5 trillion in Emirati sovereign wealth. Before he went to prison, Mr. Zhao had held talks with Peng Xiao, one of the sheikh’s top lieutenants, about selling the Emiratis a stake in Binance, the ultimate union between his company and his political guardians.
他們即將在利益上捆綁起來。阿聯酋一直在努力從石油國家轉型為新興技術強國，這一項目由謝赫·塔赫農牽頭，他監管著1.5萬億美元的阿聯酋主權財富。入獄前，趙長鵬與謝赫的一位高級副手肖鵬談過向阿聯酋出售幣安股份的事，這是他的公司與他的政治保護人的終極結合。
Rarely seen without dark glasses, Sheikh Tahnoon is chairman of a venture capital fund called MGX, which specializes in A.I. Early last year, MGX agreed to invest $2 billion in Binance, a deal that valued the company at $75 billion, according to a person familiar with the matter. MGX viewed Binance as the perfect bridge between crypto and A.I. — a high-tech money-transmission service that could someday enable automated A.I. “agents” to make payments on behalf of real people.
露面必戴墨鏡的謝赫·塔赫農是一家名為MGX的風險投資基金主席，該基金專注於人工智能。去年年初，MGX同意向幣安投資20億美元，據一位知情人士稱，這筆交易對公司的估值為750億美元。MGX將幣安視為加密幣與人工智慧之間的完美橋樑——一種高科技資金傳輸服務，有朝一日可能使自動化AI「智能體」代表真人付款。
Mr. Zhao told me that he wanted to settle the deal in Bitcoin. The Emiratis worried that it was prone to drastic price swings that would complicate the transaction. They preferred to pay Mr. Zhao in stablecoins, a type of cryptocurrency designed to maintain a price of $1.
趙長鵬告訴我，他想用比特幣結算這筆交易。阿聯酋人擔心比特幣容易出現劇烈價格波動，使交易複雜化。他們更願意用穩定幣支付趙長鵬，這是一種維持在1美元價格的加密貨幣。
What happened next is widely disputed — and likely to become the subject of a congressional investigation if the Democrats win control of Congress in November. According to MGX, the fund evaluated several stablecoins before selecting USD1, a coin created by World Liberty, citing “business suitability.” It was a stunning choice, given that USD1 was completely untested, with no track record. Last year, The Wall Street Journal reported that Binance had specifically asked MGX to pay in USD1; Mr. Zhao said MGX had made the decision. “I didn’t care too much as long as the stablecoin is reputable and is not going to disappear overnight,” he told me. “There wasn’t any other consideration.”
接下來發生的事情廣受爭議——如果民主黨在11月贏得國會控制權，這件事很可能成為國會調查的主題。據MGX稱，該基金在選擇了World Liberty創建的USD1之前評估了幾種穩定幣，最終選擇USD1的理由是「商業適用性」。考慮到USD1完全未經測試、沒有業績記錄，這是一個令人震驚的選擇。去年，《華爾街日報》報導稱，幣安特別要求MGX用USD1支付；趙長鵬說MGX做出了決定。「我不太在意，只要穩定幣信譽良好，不會一夜消失，」他告訴我。「沒有任何其他考慮。」
But all the parties had an incentive to cut World Liberty into the transaction. An Emirati-backed investment firm had taken a 49 percent stake in the start-up in January 2025, days before Mr. Trump’s inauguration; Sheikh Tahnoon got a seat on World Liberty’s board. Around the same time, the Emiratis were negotiating with the White House to secure the export of the valuable computer chips that power A.I. Binance also had business in front of the Trump administration. With its legal troubles receding, the company wanted to escape some of the restrictions imposed in the 2023 settlement.
但各方都有動機讓World Liberty參與交易。一家阿聯酋支持的投資公司於2025年1月——川普就職前幾天——收購了這家初創公司49%的股份；謝赫·塔赫農獲得了World Liberty董事會席位。大約同時，阿聯酋人正在與白宮談判，以確保驅動人工智慧的寶貴計算機晶片的出口。幣安也有業務擺在川普政府面前。隨著法律麻煩消退，公司希望擺脫2023年和解中施加的一些限制。
Mr. Witkoff’s son Zach announced the deal in May 2025, thanking Binance and MGX “for their trust in us.” Democrats in Congress condemned it as “stablecoin corruption.” An issuer like World Liberty makes money by accepting cash deposits from crypto traders, giving them stablecoins in return and then investing the deposits to generate a yield. With the $2 billion from MGX and Binance, World Liberty instantly became one of the world’s largest stablecoin companies, a boon to the Trump and Witkoff families. “It’s just such a craven effort to curry favor,” said Senator Richard Blumenthal, Democrat of Connecticut, who has investigated Mr. Zhao and Binance. “Unprecedented in its scope and scale of corruption.”
威特科夫的兒子扎克於2025年5月宣布了這筆交易，感謝幣安和MGX「對我們的信任」。國會民主黨人譴責這是「穩定幣腐敗」。像World Liberty這樣的發行商通過接受加密幣交易者的現金存款、給他們穩定幣、然後投資存款產生收益來賺錢。憑藉MGX和幣安的20億美元，World Liberty立即成為世界最大的穩定幣公司之一，這對川普和威特科夫家族是一大福音。「這根本是赤裸裸的討好，」曾調查趙長鵬和幣安的康乃狄克州民主党參議員理查德·布盧門撒爾說。「其腐敗的範圍和規模前所未有。」
Mr. Zhao is adamant that nothing untoward occurred. He said he no longer held the USD1, which he used to invest in Bitcoin and other assets. “I assume that President Trump has very good lawyers, and the setup where the sons run businesses and the father is the president is completely kosher,” he told me. He suggested that some of the outrage might reflect cultural differences. “In Kazakhstan, we can eat horse meat,” he explained. “In some other countries, we’ll be like, ‘Well, that’s crazy.’” The ruling families of two governments had struck a business deal — couldn’t that be a good thing? Mr. Zhao reached for a medieval comparison. “If you marry the daughter of one country to the prince of another country,” he said, “they maintain peace for 50 years.”
趙長鵬堅稱不存在任何不當行為。他說他不再持有USD1，已用它投資比特幣和其他資產。「我認為川普總統有非常好的律師，兒子經營企業、父親是總統的安排完全合規，」他告訴我。他暗示，一些憤怒可能反映了文化差異。「在哈薩克，我們可以吃馬肉，」他解釋說。「在其他一些國家，我們會說，『嗯，那太瘋狂了。』」兩個政府的統治家族達成了一項商業協議——這難道不是好事嗎？他打了一個中世紀的比方。「如果你把一個國家的女兒嫁給另一個國家的王子，」他說，「他們能維持50年和平。」
Two weeks after the $2 billion transaction, the White House agreed to share access to the chips, overcoming concerns from some U.S. national security officials. Steve Witkoff joined Mr. Trump last spring on a trip to Abu Dhabi, where they celebrated the deal with the Emiratis at Qasr Al Watan.
20億美元交易兩周後，白宮同意分享晶片使用權，克服了一些美國國家安全官員的擔憂。史提夫·威特科夫去年春天與川普一起訪問阿布達比，他們在國家宮與阿聯酋人慶祝了這筆交易。
Privately, Mr. Witkoff also told an acquaintance that Mr. Zhao deserved a pardon, according to a person familiar with the conversation. Mr. Zhao had filed the relevant paperwork and was waiting for the president’s verdict. The outcome would have enormous implications for Binance. After Mr. Zhao pleaded guilty, the exchange’s dormant U.S. operation lost state-level licenses. A pardon could help the company pitch itself to regulators. (A White House spokeswoman said Mr. Witkoff does not play a role in the pardon process.)
據一位知情人士稱，私下裡，威特科夫還告訴一位熟人，趙長鵬應該獲得赦免。趙長鵬已提交相關文件，正在等待總統裁決。結果將對幣安產生巨大影響。認罪後，該交易所暫時停止的美國業務失去了州級許可證。赦免可能有助於公司向監管機構推銷自己。（白宮發言人表示，威特科夫沒有在赦免程序中發揮作用。）
When he described the process to me, Mr. Zhao spoke carefully, calling it a “black box” overseen by a couple of lawyers who gave periodic updates from Washington. Once in a while, he said, he would be approached at social events by shady middlemen who said they could arrange a meeting with Mr. Trump — for the right price. “It’s a mini industry of people who want to make a buck,” Mr. Zhao said.
當向我描述這個過程時，趙長鵬措辭謹慎，稱其為一個「黑箱」，由幾位律師監督，他們定期從華盛頓提供最新情況。他說，偶爾在社交活動中，會有可疑中間人接近他，說只要價格合適，他們可以安排與川普會面。「這是一個圖利之人組成的迷你產業，」他說。
Mr. Zhao ignored them. He told me that he started thinking seriously about a pardon in March 2025, after other crypto executives received clemency and The Journal reported that he, too, was seeking it. “I was like, if the newspapers think I should be asking for a pardon, maybe I should,” he said.
他沒理他們。他告訴我，在一些加密幣高管獲得寬恕後，他於2025年3月開始認真考慮這一路徑，《華爾街日報》報導他也在尋求赦免。「我想，如果報紙認為我應該申請赦免，也許我應該，」他說。
In reality, as early as December 2024, Mr. Zhao’s legal team had been working with Brett Tolman, a prominent pardon attorney, to explore the possibility of clemency, according to two people familiar with the matter and documents reviewed by The New York Times. Ultimately, Binance paid $2.1 million to a lobbying firm run by Ches McDowell, who is close to the president’s elder sons, for services including “executive relief,” according to public filings. (A World Liberty spokesman told me the company had “zero involvement in any decisions on pardons.”)
實際上，據兩位知情人士和《紐約時報》審閱的文件，早在2024年12月，趙長鵬的法律團隊就一直在與著名赦免律師布雷特·托爾曼合作，探討獲得特赦的可能性。最終，根據公開文件，幣安向切斯·麥克道爾運營的遊說公司支付了210萬美元，麥克道爾與總統的長子們關係密切，服務包括「行政救濟」。（World Liberty發言人對我說，公司「在任何赦免決定中零參與」。）
When the pardon was announced last October, Mr. Zhao had just landed in Kyrgyzstan for a meeting with President Sadyr Japarov, who was interested in creating a digital coin pegged to the Kyrgyz national currency. Mr. Zhao posted a brief thank-you message on social media as his hosts greeted him in the capital city, Bishkek. He spent the evening at a restaurant with the president, talking about stablecoins.
去年10月赦免宣布時，趙長鵬剛抵達吉爾吉斯斯坦，準備與總統薩德爾·賈帕羅夫會面，後者有興趣創建一種與吉爾吉斯本國貨幣掛鈎的數字貨幣。趙長鵬在社群媒體上發布了一條簡短感謝信息，當時東道主在首都比什凱克迎接他。他與總統在一家餐廳共度夜晚，談論穩定幣。

On a blistering morning this summer, Mr. Zhao pulled into the driveway of his $5 million villa, where he and Ms. He host a rotating cast of grandparents and siblings. He was greeted at the door by his two dogs. In addition to the Taigan, Mr. Zhao has a Belgian Malinois that he named Broccoli, which he thought sounded a little like “blockchain.”
今年夏天一個酷熱的早晨，趙長鵬的車停在了他價值500萬美元的宅邸門前，他和何一在這裡接待陸續前來的祖父母和兄弟姐妹們。兩隻狗在門口迎接他。除了泰乾犬，趙長鵬還有一隻比利時馬犬，他給它取名「西蘭花」(Broccoli)，因為他覺得這詞聽起來有點像「區塊鏈」(blockchain)。
With a photographer trailing behind him, Mr. Zhao bounded from room to room, pointing out pictures of his young family. A stately cream-colored piano sits in the center of his children’s playroom, surrounded by toys — a scene of neglect that dismayed the tuner who came to service it. It was a Steinway, “one of the expensive ones,” Mr. Zhao told me, “but I don’t know how to use it.” In his home gym, he paused to do some bench presses.
趙長鵬身後跟著一名攝影師，他走過一個個房間，把年幼的孩子的照片指給人看。一架莊嚴的奶油色鋼琴擺在孩子的遊戲室中央，周圍是玩具——這亂糟糟的環境讓前來調音的調音師十分不滿。那是一架施坦威，「很貴的那種，」趙長鵬告訴我，「但我不會彈。」在家庭健身房，他停下來做了幾組卧推。
Mr. Zhao wanted to make clear that he doesn’t live like a normal billionaire. While Forbes estimates his wealth at over $100 billion, Mr. Zhao insists that the actual figure is much lower, somewhere between $10 billion and $30 billion. He could live comfortably on a tiny fraction of that, he said — a mere $10 million.
趙長鵬想明確表示，他不像普通億萬富翁那樣生活。雖然《富比士》估計他的財富超過1000億美元，但趙長鵬堅稱實際數字要低得多，大約在100億到300億美元之間。他說，只需要其中的一小部分——區區1000萬美元——就能過得很舒服了。
On the crypto podcast circuit, Mr. Zhao is occasionally asked about his house, and he often mentions an upstairs leak that he has never managed to fix. Sure enough, in the living room, he pointed to a patch of water damage on the floor, then gestured to the ceiling. “That’s the spot,” he said. “I don’t know if you see the difference in color.”
在加密幣播客圈，趙長鵬偶爾會被問及他的房子，他經常提到樓上有一處漏水，他一直沒能修好。果然，在客廳裡，他指著地板上的一塊水漬，然後指了指天花板。「就是那個地方，」他說。「我不知道你是否看得出顏色差異。」
It’s true that Mr. Zhao’s villa is modest by billionaire standards; a house nearby is on sale for $150 million, he told me. But it’s hard to square his affectations of humility with the world of power and privilege that he has settled into. Once, at a group dinner, Mr. Zhao listened as Mr. Altman described an emerging field of biotech research. “They can take from one single person a sperm and an egg,” Mr. Zhao recalled. “Basically, you can have a kid yourself.” Mr. Zhao has his own ambitions to defy biology; he has invested in a start-up that works with artificial wombs.
以億萬富翁標準來說，趙長鵬的宅邸的確不算奢華。他告訴我，附近一棟房子售價1.5億美元。但他故作謙遜的姿態很難與他已融入的權勢世界相協調。有一次在集體晚宴上，趙長鵬聽奧爾特曼描述一個新興的生物技術研究領域。「他們可以從一個人身上取出精子和卵子，」趙長鵬回憶說。「基本上，你可以自己生孩子。」趙長鵬自己有挑戰生物學的雄心，他投資了一家研究人造子宮的初創公司。
Since prison, Mr. Zhao has split his time among a range of ventures, including his tech investments and his travels as a kind of crypto diplomat, helping promote the industry in countries where he’s often treated like royalty. “They always send a guy with two cars to drive right up to the plane,” he told me. “There’s a girl holding the local delicacy, like you’re supposed to try one of them.”
自出獄以來，趙長鵬將時間分配在一系列事業上，包括他的科技投資和作為某種加密幣外交官的旅行，在那些常把他當王公貴族般對待的國家推廣該行業。「他們總是派一個人開兩輛車直接開到飛機旁，」他告訴我。「有個女孩拿著當地美食，那意思是你需要嘗一嘗。」
Mr. Zhao downplayed his involvement in Binance; the company’s plea deal prohibited him from “operating or managing” the business. He made a point to express frustration with Binance’s recent decision to sue The Journal, after the paper reported on turnover in the compliance division, calling the case “a gigantic waste of time.” Even if Binance wins, “they won’t get very much,” he told me. (The company dropped the lawsuit last month.)
趙長鵬淡化了他在幣安的參與；公司的認罪協議禁止他「運營或管理」業務。他特意對幣安最近決定起訴《華爾街日報》表示不滿，稱這「完全是在浪費時間」，此前該報報導了幣安合規部門的離職潮。他告訴我，即使幣安贏了，「他們也拿不到多少。」（公司已於上月撤訴。）
It’s obvious that Binance remains a major part of his life. One of the two co-chief executives appointed to replace him was Ms. He — the mother of his three young children. Two years ago, in a gesture toward traditional governance, Binance established a board of directors with three supposedly independent trustees. One of them is also the board’s chairman — Gabe Abed, the friend who first recruited Mr. Zhao to the Emirates. They have gone jet-skiing together, and Mr. Abed gave Mr. Zhao a pair of orange high-top sneakers, emblazoned with the words “TRUMP CRYPTO PRESIDENT.”
顯然，幣安仍是他生活的主要部分。被任命接替他的兩位聯席首席執行官之一是何一——他三個年幼孩子的母親。兩年前，為了體現向傳統治理的轉型，幣安成立了一個董事會，由三名據稱獨立的受託人組成。其中一人是董事會主席加布·阿貝德，最初招募趙長鵬來阿聯酋的朋友。他們一起玩過水上摩托，阿貝德送給趙長鵬一雙橙色高幫運動鞋，上面印著「川普加密幣總統」。
Lately, Binance has faced new accusations of impropriety. The company fired or suspended four compliance officials last year, after they reported that nearly $2 billion had flowed from customer accounts to a cluster of entities with ties to Iran — a possible repeat of the sanctions evasion that led to the U.S. criminal case in 2023. The company disputed the employees’ findings and denied that anyone was disciplined for raising concerns. The Justice Department said later that two Chinese firms had used Binance to launder the proceeds of Iranian oil sales, though it didn’t file any charges against the exchange.
最近，幣安面臨新的不當行為指控。去年，公司解僱或停職了四名合規官員，此前他們報告稱近20億美元從客戶帳戶流向與伊朗有關聯的一系列實體——這可能是2023年美國刑事案件中制裁逃避行為的重演。公司對員工的發現提出異議，否認任何人因提出關切而受到紀律處分。司法部後來表示，兩家中國公司利用幣安洗白伊朗石油銷售所得，儘管沒有對交易所提出任何指控。
Some cracks have also emerged in Binance’s relationship with the Emiratis. In December, the company received a license to operate out of Abu Dhabi, which would soon face a barrage of Iranian missiles; the exchange’s new overseer hailed the company as a beacon of “regulatory progress.” Over the summer, however, two Binance employees were briefly detained in the Emirates, an unusually aggressive step by the local police, who were looking into possible financial crimes on the platform.
幣安與阿聯酋人的關係也出現了一些裂痕。去年12月，公司獲得在阿布達比運營的許可證，而該國很快就將面臨伊朗導彈的猛烈攻擊；交易所的新監管者稱讚公司是「監管進步」的燈塔。然而，今年夏天，兩名幣安員工在阿聯酋被短暫拘留，這是當地警方異常激進的舉動，他們正在調查平台上可能的金融犯罪。
The detentions alarmed Binance’s local work force. Mr. Zhao told me that he had sent a message to a contact in the Emirati police and reached out to lieutenants of Sheikh Tahnoon. “I just said, ‘Look, let’s find a way not to have this happen,’” Mr. Zhao recalled. “It looks bad for the country, it looks bad for us.” He insisted that the Emirates remained a friendly home for Binance. “No one’s in danger,” he said.
拘留事件令幣安當地員工感到震驚。趙長鵬告訴我，他給阿聯酋警方一位聯繫人發了信息，並聯繫了謝赫·塔赫農的副手。「我只是說，『我們得想辦法不要讓這種事發生，』」趙長鵬回憶說。「這對國家不好看，對我們也不好看。」他堅稱阿聯酋仍是幣安友好的家。「沒有人處於危險之中，」他說。
Mr. Zhao has the confidence of someone who has glided away from trouble over and over, a consummate winner in an era that often rewards brazen rule-breaking. In his memoir, he writes for a page and a half about his belief in “simulation theory,” the notion that we might all be living in someone else’s imagined reality. He encountered the idea early in his crypto career, and now believes it’s a mathematical certainty. “In 20 years, we should be able to plug a rod into our heads like in ‘The Matrix’ for complete simulations,” he writes. “Billions of people will. There will be billions of simulations.”
趙長鵬有那種總能從麻煩中脫身的人的自信，在一個肆無忌憚地打破規則時常能獲得獎勵的時代，他堪稱常勝贏家。在回憶錄中，他用一頁半的篇幅講述他如何相信「模擬理論」，即我們可能都生活在別人想像的現實中。他在加密幣生涯早期接觸到這個想法，現在相信這是數學上的必然。「20年後，我們應該能夠像《駭客任務》那樣往我們腦子裡插一根東西，進行完全的模擬，」他寫道。「數十億人會這樣做。會有數十億個模擬。」
That belief has given Mr. Zhao some detachment from the stresses of everyday life. It’s possible we’re versions of Super Mario, he writes, running around an artificial landscape. He tries to live by a simple mantra: “It’s just a game.”
這一信念讓趙長鵬對日常生活的壓力有些超脫。他寫道，我們可能是超級馬利奧的版本，在人工景觀中跑來跑去。他試圖讓生活遵循一個簡單的口頭禪：「這只是一個遊戲。」
