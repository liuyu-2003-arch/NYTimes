金正恩取道新鴨綠江大橋突訪丹東，與王滬寧會晤

Kim Jong-un Makes First Visit to China in Over a Year

CHOE SANG-HUN, 儲百亮 • 2026-10-26
North Korea’s leader, Kim Jong-un, visited neighboring China on Saturday in his first visit there in more than a year, meeting with a senior official and declaring gratitude for Beijing’s decades of support, according to state media.
據官方媒體報導，朝鮮領導人金正恩週六訪問鄰國中國，這是他一年多來首次訪華。訪問期間，他會見了一名中國高級官員，並對北京數十年來給予的支持表示感謝。
Xinhua, China’s state news agency, confirmed Mr. Kim’s one-day visit after a day of reports from South Korean officials and news media, which said he had crossed the newly opened New Yalu River Bridge spanning the border between North Korea and northeast China.
中國官方通訊社新華社證實了金正恩為期一天的訪華行程。韓國官員及新聞媒體此前接連報導稱金正恩已跨過連接朝鮮與中國東北邊境的新鴨綠江大橋。
Photos released later by North Korean state media showedChinese officials and flag-waving children in Dandong, a hub for travel and commerce with North Korea, seeing Mr. Kim off at the bridge, which he also used to return home.
朝鮮官方媒體隨後發布的照片顯示，在對朝人員往來與商貿樞紐丹東，中國官員和揮舞旗幟的兒童在橋邊為金正恩送行，金正恩回國時同樣取道該橋。
Mr. Kim and Wang Huning, a senior Chinese Communist Party official, commemorated 76 years since Mao Zedong sent Chinese forces into North Korea to support its embattled Communist troops during the Korean War against an American-led United Nations coalition, Xinhua reported.
據新華社報導，金正恩與中共高級官員王滬寧共同紀念了毛澤東派遣中國軍隊入朝參戰76週年；當年這一行動旨在支援朝鮮戰爭中的共產黨軍隊，當時這支軍隊在與美國為首的「聯合國軍」作戰中正處於不利。
廣告
Mr. Kim presented a wreath at a Chinese memorial for the war, Xinhua said.
新華社報導稱，金正恩在中國的一處抗美援朝戰爭紀念碑前敬獻了花圈。
The North’s official Korean Central News Agency quoted Mr. Kim as saying that his “people would remember forever the glorious years when they fought together with the Chinese people.”
朝鮮官方新聞社朝中社援引金正恩的話稱，朝鮮「人民將永遠銘記與中國人民並肩作戰的光輝歲月」。
The ceremony also appeared to be an opportunity for the two governments to display their deepening ties despite heavy international sanctions against North Korea. Their partnership was highlighted by the opening this month of the bridge, according to South Korean officials and media.
儘管朝鮮面臨嚴厲的國際制裁，此次儀式似乎還為兩國政府提供了展現其日益加深的關係的契機。韓國官員及媒體指出，本月開通的新鴨綠江大橋凸顯了兩國的夥伴關係。
Mr. Kim’s trip demonstrated the “blood-sealed friendship” between China and North Korea, said Mr. Wang, who for three decades served as a senior ideological adviser to Chinese leaders, including Xi Jinping, the current leader. The visit was “of great significance for carrying forward the traditional friendship between China and North Korea” and “for deepening political mutual trust,” Mr. Wang said, according to Xinhua.
王滬寧表示，金正恩此訪彰顯了中朝之間的「鮮血凝成友誼」。過去三十年來，王滬寧曾擔任包括現任領導人習近平在內的多位中國領導人的高級意識型態顧問。據新華社報導，王滬寧指出，此次訪問對「賡續中朝傳統友誼」和「深化政治互信」具有重大意義。
But Mr. Kim did not meet with Mr. Xi this time, the Chinese report indicated. Mr. Wang passed on greetings from Mr. Xi to Mr. Kim, and Mr. Kim conveyed his best wishes to Mr. Xi, Xinhua said.
但中方的報導顯示，金正恩此次並未與習近平會晤。新華社稱，王滬寧向金正恩轉達了習近平的問候，金正恩也向習近平轉達了良好祝願。
“The prospects for cooperation between our two sides are extremely broad,” Mr. Kim was quoted as saying.
報導援引金正恩的話稱：「雙方合作前景極為廣闊。」
廣告
Youn Kun-young, a member of the intelligence committee of South Korea’s National Assembly, told The New York Times that while the specific purpose of Mr. Kim’s trip required further analysis, “it is currently presumed to serve two objectives: signaling a positive outlook for a possible North Korea-U.S. summit and addressing key developments in North Korea-China relations, including the opening of the New Yalu River Bridge.”
韓國國會情報委員會委員尹建永在接受《紐約時報》採訪時表示，儘管金正恩此行的具體目的仍需進一步分析，但「目前推測其旨在實現雙重目標：為可能舉行的朝美首腦會晤傳遞積極預期，並提示朝中關係中的關鍵進展，包括新鴨綠江大橋的開通。」
Mr. Xi’s failure to meet with Mr. Kim this time did not appear to be a snub. Starting Monday, Mr. Xi will preside over a four-day meeting in Beijing of the Chinese Communist Party’s Central Committee, an assembly of around 300 senior officials. That would leave little, if any time, for other meetings in the days beforehand. Mr. Xi visited Pyongyang, North Korea’s capital, in June, and Mr. Kim feted him with grandiose pageantry.
習近平此次未能與金正恩會面，看起來並非蓄意怠慢。從週一開始，習近平將在北京主持為期四天的中共中央委員會會議，約300名高級官員將出席此次會議；這使得他在會前的數日內幾乎無暇安排其他會晤。習近平曾於6月訪問朝鮮首都平壤，金正恩當時以盛大隆重的儀式款待了他。
The latest public appearance by Mr. Kim will help dispel recent social media rumors of his death, which South Korean intelligence officials had already dismissed as false.
金正恩最近的公開露面將有助於平息近期社群媒體上關於其去世的傳聞；韓國情報官員此前已將此類言論斥為不實謠言。
Diplomats and analysts have speculated that Mr. Kim might attend the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation leaders’ meeting next month in Shenzhen, China, though it is not clear if China has invited him.
外交官與分析人士推測，金正恩可能出席下個月在中國深圳舉行的亞太經合組織(APEC)領導人非正式會議，不過目前尚不清楚中方是否已向其發出邀請。
President Trump, who has repeatedly expressed his eagerness to resume personal diplomacy with Mr. Kim, has said he will attend that meeting.
多次表示渴望與金正恩重啟個人外交接觸的川普總統已公開表示，他將出席該次會議。
Mr. Kim’s meetings with Mr. Trump in 2018 and 2019 were either preceded or followed by meetings between Mr. Kim and Mr. Xi, talks that appeared to show that Pyongyang and Beijing were consulting each other on their approaches to Washington.
金正恩在2018年和2019年與川普舉行的多次會晤，事前或事後都伴隨著金正恩與習近平的會面；這些接觸似乎表明，平壤和北京當時一直在就對華盛頓的方針進行磋商。
廣告
The Saturday visit may be a further sign that China and North Korea are enhancing their ties, even as Mr. Kim also tries to deepen ties with Russia. North Korea has provided Russia with critical artillery shells, missiles and troops for its war in Ukraine, prompting Moscow to sign a mutual defense treaty and supply the North with food, oil and advanced military technology.
週六的訪問可能是中朝正在增強彼此關係的進一步跡象，雖然金正恩眼下同樣在竭力加深與俄羅斯的關係。朝鮮為俄羅斯在烏克蘭的戰事提供了關鍵的炮彈、導彈和兵員，促使莫斯科與其簽署了共同防禦條約，並向朝方提供糧食、石油和先進軍事技術。
In response, Mr. Xi has moved to reassert Beijing’s influence. He invited both Mr. Kim and President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia to a military parade in Beijing in September 2025. Rail service between Beijing and Pyongyang, suspended during the Covid pandemic, resumed in March.
作為回應，習近平採取行動重申北京的影響力。他邀請金正恩和俄羅斯總統弗拉基米爾·V·普丁出席了2025年9月在北京舉行的閱兵式。在新冠疫情期間暫停的北京與平壤之間的鐵路客運服務已於今年3月恢復。
Bilateral trade has expanded rapidly. Decades of international sanctions have left North Korea reliant on China for nearly all of its external trade.
雙邊貿易迅速擴大。歷經數十年的國際制裁，朝鮮在對外貿易上幾乎全面依賴中國。
Mr. Kim’s first visit to China, and his first trip abroad as North Korea’s leader, was in 2018. Since then, he has traveled to China several times, holding talks with Mr. Xi almost every time. Last year, he attended a military parade in Beijing that marked 80 years since the end of World War II.
金正恩於2018年首次訪問中國，這也是他作為朝鮮領導人的首次出國訪問。自那時起，他曾多次前往中國，且幾乎每次都與習近平舉行了會晤。去年，他出席了在北京舉行的紀念第二次世界大戰結束80週年的閱兵式。

Murphy Zhao自香港對本文有報導貢獻。

Choe Sang-Hun是時報駐首爾首席記者，報導韓國和朝鮮新聞。
