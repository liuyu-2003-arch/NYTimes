人類已經引發了第二次宇宙大爆炸

Thomas L. Friedman: We Just Set Off the Second Big Bang

湯馬斯·佛里曼 • 2026-10-26
According to the columnist Thomas L. Friedman, humanity has set off a second Big Bang — and we are not ready for it. In this video, he explains why the rise of artificial intelligence and other “godlike” powers will require us to build healthy interdependencies to survive what comes next.
專欄作家湯馬斯·佛里曼認為，人類已經引發了第二次大爆炸，而我們尚未做好準備。在這個影片中，他向我們解釋，為什麼人工智慧和其他「神一般」的力量的崛起將要求我們建立健康的相互依存關係，才能在即將到來的一切中生存下來。

以下是該影片經過編輯的文字記錄。我們建議觀看原影片以獲得完整效果。你可以使用上方影片播放器觀看。
Thomas L. Friedman: Any journalist who has been working for almost half a century is sure that he or she was here for the big one. World War I, World War II, Vietnam. The rise of the internet. The fall of the Berlin Wall. The opening to China.
湯馬斯·佛里曼：任何工作了近半個世紀的記者都確信，自己親身見證了重大事件。一戰、二戰、越南戰爭。互聯網的崛起。柏林牆的倒塌。對中國的開放。
廣告
But this one — this one really is the big one. We’re here for something I call the second Big Bang.
但這一次——這一次真的是大事。我們即將親身見證的是我稱之為第二次大爆炸的事情。
The first Big Bang was set off by our creator 13.8 billion years ago. And the second Big Bang was just set off by us. It starts with the A.I. revolution, but it doesn’t end there at all.
第一次大爆炸是我們的造物主在138億年前引發的。而第二次大爆炸剛剛由我們自己引發。它始於人工智慧革命，但絕不止於此。
So I believe the only way forward is we as a species now are going to have to learn to detribalize, depolarize and detoxify ourselves at a speed, scope and scale we have never, ever had to even contemplate. And I know how hard that sounds, but I’m here to tell you: If we don’t do that, we are in for a world of hurt.
所以我相信，前進的唯一道路是，人類現在必須學會以一種我們從未、也根本無法想像的速度、範圍和規模擺脫部落主義、消除兩極對立，並且凈化我們自己。我知道這聽起來有多難，但我在這裡告訴你們：如果我們不這樣做，我們將遭受巨大的痛苦。
Here’s why we need to pay attention to this second Big Bang: We have made ourselves godlike as a species in seven different ways.
這裡是我們需要關注第二次大爆炸的原因：人類已經在七個不同方面讓自己變得像神一樣。
We’ve created an artificial brain greater than the creator endowed us with. We have disconnected intelligence from human biology — that is a godlike act. We’ve created a planetary nervous system where we’ve connected virtually everybody on the planet, and we can now hear one another whisper. We’re not quite as omniscient as the creator, but we’re getting there.
我們創造了一個比造物主賦予我們的大腦更強大的人工大腦。我們將智能與人類生物學脫鉤——這很像神。我們創造了一個行星神經系統，將地球上幾乎所有人連接起來，現在我們能聽到彼此的耳語。我們還不像造物主那樣無所不知，但我們正在接近。
Third, we’ve changed the climate. We are the first species to actually drive itself into a basically man-made climate era.
第三，我們改變了氣候。我們是第一個真正將自身推入基本由人類造成的氣候時代的物種。
No species has ever done that before. That is a godlike act. Fourth, we’ve created our own new galaxy. It’s called cyberspace, where we’re all connected and no one’s in charge. Fifth, we split the atom.
此前沒有任何物種做到過這一點。這很像神。第四，我們創造了自己的新星系。它叫網路空間，我們在那裡彼此相連，但沒有人負責。第五，我們分裂了原子。
We can now destroy the planet just as much as God did in the age of Noah, with a flood. That is godlike.
我們現在可以像上帝在諾亞時代使用洪水那樣毀滅地球。這很像神。
Six: We’ve created deep learning, where we can now apprehend mysteries of the universe that previously were only the purview of the creator.
第六：我們創造了深度學習，我們現在能夠理解宇宙的奧秘，而這些以前只屬於造物主的範疇。
廣告
And lastly, coming to a theater near you, we don’t know exactly when — five years, 10 years, maybe 15 years. But we’ll get there: Quantum computing, fusion energy and A.I.
最後，量子計算、聚變能和人工智慧技術的「重頭戲」也將到來——我們不知道具體是什麼時候，可能是五年、十年，也可能是十五年。但終究會到來。
The conversion of those three things will allow us to create anything from the molecular level up. That will be our Genesis moment. Just one problem: We now have the power to part the Red Sea, but without the Ten Commandments. Without the rules, norms and institutions to govern our now godlike powers.
這三者的結合將使我們能夠從分子層面開始創造任何東西。那將是我們的《創世紀》時刻。只有一個問題：我們現在擁有了分開紅海的力量，卻沒有《十誡》。沒有規則、規範和制度來治理我們如今像神一般的力量。
We’re about to experience the greatest phase change in human history, and we are not ready.
我們即將經歷人類歷史上最大的相變，而我們還沒有做好準備。
A.I. is unlike any other technology. It’s like a vapor. It’s going into everything. This will drive so many new issues and challenges.
人工智慧不同於任何其他技術。它像蒸汽一樣。它正在進入一切。這將帶來許多新問題和挑戰。
Let’s imagine I go to my orthopedist one day and he says, “Tom, I’m sorry to tell you, you need a new hip replacement.” I say, “Doc, I want the best.” He says, “Today, the best hip replacement is made by shanghai.hipreplacement.ai.china. You’re going to love it. It’s going to be optimizing 24/7, adjusting all these kinds of things in your body and feeding all the data back. It’s going to be wonderful.” “But Doc, Doc, wait a minute — feeding the data back? Where does the data go?” “Oh, that will be back at shanghai.hipreplacement.china or whatever.”
讓我們想像，有一天我去看骨科醫生，他說：「湯姆，很抱歉告訴你，你需要更換髖關節。」我說：「醫生，我想要最好的。」他說：「如今，最好的髖關節是『上海.髖關節.ai.中國』製造的。你會喜歡的。它會全天候優化，調整你體內的各種狀況，反饋所有數據。會很棒的。」「但是醫生，醫生，等一下——反饋數據？數據去哪裡？」「哦，那會回到『上海.髖關節.中國』之類的地方。」
Am I going to put that hip replacement in? Are you going to put that hip replacement in? What happens when A.I. is in everything? Your Chinese-made car. Your Chinese-made watch. Your Chinese-made toaster. Your Chinese-made glasses.
我會植入那個髖關節嗎？你會植入那個髖關節嗎？當人工智慧無處不在時會發生什麼？你的中國製造汽車。你的中國製造手錶。你的中國製造烤麵包機。你的中國製造眼鏡。
廣告
And for the Chinese, what happens when A.I.’s in everything they import from us? You know what’s going to happen? All we’re going to sell to China are soybeans, and all China is going to sell to us is our soy sauce.
而對中國人來說，如果他們從我們這裡進口的所有商品當中都嵌入了人工智慧，會發生什麼？你知道會發生什麼嗎？我們將只向中國出口大豆，而中國將只向我們出口醬油。
Because nobody is going to trust anybody else’s A.I. unless we’ve agreed on the ethical guardrails. So this is a huge problem coming down the road at us. We are going to have to sit down and agree with China — the other A.I. superpower — on some norms, standards and guardrails.
這是因為，除非我們就倫理護欄達成一致，否則沒有人會信任別人的人工智慧。所以這是一個巨大的問題，正朝我們襲來。我們將不得不坐下來，與另一個人工智慧超級大國中國就一些規範、標準和護欄達成一致。
And it’s going to be very difficult. In fact, any time I bring this up, people say to me, “Friedman, that is so naïve. The U.S. and China — they’ll never do that.” And I’m here to tell you: Once we’re around that corner, we’re each going to have to contemplate things we never imagined before.
這將會非常困難。事實上，每當我提出這個問題，人們都會對我說：「弗里德曼，這太天真了。美國和中國——他們永遠不會那樣做。」而我在這裡告訴你們：一旦我們邁過那個轉折點，我們每個人都必須思考以前從未想像過的事情。
However you think geopolitics is organized today — what’s the date today?
無論你認為當今的地緣政治格局是怎樣的。今天是幾號了？
Producer off-camera: Oct. 7.
製作人（鏡頭外）： 10月7日。
Friedman: Oct. 7, 2026. I’m here to tell you: On Oct. 7, 2027, it’s going to be different. And on Oct. 7, 2028 — oh my God, I can’t even imagine what it’s going to be like.
弗里德曼：2026年10月7日。我在這裡告訴你們：到了2027年10月7日，情況會不同。而2028年10月7日——天哪，我甚至無法想像那會是什麼樣子。
廣告
Now, you could say, “Hey, Friedman, we’ve been able to get by episodically for a long time now, only collaborating part of the time — and not at the kind of depth I’m talking about.” But A.I. is different. This can — we cannot kick it down the road. So we have become fused by fate. Moses ain’t coming down with the two tablets.
現在，你可能會說：「嘿，弗里德曼，我們長期以來一直能勉強應付，只是偶爾合作——而且合作深度也遠沒有我們在說的那麼深。」但人工智慧不同。這可以——我們不能把它拖延下去。所以，我們已被命運緊緊綁在一起。摩西不會再帶著兩塊石板下山了。
We are going to have to develop those norms, those ethics, those standards, and globalize them. Because we now live in a world where more people can do unto you farther, faster, deeper, cheaper than ever before. And you can do unto others farther, faster, deeper, cheaper than ever before. And now add A.I. agents and robots to the mix.
我們將不得不制定那些規範、那些倫理、那些標準，並將它們全球化。因為我們如今生活在一個這樣的世界裡：比以往任何時候都更多的人，能夠以更遠、更快、更深、更低廉的方式影響你；而你也能以更遠、更快、更深、更低廉的方式影響他人。現在，還要把人工智慧代理和機器人再加入到這一切之中。
We are going to rise together by building healthy interdependencies. Or we’re going to fall together. But baby, whatever we’re doing, we’re doing it together.
我們將通過建立健康的相互依存關係共同崛起。否則我們將一起衰落。但是，起愛的，無論我們在做什麼，我們都是一起在做。
We can learn that early; we can learn that late. We can learn that before the massive global crisis or we can learn it after. But learn it, we will.
我們可以早點領悟到這一點；我們也可以晚點再領悟。我們可以在大規模全球危機之前領悟，也可以在危機之後領悟。但無論如何，我們終將領悟。

湯馬斯·L·佛里曼(Thomas L. Friedman)是外交事務方面的專欄作者。他1981年加入時報，曾三次獲得普立茲獎。他著有七本書，包括贏得國家圖書獎的《從貝魯特到耶路撒冷》(From Beirut to Jerusalem)。歡迎在X和Facebook上關注他。
