美國企業界日漸青睞開放人工智慧模型

Corporate America Is Getting Hooked on Open-Source A.I.

ELI TAN • 2026-09-07
AT&T turned to artificial intelligence models from Anthropic and OpenAI in recent years to help with customer service, call transcription and coding. The A.I. models charged fees and were “closed,” which meant the A.I. companies did not share their underlying code.
近年來，AT&T轉向使用Anthropic和OpenAI的人工智慧模型來協助客戶服務、通話轉錄和編程。這些人工智慧模型需要付費，而且是「封閉」的，這意味著人工智慧公司不會分享其底層代碼。
This year, AT&T changed course.
今年，AT&T改變了策略。
As A.I. costs skyrocketed, Andy Markus, the company’s chief data and A.I. officer, looked for cheaper alternatives. He landed on “open” models, which can be downloaded and modified without payment or approval.
隨著人工智慧成本飆升，該公司首席數據和AI官安迪·馬庫斯開始尋找便宜的替代方案。他選擇了開放模型，這種模型可以無需付費或批准即可下載和修改。
By May, open models accounted for 20 percent of AT&T’s A.I. use. That has since risen to 40 percent and may jump to 60 percent in the coming months, Mr. Markus said in an interview.
到今年5月，開放模型已佔AT&T人工智慧使用量的20%。馬庫斯在接受採訪時表示，這一比例此後已升至40%，並可能在未來幾個月躍升至60%。
廣告
“We believe it could go much, much higher,” he said, adding that AT&T was saving up to 80 percent on A.I. costs compared with earlier this year.
「我們相信這個比例可能會更高，」他說，並補充道，與今年早些時候相比，AT&T目前在人工智慧方面的成本最高已經節省了80%。
Open-source artificial intelligence has become all the rage in corporate America. Like AT&T, companies such as Airbnb and Deloitte are also turning to open A.I. models that are easy to customize and cheaper to use. Last month, open models accounted for 58 percent of A.I. use, up from 10 percent a year ago, according to U.S. user data from OpenRouter, a platform that lets people choose different A.I. models to complete tasks.
開源人工智慧已成為美國企業界的熱門趨勢。與AT&T一樣，愛彼迎和德勤等公司也在轉向易於定製且使用成本更低的開放模型。據OpenRouter平台的美國用戶數據顯示，上個月開放模型占人工智慧使用量的58%，而一年前這一比例僅為10%。OpenRouter是一個讓用戶選擇不同人工智慧模型來完成任務的平台。
On Thursday, the chip maker Nvidia announced it was buying Hugging Face, a library of open A.I. models, for $12.9 billion, another sign of the technology’s importance.
週四，晶片製造商英偉達宣布以129億美元收購開放人工智慧模型庫Hugging Face，再次彰顯了該技術的重要性。
“Together, we will make A.I. more open, more capable and more accessible to people and institutions around the world,” Jensen Huang, Nvidia’s chief executive, said.
「我們將共同努力，讓AI變得更加開放、更加強大，並讓世界各地的個人和機構都能更容易地使用它，」英偉達首席執行官黃仁勛表示。
Open models come in two flavors. One is an open-source model, which makes its underlying code public. The other is an open-weights model, which makes its “weights” — the numerical calculations that determine how the model reasons — public.
開放模型分為兩種。一種是開源模型，公開其底層代碼。另一種是開放權重模型，公開其"權重"——即決定模型如何推理的數值計算參數。
Many of the most popular open A.I. models are made by Chinese companies like Moonshot AI, DeepSeek and Alibaba. Rather than spending millions in fees to use a closed model, developers can download one of the Chinese systems and build their products on top of it, paying mainly for servers. OpenAI and Anthropic charge for subscriptions and other fees, depending on the job.
許多最受歡迎的開放模型由中國公司開發，如月之暗面、DeepSeek和阿里巴巴。開發者無需花費數百萬美元使用封閉模型，只需下載其中一個中國系統，在其基礎上構建產品，主要支付伺服器費用即可。OpenAI和Anthropic則根據任務收取訂閱費及其他費用。
廣告
Some Chinese open models are now 80 percent to 90 percent as powerful as those from OpenAI and Anthropic, while costing as little as 20 percent of the price, said Jerry Tang, the chief executive of Atlas Cloud, a start-up that provides access to various A.I. models. He compared the leading models from OpenAI and Anthropic to pricey sports cars — powerful, but not always necessary.
Atlas Cloud是一家提供各種人工智慧模型的初創公司，其首席執行官傑瑞·唐（音）表示，一些中國開放模型的性能已達到OpenAI和Anthropic模型的80%至90%，而成本僅為後者的20%。他將OpenAI和Anthropic的領先模型比作昂貴的跑車——強大，但並非總是必需。
“Mazdas do a fine job getting you where you need to go, and Maseratis are simply not worth the investment,” Mr. Tang said. Open-source models, he added, “offer a better bang for your buck.”
「萬事得也能很好地送你到達目的地，而瑪莎拉蒂的投資價值並不高，」他說。他還表示，開源模型「性價比更高」。
The popularity of open models could spell trouble for Anthropic and OpenAI, which are both heading toward blockbuster initial public offerings. The companies have to charge more as they spend billions on research and development and computing power.
開放模型的普及可能對Anthropic和OpenAI構成威脅，這兩家公司都在籌備大規模的首次公開募股。由於在研發和算力上投入了數十億美元，它們不得不收取更高費用。
OpenAI and Anthropic did not immediately respond to requests for comment. (The New York Times has sued OpenAI and Microsoft, claiming copyright infringement of news content related to A.I. systems. The companies have denied the suit’s claims.)
OpenAI和Anthropic未立即回應置評請求。（《紐約時報》已起訴OpenAI和微軟，指控其AI系統侵犯了新聞內容的版權。兩家公司否認了訴訟中的指控。）
The rise of open models plays into a broader debate in Silicon Valley and Washington about control over A.I. Dario Amodei, chief executive of Anthropic, has argued that A.I. should be tightly regulated to prevent national security concerns. Other tech leaders such as Mr. Huang of Nvidia and Mark Zuckerberg, chief executive of Meta, have said open-source technology is crucial for having a democratic, balanced A.I. ecosystem.
開放模型的興起引發了矽谷和華盛頓關於人工智慧控制權的更廣泛辯論。Anthropic首席執行官達里奧·阿莫代伊認為，人工智慧應受到嚴格監管以防止國家安全問題。英偉達的黃仁勛和Meta首席執行官馬克·祖克柏等其他科技領袖則表示，開源技術對於建立一個民主、均衡的人工智慧生態系統至關重要。
After OpenAI released ChatGPT in 2022, many companies and users gravitated to closed A.I. models. At the time, open-source models were mainly popular among developers.
自2022年OpenAI發布ChatGPT以來，許多公司和個人用戶都傾向於使用封閉式模型。當時，開源模型主要在開發者中流行。
廣告
The models began gaining favor last year after the Chinese start-up DeepSeek released one called DeepSeek-V3. It stood out for being nearly as cutting edge as leading closed models, while requiring less computing power. Since then, open models from Chinese companies have increasingly caught up to the performance of closed models from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and others.
去年，中國初創公司DeepSeek發布了一款名為DeepSeek-V3的模型後，這些模型開始受到青睞。它的突出之處在於幾乎與領先的封閉模型一樣先進，同時需要的算力更少。此後，中國公司的開放模型在性能上越來越接近Anthropic、OpenAI、谷歌等公司的封閉模型。
Many open models can now be powered by a phone or laptop, said David Stout, the chief executive of WebAI, an A.I. start-up. That has made the technology more accessible and even cheaper. Leading closed models are getting more complicated and requiring more computing power.
人工智慧初創公司WebAI的首席執行官戴維·斯托特表示，許多開放模型現在可以由手機或筆記型電腦驅動。這使該技術更加普及且成本更低。而領先的封閉模型正變得越來越複雜，需要更多算力。
Most U.S. companies still use a combination of open and closed models. Closed models remain the best for heavy duty tasks like coding and image and video generation, while open models often excel at simple, specialized tasks, Mr. Tang of Atlas Cloud said.
Atlas Cloud的傑瑞·唐表示，大多數美國公司仍在混合使用開放和封閉模型。封閉模型在處理編碼、圖像和影片生成等繁重任務時依然是最佳選擇，開放模型則在簡單、專業化任務上表現突出。
At AT&T, the telecom company is now taking open-weights models and customizing them to make new tools that can transcribe calls and facilitate customer service, Mr. Markus, the company’s chief data and A.I. officer, said.
AT&T首席數據和AI官馬庫斯表示，這家電信公司目前正採用開放權重模型進行定製，以開發可用於通話轉錄和客戶服務的新工具。
Some U.S. firms remain reluctant to use Chinese A.I. models because of concerns over regulation and data privacy. AT&T researches Chinese models but is not using them, Mr. Markus said. Instead, it is working with popular alternatives made by American companies such as the Gemma A.I. model from Google and the Llama A.I. models from Meta.
一些美國公司因擔心監管和數據隱私問題，仍不願使用中國的人工智慧模型。馬庫斯表示，AT&T在研究中國模型，但並未使用。該公司選擇與美國公司開發的流行替代方案合作，例如谷歌的Gemma AI模型和Meta的Llama AI模型。
As Chinese models become more popular, some U.S. companies are unveiling new open A.I. models of their own. Last month, Meta made its leading A.I. system an open-weights model and said it would offer the model at a lower cost than other top U.S. models.
隨著中國模型越來越受歡迎，一些美國公司也在推出自己的新型開放AI模型。上個月，Meta將其領先的AI系統改為開放權重模型，並表示將以低於其他美國頂級模型的價格提供該模型。
廣告
“I do not believe restricting access to foreign open-source models is an effective solution,” Mr. Zuckerberg said in a statement at the time. “Our goal should be for American open-source models to be the best globally.”
「我不認為限制外國開源模型的訪問是一種有效解決方案，」祖克柏當時在一份聲明中表示。「我們的目標應該是讓美國的開源模型成為全球最佳。」

Eli Tan是時報駐舊金山的科技行業記者。
