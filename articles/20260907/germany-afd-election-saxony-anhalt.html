德國極右翼政黨在地方選舉中大勝

Germany’s Far Right Just Misses Majority in Crucial State Election

JIM TANKERSLEY, CHRISTOPHER F. SCHUETZE, ASHLEY SOUTHALL • 2026-09-07
The Alternative for Germany party dominated a crucial state election on Sunday, according to near-final results, but fell short of the absolute majority needed to become the first far-right party to govern part of the country since the Nazi era.
根據接近最終的結果，德國選擇黨在週日一場關鍵的州選舉中佔據主導地位，但未能拿下所需的絕對多數，因而不能成為自納粹時代以來首個在德國部分地區執政的極右翼政黨。
If those results hold, the AfD, as the party is known by its German initials, could still assume power in the eastern state of Saxony-Anhalt in the coming weeks, depending on the negotiations among rival parties that have vowed not to support an AfD government.
如果這些結果保持到最後，該黨（德語縮寫為AfD）仍可能在未來幾週內在東部州薩克森-安哈爾特掌權，但這取決於各競爭政黨之間的談判——其他政黨已誓言不會支持由德國選擇黨領導的政府。
Saxony-Anhalt is a relatively small state, and the heart of the AfD’s power base in eastern Germany. But the election result sent shock waves through the country’s political system, which was designed after World War II to prevent extremist parties from ever returning to power.
薩克森-安哈爾特是一個相對較小的州，也是德國選擇黨在德國東部的權力核心地帶。但此次選舉結果在德國政治體系中引發了震動，該體系在二戰後設計，旨在防止極端主義政黨重新掌權。
“The AfD’s electoral victory in Saxony-Anhalt is less a reflection of the appeal of its political agenda than a stark demonstration of the political establishment’s failure to articulate a convincing vision for the state’s economic, political and social future,” said Jana Puglierin, the head of the Berlin branch of the European Council on Foreign Relations.
「德國選擇黨在薩克森-安哈爾特的選舉勝利與其說反映了其政治議程的吸引力，不如說更鮮明地揭示了政治建制派未能就該州的經濟、政治和社會未來提出令人信服的願景，」歐洲對外關係委員會柏林辦事處負責人雅娜·普格列林說。
廣告
The political establishment has treated the AfD as a pariah. Still, the party tapped into voter frustration on a wide range of economic and social issues to win about 44 percent of the vote in the state, and was set to claim 39 of the 83 seats in the Parliament.
政治建制派一直將德國選擇黨視為異類。儘管如此，該黨利用選民在廣泛的經濟和社會問題上的不滿情緒在該州贏得了約44%的選票，並有望在議會83個席位中獲得39席。
“We’ve made history, and we’re taking our country back,” Ulrich Siegmund, the party’s candidate to lead the state, told a cheering crowd on Sunday. “It all starts here in Saxony-Anhalt.”
「我們創造了歷史，我們正在奪回我們的國家，」該黨州長候選人烏爾裡希·西格蒙德週日對歡呼的人群說。「一切都從薩克森-安哈爾特這裡開始。」
Exit polls suggested that many of the AfD’s gains came at the expense of the center-right Christian Democrats, the party of Chancellor Friedrich Merz that also led the incumbent government in the state. Amid record turnout, the AfD won the support of many Germans who were casting ballots for the first time.
出口民調顯示，德國選擇黨許多得票增長來自中右翼基督教民主聯盟（基民盟）的票數減少——基民盟是總理弗里德裡希·默茨所屬的政黨，同時也領導著該州的現任政府。在創紀錄的投票率之下，德國選擇黨贏得了許多首次投票的德國人的支持。
Germany’s domestic intelligence agency has designated the AfD as a “suspected extremist” group and its Saxony-Anhalt branch as “extremist.” Some of the party’s leaders have played down the Holocaust and adopted Nazi slogans. Others have strong ties to Russia. The AfD has also said that Islam is incompatible with the German Constitution and has vowed to conduct mass deportations of immigrants.
德國國內情報機構已將德國選擇黨列為「疑似極端主義」組織，並將其薩克森-安哈爾特分支列為「極端主義」組織。該黨一些領導人曾淡化大屠殺的嚴重性，並採用納粹口號。還有一些領導人與俄羅斯關係密切。德國選擇黨還表示伊斯蘭教與德國憲法不相容，並誓言大規模驅逐移民。
The AfD has rejected accusations of extremism as politically motivated. And voters ignored those accusations because, many told pollsters on Sunday, they saw the AfD as providing better answers to economic, security and immigration concerns than the political mainstream.
德國選擇黨否認極端主義指控，稱這些指控帶有政治動機。選民們對這些指控置之不理，許多人在週日接受民調時表示，他們認為比起比政治主流陣營，德國選擇黨在經濟、安全和移民問題上提供了更好的答案。
Thomas Schröder, 63, cycled to his polling station in central Magdeburg to vote for Mr. Siegmund and the AfD. “He gives me hope, so he gets my vote,” Mr. Schröder, a heating technician, said of Mr. Siegmund. He added that he’d lost trust in other parties. “I experimented with almost all of them, and what did it get me?” he said.
63歲的托馬斯·施羅德騎車來到位於馬格德堡市中心的投票站，投票支持西格蒙德和德國選擇黨。「他給了我希望，所以他得到了我的選票，」身為供暖技術員的施羅德在談到西格蒙德時說。他還說，他已經對其他政黨失去了信任。「我幾乎試過所有政黨，結果得到了什麼？」他說。
廣告
The result buoyed AfD fans across the country and around the world. President Trump posted an image of an exit poll showing the AfD lead to his social media platform, Truth Social.
這一結果鼓舞了德國各地乃至全世界的德國選擇黨支持者。川普總統在自己的社群媒體平台Truth Social平台上發布了一張顯示德國選擇黨領先的出口民調圖片。
Elias Beck, 70, of Bavaria, hunkered over his phone outside the main train station in Magdeburg, the state’s capital, studying a chart that showed the AfD close to securing an absolute majority in early results. He said he hoped the party would prevail because the German government had failed to address serious problems at home like illegal migration, while spending hundreds of millions of dollars abroad.
來自巴伐利亞的70歲老人埃利亞斯·貝克在州首府馬格德堡中央火車站外俯身看手機，研究一張顯示德國選擇黨在早期結果中接近獲得絕對多數的圖表。他說，他希望該黨獲勝，因為德國政府未能解決國內諸如非法移民等嚴重問題，卻在國外花費了數億美元。
“You have all the problems in Germany, but all of the money is going to Ukraine,” he said.
「德國有各種問題，但所有的錢都去了烏克蘭，」他說。
Some rival parties appeared to have galvanized supporters against the AfD, an effort that some leaders in Berlin vowed was just the start of a backlash against a potential AfD government.
一些競爭黨派似乎已經動員支持者反對德國選擇黨，柏林的一些領導人誓言，這只是針對德國選擇黨可能組閣所引發的反撲的開始。
Lars Klingbeil, the country’s vice chancellor and a center-left Social Democrat, said on television that the election was “a turning point,” adding that there were many people “across Germany, who are afraid and worried about this election result for the AfD.”
德國副總理、中左翼社民黨人拉爾斯·克林拜爾在電視上表示，這次選舉是「一個轉折點」，並補充說，「全德國有很多人對德國選擇黨的這一選舉結果感到恐懼和擔憂。」
One of the worried was Muhammad Hassan, 27, a native of Pakistan, who said on Sunday night in Magdeburg that he has done all he can to assimilate in Germany since he arrived in 2023.
擔憂者之一是27歲的巴基斯坦人穆罕默德·哈桑。他週日晚在馬格德堡說，自2023年抵達德國以來，他已盡一切努力融入德國。
廣告
Mr. Hassan said he had learned German, obtained a driver’s license, finished a master’s in water engineering and secured a job as a technical draftsman drawing underground infrastructure like sewage and electrical lines. But he worried an AfD government would take it away, a year before he is eligible for permanent residency.
哈桑說，他學會了德語，考取了駕照，完成了水利工程碩士學位，並找到了一份技術繪圖員的工作，繪製污水和電力線路等地下基礎設施。但他擔心德國選擇黨政府會剝奪這一切，而距離他有資格獲得永久居留權只剩一年了。
“When I see today’s situation,” he said, referring to the election, “it has totally disappointed me because what I have dreamed, I cannot see right now.”
「當我看到今天的情況時，」他在談到選舉時說，「我徹底失望了，因為我曾經夢想的一切，現在看不到了。」
Rival parties could still prevent the AfD from assuming power in Saxony-Anhalt. The Christian Democrats, who finished a distant second, could end up leading the state if they can build a coalition with four others: the center-left Social Democrats and Greens, the far-left Die Linke, and the populist Sahra Wagenknecht alliance, known by its German initials BSW.
競爭黨派仍有可能阻止德國選擇黨在薩克森-安哈爾特掌權。以較大差距位居第二的基民盟如果能夠與其他四個政黨聯盟——包括中左翼的社民黨和綠黨、極左的左翼黨，以及民粹主義的莎拉·瓦根克內希特聯盟(以德文首字母BSW聞名)——最終可能領導該州。
But some Christian Democrat leaders in the state have said they would not work with Die Linke, calling it extremist. BSW leaders have also said they would not support the current state leader, Sven Schulze of the Christian Democrats, or the AfD. They have pushed for a nonpartisan state leader, an idea Mr. Siegmund rejected late on Sunday. Mr. Siegmund also seemed to invite some members of the Christian Democrats to defect and supply the votes for an AfD government.
但該州一些基民盟領導人表示，他們不會與左翼黨合作，稱該黨為極端主義。BSW領導人也表示，他們不會支持現任州長、基民盟的斯文·舒爾策，也不會支持德國選擇黨。他們一直力主由無黨派人士擔任州長，而西格蒙德在週日晚拒絕了這一想法。西格蒙德似乎還邀請基民盟的一些成員倒戈，為德國選擇黨政府提供選票。
The coming coalition wrangling could take days or weeks. Analysts said it was likely to leave the state’s next leader in fragile condition, no matter which party is in power.
接下來的聯盟組閣博弈可能會持續數天或數週。分析人士表示，無論哪個政黨掌權，該州下任領導人都可能處於脆弱境地。
Sunday’s result, said Sudha David-Wilp, the vice president of external relations at the German Marshall Fund, a think tank, “stands as a harbinger for the inability for mainstream parties to form stable coalitions.”
智庫德國馬歇爾基金會對外關係副總裁蘇達·戴維-威爾普說，週日的結果「預示著主流政黨無法組建穩定執政聯盟。」

Tatiana Firsova contributed reporting from Berlin.
