平壤盛大儀式背後，習近平與金正恩傳遞了什麼信息

Behind the Pageantry in Pyongyang With Xi and Kim

PABLO ROBLES, AGNES CHANG, CHOE SANG-HUN • 2026-06-10
China’s top leader, Xi Jinping, made a two-day trip to North Korea, his first in seven years. He received a lavish welcome from his counterpart, Kim Jong-un.
中國最高領導人習近平對朝鮮進行了為期兩天的訪問，這是他七年來首次訪問朝鮮。他受到朝鮮領導人金正恩的盛情歡迎。
The main ceremony took place on Monday in Pyongyang, the capital, at Kim Il Sung Square, the North Korean equivalent to Tiananmen Square in Beijing. It is overlooked by the Grand People’s Study House, North Korea’s national library.
主要儀式於週一在首都平壤的金日成廣場舉行，該廣場相當於北京的天安門廣場。廣場對面矗立著朝鮮國家圖書館——人民大學習堂。
Portraits of Mr. Kim’s predecessors – his grandfather, the country’s founder, and his father – usually dominate the square. But on Monday, large portraits of Mr. Xi and Mr. Kim were placed at the center.
金正恩的前任們的肖像通常佔據著廣場中心——那是他的祖父、該國創始人和他的父親。但週一，習近平和金正恩的大型肖像被擺放在了廣場中央。
Mr. Xi and Mr. Kim themselves stood in front of the portraits with their wives, Peng Liyuan and Ri Sol-ju, behind the men.
習近平和金正恩兩人站在肖像前，二人的妻子彭麗媛和李雪主站在他們身後。
Further behind were senior officials from both governments.
再後面是兩國政府的高級官員。
Massive and formally dressed crowds, including children, filled the square in a highly choreographed display of loyalty.
大批身著正裝的人擠滿廣場，其中包括兒童，他們上演精心編排的表忠心場面。
Large banners pledged a long-running friendship between the two countries.
巨大的橫幅宣示著兩國之間源遠流長的友誼。
Mr. Kim relies heavily on these spectacles to project power and political messages. This type of reception is only reserved for a small group of visiting foreign dignitaries.
金正恩極度依賴這些場面來彰顯威望並傳遞政治信息。此類接待僅限於少數來訪的外國政要。
The Chinese leader’s visit comes at a pivotal moment for both countries. China is seeking to reassert its influence as North Korea’s largest trading partner and closest ally, looking to counterbalance Mr. Kim’s recent drift toward President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia.
中國領導人此次訪問正值兩國關係的關鍵時刻。中國正尋求重申作為朝鮮最大貿易夥伴和最親密盟友的影響力，以制衡金正恩近期向俄羅斯總統普丁的靠攏。
廣告
Feeding Mr. Putin’s war effort in Ukraine has transformed Mr. Kim’s fortunes, giving him a stronger hand in talks with Mr. Xi. But he is still looking to Beijing for vital economic aid and diplomatic cover — seeking recognition as a nuclear power and a partnership in building the “multipolar” world order that both nations envision as a counterweight to American dominance.
為普丁在烏克蘭的戰爭提供支持使金正恩的處境發生了轉變，他也得以在同習近平的會談中掌握更多籌碼。但他仍需仰仗北京提供至關重要的經濟援助和外交庇護——尋求作為核大國得到承認，並建立夥伴關係，構建兩國共同設想的「多極化」世界秩序，以此作為對美國霸權的制衡。
Mr. Xi cast the visit as “an opportunity to strengthen” coordination between Beijing and Pyongyang, according to Chinese state media.
據中國官方媒體報導，習近平將此次訪問視為「加強」北京與平壤之間協調的「契機」。
China prizes North Korea as a vital buffer against U.S. influence in Northeast Asia — backing Pyongyang in the Korean War and cementing ties with a 1961 alliance treaty. Now, Mr. Kim and Mr. Xi have pledged to mark the treaty’s 65th anniversary this year by forging an even broader strategic front against Washington and its allies.
中國將朝鮮視為抵禦美國在東北亞影響力的重要緩衝，北京曾在朝鮮戰爭中支持平壤，並通過1961年的同盟條約鞏固了雙邊關係。如今，金正恩和習近平已承諾，借今年條約簽署65週年之機，共同打造一個更廣泛的戰略陣線，以對抗華盛頓及其盟友。
Although bound by the treaty, relations between China and North Korea have long been marked by tension and distrust. Fractures emerged as China built robust trade ties with South Korea during post-Cold War decades, as North Korea conducted nuclear weapons tests, and as Beijing joined Washington in imposing tough sanctions. In his first seven years as China’s top leader, Mr. Xi never visited Pyongyang, depriving Mr. Kim of the prestige of hosting his most powerful neighbor.
儘管有著同盟條約，但中朝關係長期以來一直充斥著緊張與猜疑。冷戰後的幾十年裡，隨著中國與韓國建立起牢固的貿易關係、朝鮮進行核武器試驗，以及北京與華盛頓一道對朝鮮實施嚴厲制裁，中朝關係出現了裂縫。在習近平擔任中國最高領導人的最初七年裡，他從未訪問過平壤，使得金正恩無緣享受接待這位最強大鄰國的殊榮。
The dynamic began to shift when Mr. Kim became the first North Korean leader to meet a sitting American president, holding three summit meetings with President Trump in 2018 and 2019. Mr. Xi met with Mr. Kim before or after each of those summits in an effort to keep Pyongyang within China’s orbit. Ties cooled during the pandemic, but Mr. Kim’s growing alignment with Moscow has since compelled Beijing to court North Korea once again.
當金正恩成為首位與現任美國總統會晤的朝鮮領導人，並於2018年和2019年與川普總統舉行三次峰會時，這種局面開始發生轉變。在每一次峰會之前或之後，習近平都會與金正恩進行會晤，試圖將平壤留在中國的軌道內。疫情期間雙方關係有所降溫，但自那以後，金正恩與莫斯科日益緊密的結盟迫使北京再次拉攏朝鮮。
This week's talks in Pyongyang “may be remembered as the most consequential of the seven summit meetings” the two leaders have held to date, said Sung-Yoon Lee, a senior fellow at the Sejong Institute, a think tank in Seoul. The repeated invocation of “strategic” in both governments' statements, combined with an unprecedented focus on military exchanges, he said, suggests that “Beijing is increasingly defining North Korea as a long-term strategic partner in an emerging Eurasian geopolitical contest versus the U.S. and its allies.”
首爾智庫世宗研究所高級研究員李成允（音）表示，本週在平壤舉行的會談「可能會被視為兩國領導人迄今的七次峰會中影響最深遠的一次」。他指出，兩國政府在聲明中反覆使用「戰略」一詞，再加上聲明中對軍事交流前所未有的關注，表明「在對抗美國及其盟友的新興歐亞地緣政治競爭中，北京正日益將朝鮮定義為長期戰略合作夥伴」。
