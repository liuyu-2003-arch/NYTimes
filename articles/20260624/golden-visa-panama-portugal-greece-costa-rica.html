低門檻「黃金簽證」五選

Want to Live Abroad? You Can Still Find a Great Golden Visa.

LANA BORTOLOT • 2026-06-24
Americans’ interest in a life abroad is surging. A Gallup poll conducted in 2025 found that one in five Americans said they would move permanently to another country if they could — a 54 percent increase since 2020, said Julie Ray, an analyst with Gallup.
美國人對海外生活的興趣正在激增。蓋洛普2025年的一項民意調查發現，五分之一的美國人表示，如果有機會，他們會永久移居他國。蓋洛普分析師朱莉·雷稱，這比2020年增加了54%。
Lately, said Jennifer Stevens, executive editor at International Living magazine, “We’re seeing this energy and interest in actually doing it, not just kicking the tires, not just dreaming about it, but doing it.”
《國際生活》(International Living)雜誌執行主編珍妮佛·史蒂芬斯表示，最近「我們看到大家是真的想付諸行動，而不僅僅是觀望，不僅僅是夢想，而是真的在做」。
But how? Programs such as the “golden visa,” which allow people to obtain residency or even a path to citizenship in a foreign country by making investments in things like real estate, cultural institutions and environmental projects, have been popular for more than a decade. But the playing field is changing. Mostly conceived after the global financial crisis of 2008 to boost economic development and avert bankruptcy, these programs have also spurred rapid gentrification in host countries (not to mention tax evasion and money laundering), leading some governments to scale them back or cancel them altogether.
但如何操作？諸如「黃金簽證」之類的項目——允許人們通過在房地產、文化機構和環境項目等領域進行投資來獲得居留權甚至入籍途徑——十多年來一直很受歡迎。但遊戲規則正在改變。這些項目主要在2008年全球金融危機後為提振經濟發展和避免破產而設立，也引發了東道國的快速士紳化（更不用說逃稅和洗錢問題），導致一些政府縮減項目規模或直接取消。
Still, numerous options exist, and they come at varying costs for various lifestyles. Ted Baumann, International Living’s chief of global diversification, said between 30 and 35 countries have some form of the golden visa. He said another 12 offer citizenship by investment (C.B.I.), which leads visa holders directly to citizenship with full benefits, including a passport and wider visa access to other countries, without in-place residency requirements.
儘管如此，選擇仍然很多，而且成本各異、適合不同的生活方式。《國際生活》的全球多元化主管泰德·鮑曼表示，有30到35個國家設有某種形式的黃金簽證。他說，另有12個國家提供「投資入籍」項目，這讓簽證持有者無需在當地居住即可直接獲得公民身份及全部福利，包括護照和更廣泛的免簽入境待遇。
廣告
While some residency-by-investment programs are structured to attract high net-worth investors — Singapore, New Zealand, Austria and Monaco, for example — “there’s usually something that will fit any budget,” said Patricia Casaburi, chief executive and founder of Global Citizen Solutions, a Lisbon-based consultancy specializing in Iberian residency programs.
雖然一些投資居留項目旨在吸引高凈值投資者（例如新加坡、紐西蘭、奧地利和摩納哥），但總部位於里斯本的諮詢公司環球公民解決方案創始人兼首席執行官帕特里夏·卡薩布裡表示，「任何預算一般都能找到合適的方案。」
Here’s a look at five countries that offer residency programs with particularly attractive features, including low cost of entry, investment variety and pathways to citizenship.
以下是五個提供極具吸引力的居留項目的國家概覽，包括低門檻、多樣的投資選擇以及入籍路徑。
COSTA RICA
哥斯達黎加
Long known for its high quality of life and friendly tax environment, the Central American nation, whose Nicoya Peninsula is a “Blue Zone,” is particularly compelling for retirees thanks to its low cost of entry and flexible options for temporary residency. The path to permanent residency opens other benefits to people aged 65 and older, including national health care (costs will vary).
這個中美洲國家以其高質量的生活和友好的稅收環境聞名已久，其尼科亞半島是一處「藍區」。由於門檻低和臨時居留選項靈活，它對退休人員尤其具有吸引力。獲得永久居留權還為65歲及以上人群開啟了其他福利，包括國民醫療保健（費用各異）。
Types of visa: The basic C.B.I. program, called the “Inversionista,” grants residency with a one-time, $150,000 investment in either real estate or another “qualifying financial asset,” such as shares of a Costa Rican corporation or the Costa Rican stock market. You can also contribute $100,000 to a government-approved forestry or environmental project.
簽證類型：基本的投資入籍項目名為「投資者」(Inversionista)，只需一次性投資15萬美元於房地產或其他「合格金融資產」（如哥斯達黎加公司股份或股票市場），即可獲得居留權。你也可以向政府批准的林業或環保項目投資10萬美元。
The “Pensionado” visa is for retirees who demonstrate a monthly pension/retirement income of at least $1,000. It’s valid for two years, allows dependents and grants access to the public health care system, with a low application fee, between $250 and $500. For “Rentista” status (a “person of independent means”), foreigners must make a $60,000 deposit in an approved bank, and a monthly $2,500 transfer into a Costa Rican account for two years, after which time a $60,000 deposit is required to renew.
「養老金領取者」(Pensionado)簽證適用於每月證明擁有至少1000美元養老金/退休收入的退休人員。有效期兩年，允許攜帶家屬，並可接入公共醫療系統，申請費較低，在250至500美元之間。「租房者」（Rentista，即「有獨立收入人士」）身份要求外國人在有資質的銀行存入6萬美元，並在兩年內每月向哥斯達黎加帳戶轉賬2500美元，今後的續期仍需存入6萬美元。
廣告
Occupancy requirement: Residents by investment are granted a temporary residence permit valid for two years and renewable for another two years. They must visit Costa Rica for at least one day, at least once a year. They can apply for permanent residency after three years and for citizenship after seven years (five years for Central American, Ibero-American and Spanish citizens).
居住要求：投資居留者獲發為期兩年的臨時居留許可，可再續簽兩年。他們必須每年至少訪問哥斯達黎加一天。三年後可申請永久居留，七年後可申請入籍（中美洲、伊比利亞美洲和西班牙公民為五年）。
GREECE
希臘
Greece was one of several countries that tightened their visa programs in recent years. Today, its golden visa offers several tiers of investment, including a minimum of 250,000 euros in real estate, giving it one of lowest-cost entry points in the European Union. The golden visa includes visa-free access to the Schengen Area.
希臘是近年來收緊簽證項目的幾個國家之一。如今，其黃金簽證提供多個投資層級，包括最低25萬歐元投資房地產，這使其成為歐盟內門檻最低的項目之一。黃金簽證包含免簽進入申根區。
Types of visa: Minimum capital investments via the golden visa range from 250,000 euros for historic properties needing restoration or conversion, up to 800,000 euros in high-value areas such as major cities and popular islands. You can also obtain the visa with 500,000 in either government bonds, a deposit at a Greek credit institution or real estate investment company, or an investment in an “Elevate Greece” start-up company.
簽證類型：通過黃金簽證的最低資本投資從25萬歐元（需要修復或改造的歷史房產）到80萬歐元（大城市和熱門島嶼等高價值地區）不等。其他獲得簽證的途徑：在政府債券、希臘信貸機構存款、房地產投資公司投資50萬歐元；投資「振興希臘」(Elevate Greece)初創公司。
Two other options include the “Financially Independent Person” visa, for retirees or digital nomads, who must show monthly income of at least 3,500 euros from outside sources, transferred into a Greek bank account.
另外兩個選項是針對退休人員或數字遊民的「財務獨立人士」簽證，他們必須證明每月至少有3500歐元的境外收入，並轉入希臘銀行帳戶。
Occupancy requirement: There is no requirement to reside in Greece. You can rent out your property during your residency period, or sell it if you choose not to renew the visa.
居住要求：無居住要求。在居留期間你可以出租房產，如果不續簽簽證也可以選擇出售。
PANAMA
巴拿馬
Panama is consistently lauded for its quality of life, robust service industry, ease of visa process and health care (there’s a Johns Hopkins-affiliated hospital in Panama City), and longstanding program for retirees. “Panama is very stable and they stick by their programs, unlike what we’ve seen in a lot of other places,” said Ms. Stevens, from International Living.
巴拿馬因其生活質量、強勁的服務業、便捷的簽證流程和醫療保障（巴拿馬城有一家約翰斯·霍普金斯附屬醫院）以及長期的退休者項目備受讚譽。《國際生活》的史蒂芬斯說：「巴拿馬非常穩定，且堅持執行其項目，不像我們在其他地方看到的那樣朝令夕改。」
Types of visas: The “Qualified Investor” visa requires a minimum real estate investment of $300,000; or $500,00 in Panama’s stock exchange; or a fixed-term deposit of $750,000 in the banking sector.
簽證類型：「合格投資者」簽證要求至少30萬美元的房地產投資；或50萬美元的巴拿馬證券交易所投資；或在銀行業存入75萬美元的定期存款。
廣告
Panama also has a “Friendly Nations” visa — available to residents from more than 50 countries that have cordial economic and political relationships with Panama — that can be obtained with a $200,000 investment in real estate or a fixed-term deposit, or through employment by a Panamanian company.
巴拿馬還有一個「友好國家」簽證，適用於與巴拿馬有著友好經濟和政治關係的 50 多個國家的居民。申請人可以通過20萬美元的房地產投資或定期存款、受雇於巴拿馬公司獲得該簽證。
There are also two levels of investment in the forest/teak reforestation sector: $100,000 for temporary residence for two years, and $350,000 for immediate permanent residence.
在森林/柚木重新造林領域也有兩級投資：10萬美元可獲得兩年臨時居留，35萬美元可獲得直接永久居留。
If you maintain the investment for five years, you can be eligible to apply for citizenship.
如果你保持投資滿五年，即有資格申請入籍。
Occupancy requirement: Visa holders must enter Panama at least once every two years to maintain residence status. Residency can be obtained within 30 days with qualified investment.
居住要求：簽證持有人必須每兩年至少進入巴拿馬一次以維持居留身份。符合條件的投資者可在30天內獲得居留權。
PORTUGAL
葡萄牙
Portugal is popular with expats for its weather, culinary and cultural assets, and English proficiency. It also has an affordable and reliable mix of health care options in the private and public systems.
葡萄牙因天氣、美食、文化底蘊和英語普及率深受外籍人士歡迎。其私人和公共醫療系統也提供了實惠可靠的組合。
The country issued its first golden visa in 2012 with a 250,000-euro real estate investment, but in 2023 it announced it would modify the program amid complaints that foreign investors were inflaming the country’s housing crisis. It has since increased the cost of entry and directed investments away from real estate and toward investment funds and donations to cultural or research projects.
該國於2012年發放了第一份黃金簽證，要求25萬歐元房地產投資。但在2023年，面對外國投資者加劇住房危機的指責，該國宣布將修改該項目。此後提高了門檻，並將投資從房地產轉向投資基金以及對文化或研究項目的捐贈。
廣告
Types of visas: The golden visa grants five-year residency with 500,000-euro investments in venture capital or investment funds, job creation, or donations for research projects. A 250,000-euro investment in the arts or heritage projects can also grant residency.
簽證類型：黃金簽證通過50萬歐元的風險資本或投資基金投資、創造就業或向研究項目捐款，授予五年居留權。25萬歐元對藝術或遺產項目的投資也可獲得居留權。
The two-year residence permit is renewable for a period of three years, at which point the permit holder can either extend the temporary permit again, apply for permanent residence or apply for citizenship. People from Portuguese-speaking countries and European Union nationals can apply for citizenship after seven years, but all other nationalities must wait 10 years.
兩年期居留許可可再續簽三年，屆時許可持有者可以選擇再次延長臨時許可、申請永久居留或申請入籍。葡萄牙語國家和歐盟國民七年後可申請入籍，但其他國家必須等待十年。
Occupancy requirement: Visa holders must stay seven days (consecutive or nonconsecutive) in the first year, and 14 days (consecutive or nonconsecutive) in each subsequent two-year period.
居住要求：簽證持有人第一年必須在葡萄牙停留七天（連續或非連續），隨後每兩年停留十四天（連續或非連續）。
SÃO TOMÉ AND PRÍNCIPE
聖多美普林西比
Launched in August 2025, the C.B.I. program offered by this African island nation is attracting attention for its accessibility and speed, low cost of entry to an unspoiled ecosystem, and affordable options for families. The passport offers visa-free access to more than 60 countries, particularly in the global south.
這個非洲島國於2025年8月推出了投資入籍項目，此項目因門檻低、速度快、生態系統原始未受破壞且家庭選項實惠備受關注。該國護照可免簽進入60多個國家，特別是全球南方國家。
Mo Bennis, senior vice president at Arton Capital, which assists clients with second residency and citizenship in 20 regional programs, said the microstate is becoming attractive for Americans thanks to its lower-cost and straightforward path to citizenship. “For investors looking at where the world is heading over the next 20 years, it is a peaceful, strategically located island nation that offers mobility diversification beyond the usual Caribbean options,” he said.
協助客戶辦理20個區域項目第二居留和入籍的阿爾頓資本高級副總裁莫·本尼斯表示，由於其低成本且直接的入籍路徑，這個微型國家正對美國人變得有吸引力。「對於那些展望世界未來20年走向的投資者來說，這是一個和平、地理位置優越的島國，提供了超越傳統加勒比選項的身份流動性多元化，」他說。
廣告
Types of visas: A minimum donation of $90,000 (or $95,000 for a family of two to four members) to the National Transformation Fund, plus a $5,000 submission fee. The visa comes with access for spouses, dependent children, parents over 55 and grandparents.
簽證類型：向國家轉型基金最低捐贈9萬美元（兩到四口之家為9.5萬美元），外加5000美元申請費。該簽證涵蓋配偶、受撫養子女、55歲以上父母及祖父母。
Occupancy requirement: There is no occupancy requirement. Applications typically take two to three months, with a fully remote application process.
居住要求：無居住要求。申請通常需要兩到三個月，全程可遠程辦理。
AND KEEP AN EYE ON …
值得關注的動向
Ms. Stevens said she’s seeing a shift in C.B.I. programs away from Europe and toward Latin America. “I would not say that Europe is making itself unattractive to foreign retirees, but I think this is the trend we’re beginning to see,” she said. She pointed to Paraguay and Argentina, which are recovering from financial and social upheaval, and reforming their citizenship programs.
史蒂芬斯表示，她看到投資入籍項目的重心正從歐洲轉向拉丁美洲。「我不會說歐洲讓外國退休人員望而卻步，但這確實是我們開始看到的趨勢，」她說。她提到了正在從金融和社會動盪中復甦並改革其公民計劃的巴拉圭和阿根廷。
In the South Pacific, Vanuatu, an archipelago off Australia, offers a fast-track citizenship program with 45 to 60 days for approval and an investment of $130,000 to $180,000 (depending on family size) into a local development fund.
在南太平洋，澳洲附近的萬那杜群島提供了一個快速入籍項目，審批時間為45至60天，需向當地發展基金投資13萬至18萬美元（取決於家庭規模）。
Nauru, a tiny island nation in Micronesia, relaunched its C.B.I. program last year after a 23-year hiatus, rebranded as the Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship Program. It requires an investment of $115,000 — but until June 30, it’s on offer for $90,000.
密克羅尼西亞的微型島國諾魯去年重啟中斷了23年的投資入籍項目，將其重新命名為「經濟與氣候韌性公民計劃」。它要求投資11.5萬美元——但在6月30日之前有9萬美元的優惠價。
