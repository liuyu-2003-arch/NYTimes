日本將部分旅遊簽證費用上調四倍以上

Japan to Raise Some Tourist Visa Fees More Than 400 Percent

HISAKO UENO • 2026-06-24
Japan said that it will raise the price of some tourist visas by more than 400 percent starting in July, the latest move in Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi’s campaign to tightly regulate entries by foreign nationals.
日本表示，將從7月起將部分旅遊簽證的費用上調400%以上，這是首相高市早苗嚴格管控外籍人士入境行動的最新舉措。
Foreign Minister Toshimitsu Motegi announced the changes on Friday, saying in a news briefing that the changes were necessary after 48 years without a change. He cited factors including inflation and changes in exchange rates for the increase.
外務大臣茂木敏充在上週五宣布了這些變動，並在新聞發布會上表示，鑒於相關費用48年來從未調整，此次調整勢在必行。他列舉了通貨膨脹和匯率變動等因素作為漲價的依據。
The fees will apply to travelers from more than 100 countries, including China, India and Vietnam, which were among Japan’s largest sources of visitors last year. They will go from paying about $18 to $93 for a single-entry visa, and from $37 to $186 for multiple entries.
這些費用將適用於來自100多個國家的旅客，包括中國、印度和越南，這些國家包含在去年日本最大的遊客來源國之中。單次入境簽證的費用將從約18美元上調至93美元，多次入境簽證的費用將從37美元增加到186美元。
Tourists from the United States and around 70 other countries will not have to pay the new fees because of visa exemptions. For now, those travelers can enter for free. In 2028, Japan plans to roll out an electronic system for visa-exempt travelers known as Jesta, the fee for which has not yet been set.
來自美國及約70個其他國家的遊客因享受免簽待遇，無需繳納新簽證費。目前，這些旅客可以免費入境。日本計劃在2028年針對免簽旅客推出名為Jesta的電子系統，其費用尚未確定。
廣告
The revenue will subsidize the cost of passports for Japanese nationals, who will have to pay about $43 less in fees, according to Ms. Takaichi’s governing Liberal Democratic Party.
據高市早苗所在的執政黨自民黨稱，這筆收入將用於補貼日本國民的護照費用，國民需要繳納的護照費用將減少約43美元。
Ms. Takaichi won a snap election in February with a sweeping mandate from voters, who supported her hard-line positions on issues such as immigration and China’s growing economic and military clout.
高市早苗在2月份的提前選舉中獲得了選民的壓倒性支持而勝出，選民們支持她在移民問題以及中國日益增長的經濟和軍事影響力等問題上的強硬立場。
Politicians in her party say that foreigners abuse tourist visas to stay indefinitely in Japan, a country that has long been cautious about immigration. Foreigners make up only about 3 percent of the population, but the numbers of permanent residents, students and skilled and unskilled workers has steadily increased since 2021, according to Japan’s Immigration Services Agency.
她所在政黨的政治人士表示，外國人濫用旅遊簽證在日本無限期逗留。日本長久以來一直對移民持謹慎態度。根據日本出入國在留管理廳的數據，雖然外國人僅占日本人口的約3%，但自2021年以來，永久居民、學生以及技術工人和非技術工人的數量一直在穩步增加。
Yoichi Kinoshita, a former Immigration Bureau official who works as an adviser at a visa support agency, said the Takaichi government’s focus has shifted from reducing illegal immigration to targeting legal foreign residents.
曾任出入國在留管理局官員、現為一家簽證支持機構顧問的木下洋一（音）表示，高市政府的重點已從減少非法移民轉向針對合法的外國居民。
“This harsh attitude toward foreigners really resonates with conservatives,” he said
「這種對外國人的嚴苛態度確實引起了保守派的共鳴，」他說。
One example is a recent visa rule change that forced many foreign restaurant owners to shutter the small curry, fried rice and noodle restaurants they had set up around Japan.
一個例子是最近的簽證規則變化，這迫使許多外國餐館老闆關閉了他們在日本各地開設的小型咖哩店、炒飯店和麵館。
廣告
In March, Ms. Takaichi’s cabinet proposed an amendment to Japan’s immigration laws to increase residence permit fees. Applicants for permanent residence would have to pay as much as 300,000 yen, or more than $1,800, up from the current fee of around $60. The revenue collected from the fees would help to pay for services for foreign nationals including Japanese language training, the Immigration Bureau said at the time.
今年3月，高市內閣提議對日本的移民法進行修正，以提高居留許可費用。永久居留申請者可能需支付最高30萬日元（超過1800美元）的費用，目前的費用約為60美元。出入國在留管理局當時表示，從這些費用中徵收的收入將用於支付面向外國人的服務費用，包括日語培訓等。
The bill was criticized by immigrants’ rights groups and the bar associations of Tokyo and Osaka, two of the largest organizations of lawyers in Japan.
該法案受到了移民權利團體以及日本兩大律師組織——東京律師協會和大阪律師協會的批評。
“The government’s ‘foreigner policies’ are becoming increasingly strict, including easing requirements to revoke permanent resident status,” the Tokyo Bar Association said in a statement in May, arguing that the fee increase was a “sudden and excessive burden on foreign nationals.”
東京律師協會在5月的聲明中表示：「政府的『外國人政策』正變得越來越嚴格，包括放寬撤銷永久居民身份的條件。」聲明認為，費用增加是對「外籍人士突然且過度的負擔」。
Ms. Takaichi’s party has drawn a link between excessive tourism and illegal immigration. Since Japan reopened to international tourism after the coronavirus pandemic, tourist areas in cities like Tokyo and Kyoto have felt increasingly unmanageable to locals. Last year, the country reported a record 42.6 million visitor arrivals. Residents have complained about tourists littering, parking in driveways and crowding religious sites.
高市所在的政黨將過度旅遊與非法移民聯繫起來。自日本在新冠疫情後重新開放國際旅遊以來，東京和京都等城市的旅遊區讓當地人感到越來越難以管理。去年，日本接待的入境遊客人數達到創紀錄的4260萬人次。居民們抱怨遊客亂扔垃圾、把車停在私人車道上，以及在宗教場所造成擁擠。
In a November meeting about coexisting with foreign nationals, Ms. Takaichi said that tourism was “vitally important” to Japan’s economy, but that foreign visitors would not be allowed to misbehave.
在11月一次關於與外籍人士共處的會議上，高市早苗表示，旅遊業對日本經濟「至關重要」，但不會允許外國遊客行為不端。
“Illegal acts and deviations from the rules by some foreign nationals are causing anxiety and a sense of unfairness among the public,” she said. “While drawing a clear line against xenophobia, the government will respond firmly to such misconduct.”
「一些外籍人士的違法行為和無視規則的行為正在引起公眾的焦慮和不公平感，」她說。「在劃清反對仇外心理界限的同時，政府將對這種不當行為作出堅決回應。」
廣告
Foreign visitor arrivals have been declining, according to the Japan National Tourism Organization. In May, they fell by 3.6 percent compared to the same month last year. The largest drop was among Chinese tourists, who have cut back on their trips to Japan at a time when relations between the countries are strained.
根據日本政府觀光局的數據，外國遊客的入境人數一直在下降。今年5月與去年同月相比下降了3.6%。降幅最大的是中國遊客，在兩國關係緊張之際，他們減少了前往日本的旅行。

Hisako Ueno是常駐東京的記者/研究員，撰寫日本政治、商業、勞工、性別及文化相關議題。
