PISA測試：美國學生成績大幅下滑，亞洲國家領先

U.S. Reading Scores Plummet on Global Exam

SARAH MERVOSH • 2026-09-09
The reading scores of American 15-year-olds have fallen sharply since 2022 and are near their lowest point in 25 years, according to the results of a key global exam released on Tuesday.
週二公布的一項關鍵全球測試結果顯示，美國15歲學生的閱讀成績自2022年以來大幅下滑，已接近25年來的最低水平。
U.S. math scores are also among the lowest since the test began in 2000, even as students have held their ground in science.
美國學生的數學成績也處於該測試自2000年開始以來的最低區間，不過科學成績保持穩定。
By every measure, American students are being outperformed by students in Singapore and parts of China who had top scores on the Program for International Student Assessment, or PISA, taken by 15-year-olds around the world.
無論以何種標準衡量，美國學生的表現都遜於新加坡和中國部分地區的學生，這些學生在國際學生評估項目(PISA)中得分最高，該測試面向全球15歲學生。
It is the latest warning sign for an American education system that has seen test scores fall over the last decade, starting before the pandemic.
這是對美國教育系統發出的最新警示。過去十年來，該系統的考試成績不斷下降，這種情況從新冠疫情前就已開始了。
廣告
“Our kids are going in the wrong direction on the skills that are absolutely essential for their — and our — success. Period. The end,” said Margaret Spellings, a former education secretary under President George W. Bush. She called for “urgent action at all levels.”
「在對他們自身以及我們所有人的成功都至關重要的技能方面，我們的孩子正朝著錯誤的方向發展。就這麼簡單，」喬治·W·布希政府時期的教育部長瑪格麗特·斯佩林斯表示。她呼籲「在所有層面立即採取行動」。
But the results also show that the challenges facing the United States — including the allure of smartphones, social media and A.I., and the continued fallout from the pandemic — are not just American problems.
但結果也顯示，美國面臨的挑戰——包括智慧型手機、社群媒體和人工智慧的誘惑以及疫情持續造成的後果——並非美國獨有的問題。
Reading scores are falling in many industrialized countries, down from their peak in 2012. Math scores are down globally since 2018.
許多工業化國家的閱讀成績出現下降，較2012年的峰值有所回落。自2018年以來，全球數學成績也在下滑。
Overall, the United States ranked above average in reading and science, and about middle of the pack for math, compared with 37 other countries that make up the Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development, a coalition of mostly free-market democracies that account for a majority of world trade.
與經濟合作與發展組織（一個主要由自由市場民主國家組成、佔世界貿易大部分的聯盟）的其他37個國家相比，美國在閱讀和科學方面高於平均水平，數學大約處於中等。
Experts attribute the global declines, at least in part, to the rise of technology. Students are easily distracted and reading less for pleasure.
專家將全球性的下降至少部分歸因於科技的發展。學生容易分心，並且為興趣而閱讀的時間減少。
Across countries, the share of “hasty readers” — students who skimmed texts quickly and gave incorrect answers — almost doubled from 2018 to 2025, according to the PISA.
根據PISA的數據，各國「敷衍閱讀者」——快速瀏覽文本並給出錯誤答案的學生——的比例從2018年到2025年幾乎翻了一番。
廣告
More than 760,000 teenagers from 91 countries and economies took the test last year, including about 4,600 in the United States.
去年，來自91個國家和經濟體的超過76萬名青少年參加了這項測試，其中包括美國約4600名學生。
Top-scoring countries included Singapore, Japan and Korea.
得分最高的國家包括新加坡、日本和韓國。
China also dominated the test, but the testing sample included only the provinces of Shanghai, Beijing, Jiangsu and Zhejiang, some of the country’s wealthiest coastal areas, prompting skepticism about cherry-picked results.
中國也在測試中佔據絕對優勢，但測試樣本僅包括上海、北京、江蘇和浙江這些中國最富裕的沿海省份，這引發了人們對其「擇優選取」結果的質疑。
Still, those regions are highly populated, include some poorer areas, and are home to some of China’s top talent, said Tracey Burns, chief of global strategy and research for the National Center on Education and the Economy. Those students will soon be competing with American students for jobs and future innovations.
不過，國家教育與經濟中心全球戰略與研究主管特蕾西·伯恩斯說，這幾個地區人口眾多，並且也包含一些較貧困地區，是中國一些頂尖人才的家園。這些學生很快將在就業和未來創新方面與美國學生競爭。
She estimated that the average student tested in China was more than five years ahead of American students in math, and three years ahead in science.
她估計，在中國接受測試的學生平均數學水平比美國學生領先五年以上，科學水平領先三年以上。
“Even a conservative read of these results is concerning,” Dr. Burns said.
「即使對這些結果進行保守解讀，也令人擔憂。」伯恩斯說。
廣告
The United States has long lagged the world’s best education systems, despite spending more on education than many countries.
美國長期以來一直落後於世界上最好的教育體系，儘管其在教育上的支出超過許多國家。
Just 8 percent of American teenagers scored at the highest levels in math on the PISA, compared with 37 percent in Singapore, 23 percent in Korea and 22 percent in Japan. (In the Chinese sample, 54 percent of students scored at the top level in math.)
在PISA中，只有8%的美國青少年在數學方面達到了最高水平，而新加坡為37%，韓國為23%，日本為22%。（在中國樣本中，54%的學生在數學方面達到了最高水平。）
In reading, American students performed about as well as students in Australia and Canada. In science, American students competed with students in Switzerland and Finland.
在閱讀方面，美國學生的表現與澳洲和加拿大大致相當。在科學方面，美國學生與瑞士和芬蘭不相上下。
But the share of American teenagers who failed to reach even a basic level of proficiency in reading and math, considered the minimum needed to read a utility bill, for example, has risen over the last decade.
但在過去十年中，未能達到閱讀和數學基本熟練水平的美國青少年比例有所上升，而這一水平被視為看懂水電費帳單等信息必須具備的最低能力門檻。
That is in sharp contrast to the most successful Asian countries, which do a much better job of lifting up their most disadvantaged students, often by attracting top teachers and principals to their most challenged schools, said Andreas Schleicher, who oversees the PISA for the O.E.C.D.
負責OECD的PISA項目的安德烈亞斯·施萊歇爾說，這一點與最成功的亞洲國家形成鮮明對比，這些國家在幫助最弱勢學生提升方面做得要好得多，通常是通過將最優秀的教師和校長吸引到最薄弱的學校。
“In the U.S. if you make a choice between a better teacher and a smaller class, you typically choose a smaller class,” he said. “In these countries, they save on the class size, but they get the best people to become teachers.”
「在美國，如果你在一位更好的老師和更小的班級規模之間做選擇，你通常會選擇更小的班級規模。」他說。「而在這些國家，他們在班級規模上壓縮成本，但讓最優秀的人成為教師。」
廣告
Another difference: the use of A.I., which the western world has often used to “make things easier for kids,” Mr. Schleicher said.
另一個區別是人工智慧的使用，西方世界常常用它來「給孩子們降低難度」，施萊歇爾說。
In top Asian countries, he said, A.I. is not typically used by students, but is used by teachers “to make students think harder, work harder.”
但在亞洲頂尖國家，他說人工智慧通常不是學生使用的工具，而是被教師用來「讓學生更勤于思考，更努力學習」。
Researchers urged some caution when interpreting the U.S. results. The U.S. sample included about 4,600 students from 144 schools, but response rates fell short of the PISA’s targets. The Trump administration last year slashed staffing at the federal agency responsible for administering the PISA in the United States, as part of its plan to shrink the Department of Education.
研究人員呼籲對美國的成績要謹慎解讀。美國樣本包括來自144所學校的約4600名學生，但回應率未達到PISA的目標。作為縮減教育部計劃的一部分，川普政府去年大幅裁減了負責在美國實施PISA的聯邦機構的人員。
Still, experts say there is little doubt that American students have lost ground.
不過，專家表示，美國學生在退步這一點毫無疑問。
In the absence of a federal education agenda, it will be up to every state to push for improvements, said Kevin Huffman, a former education commissioner in Tennessee who now leads a nonprofit focused on education solutions.
前田納西州教育專員、現領導一家專注於教育解決方案的非營利組織的凱文·赫夫曼說，在缺乏聯邦教育議程的情況下，改進只能在州一級進行。
“Nobody is off the hook,” he said.
「沒有人能置身事外。」他說。

Sarah Mervosh是時報教育記者，主要報導K-12學校。
