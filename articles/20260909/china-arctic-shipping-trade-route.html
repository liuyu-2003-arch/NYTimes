中國貨船探索北極捷徑：商業策略還是政治考量？

Chinese Ship Takes Arctic Shortcut: Smart Business? Or a Political Flex?

JEFFREY GETTLEMAN, JENNY GROSS, 郭莉莉 • 2026-09-09
For years, China has been talking a big game about the Arctic.
多年來，中國一直在北極問題上高調宣示自己的雄心。
The country lies far from the Arctic Circle — more than 800 miles — but still, it has declared itself a “near-Arctic state.”
中國距離北極圈相當遙遠——超過1300公里——但它仍宣稱自己是一個「近北極國家」。
It has teamed up with Russia on coast guard patrols in the Arctic Ocean and explored a flurry of resource and technology projects in Arctic towns. (Most never materialized.)
中國還與俄羅斯合作，在北冰洋開展海警巡邏，並在北極地區的城鎮探索了一系列資源開發和技術項目。（但其中絕大多數最終都沒有落地。）
The Chinese are now promoting the first regular cargo service to use the Northern Sea Route, an Arctic shortcut from Asia to Europe across the top of the world. This week, the Dubai Tower, a Chinese cargo ship, is finishing a three-week journey from Qingdao to Teesport in England.
中國現在正在推廣首個使用北方海路的定期貨運服務，這是一條從亞洲到歐洲、橫跨世界頂端的北極捷徑。本週，一艘名為「杜拜塔」的中國貨輪即將完成從青島到英國提茲港為期三週的航程。
But many of the Arctic watchers tracking the Dubai Tower’s little blinking dot as it moves across the screen on maritime websites are asking the same question: Is this smart business — or more of a bold geopolitical move?
不過，許多在海事網站上追蹤「杜拜塔號」航行軌跡——也就是海事網站上那個移動的閃爍小點的北極觀察人士都在問同一個問題：這究竟是一門精明的生意，還是一項更具野心的地緣政治行動？
The Northern Sea Route is thousands of miles shorter than alternatives through the Suez Canal or around the Cape of Good Hope. And as climate change intensifies, polar ice is disappearing, increasing its accessibility.
北方海路比經由蘇伊士運河或好望角的航線縮短了數千公里。隨著氣候變化加劇，極地冰層不斷消退，這條航道的通行條件也越來越好。
Yet shipping executives say the route is still too difficult, dangerous and expensive for China or any country to make this a regular service. And the season for operations is short — basically August and September, with maybe a few days in July and October. Even then, cargo ships need an icebreaker escort.
然而航運高管表示，這條路線對中國或任何國家來說仍然過於困難、危險且昂貴，難以成為常規航線。而且運營季節很短——基本上只有8月和9月，可能還有7月和10月的幾天。即便如此，貨船仍需要破冰船護航。
“It’s not commercially feasible,” said Vidya Mani, an associate professor at the University of Virginia who focuses on global supply chains.
「這在商業上是不可行的，」維吉尼亞大學研究全球供應鏈的副教授維迪亞·馬尼表示。
Shipping experts said that the route required specialized, reinforced vessels. Its remoteness made it riskier. And the insurance premiums run higher. The costs quickly pile up, they say, making the route unprofitable.
航運專家表示，該航線需要專門的加固船隻。其偏遠性增加了風險，保險費用也更貴。他們說，成本會迅速累積，使得這條航道無法盈利。
But behind these voyages, geopolitical analysts see something else.
但在這些航行背後，地緣政治分析人士看到了別的東西。
“This is a big deal,” said Dexter Roberts, a nonresident senior fellow at the Atlantic Council focusing on China. “China is the only non-Arctic country that is actually doing something like this.”
「這是件大事，」大西洋理事會專門研究中國問題的非常駐高級研究員德克斯特·羅伯茨說。「中國是唯一一個真正在開展這類活動的非北極國家。」
“China is a master at doing things that don’t seem commercial, necessarily, in the beginning,” he added.
「中國擅長做那些一開始看起來未必有商業利益的事情，」他補充道。
Eventually, he said, “it all adds up to a geostrategic win.”
他說，最終，「這一切加起來就是地緣戰略上的勝利。」
In the 1990s, China stepped up its Arctic research and made its first expedition to the North Pole. In more recent years, its government developed plans for trade along what it calls a polar silk road, though its “overtures have been met with suspicion,” according to a study published by Harvard’s Kennedy School.
上世紀90年代，中國加強了對北極的研究，並進行了首次北極科考。近年來，中國政府制定了沿著所謂「冰上絲綢之路」開展貿易的計劃，但根據哈佛大學甘迺迪學院發布的一項研究報告，其「提議遭到了外界的懷疑」。
廣告
The 575-foot-long Dubai Tower left China on Aug. 19, carrying 1,300 containers of electric vehicles, batteries and other renewable energy equipment. This is the first of eight planned voyages that the shipping line Sea Legend has said it will run through the Arctic this season. The route can shave two weeks off the Asia-Europe trip. As Sea Legend executives pointed out, it also avoids conflict zones in the Middle East.
長約175米的「杜拜塔號」於8月19日離開中國，船上裝載了1300個集裝箱，裡面裝有電動汽車、電池以及其他可再生能源設備。這是海傑航運公司計劃在本航季通過北極航線運營的八個航次中的第一個。這條航線可以讓亞洲至歐洲的航程縮短兩週。海傑航運的高管還指出，這條航線的另一個優勢是可以避開中東的衝突地區。
As of Tuesday morning, the Dubai Tower was off Norway’s coast, heading south toward the British coastline. Sea Legend originally said the ship would pull into Felixstowe, another British port, this past Monday. Clearly, it’s running a little late.
截至週二上午，「杜拜塔號」位於挪威海岸附近，正向南駛向英國海岸線。海傑航運最初表示該船將於本週一停靠另一個英國港口費利克斯托。顯然，它比計劃晚了幾天。
That’s another problem. Because of the extreme weather in the Arctic and the bobbing icebergs, voyage time along this route is harder to predict. And predictability is a must for many kinds of shipments.
這是另一個問題。由於北極的極端天氣和漂浮的冰山，航程時間更難預測。而對許多類型的貨物運輸來說，可預測性是必須的。
Another complication is that most of the route runs along Russia’s northern coast and so shipping companies must pay the Russian government access fees. At the moment, many Western companies would be wary of that, given the Western sanctions on Russia over its war with Ukraine.
另一個複雜因素是，這條航線大部分沿俄羅斯北部海岸，因此航運公司必須向俄羅斯政府支付通行費。目前，考慮到西方因俄羅斯與烏克蘭的戰爭而對其實施制裁，許多西方公司對此保持警惕。
South Korea is testing the route, and last month, its first commercial ship set sail for England through the Arctic. But these are pioneers. In 2025, just 13 container ships transited the Northern Sea Route, a record, according to Peter Sand, a shipping industry analyst. That compares with more than 1,800 container ships that used the Suez Canal last year, he said. Russian warships and Russian tankers carrying natural gas also use the Northern Sea Route.
韓國正在測試這條航線，上個月，其首艘商船經由北極前往英國。但這些都只是先行者。航運業分析師彼得·桑德表示，2025年僅有13艘集裝箱船穿越北方海路，這是一個創下紀錄的數字。他說，相比之下，去年有1800多艘集裝箱船使用蘇伊士運河。俄羅斯軍艦和運載天然氣的俄羅斯油輪也使用北方海路。
Lasse Kristoffersen, the chief executive of Wallenius Wilhelmsen, a global Norwegian shipping company, said his company had no plans to acquire ships equipped for the Arctic. But he said that if global warming continued at the same rate, the route could become more popular.
華倫威爾森是一家全球性的挪威航運公司，它的首席執行官拉塞·克里斯托弗森表示，他的公司沒有計劃購置專門用於北極航行的船隻。但他表示，如果全球暖化以同樣的速度持續下去，這條航線可能會變得更受歡迎。

“If you look at the current trend, unfortunately, it seems that that day will come,” he said.
「如果你看看當前的趨勢，不幸的是，那一天似乎會到來，」他說。
More Arctic trade will help Russia. Already it is using the Northern Sea Route to strengthen its bond with China. The new service “obviously brings China and Russia even closer,” said Lyle Goldstein, the director of the Asia program at Defense Priorities, a research institute in Washington.
更多的北極貿易將有利於俄羅斯。它已經在利用北方海路來加強與中國的聯繫。華盛頓智庫國防重點的亞洲項目主任萊爾·戈德斯坦表示，這項新服務「顯然會讓中國和俄羅斯走得更近」。
The crew of the Dubai Tower seemed excited for their own reasons.
「杜拜塔號」的船員們似乎有自己的興奮理由。
“I hope I will get to see the Northern Lights,” said one sailor, in a video posted on social media by the company and labeled a “Heart-to-Heart in the Cabin.”
「我希望看到北極光，」一名船員在公司發布於社群媒體的影片中說道，該影片的標題為「艙內交心」。
Maybe, the sailor added, he would even see a polar bear.
這名船員補充說，也許他還能看到北極熊。
