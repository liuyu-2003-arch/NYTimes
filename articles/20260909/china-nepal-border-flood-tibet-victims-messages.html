西藏洪災失蹤者家屬在悲痛中尋找答案

‘Mama, I Miss You’: Relatives Go Online to Grieve Tibet’s Flood Victims.

郭莉莉, PEI-LIN WU • 2026-09-09
Nearly two weeks have passed since a fierce deluge of ice, boulders and mud tore through a border complex in Tibet, but China has not released an official list of those missing. In the information void, people have found their own ways to seek help and vent their grief.
一場夾雜冰塊、巨石和泥漿的猛烈洪流席捲了西藏的一處邊境建築群，近兩週過去，中國尚未發布官方的失蹤人員名單。在信息匱乏的情況下，人們紛紛以自己的方式尋求幫助並宣洩悲痛。
Internet users have compiled lists of the missing based on public contributions and posted them online. Others have sought information by leaving comments on social media posts by travel influencers in the area, in the hope that they might have seen their missing family members.
網友們根據公眾提供的信息整理失蹤人員名單發布在網上。還有些人通過在該地區的旅遊部落客的社群媒體帖子下留言來尋找信息，希望這些部落客可能見過他們失蹤的家人。
The floods killed more than 1,000 people on both sides of the Chinese-Nepalese border, most of them in Nepal, but the full toll in Tibet remains difficult to assess. China has said more than 500 people were missing in the border area of Gyirong in Tibet.
洪水在中尼邊境兩側奪去了1000多人的生命，其中大部分在尼泊爾，但在西藏的傷亡人數仍難以評估。中國曾表示，在西藏吉隆邊境地區有500多人失蹤。
Across Chinese social media, many posts have taken the form of unanswered text messages, old family photos and simple appeals: Please come home.
在中國的社群媒體上，許多帖子以未獲回覆的短訊和家庭老照片等形式呈現，還有那些簡短的呼聲：請回家。
In Tibet, China has tried to focus public attention on the government’s rescue efforts while minimizing coverage of the victims. Since the disaster on Aug. 26, the police have punished people for spreading false information about the landslide, while state media journalists and online influencers have been warned to hew closely to the official narrative.
在西藏，中國試圖將公眾的注意力集中在政府的救援行動上，同時盡量減少對遇難者的報導。自8月26日災難發生以來，警方處罰了散布有關山體滑坡虛假信息的人，而官方媒體記者和網路部落客則被警告要嚴格保持與官方敘事一致。
Chinese state media outlets have highlighted the stories of some of the immigration officials and border police officers at the Chinese side of the border with Nepal who are missing. Those stories mainly praise the workers’ devotion and patriotic sacrifice on a harsh frontier.
中國官方媒體重點報導了中尼邊境中方一側一些失蹤的移民官員和邊防警察的故事，主要讚揚了這些工作人員在艱苦的邊疆展現了奉獻精神和愛國犧牲精神。
廣告
Many of the messages posted by people on social media, by contrast, are intimate portraits of loss, showing the personal heartbreak and uncertainty that hundreds of families are now experiencing.
相比之下，人們在社群媒體上發布的許多信息則是失去親人的真摯敘述，展現了數百個家庭此刻經歷的個人悲痛與不安。
Feng Guanglong, 26, posted about his childhood friend who worked at the border. Mr. Feng keeps thinking about their last conversation earlier this year, before his friend, Yang Shizi, went missing during the flood. They had grown up together in a village in Yunnan Province where the two swam in rivers, caught fish and had each other’s backs in fights with other kids at school.
26歲的馮光龍發帖提到了他在邊境工作的兒時好友。馮光龍一直想起他們今年早些時候最後一次交談的情景，那是他的朋友楊世子在洪水中失蹤之前的事。他們在雲南省一個村莊裡一起長大，兩人曾在河裡游泳、抓魚，在學校與其他孩子打架時互相撐腰。
Their conversations were often perfunctory. “Knowing the other was safe was usually enough,” Mr. Feng said. They spoke earlier this year when Mr. Feng went back to their hometown.
他們的交談往往只是走走過場。「我知道你平安那就夠了，」馮光龍說。今年早些時候，馮光龍回到家鄉時，他們曾經聊過。
“If I regret anything, it’s that I didn’t say more that time,” he said.
「可能後悔的也就是，那天打電話沒多跟他說幾句話吧，」他說。
After the disaster struck, Mr. Feng tried to contact Mr. Yang, but his calls went unanswered. Later, he saw his friend’s name on an unofficial list of missing people that was circulating online.
災難發生後，馮光龍試著聯繫楊世子，但電話無人接聽。後來，他在網上流傳的一份非官方失蹤人員名單上看到了朋友的名字。
He knows that there is little hope his friend could have survived, but he cannot let go until there is confirmation of his death. He said that Mr. Yang’s two brothers and an uncle traveled to Tibet to wait for news. Mr. Feng hopes that if the remains are not found, that at least Mr. Yang’s belongings will be located and will give the family some closure.
他知道朋友生還的希望渺茫，但在死訊得到確認之前，他無法釋懷。他說，楊世子的兩個兄弟和一個叔叔已經前往西藏等待消息。馮光龍希望，如果找不到遺體，至少能找到楊世子的遺物，給他的家人一些慰藉。
Chinese government updates on the death toll have been limited, in part because of how difficult the rescue work has been. It took Chinese emergency workers more than two days to reach the devastated border crossing, which was covered in more than five feet of thick mud.
中國政府對死亡人數的更新有限，部分原因是救援工作極其困難。中國的緊急救援人員花了超過兩天的時間才抵達被毀的邊境口岸，那裡被超過1.5米厚的厚重泥漿覆蓋。
On Sunday, officials in Tibet said that 43 people had been killed while 519 were still missing. During a state-guided tour for a selected group of foreign reporters, Pu Weidong, a senior police official in Tibet, said that specialists were still identifying the bodies pulled from the site using fingerprints and other genetic data. (The New York Times was not invited on the tour.)
週日，西藏官員表示，已有43人遇難，另有519人仍下落不明。在一次由官方組織、針對部分外國記者的考察中，西藏高級警官普衛東表示，專家們仍在利用指紋和其他基因數據對從現場挖出的屍體進行身份鑒定。（《紐約時報》未受邀參加此次考察。）
In response to questions about the slow release of information and online censorship of videos of the landslide, Chinese officials have said that their release of information has been “public, transparent and timely.”
針對有關信息發布緩慢以及對山體滑坡影片進行網路審查的質疑，中國官員表示，他們的信息發布一直「公開、透明、及時」。
One reason the government tightly controls information is its fear of the power of collective grief, according to Yaqiu Wang, a human rights researcher and a fellow at the University of Pennsylvania. “If families learn about one another’s experiences, private grief can become a shared grievance and eventually a collective demand for truth and accountability,” she said.
賓夕法尼亞大學研究員、人權問題專家王亞秋表示，政府嚴格控制信息的一個原因是害怕集體悲傷的力量。她說：「如果家屬們了解到彼此的經歷，個人的悲痛可能會變成共同的悲憤，最終演變成對真相和問責的集體訴求。」
The posts seeking information have become a roll call of the missing border workers. Many feature pictures of young men and women in uniform standing proudly in front of the same immigration gate whose total devastation was later caught on camera. The posts often include a brief history of how those people came to work at the border, their plans for the future, plus photos of the children, partners and other family waiting at home.
尋求信息的帖子已經成了失蹤邊境工作人員的名單。許多帖子中都有身穿制服的年輕男女自豪地站在一座移民檢查站門前的照片——後來鏡頭捕捉到了該檢查站被徹底摧毀的景象。這些帖子通常會簡要講述這些人如何來到邊防工作、他們對未來的計劃，還有家中等待他們的孩子、伴侶和其他家人的照片。
廣告
One woman searching for her husband, who was near the border, found a clue in a news report. She posted a screenshot of footage showing unopened packages that belonged to missing border workers in Gyirong County. She had spotted a box that she recognized as diapers her husband had bought for their daughter. It sent her into a state of panic. “It hurts so much I can barely breathe,” she wrote. “Please let us find you, hubby. I’m begging you.”
一位正在尋找丈夫的女士從一條新聞報導中發現了一條線索，她的丈夫當時就在邊境附近。她發布了一段影片畫面的截圖，畫面顯示了屬於吉隆縣失蹤邊境人員的未拆封包裹。她發現了一個箱子，認出那是她丈夫為女兒買的紙尿褲。這讓她陷入了恐慌狀態。「我心痛的快要死掉了，」她寫道。「你快讓我們找到你好不好，老公我求求你了。」
Other messages described going to Tibet to search for the missing. A man had been posting about his daughter, a young police officer named Wen Yan who worked at the border. Under a caption of a family photo, he listed all the family members who were waiting for her to come home. “You are the pride of our family,” he wrote.
還有些信息描述了前往西藏尋找失蹤人員的情況。一名男子一直在發布關於他女兒的帖子，她是一名在邊防工作的年輕警官，名叫文燕。在一張全家福照片的配文之中，他列出了所有盼望她回家的家庭成員。「你是我們大家的驕傲，」他寫道。
On Friday, he posted an update that he and his wife were on their way to Gyirong. “Daughter, your mother and I are coming to see you,” he said.
週五，他更新了狀態，稱他和妻子正在前往吉隆的路上。「女兒，我和你媽來看你，」他說。
Many of the messages were expressions of heartbreak.
許多信息都是心碎的表達。
A person who describes himself as a 38-year-old man based in Tibet posted a screenshot of a chat window with messages sent to his wife from their daughter on the day of the flood. “When you get connected to the internet, please call Dad. He’s really worried about you,” she wrote.
一位自稱在西藏生活的38歲男子發布了一張聊天界面的截圖，上面是洪水當天他的女兒發給他妻子的信息。「媽媽你那邊來網了，一定先給爸爸打電話，爸爸可擔心你了，」她寫道。
She followed that with another text that they would wait for her call tomorrow. “I’m sure you are fine,” she added.
緊接著她又發了一條短訊，說他們會等她明天打電話。「媽媽你一定沒事的，」她寫道。
廣告
The next day, the messages grew more panicked. “Mama, please get home quickly.” Finally, she sent the same text, four times in a row: “Mama, I miss you.”
第二天，信息變得越來越惶恐。「媽媽你快回來。」最後，她連續發了四次同樣的信息：「媽媽我想你了。」
There was no response.
她沒有收到任何回應。

郭莉莉(Lily Kuo)是《紐約時報》報導中國記者，常駐台北。

Pei-Lin Wu是《紐約時報》記者/研究員，報導台灣和中國新聞。
