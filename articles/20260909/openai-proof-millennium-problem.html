OpenAI宣布已解出一道「千禧年大獎難題」

OpenAI Says It Has Cracked One of Math’s ‘Millennium Problems’

CADE METZ • 2026-09-09
OpenAI said on Tuesday that its newest artificial intelligence technology had solved one of the “Millennium Problems,” a collection of important unanswered math questions meant to push the world’s leading mathematicians to new heights.
OpenAI週二表示，其最新的人工智慧技術解開了一道「千禧年大獎難題」。這是一組重要的未解數學難題，旨在推動世界頂尖數學家邁向新的高度。
The announcement is another clear sign that A.I. is fundamentally changing the upper reaches of mathematics, which have long been viewed as a pinnacle of human achievement. The change has excited some mathematicians, while stirring concern among others.
這一聲明再次清晰地表明，人工智慧正在從根本上改變數學的最高領域，該領域長期以來被視為人類成就的巔峰。這一變化讓一些數學家感到振奮，同時也引發了另一些人的擔憂。
Over the past year, A.I. systems successfully solved a wide range of problems that have bedeviled mathematicians for decades. But these problems were not as complex, nor as closely watched, as the one that OpenAI’s technology has solved over the past several days. The Millennium Problems are among the most heavily researched in the field.
過去一年裡，人工智慧系統成功解決了一系列困擾數學家數十年的難題。但這些難題的複雜程度和受關注程度都不及OpenAI技術在過去幾天裡解決的那一個。千禧年大獎難題是該領域研究最為密集的課題。
“This is a spectacular culmination of the arc we have seen over the past twelve months,” OpenAI researcher Sébastien Bubeck said of the company’s new solution.
「這是我們過去12個月所看到的發展軌跡的一個壯觀頂點，」OpenAI研究員塞巴斯蒂安·布貝克在談到該公司的新解法時說。
廣告
The company announced that one of its latest models, which has not yet been released to the public, needed just 88 hours to solve what mathematicians call “the Navier–Stokes existence and smoothness problem.” This problem involves a series of equations that are often used to predict the weather.
該公司宣布，其尚未向公眾發布的最新模型之一僅用88小時就解決了數學家所稱的「納維-斯托克斯存在性與光滑性問題」。這個問題涉及一組常用於預測天氣的方程。
The equations describe the movement of water and other fluids. The Navier-Stokes problem, which has no clear practical value, asks whether these equations completely break down in certain situations. OpenAI’s proof claims to have defined just such a situation.
這些方程描述水和其他流體的運動。納維-斯托克斯難題沒有明確的實用價值，它探討的是這些方程在某些情況下是否會完全失效。OpenAI的證明聲稱已經定義了這樣一種情況。
This would imply, at least theoretically, that the laws of physics themselves would break down under certain conditions: that, for example, water could be made to spontaneously explode. But mathematicians and physicists do not believe that this mathematical breakdown could really lead to such an outcome in the physical world.
這意味著，至少在理論上，物理定律本身在某些條件下會失效：例如，水可能被引發自行爆炸。但數學家和物理學家並不認為這種數學上的失效真的會在物理世界中導致這樣的結果。
The Navier-Stokes problem was one of seven “Millennium Problems” selected by the Clay Mathematics Institute in the year 2000 as a way of tracking the progress of mathematics in the new millennium. The institute, founded by an American businessman named Landon T. Clay, offered a million dollars for the first correct solution to each problem. Before OpenAI’s announcement, only one of the problems had been solved.
納維-斯托克斯難題是克萊數學研究所在2000年選定的七個「千禧年大獎難題」之一，目的是追蹤數學在新千年的進展。該研究所由美國商人蘭登·克萊創立，為每個難題的首個正確解答提供100萬美元獎金。在OpenAI宣布之前，七個難題中只有一個被解決。
“These questions are lighthouses,” said Terence Tao, a professor at the University of California, Los Angeles, who is regarded by many as the finest mathematician of his generation. “They are great focus points that attract the efforts of human scientists.”
「這些難題是燈塔，」加州大學洛杉磯分校教授陶哲軒說。他被許多人視為同代人中最傑出的數學家。「它們是吸引人類科學家努力的絕佳焦點。」
Dr. Tao is among the many mathematicians who have publicly warned that the latest A.I. systems could end up damaging the field of mathematics. If A.I. technologies can solve the most difficult problems with little input from human mathematicians, he says, they could weaken human understanding of the field.
陶哲軒是眾多公開警告最新人工智慧系統可能最終損害數學領域的數學家之一。他說，如果人工智慧技術幾乎不需要人類數學家的參與就能解決難度最大的問題，它們可能會削弱人類對這一領域的理解。
廣告
“The effort needed to solve problems is often very instructive. It teaches you something. It’s like going to the gym and having a goal to lift a weight a hundred times,” he explained. “Now, A.I. can solve questions without really getting any value out of them. It’s like having machines that can lift weights for you at the gym.”
「解決難題所需的努力往往非常有教育意義。它能教會你一些東西。就像去健身房，設定一個舉重一百次的目標，」他解釋道。「現在，人工智慧可以解決難題，卻無法從中真正獲得任何價值。這就像在健身房裡有機器代替你舉重一樣。」
But as he and others point out, mathematicians still provide a helping hand as A.I. systems work through these problems. OpenAI said that it deployed vast teams of “A.I. agents” to solve the Navier-Stokes problem and that its researchers passed key ideas between these teams.
但正如他和其他人所指出的，在人工智慧系統處理這些難題時，數學家仍然提供了幫助。OpenAI表示，它部署了龐大的「AI智能體」團隊來解決納維-斯托克斯難題，其研究人員在這些團隊之間傳遞關鍵想法。
“Our role was like a bumble bee cross-pollinating across different groups and delivering different bits of information,” said OpenAI researcher Dan Roberts.
「我們的角色就像一隻大黃蜂，在不同群體之間交叉授粉，傳遞不同的信息片段，」OpenAI研究員丹·羅伯茨說。
Companies like OpenAI build their A.I. technologies using what scientists call neural networks, systems that learn skills by analyzing vast amounts of digital data. About two years ago, such companies started to hone these systems using another technique called reinforcement learning. Through this process, A.I. systems can learn additional behavior through extensive trial and error.
像OpenAI這樣的公司使用科學家所稱的神經網路來構建其人工智慧技術，這些系統通過分析海量數字數據來學習技能。大約兩年前，這類公司開始使用另一種名為強化學習的技術來完善這些系統。通過這一過程，人工智慧系統可以通過大量的試錯來學習額外的行為。
By working through thousands of math problems, for instance, they can learn which methods lead to the right answer and which do not. Researchers inside labs like OpenAI develop complex feedback mechanisms that show the system when it has done something right and when it has done something wrong.
例如，通過處理數千道數學題，它們可以了解哪些方法能得出正確答案，哪些不能。OpenAI等實驗室的內部研究人員開發了複雜的反饋機制，讓系統知道什麼時候做對了，什麼時候做錯了。
(The New York Times has sued OpenAI and Microsoft, claiming copyright infringement of news content related to A.I. systems. The two companies have denied the suit’s claims.)
（《紐約時報》已起訴OpenAI和微軟，指控其人工智慧系統侵犯了新聞內容的版權。兩家公司否認了相關指控。）
廣告
Reinforcement learning is difficult to perfect in areas like creative writing, philosophy and ethics, where the question of which answers are right or wrong is hard to define objectively. But the technique is ideally suited to mathematics.
強化學習在創意寫作、哲學和倫理等領域難以完善，因為在這些領域，哪些答案是對是錯很難客觀定義。但這項技術非常適合數學領域。
Through this process, A.I. systems can learn to prove mathematical theorems using a computer programming language called Lean. The language was originally designed as a tool for human mathematicians. But now that A.I. systems are skillful enough to generate their own computer code, they, too, can use Lean to generate their own math proofs.
通過這一過程，人工智慧系統可以學會使用一種名為Lean的計算機編程語言來證明數學定理。該語言最初是為人類數學家的工具而設計的。但現在，既然人工智慧系統已經足夠熟練，能夠生成自己的計算機代碼，它們也可以使用Lean來生成自己的數學證明。
OpenAI said that it solved the Navier-Stokes problem using as many as 10,000 “A.I. agents” working in concert. Running such a large number of A.I. systems is likely to have cost millions of dollars, a result of the enormous amounts of electrical power needed to operate the specialized chips that drive A.I. technologies.
OpenAI表示，它使用多達1萬個協同工作的「AI智能體」解決了納維-斯托克斯難題。運行如此大量的人工智慧系統很可能花費了數百萬美元，這是因為驅動人工智慧技術的專用晶片需要消耗大量電力。
OpenAI research scientist Noam Brown called this a “very expensive process,” before adding that the costs of running A.I. technologies tend to drop as companies like OpenAI improve their efficiency.
OpenAI研究科學家諾姆·布朗稱這是一個「非常昂貴的過程」，但他補充說，隨著OpenAI等公司提高效率，運行人工智慧技術的成本往往會下降。
On Tuesday, the company released a paper describing its solution, including a Lean proof, allowing outside mathematicians to see how its agents cracked the problem. But to Dr. Tao, it is still a poor substitute for humans solving the problem on their own. He compared OpenAI to a wilderness guide who finds a path to a hidden waterfall.
週二，該公司發布了一篇論文，描述了其解決方案，包括Lean證明，讓外部數學家得以了解其智能體是如何攻克這一難題的。但對陶哲軒博士來說，這仍然無法替代人類自己解決問題。他將OpenAI比作一位找到通往隱秘瀑布之路的野外嚮導。
“This has some value,” he said. “But once someone shows a specific path to the waterfall, people just take that path. They don’t spend as much time looking for other paths.”
「這有一定價值，」他說。「但一旦有人展示了一條通往瀑布的特定路徑，人們就會直接走那條路。他們不會花那麼多時間尋找其他路徑。」
