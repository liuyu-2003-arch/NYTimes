香港主權移交後首任行政長官董建華逝世

Tung Chee-hwa, Who Led Hong Kong After Its Return to China, Dies at 89

王霜舟, KEITH BRADSHER • 2026-09-09
Tung Chee-hwa, a shipping tycoon who became Hong Kong’s first leader after the transfer of power from British to Chinese control in 1997 and resigned eight years later after the city had been battered by the Asian financial crisis, the SARS epidemic and mass protests, has died at 89.
1997年英國將香港主權移交中國後出任香港特別行政區首任行政長官的航運大亨董建華逝世，享年89歲。董建華任內香港接連遭受亞洲金融危機、非典疫情及大規模抗議活動衝擊，他在就職八年後辭職。
His death on Tuesday was announced by his office, but no other details were immediately available.
他的辦公室於週二宣布了這一消息，但未公布更多細節。
The 1997 handover officially returned Hong Kong to Chinese rule under a “one country, two systems” arrangement that promised Hong Kong a high degree of autonomy and allowed it to keep its capitalist system and many of its freedoms.
1997年的主權交接使香港在「一國兩制」框架下正式回歸中國。這一安排承諾香港享有高度自治，並允許其保留資本主義制度及諸多自由。
Much of the implementation of that policy was left to Mr. Tung. He had to carve out a path between two masters — the Chinese government and the people of Hong Kong — but his administration left no doubt that the Communist Party in Beijing was the ultimate authority, fueling public discontent in a city that cherished its civil liberties.
政策的大部分落實工作落在了董建華肩上。他不得不在中國政府與香港市民之間尋求平衡，但其施政明確無誤地表明，北京才是最高權威，這在珍視公民自由的香港引發了公眾不滿。
廣告
As a relative unknown outside the world of shipping, Mr. Tung was seen as a long shot to become Hong Kong’s first Chinese leader after more than 150 years of British colonial rule.
董建華的名聲很大程度上僅限於在船運界，因此在長達150多年的英國殖民統治結束後，很少有人認為他有望出任香港首位華人領導人。
He served on the Chinese government’s top advisory body and in the cabinet of the last British governor, Chris Patten, who considered him a polite but unrelenting advocate of China’s interests.
他曾在中國最高政治諮詢機構任職，也曾加入最後一任港督彭定康(Chris Patten)的行政局。後者認為他是一個彬彬有禮但堅定維護中國利益的人。
In Hong Kong, he was far less popular than the handful of top officials seen as favorites for the chief executive post, but the choice was Beijing’s to make.
在香港本地，他的受歡迎程度遠不及幾位被視為行政長官熱門人選的高官，但決定權在北京。
Beijing signaled its preference in early 1996, when China’s leader, Jiang Zemin, singled out Mr. Tung among a delegation from Hong Kong and shook his hand, an interaction that was shown prominently in mainland state media. He dominated a tightly controlled selection process later that year and took office when the territory was handed over to Chinese sovereignty on July 1, 1997.
1996年初，中國領導人江澤民在會見香港代表團時特意走上前與董建華握手，這一畫面在大陸官方媒體上被顯著報導，北京由此明確表達了偏好。同年晚些時候，他在受到嚴格控制的選舉程序中勝出，並於1997年7月1日香港主權移交之際正式就職。
Almost immediately, Mr. Tung faced tumult. The Asian financial crisis began the day after he took office, when Thailand devalued its currency, and led to precipitous stock sell-offs, attacks on Hong Kong’s currency and the bursting of a speculative bubble in Hong Kong apartment prices.
上任伊始，董建華便面臨動盪局面。就在他履新的第二天，泰國貨幣貶值引發亞洲金融危機，隨後導致股市暴跌、港元遭到炒家衝擊，以及香港樓市投機泡沫的破裂。
The Hong Kong government bought billions of dollars’ worth of shares in local companies in August 1998 to support the stock market and punish speculators. The economy began to recover, but housing prices fell for more than six years. By 2003, more than one in five apartments in Hong Kong was worth less than the mortgage on it.
1998年8月，香港特區政府入市收購數十億美元的本地公司股票，以穩住股市並擊退炒家。經濟隨後開始復甦，但樓價接連下跌了六年多。到2003年，香港超過兩成的住房已淪為市值低於按揭貸款額的「負資產」。
廣告
In the spring of 2003, Hong Kong was battered by the SARS virus outbreak; the reported death toll of 299 accounted for nearly 40 percent of the worldwide deaths from the disease. The city also recorded some of the earliest documented human cases of H5N1 avian influenza.
2003年春，香港遭受非典疫情重創，通報死亡299人，佔全球該病死亡病例的近四成。此前，該市還記錄到了全球最早有記載的部分人類感染H5N1禽流感病例。
Political unrest broke out that summer. Hundreds of thousands of residents joined protests against a government effort to pass a package of security laws. Among other provisions, the laws would have allowed Hong Kong to ban any organization linked to an organization outlawed by Beijing, a threat that struck fear among the city’s Catholic and Protestant leaders.
那年夏天爆發了政治動盪。數十萬市民走向街頭抗議，反對政府推動國安立法。法案條款包括授權香港特區政府禁止任何與中央政府取締組織相關的團體，這一規定引發了當地天主教和新教領袖的深切擔憂。
The government ultimately abandoned the bill, but the protests emboldened an active opposition movement. The movement remained a powerful force until a sweeping crackdown began in 2020, when China imposed its own tough security law on Hong Kong.
特區政府最終撤回了法案，但那場抗議壯大了反對派陣營的聲勢。這股反對的力量一直十分活躍，直到2020年面臨全面整頓——當時中央政府在香港頒佈實施了嚴厲的香港國安法。
Mr. Tung, his popularity badly wounded by the protests, stepped down in 2005, two years before the end of his second five-year term in office. He cited the effects of working long hours on his health.
抗議讓董建華的公眾聲望大受打擊。2005年，在他第二個五年任期還剩兩年時，他以長期超時工作損害健康為由提前請辭。
“These have been turbulent times, full of hardship for Hong Kong people,” he said. “I have done my best to serve the people and have always worked hard.”
「為疏解市民的痛楚，」他表示，「我鞠躬盡瘁，從未敢一日懈怠。」
Tung Chee-hwa was born in Shanghai on July 7, 1937. His father, Tung Chao-yung, was a shipping magnate who turned a single tramp steamer into one of the world’s largest shipping fleets, Orient Overseas International. The family fled to Hong Kong in 1949 as Mao Zedong and the Communist Party took control of China.
董建華1937年7月7日出生於上海。他的父親董浩雲是著名的「世界船王」，從一艘貨輪白手起家，打造出全球最大航運集團之一東方海外國際。1949年毛澤東和中國共產黨取得政權後，董家避居香港。
廣告
In Hong Kong, Mr. Tung’s family lived in the wealthy Kowloon Tong neighborhood, and he attended an international school in the expectation he would study abroad. To The South China Morning Post in 2017, he recalled his father telling him he did not have the “intellect” to attend the city’s premier school, the University of Hong Kong.
在香港，董建華一家住在富人區九龍塘，他被送入國際學校就讀，準備日後出國深造。2017年接受《南華早報》採訪時，他回憶起父親曾對他說，他沒有考上本地名校香港大學的「天分」。
Instead, Mr. Tung went to England and studied engineering at the University of Liverpool. After graduation, he worked in Boston, New York and San Francisco, first for General Electric and then for his father’s shipping firm.
於是，董建華赴英留學，在利物浦大學修讀工程學。畢業後，他先後在波士頓、紐約和舊金山工作，起初服務於通用電氣，隨後加入父親的航運企業。
In 1961, he married his teenage sweetheart, Betty Chiu, a nurse and daughter of a Hong Kong developer. They had three children, Alan, Andrew and Audrey Slighton Tung. A complete list of survivors was not immediately available.
1961年，他與青少年時期的戀人趙洪娉(Betty Chiu)喜結連理。趙洪娉是一名護士，父親是香港地產開發商。兩人育有三名子女：長子董立均、次子董立新及女兒董立筠。目前尚無完整的遺屬名單。
Mr. Tung’s father, who died in 1982, left the shipping business to his two sons, Tung Chee-hwa and Tung Chee-chen.
董建華的父親於1982年逝世，將航運家族事業交由兩個兒子董建華與董建成接管。
During a global recession in the early 1980s, the business ran up more than $2.6 billion in debt. Mainland companies put up a significant portion of a $120 million bailout.
在1980年代初的全球經濟衰退中，董氏航運業務累積了超過26億美元的債務。公司接受了一筆1.2億美元的紓困資金，其中相當一部分來自中國大陸企業。
The rescue fueled concerns, when Tung Chee-hwa became Hong Kong’s chief executive, that he would be unduly influenced by Beijing, a charge he rejected. In 2017, Cosco Shipping, a Chinese state-owned conglomerate, purchased Orient Overseas for $6.3 billion, with the Tung family receiving more than $4 billion.
這筆資金在董建華出任香港特首時引發外界擔憂，認為他可能會過多受到北京方面的影響，但他對此予以否認。2017年，中國央企中遠海運以63億美元收購了東方海外，董氏家族從中獲得了超過40億美元。
廣告
Mr. Tung, who was stoutly built and had close-cropped hair, was socially conservative and spoke often about the need to maintain social order and respect for authority. He endorsed a suggestion by Mahathir Mohamad, the Malaysian prime minister and another champion of “Asian values,” that the U.N. Declaration on Human Rights be revised to emphasize livelihood issues over civil and political liberties, which both leaders saw as Western values unsuited to Asia.
留著短髮、身材魁梧的董建華秉持保守的社會觀念，常強調維護社會秩序和尊崇權威的必要性。同為「亞洲價值觀」倡導者的馬來西亞總理馬哈蒂爾·穆罕默德(Mahathir Mohamad)曾主張修改《世界人權宣言》，優先強調民生保障而非公民與政治自由，董建華對此表示贊同。兩位領導人均認為後者是不適合亞洲的西方價值觀。
Before taking office, Mr. Tung called for unity in Hong Kong. “Our society has become too politicized in recent years,” he said after his selection in 1996.
上任之前，董建華曾呼籲香港社會保持團結。「近年來我們的社會過於政治化了，」他在1996年當選後這樣表示。
But division and discontent grew during his tenure.
然而在其任內，社會分歧與不滿情緒卻不斷加劇。
Mr. Tung became the focal point of public anger over the government’s unmet promises to expand direct elections. A plan to build more housing fed a glut that depressed prices, angering homeowners. Later governments were so spooked by the mess that they severely restricted home building, driving apartment prices beyond the reach of most residents.
因特區政府未能兌現擴大直選的承諾，董建華成為公眾表達不滿的焦點。他提出的建屋計劃導致供過於求並拉低房價，激怒了業主；此後的幾屆政府因害怕重蹈覆轍而大幅限制住房建設，進而推高房價，使其超出了絕大多數市民的承受能力。
Mr. Patten described Mr. Tung as “not at all a bad man” but one who was “too malleable” to the wishes of Beijing. “He combined a curious mix of indecision and stubbornness,” Mr. Patten wrote in his 2022 book, “The Hong Kong Diaries.” “He was also politically tone deaf,” he added, noting Mr. Tung’s effort to push through the security bill that he was forced to withdraw.
彭定康稱董建華「絕非壞人」，但「過於順從」北京的意志。「在他身上有一種優柔寡斷與固執己見的奇特結合，」彭定康在其2022年出版的《香港日記》(The Hong Kong Diaries)中寫道。他還提及董建華強推國安法案最終被迫撤回一事，補充道：「他在政治上也缺乏敏感度。」
Mr. Tung’s supporters described him as the victim of difficult times, troubled by problems largely not of his own making.
董建華的支持者則認為他是時代困局的受害者，困擾他的許多難題並非由他一手造成。
廣告
The Asian financial crisis, avian flu and SARS were not his fault, said Regina Ip, who as secretary for security under Mr. Tung led the failed security law initiative in 2003. She praised his attempt to increase housing construction as well as his establishment of a high-tech hub in the city.
曾在董建華任內擔任保安局局長、並主導了2003年國安立法的葉劉淑儀錶示，亞洲金融危機、禽流感和非典都不是他的責任。她讚揚了董建華增加住房供應的嘗試，以及在香港建立高科技樞紐的舉措。
“He was just unlucky,” she said. “All of his initiatives were the right ones.”
「他只是運氣不好，」她表示，「他的各項政策方向其實都是對的。」
