美國正對中國開展更廣泛間諜活動

Chinese Threat Requires a Broader Spying Mission, C.I.A. Deputy Director Says

DUSTIN VOLZ • 2026-09-09
The United States is engaging in a broader range of espionage against China, including Chinese companies, to address the country’s status as both a military and an economic threat, the deputy C.I.A. director, Michael Ellis, said on Tuesday.
中央情報局副局長麥可·埃利斯週二表示，美國正在對中國——包括中國企業——開展更廣泛的間諜活動，以應對該國作為軍事和經濟雙重威脅的地位。
During a rare public speaking appearance, Mr. Ellis said the nature of intelligence collection by the C.I.A. and other intelligence agencies had evolved considerably since they were created during the Cold War to spy on the Soviet Union.
在一次罕見的公開演講中，埃利斯表示，自中情局和其他情報機構在冷戰期間為監視蘇聯而建立以來，情報收集的性質已經發生了巨大變化。
Unlike past geopolitical rivals, China “poses a fundamentally different kind of threat to us because the competition is economic,” Mr. Ellis said at a cybersecurity conference in Washington.
埃利斯在華盛頓舉行的一場網路安全會議上說，與過去的地緣政治對手不同，中國「對我們構成了一種截然不同的威脅，因為這種競爭是經濟上的」。
Western officials say that under Communist Party rule, China does not have an independent business community akin to the United States or much of Europe. Mr. Ellis said that makes Chinese companies a legitimate target for espionage in areas like artificial intelligence, semiconductors and biotechnology.
西方官員表示，在共產黨統治下，中國沒有像美國或歐洲大部分地區那樣的獨立商界。埃利斯說，這使得中國企業在人工智慧、半導體和生物技術等領域成為間諜活動的正當目標。
廣告
The C.I.A. has needed to broaden its spying mandate because valuable intelligence about those technologies is “not found in the same places that political or military foreign intelligence is found,” Mr. Ellis said. Such information, he added, is “resident in the private sector — or in which China has to come as close as you might describe as a private sector.”
埃利斯表示，中情局需要擴大其間諜行動授權，因為關於這些技術的寶貴情報「無法在獲取政治或軍事情報的那些管道中找到」。他還說，這些信息「存在於私營部門中——或者存在於中國那些可以說最接近私營部門的實體之中」。
Mr. Ellis’s remarks are a subtle departure from a precedent established by previous administrations, which generally sought to draw a line between espionage that the United States considers permissible, such as intelligence gathering conducted for the purposes of national defense and foreign policy, and spying that is economically motivated.
埃利斯的言論與歷屆政府所確立的先例有著微妙的不同，歷屆政府通常會區分美國認為可接受的間諜活動（例如為國防和外交政策目的而進行的情報收集）與出於經濟動機的間諜活動。
The Obama administration, for example, routinely called out Chinese hacking of intellectual property from American companies, ultimately signing an accord with Beijing in 2015 in which both sides agreed not to engage in economic espionage. The United States later accused China of violating the agreement.
例如，歐巴馬政府經常公開指責中國通過駭客手段竊取美國企業的知識產權，最終在2015年與北京簽署了一項協議，雙方均同意不從事經濟間諜活動。美國後來指責中國違反了該協議。
U.S. officials, however, have long privately acknowledged that it can be difficult to define what constitutes legitimate espionage. But they have traditionally not said so publicly.
然而，美國官員長期以來在私下承認，很難界定什麼是合法的間諜活動。但他們傳統上不會在公開場合如此表態。
“We’ve always collected intelligence in some way, shape or form,” Mr. Ellis said in a short interview after his public comments. “What’s different now is it’s not just about tanks, missiles and submarines.”
「我們一直以某種方式、形式或形態在收集情報，」埃利斯在公開演講後的一次簡短採訪中說。「現在的不同之處在於，這不僅僅關乎坦克、導彈和潛艇了。」
The timing of Mr. Ellis’s remarks is also significant. President Trump is scheduled to host the Chinese leader, Xi Jinping, this month for a state visit. Competition around A.I. and trade is expected to be among the topics discussed during the summit.
埃利斯發表此番言論的時機也頗具深意。川普總統定於本月接待中國領導人習近平進行國事訪問。圍繞人工智慧和貿易的競爭預計將成為峰會期間討論的議題之一。
Mr. Ellis’s remarks came on the same day the Trump administration issued a public advisory warning that Chinese artificial intelligence companies were engaging in “industrial scale” theft of trade secrets from their U.S. counterparts.
在埃利斯發表講話的同一天，川普政府發布了一份公開諮詢警告，指出中國的人工智慧公司正對美國同行進行「工業級規模」的商業機密竊取。
廣告
The alert, issued by the National Security Agency, the Federal Bureau of Investigation and the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency, listed several leading Chinese A.I. companies that it said were engaged in the practice, which is known as distillation, since at least 2024.
這份由國家安全局、聯邦調查局以及網路安全和基礎設施安全局發布的警報列出了幾家領先的中國人工智慧公司，並稱自至少2024年以來，它們就參與了這種被稱為「蒸餾」的做法。
“The sheer scale of these campaigns and their sophistication indicate that distillation is not a supplement to these companies’ A.I. model development, but the critical core of it,” the advisory said, echoing earlier accusations from Trump administration officials.
「這些活動的龐大規模及其複雜性表明，『蒸餾』並不是這些公司人工智慧模型開發的補充手段，而是其關鍵核心，」該諮詢報告指出，這與川普政府官員早先的指控相呼應。
In his remarks, Mr. Ellis praised recent overhauls at the C.I.A., including the elevation of a cybermission center last year, that he said allowed the agency to achieve the Trump administration’s national security objectives. Among the successes, he said, was the U.S. raid in Venezuela in January that led to the capture of Nicolás Maduro.
埃利斯在講話中讚揚了中情局最近的改革，包括去年將一個網路任務中心升級為更高層級，他說這使得該機構能夠實現川普政府的國家安全目標。他提到，其中一項成功案例是今年1月美國在委內瑞拉發動的突襲行動，該行動導致尼古拉斯·馬杜羅被捕。
“That operation was only made possible by a flawless intelligence picture, and that flawless intelligence picture was built on cyberoperations” at the C.I.A., Mr. Ellis said. “Without that kind of operational picture, being driven by cyber, we wouldn’t have been able to help enable U.S. Special Forces to identify the location of Nicolás Maduro and apprehend him within four minutes of landing on the ground.”
「那次行動之所以能夠實現，得益於無懈可擊的情報圖景，而那無懈可擊的情報圖景是建立在（中情局的）網路行動基礎之上的，」埃利斯說。「如果沒有那種由網路驅動的行動圖景，我們就不可能協助美國特種部隊在著陸後四分鐘內確定尼古拉斯·馬杜羅的位置，並將其逮捕。」
Mr. Ellis also briefly spoke about threats to U.S. critical infrastructure posed by foreign nation-state hackers. He said that a compromise of U.S. telecommunications networks known as Salt Typhoon and linked to China remains “a very critical threat” nearly two years after it was discovered. That operation and recent attacks on other targets, including municipal water systems that officials believe were carried out by Iran, highlight the need for the government to do more to protect the United States, he added.
埃利斯還簡短地談到了外國國家駭客對美國關鍵基礎設施構成的威脅。他表示，被稱為「鹽颱風」並與中國有關聯的對美國電信網路的入侵活動在被發現近兩年後，仍然是「一個非常關鍵的威脅」。他補充說，那次行動以及最近對其他目標的攻擊——包括官員們認為由伊朗實施的對市政供水系統的攻擊——凸顯出政府需要採取更多措施來保護美國。
“There is a whole host of threats to our critical infrastructure, and it’s an area where the government needs to also do a better job of helping the private sector to protect itself against these threat actors,” Mr. Ellis said.
「我們的關鍵基礎設施面臨著許許多多的威脅，在這一領域，政府也需要做得更好，幫助私營部門抵禦這些威脅行為者，」埃利斯說。
