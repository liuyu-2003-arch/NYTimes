川普推遲對台軍售為何危險

A Dangerous Misunderstanding Is Brewing Over Taiwan

CRAIG SINGLETON • 2026-11-03
President Trump’s decision to hold back a $14 billion arms package for Taiwan makes a certain amount of diplomatic sense.
川普總統決定暫緩向台灣提供價值140億美元的武器計劃，從外交角度來看確實有其合理之處。
After last year’s bruising trade war, Washington and Beijing are trying to preserve a fragile tariff truce while negotiating a broader set of economic agreements. Delaying the arms sale avoids adding another immediate source of friction to an already strained relationship.
在經歷了去年激烈的貿易戰之後，華盛頓和北京正試圖維持脆弱的關稅休戰，同時談判達成更廣泛的經濟協議。推遲軍售可以避免給已經緊張的雙邊關係增加另一個直接的摩擦源。
But avoiding a confrontation over Taiwan today could set the stage for a far more dangerous one tomorrow.
但是，今天避免在台灣問題上發生對抗可能為明天更危險的對抗埋下伏筆。
For decades, the United States and China have managed their differences over Taiwan through carefully calibrated language that allowed each side to preserve its own interpretation of what had been agreed.
幾十年來，美中兩國一直通過字斟句酌的措辭來管控在台灣問題上的分歧，這使得雙方都能保留自己對已達成共識的解讀。
廣告
President Xi Jinping of China may now apply that same logic to President Trump’s actions, reading the delay as evidence that Mr. Trump is accepting Beijing’s premise that stable U.S.-China relations depend on Washington limiting its support for Taiwan. The longer the deal is held up, the greater the risk that Beijing will see future arms sales as a betrayal, triggering precisely the instability Mr. Trump is trying to avoid.
中國國家主席習近平現在可能會將同樣的邏輯應用於川普總統的行動，將這種推遲解讀為川普接受了北京的前提——美中關係的穩定取決於華盛頓限制對台灣的支持。這筆軍售交易擱置的時間越長，北京就越有可能將未來的軍售視為背叛，這恰恰會引發川普正在試圖避免的那種不穩定。
Mr. Trump has delayed the package, which reportedly includes interceptor missiles and other air-defense systems, since early this year. He has called it “a very good negotiating chip” in his dealings with Mr. Xi and said the sale’s fate “depends on China.” For Mr. Trump, this preserves negotiating leverage while leaving open the possibility of the package being approved later.
自今年年初以來，川普一直推遲這筆軍售，據報導這項軍售包括攔截導彈和其他防空系統。他將其稱為與習近平交涉時「非常好的談判籌碼」，並表示這筆軍售的命運「取決於中國」。對川普來說，這保留了談判籌碼，同時為該方案日後獲得批准留下了可能性。
But the history of U.S.-China understandings over Taiwan still matters, and Beijing may assign more lasting meaning to the pause than Mr. Trump intends.
但美中在台灣問題上的共識歷史仍然很重要，北京可能為這種暫停賦予長期意義，超出川普的本意。
Mr. Xi has made Taiwan — which China claims as its own territory — central to his vision of relations between the United States and China. At last month’s summit at the White House, he signaled that steadier relations with Beijing depended on the United States distancing itself from Taiwan. He urged Washington to explicitly oppose the island’s formal independence from China, a tougher formulation than the longstanding U.S. position that it simply does not support an independent Taiwan.
習近平已將台灣——中國聲稱是其領土——置於其美中關係願景的核心。在上個月的白宮峰會上，他發出信號，表明與北京建立更穩定的關係取決於美國與台灣保持距離。他敦促華盛頓明確反對台灣正式從中國獨立，這一表述比美國長期以來僅僅不支持台灣獨立的立場更為強硬。
Ambiguity over Taiwan has helped U.S.-China relations advance. In a joint communiqué in 1972 aimed at ending decades of Cold War hostility, Washington acknowledged Beijing’s claim that there was only one China and that Taiwan was a part of it, but stopped short of endorsing that view. Seven years later, when the United States established formal diplomatic relations with China, it recognized Beijing as China’s sole legal government while leaving the question of Taiwan’s status unresolved and maintaining unofficial ties with the island.
在台灣問題上的模糊性有助於美中關係的發展。在1972年雙方旨在結束數十年冷戰敵對狀態的聯合公報中，華盛頓稱認識到北京關於只有一個中國且台灣是中國一部分的立場，但並未直接贊同這一觀點。七年後，當美國與中國正式建交時，它承認北京是中國的唯一合法政府，但同時將台灣地位問題擱置，並與該島保持非官方關係。
On arms sales, however, Washington drew clearer lines.
然而，在武器銷售方面，華盛頓劃定了更清晰的界限。
廣告
Seeking better relations with Beijing, the administration of President Ronald Reagan issued a communiqué with China in 1982 under which Washington said it intended to gradually reduce arms sales to Taiwan. Mr. Reagan, however, insisted that any reductions depended on Beijing maintaining a peaceful approach toward Taiwan. He also assured Taipei that there was no timetable for ending arms sales and that the United States would not consult Beijing before making future decisions on the issue. Mr. Trump has drawn wno similar lines around the current pause.
為了尋求與北京改善關係，雷根政府於1982年與中國發表了一份公報，華盛頓在公報中表示打算逐步減少對台軍售。然而，雷根堅持，任何削減都取決於北京方面是否繼續對台灣採取和平方式。他還向台北保證，沒有結束對台軍售的時間表，且美國今後就該問題作出決定時不會事先徵詢北京的意見。而川普在當前的暫停軍售問題上並未劃出類似的界限。
The past has shown what can happen when Beijing believes an assurance has been broken. In 1995, U.S. officials told Beijing that Taiwan’s president at the time, Lee Teng-hui, would not receive a visa to visit his alma mater, Cornell University. Under pressure from Congress, President Bill Clinton later reversed course and approved the visit.
過去的歷史已經表明，當北京認為某項保證被打破時會發生什麼。1995年，美國官員告訴北京，計劃訪問母校康乃爾大學的時任台灣總統李登輝將不會獲得簽證。在國會的壓力下，柯林頓總統後來改變了決定，批准了這次訪問。
China recalled its ambassador and launched military exercises around Taiwan. The tension stretched into the next year, when China fired missiles into waters near the island ahead of Taiwan presidential elections, prompting Washington to deploy two aircraft-carrier groups to the region.
中國召回了大使，並在台灣周邊展開軍事演習。緊張局勢一直延續到第二年，當時中國在台灣總統選舉前向該島附近水域發射導彈，促使華盛頓向該地區部署了兩個航母戰鬥群。
A perceived breach today could carry even greater weight, given how closely Mr. Xi has tied Taiwan to his political legacy. He said in July that bringing Taiwan under Beijing’s control was a “historic mission” and an “unshakable commitment” of the Chinese Communist Party. Mr. Xi has been more blunt in the past, warning that those who “play with fire” over Taiwan will get burned.
鑒於習近平將台灣問題與自己政治遺產緊密聯繫在一起，如今任何被視為違背承諾的行為都可能產生更為重大的影響。他在7月表示，將台灣納入北京的控制是中國共產黨「矢志不渝」的「歷史任務」。習近平還曾更加直白地警告，那些在台灣問題上「玩火」的人必將自焚。
Future U.S. arms sales are inevitable. If they come after a prolonged pause, Mr. Xi could look as though he misread America’s intentions, raising pressure for him to respond forcefully. Mr. Xi has far more powerful tools at his disposal than Chinese leaders did in the 1990s, including the world’s largest navy by ship count, thousands of military aircraft, a vast missile arsenal and formidable space, electronic and cyberwarfare capabilities.
未來美國的軍售是不可避免的。如果這些軍售是在長期的暫停之後重啟，習近平可能會顯得像誤判了美國的意圖，從而面臨更大的壓力，迫使他採取強硬回應。與20世紀90年代的中國領導人相比，習近平擁有更強大的工具，包括艦艇數量位居世界第一的海軍、數千架軍用飛機、龐大的導彈武庫，以及強大的太空、電子和網路戰能力。
So far, Beijing has not reciprocated Mr. Trump’s restraint over the arms deal.
到目前為止，北京並沒有對川普在軍售問題上的剋制做出對等的回應。
廣告
Since Mr. Trump returned to office, China has continued its military intimidation of Taiwan, conducting air and naval patrols near the island and holding exercises that rehearse attacks. Beijing also expanded Coast Guard patrols to Taiwan’s Pacific Ocean side this year, signaling that it could sever vital supply routes in a blockade.
自川普重返白宮以來，中國繼續對台灣進行軍事威嚇，在該島附近進行海空巡邏，並舉行模擬攻擊的演習。今年，北京還將海警巡邏擴展到了台灣的太平洋一側，此舉表明其可能通過封鎖切斷台灣的關鍵補給線。
A confrontation over Taiwan could escalate into a sustained effort to choke off the island’s energy imports, initiate cyberattacks on critical infrastructure or create broader economic coercion. Military operations could also disrupt shipping through the Taiwan Strait, which carried more than $2.4 trillion in goods in 2024, endangering semiconductor production and global supply chains.
在台灣問題上的對抗可能會升級為一場持續的行動，包括切斷該島的能源進口、對關鍵基礎設施發起網路攻擊或製造更廣泛的經濟脅迫。軍事行動還可能擾亂通過台灣海峽的航運——2024年通過該海峽的貨物價值超過2.4萬億美元，從而危及半導體生產和全球供應鏈。
Mr. Trump should move quickly to separate Taiwan arms sales from trade talks with China and approve a smaller yet meaningful package to make clear that the United States remains committed to Taiwan’s defense before Beijing comes to see the pause as evidence of a new understanding.
川普應迅速採取行動，將對台軍售與對華貿易談判分開，並批准一項規模較小但有意義的計劃，以明確表明美國仍然致力於台灣的防禦，以免北京將這種暫停視為達成新共識的證據。
Ambiguity has served the United States and China well so far, but some things should not be left open to interpretation.
到目前為止，模糊策略對美中兩國都很有效，但有些事情不應留有解讀的空間。

Craig Singleton是智庫保衛民主基金會中國項目的高級主任。此前他曾在美國政府擔任國家安全職務十餘年，主要負責東亞事務。
