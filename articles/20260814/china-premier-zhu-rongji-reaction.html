敢於說真話的鐵腕總理

How China Remembers Zhu Rongji, the Premier Who Spoke His Mind

儲百亮 • 2026-08-14
Zhu Rongji, China’s former premier, is often remembered for opening the country to the global economy. But among Chinese people, his death has also prompted nostalgia for his blunt words and pugnacious outbursts, a contrast with the political rigidity of the present.
中國前總理朱鎔基常因推動國家走向全球經濟而被人們銘記。但在中國民眾之間，他的逝世也引發了人們對其直言不諱與敢作敢為風格的懷念，這與當下政治環境的拘謹形成了鮮明對比。
Xi Jinping, China’s autocratic leader, exercises tight control over political expression, and the public mourning of leaders is meticulously scripted. Major state-run news outlets carried the Communist Party’s official obituary of Mr. Zhu, who died aged 97 on Wednesday. Mr. Xi and other leaders are expected to attend a formal funeral and memorial service in the coming days, though no details have been announced.
中國最高領導人習近平對政治表達實施著嚴格掌控，對領導人的公開悼念活動也有著縝密的規範流程。朱鎔基於週三逝世，享年97歲，官方主要媒體刊發了中國共產黨的官方訃告。預計習近平及其他領導人將在未來幾天出席正式的遺體告別及悼念活動，不過具體細節尚未公布。
The party’s obituary in the People’s Daily emphasized how Mr. Zhu pulled back some state controls of the economy and propelled China’s entry into global commerce from 1993 to 2003, first as vice premier and then as premier, the equivalent of a prime minister. It also said Mr. Zhu “dared to speak the truth and did not fear offending others.”
中共在《人民日報》發表的訃告中重點強調了朱鎔基在1993年至2003年期間——先是擔任副總理，後擔任總理——如何部分放開國家對經濟的控制，並推動中國融入全球貿易體系。訃告還指出，朱鎔基「敢於說真話，不怕得罪人」。
Some in China celebrated Mr. Zhu’s legacy and his frankness in bolder terms.
在中國，一些人以更為大膽的措辭讚頌朱鎔基留下的政治遺產及其直率的性格。
廣告
The deaths of senior leaders have become occasions for people to point indirectly to what they see as a more dynamic and hopeful past. On Chinese social media sites, many recalled with affection Mr. Zhu’s sometimes ebullient, sometimes combative style.
高層領導人的逝世已被人們當做一個契機，來懷念過去那個更具活力與希望的時代。在中國社群媒體平台上，許多人深情地回憶起朱鎔基時而豪爽洒脫、時而針鋒相對的個人風格。
“They faced up to the opportunities of reform, they managed the pace of reform, they were the pillars of China’s economic reforms,” Rao Yi, the president of Capital Medical University in Beijing wrote in a tribute on Thursday to Mr. Zhu’s generation on WeChat, a popular Chinese social media platform. Dr. Rao did not mention Mr. Zhu by name.
「他們面臨改革的機遇，他們把握改革的步伐，他們是中國經濟改革的中堅，」北京首都醫科大學校長饒毅週四在微信上撰文悼念朱鎔基那一代人時這樣寫道。他在文中並未直接提及朱鎔基的名字。
Online commenters remembered Mr. Zhu’s gladiatorial news conferences and peppery phrases, a pointed reminder that these days, even senior Chinese officials often do little more than echo the words of Mr. Xi.
許多網友回憶起朱鎔基當年那一場場從容應對的記者招待會以及字字珠璣的表達，這耐人尋味地提醒著人們：現如今，即便是高級官員也往往只是在複述最高領導人的話語。
Some cited Mr. Zhu’s warning against corrupt officials, where he suggested the lengths he would go to attack the problem. “I’ve prepared 100 coffins,” Mr. Zhu said at a meeting in 1998. “Nine-nine of them are for corrupt officials, and I’m leaving one for myself.”
一些人引用了朱鎔基對腐敗官員發出的警告，這段話展現了他痛下決心解決該問題的鐵腕態度。「我準備了100口棺材，99口留給貪官，一口留給我自己，」朱鎔基在1998年的一次會議上說。
Others quoted Mr. Zhu’s description of poorly built flood barriers that broke under a deluge on the Yangtze River in 1998. “Tofu dregs construction, turtle eggs construction,” he said. (“Turtle eggs” has a more vulgar implication in Chinese than it does in English.)
還有人引用了朱鎔基對1998年長江特大洪水期間對潰決的劣質防洪工程的斥責。他怒罵其為「豆腐渣工程、王八蛋工程」。
The official memorializing of Mr. Zhu will likely follow the party’s scripted formula. The party’s obituary was roughly the same length and given similar prominence as the one for the former premier Li Keqiang in 2023, and for the another former premier, Li Peng, in 2019.
對朱鎔基的官方悼念活動可能將遵循既定的規格。黨的訃告在篇幅和版面位置上與2023年去世的前總理李克強以及2019年去世的前總理李鵬基本相當。
The deaths of former leaders can be politically sensitive in China. When Li Keqiang died unexpectedly in 2023 at age 68, there was widespread mourning online and in his hometown in Anhui Province. Experts saw the outpouring as an expression of public sympathy for an official who had been pushed aside by Mr. Xi.
在中國，前領導人的逝世可能具有政治敏感性。2023年李克強突然去世時，網路上以及他的家鄉安徽省出現了大規模的民間悼念。在專家看來，這種情感的傾瀉是公眾對這位政治生涯受挫官員的同情表達。
廣告
A similar, highly visible mourning seems less likely for Mr. Zhu. He belonged to an older political generation, and had long receded from public life. Controls on expression in China have also tightened since 2023.
對朱鎔基而言，出現類似且高度矚目的公開悼念活動的可能性較低。他屬於更早一代的政治人物，且早已淡出公眾視野。此外，自2023年以來，對言論的管控也進一步收緊。
Some tributes to Mr. Zhu in China were tempered with recognition that he was not an unabashed economic liberalizer, nor did his promises always come to fruition, including his vows to clean out corruption and ease burdens on farmers.
在中國對朱鎔基的讚譽中也夾雜著理性的認知：人們意識到他並非一位毫無保留的經濟自由化推動者，他的承諾——無論是清除腐敗還是減輕農民負擔——也並不總能化為現實。
“Zhu Rongji loudly declared that ‘the burden on farmers is too heavy,’ but whether through emotional appeals or rational arguments, the problem was never really solved,” said one essay that was shared on WeChat about Mr. Zhu’s legacy as premier.
「朱鎔基曾大聲疾呼『農民負擔太重』，然而不管是動之以情還是曉之以理，這一問題都沒能得到更好的解決，直到他卸任，『農民負擔太重』依然為心頭之痛，」在微信上流傳的一篇關於朱鎔基總理任期遺產的文章中這樣寫道。
Mr. Zhu focused on courting global business and not enough on the countryside, said Yasheng Huang, a professor at M.I.T. who has written extensively about China’s economic transformation.
在中國經濟轉型方面有大量著述的麻省理工學院教授黃亞生表示，朱鎔基將精力集中在爭取全球企業上，對廣大農村地區的關注有所不足。
He squeezed financial flows for rural factories that had mushroomed in the previous decade, forcing many of them to go out of business and taking the momentum out of rural development, Professor Huang said.
他說，朱鎔基限制了此前十年如雨後春筍般湧現的鄉鎮企業的資金流，迫使其中許多企業倒閉，從而扼殺了農村發展的勢頭。
“We need to incorporate into our assessment what happened in rural China, which after all was 70 to 80 percent of the Chinese population,” Professor Huang wrote in emailed comments. “Zhu was a reformer but he was one in a way far more complex than often portrayed.”
「我們在進行評估時，必須把中國農村發生的事情納入考量，畢竟農村人口當時佔到了中國總人口的70%到80%，」黃亞生通過電子郵件評論道。「朱鎔基是一位改革者，但他實現改革的方式比人們通常塑造的形象要複雜得多。」
廣告
Mr. Zhu is widely remembered abroad for negotiating China’s entry into the World Trade Organization in 2001. But in China he is also remembered for giving the central government in Beijing much greater control over revenues. That shift paved the way for the big-spending projects of later leaders, including Mr. Xi. Local governments, on the other hand, are often cash starved.
在國外，朱鎔基最被人銘記的事蹟是他在2001年就中國加入世界貿易組織展開談判。但在中國國內，人們同樣記得他讓中央政府對財政收入有了更大的掌控權。這一轉變為主政者後來的大規模投資項目鋪平了道路。然而另一方面，地方政府卻常常陷於財政拮据的困境。
“Zhu Rongji was a leader who reflected the paradoxes of reform-era China: the pursuit of rapid economic growth paired with enduring political control,” said Julian Gewirtz, a China historian at Columbia University who is writing a book on U.S.-China relations after the Cold War. “Zhu himself bristled at Western efforts to fit him into familiar liberalizing archetypes.”
「朱鎔基是一位體現了改革開放時期中國矛盾性的領導人：對經濟快速增長的追求與持久的政治控制並行不悖，」哥倫比亞大學研究冷戰後中美關係的歷史學家朱利安·格維茲表示。「朱本人很反感西方試圖將他套入熟悉的『自由化』典型模板。」

Ruoxin Zhang自北京、Amy Chang Chien自台北對本文有研究和報導貢獻。

儲百亮(Chris Buckley)是《紐約時報》首席中國記者，自台北報導中國和台灣問題，重點關注政治、社會變革以及安全和軍事問題。
