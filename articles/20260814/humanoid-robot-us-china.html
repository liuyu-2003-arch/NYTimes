美國想在人形機器人賽道與中國競爭，這並非易事

America Wants to Make Its Own Humanoid Robots. That Won’t Be Easy.

MEAGHAN TOBIN • 2026-08-14
Teddy Haggerty knew exactly what he was up against when he decided to build humanoid robots in the United States.
決定在美國製造人形機器人時，泰迪·哈格蒂非常清楚自己面臨的是什麼。
Since 2022, Mr. Haggerty had been the main North American distributor for Unitree Robotics, China’s leading humanoid robot manufacturer. That role has given him a close-up view of an industry Chinese companies command, producing robots more cheaply and at a scale that American manufacturers have yet to approach.
自2022年以來，哈格蒂一直是中國領先的人形機器人製造商宇樹科技在北美的主要分銷商。這一角色讓他近距離觀察到一個由中國企業主導的行業——中國生產機器人的生產成本更低、規模更大，美國製造商至今難以企及。
“In China, these things are just walking around the streets,” he said. “They have so much more experience than we do.”
「在中國，這些東西已經在街上走了，」他說，「他們的經驗比我們豐富得多。」
But in a warehouse in a leafy industrial park on suburban Long Island, Mr. Haggerty, 30, is trying to pull off a seemingly quixotic task — to produce humanlike robots in the United States.
但在長島郊區一個綠樹成蔭的工業園區內的倉庫裡，30歲的哈格蒂正試圖完成一項看似不切實際的任務——在美國生產類人機器人。
廣告
His new company, Robo Inc., is the latest venture for a serial entrepreneur who has spent the previous decade chasing the next big thing, from drone photography and Bitcoin mining to importing protective gear during the Covid-19 pandemic. Building robots, however, means taking on an industry China has designated a strategic priority. That is a challenge of a different magnitude.
他的新公司Robo Inc.是這位連續創業者的最新項目。過去十年間，他一直在追逐下一個風口，從無人機攝影、比特幣挖礦，到新冠疫情期間進口防護裝備。然而，製造機器人意味著要挑戰一個中國已列為戰略優先的產業。這完全是另一種量級的挑戰。
Mr. Haggerty plans to procure components from all over the world and make some in-house. But even as he sets out to establish a robotics beachhead in the United States, he has confronted a stark realization. China has near-total dominance of the supply chain. So building a robot entirely free of Chinese parts was not practical.
哈格蒂計劃從全球採購零部件，並自行生產一部分。但即使著手在美國建立了機器人產業的灘頭陣地，他也面臨一個殘酷的現實：中國在供應鏈上幾乎佔據完全主導地位。因此，製造一台完全不含中國零部件的機器人並不現實。
Companies that went to great lengths to cut China out of their supply chains were in the end just making a more expensive product, he said. It was far more cost-effective to import certain parts, like batteries and the aluminum skeleton that makes a robot’s body.
他說，那些費盡心力將中國排除在供應鏈之外的公司最終只是生產出更昂貴的產品。進口某些部件（如電池和構成機器人身體的鋁製骨架）要經濟實惠得多。
“There are some pieces that are already dialed in — what are you doing changing them?” he said. His office, in Plainview, shares space with a workshop littered with spare robot heads and hands, where a small team of engineers builds custom parts to adapt humanoid and four-legged robots for use in warehouse and health care work.
「有些部件已經是成熟的了，你何必去改動它們呢？」他說。他在普萊恩維尤的辦公室位於一個車間裡，那裡散落著備用的機器人頭部和手部，一小隊工程師正在那裡製造定製部件，以使雙足和四足機器人適應倉庫和醫療保健領域的工作。
In some ways, Mr. Haggerty is caught between China and Washington, where officials have called for more robots to be made in the United States. He believes the result will be a more fragmented global market for humanoids: Chinese robot companies will continue to sell in their home market, while companies like his will compete for customers in the United States.
在某種程度上，哈格蒂夾在中國和華盛頓之間，美國官方呼籲更多機器人要在美國本土製造。他認為，結果將是全球人形機器人市場更加分裂：中國機器人公司將繼續在本土市場銷售，而像他這樣的公司將爭取美國客戶。
The United States and China are already vying for supremacy in artificial intelligence and semiconductors. Now robots that look and move like humans are increasingly emerging as another front in that competition. Companies in both countries increasingly see robots as the physical embodiment and next evolutionary step of A.I.
美國和中國已經在人工智慧和半導體領域展開霸主之爭。如今，外形和動作都像人類的機器人正日益成為這一競爭的又一戰場。兩國企業日益將機器人視為人工智慧的實體呈現和下一個進化步驟。
The geopolitical tensions came into sharper focus last month when the Federal Communications Commission announced a ban on the import of new models of foreign-made humanoid robots into the United States, citing national security concerns. The measure did not explicitly target China, but most new humanoid robots are made by Chinese companies.
上個月，地緣政治緊張局勢更加凸顯。美國聯邦通信委員會宣布以國家安全為由，禁止新型外國製造人形機器人進口到美國。該措施並未明確針對中國，但大多數新型人形機器人由中國公司製造。
This technology is still in the early stages of development, but supporters envision a future when humanoid robots become commonplace: cleaning homes, checking vitals in hospitals and working hazardous jobs in mines and chemical plants. But such activities would also give robots access to vast amounts of data about where and how people lived. For the United States government, this is potentially too sensitive for machines not made in America.
這項技術仍處於發展初期，但其支持者展望的未來人形機器人將變得司空見慣：打掃家庭、在醫院檢查生命體征，以及在礦山和化工廠從事危險工作。但這類活動也會讓機器人獲取大量關於人們生活地點和方式的數據。對美國而言，這可能過於敏感，不宜由非美國製造的機器來處理。
Right now, though, very few are.
但目前，美國製造的機器人少之又少。
廣告
China’s robotics industry was built on billions of dollars in state funding and grew out of the country’s world-beating electric vehicle industry. The country’s leading humanoid robotics start-up, Unitree, is preparing to go public in Shanghai at a valuation of $9 billion.
中國的機器人產業建立在數十億美元的政府資金之上，並脫胎於該國領先世界的電動汽車產業。中國領先的人形機器人初創公司宇樹科技正籌備在上海上市，估值達90億美元。
Fledgling U.S. robot makers will need substantial financial and policy support to compete, said Sunny Cheung, a fellow at the Jamestown Foundation, a think tank that studies Chinese government influence. “Merely banning Chinese robots is just the first step,” he said.
研究中國政府影響力的智庫詹姆斯敦基金會研究員張昆陽(Sunny Cheung)表示，羽翼未豐的美國機器人製造商需要大量的財政和政策支持才能競爭。「單純地禁止中國機器人只是第一步，」他說。
In the United States, humanoid robotics is still in its infancy. Analysts estimate that the top humanoid companies made just a few hundred robots last year. Most aren’t available for purchase by the average consumer, but instead are part of test projects with other companies in warehouses and factories. By comparison, Unitree and another Chinese company, Agibot, together made about 10,000.
在美國，人形機器人產業仍處於萌芽階段。分析師估計，去年頂尖人形機器人公司僅生產了幾百台機器人。大多數並不面向普通消費者銷售，而是用於其他公司在倉庫和工廠的測試項目。相比之下，宇樹科技和另一家中國公司智元機器人合計製造了約1萬台。
Even Elon Musk’s Optimus line of humanoids, which he recently predicted would be “the biggest product ever,” relies heavily on components made in China.
即使是伊隆·馬斯克的Optimus人形機器人系列——他近期預測這將是「有史以來最重大的產品」——也嚴重依賴中國製造的零部件。
And makers of non-humanoid robots, like iRobot, the American company behind the Roomba, have struggled to compete with Chinese rivals. The Chinese supplier of iRobot took control when the company filed for bankruptcy in December.
Roomba的製造商、美國公司iRobot這樣的非人形機器人製造商也難以與中國競爭對手抗衡。去年12月iRobot申請破產時，其中國供應商接管了該公司。
And Chinese humanoids are far cheaper and more widely available than their American counterparts. Unitree’s flagship humanoid retails for under $14,000.
中國的人形機器人比美國同類產品便宜得多，也更容易獲得。宇樹科技的旗艦人形機器人零售價不到1.4萬美元。
廣告
Mr. Haggerty said that after he appeared at a trade show in Las Vegas alongside a Unitree robot last year, he was flooded with inquiries from companies like Amazon and OpenAI that wanted to try out humanoids. They wanted more robots than he could import.
哈格蒂說，去年他在拉斯維加斯的一個展會上與一台宇樹機器人一同亮相後，亞馬遜和OpenAI等公司紛紛向他諮詢，希望試用其機器人。這些公司的機器人需求超出了他的進口能力。
Start-up founders and investors say it is virtually impossible to set up factories in the United States that could compete with the output of China’s supply chain on cost. It is more expensive to pay American workers. And raw materials, from the lithium in the batteries to the aluminum molded into the skeleton, have to be obtained from China anyway.
初創公司創始人和投資者表示，在美國建廠幾乎不可能在成本上與中國供應鏈的產量競爭。美國工人的工資更高。而且，從電池中的鋰到模壓成骨架的鋁，原材料無論如何都須從中國獲取。
China’s dominance over the supply chain for robots that act like humans is closely tied to its electric vehicle industry. China became the largest E.V. exporter by focusing on producing virtually every component domestically, from screws to lithium-ion batteries. Now, many of the same companies that manufacture parts for electric vehicles supply robot makers, too.
中國在類人機器人供應鏈上的主導地位與其電動汽車產業密切相關。中國之所以能成為全球最大的電動汽車出口國，是因為它幾乎實現了所有零部件的國產化，從螺絲到鋰離子電池無一例外。如今，許多為電動汽車製造零部件的同一批公司也為機器人製造商供貨。
“If you are in China and you need a component, you can get it immediately — where in the United States you might get it in a couple of days,” said Brad Porter, the chief executive of Cobot, a robotics company in Santa Clara, Calif.
「如果你在中國，需要某個部件，你可以立刻拿到——而在美國，你可能要等上幾天，」加州聖克拉拉機器人公司Cobot的首席執行官布拉德·波特說。
Mr. Porter previously spent more than a decade at Amazon, where he led the company’s effort to use robots. He pointed to Amazon’s factory in Massachusetts where it makes equipment for its warehouses as an example of successful American robot making. But these are not humanoids. They are robotic devices that convey large loads across factory floors. Mr. Porter said humanoid robots were not the right solution in most situations for Amazon.
波特此前在亞馬遜工作了十多年，領導了該公司的機器人應用項目。他提到亞馬遜在麻薩諸塞州的工廠，稱這個為公司倉庫製造設備的廠是美國製造機器人的成功典範。但那些不是人形機器人，而是在工廠車間裡搬運重物的機器人裝置。波特說，對亞馬遜而言，在大多數情況下人形機器人並非合適的解決方案。
“There was no use case at Amazon where legs conferred any advantage,” he said.
「在亞馬遜，沒有任何應用場景證明腿部具有什麼優勢，」他說。
The actual usefulness of humanoids remains a live debate. Even robotics company executives concede that people are still far more efficient workers than the robots, which struggle to make complex decisions in rapidly changing settings.
人形機器人的實際效用仍存爭議。即便是機器人公司的高管也承認，人類仍是遠比機器人更高效的工人，機器人在瞬息萬變的環境中難以做出複雜決策。
While Chinese companies have a lead on producing humanoid robots, none have demonstrated a clear use case for the technology.
儘管中國企業在生產人形機器人方面處於領先地位，但尚無一家企業展示出該技術的明確應用場景。
廣告
Chinese robots, including Unitree’s, have drawn international attention for coordinated dancing displays on Chinese television and at the Super Bowl. But these robots are following preprogrammed scripts. Creating robots capable of complex decision-making in response to the changing world remains a challenge.
包括宇樹的機器人在內，中國的機器人曾因在中國電視節目和超級盃上的協調舞蹈表演而吸引國際關注。但這些機器人執行的是預編程腳本。製造能夠根據變化的環境做出複雜決策的機器人仍是挑戰。
More broadly, China has already established another key lead in robotics: using robots to automate manufacturing. In 2024, according to a report by the International Federation of Robotics, a nonprofit trade group, more than two million robots were working in Chinese factories, and another 300,000 were installed — more than in the rest of the world combined.
從更大的層面來說，中國在機器人領域還建立了另一個關鍵優勢：利用機器人實現製造自動化。根據非營利行業組織國際機器人聯合會的報告，2024年，超過200萬台機器人在中國工廠工作，而新安裝機器人達到30萬台，超過了世界其他地區的總和。
