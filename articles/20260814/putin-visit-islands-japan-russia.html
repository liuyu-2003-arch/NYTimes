普丁首次訪問日俄爭議島嶼，激怒東京

Putin Visits Islands Seized From Japan in World War II, Angering Tokyo

赫海威, PAUL SONNE • 2026-08-14
President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia on Thursday visited islands seized from Japan at the end of World War II for the first time, prompting an angry rebuke from Tokyo.
俄羅斯總統弗拉基米爾·V·普丁週四首次訪問了二戰結束時從日本奪取的島嶼，此舉引發東京方面的憤怒譴責。
Mr. Putin used the visit to reassert Russia’s territorial claims and to protest Japan’s ongoing sanctions against Russia over the invasion of Ukraine. A day before the visit, he said that Russia’s claim to the islands was a “reality” that had been “enshrined in international documents.”
普丁藉此次訪問重申俄羅斯的領土主張，並抗議日本因俄烏戰爭而對俄羅斯持續實施的制裁。訪問前一天，他表示俄羅斯對這些島嶼的主權是「客觀事實」，且已被「載入國際文件」。
“We are not threatening Japan. We have absolutely no claims against it,” Mr. Putin said. “On the contrary, Japan has territorial claims against our country.”
「我們沒有威脅日本。我們對日本沒有任何主張，」普丁說，「相反，是日本對我國有領土主張。」
The visit, which came as Russia held large-scale military exercises in the neighboring seas, was the latest flare-up in tensions between the two countries. Russia has been putting pressure on Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi, a defense hawk and critic of Moscow who was elected last year. She has pushed forward with Japan’s largest postwar defense buildup and sought closer ties with the United States.
此次訪問正值俄羅斯在鄰近海域舉行大規模軍事演習之際，是兩國緊張關係的最新一次爆發。俄羅斯一直在對去年當選的首相高市早苗施加壓力。高市在防務上採取鷹派姿態，且對莫斯科多有批評。她推動了日本戰後最大規模的防衛建設，並尋求與美國加強關係。
廣告
Ms. Takaichi criticized the visit on Thursday, saying Japan had repeatedly warned Russian leaders to stay away from the islands, which are known in Russia as the southern Kuril Islands and in Japan as the Northern Territories. She described them as “an inherent part of Japan’s territory, both historically and under international law.”
高市早苗週四批評了此次訪問，稱日本曾多次警告俄羅斯領導人遠離這些島嶼——俄羅斯稱南千島群島，日本稱北方四島。她形容這些島嶼「無論在歷史上還是根據國際法，都是日本固有領土的一部分」。
She told reporters that Mr. Putin’s visit “hurts the feelings of the Japanese people and is absolutely unacceptable.” Japan’s foreign ministry said on Thursday that it had summoned Russia’s ambassador to protest the visit.
她告訴記者，普丁的訪問「傷害了日本人民的感情，絕對不可接受」。日本外務省週四表示，已召見俄羅斯大使以示抗議。
Japan traces its claims to the 17th century, but the Russians generally date their sovereignty to the waning days of World War II, when Soviet troops occupied the islands after Japan’s surrender in 1945. Japan and Russia never signed a formal peace treaty after the war.
日本將其主權主張追溯至17世紀，但俄羅斯人通常將主權追溯至二戰末期，即1945年日本投降後蘇聯軍隊佔領這些島嶼之時。戰後日本與俄羅斯從未簽署正式和平條約。
The disputed islands are part of an archipelago stretching from the southern tip of the Kamchatka Peninsula in eastern Russia to Hokkaido in the northernmost part of Japan.Mr. Putin visited an island that is called Iturup in Russian and Etorofu in Japanese. He toured a fish-processing complex and ate caviar. He also visited a hospital and a school, the Kremlin said in a statement, releasing photos of the Russian leader’s stops.
爭議島嶼是自俄羅斯東部堪察加半島南端延伸至日本最北端北海道的群島的一部分。普丁訪問了一個在俄語中稱為伊圖魯普島、在日語中稱為擇捉島的島嶼。他參觀了一家魚類加工綜合設施並品嘗了魚子醬。克里姆林宮在一份聲明中表示，他還參觀了一家醫院和一所學校，並發布了這位俄羅斯領導人到訪地點的照片。
The governor of the Sakhalin region, which includes the islands, Valery Limarenko, accompanied Mr. Putin on the visit. Under a bright sun, Mr. Putin met residents of the region and hugged two young children, in footage posted online by a state news reporter who follows the Russian leader.
這些島嶼所屬的薩哈林州州長瓦列裡·利馬連科陪同普丁訪問。根據一名跟隨報導這位俄羅斯領導人的官方新聞記者發布在網上的影片，在明媚陽光下，普丁與當地居民見面，並擁抱了兩名年幼兒童。
“Look at this great weather, it’s like a resort!” Mr. Putin said. One woman responded that such weather is very rare, noting that it is usually windy and foggy. “The weather is smiling on you,” she said.
「看看這好天氣，簡直像度假勝地！」普丁說。一名女性回應稱這種天氣非常罕見，通常多風多霧。「天氣在對你微笑呢，」她說。
廣告
Mr. Putin’s visit is part of a broader tour across Siberia and the Far East that he has been making ahead of the Russian parliamentary election next month.
普丁的此次訪問是他在下月俄羅斯議會選舉前進行的西伯利亞及遠東地區視察行程的一部分。
On Wednesday, during a visit to Russia’s Pacific Fleet in Yuzhno-Sakhalinsk, Mr. Putin boarded the Varyag guided missile cruiser. He said Japan had placed “unprovoked sanctions” on his country over the conflict in Ukraine and singled out Russia as an enemy threat.
週三，在視察位於南薩哈林斯克的俄羅斯太平洋艦隊期間，普丁登上了「瓦良格」號導彈巡洋艦。他表示，日本因烏克蘭衝突而對俄羅斯實施了「無端制裁」並將俄羅斯列為敵方威脅。
The trip marks Mr. Putin’s first visit to the region claimed by Japan in his quarter century rule. President Dmitry A. Medvedev visited one of the islands in 2010, becoming the first Russian or Soviet leader to do so. Japan denounced the visit and summoned the Russian ambassador to Japan in protest.
此行是普丁在25年執政期間首次訪問日本聲稱擁有主權的這一地區。2010年，時任俄羅斯總統德米特里·A·梅德韋傑夫訪問了其中一座島嶼，成為首位到訪的俄羅斯或蘇聯領導人。日本當時譴責了此次訪問，並召見俄羅斯駐日大使以示抗議。
Previous Japanese leaders, including former Prime Minister Shinzo Abe, a mentor to Ms. Takaichi, made efforts to win back some of the territory.
包括高市早苗的導師、前首相安倍晉三在內，以往的日本領導人曾努力爭取收回部分領土。
But in 2019, hopes of a deal were dashed by Mr. Putin. He said that any agreement must have support from the public, which in Russia is strongly opposed to returning any islands to Japan.
但2019年，達成協議的希望被普丁打破。他表示任何協議都必須獲得公眾支持，而俄羅斯公眾強烈反對將任何島嶼歸還日本。
Japan has made small efforts to improve relations with Russia in recent months, including sending a delegation of government officials to Moscow for trade talks and organizing cultural exchange trips. But it has maintained economic and trade sanctions against Moscow over the Ukraine war.
近幾個月來，日本為改善對俄關係做出了一些小努力，包括派遣政府官員代表團前往莫斯科進行貿易談判，並組織文化交流活動。但日本仍然維持因烏克蘭戰爭對莫斯科實施的經濟和貿易制裁。
廣告
A recent investigation by The New York Times found that Russian military intelligence has been using Japan as a hub to source components for its war effort, exploiting weak Japanese espionage laws and high-tech industry.
《紐約時報》近期的一項調查發現，俄羅斯軍事情報部門利用了日本薄弱的情報法律和發達的高科技產業，將日本作為採購戰爭物資部件的樞紐。
Michito Tsuruoka, a professor at Keio University in Tokyo, said that Mr. Putin’s visit suggested that “there is no intention — particularly on the Russian side — to improve relations with Japan.”
東京慶應義塾大學教授鶴岡路人表示，普丁的訪問表明「沒有改善俄日關係的意圖——尤其是俄羅斯方面」。
“Russia’s narrative is that the deterioration of Japan-Russia relations is Japan’s fault,” he said. “From Japan’s perspective, of course, that is outrageous and untrue; the blame lies with Russia. It is essentially a case of ‘he said, she said,’ and Japan has no choice but to stand firm.”
「俄方的說法是，日俄關係惡化是日本的過錯，」他說，「從日本的角度來看，這當然荒謬且不實，責任在俄羅斯。這基本上就是各執一詞，日本別無選擇，只能堅定立場。」
