維生素、魚油、蛋白粉：你需要這些熱門保健品嗎？

Do You Need These Popular Supplements?

ALICE CALLAHAN • 2026-08-13
Marily Oppezzo, a dietitian and researcher at Stanford, said she was occasionally shocked by the number of supplements her clients take. A “stack” in the morning and another at night, she said — sometimes 20 different types or more.
史丹佛大學的營養師兼研究員瑪麗莉·奧佩佐表示，她有時會對客戶服用的保健品數量感到震驚。她說，他們早晚都要吃「一大堆」——有時多達20種，甚至更多。
In a 2024 survey of more than 3,000 U.S. adults, more than half of the respondents reported using supplements regularly. And Dr. Oppezzo said that many of her clients started taking them after being influenced by podcast hosts or social media.
在2024年針對3000多名美國成年人進行的一項調查中，超過一半的受訪者表示經常服用保健品。奧佩佐說，她的許多客戶是在受到播客主播或社群媒體的影響後開始服用的。
There can be good reasons to use supplements, Dr. Oppezzo said — if you have a nutrient deficiency, for example, or are pregnant.
奧佩佐說，服用保健品有時是有充分理由的，比如缺乏某種營養素，或者正在懷孕。
But many of the supplements people take are unnecessary, she added, and can even be risky. Supplements can contain contaminants or excessive levels of nutrients, or interact with certain over-the-counter or prescription medications, said Dr. JoAnn Manson, a professor of medicine at Harvard Medical School.
但她補充說，人們服用的許多保健品是不必要的，甚至可能有風險。哈佛大學醫學院醫學教授喬安·曼森博士表示，保健品可能含有污染物，或者營養成分含量過高，甚至可能與某些非處方藥或處方藥產生相互作用。
廣告
It’s usually safer and healthier to get important nutrients from your diet, Dr. Manson said. And in many cases, you’re probably already doing so without trying.
曼森說，從飲食中獲取重要營養素通常更安全、更健康。而且在很多情況下，你可能在不知不覺中就已經做到了這一點。
Here are 10 of the most popular dietary supplements that people in the United States take, and what to eat instead to get the same benefits.
以下是美國人最常服用的10種膳食補充劑，以及如何通過飲食來獲得同等益處。
1. Multivitamins
1. 複合維生素
Many people take multivitamins as a “backstop” just in case they don’t get enough vitamins and minerals from their diet, said Wesley McWhorter, a dietitian in Houston.
休士頓的營養師韋斯利·麥克沃特說，許多人將複合維生素視為一種「後備方案」，以防從日常飲食中攝取的維生素和礦物質不足。
And while it’s true that some people may benefit from taking multivitamins — including older adults, those on restrictive diets and those who have trouble absorbing nutrients (like those with celiac disease or Crohn’s disease) — most people can get all of the nutrients they need by following a balanced diet, Dr. Manson said.
曼森表示，雖然有些人（包括老年人、飲食受限者以及乳糜瀉或克榮恩病患者等存在營養吸收障礙的人）服用複合維生素確實可以獲益，但大多數人只要遵循均衡飲食就能獲得所需的所有營養素。
This includes eating plenty of fruits and vegetables, legumes, nuts and seeds, whole grains, dairy products and healthy sources of protein, she said.
她說，這包括多吃水果、蔬菜、豆類、堅果和種子、全穀物、乳製品以及健康的蛋白質來源。
廣告
The more colorful the fruits and vegetables you eat, the wider the range of nutrients you’ll get, said Ethan Balk, a clinical associate professor of nutrition at New York University.
紐約大學營養學臨床副教授伊桑·博爾克說，你吃的水果和蔬菜顏色越豐富，獲取的營養素種類就越廣泛。
2. Magnesium
2. 鎂
Magnesium supplements have been touted for insomnia, migraine headaches, depression, constipation and more.
鎂補充劑一直被宣傳為可以緩解失眠、偏頭痛、抑鬱、便秘等癥狀。
There is some, albeit limited, evidence to back up some of these claims, experts say. But it’s worth seeing if you can get the same benefits from eating magnesium-rich foods first, said Abeer Bader, a dietitian at Massachusetts General Hospital.
專家表示，雖然證據有限，但確實有一些證據支持其中部分說法。麻薩諸塞州綜合醫院的營養師阿比爾·巴德建議，不妨先嘗試食用富含鎂的食物來獲得同樣的益處。
About half of adults in the United States don’t consume the recommended 310 to 420 milligrams of magnesium per day.
大約一半的美國成年人未能達到每天310至420毫克的推薦鎂攝入量。
Try adding an ounce of pumpkin seeds (156 milligrams) or an ounce of almonds (77 milligrams) to yogurt or oatmeal. Or incorporate a half-cup of cooked spinach (98 milligrams), black beans (60 milligrams) or quinoa (55 milligrams) into soups or stews.
不妨在酸奶或燕麥片中加入一盎司南瓜籽（156毫克）或一盎司杏仁（77毫克）。或者在湯或燉菜中加入半杯煮熟的菠菜（98毫克）、黑豆（60毫克）或藜麥（55毫克）。
廣告
3. Probiotics and Prebiotics
3. 益生菌和益生元
Probiotic supplements contain certain strains of “good” bacteria that are supposed to support a healthy gut microbiome, and prebiotics are substances that feed those good microbes to help them grow.
益生菌補充劑含有特定「有益」細菌菌株，應該可以維持健康的腸道微生物群；益生元則是為這些有益微生物提供養分、助其生長的物質。
We don’t have good evidence that these supplements help most people, however, Dr. Oppezzo said. So unless your doctor recommends them, she advised skipping them.
但奧佩佐表示，我們並沒有充分的證據表明這些補充劑對大多數人有幫助。因此，除非醫生推薦，否則她建議不要服用。
To support a healthy microbiome (and get essential nutrients in the process), Dr. Oppezzo recommended eating fermented foods like kimchi, sauerkraut, yogurt, kefir and miso. And to ensure your gut microbes are well fed, eat plenty of fiber from plant-based whole foods, Dr. McWhorter said.
為了維持健康的微生物群（並在此過程中獲取必需的營養），奧佩佐推薦食用泡菜、酸菜、酸奶、開菲爾和味噌等發酵食品。麥克沃特說，為了確保腸道微生物得到充足的養分，應從植物性全食中攝取大量膳食纖維。
4. Fish Oil
4. 魚油
Research suggests that people who regularly eat fish — which is rich in omega-3 fatty acids — have better heart health and lower rates of dementia and depression.
研究表明，經常吃富含歐米伽-3脂肪酸魚類的人心臟更健康，患失智症和憂鬱症的機率也更低。
Large clinical trials have generally failed to find the same benefits of taking omega-3 (or fish oil) supplements, though one trial suggested a reduced risk of cardiovascular events for people who did not routinely eat much fish.
大型臨床試驗通常未能發現服用歐米伽-3（或魚油）補充劑具有同樣的益處，但有一項試驗表明，對於平時不常吃魚的人來說，服用該補充劑可降低心血管事件的風險。
If you want to get more omega-3s from your diet, the best sources are fatty fish like salmon, herring, sardines, mackerel and trout, said Whitney Linsenmeyer, an assistant professor of nutrition and dietetics at Saint Louis University. Aim for two servings per week.
聖路易斯大學營養與飲食學助理教授惠特尼·林森邁耶表示，若想從飲食中獲取更多的歐米伽-3，最好的來源是高脂肪魚類，如鮭魚、鯡魚、沙丁魚、鯖魚和鱒魚。建議每週吃兩份。
廣告
Flax seeds, chia seeds, walnuts, canola oil and soybean oil are also good sources, she said.
她說，亞麻籽、奇亞籽、核桃、菜籽油和大豆油也是很好的來源。
5. Vitamin C
5. 維生素C
Many people take vitamin C supplements to strengthen their immune systems or ward off viral infections. And while it’s true that vitamin C plays important roles in the body, including promoting wound healing and protecting against cell damage, the research supporting the benefits of vitamin C supplements is limited.
許多人服用維生素C補充劑以增強免疫系統或抵禦病毒感染。雖然維生素C確實在人體內發揮重要作用，包括促進傷口癒合和防止細胞損傷，但支持維生素C補充劑益處的研究卻很有限。
Federal guidelines recommend at least 75 milligrams of vitamin C per day for most women and at least 90 milligrams per day for most men.
聯邦指南建議大多數女性每天至少攝入75毫克維生素C，大多數男性每天至少攝入90毫克。
It’s easy to get that much from your diet, Dr. Linsenmeyer said.
林森邁耶說，通過日常飲食很容易達到這一攝入量。
Just one orange, two kiwis, a cup of cooked broccoli or a half-cup of cooked red bell pepper supplies the daily amount.
只需一個橙子、兩個獼猴桃、一杯煮熟的西蘭花或半杯煮熟的紅甜椒，就能滿足每日的需求量。
廣告
6. Collagen
6. 膠原蛋白
Collagen supplements claim to reduce wrinkles, minimize joint pain and strengthen hair and nails. But the evidence for such benefits is mixed and limited, said Emma Laing, the director of dietetics at the University of Georgia.
據稱膠原蛋白補充劑能減少皺紋、減輕關節疼痛並強健頭髮和指甲。但喬治亞大學飲食學主任艾瑪·萊恩表示，關於這些益處的證據很不一致，且十分有限。
Instead, support the natural synthesis of collagen in your body by consuming enough protein, vitamin C, copper and zinc — which you can do by following a balanced diet, Dr. Laing said.
萊恩建議，與其如此，不如通過攝入足夠的蛋白質、維生素C、銅和鋅來支持體內膠原蛋白的自然合成——這可以通過均衡飲食來實現。
To protect the collagen you already have, she added, limit sun and alcohol exposure, avoid tobacco, stay hydrated and get regular exercise.
她還說，為了保護體內已有的膠原蛋白，應限制日晒和飲酒，避免吸煙，保持水分，並進行規律的鍛煉。
7. Vitamin B12
7. 維生素B12
If you have a vitamin B12 deficiency or are at risk of developing one, your doctor may suggest that you take a B12 supplement.
如果你缺乏維生素B12或有缺乏風險，醫生可能會建議你服用B12補充劑。
Those at risk include older adults, vegans and vegetarians, people with certain autoimmune conditions, and those who take some medications (like metformin or proton pump inhibitors), said Wren Cahoon, a dietitian at UCHealth University of Colorado Hospital.
科羅拉多大學醫院的營養師倫·卡洪說，風險人群包括老年人、純素食者和素食者、患有某些自身免疫性疾病的人，以及服用某些藥物（如二甲雙胍或質子泵抑制劑）的人。
For most other adults, it’s not hard to get the recommended 2.4 micrograms per day from your diet. Fish, red meat, poultry, eggs and dairy products are all good sources, Ms. Cahoon said. In fact, just one serving of salmon, tuna or beef can provide around the daily amount.
對於大多數其他成年人來說，從飲食中獲取推薦的每天2.4微克並不難。卡洪說，魚、紅肉、家禽、雞蛋和乳製品都是很好的來源。事實上，只要吃一份鮭魚、金槍魚或牛肉，就能大致提供每日所需量。
Many breakfast cereals and plant-based milks are also often fortified with vitamin B12.
許多早餐麥片和植物奶也經常強化添加維生素B12。
8. Protein Powder
8. 蛋白粉
Those trying to lose weight or gain muscle may think that protein powder is a must-have.
正在減肥或增肌的人可能會認為蛋白粉是必需品。
But unless you struggle to consume enough — which older adults or people recovering from hospitalization or surgery often do — most people who follow a balanced diet get enough protein, Dr. McWhorter said.
但麥克沃特說，除非你難以攝入足夠的蛋白質（老年人或住院、手術後恢復期的人通常如此），否則大多數飲食均衡的人都能攝取足夠的蛋白質。
To ensure you hit your daily target, Dr. Linsenmeyer recommended including a protein-rich food at every meal, like Greek yogurt, eggs, legumes or a lean protein source like fish, chicken or turkey.
為了確保達到每日目標，林森邁耶博士建議每頓飯都加入一種富含蛋白質的食物，比如希臘酸奶、雞蛋、豆類，或者是魚、雞肉、火雞肉等低脂蛋白來源。
9. Fiber
9. 膳食纖維
The majority of people in the United States don’t get the recommended 21 to 38 grams of fiber per day. And fiber supplements — sold as pills, powders and gummies — are positioned to fill that gap.
大多數美國人沒有達到每日21到38克的推薦膳食纖維攝入量。以藥丸、粉末和軟糖形式出售的膳食纖維補充劑就是為了填補這一空白。
Getting fiber from whole foods, like legumes, nuts, whole grains, fruits and vegetables, is preferable since they can provide not only fiber but also other healthful nutrients like vitamins, minerals, protein and healthy fats, Ms. Bader said.
巴德表示，從豆類、堅果、全穀物、水果和蔬菜等全天然食品中獲取膳食纖維是更好的選擇，因為它們不僅能提供纖維，還能提供維生素、礦物質、蛋白質和健康脂肪等其他有益健康的營養素。
If you can’t get enough fiber from your diet alone, it’s fine to take a supplement, said Dr. Kyle Staller, a gastroenterologist at Massachusetts General Hospital. He recommends psyllium fiber, a soluble fiber that forms a gel in water. Unlike some other fiber supplements, he said, it can improve common digestive issues like constipation, diarrhea and abdominal pain.
麻薩諸塞州綜合醫院的胃腸病學家凱爾·斯塔勒博士說，如果僅靠飲食無法獲得足夠的纖維，服用補充劑也是可以的。他推薦洋車前子纖維，這是一種遇水能形成凝膠的可溶性纖維。他說，與其他一些膳食纖維補充劑不同，它可以改善便秘、腹瀉和腹痛等常見的消化問題。
10. Greens Powder
10. 綠色果蔬粉
These supplements usually contain a long list of powdered fruits, vegetables, herbs and seeds, and many contain added vitamins, minerals, prebiotics and probiotics. They’re advertised as a convenient way to make up for whatever might be missing in your diet, especially fruits and vegetables.
這些補充劑通常包含各種水果、蔬菜、草本植物和種子的粉末，而且其中許多還添加了維生素、礦物質、益生元和益生菌。廣告宣稱，這是一種便捷方式，可以彌補飲食中可能缺乏的東西，尤其是水果和蔬菜。
But Dr. Oppezzo said that greens powders were little more than “glorified multivitamins.”
但奧佩佐表示，綠色果蔬粉不過是「美化版的複合維生素」。
“They definitely don’t replace the act of eating your actual vegetables,” Dr. McWhorter said.
「它們絕對不能代替真正的蔬菜，」麥克沃特說。
Greens powders can also be expensive, with some running around $100 for a one-month supply. You’re better off spending that money on fresh or frozen fruits and vegetables, Dr. Balk said.
綠色果蔬粉也可能很貴，有些產品一個月的用量大概需要100美元左右。博爾克說，把這筆錢花在新鮮或冷凍的水果和蔬菜上會更划算。
“The real food is going to work just as great, if not better,” he said.
「真正的食物效果也會一樣好，甚至更好，」他說。
