「最強」聖嬰現象正如何改變世界

This Year’s El Niño Is Huge and Already Changing the World

RAYMOND ZHONG, CHICO HARLAN • 2026-08-13
El Niño is the planet’s naturally recurring chaos agent, scrambling weather patterns, spiking global temperatures and typically reaching full strength by December.
厄爾尼諾是地球上一種週期性自然出現的「混亂製造者」，它會打亂天氣模式、推高全球氣溫，而且通常會在12月前後發展到鼎盛狀態。
But this year’s El Niño, which is expected to be record-breaking, is already strong and having big effects, nudging up global temperatures and causing dry spells and crop failures on multiple continents.
然而，今年預計將破紀錄的聖嬰現象已然強勢來臨，並帶來了顯著影響——全球氣溫被進一步推高，多個大洲遭遇旱情和農作物歉收。
Parts of Panama, normally one of the wettest countries on Earth, haven’t had a major storm since early May. Ethiopia’s fertile highlands are enduring one of their driest summer stretches in four decades. And India had its fifth-driest June since the country’s modern rain records began in 1901.
巴拿馬通常是全球最多雨的國家之一，但部分地區自5月初以來未出現一場大型風暴。衣索比亞肥沃的高原地帶正經歷40年來最嚴峻的夏季旱情。印度今年6月的降水量是該國1901年開始現代雨量記錄以來第五低的水平。
“This is a good sign that the El Niño response has really kicked in,” said Chris Funk, director of the Climate Hazards Center at the University of California, Santa Barbara. Dr. Funk termed it an “early onset El Niño.”
「這是一個強烈信號，表明厄爾尼諾的影響已全面啟動，」加州大學聖塔芭芭拉分校氣候危害研究中心主任克里斯·芬克說。他將這種現象稱為「提前到來的厄爾尼諾」。
廣告
These on-the-ground consequences reflect the magnitude of the changes in the Pacific Ocean, where El Niños develop when unusually warm waters build up. These episodes occur every two to seven years and have contributed to planetary swings going back millenniums. But this year’s is on track to be the strongest in nearly 80 years of records.
這些實實在在的後果說明太平洋正在發生巨大變化。厄爾尼諾形成於太平洋異常溫暖的海水積聚。這種現象每兩到七年發生一次，幾千年來一直在攪動全球氣候。而今年的厄爾尼諾很可能成為近80年有記錄以來最強的一次。
Scientists are debating whether human-caused climate change is tilting the odds toward stronger and more frequent El Niño events. The El Niños of the past few decades have been comparably strong. But the variables that combine to create an El Niño are complex, and some experts argue the recent trend could just be a random signal.
科學家們正在爭論，人類導致的氣候變化是否在促使聖嬰現象變得更強烈、更頻繁。過去幾十年的厄爾尼諾相對較強。但形成厄爾尼諾的變量非常複雜，一些專家認為近期的趨勢可能只是隨機波動的信號。
What’s clear is that El Niño’s gyrations are adding to the stronger storms, drier dry spells and fiercer wildfires that global warming is already bringing about. El Niño will magnify those phenomena in some regions and could lift global temperatures to record levels next year, as the heat from the Pacific spreads around the world.
可以確定的是，厄爾尼諾的劇烈擾動正在疊加全球暖化已帶來的更強風暴、更嚴重的乾旱和更兇猛的野火。在部分地區，厄爾尼諾將進一步放大這些現象，並可能隨著太平洋的熱量向全球擴散，將明年的全球氣溫推至創紀錄高位。
“What we’ll see in 2027 is a sneak peak of what the new normal will be in 2035,” said Zeke Hausfather, a climate scientist at Berkeley Earth, a nonprofit research organization. “We’ll see things that have never happened before in our history in terms of temperature, drought conditions, extreme heat.”
「我們在2027年看到的將是對2035年新常態的一次預演，」非營利研究組織柏克萊地球的氣候科學家齊克·豪斯法瑟說。「在氣溫、乾旱程度和極端酷熱方面，我們將目睹前所未有的景象。」
A year of extreme heat
極端高溫之年
El Niño events are measured by looking at sea surface temperatures in a specific rectangular zone of the Pacific Ocean. Temperatures there have soared over the past three months, and forecasts suggest that, by year’s end, they could exceed normal levels by more than they did during the strongest El Niños in modern history, which occurred in 1982-83, 1997-98 and 2015-16.
聖嬰現象的衡量指標是太平洋特定矩形海域的海面溫度。過去三個月，這一區域的溫度急劇攀升，預測顯示，到年底時，其超出正常水平的幅度可能將超越現代史上最強的幾次聖嬰現象——1982至1983年、1997至1998年以及2015至2016年。
廣告
Between October and December, the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration says there’s an 81 percent chance that El Niño conditions will be “very strong,” meaning the three-month average temperature in that zone of the Pacific is 2 degrees Celsius, or 3.6 degrees Fahrenheit, higher than normal. But forecasts show that daily ocean temperatures during that period could spike to more than 3 degrees Celsius above normal.
美國國家海洋和大氣管理局表示，10月至12月間，厄爾尼諾達到"極強"狀態的概率高達81%，這意味著該太平洋區域的三個月平均氣溫較正常水平偏高兩攝氏度。但預測顯示，在這段時期內，太平洋日均海溫可能飆升至比正常水平高出三攝氏度以上。
“Maybe it doesn’t sound that big: three or even four degrees. We see that temperature variation over the course of a day,” said Samantha Stevenson-Karl, an associate professor and climate modeler at the University of California, Santa Barbara. “But the ocean doesn’t change that much. Like, ever.”
「三四度聽起來也許不算什麼，我們在一天之內的氣溫變化就有這麼大，」加州大學聖塔芭芭拉分校副教授、氣候模型專家薩曼莎·史蒂芬森-卡爾說道，「但海洋的溫度變化沒那麼大。真的，從來沒有。」
The extra heat in the waters of the Pacific affects air temperatures, too. For the first months of the year, 2026 was clearly cooler than 2024, the planet’s hottest year on record. But Dr. Hausfather still gives 2026 a 40 percent chance of being Earth’s warmest year on the books, a sign of how the pendulum could be swinging. According to NOAA, last month tied July 2024 as the world’s warmest July since global record-keeping began in 1850.
太平洋水溫的額外升高也在牽動大氣溫度。2026年的前幾個月氣溫明顯低於2024年，後者是有記錄以來地球最熱的一年。但豪斯法瑟博士仍然認為，2026年成為有記錄以來全球最熱年份的概率有40%，這說明全球氣溫的走勢可能正在發生轉變。據美國國家海洋和大氣管理局統計，今年7月與2024年7月並列為自1850年全球氣溫記錄開始以來最熱的7月。
A rise in acute hunger
面臨突發性飢餓的人口增加
El Niño causes big shifts in the winds that blow moist air across the oceans, and the effects on rain-fed agriculture are already emerging in some regions. By the end of the year, El Niño could cause nearly 50 million more people across the globe to experience acute hunger, an increase of roughly a fifth, according to the United Nations World Food Program.
厄爾尼諾會導致海洋上空輸送濕潤空氣的風向發生巨大變化，而這種變化對依賴降雨的農業造成的影響已經開始在一些地區顯現。根據聯合國世界糧食計劃署的數據，到今年年底，聖嬰現象可能導致全球近5000萬人陷入突發性飢餓，增幅約為20%。
At the western end of the tropical Pacific, El Niño has led to a dry summer in parts of Southeast Asia, delaying the sowing of rice and other crops in Thailand, Cambodia, Vietnam and the Philippines. In India, monsoon rains have picked up in some regions in recent weeks, though large areas of the country remain in a rainfall deficit.
在熱帶太平洋西端，聖嬰現象已導致東南亞部分地區出現乾燥的夏季，泰國、柬埔寨、越南和菲律賓的水稻和其他作物的播種因此推遲。在印度，最近幾週一些地區的季風降雨有所增強，但該國大片地區仍然處於降雨不足狀態。
At the other end of the Pacific, El Niño has delayed planting and disrupted crop growth in Central America. Rainfall in Guatemala was as much as 85 percent below average in July, and the stress on crops has been compounded by well-above-normal temperatures.
在太平洋另一端，厄爾尼諾已導致中美洲播種延誤、農作物生長受阻。7月瓜地馬拉的降雨量比平均水平減少了85%，遠高於正常水平的氣溫又進一步加劇了農作物所承受的壓力。
In Panama, the dry stretch has raised concerns about operations in the Panama Canal. During the last El Niño, in 2023-24, a severe rain deficit snarled traffic in the waterway, which raises and lowers ships through the locks using water from a rain-fed lake.
在巴拿馬，持續的乾旱引發了人們對巴拿馬運河運營狀況的擔憂。上一次厄爾尼諾期間（即2023至2024年），嚴重的降雨不足曾嚴重擾亂這條水道的航運。巴拿馬運河通過船閘升降船隻，使用的是一個通過降雨補給的湖泊中的水。
As El Niño has gained strength, the Panama Canal Authority has tightened limits on how heavily loaded ships can be while crossing. Officials haven’t ruled out restricting daily transits, according to a statement last week from a spokesman for the canal authority, Octavio Colindres.
隨著厄爾尼諾不斷增強，巴拿馬運河管理局已經收緊了船隻通過運河時的載重限制。運河管理局發言人奧克塔維奧·科林德雷斯上週在一份聲明中表示，官員們尚未排除給每天的通行數量設限的可能性。
廣告
It’s hard to predict just how much below normal Central America’s rainfall will be in the coming months, said Steven Paton, who monitors the oceans and atmosphere at the Smithsonian Tropical Research Institute in Panama.
位於巴拿馬的史密森學會熱帶研究所負責監測海洋和大氣的史蒂芬·帕頓說，很難預測未來幾個月中美洲的降雨量究竟會比正常水平低多少。
The reason, he said, is that scientists’ computer models are trained on past events. This year’s El Niño is expected to be so much stronger than any episode on record that we can’t be sure how accurately the models will anticipate its effects.
他說，原因在於，科學家用於預測的計算機模型是以過往聖嬰現象為基礎訓練的。而今年的厄爾尼諾預計將遠超有記錄以來任何一次同類事件，模型能否準確預判其影響仍是未知數。
“At the very least, it’s going to be as bad as the worst we’ve seen before, possibly much worse,” Mr. Paton said.
「至少，情況將與我們歷史上見過的最糟情形不相上下，甚至可能更糟，」帕頓說。
In East Africa, El Niño usually brings heavy rain, and even floods and landslides, from November through January. But when the phenomenon appears earlier in the year, it can suppress rainfall in parts of the region, including western Kenya and Ethiopia.
在東非，厄爾尼諾通常會在11月至次年1月帶來強降雨，甚至引發洪水和山體滑坡。但這種現象如果在一年中較早出現，反而可能抑制該地區部分地方的降雨，包括肯亞西部和衣索比亞。
That’s been the dire reality in recent months, said Chris Shitote, a field scientist based in Nairobi, Kenya, for the Famine Early Warning Systems Network, an agency under the U.S. State Department.
美國國務院下屬的饑荒預警系統網路駐肯亞奈洛比負責實地考察的科學家克里斯·希托特說，這正是過去幾個月裡當地面臨的嚴峻現實。
Mr. Shitote and some colleagues recently traveled through Kenya’s western grain-producing counties. The maize harvest was stunningly bad, he said. “It’s something some of us have not seen for the maybe 10 years we’ve been going out.”
希托特與幾名同事近期走訪了肯亞西部的產糧縣。玉米收成之慘令他震驚不已。「這是我們有些人外出考察十年來從未見過的情況。」
廣告
Plants that normally grow to be nine feet tall topped out at just five or six feet, Mr. Shitote said. A typical harvest might yield 20 to 30 bags of corn for each acre of field, growers told him. This year, it looks like it could be just one or two bags per acre.
他說，通常能長到近3米高的植株今年最高也只長到1.5至1.8米。農民告訴他，正常年份每英畝地能收穫20至30袋玉米，而今年，看起來每英畝可能只有一兩袋的收成。
