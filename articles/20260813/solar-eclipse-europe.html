歐洲數十年來首次日全食，民眾歡慶「黑晝」奇觀

Here’s the latest.

JASON HOROWITZ • 2026-08-13
The first total solar eclipse in decades in Europe swept across the continent on Wednesday, turning day to dark across several countries as crowds watched from hilltops, coastlines and other open spaces.
週三，歐洲數十年來首次日全食席捲整個大陸，在多個國家將白晝變為黑夜，人們在山頂、海岸線和其他開闊地帶駐足觀看。
The coincidence of Earth, the moon and the sun falling into perfect alignment — a fleeting celestial event known as totality — created a frenzy across the region. People paid exorbitant hotel rates to be in the inner part, or umbra, of the lunar shadow. Along that path, where the sky was clear, the sun became a black disk ensconced in a hazy corona, surrounded by planets and stars.
地球、月球和太陽恰好排成一線——這一被稱為「全食階段」的轉瞬即逝的天文現象在整個地區引發了一陣狂熱。人們支付高昂的酒店費用，只為身處月球陰影的內核部分（即本影區）。在那些天氣晴朗的地方，太陽變成了一個被朦朧日冕環繞的黑色圓盤，周圍點綴著行星和恆星。
In Spain, normally empty towns filled up. Other people trekked to far-flung North Atlantic destinations. But how much anyone saw depended on their luck with cloud cover.
在西班牙，平日空曠的城鎮擠滿了人。還有一些人則長途跋涉前往遙遠的北大西洋目的地。但每個人能看到多少取決於他們遭遇雲層覆蓋的運氣。
The eclipse moved over Iceland — where birds screamed as if it were dusk, while the air went cold and darkness fell — and Spain, where crowds gathered in Castrillo de la Reina in the north chanted, “Now, now, now!” as the eclipse took its first nibble. In London, people clustered in streets and parks to see a partial eclipse. “Well done, moon,” exclaimed a viewer on Primrose Hill in London.
日食掠過冰島——那裡的鳥兒像黃昏時分一樣鳴叫，空氣變冷，黑暗降臨——接著到達西班牙，在北部卡斯特里略-德拉雷納，當日食開始「初虧」時，聚集的人群高呼「現在，現在，現在！」在倫敦，人們聚集在街道和公園觀看日偏食。「幹得漂亮，月亮，」倫敦櫻草山的一位觀看者喊道。
Eclipses have seized the imagination since people began telling stories. In Homer’s “Odyssey,” the death of Penelope’s suitors is foretold with an omen that may have been a solar eclipse: “The sun has been obliterated from the sky, and an unlucky darkness invades the world.”
自從人類開始講故事以來，日食就一直激發著人們的想像。在荷馬的《奧德賽》中，珀涅羅珀的求婚者之死是由一個可能為日食的預兆預示的：「太陽已從天空中被抹去，一個不祥的黑暗侵入世界。」
That strange moment of totality was Wednesday’s main event. Much light went out of the sky and warmth out of the air. The moon seem to cut into the sun and then, over about an hour, slide across its surface until the brief totality phase — at most, just over two minutes — ended.
週三的主要看點正是那奇特的全食時刻。光線基本從天空消失，空氣的溫度也隨之下降。月亮彷彿切入太陽，然後在大約一個小時內滑過太陽表面，直到短暫的全食階段（最長不過兩分多鐘）結束。
Not everyone was lucky enough to see it, or much of it. At an eclipse festival in Hellissandur, Iceland, heavy clouds and rain were the order of the day. But just after 5:45 p.m. local time, the clouds parted just enough to let viewers see the sun narrow to a sliver.
並非所有人都幸運地看到了它，或者看到了它的全部。在冰島赫利桑德爾的日食節上，當天天氣以濃雲和大雨為主。但當地時間剛過下午5點45分，雲層剛好散開了一些，讓觀眾得以看到太陽變窄成一線。
“It was like a generosity from the sky to open up for us,” said Bjorgvin Hallgrimsson, 60, a contractor who lives in Reykjavik.
「這就像是天空大發慈悲，為我們打開了缺口，」60歲的比約格溫·哈爾格裡姆松說，他是一位住在雷克雅維克的承包商。
In Sant Antoni, on the northern side of the Spanish island of Ibiza, a cloud covered the sun halfway through the eclipse. “This is a scam,” said Chelo Ortega, one of many people who were watching from the seafront promenade. “Maybe they’ll do a rerun tomorrow,” her husband, Julián Gómez, joked.
在西班牙伊維薩島北部的聖安東尼，日食進行到一半時，一朵雲遮住了太陽。「這是個騙局，」切洛·奧爾特加說，她是眾多在海濱大道上觀看的人之一。「也許他們明天會再來一次，」她的丈夫胡利安·戈麥斯開玩笑說。
A few impatient onlookers had started getting up to leave when, suddenly, the sun reappeared.
一些不耐煩的觀眾已經開始起身準備離開，就在這時，太陽突然又重新出現了。
The light dropped, and people began to shout. It briefly turned to night over the sea, and the sun’s fiery corona came into view. Thousands of phone screens were raised toward the sight as people applauded.
光線暗了下來，人們開始歡呼。海面上短暫地變成了黑夜，太陽熾熱的日冕映入眼帘。成千上萬的手機螢幕升起，人群掌聲雷動。
