塔利班統治五年後，阿富汗瀕臨失控

Afghanistan’s Unhappy Anniversary

KATRIN BENNHOLD • 2026-08-13
Afghanistan has a track record of defeating superpowers. The mujahedeen humbled the Soviet Union in the 1980s, and five years ago the Taliban pushed out the United States.
阿富汗有著擊敗超級大國的歷史記錄。20世紀80年代，聖戰者組織讓蘇聯吃盡苦頭；五年前，塔利班又將美國趕出了阿富汗。
But can Afghanistan’s rulers overcome the discontent of their own people?
但阿富汗的統治者能否化解本國民眾的不滿？
With the country on the verge of another all-out war, this time with Pakistan, that is less than certain. Today, my colleague Elian Peltier explains why all the ingredients for things to spiral out of control are there.
隨著該國瀕臨又一場全面戰爭——這次是與巴基斯坦——這一問題的答案遠未明朗。為什麼說所有可能導致局勢失控的條件都已齊備？今天，我的同事埃利安·佩爾蒂埃將作出解釋。
What five years of Taliban rule looks like
塔利班統治五年後的阿富汗
廣告
By Elian Peltier
撰文：埃利安·佩爾蒂埃
The Taliban took over Afghanistan five years ago this week. They pushed out the world’s most powerful army. Yet the mood among Taliban officials I’ve spoken with and soldiers I’ve bumped into at checkpoints hasn’t exactly been celebratory — more like embattled.
五年前的這一週，塔利班接管了阿富汗。他們將世界上最強大的軍隊趕了出去。然而，無論是我採訪過的塔利班官員，還是我在檢查站遇到的塔利班士兵，感受到的氣氛遠非喜悅，而更像是如臨大敵。
On what could have been a triumphant anniversary for the group, the Taliban find themselves in crisis at home and abroad.
在這個本應是該組織慶祝勝利的週年紀念日，塔利班卻發現自己內外交困。
They face a conflict just short of open war with neighboring Pakistan. They’re dealing with fresh waves of attacks from armed groups who want to topple them. Add to that mass unemployment, near-total international isolation and growing domestic discontent, and it all amounts to the most serious threats the Taliban have encountered since their victory in 2021.
他們與鄰國巴基斯坦的衝突已經接近開戰的程度。國內武裝組織發動的新一波襲擊接連不斷，矛頭直指這個政權。再加上大規模失業、近乎徹底的國際孤立以及日益高漲的國內不滿情緒，種種疊加構成了塔利班自2021年奪權以來所面臨的最嚴峻威脅。
It’s too early to tell whether all this will escalate in a way that could threaten the regime’s survival. But it has cracked the thin veneer of security and stability that the Taliban herald as their primary achievement since taking over — and their primary selling point to the world.
這一切是否會進一步激化，乃至動搖該政權的根基，目前下結論為時尚早。但這已足以戳破塔利班自掌權以來一再標榜的首要成就——也是他們向世界推銷的最大賣點——即那層脆弱的安全與穩定表象。
A tight grip on power — and yet
權力掌控嚴密——但仍不穩固
The Taliban are holding Afghan society in a chokehold that spares no one. Girls and women face restrictions to a degree unseen anywhere else in the world. Religious minorities are witnessing the forced closure of their places of worship. Anyone who voices criticism is repressed.
塔利班以令人窒息的方式掌控著阿富汗社會，無人倖免。女性不分長幼，承受著世界上其他地方絕無僅有的嚴苛限制。宗教少數群體的禮拜場所遭到強制關閉。任何敢於開口批評的人都難逃鎮壓。
廣告
That grip on power over a country of nearly 45 million people seems immovable. The Taliban collect taxes and are pretty good at it. They are building roads, dams and canals. European countries are desperate to work with them to deport Afghan migrants and asylum seekers. And when someone shuts the door on them, as Pakistan did when it closed its border last year, they open new ones: Afghanistan has increasingly relied on trade with Iran, India and Central Asian states like Uzbekistan.
在這個擁有近4500萬人口的國家，塔利班的權力掌控看似牢不可破。他們徵收稅款，而且相當有一套。他們修路、建壩、開渠。歐洲國家迫切希望與他們達成合作，以遣返阿富汗移民和庇護申請者。當有人向他們關上大門——就像巴基斯坦去年關閉邊境那樣——他們便另尋出路：阿富汗越來越多地依賴與伊朗、印度以及烏茲別克斯坦等中亞國家的貿易往來。
And yet the signs of trouble that have appeared in recent months are impossible to ignore.
然而，人們對近幾個月來浮出水面的麻煩跡象已無法視而不見。
An underequipped Afghan Army waits nervously at the border with Pakistan. Once a sponsor of the Taliban, Pakistan has launched dozens of airstrikes on Afghanistan in recent months; killed 500 civilians since October, according to the United Nations; and threatened to go all in against the Taliban regime, which it accuses of harboring militants who are staging attacks on Pakistani territory.
裝備簡陋的阿富汗軍隊在巴基斯坦邊境緊張候命。曾是塔利班靠山的巴基斯坦近幾個月來已對阿富汗發動了數十次空襲；據聯合國統計，自去年10月以來已造成500名平民死亡；巴方還威脅要對塔利班政權全面開戰，指責其窩藏在巴基斯坦境內發動襲擊的武裝分子。
The mistrust goes both ways: The Taliban have accused Pakistan of supporting the armed groups that have attacked Taliban outposts in Afghanistan’s northeastern province of Badakhshan and are gearing up for a sustained offensive.
雙方都對彼此抱有不信任：塔利班指責巴基斯坦支持那些襲擊了阿富汗東北部巴達赫尚省塔利班據點的武裝組織，這些組織正在準備發動持續攻勢。
Whether, or to what extent, Pakistan is actively supporting anti-Taliban groups is not clear, but suspicions are high enough that a senior Western diplomat with decades of experience working on Afghanistan told me this summer, “This region doesn’t need another proxy war.”
巴基斯坦是否正在積極扶持反塔利班武裝，以及扶持的程度究竟幾何，目前尚無定論，但雙方猜忌之深，一位在阿富汗工作數十年的西方資深外交官今年夏天這樣告訴我：「這個地區不需要再來一場代理人戰爭。」
The picture on the domestic front is also grim. The Taliban are struggling to deal with a cascade of humanitarian and economic crises. Nearly four million Afghans have been forcibly returned from Iran and Pakistan since January 2025 — one of the world’s largest episodes of reverse migration in decades. Aid cuts have plunged millions into deeper food insecurity. And UNESCO says the Taliban have deprived 2.4 million Afghan girls of a secondary education — the equivalent of all school-age girls in New York, Mexico City and Moscow combined, forced to stay home indefinitely.
國內形勢同樣嚴峻。塔利班正努力應對一連串人道主義和經濟危機。自2025年1月以來，已有近400萬阿富汗人被伊朗和巴基斯坦強制遣返——這是幾十年來世界上最大規模的『反向移民』事件之一。援助削減將數以百萬計的民眾推入了更嚴重的糧食危機。聯合國教科文組織表示，塔利班剝奪了240萬阿富汗女孩接受中學教育的權利——相當於紐約、墨西哥城和莫斯科所有學齡女孩的總和，她們被迫無限期困守家中。
A society can withstand only so much, even under the tightest of grips. At a rare protest in June, demonstrators chanted “Work, education, freedom,” similar to what their Iranian neighbors chanted in 2022. And the leader of a large religious minority warned the Taliban in May: Don’t squeeze Afghan society too much, or it will explode.
一個社會所能承受的壓迫是有限的，哪怕處於最嚴酷的管控之下。今年6月一場罕見的抗議活動中，示威者高呼「工作、教育、自由」——與鄰國伊朗人在2022年喊出的口號如出一轍。一個大型宗教少數群體的領袖也在5月向塔利班發出警告：不要把阿富汗社會壓迫得太緊，否則它會爆炸。

The potential to spiral?
會不會局勢失控？
The Taliban know the world wants a stable Afghanistan — one that isn’t a source of refugees, opium or terrorism.
塔利班知道世界想要一個穩定的阿富汗——一個不再輸出難民、鴉片或恐怖主義的國家。
The Taliban claim the country no longer poses a security threat to the region or the West. They say that they have cracked down on the Islamic State, and that they want peaceful relations based on trade and diplomacy, including with the U.S.
塔利班聲稱，阿富汗已不再對地區或西方的安全構成威脅。他們表示，已大力打擊伊斯蘭國，尋求與包括美國在內的國家建立以貿易和外交為基礎的和平關係。
All of this may have some truth to it. The Taliban don’t want their various crises to spiral. But it also might not be entirely up to them.
這些說法或許有一定真實性。塔利班並不願看到眼下的各種危機進一步失控。但這或許也並非完全取決於他們的意願。
All the ingredients for things to get out of control in Afghanistan again are here, analysts say: Growing animosity toward Pakistan and a vast population of unemployed young Afghans are fertile ground for radicalization, especially as economic opportunities are nearly nonexistent. Under the Taliban, Afghanistan is, once again, a pariah state, with barely any allies.
分析人士說，所有可能讓阿富汗再次陷入失控局面的條件都已經具備：對巴基斯坦日益加深的敵意以及大量失業的阿富汗年輕人都為激進化提供了肥沃的土壤，尤其是在經濟機會幾乎為零的情況下。在塔利班的統治下，阿富汗再次淪為國際棄兒，幾乎沒有盟友。
As a foreign military official in Afghanistan put it, the country is no longer the hub for jihadism that it used to be — but it has everything it needs to become one again.
正如一位駐阿富汗的外國軍官所言：該國已經不再是過去那個聖戰主義的中心，但它已經具備了再次成為這種中心所需要的一切條件。
廣告
The past five years have brought some predictability and an end to almost two decades of fighting, which many Afghans have welcomed, despite the immense hardship and repression. But Afghanistan remains shattered, and what always looked like a fragile peace is already at risk.
過去的五年帶來了一定程度的可預測性，也終結了將近20年的戰亂，儘管這伴隨著巨大的苦難與壓迫，許多阿富汗人仍對此感到些許欣慰。但阿富汗依然滿目瘡痍，那個看來本就岌岌可危的和平已然處於風雨飄搖之中。
