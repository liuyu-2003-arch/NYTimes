「受夠了」：歐洲迎來今夏第五波熱浪，氣溫達43度

Europeans, Braced for 5th Heat Wave of 2026, Have Had Enough

ELISABETTA POVOLEDO, GIULIA IMBERT, CARLOS BARRAGAN • 2026-08-13
Europe braced on Wednesday for a fifth heat wave of a record-breaking summer that has already spurred wildfires of rare ferocity, caused water shortages across the continent and, for many Europeans, sapped the joy from a season they once savored.
歐洲週三準備迎接第五波熱浪，這個破紀錄的夏天已經引發了烈度罕見的野火，導致整個歐洲大陸出現水資源短缺，對許多歐洲人來說，他們曾經享受的這個季節的樂趣已經蕩然無存。
The heat is expected to sweep from the west, with a high of 109 degrees Fahrenheit, or 43 degrees Celsius, predicted in Spain on Thursday, before hitting more eastern countries like Germany over the weekend.
預計熱浪將從西部席捲而來，週四西班牙最高氣溫將達到華氏109度（攝氏43度），然後在週末襲擊德國等偏東部的國家。
In Britain, the government convened a special ministerial meeting on Wednesday morning to prepare for the fallout, as tourists and residents from Madrid to Rome spoke wistfully of the milder climates of summers past.
在英國，政府週三上午召開了一次特別大臣級會議，以做出應對準備，而從馬德里到羅馬的遊客和居民都滿懷惆悵地談起過去那些相對沒那麼難耐的夏天。
Irina Nedelcu, 42, who sells wine at a food market in Rome, said she had never felt so hot and that summer had lost its appeal. “I used to love the heat and hate the cold, but starting this year, basta,” Ms. Nedelcu said, using the Italian word for “enough.” “Now I hate the heat.”
現年42歲、在羅馬一家食品市場賣葡萄酒的伊琳娜·內德爾庫說，她從未覺得這麼熱過，夏天已經失去了吸引力。「我曾經喜歡炎熱，討厭寒冷，但從今年開始，basta，」內德爾庫說，她用了義大利語中表示「受夠了」的詞。「現在我討厭炎熱。」

At her stall, she has kept her air-conditioning unit switched off — it’s too expensive to use, she said. Instead, a fan churns warm air, making her feel as if she was “in a ventilated oven,” she said.
她的攤位一直沒開空調——她說用起來太貴了。取而代之的是一台攪動著熱空氣的風扇，讓她感覺自己就像「在一個通風的烤箱裡」，她說。
“It’s unbearable,” she said.
「難以忍受，」她說。
In Paris, Sarah Weyenbergh, 22, also used the word “unbearable” to describe her life this summer in an apartment without air-conditioning. “Taking shower after shower after shower” gave temporary relief, said Ms. Weyenbergh, who works in the fashion industry, adding that this solution was “counterproductive” because it ended up making her “feel even hotter.”
在巴黎，22歲的莎拉·韋延伯格也用了「難以忍受」來形容她在沒有空調的公寓裡度過的這個夏天。「沖一遍又一遍的澡」能帶來暫時的緩解，在時尚行業工作的韋延伯格說，但是這種解決辦法「適得其反」，因為最終會讓她「感覺更熱」。
Still, as a resident of Paris, she at least had unrestricted access to water.
不過，作為巴黎居民，她至少可以不受限制地用水。
In more than 70 percent of mainland France, water use is limited because of the widespread droughts. Groundwater levels have declined in more than 90 percent of underground water reserves in mainland France, the country’s national geological service reported this month. In some regions, people could be fined for using water in certain contexts, like washing their cars or filling swimming pools.
由於大範圍乾旱，在法國本土超過70%的地區用水受到限制。法國國家地質服務局本月報告稱，法國本土超過90%的地下蓄水層的地下水位已經下降。在一些地區，人們在某些情況下用水可能會被罰款，例如洗車或給游泳池注水。
Across the channel in Britain, nearly three-quarters of England is now in drought, according to officials, with tens of millions of people in Britain under a hose ban.
在海峽對岸的英國，官方稱英格蘭近四分之三的地區現已處於乾旱狀態，全英數千萬人被禁止使用水管澆水。
廣告
In Spain’s northern province of Burgos, Charo Barriuso, 65, said that residents of this traditionally cooler region were not used to such high temperatures.
在西班牙北部的布爾戈斯省，65歲的查羅·巴魯索說，這個傳統上較涼爽地區的居民不習慣如此高的溫度。
“The last few summers have been criminal,” Ms. Barriuso said. “We used to sleep with a blanket in the summer here. Now we sleep naked.”
「過去幾個夏天簡直太糟糕了，」巴魯索說。「我們以前夏天在這裡睡覺都要蓋毯子。現在我們都是裸睡。」
Much of the country braced for more wildfires, particularly as hundreds of thousands of people prepared to gather in the arid countryside for a rare solar eclipse.
該國大部分地區都在為更多的野火做準備，特別是當數十萬人準備聚集在乾旱的鄉村觀看罕見的日食時。
The new heat wave comes after Western Europe recorded its hottest June-to-July period on record, according to Copernicus, the European Union’s climate monitoring service.
根據歐盟氣候監測機構哥白尼的數據，新一輪熱浪到來之前，西歐剛剛經歷了有記錄以來最熱的6月和7月。
Droughts, magnified by climate change, are crippling some of the continent’s critical waterways, including the Rhine. In Serbia, the Danube River’s receding water levels exposed ships from a Nazi fleet that sank at the end of World War II.
氣候變化使乾旱加劇，導致歐洲大陸一些關鍵水道癱瘓，包括萊茵河。在塞爾維亞，多瑙河水位下降露出了二戰結束時沉沒的一支納粹艦隊的船隻。
Droughts and hail produced by uncharacteristically violent rainstorms have played havoc with farmland, harming farming revenues. Coldiretti, Italy’s largest agricultural trade association, estimates that climate-related damages have exceeded three billion euros, or nearly $3.5 billion.
乾旱和非同尋常的猛烈暴風雨產生的冰雹已對農田造成嚴重破壞，損害了農業收入。義大利最大的農業行業協會Coldiretti估計，氣候相關損失已超過30億歐元（約合233億元人民幣）。
廣告
The situation has amplified calls for cultural and logistical changes to the way that Europe operates in the summer, including a push for better water management in Italy.
這種情況加劇了要求在文化層面和實際運作上徹底改變歐洲度夏方式的呼聲，包括推動義大利改善水資源管理。
“It’s clear that we must equip ourselves and prepare, including mentally, to live in this kind of climate,” said Lorenzo Bazzana, Coldiretti’s chief economist.
「很明顯，我們必須裝備自己並做好準備，包括心理上，以適應這種氣候生活，」Coldiretti首席經濟學家洛倫佐·巴扎納說。
City life has been affected, too. In Rome, tropical evening temperatures appeared to have dulled interest in outdoor dining, while alcohol consumption was significantly down, according to Claudio Pica, the president of the Rome branch of a small business association. A survey of wholesalers and some 150 Roman eateries and bars showed a drop in sales in July, and a change in restaurant menus.
城市生活也受到了影響。在羅馬，小企業協會羅馬分會主席克勞迪奧·皮卡稱，熱帶夜間溫度似乎削弱了人們對戶外用餐的興趣，而酒精消費量則顯著下降。對批發商以及約150家羅馬餐館和酒吧進行的調查顯示，7月份銷售額下降，餐館菜單也發生了變化。
“Tourists still eat pasta carbonara and amatriciana because they’re traditional dishes, but Romans are asking for salads,” he said.
「遊客仍然會點培根蛋醬面和阿馬特里恰納面，因為它們是傳統菜肴，但羅馬人現在點沙拉，」他說。
Some tourists are rethinking vacation plans.
一些遊客正在重新考慮度假計劃。
“We knew it was going to be really hot; we weren’t expecting it to be so bad in the sun, that’s what gets you,” said Hannah Cutter, 24, a speech-language pathologist from New York sheltering in the shade in a Roman piazza.
「我們知道天氣會很熱；但我們沒想到在太陽下會這麼糟糕，這才是最要命的，」24歲的漢娜·卡特說，這位來自紐約的言語治療師正在羅馬的一個廣場上乘涼。
廣告
Her sister Julia Cutter, 23, who works for a manufacturing company in Connecticut, said the “stifling” heat would make her think twice about visiting Italy in August again. “I mean, we have heat like this at home, and this is just next level,” she said.
她的妹妹、23歲的朱莉婭·卡特在康乃狄克州一家製造公司工作，她說這種「令人窒息的」炎熱天氣讓她不確定還要不要再在8月份來義大利。「我的意思是，我們家裡也有這樣的熱浪，但這完全是另一個級別的熱了，」她說。
At a central cinema in Rome, Mario Del Pane was taking advantage of a city-sponsored initiative offering free afternoon movies at several cinemas.
在羅馬市中心的一家電影院，馬利奧·德爾·帕內正在利用市政府發起的一項舉措——在幾家電影院提供免費下午電影。
“It’s a way to pass the hottest hours in a cool place,” said Mr. Del Pane, 44. The other options were to stay home, or go to the beach, which wasn’t much of an alternative, he said.
「這是在涼爽的地方度過最熱幾個小時的一種方式，」44歲的德爾·帕內說。其他選擇就是待在家裡，或者去海灘，但這算不上是什麼真正的選擇，他說。
“The temperature is very hot on the sand and you suffer,” said Mr. Del Pane. “I much prefer to be in a cool cinema.”
「沙灘上溫度非常高，你會很難受，」德爾·帕內說。「我更喜歡待在涼爽的電影院裡。」

Elisabetta Povoledo是《紐約時報》記者，常駐羅馬，報導義大利、梵蒂岡和該地區的文化。她從事新聞工作已有35年。
