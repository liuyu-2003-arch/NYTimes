「習川會」最棘手議題之一：中國稀土出口管制

China’s Continued Control of Rare Earths Looms Over Trump-Xi Meeting

KEITH BRADSHER • 2026-09-23
China’s restrictions on exports of crucial rare-earth metals and rare-earth magnets remain one of the most difficult unresolved issues as President Trump and Xi Jinping prepare to meet this week.
隨著川普總統和中國國家主席習近平準備於本週舉行會晤，中國對關鍵稀土金屬和稀土磁鐵的出口限制依然是最棘手的未決問題之一。
For months, manufacturers in the United States, Europe, Japan and India have complained about difficulties in obtaining enough rare-earth metals from China, which dominates global production. The materials are essential for making products ranging from cars and wind turbines to semiconductors, computer displays and military equipment.
幾個月來，美國、歐洲、日本和印度的製造商一直抱怨難以從中國獲取足夠的稀土金屬，而中國主導著全球稀土生產。這些材料對於製造汽車、風力渦輪機、半導體、電腦顯示器以及軍事裝備等各類產品至關重要。
China imposed export controls in April 2025 on seven kinds of rare earths and magnets made from them. It later announced a broader set of restrictions, now scheduled to take effect Nov. 10. And in a political dispute, China has slashed shipments of rare earths and magnets to Japan, disrupting supply chains that also serve American manufacturers.
2025年4月，中國對七種稀土及由其製成的磁鐵實施了出口管制。隨後，中國宣布了一項範圍更廣的限制措施，目前定於11月10日生效。此外，在一場政治爭端中，中國大幅削減了對日本的稀土和磁鐵出口，擾亂了同樣服務於美國製造商的供應鏈。
All three issues — the existing controls, the Nov. 10 restrictions and the curbs on Japan — are expected to loom over the meeting between Mr. Trump and Mr. Xi, China’s top leader. There is little indication so far of how they might be resolved.
所有這三個問題——現行的管制、11月10日生效的限制措施以及對日本的出口限制——預計都將籠罩在川普與作為中國最高領導人的習近平的會晤之上。迄今為止，幾乎沒有跡象表明這些問題將如何得到解決。
廣告
Rare earths have become a critical trade issue between the two countries since Beijing introduced export licensing rules two days after Mr. Trump imposed steep tariffs in April 2025 on China and dozens of other countries. China’s rare-earth shipments slowed to a trickle, and some manufacturers from elsewhere had to cut output of their products.
2025年4月，川普對中國及數十個其他國家徵收高額關稅。兩天後，北京出台了出口許可規定，自此稀土便成為兩國間的關鍵貿易問題。中國的稀土出口量銳減，其他地區的一些製造商也不得不削減其產品產量。
The experience last year demonstrated the leverage China derives from its near monopoly on the production of the important materials.
去年的經歷彰顯了中國憑藉其對這些重要材料生產近乎壟斷的地位所獲得的影響力。
The White House says China has not fully followed through on previous discussions about issuing more export licenses. Deliveries have “not been up to par of what we would expect, and that, I’m sure, will be a topic of discussion,” a senior U.S. official said at a briefing on Friday.
白宮表示，中國並未完全落實先前關於發放更多出口許可證的討論。一位美國高級官員在週五的簡報會上說，交付情況「未達到我們的預期標準，我確信，這將成為一個討論話題」。
American and European business groups say shipments are frequently delayed or incomplete or never arrive. China’s Ministry of Commerce scrutinizes every export application.
美國和歐洲商業團體表示，貨物經常延誤、數量不足或根本沒有運達。中國商務部對每一份出口申請都會進行審查。
“While most of our members report getting the majority of the rare-earth supplies they need, the restrictions around the sector have given many doubts about relying on China as a dependable long-term supply chain partner,” said Michael Hart, the president of the American Chamber of Commerce in China.
中國美國商會會長何邁可(Michael Hart)表示：「雖然我們大多數的成員報稱獲得了他們所需的大部分稀土供應，但圍繞該行業的限制讓許多人擔心，還能不能將中國視為可靠的長期供應鏈合作夥伴。」
Guo Jiakun, a spokesman for China’s foreign ministry, said on Monday that Beijing remained committed to maintaining stable and secure supply chains for critical minerals.
中國外交部發言人郭嘉昆週一表示，北京方面仍致力於維護關鍵礦物供應鏈的穩定與安全。
The restrictions set to begin on Nov. 10 present another challenge. Current and former American officials said the United States and China had discussed postponing the rules but had been unable to agree on how long such an extension should last. They spoke on the condition of anonymity because of diplomatic sensitivities.
定於11月10日生效的限制措施構成了另一項挑戰。現任和前任美國官員表示，中美兩國曾討論過推遲實施這些規定，但未能就延期多長時間達成一致。由於外交敏感性，他們要求匿名。
The United States has proposed a delay of six months to a year as part of a broader freeze on American tariffs and other trade actions, the officials said.
官員們表示，美國提議推遲六個月至一年，作為對美國關稅及其他貿易行動更廣泛凍結計劃的一部分。
廣告
Such a deal would push renewed trade tensions beyond the U.S. midterm elections while leaving the Trump administration time to press Beijing before the 2028 presidential campaign over its trade surplus, import restrictions and industrial subsidies.
此類協議將把重燃的貿易緊張局勢推遲到美國期中選舉之後，同時為川普政府在2028年總統大選前留出時間，以就貿易順差、進口限制及工業補貼等問題向北京施壓。
But Beijing has its own political calendar. The Communist Party is expected to hold its once-every-five-years national congress next autumn, when it chooses the country’s top leadership. China typically seeks to minimize disputes ahead of the gathering, meaning a six-month or one-year deal could expire at an awkward time.
但北京有自己的政治日程。中國共產黨預計將於明年秋季舉行五年一次的全國代表大會，屆時將選出該國的最高領導層。中國通常會在大會召開前盡量減少爭端，這意味著一項為期六個月或一年的協議可能會在一個尷尬的節點到期。
China has instead sought a halt to further American tariffs and other trade actions for the remainder of Mr. Trump’s presidency, current and former American officials said. Chinese officials have declined to confirm that position and claim that the export controls are intended to prevent rare earths from being used in military equipment.
現任及前任美國官員表示，中國轉而尋求美國在川普剩餘總統任期內停止實施進一步的關稅和其他貿易行動。中國官員拒絕證實這一立場，並聲稱出口管制的目的是防止稀土被用于軍事裝備。
Mr. Guo declined on Monday to discuss a possible extension of the Nov. 10 rules.
郭嘉昆週一拒絕討論可能推遲11月10日規定的事宜。
Those rules would impose controls on five additional rare-earth elements, including erbium, which is vital for fiber-optic communications in artificial intelligence data centers and transoceanic cables. Even before the rules take effect, China appears to be restricting or delaying erbium exports, industry experts said.
這些新規將對包括鉺在內的另外五種稀土元素實施出口管制。鉺對人工智慧數據中心和跨洋電纜的光纖通信至關重要。行業專家表示，甚至在規定生效之前，中國似乎就已經在限制或推遲鉺的出口。
“You’ve had bottlenecks building up at several Chinese ports,” said Ellie Saklatvala, metals director at Argus Media, a commodities pricing firm in London.
倫敦大宗商品定價機構阿格斯的金屬主管埃莉·薩克拉特瓦拉表示：「幾個中國港口已經出現積壓的情況。」
The most difficult dispute may be China’s sharp reduction in shipments of rare-earth magnets to Japan and its near suspension of some particularly important rare earths, including dysprosium, which is widely used in magnets for cars and in the most advanced chips.
最棘手的爭端或許是中國大幅減少了對日本的稀土磁鐵出口，並幾乎暫停了一些尤為重要的稀土供應，其中包括鏑。鏑被廣泛應用於汽車磁鐵及最先進的晶片中。
廣告
China began imposing the restrictions in December after Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi of Japan said her country could help defend Taiwan if China invaded. Taiwan is an island democracy that Beijing considers part of its territory.
去年12月，在日本首相高市早苗表示如果中國「入侵」，她的國家可協助保衛台灣之後，中國開始實施這些限制措施。台灣是一個實行民主制度的島嶼，北京將其視為自身領土的一部分。
China has generally handled the sales of rare earths with each country separately, and it is unclear whether Mr. Xi will discuss the restrictions on Japan with Mr. Trump. Asked Monday about shipments to Japan, Mr. Guo, the Chinese government spokesman, said China prohibited exports of dual-use items intended for Japan’s military, as well as other items that could strengthen its military.
中國在稀土銷售上通常對各國區分對待，目前尚不清楚習近平是否會與川普討論針對日本的限制措施。當週一被問及對日出口時，中國政府發言人郭嘉昆表示，中國禁止向日本出口用于軍事的兩用物項，以及其他可能增強其軍事實力的物項。
China’s dominance of the industry gives it considerable leverage. Its refineries produce about 90 percent of the world’s supply of four rare-earth elements widely used in oil refining, glass polishing and low-end magnets: lanthanum, cerium, neodymium and praseodymium. Beijing has not restricted their export.
中國在這一行業的主導地位賦予了其巨大的影響力。中國的精鍊廠生產了四種稀土元素約90%的全球供應，這四種元素——鑭、鈰、釹和鐠——廣泛應用於石油煉製、玻璃拋光及低端磁鐵。北京尚未限制這四種元素的出口。
It also produces nearly all of the global supply of a dozen other rare-earth elements needed for a range of products, from semiconductors and lasers to wind turbines and robots. The export of seven of those have required special licenses since April 2025, and the Nov. 10 rules call for export licensing on the other five.
中國還生產從半導體、雷射器到風力渦輪機和機器人等一系列產品所需的另外十幾種稀土元素的幾乎全部全球供應。自2025年4月起，其中七種的出口已需獲得特殊許可證，而11月10日生效的新規要求對另外五種實施出口許可制度。
Beijing says its rare-earth controls are meant to prevent weapons proliferation, rather than to exert economic pressure. But military uses account for only a small share of global rare-earth consumption.
北京表示，其稀土管制旨在防止武器擴散，而非施加經濟壓力。然而，軍事用途僅佔全球稀土消耗量的一小部分。
The Department of Defense estimated in 2024 that American military demand accounted for only 0.1 percent of global rare-earth demand. But that figure is skewed lower because it includes enormous volumes of common rare earths used in civilian applications like oil refining and glass polishing.
美國國防部2024年估計，美國軍事需求僅佔全球稀土需求的0.1%。但這一數字偏低，因為它涵蓋了大量用於石油煉製和玻璃拋光等民用領域的普通稀土。
廣告
Even when the comparison is limited to the scarcer rare earths most important to weapon production, industry estimates put military uses at roughly 2 to 5 percent of overall demand.
即使將比較範圍限定在對武器生產最為重要的稀缺稀土上，行業估算也表明，軍事用途約佔總體需求的2%至5%。
