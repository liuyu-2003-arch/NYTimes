美中元首峰會聚焦人工智慧議題

U.S. and China Balance A.I. Risks and Rivalry

ANA SWANSON • 2026-09-23
U.S. and Chinese officials are setting up a dialogue about artificial intelligence and a hotline to report national security risks from the technology. The United States is lodging criticisms with China over its companies’ copying American A.I. models. And a state dinner hosting China’s leader on Thursday is expected to be attended by some of America’s biggest A.I. and tech titans, including executives from OpenAI, Google and Nvidia.
美國和中國官員正在籌建一個關於人工智慧的對話機制，並設立熱線，用於通報這項技術帶來的國家安全風險。美國正就中國企業複製美國人工智慧模型一事向中方提出批評。週四為中國領導人舉行的國宴預計將有美國一些最重量級的人工智慧和科技界巨頭出席，包括OpenAI、谷歌和英偉達的高管。
A.I. is front and center as officials from the United States and China meet this week in New York and Washington. But with technological competition between the countries so intense, and little consensus within the United States about the path forward on A.I. regulation, experts do not expect much headway.
本週美中官員在紐約和華盛頓會晤時，人工智慧是核心議題。但鑒於兩國之間的科技競爭如此激烈，且美國國內對人工智慧監管的前進方向缺乏共識，專家預計會談不會取得太大進展。
Concerns are mounting in the United States about potential threats from A.I., after warnings from A.I. researchers and incidents when A.I. agents escaped their training environments and hacked websites. Many U.S. officials worry that A.I. could enhance China’s ability to launch cyberattacks on the United States or crack military codes. Some believe the Trump administration should be doing more to limit China’s access to American chips and other A.I. technology.
在人工智慧研究人員發出警告，以及出現AI智能體脫離訓練環境併入侵網站的事件後，美國國內對人工智慧潛在威脅的擔憂日益加劇。許多美國官員擔心，人工智慧可能增強中國對美國發動網路攻擊或破解軍事密碼的能力。一些人認為，川普政府應採取更多措施限制中國獲取美國晶片及其他人工智慧技術。
But President Trump has taken a laissez-faire approach. The only guardrails A.I. needs are a “strong and smart president,” he has said.
但川普總統採取了放任自流的做法。他曾表示，人工智慧唯一需要的護欄是一個「強大而聰明的總統」。
廣告
“Whoever wins AI, WINS! We are leading now over China, and everyone else, and I’m going to keep it that way!” Mr. Trump wrote on social media on Monday. “I’m not going to stifle Growth, of something that will be bigger than the Industrial Revolution, or the Internet, itself.”
「誰贏得人工智慧，誰就是贏家！我們現在領先於中國，也領先於其他所有國家，我會讓這種局面保持下去！」川普週一在社群媒體上寫道。「我不會扼殺一項將比工業革命、甚至互聯網本身更為重要的技術的發展。」
Mr. Trump has opposed state regulation of A.I. And he has largely supported Nvidia’s selling the computer chips that fuel A.I. models abroad, relaxing restrictions the Biden administration set on providing the technology to China and other nations.
川普不贊成對人工智慧進行國家監管。他也基本支持英偉達向海外銷售驅動人工智慧模型的計算機晶片，放鬆了拜登政府時期對向中國及其他國家提供該技術所設的限制。
The administration has granted a limited number of licenses this year to Nvidia to sell its H200 chip to China. Nvidia and its supporters argue that not selling the chips creates an opportunity for Chinese companies like Huawei to master their own A.I. chip technology.
今年，川普政府向英偉達發放了一定數量的許可證，允許它向中國銷售H200晶片。英偉達及其支持者認為，如果不向中國出售這些晶片，就會給華為等中國企業提供機會，使其掌握自己的人工智慧晶片技術。
The Trump administration is also widely expected to extend a trade truce with China that includes an agreement to pause sanctions on thousands of Chinese companies.
外界還普遍預計，川普政府將延長與中國達成的貿易休戰協議，其中包括暫停對數千家中國企業實施制裁的安排。
Lawmakers are considering legislation that could impose tougher limits on the sale of chips and chipmaking equipment to China, but it is unclear if the bills have enough votes to pass.
議員們正在審議一項旨在更嚴格限制對華銷售晶片及晶片製造設備的法案，但它能否獲得足夠票數通過，目前尚不清楚。
In a letter to Mr. Trump on Tuesday, Senator Elizabeth Warren, Democrat of Massachusetts, accused the president of accommodating the A.I. industry’s demands and said she was concerned he would double down on a “dangerous approach to artificial intelligence” in talks with Xi Jinping, the leader of China.
麻薩諸塞州民主党參議員伊麗莎白·沃倫週二致信川普，指責總統迎合人工智慧行業的要求，並表示她擔心川普在與中國領導人習近平會談時，會加碼推行其在人工智慧問題上的「危險做法」。
廣告
Ms. Warren said that Mr. Trump had used a previous meeting with Mr. Xi to advance the interests of tech leaders including Jensen Huang, Nvidia’s chief executive, who accompanied Mr. Trump on his trip to Beijing this year. She added that Mr. Huang would be called before Congress next year.
沃倫表示，川普曾利用此前與習近平的一次會晤來推動科技行業領袖的利益，其中包括英偉達首席執行官黃仁勛；黃仁勛今年曾陪同川普訪問北京。她還說，黃仁勛明年將被傳喚到國會作證。
“The American people expect you to press President Xi about the risks of A.I. and work with China and countries around the world to set rules and regulations for this technology,” Ms. Warren wrote to Mr. Trump.
「美國人民期待你就人工智慧的風險向習主席施壓，並與中國以及世界各國合作，為這項技術制定規則和監管制度，」沃倫在給川普的信中寫道。
Scott Bessent, the Treasury secretary, who plays a leading role in negotiating with China, has acknowledged the threat that A.I.-driven attacks might pose to U.S. stability and the financial system.
在對華談判中發揮主導作用的財政部長史考特·貝森特承認，人工智慧驅動的攻擊可能對美國的穩定和金融體系構成威脅。
Speaking after a meeting with his Chinese counterparts on Sunday, Mr. Bessent said that the United States and China had discussed creating a notification system for national security issues related to A.I.
貝森特週日與中方官員會晤後表示，雙方討論了建立針對人工智慧相關國家安全問題的通報機制。
On CNBC on Monday, Mr. Bessent said that “the important thing was to start talking.” The sides agreed to meet on A.I. again, probably in two months in Shenzhen, and would consider “what the leading A.I. dangers are, whether it’s uncontrollable agents, whether it’s nonstate actors, and cyber nonstate actors in bioweapons,” he said.
貝森特週一接受CNBC採訪時表示，「重要的是開始對話。」他說，雙方同意再次就人工智慧舉行會晤，時間可能是兩個月後，地點在深圳，並將討論「人工智慧最主要的危險是什麼，是失控的智能體還是非國家行為者，抑或利用生物武器的網路非國家行為者」。
Some experts praised the idea of more dialogue on A.I. between the countries.
一些專家對兩國就人工智慧展開更多對話的想法表示讚賞。
廣告
“I think industry from both countries feels there’s a need to at least have some sort of A.I. governance conversation,” said Eric Zheng, the president of the American Chamber of Commerce in Shanghai.
「我認為，兩國業界都覺得，至少有必要就人工智慧治理進行某種形式的對話，」上海美國商會會長鄭藝說。
But many analysts have said that talks are more likely to result in an exchange of definitions and principles than concrete actions. The U.S. government asks American companies to submit their most sophisticated A.I. models for government review, but it has not made a similar request about the type of open-source A.I. models developed by Chinese companies.
但許多分析人士表示，會談更有可能停留在交換定義和原則層面，而不是產生具體行動。美國政府要求美國企業將其最先進的人工智慧模型提交政府審查，但並未對中國公司開發的那種開源人工智慧模型提出類似要求。
Aalok Mehta, an analyst at the Center for Strategic and International Studies, said that the United States and China had some common ground. Chinese policymakers had also been thinking about A.I. safety and considering more intervention to gain insight into A.I. labs.
戰略與國際問題研究中心分析師阿洛克·梅塔表示，美中確實存在一些共同點。中國的政策制定者也一直在思考人工智慧安全問題，並考慮採取更多干預措施，以便深入了解人工智慧實驗室的情況。
But any A.I. talks will be complicated by the fact that neither country is likely to agree to anything that restrains its competitive ability, Mr. Mehta said in a panel discussion in Washington last week. American officials have also accused Chinese companies of stealing A.I. technology through distillation, and considered ratcheting up export controls on semiconductors.
但梅塔上週在華盛頓的一場小組討論中表示，任何人工智慧會談都會因一個事實而變得複雜：兩國都不太可能同意任何限制自身競爭能力的事情。美國官員還指責中國企業通過蒸餾竊取人工智慧技術，並考慮進一步收緊對半導體的出口管制。
China feels the United States bears more responsibility in A.I., because it has the world’s most advanced models, Mr. Mehta said. That leaves “a very narrow set of issues that they can have productive conversations on,” he said.
梅塔說，中國認為美國在人工智慧問題上負有更大責任，因為世界上最先進的模型掌握在美國手中。他說，這使得雙方「能夠展開富有成效對話的議題範圍非常狹窄」。
Edgard D. Kagan, a senior adviser for the Center for Strategic and International Studies, said at the same event that fundamental distrust between the countries was likely to limit the information sharing that would be needed to have confidence that both sides were keeping their promises.
戰略與國際研究中心高級顧問埃德加·卡根在同一場活動中表示，兩國之間根深蒂固的不信任可能會限制信息共享，而要讓彼此相信對方會信守承諾，這種信息共享又是必不可少的。
廣告
“China is deeply suspicious of anything that looks like it’s designed to lock in U.S. advantages,” he said, adding, “It will be very, very hard to come up with a mechanism that is anything more than symbolic right now.”
「中國對任何看起來旨在固化美國優勢的東西都深懷戒心，」他說，並補充道，「就目前而言，要設計出一種不只是具有象徵意義的機制，將會非常、非常困難。」

Ana Swanson報導貿易和國際經濟新聞，常駐華盛頓。她從事新聞工作已超過十年。
