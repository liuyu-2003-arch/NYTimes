中日僵局難解，日本向川普「求助」

Japan, Suffering Xi’s Ire, Hopes Trump Can Help Temper China

赫海威, 郭莉莉 • 2026-09-23
For almost a year, China has orchestrated a bruising campaign against Japan in retaliation for a comment by its prime minister, Sanae Takaichi, in defense of Taiwan.
近一年來，中國針對日本策划了一場猛烈的打壓行動，以報復日本首相高市早苗保衛台灣的言論。
When Ms. Takaichi meets with President Trump in New York City on Tuesday, those tensions are likely to be high on the agenda.
當高市早苗週二在紐約市與川普總統會晤時，這些緊張局勢很可能成為議程上的重要議題。
Ms. Takaichi, a longtime critic of Beijing, is expected to express her concern to Mr. Trump about China’s increasingly aggressive economic and security measures, according to two people familiar with her plans, including restrictions on the export of rare earth minerals. She will also emphasize her desire for a de-escalation with China, the individuals said.
據兩名熟悉其計劃的人士透露，長期批評北京的高市早苗預計將向川普表達她對中國日益咄咄逼人的經濟和安全措施的擔憂，包括對稀土礦產出口的限制。這些人士表示，她還將強調，她希望與中國緩和緊張局勢。
The meeting between Mr. Trump and Ms. Takaichi, on the sidelines of the United Nations General Assembly, comes a day before Mr. Trump is set to welcome China’s leader, Xi Jinping, for a state visit in Washington. Ms. Takaichi remains focused on persuading Mr. Trump to maintain security commitments in East Asia, including the more than 50,000 American troops stationed in Japan.
川普與高市早苗在聯合國大會期間會晤，次日川普就將在華盛頓歡迎到訪的中國領導人習近平。高市早苗的重點依然是說服川普維持在東亞的安全承諾，包括駐紮在日本的5萬多名美軍。
廣告
Kenneth R. Weinstein, Japan chair at Hudson Institute, a think tank in Washington, said that Ms. Takaichi had a strong relationship with Mr. Trump and that she would likely seek to reaffirm their alliance as a buffer against Mr. Xi.
華盛頓智庫哈德遜研究所日本項目主席肯尼斯·溫斯坦表示，高市早苗與川普關係密切，她很可能會尋求重申兩國的同盟關係，以此作為對抗習近平的緩衝。
“She wants President Trump to send a clear signal to Xi Jinping that there is no distance between the U.S. and Japan on an array of critical issues,” he said.
「她希望川普總統向習近平發出一個明確的信號，表明在一系列關鍵問題上，美日之間沒有任何分歧，」他說。
Ms. Takaichi’s office did not immediately respond to a request for comment about the tensions with China and her meeting with Mr. Trump.
高市早苗的辦公室沒有立即回應關於與中國緊張局勢以及她與川普會晤的置評請求。
China and Japan have been at odds since November, when Ms. Takaichi said that Japan could intervene militarily if China were to attack Taiwan, a self-governed democracy that Beijing considers part of its territory. Beijing unleashed a wave of reprisals, restricting Japanese seafood imports, discouraging tourism to Japan and limiting the export of critical minerals to Japan. Chinese officials also cut off diplomatic channels to Tokyo.
自去年11月以來，中日關係持續緊張，當時高市早苗表示，如果中國攻擊台灣，日本可能會進行軍事干預；台灣是一個自治的民主政體，而北京認為它是其領土的一部分。北京隨後發起了一波報復行動，限制日本海鮮進口，勸阻民眾赴日旅遊，並限制對日本的關鍵礦產出口。中國官員還切斷了與東京的外交管道。
The economic pressure has intensified in recent months, with tourism from China falling sharply — the number of Chinese tourists was down 59 percent in August from the previous year — and Beijing further restricting the export of rare earth minerals used in a range of essential technologies.
近幾個月來，經濟壓力進一步加劇，中國遊客數量銳減——8月同比下降了59%，北京還進一步限制了用於一系列關鍵技術的稀土礦產出口。
China in July exported 122 tons of rare earth magnets to Japan, according to Chinese customs data, a 52 percent drop from the year before. In August, China’s rare earth magnets to Japan were 17 percent lower compared with a year earlier.
根據中國海關數據，中國7月向日本出口了122噸稀土磁鐵，同比下降52%。8月，中國對日本的稀土磁鐵出口量同比下降了17%。
In recent months, Ms. Takaichi’s government has attempted to re-engage with China, sending delegations of lawmakers and business leaders to Beijing.
近幾個月來，高市政府試圖重新與中國接觸，並向北京派遣了由議員和商界領袖組成的代表團。
Those visits have yielded some frank conversations. During one recent meeting in Beijing, Chinese officials peppered their Japanese visitors with questions about Ms. Takaichi’s plans to build up Japan’s military, asking if she was doing so with the goal of being able to defend Taiwan, according to a Japanese lawmaker who was present. The lawmaker asked not to be named because the meeting was private.
這些訪問促成了一些坦率的對話。據一位在場的日本議員透露，在最近於北京舉行的一次會議上，中國官員就高市女士加強日本軍力的計劃向日本來訪者提出了一連串的問題，詢問她這樣做是否是為了能夠保衛台灣。由於會議是私下進行的，該議員要求匿名。
During another recent visit, Masahiro Komura, a Japanese lawmaker who is a member of the ruling Liberal Democratic Party, pressed his Chinese counterparts on export restrictions and the recent detention of Japanese nationals on smuggling charges. Asked in an interview whether the talks had yielded any breakthroughs, he joked that it was a positive sign that no Japanese lawmakers were detained in China during the visit.
在最近的另一次訪問中，日本執政黨自民黨議員高村正大就出口限制和近期日本國民因走私指控被拘留一事向中國同級官員施壓。在接受採訪被問及談判是否取得任何突破時，他開玩笑說，在訪問期間沒有日本議員在中國被拘留，這就是一個積極的信號。
廣告
“The top priority is to continue the diplomatic relationship,” he said. “Without communication between two countries, both sides will become very skeptical, and a negative situation will escalate.”
「當務之急是繼續保持外交關係，」他說。「如果兩國之間沒有溝通，雙方都會變得非常多疑，負面局勢就會升級。」
Chinese officials have used the recent visits from Japanese lawmakers as an opportunity to underline their unhappiness with Ms. Takaichi. In private, they have remained fixated on Ms. Takaichi’s remark on Taiwan, according to Japanese participants in the meetings, demanding that she retract it.
中國官員利用日本議員最近的訪問作為機會，強調他們對高市早苗的不滿。據參加會議的日方人士透露，在私下裡，他們一直對高市早苗關於台灣的言論耿耿於懷，要求她予以收回。
China’s assistant minister of foreign affairs, Liu Bin, lectured a group of Japanese lawmakers when they met last week. He said Ms. Takaichi’s “erroneous remarks and actions” were the primary obstacle and that Japan should “demonstrate through concrete actions that it is worthy of the trust of its Asian neighbors.”
中國外交部部長助理劉彬在上週會見一群日本議員時對他們進行了嚴詞告誡。他表示，高市早苗的「錯誤言行」是主要障礙，日本應「以實際行動取信亞洲鄰國」。
In a meeting with another Japanese delegation in late August, Lu Kang, deputy head of the Chinese Communist Party’s International Department, said that “China-Japan relations are facing serious difficulties, and the root causes are clear.”
在8月底與另一個日本代表團的會晤中，中共中央對外聯絡部副部長陸慷表示，「當前中日關係陷入嚴重困難，癥結是清楚的。」
Ms. Takaichi’s advisers say she has no intention of backing down from her remarks, given her long history of support for Taiwan and widespread anxiety about China among the Japanese public.
高市早苗的顧問表示，鑒於她長期以來對台灣的支持以及日本公眾對中國普遍存在的焦慮，她無意收回自己的言論。
Ms. Takaichi and Mr. Xi are set to cross paths again in November at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation forum meeting in Shenzhen. Neither side has commented yet on whether the two leaders will set aside time to meet one on one.
高市早苗和習近平將於11月在深圳舉行的亞太經濟合作組織論壇會議上再次碰面。關於兩位領導人是否會安排時間進行一對一會晤，雙方均未發表評論。
廣告
Lian Degui, director of the Center for Japanese Studies at Shanghai International Studies University, said the recent meetings between Chinese and Japanese officials showed there was the potential for relations to be repaired, depending on the messages from Ms. Takaichi’s administration.
上海外國語大學日本研究中心主任廉德瑰表示，中日官員最近的會晤表明兩國關係有修復的潛力，但這取決於高市早苗政府發出的信息。
“If China finds the attitude acceptable, then Japan-China relations can move to the next phase of improvement,” Mr. Lian said. “The prerequisite for improving relations is for the Japanese government to admit its mistake.”
「如果中國認為這種態度可以接受，那麼中日關係就能進入下一個改善階段，」廉德瑰說。「改善關係的前提是日本政府承認其錯誤。」
The lack of a trusted intermediary between the two sides is another obstacle, analysts said. Under Ms. Takaichi, the ruling Liberal Democratic Party has lost the support of the Komeito party, formerly its main coalition partner, which had served as a conduit to China.
分析人士表示，雙方缺乏值得信賴的中間人是另一個障礙。在高市早苗的領導下，執政的自民黨失去了公明黨的支持，公明黨曾是自民黨主要的聯盟夥伴，並曾充當與中國溝通的管道。
“What is lacking,” said Chen Hong, director of the Asia Pacific Studies Centre at East China Normal University in Shanghai, “is someone who can be taken seriously by Beijing and genuinely influence Takaichi’s decision-making.”
上海華東師範大學亞太研究中心主任陳弘說，「目前缺少的是一個能被北京認真對待並且能夠真正影響高市決策的人。」
In New York, Ms. Takaichi and Mr. Trump have a full agenda, including questions about trade, defense and Tokyo’s currency woes. Ms. Takaichi is expected to highlight Japan’s efforts to make good on a promise to invest $550 billion in the United States, part of a trade deal reached with the Trump administration last year.
在紐約，高市早苗和川普的議程排得很滿，包括關於貿易、國防和東京面臨的貨幣困境等問題。預計高市早苗將重點介紹日本為兌現在美國投資5500億美元承諾所做的努力，這是去年與川普政府達成的貿易協議的一部分。
Japan has watched with concern as Mr. Trump has in recent weeks pressured allies like South Korea to provide support for the war in Iran; Ms. Takaichi appears eager to avoid the same fate.
最近幾週，川普向韓國等盟友施壓，要求其為伊朗戰爭提供支持，日本對此感到擔憂；高市早苗似乎急切希望避免遭到同樣的命運。
廣告
Ms. Takaichi also faces pressure at home to question Mr. Trump about his administration’s recent decision to sanction the president of the International Criminal Court, Tomoko Akane, a Japanese judge. The decision has stirred deep anger in Japan, and Ms. Takaichi has called it “very unfortunate.”
高市早苗在國內也面臨壓力，被要求就川普政府最近決定製裁國際刑事法院院長、日本法官赤根智子一事向川普提出質疑。這一決定在日本激起了強烈的憤怒，高市早苗稱其「非常令人遺憾」。
It is unclear if Mr. Trump wants to get further involved in China’s dispute with Japan, though he has previously vowed to speak “Japan’s praises” to Mr. Xi.
目前尚不清楚川普是否希望進一步介入中國與日本的爭端，儘管他之前曾承諾會向習近平「讚揚日本」。
Mr. Weinstein, the expert, said that is unlikely that Mr. Trump would ask Mr. Xi to “dial back the pressure” on Japan.
專家溫斯坦表示，川普不太可能要求習近平對日本「減輕壓力」。
“The best thing the president can do,” he said, “is continue to send the signal — by regularly consulting Prime Minister Takaichi — that the U.S.-Japan alliance is critical to the United States, Japan and the Indo-Pacific.”
他說，「總統能做的無非就是通過定期與高市首相磋商繼續發出信號——美日同盟對美國、日本以及印太地區至關重要。」

Hisako Ueno自東京、Pei-Lin Wu自台灣台北對本文有報導貢獻。
