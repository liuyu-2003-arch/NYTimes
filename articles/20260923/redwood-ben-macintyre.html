最不尋常的冷戰間諜：讓他選擇叛逃的性祕密

The Sexual Secret That Foiled a Communist Plot

JOSEPH FINDER • 2026-09-23
REDWOOD: The Untold Story of the Cold War’s Most Extraordinary Spy, by Ben Macintyre
《紅杉：揭秘最不尋常的冷戰間諜》，作者：本·麥金泰爾
The British writer Ben Macintyre has made an illustrious career of taking dusty government archives and transforming them into propulsive nonfiction thrillers. His “Operation Mincemeat” was adapted for both screen and stage, and “A Spy Among Friends” became a TV series. With his latest, “Redwood,” he has done it again. The story centers on the defection of the K.G.B. major Vladimir Kuzichkin, who was stationed in Tehran during the chaos following the 1979 Iranian Revolution.
英國作家本·麥金泰爾以把塵封的政府檔案變成節奏緊湊的非虛構驚悚作品，成就了一番輝煌事業。他的《攪肉行動》(Operation Mincemeat)被搬上大銀幕和舞台，《敵友難辨》(A Spy Among Friends)則被改編成電視劇。他的新作《紅杉》再次做到了這一點。故事圍繞弗拉基米爾·庫濟奇金的叛逃展開，他是一名克格勃少校，駐紮在1979年伊朗革命後陷入混亂的德黑蘭。
The reason for his defection wasn’t ideology or money but sex. Specifically, he suffered from erectile dysfunction. Kuzichkin was sure that the cause was “anatomical,” an overly tight foreskin, and that a circumcision would resolve it. Afraid that the Iranians or the Soviets might discover his impairment, he insisted the operation be performed in the West. “Kuzichkin was the first spy in history,” Macintyre writes, “to demand, as the price of defection, the removal of his foreskin.” That insistence, Macintyre argues, altered the history of the Middle East.
他叛逃的原因不是意識型態或金錢，而是性。具體來說，他患有勃起功能障礙。庫濟奇金確信病因是「器質性的」，即包皮過緊，而包皮環切術可以解決。由於擔心伊朗人或蘇聯人發現他的缺陷，他堅持手術必須在西方進行。「庫濟奇金是歷史上第一個要求把切除包皮作為叛逃條件的間諜，」麥金泰爾寫道。他認為這一堅持改寫了中東的歷史。
The stakes were enormous. The fall of the shah had deprived Washington of its closest ally in the region, the hostage crisis had severed relations with Tehran and the Soviet invasion of neighboring Afghanistan had increased fears of a greater Kremlin expansion. Iran, which shared a long border with the Soviet Union and was now at war with Iraq, seemed particularly vulnerable. Western intelligence was especially concerned about the Tudeh, Iran’s pro-Soviet Communist Party, which had penetrated the government and the military. What they didn’t know was how extensive the penetration was — or when Moscow intended to exploit it.
當時正是一個關鍵時刻。沙阿倒台使華盛頓失去了該地區最親密的盟友，人質危機切斷了與德黑蘭的關係，蘇聯入侵鄰國阿富汗則加劇了對克里姆林宮進一步擴張的恐懼。伊朗與蘇聯共享漫長邊界，當時又正與伊拉克交戰，顯得尤其脆弱。西方情報尤其擔心圖德黨——伊朗的親蘇共產主義政黨——已滲透政府和軍隊。他們不知道的是，滲透究竟有多深，也不知道莫斯科打算何時利用這一點。
廣告
On Oct. 1, 1981, Kuzichkin entered the British Embassy in Tehran, presented himself as a vice consul with an “urgent” matter to discuss, and told Nicholas Barrington, Britain’s senior diplomat in Tehran, that he wanted to spy for Britain.
1981年10月1日，庫濟奇金走進英國駐德黑蘭大使館，自稱是副領事，說有「緊急」事務要談，並告訴英國駐德黑蘭高級外交官尼古拉斯·巴林頓，他想為英國當間諜。
MI6 code-named him REDWOOD. He stood over 6 feet 4 inches, a head taller than his case officer; the cryptonym was chosen for the world’s tallest tree (the standard rule that code names be unrevealing is, as Macintyre observes, ignored by every service on earth). Nobody in London noticed the additional double entendre until later — RED for communist, WOOD for the thing he could not achieve.
軍情六處給他代號「紅杉」(REDWOOD)。他身高1米93，比他的主管官員高出一頭；這個代號取自世界上最高的樹（正如麥金泰爾所觀察到的，所有情報機構都對代號不應透露信息的準則視若無睹）。倫敦方面一開始沒人注意到這層雙關含義——紅(RED)代表共產主義者，杉(WOOD)代表他無法做到的那件事（英語中wood有勃起的意思。——譯註）。
For eight months, Kuzichkin spied from the Soviet Embassy, and what he produced was extraordinary. His intelligence portrayed the Tudeh Party as a Soviet-funded puppet, directed (and very likely armed) by the K.G.B.
庫濟奇金在蘇聯大使館從事了八個月的間諜活動，他提供的東西非同尋常。他的情報將圖德黨描繪成蘇聯資助的傀儡，由克格勃指揮（可能還提供了武器）。
Astonishingly, Macintyre writes, MI6 gave everything that Kuzichkin revealed to the ayatollah — “every Tudeh leader, every informant inside the government, every member of the military underground, every K.G.B. officer and spy in Iran” — with Kuzichkin himself presenting the evidence at a secret meeting in Islamabad.
麥金泰爾寫道，令人震驚的是，軍情六處把庫濟奇金揭示的一切都給了阿亞圖拉——「每一個圖德黨領導人，政府內部的每一個線人，軍事地下組織的每一個成員，伊朗的每一個克格勃官員和間諜」。庫濟奇金本人在伊斯蘭瑪巴德的一次祕密會議上出示了證據。
MI6 had also revealed their source and plans to the C.I.A. director William Casey. In 1982, Casey gave the operation “full-throated American support,” believing that he was thereby “signing the death warrant of the Communist Party in Iran,” Macintyre writes, which meant putting “the lives of several thousand people at risk.”
軍情六處還把他們的信源和計劃透露給中央情報局局長威廉·凱西。麥金泰爾寫道，1982年，凱西稱美國「全力支持」該行動，並且相信他的這一舉動等於是「簽署了伊朗共產黨的死刑令」，這意味著將「數千人的生命置於危險之中」。
Casey likely kept the C.I.A.’s involvement concealed not just from the Soviets but from his own government, Macintyre explains, so President Reagan could truly claim ignorance of the damning fact that the United States was publicly denouncing the Khomeini regime while secretly cooperating with it. “It is unclear,” Macintyre hedges, “whether Bill Casey sought or obtained Ronald Reagan’s authorization.”
麥金泰爾解釋說，凱西不僅向蘇聯隱瞞了中央情報局的參與，很可能也沒告知本國政府。這樣一來，雷根總統就可以真誠地聲稱，他對這個足以令他身敗名裂的事實——美國一邊公開譴責何梅尼政權、一邊暗中與之合作——是毫不知情的。「比爾·凱西是否尋求或獲得了羅納德·雷根的授權，」麥金泰爾含糊其辭地寫道，「尚不得而知。」
廣告
In Macintyre’s telling, the American spy chief was working from an interagency memo warning that the Soviets could take Iran in nine to 14 weeks, a scenario that raised the possibility of an American nuclear response. But not everyone shared the panic. Macintyre himself doubts that Moscow intended to invade. The evidence for such a plan was thin. The Tudeh Party was too weak to seize power on its own, and, in the short term at least, the Kremlin also had reasons to tolerate Khomeini: However hostile he was to communism, he was even more hostile to the United States.
根據麥金泰爾的講述，這位美國情報頭目當時依據的是一份跨部門備忘錄，該備忘錄警告稱，蘇聯可能在九到14周內拿下伊朗，這種情況甚至可能引發美國的核打擊。但並非所有人都認同這種恐慌情緒。麥金泰爾本人就對莫斯科有意入侵表示懷疑。證明此類計劃的證據十分薄弱。圖德黨力量太弱，無法僅憑一己之力奪取政權；而且，至少在短期內，克里姆林宮也有理由容忍何梅尼：儘管仇視共產主義，他對美國的敵意更深。
To forestall a danger that might have been largely imaginary, between 6,000 and 10,000 Tudeh members were apprehended and locked up, Macintyre claims, and hundreds or perhaps thousands of people died. Tudeh leaders were tortured, forced into televised confessions, and, in some cases, executed or lynched. A far wider wave of killings followed in 1988, when Khomeini issued a fatwa against those who “make war against God,” and prisoners who had already completed their sentences were put to death. “The exact Tudeh death toll may never be known,” Macintyre writes. “The executioners were not counting.”
麥金泰爾稱，為了防範一個很大程度上可能是臆想出來的危險，有6000到10000名圖德黨員被逮捕關押，數百甚至數千人喪生。圖德黨的領導人遭到酷刑折磨，被迫在電視上認罪，其中一些人甚至被處決或私刑處死。1988年，隨著何梅尼發布針對那些「向真主開戰」之人的法特瓦，一場更大規模的殺戮接踵而至，甚至連已經服刑完畢的囚犯也被處死。「圖德黨確切的死亡人數可能永遠無人知曉，」麥金泰爾寫道。「行刑者們根本沒有進行統計。」
Macintyre’s verdict is that “the REDWOOD case was both a success and a failure, a ploy that ended in victory and defeat, a significant achievement and a grave mistake, proof of the fickle pattern of unintended consequences.” By stoking the Islamic Republic’s paranoia and brutality and helping the mullahs to consolidate power, America and Britain, he writes, “inadvertently helped create the monster” they now condemn.
麥金泰爾的結論是：「紅杉案既是一次成功也是一次失敗，是一個以勝利和失敗告終的計劃，是一項重大成就也是一個嚴重錯誤，印證了意想不到的後果是何其難以捉摸。」他寫道，通過煽動伊斯蘭共和國的偏執與殘暴、幫助毛拉們鞏固權力，美英兩國「無意中幫助製造了他們現在所譴責的怪物」。
Macintyre’s larger point — that the Iran of 2026 was not foreseeable from Century House in 1982 — is unarguable, and his parallel to the arming of the Mujahedeen in Afghanistan is well made.
麥金泰爾的核心觀點——即1982年身處「世紀大樓」的人無法預見2026年的伊朗——是毋庸置疑的，而且他將其與武裝阿富汗聖戰者的做法相提並論，也非常貼切。
But, of course, the consequences were foreseen, at least by some of the people who carried it out. Two senior officials said they would resign. One of them predicted that the people named in the REDWOOD files would meet “grisly ends.” British agents anticipated the crackdown. They were told, and yet they proceeded.
但是，這些後果當然是被預見到了的，至少被參與執行的某些人預見到了。兩名高級官員表示他們將辭職。其中一人預測，紅杉檔案中點名的人「下場會很慘」。英國特工也預見到了隨後的鎮壓。他們得到了警告，但還是繼續執行了計劃。
This doesn’t mean that Casey and the MI6 members who backed the plan were cleareyed about its prospects for success. By Cold War logic, the liquidation of a bunch of communists barely mattered, especially if it could keep Iran from becoming a Soviet satellite.
這並不意味著凱西及支持該計劃的軍情六處成員，對其成功的可能性抱有清醒的認識。按照冷戰邏輯，一群共產主義者被消滅根本無足輕重，特別是如果這能阻止伊朗淪為蘇聯的衛星國的話。
廣告
One of Macintyre’s more audacious conclusions is that had Viagra existed in 1981, Kuzichkin might not have defected, and there might have been a successful Tudeh coup after all with the help of Soviet firepower. The Cold War might have ended differently, or catastrophically.
麥金泰爾更為大膽的推論之一是，如果1981年就有「威而鋼」，庫濟奇金可能就不會叛逃，那麼在蘇聯火力的支持下，圖德黨或許最終能發動一場成功的政變。冷戰可能會因此有一個不同的——甚至是災難性的——結局。
It is a startling counterfactual, and one somewhat at odds with Macintyre’s own skepticism about the Soviet threat. In fact, the Tudeh Party wasn’t popular, even within Iran’s fractured left, especially after the party joined a crackdown against independent secular and Islamist leftists who turned against the ayatollah in the immediate aftermath of the revolution. And, despite this show of loyalty, the Islamic Republic was soon closing off the political space in which the Tudeh Party operated. By the early 1980s, its newspaper had been shut down and its official protection was wavering. Given the party’s isolation and the regime’s growing grip on power, a Tudeh takeover was far from inevitable.
這是一個令人震驚的反事實假設也在某種程度上與麥金泰爾自己對蘇聯威脅的懷疑相矛盾。事實上，圖德黨並不受歡迎，甚至在伊朗四分五裂的左翼陣營中也是如此，特別是在革命剛結束後，該党參與鎮壓了那些轉而反對阿亞圖拉的獨立世俗派和伊斯蘭左派人士。而且，儘管表現出了這種忠誠，伊斯蘭共和國很快還是開始擠壓圖德黨的政治運作空間。到了20世紀80年代初，該黨的報紙已被查封，它所獲得的官方保護地位也岌岌可危。考慮到該黨的孤立處境以及政權日益穩固的統治，圖德黨奪取政權的可能性並不大。
The book’s last pages are its saddest. Resettled as Alexander Larkin, a British citizen, Kuzichkin drank, ranted about hit men and was once picked up naked at a Somerset service station. One evening in 2009 he sat down with a bottle of vodka and the film “Amadeus,” a story of betrayal. By morning there was nothing left in the bottle, and Kuzichkin was dead. (As of this writing, Wikipedia still has him living.) “In his cups,” Macintyre reports, he grieved the people he had helped send to their graves. The defector reached a harsher verdict than his historian does. But only one of them was there.
全書的最後幾頁尤其令人神傷。庫濟奇金以英國公民亞歷山大·拉金這個身份得到重新安置，但他酗酒成性，常咆哮著有人要暗殺他，甚至有一次赤身裸體地在薩默塞特郡的一個服務站被警察帶走。2009年的一個晚上，他拿著一瓶伏特加坐下，看了關於背叛的電影《莫扎特傳》(Amadeus)。到了第二天早上，酒瓶空了，庫濟奇金也死了。（在撰寫本文時，維基百科仍顯示他在世。）麥金泰爾述稱，「酒過三巡」的庫濟奇金常為那些被他送進墳墓的人感到悲痛。這位叛逃者對自己的評判要比為他作傳的歷史學家更嚴厲。但畢竟，他們中只有一個人親歷了這一切。
