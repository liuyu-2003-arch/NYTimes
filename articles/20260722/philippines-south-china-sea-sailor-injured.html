菲律賓稱該國一名水兵被中國海警打傷

Philippines Says Sailor Was Injured in Skirmish With Chinese Coast Guard

MAX KIM • 2026-07-22
The Philippine military accused China’s coast guard of injuring one of its sailors in a scuffle that broke out on Monday in the South China Sea, where the two countries have been locked in a decades-long territorial dispute.
菲律賓軍方指控中國海警在週一於南中國海發生的一場衝突中打傷了一名菲律賓水兵。因領土爭議，中菲兩國在這一海域已對峙數十年。
The episode began when an inflatable Chinese coast guard boat approached the Sierra Madre, a beached World War II-era warship that the Philippines uses as a military outpost in a disputed reef, and lashed out at the Philippine sailors who approached in a boat to chase them away, the Armed Forces of the Philippines said in a statement.
菲律賓武裝部隊在聲明中表示，事件始於一艘中國海警充氣艇駛近「馬德雷山」號——這是一艘擱淺在爭議礁石上的二戰時期軍艦，菲律賓將其用作軍事哨所——並對乘船靠近試圖驅離他們的菲律賓海軍人員進行了攻擊。
The statement added that a Philippine sailor had been struck on the head with a wooden baton in the ensuing struggle, necessitating medical care.
聲明還稱，在隨後發生的衝突中，一名菲律賓水兵頭部遭木棍擊中，需要接受醫療救治。
The Philippines’ Department of National Defense decried the incident as another act of “harassment” by the Chinese coast guard that added to a “pattern of provocative and hostile behavior,” in a statement of Monday. “We simply cannot trust this belligerent neighbor.”
菲律賓國防部在週一的聲明中譴責該事件是中國海警的又一次「騷擾」行為，進一步體現了其「挑釁和敵對行為模式」。聲明寫道：「我們根本無法信任這個好戰的鄰國。」
廣告
The Chinese coast guard has disputed that version of events, saying that the Philippine sailors rammed their patrol boat first before attacking its personnel with “oars, long sticks, and other tools.”
中國海警則對菲律賓方面的說法提出異議，稱菲律賓水兵首先駕船衝撞中國海警巡邏艇，隨後又使用「划槳、長棍等工具」攻擊中方人員。
Accusing the Philippines of spreading propaganda, China’s foreign ministry said it had summoned the Philippine ambassador in Beijing over the incident, according to a statement published by state media on Tuesday. President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. of the Philippines has done the same to China’s ambassador in Manila.
根據中國官方媒體週二發布的聲明，中國外交部指責菲律賓散布虛假宣傳，並表示已就此事召見菲律賓駐華大使。菲律賓總統費迪南德·馬科斯也召見了中國駐菲律賓大使。
While several countries have longstanding territorial claims in the South China Sea, the dispute between China and the Philippines, a U.S. treaty ally, has been the most fraught.
儘管多個國家一直以來對南中國海提出領土主張，但中國與美國的條約盟友菲律賓之間的爭端最為激烈。
Tensions in the region have steadily increased in recent years, with the Philippines denouncing the encroachment of Chinese vessels, which they say have used water cannons or lasers to harass Philippine sailors in the area. In another incident at the Second Thomas Shoal — where the Sierra Madre is beached — in 2024, a Filipino soldier lost a finger after a Chinese boat rammed his vessel.
近年來，該地區的緊張局勢持續升溫，菲律賓譴責中國船隻侵犯領土，並使用水炮或雷射騷擾菲律賓船員。2024年發生在「馬德雷山」號的擱淺地點第二托馬斯淺灘（中國稱為仁愛礁）的另一起事件中，一艘中國船隻撞擊菲律賓船隻，導致一名菲方士兵失去一根手指。
The latest flare-up came just before the start of a series of high-level diplomatic meetings hosted by the Association of Southeast Asian Nations in Manila, where, among other agenda items like the wars in Myanmar and Iran, negotiations to ease tensions in the South China Sea are expected to continue. The gathering, which will conclude Thursday, is being attended by both Secretary of State Marco Rubio and China’s foreign minister, Wang Yi.
最新衝突發生在東盟在馬尼拉主辦的一系列高級別外交會議即將召開之前。除了緬甸和伊朗戰爭等議程外，預計各方還將繼續就緩解南海緊張局勢展開談判。會議將於週四結束，美國國務卿魯比奧和中國外長王毅均出席會議。
The Philippines, the current chair of the 11-member ASEAN, has made establishing a legally binding code of conduct to prevent escalations in the South China Sea a key priority for its tenure, which concludes at the end of this year. The code was proposed decades ago and Philippine officials have voiced optimism about recent progress. But intractable differences have loomed.
作為現任東盟11個成員國的輪值主席國，菲律賓把制定一項具有法律約束力的南中國海行為準則列為任期內的重要目標，希望藉此防止局勢升級。菲律賓的主席國任期將於年底結束。制定準則的構想早在數十年前就已提出，菲律賓官員對近期進展表示樂觀，但各方之間仍存在難以調和的分歧。
廣告
Chief among them is the landmark ruling by an international tribunal that dismissed most of China’s sweeping territorial claims in the South China Sea in 2016, including those on the area marked by the Sierra Madre. While there was no mechanism for enforcing the ruling, it was widely regarded as a major victory for the Philippines, which currently sees it as a legal base line for any maritime code of conduct. China, which refused to participate in the arbitration, has steadfastly rejected it.
其中最大的分歧是國際仲裁庭於2016年作出的一項具有里程碑意義的裁決。該裁決否定了中國在南中國海提出的廣泛領土主張中的大部分，其中也包括「馬德雷山」號所在海域。雖然這一裁決缺乏執行機制，但普遍被視為菲律賓的一次重大勝利。菲律賓目前認為，這項裁決應成為未來任何南中國海行為準則的法律基礎。而拒絕參與仲裁的中國則始終拒絕承認這一裁決。
The decision provided fodder for another dispute earlier this month, when, in response to the Philippines’ commemoration of its 10th anniversary, Chinese state media posted a video depicting the Philippines as a monkey doing the bidding of Japan and the United States.
這一裁決本月早些時候再次引發爭議。當菲律賓紀念該裁決發表十週年之際，中國官方媒體發布了一段影片，將菲律賓描繪成一隻受日本和美國擺布的猴子。
While the United States does not have any territorial claims of its own in the South China Sea, it conducts regular joint military drills there with the Philippines, with which it has a mutual defense treaty. Beijing has denounced U.S. involvement as a major source of tensions in the region.
雖然美國在南中國海沒有提出任何領土主張，但它與菲律賓簽有共同防禦條約，雙方定期在這裡舉行聯合軍事演習。北京譴責美國的介入是該地區緊張局勢的主要根源。
The U.S. State Department criticized what it called “dangerous and aggressive actions” by China in a statement on the incident on Monday.
美國國務院在週一發表聲明，批評中國的所謂「危險和侵略性行為」。
In an opinion article published by Philippine media outlets ahead of the ASEAN meeting, Mr. Rubio reiterated U.S. support for the 2016 ruling, writing that “we will honor our obligations to the Philippines and other allies as they confront new and coercive threats to our shared interests.” But he stopped short of singling out China by name.
在東盟會議召開前夕，魯比奧在菲律賓媒體發表的一篇觀點文章中重申，美國支持2016年的國際仲裁裁決。他寫道：「當菲律賓以及其他盟友面對針對我們共同利益的新脅迫性威脅時，我們將履行對他們的義務。」不過，他並未點名中國。
