從黑暗之海到自由之海

A Family’s Daring Escape From North Korea ‘to Live and Stand Tall’

CHOE SANG-HUN • 2026-07-22
Rain lashed a flimsy wooden boat as it tossed through a churning sea, its engine straining against the swell. Under the cover of a stormy night three years ago, the vessel was fleeing North Korea.
三年前一個風雨交加的夜晚，一艘簡陋的木船在洶湧的海浪中顛簸，雨水劈頭蓋臉地打來，發動機在涌浪中吃力轟鳴。藉著夜色的掩護，這艘船正逃離朝鮮。
On board were nine people, including two small children and a woman who was six months pregnant. Only a couple of the passengers had seafaring experience. They eluded a patrol ship, speeding across the world’s most heavily guarded maritime border.
船上載著九個人，包括兩個幼童和一名懷孕六個月的婦女。其中只有兩三個人有航海經驗。他們躲過了一艘巡邏船，加速穿越了世界上戒備最為森嚴的海上邊界。
Soon after their boat lurched into South Korean waters, a high-speed warship of the South’s navy bore down on it, tearing the water in aggressive circles. Snipers on the vessel trained their laser sights on the chests of the three adult men on the wooden boat.
就在木船搖搖晃晃駛入韓國海域後不久，韓國海軍的一艘高速戰艦便迅速逼近，繞著木船劃出咄咄逼人的圓弧。戰艦上的狙擊手將雷射瞄準器鎖定在木船上三名成年男子的胸口。
Through a loudspeaker, a voice boomed: “Are you lost? Engine trouble?”
擴音器中傳來洪亮的聲音：「你們是迷航了嗎？還是發動機出故障了？」
廣告
The North Koreans shouted: “Please help. We are defecting.”
船上的朝鮮人大喊：「請幫幫我們。我們是來投奔韓國的。」
The red dots remained stubbornly fixed on their chests. The South Koreans were taking no chances: The three men were clad in North Korean military uniforms, camouflage for a high-stakes escape. Desperate, the men shouted into the boat’s cramped hold for the other passengers to emerge in order to prove that they were civilians. From below, the two children, a 5-year-old girl and 3-year-old boy, were hauled up first, both pale and wasted from seasickness. Only then did the red dots drop off.
然而，那幾個紅色雷射點依然牢牢停留在他們胸前。韓國方面絲毫不敢掉以輕心，因為這三名男子都穿著朝鮮軍裝，那是他們為了這場生死逃亡而準備的偽裝。情急之下，他們朝狹小的底艙裡大喊，讓其他人出來，以此證明他們都是平民。最先被拉上來的是兩個孩子——一個五歲的女孩和一個三歲的男孩，兩人因暈船而臉色蒼白、有氣無力的樣子。直到這時，那幾個紅點才消失。
The North Koreans, led by Kim Il-hyeok and his younger brother, Yi-hyeok, were bound together by blood and marriage. The brothers, both in their 30s at the time, were accompanied by their mother and their families: Il-heyok’s partner, who was pregnant, and Yi-hyeok’s wife, their two children, his mother-in-law and brother-in-law.
這群朝鮮人由金日赫（音）和弟弟金李赫（音）率領，他們之間或者是血緣關係，或者是姻親關係。當時兄弟倆都三十多歲，同行的還有他們的母親以及各自的家人：金日赫懷有身孕的女友，金李赫的妻子、兩個孩子、岳母和妻弟。
The family, who defected in 2023, were among the few who managed to escape North Korea after its leader, Kim Jong-un, imposed a crushing lockdown during the coronavirus pandemic. They arrived in their adopted country with rare insights into a nation that had been choked off from the outside world. Some details of their escape have not been previously reported.
這一大家子於2023年脫北，是朝鮮領導人金正恩在新冠疫情期間實施嚴厲封鎖後少數成功逃離朝鮮的人之一。他們來到新的家園，也帶來了一個長期與外界隔絕國家的第一手見聞。他們出逃的部分細節此前從未公開報導過。
“That night,” said Yi-hyeok’s wife, Yu-mi, “we crossed from a sea of darkness to a sea of freedom.”
「那天晚上，」金李赫的妻子柳美（音）說，「我們從黑暗之海穿越到了自由之海。」
A Father’s Wish
父親的心願
Over several months of interviews in South Korea, Il-hyeok ​spoke with the same swagger that he had developed as a self-described “troublemaker” in North Korea. Yu-mi ​was reserved, pulling her legs tightly to her chest as she spoke. Her mother, who asked not to be named but agreed to be photographed, weighed every word, fearing how her comments might affect relatives left behind in the North. But they all spoke with conviction about why they had to leave North Korea. (The rest of the extended family declined to be interviewed.)
在韓國接受持續數月的採訪期間，金日赫說話時仍帶著那股他自認在朝鮮就是個「刺頭」時練就的那股桀驁勁。柳美則顯得拘謹，說話時總是雙腿緊緊蜷在胸前。她的母親同意接受採訪和拍照，但要求不透露姓名，她每說一句話都十分謹慎，擔心自己的言論會影響仍留在朝鮮的親人。然而，他們都以堅定的語氣講述著自己為何不得不離開朝鮮。（其餘的家族成員則拒絕接受採訪。）
廣告
The brothers came from Pyoksong, a county in North Korea’s southwest.
兄弟倆來自朝鮮西南部的碧城郡。
During the famine of the late 1990s, their father, a railway security official, returned with grim accounts from the hardest-hit provinces: The emaciated bodies of children found outside train stations were frozen solid, thumping like frozen fish when they were tossed onto trucks headed to burial grounds. He was the first in the family to lose faith in a ruling class that was unable to feed its people.
20世紀90年代末大饑荒期間，他們的父親是鐵路系統的安全員。他從饑荒最嚴重的省份回來時，講述了令人毛骨悚然的見聞：火車站外面那些餓死的兒童瘦骨嶙峋，早已凍得僵硬，被扔上運往墓地的卡車時，像凍魚一樣發出悶響。父親是家中第一個對這個連溫飽都無法保障的統治階級失去信心的人。
One night after his sons reached adulthood, he took them to a hill and pointed toward a cluster of lights across the South Korean border.
在兩個兒子成年後的某個夜晚，他帶著他們來到一座山坡上，指著韓國那側密集的燈火。
“That’s where we should go,” he said. “To live and stand tall.”
「我們應該去那裡，」他說，「去活出個人樣。」
Yu-mi lived in the ​nearby coastal town of Kangryong. From there, she could see planes glinting in the sun as they rose from Incheon International Airport, just across the water — a shimmering vision of possibility, yet unreachable for North Koreans barred from travel and cut off from the internet.
柳美住在附近的沿海小鎮康翎。在那裡，她能看到對岸仁川國際機場起飛的飛機在陽光下閃閃發光——那是一幅閃耀著無限可能的景象，卻是被禁止出國、與互聯網隔絕的朝鮮人永遠無法觸及的世界。
“Our country felt like a prison without bars,” she said.
「我們的國家就像一座沒有圍牆的監獄，」她說。
Beginning in kindergarten, children sang the anthem “We Have Nothing to Envy,” even as they were mobilized to haul river water or scour hills for herbs and pine nuts.​ After long workdays, adults attended compulsory “life review sessions,” writing self-criticism and pledges of loyalty. When Kim Jong-un visited ​an island near Yu-mi’s town in 2013, soldiers and villagers swarmed into the sea in jubilation. She knew the scene was staged; ordinary residents, she said, had been banned from leaving home.
從幼稚園開始，孩子們便要唱《世上沒啥可羨慕》這首歌曲，同時還要組織去河邊挑水，或到山上采草藥、撿松子。成年人結束一天的勞動後，還必須參加「生活檢討會」，寫自我批評和效忠保證書。2013年金正恩視察柳美老家附近的一座島嶼時，士兵和村民們紛紛湧入海中歡呼。她清楚這不過是在作秀——據她說，普通民眾那天根本不許離開家門。
In the face of the propaganda, a contraband culture spread. South Korean films and dramas circulated on USB drives smuggled from China. In Kangryong, the devices also arrived inside plastic bottles released by South Korean activists across the water, along with rice and mini-Bibles. Officials warned the bottles contained poison, but South Korean entertainment was winning.
面對官方宣傳，走私文化悄然蔓延。韓國電影和電視劇通過從中國走私進來的USB隨身碟在民間流傳。在康翎，這些USB隨身碟也會隨著韓國的活動人士從對岸投放的塑料瓶一起漂過來，瓶中還有大米和袖珍版《聖經》。官方警告大家瓶子裡有毒，但韓國的娛樂節目依然深受歡迎。
廣告
Il-hyeok idolized K-pop groups like Girls’ Generation and styled his hair in a mohawk like BigBang’s Taeyang. He was arrested for “capitalist delinquency” and released only after paying a bribe, his hair razored. He and his friends once secretly watched “Valkyrie,” the Tom Cruise film about the plot to kill Hitler. When the conspirators failed and were executed, his friends wept.
大哥日赫崇拜「少女時代」等韓流組合，還把頭髮剃成了BigBang樂隊成員太陽的同款莫西乾造型。他因「資本主義墮落行為」被捕，行賄後才獲釋，頭髮被剃光。他和朋友曾偷偷觀看湯姆·克魯斯主演的電影《行動目標希特勒》。當刺殺計劃失敗、參與者被處決時，他的朋友們都哭了。
The brothers’ notion of deserting North Korea felt just as perilous. Their father forbade them from marrying, reasoning that the larger the family, the greater the risk of being caught while defecting. He then used connections to transfer Yi-hyeok, his youngest, to Kangryong, where he learned navigation, mapped guard posts, and cultivated ties with officials. Yi-hyeok became a helmet diver, who harvests seafood from the ocean floor, breathing fresh air piped through a surface hose. He eventually met Yu-mi.
兄弟倆逃離朝鮮的念頭聽起來同樣兇險萬分。父親不許他們結婚，認為家庭成員越多，逃跑時暴露的風險就越大。隨後他利用關係將小兒子調往康翎，在那裡他學習了航海技術，繪製了崗哨分布圖，並結交官員。金李赫成為了一名潛水員，在海底捕撈海產，靠連接水面的輸氣管呼吸。後來，他遇到了柳美。
Il-hyeok worked in North Korea’s shadow economy, bribing his way into opportunity and out of trouble. He butchered animals for restaurants, ran a small gold mine and bought rice stolen from state granaries, reselling it at a profit. He also grew disillusioned with the system he was gaming.
大哥日赫一直在朝鮮的地下經濟中謀生，靠行賄獲得機會，也靠行賄擺脫麻煩。他曾為餐館屠宰牲畜，經營一個小金礦，還收購從國家糧庫偷出來的大米，再轉手獲利。他也越來越厭倦這個自己一直在設法鑽空子的制度。
Then, in 2014, their father died unexpectedly. The South Korean dream seemed to die with him.
然而，到了2014年，他們的父親突然去世。去往韓國的夢想似乎也隨之消散。
Where Il-hyeok, the older brother, was restless, Yi-hyeok was steady. He married Yu-mi in 2017, and the couple soon had their two children. He joined a military-run fisheries company, operating three vessels. Despite a United Nations ban, Chinese buyers worked openly in Kangryong, and on a good day Yi-hyeok could earn more than a typical worker​’s annual salary.
大哥日赫性格不安分，小弟李赫則沉穩踏實。2017年，李赫與柳美成婚，很快就有了兩個孩子。他加入了一家軍隊經營的漁業公司，負責三艘漁船。儘管聯合國明令禁止，中國買家仍公開活躍在康翎，運氣好的日子，他一天的收入就能超過普通工人一年的薪水。
Il-hyeok began dating a kindergarten teacher. Defection was no longer a priority.
金日赫開始與一名幼稚園教師談戀愛。逃離朝鮮不再是當務之急。
廣告
“When I mentioned it, my brother told me to watch my mouth,” Il-hyeok recalled.
「每當我提起這件事，弟弟都會讓我閉嘴，」日赫回憶道。
The pandemic overturned everything.
然而，疫情改變了一切。
Lockdown
封鎖
North Korea imposed one of the world’s harshest lockdowns. In Kangryong, access to the sea was closed off with barbed wire and armed guards. Yi-hyeok’s boats rusted and his debts mounted. Officials claimed that South Korea was dumping infected bodies and waste into border waters. Yet boats supplying seafood to the top tiers of society continued to fish.
朝鮮實施了全球最嚴厲的疫情封鎖措施之一。在康翎，通往海邊的道路被鐵絲網和武裝警衛封鎖。金李赫的漁船日漸鏽蝕，債務越積越多。官方宣稱，韓國向邊境海域傾倒帶有病毒的屍體和垃圾。但與此同時，專門供應高層海鮮的漁船卻照常出海。
“The elites still ate whatever they wanted,” Yu-mi said.
「那些有權有勢的照樣想吃什麼就吃什麼，」柳美說。
Public executions grew more frequent, targeting those accused of committing violent crimes like murder, which surged during the lockdown, or of distributing “nonsocialist” content such as South Korean dramas. Thousands, including the brothers, were forced to watch as rifle fire cracked across the execution grounds. The crowds dispersed in silence.
公開處決越來越頻繁，處決對象包括那些被控犯有謀殺等暴力犯罪的人（這類案件在封鎖期間激增），以及傳播韓劇等「非社會主義內容」的人。包括兄弟倆在內，數千人被強迫觀看處決，槍聲在刑場不斷響起。人群散去時，一片寂靜。
廣告
“The goal was to instill fear,” Il-hyeok said. “To erase any fantasy about the outside world.”
「目的就是製造恐懼，」大哥日赫說，「抹去人們對外部世界的一切幻想。」
The Escape
逃離
The brothers no longer argued about whether to leave — only how.
兩兄弟不再爭論要不要走，只商量怎麼走。
By early 2023, North Korea had begun easing its pandemic restrictions. Yi-hyeok’s boats were among the first permitted back to sea. But the window to escape was already closing. The authorities were preparing to install surveillance cameras along the coast to deter defections.
2023年初，朝鮮開始放寬疫情管控。金日赫的船是最早獲准重返大海的漁船之一。但出逃的窗口也在迅速關閉——當局準備在沿海岸線安裝監控攝像頭，以遏制脫北行為。
The brothers spoke in code over the phone. “Good weather” meant a storm was coming, forcing patrol boats back to port. “We’ve got a good catch — come down here” meant Il-hyeok should bring their mother and his girlfriend to Kangryong.
兄弟倆在電話裡用暗語交流。「天氣好」意思是暴風雨要來了，巡邏船不得不回港。「今天收穫不錯，下來吧」意思是讓大哥帶著母親和女友來康翎。
The weather and the catch were ideal the night of May 6, 2023.
2023年5月6日晚，天氣和漁獲都很理想。
Yu-mi’s mot​her, a 52-year-old widow, signed up last, just five hours before departure.
柳美52歲的寡母直到出發前五個小時才決定同行。
“I could die trying to escape,” she said. “But if my family reached the South and I stayed behind, I’d be as good as dead anyway — the relative of traitors.”
「我可能會死在逃跑的路上，」她說，「但如果家人都到了韓國，只剩我留在朝鮮，那我也跟死了沒什麼區別——我會成為叛徒的家屬。」
Yi-hyeok’s brother-in-law, his fishing partner, slipped away by boat and waited offshore. The rest of the family crossed a coastal hill strewn with land mines to reach a secluded beach. To carry Yi-hyeok’s two young children more easily, the brothers sealed them inside burlap sacks and hoisted them onto their shoulders. Before they closed the sacks, Yu-mi caught her children looking up at her.
金李赫的妻弟——他的捕魚搭檔——先悄然駕船出海，在近海等候。其餘家人翻越一座布滿地雷的海岸山丘，來到一處僻靜的海灘。為了方便，兄弟倆將弟弟李赫的兩個年幼的孩子裝進麻袋，扛在肩上。在麻袋合上之前，柳美看到孩子們抬頭看著她。
“I thought it might be the last time I’d ever see them,” she said. “I cried, and my husband teared up, too.”
「我當時想，這也許是我最後一次看見他們，」她說，「我哭了，丈夫也流下了眼淚。」
Yi-hyeok’s brother-in-law spotted the prearranged signal — a flashlight blinking three times from the beach — and steered the boat in to pick up the rest of the extended family. Around 10 p.m., the 30-foot-long vessel pulled away, its engine throttled down to little more than a brisk walking pace as it tried to slip quietly toward Yeonpyeong Island in South Korea, just nine miles away.
金李赫的妻弟看到了預先約定的信號——海灘上有手電筒閃三下——便駕船靠近接上大家。晚上10點左右，這艘約九米長的船駛離岸邊，引擎收小了油門，速度慢得幾乎跟人快步走差不多，他們希望船能悄無聲息地駛向僅14.5公里外的韓國延坪島。
Below deck, Yu-mi suddenly felt ​the engine roar to full throttle, filling the hold with black exhaust and leaving the women and children seasick. Her youngest child’s eyes rolled back. She carried him up for fresh air — and found the men on deck in a panic.
在艙底，柳美突然感到引擎全速轟鳴起來，黑色的廢氣湧入船艙，女人和孩子們相繼出現了暈船。最小的孩子翻起了白眼。她抱著孩子上來透氣，卻發現甲板上的男人們一片慌亂。
“That’s when I saw a searchlight from a North Korean patrol boat sweeping across the waves behind us,” she said. “My heart dropped.”
「就在那時，我看到一艘朝鮮巡邏船的探照燈，正掃過我們身後的海面，」她說，「我的心沉了下去。」
The brothers had already agreed on what to do if they couldn’t outrun a security vessel: strap life jackets and flashlights onto the women and children and send them into the sea, trusting the current to carry them south. The men would steer the boat away as a decoy, making a last stand with 20-inch knives and homemade red-pepper bombs. To lighten the boat, Il-hyeok threw anything heavy overboard — axes, wrenches, fishing net, his brother’s air compressor for helmet diving.
兩兄弟早已商量好，萬一甩不掉安全船該怎麼辦：給婦女和孩子套上救生衣，綁上手電筒，推進大海，依靠水流將他們帶向南方。男人們則把船朝另一個方向開，吸引追兵，用半米長的刀和自製的辣椒炸彈做最後的抵抗。為了減輕載重，大哥日赫將所有重物扔進了大海——斧頭、扳手、漁網，還有弟弟的潛水用空氣壓縮機。
Gradually, the searchlight behind them faded. The brothers later speculated that the fishing net they had thrown overboard had tangled in the pursuing boat’s propeller.
漸漸地，身後的探照燈消失了。兄弟倆後來推測，可能是他們丟下的漁網纏住了追船的螺旋槳。
Their two-hour gamble ended shortly after midnight, after the South Korean vessel stopped them and all nine family members were brought aboard.
這場歷時兩個小時的生死賭注在午夜過後不久畫上了句點——韓國艦艇攔下了他們，九名家庭成員全部被帶上了船。
An officer greeted them: “Welcome to paradise.”
一名軍官迎上前來：「歡迎來到天堂。」
New Beginning
新的開始
​In the South, Yu-mi studied to gain an accounting clerk certificate and found work at a school. Her brother enrolled in college, while Yi-hyeok returned to helmet-diving for seafood.
在韓國，柳美通過努力學習拿到了會計助理證書，在一所學校找到了工作。她的弟弟上了大學，金李赫則重操舊業，從事海鮮的潛水采撈工作。
“My brother joked the diving gear here was so advanced he could fall asleep underwater if he wanted to,” Il-hyeok recalled.
「我弟弟開玩笑說，這裡的潛水設備太先進了，他要是願意，甚至可以在海底睡覺。」大哥日赫回憶道。
Il-hyeok’s girlfriend gave birth to their first child in the South. The couple married in 2024 and soon welcomed another daughter. Il-hyeok is finding his footing in the South’s capitalist economy, trying part-time jobs at restaurants and operating forklifts and excavators.
金日赫的女友在韓國生下了他們的第一個孩子。2024年，兩人成婚，不久又迎來了一個女兒。他正在努力適應韓國的資本主義經濟，一邊在餐廳打零工，一邊開叉車和挖掘機。
The plunge into the South’s noisy democracy was overwhelming. Televisions offered endless channels — so different from the North, where only government broadcasts existed. Back home, Il-hyeok’s ​used motorbike was a prized status symbol​; now he found himself on highways crowded with modern cars. City squares rang with protests.
一開始，韓國喧鬧的民主社會令他們無所適從。電視有數不清的頻道，與朝鮮只有官方電視台形成鮮明對比。在朝鮮，金日赫那輛二手摩托車曾是令人艷羨的身份象徵，如今他卻置身於車水馬龍的高速路上。城市廣場上不斷舉行各種抗議活動。
Yu-mi’s mother marveled at faucets that produced both hot and cold water. On her first day working at a nursing home, ​she faltered when colleagues asked for “napkins” — another English word she had never heard. The brothers’ mother was scandalized by a TV drama in which a wife slapped her husband. And when Il-hyeok first ventured beyond Seoul, the South’s capital, he dutifully called a police detective to ask for permission. The detective only laughed.
柳美的母親驚訝地發現，水龍頭竟然既能放熱水，也能放冷水。她第一天去養老院上班時，同事讓她拿「napkins（餐巾紙）」，這個英語詞她從來沒聽過，一時不知所措。兄弟倆的母親則被一部電視劇裡妻子扇丈夫耳光的情節震驚了。金日赫第一次去首爾以外的地方時，還規規矩矩地給警察打電話，請求批准，結果對方大笑起來。
And then there were elections.
還有選舉。
In the North, ​people lined up in formal dress to receive a ballot with only one name. ​They placed ​their vote in the “yes” box with both hands and bowed deeply to portraits of Kim Jong-un’s father and grandfather, who ruled before him. No one dared approach the “no” box.​ When Il-hyeok and his wife voted in June as South Korean citizens, they found voters arriving in shorts and slippers, earphones in place. The ballot was a bewildering list of names.
在朝鮮，人們穿著正式服裝排隊領取選票，而選票上只有一個名字。他們必須雙手把選票投入「贊成」箱，並向朝鮮曾經的統治者——金正恩的父親和祖父的畫像深深鞠躬。沒有人敢靠近「反對」箱。而當金日赫和妻子今年6月作為韓國公民參加投票時，他們看到選民穿著短褲、拖鞋，戴著耳機就來投票，選票上密密麻麻印滿了候選人姓名，令他們眼花撩亂。
Yet, South Korea was no paradise.
然而，韓國並非天堂。
After 19 months in the South, Yi-hyeok died in a diving accident in late 2024.​ The brothers’ mother and Il-hyeok’s wife were diagnosed with cancer.
來到韓國19個月後，弟弟李赫於2024年底在一次潛水事故中身亡。兄弟倆的母親和日赫的妻子也相繼被診斷出癌症。
Il-hyeok vented frustration at Seoul’s soaring housing prices. He is saving every penny to move his family out of their cramped, government-subsidized rental apartment. Still, he insisted the defection was “100 percent” the right choice.​ Before his death, Yi-hyeok appeared on television, explaining that he fled to spare his children the indoctrination of North Korean schools. His daughter is now a second grader at a South Korean school, and his son is in kindergarten. The children adapted fastest to their new lives, quickly embracing South Korean staples like fried chicken and pizza.
金日赫對首爾飛漲的房價滿腹牢騷。他正拚命攢錢，希望帶家人搬離現在這套狹小的政府廉租房。但他仍堅持認為，這次逃離「百分之百」是正確的選擇。金李赫生前曾在電視節目中表示，他離開朝鮮是為了讓孩子免受學校的洗腦教育。如今，他的女兒在韓國讀小學二年級，兒子則在上幼稚園。兩個孩子適應新生活的速度最快，很快就愛上了炸雞和披薩等韓國日常美食。
“My husband gave me and our children the freedom he promised before he died,” Yu-mi said.
「我丈夫兌現了他生前的承諾，把自由留給了我和孩子們，」柳美說。
The brothers also honored their father’s final wish: When their boat fled North Korea, they carried his ashes with them.
兄弟倆也兌現了父親的遺願：他們的船逃離朝鮮時，帶上了父親的骨灰。

Choe Sang-Hun是時報駐首爾首席記者，報導韓國和朝鮮新聞。
