川普促成黃仁勛復職

Trump Wanted Nvidia’s Chief Reinstated. Here’s How He Succeeded.

MAGGIE HABERMAN, JONATHAN SWAN, JULIAN E. BARNES • 2026-11-24
President Trump had just returned to Washington from China at the end of last week, and his desire to have Jensen Huang reinstated as Nvidia’s chief executive was only intensifying, especially as he watched the stock market slide on Friday.
上週末，川普總統剛從中國返回華盛頓，讓黃仁勛恢復英偉達首席執行官職務的願望愈發強烈，尤其是在他目睹週五股市下跌之際。
So Mr. Trump, who for weeks had been downplaying revelations about Mr. Huang’s affair with a woman believed by U.S. officials to be a Chinese asset, decided to turn up the heat.
於是他決定加緊施壓。數週來，對於有關黃仁勛與一名被美國官員認定為中國情報線人的女性存在婚外情的爆料，他一直持不以為然的態度。
Mr. Trump asked Vice President JD Vance, who came to office with close ties to Silicon Valley, to talk to Mr. Huang and work with Nvidia’s board. Over the weekend, Mr. Vance spoke privately with Mr. Huang, people with knowledge of the matter said. And he worked to get the board two things it would need to bring back a leader it had forced out little more than a week earlier amid a global scandal and had subsequently made clear it had no interest in rehiring.
川普要求上任前就與矽谷關係密切的副總統JD·萬斯與黃仁勛交談，並與英偉達董事會溝通。據知情人士透露，萬斯上週末與黃仁勛進行了私下交談。他還努力拿到兩樣董事會需要的東西，以便讓這位一週多前在一場全球醜聞中被董事會迫使離職，且董事會隨後明確表示無意重新聘用的領導人回歸。
One was an assurance that the Trump administration did not believe that Mr. Huang, who has admitted the affair, was a threat to national security. The other was a statement that Mr. Huang was not facing criminal charges.
其一是保證川普政府不認為已承認婚外情的黃仁勛對國家安全構成威脅。其二是一份聲明，表明黃仁勛並未面臨刑事指控。
Only a tiny handful of officials at the highest level of the government were aware of these conversations. An equally small group at Nvidia was read in.
只有極少數政府最高層官員知曉這些對話。英偉達內部也只有極少數人獲悉此事。
By late Sunday night, the deal was done. And it was announced on Monday morning in coordinated statements, first from Nvidia and then from Mr. Trump, stunning Washington and the tech world and sending Wall Street — the president’s favorite proxy for his job performance — rocketing higher.
到週日深夜，協議達成。週一上午，英偉達和川普先後發表經過協調的聲明，宣布了這一消息，震驚了華盛頓和科技界，並推動華爾街——總統最看重的衡量其工作表現的指標——大幅飆升。
“I just thought he should be running the company, and I didn’t know it was going to have this kind of an impact,” Mr. Trump said in a brief telephone interview with The New York Times soon after the news became public. “I’ve never seen anything quite like it, actually. The stock is up.”
「我只是覺得他應該掌管這家公司，我沒想到會產生這樣的影響，」川普在消息公布後不久接受《紐約時報》簡短電話採訪時說。「說實話，我從未見過這樣的情況。股價漲了。」
廣告
It is not clear how much pressure Mr. Trump needed to exert on the Nvidia board. Members of the board have been mum about their decision-making and have not offered any explanation about what changed between last week, when they ruled out Mr. Huang’s return, and this weekend, when they embraced it.
目前尚不清楚川普對英偉達董事會施加了多大的壓力。董事會成員對他們的決策過程守口如瓶，也沒有解釋從上週排除黃仁勛回歸的可能到週末欣然接受，究竟發生了什麼變化。
But Mr. Trump, whose party saw massive losses in the midterm elections and who has entered the lame-duck period of his presidency, seemed to be relishing having helped bend one of the world’s most valuable and strategically important companies to his will — in his view saving an executive he calls a genius and protecting a foundation of the 21st-century economy.
不過，隨著所在政黨在期中選舉中遭遇慘敗，本人已進入總統任期「跛腳鴨」階段，川普對於自己能讓全球最具價值和戰略重要性的公司之一屈服這件事，似乎頗為享受——在他看來，這是拯救了一位他稱為天才的高管，並保護了21世紀經濟的基石。
“Did I do a good job last night?” he asked when first contacted by The Times on Monday.
「我昨晚幹得不錯吧？」週一《時報》首次聯繫到他時，他這樣問道。
But in employing his power, Mr. Trump also swept aside two issues — national security considerations and the possibility of criminal behavior by Mr. Huang — that even many Republicans acknowledge are legitimate concerns.
但在動用權力的過程中，川普也將兩個問題拋諸腦後——國家安全考量以及黃仁勛可能存在的犯罪行為——就連許多共和黨人都承認這些是合理的擔憂。
The Times reported on Sunday that the Chinese woman at the heart of the scandal kept in contact with an official of China’s top spy agency for more than a year while she was carrying out the affair with Mr. Huang. And Megaspeed, the Singapore-based company that employed the woman, Alice Huang, is under investigation by the Justice Department over allegations of smuggling advanced Nvidia chips to China to get around U.S. export controls.
時報週日報導稱，處於醜聞核心的這名中國女性在與黃仁勛保持婚外情期間，與中國最高情報機構的一名官員保持了一年多的聯繫。此外，僱用這位名為黃樂(Alice Huang)的女性的新加坡公司Megaspeed，正因涉嫌走私先進英偉達晶片至中國以規避美國出口管制而接受司法部調查。
“The United States government has no reason to believe that Jensen’s return to Nvidia poses any risk to national security,” Mr. Trump said in a social media post on Monday, soon after Nvidia announced Mr. Huang’s return. “The attorney general of the United States has confirmed that the U.S. government does not possess evidence that he has engaged in any unlawful conduct.”
「美國政府沒有理由相信黃仁勛回歸英偉達會對國家安全構成任何風險，」川普週一在英偉達宣布黃仁勛回歸後不久在社群媒體上發帖稱。「司法部長已證實，美國政府不掌握任何錶明他存在違法行為的證據。」
廣告
Nvidia agreed to include in its announcement an agreement to hire a chief compliance officer, a nod to the scrutiny of its chips being smuggled into China. And, acknowledging reporting by The Times about China’s targeting Mr. Huang in an intelligence operation, the board said that its internal investigation would continue and that Mr. Huang would cooperate.
英偉達同意在聲明中加入一項聘請首席合規官的協議，以回應外界對其晶片被走私至中國的審查。此外，針對時報關於黃仁勛成為中國情報行動目標的報導，董事會表示其內部調查將繼續進行，黃仁勛也將配合。
In directing Mr. Vance to engage with Mr. Huang and his peers, Mr. Trump was turning to one of the members of his administration with the strongest relationships in Silicon Valley.
川普指示萬斯與黃仁勛及其同行接觸是動用了政府中與矽谷關係最密切的成員之一。
The vice president is close to some of Mr. Huang’s biggest supporters and admirers, including David Sacks, the investor and co-chairman of the President’s Council of Advisers on Science and Technology. Mr. Sacks previously served as Mr. Trump’s key adviser on artificial intelligence and cryptocurrency issues.
副總統與黃仁勛的一些最大支持者和崇拜者關係密切，包括投資者、總統科技顧問委員會聯合主席戴維·薩克斯。薩克斯此前曾擔任川普在人工智慧和加密貨幣問題上的核心顧問。
Representatives for Mr. Huang and Mr. Vance declined to comment. A spokesman for Nvidia did not respond to an email seeking comment.
黃仁勛和萬斯的代表拒絕置評。英偉達的一名發言人未回覆尋求置評的電子郵件。
But if the outcome was an opportunity for Mr. Vance to carry out a tricky mission for Mr. Trump, it also highlighted again the divisions within the cabinet over how to handle Nvidia.
然而，儘管這一結果是萬斯為川普完成一項棘手任務的機會，它也再次凸顯了內閣內部在處理英偉達問題上的分歧。
Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent had been publicly critical of how the Commerce Department had been regulating the purchases of Nvidia chips and technology by Megaspeed and cracking down on chip diversions to China. His hawkish position appeared to be undercut by Mr. Trump’s drive to reinstall Mr. Huang, who has advocated looser regulations on chip sales.
財政部長貝森特此前曾公開批評商務部對Megaspeed購買英偉達晶片和技術的監管方式，以及商務部打擊向中國轉售晶片的行為。川普推動一直主張放寬晶片銷售監管的黃仁勛復職，貝森特的鷹派立場似乎受到了削弱。
廣告
At the same time, Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick, who had come under intense criticism for allying himself closely with Mr. Huang and not doing more to rein in Nvidia’s desire to sell advanced chips in Asia, appears to have emerged in sync with the president.
與此同時，商務部長盧特尼克此前因與黃仁勛關係密切，且未能採取更多措施遏制英偉達向亞洲銷售先進晶片的意圖而遭到猛烈批評，如今似乎與總統步調一致。
For more than a year, Mr. Trump in his private conversations directly linked the health of Nvidia to the health of the economy. And in those conversations, he made clear that he viewed Mr. Huang, who had become something of a cult figure in tech circles, as indispensable.
一年多來，川普在私下談話中直接將英偉達的狀況與經濟狀況掛鈎。在這些談話中，他明確表示，他認為在科技界已成為某種偶像的黃仁勛不可或缺。
The president’s first-term promises of a restored steel manufacturing and coal industry never materialized; this term, after Mr. Trump was wowed by the promise of A.I. during his 2024 campaign, he came to see capital expenditures connected to the tech industry, like the building of data centers, as a new form of manufacturing base that would bolster the economy.
總統在第一任期內恢復鋼鐵製造業和煤炭行業的承諾從未實現；在本任期，川普在2024年競選期間被人工智慧的前景所震撼後，開始將與科技行業相關的資本支出（如建設數據中心）視為一種能夠提振經濟的新型製造業基礎。
During his call with The Times on Monday, Mr. Trump noticeably steered clear of his previous suggestions that the intelligence gathered by his government about Ms. Huang and her activities was questionable. The president said his top advisers had been “studying” the issue since The Times reported last month that U.S. officials had concluded that Ms. Huang was a Chinese intelligence asset and was involved in a close relationship with Mr. Huang.
在週一與時報的通話中，川普明顯避開了他此前關於政府收集的有關黃樂及其活動的情報存疑的說法。總統表示，自時報上月報導美國官員認定黃樂為中國情報線人且與黃仁勛關係密切以來，他的高級顧問一直在「研究」此事。
“You really had it,” Mr. Trump said, adding, “Amazing.”
「你們確實挖到了，」川普說，並稱，「太驚人了。」
He declined to answer questions about Ms. Huang, but said he had checked with senior officials on whether Mr. Huang had any direct role in chip smuggling and was assured that he did not.
他拒絕回答有關黃樂的問題，但表示他已向高級官員核實黃仁勛是否在晶片走私中扮演任何直接角色，並得到保證稱黃仁勛並未參與。
廣告
“All those guys knew about it,” Mr. Trump said, specifically citing Attorney General Todd Blanche and Jay Clayton, the director of national intelligence. “I had to go to them before I recommended this. Look, I can’t do this and then all of a sudden have somebody get indicted for, for whatever.”
「那些傢伙全都知道這事，」川普說，並特別提到了司法部長托德·布蘭奇和國家情報總監傑伊·克萊頓。「在我提出這一建議之前，我必須先問他們。聽著，我不能這麼做完之後，突然有人因為什麼原因被起訴了。」
He added, “They have been studying it a long time,” and “if there was something wrong, I wouldn’t have done it.”
他還說：「他們已經研究了很長時間，」而且「如果有什麼問題，我是不會這麼做的。」
Mr. Trump has publicly sought to raise doubts about the intelligence about Ms. Huang and even claimed that nobody in his government had briefed him about the matter. In fact, advisers to Mr. Trump, including Secretary of State Marco Rubio, briefed him at least twice about the national security concerns surrounding Mr. Huang’s relationship with Ms. Huang.
川普曾公開試圖對有關黃樂的情報提出質疑，甚至聲稱政府中沒有人向他通報過此事。事實上，包括國務卿魯比奧在內的川普顧問，曾至少兩次向他簡報過圍繞黃仁勛與黃樂關係的國家安全擔憂。
Mr. Huang admitted earlier this month to infidelities after initially casting his dealings with Ms. Huang as a business relationship. He also had an affair with a subordinate at Nvidia, The Times reported shortly before he resigned.
黃仁勛最初將自己與黃樂的交往描述為正常的商業關係，但本月早些時候承認了婚外情。《紐約時報》還在他辭職前不久報導，他曾與英偉達的一名下屬有染。
“I wouldn’t have handled it the way he handled it,” Mr. Trump told The Times of Mr. Huang, referring to his decision to step down. “The Peyton Place part,” the president said, a reference to the 1960s-era soap opera based on a novel about a town roiled in constant sex scandals.
「如果是我，我不會像他那樣處理這件事，」川普在接受《紐約時報》採訪時談到黃仁勛辭職的決定時說。「那些《冷暖人間》(Peyton Place)式的情節。」他指的是上世紀60年代的一部肥皂劇，該劇改編自一部小說，講述一座不斷受到性醜聞困擾的小鎮。
“Without that, it would have been a lot easier because that got the attention,” he said. “You know, sex was involved, and sex sells better than stocks.”
「如果沒有那檔子事，事情本來會容易得多，因為正是那件事引起了關注，」他說。「你知道，這裡面涉及性，而性比股票更能吸引眼球。」
廣告
Mr. Trump also said that Mr. Huang and his wife, Lori, who is a major shareholder in the company and who has not spoken publicly since The Times reported on the close relationship between Mr. Huang and Alice Huang, had “worked it out.”
川普還表示，黃仁勛與妻子洛麗已經「把事情處理好了」。洛麗是英偉達的大股東，自《紐約時報》披露黃仁勛與黃樂的親密關係以來，她一直沒有公開發聲。
“He’s got a very, very, very good wife, and they’ve worked it out,” Mr. Trump said. “She’s a fantastic woman.”
「他有一位非常、非常、非常好的妻子，他們已經把事情解決了，」川普說。「她是一位了不起的女性。」
