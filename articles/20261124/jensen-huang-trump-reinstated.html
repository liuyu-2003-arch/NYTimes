對川普來說，科技股價比中國間諜風險更重要

With Jensen Huang, Trump Strikes Another Blow for Billionaire Impunity

MICHELLE GOLDBERG • 2026-11-24
Jensen Huang, the Nvidia chief executive ousted amid a spying scandal and then shockingly reinstated on Monday, has given new meaning to the phrase too big to fail.
英偉達首席執行官黃仁勛在間諜醜聞中下台、又在週一令人震驚地復職，他為「大而不能倒」這一說法賦予了新的含義。
In recent days, there has been a cascade of revelations about the scale of the national security breach Huang created with his affair with Alice Huang, the businesswoman who U.S. intelligence agencies believe is a Chinese spy. Nvidia dominates the production of advanced A.I. chips, and The New York Times reported that China’s chief spy agency made acquiring such chips a priority. The Times found that Alice Huang was part of a network that funneled chips to China’s military. While she was sleeping with Jensen, she was in contact with an officer from China’s Ministry of State Security.
關於黃仁勛因與女商人黃樂(Alice Huang)——美國情報機構認為她是中國間諜——的婚外情所造成國家安全漏洞之嚴重，近日不斷有新的披露。英偉達主導著先進人工智慧晶片的生產，《紐約時報》報導稱，中國主要情報機構已將獲取此類晶片列為優先事項。時報發現，黃樂屬於一個向中國軍方輸送晶片的網路。在與黃仁勛保持親密關係期間，她一直與中國國家安全部的一名官員保持聯繫。
All this suggests that Jensen Huang was somehow involved, inadvertently or otherwise, in a Chinese plot to evade American restrictions on the sophisticated chips at the heart of the A.I. boom, which China has not figured out how to manufacture itself. As the contours of this scandal were coming into focus, Americans have been getting a visceral sense of the way their lives can be upended by advanced A.I. systems, especially if they fall into the wrong hands. Over the past two weeks, airplanes were grounded, banking was disrupted, many people couldn’t use their email, and an internal communications network at the National Security Agency went down. Most of these outages seem to be the result of A.I. agents revealing security vulnerabilities that needed to be hurriedly patched. But while they were not, at least as far as we know, caused by cyberattacks, they still demonstrated how easily a malign actor could use A.I. to destabilize our society.
這一切表明，黃仁勛在某種程度上——無論是有意還是無意——捲入了中國規避美國先進晶片限制的圖謀。這些精密晶片是人工智慧熱潮的核心，而中國尚未掌握自主製造技術。隨著這起醜聞的輪廓逐漸清晰，美國人正切身感受到先進人工智慧系統可能如何顛覆他們的生活，尤其是在落入不法分子之手的情況下。過去兩週，飛機停飛、銀行業務中斷、許多人無法使用電子郵件，國家安全局的一個內部通信網路也陷入癱瘓。這些故障大多似乎是由於AI智能體暴露了需要緊急修補的安全漏洞所致。但儘管這些事件並非由網路攻擊引起——至少據我們目前所知是如此，它們仍表明惡意行為者利用人工智慧破壞我們社會的穩定有多麼容易。
We don’t yet know the impact of Jensen Huang’s Chinese entanglement on American national security, but he may have caused lasting damage. His removal from Nvidia, however, harmed something Donald Trump values even more: the stock market.
我們尚不清楚黃仁勛與中國的關聯對美國國家安全產生了何種影響，但他可能已經造成了持久的損害。然而，他被英偉達解職，卻損害了唐納德·川普更加看重的東西：股市。
廣告
On Friday, Nvidia’s share price plunged, as did the stocks of many companies tied to the A.I. industry. It seemed for a moment as if the indiscretions of a single middle-aged man might pop the A.I. bubble that has been keeping America’s economy aloft.
週五，英偉達股價暴跌，許多與人工智慧行業相關的公司股票也隨之大跌。一時間，似乎一名中年男子的不檢點行為就可能刺破一直支撐著美國經濟的人工智慧泡沫。
It’s not just that Huang made himself irreplaceable within his multitrillion-dollar company. He also put Nvidia at the center of a series of circular financing agreements propping up the whole A.I. industry. Nvidia provides hundreds of billions in loan guarantees for companies building data centers, which in turn use Nvidia chips. As The Times has reported, so great are Nvidia’s loan commitments and financing arrangements that if it were a bank, it would be the 10th-largest in the country. Huang made it extremely expensive for anyone to hold him accountable.
黃仁勛不僅在自己這家市值數萬億美元的公司中變得不可或缺，還將英偉達置於一系列支撐整個人工智能行業的循環融資協議的中心。英偉達為建設數據中心的公司提供數千億美元的貸款擔保，而這些公司反過來又使用英偉達的晶片。正如時報所報導，英偉達的貸款承諾和融資安排規模如此龐大，如果它是一家銀行，將成為美國第十大銀行。黃仁勛讓所有人難以承擔追究他責任的代價。
So Trump, never a fan of rich men facing consequences, launched a rescue mission. According to The Times, he dispatched JD Vance to help broker Huang’s restoration, including by offering Nvidia’s board assurances that Huang doesn’t pose a national security risk. There’s no way to know what intelligence this assessment was based on. It seems somewhat contradicted by Trump’s suggestion on CNBC that if Huang didn’t get his job back, he might decide he’s “angry at our country” and decamp to China.
於是，向來不願看到富人承擔後果的川普發起了一場營救行動。據時報報導，他派出JD·萬斯協助斡旋黃仁勛復職，包括向英偉達董事會保證黃仁勛不構成國家安全風險。我們無從得知這一評估基於何種情報。這似乎與川普在CNBC上的暗示有些矛盾——他稱如果拿不回工作，黃仁勛可能會「對我們國家感到憤怒」，進而出走中國。
Trump has now yoked the rest of his presidency, as well as Vance’s political future, to Jensen Huang. We will likely spend the next two years learning more about how China benefited from smuggled Nvidia chips, and whether Jensen Huang enabled that smuggling.
川普如今已將他剩餘的總統任期以及萬斯的政治前途與黃仁勛捆綁在一起。我們很可能會在未來兩年裡了解到更多有關中國如何從走私的英偉達晶片中獲益，以及黃仁勛是否促成了這種走私的信息。
However this scandal develops, Trump has demonstrated that his administration’s culture of impunity, as well as his alliance with tech oligarchs, remains intact despite the repudiation of the midterms. (Under Huang, Nvidia donated $1 million to Trump’s inaugural fund and at least $20 million to his ballroom, and the C.E.O. certainly seems to have gotten his money’s worth.) One thing that has changed since Nov. 3, however, is the Republican incentive to look the other way.
無論這起醜聞如何發展，川普都已經表明，儘管在期中選舉中遭到選民否決，其政府有罪不罰的文化以及他與科技寡頭的聯盟依然完好無損。（在黃仁勛領導下，英偉達向川普的就職基金捐贈了100萬美元，並向他的宴會廳捐贈了至少2000萬美元，看起來這位首席執行官的錢確實花得很值。）不過，自11月3日以來，有一件事發生了變化，那就是共和黨人對此視而不見的動機。
After all, much of the party’s base is hawkish on China and suspicious of Big Tech. Now Trump has all but declared that tech stock prices matter more than Chinese espionage. The Nvidia affair is thus an opportunity for Republicans ready to distance themselves from their unpopular, lame-duck president. Senator Rick Scott of Florida told reporters, “This looks terrible on its face.” On X, Kevin Cramer, a Republican senator from North Dakota, wrote that while Nvidia’s board has assumed the risk of hiring a chief executive “who may very well be convicted in the United States or other nations of violating espionage and export control laws,” the “national security risk is passed along to the United States military and its citizens.”
畢竟，該黨的大部分基本盤對華持鷹派立場，且對大型科技公司心存疑慮。如今川普幾乎等於宣稱，科技股價格比中國間諜活動更重要。因此，英偉達事件為準備與這位不受歡迎的跛腳鴨總統拉開距離的共和黨人提供了機會。佛羅里達州參議員裡克·史考特對記者表示：「這件事看上去非常糟糕。」北達科他州共和黨參議員凱文·克萊默在X上寫道，英偉達董事會承擔了聘用一位「很可能在美國或其他國家因違反間諜法和出口管制法而被定罪」的首席執行官的風險，而「國家安全風險將傳導到美國軍方和國民身上」。
廣告
For a certain subset of citizens, that risk is more than worth it. Nvidia’s stock price shot up 33 percent after Huang’s return. Trump has probably benefited personally; as of the end of last year, he owned Nvidia shares worth $12 million. Cantor Fitzgerald, the investment banking company once run by Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick and now operated by his sons, put out a Monday research note on Nvidia headlined, “All is Right in the World Again (and a Very Happy Day).” For those with the right investments, it certainly was.
對一部分國民而言，這種風險完全值得。黃仁勛回歸後，英偉達股價飆升了33%。川普本人可能也從中受益；截至去年年底，他持有價值1200萬美元的英偉達股票。曾由商務部長霍華德·盧特尼克執掌、現由其兒子們經營的投資銀行坎托菲茨傑拉德週一發布了一份關於英偉達的研究報告，標題為《世界重新恢復正常（這是非常開心的一天）》。對那些押對寶的投資人來說，確實如此。
