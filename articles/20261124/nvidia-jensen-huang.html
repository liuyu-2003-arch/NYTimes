黃仁勛復職重掌英偉達

Huang’s Return to Nvidia Sparks Stock Rally and Lawmaker Skepticism

MAGGIE HABERMAN, JONATHAN SWAN, TRIPP MICKLE, JOE RENNISON, EMILY DAVIES • 2026-11-24
The surprise return of Jensen Huang as chief executive of Nvidia on Monday was greeted with a spirited rally on Wall Street and bipartisan skepticism in Washington, where a chorus of lawmakers denounced President Trump’s efforts to reinstall the leader of one of the world’s most valuable companies.
週一，黃仁勛出人意料地重返英偉達首席執行官職位，這一消息在華爾街引發了強勁的股價上漲，但在華盛頓卻遭到了兩黨的一致質疑，一些議員譴責川普總統讓這家全球市值最高公司之一的領導人復職的舉動。
Mr. Huang’s reinstatement came less than two weeks after he abruptly resigned following reports that he had conducted two extramarital relationships, including one with a woman U.S. intelligence had identified as a Chinese asset. Mr. Trump, who had previously expressed support for Mr. Huang, directed Vice President JD Vance to work with Mr. Huang and Nvidia’s board over the weekend, according to two people with knowledge of the matter.
黃仁勛此次復職距離他因被曝出兩段突然辭職還不到兩週，他的其中一個婚外情對象是被美國情報部門認定為中國線人的女性。據兩名知情人士說，曾對黃仁勛表示支持的川普上週末指示副總統萬斯與黃仁勛和英偉達董事會協調。
Shortly before the opening bell on Wall Street, Nvidia’s board announced that it unanimously backed Mr. Huang’s return after receiving “input from senior levels of the United States government” that made its members confident he posed no national security risk. Mr. Trump immediately echoed the statement on social media, adding that Mr. Huang was “a Great Patriot and National Treasure.”
在華爾街開盤前不久，英偉達董事會宣布一致支持黃仁勛回歸，此前收到了「美國政府高級層面的意見」，使董事會成員確信他不構成國家安全風險。川普立即在社群媒體上呼應這一聲明，並稱黃仁勛是「偉大的愛國者和國家財富」。
Nvidia, which dominates the market for advanced chips that are vital in artificial intelligence, saw its shares quickly soar. Nvidia closed the day up more than 31 percent, driving a broader rally that broke a gloomy stretch for the tech sector that dragged down the market following Mr. Huang’s resignation on Nov. 12.
英偉達主導著對人工智慧至關重要的先進晶片市場，其股價迅速飆升。英偉達當天收漲超過31%，帶動大盤普遍上漲，一舉扭轉了自11月12日黃仁勛辭職以來科技板塊持續低迷、拖累大盤的頹勢。

But some lawmakers immediately raised concerns about Mr. Huang’s comeback. Congressional Republicans who had rarely aimed their oversight power at Mr. Trump rejected his administration’s assurances that it had resolved national security concerns surrounding Nvidia and indicated that they would demand answers about Mr. Huang’s return.
但一些議員立即對黃仁勛的回歸提出擔憂。國會共和黨人此前很少動用監督權針對川普，但他們拒絕接受川普政府關於已解決英偉達相關國家安全擔憂的保證，並表示將要求就黃仁勛回歸一事作出解釋。
Senator John Curtis, Republican of Utah, wrote on social media that he had questions, including: “Who in our government weighed in? What did they tell the board?”
猶他州共和黨參議員約翰·柯蒂斯在社群媒體上寫道，他有幾個疑問，包括：「我們政府當中誰參與了此事？他們告訴了董事會什麼？」
Democrats, who are set to take control of both the House and the Senate in January, were deeply critical.
將於1月控制眾議院和參議院的民主黨人則提出嚴厲批評。
“The president is selling out our national security to keep the stock market high,” Senator Ruben Gallego, Democrat of Arizona, wrote on social media. Senator Chuck Schumer, Democrat of New York and the minority leader, promised in his own post to investigate “any wrongdoing by the Trump Administration, Jensen Huang, other NVIDIA executives, and anyone selling out our national security for financial gain.”
「總統正在出賣我們的國家安全，以維持股市高位，」亞利桑那州民主党參議員魯本·加列戈在社群媒體上寫道。紐約州民主党參議員、少數黨領袖查克·舒默在自己的帖子中承諾，將調查「川普政府、黃仁勛、其他英偉達高管，以及任何為經濟利益而出賣國家安全者的任何不當行為」。
Mr. Trump exulted in Mr. Huang’s reinstatement and its effect on the company’s stock during a brief phone interview with The New York Times on Monday morning, but declined to answer questions about whether he believed that the woman at the heart of the scandal, Alice Huang, was working with Chinese intelligence agencies.
川普週一上午接受《紐約時報》簡短電話採訪時，對黃仁勛復職及對公司股價的影響感到欣喜，但拒絕回答他是否認為處於醜聞核心的女性黃樂(Alice Huang)與中國情報機構合作的問題。
“I just thought he should be running the company, and I didn’t know it was going to have this kind of an impact,” Mr. Trump said. “I’ve never seen anything quite like it actually. The stock is up.”
「我只是認為他應該經營這家公司，我沒想到會產生這種影響，」川普說。「我其實從沒見過類似情況。股價上漲了。」
廣告
The rehiring of Mr. Huang was a dramatic about-face for Nvidia. The company’s board had unanimously accepted Mr. Huang’s resignation, and its chairman had told investors there were no plans to bring him back on.
重新聘用黃仁勛對英偉達來說是一次戲劇性的逆轉。公司董事會曾一致接受黃仁勛的辭職，董事長還曾告訴投資者沒有讓他回來的計劃。
But on Monday, Mr. Huang made a triumphant return to the company’s headquarters in Santa Clara, Calif., where employees roared in applause as he bowed in thanks before striking a more somber tone. He apologized and promised to be a better chief executive “the second time around.”
但週一，黃仁勛凱旋般回到公司位於加州聖克拉拉的總部。員工們爆發出掌聲，他鞠躬致謝，隨後語氣變得更加沉重。他致歉並承諾，「第二次」會做一個更好的首席執行官。
“I’ve made some very terrible mistakes, and I’m going to work on coming back,” he said.
「我犯了一些非常可怕的錯誤，我會努力重整旗鼓的，」他說。
