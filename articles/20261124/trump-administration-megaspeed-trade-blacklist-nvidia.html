美國將Megaspeed列入貿易黑名單

U.S. Imposes Sanctions on Singaporean Firm at Center of Nvidia Scandal

ANA SWANSON • 2026-11-24
The Trump administration on Monday barred a Singaporean data center firm at the center of the Nvidia scandal from buying advanced A.I. chips, according to people familiar with the matter, who spoke on the condition of anonymity since the announcement was not yet public.
據知情人士透露，川普政府週一禁止處於英偉達醜聞核心的新加坡數據中心企業購買先進人工智慧晶片。由於相關決定尚未公開，這些知情人士要求匿名。
The Commerce Department on Monday added Megaspeed to the government’s entity list, a trade blacklist that will prevent companies from shipping U.S. technology, like Nvidia’s chips, to the firm without first obtaining a government license.
美國商務部週一將Megaspeed列入政府的實體清單。被列入這份貿易黑名單意味著，未經美國政府許可，企業不得向該公司出口英偉達晶片等美國技術產品。
Megaspeed is currently under criminal investigation by the Justice Department for allegedly smuggling A.I. chips to China, in contravention of U.S. law. The firm had been established by a woman, Alice Huang, who U.S. officials believed to be a Chinese intelligence asset and who had an affair with Nvidia’s chief executive, Jensen Huang.
Megaspeed目前正因涉嫌違反美國法律向中國走私人工智慧晶片接受司法部刑事調查。該公司由名為黃樂(Alice Huang)的女性創立，美國官員認為她是中國情報機構的線人，曾與英偉達首席執行官黃仁勛有婚外情。
Mr. Huang stepped down this month after that affair and another relationship with an Nvidia employee were revealed. On Monday, Nvidia announced that it was restoring Mr. Huang as chief executive, after receiving assurances from senior U.S. officials that he did not present a national security risk.
在這段婚外情以及黃仁勛與英偉達另一名員工的私情曝光後，黃仁勛於本月辭職。週一，英偉達宣布恢復黃仁勛的首席執行官職務，此前美國高級官員已向該公司保證，黃仁勛不構成國家安全風險。
廣告
The decision to add Megaspeed to the entity list comes after weeks of debate within the administration about whether to take such a step.
在決定將Megaspeed列入實體清單之前，川普政府內部就是否採取這一措施爭論了數週。
The debate had pitted officials including Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, who favored quicker action on the Megaspeed entity listing and other A.I. chip regulations, against Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick, whose department oversees the technology export rules. The decision to add Megaspeed to the entity list was reported earlier by Semafor.
爭論的一方是財政部長史考特·貝森特等官員，他們主張加快將Megaspeed列入實體清單，並儘快出台其他人工智慧晶片監管措施；另一方則是商務部長霍華德·盧特尼克，其領導的部門負責監管技術出口。新聞網站Semafor此前已報導了將Megaspeed列入實體清單的決定。
In an interview this month, Mr. Bessent blamed Mr. Lutnick’s department for slow-walking the measure, saying that Treasury officials had pushed for weeks to add the company but that the Commerce Department “has the pen.”
本月接受採訪時，貝森特指責盧特尼克所在部門故意拖延這項措施，稱財政部官員數週來一直在推動將該公司列入清單，但「最終的決定權在商務部手中」。
Mr. Bessent said that the Treasury, Defense and State Departments and the U.S. trade representative had all been in favor of putting Megaspeed on the entity list for months.
貝森特表示，財政部、國防部、國務院以及美國貿易代表數月來一直支持將Megaspeed列入實體清單。
Unlike many other recent entity listings, critics point out, Monday’s action bans only exports from the United States to Megaspeed, not from other countries. Most A.I. chips are made in Taiwan and would not be affected by the rule. The Commerce Department put out other regulations last week that will limit the ability of firms like Megaspeed to gain access to A.I. chips, but the listing on Monday seems more symbolic than effective.
批評人士指出，與近期將許多其他企業列入實體清單的做法不同，週一的措施僅禁止從美國向Megaspeed出口相關產品，並不限制來自其他國家的出口。絕大多數人工智慧晶片都在台灣製造，因此不會受到這項規定的影響。商務部上週出台了其他監管規定，將限制Megaspeed這類企業獲取人工智慧晶片的能力，但週一將該公司列入實體清單的舉措似乎象徵意義大於實際效力。
How strictly to enforce the entity listing will now be up to the Commerce Department. Mr. Lutnick has so far recommended more surgical action on restricting technology sales overseas, drawing accusations from others in the administration and in Congress that his department had become too cozy with Nvidia and Mr. Huang.
接下來，這項實體清單限制措施的執行力度將取決於商務部。迄今為止，盧特尼克一直主張採取更具針對性的措施限制向海外銷售技術產品，這招致政府內部其他官員和國會議員的指責，稱商務部與英偉達及黃仁勛的關係過於密切。
廣告
Last Monday, his department issued sweeping regulations that cut down on China’s access to A.I. data centers around the world. But as it did so, it took the unusual step of saying it acted at the direction of the Treasury Department.
上週一，商務部出台了一系列範圍廣泛的監管規定，限制中國使用世界各地的人工智慧數據中心。但在發布這些規定時，商務部罕見地表示，此舉是奉財政部之命採取的。
Mr. Lutnick had taken more aggressive action on export controls last year, including overseeing a significant expansion of the entity list. But that provoked a harsh response from China and forced Trump officials to the negotiating table with Beijing.
去年，盧特尼克曾在出口管制方面採取更為強硬的措施，包括主導大幅擴充實體清單。但這些舉措招致中國的強硬回應，迫使川普政府官員與北京坐到談判桌前。
Since then, the department has been more reserved with its technology controls. It has been over a year since it added any companies to the entity list, the longest such stretch in 18 years. Adding Megaspeed to the list will break that streak.
此後，商務部在實施技術管制方面變得更加謹慎。該部門已有一年多沒有將任何企業列入實體清單，這是18年來間隔時間最長的一次。將Megaspeed列入清單將打破這一紀錄。
Officials at the Commerce Department began looking into Megaspeed in 2024, after the formerly unknown firm began buying billions of dollars of advanced A.I. chips and shipping them from California to Malaysia.
商務部官員於2024年開始調查Megaspeed，此前這家原本名不見經傳的公司開始購買數十億美元的先進人工智慧晶片，並從加利福尼亞運往馬來西亞。
U.S. rules ban Chinese companies from directly buying A.I. chips. But Megaspeed and other companies have been able to sidestep those rules by re-registering under Singaporean ownership. U.S. officials also became concerned that the firm was illegally smuggling A.I. chips into China.
美國的規定禁止中國企業直接購買人工智慧晶片。但Megaspeed等企業通過重新註冊為新加坡方面持有的企業，得以繞開這些規定。美國官員還開始擔心，該公司正在非法向中國走私人工智慧晶片。
A Commerce Department official visited one of the company’s data centers in Malaysia in late 2024, and found the server equipment to be packed up in boxes rather than installed, arousing suspicions about smuggling.
2024年底，美國商務部一名官員走訪了該公司位於馬來西亞的一處數據中心，發現伺服器設備仍裝在包裝箱內，並未安裝到位，由此引發了對晶片走私的懷疑。
廣告
American and Singaporean officials met with Ms. Huang in Singapore to inquire about the company’s purchases in June of last year. Singaporean officials also detained Ms. Huang for questioning.
去年6月，美國和新加坡官員在新加坡會見黃樂，詢問該公司的採購情況。新加坡官員還拘留了她並進行了訊問。
The Commerce Department’s work on the case was suddenly halted soon after, following an unusual interaction between Nvidia’s general counsel and a top department official.
不久之後，在英偉達總法律顧問與美國商務部一名高級官員進行了一次不同尋常的溝通後，商務部對該案的調查工作突然停止。
After Singaporean officials detained Ms. Huang, Nvidia’s general counsel, Tim Teter, reached out in August to Jeff Kessler, a Commerce Department official appointed by President Trump, to ask about the detention and the case.
新加坡官員拘留黃樂後，英偉達總法律顧問蒂姆·泰特於去年8月聯繫了川普任命的商務部官員傑夫·凱斯勒，詢問拘留一事及案件的有關情況。
Mr. Teter then reached out to agents working on the Megaspeed case for information, saying that Mr. Kessler had given Nvidia permission to make the inquiries.
泰特隨後聯繫了處理Megaspeed案件的調查人員，向他們了解情況，並稱凱斯勒已准許英偉達進行這些問詢。
The incident raised questions of what Mr. Kessler and Mr. Teter had discussed, and whether those interactions could become the subject of legal proceedings. Bloomberg reported earlier that Ms. Huang’s detention was the cause of Mr. Teter’s outreach.
這一事件引發了外界的疑問：凱斯勒與泰特究竟談了些什麼，兩人之間的接觸是否可能引發法律程序。彭博新聞社此前報導，正是黃樂被拘留一事促使泰特出面聯繫商務部官員。
Commerce Department officials were subsequently told not to work on or speak about the case. In a statement this month, Singaporean officials said they had continued their work on Megaspeed, but had received limited information or assistance about the company.
此後，商務部官員接到指示，不得繼續調查或談論此案。新加坡官員在本月的一份聲明中表示，他們一直在繼續調查Megaspeed，但獲得的有關該公司的信息和協助十分有限。
廣告
Critics say the delay in imposing sanctions on Megaspeed allowed the company to continue purchasing hundreds of millions of dollars of technology. Trade data obtained from ImportGenius, a trade data platform, shows that between July 2025 and February 2026, Megaspeed’s Malaysian subsidiary received more than $800 million of shipments containing controlled Nvidia chips.
批評人士稱，遲遲未對Megaspeed實施制裁使該公司得以繼續採購價值數億美元的技術產品。從貿易數據平台ImportGenius獲取的數據顯示，2025年7月至2026年2月期間，Megaspeed在馬來西亞的子公司收到了價值逾8億美元的貨物，其中包含受到出口管制的英偉達晶片。

Ana Swanson報導貿易和國際經濟新聞，常駐華盛頓。她從事新聞工作已超過十年。
