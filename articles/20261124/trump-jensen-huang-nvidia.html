黃仁勛復職凸顯川普不惜代價力保人工智慧發展

Jensen Huang’s Return Shows the Lengths Trump Will Go to Keep A.I. Booming

TONY ROMM, SYDNEY EMBER, COLBY SMITH • 2026-11-24
For President Trump, the stock market has long been a favored barometer for his economic agenda, one that he frequently promotes to showcase how artificial intelligence is booming on his watch.
長期以來，股市一直是川普總統衡量其經濟議程的慣用晴雨表，他也頻繁藉此宣傳人工智慧在其任內蓬勃發展的態勢。
So when scandal began to consume the chip-making giant Nvidia — toppling its chief executive, Jensen Huang, and sending financial markets into a tailspin — Mr. Trump intervened quickly. He rose to Mr. Huang’s defense and leveraged the muscle of the Oval Office to push for the embattled leader’s reinstatement.
因此，當醜聞開始席捲晶片製造巨頭英偉達，令公司首席執行官黃仁勛下台並引發金融市場急劇動盪時，川普迅速介入。他站出來為黃仁勛辯護，並動用橢圓形辦公室的影響力，推動這位深陷困境的領導人復職。
Mr. Trump succeeded. He helped to secure Mr. Huang’s return by Monday, sending Nvidia’s stock price soaring more than 30 percent by the closing bell, adding roughly $1 trillion to its valuation. In the process, the president demonstrated the lengths he was willing to go — and the risks he was ready to take — to keep both A.I. and a dependent stock market humming.
川普成功了。本週一，黃仁勛已經在他的協助下回歸，推動英偉達股價收盤暴漲逾30%，市值增加約1萬億美元。在這一過程中，總統展示了自己為維持人工智慧及依賴該行業的股市持續運轉願意走多遠，以及準備承擔多大的風險。
As Mr. Trump would later explain on social media, Nvidia needed Mr. Huang, and the United States needed Nvidia, to “ensure the Success and Prosperity of not only the Company, but the American Economy.” The missteps of an embattled executive appeared to matter less to Mr. Trump than the economic losses that he saw on the horizon if Nvidia remained in peril.
正如川普後來在社群媒體上解釋的那樣，英偉達需要黃仁勛，而美國需要英偉達，從而「不僅確保該公司的成功與繁榮，也確保美國經濟的成功與繁榮」。川普看到，英偉達繼續處於危險之中將給經濟帶來巨大損害，他認為，一位深陷困境的高管的不明智之舉相比這樣的損害似乎顯得不那麼重要。
廣告
Nvidia has a unique role in the A.I. ecosystem. It is both a pre-eminent chipmaker and a key financier for technological development, using its deep pockets and powerful semiconductors to backstop a wave of coming A.I. investment.
英偉達在人工智慧生態系統中扮演著獨特角色。它既是首屈一指的晶片製造商，也是技術發展的關鍵出資方，憑藉雄厚的財力和強大的半導體產品為未來人工智慧投資浪潮提供支撐。
So the return of Mr. Huang seemed to delight Wall Street, where the S&P 500 rose about 4.7 percent by day’s end. The market gains may have directly benefited the president, who has reported holding millions of dollars in Nvidia investments.
因此，黃仁勛的回歸似乎令華爾街振奮，標普500指數在收盤時上漲約4.7%。市場的上漲可能讓總統直接受益，此前他曾申報持有數百萬美元的英偉達投資。
Still, Mr. Trump’s advocacy for Mr. Huang reignited fears about his growth-at-all-costs mentality, and the extent to which his actions reflect an economy that hinges too much on the performance of a single industry.
不過，川普對黃仁勛的支持重新引發了外界的擔憂，人們不僅擔心他不惜一切代價追求增長的心態，也擔憂他的舉動在多大程度上反映出美國經濟對單一行業的過度依賴。
“There is real concern about how much everyone is resting on Nvidia,” said Nidhi Hegde, the executive director of the American Economic Liberties Project, a left-leaning group. The company had become “too big to fail” in the eyes of the administration, she said.
「人們確實非常擔心大家對英偉達的依賴程度究竟有多深，」有左派傾向的組織美國經濟自由項目的執行主任尼迪·赫格德說。她表示，在政府眼中，這家公司已經變得「大而不能倒」。
Even Mr. Trump appeared to acknowledge the extent to which the markets had factored into his vigorous and unorthodox defense of Mr. Huang, who resigned from Nvidia earlier this month after allegations surfaced of multiple affairs, including one with a woman who U.S. intelligence believes is a Chinese intelligence asset.
就連川普本人似乎也承認，他以如此強硬且非同尋常的方式為黃仁勛辯護，在多大程度上是因為市場。本月早些時候，黃仁勛在被曝出多起婚外情指控後從英偉達辭職，其中涉及一名被美國情報部門認定為中國情報線人的女性。
Asked if the stock market influenced his decision, Mr. Trump told CNBC: “Probably so, but the bigger impact was the long term.”
當被問及股市是否影響了他的決定時，川普對CNBC表示：「可能是吧，但更大的影響在於長遠。」
For Mr. Trump, the extraordinary lobbying campaign on behalf of Mr. Huang underscored the extent to which he has staked the economy on A.I. Even as many Americans are uneasy about the technology, Mr. Trump remains one of its fiercest defenders, and he has consistently portrayed the race to develop the technology as an existential struggle between superpowers for economic and military supremacy.
對川普而言，這場為黃仁勛展開的非同尋常的遊說行動凸顯出他在多大程度上將經濟押注在人工智慧上。儘管許多美國人對這項技術感到不安，川普仍是其最堅定的捍衛者之一，並一貫將發展該技術的競賽描繪為超級大國之間爭奪經濟和軍事霸權的生死存亡之戰。
As a result, the president has largely adopted a hands-off approach to A.I., as staggering sums pour into its development. Investors and major technology giants could plow as much as $10.3 trillion into A.I. between 2025 and 2032, some estimates show, helping to buoy a U.S. economy that is tempered by high prices, sluggish hiring and geopolitical discord.
因此，隨著巨額資金湧入人工智慧開發領域，總統在很大程度上對該行業採取了放任不管的態度。一些估算顯示，2025年至2032年間，投資者和科技巨頭可能向人工智慧投入高達10.3萬億美元，助力提振受高物價、招聘乏力和地緣政治分歧拖累的美國經濟。
廣告
Among the big spenders is Nvidia, whose efforts have earned praise from Mr. Trump. The president has cheered on Nvidia’s stock price, and helped secure Mr. Huang a frequent spot at the White House and on Capitol Hill.
英偉達正是這些大手筆投資者之一，其舉措贏得了川普的讚賞。這位總統曾為英偉達的股價歡呼，並幫助黃仁勛成為白宮和國會山莊的常客。
When Mr. Huang resigned from Nvidia earlier this month, Mr. Trump praised his leadership and recommended that Tim Cook, the former Apple executive, take over the embattled chipmaker. As markets soured over the turnover in Nvidia’s ranks — eventually dragging down major indexes with it — the president then ratcheted up his calls on the company to reinstate its old chief.
本月早些時候黃仁勛從英偉達辭職時，川普讚揚了他的領導能力，並推薦蘋果前高管蒂姆·庫克接掌這家深陷困境的晶片製造商。隨著市場對英偉達高層變動反應消極——最終拖累主要股指走低——總統隨即開始更積極地呼籲，要求該公司讓這位前掌門人復職。
“It would be the best thing for the Company, and also, for the U.S.A.,” the president said at one point on social media.
「這對這家公司以及美國來說，都是最好的事情，」總統一度在社群媒體上表示。
It fit a pattern for Mr. Trump, who has staged some of his most aggressive policy interventions in response to moments of unrest in financial markets. That included revising his punishing global tariffs, as investors grew spooked over a damaging, endless trade war, and claiming progress in talks with Iran whenever the war pummeled Wall Street.
這符合川普一貫的行事模式，他一些最激進的政策干預措施往往都是為了應對金融市場的動盪時刻。這包括在投資者因一場破壞性且無休止的貿易戰而陷入恐慌時修改其高額全球關稅，以及每當戰爭重創華爾街時宣稱與伊朗的談判取得進展。
But Mr. Trump’s successful lobbying on behalf of Mr. Huang proved especially troublesome for lawmakers, including some Republicans. While the president declared that “the U.S. government does not possess any evidence that he engaged in any unlawful conduct,” some members of Congress still cited national security risks in Mr. Huang’s return. Skeptics of A.I. also saw the move as a sign that the White House had become too cozy with the tech industry.
但川普為黃仁勛展開的成功遊說對包括部分共和黨人在內的國會議員而言顯得尤為棘手。儘管總統宣稱「美國政府不掌握任何錶明他存在違法行為的證據」，部分國會議員仍指出黃仁勛回歸存在國家安全風險。人工智慧懷疑論者也將此舉視為白宮與科技行業關係過於親密的信號。
“This looks like a decision to protect the wallets of Wall Street fat cats while putting profits ahead of our national security,” added Amy Kremer, a former leader of the Tea Party movement who is now chairwoman of Humans First, which has called for limits on data center development, in a statement.
「這看起來像是一個保護華爾街大亨荷包的決定，把利潤置於我們的國家安全之上，」茶黨運動前領袖、現任「人類優先」組織主席的埃米·克雷默在一份聲明中補充道。該組織一直呼籲限制數據中心的發展。
廣告
Some economists also saw signs of a troubling dependency that would warp markets, as investors came to see Nvidia as a business that had won the favor of the president. The concerns echoed the complaints that greeted efforts by the Trump administration to take ownership stakes in private companies, including Intel.
一些經濟學家也看到了令人擔憂的依賴跡象，隨著投資者開始將英偉達視為贏得總統青睞的企業，這種依賴將扭曲市場。這些擔憂與此前川普政府試圖入股包括英特爾在內的私營企業時所引發的批評如出一轍。
“We want capital flowing to companies for commercial reasons,” said Scott Lincicome, the vice president of general economics at the Cato Institute, a libertarian-leaning think tank. He said that investment should not follow “because they have the government’s backing or a politician’s backing, who might be making decisions not based on the commercial interests or even the national interest, but political ones.”
「我們希望資本出於商業原因流向企業，」自由意志主義傾向的智庫卡托研究所綜合經濟學副總裁史考特·林西科姆表示。不能因為企業「獲得了政府或政客的支持」就去投資，他說，「這些政客做決定可能並非基於商業利益甚至國家利益，而是基於政治利益。」
Mr. Trump’s intervention also arrived at a moment when policymakers across Washington were beginning to grapple openly with the extent to which the U.S. economy had become overly reliant on A.I. investment.
在川普此次介入之時，華盛頓上上下下的政策制定者正在公開表達擔憂，認為美國經濟已經過度依賴人工智慧投資。
The concern was evident at the last meeting of the Federal Reserve. Nearly every one of the 19 officials there in October mentioned the potential fallout if A.I. investments ended up disappointing investors, who have poured billions of dollars into funding the build out, according to recently released minutes of the gathering.
這種擔憂在上一次美聯儲會議上顯而易見。根據近期公布的會議紀要，在10月參會的19名官員中，幾乎所有人都提到了人工智慧投資若最終令投資者失望可能帶來的後果——這些投資者已向相關基礎設施建設投入了數十億美元。
Policymakers homed in on the risks stemming from the “complex and opaque financing structures” that A.I. companies are increasingly relying upon to fuel their growth, the October record showed. Raising the stakes, the Fed’s latest financial stability report, which was released earlier this month, overwhelmingly cited A.I. as the biggest threat in the eyes of market participants the central bank surveyed.
10月的會議記錄顯示，政策制定者重點關注了人工智慧公司為推動增長而日益依賴的「複雜且不透明的融資結構」所帶來的風險。更令局勢嚴峻的是，美聯儲本月早些時候發布的最新金融穩定報告顯示，在央行調查的市場參與者眼中，人工智慧是毫無疑問的最大威脅。
Those concerns predate the tumult facing Nvidia, reflecting investors’ continued uncertainty that they will be able to monetize their eye-popping investments into A.I. infrastructure. Against that backdrop, Shivaram Rajgopal, an accounting professor at the Columbia Business School, described Nvidia as “almost like a state-influenced enterprise,” one in which the government under Mr. Trump had established itself as a large stakeholder.
這些擔憂早於英偉達面臨的動盪，反映出投資者一直不確定自己能否將在人工智慧基礎設施上的巨額投資變現。在此背景下，哥倫比亞商學院會計學教授希瓦拉姆·拉傑戈帕爾將英偉達形容為「幾乎像是一家受國家影響的企業」，川普領導下的政府已在其中確立了自身作為重要利益相關者的地位。
廣告
“The perception is that it’s too big to fail, and Jensen Huang is too important to fire,” he said.
「外界的看法是，它大到不能倒，而黃仁勛則重要到不能被解僱，」他說。
