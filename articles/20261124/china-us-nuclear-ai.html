中國擔憂AI將幫助美國搶佔核優勢

Behind China’s A.I. Anxieties, Fear of a Nuclear Checkmate

儲百亮 • 2026-11-24
When Xi Jinping, the Chinese leader, set about expanding China’s nuclear forces a decade ago, his goal was to ensure that enough missiles could survive an initial attack so Beijing could deliver a devastating counterattack.
10年前，當中國領導人習近平著手擴充中國核力量時，他的目標是確保有足夠的導彈能夠在首次打擊中倖存下來，以便北京能夠發起毀滅性的反擊。
That ability to withstand a strike and hit back would deter any enemy from risking nuclear blackmail or war against China in the first place, Chinese strategists have said.
中國戰略家曾表示，這種承受打擊並予以回擊的能力從根本上就能威懾任何敵人，使其不敢對中國進行核訛詐或發動核戰爭。
But now, researchers in the Chinese military worry that American advances in artificial intelligence could weaken Beijing’s nuclear hand in a crisis or conflict. They have been warning that artificial intelligence could make it easier for U.S. forces to find and wipe out Chinese nuclear missiles, giving Washington a menacing advantage.
但現在，中國軍方的研究人員擔心，美國在人工智慧領域的進步可能會在危機或衝突中削弱北京的核威懾力。他們一直警告說，人工智慧可能使美軍更容易發現並摧毀中國的核導彈，從而賦予華盛頓一種極具威脅的優勢。
The worries over A.I. complicate the growing nuclear tensions between Washington and Beijing, and could make China more reluctant to consider limits to its smaller arsenal. At a summit on Thursday, Mr. Xi, President Trump and President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia spoke of reducing nuclear risks. But Mr. Xi has shown no interest in Mr. Trump’s calls for China to join arms control commitments.
對人工智慧的擔憂加劇了華盛頓與北京之間日益緊張的核局勢，並可能導致中國更不願考慮對其規模較小的核武庫進行限制。在上週四的峰會上，習近平、川普總統和俄羅斯總統普丁談到了降低核風險。但習近平對川普呼籲中國加入軍備控制承諾沒有表現出興趣。
廣告
A review of more than 100 recent studies by Chinese civilian and military researchers, as well as interviews with experts and former U.S. officials, points to a deep wariness in China about the American pursuit of A.I. in nuclear systems. At the same time, Chinese military institutes are also exploring how A.I. could make Chinese missiles harder to detect and knock from the sky.
對中國軍方和民間研究人員近期100多項研究的查閱、對專家和前美國官員的採訪表明，中國對美國在核系統中應用人工智慧抱有深深的警惕。與此同時，中國軍事機構也在探索如何利用人工智慧，使得中國導彈更難被探測和被擊落。
China, the United States and Russia use radars, satellites and sensors to monitor each other’s nuclear weapons. Breakthroughs in A.I. could help create technology to detect, track and strike nuclear weapons more quickly and accurately. Experts in China worry that it could lag the United States in applying such advances.
中國、美國和俄羅斯都利用雷達、衛星和傳感器來監視彼此的核武器。人工智慧的突破可能有助於創造出能夠更快速、更準確地探測、追蹤和打擊核武器的技術。中國專家擔心，在應用此類技術進步方面，中國可能會落後於美國。
A study from China’s top military institute described a scenario in which artificial intelligence accelerated U.S. actions in a crisis to mere moments, potentially overwhelming slower rivals.
中國頂級軍事機構的一項研究描述了這樣一種場景：在危機中，人工智慧將美國的行動加速到僅僅片刻之間，從而可能壓倒反應速度較慢的對手。
“By embedding A.I. and other technologies into nuclear decision-making systems, the United States could compress the decision-making process from the traditional 15 to 30 minutes to 90 seconds or less, drastically increasing the speed of response,” two researchers from the Academy of Military Science in Beijing wrote in an overview of global nuclear trends published several months ago.
「如美國將AI等技術嵌入核決策系統，將決策流程由傳統的15～30分鐘壓縮至90秒內，大幅提升響應速度，」兩名來自北京軍事科學院的研究人員在幾個月前發表的一篇關於全球核趨勢的概述中寫道。
China has about 620 nuclear warheads that could be delivered by land- or submarine-based missiles, or by bombers, the Federation of American Scientists estimates. China’s arsenal is widely forecast to grow to about 1,000 warheads by 2030. (The United States and Russia each have around 1,800 nuclear warheads deployed for missiles and bombers, with thousands more stockpiled.)
據美國科學家聯合會估計，中國擁有約620枚核彈頭，可通過陸基或潛射導彈或轟炸機投送。普遍預測，到2030年，中國的核武庫將增長到約1000枚彈頭。（美國和俄羅斯各自部署了約1800枚用於導彈和轟炸機的核彈頭，此外還儲備了數千枚。）
China fears that A.I. advances could enable the United States “to rapidly engage in strikes as soon as something pops out from a hiding spot or mobilizes,” said Gerald C. Brown, a former analyst with the State Department who studies China’s nuclear strategy. “Basically, in their view, it could make survivability a lot more difficult.”
研究中國核戰略的前國務院分析師傑拉爾德·布朗表示，中國擔心，人工智慧的進步可能會使美國「一旦有目標從藏身之處現身或開始調動，就能迅速發動打擊」。「基本上，在他們看來，這會大大降低核力量的生存能力。」
廣告
Fear of a First Strike
對首次打擊的恐懼
China’s missile force is closely watching the U.S. Strategic Command, which controls nuclear missiles and has said that it wants to use A.I. to enhance “decision-making capabilities.” Chinese military analysts are also studying how American conventional forces use A.I. to coordinate operations and pick targets.
中國的導彈部隊正密切關注控制核導彈的美國戰略司令部，該司令部曾表示希望利用人工智慧來增強「決策能力」。中國軍事分析人士還在研究美國常規部隊如何利用人工智慧來協調行動和選擇目標。
The U.S. edge in making advanced chips is adding to Chinese concerns that the United States can move faster to incorporate A.I. into its military, including in nuclear-related systems, said Jacob Stokes, a senior fellow at the Center for a New American Security in Washington.
華盛頓新美國安全中心高級研究員雅各布·斯托克斯表示，美國在製造先進晶片方面的優勢加劇了中國的擔憂，即美國能夠更快地將人工智慧融入其軍事體系，包括核相關係統。
Official history reinforces China’s anxieties. Chinese military officers studied the Korean War and crises over Taiwan in the 1950s, when the United States signaled that it could drop atomic bombs on Mao Zedong’s forces, which then had no nuclear weapons, said Mr. Brown, now an international affairs fellow at the Council on Foreign Relations.
官方歷史強化了中國的焦慮。現任美國外交關係委員會國際事務研究員的布朗說，中國軍官研究了朝鮮戰爭和1950年代的台灣危機，當時美國發出信號，表示可以向毛澤東的軍隊投擲原子彈，而當時這支軍隊還沒有核武器。
China has already been diversifying its nuclear weapons to enhance their “survivability.”
中國已經在使其核武器多樣化，以增強其「生存能力」。
In July, it held a test launch of a long-range missile from a submarine, showing its intention to create an undersea nuclear deterrent that could be harder for the United States to detect and destroy. China has also built about 320 launch silos in its northern deserts, which could make it harder for an enemy to determine which ones hold nuclear-armed missiles and knock them all out with precision strikes.
今年7月，中國從潛艇試射遠程導彈，表明意圖建立一種對美國來說更難探測和摧毀的海底核威懾力量。中國還在其北部沙漠建造了約320個發射井，這可能會使敵人更難確定哪些發射井裝有核導彈，從而難以通過精確打擊將它們全部摧毀。
廣告
But Chinese experts now warn that breakthroughs in military A.I. could help the United States overcome China’s efforts to protect its arsenal and, in an extreme case, could tempt Washington to attempt a crippling first strike against those forces.
但中國專家現在警告說，軍事人工智慧領域的突破可能幫助美國突破中國保護其核武庫的努力，並且在極端情況下，甚至可能會誘使華盛頓試圖對這些力量發動毀滅性的首次打擊。
“I believe these risks weigh heavily in China’s strategic considerations,” Sun Chenghao, a senior fellow at the Center for International Security and Strategy at Tsinghua University in Beijing, wrote in emailed answers to questions.
「我認為這種風險在中國的戰略考量中具有重要分量，」北京清華大學戰略與安全研究中心高級研究員孫成昊在通過電子郵件回答問題時寫道。
“What China needs to assess is whether, under extreme circumstances, the U.S. might rely on A.I. reconnaissance, precision strikes and missile defense to weaken China’s capabilities to launch nuclear retaliation after an attack,” he wrote.
「中國需要評估的是，在極端情況下，美國是否可能依靠AI偵察、精確打擊和導彈防禦，削弱中國遭受攻擊後的核報復能力，」他寫道。
A Race to Get Ahead
爭奪領先地位的競賽
China’s leaders appear determined not to fall too far behind. In July, Mr. Xi held a meeting of senior officials to study the military implications of A.I. and other technological advances. He demanded steady progress toward their deployment.
中國領導人似乎決心不讓中國落後太多。今年7月，習近平召開了一次高級官員會議，研究人工智慧和其他技術進步的軍事意義。他要求穩步推進這些技術的部署。
In China, there is “great optimism that A.I. is the tool that is going to allow you to finally catch up to or surpass the United States militarily,” said Sam Bresnick, a research fellow at the Center for Security and Emerging Technology at Georgetown University.
喬治城大學安全與新興技術中心研究員薩姆·布雷斯尼克表示，在中國，存在著「人們對人工智慧抱有極大的樂觀態度，認為它將成為最終在軍事上趕上甚至超越美國的工具」。
Studies published by China’s Rocket Force, which controls most of its nuclear missiles, show a wide interest in A.I.
掌握中國大部分核導彈的中國火箭軍發表的研究顯示，該部隊對人工智慧有著廣泛的興趣。
One study involving a Rocket Force scientist published last year examined how A.I.-enabled “deep reinforcement learning” could help hypersonic glide vehicles — weapons designed to dodge and weave as they approach their targets — better evade missile defenses.
去年發表的一項研究由火箭軍科學家參與，探討了基於人工智慧的「強化學習」如何幫助高超音速滑翔飛行器（這種武器在接近目標時能進行閃避和變向）更好地規避導彈防禦系統。
廣告
The researchers simulated millions of possible flight paths to train an A.I. system to guide a hypersonic vehicle in flight. They used no-fly zones around Alaska as real-life examples of the challenges a hypersonic weapon might encounter, and test if A.I. could help the weapon navigate the defenses.
研究人員模擬了數百萬種可能的飛行路徑，以訓練人工智慧系統在飛行中引導高超音速飛行器。他們將阿拉斯加周邊的禁飛區作為高超音速武器可能面臨的實際挑戰案例，並測試人工智慧是否能幫助該武器避開防禦。
In another study, Rocket Force scientists used computer simulations to model how A.I. could help a ballistic missile get back on course after swerving to evade high-speed interceptors. An A.I.-assisted autopilot could calculate when to fire steering thrusters, secondary rocket engines that help change direction, while conserving fuel.
在另一項研究中，火箭軍科學家利用計算機模擬，建模分析了人工智慧如何幫助彈道導彈在為規避高速攔截器而偏離航線後重回正軌。人工智慧輔助的自動駕駛儀可以計算出何時啟動轉向推進器（幫助改變方向的輔助火箭發動機），同時還能節省燃料。
China should “apply cutting-edge technologies such as artificial intelligence and hypersonics, strengthening deployment of forces, and improving their survivability and retaliatory capabilities,” five scholars from a Rocket Force university wrote in a paper published this year that examined other countries’ nuclear exercises.
幾位來自火箭軍一所大學的學者在今年發表的一篇考察其他國家核演習的論文中寫道，中國應該「運用人工智慧、高超聲速等前沿技術，加強力量部署，提高生存和反擊能力」。
Risk of Miscalculation
誤判風險
But even some researchers in the People’s Liberation Army have warned against rushing to introduce the technology in nuclear weapons systems.
但即使是中國人民解放軍的一些研究人員也警告說，不要倉促將該技術引入核武器系統。
In the most dire projections, rogue A.I.-generated internet viruses could commandeer nuclear weapons controls, disabling or launching the weapons.
在最可怕的預測中，由人工智慧生成的惡意互聯網病毒可能會接管核武器控制權，使其癱瘓或發射武器。
But short of that, A.I. could incorporate and magnify false data, leading to dangerous, even disastrous, errors in crisis decision-making. Commanders could put too much trust in A.I.-generated conclusions or advice, experts say. Chinese studies have pointed to U.S. battlefield errors in conventional wars that they say reflect too much faith in automated targeting.
但即便沒有那麼嚴重，人工智慧也可能會納入並放大虛假數據，從而在危機決策中導致危險甚至災難性的錯誤。專家表示，指揮官可能會過於信賴人工智慧生成的結論或建議。中國的一些研究指出，美國在常規戰爭中的戰場失誤反映了他們對自動定位過於信任。
Erroneous information magnified through A.I. creates “enormous decision-making risks,” researchers from China’s National University of Defense Technology and the Academy of Military Sciences wrote in a study this year. “This is becoming the core obstacle to its deep application in the highly serious, high-risk field of command and control.”
幾位來自中國國防科技大學和軍事科學院的研究人員在今年的一項研究中寫道，通過人工智慧放大的錯誤信息會產生「巨大的決策風險」。這一點「正成為其在高度嚴肅、高風險的指揮控制領域深度應用的核心障礙」。
Nuclear weapons systems have long contained high levels of automation to ensure accuracy, speed and security. But A.I. advances could reduce the role of human oversight in ways that can be dangerous during a fast-moving crisis, said Philip Reiner, the chief executive officer of the Institute for Security and Technology, a California-based group based that examines the implications of A.I. for nuclear weapons security and stability.
長期以來，核武器系統一直包含高度的自動化，以確保準確性、速度和安全性。但加利福尼亞州審查人工智慧對核武器安全與穩定影響的組織安全與技術研究所的首席執行官菲利普·賴納表示，人工智慧的進步可能會降低人類監督的作用，這在瞬息萬變的危機中可能是危險的。
廣告
“This is a technology that is moving faster than anything any human has ever seen before,” he said of advanced A.I. “Nobody’s turning launch decisions over to A.I., but everything that tells you whether you should launch is now going to be shaped by A.I. And so, does that make it riskier? Yes.”
「這是一項發展速度超過人類有史以來所見任何事物的技術，」他在談到先進的人工智慧時說。「沒有人會將發射決定權交給人工智慧，但所有告訴你是否應該發射的信息，現在都將由人工智慧塑造。那麼，這會讓它變得更危險嗎？是的。」
Yet even as the need for safeguards has become greater, the United States and China have made little progress in discussing them. Mr. Xi agreed with then-President Biden in 2024 to maintain human control over the decision to use nuclear weapons, but no official talks on the issue have followed. Mr. Xi, Mr. Trump and Mr. Putin did not mention the role of A.I. in nuclear weapons during their summit last week.
然而，儘管對安全保障措施的需求日益迫切，美中兩國在討論這一問題上幾乎沒有取得進展。2024年，習近平與時任總統拜登同意在動用核武器的決定上保持人類控制，但此後雙方並未就此問題進行任何正式磋商。在上週的峰會上，習近平、川普和普丁也沒有提及人工智慧在核武器中的作用。
“Right now, there’s a lot of attention on the risks that A.I. is going to cause which could kill us all, but it’s abstract. Well, it has to have a mechanism,” said Melanie Sisson, a senior fellow at the Brookings Institution who has discussed nuclear arms and A.I. with Chinese researchers. “Nuclear weapons are probably one of the most likely mechanisms. That’s why it’s so urgent.”
「現在，很多人關注人工智慧可能帶來導致我們全部喪命的風險，但這很抽象。實際上，它必須有一個機制，」布魯金斯學會高級研究員梅蘭妮·西森說，她曾與中國研究人員討論過核武器和人工智慧問題。「核武器可能是最有可能的機制之一。這就是為什麼這件事如此緊迫。」

儲百亮(Chris Buckley)是《紐約時報》首席中國記者，自台北報導中國和台灣問題，重點關注政治、社會變革以及安全和軍事問題。
