中美俄元首會晤：一場展示情誼的盛大表演

Trump Flaunts Camaraderie With Xi and Putin but Has Little to Show for It

KATIE ROGERS, DAVID E. SANGER • 2026-11-20
President Trump used his first-ever trilateral summit with Presidents Xi Jinping of China and Vladimir V. Putin of Russia on Thursday to say that their respective nations were “uniquely positioned to discuss how to reduce the number of nuclear weapons on earth.”
川普總統在週四與中國國家主席習近平和俄羅斯總統普丁舉行的首次三邊峰會上表示，三國「處於獨特的位置，可以討論如何減少地球上的核武器數量」。
But after an 80-minute conversation at a lakeside villa in the city of Shenzhen, a Chinese technology hub, it was unclear whether the three leaders had agreed to return to the kind of arms control talks that for decades were a centerpiece of diplomacy among superpowers during the Cold War. On new threats, including artificial intelligence, the three leaders were largely silent.
但在中國科技中心深圳市的一棟湖畔別墅進行了80分鐘的交談後，目前尚不清楚這三位領導人是否已同意重返軍控談判——冷戰期間，此類談判在長達數十年內一直是超級大國之間外交的核心。對於包括人工智慧在內的新威脅，這三位領導人基本上保持沉默。
Mr. Trump, who also met with Mr. Xi and Mr. Putin separately at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation forum this week, appeared delighted to convene the world’s largest nuclear powers for a discussion about disarmament. But he did not come with a detailed plan for how to reduce the number of nuclear weapons, or outline a path to one.
川普本週還在亞太經濟合作組織論壇上分別會見了習近平和普丁，他似乎很高興能召集世界上最大的核大國就裁軍問題進行討論。但他並沒有帶來關於如何減少核武器數量的詳細計劃，也沒有概述實現這一目標的路徑。
Instead, Mr. Trump relished what amounted to a high-profile photo opportunity of the three leaders seated around a circular wooden table. For Mr. Trump, the main achievement seemed to be a mutual commitment to keep talking about peace — without taking meaningful steps toward it.
相反，川普更樂於享受三位領導人圍坐在圓形木桌旁的高調拍照機會。對川普來說，此次會議的主要成果似乎是各方承諾將繼續就和平問題進行對話——卻沒有任何就此採取的實質性舉措。
廣告
“Too often, past leaders avoided talking to nations with whom they had disagreements, and we feel the opposite.” Mr. Trump told Mr. Xi and Mr. Putin. “No peace was ever forged by declining opportunities for engagement and speech.”
「過去的領導人往往避免與存在分歧的國家進行對話，而我們的感覺恰恰相反。」川普對習近平和普丁說。「拒絕接觸和對話的機會，從來都不能帶來和平。」
After the summit, the leaders released a joint statement saying they had agreed to reform the United Nations, increase the production and supply of petroleum, oppose perceived abuses by the International Criminal Court against state officials, and continue their dialogue on reducing nuclear risks. “A nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought,” the statement said.
峰會結束後，三位領導人發表了一份聯合聲明，表示他們已同意就改革聯合國，增加石油的生產和供應，反對國際刑事法院對國家官員的所謂濫用管轄權行為，並繼續就降低核風險進行對話。「核戰爭打不贏也打不得，」聲明稱。
While China, which has the fastest-growing nuclear arsenal on earth, agreed to participate in the three-way discussion of nuclear weapons, the leaders announced little concrete beyond agreeing to continue a dialogue on reducing nuclear risks. Mr. Xi has long been reluctant to agree to nuclear arms control because China’s arsenal remains a fraction the size of those belonging to Russia and the United States.
中國核武庫增長速度位居全球之首，儘管它同意參與關於核武器的三方討論，但除了同意繼續就降低核風險進行對話之外，領導人們幾乎沒有宣布任何具體內容。習近平長期以來一直不願同意核軍備控制，因為中國的核武庫規模仍然只是俄羅斯和美國的一小部分。
In the end, Mr. Trump’s whirlwind journey — two weeks after the Republican Party suffered a wipeout in the midterm elections and amid anxiety at home about widespread digital outages — amounted to a grand show of camaraderie.
最終，川普的這次旋風式訪問——在共和黨於期中選舉中慘敗兩周後，且國內正因大範圍網路中斷而焦慮之際——無非是一場展示情誼的盛大表演。
As host of the tightly choreographed event, Mr. Xi flattered Mr. Trump and gave him an opportunity to ask for China’s assistance on matters ranging from ending global conflicts to spending more on U.S. agricultural goods. The summit also removed Mr. Putin from his status a global pariah over his invasion of Ukraine, elevating him to a leader deserving of equal diplomatic footing.
作為這場精心策劃的活動的東道主，習近平奉承川普，並給川普提供機會，在結束全球衝突和增加美國農產品採購等一系列問題上尋求中國的協助。這次峰會也使普丁擺脫了因入侵烏克蘭而成為全球棄兒的地位，將他提升為一位值得享有平等待遇的外交領導人。
But for all of the talk of peace, there was no announcement of breakthroughs in the talks the Trump administration is engaged in to end that conflict. Nor did Mr. Trump appear to dwell on finding a way out of the its war with Iran.
但是，儘管各方都在大談和平，但關於川普政府為結束該衝突而進行的談判並未宣布任何突破性進展。川普似乎也沒有過多關注如何解決美國與伊朗之間的戰爭。
廣告
“We don’t need any help,” Mr. Trump said, when asked by a Fox News reporter before the summit whether he would seek assistance from the other leaders in ending a conflict of his own making. In fact, the help Mr. Trump could ask for would involve demanding that China and Russia stop assisting Iran: U.S. intelligence has concluded that both countries provided either technology or targeting information that likely helped the Iranians strike American military bases in the Middle East.
「我們不需要任何幫助，」川普在峰會前被福克斯新聞記者問及是否會尋求其他領導人協助，以結束這場由他自己造成的衝突時說道。事實上，川普若要尋求幫助，就必須要求中國和俄羅斯停止援助伊朗：美國情報部門已得出結論，這兩個國家提供了技術或目標信息，這些信息可能幫助伊朗襲擊了美國在中東的軍事基地。
On Fox News, Mr. Trump described the three-way conversation as a way to disrupt the growing “partnership without limits” that Mr. Xi and Mr. Putin say they have formed in recent years. Mr. Trump said that he hoped to break up that alignment and turn it into a three-way conversation, rather than a partnership focused on countering American influence.
川普將這場三方對話描述為一種手段，旨在瓦解習近平和普丁聲稱近年來建立的日益緊密的「無上限夥伴關係」。川普表示，他希望能打破這種結盟，並將其轉變為三方對話，而不是一個專注於對抗美國影響力的夥伴關係。
China and Russia’s alignment, Mr. Trump said, “should have never been allowed to happen.”
川普說，中國和俄羅斯的結盟「本來就不應該被允許發生」。
Seeking his own path into Mr. Xi’s good graces, Mr. Trump has held three meetings with the Chinese leader this year, with the goal of cooling a tit-for-tat trade war and of building up a personal relationship through mutual flattery. Mr. Trump traveled to Joint Base Andrews to welcome Mr. Xi to Washington in May, and Mr. Xi responded in kind when Mr. Trump arrived in Shenzhen on Wednesday, treating the president to a military exercise and a fleet of U.S. and Chinese flag-waving dancers in a flurry of pink, blue and white outfits.
為了尋求獲得習近平的好感，川普今年已經與中國領導人舉行了三次會晤，旨在緩和針鋒相對的貿易戰，並通過相互奉承建立個人關係。今年5月，川普專程前往安德魯斯聯合基地，歡迎習近平訪問華盛頓；而當川普於週三抵達深圳時，習近平也做出了同樣的回應，以軍事演習招待總統，還安排了一支身穿粉色、藍色和白色服裝，揮舞中美兩國國旗的舞蹈隊表演。
Mr. Xi, for his part, said a much improved relationship between China and the United States in recent months was “the right way” for the two powers to “get along.” But he also warned that the key to keeping ties stable “lies in taking concrete actions to implement the consensus reached by the two heads of state.” The remarks came days after Washington moved to close a loophole on Chinese access to advanced A.I. chips.
習近平則表示，近幾個月來中美之間大為改善的關係是兩個大國「相處」的「正確之道」。但他也警告說，保持雙邊關係穩定的關鍵是「在落實元首共識上付諸行動」。這些言論發表幾天前，華盛頓剛剛採取行動，堵住了一個中國獲取先進人工智慧晶片的漏洞。
As for Mr. Putin, the summit was his first encounter with Mr. Trump since the two men met in Alaska last year. That meeting was a diplomatic flop, and the war in Ukraine has intensified since then. The peace mechanism Mr. Trump advocated — a direct meeting between Mr. Putin and President Volodymyr Zelensky of Ukraine, moderated by Mr. Trump — has yet to materialize.
至於普丁，這次峰會是自去年他與川普在阿拉斯加會面以來，二人首次重逢。那次會晤以外交失敗告終，此後烏克蘭戰爭愈演愈烈。川普所倡導的和平機制——由川普主持、普丁與烏克蘭總統澤連斯基直接會面——至今尚未實現。
廣告
This time, White House officials were modest in their predictions. Expectations were set so low that White House officials who previewed the trip last week initially said that Mr. Trump would not bring up the war in Ukraine at all, although he ultimately did.
這一次，白宮官員對此次會晤的預期相當低調。預期被設定得如此之低，以至於上週預告此次行程的白宮官員起初表示，川普根本不會提及烏克蘭戰爭，儘管他最終還是提了。
In Shenzhen, Mr. Trump continued welcoming Russia back into the fold of great superpowers. He called Mr. Putin “my friend,” and insisted that all three leaders would like to see the war end quickly. The Russian leader reiterated after the meeting, however, that he would not be negotiating with Ukraine.
在深圳，川普繼續歡迎俄羅斯重返超級大國行列。他稱普丁為「我的朋友」，並堅稱三位領導人都希望看到戰爭儘快結束。然而，俄羅斯領導人在會後重申，他不會與烏克蘭進行談判。
During the trilateral meeting, Mr. Xi and Mr. Putin embraced the idea of peaceful leadership, without making commitments that would be much of a burden to either of their countries. In his own remarks, Mr. Putin said that their nations were “united not only by the status of being the victorious countries in World War II, but most importantly by the joint responsibility for maintaining international peace and security.”
在這次三邊會議期間，習近平和普丁贊同和平領導的理念，而沒有做出會對中俄兩國造成沉重負擔的承諾。普丁在自己的講話中表示，中俄兩國「不僅因為二戰戰勝國的地位而團結在一起，最重要的是因維護國際和平與安全的共同責任而團結在一起」。
And Mr. Xi mentioned a utopian goal that has pretty much fallen off the agenda of superpowers. He described nuclear weapons as a “sword of Damocles hanging over humanity” that should be eliminated. But Beijing has typically called on the United States to take the first steps in arms control, a demand that Mr. Trump seemed to acknowledge earlier in the day.
習近平提到了一項基本上已從超級大國議程中消失的烏托邦式目標。他將核武器描述為「懸在人類頭上的達摩克利斯之劍」，應該予以消除。但北京一貫呼籲美國在軍備控制方面邁出第一步，川普在當天早些時候似乎也承認了這一要求。
“For me, it’s a hard thing,” he told Fox News. “And it’s probably hard for them to do also.”
「對我來說，這是一件難事，」他告訴福克斯新聞。「對他們來說，可能也很難做到。」
It has been done before, by Mr. Trump’s predecessors. Today, the United States and Russia each have slightly more than 5,000 nuclear warheads, and only 1,550 deployed under the New Start treaty that expired earlier this year — down from tens of thousands on both sides during the Cold War.
川普的前任們此前曾做到過。如今，美國和俄羅斯各自擁有略多於5000枚核彈頭，在今年早些時候到期的《新削減戰略武器條約》下，僅部署了1550枚——而冷戰期間，雙方擁有的核彈頭數量曾高達數萬枚。
China, however, was never a party to past nuclear reduction treaties, and it is now intent on building up to at least current Russian and U.S. levels.
然而，中國從未參與過以往的核裁軍條約，而且現在正致力於將核武庫至少擴大到當前美俄的水平。
Mr. Trump also said in Shenzen that he wanted to crack down on nuclear proliferation by smaller powers — his justification for attacking Iran — and restrain what he said were the excesses of “rogue” organizations like the International Criminal Court.
川普在深圳還表示，他希望打擊較小國家的核擴散行為——這是他攻擊伊朗的理由——並遏制他所稱的如國際刑事法院等「流氓」組織的過度行為。
廣告
Mr. Putin is subject to an I.C.C. arrest warrant over alleged war crimes in Ukraine. Nevertheless Mr. Trump welcomed the Russian leader in Alaska last year and extending an invitation to the Group of 20 summit next month in Florida. Mr. Putin told reporters after the meeting that he was unsure if he would attend.
普丁因涉嫌在烏克蘭犯下戰爭罪而受到國際刑事法院的逮捕令通緝。儘管如此，川普仍於去年在阿拉斯加歡迎了這位俄羅斯領導人，並邀請他參加下個月在佛羅里達州舉行的20國集團峰會。普丁在會後對記者表示，他不確定自己是否會出席。
