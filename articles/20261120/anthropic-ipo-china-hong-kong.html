Anthropic上市，中資再被排除在外

China Is Shut Out of a Blockbuster Public Offering Once Again

艾莎 • 2026-11-20
It was the biggest initial public offering in history and investors from China and Hong Kong were left out.
這是史上最大的首次公開募股，而來自中國和香港的投資者被排除在外。
Anthropic, the artificial intelligence company and maker of the Claude chatbot, excluded investment from Chinese and Hong Kong firms from buying its shares in advance of the I.P.O., according to three people with knowledge of the decision, who asked not to be identified because they were not authorized to speak publicly.
據三位了解該決定的人士透露，在IPO之前，人工智慧公司、Claude聊天機器人的製造商Anthropic排除了中國和香港機構的購股資格。由於未獲授權公開發表言論，這三位人士要求不具名。
A representative for Anthropic declined to comment, and the reason for not accepting investments from the region was not clear. But it follows a move by the Trump administration to keep artificial intelligence out of China’s reach. Both Washington and Beijing are drawing red lines around their homegrown technology as they compete for supremacy in A.I.
Anthropic的一名代表拒絕置評，而不接受該地區投資的原因至今尚未明確。但此舉緊隨川普政府的一項旨在防止中國獲取人工智慧技術的措施之後。隨著華盛頓和北京在人工智慧領域爭奪霸權，雙方都在為其本土技術劃定紅線。
It also follows a similar move by SpaceX and its bankers to shut out China from its blockbuster public offering. OpenAI, another leading A.I. company, is expected to bar Chinese investors from its eventual public offering. OpenAI has already barred Chinese investors from participating in private fund-raising. All three companies have counted the U.S. government as a client.
此前SpaceX及其銀行家也採取了類似舉措，即在其轟動一時的公開募股中將中國拒之門外。另一家領先的人工智慧公司OpenAI最終進行IPO時，也將禁止中國投資者參與。OpenAI已經禁止了中國投資者參與私募融資。這三家公司都與美國政府有業務往來。
廣告
The news that Anthropic would bar Chinese investors was reported earlier by Bloomberg.
Anthropic將禁止中國投資者的消息早前由彭博社報導。
President Trump and China’s leader, Xi Jinping, met on Thursday at the APEC meeting in the Chinese city of Shenzhen, where Mr. Xi called on members to build an “open ecosystem” for technology.
週四，川普總統和中國領導人習近平在中國城市深圳舉行的亞太經合組織(APEC)會議上會晤，習近平在會上呼籲成員國構建技術的「開放創新生態」。
Both Mr. Trump and Mr. Xi are trying to inject some stability — and a little bonhomie — into an increasingly rocky economic relationship between the world’s two biggest economies. But their governments continue to build fortresses around trade, technology and capital.
川普和習近平都試圖為這兩個世界最大經濟體之間日益動盪的經濟關係注入一些穩定性——以及一絲友好氣氛。但兩國政府繼續圍繞貿易、技術和資本構築堡壘。
Hours before Mr. Trump arrived in Asia to attend meetings with regional leaders, his administration moved to restrict China’s access to advanced artificial intelligence chips. In response, China warned it could hit back, saying “if the U.S. persists, China will take countermeasures.”
在川普抵達亞洲參加與地區領導人的會議前數小時，其政府採取行動限制中國獲取先進人工智慧晶片。對此，中國警告稱可能會進行反擊，表示「若美方一意孤行，中方將採取反制措施」。
“China is deeply concerned about — and firmly opposes — a series of recent economic and trade restrictive measures taken by the U.S. against China,” a spokesman for China’s Ministry of Commerce said on Thursday.
「中方對美方近期出台的一系列涉華經貿限制措施強烈關注，堅決反對，」中國商務部發言人週四表示。
China experts warned that the new A.I. rule, the latest in a series of escalatory moves from both sides, will make it harder for both Mr. Xi and Mr. Trump to come to any meaningful agreement to de-escalate rising conflict between the world’s biggest economies.
中國問題專家警告稱，新的人工智慧規定是雙方一系列升級舉措中的最新一項，這將使習近平和川普更難達成任何有意義的協議，以緩和這兩個世界最大經濟體之間日益加劇的衝突。
廣告
“At a minimum, this step — and potential others yet to come — complicate reaching a deal to extend the cease-fire,” said Scott Kennedy, researcher at the Center for Strategic and International Studies in Washington.
「至少，這一舉措——以及未來可能出台的其他舉措——將給休戰協議的延展製造困難，」華盛頓戰略與國際問題研究中心研究員甘思德(Scott Kennedy)表示。

艾莎(Alexandra Stevenson)是《紐約時報》記者，主要報導中國經濟及其在全球的影響。
