英偉達曾申請向中國涉軍企業出售晶片，商務部批准

Nvidia Asked to Sell Chips to Companies Tied to Chinese Military and Megaspeed

ANA SWANSON, CECILIA KANG • 2026-11-20
Lawmakers released confidential information on Thursday showing that the Commerce Department had approved the sale of more than 800,000 advanced A.I. chips to Chinese companies for the year, as of May 11, including several firms on a list of Chinese military companies maintained by the Pentagon.
立法者週四發布了機密信息，顯示截至5月11日，美國商務部今年已批准向中國企業出售超過80萬枚先進人工智慧晶片，其中包括幾家被五角大廈列入中國涉軍企業名單的公司。
The department had received applications to sell more than 3.4 million of the chips to Chinese companies as of that date, lawmakers said. It approved 75,000 artificial intelligence chips for Alibaba, a Chinese A.I. giant that The New York Times reported this week has ties with a military-linked laboratory.
立法者表示，商務部截至該日期收到向中國企業出售超過340萬枚此類晶片的申請。該部批准向阿里巴巴出售7.5萬枚人工智慧晶片，阿里巴巴是一家中國人工智慧巨頭，《紐約時報》本週報導稱其與一家具有軍方背景的實驗室有聯繫。
The release also showed that Nvidia had submitted an application in April to sell more than 40,000 of its advanced H200 chips to a company tied to Megaspeed and Alice Huang, the executive who U.S. intelligence has concluded was a Chinese asset who had an affair with the former Nvidia chief executive.
此次發布的信息還顯示，英偉達曾在4月份提交申請，計劃將其超過4萬枚先進的H200晶片出售給一家與Megaspeed及其高管黃樂(Alice Huang)有聯繫的公司。美國情報部門已得出結論，認定黃樂是一名與前英偉達首席執行官有染的中國情報線人。
The company — New Century Lettall Data Service — is part of a network of Chinese companies with ties to Megaspeed and Ms. Huang. Its historical ownership traces back in part to an investment firm controlled by Ms. Huang’s sister, where Kang Wei, whom Ms. Huang often introduced as her husband, was also executive director.
這家名為新世紀利通數據服務有限公司的公司是與Megaspeed和黃樂有聯繫的中國企業網路的一部分。其歷史所有權部分可追溯至由黃樂的姐姐控制的一家投資公司，而康偉（黃樂經常將其介紹為她的丈夫）也曾是該公司的執行董事。
The Commerce Department also approved 150,000 chips for the Chinese tech conglomerate Tencent and its subsidiaries; 43,000 for Baidu, another Chinese A.I. firm; and 4,000 for BYD, a carmaker, the lawmakers said. They were Nvidia’s H200 chips and the equivalent from the chip maker AMD, the MI325X.
立法者表示，商務部還批准向中國科技集團騰訊及其子公司出售15萬枚晶片；向另一家中國人工智慧公司百度出售4.3萬枚；向汽車製造商比亞迪出售4000枚。這些晶片是英偉達的H200晶片以及晶片製造商AMD的同級別產品MI325X。
The lawmakers, from the House Foreign Affairs Committee, took special note of the Commerce Department’s approval of sales to the companies Maginfra, Beijing New Matrix Information Technologies and Aivres Systems. The committee cited reporting by The New York Times that had raised questions about those firms because of their ties to sanctioned entities and noted that many of the license requests from Nvidia had listed the companies as entities that might install the systems in data centers.
來自眾議院外交事務委員會的立法者特別關注了商務部批准向至索(Maginfra)、北京新矩陣信息技術有限公司(Beijing New Matrix Information Technologies)以及Aivres Systems出售晶片的決定。該委員會引用了《紐約時報》的報導，該報導曾因這些公司與受制裁實體的聯繫而對其提出質疑，委員會還指出，英偉達的許多許可申請都將這些公司列為可能在數據中心安裝這些系統的實體。
Two Times reports first raised concerns about the firms in September and October, saying that Aivres was the subsidiary of a sanctioned Chinese firm that had exported billions of dollars of advanced Nvidia chips to Megaspeed, the company at the center of the Nvidia scandal.
《紐約時報》分別在9月和10月的兩份報導中首先提出對這些公司的關切，稱Aivres是一家受制裁中國企業的子公司，該受制裁企業已向處於英偉達醜聞中心的Megaspeed公司出口了價值數十億美元的先進英偉達晶片。
廣告
Times reporters also visited the China offices of Maginfra and New Matrix and found them to be empty. No one present had heard of the firms, and scant information existed about them online, raising questions about whether they really existed.
《紐約時報》記者還走訪了至索和新矩陣的中國辦公室，發現裡面空無一人。在場的人都沒有聽說過這些公司，網上關於它們的信息也寥寥無幾，這引發了關於它們是否真實存在的疑問。
The revelations that Nvidia’s chief executive, Jensen Huang, was having an affair with Ms. Huang, along with a second woman at Nvidia, forced him to step down last week. The Justice and Commerce Departments said this week that Megaspeed was under criminal investigation for potentially smuggling chips into China.
由於與黃樂以及英偉達內部另一名女性有染的事件曝光，黃仁勛被迫於上週辭職。美國司法部和商務部本週表示，Megaspeed正因涉嫌向中國走私晶片而接受刑事調查。
The incident has sparked criticism from lawmakers that the Trump administration, and the Commerce Department in particular, did too little to police the flow of sensitive technology to China. Officials both in the administration and on Capitol Hill have criticized the department for maintaining close ties to Nvidia, which it is charged with regulating.
這起事件引發了國會議員的批評，他們認為川普政府，尤其是商務部，在監管敏感技術流向中國方面做得遠遠不夠。政府內部和國會的官員都批評商務部與英偉達保持密切關係，儘管該部門負有監管英偉達的職責。
The Commerce Department has defended its processes, saying that it closely follows national security regulations. Every Nvidia and AMD application also stated that the proposed transfer posed a low risk of diversion, the committee’s release said.
商務部為其審批流程進行了辯護，稱其嚴格遵守了國家安全規定。委員會發布的信息稱，英偉達和AMD提交的每一份申請均聲稱，擬議中的晶片轉讓發生違規轉移的風險很低。
John Rizzo, a spokesman for Nvidia, said that all of the company’s shipments were legal and authorized by the government, and that, despite receiving some licenses, Nvidia had already stopped shipping H200s to China. Nvidia “did not and will not” ship an H200 to the company linked to Megaspeed, or any other company, he said.
英偉達發言人約翰·裡佐表示，該公司的所有晶片出貨都是合法的，並獲得政府授權。儘管已獲得部分許可證，英偉達此前就已停止向中國發貨H200晶片。他說，英偉達「過去沒有、將來也不會」向與Megaspeed有關聯的那家公司或任何其他公司運送H200晶片。 
A spokeswoman for AMD said the company complied with applicable U.S. laws and regulations and had a rigorous process to ensure compliance.
AMD發言人表示，該公司遵守所有適用的美國法律法規，並建立了嚴格的合規審查程序。
廣告
A spokesman for the Commerce Department said the disclosure was evidence of the department’s transparency with Congress about licensing activity. Licensing decisions are the product of deliberation and consultation among multiple U.S. government agencies, he said.
商務部的發言人表示，此次信息披露證明了該部門在許可證審批方面對國會保持透明。他表示，許可證審批決定是美國政府多個機構審議和協商的結果。
President Trump approved the sale of the H200 to China in December under unusual conditions. He required the chips, which are assembled in Taiwan, to be routed through the United States on their way to China, so that the government could collect a 25 percent tariff on their value and perform a security check.
去年12月，川普總統批准向中國出售H200晶片，但附加了非同尋常的條件。他要求這些在台灣組裝的晶片在運往中國途中必須先經過美國，以便美國政府按晶片價值徵收25%的關稅，並進行安全檢查。
Wary of the arrangement and eager to promote its own technology, the Chinese government ended up approving few purchases from its firms.
中國政府對這一安排心存疑慮，同時又希望扶持本國技術，因此最終只批准了國內企業的少量採購申請。
Nvidia said in its earnings this week that it would halt sales of its H200 chips to China, though it did not give a reason for the move.
英偉達在本週的財報中表示，將停止向中國出售其H200晶片，不過並未給出此舉的理由。
In its statement, the House Foreign Affairs Committee said that withholding the information was “contrary to the national interest.” The committee plans to hold hearings next month with both Howard Lutnick, the commerce secretary, and his deputy Jeff Kessler, who has had a special role in approving license applications.
眾議院外交事務委員會在聲明中表示，隱瞞這些信息「有違國家利益」。該委員會計劃下個月舉行聽證會，傳喚商務部長霍華德·盧特尼克及其副手傑夫·凱斯勒。凱斯勒在許可證申請審批過程中承擔著特殊職責。
Representative Brian Mast, a Florida Republican and chairman of the committee, called Maginfra and New Matrix “fake companies,” and said that if the Commerce Department granted approvals to the firms, “obviously that is of tremendous concern to us,” he said.
該委員會主席、佛羅里達州共和黨眾議員布萊恩·馬斯特稱至索和新矩陣為「假公司」，並表示，如果商務部批准了這些公司的申請，「顯然，這會讓我們極為擔憂」。
廣告
Representative Gregory W. Meeks, Democrat of New York, said that the public deserved to know how decisions to sell technology to China were made, and that Congress needed to “conduct rigorous oversight.”
紐約州民主黨眾議員格雷戈裡·米克斯表示，公眾有權知道向中國出售技術的決定是如何作出的，而國會需要「進行嚴格監督」。
“Over the past month, the administration’s handling of chip export controls has put our national security and technology edge at risk,” he said.
「過去一個月，政府在晶片出口管制問題上的處理方式使我們的國家安全和技術領先優勢面臨風險，」他說。

Ana Swanson報導貿易和國際經濟新聞，常駐華盛頓。她從事新聞工作已超過十年。
