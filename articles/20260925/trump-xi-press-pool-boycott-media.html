川普的白宮歡迎專制者習近平，卻拒絕美國記者

In Trump’s White House, China’s Strongman Is Welcome. American Media, Less So.

PETER BAKER • 2026-09-25
At the White House gate, a throng of Chinese journalists was being admitted onto the grounds of the Executive Mansion to cover the visit of President Xi Jinping. Out on the street, several American journalists were blocked from entering the White House on the orders of President Trump.
在白宮門口，一群中國記者正獲准進入行政官邸，報導習近平主席的訪問。而在外面的街道上，數名美國記者卻因川普總統的命令無法入內。
The scene on Pennsylvania Avenue on Thursday morning captured a surreal week in the life of Washington. The president of the United States barred American reporters because he labeled them a threat to national security, while welcoming Chinese reporters who answer to the communist dictatorship in Beijing.
週四上午賓夕法尼亞大道上的這一幕捕捉到了華盛頓這一週荒誕離奇的政治現實。美國總統將美國記者拒之門外，稱他們是國家安全的威脅，同時卻歡迎那些聽命於北京共產黨獨裁政權的中國記者。
This would be the same president who is running government-paid television advertising vowing to stop the United States from becoming socialist or communist in the same week that he held a friendly meeting with the democratic socialist mayor of New York and rolled out a literal red carpet for the communist president of China. The same president who is waging economic war against the prime minister of Canada while inviting the president of Russia to visit his Florida golf club.
同樣是這位總統，一邊用政府經費投放電視廣告，信誓旦旦地宣稱要阻止美國變成社會主義或共產主義，一邊卻在同一週與紐約的民主社會主義市長友好會面，還為中國的這位共產黨領導人實實在在地鋪上了紅地毯。同樣也是這位總統，一邊對加拿大總理髮動經濟戰，一邊卻邀請俄羅斯總統訪問他位於佛羅里達的高爾夫俱樂部。
In the epic contest between democracy and autocracy, Mr. Trump has long given the impression that he may be on the other side. But rarely has that been brought home as starkly as this week, as he punishes news outlets for being too “negative” about him while embracing Mr. Xi and President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia, two of the world’s leading authoritarians whose governments brutally suppress free speech.
在民主與專制這場曠日持久的較量中，川普向來給人的印象是，他似乎更傾向於站在專制那一邊。但這種印象很少像本週這樣赤裸裸地呈現出來：他一邊因為新聞機構對自己的報導過於「負面」而懲罰它們，一邊卻熱情擁抱習近平和俄羅斯總統普丁——這兩位都是世界上主要的威權主義者，其政府殘酷壓制言論自由。
Mr. Xi’s arrival at the White House was celebrated with pomp and circumstance that were not covered by American television networks, which were still boycotting the White House press pool until Mr. Trump’s staff commits in writing to letting CNN cameras back into the rotation. Reporters from CNN, MS NOW and Politico who were initially barred from entering the White House on Thursday morning despite a court order were allowed in later in the day, but by late afternoon Mr. Trump’s staff had still not permitted CNN back into the pool that provides footage of the president every day.
習近平在白宮受到隆重歡迎，但美國的各家電視網卻沒有報導這一盛況，因為它們仍在抵制白宮記者團，直到川普的工作人員以書面形式承諾讓CNN的攝像機重回輪值。週四上午，儘管有法院命令，CNN、MS NOW和Politico的記者起初仍被禁止進入白宮；當天晚些時候，他們獲准進入。但截至下午晚些時候，川普的工作人員仍未允許CNN重新加入每日拍攝總統日常活動、供各方共享畫面的記者團。
Even as Mr. Trump fumed that his carefully orchestrated day of pageantry was not shown on air, he basked in Mr. Xi’s presence and lavished him with praise, making no mention of human rights issues that past presidents raised when meeting with Chinese counterparts.
儘管川普對自己精心籌劃的這場盛大儀式未能在電視上播出而大為光火，但他仍陶醉於中國領導人的到場，並對其大加讚美，隻字未提歷任總統在會晤中國領導人時通常都會提及的人權議題。
“From the time of my first election in 2016, President Xi and I have forged a friendship, a truly great friendship, actually, built on mutual respect and the vital interests of our people,” Mr. Trump said in the Grand Foyer of the White House. He later delighted in taking his great friend on a tour of the new White House ballroom that is under construction and showed off his taunting photograph of an autopen meant to represent his predecessor, former President Joseph R. Biden Jr.
「自2016年我首次當選以來，我和習主席打造了友誼關係，一段真正偉大的友誼，這段友誼建立在相互尊重和我們的人民的共同利益之上，」川普在白宮的主門廳說道。後來，他興緻勃勃地帶著這位好友參觀正在施工的新白宮宴會廳，還得意地向其展示了一張帶有嘲諷意味的照片，照片中是一個自動簽名筆，用以影射他的前任拜登總統。
Mr. Trump hopes to similarly host Mr. Putin soon. Secretary of State Marco Rubio announced this week that Mr. Trump had invited the once-ostracized Russian leader to attend the annual Group of 20 meeting in Miami in December, which would be his first appearance at the annual gathering since 2019, before his full-fledged invasion of Ukraine. The summit is scheduled to be held at Trump National Doral Miami, where the president tried to host a Group of 7 meeting in his first term only to back down after Republican complaints that it was too self-dealing — complaints not heard this time.
川普希望不久後也能以類似方式接待普丁。國務卿魯比奧本週宣布，川普已經邀請這位一度遭到國際社會排斥的俄羅斯領導人出席12月在邁阿密舉行的二十國集團年度峰會。如果成行，這將是普丁自2019年——即其全面入侵烏克蘭之前——以來首次出席這一年度聚會。峰會計劃在川普國家多拉爾邁阿密度假村舉行。川普第一個任期內曾試圖在那裡舉辦七國集團峰會，卻因共和黨內部批評此舉涉嫌以權謀私而作罷，而這一次，這樣的抱怨聲卻沒有出現。
While Mr. Putin was kicked out of the G7 and other groups because of his seizure of Crimea in 2014, Mr. Trump has been eager to bring him back into the international fold. The International Criminal Court issued an arrest warrant for Mr. Putin on war crimes charges in 2023, but Mr. Trump ignored that by inviting the Russian to Alaska last year — and just this week denounced the court in an address to the U.N. General Assembly as “an evil group of people” as his administration attempts to dismantle the body.
普丁因為2014年吞併克里米亞而被逐出七國集團及其他國際組織，但川普一直熱衷於讓他重新融入國際社會。國際刑事法院2023年以戰爭罪指控對普丁發出逮捕令，但川普對此置之不理，去年還邀請這位俄羅斯領導人前往阿拉斯加。就在本週，他又在聯合國大會發表講話時斥責該法院是「一群邪惡的人」，而他的政府目前正試圖搞垮該機構。
Mr. Trump’s affinity for despots, tyrants and autocrats has baffled democratic leaders and diplomats and reoriented American foreign policy for some 10 years now. He gushes over the likes of Kim Jong-un of North Korea (“we fell in love”), Abdel Fattah el-Sisi of Egypt (“my favorite dictator”), Mohammed bin Salman of Saudi Arabia (“I like him too much”), Recep Tayyip Erdogan of Turkey (“a very good friend of mine”) and Nayib Bukele of El Salvador (“one hell of a president”).
川普對獨裁者、暴君與威權統治者的親近，過去大約10年裡一直令民主國家的領導人和外交官感到困惑，也重新塑造了美國的外交政策。他對朝鮮的金正恩（「我們墜入了愛河」）、埃及的阿卜杜勒-法塔赫·塞西（「我最喜歡的獨裁者」）、沙烏地阿拉伯的穆罕默德·本·薩勒曼（「我太喜歡他了」）、土耳其的雷傑普·塔伊普·埃爾多安（「我的一個非常好的朋友」）以及薩爾瓦多的納伊布·布克爾（「一位了不起的總統」）等人讚不絕口。
By contrast, he regularly feuds with leaders of traditional allies like Canada, Britain, France, Germany and Italy. He derided the democratically elected president of Ukraine, Volodymyr Zelensky, last year as a “dictator without elections” while offering no concerns about Mr. Putin, whose elections have been documented as thoroughly rigged. He had the United States vote along with North Korea, Belarus and Sudan last year against a U.N. resolution condemning Russia’s invasion of Ukraine and lately has been courting Belarus’s dictator Aleksandr G. Lukashenko.
與此形成鮮明對比的是，他時常與加拿大、英國、法國、德國、義大利等傳統盟友的領導人爆發爭執。去年，他嘲諷烏克蘭民選總統澤連斯基是「一個未經選舉的獨裁者」，卻未對普丁表達過類似擔憂，而後者的歷次選舉早已被證實為徹頭徹尾地舞弊操縱。去年，他曾讓美國與朝鮮、白俄羅斯、蘇丹一道投票反對一項譴責俄羅斯入侵烏克蘭的聯合國決議，而最近更是開始向白俄羅斯獨裁者亞歷山大·盧卡申科示好。
At home, Mr. Trump regularly battles the domestic news media, denouncing journalists as “enemies of the people,” a phrase used by Stalin to condemn dissidents to the gulag. More than any American president in modern times, Mr. Trump has mounted a wide-ranging campaign against independent media through multibillion-dollar lawsuits, Federal Communications Commission pressure, subpoenas to reporters, the eviction of The Associated Press from the White House press pool and restrictions that effectively forced reporters out of the Pentagon.
在國內，川普經常與美國新聞媒體交戰，斥責記者是「人民的敵人」——史達林曾用這一說法給異見人士定罪，將他們送進古拉格。與現代任何一位美國總統相比，川普針對獨立媒體發起的打壓行動範圍都更加廣泛，包括索賠數十億美元的訴訟、通過聯邦通信委員會施壓、向記者發出傳票、將美聯社逐出白宮記者團，以及施加實質上迫使記者離開五角大廈的限制措施。
Mr. Trump seemed unbothered on Thursday by any mixed messages sent by welcoming Chinese state reporters at the same time he was trying to throw out American news outlets as part of what he called his “ban on the free press.” On the other hand, he did seem bothered by the fact that the still-unresolved showdown meant that boycotting network television cameras did not beam the festivities to the nation.
週四，川普似乎並不在意這樣做釋放出的矛盾信號：一邊歡迎中國官方媒體記者，一邊又試圖把美國新聞機構趕出去——他把自己的做法稱為「對自由媒體的禁令」。不過，另一方面，他似乎的確對這場仍未解決的對峙所造成的結果感到不滿：參與抵制的電視網沒有用攝像機把這些盛大場面傳向全國。
廣告
In a social media post late in the day, Mr. Trump praised Mr. Xi as “the Highly Respected President of China” and flattered “his beautiful wife, Madam Peng” while accusing CNN and MS NOW of having “NO CREDIBILITY” and being “Fake News.” A White House pool report indicated that at one point during the day Mr. Trump “made a joke about how the Chinese had the friendliest press corps.”
當天晚些時候，川普在社群媒體發帖，稱讚習近平是「備受尊敬的中國國家主席」，還恭維「他美麗的妻子彭女士」，與此同時，他指責CNN和MS NOW「毫無公信力」，是「假新聞」。白宮記者團的一篇報導顯示，當天川普曾「開玩笑說，中國擁有最友好的記者團」。
Which of course depends on the definition of friendly. China exercises iron-fisted control over its press corps and has at least 111 journalists and media workers locked up, according to Reporters Without Borders, making it “the world’s largest prison for journalists.” Of the 180 countries that the group ranks on freedom of the press, China ranks almost at rock bottom at 178th place, above only North Korea and Eritrea. (The United States has fallen to 64th under Mr. Trump.)
當然，這取決於怎麼定義「友好」。中國對新聞媒體實行鐵腕控制。據無國界記者組織統計，中國至少關押了111名記者和媒體工作者，因此成為「世界上關押記者最多的國家」。在該組織對180個國家的新聞自由度排名中，中國幾乎墊底，位列第178名，僅高於朝鮮和厄立特里亞。（川普執政下的美國已跌至第64位。）
“Trump doesn’t just want to meet with Xi — he wants to copy him,” Senator Chris Coons, Democrat of Delaware and a senior member of the Senate Foreign Relations Committee, wrote on social media, citing attacks on the press, politicizing the justice system and purging the civil service.
「川普不僅僅是想與習近平會面——他還想效仿他，」特拉華州民主党參議員、參議院外交關係委員會資深成員克里斯·庫恩斯在社群媒體上寫道，並列舉了其打壓新聞媒體、將司法系統政治化、清洗公務員隊伍等種種行徑作為佐證。
Even some Republicans, while not openly questioning Mr. Trump’s media ban, expressed disappointment that the president, in courting the leader of one of America’s largest trading partners, was so willing to flatter and fete Mr. Xi while overlooking China’s record of government oppression.
甚至一些共和黨人也表達了失望。雖然他們沒有公開質疑川普針對媒體的禁令，但他們認為，在爭取美國最大貿易夥伴之一的領導人時，總統如此樂於奉承、盛情款待習近平，卻對中國政府的壓迫記錄視而不見，令人失望。
“During every minute of dialogue, our commander in chief should keep in mind that his guest is a brutal, unelected and oppressive dictator who seeks to dominate his neighbors and whose massive military arsenal is aimed directly at the United States of America,” said Senator Roger Wicker of Mississippi, the Republican chairman of the Senate Armed Services Committee.
「在每一分鐘的對話中，我們的三軍統帥都應該牢記，他的客人是一名殘暴、未經選舉產生、實行壓迫統治的獨裁者，他試圖支配鄰國，而且其龐大的軍事武庫直接瞄準美利堅合眾國，」密西西比州共和黨參議員、參議院軍事委員會主席羅傑·威克說。
That was not, however, the message being emphasized at the White House, where the president’s aides this week sought to replace the news outlets he tried to bar by kicking off their own online “Trump TV,” a form of state television that Mr. Xi might recognize. That is certainly one way for Mr. Trump to have his own friendliest press corps.
然而，這並非白宮方面所著力傳遞的信息。本週，總統的助手們試圖通過推出自己的在線「川普電視」來取代他試圖封殺的新聞媒體——這是習近平可能不會陌生的國家電視台形式。對川普來說，這當然是一種擁有屬於自己的「最友好記者團」的辦法。

Peter Baker是《紐約時報》首席白宮記者。川普是他報導的第六任美國總統，有時他也撰寫將總統和美國政府置於更宏觀的背景和歷史框架下的分析性文章。
