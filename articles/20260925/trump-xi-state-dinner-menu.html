「習川會」國宴菜單：芝麻脆皮海鱸魚和爽脆嫩白菜

See the Menu for the State Dinner Honoring Xi Jinping

LEO SANDS • 2026-09-25
President Trump and the first lady, Melania Trump, are preparing to host President Xi Jinping of China for a state dinner in the East Room of the White House on Thursday evening.
川普總統與第一夫人梅拉尼婭·川普正準備於週四晚在白宮東廳為中國國家主席習近平舉行國宴。
According to the three-course menu released by the White House, the dishes were chosen to emphasize American ingredients with a “subtle Chinese influence.”
根據白宮公布的三道菜菜單，菜品的選擇主要展示美國食材，同時「巧妙融入中國元素」。
Chefs will serve Mr. Trump, Mr. Xi and other guests a main course of sesame-crusted sea bass and crisp baby bok choy. The yellow squash appetizer will be drizzled with scallion oil. According to the White House, the dishes bring “the culinary traditions of both nations together.”
廚師將為川普、習近平和其他賓客奉上一道以芝麻脆皮海鱸魚和爽脆嫩白菜為主的主菜。作為前菜的黃南瓜濃湯將淋上蔥油。白宮稱，這些菜品將「兩國的烹飪傳統融為一體」。
The East Room tables will be laid with red linens and its urns filled with red blossoms, which the administration said was a reference to the color’s significance in China. “The décor for the evening is an expression of warmth and celebration,” it said.
東廳的餐桌將鋪上紅色桌布，花器也將插滿紅花。政府表示，這一布置是為了呼應紅色在中國文化中的特殊意義。「當晚的裝飾體現了熱情與喜慶的氛圍，」白宮表示。

Diners will begin their meal by toasting with a glass of Schramsberg. That is the California sparkling wine that President Richard M. Nixon drank in 1972 during his visit to Beijing, which heralded a historic thaw in U.S.-Chinese relations.
晚宴將以世釀伯格酒莊的氣泡酒祝酒開場。1972年，尼克森總統訪問北京時喝的正是這款加州起泡酒。那次訪問開啟了美中關係歷史性的破冰。

The opera singer Christopher Macchio, who performed this month at the Republican midterm convention in Dallas, will headline the evening’s musical lineup.
男高音歌唱家克里斯托弗·馬基奧將領銜當晚的音樂演出。本月，他曾在達拉斯舉行的共和黨期中選舉大會上獻唱。
