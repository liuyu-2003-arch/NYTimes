打造韓國版澳門：美國原住民部落的一場豪賭

How the Mohegan Tribe Lost a Billion-Dollar Casino in South Korea

JOHN YOON • 2026-08-05
The Mohegan Native American tribe spent $1.6 billion and almost a decade building a gambling venue near Seoul with three five-star hotels, a 15,000-seat arena and a water park so lavish that Netflix chose to film a hit dating show there.
莫西乾美國原住民部落斥資16億美元、耗時近10年，在首爾附近建造了一座綜合博彩度假村，包括三家五星級酒店、一座有1.5萬個座位的綜藝館，以及一座豪華到連奈飛都選擇在那裡拍攝熱門戀愛真人秀的水上樂園。
The Inspire Entertainment Resort was Mohegan’s first casino project outside North America, one designed to tap into a lucrative market of Chinese gamblers, who spent $28 billion on games in 2024 in Macau. While that Chinese territory dominates the global gambling market, Inspire was set to become one of the largest high-end establishments of its kind in Asia. But going all-in on a casino resort in the region proved to be a risky bet for the tribe.
迎仕柏度假城(Inspire)是莫西乾在北美以外的首個賭場項目，原本是為了瞄準利潤豐厚的中國賭客市場——2024年，中國賭客在澳門的博彩消費達到280億美元。儘管澳門這片中國領土主導著全球博彩市場，但迎仕柏本有望成為亞洲規模最大的高端賭場度假村之一。然而，事實證明，對莫西乾來說，在該地區全力投資賭場度假村是一場冒險的賭博。
Less than a year after Inspire’s 2024 grand opening, a missed performance target caused Mohegan to lose control of the project. Now another investor, the private equity giant Bain Capital, rather than the tribe, holds the reins.
迎仕柏於2024年盛大開幕，不足一年，由於未能達到業績目標，莫西幹部落失去了對該項目的控制權。如今，接掌控制權的是另一位投資方——私募股權巨頭貝恩資本。
A South Korea casino was a gamble from the start.
從一開始，在韓國開賭場就是一場豪賭。
廣告
Mohegan operates some of the biggest casinos in the United States. Mohegan Sun, its flagship property on the tribe’s reservation in Connecticut, is a widely recognized casino with billboards across the Northeast and a major events venue that has hosted performances by Dolly Parton, Taylor Swift and Jay-Z. The casino made the tribe $260 million last year.
莫西乾經營著美國一些規模最大的賭場。位於康乃狄克州該部落保留地上的旗艦物業金神大賭場(Mohegan Sun)廣為人知，在美國東北部各地都能看到它的廣告牌；這裡還是大型演出場地，曾舉辦過多莉·帕頓、泰勒·斯威夫特和Jay-Z等藝人的演出。去年，這家賭場為部落帶來了2.6億美元收入。
Eyeing global expansion, Mohegan considered several proposals, including resorts in Greece and Japan. It settled on South Korea, where it obtained a license in 2016 to build a casino.
著眼全球擴張，莫西乾考察了多個方案，包括在希臘和日本開發度假村，最終選定韓國，並於2016年取得了在當地開賭場的牌照。
In South Korea, citizens cannot legally gamble anywhere except at one government-managed casino resort. That meant that all of Inspire’s casino customers would have to be foreign.
在韓國，除了一家政府運營的賭場度假村外，本國公民在其他任何地方賭博均屬違法。這意味著迎仕柏的賭場顧客只能是外國人。
But Mohegan executives saw an untapped Asian market for a casino near Incheon International Airport, one of the world’s busiest travel hubs with frequent, short flights to Beijing, Shanghai and Tokyo. Of particular interest were the millions of Chinese travelers who were visiting South Korea each year when work on Inspire began.
但莫希乾高管認為，在仁川國際機場附近建賭場，面向的是一個尚未開發的亞洲市場。仁川機場是全球最繁忙的交通樞紐之一，有頻繁的短途航班往返北京、上海和東京。尤其令他們感興趣的是，在迎仕柏動工之時，每年有數以百萬計中國遊客到訪韓國。
South Korean officials said over the years that they hoped to develop an alternative tourist destination to Macau, the Chinese territory widely known as the world’s gambling capital. Macau recorded a decline in casino revenues in the 2010s as it was rocked by a government crackdown on corruption.
多年來，韓國官員一直表示，希望打造一個能夠與澳門媲美的旅遊目的地。澳門這片中國領土被稱為全球博彩之都。在2010年代，澳門受到中國政府反腐行動的衝擊，賭場收入出現下滑。
Inspire had the potential to be “transformational” for Mohegan, company executives told JPMorgan investors in 2016. Mohegan declined to respond to repeated requests for specific details about Inspire.
2016年，莫西乾的高管在向摩根大通投資者的路演中表示，迎仕柏有潛力成為莫西乾的「變革性」機遇。莫西乾方面多次拒絕就迎仕柏的具體細節作出回應。
廣告
Mohegan said that it ultimately committed more than $1 billion to the project, including $300 million in cash. Bain Capital also saw a good investment. In 2021, a Bain subsidiary and other financial institutions signed a $275 million loan agreement to help Mohegan finance Inspire, according to a filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.
莫西乾表示，最終它向該項目投入逾10億美元，其中包括3億美元現金。貝恩資本也認為這是一個不錯的投資機會。根據提交給美國證券交易委員會的一份文件，2021年，貝恩旗下的一家子公司與其他金融機構簽署了一項2.75億美元的貸款協議，幫助莫西乾為迎仕柏提供融資。
“It was certainly a risky move on behalf of Mohegan,” said Andrew Tottenham, a gambling industry consultant in London. “If you’re not getting local people getting to play, you are dependent totally on foreigners staying in your hotel or hotels nearby.”
倫敦博彩業顧問安德魯·托特納姆表示：「對莫希根而言，這無疑是一步險棋。如果當地人不來玩，你就完全依賴入住你的酒店或附近酒店的外國人。」
For all its ambition, a project as large as Inspire needed many things to go right. Construction did not begin until 2019, while rising building costs cut into revenues. Then international tourism plummeted when the Covid pandemic hit. There were labor shortages, too.
儘管公司雄心勃勃，但像迎仕柏這樣龐大的項目需要諸多因素都順利推進。工程直到2019年才開工，而不斷上漲的建設成本侵蝕了項目收益。隨後，新冠疫情暴發，國際旅遊業驟降。與此同時，勞動力也出現短缺。
The opening, initially set for 2022, was postponed.
原定於2022年開業的計劃被迫推遲。
“It was just a string of bad luck,” said Steve Gallaway, a consultant who worked on Inspire after its opening. “The projected returns were never a grand slam.”
開業後參與該項目的顧問史提夫·加洛韋表示：「這就是一連串的壞運氣。而且這個項目原本預期的回報也從來沒有高到令人驚艷的程度。」
There was a soft launch in November 2023, when Mohegan opened Inspire’s hotel rooms, convention center, restaurants and arena. But the casino, a main moneymaker, did not open until February 2024.
2023年11月，迎仕柏的酒店客房、會議中心、餐廳和綜藝館場進行了試營業。但作為主要收入來源的賭場直到2024年2月才正式營業。
廣告
“They misjudged how quickly they could get this up and running and underestimated the challenges of going international at that scale,” said Robert Heller, a gambling consultant in New York State.
"他們低估了要讓這個項目順利運轉所需的時間，也低估了以這種規模進軍國際市場所面臨的挑戰，"紐約州的博彩顧問羅伯特·赫勒說。
When Mohegan fell short, Bain moved in.
當莫西乾未達預期時，貝恩資本接手。
Mohegan became the first Native American Tribe to own and operate a casino in Asia.
莫西乾成為首個在亞洲擁有並運營賭場的美國原住民部落。
But after the resort opened, Mohegan repeatedly reported low casino profits and high operational costs at Inspire. The resort lost about $104 million in its first year of operations, according to an audit submitted to the South Korean government.
但度假村開業後，莫希乾多次報告迎仕柏的賭場利潤低下、運營成本高昂。根據提交給韓國政府的一份審計報告，該度假村在運營第一年虧損約1.04億美元。
On Feb. 13, 2025, Mohegan delivered a quarterly earnings report. Although it had never missed a payment on its loans, the results fell short of earnings targets. On the same day, Bain called in its loan two years early. It said the shortfall had breached a financing covenant, according to two people familiar with the matter who spoke on the condition of anonymity to discuss confidential matters. About a year after Inspire’s grand opening, Bain seized control of the resort.
2025年2月13日，莫西乾發布季度財報。儘管貸款從未出現還款違約，但業績結果未能達到收益目標。就在當天，貝恩資本提前兩年催收貸款。據兩位因討論機密事宜而要求匿名的知情人士透露，貝恩資本表示這一業績差距違反了融資契約條款。迎仕柏盛大開幕約一年後，貝恩資本奪取了度假村的控制權。
Bain describes itself as a “long-term partner” for Inspire, and has stressed its commitment to the project. But the loan call was a shock to Mohegan, which said in its 2025 annual report that it had no intention of abandoning Inspire. Analysts noted that a project of that scale could not be expected to turn a profit so soon after opening.
貝恩資本自稱是迎仕柏的「長期合作夥伴」，並強調了對項目的持續承諾。但這次提前催貸令莫西乾深感震驚，公司在2025年年報中明確表示，無意放棄迎仕柏。分析人士指出，如此規模的項目在開業後不久就實現盈利是不現實的。
“I don’t think Inspire performed more poorly than other resorts across the globe,” said Keith Whyte, a gambling industry consultant.
博彩業顧問基思·懷特表示：「我認為迎仕柏的表現並不比全球其他度假村差。」
廣告
After the takeover, things slowly turned around. Under new management, Inspire cut its losses and reported that gambling revenues rose 150 percent while visitor numbers increased by 50 percent. The resort reported a pretax profit for the first time last year, for the six months ending in October 2025.
易主之後，形勢逐漸出現轉機。在新管理層帶領下，迎仕柏大幅減虧，報告顯示博彩收入增長150%，訪客數量增加50%。在截至2025年10月的六個月裡，度假村首次錄得稅前盈利。
Sujin Yang, an Inspire spokeswoman, said that the resort steadied its business and projected continued growth without making major changes to its strategy or budget cuts. She said what the resort needed was time.
迎仕柏等發言人楊秀真（音）表示，度假村業務已趨於穩定，並預計將持續增長，無需對戰略進行重大調整或削減預算。她表示，迎仕柏需要的是時間。
“For a project of this magnitude, it takes a few years to stabilize operations and start to see results,” she said.
「對於如此體量的項目，需要幾年時間來穩定運營並開始看到成效，」她說。
The company that envisioned the glitzy complex is no longer around to enjoy the results.
但最初構想並打造這座奢華綜合度假村的公司已經無法親自享受這些成果。
Publicly, Mohegan is projecting confidence about its business strategy. It told investors in December that, even after losing control of the South Korean resort, it was healthier than ever, pointing to the success of its North American operations.
在公開場合，莫西乾對其商業戰略依然自信。它在去年12月告訴投資者，即使失去了韓國度假村的控制權，其財務狀況比以往任何時候都更健康，並以北美業務的成功佐證這一判斷。
And there are signs that Mohegan is not completely out of the picture when it comes to the Inspire resort. Mohegan and Bain have maintained a professional relationship and have engaged in some informal conversations, according to the two people familiar with the matter.
而且，有跡象表明，莫西乾並沒有完全退出迎仕柏項目。兩名知情人士說，莫西乾與貝恩資本一直保持著專業層面的關係，雙方也進行過一些非正式溝通。
For now, Inspire’s prestige is growing. It recently served as the luxurious “paradise” destination for successful matches in the fifth season of “Single’s Inferno,” a hit South Korean dating show on Netflix. They dined in the resort’s opulent suites and swam in the private outdoor pools.
目前，迎仕柏的聲望與日俱增。它最近成為網飛韓國熱門戀愛真人秀《單身即地獄》第五季中成功配對情侶的奢華「天堂」目的地。他們在度假村豪華套房中用餐，在私人室外泳池中暢遊。
Sung Kim, 40, who was in the casino to play blackjack and baccarat, said she had flown in from Macau, where she could not gamble because the resorts there didn’t offer child care for her 5-year-old son.
40歲的金善（音）在賭場玩21點和百家樂。她說自己是從澳門飛來的，因為在那裡她沒法賭博——當地賭場不提供兒童看護服務，而她有一個五歲的兒子。
廣告
“It’s close to the airport. It’s convenient. A huge plus is all the kids stuff,” said Ms. Kim’s sister, Shannon Park, 32. Besides the water park, Inspire offers cooking classes for children, climbing walls and an indoor trampoline park.
「這裡離機場很近，非常方便。最大的加分項是有很多適合小孩子的設施，」金善的妹妹、32歲的香農·朴（音）說。除了水上樂園之外迎仕柏還為兒童提供烹飪課程、攀岩牆和室內蹦床公園。
Yu Jin, 36, who was visiting from northeastern China for the third time, said he was drawn by the “Aurora,” a dazzling 500-foot animated display that runs along the ceiling of a hallway at the resort. Videos of it were widely shared on Chinese social media.
36歲的於進（音）來自中國東北，這是他第三次來這裡。他說，吸引他的是「極光」——度假村一條走廊的天花板有一塊長達150米、絢麗奪目的動態影像螢幕。相關影片曾在中國社群媒體上廣泛流傳。
But Mr. Yu, who was holding a thick wad of bills near a baccarat table, said that he would soon be flying to Macau, where he said the rooms were better, the food was familiar and he could speak his own language.
然而，拿著一疊厚厚鈔票站在百家樂桌旁的於進說，他很快就要飛去澳門了，他說那裡的客房更好，食物更合口味，而且可以說中文。
“Everything is better in Macau,” he said.
「澳門哪哪都比這強，」他說。
