朱鎔基葬禮在北京舉行

At Ex-Premier’s Funeral, China’s Past Shadows Its Present

YAN ZHUANG • 2026-08-19
China lowered flags to half-staff across the country on Tuesday to mourn the former premier, Zhu Rongji, the last of a generation of politicians who guided China toward a market-based economy, fueling extraordinary growth but also leaving behind deep inequalities.
週二，中國各地降半旗悼念前總理朱鎔基。他是引領中國走向市場經濟的那一代政治家中的最後一位，他們推動了中國經濟的非凡增長，但也留下了深刻的不平等問題。
The country’s top leaders, including President Xi Jinping, paid their respects to Mr. Zhu, who died on Wednesday at 97, at his official funeral in Beijing, according to state media. Mr. Zhu’s body was cremated.
據官方媒體報導，包括國家主席習近平在內的最高領導層出席了在北京舉行的官方遺體告別儀式，弔唁上週三以97歲高齡逝世的朱鎔基。他的遺體已被火化。
Deaths of senior leaders are delicate occasions in Chinese politics because they can be used to express veiled criticisms of the current leadership. Mr. Zhu’s funeral has an additional element of sensitivity: Just a day earlier, Beijing celebrated the centenary of the birth of Jiang Zemin, the Communist Party leader whom Mr. Zhu worked under in the 1990s. Mr. Jiang died in 2022.
在中國政治中，高級領導人的逝世是敏感時刻，因為人們可能會藉機含蓄地表達對現任領導層的批評。而朱鎔基的送別儀式還有一個額外的敏感因素：就在一天前，北京剛剛慶祝了江澤民誕辰100週年。江澤民是上世紀90年代與朱鎔基共事過的共產黨領導人，於2022年逝世。
Mr. Jiang’s commemoration and Mr. Zhu’s funeral, together, have prompted nostalgia for their era, which saw some of China’s most consequential economic change, experts said. That can invite comparisons the Chinese Communist Party likely wants to avoid, they said, between a past that felt more optimistic and purposeful and a present sense of malaise and uncertainty.
專家表示，對江澤民的紀念和對朱鎔基的送別疊加引發了人們對他們那個時代的懷念。那個時代見證了中國一些最具深遠意義的經濟變革。專家指出，這可能會引發中國共產黨希望避免的對比——即那個充滿樂觀與幹勁的過去與當下普遍存在的低迷與不確定感的反差。
廣告
Under Mr. Xi’s leadership, the party has invested heavily in certain tech industries, supporting factories that churn out semiconductors and electric cars to feed a global boom. But many ordinary Chinese are feeling squeezed. A long-running property crisis has dragged down the economy, and job prospects for young people have shrunk. The party has also tightened its grip on private companies, adding to the uncertainty facing the entrepreneurs and investors who once drove China’s growth.
在習近平的領導下，黨對某些科技產業進行了巨額投資，支持工廠大量生產半導體和電動汽車以迎合全球熱潮。但許多普通中國百姓卻感到生活拮据。長期的房地產危機拖累了經濟發展，年輕人的就業前景急劇萎縮。黨還加強了對私營企業的控制，進一步增加了曾經推動中國經濟增長的企業家和投資者所面臨的不確定性。
Referring to Mr. Xi, Joseph Torigian, an associate professor at American University who studies China’s political elite, said: “He doesn’t want this moment to raise questions about what he’s doing.”
「他不希望這個時刻引發人們對他正在做的事情產生質疑，」研究中國政治精英的美利堅大學副教授唐志學(Joseph Torigian)在談及習近平時說。
“He really wants to convince people that what he’s doing is not a brief, stupid detour from this reformist narrative that Jiang and Zhu were doing so well,” Mr. Torigian said.
「他非常希望讓人們相信，他現在所做的事情並不是在走一段短暫而愚蠢的彎路，偏離了曾經讓江和朱大出風頭的改革敘事，」唐志學說。
That was reflected in a speech Mr. Xi gave on Monday commemorating Mr. Jiang, Mr. Torigian said, in which he sought to head off criticism about the direction the country has taken by stressing the continuity between his reign and that of Mr. Jiang’s. He highlighted similarities in their belief in Marxism and the strength of the Communist Party, and positioned himself as a modernizer who was making changes and adapting to the times as Mr. Jiang had done.
唐志學指出，這一點體現在習近平週一紀念江澤民的講話中。在講話中，習近平強調了自己的執政與江澤民執政之間的延續性，試圖消除人們對國家發展方向的批評。他強調了他們在馬克思主義信仰和共產黨力量方面的相似之處，並將自己塑造成像江澤民當年那樣順應時代發展而做出變革的現代化推動者。
In the 40-minute speech, Mr. Xi invoked Mr. Jiang’s legacy of measured market liberalization and praised Mr. Jiang’s decisiveness during crisis. In the way that Mr. Jiang responded to international emergencies including the 1997 Asian financial crisis, the party of today must actively respond to “major tests of high winds and turbulent waves with a tenacious fighting spirit,” he said. Such phrasing is often read as an implicit reference to China’s sometimes-bruising rivalry with the United States over trade, technology and global influence, which has intensified under Mr. Xi.
在這場長達40分鐘的講話中，習近平重申了江澤民適度市場自由化的遺志，並讚揚了江澤民在危機時刻的果斷抉擇。他表示，正如江澤民應對包括1997年亞洲金融危機在內的國際突發事件那樣，今天的黨必須「以頑強的鬥爭精神、高超的鬥爭本領積極應對風高浪急甚至驚濤駭浪的重大考驗」。這種表述通常被解讀為在隱喻中國與美國在貿易、技術和全球影響力方面日益激烈的競爭——這種競爭在習近平任內進一步加劇。
Many of the rituals to mark former Chinese leaders’ deaths are prescribed in rules developed over decades. Footage of Mr. Zhu’s funeral was not immediately released, but state media said that party officials paid tribute to Mr. Zhu at the Babaoshan Revolutionary Cemetery in Beijing’s west, each bowing three times to show respect. Hu Jintao, the former Chinese leader who has appeared unwell, did not attend and sent a wreath to express his condolences, Xinhua reported.
許多中國前領導人的追悼儀式是按照幾十年來制定的規矩進行的。朱鎔基遺體告別儀式的影片並未立即公開發布，但官方媒體表示，黨內官員在北京西部的八寶山革命公墓向朱鎔基致敬，每人三鞠躬以示敬意。據新華社報導，此前顯出病態的前國家領導人胡錦濤未出席儀式，但送了花圈表示哀悼。
廣告
Mr. Torigian said that Mr. Zhu’s funeral would likely adhere closely to the protocols for that of other premiers like Li Peng and Li Keqiang. Mr. Zhu’s official obituary in the People’s Daily was similarly boilerplate, using language that closely resembled that used for previous premiers, he added.
唐志學表示，朱鎔基的送別儀式可能會嚴格遵循李鵬和李克強等其他前總理的規格。他補充道，《人民日報》對朱鎔基的官方訃告同樣套用常規模板，使用的語言與以往針對前總理的訃告非常相似。
The death of Li Keqiang, in 2023, prompted an outpouring of grief online as people shared his speeches and photographs of him visiting disaster survivors. They recited Mr. Li’s most famous quotes, like “Power must not be arbitrary.” This time, after Mr. Zhu’s death was announced, there were signs that the authorities were carefully managing public discussion, with searches for his name on Chinese sites producing posts dominated by state media. Still, some used the moment to mourn a more open era in China, and others celebrated his energetic, enthusiastic style and gladiatorial news conferences.
2023年李克強的去世在網路上引發了廣泛的哀悼，人們紛紛分享他的講話和他在災區慰問倖存者的照片。他們重溫了李克強最著名的名言，比如「有權不可任性」。這一次，在朱鎔基逝世的消息公布後，有跡象表明當局正在謹慎地管理公眾討論，在中文網站上搜索他的名字，出現的帖子大多被官方媒體佔據。儘管如此，一些人仍藉此機會悼念中國的那個更開放的時代，還有一些人讚揚他充滿活力、幹勁十足的風格以及舌戰群儒的記者招待會。
The nostalgia for Mr. Jiang and Mr. Zhu’s era can be overly rosy, said Deng Yuwen, a political commentator who lives in the U.S., who described the economic changes of that era as “ruthless and cruel.”
居住在美國的政治評論員鄧聿文表示，對江澤民和朱鎔基時代的懷念可能過於美化了，他將那個時代的經濟變革描述為「冷酷無情」。
The changes that pushed the country deeper into the market economy and global trading system also left enduring social and economic problems. State-run factories were shut down or turned over to private ownership, leading to the layoffs of tens of millions of workers. The old guarantees of state-provided benefits were dismantled before China had a social safety net.
那些將國家進一步推向市場經濟和全球貿易體系的變革也留下了長期的社會和經濟問題。國營工廠倒閉或轉為私有導致數以千萬計的工人下崗。由國家提供福利的舊保障制度已經廢除，社會安全網卻還沒有成形。
Still, the changes at the time gave people a sense of hope and momentum, Mr. Deng said, in contrast to the current era.
鄧聿文說，儘管如此，與當前時代相比，當時的變化確實給人們帶來了一種希望和前進的動力。
“The problem now is that many people are unemployed and laid off, and many people also feel like there is no hope for the future,” he said.
「現在的問題是很多人失業和被裁員，很多人也覺得未來沒有希望，」他說。
廣告
“That’s the connection the authorities and Xi Jinping likely are concerned about people making,” Mr. Deng said, “because comparing that era to now will reflect the difference in people’s feelings.”
「這正是當局和習近平可能會擔心人們產生的聯想，」鄧聿文說，「因為將那個時代與現在相比較，會反映出人們感受上的落差。」
