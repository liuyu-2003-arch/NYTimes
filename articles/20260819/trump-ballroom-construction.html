川普趕工白宮宴會廳，力求「跑贏司法審查」

Trump’s Ballroom Is Rising at Breakneck Speed: 20 Hours a Day, 7 Days a Week

LUKE BROADWATER, ANN E. MARIMOW • 2026-08-19
With the fate of his ballroom tied up in court, President Trump is racing against the clock.
宴會廳的命運正繫於法庭，川普總統在與時間賽跑。
He has enlisted a team of 250 workers to push ahead with construction 20 hours a day, seven days a week to complete as much work as quickly as possible. This week, there are plans to install one million pounds of rebar and pour 3,000 cubic yards of concrete for a project that is now two-thirds complete, the administration said in a court filing.
他已招募一支由250名工人組成的團隊，以每週七天、每天20小時的強度推進施工，盡可能快地完成盡可能多的工作。政府在一份法庭文件中表示，本週計劃安裝450噸鋼筋，並澆築2300立方米混凝土，項目目前已完成三分之二。
Now, with the matter pending before the Supreme Court, the Trump administration is making a practical appeal to the justices: The ballroom is simply too far along to be stopped now.
如今，此案正待最高法院審理，川普政府向大法官們提出了一個現實訴求：宴會廳工程已推進至此，無法再叫停。
“His strategy clearly is to change the reality on the ground so that instead of preserving the status quo, those who rightly invoke the law against what he’s doing are put in a position of having to undo something that is already a fait accompli,” said Laurence H. Tribe, an emeritus university professor of constitutional law at Harvard.
「他的策略顯然是要改變現實狀況，這樣一來，不是維持現狀，而是讓那些依法反對其行為的人不得不去推翻一個既成事實，」哈佛大學憲法學榮休教授勞倫斯·H·特賴布表示。
廣告
For years, Mr. Trump has argued that the White House should have its own ballroom, saying it was a matter of prestige on the world stage. A ballroom, he said, would allow officials to hold big events for special guests without needing to erect a tent on the South Lawn.
多年來，川普一直主張白宮應擁有自己的宴會廳，稱這關乎在世界舞台上的威望。他說，有了宴會廳，官員們就能為重要賓客舉辦大型活動，而無需在南草坪上搭帳篷。
In recent months, as preservationists and other critics have tried to halt or at least slow down the project, Mr. Trump has offered a new justification: national security. Essentially, Mr. Trump is treating the ballroom and the military bunker underneath it as one and the same.
近幾個月來，隨著文保人士和其他批評者試圖叫停或至少放緩該項目，川普提出了一個新的理由：國家安全。簡單說就是他已將宴會廳與其下方的軍事掩體視為一體。
The bunker — officially known as the Presidential Emergency Operations Center, or PEOC — was built during World War II beneath what was once the East Wing, which Mr. Trump tore down last year to make way for his ballroom.
該掩體——正式名稱為總統緊急行動中心——建於二戰期間，位於曾經的東翼下方，而川普去年拆除了東翼，為宴會廳騰出空間。
Now, he speaks about the two projects in the same breath.
如今，他在談論這兩個項目時總是相提並論。
After an armed man rushed the White House Correspondents’ Association dinner in April, for example, Mr. Trump said on social media that the mayhem had proved the need for “the Militarily Top Secret Ballroom currently under construction at the White House.”
例如，在今年4月一名持槍男子衝撞白宮記者協會晚宴後，川普在社群媒體上表示，這場混亂證明「白宮內目前正在建設的軍事絕密宴會廳」確有必要。
Mr. Trump demolished the East Wing last October without seeking any approvals or submitting construction or demolition plans to Congress. While he did bring the project before two review panels whose leaders he had appointed, Mr. Trump has continued to change the plans from the designs they authorized.
川普去年10月拆除東翼時未尋求任何批准，也未向國會提交施工或拆除計劃。儘管他曾將該項目提交給兩個由他任命的負責人選的審查小組，但川普不斷更改已獲授權的設計方案。
廣告
Several federal court rulings have found that the president exceeded his authority by moving ahead without congressional approval, but the decisions have been paused pending appeals — which means the construction can go on at least until Friday, when an appeals court order to halt the aboveground construction goes into effect.
若干聯邦法院裁定，總統未經國會批准擅自推進工程超越了其權限，但這些裁決在上訴期間被暫停執行——這意味著施工至少可以持續到本週五，屆時上訴法院要求停止地上施工的命令將生效。
The case is also pending before the Supreme Court.
該案也在最高法院待審。
Last week, the Trump administration told the Supreme Court the president was adding flashy golden seals on the outside of the ballroom, a design element that was never submitted to the Commission of Fine Arts.
上週，川普政府告訴最高法院，總統正在宴會廳外部添加華麗的鍍金徽章，這一設計元素從未提交給美術委員會。
Joshua Fisher, the director of White House management and administration, told the court that a concrete-and-steel superstructure had already been built that stretches five stories deep and 70 feet high.
白宮管理與行政事務主任約書亞·費舍爾告訴法庭，一座混凝土和鋼結構的超級骨架已經建成，深達五層，高20餘米。
Mr. Fisher said 80 percent of the rebar for the project had been placed, and the concrete poured.
費舍爾表示，該項目80%的鋼筋已鋪設完畢，混凝土也已澆築。
He said $335 million of the $400 million needed for the project had been raised from private donors. He estimated the construction was 65 percent complete.
他說，該項目所需4億美元中已有3.35億美元來自私人捐贈者。他估計工程已完成65%。
Mr. Fisher said that the ballroom’s columns were being carved by renowned Italian artists, and that the building would be sturdy enough to withstand a nuclear blast.
費舍爾稱，宴會廳的立柱正在由著名義大利藝術家雕刻，且該建築將堅固到足以抵禦核爆。
廣告
“Given the current progress, the superstructure is beyond the point of return,” Mr. Fisher wrote. He added that even if the Supreme Court ordered the plans for the building altered or the building taken down, “there would be no way to do so.”
「鑒於目前的進度，該主體結構已無法回頭，」費舍爾寫道。他補充說，即使最高法院下令修改建築方案或拆除建築，「也將無法做到」。
The message was clear: The ballroom cannot be undone.
信息很明確：宴會廳已不可逆轉。
But in a filing on Tuesday, the preservationists challenging the project told the justices that the administration was trying to “outrun judicial review” by suggesting that it was too late to stop the construction.
但在週二的一份文件中，質疑該項目的文保人士告訴大法官，政府試圖通過暗示「現在叫停為時已晚」來「跑贏司法審查」。
“They tell this court that because the ballroom is being built with the type of concrete used ‘in nuclear power plants,’ it will soon be ‘virtually impossible to deconstruct,’” according to the filing.
「他們告訴本法院，因為宴會廳使用的是『核電站所用』類型的混凝土，很快將『幾乎無法拆除』，」文件中寫道。
Lawyers for the National Trust for Historic Preservation in the United States, a nonprofit chartered by Congress to guard America’s historic buildings, said the administration had intentionally accelerated construction “in an effort to put their illegal ballroom” beyond judicial intervention.
美國國家歷史保護信託基金（由國會特許設立、負責保護美國歷史建築的非營利組織）的律師表示，政府故意加快施工速度，「試圖將其非法宴會廳」置於司法干預範圍之外。
They have pointed to the Constitution and several federal laws that state Congress must approve such a project. One federal law says, “A building or structure shall not be erected on any reservation, park or public grounds of the federal government in the District of Columbia without express authority of Congress.”
他們援引憲法和若干聯邦法律，指出此類項目必須經國會批准。其中一項聯邦法律規定：「未經國會明確授權，不得在哥倫比亞特區聯邦政府的任何保留地、公園或公共用地上建造任何建築物或結構。」
廣告
Lower court judges have sided with the preservationists, ruling that Congress must first sign off on the expansion and renovation of the White House complex.
下級法院法官已支持文保人士的主張，裁定白宮擴建和翻新必須首先獲得國會批准。
“Efforts to foil judicial review and arrogate Congress’s exclusive powers should not be rewarded with a stay that allows petitioners to complete a ballroom they lacked any authority to commence in the first place,” said the trust’s lawyers, led by Thaddeus A. Heuer.
「對阻止司法審查、僭越國會專屬權力的企圖，不應報之以暫緩執行令的獎賞，進而令上訴人得以完成他們本無權啟動的宴會廳，」由撒迪厄斯·A·豪爾領導的信託基金律師團隊表示。
In its filing on Tuesday, the trust suggested that the Supreme Court temporarily halt the aboveground work and then quickly schedule oral arguments for October or November to formally decide whether the project can proceed. If the justices do not press pause now, the trust said, the project is on track to be substantially finished this fall and it will be too late.
信託基金在週二的文件中建議，最高法院應暫時叫停地上施工，然後迅速安排在10月或11月進行口頭辯論，以正式決定該項目能否繼續。信託基金表示，如果大法官現在不按下暫停鍵，那麼該項目將於今年秋季基本完工，屆時將為時過晚。
D. John Sauer, the solicitor general, has argued that the ballroom is inextricably linked to the broader national security needs of the White House complex. He said the structure is built with hardened concrete, steel and rebar; protective missile-resistant columns, roofs and beams; droneproof ceilings and roofs; and bullet-, ballistic- and blastproof glass. It includes bomb shelters, state-of-the-art hospital and medical facilities, protective partitioning between all areas of the building, top secret military structures and equipment, military-grade venting, a single integrated air conditioning and heating system, sniper nests and a drone port on top.
副檢察長D·約翰·索爾辯稱，該宴會廳與白宮建築群更廣泛的國家安全需求密不可分。他說，該結構採用硬化混凝土、鋼材和鋼筋建造；配有防導彈的立柱、屋頂和橫樑；防無人機天花板和屋頂；以及防子彈、防彈片和防爆玻璃。其內包括防空洞、最先進的醫院和醫療設施、建築物所有區域之間的防護隔斷、絕密軍事建築和設備、軍用級通風系統、一套集成暖通空調系統、狙擊手陣地和樓頂無人機停機坪。
Mr. Sauer has also pushed back on the assertion in the unfavorable appeals court ruling that Mr. Trump is a temporary tenant, not the owner, of the White House. “The president of the United States of America is not a tenant, but rather the sole, elected head of the executive branch,” he wrote, adding that other presidents had taken steps to renovate the White House.
索爾還對上訴法院做出的不利裁決中「川普是白宮的臨時租戶，而非所有者」的說法進行了反駁。「美利堅合眾國總統不是租戶，而是經選舉產生的行政部門唯一首腦，」他寫道，並補充說其他總統也曾採取措施翻修白宮。
The Supreme Court’s conservative majority has often been receptive to the Trump administration’s efforts to expand presidential power, but it has also ruled against some of Mr. Trump’s signature initiatives. The justices are expected to issue an order before the appeals court ruling that would halt construction takes effect on Friday.
最高法院的保守派多數通常樂於接受川普政府擴大總統權力的行動，但也曾對川普的一些標誌性舉措作出不利裁決。預計大法官們將在週五上訴法院停建令生效前發布一項命令。
Kimberly Wehle, a law professor at the University of Baltimore, said that Mr. Trump had been pushing to expand the powers of the presidency for years, and that attempting to construct a massive new building on White House grounds without lawmakers’ approval is his latest step.
巴爾的摩大學法學教授金伯利·韋勒表示，川普多年來一直推動擴大總統權力，而試圖在未經立法者批准的情況下在白宮場地上建造一座龐大的新建築，是他最新的舉措。
廣告
“It’s so symbolic that he literally bulldozes the White House and says, ‘What are you going to do about it?’” she said. “They’re making this general generic argument that ‘I’m the president, so it’s national security, I can do whatever I want.’ That is a very steep, slippery slope into unlimited power in the presidency.”
「他直接推平白宮，然後說『你能拿我怎樣』，這太有象徵意義了，」她說。「他們提出一個籠統的通用論點，『我是總統，所以關乎國家安全，我想做什麼就做什麼。』這是一條滑向無限總統權力的非常陡峭的斜坡。」
