攻擊朋友、討好敵人：川普「黑白顛倒」的世界觀

Threatening Friends and Courting Enemies in Trump’s ‘Upside-Down World’

PETER BAKER • 2026-08-19
Once again, President Trump is threatening his country’s own partners with everything from tariffs to bombs. Once again, he is fawning over a nuclear-armed tyrant deemed to be one of the biggest dangers to U.S. security. And once again, the world is left trying to make sense of a United States that has upended the world order that it established eight decades ago.
再一次，川普總統用從關稅到炸彈等各種手段威脅自己國家的夥伴。再一次，他對一名被視為美國安全最大威脅之一的擁核暴君百般討好。而再一次，全世界都在試圖理解一個將其自己在八十年前建立的世界秩序徹底顛覆的美國。
Nearly 10 years after Mr. Trump first came to the White House on the slogan of America First, his attack-thy-friends, befriend-thy-enemies approach to international relations has become the feature, not the bug, of U.S. foreign policy. Traditional alliances from Europe to Asia to the Middle East have frayed while the president courts those once seen as its adversaries.
距離川普第一次打著「美國優先」的旗號入主白宮已將近10年時間。他那套「攻擊朋友、討好敵人」的國際關係理念已經成為美國外交政策的核心特徵，而非偶然偏差。從歐洲到亞洲再到中東，美國與傳統盟友之間的關係日漸疏遠，而總統卻頻頻向那些曾被視為對手的國家示好。
Even so, the past few days have thrown the Trumpian worldview into stark relief. Mr. Trump declared that he would bomb Oman, a key friend in the complicated diplomacy of the Middle East, if he decides it is getting in the way of his war with Iran. He was set to impose punitive new tariffs on Canada, once considered the United States’ closest friend. And he announced that he would pull back military support from South Korea, the bulwark of the U.S. network of partners in the Asia-Pacific.
即便如此，過去幾天發生的一連串事件還是讓川普式的世界觀顯得格外觸目驚心。川普宣稱，如果阿曼妨礙他對伊朗的戰爭，他就會轟炸這個在中東錯綜複雜的外交關係中舉足輕重的友好國家。他準備對加拿大加徵懲罰性新關稅，而加拿大曾被視為美國最親密的朋友。他還宣布將減少對韓國的軍事支持，而韓國一直是美國在亞太地區夥伴網路的重要支柱。
At the same time, he again began sending public mash notes to North Korea’s absolute dictator, Kim Jong-un, with whom by his own description he “fell in love” in his first term. Mr. Kim, who rules one of the most oppressive nations in the world, never did give up his nuclear weapons, as Mr. Trump once declared he would. Instead, Mr. Kim has vastly expanded his nuclear arsenal in defiance of Mr. Trump, who nonetheless remains a suitor, no matter how unrequited.
與此同時，他又開始向朝鮮的專制獨裁者金正恩公開發送情意綿綿的訊息——用他自己的話說，他在第一個任期內與金正恩「墜入了愛河」。金正恩統治著世界上極權程度最高的國家之一，他從未像川普曾宣稱的那樣放棄核武器。相反，金正恩不顧川普的反對，大幅擴張了自己的核武庫——而川普卻始終像個單相思的追求者。
廣告
“He dumps on our friends and cozies up to our enemies,” said James J. Blanchard, a former ambassador to Canada and Democratic governor of Michigan. “If someone once told me that Russia is our friend and Canada is our enemy, I’d have told you we’re in an upside-down world.”
「他對我們的朋友惡言相向，卻對我們的敵人百般討好，」曾任美國駐加拿大大使、密西根州原民主黨州長詹姆斯·布蘭乍得說。「要是以前有人跟我說俄羅斯是朋友，加拿大是敵人，我一定會說這世界簡直黑白顛倒了。」
Richard Fontaine, the chief executive of the Center for a New American Security and a former adviser to Senator John McCain, Republican of Arizona, said that none of the bombastic threats were new. But he pointed out that Mr. Trump had not always followed through on them. The United States, he noted, had not pulled out of NATO, abandoned South Korea, bombed Oman or seized Canada and Greenland.
新美國安全中心首席執行官、曾擔任亞利桑那州共和黨參議員約翰·馬侃顧問的理查德·方丹表示，這些虛張聲勢的威脅都不是新鮮事。但他指出，川普並不總是會把這些威脅付諸行動。他指出，美國並沒有退出北約，沒有拋棄韓國、轟炸阿曼，也沒有吞併加拿大和格陵蘭。
“Increasingly, the bullying rhetoric is being priced in by countries around the world,” Mr. Fontaine said. “They don’t dismiss it, but they don’t take the threats literally either. As more and more unfulfilled threats are issued, people listen less. It undermines the president’s credibility.”
「世界各國正在逐漸把這些霸凌言論納入他們的考量之中，」方丹說。「他們不會置之不理，但也不會把那些威脅當真。隨著越來越多的威脅最終沒有兌現，人們也越來越不把那些威脅當回事。這正在削弱總統的公信力。」
Mr. Trump has long argued that his approach to other countries, however unorthodox, makes sense for the United States. Under previous presidents, allies took advantage of America’s security presence and trade rules without reciprocating, Mr. Trump maintains. He sees relatively high trade deficits and relatively low NATO military spending as proof that so-called friends are exploiting the United States.
川普長期以來一直認為，他對待其他國家的方式雖然不同尋常，但符合美國利益。在他看來，過去的總統任期裡，盟友利用美國提供的安全保護和貿易規則，卻沒有給予相應回報。川普認為，美國相對較高的貿易逆差以及北約成員國相對較低的軍費支出都證明了所謂的朋友正在利用美國。
As for sweet talking North Korea, China, Russia and other longtime adversaries, Mr. Trump contends that the confrontational policies of the past failed to achieve what they set out to, so better to try more diplomacy. He still hopes to reach a nuclear accord with North Korea, seal a comprehensive trade deal with China and reestablish full ties with Russia by brokering an end to Moscow’s war in Ukraine. He has pushed aides for a meeting this fall with Mr. Kim, The Wall Street Journal reported.
至於向朝鮮、中國、俄羅斯和其他長期對手示好，川普認為過去對抗性的政策未能達到目標，所以不如嘗試更多的外交手段。他仍希望能與朝鮮達成核協議、與中國達成全面貿易協定，並通過斡旋結束莫斯科在烏克蘭的戰爭，重新與俄羅斯建立全面關係。《華爾街日報》報導稱，他已推動助手安排今年秋天與金正恩的會面。
The war with Iran has provided a new scorecard for the allies that deserve support and those that do not. Countries that have declined to facilitate military operations against Tehran or use force to help guarantee passage through the Strait of Hormuz have in his view betrayed the United States, even though he never asked for their opinions before starting the war. After targeting Britain, France, Spain, Italy and others for not doing more to help him against Iran, Mr. Trump has now focused his ire on South Korea.
伊朗戰爭則為哪些盟友值得支持、哪些不值得提供了一份新的評分表。在他看來，那些拒絕為針對德黑蘭的軍事行動提供便利、或拒絕使用武力幫助確保霍爾木茲海峽通航的國家已經背叛了美國——儘管他在開戰之前從未徵求過他們的意見。在指責英國、法國、西班牙、義大利等國未能在對伊問題上給予更多幫助之後，川普現在將怒火集中到了韓國身上。
廣告
“We have 39,000 soldiers over there guarding you from Kim Jong-un, your next-door neighbor, and you’re not going to help us on a very easy military operation in Iran?” he said this week after announcing that he would scale back joint exercises with South Korea.
「我們有3.9萬名士兵在那裡保護你們，讓你們免受金正恩這個鄰居的威脅，而你們卻不願意在伊朗這樣一場非常簡單的軍事行動中幫助我們？」本週，他在宣布將縮減與韓國的聯合軍演後說道。
By contrast, he went on, “Kim Jong-un has always treated me with great respect, and we’ve met on numerous occasions, actually, two primary occasions — spoken and spent time. I understand him. He understands me.”
相比之下，他接著說：「金正恩一直對我非常尊重，我們見過很多次面——確切地說，是有過兩次主要的會晤和交流。我了解他，他也了解我。」
If so, that distinguishes Mr. Kim from European and Asian allies who still do not understand Mr. Trump, even if they have come to expect his volatile outbursts. Until now, presidents of both parties have valued their relationships with overseas friends. And while there have been plenty of moments of tension over the years, they have worked to keep them from escalating too far.
如果真是這樣，這倒使得金正恩不同於美國的歐亞盟友了——儘管後者對川普的反覆無常早已習以為常，卻依然無法理解他。在此之前，兩黨總統都重視與海外盟友的關係。儘管多年來有過不少緊張時刻，但他們還是一直努力避免局勢升級到無法收拾的程度。
Now the allies are the ones trying to avoid exacerbating the situation. “They grit their teeth and bear it because in the end they do not want to see irreparable damage done to the alliance over the next two years,” said Victor D. Cha, the president of the Geopolitics and Foreign Policy Department at the Center for Strategic and International Studies. “In short, allies may like and trust the U.S. less today, but they still need the U.S.”
如今，反而是盟友們在努力避免讓局勢進一步惡化。「他們咬緊牙關忍受這一切，因為歸根結底，他們不希望看到聯盟關係在未來兩年遭受無法彌補的損害，」戰略與國際問題研究中心地緣政治與外交政策部門主任車維德(Victor D. Cha)說。「簡而言之，盟友如今可能比過去更不喜歡、也更不信任美國，但他們仍然需要美國。」
Mr. Cha, who was an Asia adviser to President George W. Bush, said that President Lee Jae Myung of South Korea actually has a good relationship with Mr. Trump and does not want to harm that. “Lee is also as transactional as Trump, so if Trump’s outreach to Kim Jong-un works, that is very much in South Korea’s interests,” Mr. Cha said.
曾擔任小布希總統亞洲事務顧問的車維德表示，韓國總統李在明實際上與川普關係良好，也不希望破壞這種關係。「李在明也和川普一樣具有交易型思維，所以如果川普對金正恩的示好奏效，那也符合韓國的利益，」車維德說。
The presence of U.S. forces in South Korea since the 1950s war has long irritated Mr. Trump, who sees it in zero-sum terms. In his first term, he sought to pull troops out and cancel military exercises, judging them wasteful and unnecessarily provocative toward his friend in North Korea. He sought to charge South Korea billions of dollars for U.S. protection, and linked security issues to his complaints about Seoul’s trade policies.
自20世紀50年代朝鮮戰爭以來，美國軍隊一直駐紮在韓國，這一點長期令川普感到不滿。在他看來，這是一種零和關係。第一任期內，他曾試圖撤走駐韓美軍並取消雙方的聯合軍演，認為這既浪費資源，又會無謂地刺激他在朝鮮的朋友。他還曾要求韓國支付數十億美元作為美國保護的費用，並把安全問題與他對首爾貿易政策的不滿聯繫起來。
廣告
Mr. Trump is not the first president interested in bringing troops home from South Korea so long after the war. President Jimmy Carter promised to scale down U.S. forces there, but gave up amid resistance from Congress and his own administration. Donald H. Rumsfeld, who was Mr. Bush’s defense secretary, surprised the South Koreans by redeploying a brigade to Iraq, and never returned it. Today, about 28,500 U.S. troops are stationed in South Korea, according to the Congressional Research Service.
川普並不是第一位在戰爭結束這麼多年後考慮從韓國撤軍的美國總統。吉米·卡特總統曾承諾縮減駐韓美軍規模，但在國會和自己政府內部的反對下最終放棄。小布希總統時期的國防部長唐納德·拉姆斯菲爾德曾將一個駐韓旅抽調到伊拉克，而且再也沒有將其調回韓國，令韓國人感到震驚。根據美國國會研究處的數據，目前約有2.85萬名美軍駐紮在韓國。
“There could be a strategic case for restructuring the U.S. military presence on the peninsula,” said Michael J. Green, the chief executive of the United States Studies Centre at the University of Sydney in Australia, “but none for making them a pawn in a flirtation with the world’s cruelest dictator — particularly at a time when North Koreans are coming back from the Ukraine war battle hardened and backed by a larger missile inventory than they had in 2018.”
「調整美軍在朝鮮半島的軍事存在可能有戰略上的理由，」澳洲雪梨大學美國研究中心首席執行官麥可·格林說，「但沒有任何理由讓它們成為與世界上最殘忍的獨裁者調情的玩物——尤其是在朝鮮軍人從烏克蘭戰場歸來、作戰經驗更加豐富，且其導彈庫存比2018年更加龐大的時候。」
Oman, for its part, has for years been an important interlocutor between the United States and Iran, helping to facilitate negotiations when the two sides were open to talks. But Mr. Trump has grown increasingly aggravated that Oman has sought to broker a deal with Iran to control the Strait of Hormuz.
至於阿曼，該國多年來一直是美國與伊朗之間的重要溝通者，在雙方願意進行談判時幫助促成談判。但川普對阿曼試圖斡旋伊朗達成一項控制霍爾木茲海峽的協議越來越感到惱火。
He first threatened to mount an unprovoked war on the sultanate in May, saying that “Oman will behave just like everybody else or we’ll have to blow them up.” On Monday, he lashed out again, declaring that if Oman “gets in the way” of his efforts to reach an agreement with Iran, “we’ll bomb the shit out of them.”
5月，他首次威脅要對這個蘇丹國發動無端戰爭，稱「阿曼要像其他所有人一樣守規矩，否則我們就要把他們炸掉」。週一，他再次發火，宣稱如果阿曼「妨礙」他與伊朗達成協議的努力，「我們就把他們炸個稀巴爛」。
Canada faces a different form of hostility from Mr. Trump, who vowed to slap new tariffs of 50 percent on more than 500 Canadian goods, including hockey sticks and cheese, as of Tuesday at midnight. As of Tuesday afternoon, Canada was still trying to stave off the tariffs by offering trade concessions meant to appeal to Mr. Trump.
加拿大遭遇的是川普的另一種敵意。川普曾威脅從週二午夜開始，對500多種加拿大商品徵收50%的新關稅，其中包括冰球杆和奶酪。截至週二下午，加拿大仍在努力通過提出能夠迎合川普的貿易讓步來阻止這些關稅落地。
But even if the two sides reach an accord, there remain many points of friction between the neighbors, exacerbated by Mr. Trump’s oft-expressed ambition to take over Canada and make it “the 51st state.” Just last month, the opening of the new Gordie Howe International Bridge linking Detroit with Windsor, Ontario, was delayed for weeks over various grievances Mr. Trump has with Canada.
但即使雙方最終達成協議，兩國之間仍然存在許多摩擦點，而川普多次表達吞併加拿大、使其成為「第51州」的野心更使矛盾加劇。就在上個月，連接底特律和安大略省溫莎市的新戈迪·豪跨國大橋啟用被推遲了數週，原因就是川普對加拿大的種種不滿。
廣告
Mr. Blanchard said he suspected that the latest eruptions against Canada and other U.S. allies stemmed from Mr. Trump’s inability to bring the Iran war to a close. Neither bombing nor negotiations have forced Iran to surrender control of the Strait of Hormuz or to make verifiable commitments to abandon its nuclear program. The president appears stuck in a geopolitical trap without a clear path forward.
布蘭乍得說，他懷疑川普最近再次對加拿大以及其他美國盟友發難源於他無法結束伊朗戰爭。無論是轟炸還是談判都沒能迫使伊朗放棄對霍爾木茲海峽的控制，也沒能迫使伊朗就放棄核計劃作出可驗證的承諾。總統似乎陷入了一場地緣政治困局，卻找不到明確的出路。
Mr. Fontaine said that Mr. Trump’s approach to allies and adversaries undermined U.S. interests. “There should be benefits to being a friend of America, and costs for being an enemy,” he said, “not the other way around.”
方丹說，川普對待盟友和對手的方式正在損害美國利益。「成為美國的朋友應該得到好處，成為美國的敵人應該付出代價，」他說，「而不應該是反過來。」

Peter Baker是《紐約時報》首席白宮記者。川普是他報導的第六任美國總統，有時他也撰寫將總統和美國政府置於更宏觀的背景和歷史框架下的分析性文章。
