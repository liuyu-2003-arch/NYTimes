宇樹科技上市首日股價飆升500%

Chinese Robot Maker Unitree Soars 500 Percent in Trading Debut

STEVE LOHR • 2026-08-19
Shares of Unitree Robotics, a Chinese humanoid robot maker, soared in its Shanghai trading debut, as investors piled into a young homegrown company developing technology at the frontier of artificial intelligence.
中國人形機器人製造商宇樹科技的股票在上海上市首日大幅飆升，投資者紛紛湧入這家致力於人工智能前沿技術研發的年輕本土企業。
Unitree’s stock price rose almost 500 percent, or sixfold, in early trading on Wednesday, valuing it at about $53 billion. Earlier this month, Unitree priced its initial public offering at the equivalent of about $22 a share, seeking to raise around $900 million and valuing the company at roughly $9 billion. At one point, the shares rose as much as 629 percent.
宇樹科技股價在週三早盤交易中上漲近500%，即發行價的六倍，市值達到約3600億元（530億美元）。本月早些時候，宇樹科技將首次公開募股發行價定為每股150.80元（約22美元），擬籌資61億元（約9億美元），彼時公司估值約為603億元（約90億美元）。盤中股價一度漲幅高達629%。
“Unitree is a company with strong fundamentals and very impressive technology, but we’re seeing a lot of speculative optimism in this first day of trading,” said Lian Jye Su, a Singapore-based analyst for Omdia, a technology research firm. “This is being fueled in part by Chinese retail investors with a willingness to invest at almost any price.”
「宇樹科技是一家基本面紮實、技術非常出眾的公司，但我們在首個交易日看到了大量投機性樂觀情緒，」科技研究公司Omdia駐新加坡分析師蘇廉節表示，「部分原因是中國的散戶投資者願意以幾乎任何價格買入。」
The frenzy over Unitree shares comes less than a month after investors sent the shares of another beneficiary of China’s A.I. boom soaring: ChangXin Memory Technologies, also known as CXMT, the country’s leading maker of memory chips. The global race to build ever more powerful A.I. systems has unleashed enormous demand for the chips, which rapidly shuttle data to and from the processing engines of computers.
在宇樹科技股票掀起狂熱不到一個月前，投資者剛剛將中國人工智慧熱潮的另一受益者長鑫存儲技術(CXMT)的股價推向高位。長鑫是中國領先的內存晶片製造商。全球為建造更強大人工智慧系統展開的競賽釋放了對晶片的巨大需求，這些晶片負責在計算機處理引擎之間快速往返傳輸數據。

CXMT’s shares surged 470 percent on the first day of trading and have climbed further since. The company is now worth around $545 billion, surpassing Tencent as China’s most valuable publicly traded company.
長鑫股價在上市首日飆升470%，此後進一步上漲。該公司目前市值約5450億美元，超過騰訊成為中國市值最高的上市公司。
Investors buying Unitree on its first day brushed aside considerable uncertainty about its prospects. It remains unclear how quickly a mass market will emerge for machines that look and move like humans. In factories, for example, most humanoid robots are still being tested in pilot projects rather than deployed at scale.
在首日買入宇樹科技的投資者對其前景的諸多不確定性不以為然。目前尚不清楚外形和動作像人的機器究竟何時會形成大規模市場。例如，在工廠中，大多數人形機器人仍在試點項目中測試，尚未大規模部署。
Unitree, based in the eastern Chinese city of Hangzhou, is renowned for demonstrations showcasing the strength and agility of its machines. Videos of its robots performing kung fu routines, scaling walls and doing back flips have drawn millions of views online.
宇樹科技總部位於中國東部城市杭州，以其機器人展示的力量和靈活性而聞名。其機器人表演功夫套路、攀爬牆壁和後空翻的影片在網上吸引了數百萬次觀看。
For all the attention, Unitree’s business remains relatively small. It generated about $250 million in revenue in 2025, though sales more than quadrupled, and the company was profitable. It also faces intense competition from rivals in China and humanoid robot start-ups in the United States.
儘管備受關注，宇樹科技的業務規模仍然相對較小。2025年其營收約為2.5億美元，儘管銷售額增長了四倍多，且實現了盈利。此外它還面臨著來自中國國內競爭對手以及美國人形機器人初創企業的激烈競爭。
Still, many analysts expect humanoid robots to develop into a major industry. CLSA, an investment research firm, forecasts that the global market will reach $69 billion by 2030, up from roughly $2 billion this year.
不過，許多分析師預計人形機器人將發展成為一個重要產業。投資研究機構里昂證券預測，全球市場將從今年的約20億美元增長到2030年的690億美元。
Governments, too, increasingly view humanoid robots as a strategically important A.I. technology. Last month, the Federal Communications Commission announced plans to ban imports of new foreign-made humanoid and quadruped robots, citing national security concerns.
各國政府也越來越將人形機器人視為具有戰略重要性的人工智慧技術。上個月，美國聯邦通信委員會宣布計劃禁止進口新的外國製造的人形和四足機器人，理由是國家安全擔憂。
Unitree flagged that risk in its I.P.O. filing, warning that its new models could be barred from the United States. The company still makes most of its sales in China, but American buyers accounted for 13 percent of sales last year.
宇樹科技在其IPO文件中指出了這一風險，警告稱其新型號可能被禁止進入美國。該公司目前仍主要在中國銷售，但去年美國買家占其銷售額的13%。
