中美磋商習近平訪美事宜

Rubio and Chinese Diplomat Make Plans for Trump and Xi’s Washington Summit

黃安偉 • 2026-07-23
Secretary of State Marco Rubio said he spoke with China’s top diplomat on Wednesday about a state visit scheduled for September by Xi Jinping, China’s leader, to Washington, where President Trump intends to try to bolster what he sees as a personal friendship the two men are developing.
國務卿魯比奧表示，他週三與中國最高外交官就中國領導人習近平定於9月對華盛頓進行的國事訪問進行了磋商。川普總統打算藉此機會努力鞏固他眼中兩人正在建立的個人友誼。
The fact that the two nations’ top diplomats spent much of a meeting in Manila talking about the visit is the strongest sign yet that the summit is on schedule, despite remarks last week from Mr. Trump blaming China for trying to interfere in American elections.
在馬尼拉的會晤中，兩國最高外交官將大部分時間用於討論此次訪問。這是迄今為止最為強烈的信號，表明峰會正在按計劃進行，儘管川普上週發表言論，指責中國試圖干預美國選舉。
“It’s just to create the groundwork for another positive visit,” Mr. Rubio said of his talk with Wang Yi after it ended in the evening after more than an hour. “I think President Xi will have a very positive visit when he comes to Washington.”
「這只是為了給又一次積極的訪問奠定基礎，」魯比奧在晚上結束了與王毅一個多小時的會談後這樣評價。「我認為習近平主席來到華盛頓時，將進行一次非常積極的訪問。」
He said that the two governments were putting in place a board of trade and a separate board of investment, and that more details would be announced during the September summit. The two leaders agreed to establish the groups when Mr. Trump visited Beijing in May, more than half a year after he had to retreat from a trade war he had started with China.
他表示，兩國政府正在設立一個貿易委員會和一個獨立的投資委員會，更多細節將在9月的峰會上公布。今年5月川普訪問北京時，兩國領導人同意成立這兩個機構，此時距離川普被迫從自己挑起的對華貿易戰中退卻，已過去了半年多。
Mr. Rubio told reporters traveling with him that he and Mr. Wang did not talk about Mr. Trump’s recent accusations that China had tried to interfere in U.S. elections, mainly in 2020. The intelligence material that Mr. Trump brought up in a speech last Thursday was not new and had been reviewed by U.S. officials years ago. U.S. intelligence agencies have concluded that Russia is the main rival that has tried to interfere in American elections.
魯比奧告訴隨行記者，他與王毅沒有談論川普最近關於中國試圖干預美國選舉（主要是在2020年）的指控。川普在上週四的演講中提到的情報材料並不新鮮，幾年前就已被美國官員審閱。美國情報機構得出的結論是，俄羅斯才是試圖干預美國選舉的主要對手。
The Chinese foreign ministry has given pro forma statements pushing back on Mr. Trump’s accusations. But it has also indicated that Chinese officials understand that the American president made his speech for the purposes of domestic politics rather than out of actual foreign policy concerns.
中國外交部發表了例行聲明，反駁川普的指控。但中方也暗示，中國官員知道美國總統發表該演講是出於國內政治目的，而不是出於實際的外交政策顧慮。
“Obviously, there are areas of great differences,” Mr. Rubio said of U.S.-China relations. “Both sides will acknowledge that we’ll have to work through those, and I think these differences will exist for the foreseeable future.”
「顯然，雙方在一些領域存在巨大分歧，」魯比奧在談到美中關係時說。「雙方都會承認，我們必須解決這些問題，而且我認為這些分歧在可預見的未來仍將存在。」
廣告
Mr. Rubio was in Manila for an annual conference of top diplomats from Southeast Asian nations. Mr. Wang and Sergey Lavrov, Russia’s top diplomat, are also in Manila this week.
魯比奧當時在馬尼拉參加東南亞國家最高外交官年度會議。王毅和俄羅斯最高外交官拉夫羅夫本週也在馬尼拉。
Mr. Rubio accompanied Mr. Trump on his visit to Beijing in May, where Mr. Xi showered the American president with flattery — even saying he supported the “Make America Great Again” movement — and hosted a state banquet. A video showed Mr. Rubio marveling at the ceiling of a meeting room in the Great Hall of the People, next to Tiananmen Square. During his 14 years as a senator representing Florida, Mr. Rubio became one of the leading China hawks in Congress, but he has since tempered his tone to stay in alignment with Mr. Trump.
盧比奧曾在5月陪同川普訪問北京，期間習近平對美國總統大加讚譽——甚至表示支持「讓美國再次偉大」運動——並舉辦了國宴。一段影片顯示，魯比奧在天安門廣場旁的人民大會堂的一個會議室裡，對天花板讚嘆不已。在擔任代表佛羅里達州的參議員的14年間，魯比奧曾是國會中主要的對華鷹派人物之一，但此後他緩和了語氣，與川普保持一致。
In the Manila meeting with Mr. Rubio, Mr. Wang protested “recent negative remarks and actions by the U.S. side,” but also hailed the leadership summit in Beijing, according to an official summary of the talks posted by Xinhua, the Chinese state news agency. “Our responsibility now is to follow the course set by the two heads of state,” Mr. Wang said.
根據中國國家通訊社新華社發布的會談官方摘要，在馬尼拉與魯比奧的會晤中，王毅對「近期美方的一系列消極言行」表示抗議，但也高度評價了北京的領導人峰會。「現在我們的責任是，沿著兩國元首設定的軌道，」王毅說。
Mr. Rubio was asked by a reporter whether China and Russia were giving information to Iran to help the Iranian military fire projectiles at U.S. troops, as some U.S. officials have said was happening. Mr. Rubio did not give a direct answer.
有記者問魯比奧，中俄兩國是否如一些美國官員所稱的那樣，正在向伊朗提供信息，幫助伊朗軍隊向美軍發射彈藥。魯比奧沒有給出直接回答。
“Nothing that’s happened, nothing that China has done, has in any way changed the trajectory of what you’re seeing in terms of the conflicts we’re having with Iran,” he said.
「沒有發生任何事情、也沒有中國採取的任何行動在任何方面改變了你們所看到的我們與伊朗衝突的進程，」他說。
Mr. Rubio said China is unhappy with Iran’s attacks on commercial tankers in the Strait of Hormuz, a critical waterway for the transport of oil and gas to China and many other countries.
魯比奧表示，中國對伊朗在霍爾木茲海峽襲擊商業油輪感到不滿，該海峽是向中國和其他許多國家運輸石油和天然氣的關鍵水道。
廣告
Mr. Rubio did criticize recent aggressive encroachments by Chinese coast guard ships into waters near Taiwan. In recent years, China has increased its naval and aerial military operations around Taiwan, a de facto independent island that Mr. Xi has said must eventually be brought under the control of the Chinese government.
魯比奧確實批評了中國海警船近期對台灣附近水域咄咄逼人的入侵行為。近年來，中國增加了在台灣周邊的海空軍事行動，台灣是一個事實上的獨立島嶼，習近平曾表示該島最終必須置於中國政府的控制之下。
Mr. Rubio said the activity of Chinese ships “comes up in every one of our meetings because we obviously disagree with those actions, and we actually think that they run contrary to the spirit of what we’re trying to work out with them with regards to strategic stability.”
魯比奧說，中國船隻的活動「在我們每一次會議中都會被提及，因為我們顯然不贊同這些行動，而且我們實際上認為，這些行動違背了我們在戰略穩定方面試圖與中國達成的共識精神」。
Mr. Rubio said the United States would honor treaty commitments made to allies such as the Philippines, whose navy regularly has hostile encounters with Chinese vessels.
魯比奧表示，美國將履行對菲律賓等盟友的條約承諾，菲律賓海軍經常與中國船隻發生敵對交鋒。
“It speaks to areas of disagreement when it comes to our policies with China,” he said. “Those need to be managed very carefully because, obviously, a conflict, be it economic or otherwise, between the U.S. and China would have a dramatic global impact.”
「這反映了我們在對華政策上存在的不一致，」他說。「這些不一致需要非常謹慎地處理，因為很明顯，美中之間的衝突，無論是經濟衝突還是其他方面的衝突，都會產生巨大的全球影響。」
Mr. Rubio also said the United States would like to eventually have detailed discussions about nuclear issues with China, given the country’s rapidly growing arsenal.
魯比奧還表示，鑒於中國不斷迅速擴大的核武庫，美國希望最終能與中國就核問題進行詳細討論。

黃安偉(Edward Wong)為《紐約時報》報導全球事務、美國外交政策和國務院新聞。
