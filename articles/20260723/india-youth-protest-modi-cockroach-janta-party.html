關於印度青年抗議活動，你應該知道的

What Now for India’s Young Protesters?

ANUPREETA DAS • 2026-07-23
Young people in India — 40 percent of the world’s biggest country — have been taught that they are inheriting a country that is shining brightly, thanks to Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s leadership. Their disillusionment, with a lack of jobs and a troubled education system, now poses one of the biggest challenges to his popularity in 12 years in power.
印度的年輕人——占這個世界人口第一大國總人口的40%——一直被灌輸這樣的觀念：得益於總理莫迪的領導，他們繼承了一個光芒四射的國家。如今，由於缺乏就業機會和教育體系問題重重，他們如今的幻滅感對莫迪執政12年來的支持率構成了最大挑戰之一。
These long-simmering frustrations have spilled into the open in recent days, with thousands of people from around the country protesting on the streets of New Delhi, the capital.
這些長期積壓的不滿情緒近日終於爆發，來自全國各地，數以千計的人在首都新德里街頭抗議。
The demonstrations were triggered by the canceling of medical school entrance exams after a question paper leaked, but they are about something much deeper: holding the government to account.
遊行示威的導火線是醫學院入學考試因試卷洩漏而被取消，但背後是更為深刻的原因：要求政府承擔責任。
Ignoring their demands brings political risk for Mr. Modi, because India’s largest single demographic is under 25 years of age — a voting bloc too big to ignore.
忽視他們的訴求會給莫迪帶來政治風險，因為印度最大的單一人口群體是25歲以下人群——這是一個大到不容忽視的票倉。
But the protests aren’t like the Gen Z uprisings that shook India’s neighbors, Bangladesh and Nepal, where joblessness was also a common underlying cause. The two smaller countries underwent uprisings that quickly spiraled into bloodshed and violence, but toppled governments and installed new leaders; Indian protesters have been deliberate and clear about taking a peaceful approach. Also, in a country as large and diverse as India, with multiple states, political parties and agendas, overarching issues rarely unify enough people to bring that kind of threat.
但這些抗議活動不同於震撼了印度鄰國孟加拉國和尼泊爾的Z世代起義，在那些國家，失業也是共同的根本原因。這兩個較小的國家經歷了迅速演變成流血和暴力的起義，但最終推翻了政府並任命了新領導人；印度的抗議者則明確且堅定地採取了和平方式。此外，印度是一個幅員遼闊的多元化國家，擁有多個邦、政黨和政治議程，全局性的議題很少能團結足夠多的人，從而構成孟加拉國和尼泊爾那種程度的威脅。
Thousands of protesters remained camped out in Delhi on Wednesday with a list of demands for the government, which has made no concessions so far. Here’s what to know.
週三，數以千計的抗議者繼續駐紮在德里，並向政府提出了一系列訴求，但政府迄今尚未做出任何讓步。以下是需要了解的情況。
What is causing the frustration?
是什麼導致了這種不滿？
Students navigate a pressure-cooker education system that many say often deters rather than supports them. They spend years studying for competitive exams, seeking upward mobility and financial security. But increasingly, those with higher education degrees are finding there are far fewer suitable jobs. More than 14,400 students took their own lives in 2024, according to India’s National Crime Records Bureau. The bureau said that student suicides are increasing faster than those of the overall population.
學生們身處一個高壓的教育體系中，許多人說這種體系往往是在阻礙而不是在支持他們。他們花費數年時間準備競爭激烈的考試，以期實現社會階層上升和經濟保障。但越來越多擁有高等學位的年輕人發現，合適的職位遠沒有那麼多。根據印度國家犯罪記錄局的數據，2024年有超過14400名學生自殺。該局表示，學生自殺率的增長速度超過了總人口的自殺率增長速度。
That frustration erupted in early May, after more than 2.2 million aspiring doctors and dentists sat for an entrance exam that is the only way to get into undergraduate medical school in India. It later turned out that the questions for some exams matched closely with so-called “guess” papers — sample exam questions — that had circulated online before the exam. The government testing agency scrapped the exam and ordered everyone to take the test again.
這種挫敗感在5月初爆發，當時有超過220萬名有志成為醫生和牙醫的考生參加了進入印度本科醫學院唯一途徑的入學考試。後來人們發現，部分考試的題目與考前在網上流傳的所謂「押題卷子」——即模擬試題——高度吻合。政府考試機構取消了這次考試，並要求所有考生重新考試。
It wasn’t the first time. India’s National Testing Agency, set up under the Modi government to centralize the process for many entrance exams, has been criticized for its inefficiency, and a failure to prevent leaks or ensure exams are fair for all.
這種事已經不是第一次發生。印度國家考試局是莫迪政府為集中管理多項入學考試而設立的機構，一直因效率低下、不能防止泄題以及不能確保所有人都能接受公平考試而受到批評。
Getting into a school itself is tough, with too many students vying for too few seats. The pattern repeats itself when students enter the job market, with millions of people applying for thousands of government jobs. Nearly 40 percent of graduates between the ages of 15 and 25 are unemployed, according to a 2026 study on India’s work force.
進入學校本身就很困難，因為競爭者太多，而名額卻太少。當學生進入就業市場時，這種情況再次重演，數以百萬計的申請者要申請數以千計的政府職位。根據一份關於2026年印度勞動力的研究，在15至25歲的畢業生中，近40%的人處於失業狀態。
Stories of college graduates working as full-time delivery drivers have become commonplace.
大學畢業生全職送外賣的故事已是屢見不鮮。
Nidhi Sah, a designer and artist who flew in from the city of Ahmedabad to Delhi to join Monday’s protest, said she considered this underemployment a bigger problem than unemployment. “The cost of quality education in India is high, so a well-paying job is a justified expectation,” she said. So when people work longer hours for lower salaries, or take jobs below their qualifications, she added, “the frustration builds up.”
尼迪·薩是一名設計師兼藝術家，她從艾哈邁達巴德市飛往德里參加週一的抗議活動。她說，她認為這種就業不足問題比失業更為嚴重。「在印度，獲得優質教育的成本很高，所以期望一份高薪工作是合理的，」她說。因此，當人們工作時間更長但工資更低，或者從事低於自身資質的工作時，她補充說，「挫敗感就會不斷累積。」
How has the government responded to the protests?
政府對抗議活動如何回應？
Mr. Modi has so far refrained from speaking publicly about them. Nor has he addressed the demands of the prime organizer, the Cockroach Janta Party, a satirical movement inspired by a disparaging remark by a judge comparing India’s youth to “cockroaches,” which has since turned into a political force.
莫迪迄今為止一直避免公開談論這些抗議活動。他也沒有回應主要組織者「蟑螂人民黨」的訴求——該黨是一個諷刺性運動，其靈感來自於一位法官將印度青年貶稱為「蟑螂」的言論，此後該運動轉變成了一股政治力量。
On Tuesday, a senior government minister said Mr. Modi had addressed students’ concerns about the exam questions leak during an internal meeting, saying that those involved will be brought to account. Mr. Modi also said the safety and security of India’s youth was a priority, according to the minister.
週二，一位高級政府部長表示，莫迪在一次內部會議上回應了學生們對考題洩漏的擔憂，稱將追究相關人員的責任。據該部長稱，莫迪還表示，印度青年的安全和保障是首要任務。
Even that subtle acknowledgment was rare for Mr. Modi, a strongman leader, and is a sign of the delicate dance he has to perform between placating students and ensuring that his political party doesn’t look weak.
對於強人領導者莫迪來說，即使這種含蓄的承認也屬罕見，這表明他必須在安撫學生和確保其政黨不顯軟弱之間進行微妙的平衡。
The C.J.P. has demanded the resignation of Dharmendra Pradhan, the education minister, whom they hold directly accountable for the recent leak. But Mr. Pradhan is a key operative for the B.J.P., credited with helping the party come to power in some recent state elections.
蟑螂人民黨要求教育部長達曼德拉·普拉丹辭職，他們認為他應對近期的泄題事件負直接責任。但普拉丹是印度人民黨的關鍵人物，曾幫助該黨在近期的一些邦選舉中掌權。
As the protests persist, Mr. Modi’s ruling Bharatiya Janata Party has begun blaming the main opposition party, the Indian National Congress, its longtime rival, for exploiting the students’ concerns after it began its own related protests.
隨著抗議活動的持續，莫迪領導的印度人民黨已開始指責其長期競爭對手、主要反對黨印度國民大會黨利用學生的訴求，此前國大黨也發起了類似的抗議活動。
How much momentum is there for the protesters?
抗議者的動力有多大？
Protests in Delhi and elsewhere have been going on for more than a month, ever since Abhijeet Dipke, the 30-year-old founder of the C.J.P. and a graduate of Boston University, turned his joke into a serious movement. Sonam Wangchuk, a prominent education activist, joined the protest and is on the 25th day of a hunger strike. He was forcibly moved by the authorities to a private hospital and is on an intravenous drip as his health deteriorates. But the C.J.P.’s call to march to Parliament on its first day in session on July 20 gave the movement enormous momentum.
自從蟑螂人民黨30歲的創始人、波士頓大學畢業生阿比吉特·迪普克把他的玩笑變成一場嚴肅的運動以來，德里和其他地方的抗議活動已經持續了一個多月。著名教育活動家索南·旺楚克加入了抗議活動，目前絕食抗議已進入第25天。隨著健康狀況惡化，他被當局強行轉移至一家私立醫院，目前正在接受靜脈輸液治療。但蟑螂人民黨呼籲在7月20日議會開會首日向議會進軍，這給該運動帶來了巨大的動力。
Shabana Azmi, a well-known actress, and Arundhati Roy, a Booker Prize-winning author and activist, have expressed their solidarity.
知名女演員莎芭娜·阿茲米和布克獎得主、作家兼活動人士阿蘭達蒂·羅伊表達了聲援。
The protests have also provided opposition parties an opportunity to attack the B.J.P. On Tuesday, Rahul Gandhi, a leader of the Congress party, and also the leader of the opposition in the lower house of Parliament, marched toward the prime minister’s residence with his own list of demands to support the students’ cause. He and his sister, Priyanka Gandhi Vadra, also a Congress leader, and other party members were detained but later released.
抗議活動也為反對黨提供了攻擊印度人民黨的機會。週二，國大黨領導人、議會下院反對黨領袖拉胡爾·甘地帶著他自己的一系列訴求清單向總理官邸進發，以支持學生的事業。他與同樣是國大黨領導人的姊妹普裡揚卡·甘地·瓦德拉及其他黨員被拘留，但隨後獲釋。
“The government has lost the first round,” said Ashutosh Varshney, a professor at Brown University who studies Indian politics. The attention around Mr. Gandhi’s detention has given the opposition an unexpected lift, but its will to continue and the length of the protests are likely to determine whether Mr. Modi can “overcome this moment,” Mr. Varshney said.
「政府在第一回合裡輸了，」研究印度政治的布朗大學教授阿舒托什·瓦什尼說。瓦什尼表示，圍繞甘地被拘留一事引發的關注給反對派帶來了意想不到的助力，但反對派是否堅持抗議以及抗議持續的時間長短可能將會決定莫迪能否「挺過這一關」。
What happens next?
接下來會發生什麼？
Mr. Dipke and other C.J.P. leaders said they would continue to protest until their demands are met. But they said they would not lead more marches to prevent further injuries to protesters from police crackdowns.
迪普克和其他蟑螂人民黨領導人表示，他們將繼續抗議，直到訴求得到滿足。但他們表示，為了避免抗議者在警方鎮壓中遭受更多傷害，將不再組織遊行。
On Wednesday, the protest site in central Delhi was surrounded by security forces and partly barricaded, but the protests stayed largely peaceful. In some places, dozens of delivery drivers waited nearby on their bikes, bearing food that people had sent to the protesters. Some said there was no specific delivery address provided other than “Jantar Mantar,” the name of the 18th century observatory next to which the government has built a dedicated protest space.
週三，位於新德里中部的抗議地點被安全部隊包圍，部分區域設置了路障，但抗議活動基本保持和平。在一些地方，數十名外賣騎手騎著車在附近等候，運送民眾送給抗議者的食物。一些人說，除了「簡塔·曼塔」外，他們沒有得到具體的送貨地址——這是一座18世紀天文台的名字，政府在它的旁邊建了一個專門的抗議區。
Anurag Kumar, who works for Swiggy, a delivery platform, said he was handing over orders to people outside the site because the police wouldn’t allow him to go any further. Mr. Kumar said he had delivered 11 orders since 7:30 a.m. Other workers said deliveries included sanitary pads, ointments and other products that protesters might need to sustain the protest.
為配送平台Swiggy工作的阿努拉格·庫馬爾說，由於警方不允許他繼續向前，他只能將訂單交給現場之外的人。庫馬爾說，從早上7點30分起，他已經送了11個訂單。其他工作人員表示，配送的物品包括衛生巾、藥膏以及抗議者維持抗議可能需要的其他物品。
Mr. Dipke put out a video urging Mr. Wangchuk to end his fast. But the veteran activist said he had no plans to end protests until their demands are met.
迪普克發布了一段影片，敦促旺楚克結束絕食。但這位資深活動家表示，在他們的訴求得到滿足之前，他沒有結束抗議的計劃。

Suhasini Raj和Akansha Bahadur對本文有報導貢獻。

Anupreeta Das為時報報導印度和南亞新聞，常駐新德里。
